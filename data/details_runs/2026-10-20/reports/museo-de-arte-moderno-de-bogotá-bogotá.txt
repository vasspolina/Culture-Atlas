MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE BOGOTÁ (MAMBO): Bogotá, Colombia
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-bogotá-bogotá
Website: https://www.mambogota.com (checked 2026-10-24; loads). The /alianzas page lists only contact emails and names no allies.

OVERVIEW
- Modern and contemporary art museum at Calle 24 #6-00 in central Bogotá, with about 4,000 works (Visit Bogotá).
- Opened in February 1963 under the art historian and critic Marta Traba, who started the initiative in November 1962 and became its director. Declared a District Cultural Heritage Site in 2001 (Visit Bogotá).
- Spanish Wikipedia gives 27 July 1955 as the creation date and says Gloria Zea led it from 1969 to March 2016, followed by the artist Claudia Hakim.
- Rogelio Salmona designed the current building; Spanish Wikipedia dates it to 1985, with about 5,000 m² on four floors.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00, Sundays and holidays 12:00 to 17:00 (Visit Bogotá). The museum's site has a "MAMBO Accesible" education section. No reporting found of current admission prices.

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Bogotá: mixed: Bogotá and national public money, donations, gala sponsors and earned income
- In its February 2026 statement the museum listed national and district public money, grants, donations and earned income such as tickets, rentals and events including the Gala MAMBO (El Espectador). The statement lists "la Nación, el Distrito de Bogotá, becas, donaciones" and the museum's own income. Las2orillas (8 March 2026) calls the museum "una entidad privada" that runs on private and public resources.
- Gala sponsors, read from logo labels on the gala site (some names are incomplete). 2025: Fundación BAT under "Donación Privada"; Constructora Capital and Davivienda at corporate tables. 2024: Chopard and Bauer as main sponsors. 2023: Chopard, Banco de Bogotá, Postobón, Porsche and Scotia Wealth Management.
- Corporate membership "Mecenas MAMBO" starts at COP 15,000,000 (museum site). The donations page names no donors.
- No reporting found of published accounts or a named board.

Red flags: Tobacco
- Fundación BAT: the 2025 gala sponsors page shows a logo labelled "Fundacion BAT" under a "Donación Privada" heading. The page gives no description, country or amount, and the museum's donations page names no donors.
- BAT link: a sponsored article in La República (4 July 2025, marked "contenido patrocinado") says "La Fundación BAT en Colombia" runs social programmes and calls these entities "clave en la estrategia de sostenibilidad de BAT Group". The article does not say who owns the foundation, and no page opened states that this foundation is the gala donor.

Red flags: Protest over the dismissal of the chief curator
- Dismissal: on 6 February 2026 the board announced it would end the contract of chief curator Eugenio Viola, in post since 2019, citing a "revisión integral y permanente" of the museum (El Espectador). The museum later said talks on operational structure and curatorial resources reached no agreement.
- Viola says he formally alerted the board by the end of September 2025 to deteriorating working conditions (The Art Newspaper), and that the board ended his contract instead of ordering an internal review (ArtReview).
- Open letter: more than 140 artists, curators and cultural figures, including David Manzur, Óscar Muñoz and Delcy Morelos, asked the board for a "pública, clara y argumentada" explanation (El Espectador, 23 February 2026).
- Staff: at least 19 employees left in the last year, a reported figure (Las2orillas, 8 March 2026). The museum said it has no formal evidence of workplace mistreatment (El Espectador).
- Director: Martha Ortiz, director since 2024, resigned by a letter dated 8 April, published by El Espectador on 9 April 2026; the letter gives no reason. The Art Newspaper reports the director, whom it does not name, left amid accusations of harassment and a toxic work environment; board chair Ángela Royo oversees strategic decisions meanwhile.
- Unverified: Revista Papel alleges that Ortiz ordered by WhatsApp on 25 July 2024 that Felipe Hernández Parias, dropped in the first screening of an open call for a design coordinator, be given the post. His mother, María Claudia Parias, directs IDARTES and said he had won the contest. Las2orillas relays the allegation; the Papel article was not opened.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum mixes public money with gala sponsors and private donors. The one flagged name is Fundación BAT, listed as a 2025 gala donor: a sponsored article links the Colombian Fundación BAT to BAT Group, but no page opened says who owns the foundation on the gala list. The weak points are the opaque dismissal of its chief curator, staff departures and the director's resignation. Worth watching: the new director and curator, and whether accounts or a donor list are published.
