TANOTO ART FOUNDATION: Singapore, Singapore
ID: tanoto-art-foundation-singapore
Website: https://www.tanotoartfoundation.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Singapore-based not-for-profit foundation for contemporary art in Southeast Asia (stated by the foundation), registered at UOB Plaza 1, 80 Raffles Place (e-flux directory).
- Belinda Tanoto, daughter of the Indonesian tycoon Sukanto Tanoto, founded it (ART SG); Belinda and Anderson Tanoto are its trustees and Xiaoyu Weng, a former Guggenheim curator, is artistic director (e-flux; ART SG).
- Belinda Tanoto calls it "one of the first private art foundations in Singapore" (e-flux).
- It launched with the symposium "Soul Song of a New Organisation" at the School of the Arts on 14 January 2025, during Singapore Art Week (e-flux).
- Programme: a Conversation Series in Singapore, Hong Kong, São Paulo and Gwangju, and support for commissions by Ayesha Singh, Gala Porras-Kim, Cui Jie and Eisa Jocson at the Singapore Biennale 2025 (foundation website; ART SG).
- First exhibition: "Rituals of Perception", 21 January to 1 March 2026 at New Bahru School Hall, curated by Weng, with recent work and new commissions by more than twenty artists and free weekly public programmes (e-flux).
- It plans a permanent space for research, residencies and exhibitions (ART SG).
- Its About page names a curator, a communications manager and Tobias Berger, head of art at Tai Kwun Contemporary from 2015 to 2024 (foundation website).

FUNDING
Tanoto Art Foundation: family-funded: the Tanoto family, whose fortune is the RGE group; the foundation publishes no funders or accounts
- The trustees are children of Sukanto Tanoto (Wikipedia), owner of Royal Golden Eagle (RGE), a group with businesses in paper, palm oil, viscose, construction, energy, property and asset management (Wikipedia).
- Trustee Anderson Tanoto is managing director of RGE (ICIJ, 2024).
- RGE's energy arm Pacific Energy, formerly Pacific Oil & Gas, explores for gas in Canada and runs an LNG receiving terminal and a gas-fired power plant in China (Wikipedia).
- Several works in the first exhibition came from the Tanoto Family Collection (e-flux).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Greenpeace's 2013 report called Sukanto Tanoto, through his business empire, "the single largest driver of deforestation in the world identified by Greenpeace"; APRIL denied the claims and pledged in June 2015 to end deforestation in its supply chain (Wikipedia).
- In December 2012 Indonesia's Supreme Court ordered the palm oil group Asian Agri, an RGE company (Wikipedia), to pay Rp 2.52 trillion in fines, twice the Rp 1.26 trillion in tax it had evaded, and sentenced its former tax manager Suwir Laut to two years in prison (The Jakarta Post, 29 December 2012).
- 30 October 2024: ICIJ reported The Gecko Project's finding that seven of nine former employees said the Tanoto family controlled Borneo Hijau Lestari, a pulpwood group linked to deforestation, and that Anderson Tanoto oversaw it. Unverified: the ownership claim, from those former employees; RGE calls it "unfounded, unsupported and untrue" (ICIJ).
- OCCRP reported in 2021 that Sukanto Tanoto bought the Ludwig complex in Munich in mid-2019 for almost 350 million euros through a Luxembourg-registered company (OCCRP, archive copy).
- None of these reports names the foundation; the connection is that its trustees are the family's children and one of them runs RGE.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation does not say where its money comes from; the only documented link is the family that owns RGE, a group with a gas business, a 2012 tax evasion ruling against its palm oil arm and deforestation allegations. Worth watching: any donor list or accounts, and the permanent space.
