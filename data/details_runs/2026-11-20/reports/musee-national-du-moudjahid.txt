MUSÉE NATIONAL DU MOUDJAHID: Algiers, Algeria
ID: musee-national-du-moudjahid
Website: https://museenat-moudjahid.dz (checked 2026-11-20; down, the server refused connections)

OVERVIEW
- National museum of the war of independence, underground in the esplanade of the Maqam Echahid, the Martyrs' Memorial on the heights of Algiers in the municipality of El Madania; the esplanade also holds the eternal flame, a crypt and an amphitheatre (Wikipedia; APS).
- A first museum opened in 1966 under Boumediene; the present museum was created by ordinance in December 1972 and opened to the public only in 1984 (Les musées au Maghreb et leurs publics, La Documentation française).
- The memorial was conceived under President Houari Boumediene and built by the Canadian company Lavalin, from a model made at the Fine Arts Institute of Algiers under the painter Bachir Yelles, between 15 November 1981 and 5 July 1982; President Chadli Bendjedid inaugurated it on the 20th anniversary of independence (Wikipedia).
- The 96 metre concrete monument takes the form of three palm leaves, with a statue of a soldier at the edge of each representing a stage of the struggle for independence; the calligrapher Abdelhamid Skander and the Polish sculptor Marian Konieczny worked on it (Wikipedia).
- The museum collects and displays objects and memories of the struggle against colonialism, starting with the French invasion of 1830 and focusing on the uprisings in Sétif, Constantine and Guelma through to independence in July 1962; its lower floor is a domed sanctuary with Quranic verses on the walls, and labels are in Arabic (Lonely Planet). Its wings cover popular resistance, the political struggle, the Liberation Revolution and the restoration of sovereignty (Radio Algérie, 22 Dec 2025).
- Its director in January 2025 was Hassan Maghdouri (Radio Algérie, 18 Jan 2025).
- The museum hosts state commemorations: in the night of 31 October to 1 November 2026 the Ministry of Moudjahidine and Beneficiaries held its ceremony there for the 72nd anniversary of the Revolution (APS).

FUNDING
Musée national du Moudjahid: state: Algeria's Ministry of Moudjahidine and Beneficiaries
- Operator: the Ministry of Moudjahidine holds supervisory authority over the museum (Les musées au Maghreb et leurs publics). A 1999 law tasks the ministry with keeping the history of popular resistance and the national movement prominent in national life (same source, citing Law 99-07).
- Programme: Minister Abdelmalek Tachrift received children of Algerians living abroad at the museum on 22 December 2025 under the ministry's "Memory Journey" programme (Radio Algérie).
- Building: the memorial complex that houses the museum was a state commission built by Lavalin of Canada in 1981 and 1982 (Wikipedia).
- Purpose: Maghdouri said the ministry commemorates the Revolution's figures to link its generation with younger ones and protect national unity, following the directives of President Abdelmadjid Tebboune (Radio Algérie, 18 Jan 2025, from APS; Arabic). The minister said the Memory Journey helps to "instil the national spirit" in young Algerians abroad (Radio Algérie, 22 Dec 2025; translated from Arabic).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean state money with an official purpose: the museum is an instrument of the veterans ministry's memory policy. Worth watching: how far its displays leave room for histories outside that line.
