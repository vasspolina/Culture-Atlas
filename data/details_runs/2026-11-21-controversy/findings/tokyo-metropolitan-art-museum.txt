ID: tokyo-metropolitan-art-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 16 February 2014 the museum's deputy curator asked the sculptor Nakagaki Katsuhisa to remove his cardboard dome work from an exhibition hall, saying "It directly sends out a political message"; after negotiation he removed a sheet of paper from the work with words calling to protect Article 9 of the Constitution and criticising Prime Minister Abe's visit to the Yasukuni Shrine (Akahata Sunday Edition via Japan Press Weekly, 2 March 2014).
- The museum's deputy director, Akiko Komuro, admitted that in February 2014 the museum requested the removal or modification of the work, citing a guideline of the city's art museums on works that support or oppose a particular political party or religion (Noi Sawaragi, ART iT, 22 February 2016).
- Unverified: two years earlier the museum removed a Korean artist's work on the wartime comfort women issue after a visitor complained, citing its operating rules, from Akahata Sunday Edition via Japan Press Weekly, 2 March 2014.
