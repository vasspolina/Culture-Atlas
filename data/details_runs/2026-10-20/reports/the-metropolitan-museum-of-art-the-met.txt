THE METROPOLITAN MUSEUM OF ART (THE MET): New York, USA
ID: the-metropolitan-museum-of-art-the-met
Website: https://www.metmuseum.org (checked 2026-10-24; repeated fetches returned "too many requests", so its pages could not be read)

OVERVIEW
- Encyclopaedic art museum at 1000 Fifth Avenue on the eastern edge of Central Park, with a second site, The Cloisters, in Fort Tryon Park (Wikipedia).
- Established on 13 April 1870 by Americans including philanthropists, artists and businessmen; the first part of the Fifth Avenue building went up in 1880 (Wikipedia).
- Calvert Vaux and Jacob Wrey Mould designed the original red-brick building; Richard Morris Hunt and Richard Howland Hunt the Fifth Avenue facade and Great Hall; McKim, Mead & White completed the wings in 1910 (Wikipedia).
- About 1.5 million works in 17 curatorial departments; 5,727,258 visits in fiscal 2025 (Wikipedia).
- The City of New York owns the building and contributes utilities, heat and part of the guardianship costs; the collections belong to a private corporation (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: New York State residents pay what they wish; out-of-state and foreign visitors have paid a fee since March 2018, $25 at the time (Wikipedia). Opening hours and accessibility could not be confirmed.

FUNDING
The Met: mixed: endowment, private gifts, admissions and city support
- Form 990, year to June 2025 (ProPublica): revenue $907.4M, expenses $502.0M, contributions $356.4M, net assets $5.77B. Director Max Hollein was paid $1,804,586.
- Endowment $3.3B as of 2021 (Wikipedia). Oscar L. Tang and Agnes Hsu-Tang gave $125M in November 2021, the largest gift in the museum's history (Wikipedia). Leonard Lauder's Cubist collection, valued at $1B, was reported in April 2013 (Wikipedia).
- David H. Koch paid for the $65M plaza and fountains renovation (Hyperallergic).
- A 2005 US embassy cable (WikiLeaks) calls Shaykh Nasser Al-Sabah Al-Ahmed Al-Sabah of Kuwait "an honorary board member"; it describes no gift.
- No reporting found, on pages I could open, of Bloomberg Philanthropies, Tiffany & Co. or Bank of America amounts.

Red flags: Sackler family, opioids
- The Sackler family gave $3.5M toward the Sackler Wing in 1974 (Hyperallergic, 2018).
- 10 March 2018: about 100 people in the PAIN Sackler group, with Nan Goldin, protested at the wing (Hyperallergic).
- 15 May 2019: the Met said it would stop accepting gifts from Sacklers tied to Purdue Pharma (The Art Newspaper).
- The Sackler name was removed from locations in December 2021 (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Koch Plaza
- David H. Koch paid for the $65M plaza (Hyperallergic, 2014).
- September 2014: three members of The Illuminator were arrested after projecting "Koch=Climate Chaos" on the facade; protesters wanted the plaza renamed (Hyperallergic).

Red flags: Epstein donation
- Documents released by the US Department of Justice include a copy of a $5,000 cheque dated 29 April 2014 from Epstein's Enhanced Education foundation to the Met for the Costume Institute Benefit (ARTnews; paywalled, excerpt only).
- In 2019 a Met spokesperson said the museum had received no major donations from Epstein; the only known transaction was Epstein buying tickets to two benefits in the early 1990s (NBC News).

Red flags: protest at trustees' arms investments
- Demonstrators targeted trustees' investments in the defence contractor RTX during the 2024 Gaza war (Wikipedia).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Extensive criticism of the museum's donors, in protests and press.
1. Hyperallergic: Benjamin Sutton, "Protesters at Metropolitan Museum Chant 'Shame on Sackler'" (2018)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/431941/protest-metropolitan-museum-sackler-wing-opioid-crisis-nan-goldin/
- Reports the PAIN protest at the Sackler Wing against donors who profited from the opioid crisis; the petition had over 32,000 signatures.
- Jillian Sackler said Arthur Sackler died in 1987, before OxyContin existed. The Met declined to comment.
2. Hyperallergic: Julia Friedman, "Three Arrested, Charged in Koch Plaza Protest at Metropolitan Museum" (2014)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/148627/three-arrested-charged-in-koch-plaza-protest-at-metropolitan-museum/
- Protester Kyle Depew called the Koch tie "a gross combination of big money and low culture".
3. The Art Newspaper: Nancy Kenney, "The Met says it will stop accepting gifts from Sacklers associated with Purdue Pharma" (2019)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2019/05/15/the-met-says-it-will-stop-accepting-gifts-from-sacklers-associated-with-purdue-pharma
- News report: president Daniel Weiss called suspending the gifts "the prudent course of action"; the name was to stay on the wing.
- Not criticism in itself.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Wikipedia reports that New York Times critic Roberta Smith called the 2018 out-of-state fee "greedy and inappropriate".

BOTTOM LINE
Most of the Met's money is the endowment, gifts and admissions, with the city owning the building. The weak points are the Sackler and Koch money that protesters targeted, a small documented Epstein-linked cheque, and the 2024 protests over trustees' investments. Watch the donor lists and new naming gifts.
