FIRE STATION: ARTIST IN RESIDENCE: Doha, Qatar
ID: fire-station-artist-in-residence-doha
Website: https://firestation.org.qa/en/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre and residency on Mohammed Bin Thani Street, Doha, opposite Al Bidda Park and near Hamad Medical City (stated by the space).
- Qatar Museums announced the residency in March 2014 and opened the building and its first exhibition, "555", in March 2015 (ArtAsiaPacific; Gulf Times).
- Built in 1982 as Qatar's Civil Defence building; the fire brigade used it until late 2012, when it passed to Qatar Museums. Qatari architect Ibrahim Mohamed Jaidah converted it and kept the original façade (ArtAsiaPacific; Gulf Times; Qatar Museums).
- 24 studios, 20 for residents and 4 for visiting artists; the Garage Gallery in the former fire-truck garage; a theatre, Café #999, a bookshop and an art supply shop (Gulf Times; Qatar Museums).
- Nine-month residency with mentoring, curatorial advice and stints at the Cité Internationale des Arts in Paris and ISCP in New York; 92 artists had passed through by 2022 (Qatar Museums). The Ruwad in Residence strand for established Qatar-based artists began in 2021 (The Peninsula).
- Visiting: Qatar Museums said in 2022 that admission to the exhibitions was free; the site now carries a Tickets link with no prices. No reporting found of current opening hours or accessibility.

FUNDING
Fire Station: state-run: Qatar Museums, a Qatari government entity
- Qatar Museums, described as "a Qatari government entity" founded in 2005 (Wikipedia), took over the building in 2012, runs the residency and mounts the shows (Gulf Times; The Peninsula, 16 November 2025).
- Qatar Museums is chaired by Sheikha Al Mayassa bint Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, who opened the building in 2015 and named its first artistic director in 2024 (Gulf Times; Qatar Tribune).
- Leadership: Hala al-Khalifa was the first director; Khalifa Ahmad Al Obaidly was director in 2022; Wael Shawky became the first artistic director in November 2024 (ArtAsiaPacific; Qatar Museums; Qatar Tribune).
- No reporting found of a budget, accounts, private sponsor or amounts.

Red flags: Gulf state body (Qatar Museums)
- The space is run by Qatar Museums, a government entity chaired by Sheikha Al Mayassa bint Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani (Wikipedia; Qatar Tribune). No reporting found of its accounts.
- Its artistic director Wael Shawky was also artistic director of the first Art Basel Qatar, 5 to 7 February 2026 (Cultured), a fair run in partnership with the state-operated Qatar Sports Investments and QC+ (Hyperallergic, 2025).
- The German artist Volker-Johannes Trieb urged artists to boycott Art Basel Qatar over the treatment of migrant workers (Hyperallergic, 2025). The call targets the fair, not the Fire Station.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No art-press criticism found that names the Fire Station. The criticism of Qatar's art push in 2025 and 2026 targets Art Basel Qatar, whose artistic director also leads the Fire Station.
1. Hyperallergic: Rhea Nayyar, "Does the Art World Need a New Art Fair in Qatar?" (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1014206/does-the-art-world-need-a-new-art-basel-fair-in-qatar/
- Reports critics' objections over the deaths of migrant workers building World Cup 2022 stadiums, women's rights, free speech and anti-LGBTQ+ discrimination; Art Basel said the fair would be independent.
- Names Qatar Museums and its chair; does not name the Fire Station or Shawky.
2. Hyperallergic: Nasser Mohamed, "Don't Believe What Art Basel Qatar Is Trying to Sell You" (2026)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/dont-believe-what-art-basel-qatar-is-trying-to-sell-you
- A queer Qatari writer argues the fair is "artwashing": visitors can live openly while locals cannot.
- Does not name the Fire Station, Qatar Museums or Shawky.
3. Cultured: Devorah Lauter, interview with Wael Shawky (2026)
URL: https://www.culturedmag.com/article/2026/01/23/art-wael-shawky-art-basel-qatar-curator/
- The Fire Station's artistic director answers the censorship question: he calls the talk of Gulf censorship "nonsense" and says he can be censored in Germany too.
- Says Qatar is "much more generous" than many places today. Not criticism; the space's own leadership answering it.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Trieb said migrant workers' "living and working conditions remain catastrophic" in Qatar (Hyperallergic, 2025).
- The Fire Station is run by Qatar Museums, the state body leading this art programme, and its artistic director curates the fair under attack.

BOTTOM LINE
The Fire Station is the Qatari state's own residency: Qatar Museums, a government entity, runs it, and no figures for it are published. There is no private sponsor to check. The weak point is the state behind it and the overlap with Art Basel Qatar through Wael Shawky; worth watching is whether the criticism of the fair reaches the residency.
