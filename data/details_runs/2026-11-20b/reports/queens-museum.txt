QUEENS MUSEUM: New York, USA
ID: queens-museum
Website: https://queensmuseum.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum in the city-owned New York City Building in Flushing Meadows Corona Park, Queens (museum website).
- Aymar Embury II designed it as the New York City Pavilion for the 1939 World's Fair; the UN General Assembly met there from 1946 (Wikipedia) and cast the 1947 vote to partition Palestine there (QNS).
- Opened in 1972 as the Queens County Art and Cultural Center (Wikipedia).
- After a $69 million expansion that reopened in November 2013 it covers 105,000 square feet, with the Panorama of the City of New York, built for the 1964 World's Fair, and about 10,000 objects from the two fairs (Wikipedia).
- Debra Wimpfheimer, at the museum since 2002 and lately deputy director, was named executive director on 11 February 2026, succeeding Sally Tallant, who left for the Hayward Gallery in London (The Art Newspaper).
- Next: the 15,000 square foot Suna Children's Museum, due in 2028 (The Art Newspaper).
- Open Wednesday to Sunday, from 12 pm on weekdays and 11 am at weekends, to 5 pm (museum website).

FUNDING
Queens Museum: mainly public: New York City and State, with trustees, members and friends
- Public funds from the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs support it in part; the New York State Council on the Arts, the trustees, members and friends add to it (museum website).
- Form 990 for the year to June 2025: revenue $7,793,344, of which contributions and grants $7,449,736; expenses $6,377,998 (ProPublica).
- Capital money: $10.6 million from Queens Borough President Helen Marshall in 2006; $26.4 million from the city in September 2021 for the second phase of expansion; $8.5 million from the city in August 2023 and $8.5 million from the state in July 2025 for the children's museum (Wikipedia).
- The children's wing is named for trustee Alan Suna (Wikipedia).

Red flags: 2023 reuse of a Xaviera Simmons structure without permission
- The museum painted the walled structure of Simmons's installation Align, from her 2022 to 2023 solo show, pink, moved its entrance and used it for Tracey Rose's following show without her permission (Artforum, 22 September 2023).
- After Simmons demanded its removal, the museum closed Rose's show for 13 days to dismantle it and build a new enclosure; a spokesperson said reuse is "common practice for museums" (Artforum).

WIDER CONTEXT
- May 2023: according to the Hyperallergic standfirst, a group of cultural workers set out artists' disillusionment with the residency programme and one participant's account of harassment; Wikipedia, citing it, says artists accused Tallant of failing to act on a harassment complaint.
- November 2023: according to the Hyperallergic headline and standfirst, Tallant told workers the museum "is not able to make political statements" and declined to issue a statement on Gaza; Wikipedia, citing Hyperallergic, says staff who worked on a pro-Palestinian statement reported "heightened surveillance" and "intimidation".
- In August 2017 the museum cancelled an event marking the 1947 partition vote, organised by Israel's UN ambassador Danny Danon and co-sponsored by StandWithUs, then reinstated it the same day after politicians including Congresswoman Grace Meng objected (QNS).
- Director Laura Raicovich, who edited a book on the Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions movement, came under scrutiny (QNS); after a board investigation she resigned in January 2018 and her deputy was fired (Wikipedia).
- The first two points concern the museum's last director; the last two are older disputes over the building's history.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum carries no flag. Its weak points are disputes with artists and staff under the last director; watch how the new director handles them while the children's wing is built.
