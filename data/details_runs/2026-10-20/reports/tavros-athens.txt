TAVROS: Athens, Greece
ID: tavros-athens
Website: https://tavros.space

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art space at Anaxagora 33 (1st floor), Tavros (stated by the space), in an inner suburb of Athens, above a boxing gym (Flash Art, Ellena Savage, 22 April 2025).
- Founded by locus athens, Maria-Thalia Carras and Olga Hatzidaki; it opened in October 2019 with Jonas Dahlberg's "(Of) Public Interest", on view 31 October to 27 December 2019 (e-flux, 29 October 2019). Carras co-created locus athens with Sophia Tournikiotis in 2004, and the project travelled between sites until TAVROS opened (Flash Art).
- The space says Tavros is named after the Toros mountain region in Turkey, whose migrants settled in the area in the 1920s (stated by the space, translated from its text).
- The site was a small shoe factory that closed in 2009 during the financial crisis; the team chose it for its rent, its position slightly outside the centre and its potential to work with communities (Flash Art).
- The architecture studio AREA (Architecture Research Athens) designed a movable lightweight steel mobile office for exhibitions and events (e-flux).
- Programme: exhibitions, films, collaborations and public programmes; the 2019 e-flux announcement also listed the Stories development fund for artists, the TechNoTech arts education programme at the 2nd primary school of Tavros, and the Crossings conversations series. The current project is AMAN AMAN, from 8 October 2026 to 30 January 2027 (stated by the space).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Friday 4pm to 8pm and Saturday 12pm to 5pm (stated by the space).

FUNDING
TAVROS: independent non-profit: project funders named at the 2019 opening were a Greek ministry, a Greek foundation, the EU and Faliro House, plus donations from Friends
- At its 2019 opening the space listed project funders: the Hellenic Ministry of Culture and Sports for TechNoTech, the J.F. Costopoulos Foundation for Crossings, the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union for the La Table et le Territoire project with Paky Vlassopoulou, and Faliro House for the first Stories recipients, Rosalind Nashashibi and Adrian Paci (e-flux, 29 October 2019).
- PCAI (Polygreen Culture & Art Initiative) states on its own site, in a page headed "A new project space by locus athens, 2019", that it is "happy to support and partner with" TAVROS and locus athens.
- Friends of TAVROS donate €200 for operating costs, €400 for new commissions and productions, or €1,000 for community work in the Tavros area; higher gifts are accepted (stated by the space).
- Carras says she spends "way too much time" sourcing funding for the space and its artists (Flash Art).

BOTTOM LINE
TAVROS is an independent non-profit. At its 2019 opening it named the Hellenic Ministry of Culture and Sports, the J.F. Costopoulos Foundation, the Creative Europe Programme and Faliro House as funders of specific projects, and PCAI states that it supports and partners with the space. Friends of TAVROS donate from EUR 200.
