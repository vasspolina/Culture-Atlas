WIDER CONTEXT
- After Angela Tecce replaced Laura Valente as president of the Fondazione Donnaregina, the collector Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo and the critic Vincenzo Trione resigned from the museum's scientific committee, named in November 2020 (Artribune, 16 April 2021; Artribune, 8 May 2021).
- Artribune reported that the two felt "sfiduciati dalla mancanza di trasparenza" ("let down by the lack of transparency", translated) after the change of president (Artribune, 16 April 2021).
- In a joint note they said they had learned "da siti e social" ("from websites and social media", translated) that they belonged to a committee "non ancora ratificato" ("not yet ratified", translated) and "in corso di rimodulazione" ("being reshaped", translated) (Artribune, 8 May 2021).
- The Campania Region named a new five-member scientific committee in May 2021 (Artribune, 8 May 2021).
