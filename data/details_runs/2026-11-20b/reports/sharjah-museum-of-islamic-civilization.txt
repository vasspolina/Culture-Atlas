SHARJAH MUSEUM OF ISLAMIC CIVILIZATION: Sharjah, UAE
ID: sharjah-museum-of-islamic-civilization
Website: https://www.sharjahmuseums.ae/en-US/Museums/Sharjah-Museum-of-Islamic-Civilization (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- State museum of Islamic art and science on the Al Majarrah waterfront in the Heart of Sharjah, within walking distance of Souq Al Arsa and the Sharjah Art Museum (Sharjah Museums Authority; The National, 24 July 2026).
- First opened as the Islamic Museum in the Heritage Area on 6 November 1996; on the ruler's orders it moved to the Souq Al Majarrah, a market opened in 1987, and reopened under its current name on 6 June 2008 (Sharjah Museums Authority).
- The ruler, Sheikh Sultan bin Mohammed Al Qasimi, opened the 2008 museum; Aisha Rashed Deemas was its first curator, and a loan show from the Museum of Islamic Art in Berlin ran for four months (Khaleej Times, 9 June 2008).
- Building: the domed souq, decorated with geometric tilework and a gilded dome, with a mosaic of the night sky and the zodiac on the top floor (The National, 24 July 2026).
- Collection: more than 5,000 objects from the 7th to the 20th century in seven galleries, from Quran manuscripts and a Kaaba door curtain made under the patronage of King Fahd of Saudi Arabia to astrolabes, surgical instruments, weapons and early Islamic coins; the Al Majarrah Gallery hosts two temporary shows a year (Sharjah Museums Authority; The National).
- Ulrike Al-Khamis, now director of the Aga Khan Museum in Toronto, was its co-director (The Art Newspaper, 2025).
- Open Saturday to Thursday 8:00 to 20:00 and Friday 16:00 to 20:00; Dh10 for adults, Dh5 for children aged 2 to 12, free under two (The National, 24 July 2026).

FUNDING
Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization: state-run: the Sharjah Museums Authority, a government body of the emirate
- The Sharjah Museums Authority was set up in 2006, on the ruler's direction, "as an autonomous government authority" and oversees 16 museums, this one among them (Sharjah Museums Authority).
- In 2008 it was the Sharjah Museums Department, directed by Manal Ataya, which opened the museum under its plan to open several museums (Khaleej Times, 9 June 2008).
- In July 2026 the ruler appointed Hoor Al Qasimi President of the Authority by Emiri Decree (Sharjah Art Foundation press release); Aisha Rashid Deemas is its director-general (The Art Newspaper, 2025).
- Besides tickets, the Authority hires out the museum's indoor and outdoor spaces for events (Sharjah Museums Authority).
- Many Sharjah museums began from the ruler's own collections (The Art Newspaper, 2025).

Red flags: run by the Sharjah Museums Authority, a Gulf state body
- A government authority of the emirate runs the museum; its president since July 2026 is the ruler's daughter (The Art Newspaper, 2025, names Hoor Al Qasimi as Sheikh Sultan's daughter).

BOTTOM LINE
State money from a Gulf emirate, through an authority the ruling family heads. Watch for a published budget or outside sponsors.
