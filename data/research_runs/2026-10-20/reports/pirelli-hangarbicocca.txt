PIRELLI HANGARBICOCCA – Milan, Italy
Website: https://pirellihangarbicocca.org (ok, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit foundation established in 2004 in a former industrial plant in Bicocca, Milan; about 15,000 square metres, which the space describes as one of the largest single-level exhibition spaces in Europe (stated by the space).
- Pirelli joined the foundation as founding promoter in March 2011, replacing Prelios (formerly Pirelli & C. Real Estate); Marco Tronchetti Provera became president and Carlo Alessandro Puri Negri resigned (Artribune, 21 March 2011).
- Exhibitions open Thursday to Sunday, 10:30-20:30; admission is free. The site carries an accessibility statement; no physical-access detail found on the page read.

FUNDING
Pirelli HangarBicocca: corporate-founded and corporate-supported.
- Pirelli is named as founder and supporter; no annual report, accounts or funding figures published (stated by the space).
- Technical sponsors named in the site footer: Mainstreaming, M&C Saatchi and K-Way (stated by the space).
- Board: chairman Marco Tronchetti Provera; directors Maurizio Abet, Federica Barbaro, Andrea Casaluci (Pirelli CEO), Ilaria Tronchetti Provera; general manager Alessandro Bianchi (stated by the space).
- One step back: Pirelli's largest shareholder is Sinochem, a Chinese state-owned group, with 34.1%; at the June 2026 AGM Camfin (Tronchetti Provera's vehicle) took 12 of 15 board seats under Italian "golden power" conditions, and Tronchetti Provera returned as Pirelli chairman (Linkiesta, June 2026; reported by an independent outlet).
- No reporting found of public grants to the space.

Red flags: Pirelli's Russian operations
- On 4 June 2026 US short seller Grizzly Research alleged Pirelli hides the scale of its Russian business (it estimates about 10% of net profit) and that its Kirov plant shares infrastructure with a state-owned military tyre maker owned by Rostec. Unverified: Grizzly is a short seller with a financial position against Pirelli.
- The Insider reports that Rostec holds 25.005% of Pirelli Tyre Russia, which controls the Kirov and Voronezh plants (reported by an independent outlet, citing the Grizzly report).
- Pirelli says the report "does not reflect the truth", says it makes no military tyres and has instructed lawyers to act (European Rubber Journal, 5 June 2026).

Red flags: State-owned Chinese shareholder
- Sinochem holds 34.1% of Pirelli; Italian government restrictions on Sinochem remain while its stake exceeds 9.9% (Linkiesta, June 2026).

BOTTOM LINE
The space is free to visit and transparent about its single corporate founder, but publishes no accounts. What is worth watching sits one step back at Pirelli: unverified short-seller allegations about its Russian business and Rostec ties, and a Chinese state-owned 34.1% shareholder.
