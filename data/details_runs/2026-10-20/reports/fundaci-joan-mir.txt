FUNDACIÓ JOAN MIRÓ: Barcelona, Spain
ID: fundaci-joan-mir

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Joan Miró's work and of modern and contemporary art in Montjuïc Park, Barcelona, reached by buses 55 and 150 and the Montjuïc funicular (stated by the space).
- Set up by Joan Miró, who conceived it in 1968 with his friend Joan Prats (Wikipedia); the private foundation was established on 7 April 1972 and opened to the public on 10 June 1975 (stated by the space).
- Building by Josep Lluís Sert, a friend of Miró, with courtyards and terraces; a 1986 expansion added an auditorium and a library (Wikipedia).
- Holds the Joan Miró collection, which began mainly as the artist's private collection, Espai 13 for young artists, the Jacques Dupin Library in the octagonal tower, a bookshop, a shop and a bar-restaurant with a garden terrace (stated by the space).
- Maribel López Zambrana, director of ARCOmadrid, was appointed director on 30 June 2026, succeeding Marko Daniel; the board of up to 25 trustees is chaired by Sara Puig Alsina.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10am to 8pm and Sunday 10am to 7pm (winter: 7pm every day), closed Monday; general admission €18 on site, €12 for students aged 13 to 25 and over-65s, free for under-12s, unemployed people and people with disabilities; Espai 13 is free (stated by the space).

FUNDING
Fundació Joan Miró: mixed: ticket and shop income, public grants and corporate sponsors
- In 2025 it took in €11,605,368: €6,486,078 from its activities (53%), €3,701,841 in institutional grants (30%), €1,817,103 private income (15%) and €311,946 other income; it closed with a loss of €164,063 (transparency portal).
- 2025 operating grants: Generalitat de Catalunya €1,258,591, Barcelona City Council €1,016,251, Spain's Ministry of Culture €1,300,000; public grants totalled €3,876,842 including investment money (transparency portal).
- The Generalitat, the Ministry of Culture and Barcelona City Council each name trustees; the Diputació de Barcelona is also listed as a public backer (stated by the space).
- Strategic allies: Fundación BBVA, Fundació la Caixa, the Stavros Niarchos Foundation, Fundació Vila Casas, Integrated Systems Europe, Puig, Damm and Cupra (sponsor page, October 2026).
- Miró Drivers: Fundació Banc Sabadell, Fundació Catalunya La Pedrera, Dale Carnegie, Gallardo Family Group, Cassina and Louis Vuitton. Hotels Circle: Catalonia Hotels, H10 Hotels and Hotel Arts Barcelona (sponsor page).
- Corporate Art Lovers: Fundació Damm, Fundación Dvesta, Arquia Banca, MoraBanc, Colonial, the Barcelona notaries' college and Savills; collaborating companies include Cuatrecasas, RACC, TMB, Aon and the Han Nefkens Foundation (sponsor page).
- The Joan Miró Prize, for mid-career artists, returned for an eighth edition in 2023 with the Stavros Niarchos Foundation (stated by the space).
- Abertis and Sorigué, named in the current record, no longer appear on the sponsor page.
- The director was paid €122,725 in 2025 (transparency portal).

Red flags: la Caixa money comes partly from gas
- Fundació la Caixa, a strategic ally, owns the holding CriteriaCaixa, which holds 28.5% of the gas and power utility Naturgy (El Independiente, July 2026).
- In the first half of 2026 Naturgy paid Criteria €157 million of the €1,174 million in dividends it received (El Independiente).
- No reporting found of a protest or open letter aimed at the Fundació over this sponsor.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. In July 2026 the general press (Que.es, Ara) reported that trustee Robert Lubar, a Miró historian, resigned in protest at López's appointment; Que.es cited sources close to the board on his disagreement with the profile chosen and wrote that for some her profile is too tied to the commercial circuit.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs mainly on its own ticket and shop income, with €3.9 million in 2025 from Catalan, city and Spanish public bodies and a long list of Catalan banks, foundations and brands. The weak point is la Caixa, whose foundation is paid from a holding with a large gas utility stake. Worth watching: whether the new director, Maribel López, who led the ARCO fair until 2026, changes the sponsor list.
