DAZIBAO: Montreal, Canada
ID: dazibao-montreal
Website: https://dazibao.art (checked 2026-11-20; the English site is https://en.dazibao.art)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre and non-profit organisation at 5455 de Gaspé Avenue, suite 109, on the ground floor of the Pôle de Gaspé in the Mile End, Montreal (stated by the space; e-flux directory).
- Founded and operating as an exhibition space since 1980, it was one of the first centres in Quebec and Canada devoted exclusively to photography, and has taken in the full range of image-based practices since the early 1990s (stated by the space).
- It left 4001 Berri in 2009 for a two-year residency at the Cinémathèque québécoise, moved to the Pôle de Gaspé in 2014 and added a digital component in 2020 (stated by the space).
- Programme: exhibitions, a screening room, residencies including one with the production centre PRIM since 2005, the Jeune tête d'affiche competition since 2008, the Sessions programme, Dazibao satellite billboards on Café Cherrier and Marché Bonsecours, and a publishing programme begun in 1996 with more than 80 bilingual titles (stated by the space).
- Emma-Kate Guimond holds the artistic and general direction; the board is chaired by Chih-Chien Wang (Dazibao team page).
- Current season: Lisa Lipton in the gallery and Miko Revereza in the screening room, 19 November 2026 to 23 January 2027 (Dazibao).
- Dazibao states that it has committed to the Palestinian Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of Israel (PACBI), with the support of its board and staff (stated by the space); it was one of eleven Montreal organisations named in an 18 February 2025 joint statement endorsing PACBI, whose signatories do not accept funding from the Israeli government or related bodies (The Link, Olivia Johnson, 4 March 2025).
- Visiting: gallery open Tuesday to Saturday, 12 pm to 5 pm; entry is free; a wheelchair ramp is on the northwest side of the building and the gallery is on the ground floor (stated by the space).

FUNDING
Dazibao: public: Quebec, Canadian and Montreal arts councils and governments
- Dazibao lists financial support from the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec (CALQ), the Canada Council for the Arts, the Conseil des arts de Montréal, the Quebec ministère de la Culture et des Communications and the Ville de Montréal (Dazibao call for proposals and exhibition pages).
- CALQ granted "Dazibao images, expositions, éditions" C$142,150 in mission support and a further C$14,865 in activities support in 2024-2025 (CALQ annual report appendices 2024-2025).
- Dazibao thanks its members for their support on exhibition pages (Dazibao).
- Earned income: the gallery is rented for events such as launches, cocktails and conferences; renters are clients, not funders (Dazibao rental page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Dazibao is clean: public arts money with no corporate sponsor. Its dependence on arts councils is the one weak point, since a cut at CALQ would hit its mission funding directly.
