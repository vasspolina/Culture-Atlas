CRICOTEKA (CENTRE FOR THE DOCUMENTATION OF THE ART OF TADEUSZ KANTOR): Krakow, Poland
ID: cricoteka-krakow
Website: https://www.cricoteka.pl

OVERVIEW
- Museum, archive, gallery and research centre devoted to the director and painter Tadeusz Kantor (1915 to 1990), in the Podgórze district of Kraków at ul. Nadwiślańska 2-4. It also runs the Galeria-Pracownia Tadeusza Kantora at ul. Sienna 7/5.
- Founded in Kraków in 1980 on Kantor's initiative as a "living archive" of his Cricot 2 theatre, which Kantor co-founded in 1955 with Maria Jarema and Kazimierz Mikulski (stated by the space).
- Polish Wikipedia says Cricot 2 rehearsed in the basement of a chapter house and that Cricoteka was at ul. Szczepańska 2 until 2014. Natalia Zarzecka has directed it since 2004 (Polish Wikipedia).
- Building: a conversion of the former Podgórze power station, opened on 12 September 2014; construction started in 2006 (stated by the space). Polish Wikipedia credits the design to the IQ2 Konsorcjum (nsMoonStudio and Wizja), winner of the 2014 SARP Building of the Year award.
- On site: the permanent exhibition "Kantor. Terapia widzeń", temporary shows, an archive and collections department, a café, a shop, and an education programme for children, young people, adults and schools. Events cover theatre, dance, performance and music.
- Roof: RMF FM and PAP reported on 29 October 2014 that a leaking roof closed the Kantor exhibition and the show "Nic 2 razy" shortly after opening. Cricoteka spokesperson Agnieszka Oprządek said about 10,000 people visited in the first three days.
- Visiting: Cricoteka is open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 19:00; the Galeria-Pracownia is open Monday to Wednesday, 11:00 to 18:00. Standard ticket to the permanent exhibition is 25 zł (reduced 15 zł); entry to it is free on Wednesdays. Holders of the Karta Krakowska pay the reduced price. The site has accessibility pages for vision, mobility and hearing needs, and guided tours with translation into Polish Sign Language cost 180 zł.

FUNDING
Cricoteka: mainly public: Małopolska Voivodeship, with the building co-financed by the European Union
- Polish Wikipedia calls Cricoteka an "instytucja kultury samorządu województwa małopolskiego", a cultural institution of the Małopolska regional government.
- The new headquarters, its equipment and the permanent exhibition cost over 50 million zł, and the European Union covered 85% of the cost (RMF FM/PAP, 2014).
- Cricoteka says the building project was co-financed by the EU under the Małopolska Regional Operational Programme 2007 to 2013, with further funds from the Małopolska Voivodeship.

BOTTOM LINE
Cricoteka is a cultural institution of the Małopolska regional government, and the EU paid 85% of the cost of the 2014 building, equipment and permanent exhibition, which exceeded 50 million zł.
