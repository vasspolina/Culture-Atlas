KUNSTHAL CHARLOTTENBORG: Copenhagen, Denmark
ID: kunsthal-charlottenborg
Website: https://kunsthalcharlottenborg.dk

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition space for contemporary art at Nyhavn 2 and Kongens Nytorv 1 in central Copenhagen, with entrances from both; it describes itself as one of the oldest exhibition spaces for contemporary art in Northern Europe.
- It is the official exhibition gallery of the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts, and its activities are run by the academy's Schools of Visual Arts (Billedkunstskolerne). Director: Helene Nyborg Bay.
- Building: the Charlottenborg Exhibition Building, built on the former Botanical Garden site behind Charlottenborg Palace by the architects Albert Jensen and Ferdinand Meldahl, opened in 1883 (Wikipedia). The palace was built from 1672 and has belonged to the academy since 1787 (Wikipedia).
- Programme: exhibitions of Danish and international artists, a cinema on the first-floor mezzanine, artist talks, performances, concerts and a bookshop; the building spans three floors.
- Visiting: Monday and Wednesday to Friday 11:00 to 19:00, Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 17:00, closed Tuesday. Admission DKK 95; students DKK 55; free under 16; free on Wednesdays after 17:00. Elevator to all three floors, automatic outer door, manual wheelchairs to borrow, companions free for wheelchair users, audio description for at least one exhibition.

Red flags: Tobacco, funders tied to Scandinavian Tobacco Group shareholders
- Augustinus Fonden: it fully owns Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, its holding company (Wikipedia). As of 1 May 2025 Scandinavian Tobacco Group's share information page lists Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker Aktieselskab as holding more than 25% of the share capital or voting rights (Scandinavian Tobacco Group).
- Det Obelske Familiefond: established in 1956, it fully owns the investment company C.W. Obel A/S (Wikipedia). The same share information page lists C.W.Obel A/S as holding more than 10% as of 1 May 2025; C.W. Obel's history page gives "+10%" for 2017.
- Both foundations are named as supporters of the 2025 exhibitions (press release of 11 December 2024), and Augustinus Fonden's page lists Kunsthal Charlottenborg among the venues it supported for 2026. These are the only two holders above 5% on the page.

BOTTOM LINE
Kunsthal Charlottenborg is financed by the Ministry of Culture, the Danish Arts Foundation and a long list of Danish foundations. Two of those foundations, Augustinus Fonden and Det Obelske Familiefond, fully own Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker (more than 25%) and C.W.Obel A/S (more than 10%), the two holders above 5% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group on 1 May 2025, and both backed the 2025 programme.
