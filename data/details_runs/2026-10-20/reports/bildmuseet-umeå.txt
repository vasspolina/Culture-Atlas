BILDMUSEET: Umeå, Sweden
ID: bildmuseet-umeå
Website: https://www.bildmuseet.umu.se

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum of Umeå University on the Umeälven riverbank, in the university's arts campus (Konstnärligt campus), a five-minute walk from central Umeå (museum website). It shows Swedish and international contemporary art, visual culture, design and architecture (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1981 by Umeå University, which still owns it (Wikipedia). After 30 years of operation it moved to a new building in 2012; the new building opened to the public on 19 May 2012.
- Building: seven storeys, designed by Henning Larsen Architects with White Architects, clad in larch that is expected to fade to grey (Wikipedia). The arts campus was a collaboration between Umeå University and the developer Balticgruppen Design AB (Umeå University news, December 2010).
- On site: exhibitions, a shop, and a programme of lectures, screenings, concerts, performances and workshops. The museum sits next to the Umeå Academy of Fine Arts, Umeå Institute of Design and Umeå School of Architecture.
- Past exhibitions have featured Walid Raad, Agnès Varda, Ana Mendieta and Faith Ringgold (Wikipedia). It received 76,173 visitors in 2024 (annual report 2024).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 17:00; admission is free.

FUNDING
Bildmuseet: mainly public: Umeå University (a state university), with municipal, regional and foundation grants
- 2024 income was 35.7 million kronor (annual report 2024). Umeå University contributed 26.0 million, of which 2.2 million was a specific commitment from the Ministry of Education.
- The Ministry of Culture gave 0.7 million kronor in 2024.
- External grants in 2024: 5.0 million from Umeå municipality, 1.5 million from Campus X, 1.4 million from Region Västerbotten/Tillväxtverket, 0.5 million from the Kempe Foundations, 0.3 million from Nordisk kulturkontakt, 0.6 million from Nordisk kulturfond, plus smaller grants from Kulturfonden för Sverige och Finland and the Henry Moore Foundation.
- 0.6 million kronor came from partners for costs in joint projects; shop sales and guided tours brought in 0.6 million (annual report 2024).
- Costs in 2024 were 35.5 million kronor, of which staff 16.1 million and premises 10.8 million; the year closed with a surplus of 0.2 million.
- A five-year guest professorship in art and AI (WASP-HS), held by Sarah Cook from 2023, resulted from an application to the Wallenberg Foundations by Umeå University's arts units, Bildmuseet and the School of Architecture (annual report 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Bildmuseet is public: about three quarters of its 2024 income (26.0 of 35.7 million kronor) came from Umeå University, with further grants from the Ministry of Culture (0.7 million), Umeå municipality (5.0 million), Campus X, Region Västerbotten, the Kempe Foundations, Nordic funds and the Henry Moore Foundation, all itemised in its annual report 2024.
