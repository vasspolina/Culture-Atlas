Red flags: 2025 refused sale of two Débora Arango paintings
- The museum sought to sell two paintings by Débora Arango, "Rojas Pinilla" and "Madona del silencio", to the Banco de la República. Arango donated them in 1986 with more than 200 other paintings, and they are among works declared Bienes de Interés Cultural in 2004 (El Tiempo, María Jimena Delgado Díaz, 25 September 2025).
- Colombia's Ministerio de las Culturas rejected the request by resolution 1489 of 22 September 2025, citing "el carácter irrevocable de la donación realizada por Débora Arango al MAMM" and the unity of the collection. El Tiempo reported that the museum had first offered to sell the paintings to the Ministry, which declined (El Tiempo).
- El Colombiano (Ángel Castaño Guzmán, 18 October 2025) reported that the request was made by the then director María Mercedes González, that its disclosure by the online magazine Papel set off a press debate over dispersing Arango's paintings, and that cultural managers spoke both against the sale and for it, the latter saying the bank's cultural network would make the work more visible.
- González resigned as director in October 2025 after 13 years; the museum said she was taking up a public post in the culture sector and would stay on its board, and its statement did not mention the Arango case (El Colombiano, Ángel Castaño Guzmán and Sara Kapkin, 17 October 2025; El Colombiano, 18 October 2025).
