PERM STATE ART GALLERY: Perm, Russia
ID: perm-state-art-gallery-perm
Website: https://permartmuseum.ru

OVERVIEW
- Regional art museum at Sovetskaya Street 1, building 5, in Perm, now in a new building at the Zavod Shpagina cultural cluster on the Kama embankment.
- The museum grew out of Perm's Scientific-Industrial Museum, founded in 1890; its art museum opened on 7 November 1922 and took the name Perm State Art Gallery in 1936 (Russian Wikipedia).
- Its founder is the Ministry of Culture of Perm Krai, and it is a state regional budgetary cultural institution (the gallery's information page).
- Yulia Tavrizyan has directed the gallery since 2011 (Russian Wikipedia).
- For nearly a century the gallery was housed in the Spaso-Preobrazhensky Cathedral, which was handed to the Russian Orthodox Church in 2023; the main exhibitions there closed on 3 October 2023 (Russian Wikipedia).
- The new building covers 21,600 square metres, was designed by the bureau SPEECH, is clad in copper, and combines a new five-storey block with three restored 19th-century industrial buildings (Snob; Iskra newspaper citing the Perm Krai government site). The opening ceremony took place on 31 March 2026 and visitor days began on 2 April (Iskra).
- It holds about 60,000 items, up to 14 exhibition spaces (4,700 square metres), an education centre, restoration workshops, a library, a multimedia hall, a café and a shop (gallery news item of 15 December 2025; Iskra).
- Open Monday closed, Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 21:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 21:00.

FUNDING
Perm State Art Gallery: mainly public: Perm Krai government, with a Sber partnership for the opening season
- The gallery is a regional budgetary institution founded and overseen by the Perm Krai Ministry of Culture (the gallery's information page).
- The Perm Krai Ministry of Property Management and Urban Development handed the new building to the gallery for operational management in December 2025 (gallery news item, 15 December 2025).
- Governor Dmitry Makhonin said the new building would have been impossible without support from the Russian government (Iskra, citing the Perm Krai government site).
- Sber is named on the gallery's homepage as "general partner of the opening season" (title translated). Sber built a digital tour of the gallery, "History of the Place", using its generative AI, with a GigaChat-based guide (Iskra).
- The gallery publishes its charter, its organisational structure and an independent quality assessment for 2025 on its information page.

Red flags: sanctioned bank as opening partner
- Sber is the gallery's named general partner of the opening season, which began in 2026 (gallery homepage), and built the digital tour "History of the Place" with its generative AI (Iskra).
- The United States Treasury announced sanctions on Sberbank on 24 February 2022 (US Treasury press release); Wikipedia dates the European Union sanctions to July 2022.
- Sberbank's majority stake (50 percent plus one voting share) is held by the Russian National Wealth Fund, managed by the Government of Russia (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the gallery is the Perm Krai budget, with the regional ministry as founder and the region as owner of the new building, plus federal government support the governor cites. The one named outside partner is Sber, a bank under United States and European Union sanctions, which the gallery names as general partner of its 2026 opening season and which built its digital tour.
