CATALYST ARTS: Belfast, United Kingdom
ID: catalyst-arts-belfast
Website: https://www.catalystarts.org.uk

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run gallery at 6 Joys Entry in Belfast city centre, run by volunteers as a non-profit, members-based organisation: the committee is responsible to members, and directors serve limited terms (Catalyst Arts).
- Founded in 1993 by MFA graduates of the Belfast School of Art as an artist-led project, led by joint co-directors who each served a two-year term. Nearly 100 people had held the role by 2018 (Catalyst Arts, "25 Years of Catalyst").
- Wikipedia states it was founded to address what was seen as a lack of cultural activity and modelled on Transmission Gallery.
- Programme: an annual programme of exhibitions and independent artist publishing. Wikipedia lists David Shrigley, Ross Sinclair, Phil Collins, Simon Starling and Bill Drummond among artists shown, and states the archive holds more than 2,000 documents on Northern Irish art history.
- Current directors: Niamh Birch, Lauren Morrisson, Nicola Kenny and Tori Clarkson.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday, 11am to 5pm.

FUNDING
Catalyst Arts: Arts Council of Northern Ireland and Belfast City Council, with Patreon members
- Arts Council of Northern Ireland: Catalyst Arts Ltd (Belfast South) is on the list of organisations that benefited from the Arts Council's most recent funding award, set out in the Minister for Communities' answer to Assembly question AQW 26643/22-27, dated 23 June 2025.
- Arts Council of Northern Ireland and Belfast City Council supported the 25th-anniversary programme of 15 November to 15 December 2018 (Catalyst Arts).
- The Patreon page of the space lists 149 members and a lowest tier of €4.50 a month.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Catalyst Arts is Arts Council of Northern Ireland funding, Belfast City Council support for the 2018 anniversary programme, and Patreon contributions. It is a volunteer-run artist collective with rotating directors.
