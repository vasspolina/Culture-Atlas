KX SPACE / KRYLY KHALOPA THEATRE (PRASTORA KX, CLOSED 2021): Brest, Belarus
ID: kx-space-kryly-khalopa-theatre-prastora-kx-closed-2021-brest
Website: http://teatrkh.com/krylykhalopa

OVERVIEW
- Independent theatre and cultural space in Brest, Belarus; KX Space (Prastora KX) opened in November 2014 as a theatre, gallery and venue for cultural events (the theatre's website, BGmedia).
- Founded in 2001 as an activist theatre combining art with critical views on social and political issues in Belarus; called the Free Theatre (Svobodny teatr) from 2001 to 2011, then Kryly Khalopa (the theatre's website, BGmedia).
- Led by director Oksana (Aksana) Gaiko, who calls it the first Free Theatre in Belarus (BGmedia, 8 June 2022).
- On site: a theatre, a gallery, a venue for cultural events and, since 2016, a School of Collective Art that recruits non-professionals each year (the theatre's website).
- The theatre has made 12 productions, five of them street performances, and has played more than 50 international festivals (the theatre's website).
- The theatre's website lists a later production, "Brest HERstory", an audio walk voiced by women and girls from Brest, hosted on the izi.travel platform.

FUNDING
KX Space / Kryly Khalopa Theatre: self-funded: workshops, theatre schools and space rentals, according to its director
- Gaiko said in 2019 that the group solves financing "in different ways," including paid workshops, theatre schools and renting out spaces (The Theatre Times, Alexander Mantush, 4 November 2019).
- She said the production "Chernobyl" was made without fundraising and that the company had no box office before "Brest Stories Guide" (The Theatre Times).
- In late December 2020 the theatre and Prastora KX announced an auction: half of the proceeds were to go to the BYSOL solidarity fund or directly to Brest activists who had been harmed, and the other half to the KX development fund (BGmedia).
- The theatre's website names one partner: the Remus theatre in Warsaw, with which it ran cultural animation projects in 2006 to 2008.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In late January 2021 the financial investigation department came to the space, and Gaiko was taken to the department for questioning after the inspection; a few days later emergency services inspected the premises, and the space suspended all its projects (BGmedia).
- On 18 June 2021 the Moscow district administration of Brest liquidated the socio-cultural institution "Theatre Kryly Khalopa" at the Brest prosecutor's request, citing activity that did not match its charter (BGmedia).
- A Prosecutor General's Office statement on Telegram, relayed by BGmedia, said the theatre organised exhibitions and art presentations "aimed at forming among citizens an approving attitude to unlawful acts" and engaged in entrepreneurial activity in violation of its statute (translation).
- Connection to the space: these state bodies acted against the theatre's institution, while its director described the funding as the theatre's own workshops, theatre schools and space rentals (The Theatre Times, 4 November 2019).

BOTTOM LINE
The theatre pays for itself through workshops, theatre schools and space rentals, according to its director, with a December 2020 art auction whose proceeds were to be split between the BYSOL solidarity fund or Brest activists and the KX development fund. Belarusian state bodies inspected the space in early 2021, and the Moscow district administration of Brest liquidated the institution on 18 June 2021 at the prosecutor's request.
