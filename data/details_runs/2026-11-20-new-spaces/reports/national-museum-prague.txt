NATIONAL MUSEUM: Prague, Czechia
ID: national-museum-prague
Website: https://www.nm.cz/en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Czech state's central museum, whose Museum Complex stands at the head of Wenceslas Square at Václavské náměstí 68.
- Founded on 15 April 1818 by Count Kaspar Maria Sternberg, with the historian František Palacký closely involved (Wikipedia). The journal Živa also dates the founding to 1818.
- The Historical Building by Josef Schulz was built between 1885 and 1891, was declared a national cultural monument in 1962 and reopened on 28 October 2018 after reconstruction.
- The New Building beside it is the former Federal Assembly, assigned to the museum in 2009 and joined to the Historical Building by a tunnel in 2019 (Wikipedia).
- The museum is made up of five specialist museums, among them the Natural History Museum, the Historical Museum, the Náprstek Museum and the Czech Museum of Music.
- The Museum Complex is open daily 10:00 to 18:00. Admission is CZK 360, reduced CZK 260.

FUNDING
National Museum: mainly public: the Czech Ministry of Culture as founder, with record ticket income in 2025
- The Ministry of Culture, the museum's founder, paid an operating contribution of CZK 553,578,393 in 2025 and CZK 34,331,000 in institutional research support (annual report 2025).
- Admissions brought CZK 266,481,441, up 33 per cent on 2024 and the highest in a decade, and rents CZK 17,323,609.
- Total costs in 2025 were CZK 942,943,759 and the year closed with a surplus of CZK 6,819,342.
- State investment grants in 2025 included CZK 45,000,000 for an archaeology and anthropology exhibition and CZK 153,432,780 for a central depository for the Náprstek Museum. Projects worth CZK 67,420,318 were approved under the National Recovery Plan.
- The museum's site shows Škoda Auto and Mattoni as partners of the National Museum, Kores as partner of the accompanying programmes and Czech Television and Czech Radio as media partners.
- All sites had 1.34 million visitors in 2025, 12.33 per cent more than in 2024, and the Museum Complex over 1.07 million. The display of the fossils Lucy and Selam from late August to mid-October drove the rise.
- A ministry audit of the 2024 accounts, carried out from 16 April to 9 June 2025, found nothing to correct (annual report 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Czech state's plus the museum's own ticket sales, and the accounts are published in detail. The commercial partners shown are Škoda Auto and Mattoni.
