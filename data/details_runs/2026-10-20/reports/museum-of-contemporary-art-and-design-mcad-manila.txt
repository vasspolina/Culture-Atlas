MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART AND DESIGN (MCAD): Manila, Philippines
ID: museum-of-contemporary-art-and-design-mcad-manila
Website: https://www.mcadmanila.org.ph

OVERVIEW
- Non-collecting museum on the ground floor of the Design and Arts Campus of De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde, Dominga Street, Malate, Manila (e-flux directory; museum website).
- It is a not-for-profit institution that calls itself "the external face" of De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde (museum website, About page).
- It runs exhibitions and educational programmes showing contemporary art from the Philippines and abroad, with an emphasis on local context, and its founding principle is that "art should be free to all" (e-flux directory). Its projects include Curatorial Conversations (a mentoring programme for young curators), MCAD Commons (a collaborative exhibition platform) and MCAD Publications (Daily Tribune, 4 November 2025).
- Past exhibitions listed by e-flux include Michael Lin (2016), Pacita Abad (2018) and Haegue Yang (2020); the dates are e-flux announcement dates.
- Hours listed by e-flux: Tuesday to Saturday 10 am to 6 pm, Sunday 10 am to 2 pm; the museum's site notes timed entry.

FUNDING
Museum of Contemporary Art and Design: school-run nonprofit, funded by patrons, donations and benefit auctions
- Institutional home: it is part of De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde (museum website, Funding the Future page).
- Art Circle: a community of patrons of contemporary art whose donations support exhibitions, learning programmes, catalogues and Art Circle administration. Tiers are Patron at 25,000 pesos, Donor at 50,000 pesos and Benefactor at 100,000 pesos (museum website, Support page).
- Donations and in-kind sponsorship are accepted, the museum is accredited by the Philippine Council for NGO Certification, and corporate donations are fully tax deductible (museum website, Support page).
- 2018 benefit auction, "Funding the Future": a show of works donated by Philippine and international artists, then a live auction at Leon Gallery, Makati, on 8 September 2018. The funds were to launch five programmes (a yearly conference, postgraduate research support, an in-house publishing arm, biannual artist residencies and a mentoring programme) and support exhibitions for three years (museum website).
- 2025 benefit auction: a benefit dinner and auction on 15 November 2025 at MCAD, with an exhibition of the works from 9 to 16 November. Leon Gallery is the official auction partner. The Daily Tribune (4 November 2025) lists 39 artists who donated works, including Pacita Abad, Pio Abad, Wawi Navarroza, Manuel Ocampo and Paul Pfeiffer. BluPrint (Elle Yap, 7 November 2025) says the goal is to raise at least five years of funding.
- The Daily Tribune says the museum relies on the "generosity of patrons, supporters and its closest advocates".

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is private and educational: a nonprofit run by De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde that raises money through an Art Circle of patrons, donations and artist-donated benefit auctions in 2018 and 2025.
