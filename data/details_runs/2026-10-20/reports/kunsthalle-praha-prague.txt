KUNSTHALLE PRAHA: Prague, Czechia
ID: kunsthalle-praha-prague
Website: https://www.kunsthallepraha.org/en (checked 2026-10-24). The atlas record linked to a news article instead of the space's own site.

OVERVIEW
- Private, non-profit art hall at Klárov in Prague, near Malostranská metro station (Aktuálně.cz), showing temporary exhibitions of modern and contemporary art.
- Founded by the collectors Petr and Pavlína Pudil through The Pudil Family Foundation; opened to the public on 22 February 2022. Director: Ivana Goossen.
- The Pudil Family Foundation was founded in 2014 and bought the building in 2015 (Archiweb).
- Building: the Zenger transformer station of 1932, 5,687 square metres. The facade was kept and most of the interior rebuilt by the studio Schindler Seko over six years (Aktuálně.cz). Archiweb quotes experts from the CTU Research Center for Industrial Heritage saying the building was "rather demolished than reconstructed".
- On site: three galleries, a design shop, a bistro and a café with a terrace.
- Visiting: open daily except Tuesday, 11:00 to 19:00 (Friday to 21:00); 320 CZK adults, 200 CZK for ages 15 to 26, over-65s and teachers; members free. Barrier-free except the Tower and the Box.

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Praha: self-funded: the founders' family foundation, with corporate partnerships and earned income
- The Pudil Family Foundation pays for the project and states it uses no public money (founders' letter, foundation annual report 2023). The foundation bought the building and paid for the refurbishment, about 800 million CZK in total (Aktuálně.cz); The Art Newspaper gives €35m for the refurbishment.
- In 2023 the foundation gave 69 million CZK to the Kunsthalle Praha endowment fund. The one cash gift to the foundation listed in its 2023 report was 90 million CZK from Nelson Anstalt. No reporting found of who owns Nelson Anstalt.
- In 2022 own income (tickets, membership, partnerships) covered about one fifth of costs; the main partners were BMW, UBS and Dentons, and bpd nadace gave a cash gift for education programmes (Kunsthalle annual report 2022). Current partners listed: bdp nadace, MyClinic, Dentons.
- Petr Pudil is a co-founder of BPD Partners, a firm with interests in property development and agrarian chemicals (The Art Newspaper).

Red flags: founder's coal business
- Pudil took part in the 2005 buyout of Mostecká uhelná společnost from Appian Group and sold his stake in 2010 to companies linked to Pavel Tykač (ČTK, 22 February 2022; ČT24).
- Aktuálně.cz says part of his fortune came from coal. It reports that the weekly Respekt apologised for claiming he gained wealth through the privatisation, and that he was never charged in the Appian case. ČTK adds that public sources do not show he took part in the privatisation.
- Money today: the stake was sold in 2010. No reporting found of coal proceeds in the foundation's accounts.

Red flags: opening-day protest over artwashing
- About thirty people gathered outside on opening day, 22 February 2022. The organisers, the group Společnost pro lepší současnost (ČTK), said they could not close their eyes to oligarchs laundering their money through art (Radio Prague) and held an "artwashing" workshop. The Kunsthalle said the organisers had not contacted it (ČTK).
- Artists including Vladimír Turner call the foundation's activities artwashing (Archiweb).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism of the funding exists in Czech news and architecture media, mostly around the opening in 2022; no English-language art-magazine piece making a full case was found. The Art Newspaper's opening report is descriptive.
1. Archiweb: Kateřina Tobišková, "Expected and controversial: Kunsthalle Prague" (2022)
URL: https://archiweb.cz/en/n/home/ocekavana-i-kontroverzni-kunsthalle-praha
- Says the programme is "problematic for some due to its financing" and reports the opening-day protest and the artwashing charge.
- Reports the heritage objections to the conversion. Architecture site, not an art magazine.
2. Czech Television (ČT24): headline "Critics reproach Kunsthalle Praha for ties to oligarchs" (my translation) (2022)
URL: https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/kultura/v-hlavnim-meste-otevrela-nova-galerie-kritici-vycitaji-kunsthalle-praha-provazanost-s-oligarchy-23758
- Names the protest organisers and Pudil's coal history; names no individual critics.
- Goossen said the gallery does not want public funds and will rely more on tickets, the shop, catering and partnerships.
3. The Art Newspaper: Gareth Harris, "New €35m Kunsthalle Praha gallery backed by private collectors opens in Prague" (2022)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/02/21/new-euro35m-kunsthalle-praha-gallery-backed-by-private-collectors-opens-in-prague
- Not criticism: reports that running costs are covered by grants from the Pudil Family Foundation, and quotes Goossen saying her main goal for 2022 was to establish the carbon footprint.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is private and traceable to the Pudil family foundation, which published audited accounts; its founder's earlier coal ownership is the documented objection, sold in 2010 and raised by protesters at the opening. The weak points are the dependence on one donor and the unnamed owner of Nelson Anstalt, which gave 90 million CZK in 2023. Watch the foundation's yearly donor list.
