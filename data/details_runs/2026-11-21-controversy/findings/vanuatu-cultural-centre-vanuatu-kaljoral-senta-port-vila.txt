WIDER CONTEXT
- Minister of Internal Affairs Hosea Nevu ordered the sacking of staff at the Vanuatu National Cultural Council three days after the Sato Kilman Government came to power; the Daily Post reported that the sacking included the director of the Cultural Centre, Lazare Asal (RNZ, 2 July 2015).
- Senimao Tirsupe, chairman of the Malvatumauri Council of Chiefs, warned the Government about "interference in traditional practices" and said the minister could take such action only after consulting the chiefs. The Daily Post reported there was no consultation and no vacancies at the centre had been advertised (RNZ, 2 July 2015).
