CRÁTER INVERTIDO: Mexico City, Mexico
ID: crater-invertido-mexico-city
Website: https://craterinvertido.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Artist cooperative that self-manages a multidisciplinary house-workshop in Mexico City with a risograph press and an experimental radio, among other tools for dialogue and self-publishing (stated by the space; Arts Collaboratory).
- Its members are graduates of UNAM's National School of Visual Arts and of the "La Esmeralda" School of Painting, Sculpture and Printmaking (MUAC, UNAM); Time Out Mexico (2016) says the group formed at La Esmeralda under the name Grupo D.
- Founding dates differ: the cooperative's own site and MUAC give 2011; Kunstinstituut Melly gives 2012 and places its archive space and publishing workshop in the Obrera neighbourhood. Time Out Mexico (29 February 2016) gave the address as Joaquín García Icazbalceta 32, colonia San Rafael, near Metro San Cosme.
- Programmes on its site: Casa Común (the house), Radio Tropiezo, Imaginarios (workshops and residencies) and Taller XD, its editorial project. Radio Tropiezo, run by an assembly, has broadcast online since 2016, first through radiolibre.co and now through the servers of Primero de Mayo (stated by the space).
- Its risograph books carry its own imprint, Cráneo Invertido; MUAC, UNAM's contemporary art museum, holds a fond of its published output (MUAC).
- Took part in the 56th Venice Biennale (2015), the Jakarta Biennale (2015), Sonsbeek '16, the Gwangju Biennale (2016) and the Conundrum of Imagination in Vienna (2017) (Arts Collaboratory).
- Afterall journal (issue 41) published an essay on the cooperative by Sol Henaro, illustrated with its 2012 installation "Pabellón 1994. Solidaridad, Venceremos" (Afterall).
- Visiting: Time Out Mexico (2016) listed free entry, by appointment, depending on the event.

FUNDING
Cráter Invertido: member-run cooperative; its one named backer is the Arts Collaboratory network
- Arts Collaboratory lists Cooperativa Cráter Invertido among its member organisations, beside the DOEN Foundation, which contributes to the network "through its funding, its network and its knowledge" (Arts Collaboratory).
- DOEN Foundation is a lottery distributor: the fund of the Dutch Postcode Loterij and VriendenLoterij (DOEN).
- Arts Collaboratory was established in 2007 by two Dutch foundations, DOEN and Hivos, as a funding programme and a platform for artist initiatives in Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Middle East; the Mondriaan Fund joined soon after (Arts Collaboratory, history page).

BOTTOM LINE
The one documented backer, a Dutch network paid for by a lottery distributor and a foundation, raises no flag. How the cooperative covers its running costs is worth watching.
