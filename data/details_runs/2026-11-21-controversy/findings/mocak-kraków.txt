Red flags: 2024 dismissal of the director Maria Anna Potocka
- Kraków's mayor Aleksander Miszalski began the procedure to dismiss Potocka in early October 2024, and the decision was delivered to her on 8 November 2024; deputy director Grzegorz Kuźma was to act as director from the next day (TVN24, from PAP, 8 November 2024).
- The mayor gave as his reason "niedbałość o renomę muzeum i miasta" ("negligence of the reputation of the museum and the city", translated), and said surveys of MOCAK staff confirmed problems in relations between the director and employees (TVN24, 8 November 2024).
- TVN24 linked the decision to a Kraków court ruling of July 2024 in favour of the curator Lidia Krawczyk, who, as Gazeta Wyborcza reported, had been subjected to mobbing at Bunkier Sztuki while Potocka ran it (TVN24, 8 November 2024).
- Potocka replied that she had been only a witness in that case, that its reasoning "nie daje podstaw do stwierdzenia mobbingu" ("gives no grounds for finding mobbing", translated), and that the decision went against the opinion of the MOCAK branch of the union OZZ Inicjatywa Pracownicza and against an inspection the mayor had ordered (TVN24, 8 November 2024).
- In February 2025 Potocka filed a suit against the museum in the labour court, asking for compensation rather than reinstatement (Radio Kraków, 25 February 2025).
- The Labour and Social Insurance Division of the District Court for Kraków-Nowa Huta held a preliminary hearing on 30 July 2025 and set the start of the trial for 25 March 2026 (Radio Kraków, 30 July 2025).
