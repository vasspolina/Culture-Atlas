WIDER CONTEXT
- In April 2009 about half the centre's staff were on strike against the outside hiring of a communications officer when local candidates had applied, and the strike was continuing (Les Nouvelles Calédoniennes, 7 April 2009; paywalled, only excerpts were read).
- In June 2020 the STKE, the majority union at the Centre culturel Tjibaou, held a stoppage over "un manque de transparence dans le choix des embauches, des intégrations" ("a lack of transparency in the choice of hires and integrations", translated) (Les Nouvelles Calédoniennes, 26 June 2020; paywalled, only excerpts were read).
- In July 2020 the USTKE section at the centre went on strike, claiming about twenty strikers out of some fifty employees, and denounced "dérives" ("abuses", translated) in its management (Les Nouvelles Calédoniennes, 25 July 2020; paywalled, only excerpts were read).
