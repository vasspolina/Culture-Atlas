PRIMARY: Nottingham, United Kingdom
ID: primary-nottingham
Website: https://www.weareprimary.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist-led contemporary visual arts organisation and charity at 33 Seely Road in the Radford neighbourhood of Nottingham, about 15 minutes' walk from the city centre (Primary site).
- It is housed in a Grade II listed former school; in 2020 it bought the building to secure its long-term future (Primary site).
- Registered as the charity and company Nottingham Studios, charity number 1132064; Companies House shows the company incorporated on 12 September 2006 with its registered office at Primary, 33 Seely Road.
- It is home to over 50 artists, with workshops for clay, weaving and design, outdoor and garden spaces, independent galleries, a bookshop and the Small Food Bakery (Primary site).
- It runs a free public programme of commissions, exhibitions and events (Primary site).
- The first stage of its capital development won a RIBA East Midlands Award; the new main entrance on Seely Road is step-free (Primary site).
- Visiting: open Thursday to Saturday 10:00 to 17:00 during exhibitions or by appointment; the first-floor gallery, Forth and TG have no step-free access; large print and braille exhibition texts are available on request; there is no general visitor parking (Primary visit page).

FUNDING
Primary: public and charitable: Arts Council England and the Paul Hamlyn Foundation as core funders, with trusts, a lottery fund and individual Friends
- Primary names Arts Council England and the Paul Hamlyn Foundation as its core funders (Primary news, 20 January 2025); its site says it is an Arts Council England National Portfolio Organisation.
- The same news item thanks the Art Fund, Bagri Foundation, Henry Moore Foundation, National Lottery Community Fund and Paul Mellon Centre for Studies in British Art for support in the financial year.
- Friends of Primary pay £20, £60 or £150 a month for the Blue, Yellow and Red Circle tiers, with one-off donations also taken (Primary support page).
- Donations help maintain the Grade II listed building and keep studios affordable (Primary support page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Primary is public and charitable: Arts Council England and the Paul Hamlyn Foundation as core funders, grants from the Art Fund, Bagri Foundation, Henry Moore Foundation, National Lottery Community Fund and Paul Mellon Centre, and monthly gifts from Friends. The charity owns its building, bought in 2020.
