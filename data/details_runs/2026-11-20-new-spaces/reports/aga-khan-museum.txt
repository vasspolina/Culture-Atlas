AGA KHAN MUSEUM: Toronto, Canada
ID: aga-khan-museum
Website: https://agakhanmuseum.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of the arts of Muslim civilisations at 77 Wynford Drive in Toronto, beside the Aga Khan Park.
- Founded in 2014 (museum About page).
- Prince Amyn Aga Khan chairs the board; Dr. Ulrike Al-Khamis is director and CEO (2023 annual report).
- On site: collection galleries, temporary exhibitions and the Nanji Family Foundation Auditorium for performing arts.
- The museum counted 192,805 visitors in 2023.
- Visiting: Tuesday and Thursday to Sunday 10 am to 5:30 pm, Wednesday 10 am to 8 pm, closed Monday; free on Wednesdays from 4 to 8 pm.

FUNDING
Aga Khan Museum: donor-funded: individual gifts, corporate sponsors and small public grants
- Donations, sponsorships, patrons and friends made up 70% of 2023 revenue; private hire 17%, grants 7%, admission fees 4% and education and performance programmes 2% (2023 annual report).
- The museum raised $11.7 million in 2023 and calls it its most successful fundraising year to date; the Lapis Ball of 28 September 2023 raised over $1 million.
- The auditorium was renamed for the Nanji Family Foundation in recognition of its giving.
- Exhibition and programme sponsors in 2023: BMO, TD Bank Group, Sun Life, Soneri Investment Group, Sunray Group, Texas Jasmine, The Hilary and Galen Weston Foundation and the Weston Family Foundation.
- Lapis Ball presenting sponsors: Anwar and Nasreen Aman, and Steel Canada Resources Limited.
- Government partners: Canada Council for the Arts, City of Toronto, Department of Canadian Heritage, Ontario Arts Council, Ontario Cultural Attractions Fund and Ontario Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Sport.
- The Aga Khan Trust for Culture and the Aga Khan Foundation are listed as institutional partners.

Red flags: partnership with the United Arab Emirates Ministry of Culture
- The UAE Ministry of Culture and the museum signed a memorandum of understanding at the Louvre Abu Dhabi on 19 December 2023, in force until 18 December 2026; it covers residencies, workshops, internships and fellowships for young Emirati curators and museum professionals (AKDN, 10 January 2024).
- In June 2025 the museum opened "As the Sun Appears from Beyond: Twenty Years of the Al Burda Award", the first exhibition under the memorandum; the ministry launched the Al Burda Award in 2004 (AKDN, 18 June 2025).
- The 2023 annual report lists the ministry and Louvre Abu Dhabi under institutional partners, not among the donors or sponsors.

Red flags: sponsor TD Bank fined for anti-money-laundering failures
- TD Bank Group sponsors the museum's TD Pop-Up Performances, 423 of them in 2023, and was a Gold Sponsor of the Lapis Ball.
- On 10 October 2024 the US Treasury's FinCEN assessed a $1.3 billion penalty against TD Bank, which admitted it wilfully failed to maintain an anti-money-laundering programme (FinCEN).

BOTTOM LINE
Most of the money is private giving from named families and individuals, with bank sponsors and modest public grants. The weak points are a sponsor fined for money-laundering control failures and a three-year agreement with the United Arab Emirates Ministry of Culture. Worth watching: whether that partnership carries money.
