PADRÃO DOS DESCOBRIMENTOS: Lisbon, Portugal
ID: padrao-dos-descobrimentos
Website: https://padraodosdescobrimentos.pt (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Monument to the Portuguese voyages of discovery in Belém, Lisbon, on the Tagus, with a sculptural group on the theme of the discoveries (EGEAC).
- Architect José Ângelo Cottinelli Telmo and sculptor Leopoldo de Almeida designed it for the 1940 Portuguese World Exhibition as a temporary structure of iron and cement with plaster sculpture, dismantled in 1943 (EGEAC; Genius Loci, Universidade do Porto).
- Decree-Law 41,157 authorised a permanent version in concrete and stone, with Pardal Monteiro as architect after Cottinelli Telmo's death; it was rebuilt in 1960 for the 500th anniversary of Henry the Navigator's death (Genius Loci; EGEAC).
- The compass rose in the square, inaugurated on 5 August 1960, was designed in Luís Cristino da Silva's studio and given by the Republic of South Africa (EGEAC).
- Remodelled in 1985 with an auditorium, exhibition halls and a viewpoint more than 50 metres up (EGEAC).
- The 2025 programme follows the themes of expansion, empire and colonial memories; it included "Álbuns de Família", photographs of the Black diaspora in Portugal (EGEAC plan 2025). "PADRÃO. House-Obsession", from 17 May to 30 December 2026, gathers images of the monument from 1940 to today, with Ellen W. Sapega as scientific consultant (padraodosdescobrimentos.pt).
- Open daily 10:00 to 18:00 from October to February and 10:00 to 19:00 from March to September; closed 1 January, 1 May and 24, 25 and 31 December (EGEAC).

FUNDING
Padrão dos Descobrimentos: public: Lisbon city council through its company EGEAC
- Lisbon city council owns the monument (DN, 2021).
- EGEAC, the city's company for cultural venues, has run it since 2003, when the company took its present name (EGEAC).
- EGEAC spent €639,431 on its upkeep and restoration in the five years to 2021 (Lisbon city council, quoted by DN).
- EGEAC's 2025 investment plan set aside €132,650 for the monument (EGEAC plan 2025).
- EGEAC as a whole budgeted €44,623,479 of income for 2025, including €24,952,417 from its own operations such as ticket and shop sales and a €19,323,453 operating grant from Lisbon city council under a programme contract (EGEAC plan 2025).

Red flags: Estado Novo dictatorship monument (1940 to 1974)
- Alice Nogueira Alves and Vera Mariz argue that both the 1940 structure and the 1960 rebuilding were bets of the Salazar regime to pass on an ideological message, and call it the regime's "imagem de marca" (brand image) (Genius Loci, Universidade do Porto).
- They write that returning to the same iconography in concrete and stone in 1960 stressed the continuity of the regime's ideology (Genius Loci).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In February 2021 the Socialist deputy Ascenso Simões wrote in Público that "num país respeitável, devia ter sido destruído" ("in a respectable country it should have been destroyed"; translation), as a product of myths built by Salazar's regime. Former culture minister João Soares rejected the idea; the civic group Cidadania LX asked the DGPC that month to classify the monument as a national monument, a request still under review in September 2021 (DN).
- On 8 August 2021 a graffiti about 20 metres long, in English, appeared on one side of the monument, reading "Blindly sailing for monney, humanity is drowning in a scarllet sea" (sic). The Polícia Judiciária investigated it as qualified damage and identified a foreign woman as the suspect (DN, 10 August 2021).
- In 2021 signatories including Chega vice-president António Tânger Corrêa and Nova Portugalidade president Rafael Pinto Borges wrote an open letter against screening Pedro Coquenão's short film "O Princípio, o Meio, o Fim e o Infinito", on colonialism and racism, at the monument. They called it "um insulto mesquinho à nossa História" ("a petty insult to our History"; translation) and said EGEAC contradicted its own mission by hosting it (DN, 1 October 2021).
- Each dispute concerns the monument itself or a film shown inside it.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is municipal and clean. The weak point is political, not financial: the monument's origin as propaganda of the dictatorship.
