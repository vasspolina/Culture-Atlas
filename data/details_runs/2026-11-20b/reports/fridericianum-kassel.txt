FRIDERICIANUM: Kassel, Germany
ID: fridericianum-kassel
Website: https://fridericianum.org/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Kunsthalle for contemporary art at Friedrichsplatz 18 in central Kassel, and the central venue of every documenta since the first in 1955 (Fridericianum; German Wikipedia).
- Built from 1769 to 1779 by Simon Louis du Ry for Landgrave Friedrich II to show the landgraves' collections; German Wikipedia calls it "eines der ersten öffentlichen Kunstmuseen auf dem europäischen Kontinent".
- It was the Westphalian estates' palace from 1810 to 1813; its library burned in a bombing raid in September 1941, with about 350,000 volumes lost (German Wikipedia).
- A Kunsthalle without its own collection since 1988, with over 2,000 square metres of exhibition space; directors were Veit Loers, René Block, Rein Wolfs and Susanne Pfeffer, and Moritz Wesseler has led it since November 2018 (Fridericianum; e-flux).
- Wesseler's contract was extended to 2027 (documenta, 9 December 2021); the company advertised for a new director with applications due 15 May 2026 (e-flux, 2 May 2026), and the Fridericianum holds a farewell for Wesseler on 1 December 2026 (Fridericianum).
- 2026 programme: solo shows by Catherine Opie, Peter Fischli and Mohammed Sami, a show by Charles Ray, and interventions by Raque Ford, Karl Holmqvist and Kerstin Brätsch (Fridericianum).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 11 am to 6 pm, Thursday to 8 pm; 6 euros, reduced 4 euros, free on Wednesdays and for under-18s; ramp and two lifts (Fridericianum).

FUNDING
Fridericianum: public: the City of Kassel and the State of Hesse in equal shares, through documenta und Museum Fridericianum gGmbH, with private backers on single shows
- The building is owned by the State of Hesse and run by documenta und Museum Fridericianum gGmbH (German Wikipedia).
- That non-profit company is "funded to an equal extent by the City of Kassel and the State of Hesse", its two shareholders; it also stages documenta and runs the documenta archiv and the documenta Halle (documenta Code of Conduct; documenta). Its managing director is Andreas Hoffmann (Monopol).
- The Catherine Opie show of 2026 was sponsored by Maria Seferian, and the Kunsthalle receives "further significant support" from the Rudolf Augstein Stiftung; Monopol was media partner (Fridericianum).
- The International Friends of documenta association supports the company; the director must raise third-party money (documenta; e-flux).

Red flags: 2025 Marisa Merz show cancelled over the documenta code of conduct
- After the antisemitism scandal at documenta fifteen, the company adopted a code of conduct, posted on 3 February 2025, that treats the IHRA working definition of antisemitism as binding (documenta; Hyperallergic).
- The code lets documenta comment on works it judges in conflict with its principles and contextualise them "in the immediate visual context of the exhibited works"; ArtAsiaPacific reported that this lets the supervisory board step in.
- A Marisa Merz show planned from late August 2025 to January 2026 did not take place: Beatrice Merz, president of the Fondazione Merz, withdrew because she does not agree with every point of the IHRA definition and preferred the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism (Monopol, 4 December 2025).
- Beatrice Merz: "Ich bin der Überzeugung, dass Kunst nicht durch Grenzen eingeschränkt werden sollte" (Monopol; translated: "I am convinced that art should not be restricted by boundaries").
- Hoffmann told Monopol the conditions "nicht vollständig gedeckt" (translated: did not fully match) and that the code binds staff, not the artistic team or outside parties; works by Robert Grosvenor and Portia Zvavahera were shown instead (Monopol).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism is of the documenta code of conduct that governs the Fridericianum, not of its programme; the one case that names it is the Merz withdrawal.
1. Monopol: editorial staff, "Ausstellung in Kassel wegen Code of Conduct der Documenta abgesagt" (2025)
URL: https://www.monopol-magazin.de/ausstellung-kassel-wegen-code-conduct-der-documenta-abgesagt
- Reports the Merz withdrawal and Hoffmann's answer; names the Fridericianum.
2. Finestre sull'Arte: editorial staff, "Germany, an exhibition dedicated to Arte Povera artist Marisa Merz will not take place" (2025)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/en/news/germany-skips-marisa-merz-exhibition-disagreement-over-definition-of-anti-semitism
- States that the IHRA definition is disputed because it lends itself to classifying protests against the Israeli government as antisemitic; names the Fridericianum.
3. Hyperallergic: Adam Broomberg, "The Twisted Logic of Documenta's 'Artistic Freedom'" (10 September 2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1040816/the-twisted-logic-of-documentas-artistic-freedom/
- Argues the IHRA-based code means documenta 16 must exclude works critical of Israel, so "the real curatorial responsibility of the team is the execution of omission".
- Calls for a boycott of these institutions.
- Does not name the Fridericianum. Now behind a free registration wall; read in an archived copy.

WIDER CONTEXT
- ArtAsiaPacific (2025) reported that critics fear the code will be used to censor pro-Palestinian artists, and linked it to the Bundestag resolution of November 2024 that curbs state funding for projects with "antisemitic aims and content".
- The Fridericianum is run by the same company and bound by the same code, which is how the Merz show fell through.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is public, from Kassel and Hesse in equal shares, with named private backers on single shows. The weak point is political control through the documenta code of conduct. Watch who succeeds Wesseler and whether more lenders withdraw before documenta 16.
