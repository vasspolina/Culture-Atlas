ID: the-nelson-atkins-museum-of-art

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 21 October 2020 the museum announced a 15 percent staff reduction and a 25 percent budget cut to about $26 million, citing the pandemic; 36 employees were laid off, among them photography curator Jane L. Aspinwall (KC Studio, Julius Karash, 23 November 2020).
- Keith Davis, founder and senior curator of the photography department, resigned in a 3 November 2020 email after trying to save Aspinwall's position (KC Studio, 23 November 2020).
- A petition titled "Nelson-Atkins Workers Deserve Respect and Dignity" asked the museum for no further layoffs through fiscal year 2021 and for transparent budgeting; director Julián Zugazagoitia said "many of the things that they're requesting are things that we are already involved in and we're doing" (KC Studio, 23 November 2020).
