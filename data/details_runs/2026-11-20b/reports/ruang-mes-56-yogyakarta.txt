RUANG MES 56: Yogyakarta, Indonesia
ID: ruang-mes-56-yogyakarta
Website: https://www.mes56.org (checked 2026-11-20; shows only an "under construction" page)

OVERVIEW
- Artist collective in Yogyakarta that develops photography and contemporary art through a critical, contextual approach, and runs a shared house used as a studio, an education space, a playground and a home (Good News from Indonesia, 20 October 2026).
- Formed in 2002 by photography students of the 1994, 1995 and 1996 intakes at the Institut Seni Indonesia (ISI) Yogyakarta; Wimo Ambala Bayang co-founded it with friends from ISI and was still an active member in 2018 (Balairung, 2018; oddweb.org).
- ArtAsiaPacific calls it Indonesia's first contemporary photography collective; its members are predominantly male, and many studied at the state-run ISI, the first school in the country to offer a photography degree.
- The name comes from number 56, the house the students rented together on Jalan Kolonel Sugiyono, whose front room became their gallery (Balairung, 2018).
- In 2018 the collective, then 18 members, worked from a house at Jalan Mangkuyudan 53A, with the Afdruk 56 analogue darkroom opened in July 2017 and a Tuesday film night, Cafe Society, that sells food (Balairung, 2018).
- Its first exhibition, "Keren dan Beken", was part of Biennale Jogja VII in 2003; the house also hosts workshops and exhibitions (Balairung, 2018).
- Shows abroad include "The History of Boys: The MES 56 and Beyond" at DECK, Singapore, to 23 April 2017 (ArtAsiaPacific) and "Foam X Ruang Mes 56" at Foam, Amsterdam, in 2017 (Artdaily).
- Current show: "Di Atas Hitam dan Putih", with 17 members, at EDSU House, Sleman, from 16 October 2026 to 3 January 2027, Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 19:00 (Good News from Indonesia).

FUNDING
Ruang MES 56: self-funded: the members' own money and programmes
- Formed in 2002 with self-funding (oddweb.org biographies).
- The collective uses the practice of gotong royong (mutual help) to meet everyday needs, "creating programmes to sustain the collective independently for more than fifteen years" (Foam announcement, Artdaily, 2017).
- Partners on single projects: "Alhamdulillah We Made It", shown at Jimei x Arles in 2017, was a collaboration with the Jesuit Refugee Service Yogyakarta (Jimei x Arles); the Di Atas Hitam dan Putih show is a collaboration with EDSU House (Good News from Indonesia).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: a member-run collective living on its own money and programmes. Worth watching is any move to outside grants.
