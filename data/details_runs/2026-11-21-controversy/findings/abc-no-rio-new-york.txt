ID: abc-no-rio-new-york

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 1994 the City of New York stopped accepting ABC No Rio's rent checks, and for three years the group fought eviction from its city-owned building. Asian Americans for Equality planned to convert the four-storey tenement into affordable housing (The New York Times, Andrew Jacobs, "Arts Group Wins Eviction Battle", 23 February 1997).
- After a sit-in at the headquarters of the Department of Housing Preservation and Development on 5 February 1997, outgoing commissioner Liliam Barrios-Paoli agreed to drop the eviction proceedings. Under the tentative agreement about six squatters would leave the building, and the group had to raise at least $100,000 for renovation. ABC No Rio's president, Dave Powell, said "It just goes to show you that resistence pays" (The New York Times).
- ABC No Rio states that more than 2,500 people signed petitions against the eviction and that five supporters were arrested in New York in February 1997 while demonstrating (ABC No Rio).
