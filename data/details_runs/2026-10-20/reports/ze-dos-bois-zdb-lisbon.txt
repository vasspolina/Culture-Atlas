GALERIA ZÉ DOS BOIS (ZDB): Lisbon, Portugal
ID: ze-dos-bois-zdb-lisbon
Website: https://zedosbois.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit cultural association and art centre in a 2,500 m2 palace at Rua da Barroca 59 in the Bairro Alto, Lisbon, with a second venue, ZDB 8 Marvila (stated by ZDB).
- Created in 1994 as a non-profit by a civic initiative of fifteen founders, who wanted an alternative structure to the institutional and commercial panorama (stated by ZDB). Natxo Checa is one of the founders and the current director (Observador, Agência Lusa, 29 November 2024).
- Programmes: visual arts exhibitions; NEGÓCIO, for performing arts, with about 12 premieres a year; ZDBmüzique, for experimental, improvised and electroacoustic music; an education service founded in 2005; a bookshop and a rooftop terrace (stated by ZDB). ZDB says it hosts more than 150 events a year.
- Visiting: exhibitions Monday to Saturday, 18:00 to 22:00. Annual membership costs 30 euros and includes free entry to all exhibitions (stated by ZDB).
- On the night of 8 February 2026 a fire broke out in a first-floor room; four firefighters were burned and the Lisbon fire commander said the floor and walls were damaged. Checa said the structure was not damaged. ZDB announced it was closed to the public for cleaning and would reopen as soon as possible (Observador, Larissa Faria with Lusa, 8 February 2026).

FUNDING
Galeria Zé dos Bois (ZDB): public and earned income: Direção-Geral das Artes, Lisbon City Council and paid attendance at shows and exhibitions
- ZDB's mission page says it is funded by the Portuguese Republic (Culture, Youth and Sports / Direção-Geral das Artes), the Instituto de Gestão Financeira da Segurança Social and the Lisbon City Council.
- The association's annual support began at 25,000 euros and now stands at 400,000 euros; its annual budget is about one million euros, 60% of it from its own revenue, mainly paid attendance at shows and exhibitions. It also receives quadrennial support from the Direção-Geral das Artes (Checa interview, Agência Lusa, via Observador, 29 November 2024).
- ZDB is part of the Rede Portuguesa de Arte Contemporânea (stated by ZDB).

BOTTOM LINE
ZDB is a non-profit association funded by the Direção-Geral das Artes, the Instituto de Gestão Financeira da Segurança Social and Lisbon City Council, with about 60% of an annual budget of about one million euros coming from its own revenue (Lusa via Observador, 29 November 2024).
