ZOLAK: Vilnius, Lithuania
ID: zolak-vilnius
Website: https://zolak.lt

OVERVIEW
- Belarusian community-run venue, "ZOLAK Micro Art Residence", on the third floor at Vytenio g. 50D, Vilnius, entered through the former ELFA factory grounds at Panerių g. 37 (stated by Zolak).
- Reform.news reported on 11 June 2025 that the space would open on 14 June 2025, and named its creators as the activist Piotr Markielau, the writer Alina Nahornaja and the photographer Siarhei Hudzilin. Zolak's footer credits Markielau.
- Reform.news puts the space at about 150 m2, with an event space, coworking area, rest zone, kitchen and a sauna. Zolak lists a gallery of 39.14 m2, a crypto bar of 21.36 m2, a coworking and meeting room, a kitchen and shower, and guest rooms for up to 8 people.
- Programme: film screenings, discussions, raves, coworking and residencies for artists and activists; the opening on 14 June was to bring workshops, lectures and a music party, and a masterclass by the artist Andrei Busel had already run (Reform.news).
- Aim: to give Belarusian creators and activists a venue, equipment and a community; the stated values are freedom and radical self-expression (Reform.news).
- Visiting: Zolak says it is open 24 hours. It is booked as a space, with hourly rates on weekdays of 20 euros by day (10:00 to 20:00), 30 euros in the evening (20:00 to 00:00) and 40 euros at night (00:00 to 05:30); Bitcoin payments get a 10% discount. Events run on a donations basis (stated by Zolak).

FUNDING
Zolak: privately funded: private contributions, a residency subscription, space rentals and event donations; does not plan to take grants
- Reform.news (11 June 2025) reports that Zolak exists on private contributions and a residency programme and does not plan to be funded by grants; it names financial sustainability as a stated priority.
- Residency is a monthly subscription, and residents can also contribute skills or ideas (Reform.news). The space can also be rented for private events (Reform.news).
- Zolak charges for rentals in euros, converted to sats for Bitcoin payments, and accepts cash and bank transfer (stated by Zolak).
- Zolak offers free short-term accommodation to visiting activists and artists (stated by Zolak).

BOTTOM LINE
Zolak is a privately run Belarusian venue in Vilnius that Reform.news reported would open on 14 June 2025. Its money is private contributions, residency subscriptions, rentals and event donations, and it says it does not plan to take grants (Reform.news, 11 June 2025).
