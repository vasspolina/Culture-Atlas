RAHMI M. KOÇ MUSEUM: Istanbul, Turkey
ID: rahmi-m-koc-museum
Website: https://www.rmk-museum.org.tr (checked 2026-11-20; its security certificate could not be verified)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of industrial, transport and communications history at Hasköy on the northern shore of the Golden Horn, covering 27,000 square metres in two sections, the Lengerhane Building and the Hasköy Dockyard (Koç Holding; Wikipedia).
- Founded by Rahmi M. Koç, honorary chairman of Koç Holding, in 1991; it opened on 13 December 1994. A visit to the Henry Ford Museum in Dearborn, Michigan inspired it (Wikipedia).
- The Lengerhane, built under Sultan Ahmed III for dockyard work and left derelict after a 1984 roof fire, was bought by the foundation in 1991 and restored over two and a half years; it was named after Mustafa V. Koç in 2016. The Hasköy Dockyard, bought in 1996, joined the museum in 2001 (Koç Holding; Wikipedia).
- What is there: land, rail, air and sea transport, machinery, communications, scientific instruments, models and toys, mostly from Rahmi M. Koç's private collection, with donations and loans (Koç Holding).
- Sister sites: the Ankara museum in Çengelhan (opened April 2005, leased from the Ankara Regional Directorate of Foundations) and Safranhan (added May 2016), and museums and a library at Ayvalık and Cunda (Koç Holding; museum website).
- Awards: Rahmi M. Koç received the Council of Europe Museum Special Prize in 1996; the museum won the Ministry of Culture's Culture and Arts Grand Prize in 2001 (Koç Holding).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm, weekends 9:30am to 6pm, closed Monday; adults 550 lira, students 280 lira, Golden Horn boat tours extra (museum website).

FUNDING
Rahmi M. Koç Museum: private: the Rahmi M. Koç Museology and Cultural Foundation, built on the Koç family's Koç Holding fortune
- Owner and operator: the Rahmi M. Koç Museology and Cultural Foundation, whose Board of Trustees Rahmi M. Koç chaired; it bought both Istanbul buildings and restored them (Koç Holding).
- Ankara: the foundation restored Çengelhan from 2003 to 2005 and Safranhan over four years to 2016 (Koç Holding).
- Ayvalık and Cunda: the Council of Foundations allocated the Taksiyarhis Church to the foundation in 2011; Ark İnşaat restored it, and Rahmi M. Koç paid for restoring a windmill and the Agios Yannis Church (Koç Holding). Muhtar Kent gave more than 1,300 books from his late father's library to the Sevim and Necdet H. Kent Library (Koç Holding).
- Income: admission tickets, boat tours and on-site cafes and restaurants (museum website; Koç Holding).
- Koç Holding presents the museums in the sustainability section of its own website (Koç Holding).

Red flags: founder's fortune from Koç Holding, owner of the refiner Tüpraş and the defence maker Otokar
- Tüpraş operates the vast majority of Turkey's oil refineries and makes up almost half of Koç Holding's revenue (Wikipedia).
- Koç Holding's subsidiaries include Otokar Otomotiv ve Savunma Sanayi, and the group works in defence (Wikipedia).
- Rahmi Koç chaired Koç Holding from 1984 to 2003 and remains honorary chairman with a board seat (Wikipedia).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2026 the Justice Minister announced a prosecutors' investigation into Rahmi Koç over a joke about a Kurdish woman made at a hospital opening in İzmir on 5 June; Koç apologised (Hürriyet Daily News, 7 June 2026).
- On 7 June 2026 gunmen fired on the Istanbul headquarters of Otokoç, a Koç Holding company; authorities were investigating a link to the joke (Hürriyet Daily News).
- The museum is the founder's personal foundation, which ties it to both events.

BOTTOM LINE
Private family money from a conglomerate whose largest business is oil refining and which has a defence arm.
