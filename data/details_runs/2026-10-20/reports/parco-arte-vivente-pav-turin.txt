PAV, PARCO ARTE VIVENTE: Turin, Italy
ID: parco-arte-vivente-pav-turin
Website: https://parcoartevivente.it

OVERVIEW
- Experimental contemporary art centre at Via Giordano Bruno 31, Turin, built on the relationship between art and nature, with an open-air exhibition site and an interactive museum on art, biotechnology and ecology (PAV website; City of Turin notice, 2025).
- Conceived by the artist Piero Gilardi (Finestre sull'Arte, 9 June 2020). The City of Turin notice cites a city executive deliberation of 15 March 2005 on the complex. The association acPav ran the project (Artribune, Helga Marsala, 13 January 2014). The PAV website names Enrico Bonanate as director.
- The first environmental installation, "Trèfle" by Dominique Gonzalez-Foerster, dates from 2006 (PAV website).
- Building and site: the City of Turin owns the complex; the park occupies a former industrial area of about 21,600 square metres with 21 installations made of organic and living materials, and the building hosts four to five temporary exhibitions a year (City of Turin notice).
- Visitors: about 25,000 a year (City of Turin notice). Artribune reported 70,000 visitors in 2013 against 20,000 in 2012.
- Education work includes school workshops and art therapy workshops for people with disabilities (City of Turin notice).
- Open Friday 15:00 to 18:00 and Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 18:00; schools and groups book Tuesday to Friday (PAV website). Finestre sull'Arte reported a 2 euro ticket on 9 June 2020.

FUNDING
PAV, Parco Arte Vivente: mainly public: City of Turin and Regione Piemonte
- City of Turin: owns the site. It paid 100,000 euros on 23 June 2025 for 2024 activities and assigned another 100,000 euros for 2025 (decree no. 4641 of 29 July 2025) (PAV website, transparency section for 2025).
- Regione Piemonte: paid a balance of 28,840 euros on 13 August 2025 for 2024 activities and assigned 64,271 euros for 2025 (decree of 9 December 2025) (PAV website, transparency section for 2025).
- Site concession: by determination no. 5860 of 29 September 2025 the City of Turin opened a public call to award the complex to a non-profit body for six to twenty years, at an estimated annual rent of 36,000 euros, with bids due by 3 November 2025 (City of Turin notice).
- Earlier years: Regione Piemonte decree no. 1171 of 25 November 2009 awarded the Associazione Culturale Parco d'Arte Vivente 200,000 euros for its 2009 programme and running costs. In January 2014 Artribune reported that the PAV had cut its costs from about 1 million to 500,000 euros and its staff from 14 to 7 after city culture cuts and a delayed renewal of its agreement with Turin's museum system.

BOTTOM LINE
PAV is financed by the City of Turin, which owns the site and paid 100,000 euros in June 2025, and by Regione Piemonte, which paid 28,840 euros in August 2025 and assigned 64,271 euros for 2025.
