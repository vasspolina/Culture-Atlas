Red flags: 2023 plan to replace the museum, dropped in 2024
- In December 2023 the government revealed plans to build a museum of national development and achievements on the Science Museum's Tsim Sha Tsui site; lawmaker Edward Leung Hei, quoting the Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism Kevin Yeung, said the Heritage Museum in Sha Tin would be demolished and rebuilt as the new Science Museum, due for completion in 2031 (The Standard, 8 December 2023).
- Leung said he was concerned whether the government could find another location for the Heritage Museum, and that officials had promised a separate new museum dedicated to pop culture (The Standard, 8 December 2023).
- Unverified: a change.org petition started on 9 December 2023 by the artist Loretta Lau condemned the decision to abandon the museum, said it "is bound to result in the loss of a substantial amount of crucial archives and artworks" and had 800 supporters when it closed, from change.org.
- On 7 March 2024 the director of Leisure and Cultural Services, Vincent Liu Ming-kwong, said a new site would be found for the national museum after public feedback; the Science Museum stays in Tsim Sha Tsui and the Heritage Museum stays in place (The Standard, 7 March 2024).
