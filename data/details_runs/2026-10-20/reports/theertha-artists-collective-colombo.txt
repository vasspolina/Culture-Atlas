THEERTHA INTERNATIONAL ARTISTS' COLLECTIVE: Colombo, Sri Lanka
ID: theertha-artists-collective-colombo
Website: https://theertha.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist-led non-profit arts organisation in Colombo that runs residencies, workshops, exhibitions and publications (Khoj Studios, Mediamatic).
- Founded in 2000 by 11 visual artists (Khoj Studios); Mediamatic's listing says it is run by 17 visual artists.
- Started its own exhibition space, the Red Dot Gallery, in 2007 to show experimental and contemporary art (Khoj Studios, artscollaboratory.org). The Sunday Times (30 September 2007) places the gallery at 36A Baddegana Road South, Pita Kotte.
- Address: 39/4A D. S. Senanayake Mawatha, Borella, Colombo 08 (Khoj Studios; TransArtists).
- Founding partner, with Vasl (Pakistan), Khoj (India), Sutra (Nepal) and Britto Arts Trust (Bangladesh), of a South Asia-based artist network, listed on the Khoj Studios page as SANA with a start date of 1 February 2004.
- Programmes: international artist residencies and workshops, art teacher training, publications on Sri Lankan art in local languages and English, and an archive of Sri Lankan art (Khoj Studios, Mediamatic).
- Residencies: open only to self-sponsored artists for 2 weeks to 2 months; the space offers two non-air-conditioned rooms, a kitchen, a studio, a library and Wi-Fi (TransArtists).

FUNDING
Theertha: artist-led non-profit: participant in the Arts Collaboratory programme of the Dutch foundations Hivos and DOEN, plus self-sponsored residency fees
- Arts Collaboratory: Theertha is listed among the programme's participating organisations for 2013 to 2015 (e-flux, "A new phase", 4 December 2013) and as the Sri Lanka partner (Wikipedia). The programme was set up in 2007 by the Dutch foundations Hivos and the DOEN Foundation as a financial support structure and exchange programme (e-flux); Wikipedia says it provides funding and supports knowledge sharing and artistic exchange.
- Residency participants pay their own costs (TransArtists).
- Theertha describes itself as an autonomous, artist-led non-profit initiative (artscollaboratory.org).

BOTTOM LINE
Theertha takes part in the Arts Collaboratory programme set up by the Dutch foundations Hivos and DOEN Foundation, and residency participants pay their own costs.
