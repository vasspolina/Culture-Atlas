MUSÉE NATIONAL DU BURKINA FASO: Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso
ID: mus-e-national-du-burkina-faso-ouagadougou
Website: https://www.museenational.gov.bf

OVERVIEW
- State museum on a 29-hectare site in Ouagadougou, facing the Charles de Gaulle paediatric hospital, at the end of Boulevard Thomas Sankara (Wikipedia).
- Created in 1962 and long a project; the foundation stone was laid on 23 October 2000 and the museum was inaugurated on 23 December 2004. It became a state public institution of a scientific, cultural and technical character (EPSCT) on 20 March 2002 (Wikipedia).
- Mission: to collect and conserve the material and immaterial witnesses of the country's ethnocultural communities and to teach Burkinabè people about their heritage (Director General Sabari Christian Dao, Lefaso.net, 3 November 2024).
- Collection of about 14,000 objects in reserve, around a hundred on display (archaeology, contemporary art, ethnography), in two exhibition halls with a larger hall under construction (Lefaso.net, 3 November 2024).
- Permanent exhibition "Masque au-delà du visible" opened on 14 June 2024; the "Habitats du Faso" project, opened in June 2022, groups the traditional dwellings of eleven ethnic groups (Wikipedia).
- Attendance: more than 30,000 visits from January to October 2024, according to the director (Lefaso.net, 3 November 2024). The culture minister stated in April 2026 that attendance rose from 40,000 in 2024 to more than 100,000 in 2025 (Infos Culture du Faso, 8 April 2026).

FUNDING
Musée National du Burkina Faso: public: a state museum, with local government and company donations for its 2026 renovation drive
- The museum is a state public institution (EPSCT) (Wikipedia).
- On 7 April 2026 the Minister of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism, Pingdwendé Gilbert Ouédraogo, led a visit launching the clean-up and beautification of the museum and its surroundings, in partnership with the Agence Faso Mɛbo, the Ouagadougou commune and Salif Sanfo, whom the article calls "l'honorable". Partners and companies handed over donations as a first step (Infos Culture du Faso, 8 April 2026).
- The Association des Amis du Musée National, whose president Sayouba Sawadogo spoke at the event, called on volunteers, companies and media to support the effort (Infos Culture du Faso, 8 April 2026).
- African Initiative: on 24 July 2024 Burkina24 reported that the associations Golden Team and African Initiative cleared weeds from the museum grounds. Burkina24 describes African Initiative as an association created by Burkinabè and Russian people to bring the two peoples closer, and says it supported the clean-up with money and equipment (hoes, shovels, rakes, wheelbarrows) (Burkina24, "Ouagadougou : Golden Team et African Initiative désherbent l'enceinte du musée national", 24 July 2024).
- December 2018: staff reminded the new director, Rasmata Sawadogo, of the challenge of raising the museum's budget (Burkina24, 7 December 2018).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the Burkinabè state, with the Ouagadougou commune, Agence Faso Mɛbo and company donors joining its 2026 renovation drive. The association African Initiative supplied money and equipment for a July 2024 clean-up of the grounds.
