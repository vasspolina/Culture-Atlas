Red flags: 2023 protest demanding removal of the Ukrainian flag
- The museum hung a Ukrainian flag on its historic building as a sign of solidarity after Russia's invasion of Ukraine in February 2022 (Novinky, Pavel Nádvorník, 24 October 2025).
- On 11 March 2023, after the rally "Česko proti bídě" organised by the party Právo Respekt Odbornost, several hundred people moved to the museum and called for the flag to be torn down; police detained 18 who did not obey an order to leave, and the museum thanked the police for keeping visitors safe (ČTK, 11 March 2023).

Red flags: 2025 removal of the Ukrainian flag and protest for its return
- In August 2025 the museum replaced the flag with a banner for an exhibition of fossils lent by Ethiopia and did not put it back when the exhibition closed; spokeswoman Kristina Kvapilová said "Nic za tím nehledejme" ("Let us not look for anything behind it", translated) and ruled out international pressure (Novinky, 24 October 2025).
- Unverified: the director is yielding to the incoming government, from Otakar Stephan van Gemund, chairman of the association Kaputin, quoted by Novinky (Petr Svorník, 29 October 2025).
- Interior Minister Vít Rakušan called for the flag's return (Novinky, 29 October 2025).
- On 30 October 2025 about a hundred people demonstrated at the museum's main entrance at an event organised by Kaputin and raised a Ukrainian flag on two poles there (Novinky, Marek Tereba, 30 October 2025); speakers criticised director Michal Lukeš and Culture Minister Martin Baxa for the removal (Radio Prague International, Ruth Fraňková, 31 October 2025).
- Lukeš said it was an internal decision of the institution, and the Ministry of Culture said flags and banners on buildings are a matter for each organisation it funds (Novinky, 30 October 2025).
