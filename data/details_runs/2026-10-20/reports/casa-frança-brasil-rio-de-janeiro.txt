CASA FRANÇA-BRASIL: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
ID: casa-frança-brasil-rio-de-janeiro
Website: https://casabrasil.rj.gov.br/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Cultural centre of the Rio de Janeiro state government, under the Secretaria de Estado de Cultura e Economia Criativa, in the Centro district of Rio de Janeiro (Wikipedia).
- Opened as Casa França-Brasil on 29 March 1990; French museologist Pierre Castel conceived its multi-function cultural project with a Brazilian team in 1989; state culture secretary Darcy Ribeiro conceived the redevelopment in 1983 (Wikipedia). It took the name Casa Brasil in 2025 after a curatorial repositioning (stated by the space).
- The building is the city's first Praça do Comércio, designed by Grandjean de Montigny of the 1816 French Artistic Mission for King João VI and opened on 13 May 1820; it became the customs house in 1824 and housed the 2nd Tribunal do Júri from 1956 to 1978 (Wikipedia).
- Exhibitions, an artist residency and education courses for artists, curators and cultural mediators; director Tania Queiroz and general coordinator Jocelino Pessoa (stated by the space).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, with free entry (Super Rádio Tupi, April 2026). No accessibility information was found.

FUNDING
Casa França-Brasil (Casa Brasil): mainly public, with Petrobras as official sponsor through Lei Rouanet
- The centre is linked to the Rio de Janeiro state Secretaria de Estado de Cultura e Economia Criativa (stated by the space).
- Petrobras is the official sponsor. The restructuring project was selected in Petrobras's Novos Eixos call, in the line Ícones da Cultura Brasileira, and is carried out by the organisation V ARTE with the federal Ministry of Culture through the Lei Rouanet (stated by the space; Super Rádio Tupi).
- The Novos Eixos call, announced on 23 February 2024, set aside 250 million reais of Petrobras money for culture (Bahia Notícias).
- The Fundação Roberto Marinho was one of several bodies that supported the restoration (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of the amount Petrobras pays Casa Brasil.
- No reporting found of a sponsor that dropped the centre under pressure.

Red flags: Petrobras oil money
- Petrobras, the Brazilian oil company, is the current official sponsor (stated by the space).
- Petrobras plans nearly 100 billion US dollars of fossil fuel investment and planned about 3 billion dollars of drilling 175 kilometres off the coast of northeastern Brazil, where Ibama approved its latest proposal in May 2025 although its own technical evaluation suggested the plans were insufficient (DeSmog with Agência Pública, September 2025).
- Other environmental influencers accused Petrobras of co-opting social media influencers to improve its image; Petrobras called the accusations unfounded (DeSmog).
- No reporting found of a protest aimed at Casa Brasil over Petrobras.

Red flags: closure of an exhibition, 2019
- City authorities closed the exhibition "Literatura exposta" early in January 2019, before a final performance by the collective És Uma Maluca in which two naked women read a script about torture under the dictatorship (ArtReview).
- The closure notice from the city's secretary of culture, Ruan Lira, said the organisers were in breach of contract because they had not run the content past the municipality; curator Alvaro Figueiredo claimed Governor Wilson Witzel, a close ally of President Bolsonaro, had requested the intervention (ArtReview).
- A sound recording of speeches by Jair Bolsonaro had earlier been removed from an installation by the institution's director on advice; the artists performed outside the building the next evening (ArtReview).

Not related: Casa Brasil at the Paris 2024 Olympics. It was an initiative of the Brazilian Olympic Committee at Parc de la Villette, sponsored by Embratur and Sebrae, and has nothing to do with the Rio cultural centre.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the centre in art media; the one piece found concerns the 2019 state closure of an exhibition.
1. ArtReview: staff, "Exhibition at Casa França-Brasil in Rio de Janeiro closed down" (2019)
URL: https://artreview.com/news-14-jan-2019-casa-franca-brasil-rio-de-janeiro-censored/
- Reports the early closure of "Literatura exposta" and the blocked performance by És Uma Maluca.
- Names culture secretary Ruan Lira, Governor Wilson Witzel and curator Alvaro Figueiredo, and places the closure next to Bolsonaro's folding of the federal culture ministry.
- The link to the governor rests on the curator's claim.

BOTTOM LINE
The centre is a state venue whose current programme runs on Petrobras sponsorship through the Lei Rouanet, so its main outside money is oil money from a company with a planned 100 billion dollar fossil fuel investment. The other weak point is the 2019 closure of an exhibition by city authorities, which ArtReview reported as censorship of the artists' work. Worth watching: the size and term of the Petrobras sponsorship, and any campaign that names Casa Brasil.
