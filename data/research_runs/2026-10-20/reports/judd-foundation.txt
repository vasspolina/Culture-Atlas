JUDD FOUNDATION – Marfa, USA
Website: https://juddfoundation.org (supporters PDF and press release loaded 2026-10-20; /about/ could not be fetched)

OVERVIEW
- Set up by Donald Judd's estate in 1996 (Taipei Times / NYT News Service; David Zwirner). IRS ruling year 1997, address 101 Spring St, New York (GuideStar).
- It maintains Judd's permanently installed living and working spaces, libraries and archives in Marfa, Texas, and at 101 Spring Street, New York, and runs public visits and scholarly programmes. The estate's artworks and buildings were transferred to it (David Zwirner).
- Judd's children, Flavin and Rainer Judd, are co-presidents (David Zwirner, 2016). Flavin Judd is artistic director (Artnet, 2021).

FUNDING
Judd Foundation: art-market income plus private donors and some public and foreign-government grants
- In 2006 the foundation consigned about 35–36 Judd works to Christie's to pay debts and create an endowment, after reports of "dire economic straits". Christie's reportedly guaranteed about $20M, and 25 works sold. The sale was criticised for its effect on the Judd market (NYT via Taipei Times; David Zwirner).
- Gallery representation: David Zwirner 2011–2021, then Gagosian from September 2021, with Thaddaeus Ropac in Europe (Artnet). Gagosian, Paula Cooper Gallery, Galerie Mitterrand and Thaddaeus Ropac also appear as donors.
- Revenue was $5.4M in fiscal 2013 and the operating budget $3.1M in 2016 (David Zwirner).
- Supporters, 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026 (stated by the space). Government and foundations: Bloomberg Philanthropies, Texas Commission on the Arts, Henry Moore Foundation, Japan Foundation New York, Villa Albertine (French government), New York Community Trust, the Carl Andre and Melissa L. Kretschmer Foundation and others. Corporate: Bristol Myers Squibb, Gagosian, Neuberger Berman, J. Crew, KHAITE, Sony Music, TenBerke, Michael Maltzan Architecture. Patrons include Henry and Marie-Josée Kravis, Alice and Thomas Tisch, Ed Ruscha and Fairfax Dorn.
- Bloomberg Philanthropies paid for the expanded digital guide on the Bloomberg Connects app (2024 press release).
- Most of the existing record's supporter list comes from the period labelled FY2025-26.

Red flags: none specific to the foundation
- No reporting found of protests or campaigns about the foundation's funders.

BOTTOM LINE
Largely self-funded through the Judd estate's art and market relationships, with a growing donor base including Bloomberg Philanthropies and pharmaceutical and finance corporates. No flagged conduct found at the foundation itself. Worth watching: dependence on art-market sales and gallery partners who also donate.
