ID: kassak-muzeum-budapest

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 30 November 2020, 75 staff of the Petőfi Literary Museum and its member institutions, the Kassák Múzeum among them, announced on Facebook that they distanced themselves from director Szilárd Demeter's statement, from its content and style and from the way it was published and withdrawn. They said they would stay in their posts (Telex, Tenczer Gábor, "Elhatárolódnak Demeter Szilárdtól a munkatársai", 30 November 2020; title translated: his staff distance themselves from Szilárd Demeter).
