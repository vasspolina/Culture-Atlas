LE CUBE, INDEPENDENT ART ROOM: Rabat, Morocco
ID: le-cube-independent-art-room-rabat
Website: https://lecube-art.com

OVERVIEW
- Independent exhibition, residency and research space at 2 rue Benzerte in Rabat, with exhibition, projection, conference, workshop and performance spaces, a library and a freely accessible archive (Le Cube).
- Founded in 2005 by Elisabeth Piskernik, born in Vienna, who lives and works between Rabat and Vienna; it was founded with the artists' collective 212 (Amina Benbouchta, Hassan Echair, Safaa Erruas, Jamila Lamrani, Imad Mansour, Myriam Mihindou, Younes Rahmoun) (Le Cube).
- Became Le Cube, independent art room in 2010; Afrik.com, on 24 January 2011, announced the inauguration of the new space for 2 February 2011.
- Residencies began in 2007, the "summers lab" for young Moroccan artists in 2011, the "curators zone" in 2013, and since 2021 it also works as a nomadic project, Le Cube expanded (Le Cube).
- Since founding it has held 111 exhibitions, 138 events and 70 artist residencies, and counts more than 80 partner institutions (stated by the space, 20 years page, 2025).
- Visits are by appointment only, arranged through WhatsApp.

FUNDING
Le Cube: independent artist-run space, working with foreign cultural institutes and project partners
- Exhibitions are made with named partners: in 2026 Goethe-Institut Maroc and INBA on "Le cœur sur la langue", and Museo MARTE in San Salvador on "Palmeras Cansadas" (Le Cube).
- Afrik.com reported in January 2011 an artist exchange programme with the Goethe-Institut and Instituto Cervantes.
- Piskernik's biography on the space's site names Pro Helvetia Cairo, the French Institute of Morocco, La Maison Salvan and the Austrian Ministry of Culture among the partners of her residency and research programmes (Le Cube).
- Collaborations with institutions abroad include the Villa du Parc in Annemasse (2022), the Climate Cultures Festival in Berlin (2023), and the Art Sonje Center in Seoul and MAXXI in Rome (2024) (stated by the space).
- TransArtists lists the host as paying for artist housing, gallery preparation, a technician for one to two days and exhibition promotion, and states that the artist pays the entire costs of the residency, including travel, accommodation, supplies and food.

BOTTOM LINE
Le Cube is a small independent space founded and directed by Elisabeth Piskernik, whose residencies are paid for by the artists. The partners named for single exhibitions and programmes are cultural institutes, museums and an arts ministry, among them Goethe-Institut Maroc, Pro Helvetia Cairo and the Austrian Ministry of Culture.
