MUSEUM OF FREE BELARUS: Warsaw, Poland
ID: museum-of-free-belarus-warsaw
Website: https://www.belarus-nau.org/en/post/museum-of-free-belarus

OVERVIEW
- Museum of the 2020 Belarusian protest movement at ul. Foksal 11 in central Warsaw (Radio Svaboda).
- A project of the National Anti-Crisis Management, led by Pavel Latushka, with support from Polish authorities. It began operating on 17 July 2022 (Radio Svaboda, 17 July 2022).
- Premises: the Warsaw municipality provided over 367 square metres (National Anti-Crisis Management page, 14 May 2023).
- Its stated purpose, in Latushka's words, is to collect, preserve and display art and objects of Belarusians' fight for democracy, and later to present them in Belarus (Radio Svaboda).
- Collection: signed printed documents, belongings of political prisoners, posters and flags, collected on a volunteer basis (National Anti-Crisis Management page, 14 May 2023). The first exhibits in 2022 included drums of the musician Vasil Hrudavik, a white-red-white flag bearing the names of all political prisoners, and an exhibition by the artist Ihar Tsishyn (Radio Svaboda).
- Preliminary cooperation agreements with Poland's National Institute of Museology and Heritage Protection, the Karta Foundation, the Hoover Institution at Stanford and the Skharyna Belarusian Library and Museum in London (stated by the National Anti-Crisis Management, 14 May 2023).
- It ran the Belarusian Museum Laboratory, a series of workshops on digital collections, in 2025 (Reform.news, 17 November 2025).
- On 23 March 2023 the Polish National Centre for Culture formally handed over to it the poster exhibition "Biało-czerwono-biały", prepared with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Culture and National Heritage Piotr Gliński was announced as a speaker (Polish foreign ministry announcement).
- Unverified: Latushka wrote on 25 May 2024 that his team had secured control of the State Catalog of the Museum Fund of Belarus, covering nearly 165,000 objects, and that control had passed to the Museum of Free Belarus; the claim comes from his own organisation's site.

FUNDING
Museum of Free Belarus: exile political project: Polish public premises and grant, plus private donations
- Premises: more than 367 square metres in central Warsaw provided by the Warsaw municipality (National Anti-Crisis Management page, 2023).
- Polish state grant: the digitisation project "Digitisation and access provision to materials of the Museum of Free Belarus" belongs to the Digital Culture 2025 programme and is co-financed by the Polish Ministry of Culture and National Heritage with funds from the Fund for Support of Culture (Reform.news, 17 November 2025).
- Donations: the parent's museum page lists PayPal, cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum) and bank transfers to the Fundacja Białoruś Przyszłości (The Future of Belarus Foundation).
- Parent: the National Anti-Crisis Management, headed by Pavel Latushka, who is also Deputy Head of the United Transitional Cabinet of Belarus (his own post on the National Anti-Crisis Management site, 25 May 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a project of a Belarusian exile political movement. The documented support is Polish public money: premises from the City of Warsaw and a Ministry of Culture digitisation co-financing under Digital Culture 2025, plus a donation page.
