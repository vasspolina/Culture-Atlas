ID: opernhaus-zurich

Red flags: 2022 cancellation of Anna Netrebko's appearances
- In early March 2022 the Opernhaus Zürich and Anna Netrebko agreed that no collaboration was currently possible and her appearances as Lady Macbeth were cancelled; Veronika Dzhioewa stepped in (Blick, 29 March 2022).

Red flags: 2022 complaint over Veronika Dzhioewa
- Yevgeniy Protsyuk, president of the Ukrainian association for Central and Eastern Switzerland, objected to Dzhioewa, saying "Unzählige Fotos und Videos zeigen sie mit dem Kriegsverbrecher Putin" ("Countless photos and videos show her with the war criminal Putin", translated) (Blick, 29 March 2022).
- Spokesperson Bettina Auge said the singer had assured the house that a photograph of her with Putin was a chance and unplanned picture taken at a concert five years earlier (Blick).

Red flags: 2025 protest over Anna Netrebko in La Forza del Destino
- The house cast Netrebko as Leonora in Verdi's La Forza del Destino from 2 November 2025; Riccardo Tognetti of the association Helvetia for Ukraine wrote an open letter against the engagement, and the Ukrainian ambassador in Bern and Ukrainian members of the opera chorus also criticised it (SRF, 2 November 2025).
- Intendant Matthias Schulz called the engagement an artistic decision and said "Sie profitiert heute nicht mehr vom russischen System" ("She no longer profits from the Russian system today", translated) (SRF).
- About 20 people with Ukrainian flags protested outside the entrance at the premiere; the ambassador called Netrebko a "Marke des Kremls" (brand of the Kremlin) and demanded that her appearances be cancelled (SRF, 3 November 2025).
