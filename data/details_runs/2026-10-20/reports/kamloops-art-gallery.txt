KAMLOOPS ART GALLERY: Kamloops, Canada
ID: kamloops-art-gallery
Website: https://kag.bc.ca

OVERVIEW
- Public art gallery at 101 - 465 Victoria Street, Kamloops, British Columbia, in a civic building it shares with Thompson-Nicola Regional District offices and a downtown TNRD Library branch.
- Incorporated in 1978 (gallery's about page); it is a registered Canadian charity (CanadaHelps).
- Moved in 1998 into a purpose-built building designed by the architects Peter Cardew and Nigel Baldwin. The building is 20,853 square feet, including 4,500 square feet of exhibition space (stated by the gallery).
- Programmes listed on its about page: studio programs, talks and tours, the BCLC Community Ticketing Program and Open Gallery.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 5:00 pm, and until 8:00 pm on Thursdays; closed Sundays, Mondays and statutory holidays.
- Admission: adults $5, families $10, seniors (62+) $3, groups of 10 or more $3; free for members, students and children, and for support persons. Thursday admission is free, "sponsored by BCLC".
- Accessibility: automatic door opener at the main entrance, elevator to both floors, a free standard wheelchair, a wheelchair-accessible single-user washroom on the first floor; service animals are welcome.

Red flags: BCLC sponsorship (provincial Crown corporation named in the Cullen Commission)
- BCLC is listed as the gallery's Benefactor Level sponsor and a 2025 sponsor, and sponsors free Thursday admission (gallery's sponsorship, supporters and visit pages). Its Community Ticketing Program is listed among the gallery's programmes.
- BCLC is a provincial Crown corporation headquartered in Kamloops. It conducts and manages lottery, sports betting and casino gambling in British Columbia, and through its PlayNow subsidiary it is the sole licensed online casino operator in B.C., Manitoba and Saskatchewan. Casino gaming made up 65% of its net revenue of $1.408 billion in fiscal year 2024 to 2025 (Wikipedia).
- Cullen Commission: the B.C. government announced the inquiry into money laundering in May 2019, and the commission submitted its final report on 3 June 2022 (Cullen Commission website; Justice Austin Cullen led it per Wikipedia). Wikipedia states that the commission found BCLC "actively ignored repeated warnings from law enforcement surrounding money laundering" and describes the commission calling BCLC's approach "a completely unacceptable and unreasonable risk tolerance".

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery is funded by the City of Kamloops, the Province of British Columbia, the BC Arts Council, the Canada Council and the Thompson-Nicola Regional District, with named foundations and individual donors. BCLC, the provincial gambling corporation, is listed as its Benefactor Level sponsor and funds free Thursdays; Wikipedia states that the Cullen Commission found BCLC ignored law enforcement money laundering warnings until 2015.
