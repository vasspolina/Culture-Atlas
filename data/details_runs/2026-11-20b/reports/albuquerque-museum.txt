ALBUQUERQUE MUSEUM: Albuquerque, USA
ID: albuquerque-museum
Website: https://www.cabq.gov/artsculture/albuquerque-museum (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- City museum of art, history and culture at 2000 Mountain Road NW in Old Town, a division of the City of Albuquerque's Department of Arts and Culture (city website).
- Established in 1967; it marks its 60th anniversary in 2027 (city website; Albuquerque Museum Foundation).
- Holds about 10,000 works of art, 35,000 historical objects and more than 130,000 items in its photo archives, searchable in its e-Museum (city website).
- The city budget describes it as protecting the art and historical items of the middle Rio Grande valley and bringing traveling exhibitions to the city; it counted 92,671 visitors in 2022 (City of Albuquerque FY26 Approved Budget).
- On site: a sculpture garden, an amphitheater, a Museum School, a store and a café; the museum also shows a historic plazuela-style rancho with a collection of New Mexico vernacular art (city website).
- Ground was broken in October 2026 for a new Education Center that the Foundation says will nearly double the 10,000 students a year served (Albuquerque Museum Foundation).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 to 17:00; general admission is free on Sunday mornings, 9:00 to 13:00, except during Balloon Fiesta; sign language interpreters and accessible materials on two weeks' notice (city website).

FUNDING
Albuquerque Museum: mainly public: the City of Albuquerque, with a private support foundation
- City general fund: the "CS-Museum" program spent $4.589 million in FY24 and has an approved $4.368 million for FY26, within a $53.7 million Arts and Culture budget (City of Albuquerque FY26 Approved Budget).
- Earned income: the budget estimates about $241,000 a year in museum charges, plus rental and Museum School fees, paid into the city general fund (FY26 Approved Budget).
- Capital: the city's capital programme lists $680,000 in future operating costs for the Albuquerque Museum Education Center (FY26 Approved Budget); the Foundation thanks the City, elected officials and donors for the project (Albuquerque Museum Foundation).
- Albuquerque Museum Foundation (EIN 85-0201054), founded in 1968 to raise funds for exhibitions, education and acquisitions: in the year to June 2025 it reported $2.55 million revenue, $1.73 million of it in contributions, $450,393 investment income and $19.3 million net assets (Form 990, ProPublica).
- Corporate partners: partnerships start at $10,000; the Foundation names Heritage Hotels & Resorts, Singleton Schreiber, French Funerals & Cremations, Parnall Law Firm and TABLE Magazine (Albuquerque Museum Foundation).
- The Foundation's headquarters, the 1875 Juan Cristóbal Armijo hacienda, came to it through the Lucia v.B. Batten Trust (Albuquerque Museum Foundation).

WIDER CONTEXT
- June 2020: the city removed the bronze statue of the conquistador Juan de Oñate from the La Jornada sculpture in front of the museum after a protest in which a man was shot and wounded (Albuquerque Journal, 2023).
- The City Council heard the Arts Board's recommendations in December 2020 and postponed a decision; as of 2023 the statue remained in storage (Albuquerque Journal).
- The museum's grounds held the statue, and the city that funds the museum controls its future.

BOTTOM LINE
City money with a local support foundation and small local business sponsors; nothing in its funding carries a flag. Worth watching: the council's decision on the Oñate statue and who funds the Education Center.
