BASIS FRANKFURT: Frankfurt am Main, Germany
ID: basis-frankfurt
Website: https://basis-frankfurt.de (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Production and exhibition platform and studio house at Gutleutstraße 8-12 in the Bahnhofsviertel, Frankfurt (Journal Frankfurt).
- The association basis e.V. has run the studio house there since 2007, with low-cost workspaces and a support programme for young artists (Journal Frankfurt).
- Artistic directors named in 2016: Felix Ruhöfer and Jakob Sturm (Journal Frankfurt).
- Building: dates from 1907 and is a listed monument (Journal Frankfurt). The city's 2019 grant covered converting its reading room and greening the courtyard.
- On site: about 350 m² of exhibition space and 75 studios in 2019 (Journal Frankfurt). The association now states about 120 workspaces for roughly 150 creative professionals at five locations, plus a project room of about 100 m².
- Programmes: exhibitions, readings, lectures, concerts, performances, the Hessisches Atelierprogramm (HAP), the AIR_Frankfurt and AIR_Offenbach residencies, a reading room and art education for school classes.
- Visiting: exhibition entry is free (stated by the association). The reading room is open during exhibition hours.

FUNDING
basis Frankfurt: mainly public: City of Frankfurt and the state of Hesse
- The State of Hesse owned the building. On 25 January 2016 Journal Frankfurt reported the planned sale to the City of Frankfurt for 4.7 million euros, pending approval by the budget committee of the Hessian Landtag and Frankfurt's city council; the purchase contract provides for ten years of use for the creative industries. On 29 February 2016 it reported that the city had bought the building for 4.7 million euros and made it available to basis for the next ten years.
- The association's support programme for young artists is also funded by the city (Journal Frankfurt, 25 January 2016).
- In March 2019 the city's planning commissioner Mike Josef approved a grant of up to 96,000 euros for converting the reading room and greening the courtyard (Journal Frankfurt, 19 March 2019).
- Stated by the space: its homepage carries the heading "basis e.V. wird dauerhaft gefördert von" ("is permanently supported by", translation) above the supporters' logos.
- HAP studio programme: twelve free studios for four years for young artists from Hesse; the programme page shows a Hessian science ministry logo under "supported by" (stated by the space).
- "Frankfurt schreibt…", a collection of resident stories, is funded by Stiftung Polytechnische Gesellschaft and World Design Capital Frankfurt RheinMain 2026 (stated by the space).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind basis is public: the City of Frankfurt bought the building in 2016 and supports the programme, and a Hessian science ministry logo appears on the HAP studio programme page. The documented sums are the 4.7 million euro purchase in 2016 and a grant of up to 96,000 euros in 2019, with Stiftung Polytechnische Gesellschaft funding one project.
