WIDER CONTEXT
- Unverified: that Cuban state bodies ran a campaign to discredit INSTAR's fourth film festival, held in seven countries from 4 to 10 December 2023, from a joint statement of 7 December 2023 by PEN International, PEN America, Artists at Risk Connection and other organisations. The statement says state-controlled media suggested the festival was "an attack on Cuban culture and an endorsement of terrorism", that the culture minister Alpidio Alonso Grau and Lis Cuesta Peraza posted smear messages on social media, and that Cuban cultural institutions sent a mass mailing to institutions and individuals abroad. It also says Bruguera "was forced to leave Cuba in 2021 as a result of government pressure and persecution for her artistic work."
