Red flags: 2023 return of the Ondonga power stone fragments to Namibia
- On 27 April 2023 the National Museum of Finland handed two fragments of the sacred power stone of the Kingdom of Ondonga to the National Museum of Namibia in Windhoek (National Museum of Finland, 27 April 2023).
- Missionary Martti Rautanen and botanist Hans Schinz cut the fragments from the stone in February 1886; touching the stone was forbidden under the kingdom's law, and the two men had to pay compensation. The fragments reached the National Museum of Finland in 2015 with the collection of the Finnish Evangelical Lutheran Mission (National Museum of Finland, 27 April 2023).

Red flags: 2025 return of a looted Dahomey stool to Benin
- French forces looted a kataklé, a ceremonial stool from the Kingdom of Dahomey, from the royal palace in Abomey in 1892; it passed to the Musée de l'Homme in Paris, which exchanged it to the National Museum of Finland in 1939 (National Museum of Finland, 4 November 2024).
- Benin made an official request to the Finnish government for its return, and on 4 November 2024 the museum said it "has responded positively and is proceeding the return in accordance with official procedures" (National Museum of Finland, 4 November 2024).
- The museum returned the kataklé at a ceremony in Cotonou on 13 May 2025, attended by Finland's Minister of Science and Culture Mari-Leena Talvitie and Benin's Minister of Culture Jean Michel Abimbola (National Museum of Finland, 2025).
