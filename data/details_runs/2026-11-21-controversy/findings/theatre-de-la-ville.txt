ID: theatre-de-la-ville

WIDER CONTEXT
- The deputy André Gerin wrote that in October 2011 Christian fundamentalist groups, in particular Civitas and Renouveau français, tried to stop performances of Romeo Castellucci's "Sur le concept du visage du fils de Dieu" at the Théâtre de la Ville: they forced their way in on 20 October, threw engine oil and eggs at the audience on 21 October and invaded the stage on 22 October (Assemblée nationale, written question 120824, 1 November 2011).
- The groups had sought a ban on the play in court and lost before the Tribunal de grande instance de Paris on 18 October 2011; the theatre's management and the City of Paris filed complaints over the events of 20, 21 and 22 October (Assemblée nationale written question 120824).
- On 30 October 2011 about 200 Catholic fundamentalists gathered on the Place du Châtelet as spectators entered, after 1,500 to 5,000 people marched the day before; the Archbishop of Paris, Cardinal André Vingt-Trois, condemned "un groupuscule qui se réclame de l'Eglise catholique sans aucun mandat" ("a small group that claims to speak for the Catholic Church without any mandate", translated) (AFP via La Libre Belgique, 30 October 2011).
