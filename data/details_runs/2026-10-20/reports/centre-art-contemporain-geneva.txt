CENTRE D'ART CONTEMPORAIN GENEVE: Geneva, Switzerland
ID: centre-art-contemporain-geneva
Website: https://centre.ch (checked 2026-10-24; the site loads, but its About, Visit and Members pages returned only navigation text, so history and funding were read from the archived 2016.centre.ch pages and Wikipedia)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre in Geneva, recognised as the city's Kunsthalle; Wikipedia says it was the first contemporary art institution in French-speaking Switzerland and was founded in 1974.
- Founded by Adelina von Fürstenberg; directors since then were Paolo Colombo (from 1989, for thirteen years), Katya García-Antón (2002 to 2012) and Andrea Bellini (from 2012). The city recognised it as a Kunsthalle in 1983 and it joined the Bâtiment d'art contemporain (BAC), Rue des Vieux-Grenadiers 10, in 1989 (Wikipedia, French).
- Building: the BAC, which French Wikipedia says the centre shares with other institutions. The centre's archived page says a complete renovation led by the Berlin architects Kuehn Malvezzi would start on 17 February 2025 and last four years, and that the City Council approved the project unanimously on 29 October 2025 (the page's dates do not fit together).
- During the works the offices are at Rue de Chantepoulet 1, Mont-Blanc Centre (1 March 2025 to 1 March 2029), and the programme runs off-site in Geneva and abroad. Wikipedia reports a new space called L'Arcade opening there in 2026.
- On site before the closure: the Cinéma Dynamo, about thirty seats, opened in 2013 (Wikipedia, French). The centre also runs the Biennale de l'Image en Mouvement, founded in 1985 by André Iten as International Video Week; the centre inherited it in 2009 (stated by the centre).
- Visiting: the 2025 City of Geneva grants exhibition at Le Commun, Rue des Vieux-Grenadiers 10, was open Wednesday to Sunday 2pm to 6pm. No admission price or accessibility information was found on the pages read.

FUNDING
Centre d'Art Contemporain Genève: mainly public: City of Geneva and Canton of Geneva, with Loterie Romande, 1875 Finance and Arab Bank Switzerland as sponsor of one programme
- The centre's annual partners are the Ville de Genève, the Canton de Genève and 1875 Finance (stated by the centre, Agora page, 2025).
- In 1983 the City of Geneva granted the centre an annual subsidy (Wikipedia, French). No current amount was found.
- Programme sponsors: Arab Bank Switzerland is the main sponsor of Agora, an online programme of lectures and mentoring for artists and philosophers (open call from 30 September to 1 November 2025). Mousse Magazine is its official media partner (stated by the centre). Arab Bank (Switzerland) Ltd. is a separate legal entity from Arab Bank plc; Arab Bank's own site calls them sister companies with identical shareholders, and does not call the Swiss bank a subsidiary. No penalty or objection against the Swiss bank itself was found on the pages read.
- The City of Geneva's grants for young artists are hosted by the centre, with logos of the Canton of Geneva, City of Geneva, Loterie Romande, 1875 Finance and TPG (stated by the centre; names read from logo file names).
- For the 2014 relaunch of the Biennale the centre worked in close collaboration with the Fonds Municipal d'Art Contemporain (FMAC) and the Fonds Cantonal d'Art Contemporain (FCAC); the 2021 edition included works co-produced with the Fonds cantonal d'art contemporain Genève (stated by the centre).
- No reporting found of the Swiss Federal Office of Culture as a funder, or of the centre's annual accounts, on the pages read.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In September 2014 a US federal jury in Brooklyn found Arab Bank, headquartered in Jordan, liable under the Anti-Terrorism Act for three Hamas-associated attacks. A $100 million judgment was entered by agreement on 24 May 2016 alongside a confidential settlement; the Second Circuit vacated the judgment in February 2018, and the settlement foreclosed a new trial (Lawfare, 26 February 2018).
- The defendant was Arab Bank, not Arab Bank (Switzerland) Ltd., the Agora sponsor. Arab Bank's own site calls the Swiss bank a sister company of Arab Bank plc with identical shareholders.
- No reporting found of a campaign against the sponsorship.

BOTTOM LINE
The core money is Geneva city and cantonal public funding plus Swiss foundations; no funder with a documented controversy was found among the core backers. The weak points are that no amounts are published on the pages read, and that the main sponsor of Agora, Arab Bank Switzerland, is a sister company of a bank that lost a 2014 US terrorism-financing jury trial; the verdict was vacated in 2018 and concerned the other bank. Watch the renovation funding and the sponsor list.
