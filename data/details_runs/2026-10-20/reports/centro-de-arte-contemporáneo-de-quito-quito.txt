CENTRO DE ARTE CONTEMPORÁNEO DE QUITO: Quito, Ecuador
ID: centro-de-arte-contemporáneo-de-quito-quito
Website: https://fundacionmuseosquito.gob.ec/

OVERVIEW
- Municipal contemporary art centre at Montevideo and Luis Dávila, in the Antiguo Hospital Militar, San Juan neighbourhood of Quito; its guided tours end at the San Juan viewpoints (Expreso). The museum system's site calls it "el único espacio cultural especializado en arte contemporáneo en la ciudad y el país" (translation: the only cultural space specialised in contemporary art in the city and the country).
- Part of the Sistema de Museos Quito, and one of five spaces run by the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad, with the Museo de la Ciudad, Museo del Carmen Alto, Museo Interactivo de Ciencia and Yaku Parque Museo del Agua.
- Building: constructed between 1900 and 1913 as a military hospital (El Diario, July 2026). Expreso describes a heritage building of more than 6,000 square metres that has served as a sanatorium, military hospital, barracks and housing for several families; its prison areas and the site of a former common grave are part of the tours.
- On 23 July 2026 its north wing reopened with two free exhibitions, "Montajes. Reserva Abierta", with works from the Reserva Alberto Mena Caamaño of the Centro Cultural Metropolitano, and "A veces volcán" by Luis Loaiza (El Diario).
- Programme: an open programme the centre says promotes non-formal education, critical thinking, research, creation and memory; three or four free temporary exhibitions at a time, free historical tours of about an hour, and paid summer programmes for ages 9 to 17 at $10 a week (Expreso). Santiago Ávila is named as coordinator (Expreso).
- Visiting: free entry. Open Wednesday to Friday 09:00 to 16:30 (last entry 15:45); weekends, public holidays and summer 10:00 to 17:30 (last entry 16:45).

FUNDING
Centro de Arte Contemporáneo de Quito: run for the Municipality of Quito by the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad
- The Fundación Museos de la Ciudad describes itself as a private non-profit institution working on behalf of the Ilustre Municipio de Quito (Fundación Museos de la Ciudad).
- The 2026 reopening of the north wing was promoted by the Municipality of Quito, the Instituto Metropolitano de Patrimonio and the Secretaría de Cultura (El Diario).
- Expreso reports that the centre is part of the cultural offer of the Municipality of Quito and that the Premio Brasil: Arte Emergente is organised with the Embassy of Brazil in Ecuador.
- Entry and tours are free.

BOTTOM LINE
The Centro de Arte Contemporáneo de Quito is run by the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad, a private non-profit working on commission of the Quito municipality, and is part of the municipality's cultural offer. The Instituto Metropolitano de Patrimonio and the Secretaría de Cultura promoted the 2026 reopening of the north wing, and entry is free.
