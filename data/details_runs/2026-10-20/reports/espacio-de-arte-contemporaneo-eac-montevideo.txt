ESPACIO DE ARTE CONTEMPORÁNEO (EAC): Montevideo, Uruguay
ID: espacio-de-arte-contemporaneo-eac-montevideo
Website: https://eac.gub.uy

OVERVIEW
- National public contemporary art space at Arenal Grande 1930, Montevideo, in the former Cárcel de Miguelete prison in the La Comercial neighbourhood (Wikipedia).
- Opened on 27 July 2010; the project began in 2008 on an initiative driven by the National Directorate of Culture of the Ministry of Education and Culture (Wikipedia). Wikipedia calls it the only national public institution devoted exclusively to contemporary art.
- The prison was designed by Juan Alberto Capurro and built in 1888 on Jeremy Bentham's panopticon model, with a central watchtower and four radial three-storey pavilions; it held inmates for over a century (Wikipedia). It is a national historic monument (Artefacto, 2021).
- The EAC occupies the wing facing Arenal Grande. Wikipedia says a 2018 addition, Sala Seis, gives 160 square metres of exhibition space; Artefacto (substack account _artefacto, 19 August 2021) says the Natural History Museum joined the site in 2018.
- Programmes: open calls for individual, collective and curatorial proposals, residencies, training and research, a public programme of talks, workshops and guided tours, and the LAB EAC. Since 2018 it has hosted the Premio Nacional de Artes Visuales exhibition (Wikipedia). The grounds include an open-air photo gallery from the CdF (Artefacto).
- Galleries open Wednesday to Saturday 13:00 to 19:00; plazas and photo gallery daily 9:00 to 19:00; admission free (Ministry of Education and Culture notice, 23 March 2026). A ministry notice of 21 December 2023 for January and February gave Wednesday to Saturday 13:00 to 19:00 and Sunday 11:00 to 17:00.

FUNDING
Espacio de Arte Contemporáneo: public: Uruguay's Ministry of Education and Culture
- The ministry owns the site and its National Directorate of Culture administers the EAC (Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia states that the project received support from the Spanish Agency for International Development Cooperation (AECID).

BOTTOM LINE
The EAC is a state-run space of Uruguay's Ministry of Education and Culture, housed in a former prison. Wikipedia states that the project received support from the Spanish Agency for International Development Cooperation (AECID).
