Red flags: 2025 Jeju claim to a Korean bell in the collection
- MBC News reported on 8 May 2025 that a bell 1 metre high and weighing 300 kg, labelled as made in 1690 at Unheungsa temple in Joseon, stands in an empty space under a basement staircase of the Nezu Museum, beside the visitors' lockers and next to a toilet (MBC News, 8 May 2025).
- MBC News said the bell had recently been identified as the one that hung before the outer gate of the Jeju-mok government office, rung from 1850 to mark the hours and the opening and closing of the city gates, and that it disappeared in 1916 when the Japanese colonial authorities demolished the office (MBC News, 8 May 2025).
- Jeju Province began talks with the Korea Heritage Service to get the bell back; an official said recovery by negotiation, donation or purchase is difficult (MBC News, 8 May 2025).
- MBC News reported that the Nezu Museum had stated no position on the bell, which it said hampered both its return and its proper restoration (MBC News, 8 May 2025).
