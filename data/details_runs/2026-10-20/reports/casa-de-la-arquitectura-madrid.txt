CASA DE LA ARQUITECTURA: Madrid, Spain
ID: casa-de-la-arquitectura-madrid
Website: https://casadelaarquitectura.gob.es (checked 2026-10-20; serves spam: a "Send crypto" web app behind a certificate for *.sendcrypto.com). The museum's working site is https://lacasadelaarquitectura.es (checked 2026-10-20).

OVERVIEW
- National architecture museum in the Arquería exhibition space of the Nuevos Ministerios complex, Paseo de la Castellana 67, next to Nuevos Ministerios metro.
- Opened on 18 December 2023; Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez spoke at the opening and said it fulfils a mandate of the Ley de Calidad de la Arquitectura; earlier the Museo Nacional de Arquitectura y Urbanismo, established on 29 December 2006 (Wikipedia).
- Ley 9/2022 on the Quality of Architecture, approved on 14 June 2022, gave it the name Casa de la Arquitectura; a royal decree of December 2023 created and regulates it (The Objective).
- It holds a permanent collection of drawings, plans, documents and images from the Ministry of Housing, temporary exhibitions and an online catalogue; a board of trustees includes the Housing and Culture ministries and architects' associations (Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 11:00 to 20:00, Sundays and holidays 11:00 to 15:00; exhibitions, guided tours and workshops are free. No accessibility information was found on the site.

FUNDING
Casa de la Arquitectura: public: Spain's Ministry of Housing and Urban Agenda
- The Ministerio de Vivienda y Agenda Urbana owns and runs the museum (Wikipedia, SUT).
- In December 2025 the ministry put out to tender the design of the museum's extension for 1,722,974.15 euros including VAT, covering new galleries, a library, conservation areas and a plan for the Nuevos Ministerios outdoor spaces (Forbes España).
- The website footer shows the logos of the Gobierno de España and Fundación Arquia; no reporting found of what Fundación Arquia pays for.
- No reporting found of the museum's annual budget.

Red flags: labour dispute with contractor staff
- Magmacultura SL took over the cultural mediation service in November 2025; on 6 February 2026 it cut two mediators from 90 to 60 percent of a full day from 21 February (SUT, the union that organised the strike).
- SUT called an indefinite strike from 5 March 2026, joined by the information staff of Sedena SL; the union said the ministry, as main employer, did not attend a mediation on 4 March (SUT).
- The strike lasted eight days and closed the museum; Magmacultura then revoked the cut and agreed to pay the lost wages from 21 February 2026 (SUT).
- No reporting found of the dispute in independent press.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found, such as Arquitectura Viva's December 2023 opening report, is descriptive.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state body paid for by the Ministry of Housing, with no private funder named beyond a Fundación Arquia logo. The weak points are a contractor pay dispute in 2026 that closed the museum for eight days, and a .gob.es address that now serves an unrelated crypto app. Worth watching: the 1.7 million euro extension and who holds the old domain.
