Red flags: 2022 to 2025 cost overrun on the redevelopment
- In March 2022 the cost of the gallery's redevelopment rose by about $9.4 million to $64.4 million, and the mayor said the Whanganui District Council would have to look at covering part of it; the project team put the delays down to Covid-19, supply chain disruption and earthquake strengthening of the 1919 building (Whanganui Chronicle, 10 March 2022).
- Councillor Philippa Baker-Hogan said she was disappointed in the increase and that residents picking up the "cost blow-out" would severely test a community already struggling with the cost of living; councillor Hadleigh Reid called the extra costs disappointing after a thorough planning process. Councillor Helen Craig pointed to earthquake strengthening and Covid as causes (Whanganui Chronicle, Jacob McSweeny, 11 March 2022).
- On the final cost reported in August 2025, Mayor Andrew Tripe said a ratepayer contribution of a third was an extraordinary outcome, and chief executive David Langford said the project was "completed to a high standard and with full transparency about cost and timeline impacts" (RNZ, Local Democracy Reporting, 1 August 2025).
