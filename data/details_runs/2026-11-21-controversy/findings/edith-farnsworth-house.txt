ID: edith-farnsworth-house

WIDER CONTEXT
- The house's final price was more than $74,000, almost twice the original budget. Mies van der Rohe filed a mechanic's lien against the building to recover unpaid design and construction costs, and Edith Farnsworth countersued, alleging malpractice. Her countersuit was dismissed, and she eventually reached a favorable settlement (Preservation Magazine, Lee Bey, "The Past, Present, and Future of Farnsworth House", Fall 2020).
