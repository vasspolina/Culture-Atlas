PROYECTO MARTADERO: Cochabamba, Bolivia
ID: proyecto-martadero-cochabamba
Website: https://martadero.org

OVERVIEW
- Social development and arts project on Calle 27 de Agosto y Ollantay in the Villa Coronilla neighbourhood of Cochabamba, a few blocks from the city centre (mARTadero site; history page).
- Housed in a former municipal slaughterhouse. After 1992 it served as a small school, a sports space and storage for public lighting and unused municipal furniture, and it deteriorated until 2004 (mARTadero history page).
- In 2004 the building hosted the II Concurso Nacional Bienal de Arte Contemporáneo (CONART), co-organised with the Alcaldía of Cochabamba, which began its recovery. The competition was promoted by the Oficial Mayor de Cultura, Jenny Rivero, and by a group of artists led by Angélika Heckl and Fernando García; the NADA association began the works, and a loan of use was requested from the Concejo Municipal (mARTadero history page).
- NADA generated the project and holds a 30-year concession on the building; Fundación Imagen manages it (mARTadero site; Quiénes somos page).
- The site is about 2,900 square metres, with a survey of each room and a progressive recovery plan to adapt the spaces to multiple uses (mARTadero history page).
- Programmes: Formarte, Residencias, Acción Urbana, a children's creativity workshop, cultural policy, an entrepreneurship incubator and a Maker Space, across performing arts, visual arts, architecture, audiovisual, literature and music (mARTadero site).
- It describes its management as self-managed and assembly-based, with seven principles including innovation, research and interculturality (stated by the project, Quiénes somos page).

FUNDING
Proyecto mARTadero: supporter-funded: Misereor and Oxfam International listed as current supporters, in a building held on a 30-year concession
- The Apoyos page lists Misereor and Oxfam International as current supporters, and Hivos, Conexión, Solidar Suiza, Cooperación Italiana and CAF as previous supporters. The page was posted on 25 February 2019.
- Building: NADA holds the 30-year concession on the former slaughterhouse; the project's history page says the municipal council was asked for a loan of use in 2004 and the Alcaldía helped with the first clean-up.

BOTTOM LINE
The Apoyos page lists Misereor and Oxfam International as current supporters and Hivos, Conexión, Solidar Suiza, Cooperación Italiana and CAF as previous supporters; the building is held by NADA on a 30-year concession. The project states that it is self-managed.
