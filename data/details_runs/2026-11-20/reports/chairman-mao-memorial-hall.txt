CHAIRMAN MAO MEMORIAL HALL: Beijing, China
ID: chairman-mao-memorial-hall
Website: http://cpc.people.com.cn/GB/143527/143528/index.html (checked 2026-11-20; the hall's page on People's Daily, where it posts notices and bookings)

OVERVIEW
- Mausoleum of Mao Zedong on Tiananmen Square, on a 5.72 hectare site with 33,867 square metres of floor space (People's Daily, 2020 and 2024; Our China Story).
- Building began on 24 November 1976, the main structure was finished on 24 May 1977 and it opened on 9 September 1977 (People's Daily, 2020 and 2024).
- A colonnaded cube with 44 square granite columns; Mao lies in a crystal coffin in the Hall of Tribute, draped with the Party flag (Our China Story).
- The second floor holds memorial rooms for Mao, Zhou Enlai, Liu Shaoqi, Zhu De, Deng Xiaoping and Chen Yun (People's Daily).
- People's Daily counted more than 240 million visits by January 2024. On 9 September 2026, the 50th anniversary of Mao's death, long queues formed outside (Beijing Daily).
- Visiting: by real-name booking one to six days ahead through the hall's WeChat account, WeChat mini-program or People's Daily page; open Tuesday to Sunday 8.00 to 12.00 since reopening on 1 September 2026; since 1 January 2025 visitors can download an electronic commemorative ticket (People's Daily notices).

FUNDING
Chairman Mao Memorial Hall: state-funded: the Communist Party and the Chinese state
- Founders: built by joint decision of the CCP Central Committee, the National People's Congress Standing Committee, the State Council and the Central Military Commission, taken on 8 October 1976 (People's Daily, 2020 and 2024).
- Operator: the Chairman Mao Memorial Hall Administration Bureau, which issues its notices and closed the hall from 1 March to 31 August 2017 for repairs to the building and equipment (Phoenix News, 2017).

BOTTOM LINE
State money only, with no outside funder. The weak point is its purpose: a Party shrine to its founder, not an independent museum.
