LE CONSORTIUM – Dijon, France
Website: https://www.consortiummuseum.com (loaded 2026-10-20; the space now goes by "Consortium Museum". leconsortium.fr is still used for its contact email and appears in search results; that domain was not loaded directly.)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre founded in 1977 by Xavier Douroux and Franck Gautherot. It began above an alternative bookshop in Dijon (own site; Ministry of Culture tribute to Douroux, June 2017).
- Since 2011 it has been at 37 rue de Longvic, in a 4,000 m² building by Shigeru Ban with Jean de Gastines (own site; Wikipedia).
- Directors have included Eric Troncy, Seungduk Kim, Stéphanie Moisdon and Anne Pontégnie. Linked ventures: the publisher Les presses du réel (1992) and Anna Sanders Films. It runs the Nouveaux Commanditaires programme with the Fondation de France.
- Open Wednesday to Sunday 2–6pm, Fridays to 8pm. Admission €5, free on Friday evenings; free for under-18s, disabled visitors and a helper, students and others. Full access for people with reduced mobility, induction loops, and tours in sign language and with audio description (own site; Destination Dijon).

FUNDING
Le Consortium: mainly public, with a growing circle of private patrons.
- Public: Ministry of Culture / DRAC Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, City of Dijon, Région Bourgogne-Franche-Comté and the Côte-d'Or departmental council (own visit page; Wikipedia).
- Private, as listed by the space: Fonds de dotation Le Consortium Unlimited, Les Amis du Consortium, DVF, Groupe Roger Martin, Daniel Xu & Flora Huang Foundation, M ART Foundation, Vanessa Guo & Jean-Mathieu Martini (Galerie Marguo), Page Gallery, and Yan Pei-Ming and Ellen Wu. A separate Asia Pacific Society for Consortium Museum is also listed under Support.
- Its 2023 Friends bulletin calls the Consortium an independent structure in which patronage plays "a decisive role" in its economy. The Friends society is run by the endowment fund Le Consortium Unlimited.
- No reporting found of budget figures or published accounts.

BOTTOM LINE
Funding is mostly public: French state, region, department and city, together with named private patrons and dealers. No contested funder was found. Worth watching: it is relying more on private and Asia-Pacific patron circles that the space names but does not quantify.
