WIDER CONTEXT
- The Gwangju Biennale Foundation began calling the planned "Taiwan Pavilion" the "NTMoFA Pavilion" on 10 June 2026; Taiwan's Ministry of Culture called the removal of "Taiwan" an act of political censorship (Taiwan News, 20 August 2026).
- The National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts wrote to the Biennale Foundation and to the Asia Culture Center asking both to meet their contractual obligations, including providing the exhibition venue and coordinating installation (Taiwan News).
- The Biennale Foundation approved the "Taiwan Pavilion" name on 25 August 2026, and the Jeonnam-Gwangju Special Self-Governing City apologised for "insufficient communication between the Gwangju Biennale and the participating institutions" (Asia Economy, 31 August 2026).
- The ACC is the venue of the Taiwanese exhibition: setup of the space there was halted for some time during the dispute, and the ACC later told the museum it could begin installation on 27 August 2026 (Asia Economy).
