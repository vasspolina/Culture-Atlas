RAK ART FOUNDATION: Riffa, Bahrain
ID: rak-art-foundation-ras-al-khaimah
Website: https://rakartfoundation.com

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art foundation in Riffa, in the centre of Bahrain, housed in the restored childhood home of its founder, Rashid Al Khalifa (Identity, Lemma Shehadi, 15 June 2022).
- Founder Rashid Al Khalifa, born in Riffa in 1952, is an artist, patron and collector who founded the Bahrain Arts Society in 1983 (foundation website); Identity describes him as a member of the Bahraini royal family.
- Launched in 2020 as the Rashid Al Khalifa Art Foundation (Identity). The foundation's own site says a white-cube gallery extension was completed in 2020.
- The house dates from the 1920s with a 1960s extension that includes his childhood bedroom; he acquired it from his family in 2014 and restored and expanded it with Italian architect Davide Chiaverini, adding a two-storey courtyard pavilion with steel mashrabiya panels (Identity).
- The collection holds hundreds of international works of fine and decorative art (foundation website), including Pierre Soulages, Damien Hirst, Fernando Botero, Mohammed Melehi and Fateh Al Moudarres, and Al Khalifa's own work (Identity).
- The foundation's site lists exhibitions, the RAK Art Prize and The Art Station as its programmes. Bahrain Confidential states that Shaikh Rashid bin Khalifa Al Khalifa founded it in Riffa to make an enormous art collection available to the Bahraini people (Bahrain Confidential, 14 May 2025).
- The Art Station ran a three-month residency in Bahrain from 7 March to 30 May 2024 for US citizens over 21, with housing, a monthly stipend of 1,500 USD and a 5,300 USD artwork production grant (e-flux announcement, 20 January 2024).

FUNDING
RAK Art Foundation: founder-led non-profit; one documented outside grant, from the US Embassy
- The foundation's own site describes it as a non-profit. It occupies the founder's childhood home, which he acquired from his family in 2014 (foundation website, Identity).
- The 2024 Art Station residency was "funded by the US Embassy in Manama" (e-flux announcement).

Not related: Ras Al Khaimah Art (RAK Art) in the United Arab Emirates. The Al Qasimi Foundation's arts and culture page says the Ras Al Khaimah Art Festival was founded in 2013 and is now Ras Al Khaimah Art, a year-round hub, and that the Al Qasimi Foundation convenes the community through the annual festival and funds its Artist-in-Residence Program and Film Grants. It is a different organisation from the Bahrain foundation, which is based in Riffa and founded by Rashid Al Khalifa; the two share only the abbreviation "RAK" (Al Qasimi Foundation, Ras Al Khaimah Art website).

BOTTOM LINE
The RAK Art Foundation is a non-profit built around one collector's collection and childhood home in Riffa, Bahrain. The documented outside money is the US Embassy in Manama's funding of the 2024 Art Station residency.
