ID: museo-archeologico-nazionale-di-napoli

Red flags: 2025 criticism of an unpaid photography competition
- In March 2025 the museum opened a photography competition for entrants aged 18 to 30, offering winners exposure on its social media and on banners for its main façade but no fee; submissions closed on 16 May (The Art Newspaper, 20 June 2025).
- Mi Riconosci, a group representing culture-sector workers and students, posted that "MANN offers no payment, even though it will receive a large number of high-quality photos to use in ways that will even generate economic returns"; Corriere della Sera called it "marketing with unpaid photos" (The Art Newspaper).
- Raffaella Bosso, the museum administrator who devised the competition, said "Photographers should be paid for their work" but "in this case, we did not feel it amounted to an employment relationship"; the museum did not withdraw or change the notice, which "remains as it is" (The Art Newspaper).
