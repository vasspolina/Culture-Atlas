CENTRO PECCI: Prato, Italy
ID: centro-pecci-prato
Website: https://www.centropecci.it

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Viale della Repubblica 277, Prato, Tuscany, run by the Fondazione per le Arti contemporanee in Toscana, a non-profit foundation established on 30 July 2015 (Regione Toscana).
- Founded in 1988 by the Municipality of Prato and the Pecci family, local textile entrepreneurs who donated the land and building; the centre's own history credits the initiative also to Cassa di Risparmi e Depositi di Prato, Unione Industriale Pratese and Monte dei Paschi di Siena, with many individuals as founding members. Wikipedia credits curator Amnon Barzel and industrialist Enrico Pecci, and says it was founded in memory of Pecci's son Luigi.
- Named for Luigi Pecci, son of the industrialist Enrico Pecci (Wikipedia).
- Building: the original was designed by Italo Gamberini and is described by the centre as the first building in Italy built specifically to host contemporary art exhibitions. The extension by Maurice Nio opened in 2016, a golden structure wrapping the original, backed by European funding, the Municipality of Prato and the Region of Tuscany (centre's own history).
- What is there: museum, information and documentation centre for visual arts (CID), education department, a collection of over 1,000 works by more than 300 artists, a library, auditorium-cinema, bookshop, restaurant and a sculpture garden (Wikipedia).
- Stefano Collicelli Cagol has led the museum since January 2022 (Il Sole 24 Ore); Lorenzo Bini Smaghi chaired the foundation in 2023 (Artribune).

FUNDING
Centro Pecci: mainly public: Comune di Prato and Regione Toscana
- Comune di Prato: founding member of the foundation, with 47.09 percent and a contribution of EUR 1,068,000 covering use of the building plus an annual payment (Regione Toscana, page updated 17 July 2019). 2022 budget: EUR 1.158 million, including EUR 90,000 for library services (Il Sole 24 Ore, 26 December 2022). 2025 forecast: EUR 1,158,000 (La Nazione, 4 January 2025).
- Regione Toscana: supporting member since 2017 with 35.27 percent and EUR 800,000 (Regione Toscana). Il Sole 24 Ore reports EUR 800,000 in the 2022 budget, based on the 2020 contribution, and a 26 percent fall to EUR 635,000 in 2021. 2025 forecast: EUR 775,000 (La Nazione).
- Associazione per l'Arte contemporanea L. Pecci: founding member with 17.64 percent and EUR 400,000, made up of an initial payment of EUR 50,000 and the transfer of the museum business (Regione Toscana).
- 2025 forecast (La Nazione): total income EUR 3,196,470, of which public contributions EUR 2,026,500 (Comune di Prato EUR 1,158,000, Regione Toscana EUR 775,000, Ministry of Cultural Heritage EUR 13,500, CID EUR 80,000); sponsorships EUR 358,000; projects and Art Bonus EUR 337,000; memberships EUR 93,000; supporting members EUR 50,000.
- Results: a surplus of about EUR 26,000 in 2021 (Il Sole 24 Ore); a loss of EUR 333,000 in 2022, which the foundation put down to higher energy costs (Artribune, 2023). The Fratelli d'Italia city councillor Tommaso Cocci called the 2022 results indefensible, said the Comune gives the Pecci about EUR 1.2 million a year and said something was not working in the museum's cultural offer (La Nazione, 7 July 2023).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Press criticism of the Pecci concerns its finances, its dependence on public money and its handling of staff.
1. Artribune: Livia Montagnoli, "Il Centro Pecci di Prato licenzia dipendenti per problemi di bilancio. Polemiche e proteste" (2023)
URL: https://www.artribune.com/?p=1007160
- Reports that two of the museum's 18 staff were dismissed, the first notice dated 28 August 2023; the unions CGIL and UIL said the Federculture contract requires consultation before restructuring and that this was not done, and that external collaborators were already arranged to replace the dismissed roles.
- Names the 2022 loss of EUR 333,000 and cuts to contributions from the Comune di Prato and Regione Toscana. The mayor Matteo Biffoni and culture councillor Simone Mangani asked for explanations; regional councillor Ilaria Bugetti called the episode "fatto grave e irricevibile" (translation: serious and unacceptable).
2. Il Sole 24 Ore: Maria Adelaide Marchesoni, "Pecci, per la programmazione artistica fondamentale l'intervento pubblico" (2022)
URL: https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/pecci-la-programmazione-artistica-fondamentale-l-intervento-pubblico-AE1xLuPC
- Describes the foundation's reliance on contributions from the Comune di Prato and the Regione Toscana as "una dipendenza troppo vincolante per la programmazione artistica del museo" (translation: a dependence too binding for the museum's artistic programming); this is the author's own assessment.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Centro Pecci is public: the Comune di Prato and the Regione Toscana together provide about EUR 1.9 million of a EUR 3.2 million 2025 forecast budget, with sponsorships (EUR 358,000), projects and Art Bonus (EUR 337,000) and memberships (EUR 93,000) among the rest. The founders were the Pecci family, the Comune and local banks and industry.
