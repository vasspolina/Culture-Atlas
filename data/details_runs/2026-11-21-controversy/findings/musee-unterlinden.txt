ID: musee-unterlinden

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2016 France Bleu Alsace (now ICI Alsace) reported that the renovation of the Musée Unterlinden would cost 48.7 million euros, and that the City of Colmar would pay 1.9 million euros more, 10 per cent above the initial cost (Guillaume Chhum, 16 November 2016).
- The overrun comprised close to 900,000 euros in extra payments to some firms, 540,000 euros of safety works, 195,000 euros of unplanned works in the archaeology room and 380,000 euros of extra works requested by the client (France Bleu Alsace).
- In the council deliberation, mayor Gilbert Meyer blamed his own services, calling it a "bricolage budgétaire inadmissible" ("unacceptable budget tinkering", translated) (France Bleu Alsace).
- Socialist councillor Julien Ernst asked the mayor to withdraw these remarks from the deliberation, which the mayor refused, and planned to question him at the council meeting of 21 November 2016 (France Bleu Alsace).
