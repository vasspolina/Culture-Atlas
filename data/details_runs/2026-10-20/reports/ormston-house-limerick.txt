ORMSTON HOUSE: Limerick, Ireland
ID: ormston-house-limerick
Website: https://ormstonhouse.com

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery and cultural resource centre at 9-10 Patrick Street, Limerick city (Ormston House website; Wikipedia).
- Opened in 2011. Wikipedia says it began when art students occupied the ground floor of the 19th-century building, with support from the Creative Limerick scheme; Mary Conlon is named as co-director and founder (Limerick Post, 2021).
- Named for the building's earlier use: Wikipedia says Jack Ormston opened Limerick's first self-service supermarket there in 1961.
- Run as a company limited by guarantee (Limerick Post, 2021). In July 2018 Mayor James Collins described it as run on a voluntary basis (Limerick Post).
- Programme of exhibitions, talks and performances; by January 2021 it had hosted around 350 exhibitions and events with 296 artists from 27 countries (Limerick Post). Wikipedia names Richard Mosse's "The Enclave" (2014) and Mark Dion's "Against the Current" (2015) among exhibitions, and Pádraic E. Moore as artistic director from 2023.
- Open Wednesday to Saturday, 12 to 6pm (Ormston House website).

FUNDING
Ormston House: mainly public: the Arts Council of Ireland and Limerick City and County Council
- Arts Council Arts Grant Funding of 200,000 euros, listed for Ormston House among Limerick recipients (Limerick Post, Alan Jacques, 5 August 2025).
- Limerick Council: Mayor James Collins said council funding had helped Ormston House operate since it opened in 2011 (Limerick Post, Bernie English, 25 July 2018).
- Building: the building was on the market for 500,000 euros in July 2018 (Limerick Post, 25 July 2018). In April 2020 Limerick City and County Council bought the ground floor and basement of 9-10 Patrick Street from Mount Kennett Investment Company for 280,000 euros; the council and the JP McManus Benevolent Fund funded the purchase (Limerick Post, 29 January 2021). Wikipedia says the council then gave the centre a 30-year lease and dates the purchase to 2021; the Limerick Post reported councillors adopting a long-term draft lease.
- Wikipedia says the earlier threat of sale followed the sale of the building's loan book by NAMA to Cerberus Capital Management, and that more than 3,000 people signed an online petition against sale on the open market.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Ormston House is public: Arts Council grants and Limerick council funding, with the council owning the building. The Limerick Post (29 January 2021) says the JP McManus Benevolent Fund and the council funded the April 2020 purchase of the building.
