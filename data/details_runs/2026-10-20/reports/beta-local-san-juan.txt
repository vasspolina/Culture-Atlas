BETA-LOCAL: San Juan, Puerto Rico
ID: beta-local-san-juan
Website: https://betalocal.org (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run platform for artistic practice in Puerto Rico and the Caribbean at Avenida Manuel Fernández Juncos 1412, San Juan; it describes itself as "an organization, a working group, and a physical space" (Beta-Local; e-flux directory).
- Founded in 2009 in San Juan by the artists Beatriz Santiago Muñoz and José "Tony" Cruz and the curator Michy Marxuach, in response to the lack of local contemporary galleries, museums and art schools (e-flux, Marina Reyes Franco, 6 November 2018).
- It has administered Casa del Sargento, a historic building in Old San Juan owned by the Puerto Rican Institute of Culture, since 2014 (e-flux directory).
- Programmes listed on the archived site: La Práctica, The Harbor, La Iván Illich, Listening Sessions and Publicaciones, with Becas (grants) (Beta-Local archive). La Práctica is a nine-month programme running September to May for five to eight fellows, and Beta-Local does not charge fellows (e-flux).
- Co-directors listed on the 2025 tax filing: Michael Linares and Pablo Guardiola; board president Marilucy Gonzalez Baez (ProPublica).
- Registered as the 501(c)(3) Beta-local Inc (EIN 66-0736115, exempt since October 2011).

FUNDING
Beta-Local: foundation-funded: Mellon, Warhol and Rauschenberg foundations, and contributions
- Mellon Foundation: 230,000 dollars awarded on 6 March 2019 over 27 months for staff infrastructure and re-granting to other arts spaces and projects, and 40,000 dollars awarded on 25 August 2021 over 7 months for strategic planning (Mellon Foundation grant pages).
- Andy Warhol Foundation: a 100,000 dollar multi-year program support grant in the fall 2018 cycle (Warhol Foundation). After Hurricane Maria the Warhol Foundation, the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation and Lin-Manuel Miranda through the Hispanic Federation each gave 100,000 dollars to Beta-Local's El Serrucho: Hurricane Maria Emergency Fund, announced 17 October 2017. Beta-Local distributed over 400,000 dollars to artists, organisers and cultural workers with Warhol and other funders.
- Revenue for the fiscal year ending June 2025 was 328,157 dollars, all from contributions, down from 688,867 dollars in fiscal 2021 (ProPublica).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Beta-Local is US arts philanthropy: the Mellon Foundation, the Andy Warhol Foundation and the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation, plus contributions. It administers Casa del Sargento, a building owned by the Puerto Rican Institute of Culture.
