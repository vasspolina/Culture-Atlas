MINSHENG ART MUSEUM: Shanghai, China
ID: minsheng-art-museum
Website: http://www.minshengart.com/en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum on the fifth and sixth floors of the Minsheng Bank Building at 48 Weihai Road, Huangpu District, west of People's Square (museum website; Yicai, 23 May 2024).
- China Minsheng Bank founded it in 2008; the museum calls itself the first art museum dedicated to public welfare founded by a Chinese financial institution (museum website).
- It has moved four times: from the Red Town site on West Huaihai Road to the former French Pavilion of the Expo site, where a partner museum opened in 2014 and merged with it in 2017; to an old factory at 210 Wenshui Road, Jing'an, in May 2019; and to Weihai Road in May 2024 (museum website; The Paper; Yicai).
- The museum left the French Pavilion at the end of 2018 because of city construction work, said its then director Gan Zhiyi (甘智漪) (The Paper, 28 December 2018).
- On site: a 1,200 square metre exhibition hall on the sixth floor, a shop and cafe, a multimedia room and the Minsheng Art Archives, with nearly 8,000 volumes from art institutions, media, artists and critics (museum website).
- Recent shows include Shanghai: Capital of Photography 1910s to 2020s and Tales of Now, Swiss comics from the Fumetto festival (museum website); the Weihai Road space opened with a history of Shanghai animation films (Yicai).
- The museum says it has held more than a hundred exhibitions and reached about one million people (museum website).

FUNDING
Minsheng Art Museum: corporate: China Minsheng Bank, its founder, with district government support for its archive
- China Minsheng Bank set up the museum as part of its corporate social responsibility platform and supports it; the museum gives no figures (museum website).
- The museum now sits in the bank's own Minsheng Bank Building (Yicai, 2024).
- The archive began in 2017 as a government project backed by the Pudong Expo administrative committee; the Jing'an district government gave money to buy books before the 2019 reopening, and the archive became the art branch of the Jing'an District Library (The Paper, 24 March 2020).

Red flags: 2025 regulatory fines on China Minsheng Bank
- On 31 October 2025 the National Financial Regulatory Administration fined China Minsheng Bank 58.65 million yuan for imprudent management of loans, bills and interbank business and non-compliant regulatory data reporting; six managers were warned and fined a total of 360,000 yuan (Tencent News, 3 November 2025).
- In January 2025 the People's Bank of China fined the bank 17.055 million yuan and confiscated 990,700 yuan for eight violations, including failures in customer identification and dealing with unidentified customers; in September 2025 the regulator fined it 5.9 million yuan over IT management (Tencent News).
- In 2017 China's banking regulator fined the bank 27.5 million yuan for selling 1.65 billion yuan of fake wealth management products through its Hangtianqiao sub-branch in Beijing (Caixin Global, 1 December 2017).

BOTTOM LINE
A bank's own museum, paid for by China Minsheng Bank, which publishes no figures for it. The weak point is the bank's record of regulatory fines, including anti-money-laundering failures in 2025.
