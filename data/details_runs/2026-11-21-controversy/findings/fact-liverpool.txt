ID: fact-liverpool

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 28 March 2014 FACT announced that its front-of-house team would include volunteers as well as paid staff, and that 11 of the 13 staff of the previous team were continuing in paid roles (FACT, 28 March 2014).
- The Double Negative reported that the previous front-of-house team had nine casual staff on zero-hour contracts, three part-time staff and one full-time member, with 21 volunteers recruited the year before, and the new structure three full-time staff, six casual staff, no part-time roles and 71 volunteers. One staff member took voluntary redundancy, and 60 hours a week were lost across the part-time and casual pool (The Double Negative, Laura Robertson, 7 May 2014).
- The Precarious Workers Brigade, in an open letter, said FACT had been unethical in pushing zero-hour contracts and replacing front-of-house staff with volunteers (The Double Negative, 7 May 2014).
- Five affected staff, speaking anonymously, said their biggest grievance was how the changes were handled and communicated (The Double Negative, 7 May 2014).
- Executive Director Iona Horsburgh said the restructure was a difficult decision made in reaction to supply and demand, that FACT "undertook a full consultation process including individual meetings, group meetings and invited feedback", and: "We knew that we needed to restructure the department" (The Double Negative, 7 May 2014).
- Unverified: Liverpool IWW, a trade union branch, wrote that FACT had decided to make eleven front-of-house staff redundant and replace them with unpaid volunteers, and with Liverpool Class Action announced a picket of FACT for 1 May 2014 demanding "the reinstatement of the sacked FACT staff", mainly in solidarity with striking Ritzy cinema workers in London (Liverpool IWW, 28 April 2014).
