ID: dunedin-public-art-gallery-dunedin

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2008 the gallery left six nude dwarf figures, some in sexual positions, out of Michele Beevors' exhibition "Debbie Does Disney". Beevors said: "I've never been censored before. It's just bizarre," and said she would protest against the decision outside the gallery that day (NZ Herald, from the Otago Daily Times, 21 November 2008).
- Curator of contemporary art Aaron Kreisler replied: "It was always a curated show and when we're asked to curate things we've got to make a judgment call on what we consider are the strongest works" (NZ Herald, 21 November 2008).
