NORTH HAMGYONG PROVINCIAL REVOLUTIONARY HISTORY MUSEUM: Chongjin, North Korea
ID: north-hamgyong-provincial-revolutionary-history-museum
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Provincial museum on the main square of Chongjin, the seat of North Hamgyong Province, immediately next to the statues of Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Il (Koryo Tours, May 2020).
- Koryo Tours says every North Korean province has a Provincial Revolutionary History Museum, and that this one covers the revolutionary activity of the north of the country, where most of the guerrilla activity took place.
- Founding: the Ministry of Unification's 2022 directory lists a 함경북도혁명사적관 (North Hamgyong Provincial Revolutionary Museum, my translation) separately from the province's Comrade Kim Il Sung Revolutionary Museum, and the atlas matches this museum to the first by name only. On that match, the ministry's 2022 personnel profiles record that Ri Hi Yong, then chairman of the North Hamgyong Provincial Party Committee, attended a meeting in October 2019 for the 50th anniversary of its founding, which dates it to 1969.
- Exhibits include a room of slogan trees, which Koryo Tours says are officially presented as trees inscribed by Kim Il Sung's guerrilla followers, with a story of local students who died shielding them from a forest fire.
- Visiting: Koryo Tours says all tours are guided by museum staff, who focus on the activities of the North Korean leaders, that a tour covers only some of the halls, and that photos are not allowed inside.

FUNDING
North Hamgyong Provincial Revolutionary History Museum (Chongjin): state-run: a provincial state museum
- Staff: the ministry's 2022 directory lists Kim Un Hwa (김은화) as a section chief of the 함경북도혁명사적관, and Ri Chang Il (리창일) as director of the province's Comrade Kim Il Sung Revolutionary Museum (함경북도김일성동지혁명사적관) (my romanisations).
- North Korea announces its state budget as yearly percentage changes, not amounts. KCNA claimed on 24 March 2026 that 2026 spending would reach 105.8 percent of 2025, with funds for the cultural sector at 103.1 percent of last year's.

Red flags: US sanctions on the North Korean government, 2016
- US Executive Order 13722 of 15 March 2016 blocks property in US jurisdiction of the Government of North Korea and the Workers' Party of Korea; section 9 defines the Government as the DPRK government and its agencies, instrumentalities and controlled entities (Federal Register, 18 March 2016). The order does not name the museum.

Not related: the North Hamgyong Province E-Library, a separate state library opposite the museum on the other side of the statues (Koryo Tours).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is paid for and run by the North Korean state, and the US blocking order on that state is its one Red flag.
