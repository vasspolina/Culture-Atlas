ARGOS: Brussels, Belgium
ID: argos-brussels
Website: https://www.argosarts.org/ (checked 2026-11-20; redirects to visit.argosarts.org)

OVERVIEW
- Centre for audiovisual arts at Werfstraat 13 (rue du Chantier), 1000 Brussels, in the historic Les Quais district (argos, "Our history" and "visit us").
- Founded in Brussels in 1989 to promote the emerging Belgian audiovisual arts scene, first with screenings in cultural centres around Belgium and early distribution of videos to festivals, art institutions and television abroad (argos, "Our history"). The Belgian company register lists the non-profit association (vzw) behind it under the name ARGOS, with a start date of 18 July 1985 (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises).
- Since 1999 it has occupied a former storage facility, after several moves around the city, converted in stages into two exhibition spaces, a projection room, a media library, a climate-controlled archive, studios, offices, a café and a bookshop (argos, "Our history").
- The collection holds more than 5,000 artist films and videos, and argos distributes works from it to screenings and teaching (argos, "Our history" and FAQ).
- Directors: founding directors Koen Van Daele (1989 to 1993) and Frie Depraetere (1989 to 2013), Katerina Gregos (2006 to 2007), Paul Willemsen (1996 to 2012), Rolf Quaghebeur (2011 to 2018) and Niels Van Tomme (December 2018 to February 2024). Joachim Naudts is now general and artistic coordinator (argos, "Our history" and "Our team").
- Current season: "What the Land Remembers: Counter-Archives from Iran", 25 September 2026 to 21 February 2027; earlier shows were "Becoming Ancestors" (February to June 2026) and "Magical Realism: Imagining Natural Dis/order" with WIELS (May to September 2025) (argos programme).
- Visiting: Thursday to Sunday, 11:00 to 18:00. Visitors choose 10 euros (recommended), 5 or 15; free for under-18s, ICOM members, Museumpass and Brussels Card holders, disabled visitors and their assistant, and press; Paspartoe holders pay less, and Article 27 entry is 1.25 euros. The media library opens by appointment on Thursday and Friday (argos, "visit us" and FAQ).

FUNDING
Argos: mainly public: the Flemish Community and the Flemish Community Commission
- Structural support comes from the Ministry of the Flemish Community and the Flemish Community Commission (VGC); argos has been primarily subsidised by the Flemish Community since 1994 (argos, FAQ and "Our history").
- The City of Brussels, the Flemish Community's Cultural Heritage Decree and the Flemish Audiovisual Fund support individual projects (argos FAQ).
- Accounts for 2025 filed with the National Bank of Belgium show gross margin 574,867 euros (604,276 in 2024), staff costs 551,816 euros (462,785 in 2024), a loss of 11,231 euros after a 2024 profit of 94,845 euros, an average of 8.1 full-time staff and total assets of 471,392 euros.
- Earned income: pay-what-you-can tickets, distribution fees for films from the collection, occasional space hire, the café and the rile* bookshop (argos, "visit us", "Our team" and FAQ).
- Board (argos, "Our team"): presidents Mohanad Yaqubi and Jens Van Lathem; members Anouk De Clercq, Kristof Van Hoorde, Tom Bonte, Sorana Munsya and Dirk De Wit. The company register lists six office holders, among them Mohanad Alyacoubi since 11 December 2024, Tom Bonte since 9 July 2020 and Anouk De Clercq since 24 November 2023.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money. The one thing worth watching is its reliance on a single structural funder, the Flemish Community.
