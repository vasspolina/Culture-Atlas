LA STATION: Nice, France
ID: la-station-nice
Website: https://www.lastation.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run contemporary art space and residency in the Halle sud of Le 109, 89 route de Turin, Nice (lastation.org).
- Founded in 1996 in Nice and run by the association Starter; it began in a former petrol station at 26 boulevard Gambetta, from which it takes its name (lastation.org).
- In October 2009 it moved into the south hall of the former refrigerated warehouses (entrepôts frigorifiques) "mis à disposition par la ville de Nice" (lent by the City of Nice); the renovated premises cover 1,000 m², shared between exhibition spaces and studios, with about a dozen artists working there (lastation.org).
- Le 109 is a contemporary culture site that brings together more than a dozen associations; La Station was one of its founding structures (lastation.org, "30 ans de La Station").
- Programme: exhibitions on site and outside the walls, performances, readings, concerts, residency showings, residency calls, publications (Editions) and arts education.
- Residency: selected artists get a 40 m² studio and accommodation in Nice, a 5,000 euro grant covering per diem, production and travel, and a public presentation (ArtConnect call for applications).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Saturday 2 pm to 7 pm except public holidays, and by appointment.

FUNDING
La Station: self-organised artists' association with public support: DRAC PACA for the residency, and the City of Nice for the premises
- The association says it runs on its own economy by pooling the skills of its participants, and offers studio spaces in exchange (lastation.org, "L'espace et le temps de l'exposition" page, translation).
- The City of Nice lends the warehouse premises (lastation.org).
- The residency programme has been funded since 2017 by the DRAC PACA (the regional cultural affairs office of the Ministry of Culture); the call also credits support from local authorities and the State (ArtConnect).
- For the 2026 solo show by Camille Llobet, the site names La Fondation des Artistes, the ADAGP (2025 video creation grant) and "AIC DRAC Rhône alpes" as partners (lastation.org).

BOTTOM LINE
La Station is run by its resident artists on the pooled skills of its members, with the City of Nice supplying the building and the DRAC PACA funding its residency since 2017. For the 2026 solo show by Camille Llobet, the site names La Fondation des Artistes and the ADAGP as partners.
