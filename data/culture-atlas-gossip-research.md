# Culture Atlas: Gossip Mode research beyond Reddit and X

Research checked **9 October 2026**. This expanded research set contains **25 cases across 22 institutions**, **77 separate review leads** (75 employee accounts, including two title-only leads, and two visitor accounts), and **126 linked sources**. It starts with London, New York, Paris, Berlin, Amsterdam, and Madrid, with Utrecht added for BAK, which appeared in the supplied screenshot.

This is a purposeful sample of disputes and their outcomes. It is not an audit of the 1,074 spaces displayed in Culture Atlas, a prevalence estimate, or an ethical ranking. The application and its institution master list were not supplied; nothing has been integrated or reclassified.

## What this adds to Gossip Mode

The most useful additions are union statements, artist letters, campaign documents, formal decisions, and arts reporting that includes replies and follow-ups. These sources expose questions about pay, subcontracting, artistic freedom, board conflicts, access, and funding changes that sponsor lists alone do not answer.

### London

#### C01 · British Museum — BP funding and information disclosure

A £50m, ten-year BP partnership was announced for the museum masterplan. [S01](https://www.museumsassociation.org/museums-journal/news/2023/12/british-museum-announces-new-50m-bp-deal-to-fund-masterplan/).

- **Claim:** Culture Unstained opposes continued fossil-fuel sponsorship. [S03](https://cultureunstained.org/2026/08/28/science-museum-drops-oil-sponsor-bp-after-mounting-pressure-on-controversial-partnership/).
- **Reply:** The museum said BP funding supports transformation and continued public access. [S01](https://www.museumsassociation.org/museums-journal/news/2023/12/british-museum-announces-new-50m-bp-deal-to-fund-masterplan/).
- **Outcome:** July 2026 ICO ruling upheld some withholding, required release of already-public material, and found response-deadline breaches. [S02](https://ico.org.uk/action-weve-taken/decision-notices/2026/07/ic-412309-r0n3/).
- **Limits:** The ICO ruling concerns information handling; it is not a ruling that BP sponsorship is unlawful.
- **Next check:** Check current contract disclosures and compliance with the ICO notice.

## Suggested representation in Culture Atlas

Give each case a topic such as **Labour**, **Sponsorship**, **Boards**, **Programming**, **Artist disputes**, **Access**, **Funding changes**, or **Outcomes**. Show a separate status such as **Ongoing as last reported**, **Agreement reached**, **Relationship ended**, **Historical event**, **Disputed claim**, **Current outcome unknown**, or **Review lead**.

A useful card contains the event date, the named source of the claim, the institution's reply, the latest outcome date, and links to the underlying evidence. Keep employer and contractor identities explicit. A V&A union dispute is not interchangeable with a contractor guards' dispute; Concertgebouw venue policy is not automatically Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra policy.

Funding status, workplace conditions, and programming controversies should remain separate dimensions. This research does not justify automatically moving an institution into a single “clean” or “flagged” category. A settled dispute should retain its history and display its outcome.

The companion JSON contains stable research IDs, source references, date precision, responses, outcomes, limitations, and follow-up tasks. Its `atlas_id` values are null until the cases can be matched against the actual institution master list.

## Research limits

- Public sources were searched; no institution or individual was contacted. “Reply not located” means this research did not find one.
- Campaign and union allegations remain attributed. Court and regulatory outcomes are limited to the question actually decided.
- Current status is not continuously monitored. Later outcomes were sought, but cases without a located update remain explicitly uncertain.
- Linked social posts were not systematically accessible; this is not an Instagram or Facebook comment analysis. Private groups were not accessed.
- Some article sections require registration. Only accessible text was used, including the public contract-ratification confirmation in S15.
- Wayback snapshot retrieval failed. No historical website change reconstruction is claimed; dated documents and reporting provide the historical evidence here.
- Individual review identities and employment are unverified. Old access accounts do not establish current physical accessibility. Employee posting dates can describe earlier employment; parent and contractor accounts require site matching.
- Public Glassdoor excerpts varied across profile, role, locale, and mirror pages. Related-review locators remain explicit; no missing permalink or reviewer vote was inferred.
- Two KW accounts expose titles and metadata only. Tate's matched profile includes ambiguous overview roles; only specified retail and curator records were retained, with identity limits. Eye had no readable public account in this view.
- Official KBB and Paris Musées scope text was read through public indexed excerpts; direct pages were unavailable.
- The sample was found through dispute-oriented searches. It cannot estimate how common misconduct is or support city comparisons.
