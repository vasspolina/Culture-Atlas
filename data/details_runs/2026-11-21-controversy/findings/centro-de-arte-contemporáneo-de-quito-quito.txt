WIDER CONTEXT
- In early 2017 the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad announced the departure of León Sierra Páez, coordinator of the Centro de Arte Contemporáneo, and his team; Sierra wrote that the team numbered five (El Comercio, 6 March 2017).
- In an open letter dated 21 February 2017 Sierra said the Secretaría de Cultura had ordered his removal without explanation, evaluation or stated cause, and called it illegitimate because he had been chosen by a merit competition among 134 applicants (El Comercio, 6 March 2017).
- Quito culture secretary Pablo Corral Vega told El Comercio there had been "un maltrato sistemático a los curadores, artistas" (systematic mistreatment of curators and artists, translated) and that "Se estaban tomando decisiones que no eran consultadas con el resto del sector" (decisions were being taken without consulting the rest of the sector, translated); he said the selection committee had doubted appointing someone from the performing arts, and that all legal procedures had been followed in ending Sierra's employment (El Comercio, 6 March 2017).
- On 4 March 2026 the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad and the Fundación Teatro Nacional Sucre filed an unconstitutionality suit at the Constitutional Court against the reform of the COOTAD law; Mayor Pabel Muñoz said the reform could affect the continuity of municipal cultural bodies (Expreso, 8 May 2026).
- The reform requires municipalities to spend 70 per cent of their income on physical works, leaving 30 per cent for current spending, where culture is now classed (Prensa Latina, 18 May 2026).
- The Centro de Arte Contemporáneo is one of the spaces the Fundación Museos de la Ciudad runs (Expreso, 8 May 2026).
