ID: cantor-arts-center

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2020 Stanford said that director Susan Dackerman would leave the museum by mutual agreement, after "a careful analysis of the situation at the Cantor Arts Center", "including an external investigation of specific issues" (The Art Newspaper, Nancy Kenney, 19 November 2020).
- Several employees had described a "toxic" environment, and a staff document cited by the Stanford Daily said at least 30 staff members had left since Dackerman arrived in 2017 (The Art Newspaper, 19 November 2020).
- Stanford said deputy director James Gaddy had also left the museum (The Art Newspaper, 19 November 2020).
