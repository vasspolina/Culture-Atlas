MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE SANTO DOMINGO: Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-santo-domingo
Website: No museum website found. Searched 2026-10-24.

OVERVIEW
- State modern art museum in the Plaza de la Cultura Juan Pablo Duarte complex, Santo Domingo (El Día, 2016).
- Opened on 15 December 1976 (El Día, 2016).
- Designed by architect José Miniño; a concrete building with three levels and a basement (El Día; El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Remodelled for about four years and reopened during the COVID-19 pandemic; holds more than 1,500 pieces, 80 to 100 shown at a time (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Hosts the Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales; the 2025 edition showed 207 works chosen from 609 registered, at the museum (Acento, José Rafael Sosa, 2025).
- Began as the Galería de Arte Moderno in 1976 and was renamed in 1992 (El Inmobiliario). Open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00 with free admission, as reported in October 2022 (El Inmobiliario). No reporting found of current hours or accessibility information.

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Santo Domingo: public: Dominican Ministry of Culture
- The Ministry of Culture has overseen the museum since 2000 (El Inmobiliario, 2022). Artists' open letters hold the Ministry of Culture and the museum jointly responsible for the Bienal (El Nuevo Diario, 2020; Diario Libre, 2021).
- Museum director Federico Fondeur and curator Ana A. Fernández sit on the organising commission for the XXX Bienal, sworn in by Culture Minister Milagros Germán (El Día, 25 October 2022).
- No reporting found of a budget figure, a private sponsor or a donor list for the museum.

Red flags: Protest or boycott
- Postponement: the Ministry postponed the opening of the 29th Bienal on 16 December 2019, one day before the scheduled date, saying renovations at the museum were unfinished (Diario Libre, 16 December 2019; El Nuevo Diario, 16 July 2020).
- Protest: the Comité de la Bienal Invisible called a protest at the Plaza de la Cultura on 17 December 2019 at 5:30 p.m., demanding that the biennials be held on schedule (Diario Libre).
- July 2020 letter: La Bienal Invisible, La Otra Bienal and La Cooperativa called the handling of the 29th Bienal negligent, blaming the Ministry and the museum; the 25 signers included Guadalupe Casasnovas and Raquel Paiewonsky. They said works submitted for the 29th edition had been kept at the museum for nearly two years without explanation, and named Minister Eduardo Selman and María Elena Ditrén, who it says had directed the museum for 16 years (El Nuevo Diario, 16 July 2020).
- June 2021 letter: more than 60 artists, curators and other cultural workers signed a letter dated 11 June 2021 denouncing the "deficiente manejo" of the 29th Bienal, saying the selection list included works withdrawn before selection. They named Minister Carmen Heredia and museum director Federico Fondeur (Diario Libre, 23 June 2021).
- 2025: the lack of published catalogues for the 2021 and 2023 Bienales drew criticism, and artist Ramón Peña said he would no longer take part (Acento, 9 September 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism is frequent but comes from newspapers and artists' collectives, not art press. It concerns the Bienal's management and the Ministry, more than the museum's collection.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution with no private sponsor named, so the money is not the issue. The weak points are the repeated delays and Bienal management that artists blamed on the Ministry and the museum in 2019, 2020 and 2021, and the lack of published budget or catalogue information. Worth watching: whether the permanent Bienal committee that artists asked for in 2020 is created and publishes accounts.
