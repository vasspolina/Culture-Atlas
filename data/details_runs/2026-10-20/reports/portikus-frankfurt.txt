PORTIKUS: Frankfurt am Main, Germany
ID: portikus-frankfurt
Website: https://www.portikus.de

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art institution at Alte Brücke 2 on the Maininsel, the island in the River Main in Frankfurt; it is part of the art academy Hochschule für Bildende Künste, the Städelschule (Portikus).
- Founded in 1987 by Kasper König, who was its first director until 2001 (Portikus; English Wikipedia).
- Named for a portico of a public library built in 1825 that was destroyed in the Second World War (English Wikipedia).
- The first space was a simple cube made of shipping containers by the architects Marie-Theres Deutsch and Klaus Dreißigacker; in 2003 Portikus moved to the Leinwandhaus, and in 2006 to its present building (English Wikipedia).
- The present building was designed by Christoph Mäckler Architekten for the client Stiftung Giersch: a narrow tower with a steep slate roof, red-plastered masonry and one tall exhibition hall with a surrounding gallery (Baunetzwissen). Olafur Eliasson's light installation, the Lichtbogen, shows when the interior lights are on.
- Later directors include Daniel Birnbaum, Nikolaus Hirsch, Philippe Pirotte and Yasmil Raymond (2020 to 2024); Barbara Clausen is director and rector of the Städelschule from 2024 (Portikus; Journal Frankfurt, 11 September 2024).
- Visiting: free admission; open Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 19:00, Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 19:00, closed Monday. The main exhibition space is on the ground floor and wheelchair accessible, the lower gallery and mezzanine are reached only by stairs, and there is an accessible toilet (Portikus).

FUNDING
Portikus: publicly funded: the City of Frankfurt and the state of Hesse, with a non-profit foundation behind the building
- The City of Frankfurt Department for Culture funds Portikus permanently (Portikus).
- The Hessian Ministry for Science and Research, Art and Culture funds Portikus for 2022 to 2026 (Portikus).
- Stiftung Giersch financed the new building on the Maininsel, completed in 2006 (Portikus). Baunetzwissen names it as the client.
- Städelschule Portikus e.V., founded in 1973, supports the Städelschule, its students and Portikus (Portikus).
- THE FLAG became an official cooperation partner of Portikus in 2025 (Portikus).
- Ina Hartwig, city councillor for culture and science, chairs the Städelschule's Hochschulrat (university council) (Journal Frankfurt, 11 September 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Portikus is public funding: permanent funding from the City of Frankfurt Department for Culture and funding from the Hessian ministry for 2022 to 2026, with the 2006 building financed by the non-profit foundation Stiftung Giersch. THE FLAG became an official cooperation partner of Portikus in 2025.
