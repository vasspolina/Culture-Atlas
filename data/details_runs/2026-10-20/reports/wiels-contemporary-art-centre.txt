WIELS CONTEMPORARY ART CENTRE: Brussels, Belgium
ID: wiels-contemporary-art-centre
Website: https://wiels.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Avenue Van Volxemlaan 354, 1190 Brussels, in Forest, a municipality of Brussels. It has no collection and shows temporary exhibitions (Wikipedia).
- Opened in 2007; the centre says it was "initiated by art lovers in 2007". The Brussels-Capital Region acquired the building in 2001 and renovated it for the centre (Wikipedia, WIELS).
- The name comes from the Wielemans-Ceuppens brewery.
- Building: the Blomme building, designed by Adrien Blomme and built by the Wielemans-Ceuppens family in 1931 to expand its brewing business. The brewery closed in 1988 and the building was given protected status in 1993 (Wikipedia, WIELS).
- On site: exhibitions, events, guided tours, a café, a bookshop, education and participation programmes, an artist residency programme and venue hire (WIELS website).
- Visiting: open 11:00 to 18:00 on the day checked; other days are not listed. Tickets €12, with reduced prices of €8, €5 and €0. The page gives no accessibility information.

FUNDING
WIELS: mainly public with corporate and gallery partners: Flemish, French-speaking and Brussels public bodies, plus Duvel Moortgat, Phillips and others
- The website shows logos of Flemish, Wallonia-Brussels, Brussels-Capital and Brussels-French community bodies and the National Lottery, with no statement of what each pays (WIELS).
- The Brussels-Capital Region bought the building in 2001 and began renovating it for the centre (WIELS, Wikipedia); no cost is stated on the pages opened.
- Structural partners: Aureus Ars & Scientia, Duvel Moortgat, Phillips and the WIELS Club.
- Exhibition and performance partners: the Netherlands embassy in Belgium, David Zwirner, Eeckman Art & Insurance, europalia, FABA, Fondation Willame Stichting, Fundacao Calouste Gulbenkian (Delegation en France), Galerie Buchholz, Goethe-Institut, Hauser & Wirth, Kurimanzutto, Lhoist Group, Loterie Nationale Loterij, Marian Goodman Gallery, Mondriaan Fonds, Richard Saltoun Gallery and the Terra Foundation for American Art.
- Education partners: Apheon, Cocof, GBL and PECA. The partners page gives no dates or amounts.
- GBL (Groupe Bruxelles Lambert) is a holding company controlled by the Frere and Desmarais families. Wikipedia lists its major holdings as of September 2023 as SGS, Pernod Ricard, adidas, Imerys, Concentrix + Webhelp and Ontex, and says GBL divested its TotalEnergies and Engie stakes from 2012, down to 4.2% of its portfolio by March 2017. No reporting found of a current fossil fuel holding by GBL.
- No reporting found of annual accounts or a subsidy breakdown on the pages opened.

BOTTOM LINE
WIELS rests on regional public money, a lottery partner and Belgian corporate and gallery partners, none with a documented controversy in the pages read. The one atlas-relevant name, GBL, shows no current fossil fuel holding in the source opened, and its divestment of TotalEnergies and Engie is recorded. Worth watching: published accounts and a subsidy split, which the centre does not post on its public pages.
