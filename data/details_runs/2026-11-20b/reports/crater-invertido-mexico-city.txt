CRÁTER INVERTIDO: Mexico City, Mexico
ID: crater-invertido-mexico-city
Website: https://craterinvertido.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Artist cooperative, active since 2011, that self-manages a multidisciplinary house-workshop in Mexico City with a risograph press and an experimental radio, among other tools for dialogue and self-publishing (stated by the space; Arts Collaboratory).
- Its members are graduates of UNAM's National School of Visual Arts and of the "La Esmeralda" School of Painting, Sculpture and Printmaking (MUAC, UNAM); Time Out Mexico (2016) says the group formed at La Esmeralda under the name Grupo D.
- Kunstinstituut Melly dates the collective to 2012 and places its archive space and publishing workshop in the Obrera neighbourhood; Time Out Mexico (29 February 2016) gave the address as Joaquín García Icazbalceta 32, colonia San Rafael, near Metro San Cosme.
- Programmes on its site: Casa Común (the house), Radio Tropiezo, Imaginarios (workshops and residencies) and Taller XD, its editorial project. Radio Tropiezo, run by an assembly, has broadcast online since 2016, first through radiolibre.co and now through the servers of Primero de Mayo (stated by the space).
- Its risograph books carry its own imprint, Cráneo Invertido; MUAC, UNAM's contemporary art museum, holds a fond of its published output (MUAC).
- Took part in the 56th Venice Biennale (2015), the Jakarta Biennale (2015), Sonsbeek '16, the Gwangju Biennale (2016) and the Conundrum of Imagination in Vienna (2017) (Arts Collaboratory).
- Afterall journal (issue 41) published an essay on the cooperative by the curator Sol Henaro, illustrated with its 2012 installation "Pabellón 1994. Solidaridad, Venceremos" (Afterall).
- Visiting: Time Out Mexico (2016) listed free entry, by appointment, depending on the event.

FUNDING
Cráter Invertido: member-run cooperative in the Arts Collaboratory network, set up by the Dutch funders Hivos and DOEN
- Arts Collaboratory lists Cooperativa Cráter Invertido among its member organisations, beside the DOEN Foundation, which contributes to the network "through its funding, its network and its knowledge" (Arts Collaboratory).
- DOEN Foundation is the fund of the Dutch Postcode Loterij and VriendenLoterij (DOEN).
- Arts Collaboratory was set up in 2007 by the Dutch organisations Hivos and DOEN Foundation, with the Mondriaan Foundation, to support independent visual arts organisations in Africa, Asia and Latin America; it offered project funds of up to 15,000 euros per project (Wikipedia).
- The cooperative describes itself as self-managed (stated by the space); its website lists no accounts or funders.

BOTTOM LINE
Cráter Invertido is a small self-managed cooperative whose only named backer is a Dutch network set up by Hivos and the lottery-funded DOEN Foundation, neither a flagged source. The weak point is transparency, not the source of the money.
