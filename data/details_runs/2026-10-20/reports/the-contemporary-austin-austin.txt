THE CONTEMPORARY AUSTIN: Austin, USA
ID: the-contemporary-austin-austin
Website: https://thecontemporaryaustin.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum with two sites: the Jones Center at 700 Congress Avenue downtown, and Laguna Gloria, a fourteen-acre art-in-nature site at 3809 West 35th Street.
- Traces its roots to the Texas Fine Arts Association, formed in 1911. The association split in 1961 into the Laguna Gloria Art Museum and the body that became Arthouse at the Jones Center (renamed in 2002). The Laguna Gloria museum became the Austin Museum of Art in 1996. The two merged in 2011 as AMOA-Arthouse, and the name The Contemporary Austin was introduced in 2013.
- Laguna Gloria is Clara Driscoll's 1916 lakeside estate, which she donated in 1943 to be used "as a Museum to bring pleasure in the appreciation of art to the people of Texas."
- Jones Center building: the 1998 museum home at 700 Congress Avenue was renovated and expanded by Lewis.Tsurumaki.Lewis Architects (reopened 2010), then renovated again with the Moody Rooftop (reopened December 2016). The site held Tobin's Drug Store from 1877 and the Queen Theater from 1926.
- Laguna Gloria: the Driscoll Villa, the Betty and Edward Marcus Sculpture Park (master plan by Reed Hilderbrand, engaged in 2014), the Art School at Laguna Gloria and the Moody Pavilions with a visitor center, cafe and shop (2019).
- Research into the land's history before Driscoll, including its indigenous and laboring communities, was published in 2022 and informed new signage installed in 2023 (stated by the museum).

FUNDING
The Contemporary Austin: private philanthropy: foundations, trustees and members, with earned income
- 2024 Form 990: revenue $8,611,154, of which contributions $4,505,413 (52.3%), program services $2,193,193 (25.5%), investment income $558,001 and asset sales $886,041; expenses $10,265,447; net assets $37,886,408 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Edward and Betty Marcus Foundation, Dallas: $9 million awarded in 2013 for the Betty and Edward Marcus Sculpture Park at Laguna Gloria, about $5 million of it for commissions and acquisitions and the rest for maintenance, conservation and programmes (CultureMap Dallas, Katie Friel, 13 September 2013).
- Moody Foundation: a $1.3 million grant in 2015 for the Jones Center renovation (the museum's own history page); the foundation also gave the lead gift for Phase I of the Laguna Gloria master plan (Moody Foundation, 5 July 2018).
- The City of Austin Economic Development Department (Heritage Tourism Division) supported the Laguna Gloria history research, and with the Texas Historical Commission supports a walking tour app launching in 2026 (museum's history page).
- Suzanne Deal Booth, a trustee, founded the Suzanne Deal Booth / FLAG Art Foundation Prize in 2016. It is administered by The Contemporary Austin and the FLAG Art Foundation, New York, and carries $200,000 plus a solo exhibition (The Art Newspaper, Benjamin Sutton, 21 May 2024).

WIDER CONTEXT
- April 2011: Paddy Johnson wrote in ART F CITY ("Austin's Arthouse Eliminates Its Only Curator Citing Budget Problems," 14 April 2011) that Arthouse cut the post of associate director and curator Elizabeth Dunbar in a round of budget cuts. She had written to the board questioning how leadership handled exhibits by Michelle Handelman and Graham Hudson. At South by Southwest, Arthouse rented its galleries to Warner Music Group, which altered Hudson's exhibit without his permission, and Handelman's video was turned off at certain times. Board member Dario Robleto resigned, staffer Jenn Gardner left and several artists withdrew work from the "5 x 7" fundraiser.
- The piece concerns Arthouse at the Jones Center, one of the two bodies that merged in 2011 into AMOA-Arthouse, renamed The Contemporary Austin in 2013. It does not name The Contemporary Austin.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind The Contemporary Austin is private and philanthropic: large capital gifts from the Marcus and Moody foundations, trustee support, members and earned income, with city and state heritage grants for research.
