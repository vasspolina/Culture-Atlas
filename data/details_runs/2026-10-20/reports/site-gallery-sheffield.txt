SITE GALLERY: Sheffield, United Kingdom
ID: site-gallery-sheffield
Website: https://www.sitegallery.org (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art space specialising in moving image, new media and performance, at 1 Brown Street, Sheffield S1 2BS, in the Cultural Industries Quarter (Site Gallery, Wikipedia).
- It began as an independent photography gallery in Walkley in 1978, was officially founded in 1979 with a grant from Yorkshire Arts under the name Untitled Gallery, and was renamed Site Gallery in 1996 (Wikipedia).
- It moved to 1 Brown Street in 1988 under director Matthew Conduit (Wikipedia). A 2018 capital expansion was paid for by grants from Arts Council England, Sheffield City Council and a group of trusts (Site Gallery supporters page).
- Sharna Jackson has been artistic director since July 2018 (Wikipedia); Judith Harry is Chief Executive (accounts).
- Site Gallery Ltd is a registered charity (number 510322; company number 01495927) (accounts).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 11:00 to 17:00 and Sunday 11:00 to 16:00, closed Mondays; the Kollective Kitchen café is on site. Current show: Alexis Kyle Mitchell, "The Goal of Our Health", 16 October 2026 to 21 March 2027 (Site Gallery).

FUNDING
Site Gallery: mixed: Arts Council England core funding, local council and trust grants, earned income
- Income for the year to 31 March 2025 was £564,870 against expenditure of £654,207; total funds fell from £1,365,269 to £1,275,932 (Site Gallery Ltd accounts, via OpenCharities).
- Arts Council England National Portfolio funding was £319,887 and Sheffield City Council funding £21,700; the council also gave a £25,658 Low Carbon Capital grant (accounts).
- Restricted grants: Esmée Fairbairn Foundation £37,500 for Creative Producers plus £1,100, and JG Graves Charitable Trust £5,000 (accounts).
- Other named funders in the accounts: Jerwood Arts and Southwark Park Galleries, University of Sheffield, the Department for Work and Pensions (Access to Work scheme) and Freelands Foundation (2024 only). The accounts name the Fidelity UK Foundation as supporting strategic development work over two years.
- Earned income: rent £43,507, café franchise £18,008, shop sales, space hire and exhibition sales £40,934, Museums and Galleries Exhibition Tax Relief £31,244, donations and legacies £1,449 (accounts).
- The supporters page lists these funders: Arts Council England, British Council, Church Burgesses Educational Foundation, Clore Leadership Programme, The Elephant Trust, Esmée Fairbairn Foundation, The Foyle Foundation, Freelands Foundation, The Freshgate Trust Foundation, Garfield Weston Foundation, Henry Moore Foundation, Jerwood Arts, JG Graves Charitable Trust, Mondriaan Fund, Office for Contemporary Art Norway, Paul Hamlyn Foundation, PRS Foundation, Sheffield City Region Infrastructure Fund, Sheffield City Council and Sheffield Town Trust. It also thanks its individual donors.
- The 2018 capital expansion was funded by Arts Council England, Sheffield City Council, Sheffield City Region Infrastructure Fund, Garfield Weston Foundation, Foyle Foundation, Wolfson Foundation, Sheffield Town Trust, JG Graves Charitable Trust, Freshgate Foundation and Church Burgesses Educational Foundation (supporters page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Site Gallery is public and charitable: Arts Council England provides £319,887 of its £564,870 income for the year to March 2025, with Sheffield City Council, named grant-making trusts and earned income making up the rest. The accounts for the year to 31 March 2025 name the funders and the grants.
