MGLC INTERNATIONAL CENTRE OF GRAPHIC ARTS: Ljubljana, Slovenia
ID: mglc-international-centre-of-graphic-arts-ljubljana
Website: https://mglc.si

OVERVIEW
- Museum of fine art prints, contemporary art gallery and print publisher with its own printmaking workshops, in Tivoli Mansion in Tivoli Park, Ljubljana; its address is Pod turnom 3 (Visit Ljubljana, MGLC).
- Established as a public institute by the City of Ljubljana through a decree adopted by the city council on 15 April 1999 and published in Uradni list RS 42/1999 (3 June 1999). It produces the Ljubljana Biennale of Graphic Arts, which began in 1955 (Uradni list, Visit Ljubljana).
- The decree (Article 3) lists its activities: the international graphic biennial, domestic and foreign exhibitions, a permanent exhibition of modern Slovenian and foreign graphics, an experimental graphics workshop, publishing and symposia.
- The building is a 17th-century mansion on the ruins of an earlier Renaissance castle; the Jesuits owned it first, then the Diocese of Ljubljana used it as the bishop's summer residence, and in 1852 Emperor Francis Joseph I gave it to Marshal Joseph Radetzky. The Municipality of Ljubljana bought it in 1863, and Wikipedia says it became the venue of the International Centre of Graphic Arts after a renovation in 1967 (Wikipedia).
- The collection holds over 3,600 fine art prints and 800 artists' books, described by Visit Ljubljana as the largest Slovenian collection of post-World War II graphic art and authorial publications.
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00; the adult ticket is 8 EUR (MGLC). Visit Ljubljana lists designated parking, an accessible entrance, accessible restrooms and a lift.

FUNDING
MGLC International Centre of Graphic Arts: public institute of the City of Ljubljana, founded and budgeted by the city
- The founding decree (Article 10) says the institute obtains its funds from the founder's budget, payments for services and other sources permitted by law; surpluses from public-service activities can be used only to run and develop those activities (Article 11, Uradni list RS 42/1999).
- The founder is liable for the institute's obligations on a limited, subsidiary basis, up to the funds in the founder's budget for the institute's activities (decree, Article 12, title translated).
- The founder appoints the director through a public tender for five years (Article 5). The institute's council has six members: two appointed by the Ljubljana City Council, two elected by employees and two appointed by the Academy of Fine Arts Ljubljana (Article 6).

BOTTOM LINE
MGLC is a public institute of the City of Ljubljana, founded by a 1999 decree that names the city budget first among its funding sources, followed by payments for services and other sources permitted by law.
