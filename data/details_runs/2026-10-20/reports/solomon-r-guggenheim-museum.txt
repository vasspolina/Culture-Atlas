SOLOMON R. GUGGENHEIM MUSEUM: New York, USA
ID: solomon-r-guggenheim-museum
Website: https://www.guggenheim.org (checked 2026-10-24; live, but only a cookie notice showed)

OVERVIEW
- Modern and contemporary art museum on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, run by the Solomon R. Guggenheim Foundation (Wikipedia).
- Solomon R. Guggenheim set up the Foundation in 1937; its first venue, the Museum of Non-Objective Painting, opened in 1939 and took his name in 1952 (Wikipedia).
- Frank Lloyd Wright was hired to design the building in June 1943; it opened in October 1959 (Wikipedia).
- Mariët Westermann became director in June 2024; Melissa Chiu follows from September 2026. J. Tomilson Hill has chaired the board since 2021 (Wikipedia; ProPublica).
- Visiting: No reporting found of hours or ticket prices in the pages opened.

FUNDING
Solomon R. Guggenheim Foundation: private non-profit: gifts and earned income
- Fiscal 2024 (Form 990 data, ProPublica): revenue $88.2 million, of which contributions $40.1 million and program service revenue $34.5 million; expenses $84.9 million.
- Sackler family: the Mortimer D. Sackler family gave $7 million between 1995 and 2006 for the arts education center and $2 million between 1999 and 2015 for general support (The Art Newspaper).
- Abu Dhabi: the Foundation partners with the Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi (see Red flags). No reporting found of what the Abu Dhabi side pays the Foundation.
- No reporting found of a corporate partner list, or of Lavazza or BMW partnerships, in the pages opened.

Red flags: Opioids (Sackler money)
- On 25 March 2019 the museum said "No contributions from the Sackler family have been received since 2015" and that it "does not plan to accept any gifts"; a spokeswoman said the policy covers the Mortimer D. Sackler branch, named in lawsuits over OxyContin marketing (The Art Newspaper, Victoria Stapley-Brown). Nan Goldin's group P.A.I.N. had protested inside the museum that February.
- In March 2019 it did not plan to rename the Sackler Center for Arts Education; in 2022 the center was renamed the Gail May Engelberg Center for Arts Education for a trustee who with her husband gave $15 million (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Gulf state partner (Guggenheim Abu Dhabi)
- The Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi announced on 28 July 2026 that Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, designed by Frank Gehry, opens on 11 December 2026, a partnership with the Foundation (DCT release via Kyodo PR Wire; The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 28 July 2026).
- Abu Dhabi signed an agreement with the Foundation on 8 July 2006 for a museum on Saadiyat Island; a 2008 Guggenheim statement said Abu Dhabi's Tourism Development & Investment Company will own it (Wikipedia, Guggenheim Abu Dhabi).
- Protest: on 27 April 2019 the Gulf Labor Coalition, which says it represents several thousand signatories, asked artists to withhold participation, saying the museum is "built on the back of exploited, indebted, and abused workers" (Hyperallergic, 1 May 2019). The Foundation ended negotiations with the Coalition in April 2016 (same article).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Sustained criticism over Gulf Labor and the Abu Dhabi branch, and over staff layoffs. The Sackler episode was reported as news.
1. Hyperallergic: Carey Dunne, "Protesters Shame Guggenheim and Its Trustees with Light Projections" (2016)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/protesters-shame-guggenheim-and-its-trustees-with-light-projections/
- Artist groups G.U.L.F. and the Illuminator projected "YOU BROKE TRUST" and trustees' faces on the museum facade after the Foundation ended talks with Gulf Labor.
- Names board chairman William L. Mack.
2. Hyperallergic: Hakim Bishara, "Gulf Labor Coalition Calls on Artists to Boycott Guggenheim Abu Dhabi" (2019)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/gulf-labor-coalition-calls-on-artists-to-boycott-guggenheim-abu-dhabi
- The Coalition asks for fair pay, no recruitment-fee debt, decent housing and an independent monitor; it says only the monitor request was partly met.
3. Hyperallergic: Maya Pontone, "Workers Protest Abrupt Layoffs at Guggenheim Museum" (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1004134/workers-protest-abrupt-layoffs-at-guggenheim-museum/
- More than 50 workers rallied on 16 April 2025 over 20 layoffs, the third round in five years; 14 belonged to UAW Local 2110.
- Concerns the museum's labour practice, not its funders.

WIDER CONTEXT
- US diplomatic cables published by WikiLeaks mention the Guggenheim in passing: a July 2009 cable from Abu Dhabi notes the UAE government rejected Human Rights Watch's allegations of labour abuses at Saadiyat Island; a January 2010 cable from Baku links the Pashayev family to an effort to open a Guggenheim branch on Baku's waterfront. The cables mention no payments.

BOTTOM LINE
The New York museum's money is mostly gifts and earned income, with filings public. The weak points are the Sackler gifts it stopped receiving after 2015 and later renamed, and its partnership with Abu Dhabi on a museum that opens in December 2026 after migrant-labour protests. Watch the Abu Dhabi deal's terms and the Gulf Labor response at the opening.
