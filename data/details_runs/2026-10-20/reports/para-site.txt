PARA SITE: Hong Kong
ID: para-site
Website: https://www.para-site.art

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit contemporary art space in Hong Kong; its office is at Cheung Hing Industrial Building, Kennedy Town (Para Site website).
- Founded in 1996 by seven Hong Kong-based artists (Para Site website); Wikipedia names them as Patrick Lee, Leung Chi-wo, Phoebe Man Ching-ying, Sara Wong Chi-hang, Leung Mee-ping, Tsang Tak-ping and Lisa Cheung and calls it Hong Kong's first artist-run art space.
- Occupied a shopfront in Sheung Wan from 1997 to 2015, then moved to expanded quarters in Quarry Bay in 2015 (Para Site website).
- Wikipedia says it published the quarterly bilingual magazine P/S from 1997 to 2006 and has hosted an annual international conference since 2013. It has mounted over 300 exhibitions and hosted over 80 international residents (Para Site website).
- Board chair since February 2025 is Alan Lo, a board member since 2015, whom Tatler Asia (21 February 2025) calls "an established entrepreneur and art patron". Junni Chen is interim director (City Nomads, Sharmaine Khoo, 18 December 2025).
- Visiting: all programmes are free and open to the public (Para Site website).

FUNDING
Para Site: patron- and foundation-funded: patrons, foundations and an annual benefit auction
- 2025 Gala and Benefit Auction (20 November 2025, St. Regis Hong Kong): tiers from HK$15,000 for an individual ticket to HK$160,000 for the Visionaries level (Para Site website). The Para Site site lists "Major Supporters" including the Yenn and Alan Lo Foundation and the SUNPRIDE FOUNDATION, and the Cheng-Lan Foundation under "Supporters", alongside individual patrons.
- The 2025 gala donors page lists artists and galleries including Gagosian, White Cube, Almine Rech, Axel Vervoordt Gallery, Galerie Templon and Tina Kim Gallery. A separate benefactors page lists David Zwirner Gallery, Perrotin, Hauser and Wirth and Gagosian under its Global Council and Founding Friends, Friends and Associates headings.
- Patron tiers: Friends at HK$18,000 (single) or HK$30,000 (dual), and Founding Friends at HK$70,000 (Para Site website). Para Site is a registered section 88 charity, so donations of HK$100 or more are tax deductible.
- Public money, per Wikipedia only: support from the Hong Kong Arts Development Council and a HK$4.5 million Springboard Grant from the Home Affairs Bureau's Art Capacity Development Funding Scheme in 2014, plus grants from the S.H.Ho Foundation and the Foundation for Arts Initiatives.

BOTTOM LINE
Para Site's documented money is patron tiers, foundation support from the Yenn and Alan Lo Foundation and the SUNPRIDE FOUNDATION (2025 gala Major Supporters) and the Cheng-Lan Foundation (Supporter), and artist and gallery donations to its annual auction; Wikipedia lists Hong Kong arts council and government grants.
