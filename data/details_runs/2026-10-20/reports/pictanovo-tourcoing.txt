PICTANOVO: Tourcoing, France
ID: pictanovo-tourcoing
Website: https://www.pictanovo.com/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Regional film, series, animation and video game agency, run as an association, based at 21 rue Edgar Quinet in Tourcoing; it presents itself as "Images en Hauts-de-France".
- Created in 1985 as the Centre régional de ressources audiovisuelles (CRRAV) on the initiative of the Nord-Pas-de-Calais region; merged with the Pôle Images Nord-Pas-de-Calais in 2013 and took the name Pictanovo; its remit covered the whole Hauts-de-France region from 1 January 2016 (French Wikipedia).
- Aid goes through eight funds: feature films and series, short films, video games, animation, documentaries, new media, young authors, and editorial programmes covering two to five works; reading committees select the projects (French Wikipedia).
- Services on its site: equipment rental, training, a talent directory, project submission and a location office for film shoots.
- Its president is Grégory Tempremant and its managing director is Godefroy Vujicic (Pictanovo site).- Visiting: No reporting found of public opening hours or admission; the site is aimed at film and media professionals.

FUNDING
Pictanovo: mainly public: Hauts-de-France region, with the state and the CNC (national film centre)
- The Hauts-de-France region is the main funder and presides over the association; the Métropole européenne de Lille, the Porte du Hainaut and Valenciennes communities and Amiens Métropole are members by right (French Wikipedia).
- The grants sit within a partnership between the state, the region and the CNC (French Wikipedia). Pictanovo's site shows funder logos for the region, the CNC and the Métropole européenne de Lille, with no amounts.
- Pictanovo states that it invests 8.6 million euros a year in film and audiovisual creation and backs about 230 works a year (stated by Pictanovo).
- No reporting found of an annual report or accounts on its site, or of private donors.

Red flags: Epstein-linked, Caroline Lang
- Caroline Lang presided over Pictanovo's economic support fund for films and series and resigned on 4 February 2026 (L'Essentiel de l'éco; Mediacités). She also left the board of Séries Mania and her new post as general delegate of the independent producers' union SPI.
- Mediapart revealed that in 2016 she co-founded Prytanee, an offshore art-investment company, with Jeffrey Epstein, holding half the shares; she did not declare it to French tax authorities (L'Essentiel de l'éco, citing Mediapart and US Justice Department documents).
- On 6 February 2026 the French national financial prosecutor (PNF) opened a preliminary investigation into Caroline Lang and her father Jack Lang for "blanchiment de fraude fiscale aggravée", laundering of aggravated tax fraud (Public Sénat).
- Epstein's 2018 will names her and leaves her five million dollars; she says she never received money from the company and learned of the bequest from the press (Public Sénat; L'Essentiel de l'éco).
- She and her father deny wrongdoing; no charge has been brought and no source links her to Epstein's crimes (Public Sénat; L'Essentiel de l'éco).
- No reporting found of Epstein money reaching Pictanovo itself.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
Pictanovo's own money is public, from the Hauts-de-France region and its partners, and no reporting links it to a flagged funder. The weak point is its former fund president, Caroline Lang, who is under a preliminary prosecutor's investigation over a company she co-founded with Jeffrey Epstein; she resigned on 4 February 2026. Worth watching: the outcome of the PNF inquiry and who now presides over the fund, and whether Pictanovo publishes accounts or a board list.
