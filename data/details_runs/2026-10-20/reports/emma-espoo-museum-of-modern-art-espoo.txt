EMMA ESPOO MUSEUM OF MODERN ART: Espoo, Finland
ID: emma-espoo-museum-of-modern-art-espoo
Website: https://emmamuseum.fi

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at the WeeGee Exhibition Centre, Ahertajantie 5, in Tapiola, Espoo, with about 6,000 m² of gallery space; the museum calls itself Finland's largest art museum (EMMA).
- The City of Espoo founded the EMMA Art Museum Foundation in 2002, and the museum opened to the public on 13 October 2006 (Wikipedia). Saija Äikäs chairs the foundation's board (EMMA).
- Building: the former Weilin & Göös printing house, designed by Professor Aarno Ruusuvuori (EMMA). The WeeGee complex also holds other museums, a media-art centre, a café, a museum shop and an art school (Wikipedia).
- Collections: the EMMA Collection and the collections of the Saastamoinen Foundation and the Tapio Wirkkala Rut Bryk Foundation. The Saastamoinen collection of over 2,800 works was deposited when the museum was founded. EMMA received the Museum of the Year prize in spring 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Monday closed; Tuesday 11 to 17; Wednesday and Thursday 11 to 19; Friday 15 to 17; Saturday and Sunday 11 to 17. Tickets: adults €20, discounted €10, under 18 free; free admission every Friday from 15:00. The second floor has a lift and the route is threshold-free; wheelchairs and walkers can be borrowed at the entrance.

FUNDING
EMMA Espoo Museum of Modern Art: City of Espoo as chief financier, with corporate partners JCDecaux and Länsiväylä and foundation collections
- "EMMA's chief financier is the City of Espoo", and sponsorship is an important source of funding; its main corporate partners are JCDecaux and Länsiväylä (EMMA).
- The Saastamoinen Foundation is a founding member and partner of EMMA. The foundation's page traces its roots to Herman Saastamoinen's shop in Kuopio, bought in 1876, which grew into a multi-sector company that ceased operations in 1996; Liisa Karttunen and Osmo P. Karttunen founded the foundation in 1968.
- The Tapio Wirkkala Rut Bryk Foundation, founded in 2003, deposited over 3,000 objects; in 2015 an agreement placed its collection permanently at WeeGee (Wikipedia).
- EMMA curates public art for the City of Espoo, for example the 2022 selection for the city's Family Centre, which used 44 works from the Saastamoinen Foundation collection (EMMA).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind EMMA is mainly municipal: the City of Espoo founded the museum foundation in 2002 and is its chief financier, with JCDecaux and Länsiväylä as main corporate partners and the Saastamoinen and Tapio Wirkkala Rut Bryk foundations supplying collections. The Saastamoinen Foundation traces its roots to a family company that ceased operations in 1996.
