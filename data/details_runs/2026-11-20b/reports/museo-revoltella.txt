MUSEO REVOLTELLA: Trieste, Italy
ID: museo-revoltella
Website: https://museorevoltella.it/english/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Civic gallery of modern art at Via Diaz 27, Trieste, filling a block between Piazza Venezia, Via Diaz, Via Cadorna and Via San Giorgio in three buildings: the Revoltella Palace, the Brunner Palace and the smaller Basevi building, which holds the offices (museum website).
- Baron Pasquale Revoltella (1795 to 1869), an entrepreneur and financier and one of the first shareholders of Assicurazioni Generali, left his palace and collection to the city; it opened as the Museo Revoltella in 1872 (museum website).
- The Berlin architect Friedrich Hitzig designed the palace in 1853; the city bought the Brunner Palace in 1907, and its conversion to a design by Carlo Scarpa began in 1968 and was finished in 1991 by Franco Vattolo and Giampiero Bartoli (museum website).
- Collection: 19th century painters such as Francesco Hayez and Domenico Morelli and 20th century Italians including Felice Casorati, Mario Sironi, Giorgio Morandi, Giorgio de Chirico, Lucio Fontana and Alberto Burri (museum website).
- The founder's library survives in a corner room of the palace, with travel memoirs of his 1861 journey to Egypt; the museum library is named for Sergio Molesi (museum website).
- An entrance, ticket area, bookshop and toilets rebuilt with EU NextGenerationEU money under the PNRR reopened in May 2025; the auditorium and the Sala Scarpa are next (InTrieste, 20 May 2025).
- Current show: Achille Funi's 1918 "Autoritratto in riva al mare (Chiavari)", newly acquired, with related works, to 11 January 2027 (museum website).
- Visiting: admission is charged; entry is free on the first Sunday of the month under the Culture Ministry's #domenicalmuseo scheme (InTrieste, 3 January 2026), and a 12-month card for the city's paying civic museums costs 15 euros from 1 December 2026 to 6 January 2027 (museum website).

FUNDING
Museo Revoltella: public: the Comune di Trieste, on the founder's bequest, with regional acquisition grants
- Bequest: Revoltella's codicil of 13 October 1866 left the palace to the Comune "franco di passività, e tasse" (free of debts and taxes, own translation) as a perpetual foundation, plus a capital of 100,000 Austrian florins whose interest was to pay each year for new acquisitions; the city was to bear only taxes, custody and upkeep (museum website, Curatorio page).
- Operator: the Comune di Trieste runs the museum; the site carries the city's copyright and VAT number (museum website).
- Governance: the Curatorio, a board named by the city council under the will, last listed in 2017 with Manuela Declich as president (museum website, page updated 21 July 2020).
- Region: the Funi self-portrait was bought with a grant from the Regione Friuli Venezia Giulia under its line for civic museum acquisitions in regional law 13 of 30 December 2024, the 2025 stability law (museum website, 18 November 2026).
- European Union: the 2025 entrance and visitor-services works were paid for by NextGenerationEU through the PNRR (InTrieste, 20 May 2025).
- Volunteers: since 1994 two associations, the Associazione triestina Amici dei Musei "Marcello Mascherini" and Cittaviva, provide about eighty volunteers for the library, room supervision and the switchboard (museum website).

Red flags: 2022 abolition of the civic museums' director post
- From 1 July 2022 the city gave the direction of all its museums to an official who also ran tourism promotion and cultural and sports events, and merged the libraries into schools and education (open letter to the mayor, 30 July 2022).
- The letter asked the mayor to reverse the abolition of the director of Trieste's museums and libraries; its first signatories were Giuliana Carbi, Veit Heinichen, Ariella Reggio and Nicoletta Zanni, followed by 213 signatures (Trieste Contemporanea).
- The council's executive answered with a refusal, summed up in its aim of "meno generali e più colonnelli" (fewer generals and more colonels, own translation) (il manifesto, 25 February 2023).

Red flags: 2023 to 2026 disputes over pay and conditions of museum attendants
- Attendants hired through the city's contractor Euro & Promos FM spa, founded by Sergio Bini, in 2023 the region's assessor for productive activities and tourism, were paid 5 euros gross an hour, 600 to 700 euros net a month, which led to strikes, labour court cases and a file at the Corte dei conti; the company said it kept to the tender terms (il manifesto, 25 February 2023).
- The union Fesica-Confsal filed a complaint on 19 December 2025 against the cooperative Cristoforo of Pontassieve, which runs security and visitor services at the civic museums with about a hundred staff, many on call or part time, accusing it of fines, suspensions and dismissals of union members (il Dolomiti, December 2025).
- On 24 March 2026 the union protested outside the town hall, saying the cooperative, which has held the contract since February 2025, paid public holidays without the 35 percent supplement and called the city "complice"; culture assessor Giorgio Rossi said he was not satisfied with the cooperative's lack of transparency (il Dolomiti, March 2026).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Two pieces criticise how the city runs the Revoltella and its other museums; neither criticises the collection.
1. Finestre sull'Arte: Federico Giannini, "Sul caso dei musei di Trieste. Il vero problema? Le abitudini delle amministrazioni" (2022)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/opinioni/caso-musei-trieste-problema-abitudini-amministrazioni-locali
- Argues the 2022 reorganisation was a reshuffle, not an abolition: museums were split from libraries and put under the same manager as tourism and sport, which he calls "molto discutibile" (highly questionable, own translation).
- Since Laura Carlini was named head of a single Museums and Libraries service in 2017, the civic museums had been without directors for at least five years.
- The real problem is that local authorities ignore the ICOM recommendations and Ministerial Decree 113/2018, which make a qualified director a minimum standard. Does not name the Revoltella.
2. il manifesto: Marinella Salvi, "Il triste destino del Revoltella" (2023)
URL: https://ilmanifesto.it/il-triste-destino-del-revoltella
- The Revoltella is "oggi senza un direttore" (today without a director, own translation); the city says curators are enough.
- The Curatorio has no role and no budget, was never fully appointed and met once, so that the assessor could praise rising visitor numbers.
- The city fills its programme through a contract with Arthemisia arte e cultura srl for ready-made shows, and assessor Giorgio Rossi sees museums as serving tourism.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean money: city funds on a 19th century bequest, with regional and EU grants. The weak points are governance and labour: no museum director since the 2022 reorganisation, a dormant board, and unresolved disputes over how the city's contractors treat museum staff.
