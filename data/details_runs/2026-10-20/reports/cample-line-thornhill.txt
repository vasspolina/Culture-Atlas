CAMPLE LINE: Thornhill, United Kingdom
ID: cample-line-thornhill
Website: https://campleline.org.uk/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary arts organisation presenting contemporary art and film of international scope, at Cample Mill in Cample, Dumfriesshire, two miles south of Thornhill and about 14 miles north of Dumfries, overlooking the Cample rail viaduct.
- Established in late 2016 and registered as a Scottish charitable incorporated organisation (SC047031) on 7 December 2016 (OSCR). Tina Fiske is Director; David Clark chairs the board.
- Building: the mill was once part of a row of millworkers' cottages.
- Programme: exhibitions, screenings, events, residencies and Print Matter, plus Film at Cample and 15 Reads. The site shows the Green Arts Initiative and Living Wage employer logos.
- Visiting: open Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 4pm, or by appointment. Paid events and screenings use a sliding scale, with single tickets usually priced from £0 to £5.
- Access: the building including the first floor is wheelchair accessible, with an external lift to the first floor; the toilets are not wheelchair accessible. Large-print exhibition literature, audio descriptions for some artworks, subtitled screenings, sensory backpacks and a free companion ticket are offered.

FUNDING
Cample Line: registered Scottish charity, income £126,761 in 2025 (OSCR)
- Income by year to 31 December (OSCR): £68,419 in 2021, £108,291 in 2022, £99,548 in 2023, £51,890 in 2024, £126,761 in 2025. Expenditure was £110,613 in 2025.
- The site takes donations through Donorbox, runs a Friends of CAMPLE LINE scheme, and sells a limited-edition cloth bag (£7 plus postage) and limited-edition artworks.
- OSCR lists seven trustees: Alison Gordon, David Clark, Zoe Roberts, Chloe Ackland, Amy Winstanley, Amy Marletta and Saskia Coulson. The charity's site lists eight board members, adding Sorcha Carey.

BOTTOM LINE
Cample Line is a small Scottish charity whose annual income was £51,890 in 2024 and £126,761 in 2025 (OSCR). Its site takes donations through Donorbox and runs a Friends scheme, and it sells limited-edition cloth bags and artworks.
