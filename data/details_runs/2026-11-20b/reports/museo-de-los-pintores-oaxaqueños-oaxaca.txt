MUSEO DE LOS PINTORES OAXAQUEÑOS: Oaxaca, Mexico
ID: museo-de-los-pintores-oaxaqueños-oaxaca
Website: https://www.oaxaca.gob.mx/comunicacion/cumple-mupo-19-anos-como-referente-del-arte-contemporaneo-en-oaxaca/ (checked 2026-11-20; down, server error)

OVERVIEW
- State art museum (MUPO) at Avenida Independencia 607, at the corner of García Vigil, in the historic centre of Oaxaca de Juárez (Excélsior; Spanish Wikipedia).
- Opening dates differ: the Spanish Wikipedia infobox says 2003 and its text 2004; Excélsior (2025) says the museum has run since 2006.
- The building opened on 26 January 1686 as the Colegio de las Doncellas de Nuestra Señora de la Presentación, a girls' school paid for by Juan Gómez de Tapia, a priest of Tututepec, and the Portuguese philanthropist Miguel Fernández Fiallo (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Later uses: the first place the jewels from Tomb 7 at Monte Albán were shown, the first seat of the Museo Nacional de Antropología e Historia in 1933, the State Regional Museum until 1986, the City Council until 1992 and the state tourism secretariat from 1993 to 2003 (Oaxaca Lending Library kiosk; Spanish Wikipedia).
- Four temporary exhibition rooms named for Rufino Tamayo, Rodolfo Morales, Rodolfo Nieto and Francisco Gutiérrez, with dance classes, guided tours, talks, book launches and educational services (Spanish Wikipedia). The museum also holds a collection: its 2026 Pedro Friedeberg show combined works from MUPO's own holdings with loans from local collectors (Excélsior, 17 April 2026).
- It states its mission as promoting art in Oaxaca and the world and conserving, analysing and sharing works produced from Oaxaca (Oaxaca Lending Library kiosk).
- In October 2025 the writer and editor Jorge Pech Casanova became its coordinator, appointed by the state culture secretary Flavio Sosa; he planned an artistic council to choose exhibitions (Excélsior, 4 October 2025).
- Name: in April 2025 Irma Pineda Santiago, PT deputy and chair of the state Congress culture committee, proposed renaming it to recognise women artists, echoing the collectives Arte Mujer Oaxaca and the Red de Pintoras y Grabadoras de Oaxaca (Excélsior, 17 April 2025). In October 2025 Pech said it would become the Museo de la Plástica Oaxaqueña (Excélsior).
- Recent programme: "De vacaciones por la vida", a tribute to Pedro Friedeberg, 18 April to 2 August 2026 (Excélsior).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00 (Spanish Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Museo de los Pintores Oaxaqueños: public: Oaxaca state government
- Administered by the Patronato de las Unidades de Servicios Culturales y Turísticos del Estado de Oaxaca (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Building: the state government started the rehabilitation that adapted it for art; later additions were demolished, walls and finishes replaced and all electrical, water and sanitary systems renewed (Spanish Wikipedia).
- 2025 works: the state Secretaría de las Culturas y Artes (Seculta) and the Instituto del Patrimonio Cultural del Estado de Oaxaca (Inpac) began repairs at MUPO and other state venues, covering façades, doors and windows, signage, disability access, electrics and civil protection (Excélsior, Patricia Briseño, 30 May 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution paid for by the Oaxaca government, with no private sponsor on record. Nothing in its funding raises a flag; the open question is whether the state completes the promised rename and the building repairs.
