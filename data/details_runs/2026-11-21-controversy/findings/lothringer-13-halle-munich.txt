Red flags: 2026 campaign by Nius and the AfD against the exhibition "Antifascism: Now"
- In February 2026 the online outlet Nius published an article claiming that the publicly funded exhibition "Antifascism: Now" at Lothringer 13 calls for violence, with the demand in its subtext that the show's funding be cut; the AfD took up the article and spread it through its channels (taz, Chris Schinke, 17 February 2026).
- The Nius author targeted flags by the Office for Postartistic Services and equated the Iron Front with the SA, a comparison taz calls wrong (taz, 17 February 2026).
- The artistic director Kalas Liebfried told taz: "Leute standen bei uns, stellten seltsame Fragen, bedrängten meine Mitarbeiter, scannten die Räume ab" ("People stood in our space, asked strange questions, pressed my staff, scanned the rooms", translated); two people wearing neo-Nazi symbols came over a weekend and the police were called (taz, 17 February 2026).
