ID: khalil-sakakini-cultural-center-ramallah

WIDER CONTEXT
- In a letter published by bianet, the centre's director Adila Laidi wrote that, by eyewitness accounts, the Israeli army was at the centre at 10:50 on Saturday 13 April 2002: two tanks and four armoured vehicles came in through the front gate, soldiers broke the iron door of the second floor with explosives and every window on both floors was shattered; they left at 11:30 after 40 minutes (bianet, 16 April 2002).
- Am Johal, writing in Countercurrents on 23 September 2004, quotes the Miami Herald as reporting that the soldiers "seized a computer and a cellphone, broke dozens of windows, swept books off shelves"; Laidi said at the time, "It was just vandalism, part of a conscious desire to ruin everything Palestinian."
- The raid was an act of the Israeli army; Israel neither funds nor runs the centre.
