Red flags: 2025 protest at the Luma tower
- On 11 September 2025 about fifteen Arles members of the "Bloquons tout" movement gathered in the Parc des Ateliers at the foot of the Luma tower and planted a banner reading "Maja, malgré tes sous la ville est à nous" ("Maja, despite your money the city is ours", translated); the action took the form of a picnic (Objectif Sud, Louise Gal, updated 12 September 2025).
- A participant told Objectif Sud: "C'est un espace qu'on a eu l'impression de se faire voler par Maja Hoffmann et ses projets" ("It is a space we felt had been stolen from us by Maja Hoffmann and her projects", translated), calling her a symbol of the city's economic disparities and of "l'accaparement d'Arles par le capital" ("the takeover of Arles by capital", translated).
