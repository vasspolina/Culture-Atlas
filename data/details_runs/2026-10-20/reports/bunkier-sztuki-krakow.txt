BUNKIER SZTUKI GALLERY OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Krakow, Poland
ID: bunkier-sztuki-krakow
Website: https://bunkier.art.pl

OVERVIEW
- Municipal contemporary art gallery at pl. Szczepański 3a, Krakow (Wikipedia).
- Polish Wikipedia dates the institution's history to 1950, when the Bureau of Art Exhibitions (BWA) was created; the gallery received municipal status in 1994 and adopted the name Bunkier Sztuki in 1995.
- The building was designed by Krystyna Tołłoczko-Różyska (Polish Wikipedia); English Wikipedia dates its construction to 1965 and credits Stefan Borzęcki and Antoni Hajdecki with the concrete facade.
- Directors: Krzysztof Głuchowski (1995 to 1999), Jarosław Suchan (1999 to 2002), Maria Anna Potocka (2002 to 2010 and 2019 to 2024), Piotr Cypryański (2010 to 2014), Magdalena Ziółkowska (2014 to 2018) and Delfina Jałowik (since 2024) (Polish Wikipedia).
- The building was under renovation from 2021 to 2024 (Polish Wikipedia). English Wikipedia counts more than 980 exhibitions and a collection of 445 works by 170 artists.

FUNDING
Bunkier Sztuki: mainly public: the City of Krakow
- The gallery is financed by the City of Krakow (Polish Wikipedia: "finansowana przez Miasto Kraków").

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Three pieces from 2019 to 2024 cover the 2019 merger plan, how the city appoints directors, and a 2024 labour court ruling.
1. The Art Newspaper: Richard Unwin, "Krakow City Council rejects mayor's plan to merge the city's two leading contemporary art spaces" (29 April 2019)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2019/04/29/krakow-city-council-rejects-mayors-plan-to-merge-the-citys-two-leading-contemporary-art-spaces
- A clear council majority voted on 24 April against Mayor Jacek Majchrowski's plan to combine Bunkier Sztuki with MOCAK under the MOCAK name; MOCAK's director, Potocka, was appointed to lead Bunkier in February and one person runs both.
- Artists Monika Drożyńska and Iwona Demko wrote to Culture Minister Piotr Gliński on 3 February, asked for an open competition for the director, and warned of a "detrimental impact on the local, regional and national contemporary art scene." Drożyńska said it was "impossible for one person to ensure the diversity of" the two programmes.
- The mayor attributed his plans to a decline in Bunkier's "position and prestige" and in visitor numbers.
2. Magazyn Szum: editorial board, "Delfina Jałowik dyrektorką Bunkra Sztuki, czyli w Krakowie bez zmian. Komentarz redakcji" (1 December 2023)
URL: https://magazynszum.pl/delfina-jalowik-dyrektorka-bunkra-sztuki-czyli-w-krakowie-bez-zmian-komentarz-redakcja
- The city announced on 30 November 2023 that Jałowik, a long-time MOCAK curator described as Potocka's "prawa ręka" (my translation: right hand), won the director competition. The editors write "wszystko zostaje w rodzinie" (my translation: everything stays in the family) and argue it confirms Bunkier's dependence on MOCAK.
- Says a competition announced after Magdalena Ziółkowska's dismissal in 2018 never took place, that Majchrowski appointed Potocka in February 2019 without one, and that the candidates' programmes were not published.
3. OKO.press: Katarzyna Kojzar, "Masza Potocka traci stanowisko dyrektorki MOCAK-u. Część pracowników oskarżyła ją o mobbing" (2 October 2024)
URL: https://oko.press/masza-potocka-traci-prace
- A national news outlet, not art press. Reports that curator Lidia Krawczyk challenged her dismissal from Bunkier Sztuki in the labour court and that in July 2024 the court found, in the article's quoted words, that the curator was dismissed unlawfully and that Potocka "dopuściła się mobbingu" (my translation: committed mobbing). The article calls the ruling final.
- Potocka disputes the verdict. President Aleksander Miszalski announced on 2 October 2024 that she would no longer direct MOCAK.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the gallery is the City of Krakow. The documented points are the 2019 merger plan rejected by the city council, Magazyn Szum's account of Potocka's 2019 appointment without a competition, and the July 2024 labour court finding, reported by OKO.press, that Potocka committed mobbing in a case concerning Bunkier Sztuki.
