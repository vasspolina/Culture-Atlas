CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Afterall: Eddie Chambers, "Iniva: Everything Crash" (2015)
URL: https://afterall.org/articles/iniva-everything-crash
- Chambers argues that although Iniva grew out of Black artists' activity and activism, it "only ever declared its embrace of Black artists in the most furtive of ways."
- He writes that the Arts Council was thinking in terms that amounted to "separate development for Black artists", and that Iniva became fixated on becoming a contemporary art gallery modelled on existing public institutions.
- He describes the Iniva of 2015 as practising "a dull top-down approach to its projects, with little to nothing in the way of lateral working relationships and collaborations."
- He concludes: "It perhaps goes without saying that it has failed those for whom it was, in part at least, established."
