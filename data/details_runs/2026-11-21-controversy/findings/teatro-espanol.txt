ID: teatro-espanol

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2016 the government of Mayor Manuela Carmena removed Juan Carlos Pérez de la Fuente as director of the Teatro Español, a post he had won in a 2014 public competition run through the city company Madrid Destino against 27 other candidates (Vozpópuli, Karina Sainz Borgo, 28 May 2016).
- The city council plenary asked for his continuation and reinstatement, but the city government called a new competition for the post (Vozpópuli, 28 May 2016). Pérez de la Fuente told the paper that Ciudadanos and PP voted for his return and PSOE abstained.
- Pérez de la Fuente said that on 18 January 2016 Ana Varela, consejera delegada (managing director, translated), came to his office, with Santiago Eraso, director of Madrid Destino, present, and he was told to stop programming, that Madrid Destino barred him from press conferences on his own programme, and that the theatre had had no manager or artistic director since 1 January. He called this "acoso" (harassment, translated): "Eso tiene un nombre: acoso. Hasta mis enemigos me dan la razón" ("That has a name: harassment. Even my enemies say I am right", translated) (Vozpópuli, 28 May 2016).
- He said the culture department wanted someone more like-minded, which he called "absurdo" (absurd, translated), and that the culture councillor Celia Mayer had said she had nothing against his programming but wanted a change of model (Vozpópuli, 28 May 2016).
