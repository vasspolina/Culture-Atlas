CAPC MUSÉE D'ART CONTEMPORAIN – Bordeaux, France
Website: https://www.capc-bordeaux.fr (homepage loads, checked 2026-10-20; the /partenaires page could not be fetched)

OVERVIEW
- Bordeaux's municipal contemporary art museum, in the Entrepôt Lainé, a heritage-listed 1824 warehouse for colonial goods (sugar, coffee, spices) on the Garonne quays, about 3,500 m² of exhibition space.
- Founded 1973 as Centre d'Arts Plastiques Contemporains by Jean-Louis Froment; became the city's museum of contemporary art in 1984; "Musée de France" label 2002/2003 (sources differ on the year). Renovation by Valode & Pistre with interiors by Andrée Putman; shares the building with arc en rêve architecture centre.
- Director: Sandra Patron (since 2019). Previous director María Inés Rodríguez (2014–2018).
- Open Tuesday–Sunday 11:00–18:00, closed public holidays (homepage). Offers free programmes for social and medico-social groups and people with disabilities (Museum for All). No detail found on physical accessibility.
- 2026–27: framework partnership with the Centre Pompidou for an exhibition in the nave, 18 March–3 October 2027 (city council deliberation D-2026/133, 6 July 2026).

FUNDING
CAPC: mainly public (City of Bordeaux), with corporate and private patronage.
- City of Bordeaux: owns and runs the museum (stated by the museum and by city council documents). The city bears most costs of the 2027 Pompidou exhibition.
- DRAC Nouvelle-Aquitaine (French state, Ministry of Culture): named as supporter (museum's 50th-anniversary announcement on e-flux, 2023).
- Major patrons (2023, stated by the museum via e-flux): Les Amis du Capc, Cultura, Banque Palatine, CIC.
- Patrons (2023): Lacoste, Unikalo, Château Haut-Selve, Château de Camensac, Hôtel de Normandie.
- 50th-anniversary weekend partners: Novelty, Seeko'o Hôtel, Hôtel de Normandie (press kit, culture.gouv.fr).
- No reporting found of the €3.8M budget figure in the existing record; no published budget found.
- No reporting found on who funds or sits on Les Amis du Capc.

Red flags: Director dismissed by the city (2018)
- In March 2018 the City of Bordeaux dismissed director María Inés Rodríguez, citing "managerial difficulties" (The Art Newspaper, Hyperallergic). Le Monde reported falling visitor numbers and staff complaints; Rue89Bordeaux disputed that account.
- The city's culture deputy, Fabien Robert, publicly asked "Does contemporary art still exist?" and proposed the CAPC become a "vitrine" (Hyperallergic).
- 79 artists and curators, including Christian Boltanski, Hans-Ulrich Obrist and Frances Morris, signed an open letter in Libération criticising municipal pressure and shrinking funds and asking the culture minister to investigate (Hyperallergic, independent outlet).

BOTTOM LINE
The CAPC is a city-owned museum funded mainly by the City of Bordeaux with state support through the DRAC, plus banks (Banque Palatine, CIC), retailers and Bordeaux wine estates as patrons. No funding problem was found; the record's €3.8M budget figure could not be confirmed. Worth watching: the 2018 political dismissal of its director over the city's direction for the museum.
