MCA DENVER: Denver, USA
ID: mca-denver-denver
Website: https://mcadenver.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum with no permanent collection, in the lower downtown area of Denver at 1485 Delgany Street; exhibitions run for two to four months, three to four times a year (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1996 by philanthropist Sue Cannon with Marina Graves, Mark Sink, Dale Chisman and Lawrence Argent; it first occupied a renovated fish market in Sakura Square for seven years (MCA Denver).
- In 2003 Mark Falcone and Ellen Bruss donated land in the Central Platte Valley for a permanent home; Wikipedia gives the land's appraised value as $1.5 million.
- The 27,000 square foot building, designed by David Adjaye, opened in October 2007; it was named the Fries Building in 2019 (MCA Denver).
- Wikipedia lists five galleries, education spaces, a shop, a library and a rooftop cafe. A second venue, the 400-seat Holiday Theater at 2644 W 32nd Avenue, opened in April 2022 (MCA Denver).
- Anthony Kiendl became Mark G. Falcone Director in December 2025; Nora Burnett Abrams held the post from 2019 and Adam Lerner led the museum from 2009.
- Open Wednesday to Friday 12pm to 7pm and Saturday and Sunday 10am to 5pm; closed Monday and Tuesday. Galleries and restrooms are wheelchair accessible, service animals are welcome, and members get free admission.

FUNDING
MCA Denver: mainly private giving: contributions were $3.5 million of $4.7 million revenue in the year to June 2025
- Form 990 for the year to June 2025: revenue $4,717,761, expenses $5,797,923, contributions $3,500,441 (74.2% of revenue), net assets $18,917,555 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Earlier years on the same source: revenue $5,103,941 and contributions $3,871,604 in 2024, revenue $6,484,676 and contributions $5,512,783 in 2023.
- Corporate partners listed by MCA Denver: Griffis Residential (Community Leader tier); DaVita, First Western Trust Bank, Foley & Lardner LLP, Limelight Denver and Mercer Advisors (Great Choice tier); Riverfront Park Community Foundation (Friend tier); Balfour Riverfront Park, Bartlit Beck LLP and Liberty Global (Basic tier).
- John Caulkins underwrote the first three years of the museum's "I'm an Artist" membership programme (MCA Denver).
- Board: Steve Cohen chairs and is listed as Co-President and Chief Operating Officer of The Anschutz Corporation; Zack Neumeyer, Chairman of Sage Hospitality Group, and Katherine Gold, CEO, owner and president of Goldbug, Inc., are co-vice chairs; Hollie Velasquez Horvath, Regional Vice President of Xcel Energy, is a trustee. Mike Fries (President and CEO of Liberty Global, Inc.), Mark Falcone and Ellen Bruss are trustees emeriti (MCA Denver). Wikipedia lists oil and gas, through Anschutz Exploration Corporation, among The Anschutz Corporation's holdings.
- Naming: the building was named the Fries Building in 2019 (MCA Denver). Liberty Global is listed as a corporate partner in the Basic tier.

Not related: the Denver Art Museum. It is a separate nonprofit (Denver Art Museum Inc, EIN 84-6038240, its own Form 990) whose year to September 2025 showed $79.7 million in expenses and $75.5 million in contributions (ProPublica). Those figures do not belong to MCA Denver.

BOTTOM LINE
MCA Denver is a small private nonprofit, funded mostly by contributions: $3.5 million of $4.7 million revenue in the year to June 2025. Its own page names ten corporate partners by tier, and its board chair is an executive of The Anschutz Corporation.
