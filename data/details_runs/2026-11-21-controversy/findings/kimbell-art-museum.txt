ID: kimbell-art-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- The dealer Phoenix Ancient Art, S.A. sued the Kimbell over an alleged contract to buy a Roman torso the museum had received "on approval"; after the museum's counsel advised against the purchase "because of certain legal issues", the museum said it would not go forward with it (The Art Newspaper, Martha Lufkin, 1 March 2004).
- 17 November 2003: Judge Rakoff of the US District Court for the Southern District of New York granted the museum summary judgment and dismissed the suit, finding no meeting of the minds and no written contract; he wrote that "all that 'on approval' signifies is an offer, not an acceptance". The court found the torso had been sent as security for a repurchase agreement on a statuette and that the museum never paid any part of the price. No appeal was made (The Art Newspaper).
