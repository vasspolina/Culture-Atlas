OHARA MUSEUM OF ART: Kurashiki, Japan
ID: ohara-museum-of-art-kurashiki
Website: https://www.ohara.or.jp/official/ (checked 2026-10-24; loads normally)

OVERVIEW
- Private art museum in Kurashiki, Okayama prefecture; the museum's history page calls it Japan's first private museum centred on Western art (translated from the Japanese).
- Founded in 1930 by Magosaburo Ohara in memory of the painter Kojima Torajiro, who had died the year before; the building was completed on 5 November 1930 and Takeuchi Kiyomi was its first director (museum history page).
- Kojima travelled to Europe three times at Ohara's urging and chose many of the core works, including pieces by El Greco, Gauguin, Monet and Matisse (museum website).
- On site: the main gallery, the Craft and Oriental Hall and the Kojima Torajiro Memorial Museum, on one ticket (museum visitor page).
- Visiting: closed Mondays (open if Monday is a public holiday, no closures in August). March to November 9:00 to 17:00, December to February 9:00 to 15:00, last entry 30 minutes before closing. Adults 2,000 yen, under 18 500 yen, preschoolers free, Kurashiki residents and workers 1,900 yen. People holding disability certificates enter free, and one companion with some grades. Wheelchairs and strollers can be borrowed free inside; the site links a separate accessibility page that was not read.

FUNDING
Ohara Museum of Art: private public-interest foundation: admission income, donations and local corporate partners
- Operator: the Ohara Art Foundation, formed on 1 April 2024 by merging the Ohara Museum of Art and the Kurashiki Archaeological Museum. The museum became a public interest foundation in June 2011 (museum history page). Representative director: Ohara Akane (foundation website).
- Admission: in October 2020 Bijutsu Techo reported that admission fees cover about 80% of operating costs (Bijutsu Techo, 26 October 2020). That year the museum closed for 136 days because of COVID-19 and ran a crowdfunding appeal with a target of 10 million yen.
- Corporate partners: 220 companies were sponsoring as of June 2026, mainly local firms (Bijutsu Techo, 26 June 2026). The museum's "Official Partner" page, dated 1 April 2026, lists them in four unlabelled blocks with no amounts or tiers. It says the foundation needs continuing renovation to preserve and show its works and seeks corporate partners for four purposes: the viewing environment, young artists, children's creativity and conservation (translated summary). Block 1 includes Kurabo, Kuraray, Chugoku Bank and Sanyo Shimbun; block 2 Tomato Bank and Mitsubishi Motors; block 3 Okayama Gas, Yokoyama Sekiyu (Yokoyama Petroleum), Nippon Life and Kanto Denka Kogyo's Mizushima plant; block 4 the ENEOS Mizushima refinery, Japan Tobacco Inc., JFE Steel, Asahi Kasei, Mitsubishi Chemical, Mitsubishi Gas Chemical, Nippon Zeon's Mizushima plant, Tokio Marine & Nichido, West Japan Railway and Nomura Securities (names translated from the Japanese list).
- Individuals: the Supporters Association takes annual members at 10,000 yen, or 30,000 yen for premium members. Its activities include conservation and research of the works and upkeep of facilities (museum website). No member count is given.
- Origin of the founding money: Magosaburo Ohara was the second president of Kurabo (1906 to 1939), founded Kurashiki Kenshoku (now Kuraray) in 1926 and Chugoku Bank in 1930, and founded Kurashiki Dento (now Chugoku Electric Power) in 1909 (Kurabo biography). Kurabo, Kuraray and Chugoku Bank are listed partners today.
- No reporting found of the foundation's total annual income, donation totals or the size of any single partner's payment. The foundation's site links financial statements as PDFs that were not read.

Red flags: fossil fuels
- ENEOS Mizushima refinery (block 4), Okayama Gas and Yokoyama Sekiyu (block 3) are listed as official partners on the museum's partner page dated 1 April 2026. The page gives no amounts and does not describe what each company contributes beyond partnership in the four purposes above.

Red flags: tobacco
- Japan Tobacco Inc. (日本たばこ産業, block 4) is listed as an official partner on the partner page dated 1 April 2026. The page gives no amount.
