SÉRIES MANIA: Lille, France
ID: s-ries-mania-lille
Website: https://seriesmania.com/en (checked 2026-10-24; working)

OVERVIEW
- International television series festival in Lille, held each March; the 2026 edition ran from 20 to 27 March, with a professional Forum from 24 to 26 March (festival website).
- Founded in 2010 at the Forum des images in Paris by Laurence Herszberg, general director, and Frédéric Lavigne, artistic director (Wikipedia, French edition).
- In December 2015 the then Culture Minister Fleur Pellerin asked Herszberg to create a major international series festival; Lille was chosen over Cannes, Bordeaux, Nice and Paris after a CNC call for applications, and the festival has been in Lille since 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Lille venues include the Tripostal, the Nouveau Siècle, UGC cinemas and the CCI; the professional forum is at Lille Grand Palais (Wikipedia).
- Attendance was above 85,000 in 2023, with nearly 4,000 accredited professionals from 64 countries (Wikipedia).
- It also runs the Séries Mania Institute, launched in Lille in August 2021 for training in the series trades (CB News).
- Visiting: the Village Festival by Crédit Mutuel at the Tripostal is free (Crédit Mutuel Nord Europe); no ticket prices were found on the pages opened.

FUNDING
Séries Mania: mainly public with one bank as lead sponsor: Hauts-de-France region, CNC, Lille city and metropole, Crédit Mutuel Nord Europe
- 2018, first Lille edition: a budget of 5 million euros, of which 1 million from the CNC, 3.5 million from the Hauts-de-France region and 500,000 euros from private sponsors (Wikipedia, French edition). No later budget was found.
- The Lille bid was backed by the regional council, the city and the Métropole européenne de Lille, and validated by the CNC and the Ministry of Culture (Wikipedia). The Institute's partners are the regional council, the CNC and the Métropole, with France Télévisions and Newen as launch partners (CB News).
- Crédit Mutuel Nord Europe: Grand Partenaire and founding partner since the festival came to Lille in 2018; the 2 March 2026 press release marks the ninth year. No amount is published; the bank kept part of its support in 2020 when the festival was cancelled.
- The festival website footer shows logos for Crédit Mutuel, the CNC, the City of Lille, Lille Métropole and the Hauts-de-France region; the page names no tiers.
- No reporting found of annual accounts, a donor list or a published board.

Red flags: board member tied to Jeffrey Epstein, Epstein-linked
- Caroline Lang, daughter of former culture minister Jack Lang, sat on the board of directors of Séries Mania (Mediaterranée, 7 February 2026; L'Essentiel de l'éco); no start date is given. She announced on 2 February 2026 that she was leaving her post at the producers' union SPI (AFP via La Libre). On Wednesday 4 February she left Séries Mania and Pictanovo (Mediacités, 5 February 2026, which says La Voix du Nord first reported it).
- In 2016 she co-founded Prytanee LLC with Epstein in the US Virgin Islands, owned equally by both; Mediapart reports its accounts received 1.4 million dollars from Epstein's Southern Trust Company (Mediaterranée, AFP via La Libre). She said she received no pay and acknowledged she never declared the company to the French tax authorities (AFP via La Libre).
- Epstein's will, signed two days before his death, named her for 5 million dollars; she says she learned of it from the press and received nothing (AFP via La Libre, L'Essentiel de l'éco).
- 6 February 2026: the French financial prosecutor (PNF) announced a preliminary investigation into Caroline and Jack Lang for "blanchiment de fraude fiscale aggravée" (laundering of aggravated tax fraud), over facts revealed by Mediapart (Public Sénat).
- Denials: Jack Lang "continue de clamer son innocence"; their lawyer, Laurent Merlet, says they received no funds and that what they said is "la pure vérité" (Public Sénat, translation). No charge has been brought (L'Essentiel de l'éco). Le Monde noted that none of the emails shows involvement in Epstein's trafficking (Mediacités, translation).
- No reporting found of any Epstein money reaching Séries Mania itself.

BOTTOM LINE
The festival's money is mainly regional and national public funding plus a lead bank sponsor, with no amounts published after 2018. The one documented issue is the Epstein link of board member Caroline Lang, who left on 4 February 2026; the PNF opened a preliminary investigation into her and her father on 6 February, and both deny wrongdoing. Watch for published accounts and for any change to the board.
