ID: teatro-carlo-felice

Red flags: 2025 labour dispute over staffing and pay
- On 4 July 2025 the CISL union called off a strike set for that evening while keeping the state of agitation in place, after the deputy mayor Alessandro Terrile, the culture councillor Giacomo Montanari, the superintendent Michele Galli and the foundation's vice-president Enrico Musso met the theatre's unions (Mentelocale, 4 July 2025).
- The institutions undertook to schedule talks with all unions on staffing levels, pay, the training academy, generational turnover and precarious workers, with a first meeting set for 22 July (Mentelocale, 4 July 2025).
- Slc Cgil called a strike of the theatre's staff for 7 December 2025, the night of Roberto Bolle's Caravaggio, over unresolved issues of staffing with particular reference to precarious workers, pay and early-retirement schemes, and over a lack of communication with staff on the organisation of work (Mentelocale, 5 December 2025).

Red flags: 2022 strike over pay of evening staff
- Slc Cgil Genova general secretary Fabio Allegretti said the dispute began with a strike the union called alone on 31 December 2022; an agreement signed at the foundation on 18 December 2023 moved about thirty evening workers, among them ushers, make-up artists and wardrobe staff, from an hourly rate comparable to the value of a voucher up to two contract levels, in some cases three (Liguria Business Journal, 19 December 2023).
- Allegretti said the union had been singled out as defeatist over the strike and that the agreement "restituisce dignità professionale ed economica al personale definito serale" ("restores professional and economic dignity to the staff called evening staff", translated) (Liguria Business Journal).

WIDER CONTEXT
- A strike by the Snater union, strongest in the chorus, led the theatre to cancel the premiere of Il cappello di paglia di Firenze on 20 November 2007; Snater cited pay, precarious work and the relaunch of the theatre, and the mayor Marta Vincenzi said the cancelled premiere would cost 120,000 to 150,000 euros (il Giornale, 20 and 21 November 2007).
