DUBAI OPERA: Dubai, UAE
ID: dubai-opera
Website: https://www.dubaiopera.com (checked 2026-11-20; refuses automated access)

OVERVIEW
- Multi-format performing arts centre of 2,000 seats in the Opera District of Downtown Dubai, near the Burj Khalifa, reached from Mohammed Bin Rashid Boulevard (Gulf News, 23 March 2016; Wikipedia).
- Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum announced the plans in March 2012; it opened on 31 August 2016 with a concert by Plácido Domingo (Wikipedia; Emaar).
- Building: designed by Janus Rostock of Atkins in the shape of a dhow, with an all-glass foyer and over 35,500 square metres built up; Consolidated Contractors Company built it (Wikipedia; Gulf News).
- What is there: a main hall that converts between theatre, concert hall and flat-floor event space, the last seating up to 1,000 for a gala dinner, and a rooftop restaurant (Gulf News; Emaar).
- Leadership: Emaar named Jasper Hope, formerly chief operating officer of London's Royal Albert Hall, chief executive in January 2015 (Wikipedia); Paolo Petrocelli was head from 2023 (Wikipedia) and for the 2025 to 2026 season (Gulf News); Lara Ewing is head for 2026 to 2027 (Arab News, September 2026).
- Audience: more than 250,000 in the 2023 to 2024 season, its highest since opening (Emaar).
- Current season: its tenth anniversary season; the Bolshoi Theatre's first official UAE tour, co-presented with Art Seasons, brings "The Tsar's Bride" and an orchestra concert on 2 and 3 December 2026 (Khaleej Times; Arab News).

FUNDING
Dubai Opera: Gulf state body: built and run by Emaar, whose largest shareholder is the Dubai ruler's Dubai Holding; corporate sponsors HSBC and American Express
- Building: Emaar chairman Mohamed Alabbar said Emaar invested 1.2 billion dirhams (about 330 million US dollars) to develop Dubai Opera, part of its 20 billion dollar Downtown Dubai development (Emaar press release, 2016).
- Operator: Dubai Opera is part of Emaar Entertainment, Emaar's attractions arm (Emaar).
- Sponsors: American Express Middle East became an Official Partner in 2024, sponsoring four productions a year (Zawya, September 2024); HSBC joined as Official Partner for 2025 to 2026, with Van Cleef & Arpels and American Hospital as partners (The Arabian Post, September 2025).
- Box office and hire: tickets, gala dinners and corporate events (Emaar).

Red flags: run by Emaar, whose largest shareholders are Dubai state investors
- Emaar Properties is listed on the Dubai Financial Market; in May 2026 Dubai Holding bought the Investment Corporation of Dubai's 22.27 percent stake, raising its holding to 29.73 percent and becoming the largest shareholder (Dubai Media Office; Khaleej Times).
- Wikipedia describes Dubai Holding as the investment company and personal portfolio of Dubai ruler Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, and the Investment Corporation of Dubai as Dubai's sovereign wealth fund.
- Alabbar called the house "our tribute to our city and the vision" of Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid (Gulf News, 2016).

Red flags: HSBC, official partner since 2025, fined by the Bank of England in 2024
- On 30 January 2024 the Prudential Regulation Authority fined HSBC Bank plc and HSBC UK Bank plc 57,417,500 pounds for years of failures to apply the Depositor Protection Rules (Bank of England).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism; one opera-press piece objected to the house's bookings of Russian artists who back the Kremlin.
1. OperaWire: Francisco Salazar, "José Carreras & Plácido Domingo to Perform with Putin Allies Valery Gergiev & Hibla Gerzmava in Dubai" (2025)
URL: https://operawire.com/jose-carreras-placido-domingo-to-perform-with-putin-allies-valery-gergiev-hibl-gerzmava-in-dubai/
- Reports a December 2025 gala at Dubai Opera with the Mariinsky Theatre Orchestra, and names Gergiev and Gerzmava as Putin supporters.
- Notes that Western houses dropped Gergiev after the invasion of Ukraine and that Canada sanctioned him.
- Criticises the artists and the booking; it does not discuss the house's funding.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Valery Gergiev, sanctioned by Canada, became general manager of the Bolshoi Theatre (OperaWire); the Bolshoi tours Dubai Opera in December 2026 (Khaleej Times).

BOTTOM LINE
A developer's flagship venue whose money traces to the Dubai ruler's own investment company, with a sponsor bank that carries a recent regulatory fine. Worth watching: further bookings of sanctioned Russian artists.
