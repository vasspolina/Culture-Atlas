WIDER CONTEXT
- In August 2026 Russia's Ministry of Justice added Pikene på Broen to its register of "undesirable" organisations, accusing it of posing a threat to "the foundations of Russia's constitutional order and security". The Independent Barents Observer says the status "effectively bans the organisation from operating in Russia" and that its members and partners face administrative or criminal liability; Pikene på Broen is the third organisation in Kirkenes and the ninth in Norway declared "undesirable" by Russia's Prosecutor General (The Independent Barents Observer, 29 August 2026).
- Administrative director Franziska Kraiczy said the designation creates a "major problem" because the group works with artists based in Russia, called it "not such a big surprise", and said: "We have to find a way to continue our work" (The Independent Barents Observer, 29 August 2026).
