ART-SIADZIBA: Minsk, Belarus
ID: art-siadziba-minsk
Website: https://artsiadziba.by

OVERVIEW
- Independent cultural platform in Minsk that promoted the Belarusian language through concerts, lectures and entertainment events for young people (Radio Svaboda).
- Founded on 24 December 2011. Radio Svaboda names Pavel Belavus as founder and first director; Wikipedia lists Stas Babaytsau, Franak Viachorka and Zmicier Afanasenka as founders. Kalektar names Belavus as founder and Afanasenka and Viachorka as co-founders.
- The English Wikipedia entry translates the name as "Art Headquarters".
- Venues: the Horizont factory building, then 28a Pushkin Avenue, then a two-room flat in central Minsk and, from 2013, 6 Instrumentalny Lane (Kalektar). A new space planned for 4 Maskvin Lane was lost in December 2012 before it opened (Nasha Niva, 6 December 2012).
- Programme: concerts, presentations and film screenings (Nasha Niva, December 2012), book launches and lectures (Nasha Niva, July 2012).
- It announced on 3 January 2022 that it was ending its activity (Radio Svaboda).

FUNDING
Art-Siadziba: independent venue in rented premises, with a 2014 crowdfunding appeal for a building of its own
- In 2014 the "Dom" (House) campaign raised a little over $4,000 in 10 days toward a goal of $200,000, sold as 40,000 virtual bricks at $5 each (Charter97, 6 March 2014).
- Premises were rented: the planned Maskvin Lane space belonged to JSC Sukno and was leased by JSC RefOilProdukt, with Art-Siadziba as sub-tenant (Nasha Niva, 6 December 2012); at Instrumentalny Lane the owner was the Belarusian Society of Hunters and Fishermen, which let Art-Siadziba stay until 1 May 2014 (Charter97, 6 March 2014).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Radio Svaboda reports that by the end of 2021 the Belarusian authorities had liquidated at least 275 non-governmental organisations, with more in liquidation or under threat. An update on the same page says that in July 2022 it became known that 857 had been liquidated or driven to self-liquidation.
- Amnesty International (Aisha Jung, 22 February 2021): "The authorities are methodically destroying Belarus's spirited cultural life and its most creative members." The statement is about the country's cultural scene as a whole.
- In July 2012, when the space had closed for the first time, the city culture department told Nasha Niva it does not handle decisions of this kind.
- Unverified: after the third loss of premises in December 2012, Pavel Belavus said the landlord was summoned to the Minsk City Executive Committee and strongly advised to cancel the lease, and that the order came from its ideology department; Nasha Niva reports this as Belavus's account.
- Connection to Art-Siadziba: Radio Svaboda reports that it was placed on a list of organisations subject to liquidation in late July 2021 and announced on 3 January 2022 that it was ending its activity. Belavus faced a criminal case under Article 342 of the Criminal Code and was held in SIZO-1 in Minsk when Svaboda published.

BOTTOM LINE
Art-Siadziba was an independent venue in rented premises, with a 2014 crowdfunding appeal for a building of its own. It was placed on a liquidation list in July 2021 and ended its activity in January 2022, during the wider Belarusian liquidation of non-governmental organisations.
