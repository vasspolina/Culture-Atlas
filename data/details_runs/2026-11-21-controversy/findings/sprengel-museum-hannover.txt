ID: sprengel-museum-hannover

WIDER CONTEXT
- In January 2017 Germany's advisory commission on Nazi-looted art, the Limbach Commission, asked the Sprengel Museum to return Karl Schmidt-Rottluff's 1922 watercolour "Marsh Landscape With Red Windmill" to the grandchildren of Max Rüdenberg, a Jewish businessman who died in Theresienstadt in 1942 (The Art Newspaper, Catherine Hickley, 10 January 2017).
- The work came to the museum through Bernhard Sprengel's donation; the City of Hanover argued there was "not enough evidence that the watercolour belonged to Rüdenberg before the Second World War", and the commission's decision ended five years of dispute (The Art Newspaper, 10 January 2017).
