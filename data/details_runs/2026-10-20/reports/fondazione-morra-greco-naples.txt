FONDAZIONE MORRA GRECO: Naples, Italy
ID: fondazione-morra-greco-naples
Website: https://www.fondazionemorragreco.com

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre for exhibitions, research and international artist residencies in the historic centre of Naples, in Palazzo Caracciolo di Avellino (the foundation's website).
- Established in 2003 by the dentist and collector Maurizio Morra Greco and opened to the public in 2006 with an exhibition by Gregor Schneider; it grew out of the Collezione Morra Greco, begun in the 1990s, which holds over a thousand works by about two hundred artists (the foundation's history page).
- Building: a sixteenth-century residence that was once a Benedictine convent, with a five-bay cloister; the poet Torquato Tasso lived there from 1550 to 1554, and Giacomo del Po painted illusory architectural frescoes on the first floor in the early eighteenth century. The palazzo was sold in 1806 and divided; Morra Greco began acquiring units in 1996, and ancient Greek walls were found in the basement (the foundation's venue page).
- A renovation from 2015 to 2019 opened two more floors, giving 2,000 m2 of exhibition space on five levels, and the foundation reopened on 29 June 2019 (the foundation's history page; Artribune, Marilena Di Tursi, 24 June 2019).
- Programme: exhibitions, residencies, the EDI Global Forum (editions in 2022, 2023 and 2026), school and education projects and the Biblioteca Morra Greco; a scientific committee includes Elena Filipovic, Luigi Fassi, Jörg Heiser, Francesco Manacorda and Heike Munder (the foundation's website and history page).
- Admission is always free (the foundation's history page).

FUNDING
Fondazione Morra Greco: public-private: founder Maurizio Morra Greco and the Region of Campania
- Since 2008 the foundation has been a mixed public-private foundation, the first of its kind in Italy, with the Region of Campania on its board of directors (the foundation's history page).
- Morra Greco said in 2019 that the Region of Campania holds 50% of the board, that public money comes through the regional programme "Attrattori Culturali, Naturali e Turismo" (FESR 2007 to 2013), and that on the private side he is the only contributor and is building partnerships (Artribune, 2019).
- The renovation was financed in part through that same programme, run by the Campania regional office (the foundation's history page).
- The collection and the palazzo units were assembled by Morra Greco himself from 1996 (the foundation's venue page).

BOTTOM LINE
The founder, the dentist Maurizio Morra Greco, was the only private contributor in 2019 (Artribune). The Region of Campania holds half of the board, and public money comes through the regional programme "Attrattori Culturali, Naturali e Turismo" (FESR 2007 to 2013). Entry is free.
