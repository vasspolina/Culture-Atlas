MULTIMEDIA ART MUSEUM, MOSCOW (MAMM): Moscow, Russia
ID: multimedia-art-museum-moscow-mamm-moscow
Website: https://mamm.art (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of photography and contemporary art at Ostozhenka 16, Moscow, with seven floors, a glass lift and a constructivist staircase (MAMM website).
- Founded by the Government of Moscow in 1996 as the Moscow House of Photography, the first Russian state art institution focused on photography; it became the Multimedia Complex of Contemporary Arts in 2001 (MAMM website, Wikipedia).
- Olga Sviblova has directed it since its founding (MAMM website, Wikipedia).
- Construction on the Ostozhenka building began in 2005 while the museum kept running; the Complex returned to the renovated building in 2010, with about 9,000 square metres and four exhibition floors (Wikipedia).
- The collection holds more than 180,000 items; the museum has no permanent display and runs about 60 exhibitions a year (MAMM website).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday 12:00 to 21:00, closed Monday; the site lists no ticket prices and offers a version for visually impaired visitors and a page on facilities for visitors with disabilities.

FUNDING
MAMM: mainly public: the Moscow government, with corporate partners
- The museum was founded by the Government of Moscow and is supported by the Moscow Department of Culture (MAMM website).
- Nornickel is named as corporate patron, supporting exhibitions, the "History of Russia in Photography" programme and publishing; Alfa-Bank is strategic partner (MAMM partners page, stated by the museum, undated).
- Other partners: Live Sound (technical), .ART (innovation), Priorov Center (healthcare), RBC, Snob and The Art Newspaper Russia (media).
- No reporting found of amounts, years or a board; the "Open information" section of the site was not readable.

Red flags: sanctioned partners
- Alfa-Bank: the United States announced sanctions on it on 24 February 2022, restricting new debt and equity; the United Kingdom's sanctions took effect on 24 March 2022; the EU adopted sanctions in its 10th package in February 2023 (Wikipedia). Mikhail Fridman is its controlling owner (Wikipedia).
- Nornickel: its main beneficiary Vladimir Potanin, who owns the Interros holding, was sanctioned by the United Kingdom on about 29 June 2022 for owning Rosbank (Interfax) and by the United States on 15 December 2022. The US restrictions were not extended to Nornickel, which the notice did not mention (AJOT).
- No reporting found of a protest or objection aimed at MAMM over these partners.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a Moscow government institution with two large corporate partners, Alfa-Bank, which is under US, UK and EU sanctions, and Nornickel, whose main beneficiary is sanctioned in the UK and US. Nothing is published on amounts or years. Worth watching: whether MAMM discloses what these partners pay and whether Nornickel itself is sanctioned.
