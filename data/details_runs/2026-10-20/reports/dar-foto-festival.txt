DAR FOTO FESTIVAL: Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
ID: dar-foto-festival
Website: https://www.darfotofestival.com

OVERVIEW
- Annual photography festival in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, with exhibitions, portfolio reviews, street and documentary photo walks, and conversations with professionals (PH Museum).
- Founded by the Tanzanian photographer Andrew Stephen Munuwa, its Creative Director, with co-founder Valentin Rudloff of Germany (PH Museum; The Citizen; Humboldt Forum). Sources give different founding years: 2020 (Humboldt Forum), 2021 (PH Museum) and 2023 (The Citizen).
- Munuwa is an independent photographer and curator from Dar es Salaam whose work focuses on everyday urban life in African cities (Humboldt Forum).
- 2024 edition (the second, per The Citizen): 2 February to 8 March 2024 at the Old Boma building in Posta, Dar es Salaam, with theme "People, Economy, Change", 12 Tanzanian artists, photo walks, panel discussions and mentorship; more than 800 visitors; a follow-up exhibition at the Emerson Art Gallery in Stone Town, Zanzibar, 21 June to 21 July 2024 (The Citizen, Ramadhani Ismail, 15 June 2024).
- 2025 edition: "Coexistence", 31 January to 1 March 2025 in Dar es Salaam and Stone Town (PH Museum); 15 artists from Congo, Sudan, Kenya and Tanzania (Musée Magazine, 14 February 2025).
- The festival also staged a pop-up presentation on coexistence at the Humboldt Forum in Berlin, as part of its "Histories of Tanzania" exhibition (Humboldt Forum).

FUNDING
Dar Foto Festival: founder-led: Andrew Munuwa and Valentin Rudloff
- Andrew Munuwa founded the festival and is its Creative Director (Humboldt Forum); the Humboldt Forum hosted the Berlin pop-up presentation.
- Kedmon Mapana, Executive Secretary of BASATA, Tanzania's National Arts Council, said at the 2024 opening that "BASATA is keen to support the Dar Foto Festival in the coming years" (The Citizen).

BOTTOM LINE
Dar Foto Festival was founded by Andrew Munuwa and Valentin Rudloff, and Munuwa is its Creative Director. BASATA's Executive Secretary said in 2024 that the council is keen to support it.
