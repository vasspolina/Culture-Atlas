ZHEJIANG MUSEUM: Hangzhou, China
ID: zhejiang-museum
Website: https://www.zhejiangmuseum.com/ (checked 2026-11-20; refused the automated check)

OVERVIEW
- The Zhejiang Provincial Museum (浙江省博物馆) is the largest comprehensive museum in Zhejiang and one of the first national first-grade museums (The Paper, 2023).
- Founded in 1929 as the Zhejiang Provincial West Lake Museum (The Paper, 2023; Travel China Guide); Chinese Wikipedia states the provincial government set it up to keep objects from the West Lake Expo.
- It now has two main sites: the Gushan (Solitary Hill) site by West Lake, rebuilt in 1993 (Hangzhou News, 2019), and the Zhijiang site in the new Zhijiang Cultural Center, opened on 29 August 2023; a Wulin site on West Lake Cultural Square opened in 2009 (The Paper, 2023; Travel China Guide).
- The Zhijiang building has 100,000 square metres of floor space and 32,012 square metres of galleries on eight floors above ground; it opened with 13 exhibitions in 15 halls (The Paper; Hangzhou News, 2 September 2023).
- The collection has more than 100,000 objects, including over 3,700 works by Huang Binhong and Huang Gongwang's Remaining Mountain section of "Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains", shown for about a month a year (Travel China Guide; The Paper).
- Chen Shuihua (陈水华), director at the 2023 opening, said the Gushan and Wulin sites together had only about 36,000 square metres, which led to the Zhijiang building (The Paper, 2023).
- In summer 2026 the museum showed "Recreating the Old Summer Palace", with the original ox, tiger, monkey and pig zodiac heads; it apologised publicly on 6 August 2026 after crowding at the entrance, and again that month for errors in the texts of a Long March print show (Tide News; Guangming Online, reporting The Paper and the museum's WeChat account).
- Visiting: the Zhijiang site opens 9am to 5pm, last entry 4.30pm, closed Mondays, and regular visits need no booking (Tide News; Guangming Online).

FUNDING
Zhejiang Museum: public: Zhejiang provincial government, with central government co-funding
- On 1 January 2004 it became the first provincial museum in China to open free to the public (People's Daily Overseas Edition, 28 November 2015).
- In 2009 the Ministry of Finance and the National Cultural Heritage Administration named it a centrally and locally co-built national museum (People's Daily Overseas Edition; The Paper, 2023).
- The Zhijiang site is part of the Zhijiang Cultural Center, a group of four provincial venues and one service centre that The Paper calls the largest provincial public culture cluster in China; building began on 28 February 2019 (The Paper, 2023).
- The 1993 Gushan building cost 20 million yuan (Chinese Wikipedia).
- Gifts of objects include more than 5,000 works and 10,000 relics from Huang Binhong's family, and 160 Song to Qing lacquer pieces from Cao Qiyong (曹其镛) and his wife in 2012; a museum board was set up in December 2018 (Chinese Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the province and the central state; nothing in its funding raises a flag.
