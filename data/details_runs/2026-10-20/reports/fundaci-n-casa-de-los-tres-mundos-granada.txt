FUNDACIÓN CASA DE LOS TRES MUNDOS: Granada, Nicaragua
ID: fundaci-n-casa-de-los-tres-mundos-granada
Website: https://c3mundos.org

OVERVIEW
- Cultural centre in the Casa de los Leones (House of the Lions) in Granada, a house dated to 1720 and named for the two stone lions at its portal (Casa de los Tres Mundos, La Verdad Nica).
- Founded in 1987 by the Austrian actor Dietmar Schönherr and the Nicaraguan poet Ernesto Cardenal, who acquired the building from the heirs of Julio Cardenal to restore it as an international cultural centre (Casa de los Tres Mundos).
- The house had been rebuilt by Julio Cardenal Argüello in 1924 with a neoclassical facade and a second floor, and served as a residence, shop, factory, toy workshop and private primary school until 1987 (Casa de los Tres Mundos).
- The main building and first two patios were restored by 1992 using traditional methods, the auditorium was finished in 1994 and the old Granada courthouse was added and restored (Casa de los Tres Mundos).
- Programmes listed on its archive site: a music school, the Infantilarte art school, an artists' studio, a graphic studio, a school of scenic arts, LoCreo, local development in Malacatoya, Música en los Barrios, Radio Volcán, a municipal archive and an art library (Casa de los Tres Mundos).
- The government of Nicaragua closed the foundation in August 2024, including Radio Volcán, which employed about 20 workers; the house had been operating with the radio station and a group of painters who kept an exhibition space and an art school (La Verdad Nica, Augusto Cermeño, 27 August 2024).

FUNDING
Fundación Casa de los Tres Mundos: funded by the governments of Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Netherlands and by the City of Granada
- The foundation's history page names its funders as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the German federal government (for monument conservation), the federal government of Austria (North-South Institute), the Swiss government (COSUDE), the Dutch government (News Project) and the City of Granada (stated by the space).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Confidencial (Redacción Confidencial, 12 February 2026) reports that on 19 August 2024 the Nicaraguan Executive outlawed the foundation along with 1,499 other nonprofit associations "for alleged noncompliance with the law", and ordered the movable and immovable assets of those organisations transferred to the State of Nicaragua. It reports that the building was seized by the State in August 2024.
- La Verdad Nica (Augusto Cermeño, 27 August 2024) reported that the government closed the foundation, including Radio Volcán, and that the government had begun removing NGOs from circulation about three years earlier.
- Confidencial reports that on 11 February 2026 a presidential decree declared the seized building National Cultural Heritage, and that Granada City Hall sources said an event centre with a restaurant would open there.
- Connection to the space: the foundation is one of the organisations outlawed on 19 August 2024; its funders named on its own history page are foreign governments and the City of Granada.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation's named funders are the governments of Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Netherlands and the City of Granada, according to its own history page. The Nicaraguan government outlawed it on 19 August 2024 along with 1,499 other organisations and took the building, which a presidential decree declared National Cultural Heritage in February 2026.
