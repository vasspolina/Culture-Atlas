SCHAULAGER – Basel (Münchenstein), Switzerland
Website: https://schaulager.org (not checked: fetch not permitted in this run, 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art storage, research and exhibition building by Herzog & de Meuron, built 2002-03 on commission from the Laurenz Foundation and opened 2003. Core holding is the Emanuel Hoffmann Foundation collection, founded in 1933 by Maja Hoffmann-Stehlin (later Maja Sacher) (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Schaulager: privately funded by one foundation.
- The Laurenz Foundation is its carrier foundation ("Trägerstiftung") (Wikipedia). Existing record: founded 1999 by Maja Oeri and Hans U. Bodenmann; does not give grants. Not re-checked this run.
- The Laurenz Foundation also co-funded the 2016 Kunstmuseum Basel extension with the canton (Kunstmuseum Basel Wikipedia).
- Ultimate source: Roche founding-family wealth; Maja Oeri holds 5% of Roche voting shares outside the family pool (Roche Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of other funders, accounts or public subsidy.

Not related: Emanuel Hoffmann Foundation — a separate collection foundation whose works Schaulager stores and shows.

BOTTOM LINE
No issues found. Schaulager is funded by a single private family foundation linked to the Roche fortune and publishes no accounts.
