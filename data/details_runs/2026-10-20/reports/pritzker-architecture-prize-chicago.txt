PRITZKER ARCHITECTURE PRIZE: Chicago, USA
ID: pritzker-architecture-prize-chicago
Website: https://www.pritzkerprize.com/about (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Annual international architecture prize awarded to a living architect or architects, run from Chicago through the Hyatt Foundation; it is an award, not a building or a museum.
- Founded in 1979 by Jay A. Pritzker and his wife Cindy Pritzker (prize site); the first prize went to Philip Johnson (Euronews).
- Each laureate receives $100,000 and a bronze medallion, presented since 1987; before that laureates received a limited-edition Henry Moore sculpture (prize site).
- A jury of internationally respected professionals chooses the laureate; the prize says the jury works confidentially and free from external influence.
- The 2026 laureate is the Chilean architect Smiljan Radić Clarke, announced on 12 March 2026, a week after the date it had been due (Euronews).
- Visiting: the ceremony is invitation-only and usually held in May at an architecturally significant site; since 2020 ceremony videos have been published.

FUNDING
Pritzker Architecture Prize: privately funded: the Pritzker family, through the Hyatt Foundation
- The Pritzker family established and funds the prize through the Hyatt Foundation, which sponsors it (prize site; Wikipedia).
- The Hyatt Foundation, a private foundation in Chicago, reported total assets of $20.9 million, revenue of $2.19 million, expenses of $1.63 million and charitable disbursements of $1.37 million for 2024 (ProPublica, Form 990-PF).
- Thomas J. Pritzker is listed as director and president of the Hyatt Foundation with no pay; Margot Pritzker and Marian F. Pritzker are listed as directors and vice presidents (ProPublica, 2024 filing).
- No reporting found of outside sponsors or public money.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Unredacted US Department of Justice files show Tom Pritzker in frequent contact with Jeffrey Epstein, including after Epstein's 2008 plea deal (Architectural Record). Emails show them discussing invitations to prize events, including the 2012 Beijing ceremony; there is no evidence Epstein attended any ceremony (Architectural Record).
- Pritzker is not accused of wrongdoing and is not named in any active Epstein investigation; a 2016 deposition by Virginia Giuffre named him, and he denies it (Architectural Record).
- He retired as executive chairman of Hyatt Hotels Corporation in February 2026; Architectural Record (17 February 2026) quotes him: "I exercised terrible judgment in maintaining contact with them". No reporting found of money passing between him and Epstein, or of Epstein holding any role in the prize.
- Connection to the prize: ArtReview (24 February 2026) calls Pritzker chairman and president of the Hyatt Foundation, which established the prize; the prize site says he presents the prize at the ceremony. Euronews (12 March 2026) says he has since stepped back from the foundation but remains a director and vice president. His tie to Epstein is documented contact, not money or a shared role, so it stays here.

BOTTOM LINE
The prize is paid for by the Pritzker family through one private foundation, and no source opened shows money from a flagged industry. The weak point is its president: Tom Pritzker's documented contact with Epstein after 2008, which delayed the 2026 announcement by a week. Worth watching: whether the foundation changes its leadership or jury process.
