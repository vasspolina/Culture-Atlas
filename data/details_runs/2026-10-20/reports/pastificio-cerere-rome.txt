FONDAZIONE PASTIFICIO CERERE: Rome, Italy
ID: pastificio-cerere-rome
Website: https://www.pastificiocerere.it

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art foundation at Via degli Ausoni 7 in the San Lorenzo district of Rome, in a former pasta factory.
- The Cerere pasta factory was founded in San Lorenzo in 1905 and supplied pasta and flour to the city until 1960; from the early 1970s artists, later known as the San Lorenzo group, took studios in it (the foundation's history page).
- The Fondazione Pastificio Cerere was founded in 2005, and its headquarters opened in the building with the exhibition "RESIDENTI"; Flavio Misciattelli, who took a room in the building in 1998 on his family's property, is its president (the foundation's history and governance pages).
- Marcello Smarrelli has been artistic director since 2011 and Claudia Cavalieri is director (the foundation's governance page).
- The foundation runs exhibitions, workshops, artist residencies, educational programmes and public and participatory art projects (the foundation's governance page).
- Since 2015 the foundation has belonged to the Comitato Fondazioni Arte Contemporanea, set up at the invitation of the Ministry of Culture (the foundation's governance page).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday, 3 pm to 7 pm, from October to May, and Monday to Friday, 3 pm to 7 pm, from June to September; admission is free.

FUNDING
Fondazione Pastificio Cerere (Rome): donor-funded with public project grants: private gifts plus Ministry of Culture, SIAE and Ministry of the Interior money for specific shows
- 2024: income of 66,503 euros, of which 58,000 euros was private donations (elargizioni liberali) and 8,500 euros came from the Ministry of the Interior for the show "Radical Pleasure" (foundation financial statement for 2024).
- 2024: the project "Anche il sole sorge" cost 27,931 euros and was made with support from the Ministry of Culture and SIAE under the programme "Per Chi Crea" (financial statement for 2024).
- 2025: income of 81,350 euros, made up of 30,000 euros from SIAE "Per Chi Crea 2023", 32,203.50 euros from "Italian Council XIV" and 19,141 euros in private donations (foundation financial statement for 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the foundation is small and mostly project-based: private donations plus Ministry of Culture, SIAE and Ministry of the Interior grants for specific shows, as its own 2024 and 2025 financial statements record.
