WIDER CONTEXT
- On 14 March 2018 Adel Abdessemed and MAC Lyon withdrew his video Printemps (2013), which shows chickens with tied legs as flames lick their feathers, from the exhibition L'antidote, after an animal rights activist criticised it on Twitter and other social media users followed (The Art Newspaper, Anna Sansom, 15 March 2018).
- The museum's press statement said the chickens were exposed to flames for three seconds under the control of technicians and the artist (The Art Newspaper).
- Director Thierry Raspail said: "The decision was taken by Adel", adding "I'd never have taken it alone because that would have been censorship", and that the aim was "to calm the situation down"; he said some of the tweets were racist (The Art Newspaper).
