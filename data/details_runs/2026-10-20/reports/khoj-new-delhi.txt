KHOJ INTERNATIONAL ARTISTS' ASSOCIATION: New Delhi, India
ID: khoj-new-delhi
Website: https://khojstudios.org

OVERVIEW
- Autonomous not-for-profit contemporary arts organisation at S-17, Khirkee Extension, New Delhi, across from Select Citywalk (stated by Khoj).
- Founded in 1997; the founding working group was Ajay Desai, Anita Dube, Bharti Kher, Subodh Gupta, Prithpal Singh Ladi, Manisha Parekh and Pooja Sood, and Sood is currently its director (Wikipedia). Khoj's story page says the idea began as "a gift of possibility given by Robert Loder", founder of the Triangle Arts Trust.
- The first workshop in 1997 brought 22 artists together in Sikribagh near Modinagar (Wikipedia). In 2002 Khoj acquired a building in Khirkee Extension; it reopened refurbished to the public in 2013 (Khoj's story page).
- Programmes (Khoj's story page): the South Asia Network for the Arts (initiated 2001), the Peers student residency (from 2003), Curatorial Intensive South Asia (from 2017) and the Khoj Support Grant (from 2020).
- Governance: Khoj's governance page links grant information documents on foreign contributions for each financial year from 2012-2015 to 2026-2027, a sexual harassment policy, a data privacy policy and an accessibility statement.

FUNDING
Khoj International Artists' Association: donor-funded: foundations, cultural institutes and private donors
- Khoj's supporters page names its main supporters as the Prince Claus Fund for Culture and Development, Goethe-Institut New Delhi and the Ford Foundation.
- Capital Circle donors: Amrita Jhaveri, Apeejay Trust in memory of Surrendra Paul, Czaee Shah, Geeta Kapur and Vivaan Sundaram, Inlaks India Foundation, Mrs. Lata Sud, Nature Morte, Poonam Bhagat Shroff, SH Raza, Shalini Passi, Sunita Choraria and Weikfield Products Pvt. Ltd. The Benefactor is Sangeeta Jindal.
- The institutions list includes the British Council, Hivos, the Rockefeller Foundation, Pro Helvetia, the Japan Foundation, the Samdani Foundation, Jamsetji Tata Trust, the Wellcome Trust / DBT India Alliance, Arts Collaboratory, Kadist and the Triangle Arts Trust.
- Fundraiser: in February 2025 Khoj held the exhibition-fundraiser "Peers Continuum: A Relay of Reciprocity" during India Art Fair, with more than 70 works from former Peers residency artists, to help secure the programme's future. The article says Khoj has long relied on its artist alumni to donate works for fundraisers, and that past proceeds enabled it to enlarge and renovate its building (The Art Newspaper, Cleo Roberts-Komireddi, 3 February 2025).
- The same article says Sood suggests arts funding in India is "at its most perilous", that non-profits contend with private foundations and corporations that redirect resources to their own spaces, and that international funding has declined overall. It says Hivos and the Ford Foundation funded Sood's work establishing the South Asian Network for the Arts.

BOTTOM LINE
Khoj is a non-profit whose supporters page names the Prince Claus Fund, Goethe-Institut New Delhi and the Ford Foundation as its main supporters, with Indian trusts, companies and individual donors, and it raises money from its artist alumni through fundraising exhibitions such as "Peers Continuum" in February 2025.
