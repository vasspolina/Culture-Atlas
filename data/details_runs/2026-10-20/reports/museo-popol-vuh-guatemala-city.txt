MUSEO POPOL VUH: Guatemala City, Guatemala
ID: museo-popol-vuh-guatemala-city
Website: https://popolvuh.ufm.edu

OVERVIEW
- Archaeology and colonial art museum on the campus of Universidad Francisco Marroquín (UFM) in Zone 10, Guatemala City (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1977 by Jorge and Ella Castillo from their private collection; in 1978 they donated it to UFM to establish the museum (Brenda Larios, Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias, 30 May 2021).
- Named for the Popol Vuh, the book written soon after the Spanish conquest that narrates the myths and pre-Columbian history of the K'iche' people (Wikipedia).
- First housed at Avenida La Reforma and 16 calle, zone 10; three years later it moved to zone 9 for 16 years, and in 1997 to UFM's campus (AGN).
- Over 400 archaeological and colonial pieces are on display, arranged in time order from the Paleoindian period to the colonial era, including funerary ceramics and Preclassic stone sculpture (English Wikipedia). AGN lists painted Classic ceramics from Petén and Alta Verapaz, funerary urns from Kaminaljuyú, incense burners from Lake Amatitlán, and colonial silver, sculpture and painting.
- Visiting: Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00, Saturday 9:00 to 13:00, closed Sunday; wheelchair accessible (English Wikipedia). Admission was Q35 in 2012 (Revue Magazine, Anna-Claire Bevan, 16 May 2012).

FUNDING
Museo Popol Vuh: university museum supported by its own resources and corporate donors
- English Wikipedia describes it as a private, non-profit scientific institution funded by its own resources and external donations, run by a board of Guatemalan citizens.
- UFM's news item "Museo Popol Vuh ofrece más contenido en línea" (20 April 2020, no byline) says the museum is supported by external donations from "empresas pilares" (pillar companies): Pepsi, CBC, Pollo Campero and Corporación Multi Inversiones (stated by the university).
- UFM's donations page takes gifts for the museum, either directly to UFM with a Guatemalan tax deduction or through Donors Capital Fund for a US tax deduction.
- The Castillos' 1978 donation of their collection is the origin of the holdings (AGN).
- UFM was founded in 1971 by Manuel Ayau (English Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum rests on a private couple's collection donated to UFM, and it is supported by its own resources and by named corporate donors: Pepsi, CBC, Pollo Campero and Corporación Multi Inversiones, as named by UFM in April 2020.
