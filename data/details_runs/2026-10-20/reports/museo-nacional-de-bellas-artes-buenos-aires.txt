MUSEO NACIONAL DE BELLAS ARTES: Buenos Aires, Argentina
ID: museo-nacional-de-bellas-artes-buenos-aires
Website: https://2024portal.amigosdelbellasartes.org.ar/nuestros-socios/

OVERVIEW
- National fine arts museum in Recoleta, Buenos Aires (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Created by decree of President José Evaristo Uriburu in 1895 with Eduardo Schiaffino as first director; it opened on 25 December 1896 in the Bon Marché building on calle Florida with 163 works (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Its permanent home is the former Casa de Bombas, a pumping station in Recoleta, redesigned by the architect Alejandro Bustillo (Spanish Wikipedia).
- The collection holds more than 12,000 pieces, from pre-Columbian, colonial and Argentine art to international art, with 747 works on display (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Andrés Duprat has been executive director since December 2015, appointed after a public competition (Spanish Wikipedia). In 2019 La Nación described a reopening with renovated galleries and a restored pre-Hispanic and colonial collection (Mariana Arias, "La nueva fundación del Bellas Artes completa su historia", 10 April 2019).
- Visiting: entry is free every day (Meteored, Agustina Fontirroig, 4 November 2024). Spanish Wikipedia lists Tuesday to Friday 11:00 to 20:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 20:00, closed Monday.

FUNDING
Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes: national state museum, with a private Friends association
- Spanish Wikipedia says acquisitions made in 2013 "con fondos del Estado Nacional" (with national state funds) added more than 130 objects.
- La Nación credited the 2019 reopening to the "apoyo incondicional" of the national Culture Secretariat, together with unnamed sponsors.
- Prensa Latina (23 May 2025, no byline) reported that decrees turned the museum, the Centro Cultural Kirchner and Tecnópolis into organisational units inside the Secretaría de Cultura. Presidential spokesperson Manuel Adorni said the measures aim to save funds, improve state organisation and remove "militancia" from these institutions.
- The Asociación Amigos del Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes, a non-profit founded in 1931 to promote local art and enlarge the museum's holdings, runs an acquisitions programme (Spanish Wikipedia). Its members page lists these corporate members: ArtHaus, Bagó, Banco Hipotecario, Compañía Sudamericana de Dragados, Fundación María Calderón de la Barca, Gador, Hospital Alemán, KPMG, La Recova de Posadas and Nicholson y Cano Abogados. Membership starts at $1,500 a month.
- Julio Crivelli was president of the Amigos association in 2019 (La Nación).

Not related: the Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes of Santiago, Chile, directed by Varinia Brodsky. It suspended a León Ferrari retrospective in June 2026, which Brodsky attributes to lack of budget (Emilia Aparicio Ulloa, El Mostrador, 14 June 2026). Andrés Duprat, director of the Buenos Aires museum, is a curator of that show; the two museums are separate.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a national state museum, and its Friends association lists ten corporate members: ArtHaus, Bagó, Banco Hipotecario, Compañía Sudamericana de Dragados, Fundación María Calderón de la Barca, Gador, Hospital Alemán, KPMG, La Recova de Posadas and Nicholson y Cano Abogados.
