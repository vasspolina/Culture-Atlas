HO CHI MINH MUSEUM: Hanoi, Vietnam
ID: ho-chi-minh-museum
Website: https://baotanghochiminh.vn (checked 2026-11-20; online ticket sales not yet working, tickets by telephone)

OVERVIEW
- National museum in Hanoi on the life, career, thought and revolutionary ethics of President Ho Chi Minh; it also helps receive visitors to the Ba Đình historical and cultural relic area (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, Decision 2372/QĐ-BVHTTDL).
- On 25 November 1970 the Party Central Committee Secretariat issued Resolution 206-NQ/TW setting up a board to build the museum; after 20 years of preparation and five of construction it opened on 19 May 1990, Ho Chi Minh's centenary (Nhân Dân).
- Nhân Dân describes the building as a white lotus and as the "crystallisation of Vietnam-Russia friendship" (translation by the atlas).
- Holds 170,000 documents, objects and images and has received more than 40 million visitors, over 10 million of them from abroad; in the five years to 2025 it added 231 items and staged 62 thematic and travelling exhibitions (Nhân Dân, 2025).
- Departments for collections, inventory and conservation, display, education, professional guidance to other Ho Chi Minh museums and sites, and communication (Decision 2372).
- Deputy director: Phạm Thị Thanh Mai (Nhân Dân, 2025).

FUNDING
Ho Chi Minh Museum: public: the central state, through the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism
- Operator: a public service unit directly under the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, with its own seal and accounts; Minister Nguyễn Văn Hùng set its functions in Decision 2372/QĐ-BVHTTDL of 4 July 2025, replacing a 2018 decision (Decision 2372).
- Mandate: the decision charges it with propaganda and education on Ho Chi Minh's life, thought and revolutionary ethics, and with publishing documents on him "according to the regulations of the Party and the State" (Decision 2372; translation by the atlas).
- Income of its own: the decision allows it to collect fees and charges, run cultural, entertainment and service activities, and manage "other sources of revenue" under the law (Decision 2372; translation by the atlas).

Not related: the Ho Chi Minh Museum, Ho Chi Minh City Branch, at Nhà Rồng Wharf. It is one of nine museums under Ho Chi Minh City's Department of Culture and Sports (Vietbao, 2026), and its 2026 renovation was started by the city government with Sun Group as part of a riverside park (Báo Văn Hóa). It is a separate city museum.

BOTTOM LINE
State money alone: no funding concern. What matters is the museum's political mandate, not its funders.
