REDCAT (ROY AND EDNA DISNEY/CALARTS THEATER): Los Angeles, USA
ID: redcat-los-angeles
Website: https://www.redcat.org

OVERVIEW
- Downtown Los Angeles contemporary arts centre of CalArts (California Institute of the Arts) at 631 W 2nd Street, in the Walt Disney Concert Hall complex; it presents performances, exhibitions, screenings and literary events (REDCAT website).
- Opened in November 2003 as an extension of CalArts into downtown Los Angeles (Wikipedia).
- Named for Roy and Edna Disney: Patty and Roy E. Disney matched the Walt Disney Company's gift and named the venue after his parents (Wikipedia).
- Frank Gehry, who also designed Walt Disney Concert Hall, designed the building; the entrance has a stainless steel and neon marquee by Gehry (REDCAT website, Wikipedia).
- On site: a 250-seat flexible black-box theatre, a 3,000-square-foot exhibition space and the Olga Garay-English and Dr. Kerry English REDCAT Lounge (REDCAT website).
- Programmes include the NOW Festival, which takes applications from artists (REDCAT website).
- João Ribas was hired in 2019 as the first CalArts Vice President for Cultural Partnerships and Executive Director of REDCAT; Mark Murphy was the first executive director of REDCAT (Wikipedia).
- A REDCAT Council, chaired by Diane Levine, is listed on the site (REDCAT website).

FUNDING
REDCAT: university-run, with foundation and arts-agency grants
- CalArts operates REDCAT as its downtown arts centre (REDCAT website).
- Founding support: Wikipedia states that the venue was developed with the financial support of the Walt Disney Company and the County of Los Angeles, and that Patty and Roy E. Disney personally matched the Disney Company's gift.
- National Endowment for the Arts: a $45,000 grant for "adventurous programming" in December 2015 (Time Out, Seth Kelley, 9 December 2015).
- Andy Warhol Foundation: a Multi-year Program Support grant of $80,000 in the Fall 2023 cycle (Warhol Foundation grant archive).
- Mike Kelley Foundation for the Arts: a $50,000 Artist Project Grant in 2020 for Nao Bustamante's The Wooden People (CalArts blog, 23 April 2020), and a $26,000 organisational support grant in 2024 (Mike Kelley Foundation).

BOTTOM LINE
REDCAT is run by CalArts and supported by named arts funders: the National Endowment for the Arts, the Andy Warhol Foundation and the Mike Kelley Foundation. Wikipedia states that the venue, which opened in 2003, was developed with support from the Walt Disney Company and Los Angeles County, and that Patty and Roy E. Disney matched the Disney Company's gift.
