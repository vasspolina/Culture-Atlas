HANGAR, CENTRO DE INVESTIGAÇÃO ARTÍSTICA: Lisbon, Portugal
ID: hangar-lisboa
Website: https://hangar.com.pt

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run centre for artistic research, exhibitions, residencies and studios at Rua Damasceno Monteiro 12 in the Graça neighbourhood of Lisbon (Hangar website, e-flux directory).
- A project of XEREM, a non-profit cultural association; Mónica de Miranda says XEREM was founded in 2010. FIELD says Hangar was created in 2015, after XEREM's residencies and exchanges within the Triangle Network created a need for a permanent space (FIELD, Carlos Garrido Castellano, issue 6, winter 2017).
- FIELD describes Mónica de Miranda as a founder and Bruno Leitão as Hangar's curatorial director and a board member.
- The building has three storeys, with a blackboard at the entrance announcing the programme (FIELD).
- On site: an exhibition hall, studios including a co-work space, a photographic lab and a sound studio, and creative accommodation for visiting artists (Hangar website).
- Residency programmes include International Residencies, 180º Artists to the South, Triangle Network, Residencies in Transit and Permanent Residencies (Hangar website). The 180º programme exchanges artists from Angola, Cape Verde, Mozambique and São Tomé and Príncipe (Gulbenkian, 1 February 2017).
- The e-flux directory describes it as an independent space managed by artists and curators, with projects focused on Lisbon.
- Visiting: gallery open Wednesday to Saturday 15:00 to 19:00 or by appointment, free entry; the office is open Monday to Friday 11:00 to 19:00 (Hangar website).

FUNDING
Hangar: small non-profit project: public and private grants, partnerships and studio rents
- The Hangar website says "A contribuição de apoios privados e públicos é fundamental para a continuidade do projecto" (translated: the contribution of private and public support is essential to the continuity of the project).
- Hangar's partners page says "O HANGAR é lançado com o apoio financeiro do programa BipZip Parcerias Locais da Câmara Municipal de Lisboa" (translated: Hangar is launched with the financial support of the BipZip Local Partnerships programme of the Lisbon City Council). FIELD (2017) says a local exchange programme "supported by the Lisbon City Council" provided the initial input.
- The Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation supported the 180º Artists to the South residencies held at Hangar for the rest of 2017 (Gulbenkian announcement, 1 February 2017).
- Renting studios to guest and visiting artists also contributes to funding (FIELD).
- Project partners listed by Hangar include the architecture firm Artéria, the publisher Orfeu Negro, the Centro de Estudos Comparatistas, Gasworks in London and Beyond Entropy; it belongs to the Triangle Network and the Anna Lindh Foundation network.

BOTTOM LINE
Hangar is a small artist-run project of a non-profit association, funded by Lisbon City Council money at its launch, Gulbenkian Foundation support for its 2017 residencies, other private and public support, and studio rents. The documented funders are a city council and a charitable foundation.
