CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Revista Arta: Ionuț Cioană, "The Museum of Cold Art" (2018)
URL: https://revistaarta.ro/en/column/the-museum-of-cold-art/
- Objects to works by the curator's wife in the permanent collection: "I think it was wrong to include his wife's works in the permanent collection."
- Questions paintings attributed to Andrei Cădere that are shown without public documentation, calling the attribution "a huge red flag".
- Says the display "lacks a coherent vision" and "gives off a '70s or '80s Union of Artists art show impression".
- Calls Erwin Kessler's reason for leaving Geta Brătescu out of the collection, her affiliation with the communist regime, "a blatant exaggeration" and "a petty remark", since many artists in the collection were also members of the Artists' Union.
