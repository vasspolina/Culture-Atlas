Red flags: 2025 dismissal of the director after misconduct reports
- The supervisory board dismissed Kris Callens, director since 2015, who left on 1 February 2025, officially because of a reorganisation; in the week before 16 March 2025 it emerged that several women had complained about his boundary-crossing behaviour (NOS with Omrop Fryslân, 16 March 2025).
- Supervisory board chair Hayo Apotheker said the first report reached the board in December 2024 and the confidential counsellor had received nine reports, not all of them about sexual misconduct; no one filed a formal complaint. He said financial problems and a coming reorganisation already called for new leadership, and the reports removed any remaining doubts (NOS, 16 March 2025).
- The Leeuwarder Courant spoke with more than 70 current and former employees, witnesses and others in the cultural sector who had dealt with Callens (NOS, 16 March 2025).
- The union Kunstenbond asked for an external investigation into what happened at the museum while Callens was director; the supervisory board did not want one (NOS, 16 March 2025).
- On 21 March 2025 the museum said "We zijn geschrokken van hun verhalen" ("We were shocked by their stories", translated) and "Achteraf gezien was meer openheid vanaf het begin de betere keuze geweest" ("In hindsight, more openness from the start would have been the better choice", translated), and said it was considering an independent investigation (VRT NWS, 21 March 2025).
