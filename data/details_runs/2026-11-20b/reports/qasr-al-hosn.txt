QASR AL HOSN: Abu Dhabi, UAE
ID: qasr-al-hosn
Website: https://qasralhosn.ae/en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Fort and palace complex in central Abu Dhabi, between Zayed the First Street, Khaled Bin Waleed Street, Hamdan Bin Mohammed Street and Sheikh Rashid Bin Saeed Street, near the Etisalat headquarters and the World Trade Centre (museum website).
- The coral-and-sea-stone Inner Fort was built around 1795 as a watchtower; Sheikh Shakhbut bin Sultan built the Outer Palace in 1939 as his majlis and the Al Nahyan family's winter home, and Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan added the National Consultative Council building between 1968 and 1970 (The National, 25 October 2018).
- The site closed for renovation in 2008, opened for a few weeks a year during the Qasr Al Hosn Festival from 2013 and reopened permanently on 7 December 2018 as a museum (The National, 25 October 2018).
- BDP restored the fort over ten years; the restored pavilions and tower house show artefacts dating from 6,000 BC (BDP).
- The 140,000 square metre Al Hosn Master Plan, a project shown by the architecture firm Lemay, turned the grounds into a park shared with the Cultural Foundation, with a desert landscape around the fort and the Al Musalla prayer hall (Lemay).
- In the 2000s the authorities decided to remove the neighbouring Cultural Foundation; residents protested, stopped the demolition and set off the preservation scheme for the whole site, including the fort's renovation (Yasser Elsheshtawy, Arab Gulf States Institute in Washington).
- Also on site: the House of Artisans, with Al Sadu weaving, Khoos palm-leaf weaving and Talli embroidery, and Bait Al Gahwa, a demonstration of Arabic coffee making (Visit Abu Dhabi; The National).
- Open Saturday to Thursday 9:00 to 20:00 and Friday 14:00 to 20:00. The fort needs a ticket; the House of Artisans, the Cultural Foundation and the grounds are free, and school groups enter free (museum website).

FUNDING
Qasr Al Hosn: state-run: Abu Dhabi's Department of Culture and Tourism
- The Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi led the reopening, which came with a full-scale redevelopment of the city block around the fort (The National, 25 October 2018).
- Tickets for the fort and the other Al Hosn venues are sold through tickets.abudhabiculture.ae (museum website).
- The Department and the former Abu Dhabi Tourism and Culture Authority issued the renderings and conservation photographs of the restoration (The National, 25 October 2018).

Red flags: run by Abu Dhabi's Department of Culture and Tourism, a Gulf state body
- The emirate's government department runs the site; its chairman, Mohamed Khalifa Al Mubarak, announced the 2018 reopening (The National, 25 October 2018).

BOTTOM LINE
The only money on record is the Abu Dhabi government's, through its culture department, which makes this a Gulf state body site. Watch for any published restoration cost or budget.
