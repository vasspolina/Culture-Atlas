ID: tower-of-david-museum

Red flags: 2023 move of a conference on attacks on Christians after pressure from the municipality
- A conference on attacks on Christians in Jerusalem was scheduled for Friday 16 June 2023 in the hall of the Tower of David Museum; citing Haaretz, Al-Quds reported that it was moved to another place in the Old City because of pressure from the Jerusalem municipality on the museum's management (Al-Quds, 15 June 2023). All Israel News reported that after complaints from several municipal officials the conference moved to an online format (All Israel News, 19 June 2023).
- Unverified: several sources told Haaretz that the office of Mayor Moshe Lion threatened to dismiss the museum's executive director if the conference went ahead there, from Al-Quds and All Israel News citing Haaretz; the mayor's office denied the report and said there had been no threats (Al-Quds, 15 June 2023; All Israel News, 19 June 2023).

Red flags: 2023 call by Jerusalem's Chief Sephardi Rabbi to boycott the museum
- Jerusalem's Chief Sephardi Rabbi Shlomo Amar issued a letter against the conference, saying "The Tower of David Museum is inviting the public with seeming innocence to presentations and discussions", condemned the museum for opening on Shabbat and called on Jews to boycott it (All Israel News, 19 June 2023).
