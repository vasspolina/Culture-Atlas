ARTSPACE AOTEAROA: Auckland, New Zealand
ID: artspace-aotearoa-auckland
Website: https://artspace-aotearoa.nz/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit public contemporary art gallery at 292 Karangahape Road, on the corner of East Street, in Newton, Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland.
- Founded in 1987 by artists and arts workers, with Mary-Louise Browne as first director; it was earlier called Artspace NZ.
- A charitable trust (Artspace Aotearoa Trust, charity CC20321): the Tiaki Board of Trustees, chaired by Philip M. Clarke, appoints the Kaitohu Director, Ruth Buchanan for 2022 to 2027.
- Commissions conceptual work by emerging artists and runs a public reading room and education programmes; past exhibitors include Billy Apple, Yuki Kihara, Michael Parekowhai and Francis Upritchard (Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Friday 10am to 6pm and Saturday 11am to 4pm, closed Sunday and Monday; free entry, low-sensory hours on Tuesdays 10am to 12pm and late opening on Thursdays 6pm to 8pm.

FUNDING
Artspace Aotearoa: mainly public: Creative New Zealand and Auckland Council
- Creative New Zealand: NZ$637,007 a year for 2027, 2028 and 2029 from its General Arts Fund, visual arts (Creative New Zealand).
- Government grants made up 73.0 percent of revenue and donations 9.7 percent in the latest return; FY2025 revenue was NZ$901.7K, expenses NZ$970.1K and net assets NZ$219.3K (Charities Register figures via CharityData).
- Auckland Council is a lead partner; no reporting found of the amount.
- Foundation North, a strategic partner, gave NZ$70,000 in FY2019 and FY2020, NZ$50,000 in FY2021, NZ$26,000 in FY2025 and NZ$25,000 in FY2026 (CharityData).
- Other strategic partners: The Chartwell Trust, Goethe-Institut New Zealand and Wesfarmers; no reporting found of what Wesfarmers pays or for what.
- Wesfarmers' chemicals, energy and fertilisers division includes Kleenheat, which retails natural gas in Western Australia, and ammonia, ammonium nitrate and sodium cyanide plants (Wesfarmers).
- Donor circles pledge from NZ$200 to NZ$5,000+ a year; Enduring Circle members include the Gow Family Trust, The Hynds Foundation and the artist Katharina Grosse (stated by the space).
- A NZ$4,000 grant from Pub Charity Limited, a poker machine-funded charitable trust (Otago Daily Times), is listed without a year (CharityData).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Artspace is mainly public and modest, and the space names its partners and donors. The weak point is Wesfarmers as a strategic partner, whose chemicals, energy and fertilisers arm retails natural gas, with no published amount. Worth watching: what the Wesfarmers deal pays for and the size of the Auckland Council grant.
