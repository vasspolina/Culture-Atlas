CRAC OCCITANIE: Sète, France
ID: crac-occitanie-sète
Website: https://crac.laregion.fr

OVERVIEW
- Regional contemporary art centre on the Canal Royal in Sète, facing the port and the Mediterranean (Crac Occitanie).
- Opened in 1997, managed by the Occitanie region; Noëlle Tissier directed it from 1997 to 2017 and Marie Cozette has directed it since 2018 (Crac Occitanie). French Wikipedia says Cozette was appointed by Carole Delga, president of the Regional Council.
- Building: a former refrigerated warehouse for fish preservation, renovated in 1997 by the architect Lorenzo Piqueras, with about 1,200 square metres of exhibition space over two levels (Crac Occitanie). French Wikipedia says the conversion ran from 1995 to 1997.
- It grew out of collaboration between the Sète fine arts school, the villa Saint-Clair artist residency and the espace Paul Boyé (French Wikipedia).
- Programme: production facilities and space for large-scale projects, temporary exhibitions, catalogues and artist books, and an educational programme of guided tours, workshops, talks, concerts and performances; it has shown works by over 600 French and international artists since 1997 (Crac Occitanie).
- Visiting: free entry, open daily 12:30 pm to 7 pm (2 pm to 7 pm at weekends), closed Tuesday (Crac Occitanie).

FUNDING
Crac Occitanie: managed by the Région Occitanie, with State support
- The Région Occitanie / Pyrénées-Méditerranée manages the centre (Crac Occitanie).
- The centre is under agreement with the State and receives support from the Ministry of Culture, with aid from the Préfecture de la région Occitanie and the DRAC, Direction régionale des affaires culturelles (Crac Occitanie partners page, translated).
- For its educational actions the centre names the Mairie de Sète, Sète Agglopôle Méditerranée and the Conseil départemental de l'Hérault as partners (Crac Occitanie partners page).
- Works: French Wikipedia says the State and the Region financed the conversion works equally, and that the centre has been under an agreement with the Ministry of Culture since its creation.

BOTTOM LINE
Crac Occitanie is managed by the Région Occitanie. Its partners page says it is under agreement with the State and supported by the Ministry of Culture, with aid from the DRAC. French Wikipedia says the State and the Region paid equally for the 1995 to 1997 conversion works.
