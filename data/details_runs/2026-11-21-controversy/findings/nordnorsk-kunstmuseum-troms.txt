WIDER CONTEXT
- At a board meeting on 27 March 2020, at which a letter from the staff about the matter was put forward, the board voted four to one to end the director's fixed-term contract. Three departing board and deputy members wrote on 31 March 2020 that they were critical of both the process and the decision, and the supervisor received notices of no confidence in the board chair and the board (Stiftelsestilsynet, supervisory report, 5 March 2021).
- The Norwegian foundation supervisor, Stiftelsestilsynet, opened an inspection on 10 June 2020 after media coverage and inquiries. It concluded that the board meeting of 27 March 2020 and the process of replacing the director "burde vært bedre planlagt" ("should have been better planned", translated), and found that most board minutes were not signed by all participants, notices were dated the day of the meeting and case papers were sent out just before meetings. The board told the supervisor that ending the contract was necessary and urgent, and later confirmed its first decision (Stiftelsestilsynet, 5 March 2021).
