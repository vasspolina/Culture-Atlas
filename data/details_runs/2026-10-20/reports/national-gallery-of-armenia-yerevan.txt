NATIONAL GALLERY OF ARMENIA: Yerevan, Armenia
ID: national-gallery-of-armenia-yerevan
Website: https://www.gallery.am

OVERVIEW
- National art museum on Republic Square in Yerevan, in the building it shares with the History Museum of Armenia (Wikipedia, Republic Square article).
- Founded in 1921 under a decree of the Armenian Soviet Socialist Republic as the artistic section of the state museum. Its core came from the collection of the Armenian Cultural Center (the former Lazarian Seminary in Moscow) and gifts from Armenian artists; by 1925 about 400 works hung in six halls (Wikipedia).
- It became a separate art museum in 1935, was renamed the State Picture Gallery of Armenia in 1947 and became the National Gallery of Armenia in 1991 (Wikipedia).
- The Museums Building was designed by Mark Grigorian and Eduard Sarapian; work began in the 1950s and the National Gallery building was completed in 1977 (Wikipedia, Republic Square article).
- Collection of about 42,000 works and 56 halls, plus an archive of about 200,000 items (Wikipedia). The Ara Sargsyan and Hakob Kojoyan House Museum in central Yerevan is a branch of the gallery (Armenpress, 25 April 2019).
- Directors: Marina Hakobyan from 2020, Arman Tsaturyan 2015 to 2020, Pharaon Mirzoyan 2002 to 2015 (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
National Gallery of Armenia: run by the Armenian government, with the Ministry of Culture involved in staffing and a government programme for a new building
- Hetq (3 February 2011) quotes a Ministry of Culture source who says staff changes at the gallery followed a government directive.
- In January 2017 then-director Arman Tsaturyan said the building "does not comply with the international standards at all" and that the government had started a programme to build a new building; he said that if the government cannot afford it, measures to preserve the collection are still needed (Panorama.am, 17 January 2017).
- In April 2019 Anna Sargsyan, director of the House Museum branch, said the building needed renovation and that its condition made preserving the exhibits difficult (Armenpress, 25 April 2019).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The coverage is local news, not the art press, and concerns the state of the building and staffing.
1. Panorama.am: "Collection of National Gallery of Armenia in poor conditions" (17 January 2017, no author named)
URL: https://panorama.am/en/news/2017/01/17/Collection-National-Gallery-of-Armenia-poor-condition/1710089
- Director Arman Tsaturyan says heating and moisture problems persist, the building is cold in winter and very hot in summer, and the windows are old.
- A criticism of the building made by the gallery's own director.
2. Hetq: "Staff Changes at National Picture Gallery" (3 February 2011, no author named)
URL: https://hetq.am/en/article/50022
- Reports, citing Hetq sources, that Deputy Director Hasmik Harutyunyan was dismissed and Armen Gasparyan, head of the painting department, was to work as a senior researcher in that department; Lilit Sargsyan, who had worked in the Ministry of Culture's modern art department, was to take over the painting post.
- A Ministry of Culture source tells Hetq the changes followed a government directive aimed at bringing in "some younger people working in management positions".
- Hetq notes that both Gasparyan and Harutyunyan had given it interviews for a series of articles on the state of Armenia's museums.

BOTTOM LINE
The documented ties are to the Armenian government: a Ministry of Culture source tied 2011 staff changes to a government directive, and in 2017 its then director said the government had started a programme to build a new building.
