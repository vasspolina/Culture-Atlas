ARTISTS SPACE: New York, USA
ID: artists-space
Website: https://artistsspace.org (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit exhibition space at 11 Cortlandt Alley in Tribeca, Lower Manhattan, its sixth downtown home since 1972 (Artists Space).
- Founded in 1972 by arts administrator Trudie Grace and critic Irving Sandler as a pilot project of the New York State Council on the Arts (NYSCA), to show emerging and unaffiliated artists; it opened at 155 Wooster Street (Artists Space, Wikipedia).
- It moved to 105 Hudson Street (1977), 223 West Broadway (1984), 38 Greene Street (1993) and then to Cortlandt Alley, where board member Martin Cox of Bade Stageberg Cox renovated almost 8,000 square feet on two floors (Artists Space).
- Its history includes Douglas Crimp's "Pictures" (1977), Adrian Piper's "It's Just Art" (1981) and Nan Goldin's "Witnesses: Against Our Vanishing" (1989); in 1979 a show by Donald Newman with a racial slur in its title drew protests from the Black Emergency Cultural Coalition (Artists Space, Wikipedia).
- Programmes: exhibitions, film, performance, talks and an education programme; Jay Sanders has been executive director and chief curator since 2017 (Wikipedia).
- Open Wednesday to Saturday, 12:00 to 18:00; the site links an accessibility page.

FUNDING
Artists Space: privately funded: foundations, art-world patrons and galleries, with city and state grants
- Fiscal year to June 2025: revenue $3,228,059, of which $3,135,334 contributions; expenses $2,598,636; net assets $4,703,592; Sanders was paid $175,000 (ProPublica, Form 990).
- Core contributors, undated: Lambent Foundation Fund of Tides Foundation, the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation, NYC Department of Cultural Affairs with the City Council, Horace W. Goldsmith Foundation, NYSCA, A.G. Foundation, I.A. O'Shaughnessy Foundation, Keith Haring Foundation, Helen Frankenthaler Foundation, Hearthland Foundation, Cowles Charitable Trust, Milton and Sally Avery Foundation (Artists Space).
- Cortlandt Alley building founders at the top "Visionary" level: Eleanor Cayre, Barbara Gladstone, Eleanor Heyman Propp and Allan Schwartzman; the Stavros Niarchos Foundation gave at "Leader" level (Artists Space).
- Friends memberships run from $2,500 to $20,000; members include Gladstone Gallery, Hauser & Wirth, David Zwirner, Lisson Gallery, Matthew Marks and Katherine Farley and Jerry Speyer; the Artists Fund aims to raise $2.5 million (Artists Space).
- The board is led by president Steven Schindler, treasurer Andy Stillpass and secretary Liam Gillick, with Cindy Sherman as honorary lifetime member (Artists Space).

Red flags: oil family money through the I.A. O'Shaughnessy Foundation
- The foundation is a current core contributor (Artists Space, checked October 2026).
- Oilman I.A. O'Shaughnessy founded Lario Oil and Gas in 1927 and created the foundation in 1941; his five children later sat on its board (DeSmog, an advocacy source).
- His grandson Gerald O'Shaughnessy, listed as a foundation board member in a Bloomberg profile archived in 2019, worked at Lario from 1976 and co-founded Latin American oil and gas producer GeoPark, its executive chairman since 2006 (DeSmog).
- No reporting found of the size of the foundation's gifts or of any protest about them.

BOTTOM LINE
Artists Space runs on foundation grants, public arts money and gifts from collectors and commercial galleries. The one flagged link is an oil family's foundation among a dozen core contributors, with no amount published. Worth watching: dependence on blue-chip galleries and collectors, and the O'Shaughnessy money.
