SUZHOU MUSEUM: Suzhou, China
ID: suzhou-museum
Website: https://www.szmuseum.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Suzhou Museum (苏州博物馆) describes itself as a local comprehensive museum and the centre for collecting, protecting, studying and showing Suzhou's cultural relics (museum website, our translation).
- It was founded on New Year's Day 1960 in the former residence of the Taiping general Li Xiucheng, the Zhong Wang Fu (忠王府) (Chinese Wikipedia; English Wikipedia).
- I.M. Pei took on the design of a new main building on 30 April 2002, at the age of 85; it stands west of the Humble Administrator's Garden at 204 Dongbei Street, has 19,000 square metres of floor space, and opened in 2006 (museum website).
- A second site, the Suzhou Museum West at 399 Changjiang Road in the Suzhou New District, designed by gmp, began trial opening on 25 September 2021, with 48,365 square metres of floor space and 13,391 square metres of galleries (museum website; ArchDaily).
- The collection has more than 40,000 objects, mostly finds from Suzhou, literati painting and calligraphy, and folk crafts of the Jiangnan region (Chinese Wikipedia).
- The director is Xie Xiaoting (谢晓婷). The West site runs a "World Civilisation" exhibition series with the British Museum; its 2024 to 2025 Ashurbanipal show won a national award, and the main building marks its 20th year in 2026 (People's Daily Overseas Edition, 18 May 2026).
- Opening on 25 November 2026: a show of relics transferred from the Suzhou Cultural Relics Store (museum website).
- Visiting: the main building is free and open 9am to 5pm, last entry 4pm, Tuesday to Sunday, with real-name timed booking up to seven days ahead; the West site needs no booking (museum website; China for Travelers).

FUNDING
Suzhou Museum: public: buildings commissioned by Suzhou city bodies, with free admission
- The Pei building had a total investment of 339 million yuan; with the Zhong Wang Fu, the main site has 26,500 square metres of floor space (museum website).
- The client for the West site was the Suzhou City Planning Bureau with the High-Tech Zone bureau (ArchDaily, gmp project data).
- Admission is free at both sites (museum website; Chinese Wikipedia).
- The British Museum is an exhibition partner for the West site's international gallery, not a funder (People's Daily Overseas Edition).

BOTTOM LINE
A public museum whose two buildings were commissioned by Suzhou city bodies, with free entry and no private or corporate funder named. Nothing in its money raises a flag.
