PALLAS PROJECTS/STUDIOS: Dublin, Ireland
ID: pallas-projects-dublin
Website: https://pallasprojects.org

OVERVIEW
- Not-for-profit artist-run gallery and studios at 115-116 The Coombe, The Liberties, Dublin 8 (Pallas website).
- Set up in 1996 by artists Mark Cullen and Brian Duggan; Gavin Murphy joined in 2006 (Pallas website). Wikipedia names Cullen and Murphy as directors.
- Named for the previous occupier of its first building, a former knitwear factory on Foley Street, Dublin 1 (Pallas website).
- Moved between premises: Foley Street 1996 to 2008, Sean Tracey House on Buckingham Street 2002 to 2007 (Pallas Heights), Grangegorman Road Upper 2007 to 2011, Lower Dominick Street 2011 to 2012, and The Coombe from 2012. Over 30 years it has occupied as many as 16 premises and has yet to secure a permanent home (Pallas website).
- The Coombe gallery is in an old school building at the end of an alley (Wikipedia).
- Other sites: a studio building at The Digital Hub and Pallas Open Projects at New Market Yards, Dublin 8, listed for 2026 to 2040 (Pallas website). On 22 September 2022 The Digital Hub announced 25 large single and double new studios there for Pallas.
- Programmes: annual Artist-Initiated Projects chosen by open call, the Periodical Review thematic survey show, and the publication "Artist-Run Europe" (2016, second edition 2023) (Pallas website; Wikipedia).
- Gallery open 12 to 6pm, Wednesday to Saturday, during exhibitions (Pallas website).

FUNDING
Pallas Projects/Studios: the Arts Council of Ireland is the key funder, plus grants and capital funding
- The Arts Council is listed as the key funder on the Pallas website. The Arts Council added 70,000 euros in 2022 to create the Digital Hub studios (The Digital Hub, 2022), and funds the Artist-Initiated Projects programme (Pallas website, 11 October 2024).
- Grant funding from Culture Ireland and Community Foundation Ireland (Pallas website).
- Capital funding: the Pallas website states "Part-funded by the Department of Culture, Communications and Sport under Project Ireland 2040" and lists Dublin City Council under capital funding.
- Partners listed by Pallas: IADT Dun Laoghaire, Dublin Modular, Paper Visual Art Journal, Beta Festival, Outlandish Theatre Platform, Set Margins (Netherlands), HDLU (Croatia), the Mercy Centre and The Digital Hub.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Pallas is the Arts Council, the Department of Culture, Communications and Sport, Dublin City Council, Culture Ireland and Community Foundation Ireland.
