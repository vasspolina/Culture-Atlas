NATIONAL MUSEUM OF MODERN AND CONTEMPORARY ART (MMCA): Seoul, South Korea
ID: national-museum-of-modern-and-contemporary-art-mmca
Website: https://www.mmca.go.kr

OVERVIEW
- South Korea's national museum of modern and contemporary Korean art, with four sites: Gwacheon, Deoksugung, Seoul (next to Gyeongbokgung) and Cheongju (a storage and conservation centre) (Wikipedia).
- Established in 1969; it first opened in Gyeongbokgung on 20 October 1969, moved to Deoksugung in 1973 and to Gwacheon in 1986. The Seoul site opened on 13 November 2013 (Wikipedia).
- It is a state museum under the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, which removed one director in 2014 and accepted another's resignation in 2023 (Korea JoongAng Daily; ArtReview).

FUNDING
National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (MMCA): state museum under the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, with corporate series sponsors
- Hyundai Motor sponsored one solo exhibition of a mid-career Korean artist each year from 2014: 12 billion won over 10 years, announced in November 2013 and the largest sponsorship in the museum's history; the tenth and final show ran through 25 February 2024 (Hankyung Magazine, Lee Seung-ryul, 18 September 2023, translated).
- LG Electronics is title sponsor of the MMCA x LG OLED Series, a multi-year partnership whose first exhibition, TZUSOO, ran from 1 August 2025 to 1 February 2026 (LG newsroom, 31 July 2025).

Red flags: protest against the director's appointment (2015)
- In November 2015 the Korean campaign group petition4art launched a petition against the candidacy of Bartomeu Marí as MMCA director over his handling of an exhibition at Macba in Barcelona. More than 830 Korean artists, curators and cultural figures signed it (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 8 December 2015).
- A second petition, launched on 3 December 2015 and signed by 450 people by the article's date, was aimed at the Ministry of Culture; a petition4art spokesperson said the ministry must not attempt any kind of censorship and asked the new director for a public declaration against censorship. Marí was appointed in early December 2015 (The Art Newspaper).

Red flags: state censorship at the opening show (2013)
- Unverified: six politically awkward works were dropped from the opening show Zeitgeist Korea less than a week before opening, and the Blue House had a hand in the final selection, from the Korea Times as relayed by Philip Gowman on the blog London Korean Links (28 November 2013); the post names no individual source.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism concerns appointments, nepotism and political pressure on the state museum.
1. The Art Newspaper: Gareth Harris, "Korean artists protest appointment of former Barcelona museum director due to censorship concerns" (8 December 2015)
URL: http://www.theartnewspaper.com/news/korean-artists-protest-appointment-of-former-barcelona-museum-director-due-to-censorship-concerns
- Reports two petitions started by petition4art against Marí, who cancelled the Macba exhibition The Beast and the Sovereign (including a work by Ines Doujak) on the eve of its launch, reopened it on 21 March 2015 and resigned that month.
- Marí says he opposes "all sorts of censorship" and defends his Macba decision.
2. ArtReview: "Director of South Korea's MMCA museums steps down amid alleged political pressure" (21 April 2023)
URL: https://artreview.com/director-of-south-koreas-mmca-museums-steps-down-amid-alleged-political-pressure
- Youn Bum-mo, director from 2019 and reappointed in 2022, resigned; Korean media speculated about political pressure after the March 2022 presidential election, and the article says the exact cause is unclear.
- A ministry audit listed 16 allegations of illegal or inappropriate practices, including questionable auction acquisitions and failure to address workplace bullying; critics alleged nepotism in his reappointment.
3. Korea JoongAng Daily: Moon So-young, "MMCA chief dismissed in recruitment controversy" (22 October 2014)
URL: https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/lifestyle/mmca-chief-dismissed-in-recruitment-controversy/10799417
- The ministry dismissed director Chung Hyung-min after the Board of Audit and Inspection found evidence that she intervened in an October 2013 curator recruitment; the board asked prosecutors to investigate her for "abuse of authority".

WIDER CONTEXT
- Wikipedia (Hyundai Alabama child labor allegations) says Reuters reported in July 2022 that SMART, a Hyundai subsidiary in Luverne, Alabama, hired minors as young as 12 at a metal stamping plant. It says the US Department of Labor fined the supplier SL Alabama $30,076 on 29 September 2022, that neither labor department accused Hyundai or Kia of wrongdoing, and that on 24 February 2023 Hyundai's CEO Jae Hoon Chang said Hyundai would divest its controlling stake in SMART.
- Connection to the museum: Hyundai Motor sponsored the Hyundai Motor Series from 2014 to February 2024; the report concerns its subsidiary SMART.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution under the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, with a 12 billion won, ten-year Hyundai Motor sponsorship that ended in February 2024 and LG Electronics as title sponsor of a media art series from 2025. The weak points are the 2015 petitions against the director's appointment, the 2014 dismissal and 2023 resignation of directors, and the Unverified 2013 report on the opening show.
