ID: the-cleveland-orchestra

WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 2018 the orchestra suspended concertmaster William Preucil after a Washington Post report carried allegations against him, and hired the law firm Debevoise & Plimpton to investigate (NPR, 24 October 2018).
- On 24 October 2018 the orchestra fired Preucil and principal trombonist Massimo La Rosa; it said investigators had found both "engaged in sexual misconduct and sexually harassing behavior with multiple female students and colleagues over a period of years while employed by the orchestra" (NPR).
- The investigators' report said members of the board's executive committee had been approached by players concerned about allegations against Preucil published in Cleveland Scene in 2007, and that the orchestra did not follow up (NPR).
