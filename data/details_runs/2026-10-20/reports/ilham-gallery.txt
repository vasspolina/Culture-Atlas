ILHAM GALLERY: Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
ID: ilham-gallery
Website: https://www.ilhamgallery.com

OVERVIEW
- Public art gallery for modern and contemporary art at Levels 3 and 5, Ilham Tower, No 8 Jalan Binjai, Kuala Lumpur (Art Rabbit; ILHAM Gallery website).
- Founded in 2015. IB Tower Sdn Bhd ran the gallery and gift shop from 2015, and the non-profit Yayasan ILHAM, established on 22 October 2018, took over on 1 January 2019 (stated by the space, About page).
- Gallery director Rahel Joseph describes it as a "privately-funded public art gallery" opened to fill an institutional gap in Kuala Lumpur (Art & Market, Rahel Joseph, "10 Years of ILHAM Gallery", 23 October 2025).
- Building: Ilham Tower, completed in 2015 and designed by Foster and Partners; the developer was IB Tower Sdn Bhd (Wikipedia).
- Programme: rotating exhibitions of borrowed works with partners including Para Site, MAIIAM, National Gallery Singapore and Singapore Art Museum; a triennial, the ILHAM Art Show, launched in 2021; and a mobile gallery truck, Skola Gambar ILHAM, launched in July 2025 (Art & Market). In 2026 it presents "Robert Rauschenberg and Asia", organised by M+ with ILHAM Gallery.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday 11am to 7pm, Sunday 11am to 5pm, closed Monday and selected public holidays. Admission is free (ILHAM Gallery website).

FUNDING
ILHAM Gallery: privately funded, by the space's own description: run by the non-profit Yayasan ILHAM
- The gallery calls itself privately funded (Art & Market, written by its director).
- From 2015 to 2018 IB Tower Sdn Bhd managed the gallery; Yayasan ILHAM has run it since 1 January 2019 (stated by the space).
- Yayasan ILHAM states its purposes as philanthropy and charity, especially promoting arts, culture and education in Malaysia.
- Admission is free.

WIDER CONTEXT
- December 2023: the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) seized Ilham Tower under a notice dated 18 December 2023, addressed to the former finance minister Daim Zainuddin as owner. The Star reported that the tower is owned by Daim's family (The Star, Tarrence Tan, "Daim's Ilham Tower seized by MACC", 21 December 2023).
- The Edge Malaysia Weekly tied the seizure to an MACC investigation, begun in May 2023, of the Renong Bhd and United Engineers Malaysia Bhd deal of the late 1990s that cost RM2.3 billion ("MACC moves in on Daim", issue of 25 to 31 December 2023).
- Wikipedia names the owner as Ilham Tower Sdn Bhd, a majority-owned subsidiary of Ilham Baru Sdn Bhd, controlled by Daim's wife Na'imah Abdul Khalid and their two sons.
- June 2025: the MACC seized the tower a second time under the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2001 (The Star, Benjamin Lee, "MACC seizes Ilham Tower again", 5 June 2025). The Vibes reported on 25 June 2025 that the MACC planned to file for forfeiture that day at the Kuala Lumpur High Court, and valued the tower at RM1.5 billion to RM2 billion ("MACC to file for Ilham Tower forfeiture today").
- Connection to the gallery: the gallery's address is Levels 3 and 5 of Ilham Tower (Art Rabbit), and the company that ran the gallery from 2015 to 2018, IB Tower Sdn Bhd, is listed by Wikipedia as the tower's developer.

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery states that it is privately funded and run by the non-profit Yayasan ILHAM. It sits in a tower owned by the family of Daim Zainuddin, which Malaysian anti-corruption authorities seized in December 2023 and again in June 2025, when they moved to forfeit it.
