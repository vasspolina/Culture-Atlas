NESR ART FOUNDATION: Luanda, Angola
ID: nesr-art-foundation
Website: https://nesrartfoundation.com (checked 2026-10-25; loads; the address www.nesrartfoundation.org did not resolve)

OVERVIEW
- Independent art foundation in Luanda that runs an artist residency, a collection with an active loan programme, and educational projects, based in a creative hub with studios in Boavista (its site).
- Founded in 2021 by Hiba and Wissam Nesr (its site; The Art Newspaper, 29 October 2021).
- The Art Newspaper describes the founders as members of the Lebanese-Belgian family behind the Webcor Group, and Wissam Nesr as Webcor's chief executive.
- Residency: when it opened in 2021 it hosted eight artists a year in four sessions (The Art Newspaper). The site now describes six artists a year in three 10-week sessions, two at a time, with a furnished studio, shared accommodation, a stipend and a production grant. The 2026 open call closes on 20 November 2026.
- Residents come from Angola, Cape Verde, Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique and São Tomé e Príncipe (WAAU, 21 November 2025).
- Governance: an Advisory Board of Diana Campbell, N'Goné Fall, Samy Ghiyati, Hala Khayat, Jozef Smets, Suzana Sousa, Mercedes Vilardell and Paula Nascimento, and an Artistic Committee of Azu Nwagbogu, Tandazani Dhlakama and Thiago de Paula Souza (its site). The Art Newspaper in 2021 named Paula Nascimento as chair of the artistic committee.
- Visiting: the site gives no address, opening hours or visitor access.

FUNDING
Nesr Art Foundation: self-funded: the founders' own money
- The Art Newspaper reports that the foundation is funded by Wissam and Hiba Nesr; it gives no amount.
- The site calls Hiba and Wissam Nesr philanthropists who started the foundation and does not state how it is funded. It adds that one residency was supported by "The Arts Council/An Chomhaile Ealaíon" (spelling as on the site).
- The founders' business: Webcor Group, described as "one of Angola's major food manufacturing entities", led by Wissam Nesr (Food Business Africa, 10 June 2022). That report covers a US$45 million confectionery factory in Luanda, built with Argentina's Arcor Group and inaugurated by Angolan President João Lourenço, and wheat mills in Luanda and Lobito. In May 2026 Proparco and BIC-BRED (Suisse) SA announced trade finance for Webcor, with Sergio Limonti named as its CEO (Proparco).
- No reporting found of state funding for the foundation, annual accounts or a funder list.

Not related: the Sindika Dokolo Foundation, which ArtPaper reports was the main funding source artists looked to in Angola before 2020 and which ended before the Nesr foundation began. No reporting found of any financial link.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found describes the foundation in positive terms.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the foundation is the Nesr family's private money from a food manufacturing and distribution group, with no flagged industry named. The weak points are that it publishes no accounts or funder list, and that its site names an outside arts council as supporter of one residency without detail. Worth watching are whether outside funders are disclosed and whether the founders' business money stays the only source.
