MUSEO EXPERIMENTAL EL ECO: Mexico City, Mexico
ID: museo-experimental-el-eco-mexico-city
Website: https://eleco.unam.mx/

OVERVIEW
- University museum of art, architecture and design on James Sullivan street in the colonia San Rafael, Mexico City (Crónica, 6 August 2025).
- Designed by Mathias Goeritz at the invitation of Daniel Mont and opened in 1953 (Crónica); the Gaceta UNAM article (Roberto Frías, 9 October 2025) says Mont commissioned it and Goeritz conceived it as an inhabitable sculpture ("escultura habitable").
- In its first years it showed works by Henry Moore and Carlos Mérida and presented dance and music. After Mont's early death the building was used as a restaurant, cabaret and theatre, then occupied by the Centro Universitario de Teatro and CLETA, and lay unused for years (Crónica).
- UNAM bought the building in 2004 and reopened it on 7 September 2005 (Crónica). The curator of the 2025 show credits UNAM's 2004 rescue with preventing the building from becoming a parking lot (Gaceta UNAM). It belongs to UNAM's Dirección General de Artes Visuales.
- On 5 August 2025 the Diario Oficial de la Federación published the order that starts the procedure to declare the building a national artistic monument; INBAL and the Secretaría de Cultura will carry out the next steps (Crónica).
- Pablo Landa Ruiloba, an anthropologist and the museum's director, said in April 2024 that the museum would widen its focus to architecture and design (El Universal, Frida Juárez Bautista, 26 April 2024).
- For its 20 years under UNAM the museum showed "Atmósfera total", curated by David Miranda, from 25 September 2025 to February 2026, with 41 original drawings from "Los amantes y la noche", new commissions by Leo Marz and Alberto Odériz and a temporary library, "Goeritz escrito" (Gaceta UNAM).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 18:00 (museum site).

FUNDING
Museo Experimental El Eco: mainly public: UNAM, a public university funded by the federal government
- The museum is part of UNAM's Dirección General de Artes Visuales, and UNAM bought the building and reopened it in 2005 (Crónica).
- UNAM's 2025 annual account showed spending of slightly more than 60 billion pesos, 89.2 percent from the federal subsidy and 10.8 percent from own income (UNAM Global, 1 July 2026).
- The 1953 building was commissioned from Goeritz by Daniel Mont (Gaceta UNAM).
- Landa said the museum would draw on resources of UNAM's Facultad de Arquitectura and Centro de Investigaciones de Diseño Industrial for a new design collection (El Universal).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind El Eco is UNAM, which is 89 percent funded by the Mexican federal government. Daniel Mont commissioned the 1953 building, and UNAM bought it in 2004 and reopened it in 2005.
