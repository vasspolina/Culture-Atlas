CASCO ART INSTITUTE: WORKING FOR THE COMMONS: Utrecht, Netherlands
ID: casco-art-institute
Website: https://casco.art (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art institute at Lange Nieuwstraat 7 in the Abraham Dolehof courtyard, in Utrecht's Museum Quarter; the courtyard is publicly accessible during the day (Casco).
- Founded in 1990 as Casco; renamed Casco Projects in 1995, the Office for Art, Design and Theory in 2003, and Casco Art Institute: Working for the Commons in 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Moved to the Nieuwekade in 2007, to a building designed by ifau and Jesko Fezer, and to Lange Nieuwstraat in 2014 (Wikipedia).
- Directed by Binna Choi from June 2008 to June 2023, after Emily Pethick (2005 to 2008) and Lisette Smits (1996 to 2005). Aline Hernández (artistic director) and Marianna Takou (executive director) now govern Stichting Casco (Casco team page).
- Programme: exhibitions presented intermittently, collective projects, a library and office open by appointment, and organisational experiments; it describes itself as "an experimental platform where art invites a social vision" (stated by Casco).
- Visiting: no fixed opening hours or admission information is published on the visit page; accessibility details sit on a separate Access Note page.

FUNDING
Casco Art Institute: mainly public and foundation grants: Mondriaan Fund, City of Utrecht, and Stichting DOEN via Arts Collaboratory
- Casco's About page says structural support comes from the Mondriaan Fund, the City of Utrecht and Stichting DOEN via Arts Collaboratory. No amounts are given there.
- Arts Collaboratory is a network of 25 art spaces, mainly from the Global South, which ArtReview says started in 2007 and names the Dutch NGOs DOEN Foundation and Hivos and the publicly funded Mondriaan Fund as its initiators (ArtReview, 20 November 2023). The Institute of Network Cultures says Casco founded it in 2013, describes DOEN as a distributor of lottery money, and says the network's jointly managed "common pot" was first funded by Hivos and DOEN; Hivos ended its support after several years, and DOEN and Casco continue (2024).
- Casco is a non-profit foundation with cultural ANBI status, so donors can deduct gifts from income or corporate taxes; donations can be sent with the subject line "Donation to Collective Pot" (Casco).
- Governed by a Supervisory Board of five: Betina Abi Habib, Maja Bekan, Ansuya Blom, Irene van Renswoude and Godelieve Spaas (Casco team page).
- The Foundation page links annual and financial reports, written in Dutch. No reporting found of income figures or amounts per funder in the pages opened.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Casco is Dutch public funding plus a lottery-funded foundation, and no funder with a documented controversy was found. The weak point is that no amounts are published on the pages read; the financial reports sit in a Dutch-language folder. Watch the Mondriaan Fund and DOEN support, since Hivos has already left the shared fund.
