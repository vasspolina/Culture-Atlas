DARAT AL FUNUN (THE KHALID SHOMAN FOUNDATION): Amman, Jordan
ID: darat-al-funun-amman
Website: https://daratalfunun.org

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre ("a home for the arts") in Jabal al Weibdeh, Amman, with its main entrance at 13 Nadeem al Mallah Street, on a 5,196 square metre compound overlooking downtown Amman (stated by the space).
- Founded by the artist Suha Shoman, who from 1988 hosted exhibitions and lectures on contemporary Arab art at the Scientific and Cultural Center of the Abdul Hameed Shoman Foundation; Darat al Funun opened in 1993 (Sarah Rogers, Darat al Funun essay, 2013). Restoration of the current site began in 1992 and the Main Building opened in 1993 (stated by the space).
- Came under the Khalid Shoman Foundation in 2002; the third house was renamed Dar Khalid in memory of the patron Khalid Shoman (stated by the space).
- Building and site: six renovated 1920s and 1930s houses and warehouses built by Jordanian, Palestinian, Syrian and Lebanese families, plus a restored sixth-century Byzantine church built over a Roman temple (stated by the space). Rogers credits the Jordanian architect Ammar Khammash with restoring the original three buildings.
- On site: exhibitions, The Lab for emerging artists, residencies, a PhD fellowship in modern and contemporary Arab art, talks, films, concerts, workshops, an art library and archives, and a museum in Beit al Beiruti opened in 2018 (stated by the space). The Khalid Shoman Collection is displayed in the foundation's headquarters, a restored 1930s building.
- Visiting: open Saturday to Thursday, 10am to 7pm; closed in August and on public holidays; during Ramadan it closes at 3pm (stated by the space).

FUNDING
Darat al Funun: private non-profit initiative: founded and led by Suha Shoman, under the Khalid Shoman Foundation
- The centre describes itself as "the first of its kind non-profit private initiative in the region" and was founded and led by Suha Shoman (UNESCO Creativity platform).
- It operates under the Khalid Shoman Foundation (stated by the space).
- Khalid Shoman, the centre's patron, was the son of Arab Bank founder Abdel Hamid Shoman and Vice-Chairman of the bank's board from 1974 until he resigned in June 2001 (The Star, Amman, 5 to 11 July 2001, reproduced on the centre's site). Rogers describes him as the centre's patron and deputy chairman of the Abdul Hameed Shoman Foundation.
- Residency partnerships since 1999 with UNESCO-Aschberg (1999 to 2007), Pro Helvetia (2004 to 2012), Nuova Icona, Venice (2014) and Fondazione Fotografia di Modena (2014 to 2019) (UNESCO Creativity platform).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Arab Bank: on 22 September 2014 a jury in the US District Court, Eastern District of New York, found the bank liable for knowingly supporting terrorism relating to 24 terrorist acts (Linde v. Arab Bank). On 9 February 2018 the US Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit overturned the verdict, finding that the District Court failed to give the jury adequate instructions (Wikipedia).
- Arab Bank: in 2005 the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency and the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network assessed a $24 million penalty against its New York branch for failing to implement an adequate anti-money laundering programme (Wikipedia).
- Connection to the space: The Star says Khalid Shoman, son of the bank's founder, joined the bank's board in 1957 and was Vice-Chairman from 1974 until June 2001. Rogers calls him Darat al Funun's patron and deputy chairman of the Abdul Hameed Shoman Foundation, whose Scientific and Cultural Center hosted Suha Shoman's exhibitions from 1988, before Darat al Funun opened in 1993. Wikipedia says Arab Bank created that foundation in 1978.

BOTTOM LINE
Darat al Funun is a private non-profit initiative founded and led by Suha Shoman and run through the Khalid Shoman Foundation. Its patron, Khalid Shoman, was Vice-Chairman of Arab Bank until 2001; a US jury found the bank liable in 2014 over terrorism financing and the Second Circuit overturned that verdict in 2018.
