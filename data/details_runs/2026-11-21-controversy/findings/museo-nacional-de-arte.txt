ID: museo-nacional-de-arte

Red flags: 2023 staff protest over cuts to guards
- The museum was closed until further notice from 11 March 2023 after workers shut it in protest at a reduction in its guard staff, with placards reading "Basta de opresión laboral" ("Enough labour oppression", translated) (Time Out México, 15 March 2023).
- The cultural promoter Víctor Velázquez told El Universal that custodians had fallen from 70 to 30 for the whole museum because they were not being paid, leaving some rooms closed, and that the protest was set off by a plan to remove the workers' lunch hour (Time Out México).
- Pedro Fuentes Burgos, INBAL's deputy director general of administration, said the lunch hour was being removed because some workers signed in and left for hours, and that INBAL's position was dialogue; protesting workers said he did not attend the talks (Time Out México).

Red flags: 2025 union closure over uniforms
- The museum, the Palacio de Bellas Artes and the Museo de Arte Moderno closed abruptly on 9 September 2025 over union demands on working conditions and uniforms, and reopened on 11 September after negotiations behind closed doors (The Art Newspaper, 19 September 2025).
- Unions representing INBAL workers asked for prepaid cards to buy uniforms instead of garments INBAL had already bought; INBAL did not respond to the paper's requests for comment (The Art Newspaper).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 27 March 2019 INBAL suspended the Noche de Museos programme at the Palacio de Bellas Artes and the museum after protests by members of the SINTINBAL union, which had threatened to close both buildings (La Razón, 28 March 2019).
