Red flags: 2024 union dispute over the end of the ticketing concession
- The Ministry of Culture revoked Opera's concession for ticketing, reception and access control at the Bargello Museums and the Accademia Gallery; the decision was taken by the director general of museums Massimo Osanna, who also directed the two museums, and about fifty workers were affected (Finestre sull'Arte, 12 July 2024).
- The unions Filcams CGIL Firenze and Uiltucs Toscana and the workplace representatives said the decision fell from above with little participation or transparency, that it had a "very strong impact on the lives of the workers", demanded "that not even 1 hour of work be lost" and said they were ready "for a summer of mobilization and struggle" (Finestre sull'Arte, 12 July 2024).
- The same day the ministry published a letter from Osanna to the unions and the workplace representatives of Opera Laboratori Fiorentini stating "Non ci saranno perdite di posti di lavoro alla Galleria dell'Accademia e ai Musei del Bargello, a Firenze" ("There will be no job losses at the Galleria dell'Accademia and the Musei del Bargello in Florence", translated), and that all services under the concession would continue without interruption (Italian Ministry of Culture, 12 July 2024).
