FUNDACIÓN OSWALDO VIGAS: Caracas, Venezuela
ID: fundación-oswaldo-vigas-caracas
Website: https://oswaldovigas.com

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit foundation based in Caracas that works to deepen understanding of the Venezuelan painter Oswaldo Vigas and to keep a selection of his work preserved and accessible to institutions, curators, writers and art lovers (Fundación Oswaldo Vigas).
- Named for Oswaldo Vigas, a Venezuelan painter, muralist and sculptor born in 1923 in Valencia, Carabobo, who died in Caracas on 22 April 2014; Wikipedia calls him the first artist to represent Venezuela at the Venice Biennale, in 1954.
- Wikipedia states the foundation "was created to expand his art legacy worldwide."
- Lorenzo Vigas is chairman, Janine Vigas is custodian and Dilia Hernández is director, with a team that includes an archive and registry coordinator, a documentalist and a conservation and restoration role (Fundación Oswaldo Vigas).
- Launched an online catalogue raisonné on 15 September 2022 at Estrellita Brodsky's Another Space in Chelsea, New York; e-flux calls it the first complete online catalogue raisonné of any Venezuelan modern or contemporary artist, documenting more than 3,000 paintings, free to browse (e-flux).

FUNDING
Fundación Oswaldo Vigas: artist-estate foundation chaired by Lorenzo Vigas
- The foundation is chaired by Lorenzo Vigas and its custodian is Janine Vigas (Fundación Oswaldo Vigas). Wikipedia describes the filmmaker Lorenzo Vigas as the artist's son and Janine Vigas as his wife.
- Axel Stein, former head of Sotheby's Latin American Art Department (2011 to 2018), supported the catalogue raisonné and is the foundation's head of outreach and development (Arte al Día, 22 September 2022; Fundación Oswaldo Vigas).
- Kwai Fung Hin Art Gallery, Hong Kong, announced in March 2023 that it would represent Vigas's work on an exclusive basis throughout Asia (e-flux).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation is a non-profit chaired by Lorenzo Vigas, with Janine Vigas as custodian. Its documented activity is the 2022 online catalogue raisonné, supported by the former Sotheby's Latin American art head Axel Stein, and a March 2023 announcement that Kwai Fung Hin Art Gallery would represent Vigas's work in Asia.
