ASSOCIAÇÃO NÚCLEO DE ARTE: Maputo, Mozambique
ID: associacao-nucleo-de-arte

OVERVIEW
- Artists' association in an old villa in the centre of Maputo (africaserver.nl profile); Globalnyt (29 December 2016) places it in the Polana Cimento neighbourhood near the Mercado do Museu.
- The profile on africaserver.nl calls it the oldest artists' collective in Mozambique and gives 1948 as the "more official foundation"; the name first appeared in 1937 as the title of an exhibition in Lourenço Marques that featured only Portuguese artists. Culturgest says Núcleo de Arte was founded in 1936 by the Portuguese painter Frederico Ayres. Globalnyt gives 1921.
- Mozambican artists were gradually admitted; a 1964 exhibition of Malangatana's work, staged by the Núcleo dos Estudantes Africanos, was closed by the secret police. After independence in 1975 the group was renamed COAP and the Associação Núcleo de Arte name was later restored. It closed several times during the civil war and in the early 1990s, and in 1993 a group of young artists reopened the building (africaserver.nl).
- Malangatana joined its painting course in 1958 (Culturgest).
- The villa has a garden with studios and a self-built ceramics oven, a workshop building, a permanent exhibition and a terrace serving meals and drinks (africaserver.nl; Globalnyt).
- Members: over one hundred painters, sculptors and ceramists, including Bertina Lopes, Malangatana and Alberto Chissano (africaserver.nl); Globalnyt gives 350 members and says the association is run by its artists.
- In 2021 it operated a gallery and offered workshops throughout the year (Club of Mozambique, 13 May 2021).

FUNDING
Associação Núcleo de Arte: member-run artists' association; the profile names Hivos as a donor
- The africaserver.nl profile says "A few donors, such as Hivos in the Netherlands, grant financial assistance." It mentions a workshop in Durban in 1997.
- The same profile says the permanent exhibition is popular with buyers and the terrace serves inexpensive meals and drinks.
- Club of Mozambique (13 May 2021) says the Nucleo de Arte "operates a gallery and offers workshops throughout the year".

BOTTOM LINE
The association is a member-run artists' group with a gallery, a terrace and workshops; its profile on africaserver.nl names Hivos in the Netherlands as a donor. The founding year differs by source: 1921 (Globalnyt), 1936 (Culturgest) and 1948 as the "more official foundation" (africaserver.nl).
