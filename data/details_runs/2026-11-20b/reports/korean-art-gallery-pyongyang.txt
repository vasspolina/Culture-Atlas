KOREAN ART GALLERY: Pyongyang, North Korea
ID: korean-art-gallery-pyongyang
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- National art museum (조선미술박물관) on the south side of Kim Il Sung Square in the Central District of Pyongyang (Wikipedia, Koryo Studio). Koryo Tours calls it the Korean Central Art Museum.
- Opening year: Koryo Tours (13 April 2020) says 1954; Koryo Studio (2024) and Wikipedia's text say 1948, Wikipedia giving 11 August 1948, though its categories file the gallery under 1954. Wikipedia, citing KCNA, dates a reconstruction to 2010, and Koryo Studio a renovation.
- Four storeys and 22 display rooms (Koryo Studio, Wikipedia). Wikipedia adds an exhibition hall, preservation rooms, a study room and over 10,000 square metres of floor space.
- The tour runs from reproductions of Koguryo tomb paintings through Korean history to socialist realist works (Koryo Tours). Koryo Studio says paintings of the leaders giving on-the-spot guidance hang there and some displayed works are copies, the originals being stored elsewhere because of climate and light sensitivity.
- It hosts state shows: the exhibition for the 70th anniversary of Pyongyang University of Fine Arts, about 600 works, opened there in September 2017 with Culture Minister Pak Chun Nam attending, and the National Art Exhibition opened there on 7 October 2026 for the Workers' Party's 81st anniversary (Ministry of Unification, from North Korean media).
- Visiting: Monday to Saturday all year, closed on public holidays; free; no photography inside; a shop sells artworks and souvenirs (Koryo Tours).
- Visitors: Wikipedia, citing KCNA, says over 6 million people had visited by 2020.

FUNDING
Korean Art Gallery: state-run: an art institution of the North Korean Ministry of Culture
- Ministry: Yonhap reported in 2019 that the Ministry of Culture's new website, Korean Art (조선예술, my translation), lists the gallery among its art institutions, beside the Mansudae Art Studio (Ministry of Unification, from Yonhap).
- Staff: the Ministry of Unification's 2022 directory of North Korean institutions lists Ri Kwang Yong (리광영) as director and Kim Ok Sun (김옥순) as secretary of the gallery's primary Party committee (my romanisations). Ri gave the report at a March 2015 meeting marking 50 years since Kim Il Sung's on-the-spot guidance at the gallery (Ministry of Unification).
- KCNA claimed in March 2020 that the gallery had shown over 10,000 works in the previous 55 years (Ministry of Unification, from KCNA).
- North Korea announces its state budget as yearly percentage changes, not amounts. KCNA claimed on 24 March 2026 that 2026 spending would reach 105.8 percent of 2025, with funds for the cultural sector at 103.1 percent of last year's.

Red flags: US sanctions on the North Korean government, 2016
- US Executive Order 13722 of 15 March 2016 blocks property in US jurisdiction of the Government of North Korea and the Workers' Party of Korea; section 9 defines the Government as the DPRK government and its agencies, instrumentalities and controlled entities (Federal Register, 18 March 2016).

Not related: the Mansudae Art Studio (Mansudae Overseas Project Group of Companies), which the US Treasury blocked under the same order on 2 December 2016 (Federal Register, 7 December 2016). It is a separate state studio; Koryo Studio notes the sanctions.

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery is a state institution under the North Korean culture ministry, and the US blocking order on that state is its one Red flag.
