TRANZIT.CZ / MATTER OF ART BIENNALE: Prague, Czech Republic
ID: tranzit-cz-prague
Website: https://cz.tranzit.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art initiative in Prague, at Dittrichova 349/13, Prague 120 00, working through critical platforms, exhibitions, lectures, publications, research, mediation and alternative education (e-flux directory).
- The Czech member of the tranzit.org network of independent contemporary art associations in Austria, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Slovakia and Romania, which was founded in 2002 (ERSTE Foundation). ERSTE Foundation names Tereza Stejskalová as a co-founder and director of tranzit.cz.
- It organises the Biennale Matter of Art in Prague, the first edition of which was held in 2020 (ERSTE Foundation). The 2022 edition ran from 21 July to 23 October 2022 at Prague City Gallery, a former laundry of the General University Hospital and Šaloun Studio, with admission free (e-flux; ERSTE Foundation).
- The 2024 edition, curated by Katalin Erdődi and Aleksei Borisionok, ran from 14 June to 29 September 2024 at the National Gallery Prague's Trade Fair Palace and the Lidice Gallery, organised with the National Gallery Prague and the Lidice Art Collection (e-flux).
- The 2026 edition runs from 12 June to 13 September 2026, curated by Jaroslava Tomanová, Jakub Gawkowski and František Fekete, at the Trade Fair Palace, GAMPA City Gallery Pardubice and Tusculum Prague, with admission free (National Gallery Prague).
- The 2024 edition drew more than 15,000 visitors (Biennial Foundation).
- tranzit.cz publishes a bilingual anthology with each edition; the 2022 anthology is distributed worldwide by Spector Books (e-flux).

FUNDING
tranzit.cz: foundation-funded: ERSTE Foundation, with EU and EEA Grants money for biennale editions
- ERSTE Foundation: named as main partner of the 2022, 2024 and 2026 editions of the biennale (e-flux; National Gallery Prague; ERSTE Foundation) and has been the tranzit network's main partner since 2010 (e-flux, 15 December 2011). Wikipedia calls ERSTE Foundation the biggest Austrian savings bank foundation and the main anchor shareholder of Erste Group Bank AG, using part of its dividend income to fund projects in Central and South Eastern Europe.
- 2022 edition: part of the project "Center and Periphery: Cultural Deserts in Eastern Europe", supported by EEA Grants from Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway under the Culture Programme (e-flux).
- 2024 edition: part of Art Space Unlimited, co-funded by the European Union; its artistic and curatorial residencies and public programme are funded by NextGenerationEU (e-flux).
- The 2024 biennale was organised with the National Gallery Prague and the Lidice Art Collection (e-flux); the 2022 edition used Prague City Gallery as a venue and the 2026 edition uses the National Gallery Prague's Trade Fair Palace and GAMPA City Gallery Pardubice.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind tranzit.cz is ERSTE Foundation, its main partner, which draws on dividends from its stake in Erste Group Bank (Wikipedia), plus EEA Grants money for the 2022 biennale and European Union money for the 2024 one.
