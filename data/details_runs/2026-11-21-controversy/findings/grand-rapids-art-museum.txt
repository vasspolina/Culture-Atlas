ID: grand-rapids-art-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- 22 September 2017: D. Neil Bremer, the museum's former chief operating officer, filed a whistleblower protection suit against the museum in Kent County Circuit Court (FOX 17, 5 October 2017; WOOD Radio, 6 October 2017, crediting WOOD TV 8).
- Unverified: the suit claims the museum used donor-restricted funds for general operating purposes and that Bremer reported this to chief executive Dana Friis-Hansen without the problem being corrected, from FOX 17. The Howard Law Group, Bremer's lawyers, said the museum's accounts were at least $1 million short; his lawyer Bill Howard said "[GRAM officials] were taking from Peter to pay Paul" (FOX 17).
- Unverified: Bremer claims he was fired without notice so that he would not report the matter to public officials, from WOOD Radio.
- The museum said it could not comment in detail on the pending litigation, but that it did "fully reject the accusations cited in the complaint and have strong documentation to refute the complaint", and that its actions had at all times been lawful and appropriate (FOX 17).
