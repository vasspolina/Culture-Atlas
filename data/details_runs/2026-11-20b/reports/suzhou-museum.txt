SUZHOU MUSEUM: Suzhou, China
ID: suzhou-museum
Website: https://www.szmuseum.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Suzhou Museum (苏州博物馆) describes itself as a local comprehensive museum and the centre for collecting, protecting, studying and showing Suzhou's cultural relics (museum website, our translation).
- It was founded in 1960 (museum website; Ministry of Culture and Tourism); its old site is the Zhong Wang Fu (忠王府), former residence of the Taiping general Li Xiucheng (Chinese Wikipedia).
- I.M. Pei took on the design of a new main building on 30 April 2002, at the age of 85; it stands west of the Humble Administrator's Garden at 204 Dongbei Street, has 19,000 square metres of floor space (museum website), and opened in 2006 (Chinese Wikipedia).
- A second site, the Suzhou Museum West at 399 Changjiang Road in the Suzhou High-Tech Zone, designed by gmp, began trial opening on 25 September 2021, with 48,365 square metres of floor space and 13,391 square metres of galleries (museum website; ArchDaily).
- The collection centres on finds from Suzhou, literati painting and calligraphy, and folk crafts of the Jiangnan region (Chinese Wikipedia).
- The director, Xie Xiaoting (谢晓婷), writes that the West site runs a world civilisation exhibition series with the British Museum, that its 2024 to 2025 Ashurbanipal show won a national award, and that the main building marks its 20th year in 2026 (People's Daily Overseas Edition, 18 May 2026).
- Opening on 25 November 2026: a show of relics transferred from the Suzhou Cultural Relics Store (museum website).
- Visiting: the main building opens 9am to 5pm, last entry 4pm, Tuesday to Sunday, with real-name timed booking up to seven days ahead (museum website); the West site needs no booking (Chinese Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Suzhou Museum: public: buildings commissioned by Suzhou city bodies, with free admission
- The Pei building had a total investment of 339 million yuan; with the Zhong Wang Fu, the main site has 26,500 square metres of floor space (museum website).
- The client for the West site was the Suzhou City Planning Bureau with the High-Tech Zone bureau (ArchDaily, gmp project data).
- Admission is free at both sites (Chinese Wikipedia; China for Travelers).
- The British Museum is an exhibition partner for the West site's international gallery, not a funder (People's Daily Overseas Edition).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from Suzhou city bodies; nothing in its funding raises a flag.
