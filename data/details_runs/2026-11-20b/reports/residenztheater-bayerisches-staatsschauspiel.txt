RESIDENZTHEATER (BAYERISCHES STAATSSCHAUSPIEL): Munich, Germany
ID: residenztheater-bayerisches-staatsschauspiel
Website: https://www.residenztheater.de/en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Bavarian State Theatre for spoken drama plays in three venues near the Munich Residenz: the Residenztheater on Max-Joseph-Platz with 881 seats, the Cuvilliéstheater with 437 seats and the Marstall with about 146 seats (theatre website).
- It grew out of the princely court and national theatre of the 18th century; the royal court theatre became the Staatstheater after the revolution of 1918 (theatre website; German Wikipedia).
- It is one of four Bavarian State Theatres in Munich, with the State Opera, the Theatre Academy August Everding at the Prinzregententheater and the Gärtnerplatztheater (theatre website).
- An ensemble of over 50 actors and a staff of over 450 (theatre website).
- Andreas Beck has been Staatsintendant since 2019, after Martin Kušej (2011 to 2019); Katja Funken-Hamann is managing director (theatre website; German Wikipedia; season brochure 2026/27).
- November 2026 repertory includes Faust I, a co-production with the Salzburg Festival, Cabaret, King Lear with Manfred Zapatka and Ödipus; some performances carry English surtitles (theatre website).
- A new rehearsal and workshop centre on Hohenlindener Strasse in Bogenhausen will hold three rehearsal stages and the workshops on about 12,500 square metres, bringing together operations now spread across the city (Bavarian building ministry, 16 December 2024).

FUNDING
Residenztheater: public: the Free State of Bavaria, with a friends' association and foundations on single projects
- The Bayerisches Staatsschauspiel is one of the Bavarian State Theatres (German Wikipedia; theatre website).
- New rehearsal and workshop centre: the Free State is investing about 196 million euros as part of the Kulturagenda Bayern; groundbreaking was in June 2023, the topping-out on 16 December 2024, and completion under the Staatliches Bauamt Regensburg is planned for December 2026, with Implenia Hochbau GmbH as total contractor (Bavarian building ministry, 16 December 2024). Implenia names the Bavarian Ministry of Science and the Arts as its client (Implenia).
- The Verein der Freunde des Bayerischen Staatsschauspiels, founded on 21 October 1976 by Bavarian MPs, a banker, a publisher, an actor and a ministry official, funds projects and gives the 5,000-euro Kurt-Meisel-Preis each year (German Wikipedia). It backs the 2026/27 King Lear and celebrates its 50th anniversary on 11 October 2026 (season brochure 2026/27).
- The Kulturstiftung des Bundes funds "Kaspar 2028", a two-year project on artificial intelligence with the Filmuniversität Babelsberg, under its "Kunst & KI" programme (season brochure 2026/27).
- The Hubertus-Altgelt-Stiftung and the Friends support the outreach programme "Resi für alle" (season brochure 2026/27).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is Bavarian state money, with small private and federal grants for single projects. Nothing in the funding draws a red flag. Watch whether the new production centre opens on time and within its budget.
