NEUER BERLINER KUNSTVEREIN (N.B.K.): Berlin, Germany
ID: neuer-berliner-kunstverein
Website: https://www.nbk.org/de

OVERVIEW
- Art association at Chausseestrasse 128/129, 10115 Berlin, in the Mitte district, where it has been based since 1994; earlier it was in Charlottenburg (German Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1969 as the successor to the Deutsche Gesellschaft fuer Bildende Kunst, which was dissolved that year; on 5 December 1968 students had disrupted its general meeting with the slogan "Der Kunstverein stinkt!" (German Wikipedia).
- Directors: Lucie Schauer from 1975 to 1994, Alexander Tolnay from 1995 and Marius Babias from 2008 (German Wikipedia).
- The Artothek, founded in 1970, holds more than 4,000 contemporary works available for loan; the Video-Forum, founded in 1971, holds 1,700 video works and calls itself "the oldest and largest video art collection in Germany" (n.b.k. website).
- Its facade and billboard host commissioned works; in autumn 2026 these were Guerrilla Girls on the facade (to 29 August 2027) and Dora Budor and Noah Barker's "Monte Carlo Method" on the billboard (to 29 November) (n.b.k. website).
- Visiting: exhibition floor Tuesday to Sunday 12:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00; showroom Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 18:00 (n.b.k. English site).

FUNDING
Neuer Berliner Kunstverein: mainly public: Berlin Senate culture department, with lottery foundation money at its origin
- The n.b.k. gGmbH is funded by the Berlin Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion (n.b.k. institution page).
- German Wikipedia says the Berlin Senate divided the funds of the Lotto-Stiftung evenly between the n.b.k. and the Neue Gesellschaft fuer Bildende Kunst, and names the Stiftung Deutsche Klassenlotterie as the funder of the earlier Deutsche Gesellschaft fuer Bildende Kunst.
- Membership: the association had a little over 700 members in 2007 (German Wikipedia).

Red flags: artists' boycott over the Gaza war
- Banu Cennetoglu and Pilvi Takala announced on Instagram on Monday 5 February 2024 that they were cancelling their solo exhibitions at the n.b.k.; the Berliner Zeitung lead says they did so because the n.b.k. does not share their political opinion on the Gaza war (Berliner Zeitung, Timo Feldhaus, 6 February 2024; paywalled, lead read). The cancellation was aimed at the n.b.k.; the lead does not name the Berlin Senate or the German state.
- Director Marius Babias was interviewed by the Berliner Zeitung under the headline "Wir wollen uns politisch nicht instrumentalisieren lassen" (title translated: "We do not want to be politically instrumentalised"), 4 April 2024 (Timo Feldhaus; paywalled, headline, a caption quote and the teaser read). A caption quotes him: "Die Staatsraeson spielt in der Praxis keine Rolle." The teaser says the n.b.k. is looking for solutions.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the n.b.k. is Berlin public funding, from the Senate culture department with lottery-foundation money at its origin; no private or flagged funder is documented. The one documented objection is the February 2024 cancellation of two solo shows by artists who disagreed with the association's position on the Gaza war.
