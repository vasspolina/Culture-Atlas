NARA NATIONAL MUSEUM: Nara, Japan
ID: nara-national-museum
Website: https://www.narahaku.go.jp/english/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- National museum at 50 Noborioji-cho, Nara City.
- Established in 1889 as the Imperial Museum of Nara and opened to the public on 29 April 1895 (museum history page).
- Renamed the Imperial Household Museum of Nara in 1900 and Nara National Museum in 1952.
- The original building, by Katayama Tokuma, was completed in 1894 and designated an Important Cultural Property in 1969.
- The West Wing, by Junzo Yoshimura, was completed in 1972 and the East Wing opened in 1998; the Hassoan tea house was moved to the grounds in 1892.
- Each autumn it exhibits objects kept in the Shosoin repository.
- Visiting: 9:30 am to 5:00 pm, closed Monday and from 28 December to 1 January; general admission JPY 700, college students JPY 350, free for visitors 18 and under and 70 and over. The Buddhist Sculpture Hall is closed for renovation from 14 September 2026 until around spring 2028.

FUNDING
Nara National Museum: public: the National Institutes for Cultural Heritage, on Japanese government funding
- The museum is one of the national museums run by the National Institutes for Cultural Heritage, with those of Tokyo, Kyoto and Kyushu and the Museum of the Imperial Collections.
- The institute's fiscal 2026 budget is JPY 15.75 billion: JPY 9.92 billion in government funding for operating expenses, JPY 2.39 billion of self-generated income, JPY 1.31 billion in a grant for facilities improvement and JPY 1.0 billion in donations and other income (institute's fiscal 2026 booklet).
- The museum itself drew outside research money: JPY 36.2 million for commissioned work and JPY 11.8 million in research grants in fiscal 2025.
- The institute takes donations and memberships through its own donation portal.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Japanese state's, passed through the body that runs the national museums, with ticket income and donations on top. No funder is objected to. Worth watching: the share of donations and other outside income in the institute's budget.
