WIDER CONTEXT
- On 2 June 2017 members of the group Srpski zavetnici tried to enter a book promotion at the centre, held as part of the "Mirdita, dobar dan" festival: "Želim da me čuju", stories of women who survived rape in the last war in Kosovo. Police at the entrance kept them out, and they sang fan chants and the songs "Oj Kosovo, Kosovo" and "Kosovo je srce Srbije" (N1, 2 June 2017).
- On 30 May 2018 between 150 and 200 members and followers of the Serbian Radical Party gathered at the St Sava temple and headed to the centre to try to stop the opening of the "Mirëdita, Dobar dan" festival. Police had banned the protest, and uniformed and plain clothes police closed off the area around the venue (N1, 30 May 2018).
- In 2020 the play "Srebrenica", directed by Zlatko Paković, which had its Belgrade premiere at the centre on 24 September, became a target of extremists, and in the week before 16 October Miša Vacić, leader of Srpska desnica, with several associates, tried to interrupt an exhibition about war crimes at the centre (021.rs, 16 October 2020).
- The 2017 and 2018 protests were aimed at the festival, which the Youth Initiative for Human Rights organises (N1, 30 May 2018), at its venue at the centre.
