MUSEION: Bolzano, Italy
ID: museion-bolzano
Website: https://www.museion.it/it/pagine/5354-chi-siamo

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern and contemporary art in Bolzano, South Tyrol, at Piazza Piero Siena 1 (Museion activity report 2021).
- Founded in 1985 by the Associazione Museion; it began operating in 1987 in the former hospital building on via Sernesi (Museion website).
- Became a private-law foundation in 2006, with the Autonomous Province of Bolzano-South Tyrol and the Museion Private Founders as its founders (Museion website).
- Opened in its current building on via Dante on 24 May 2008, designed by KSV (Krüger Schuberth Vandreike), a rectangular prism 54 m long and 25 m high (Wikipedia).
- Collection of about 4,400 works and a library of about 30,000 catalogues and monographs (Museion website); a satellite venue, Piccolo Museion Cubo Garutti, stands in the Don Bosco neighbourhood (Wikipedia).
- Director since June 2020: Bart van der Heide (Museion website).

FUNDING
Museion: mainly public: the Autonomous Province of Bolzano, with small shares from the ministry, a bank foundation, tickets and sponsors
- Province: 3,730,000 euros of a financial plan of 4,387,779 euros (Il Dolomiti, Martina Capovin, 30 March 2025).
- Other income in that plan: 73,406 euros from the Ministry of Culture, 77,000 euros from Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio, 70,500 euros from the bookshop, and ticket sales of 134,000 euros, which cover 3 per cent of total costs, with about 30,000 visitors a year (Il Dolomiti).
- In March 2025 Bolzano's culture commission approved by 4 votes to 3 a 3,000 euro city contribution to Fondazione Museion, covering part of a 25,000 euro deficit; a councillor asked whether the millions the Province already gives were not enough (Il Dolomiti).
- Governance: the foundation board is Tanja Pichler (president), Paolo Vanoni (vice president), Antonella Arseni, Peter Paul Kainrath, Stefania Pitscheider Soraperra and Ivan Rampelotto (Museion website).
- Museion Private Founders: a group linking the creative sector with private business in South Tyrol, which supported the Museion Art Club and, in 2021, the purchase of some artworks (Museion website; activity report 2021). Its board is Ivan Rampelotto (president), Eva von Ingram Harpf (vice president), Federico Giudiceandrea, Traudi Messini, Maria Niederstätter and Patrizia Spadafora (Museion website).
- Sponsors: in 2021 Museion received in-kind sponsorship from Museart SRL, Dr. Schaer, Hotel Sheraton Bozen SRL, Santa Margherita with Kettmeir and Cantine Torresella, A. Loacker Spa, Parkhotel Laurin and Hotel Greif. Alperia Spa sponsored the workshop "Body Sound & Space", and its former power station Alperia St. Anton hosted Isabel Lewis's performance events (activity report 2021).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the Autonomous Province of Bolzano, which pays most of its costs, with small contributions from the Ministry of Culture, Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio, ticket sales and in-kind sponsors from the local hotel and food trade.
