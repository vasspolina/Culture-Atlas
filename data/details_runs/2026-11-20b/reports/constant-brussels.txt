CONSTANT (ASSOCIATION FOR ART AND MEDIA): Brussels, Belgium
ID: constant-brussels
Website: https://constantvzw.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit association for art, media and technology with an office at Rue du Fort 5A, Saint-Gilles, Brussels, and a studio at Chaussée de Jette 388, Koekelberg (Constant, "Contacts").
- Founded in 1997; the association has been on the Belgian company register since 12 February 1997 as Constant Vereniging voor Kunst en Media / Constant association pour l'Art et les Médias (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises). Its 1997 statutes set out to support audiovisual media, film, video, electronic arts and software (Constant, "More about Constant").
- Its focus widened to free software, feminisms, gender, copyright alternatives, science fiction and networked art; it describes itself as run by artists, designers, researchers and hackers working in Dutch, French and English (stated by Constant).
- Programme 2023 to 2027: SPLINT, "Speculative, Libre, Intersectional Technologies", with yearly themes: techno-disobedience (2023), accessibility (2024), "Otherworldly communications" (2025) and "Unbordering" (2026), on border control technologies around Europe (Constant, "More about Constant").
- Constant Studio, rented since 2022, is a former workshop for electronic appliances on two floors of about 150 square metres each: the ground floor holds residencies, workshops and production, with a garden, and the first floor is split into 11 units used by artists, collectives and researchers (Constant, "Constant Studio").
- Constant organises worksessions, installations and publications (stated by Constant). Its network includes OSP (Open Source Publishing, founded in 2006 within Constant) and the film lab Labobis in its office cellar (Constant, "About" and "Network").
- Team named by Constant: Elodie Mugrefya (artistic coordination) and Donatella Portoghese (financial coordination and production), with seven others (Constant, "Contacts").
- Visiting: office open Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 17:00, best to call ahead. All events are free. The office has a 20.5 cm entrance step, no lift and a basement toilet; the studio has a small ramp and an accessible ground-floor toilet, with the upper floor reached by stairs (Constant, "Accessibility").

FUNDING
Constant: publicly funded: Flemish, Francophone, Brussels and municipal authorities, with EU and foundation project grants
- Structural and general supporters on Constant's Support page: the Department of Culture, Youth, Sport and Media of the Flemish government; the Flemish Community Commission (VGC); Maribel employment funding through FE.BI; the Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles (Digital Arts); the Brussels-Capital Region; the Services of the Francophone Brussels Government; and the Dutch-language services of the municipality of Saint-Gilles.
- Project grants on the same page: Wallonie-Bruxelles International (ATNOFS, Iterations and Cqrrelations), the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union (Iterations), the European Cultural Foundation (ATNOFS, A Transversal Network of Feminist Servers), the City of Brussels (Open coil) and Innoviris (SpecXcraft).
- Constant Studio is part of a network of Brussels artists' spaces supported by the VGC, alongside Level 5, Max and Globe Aroma (Constant, annual report 2025).
- Earned income: workspace rental in the studio, which had no units free when checked (Constant, "Constant Studio").
- Board (Constant, "Members"): Rafaella Houlstan-Hasaerts, Laurence Claeys and Sarah Magnan.

BOTTOM LINE
Constant runs on Belgian public money from every level of government, with European and foundation grants for single projects. No corporate sponsor or flagged funder is on record.
