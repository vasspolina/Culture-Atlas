Red flags: 2022 return of a Benin bronze head to Nigeria
- The "Head of a King" or "Oba" from the RISD Museum, a gift from Lucy Truman Aldrich in 1939, was among 31 objects returned to the Nigerian government in October 2022; the pieces were stolen by the British in the late 19th century (NBC News and Associated Press, Claretta Bellamy, 13 October 2022).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 30 November 2018 students and faculty from the Rhode Island School of Design and Brown University held an action to request that the museum return the sculpture looted from the Kingdom of Benin; the museum said it had begun conversations with the Nigerian government to return the bronze (Hyperallergic, Dana Heng, 30 November 2018; registration wall, only the summary was read).
