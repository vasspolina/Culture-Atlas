PUSHKIN STATE MUSEUM OF FINE ARTS: Moscow, Russia
ID: pushkin-state-museum-of-fine-arts-moscow
Website: https://pushkinmuseum.art/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Federal state museum of European art at Volkhonka 12, Moscow, with the Gallery of Art of European and American Countries and branches in Nizhny Novgorod and Tomsk (museum site).
- Founded in 1912 by Ivan Tsvetaev, who persuaded the philanthropist Yuriy Nechaev-Maltsov and the architect Roman Klein of the need for it; first named the Alexander III Museum, renamed for Pushkin in 1937 (Wikipedia).
- Roman Klein and Vladimir Shukhov designed the main building, built from 1898 to early 1912; the collection holds about 700,000 items (Wikipedia).
- Ekaterina Pronicheva was named director on 14 January 2026, announced by Culture Minister Olga Lyubimova; she had directed the Vladimir-Suzdal Museum-Reserve since 2022 (BFM). Marina Loshak, director since 2013, resigned on 20 March 2023 (Moscow Times).
- Visiting: Main Building ticket 700 roubles, 500 discounted; free for children 6 and under, disabled adults, wheelchair users, ICOM members and students of fine arts and architecture at Russian colleges; free on Thursdays for children 7 to 17 and Russian students. Opening hours were not on the pages opened.

FUNDING
Pushkin State Museum of Fine Arts: mainly public, with corporate sponsors and a board of state-linked companies
- The museum is a state museum. No budget amounts were read.
- Sponsors named on the museum's sponsors page, with no years or amounts: VTB (general sponsor), LG (museum partner and partner of the exhibition "Diamond Chariot: Buddhist Art of Russia"), PSB (partner for developing the collection) and Yandex Eda (listed among the partner logos, role not stated).
- Board of trustees, chaired by Deputy Prime Minister Marat Khusnullin: Culture Minister Olga Lyubimova; German Gref of Sberbank; Gazprombank; Vladimir Kalanda of Transneft; Mikhail Karisalov of Sibur; Russian Railways; Pyotr Fradkov, chairman of Promsvyazbank; Natalia Sergunina, Deputy Mayor of Moscow; and 12 more members.
- No reporting found of foreign or private-donor funding in the pages opened.

Red flags: Sanctions, sponsors and trustees
- VTB, the general sponsor, was added to the US SDN list on 24 February 2022, a full blocking sanction (Kelley Drye).
- Gazprombank, a trustee, was added to the US SDN list on 21 November 2024 to "curtail Russia's use of the international financial system" (Baker McKenzie).
- Ukraine's sanctions register lists the museum itself, dated 12 May 2023, for receiving Russian Ministry of Culture permits for archaeological work in occupied Crimea, period given as 2014 to 2023 (Main Intelligence Directorate register). This is a Ukrainian listing only; no page opened shows a US, EU or UK listing of the museum.

Red flags: Arms and defence, Promsvyazbank
- PSB, the partner for developing the collection, is a state-owned bank that "has become a captive bank for the Russian defense sector"; a December 2019 law gave it the functions of a supporting bank (Wikipedia). Wikipedia says the UK announced sanctions on it on 22 February 2022 and the US imposed sanctions on it in a package it dates 22 February 2022.
- Pyotr Fradkov, listed as chairman of PSB, sits on the museum's board of trustees (museum site).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the museum's money; the coverage is about the 2023 change of director and the pressure on Russian cultural leaders.
1. USA Art News: "Marina Loshak steps down as director of Moscow's Pushkin Museum in latest high-level Russian departure" (21 March 2023)
URL: https://usaartnews.com/news/marina-loshak-steps-down-as-director-of-moscows-pushkin-museum-in-latest-high-level-russian-departure
- Links the wave of museum leadership changes to ideological repression after the invasion of Ukraine; Loshak's daughter and nephew are journalists labelled "foreign agents".
- Reports, citing Agentstvo, that her successor Elizaveta Likhacheva was salaried by a pro-Kremlin youth movement in the early 2000s; the article does not say it was independently verified.
- Does not discuss the museum's sponsors or trustees.

WIDER CONTEXT
- The Moscow Times (21 March 2023) linked Loshak's exit and the February 2023 change at the Tretyakov Gallery as examples of security-linked appointments in Russian cultural institutions; it describes no wider pattern.
- Connection to the space: Loshak's resignation and Elizaveta Likhacheva's appointment as director of the Pushkin Museum were one of the two changes the Moscow Times describes.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a Russian state museum, which alone is not a flag, but its general sponsor VTB (US, 24 February 2022) and trustee Gazprombank (US, 21 November 2024) are on the US SDN list, and PSB, a defence-sector bank sanctioned by the UK and US in February 2022, is a collection partner and its chairman sits on the board. Ukraine alone lists the museum itself, over Crimea digs. Worth watching: sponsor amounts, which are unpublished on the pages opened, and any change to the board.
