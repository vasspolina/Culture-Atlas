MODERN ART MUSEUM OF FORT WORTH: Fort Worth, USA
ID: modern-art-museum-of-fort-worth-fort-worth
Website: https://www.themodern.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of international modern and contemporary art, known as the Modern, at 3200 Darnell Street in the Fort Worth Cultural District (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1892; earlier names include the Fort Worth Art Association, Fort Worth Art Center and Fort Worth Art Museum (Wikipedia). It files its Form 990 as the Fort Worth Art Association (ProPublica).
- Building by Tadao Ando, opened in 2002: three long pavilions set into a reflecting pond with 53,000 square feet of galleries (Wikipedia).
- Permanent collection of more than 3,000 works (Wikipedia).
- Halona Norton-Westbrook began as director on 1 July (museum website), succeeding Marla Price, director in the year to June 2025; Marsland Moncrief chairs the board (Form 990).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday from 10:00 to 17:00, Fridays until 20:00, closed Mondays; $16 general, $12 seniors, military and first responders, $10 students, free under 18; free on Fridays, half price on Sundays, and free for up to four people with a SNAP EBT card (museum website).

FUNDING
Modern Art Museum of Fort Worth: private: donations and its own endowment, with Texas foundations, bank charities and city and state arts money
- Fiscal year to June 2025: $21.0 million revenue, $10.6 million (50%) from contributions and $9.6 million (46%) from investment income; $17.2 million expenses; $109.1 million net assets (Fort Worth Art Association, Form 990).
- Fort Worth Art Association Endowment Foundation: $204.1 million net assets and $3.5 million investment income in the same year (Form 990).
- The museum lists 21 "2025-2026 Grants" of $250 or more, among them the Sid W. Richardson Foundation, Ben E. Keith Foundation, Bank of America Charitable Foundation, the Crystelle Waggoner Charitable Trust (Bank of America trustee), Communities Foundation of Texas, Three G Foundation, Fidelity Charitable and DAFgiving360 (museum website).
- Public money on the same list: the City of Fort Worth, the Fort Worth Promotion and Development Fund, the Fort Worth Tourism Public Improvement District, Arts Fort Worth and the Texas Commission on the Arts (museum website).

Red flags: 2025 operating grant from the Sid W. Richardson Foundation, an oil fortune
- The foundation was set up in 1947 by Sid W. Richardson, "a Texan who earned his fortune in the oil industry"; it had $526.8 million in assets in December 2024 (Wikipedia).
- Its own site says major grantmaking began in 1962, when income from his estate's assets became available; Edward P. Bass chairs its board (Sid W. Richardson Foundation).
- Its grants record lists $750,000 to the Fort Worth Art Association for the Modern's operating support in 2025, as in 2024 (Sid W. Richardson Foundation); it gave the same sum in 2017 (Wikipedia) and appears on the museum's 2025-2026 grants list (museum website).

Red flags: 2025 police seizure of Sally Mann photographs
- Fort Worth police seized Sally Mann photographs from the group show "Diaries of Home" after a complaint that they showed naked children and could be seen as pornographic; the first story ran in The Dallas Express on 23 December 2024 (The Art Newspaper, 9 January 2025).
- The museum said four works were under inquiry and that they had been published and exhibited for more than 30 years (The Art Newspaper).
- On 8 January 2025 a Tarrant County judge urged police on X to investigate (Cardinal News).
- A Tarrant County grand jury declined to indict Mann or the museum in the week of 27 March 2025, and police said the photographs would be returned (Cardinal News).
- The City of Fort Worth, whose police made the seizure, is a listed funder (museum website).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The press coverage found criticises the police and complainants over the 2025 seizure, not the museum.
1. The Art Newspaper: Elena Goukassian, "Police seize Sally Mann photographs at Texas museum amid accusations of child pornography" (2025)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2025/01/09/sally-mann-photographs-seized-fort-worth-modern-art-museum
- Calls the case "a 1990s culture-war déjà vu" and quotes the National Coalition Against Censorship calling the child abuse allegation "disingenuous" and "deeply dangerous".
- Criticises the seizure, not the institution.

BOTTOM LINE
Mostly private money, flagged for current oil-fortune grants and for a funding city whose police seized works from a show. Watch the Richardson grants and any further action by city authorities.
