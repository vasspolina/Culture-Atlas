MAAT: Lisbon, Portugal
ID: maat-lisbon
Website: https://www.maat.pt/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology on the River Tagus in Belém, Lisbon, with two venues: MAAT Gallery, a new building, and MAAT Central, the Tejo Power Station (Wikipedia, stated by the museum).
- Opened in October 2016. The new building opened to the public on 5 October 2016; the programme had started on 30 June 2016 in the renovated power station (Wikipedia).
- Owned and run by Fundação EDP, the foundation of the energy company EDP (Wikipedia, ECO).
- Building: designed by Amanda Levete's firm AL_A; the Central Tejo power station, built in 1908, was converted into MAAT Central (stated by the museum).
- Cost: roughly 20 million euros (ECO).
- On site: exhibitions, the EDP Foundation Art Collection, the Energy Heritage Collection and a documentation centre (stated by the museum).
- Open Wednesday to Monday 10:00 to 19:00; closed Tuesdays, 1 January, 1 May and 25 December. Admission prices and accessibility details are not listed on the page opened.

FUNDING
MAAT: corporate: Fundação EDP, the foundation set up and financed by EDP
- Fundação EDP is described as a non-profit set up and financed by the company (Wikipedia, EDP Group).
- EDP's 2023 shareholder meeting proposal kept a transfer of 6.2 million euros to Fundação EDP from the 2022 results (ECO, 14 March 2023).
- EDP's largest shareholder is China Three Gorges with 22.2%, followed by BlackRock with 8.6% (Wikipedia, EDP Group, as of October 2026).
- In November 2021 Fundação EDP and Fundação de Serralves announced a plan to move MAAT's management to Serralves. It was scaled back: MAAT stays under Fundação EDP, which funds one major Serralves exhibition each year (ECO).
- No reporting found of public grants or other donors.

Red flags: Fossil fuels, EDP
- Climáximo, a climate group, painted MAAT's facade and covered its staircase in red paint on 3 December 2023 and called EDP one of Portugal's largest importers of fossil fuels for power generation, using the foundation to improve its image (Diário de Notícias).
- EDP condemned the vandalism and said about 80% of its energy is renewable and it closed its Portuguese coal plant in 2021 (Diário de Notícias, EDP's statement).
- EDP's subsidiaries include EDP Gás and Naturgás (Wikipedia, EDP Group).

Red flags: EDP leadership case
- António Mexia, EDP's chairman, and João Manso Neto, chairman of EDP Renováveis, became formal suspects in June 2017 and were accused of four counts of active corruption and one of economic participation in business over the CMEC contracts. Prosecutors allege benefits to EDP of about 1.2 billion euros (24noticias, 6 July 2020).
- EDP says no irregularity can be attributed to it and the payments were fair. No verdict is reported in the sources opened.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the programme; the criticism found targets the building, the funder and a planned change of management.
1. Metropolis: Francisco Moura Veiga, "Portugal's MAAT Museum a Monument to Corporate Ego" (2017)
URL: https://metropolismag.com/viewpoints/portugal-maat-museum-monument-corporate-ego/
- Calls the building a "corporate maison" that ignores its surroundings and presents itself as an isolated jewel.
- Says EDP, exempt from public procurement rules, commissioned Levete directly and used the building as a marketing tool.
- Finds the exhibition spaces work well; criticises the building, not the programme.
2. Associação de Artistas Visuais Portugueses, statement on the Serralves and EDP plan (10 December 2021; reported by 24noticias)
URL: https://24noticias.sapo.pt/atualidade/artigos/associacao-de-artistas-visuais-desagradada-com-protocolo-entre-serralves-e-fundacao-edp
- Warned of business-minded cultural policy leading to uniform programming and weaker critical discourse (translation).
- Asked who would manage MAAT's contemporary art collection and the EDP prizes. Artist-association statement, not an art outlet.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is EDP's: the museum is a corporate foundation project, with no public or outside donors found. The weak points are EDP's gas and fuel business, named in the 2023 Climáximo protest, and the corruption case against its former chairman, which is not tied to the museum. Watch EDP's transfers to the foundation and the Serralves ties.
