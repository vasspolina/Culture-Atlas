MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART BELGRADE (MUZEJ SAVREMENE UMETNOSTI): Belgrade, Serbia
ID: museum-of-contemporary-art-belgrade
Website: https://msub.org.rs (checked 2026-10-24; the English page /en returned an error, the Serbian documents page loaded)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern and contemporary art in Ušće park, New Belgrade, at the Sava-Danube junction across from the Belgrade Fortress (museum history page).
- Its activities began in 1958, when the Cultural Council of the Belgrade People's Committee established the Modern Gallery; the building opened as the Museum of Contemporary Art on 20 October 1965, with Miodrag B. Protić as founder and first director (museum history page).
- Designed by Ivan Antić and Ivanka Raspopović, winners of an open competition in 1960 (museum history page).
- A renovation began in 2007; the first contractor, Montera, went bankrupt, and the museum reopened on 20 October 2017 (Wikipedia).
- Collects work produced in Serbia and Yugoslavia since 1900, over 35,000 works (Wikipedia).
- Also runs the Salon of the Museum and the Gallery-Legacy of Milica Zorić and Rodoljub Čolaković, both with free admission.
- Hours listed on the history page: main building 11:00 to 19:00 (Thursday and Saturday 12:00 to 20:00), closed Tuesdays. The pages read give no main-building ticket price or accessibility details.

FUNDING
Museum of Contemporary Art Belgrade: mainly public: Serbia's Ministry of Culture
- The documents page posts Ministry of Culture financial plans for 2023 to 2025, museum financial plans for 2021 to 2026, final accounts for 2020 and 2021, a balance sheet, a budget execution report and a 2022 annual report.
- In 2019 the museum's board sent the Ministry a report on four director candidates, and the Minister nominated one in March 2019 (CIMAM).
- The museum's own pages list no funders or board members.
- No reporting found of a corporate sponsor.

Red flags: Government replaced the acting director (30 January 2020)
- A public competition for director opened in January 2019. The board recommended four applicants to the Ministry: Slobodan Nakarada, Zoran Erić, Branislav Dimitrijević and Vladislav Šćepanović. In March 2019 the Minister nominated Šćepanović, but no further steps were taken (CIMAM).
- On 30 January 2020 the government named Viktor Kiš acting director. He had not applied to the competition (Danas, 3 February 2020). Prime Minister Ana Brnabić made the appointment at the initiative of the "Council for Creative Industries", which CIMAM calls an alternative to the Ministry.
- Board chair Nikola Šuica resigned, writing that the board's work had been reduced to approving budget and legal matters without advance information (Danas).
- CIMAM, the international committee of modern art museums, issued a statement on 18 March 2020 calling for a return to the 2019 shortlist.
- Autumn 2020: curators and the Independent Cultural Scene of Serbia (NKSS) protested the removal of Zoran Todorović's installation "Toplina" from the acquisitions exhibition. Unverified: NKSS says Kiš removed it to make room for a museum café (021.rs, 31 October 2020); N1 reports Kiš did not answer questions about the work directly. NKSS demanded his removal.
- On 11 December 2020 the government appointed Vladislav Šćepanović board president (Novosti). The museum's history page lists Marijana Kolarić as director from 2021.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism comes from museum professionals' bodies and local press, not the international art press, and concerns government control of the director post, not funders.
1. CIMAM: "Belgrade: Leading Institution Damaged by Local Politics" (18 March 2020)
URL: https://www.cimam.org/museum-watch/museum-watch-actions/belgrade-leading-institution-damaged-local-politics/
- Says acting director Nakarada was released "without any explanation" and that the authorities gave no proper reason for ignoring the open call.
- Names Brnabić and the Council for Creative Industries, and notes the board president and one other board member resigned.
- Calls for a return to the 2019 shortlist.
2. CIMAM: Dragana Nikoletić, NIN, republished as "CIMAM points irregularities regarding appointment of Viktor Kish" (NIN 26 March 2020, CIMAM 8 April 2020)
URL: https://cimam.org/news-archive/cimam-points-irregularities-regarding-appointment-viktor-kish-leading-position-museum-contemporary-art-belgrade/
- Quotes CIMAM's "deep concern about abandoning the good practice of competent museum management" and says an acting role "always indicates a crisis, a temporary problem, or a weakness in management".
3. N1: unsigned, "Kafić i venčanice umesto savremene umetnosti u MSUB, kustosi protiv" (30 October 2020)
URL: https://n1info.rs/kultura/a666785-Kafic-i-vencanice-umesto-savremene-umetnosti-u-MSUB-kustosi-protiv/
- Curators including Zoran Erić objected to removing "Toplina" for a café and to hosting Jean-Paul Gaultier's wedding-dress exhibition "Love is Love", which they said does not suit the programme.
- Kiš said the acquisitions exhibition ended in late October. Local news outlet, not art press.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is public and the museum posts financial plans and accounts, so the funding is traceable. The weak point is political control of the director post: the government overrode a 2019 competition in January 2020, the board chair resigned and museum bodies protested. Worth watching: how the Ministry fills the board and director posts.
