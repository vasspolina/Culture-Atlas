ID: la-virreina-barcelona

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 16 June 2016 La Virreina Centre de la Imatge closed completely during a strike against subcontracting by workers of Serveis Educatius Ciut'art, the contractor for visitor services at several Barcelona museums, called by the union SUT. It was one of four venues that closed completely; the Institut de Cultura de Barcelona (ICUB) said 11 museums were affected to varying degrees (NacióDigital).
- The workers demanded "la regularització dels contractes en frau de llei i el reconeixement del caràcter indefinit de les relacions laborals" (translated: the regularisation of contracts in breach of the law and recognition of permanent employment), the correct allocation of holidays and an end to the discriminatory treatment of reinforcement staff (NacióDigital).
- ICUB said the city council had set up a mediation table bringing together the companies and the union sections (NacióDigital).
