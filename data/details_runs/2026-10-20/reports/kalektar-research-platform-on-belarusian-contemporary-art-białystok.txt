KALEKTAR RESEARCH PLATFORM ON BELARUSIAN CONTEMPORARY ART: Białystok, Poland
ID: kalektar-research-platform-on-belarusian-contemporary-art-białystok
Website: https://kalektar.org/about/ (checked 2026-10-25; loads)

OVERVIEW
- Online research platform, archive and journal on contemporary Belarusian art, described by its site as "a tool for archiving, analyzing and popularizing contemporary Belarusian art" (KALEKTAR). It has no public venue; the site is in English, Belarusian and Russian.
- Founded in the second half of 2014 by Sergey Kiryushchenko and Sergey Shabohin; the portal launched on 30 March 2015 and was officially presented in Minsk (KALEKTAR history). Shabohin has been co-founder and editor-in-chief since 2015 (Contexts).
- The history page says the project shifted from a proposed private museum to an open online archive, and that the artists developed the concept with the curator Alyaksei Barysyonka. The site does not explain the name.
- Registered in Białystok because of risks in Belarus, as the platform states; it moved temporarily to Poznań in September 2021, and its partners page says Gray Mandorla Studio hosts its office temporarily (KALEKTAR history; partners page).
- Main projects: the INDEX encyclopedia and the ZBOR essay collection, launched on 19 May 2015, exhibitions in Minsk, Białystok, Kyiv and Wrocław, a new website in November 2021 and a journal of Belarusian visual art from May 2023 (KALEKTAR history).
- Visiting: online; No reporting found of an address, opening days or admission on the pages opened.

FUNDING
KALEKTAR: grant-funded: Rönne Stiftung (main financial partner) and the German Federal Foreign Office, Civil Society Cooperation
- The about page states the site is developed with support from the German Federal Foreign Office, Civil Society Cooperation, and the Rönne-Stiftung.
- The partners page names the Rönne Stiftung as main financial partner: a fund founded in Berlin in 2020, for which this platform was its first project. It lists the German Federal Foreign Office and Civil Society Cooperation as supporters of "the Foundation" (KALEKTAR partners page).
- The history page says the new portal of 2021 was implemented "thanks to the support of the Federal Foreign Office, Civil Society Cooperation and the fund", the 2022 residence in Poznań had "the support of the Rönne Stiftung and the Domie space", and the 2023 journal was developed "thanks to funding from the fund with the support of the German Federal Foreign Office".
- No amounts are published. No reporting found of the funding of the platform from 2014 to 2020, of annual reports, of accounts or of a board; the team page lists roles without names.
- The partners page also lists Gallery Arsenal in Białystok, DOMIE (a free space in Poznań), Gray Mandorla Studio and the cultural club I-DZE-JA; it states no amounts for any of them.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Results for the platform came from its own site and from Shabohin's personal pages.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind KALEKTAR is a Berlin fund and the German Federal Foreign Office, with no flagged funder found; neither is the Belarusian state. The weak points are that no amounts or years are published for each grant and that no accounts or named board are posted. Worth watching: the Rönne Stiftung's own funding, since the platform was its first project.
