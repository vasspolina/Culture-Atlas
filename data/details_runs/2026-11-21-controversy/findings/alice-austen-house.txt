ID: alice-austen-house

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 31 July 1994 the Lesbian Avengers disrupted the annual Nautical Festival at the Alice Austen House, wearing old-fashioned bathing suits and life preservers labelled "Dyke Preservers", and proclaimed the museum "a national lesbian landmark". The protest followed the Board of Directors' public denial of Alice Austen's lesbianism (Gotham Center for New York City History, Bonnie Yochelson, "The Public meets Alice Austen").
