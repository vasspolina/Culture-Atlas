FRIDERICIANUM: Kassel, Germany
ID: fridericianum-kassel
Website: https://www.fridericianum.org/ (checked 2026-10-20; the record's website field points to a Monopol article instead)

OVERVIEW
- Kunsthalle for contemporary art at Friedrichsplatz 18 in central Kassel, and the central venue of documenta since the first edition in 1955 (Wikipedia).
- Completed in 1779 for Landgrave Friedrich II to show the landgraves' art collections; German Wikipedia calls it "eines der ersten öffentlichen Kunstmuseen auf dem europäischen Kontinent". A Kunsthalle since 1988, opened by Veit Loers (Wikipedia).
- Architect Simon Louis du Ry. It served as the Westphalian estates' palace from 1810 to 1813, and its library burned in a bombing raid in September 1941 with about 350,000 volumes lost (Wikipedia).
- Directors have included René Block, Rein Wolfs and Susanne Pfeffer; Moritz Wesseler has led it since November 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Programme in 2026 includes interventions by Raque Ford, Karl Holmqvist and Kerstin Brätsch (stated by the space).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday and public holidays 11 am to 6 pm, Thursday to 8 pm (stated by the space). No reporting found of admission prices or accessibility.

FUNDING
Fridericianum: public: the City of Kassel and the State of Hesse in equal shares, through documenta und Museum Fridericianum gGmbH
- The building is owned by the State of Hesse and run by documenta und Museum Fridericianum gGmbH (Wikipedia).
- That company is "funded to an equal extent by the City of Kassel and the State of Hesse" (documenta Code of Conduct). Its managing director is Andreas Hoffmann (Monopol).
- No reporting found of annual amounts, private sponsors or accounts.

Red flags: show cancelled over the documenta code of conduct (2025)
- After the antisemitism scandal at documenta fifteen, documenta and the Fridericianum adopted a Code of Conduct, posted in February 2025, that treats the IHRA working definition of antisemitism as binding (Monopol; documenta; ArtAsiaPacific).
- The code lets documenta step in if the supervisory board finds "artistic forms of expression to be in conflict with [its] principles" (ArtAsiaPacific).
- A Marisa Merz exhibition planned from late August 2025 to January 2026 did not take place: the Fondazione Merz withdrew because its president Beatrice Merz does not fully agree with the IHRA definition and preferred the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism (Monopol, 4 December 2025).
- Beatrice Merz: "Ich bin der Überzeugung, dass Kunst nicht durch Grenzen eingeschränkt werden sollte" (Monopol).
- Hoffmann said the framework "was not entirely compatible" and the code binds staff but not the artistic team or outsiders; works by Robert Grosvenor and Portia Zvavahera were shown instead (Monopol). Finestre sull'Arte quotes Hoffmann saying the framework was "not entirely compatible".

Overall: The criticism is of the documenta code of conduct that governs the Fridericianum, not of its programme; the one case that names it is the Merz withdrawal.

WIDER CONTEXT
- ArtAsiaPacific (2025) reported that critics denounced the code, worried it could be used to suppress pro-Palestinian artists.
- The Fridericianum is run by the same company and bound by the same code, which is how the Merz show fell through.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is public: the City of Kassel and the State of Hesse fund the company that runs it, with no private sponsor on record. The weak point is political control through the documenta code of conduct, which cost it the Marisa Merz show in 2025. Worth watching: whether other artists or lenders withdraw over the code, and documenta 16.
