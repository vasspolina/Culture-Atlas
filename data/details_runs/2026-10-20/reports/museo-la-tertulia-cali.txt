MUSEO LA TERTULIA: Cali, Colombia
ID: museo-la-tertulia-cali
Website: https://museolatertulia.com/

OVERVIEW
- Modern and contemporary art museum on the bank of the Cali River at El Charco del Burro, in the west of Cali (Wikipedia, El País).
- Founded on 9 March 1956 in a house in the San Antonio neighbourhood rented by the journalist Alfonso Bonilla Aragón (Wikipedia). El País (Rosa Isabel Pelaez, 9 March 2023) credits a group of Cali cultural figures led by Maritza Uribe Urdinola; Wikipedia says Clara Inés Suárez and Maritza Uribe de Urdinola set up the cultural group. It began as a free school for 50 low-income pupils and a café bar with talks, exhibitions and film at night (El País). Earlier name: Museo de Arte Moderno La Tertulia, changed in 2016 (El País).
- A "tertulia" is a social gathering, especially to talk about art or literature (English Wikipedia).
- The foundational building was due to open on 20 June 1968 (Spanish Wikipedia); it was built by the firm Lago, Sáenz y Edmond Cobo from plans by Manuel Lago Franco (El País). Benjamín Barney Caldas designed extensions from 1982 (Spanish Wikipedia). The site was vacant land left when the Cali River was diverted in 1958 (El País). The Casa Obeso Mejía, built in 1945, joined the museum in 2015, and a pedestrian bridge over the river linking the museum to Paseo de los Gatos and the Casa Obeso was completed in 2020 (El País).
- On site: a main gallery, the Cinemateca film theatre, a printing and restoration workshop building, a children's workshop and a documentation centre (Wikipedia). The collection holds more than 1,900 pieces, mostly Colombian and Latin American (El País); 80% of it is on paper (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Visiting (El País, August 2024): open Tuesday 10:00 to 18:00, Wednesday to Saturday 10:00 to 19:00, Sunday 14:00 to 18:00, closed Monday. Gallery tickets were 20,000 pesos general, 14,000 for Colombians and 9,000 for students; Cinemateca tickets 12,000 general and 9,000 for students.

FUNDING
Museo La Tertulia: partners named by the museum are Colegio Alemán de Cali, Bancolombia, Fundación Sura, Comfandi and Fundación Scarpetta Gnecco; Ministry of Culture project calls
- Colegio Alemán de Cali is named among the "patrocinadores y aliados estratégicos" in the museum's 24 June 2025 anniversary statement, written by its communications coordinator Laura Sáenz Cervantes and published on the school's site (stated by the museum).
- Bancolombia: partner of the education programme "Museo + Escuela", which turns school classrooms into spaces for art (stated by the museum, June 2025).
- Fundación Sura: partner of "Barrio Adentro", which supports neighbourhood research projects on local memory (stated by the museum, June 2025).
- Comfandi: partner of "Tertulia Itinerante", which takes works from the collection to different parts of Cali (stated by the museum, June 2025).
- Fundación Scarpetta Gnecco: partner of "Conexión Maestro", workshops and visits for teachers (stated by the museum, June 2025).
- Ministry of Culture: the Reactivarte 20x21 call added works by 21 artists and collectives to the collection in 2021 (El País, 2023).
- Collection: mostly donations from artists, with more than 1,900 pieces. On 15 May 2017 the heirs of the artist Ever Astudillo lent 167 works for 20 years (El País, 2023).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum names Colegio Alemán de Cali, Bancolombia, Fundación Sura, Comfandi and Fundación Scarpetta Gnecco as its partners as of June 2025. The Ministry of Culture's Reactivarte 20x21 call added works to the collection in 2021, and artists' donations built most of it.
