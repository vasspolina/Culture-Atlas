FONDATION CAB: Brussels, Belgium
ID: fondation-cab-brussels
Website: https://fondationcab.com/about-us

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit space for minimal and conceptual art at Rue Borrens 32-34, 1050 Ixelles, Brussels, near the Étangs d'Ixelles (Brussels Museums; the foundation's website).
- Founded in 2012 by the collector and property developer Hubert Bonnet; he found the 800 m2 former coal warehouse and is involved in its programming (Wikipedia on Bonnet).
- Building: a 1930s Art Deco warehouse built for the coal mining industry, restored by the architect Olivier Dwek into an 800 m2 open, modular exhibition space, with a studio for artists in residence (the foundation's website).
- Programme: two exhibitions a year, one major show of internationally known minimal art and one of young artists inspired by minimalism, plus special projects from galleries, museums and curators (the foundation's website).
- A second site, Fondation CAB in Saint-Paul-de-Vence in a 1950s building renovated by Charles Zana, opened in 2021 and shows the collection with two themed exhibitions a year linked to Brussels (La Libre, Guy Duplat, 13 July 2021).
- Director: Eléonore de Sadeleer (the foundation's website).
- Open Wednesday to Saturday 12:00 to 18:00; free for students and children; wheelchair accessible with an adapted toilet; assistance dogs allowed; the museumpass is accepted (Brussels Museums).

FUNDING
Fondation CAB (Brussels): private: founder Hubert Bonnet's property and finance wealth, with paying members and corporate partners
- Bonnet is a Belgian businessman and collector; after his mother died in 1996 he returned to Belgium, became a director of Cobepa until its 2000 takeover, then founded H Group, which develops luxury residences (Wikipedia).
- La Libre describes him as an heir to the family that ran the Forges de Clabecq steelworks and says he holds 70% of the Flagey building in Brussels together with the Moorgat family (La Libre, 2021).
- Wikipedia states that his parents' family, the Germeau-Bonnet, sold its Clabecq stake to Cobepa, a holding company of the Paribas group, in 1974.
- Supporters pay EUR 500 a year as members or EUR 2,000 a year as friends; the support page lists 54 entries by name, including Bozar Bruxelles, of which 13 are tagged as friends (the foundation's website).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Fondation CAB is its founder Hubert Bonnet's private wealth from real estate and finance, plus membership fees of EUR 500 and EUR 2,000 a year. His family sold its Clabecq steelworks stake in 1974. The support page lists 54 named supporters, including Bozar Bruxelles.
