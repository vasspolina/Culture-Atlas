POST-MUSEUM: Singapore, Singapore
ID: post-museum-singapore
Website: https://post-museum.org

OVERVIEW
- Independent cultural and social space in Singapore that describes itself as an open platform for examining contemporary life, promoting the arts and connecting people (Post-Museum site).
- Founded in 2007 by the husband-and-wife artist-activists Jennifer Teo and Woon Tien Wei (On Curating interview; Artshelp).
- From 2007 to 2011 it ran a physical space in two shophouses on Rowell Road in the Kampong Kapur area; it has operated nomadically since, holding events in different venues (On Curating; Post-Museum site).
- The founders describe going nomadic as a way to "practice the city" (Artshelp).
- Projects include Awaken the Dragon (2012) and the Bukit Brown Index (2014 onward), which used hauntings and magical creatures to read contested places, plus the recurring Singapore Really Really Free Market (Post-Museum site).
- Its Soup Kitchen project involves volunteers working with the urban poor in a neighbourhood (On Curating).
- Bamboo Broadcast Studio is described as its current project, part of the 2024 Singapore Art Week exhibition Alternative Ecology, with free programmes on sustainability, nature and ecology (Artshelp).
- Visiting: the site says it is "currently operating nomadically", and its site lists social media channels as its contact.

FUNDING
Post-Museum: independent: no government funding in its early years, according to the founders
- In the On Curating interview (Carolina Sanchez, Issue 41) the founders said of the early years: "we didn't want to take any funding from government agencies."
- The interviewer's question described the funding as individual contributions with very little corporate sponsorship, and the interviewer's introduction describes the venue as a rental space (On Curating).
- The founders' position has since shifted: "we want to see more public engagement with government agencies" (On Curating), which Artshelp describes as a wish for larger-scale collaborations.

BOTTOM LINE
The On Curating interview describes Post-Museum's money as individual contributions and venue rental, with the founders declining government funding in the early years. The founders now say they want more public engagement with government agencies.
