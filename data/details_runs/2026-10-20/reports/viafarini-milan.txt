VIAFARINI: Milan, Italy
ID: viafarini-milan
Website: https://www.viafarini.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit association for contemporary art in Milan, an Italian APS, combining exhibitions, residencies, education, a coworking service and a visual arts archive (Viafarini website).
- Founded in 1991 to support young artists' professional growth; Artribune describes it as modelled on the European Kunstverein, a project room for exchanges between artists and professionals (Artribune, Giulia Ronchi, 9 June 2020).
- It began working with Careof in 1994. In 2008, together with Careof, it won a Comune di Milano competition for spaces at the Fabbrica del Vapore, where the DOCVA archive opened, and its Viafarini-in-residence programme began the same year at the original Via Farini location (Artribune).
- In 2017, through the "Spazi al Talento" call, it took over about 840 square metres on the first floor of the Fabbrica del Vapore, combining the archive with a creative coworking space (Artribune).
- Venues: the archive at via Procaccini 4 (Fabbrica del Vapore), open Monday to Friday 11:00 to 18:00; via Carlo Farini 35; and Viafarini.work at via Marco d'Agrate 33. Each offers studio visits by appointment (Viafarini website).
- The team includes Patrizia Brusarosco (direction and president of the board), Giulio Verago (curation) and Ibrahima Mbengue (documentation); Angela Vettese has been honorary president since 1995 (Viafarini website).

FUNDING
Viafarini: public spaces and institutional support, foundations, companies, coworking fees and donations
- The Viafarini supporters page, under "Supporto istituzionale negli anni", lists with start years: Regione Lombardia (from 1994), Comune di Milano (from 1995), Fondazione Cariplo (from 1997), Fondazione Banca del Monte di Lombardia (from 2008), and departments of the Italian Ministry of Culture (from 2008, 2016 and 2019).
- Its Fabbrica del Vapore spaces came through Comune di Milano calls in 2008 and 2017 (Artribune).
- The same page lists companies including UniCredit, Vodafone, Zanotta and Furla, and training institutions including Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera, NABA, Politecnico di Milano and Università Bocconi.
- Coworking users pay a fee for a workstation, and tax-deductible donations (erogazioni liberali) are accepted (Viafarini website).

BOTTOM LINE
Viafarini is a non-profit association whose board president is Patrizia Brusarosco. It won its spaces through Comune di Milano calls in 2008 and 2017, and its supporters page lists regional, municipal and national public bodies, two foundations and companies.
