FRIART KUNSTHALLE FRIBOURG: Fribourg, Switzerland
ID: friart-kunsthalle-fribourg
Website: https://friart.ch

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Petites-Rames 22 in the lower town of Fribourg, on the border between French- and German-speaking Switzerland (Friart).
- Founded in 1981; it describes itself as independent (Friart).
- Housed since 1991 in an industrial building at Petites-Rames 22, whose spaces are in a former cardboard factory: a ground floor exhibition space of about 100 square metres, an L-shaped first floor space of about 70 square metres, reception and a bookstore in the basement and an apartment for visiting artists on the third floor (Sculpture Network).
- Gave Roman Signer (1992), Thomas Hirschhorn (1995) and Latifa Echakhch (2010) their first exhibitions in Switzerland; presented and commissioned work by Ei Arakawa (2023) and Sara Deraedt (2024) (Friart).
- Open Wednesday to Friday 12:00 to 18:00 and Saturday and Sunday 13:00 to 18:00, closed Monday and Tuesday; admission is free (Friart).

Friart is supported by the Canton, the Agglomeration and the City of Fribourg, by Loterie Romande, by the Banque Cantonale de Fribourg, which makes free admission possible, and by the Sandoz family foundation. All are named on the space's own supporters page.
