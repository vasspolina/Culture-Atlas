BROOKLYN MUSEUM: New York, USA
ID: brooklyn-museum-new-york
Website: https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/ (checked 2026-10-20; refused automated checks with HTTP 429)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 200 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn, near Prospect Heights, Crown Heights, Flatbush and Park Slope; about 560,000 square feet, the second-largest museum in New York City (Wikipedia).
- Grew from the Brooklyn Apprentices' Library, founded in August 1823 by citizens including Augustus Graham; the present building opened in October 1897 (Wikipedia).
- Building by McKim, Mead & White; the city owns it, and the museum is run by a nonprofit, the Brooklyn Institute of Arts and Sciences, set up in 1935 (Wikipedia).
- Holds about 500,000 objects, with strengths in Egyptian antiquities and American art (Wikipedia).
- Anne Pasternak has been director, formally the Shelby White and Leon Levy Director, since 2015 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: admission is pay-what-you-wish (Hyperallergic, 2025). No reporting checked of opening hours or accessibility.

FUNDING
Brooklyn Museum: mixed: private contributions with New York City support
- Year to June 2025: revenue $61.7M, of which contributions $46.3M and programme revenue $6.4M; expenses $70.9M; a loss of $9.2M (ProPublica, Form 990).
- Year to June 2024: revenue $68.3M, expenses $77.9M, a loss of $9.6M (ProPublica).
- The museum belongs to New York City's Cultural Institutions Group, which receives city funding; the city gave $50 million in 2021 (Wikipedia).
- The endowment was $175.2M in June 2023, small against peers; the FY2025 operating budget was $64M, about 70% salaries (Hyperallergic, 2025).
- February 2025: facing a $10M deficit, the museum planned to cut 47 full- and part-time posts according to the union District Council 37, freeze hiring and cut senior pay by up to 20% (Hyperallergic).
- BNY Mellon is a corporate partner (protest organisers, Hyperallergic, 2023). No reporting found of the amounts any corporate partner gives.

Red flags: protest over Gaza and corporate ties
- 9 December 2023: hundreds marched on the museum with Within Our Lifetime and Decolonize This Place, asking it to cut ties with companies "complicit" in the war (Hyperallergic).
- Unverified: BNY Mellon holds a $13 billion investment in the Israeli arms maker Elbit Systems and houses the Friends of the Israel Defense Forces donor-advised fund, from the protest organisers, pro-Palestine activist groups (Hyperallergic; The New Arab). The museum denied activists' claims about trustees' links to Israel's military industry (ArtReview).
- 31 May 2024: over 1,000 protesters took over the entrance and plaza; about 80 occupied the lobby; police arrested 34; Deborah Kass's sculpture "OY/YO" was vandalised (The Art Newspaper).
- The May 2024 organisers, the Cultural Front for Free Palestine, demanded disclosure of donor and trustee money tied to the occupation of Palestinian territory (The Art Newspaper).

Red flags: homes of museum leaders vandalised
- 12 June 2024: the homes of Pasternak, who is Jewish, president Kimberly Panicek Trueblood and two trustees were daubed with red paint; a banner called Pasternak a "white-supremacist Zionist" (ArtReview).
- Pasternak said "Violence, vandalism and intimidation have no place in that discourse"; police charged Taylor Pelton, 28, with criminal mischief as a hate crime in August 2024 (ArtReview).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism centres on the museum's silence on Gaza and its handling of protest, plus its finances; Hyperallergic covers it most.
1. Hyperallergic: Valentina Di Liscia, "Artists Wanted to Make a Pro-Palestine Statement. The Brooklyn Museum Said No." (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/984677/artists-wanted-to-make-a-pro-palestine-statement-the-brooklyn-museum-said-no/
- 22 artists in "The Brooklyn Artists Exhibition" asked to hang keffiyehs beside their works; the museum refused, citing loan agreements, and compared the request to showing Israeli or American flags.
- Artist Catherine Tafur called the loan-form reason disingenuous; Wendy Cohen said museums "want the art but not the artist".
- Notes criticism that the museum did not do enough about the police response in May 2024.
2. Hyperallergic: Valentina Di Liscia, "Facing $10M Budget Deficit, Brooklyn Museum Will Lay Off Dozens of Workers" (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/988717/facing-10m-budget-deficit-brooklyn-museum-will-lay-off-dozens-of-workers/
- Pasternak blamed inflation, government money that has not kept pace, a small endowment and pay-what-you-wish admission for a "significant cash flow problem".

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs on private gifts and city support, and has lost about $9 million a year in each of the last two years. The weak points are the disputed tie to BNY Mellon raised by Gaza protesters, the 2024 attacks on its leaders' homes, and the deficit. Worth watching: whether the museum names its corporate partners and what they pay.
