BANGABANDHU MEMORIAL MUSEUM: Dhaka, Bangladesh
ID: bangabandhu-memorial-museum
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20; the museum was demolished in February 2025)

OVERVIEW
- Museum in the house at Road 32, Dhanmondi, Dhaka, home of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman, Bangladesh's founding president, where he and most of his family were killed by army officers on 15 August 1975 (Wikipedia).
- His daughter Sheikh Hasina obtained ownership of the house in June 1981. The museum opened on 14 August 1994 and passed to the Bangabandhu Memorial Trust on 6 September 1994; a six-storey extension opened on 20 August 2011 (Wikipedia).
- Architects Ehsan Khan, Ishtiaque Jahir and Iqbal Habib, chosen by competition, kept the original structure of the house (Wikipedia).
- It showed the family's rooms, with bullet marks in the bedroom, Sheikh Russel's bicycles and toys, letters and manuscripts; a library and research centre opened on the extension's fifth floor in 2011 (UNB, 2020).
- It served as a state stage: on National Mourning Day, 15 August 2023, President Mohammed Shahabuddin and Prime Minister Hasina laid wreaths there, an armed forces contingent gave a state salute, and Hasina laid a further wreath for the Awami League (The Business Standard).
- Protesters set it on fire on 5 August 2024, the day Hasina's government fell (Wikipedia). On 5 February 2025, as Hasina spoke online from exile in India, crowds answering a "Bulldozer Procession" call by Students Against Discrimination set parts of it on fire and demolished it with two excavators and a crane; the group's convenor Hasnat Abdullah called it the "shrine of fascism" (BenarNews).

FUNDING
Bangabandhu Memorial Museum: run by a Hasina family trust funded with bank donations handed to the prime minister
- Operator: the Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Memorial Trust, founded by Hasina on 11 April 1994 (Wikipedia). Hasina chairs it and her sister Sheikh Rehana is a trustee (Financial Express, citing the Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit).
- On 15 May 2017, 37 commercial banks gave over Tk 136 crore through the Bangladesh Association of Banks to the trust, the Prime Minister's Education Assistance Trust and the Shuchona Foundation; Hasina received the money at Gono Bhaban. IFIC, Standard, Prime, Exim and First Security Islami banks gave Tk 5 crore each (The Daily Star).
- On 6 January 2020 Standard Bank's chairman Kazi Akram Uddin Ahmed handed Hasina a Tk 10 crore cheque for the trust to mark Mujib Year (The Business Standard).
- A 2018 notification exempted interest on the trust's deposits from income tax for 10 years; after the change of government the National Board of Revenue revoked it in January 2025 (bdnews24).

Red flags: 2024 to 2026 corruption investigation of the trust's money
- In December 2024 the Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit ordered banks to hand over account records of Hasina, Rehana and the trust, including any accounts that received the trust's money (Financial Express).
- On 11 March 2025 Dhaka Metropolitan Senior Special Judge Md Zakir Hossain Galib froze 124 accounts of the family and linked bodies on an Anti-Corruption Commission petition, among them Tk 4.79 billion in 32 trust accounts and Tk 193.5 million in two accounts of the museum (bdnews24).
- On 3 September 2026 Anti-Corruption Commission Assistant Director Md Ismail Hossain filed a case at the commission's Integrated District Office, Dhaka-1, against Hasina, Rehana and Md Nazrul Islam Mazumder, former president of the Bangladesh Association of Banks (Financial Express; bdnews24).
- Unverified: the commission alleges that the three collected and embezzled Tk 2.76 billion from the CSR funds of 41 banks in the trust's name, that the banks were "forced" to pay although Bangladesh Bank's CSR rules make no provision for such donations, and that investigators found no office activity at the trust's address (case documents, as reported by Financial Express and bdnews24).

WIDER CONTEXT
- BenarNews reported in 2025 that Mujib had fallen out of favour since Hasina made it a crime to speak against him.
- Connection: the museum was the main stage of that state remembrance.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's money was tied to Hasina's office, which is the weak point whatever the court decides. The embezzlement charge itself is unproven. Worth watching: the outcome of the case.
