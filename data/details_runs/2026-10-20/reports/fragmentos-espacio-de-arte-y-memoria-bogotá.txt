FRAGMENTOS, ESPACIO DE ARTE Y MEMORIA: Bogotá, Colombia
ID: fragmentos-espacio-de-arte-y-memoria-bogotá
Website: http://fragmentos.gov.co/Paginas/default.aspx (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Memorial, gallery and counter-monument at Carrera 7 # 6B-30 in La Candelaria, central Bogotá (stated by the space; Wikipedia).
- Created by artist Doris Salcedo with architect Carlos Granada, chosen from 28 entries in an open competition; opened on 10 December 2018 as one of three art works required by the peace agreement with the FARC (Wikipedia; Radio Nacional).
- The floor is 1,288 tiles cast from about 37 tons of steel melted from 8,994 weapons handed in by the FARC; women who survived sexual violence in the armed conflict hammered the sheets (Wikipedia; stated by the space).
- Built among the ruins of an abandoned colonial-era house (Wikipedia).
- Since 2019 the Ministry of Culture and the Museo Nacional have held an annual call for artists' projects that answer the work; shows have included Francis Alÿs (2020 to 2021), Beatriz González (2022 to 2023), Gabriela Golder (2023), Tania Candiani (2024) and Goya's "Los Desastres de la Guerra" (from 5 September 2026) (Wikipedia; Radio Nacional).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 9 am to 5 pm, closed Mondays, 1 January, 1 May and 25 December; free entry and free programmes (stated by the space). No reporting found on accessibility.

FUNDING
Fragmentos: public: the Colombian Ministry of Culture, run by the Museo Nacional
- The government, through the Ministry of Culture, owns and pays for the site; the Museo Nacional de Colombia runs the gallery and its shows (Wikipedia).
- The building and its floor came out of the 2016 peace agreement process with the FARC (Radio Nacional).
- No reporting found of a private sponsor, a budget or amounts.

Red flags: protest over President Duque's use of the space (May 2021)
- On 9 May 2021 President Iván Duque, members of his government and church leaders held a meeting in Fragmentos during the national strike, before the government opened talks with the strikers (El Universal).
- Francis Alÿs's works from Iraq on show there were covered with white fabric; Alÿs, the museum's board and Salcedo were not consulted (Hyperallergic).
- Salcedo said the work had been "abusively used, breaking all the international norms of conservation and copyright", and of the meeting: "It was theater" (Hyperallergic).
- Mexican curator Cuauhtémoc Medina published "El Museo Ocupado": "Así es como los gobernantes vampirizan la cultura" (El Universal).
- El Universal reported that the episode led Alÿs to demand that his show be taken down. No reporting found of whether it was.
- The Ministry of Culture said "en ningún momento se rompió alguna norma" and the works "no presentan ninguna alteración" (El Universal, 14 May 2021).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism is of the state's use of the space in 2021, not of Salcedo's work or the programme. Coverage of the memorial itself is admiring.
1. Hyperallergic: Valentina Di Liscia, "Colombian Government Exploited Doris Salcedo's Art to Denounce Nationwide Protests" (2021)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/645108/colombian-government-exploited-doris-salcedo-art-to-denounce-nationwide-protests/
- Argues the Duque government used a memorial to the conflict's victims as a stage while riot police met the protests with force; it cites dozens of deaths and at least 87 missing.
- Quotes Salcedo's objection and notes that the Ministry of Culture did not respond.
2. El Universal: Sonia Sierra, "Gobernantes vampirizan la cultura" (2021)
URL: https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/cultura/gobernantes-vampirizan-la-cultura-cuestionan-conversacion-del-gobierno-colombiano-e-iglesias/
- Reports Medina's case that the space should serve reflection on the conflict's memory, not political ends, and that Alÿs was not asked.
3. El Universal: Sonia Sierra, "Se protegió la obra de Francis Alÿs" (2021)
URL: https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/cultura/se-protegio-la-obra-de-francis-alys-ministerio-de-cultura-de-colombia/
- Not criticism: the Ministry of Culture's answer, which says the space was chosen "dada su importancia simbólica".

WIDER CONTEXT
- During the 2021 national strike the state met protests over poverty, corruption and inequality with militarised riot police; the government called the protests "incitement of violence, hate, discord, and the destruction of society" (Hyperallergic).
- Fragmentos is a Ministry of Culture site, and the government used it for its own meeting in the middle of that crackdown.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is state money alone: the Ministry of Culture owns and funds it and the Museo Nacional runs it, with no private sponsor. The weak point is control: in May 2021 the president used the memorial for a political meeting over the artist's objection. Worth watching: whether later governments use the space the same way.
