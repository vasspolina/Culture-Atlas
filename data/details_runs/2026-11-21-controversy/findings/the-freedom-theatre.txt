ID: the-freedom-theatre

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 12 December 2023 Israeli soldiers arrested the artistic director Ahmed Tobasi and a recent theatre graduate. The Irish Times reported that both were released days later and that the Israel Defense Forces declined to comment on the arrests (The Irish Times, 23 June 2024). Tobasi told Al Jazeera he was handcuffed and blindfolded before soldiers kicked him in the head and stomach, and that "They did not tell me why they were there" (Al Jazeera, 20 December 2023).
- On 31 December 2023 an Israeli military court ordered administrative detention without charge for Mustafa Sheta, the theatre's producer and general manager. PEN International gives the order as six months, renewed several times until his release. The Irish Times gave it as four months, extended in April 2024 for another four, and reported that the Israel Defense Forces declined to comment on his detention.
- Mondoweiss (Majd Jawad, 15 November 2025) reported that after the arrests and the attack on the theatre, "followed by the army's looting of its lighting and sound equipment", its productions came to a complete halt, and that the theatre was "forced to close its doors entirely" after Israel's Operation Iron Wall displaced the camp's entire population in early 2025. It now works from a temporary location in central Jenin, where about a third of its staff from before 7 October 2023 still come to work.
- These are acts of the Israeli state, which neither funds nor runs the theatre.
