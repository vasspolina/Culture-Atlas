ARTIUM MUSEOA: Vitoria-Gasteiz, Spain
ID: artium-museoa-vitoria-gasteiz
Website: https://www.artium.eus

OVERVIEW
- Basque Museum Centre of Contemporary Art at C/ Francia 24, Vitoria-Gasteiz (Álava tourism board).
- Opened on 26 April 2002 (Álava tourism board). The Diputación Foral de Álava, the provincial council, created the managing foundation as its sole founder: the foundation was constituted by public deed on 16 February 2001 under Foral Decree 112/2000 of 14 November 2000 (Basque Government public-sector register, 2023).
- Wikipedia says the museum occupies a former bus station and names José Luis Caton as architect.
- The collection holds more than 3,000 works, from paintings to multimedia installations; the provincial council began building it in the last quarter of the twentieth century (Álava tourism board).
- The foundation's stated purposes are to run the museum, conserve its holdings, exhibit the collections, support study and research, and take part in museology training (Basque Government register).
- Visiting: general admission 6 euros, free for under-14s; a pay-what-you-decide rate on Wednesdays and the weekend after an exhibition opens, and every day for students, unemployed people, pensioners and under-14s; annual pass 10 euros. Wheelchairs and strollers for the galleries are available from the cloakroom; the audio guide is free with admission (Álava tourism board).

FUNDING
Artium Museoa: mainly public, run by a foundation the Álava provincial council founded and holds as sole capital contributor
- The Diputación Foral de Álava contributed the foundation's entire initial fund of 180,304 euros and is listed as holding 100% of the capital (Basque Government register, 2023 and 2024).
- Board (2024 listing): chair Ramiro González Vicente and vice-chair Ana María Del Val Sancho, both for the Diputación Foral de Álava, which holds three further seats; the Basque Government holds two seats; the Vitoria-Gasteiz city council one; the Ministry of Education, Culture and Sport one; the Diputación Foral de Bizkaia the secretary's seat, held by Susana Guede Arana (Basque Government register, 2024).
- Two private members sit on the board: Diario El Correo S.A. (Iñigo Barrenechea Lombardero) and Fundación Vital (Silvia García de Garayo Díaz in the 2024 listing; Iñigo Recio Alvarez in 2023, as the register prints the name) (Basque Government register).
- Total assets were 55,416,037 euros in 2023 and 55,424,946 euros in 2024; net worth 54,674,414 euros and 54,593,233 euros. Sales and other commercial income were 103,194 euros in 2023 and 118,002 euros in 2024, with an average of 22.46 and 23.63 employees (Basque Government register).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Artium is public: the Álava provincial council founded and holds the foundation, with the Basque Government, the Vitoria-Gasteiz council and the culture ministry on its board. The two private seats, held by Diario El Correo and Fundación Vital, are the points to watch.
