Red flags: 2023 dispute with Italy over the Discobolus Lancellotti and its base
- Stephan Verger, director of the Museo Nazionale Romano, wrote to the head of the Glyptothek asking for the return of the statue's 18th-century marble base, which stayed in Munich when the Discobolus went back to Italy on 16 November 1948 (Open, 2 December 2023).
- Open published the Munich reply: "Non sono nella condizioni di abbandonare la nostra rivendicazione legale di una restituzione del Discoboo al nostro museo" ("I am not in a position to abandon our legal claim to a return of the Discobolus to our museum", translated), and "Il rimpatrio in Italia ha violato la legge, secondo l'opinione legale dello stato bavarese e del nostro museo" ("The repatriation to Italy broke the law, in the legal opinion of the Bavarian state and of our museum", translated) (Open, 2 December 2023).
- Italy's culture minister Gennaro Sangiuliano said "devono passare sul mio cadavere" ("they will have to pass over my dead body", translated) and asked for the base as well (Open, 2 December 2023).
- On 5 December 2023 Sangiuliano said the Discobolus is "patrimonio della Nazione e proprietà dello Stato italiano" ("heritage of the Nation and property of the Italian State", translated) and will stay in the Museo Nazionale Romano; after meeting the German ambassador Hans-Dieter Lucas, he said the Berlin government was not seeking the statue, called the Munich director's letter unacceptable and said the director owed an apology (Italian Ministry of Culture, 5 December 2023).
- Florian Knauss, director of the Glyptothek, told Finestre sull'Arte that "the Glyptothek has not requested the return of the Lancellotti Disk sculpture and does not intend to do so" (Finestre sull'Arte, 5 December 2023).
- On 11 December 2023 the Uffizi director Eike Schmidt said Germany must return the base to Rome as soon as possible; the Glyptothek said talks on the base were going on (Abendzeitung, 11 December 2023). Knauss said he and Verger wanted to talk soon and that he was "fermamente convinto che raggiungeremo un accordo amichevole" ("firmly convinced that we will reach an amicable agreement", translated) (Libero, 12 December 2023).
