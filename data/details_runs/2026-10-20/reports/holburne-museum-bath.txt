HOLBURNE MUSEUM: Bath, UK
ID: holburne-museum-bath
Website: https://holburne.org

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at the end of Great Pulteney Street in Bath, which calls itself "Bath's first art museum" (holburne.org, Wikipedia).
- The collection of Sir William Holburne, over 4,000 objects, pictures and books, was bequeathed to the people of Bath by his sister, Mary Anne Barbara Holburne, in 1882 (Wikipedia).
- Named for Sir William Holburne, who inherited the baronetcy in 1820 (Wikipedia).
- Housed since 1916 in the former Sydney Hotel. Eric Parry designed a £11.2 million extension that opened in May 2011 (Wikipedia).
- Critics objected to the modernist faience cladding of the extension, which replaced traditional Bath Stone; Bath and North East Somerset Council's planning committee approved the design in 2008 (Wikipedia).
- Open daily 10am to 5pm, Sundays and bank holidays 11am to 5pm, last admission 4.30pm; closed 24 to 26 December and 1 January. Members go free (holburne.org).

FUNDING
Holburne Museum: charity supported by trusts and foundations, National Lottery funds, patrons and corporate supporters listed on its supporters page
- Governed by a sole trustee, The Holburne Museum Trust Company, a company limited by guarantee; registered charity 310288. Financial reports for 2016 to 2024 are posted (holburne.org governance page).
- Public funders listed on the museum's supporters page: the National Lottery Community Fund, the National Lottery Heritage Fund and Postcode Neighbourhood Trust (holburne.org supporters page). Wikipedia says the Heritage Lottery Fund supported the restoration and extension.
- Trusts and foundations listed on the supporters page include AKO Foundation, Art Fund, the Blavatnik Family Foundation, the Clore Duffield Foundation, the Garfield Weston Foundation, the Henry Moore Foundation, the Linbury Trust, the Wolfson Foundation and the Headley Trust (holburne.org supporters page).
- Art Fund gave £44,595 in a Reimagine grant announced on 4 February 2022 to rework the museum's online collection with The Studio and Bath Spa University (holburne.org grant page).
- The supporters page says the museum's website was developed with support from Bloomberg Philanthropies through its Digital Accelerator Programme (holburne.org supporters page).
- Galleries named on the supporters page: Hauser & Wirth, White Cube, Ingleby Gallery, The Modern Institute and Thomas Dane Gallery (holburne.org supporters page).
- Individual giving is organised in a Director's Circle, Major Donors, Patrons and an 1882 Circle (holburne.org supporters page).
- During the 2020 closure the museum crowdfunded, raising £23,000 of a £50,000 target by 19 April 2020 (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The Holburne is supported by trusts and foundations, National Lottery bodies, private patrons and galleries, all named on its supporters page. The Blavatnik Family Foundation and Bloomberg Philanthropies appear as entries on that page.
