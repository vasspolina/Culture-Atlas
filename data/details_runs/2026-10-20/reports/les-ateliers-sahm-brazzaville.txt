LES ATELIERS SAHM: Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo
ID: les-ateliers-sahm-brazzaville

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre with its headquarters in Mpissa-Bacongo, in the second arrondissement of Brazzaville (ADIAC, 2023).
- Founded by the artist and writer Bill Kouélany (Wikipedia); it opened in September 2012 (Almayuda) under his artistic direction (Contemporary And).
- Facilities: an exhibition and screening room, a space for art criticism, a media archive, four workshop rooms and an esplanade for events (Almayuda).
- Programmes: the annual Rencontre Internationale de l'Art Contemporain (RIAC) since 2012, the exhibition "Shared Aesthetics beyond Geographies" at Dak'art Off in Dakar since 2014, the children's workshops Bana'arts since 2016, and a mentoring and residency programme (Almayuda).
- Kouélany exhibited at Documenta 12 in 2007 and received the Prince Claus Prize in 2019 (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Les Ateliers Sahm: project-funded: a foundation, the Institut Français and the European Union fund individual programmes
- Almayuda states that it has supported the centre's most notable activities since 2018, including "Shared Aesthetics beyond Geographies"; it describes the RIAC as an annual event regularly supported by the Institut Français and Almayuda (Almayuda).
- Popul'Art: the second phase, launched on 1 May 2023 for eight months, is funded by the European Union with support from the Organisation of African, Caribbean and Pacific States (OACPS), and reaches 150 young people aged 10 to 25 in each of the twelve departments of the Congo (ADIAC, Bruno Okokana, 2 May 2023). The first phase ran in 2021 and 2022.
- The same article names the Institut français du Congo among the structures the centre works with on training events (ADIAC).
- Almayuda is a Swiss foundation seated in Geneva, registered on 16 January 2023, with Marie-France Lasry as president and Laurent Kyd and Albert Lasry as board members; its stated purpose is better access to education and training for the most disadvantaged populations, sustainable development and biodiversity (Fundraiso).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Les Ateliers Sahm is project funding from Almayuda, a Geneva foundation, the Institut Français and the European Union with the OACPS (Popul'Art, 2023).
