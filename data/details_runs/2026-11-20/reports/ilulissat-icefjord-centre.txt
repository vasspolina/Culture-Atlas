ILULISSAT ICEFJORD CENTRE (KANGIATA ILLORSUA): Ilulissat, Greenland
ID: ilulissat-icefjord-centre
Website: https://icefjord.gl (checked 2026-11-20, twice; down, the domain did not resolve)

OVERVIEW
- Visitor centre at Sermermiut Aqqut B 2089, on the edge of the Ilulissat Icefjord, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2004, 250 kilometres north of the Arctic Circle (Realdania).
- Built from 2019 to Dorte Mandrup's design, chosen in an international competition; it opened to its first visitors in July 2021 (Realdania, BYGGERI+arkitektur).
- The building is a twisted structure that walkers can cross on the roof; it covers just under 1,500 square metres, 900 of them heated (designboom, BYGGERI+arkitektur).
- Inside are a 400-square-metre permanent exhibition by JAC Studios with a cinema, a shop and a café; the exhibition uses mouth-blown glass blocks cast from 3D scans of fjord ice and ice cores reaching back to 124,000 BC (BYGGERI+arkitektur, designboom).
- Elisabeth Momme heads it (Scan Magazine, 2024).

FUNDING
Ilulissat Icefjord Centre: foundation-built with public partners: Realdania, Avannaata Municipality and the Government of Greenland; exhibition paid for by four foundations
- The Government of Greenland, Avannaata Municipality and the Danish philanthropic association Realdania formed a partnership in 2015 to build the centre; Realdania By & Byg was the client, and the project stayed within its timetable and budget (BYGGERI+arkitektur).
- Realdania's funding was DKK 108 million over the 2012 to 2021 project (Realdania).
- The exhibition and education programme were funded by Nordea-fonden, Augustinus Fonden, Bloomberg Philanthropies and the Oak Foundation; part of the Nordea-fonden grant pays for a physical and online school service (BYGGERI+arkitektur).
- Realdania is an association of about 170,000 members, open free to anyone who owns property; since 2000 it has supported about 4,300 projects with more than DKK 20 billion (BYGGERI+arkitektur).

Red flags: Exhibition funder Augustinus Fonden and its 2025 stake in Scandinavian Tobacco Group
- Augustinus Fonden owns 100% of Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, a foundation-owned investment company that began as a tobacco spinning mill founded in 1750 (Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker).
- Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker owns about 25% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group, a holding worth about 6% of Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker's balance sheet (Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker); STG listed its holding at more than 25% as of 1 May 2025 (Scandinavian Tobacco Group).

Red flags: Exhibition funder Nordea-fonden and Nordea Bank's 2024 anti-money-laundering penalty
- In the first quarter of 2026, 71% of Nordea-fonden's capital was invested in Nordea Bank Abp, down from 89% in 2016 (Nordea-fonden).
- On 27 August 2024 Nordea Bank Abp agreed to pay USD 35 million in penalties to the New York State Department of Financial Services for anti-money-laundering compliance failures and poor due diligence on correspondent banks, after an investigation into its role in the Panama Papers (NYDFS).

BOTTOM LINE
The building money is clean. Two of the four exhibition funders carry red flags, one for tobacco and one for a bank penalty.
