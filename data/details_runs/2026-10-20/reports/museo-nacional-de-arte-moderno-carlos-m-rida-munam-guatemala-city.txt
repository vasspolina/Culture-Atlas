MUSEO NACIONAL DE ARTE MODERNO CARLOS MÉRIDA (MUNAM): Guatemala City, Guatemala
ID: museo-nacional-de-arte-moderno-carlos-m-rida-munam-guatemala-city
Website: https://mcd.gob.gt

OVERVIEW
- National modern art museum in Salón 6 of La Aurora, zone 13, Guatemala City; it is a national monument (Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias, AGN, June 2023).
- Its roots are the Museo Nacional de Historia y Bellas Artes, opened on 10 November 1934 in the former El Calvario temple in zone 1. The temple was demolished in 1947, the collections moved to La Aurora, and the museum took over Salón 6 there in 1968 (Prensa Comunitaria).
- Renamed Museo Nacional de Arte Moderno de Guatemala in 1975, and named after the artist Carlos Mérida in 1999 by ministerial agreement 428-99 (Prensa Comunitaria).
- The building dates from 1939 and was designed by the architect Roberto Moreno. It has a mahogany coffered ceiling, a terrace with wooden beams and 52 windows (Prensa Comunitaria).
- Before it closed it had three exhibition spaces: a permanent exhibition of a century of Guatemalan art, the Sala Carlos Mérida and a temporary exhibitions hall (AGN).
- Visiting: closed since November 2022 for building work. In October 2025 the Viceministry of Cultural and Natural Heritage said it hoped to reopen "a la brevedad posible" (as soon as possible, translation) and gave no date (Prensa Comunitaria).

FUNDING
Museo Nacional de Arte Moderno Carlos Mérida: mainly public: the Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes (state budget)
- Building works: the ministry said it invested Q11.9 million in the roof, windows, steel structure, coffered ceiling, drainage and floor, and allocated Q8 million to a new museography script (AGN, 4 June 2023).
- Roof contract: ARCOS Proyecciones, S.A. won the roof tender on 12 October 2022 with a bid of Q9,802,890 against Constructora Archila's Q5,457,252.17; the final cost was Q11,970,510 (Claudia Méndez Villaseñor, Prensa Comunitaria, 1 October 2025, based on Guatecompras records).
- Museography contract: Constructora y Transportes J.P., S.A. was awarded the contract on 8 September 2023 for Q4,270,150. The article lists the other bids as CONSTRUMERSA Q4,270,150 and ARCOS Q4,072,102.66, and J.P.'s own bid as Q4,250,150. Prensa Comunitaria puts the cost of the museum's closure and repairs at more than Q16 million.
- Earlier repair attempts in 2020 (a direct purchase) and 2021 (public quotations) were cancelled or rejected; the CONSA bid of Q728,640 was rejected for exceeding a budget of under Q700,000 (Prensa Comunitaria).
- Prensa Comunitaria reports that the former administrator, the visual artist Rudy Cotton, had asked for repairs costing under Q500,000 and was later removed from his post.
- Prensa Comunitaria reports that a terrace was covered with a 10 cm layer of cement. Erick Ortiz and Rony Chávez of the ministry's conservation department said the museum had a concrete terrace, not one of Spanish influence. The article says it is unknown whether the Ministry of Environment issued an environmental licence before the work, and that Guatecompras holds no record of one.
- In July 2022 Cotton told Prensa Libre that the ministry wanted to take apart the Mudéjar, a 1939 wooden work, and that he refused to sign the spending request without a technical opinion from the ministry's conservation department. The ministry replied that the museum had gone years without maintenance and that its damage predates the current administration (Marysabel Aldana, Prensa Libre, 29 July 2022).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state museum paid for by the Ministry of Culture and Sports. The documented money is more than Q16 million in public contracts for the roof (awarded October 2022) and museography (awarded September 2023), which Prensa Comunitaria examined through Guatecompras procurement records.
