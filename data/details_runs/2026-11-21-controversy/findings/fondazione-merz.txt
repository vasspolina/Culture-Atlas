ID: fondazione-merz

WIDER CONTEXT
- Finestre sull'Arte reported on 4 December 2025 that the Fondazione Merz had cancelled a Marisa Merz exhibition planned for the autumn at the Fridericianum in Kassel, because the venue's Code of Conduct uses the IHRA definition of antisemitism.
- The foundation's president, Beatrice Merz, told Monopol that she did not fully agree with that definition and that the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism would have been more appropriate (Finestre sull'Arte, 4 December 2025, read in its machine translation).
- Andreas Hoffmann, managing director of the Fridericianum's managing body, said the planned framework for the exhibition was not entirely compatible, that the venue respected the foundation's decision, and that the Code of Conduct binds the organisation's employees, not outside parties (Finestre sull'Arte, 4 December 2025).
