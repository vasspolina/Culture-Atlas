GUANGDONG MUSEUM: Guangzhou, China
ID: guangdong-museum
Website: https://www.gdmuseum.com (checked 2026-11-20; redirects to https://www.gdmuseum.org.cn, which did not answer the automated check)

OVERVIEW
- The provincial museum of Guangdong at 2 Zhujiang East Road, in the Cultural and Art Square of Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe District, on Guangzhou's new central axis (China Daily).
- A preparatory committee was formed on 19 July 1957, and the museum and the Guangzhou Lu Xun Memorial Hall opened on 1 October 1959 on Wenming Road, on the site of the old Guangdong examination hall (China Daily; Chinese Wikipedia).
- In 2003 the provincial government decided to build a new museum and held an invited international design competition; Rocco Design of Hong Kong won among ten firms from eight countries, building began on 12 December 2004 and the new museum opened on 18 May 2010 (Chinese Wikipedia).
- The building has four sections, for history, nature, art and temporary exhibitions (China Daily), and 77,000 square metres of floor space (museum website; China Daily gives 67,000); its design draws on the Cantonese carved ivory puzzle ball (English Wikipedia).
- Permanent displays cover Guangdong history and culture, the province's natural resources, Duan inkstones, Chaozhou woodcarving and ceramics (Chinese Wikipedia). The collection numbers more than 320,000 items or sets; the museum is in the first batch of national first-grade museums and is a centrally and locally co-built national key museum (museum website).
- Xiao Haiming is director (Dayoo, Guangzhou Daily, 23 August 2026).
- 1,557,000 visitors in 2016 (TEA-AECOM figures, English Wikipedia).
- Since June 2024 it has run monthly sign-language and audio-description tours, according to deputy director Ling Lili (People's Daily, 3 June 2024).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 9 am to 5 pm, closed on Mondays except public holidays (China Daily); visitors book in advance under their real names through a WeChat mini program (Chinese Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Guangdong Museum: public: owned and paid for by the Guangdong provincial government
- Operator: the museum is a public institution and one of the 16 subordinate budget units of the Guangdong Department of Culture and Tourism (museum website; 2020 departmental final accounts).
- Building: in 2003 the provincial authorities committed 884.2 million yuan ($129.39 million) to the new facility (China Daily).
- Running costs: the department's 2020 income of 1.497 billion yuan, which covers the museum, included 1.396 billion from the provincial general public budget (2020 departmental final accounts).
- Earned income: in 2020 the museum's service income fell by 6.13 million yuan, or 48.25%, after Covid-19 measures capped visitor numbers and cancelled exhibitions; the department's operating income, 1.463 million yuan, fell by 565,100 yuan because the museum cancelled exhibitions (2020 departmental final accounts).

Not related: the Guangdong Museum of Art, a separate provincial art museum listed as its own budget unit in the same departmental accounts, and the Guangzhou Museum, a city museum under the Guangzhou culture bureau (2020 departmental final accounts; Chinese Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Guangdong Museum is clean: provincial public money, with no sponsor or flagged funder. Its spending is reported only inside the culture department's accounts. Worth watching: the department's annual accounts.
