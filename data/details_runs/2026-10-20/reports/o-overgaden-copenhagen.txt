O-OVERGADEN INSTITUT FOR SAMTIDSKUNST: Copenhagen, Denmark
ID: o-overgaden-copenhagen
Website: https://overgaden.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art institution at Overgaden neden Vandet 17 on the Christianshavn canal, Copenhagen, with about eight large exhibitions a year by emerging Danish and international artists, plus concerts, performances and film screenings (Overgaden website).
- Founded in 1986 in a former printing workshop (Overgaden website). Alumni named on its site include Olafur Eliasson, Michael Elmgreen and Pia Arke.
- Building: red brick on a granite foundation, designed by architect Frederik Bøttger and constructed in 1887 for a society that ran public dining and coffee houses. C. Ferslew & Co. bought it in 1893 and ran the printing workshop Centraltrykkeriet there; a funkis-style rear wing was added in 1936. It was listed as a protected building in 1989, excluding the 1936 extension (Wikipedia).
- Renovated from August to December 2019 by Christoffer Harlang Architects and reopened on 11 September 2020 (Overgaden website).
- Rhea Dall is Director and Chief Curator; Sanne Kofod Olsen, Vice-Chancellor at the Royal Institute of Art in Stockholm, chairs the board for January 2024 to December 2028. Board members include Rasmus Myrup, Lea Porsager, Jeannette Ehlers and Rolf Hay, CEO and founder of HAY (Overgaden website).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday during exhibition periods; admission is free to exhibitions, openings and most events (Overgaden website).

FUNDING
O-Overgaden: mixed public and private: the Danish Arts Foundation as principal supporter, with private foundations and the City of Copenhagen
- The Danish Arts Foundation is the principal supporter; the current framework agreement runs 2024 to 2027 (Overgaden website).
- General support comes from the A.P. Møller Foundation, Aage and Johanne Louis-Hansen's Foundation, Beckett-Fonden, the Augustinus Foundation, the New Carlsberg Foundation and The Obel Family Foundation (Overgaden website).
- The individual exhibitions of 2022 and 2023 received support from 25 named funders, among them the Danish Arts Foundation, the City of Copenhagen, the City of Aarhus, Goethe-Institut Denmark, the Novo Nordisk Foundation, the Nordic Culture Fund, Knud Højgaard's Foundation, Arne V. Schlesch's Foundation and Gottfred and Gerda Eickhoff's Foundation (Overgaden website).
- Aage and Johanne Louis-Hansens Fond gave 5.1 million DKK for the renovation of the exhibition and visitor facilities, which ran from August to December 2019 (Overgaden website).
- The website was made with support from the New Carlsberg Foundation (Overgaden website).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Overgaden is public and charitable: the Danish Arts Foundation under a 2024 to 2027 framework agreement, the City of Copenhagen for exhibitions, and named Danish private foundations, including a 5.1 million DKK gift for the 2019 renovation.
