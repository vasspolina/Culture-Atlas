ID: saint-louis-symphony-orchestra

WIDER CONTEXT
- In January 2005 the musicians rejected the orchestra's latest contract offer; they said management had locked them out, while Symphony President Randy Adams said the orchestra could not give the raises they wanted because only the interest on an endowment of close to $100 million could be spent (St. Louis Public Radio, Matt Sepic, 3 January 2005).
- Base pay was just under $74,000 a year, and the offer would have taken it below $73,000 by 2008 (St. Louis Public Radio, 3 January 2005).
- On 24 February 2005, with the orchestra shut down since 3 January, management said the National Labor Relations Board would issue a formal complaint against the musicians' union and seek a federal court order if the walkout continued; the musicians maintained they had been locked out (St. Louis Public Radio and AP, 24 February 2005).
