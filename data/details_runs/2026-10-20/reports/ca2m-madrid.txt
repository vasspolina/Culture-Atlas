CA2M CENTRO DE ARTE DOS DE MAYO: Móstoles, Spain
ID: ca2m-madrid
Website: https://ca2m.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at Avenida de la Constitución 23 in the heart of Móstoles, in the Comunidad de Madrid (Es Madrid).
- Opened in May 2008 on the site of a traditional building called La Casona (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Building of 5,886 square metres with exhibition rooms on three floors, a 100-seat auditorium, a library and a terrace; the basic architectural project is by Celia Vinuesa, executed by Pablo Pérez-Urruti, and since 2016 Andrés Jaque Studio, Office for Political Innovation, has carried out a series of "architectural acupuncture" interventions (Es Madrid).
- Ferran Barenblit was the first director and left in September 2015 for MACBA; Manuel Segade directed from 2015 until 2023 (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Holds about 1,500 works from the Comunidad de Madrid's contemporary art collection and about 300 from the Fundación ARCO collection (Spanish Wikipedia); runs exhibitions, guided visits with artists, workshops, a reading group and a university course (museum site).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 21:00; closed Mondays, 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 and 6 January. Admission and all activities are free (museum site). Es Madrid lists a lift, baby-changing facilities and a nursing room.

FUNDING
CA2M Centro de Arte Dos de Mayo: public: Comunidad de Madrid (regional government)
- The contemporary art collection housed at the centre "belongs to the Regional Government of Madrid" (Es Madrid); Visit Madrid likewise says the collection belongs to the Community of Madrid.
- On 11 September 2020 the Comunidad de Madrid announced "Destinaremos 1,3 millones de euros a la adquisición de obras de arte contemporáneo en 2020" (my translation: we will allocate 1.3 million euros to buying contemporary art in 2020), the works to join the CA2M collection.
- On 5 October 2020 it announced "Adquirimos obras de 54 artistas contemporáneos por valor de 500.000 euros": 54 works bought for 500,000 euros for the CA2M collection, within 1.3 million euros invested in contemporary art that year, the highest figure since the collection was created. The acquisitions commission includes Manuel Segade, director of the CA2M, and Tania Pardo, deputy director (Comunidad de Madrid).
- Tenth anniversary: the Comunidad de Madrid released a documentary on the centre on 2 October 2018 and counted more than 70 exhibitions in the decade (Comunidad de Madrid).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the centre is the regional government of the Comunidad de Madrid, which owns the collection and bought 54 works for it in 2020 as part of 1.3 million euros of acquisitions. The Fundación ARCO collection of about 300 works is also held there.
