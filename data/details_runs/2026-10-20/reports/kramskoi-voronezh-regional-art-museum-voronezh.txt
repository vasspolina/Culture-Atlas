KRAMSKOI VORONEZH REGIONAL ART MUSEUM: Voronezh, Russia
ID: kramskoi-voronezh-regional-art-museum-voronezh
Website: https://mkram.ru

OVERVIEW
- Regional art museum at Prospekt Revolyutsii 18 in Voronezh (address in the footer of the museum's site), in a Baroque building of 1777 to 1779 designed by the architect N. N. Ievsky, listed as a monument of federal significance (stated by the museum; museum.ru).
- Created in 1933 as the Museum of Fine Arts from the collections of the Provincial Museum and the Museum of Antiquities and Fine Arts of Voronezh University; it has carried the name of the painter Ivan Kramskoi since 1984 (stated by the museum).
- The building's first owner was the Voronezh governor I. A. Potapov; it passed to the Voronezh Provincial Museum in 1911, has housed the art museum since 1947, was restored in 1952 and 1953 after wartime fire damage, and gained an exhibition hall in 1984 (stated by the museum).
- Exhibition space: 832.4 m² in the main building and 724.7 m² in the exhibition hall; the holdings number more than 23,000 items, including Egyptian and classical antiquities, Russian and Western European painting, graphics, sculpture, numismatics and porcelain (stated by the museum).
- The museum's About page names Maria Aleksandrovna Mazur as head ("Руководитель"); Kommersant (Daria Verbitskaya, 22 September 2025) identifies Yulia Zaichenko as museum director and reports her presenting September plans at a press conference on 19 September 2025.
- Open Tuesday to Thursday 12:00 to 20:00, Friday 12:00 to 19:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, closed Monday. Standard admission is 300 rubles and 150 rubles for pensioners, students and schoolchildren; free entry for several groups, including under-18s once a week and students in professional education once a month. The site has a version for visually impaired visitors and a section "Accessible museum" (stated by the museum).
- Free admission for participants in the "special military operation" and their eligible family members, under Order No. 771-OD of the Voronezh Region Ministry of Culture dated 11 December 2025 (stated by the museum).

FUNDING
Kramskoi Voronezh Regional Art Museum: public: a regional state museum founded by the Voronezh Region Ministry of Culture, with ticket and service income
- Founder: the Ministry of Culture of the Voronezh Region (stated by the museum on its About page).
- The museum posts its charter, financial plans for 2022 to 2025, state task reports for 2020 to 2026 and annual reports for 2020 to 2024 as files on its Documents page; the page itself gives no amounts.
- Own income comes from tickets, tours (individual tour 1,000 rubles, group tour 200 rubles per person), events and photo shoots in the palace at 2,000 rubles, listed in the museum's price list.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution whose founder is the Voronezh Region Ministry of Culture, and it also earns ticket and service income. Its financial plans and state task reports are posted on the museum's site.
