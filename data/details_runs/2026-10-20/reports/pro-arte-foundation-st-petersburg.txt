PRO ARTE FOUNDATION: St Petersburg, Russia
ID: pro-arte-foundation-st-petersburg
Website: https://www.proarte.ru/about/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Non-state charitable foundation for culture and art, registered at Rubinshteyna Street 6, St Petersburg; its areas are visual arts, museum projects and inclusion.
- Founded in 1999; the site does not name the founders.
- It has run inclusive programmes for people with visual impairments since 2011, including the inclusive theatre laboratory "NE ZRYA" and the "Music for All" project with blind musicians.
- It works with the State Hermitage, the Bolshoi Drama Theatre (BDT), the Museum of the History of St Petersburg and the Vladimir Potanin Foundation. Its festivals are "Contemporary Art in a Traditional Museum" and the St Petersburg Biennale of Museum Design, which together involved about 100 museums and more than 250 artists and designers from 20 countries (stated by the foundation).
- It won the state "Innovation" prize in contemporary art in 2021 (stated by the foundation).
- Visiting: the foundation is an organiser and funder, not a venue; its events are listed in an "Afisha" section. No reporting found of opening hours or admission.

FUNDING
PRO ARTE Foundation: mixed: Potanin Foundation, St Petersburg city committees and Friends of BDT
- The site names four supporters: the St Petersburg Committee for Culture, the St Petersburg Committee for Social Policy, the Vladimir Potanin Charitable Foundation and the Friends of BDT Foundation. The English page also names the Mikhail Prokhorov Foundation.
- Since 2016 PRO ARTE has been operator of the Potanin Foundation's grant competitions and organiser of events under its "Museum without Borders" programme: the Museum Guide Forum, the Intermuseum festival and the School of Museum Leadership. In 2026 it also runs entries for the Potanin Foundation's "Laboratory of Ideas of L. Yu. Kopylov" in Moscow and Yaroslavl.
- Reports and audits for 2023, 2024 and 2025 are posted as PDFs; I could not read the text of the 2025 report, so no amounts are given here.
- Board: Sergey Basov, Elena Kolovskaya (foundation director), Irina Prokhorova, Leonid Romankov and Kirill Krutikov, chairman of the board of directors of GK FSK Severo-Zapad.
- No reporting found of amounts from any funder.

Red flags: Sanctions, Vladimir Potanin
- The Potanin Foundation is a named funder and partner, and PRO ARTE has operated its grant competitions since 2016 (PRO ARTE site).
- The UK sanctioned Vladimir Potanin on 29 June 2022 (Clyde & Co).
- The US Treasury sanctioned him on 15 December 2022, with his holding company Interros and Rosbank, in a package aimed at limiting Russia's ability to fund its war against Ukraine; it did not designate Norilsk Nickel (Fortune).
- Canada sanctioned him in April 2022 (Focus).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Potanin stepped down as a Guggenheim trustee on 3 March 2022, after funding Guggenheim programmes through his foundation for about two decades (Hyperallergic, Valentina Di Liscia).
- The Centre Pompidou turned down a third tranche of 586,000 euros from the Potanin Foundation in May 2022; it had earlier called the donations a "source of embarrassment" (Focus, 24 May 2022).
- Connection to the space: PRO ARTE has run the Potanin Foundation's grant competitions since 2016.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation's money comes from St Petersburg city committees, the BDT friends' foundation and Vladimir Potanin's foundation, and the last is a sanctioned person's foundation: the UK and US listed Potanin in 2022. No amounts are read, and no criticism of PRO ARTE itself is found. Worth watching: how much of its budget the Potanin Foundation supplies, which the 2025 report would show.
