HAUS DER KULTUREN DER WELT (HKW): Berlin, Germany
ID: haus-der-kulturen-der-welt-hkw
Website: https://www.hkw.de

OVERVIEW
- Venue for international contemporary arts and debate in the former Kongresshalle on the Spree in the Grosser Tiergarten, Berlin; the HKW has used the building since 1989 (German Wikipedia).
- The American architect Hugh Stubbins designed the Kongresshalle for the 1957 Interbau building exhibition, as a gift from the United States; Berliners nicknamed it the "pregnant oyster" (Wikipedia, German Wikipedia).
- The grounds were earlier the site of Magnus Hirschfeld's Institut für Sexualwissenschaft (Wikipedia).
- Part of the roof collapsed on 21 May 1980, killing a Sender Freies Berlin radio journalist; the hall was rebuilt in its original style and reopened in 1987 (Wikipedia). A partial restoration was completed in 2019 (German Wikipedia).
- Run by Kulturveranstaltungen des Bundes in Berlin GmbH (German Wikipedia).
- Bernd M. Scherer led the house from 2006; Bonaventure Soh Bejeng Ndikung became director on 1 January 2023 (German Wikipedia).
- Work is organised in three areas: literature, society and science; visual arts and film; music and performing arts. Since 2013 it has run multi-year projects including the Anthropozän-Projekt, "100 Jahre Gegenwart" and "Das Neue Alphabet" (German Wikipedia).

FUNDING
HKW: mainly public: German federal government
- Regular funding comes from the Federal Commissioner for Culture and the Media (BKM) and the Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt) (German Wikipedia). English Wikipedia names HKW as one of the federal "lighthouses of culture" funded by the federal culture minister and the Federal Foreign Office.
- Hyperallergic's Adam Broomberg writes that HKW "is funded almost entirely by the federal government" (Hyperallergic, 5 November 2025).

Red flags: State censorship or punishment, concert cancelled after the federal culture minister's call
- Funder: the federal culture minister's office (BKM) is one of HKW's two regular funders (German Wikipedia).
- Federal culture minister Wolfram Weimer sharply criticised the appearance of the rapper Chefket, planned for 7 October 2025 at HKW as part of Jan Böhmermann's exhibition "Die Möglichkeit der Unvernunft" (title translated: "The Possibility of Unreason"). He objected to a T-shirt Chefket wears in photos, showing a map of Palestine without Israel, which the federal government considers antisemitic, and called on the HKW director to intervene (Jüdische Allgemeine, dpa, 29 September 2025).
- The HKW director had earlier pointed to Böhmermann's artistic freedom and clear stance on antisemitism (Jüdische Allgemeine).
- Cancellation: on 29 September 2025 the organisers, listed in the statement as Böhmermann, his group Royale and HKW as venue, announced that the 7 October event was cancelled. The statement says "Diesen Einspruch nehmen wir ernst" (translated: "We take this objection seriously") and gives it as the reason for cancelling (Jüdische Allgemeine).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One piece in the art press argues that HKW's federal funding brings state control, and criticises the handling of Gaza under the Ndikung directorship.
1. Hyperallergic: Adam Broomberg, "The Irony of a Fascism Exhibition in Germany" (5 November 2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1055123/the-irony-of-a-fascism-exhibition-in-germany-berlin-hkw/
- Argues the HKW, "once a custodian of critical culture", has become "a model of capitulation to state control", and that its near-total federal funding "has a price: preemptive obedience".
- Says Ndikung's appointment came with the condition that he publicly renounce the BDS movement, which he did in 2022 (Broomberg's claim).
- Criticises the exhibition "Global Fascisms" ("Not one work in the exhibition acknowledges the world burning just beyond the door") and calls Roee Rosen's "Gaza War Tattoos" an aestheticisation of atrocity.
- Does not mention Weimer or Böhmermann.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2022 appointment: the federal government's antisemitism commissioner Felix Klein and the Israeli ambassador Ron Prosor criticised the incoming director Ndikung in papers of the Funke Mediengruppe and accused him of closeness to the BDS movement. Ndikung said "Ich habe die BDS-Bewegung nicht unterstützt und unterstütze sie auch heute nicht." Culture minister Claudia Roth said she had no objection to the procedure or its result (Jüdische Allgemeine, dpa, 28 October 2022).
- Roth said she was not involved in the selection, which fell in the term of her predecessor Monika Grütters (Jüdische Allgemeine).
- Connection: the accusers were the federal antisemitism commissioner and a foreign ambassador; the minister whose office funds HKW defended the appointment, and Ndikung took office at HKW on 1 January 2023.

BOTTOM LINE
HKW is paid for by the German federal government through the culture commissioner's office and the Foreign Office. The documented weak point is political pressure on its programme: the federal culture minister's call to the director over the Chefket concert, cancelled on 29 September 2025 by the organisers including HKW, and the 2022 accusations against its incoming director from the federal antisemitism commissioner and the Israeli ambassador.
