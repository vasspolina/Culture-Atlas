STAATLICHE MUSEEN ZU BERLIN: Berlin, Germany
ID: staatliche-museen-zu-berlin
Website: https://www.smb.museum/en/home/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Fifteen collections, three research institutes and the Gipsformerei, covering European and non-European art, archaeology and ethnology, on sites including Museum Island, the Kulturforum, Charlottenburg, Dahlem and the Humboldt Forum (smb.museum).
- Founded in 1823 by King Friedrich Wilhelm III of Prussia as the Königliche Museen (Wikipedia). It belongs to the Prussian Cultural Heritage Foundation (SPK), set up in 1957 to preserve the cultural property of the former state of Prussia (smb.museum).
- Leadership: since 1 December 2025 a collegial Executive Board under SPK president Marion Ackermann runs the foundation (SPK).
- Visitors: SPK museums had around 3.6 million visits in 2025; the Neue Nationalgalerie had 608,000 and the Hamburger Bahnhof about 445,000 (SPK).
- The Pergamon Museum has been closed since October 2023, until 2036 or 2037 (Berliner Zeitung; The Irish Times).
- Visiting: free for under-18s, people on benefits such as Bürgergeld and members of the museums' friends' associations; students and severely disabled visitors pay half (smb.museum).

FUNDING
Staatliche Museen zu Berlin: mainly public: the federal government and the states through the SPK, plus friends, collectors and sponsors
- The SPK's 2025 operating budget is about EUR 275.3 million: federal allocations about EUR 196.4 million, Berlin about EUR 38.6 million, the other states about EUR 20 million. A further EUR 143.1 million goes to construction; since 2003 the federal government has borne the whole cost of SPK construction (SPK budget page).
- Sparkassen-Finanzgruppe has been main sponsor since 2011; it renewed in April 2014 for three years and in July 2017 for two, when the insurer Feuersozietät Berlin Brandenburg joined (smb.museum press releases).
- Single shows: the 2014 Schlüter exhibition was sponsored by the Kulturstiftung der Länder, the Kuratorium Museumsinsel and Sparkassen-Finanzgruppe, and supported by the Ernst von Siemens Kunststiftung and the Rudolf-August Oetker-Stiftung (smb.museum).
- Friends' associations, from the Numismatische Gesellschaft zu Berlin (1843) and the Kaiser Friedrich-Museums-Verein (1897) to the Freunde der Nationalgalerie, pay for events and acquisitions (smb.museum).
- Collectors: Friedrich Christian Flick gave the Nationalgalerie 166 works in 2008 and 104 in 2015; Egidio Marzona gave 372 works in 2014; Heinz Berggruen let the museums buy 165 works in 2000 "for a fraction of their estimated value" (smb.museum).

Red flags: 2024 Pergamon Museum renovation cost rise
- The Federal Office for Building and Regional Planning told Der Spiegel the renovation would cost at least EUR 121.4 million more, about EUR 1.5 billion in all, and that further rises could not be ruled out (Berliner Zeitung, 16 February 2024).
- Original costings had risen 500 per cent; Der Spiegel said the SPK shared the blame, and SPK officials gave architects new ideas late in the day (The Irish Times, Derek Scally, 15 August 2024).

Red flags: 2024 Federal Audit Office criticism of the berlin modern building
- The Federal Audit Office criticised the Nationalgalerie's new building at the Kulturforum as too expensive and harmful to the climate; costs then stood at about EUR 364 million (taz, 9 February 2024).
- In July 2025 costs were due to rise to about EUR 526 million, against an early EUR 200 million, with completion delayed to 2028 (Berliner Zeitung, 24 July 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: News reports criticise the SPK's structure and its handling of restitution claims.
1. The Art Newspaper: Catherine Hickley, "Panel recommends dissolution of 'dysfunctional' Prussian Cultural Heritage Foundation" (2020)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2020/07/08/panel-recommends-dissolution-of-dysfunctional-prussian-cultural-heritage-foundation
- Citing Die Zeit, a panel commissioned in 2018 by culture minister Monika Grütters called the SPK "dysfunctional" and "structurally overwhelmed".
2. The Art Newspaper: Chiara Zampetti Egidi, "Overdue review shakes up Germany's largest cultural institution" (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/01/23/overdue-review-shakes-up-german-culture-foundation
- The reform approved in December 2022 gave the institutions their own budgets but postponed financing; quotes the Tagesspiegel calling the response to the reform paper "devastating".
3. The Art Newspaper: Catherine Hickley, "Yet again, a US court dismisses Nazi-era Guelph Treasure art lawsuit" (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/07/18/once-again-a-us-court-dismisses-guelph-treasure-art-lawsuit
- The descendants of Jewish Frankfurt dealers say the 1935 sale to the Prussian state, backed by Hermann Goering, was coerced; their lawyer Nicholas O'Donnell called it theft.
- On 13 July 2023 a US appeals court upheld a ruling that the claim could not be heard in a US court; Germany's Advisory Commission rejected it in 2014.
4. AFP via Hürriyet Daily News: "Row erupts in Germany over restitution of bronzes" (13 June 2023)
URL: https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/row-erupts-in-germany-over-restitution-of-bronzes-183882
- After Muhammadu Buhari said returned bronzes would go to the Oba of Benin, Saxony paused returns; SPK president Hermann Parzinger said future returns were not in doubt.
- The Nigerian art historian Peju Layiwola called the Western museums' reaction overblown and "an excuse".

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2004: the Flick Collection opened at the Hamburger Bahnhof amid protests; the collector's grandfather employed up to 50,000 forced labourers and camp inmates, and victims' groups called it "blood money" (The Irish Times, 23 September 2004).
- In May 2021 the Hamburger Bahnhof was losing the Flick Collection's loans; the investor CA Immo planned to demolish the Rieckhallen that housed it (dpa via t-online, 29 May 2021).

BOTTOM LINE
Federal and state money carries the museums, and no flagged funder pays them. The weak point is building projects far over budget.
