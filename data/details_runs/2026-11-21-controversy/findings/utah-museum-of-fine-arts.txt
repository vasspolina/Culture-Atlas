ID: utah-museum-of-fine-arts

WIDER CONTEXT
- In April 2004 the Utah Museum of Fine Arts returned François Boucher's 18th century painting "Les Jeunes Amoureux" to the heirs of Andre Jean Seligmann, a Jewish gallery owner from whom it was confiscated in 1940 during the Nazi occupation of France (BBC News, 17 June 2004).
- Seligmann's daughter said Hermann Goering had confiscated the painting for his own collection; it resurfaced in a New York gallery in 1967 and was bought by the Utah businessman Val Browning five years later (BBC News, 17 June 2004).
