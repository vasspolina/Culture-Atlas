LABORAL CENTRO DE ARTE Y CREACIÓN INDUSTRIAL: Gijón, Spain
ID: laboral-centro-de-arte-y-creaci-n-industrial-gij-n
Website: https://laboralcentrodearte.org

OVERVIEW
- Art centre at Los Prados 121, Gijón (LABoral website).
- Created in 2007 and occupying about 14,000 square metres of the former Universidad Laboral; the 2017 article credits Rosina Gómez Baeza with the initiative (elDiario.es, J.M. Costa, 2 February 2017).
- Run by Fundación La Laboral. Centro de Arte, Creación Industrial y Promoción Cultural, a public cultural entity declared of general interest; its purpose in the statutes is "the promotion and dissemination of art and industrial creation" through the centre (LABoral website).
- On site: exhibitions, artistic residencies and an educational programme, which the centre's support page says corporate patrons fund (LABoral website).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Friday 10 am to 7:30 pm and Saturday 11 am to 7:30 pm; free entrance; free guided tours for groups who book at least 72 hours ahead; free parking by car (LABoral website).

FUNDING
LABoral: mainly public: the Principality of Asturias, Gijón City Council and the Port Authority of Gijón
- The Board of Trustees, the supreme governing body of the foundation, includes representatives of the Government of the Principality of Asturias, Gijón City Council and the Gijón Port Authority; the president and first vice president represent the Government of Asturias (LABoral website).
- In 2017 elDiario.es reported that private patrons had reduced their contribution from 200,000 euros a year to 5,000 euros, that Gijón City Council gave about 20,000 euros, and that the Principality government had cut staff to seven people (J.M. Costa, 2 February 2017; the figures are the author's).
- The exhibition page for "Climate Engines" (6 October 2023 to 18 May 2024) lists under "Supports": Onassis Stegi, EDP, the Principality of Asturias, Acción Cultural Española, the Austrian Cultural Forum, Fundación Caja Rural and UE33. The show was part of the cultural programme of Spain's 2023 Presidency of the Council of the EU. Its earlier version "Weather Engines" was supported by Onassis Stegi's "Outwards-Turn" Cultural Export Programme and was presented at Onassis Stegi in Athens in 2022 (LABoral website).
- Eco-Visionaries (2018): LABoral was one of four venues, with MAAT in Lisbon, Bildmuseet in Umeå and HeK in Basel; Fundação EDP published the catalogue in 2018 (Fundação EDP).
- The centre invites companies to join as corporate patrons, sponsors or partners, and individuals as Friends (LABoral website).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Two critical pieces, both in the arts section of the Spanish daily elDiario.es by the same author, about the centre's working conditions and funding; the 2017 piece also notes a programme that is hard for the general public to access.
1. elDiario.es: J.M. Costa, "Estalla el primer conflicto LABoral en un centro de arte contemporáneo" (25 February 2016)
URL: https://www.eldiario.es/cultura/arte/conflicto-laboral-primera-burbuja-estallar_1_4137443.html
- Three workers stopped being paid in January when their contracts ended and were required to keep their posts until the situation was clarified; they had long been treated as false self-employed and were later told they had never been regular employees.
- Other workers formed an assembly that denounced the centre's irregular hiring practices, and thirty Asturian artists signed a statement supporting the staff.
- The centre had no artistic director since February 2015, when Óscar Abril was dismissed.
2. elDiario.es: J.M. Costa, "LABoral de Gijón, del mayor lujo a la más absoluta miseria en los centros de arte" (2 February 2017)
URL: https://www.eldiario.es/cultura/arte/laboral-gijon-mayor-absoluta-miseria_1_3606081.html
- The centre announced it would close three months a year and three days a week because of budget problems; the author says the size of the building made its running costs very high.
- He blames mainly politicians and private patrons, citing patrons cutting from 200,000 to 5,000 euros, the city giving about 20,000 euros and the Principality cutting staff to seven.
- He compares the city's 20,000 euros with the more than 300,000 euros that the comparable centre in Avilés receives.
- He also points to a programme he describes as little accessible to the general public.

BOTTOM LINE
LABoral is a public foundation governed by the Principality of Asturias, Gijón City Council and the Gijón Port Authority, with EDP, Onassis Stegi and Fundación Caja Rural among the supporters listed for "Climate Engines" in 2023 and 2024. Press criticism in 2016 and 2017 concerned precarious staff contracts and shrinking public and private funding.
