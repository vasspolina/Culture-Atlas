JAMEEL ARTS CENTRE – Dubai, UAE
Website: https://jameelartscentre.org (about page loaded 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary arts institution at Jaddaf Waterfront on Dubai Creek. It opened in November 2018 (own site).
- Building by Serie Architects, about 10,000 m², with 1,000 m² of galleries, a research centre and library, an events space and a sculpture park; the sculpture park was designed by Ibda Design. Founding director: Antonia Carver, formerly director of Art Dubai (The Art Newspaper, 2018; Al-Fanar Media, 2018).
- The about page gives conflicting hours ("Monday to Sunday 10am–8pm" alongside "Tuesday closed"). Admission and accessibility details were not confirmed.

FUNDING
Jameel Arts Centre: privately funded by Art Jameel, with stated support from Dubai government-linked developers and UAE government bodies.
- Art Jameel founded and funds the centre. Art Jameel is the cultural philanthropy of the Jameel family, whose Saudi conglomerate is Abdul Latif Jameel (own site; The Art Newspaper; Al-Fanar).
- The centre's own about page says Dubai Holding and Dubai Properties, the master developers of Jaddaf, "along with other UAE government ministries and entities", supported its development. The sources do not say what form that support took or how much it was.
- Independent coverage from 2018 says it is "not financed or run by the state" (The Art Newspaper) and "not supported or run by the government" (Al-Fanar).
- No reporting found of annual accounts, budget figures or a board.

Red flags: State-linked support for development
- The space says Dubai Holding, Dubai Properties and UAE government ministries supported its development (stated by the space, undated). This sits awkwardly with 2018 press describing it as not state-financed. No figures are given.

Not related: Community Jameel — the sister organisation within the Jameel family's philanthropy. It is the same family but a different body; it is not the centre's operator.

BOTTOM LINE
Its running costs come from a private Saudi family's philanthropy. Its own site, however, credits Dubai's government-linked master developer and UAE ministries with supporting its development. The terms of that support, and any land arrangement, are not published, so that is the thing to watch.
