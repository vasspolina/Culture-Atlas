LOUISIANA MUSEUM OF MODERN ART: Humlebæk, Denmark
ID: louisiana-museum-of-modern-art
Website: https://louisiana.dk (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum in Humlebæk, about 30 km north of Copenhagen on the Øresund coast; it draws over 700,000 visitors a year (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1958 by businessman Knud W. Jensen (Wikipedia).
- Named after a 19th-century landowner whose three wives were all called Louise (Wikipedia).
- Original building by architects Jørgen Bo and Vilhelm Wohlert, expanded several times; a sculpture park overlooks the sea (Wikipedia).
- On site: 6 to 10 temporary exhibitions a year, a permanent Yayoi Kusama installation, a concert hall opened in 1976, a children's wing, a café and a shop. The Louisiana Literature festival has run since 2010 and the Louisiana Channel web TV since 2012 (Wikipedia, stated by the museum).
- Poul Erik Tøjner has been director since 2000 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Friday 11:00 to 22:00, weekends 11:00 to 18:00, closed Monday; under-18s enter free. The site lists a practical info and accessibility page but gives no details on its front page.

FUNDING
Louisiana Museum of Modern Art: mixed: own revenue, state grants and private foundations, with Augustinus Fonden a repeat funder
- Wikipedia states the museum is independent, receives government grants, and draws most of its funding from its own revenue and private foundations.
- Augustinus Fonden's list "Uddelinger 2025" names "Louisiana Museum / Louisiana-Fonden" for DKK 2,000,000 to realise the exhibition "Lucian Freud: Drawing into Painting" in 2026.
- Augustinus Fonden also paid for the museum's expansions in 1979 to 1984 and 1989 to 1990 (Wikipedia, Augustinus Fonden article).
- The 2024 annual report (a scanned PDF, read from the page images) shows net turnover of DKK 100.1 million, 716,000 visitors, and a state operating grant from the Danish Agency for Culture (Kulturstyrelsen) of DKK 36.125 million in 2024, against DKK 34.243 million in 2023.
- The same report shows DKK 27.157 million of grants toward artwork purchases in 2024 (DKK 16.157 million fully grant-funded purchases, DKK 11.0 million toward partly funded ones), and credits Augustinus Fonden, Ny Carlsbergfondet and Lemvigh-Müller Fonden in the acquisition entries; many 2024 acquisitions listed say "Erhvervet med midler fra Augustinus Fonden" (bought with funds from Augustinus Fonden).
- The annual report's page "Sponsorer og fonde som har støttet Louisiana i 2024" lists, with no amounts: A.P. Møller og Hustru Chastine Mc-Kinney Møllers Fond til almene Formaal, Augustinus Fonden, C.L. Davids Fond & Samling, Den A.P. Møllerske Støttefond, Det Norske Utenriksdepartement, Det Obelske Familiefond, Diana Nelson & John Atwater, Dreyers Fond, Fritz Hansen, Furi Appel & Gunnar Niskers Fond til Almennyttige Formål, Lemvigh-Müller Fonden, Louis-Hansen Fonden, Novo Nordisk Fonden, Ny Carlsbergfondet, Panduro, Portugals Ambassade, Realdania, Villum Fonden and William Demant Fonden.

Red flags: Tobacco, Augustinus Fonden
- Augustinus Fonden was founded in 1942; lex.dk says by tobacco manufacturer Ludvig Augustinus, and Wikipedia says by Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker at his initiative. Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, a former tobacco company, is called "the investment company of the Augustinus Foundation" (Wikipedia).
- That company reported holdings above 25% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group as of 1 May 2025 (Scandinavian Tobacco Group's shareholder page).
- Augustinus Fonden's 2025 grant for the 2026 Freud exhibition is current funding. It is on the museum's 2024 donor list and is credited with many of the 2024 acquisitions, and Wikipedia and lex.dk record its funding of the museum's earlier expansions.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's income is a mix of its own revenue, a state grant of DKK 36.125 million in 2024 and private foundations. The weak point is Augustinus Fonden, a repeat funder of expansions and a 2026 exhibition, which is tied to the tobacco group through its investment company. Watch the amounts: the 2024 donor list gives names without sums.
