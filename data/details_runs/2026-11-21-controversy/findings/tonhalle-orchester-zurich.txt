Red flags: 2022 withdrawal of Prokofiev's Alexander Nevsky
- In December 2022 the Tonhalle Orchestra dropped Prokofiev's cantata Alexander Nevsky from a January concert after the choir raised objections, and replaced it with Ella Milch-Sheriff's The Eternal Stranger (OperaWire, 19 December 2022).
- The orchestra called the cantata "a sensitive work in the current war situation because it tells of a historical war" and said "the singers of the Zürcher Singakademie were so uneasy about the work that we had to discontinue" it (OperaWire).
- About a third of the choir's members protested against performing the piece, and the choir's governors granted all members 50% of their fee (Slipped Disc, 13 December 2022).
