SHEPPARTON ART MUSEUM: Shepparton, Australia
ID: shepparton-art-museum-shepparton
Website: https://sheppartonartmuseum.com.au/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 530 Wyndham Street, Shepparton, Victoria (Shepparton Art Museum).
- Established in 1936 as the Shepparton Art Gallery, a council gallery; the collection had no purpose-built home until the current building opened in November 2021 (Wikipedia).
- Denton Corker Marshall won the 2017 design competition; construction began in 2019. The building cost $50 million and won the 2022 Victorian Architecture Award for Public Architecture (Wikipedia).
- It holds over 4,000 artworks, including one of Australia's largest ceramic art collections, and strong holdings of Indigenous Australian art; it hosts the Sidney Myer Fund Australian Ceramic Award and the Indigenous Ceramic Art Award (Wikipedia).
- The building also houses the Greater Shepparton Visitor Centre, Kaiela Arts, an outdoor amphitheatre and the Art Hill café (Wikipedia).
- The City of Greater Shepparton previously managed the museum; it is now governed by the non-profit Shepparton Art Museum Ltd (SAM Ltd) (Wikipedia). Its 2024-25 annual report describes the journey "from a council gallery established in 1936 to an independent museum".
- Free entry; open daily 10am to 4pm, closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. A banner on the site announces closure on Tuesdays from 3 November (Shepparton Art Museum).

FUNDING
Shepparton Art Museum: Greater Shepparton City Council and Creative Victoria, with named donors and the SAM Foundation
- Its 2024/25 annual report thanks donors in the year, including Greater Shepparton City Council, Creative Victoria, Bega Cheese Limited, the Sir Andrew & Lady Fairley Foundation, Carrillo Gantner AC & Ziyin Gantner, the Zurcas Family, the Attwood Foundation, the Bowden Marstan Foundation, the Montgomery Foundation, the Committee for Greater Shepparton, Friends of SAM, La Trobe University, the University of Melbourne and the Victorian Challenge and Enrichment Series (Department of Education), and many individuals. The report also thanks cash donors who gave at the museum and attended SAM Foundation events.
- The SAM Foundation, set up in 2013 to raise money for the new building, has completed its capital build fundraising and now focuses on ongoing funding and philanthropy (Wikipedia, annual report chair's report). The annual report describes the museum's journey from a council gallery established in 1936 to an independent museum as catalysed in 2012 by the promise of a gift from Carrillo and Ziyin Gantner.
- The annual report shows the launch of the Zurcas Landing and refers to the Sir Andrew Fairley and Lady Fairley Foundation Amphitheatre.
- The Director of Business report says the organisation posted "a healthy year-end" in 2024/25 (annual report).
- Board: Stephen Merrylees is Chair of the SAM Ltd Board and Clare Pullar is Chair of the SAM Foundation; John Dewar AO, previously vice-chancellor of La Trobe University, joined the board in 2024/25. Danny Lacy is Artistic Director and Andrew Gooley Director of Business (annual report).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Shepparton Art Museum is the Greater Shepparton City Council, which previously managed the museum before the non-profit SAM Ltd took over (Wikipedia), Creative Victoria, and donors named in the 2024/25 annual report, including Bega Cheese Limited, the Sir Andrew & Lady Fairley Foundation, Carrillo Gantner AC & Ziyin Gantner and the Zurcas Family. The annual report also names the board.
