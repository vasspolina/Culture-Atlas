OVERVIEW
- Dia Art Foundation's museum in a 1929 Nabisco printing plant in Beacon, New York, opened 2003; galleries named the Riggio Galleries; listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2003 (Wikipedia). Dia director Jessica Morgan since 2015.

FUNDING
Dia Beacon: private philanthropy.
- Built for about $50M: Leonard Riggio at least $35M, Lannan Foundation $10M, Ann Tenenbaum and Thomas H. Lee $2.5M (Wikipedia, Dia Art Foundation).
- Dia was founded in 1974 by Philippa de Menil, Heiner Friedrich and Helen Winkler; early funding came from de Menil's Schlumberger oil-services fortune. After Schlumberger stock fell (1980-82), Dia took a $3.87M Citibank loan secured on that stock, prompting a New York attorney general investigation; the board was reorganised in 1985 (Wikipedia).
