SALONUL DE PROIECTE: Bucharest, Romania
ID: salonul-de-proiecte-bucharest
Website: https://salonuldeproiecte.ro

OVERVIEW
- Research and production programme for contemporary art in Palatul Universul, Corp B, first floor, Actor Ion Brezoianu 23-25, Bucharest. It promotes Romanian contemporary art through exhibitions, publications, presentations and debates, in dialogue with the regional and international context (stated by the space).
- Founded in 2011 in the framework of MNAC Anexa; since 2016 it has functioned as an independent art space in Palatul Universul. Organised by the non-profit Asociația Salonul de proiecte.
- Team: Alexandra Croitoru, Magda Radu, Ștefan Sava and Ana-Maria Ștefan.
- From 5 December 2018 to 10 February 2019 it showed a first selection from the photographic archive of Mihai Oroveanu in "Arhiva fotografică și istoria în transformare". The archive is held by Anca Oroveanu, who agreed to the display of the images (exhibition page).
- Programme: exhibitions, open calls, talks, book launches and workshops. Current exhibitions: "Negativul unei lumi" (25 June to 16 August 2026) and "Poiana Mărului. Locuri ale imaginii" (9 October to 22 November 2026).
- Visiting: Thursday to Sunday, 15:00 to 19:00. The space says it wants to keep public access to its programmes free (fundraising auction page, June 2026).

FUNDING
Salonul de proiecte (Bucharest): mixed public and private project funding: Romania's national cultural fund, foundations, a bank and small sponsors
- Administrația Fondului Cultural Național (AFCN) co-finances its projects, including "Universul rural. Cercetare și demitizare" (Poiana Mărului exhibition, 2026), "Negativul unei lumi" (2026), "Ion Grigorescu. Legenda familiei" (24 April to 31 May 2026), "Schimburi strategice" (Strategic (Ex)Changes, 10 December 2025 to 15 February 2026) and the Horia Bernea workshop project (exhibition 25 September to 2 November 2025).
- Foundation for Arts Initiatives supports "Negativul unei lumi", "Legenda familiei" and the Poiana Mărului exhibition.
- UniCredit Bank supports the Poiana Mărului exhibition (exhibition page).
- ERSTE Foundation supported "Strategic (Ex)Changes" (10 December 2025 to 15 February 2026).
- Goethe-Institut România supported "Împreună" (26 June to 3 August 2025).
- Sponsors listed on exhibition pages: Corcova Roy & Dâmboviceanu, C-Side Tonic and Doka.
- Own fundraising: an artist-donated benefit auction on 11 June 2026 to continue its work and keep access to programmes free (fundraising auction page).

BOTTOM LINE
Salonul de proiecte is a small independent non-profit funded project by project: the Romanian national cultural fund AFCN, foundations (ERSTE Foundation, Foundation for Arts Initiatives), UniCredit Bank, the Goethe-Institut România and small sponsors, with a benefit auction in 2026.
