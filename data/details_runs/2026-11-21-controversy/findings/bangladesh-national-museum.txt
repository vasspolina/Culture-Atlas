WIDER CONTEXT
- In March 2010, 26 objects, among them two gold and 24 bronze coins, went missing from gallery no. 21 of the museum's Department of History and Classical Art; 20 were found the following year and six were still missing in January 2021. An inquiry in 2010 found that attempts were made to fill the empty spots with replicas to cover up the discovery (The Business Standard, Sukanta Halder, 21 January 2021).
- The museum's then secretary filed a case at Shahbagh Police Station; a deputy keeper, an assistant keeper and three other employees were suspended and later got their jobs back because the negligence allegations were not proven, and the police case stalled (The Business Standard, 21 January 2021).
- Professor Sufi Mostafizur Rahman of Jahangirnagar University said "the inquiry reports are kept under wraps. And no one has been tried for theft so far", and objected to appointing government bureaucrats as the museum's director general; senior museum officials declined several requests for comment (The Business Standard, 21 January 2021).
- The National Museum was responsible for the sculptures, artworks, gallery design and documentaries of the July Mass Uprising Memorial Museum project, and the Public Works Department and the National Museum received its allocation of Tk 1.23 billion, of which Tk 1.10 billion was spent (Prothom Alo, Mansura Hossain, 27 July 2026).
- Unverified: inflated spending on sculptures, documentaries, hospitality and other sectors, the use of a false contractor address and other irregularities, from media reports summarised by Prothom Alo; the museum authorities issued written rebuttals, and director general Tanzim Wahab said all work followed government rules and proper verification (Prothom Alo, 27 July 2026).
- After the government approved direct procurement for the project on 15 July 2025, Transparency International Bangladesh executive director Iftekharuzzaman said the process seriously undermined standards of transparency and accountability in the use of public funds; Wahab called the direct procurement "not the ideal approach". An audit had not been completed (Prothom Alo, 27 July 2026).
- Connection to this museum: Tanzim Wahab is director general of both the National Museum and the July Memorial Museum (Prothom Alo, 27 July 2026).
