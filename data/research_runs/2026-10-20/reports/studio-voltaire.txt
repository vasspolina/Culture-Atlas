STUDIO VOLTAIRE – London, UK
Website: https://studiovoltaire.org (ok, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit gallery and artists' studios, 1A Nelsons Row, Clapham, London SW4 7JR. Founded 1994 by a collective of twelve artists in a former tram shed on Voltaire Road; moved in 1999 to a Victorian former Methodist chapel/mission on Nelson's Row (Wikipedia, Frieze).
- Director: Joe Scotland. Reopened October 2021 after a £2.8m capital project designed by Matheson Whiteley; studio capacity rose from 30 to 75 artists, with an education space, café, public garden, space for the charity ActionSpace, and a permanent home for its shop House of Voltaire (Wikipedia, Frieze, Greater London Authority).
- Open Wed–Sun 11am–6pm; free admission (stated by the space). A separate access page exists; its content was not retrieved.

FUNDING
Studio Voltaire: mainly public core funding plus trusts, galleries and private donors.
- Arts Council England: National Portfolio Organisation since 2011 (Wikipedia); £300,000 toward the expansion (The Art Newspaper, Oct 2018).
- Mayor of London: £500,000 from the Good Growth Fund toward the £2.8m capital project, completed November 2022 (Greater London Authority). The supporters page names the Mayor's Strategic Investment Fund; Lambeth Council paid for highway works.
- Trusts named by the space: Garcia Family Foundation, Wolfson Foundation, City Bridge Trust, Garfield Weston Foundation, Foyle Foundation, Cockayne – The London Community Foundation, Mila Charitable Organisation, Henry Moore Foundation, Hartfield Foundation, Span Trust (stated by the space, undated).
- Corporate patrons: BakerMcKenzie and Fletcher Gallery Services (stated by the space).
- Bloomberg Philanthropies paid for the website through its Digital Accelerator Programme (stated by the space).
- Programme supporters include the LOEWE Foundation (co-funds the LOEWE Foundation / Studio Voltaire Award since 2021), British Council, Creative Scotland, Paul Hamlyn Foundation, Freelands Foundation, the "Buhkman Family Foundation" (spelling as on the page), and galleries Sadie Coles HQ, PACE, Bortolami (stated by the space).
- No reporting found of charity income totals; the charity register could not be read in this run.
- No reporting found of any controversy, protest or boycott directed at Studio Voltaire.

Red flags: Bloomberg Philanthropies campaign elsewhere
- In May 2026, 261 artists in a letter published via Artists for Palestine UK (an openly pro-Palestinian campaign group) asked five London theatres to drop Bloomberg Philanthropies (Morning Star, left-wing; The New Arab). Studio Voltaire was not named.
- Unverified: the letter alleges Bloomberg Philanthropies trains Israeli settlement officials via the Bloomberg-Sagol Centre at Tel Aviv University (Artists for Palestine UK, advocacy).

BOTTOM LINE
Studio Voltaire is a small charity funded by Arts Council England, the Mayor of London, trusts and art-world donors. No source reports a problem with the space itself. Bloomberg's website grant is the only link to a funder that campaigners target elsewhere.
