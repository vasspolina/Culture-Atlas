Red flags: 2022 protest against the Mac's drag storytime event
- On 31 July 2022 former TUV councillor Jolene Bunting and a handful of others protested outside the Mac against Dragtime Stories with Cherrie Ontop, a family story time event that was part of Belfast's Pride Weekend, because it was hosted by a drag queen (The Irish News, 1 August 2022).
- A counter-protest of up to two dozen people supported the event and outnumbered the protesters (The Irish News, 1 August 2022).
- The Mac's statement said its management "believe every child should enjoy creative experiences that fuel their imagination" and "We are proud to present Dragtime Stories for MAC families." (The Irish News, 1 August 2022).
