TRANZIT.RO: Bucharest, Romania
ID: tranzit-ro-bucharest
Website: https://ro.tranzit.org

OVERVIEW
- Romanian non-governmental organisation for contemporary art, "initiated and funded by ERSTE Foundation", announced on 15 December 2011 and fully active from early 2012 with centres in Bucharest, Iași and Cluj (e-flux).
- Five people were chosen through a closed call to run it: Matei Bejenaru, Livia Pancu, Lia Perjovschi, Attila Tordai-S and Raluca Voinea (e-flux).
- It is the fifth member of the tranzit.org network, which also has members in Austria, the Czech Republic, Hungary and the Slovak Republic. The network was founded in 2002 and ERSTE Foundation has been its main partner since 2010 (e-flux).
- The Cluj space is at Sámuel Brassai Street 5, open on weekdays from 15:00 to 18:00 (myartguides). It describes itself as working in three permanent spaces, in Iași, Cluj and Bucharest (myartguides).

FUNDING
tranzit.ro: foundation-funded: ERSTE Foundation
- ERSTE Foundation initiated tranzit.ro and funds it (e-flux, 15 December 2011). Temporary Art Review (Adriana Ledecouvreur, 6 April 2017) calls ERSTE Foundation the primary financial supporter, with additional applications to Romanian state funds, the Goethe-Institut and EU programmes.
- Wikipedia calls ERSTE Foundation the biggest Austrian savings bank foundation and the main anchor shareholder of Erste Group Bank AG, using part of its dividend income to fund projects in Central and South Eastern Europe.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One Q&A profile, not a critical review, which describes the funding conditions of the Romanian art scene.
1. Temporary Art Review: Adriana Ledecouvreur, "tranzit.ro" (2017)
URL: https://temporaryartreview.com/?p=14516
- The introduction says financing artistic projects in post-communist economies "demonstrates a particular (old) mixture of arts, culture, financing and politics."
- An unnamed respondent says the local art scene is "constantly menaced by underfunding".
- Not criticism of the institution: the answers come from the tranzit.ro team.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind tranzit.ro is ERSTE Foundation, which started the organisation in 2011 and pays for it from dividends on its Erste Group Bank stake, supplemented, according to Temporary Art Review (2017), by applications to Romanian state funds, the Goethe-Institut and EU programmes.
