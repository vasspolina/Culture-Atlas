SELASAR SUNARYO ART SPACE: Bandung, Indonesia
ID: selasar-sunaryo-art-space-bandung
Website: https://selasarsunaryo.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art space at Jalan Bukit Pakar Timur 100, Ciburial, Cimenyan, Bandung Regency, in the Dago area (CNN Indonesia, 2017; detikJabar, 2024; culture360).
- Founded by the artist Sunaryo, a former lecturer at the art and design faculty of the Bandung Institute of Technology, and opened on 5 September 1998 (detikJabar). Director General of Culture Edi Setyawati opened it (CNN Indonesia, 2017).
- The first show, "Titik Nadir", covered Sunaryo's works in black cloth, which Manual calls "a sombre expression of the grief and uncertainty" of the turmoil of the time.
- Site: Sunaryo bought about 5,000 square metres of land and built the space himself (CNN Indonesia).
- What is there: Ruang A for Sunaryo's own work, Ruang B for other artists, a stone garden, Bale Tonggoh, an amphitheatre, a library, a shop and the Kopi Selasar cafe (CNN Indonesia), and the Ruang Sayap gallery (Manual). Its programme is guided by a curatorial advisory council of academics, critics and practitioners (culture360).
- Director: Arin Dwihartanto Sunaryo, the founder's son; the curatorial advisers are Bambang Sugiharto and Agung Hujatnikajennong, and the senior curator is Heru Hikayat (museum website).
- Hundreds of exhibitions, workshops and residencies since 1998, including Agus Suwage, Melati Suryodarmo, Rita Widagdo and Le Corbusier; the 25th-anniversary show "Seperempat Abad" ran to April 2024 (Manual).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 17:00; ticket Rp35,000 (detikJabar, 2024). Arin Sunaryo said it launched a public programme for disabled visitors with two 2023 shows (Manual).

FUNDING
Selasar Sunaryo Art Space: self-funded: the founder's land and building, with his foundation's support, run by his family
- culture360 (2010) says Sunaryo founded it "with direction and support from the Selasar Sunaryo Foundation" and calls it a non-profit; the profile names no other funder.
- Sunaryo paid for the land and the building (CNN Indonesia, 2017).
- The founder's family runs it: Arin Dwihartanto Sunaryo is director and his wife, the artist Syagini Ratna Wulan, heads development, marketing and communications (museum website; Manual). Manual described the 2023 anniversary show as passing the baton from Sunaryo to them.
- Earned and donated income: the staff list includes a patron programme manager and a partnerships manager, and the support page's description asks for support "melalui donasi dan kemitraan" (through donations and partnerships; page description only, our translation). Entry was free in 2017 (CNN Indonesia) and cost Rp35,000 by 2024 (detikJabar); the grounds are also hired for pre-wedding photo shoots (CNN Indonesia).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little criticism; the pieces found are descriptive or admiring and treat single shows, not the institution or its money.
1. Art & Market: Tanya Michele Amador, "Selasar Sunaryo Art Space in Bandung Turns 20" (2018)
URL: https://www.artandmarket.net/exhibition/2018/10/21/selasar-sunaryo-art-space-in-bandung-turns-20
- Calls the space an artist-run initiative built on a vision to nurture talented and emerging Indonesian artists.
- Finds Sunaryo's stitched, patched canvases skilled: "certainly not an amateur". Mostly celebratory; it does not discuss funding or governance.
2. The Jakarta Post: Anindito Ariwandono, "Reconnecting with earth at Leuwigoèng" (2022)
URL: https://www.thejakartapost.com/culture/2022/12/13/reconnecting-with-earth-at-leuwigong
- Reviews the group show "Pulang Pada Tanah" ("Home to Soil"); a photo caption notes some works are "rather raw in terms of their treatment".
- Criticises the treatment of some works, not the institution. Paywalled; only excerpts were read.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: the founder's own money and his foundation. The weak points are thin disclosure of accounts and donors, and a family that both runs the space and shows in it.
