FLUTGRABEN: Berlin, Germany
ID: flutgraben-berlin
Website: https://flutgraben.org

OVERVIEW
- Self-organised, non-profit artists' studio house at Am Flutgraben 3, 12435 Berlin, on the Treptow side of the Flutgraben canal that separates Kreuzberg and Treptow (visitBerlin, Flutgraben).
- The association was founded in 1997 as "Kunstfabrik am Flutgraben" and runs the house as Flutgraben e.V. (Flutgraben). Since 2004 it has also managed the former watch tower at Schlesischer Busch, which the initiative The Watch uses for exhibitions, lectures, screenings and residencies (Flutgraben).
- The building is a former workshop of VEB OLW in brick, on the border with West Berlin; the Berlin State Monuments Office calls it the only East Berlin border house that survived the expansion of the border fortifications. Roof catwalks were built in 1988, and the 1990s facade renovation preserved traces of the border (Landesdenkmalamt).
- The house covers about 3,800 square metres with about 50 studios for more than 80 artists, plus a project space and a multipurpose hall of about 220 square metres each (visitBerlin). Flutgraben also lists a roof terrace and residents including raumlaborberlin, Fehras Publishing Practices and The Watch.
- Programmes: events under the heading Spree (canteen, performances, screenings, workshops, talks, festivals and exhibitions), community activity under Eddies, and collaborations with festivals, collectives and neighbourhood groups (Flutgraben).
- The Gaza Biennale Berlin Pavilion ran from 22 November to 21 December 2025, with its opening held in Flutgraben's main exhibition space; the pavilion showed the work of over 30 Gazan artists across venues in Berlin (e-flux, 19 November 2025).

FUNDING
Flutgraben: self-funded: studio rents and room hire
- Flutgraben says it is "financially fully self-supporting" through permanent rental of the studio space and temporary rental of its communal areas (Flutgraben, ArtRabbit).
- A general assembly of members decides structure, programming and major economic matters; the board is elected once a year. Six board members are named: Nino Bulling, Sami Rustom, Sina Ahmadi, Julia Lipinsky, Axel Timm and Matthias Krause Hamrin (Flutgraben).
- The association says internships can be supported by the EU's Erasmus+ programme, and the page carries a Donate link (Flutgraben).
- The Arab Culture Fund lists Flutgraben e.V. as the 2025 grantee for the Gaza Biennale Berlin Pavilion. The pavilion's e-flux announcement says the exhibition was "realized solely through the effort and personal engagement of numerous volunteers."

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One news report criticises how the association handled a dispute with a studio tenant.
1. Berliner Zeitung: Ingeborg Ruthe, "Herrscht etwa Darwinismus im Atelierhaus Flutgraben e.V.?" (8 December 2023)
URL: https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/2166621
- Flutgraben e.V. is suing the painter Frank Nitsche, who took over the studio of the painter Eberhard Havekost after Havekost's death in 2019, to evict him from the house. The association's leadership gives changing reasons, among them "diffuse" allocation guidelines and the claim that the studio was only ever temporary.
- Ruthe argues that the approach, whether or not the reasons hold, endangers everyone who works in the rooms of non-profit associations and unsettles Berlin's creative scene.
- Reports an open letter of solidarity signed by artists, some of them association members working next to Nitsche, with art critics also among the signatories. It calls the association's conduct "a form of studio Darwinism" (Atelier-Darwinismus, translated) and asks Flutgraben e.V. to end the lawsuit and settle with Nitsche.

BOTTOM LINE
Flutgraben pays for itself from studio rents and room hire, run by an artist-member association in a protected former border workshop building. Its 2025 Gaza Biennale Berlin Pavilion was supported by an Arab Culture Fund grant.
