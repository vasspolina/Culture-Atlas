UTAH MUSEUM OF FINE ARTS: Salt Lake City, USA
ID: utah-museum-of-fine-arts
Website: https://umfa.utah.edu/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum of the University of Utah in the Marcia and John Price Museum Building, 410 Campus Center Drive, between Rice-Eccles Stadium and the Jon M. Huntsman Center (museum website).
- It traces its start to a small gallery opened in 1914 in the university's Park Building; after a gift of art from Winifred Kimball Hudnut it was incorporated as the Utah Museum of Fine Arts and reopened in 1951 (museum website).
- It moved to the Arts and Architecture Center in 1970 and in 2001 to the 74,000 square foot Price Building, designed by Machado and Silvetti with Prescott Muir Architects (museum website).
- In February 2005 the Utah State Legislature declared it the state's fine arts museum; it has been accredited by the American Alliance of Museums since 1972 and was reaccredited in December 2025 (museum website).
- The collection holds more than 22,000 works; Gretchen Dietrich has been executive director since 2010 (museum website). Collections visits are paused until March 2027 for construction work in the building (museum website).
- Season: "For the Future: Collecting at the UMFA 2001-2026", marking 25 years of the Price Building, runs to 3 January 2027; salt 18, by the Mexico City artist Amor Muñoz, runs from 24 October 2026 to 6 June 2027 (museum website).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 5 pm, to 8 pm on Wednesdays; adults $19.95, seniors and ages 6 to 18 $16.95; free for university students and staff, students at Utah's public colleges, and on the first Wednesday and third Saturday of each month (museum website).

FUNDING
Utah Museum of Fine Arts: university and public money with private gifts, led by the Price family
- Marcia and John Price were closely involved in building the Price Building; their gift was then the largest to a cultural institution in Utah's history, and Marcia Price chaired the museum's board in 2023 (@theU, University of Utah, 6 February 2023).
- In February 2023 the Price family gave $5 million to endow the executive director's post (@theU). The John and Marcia Price Family Foundation is presenting sponsor of the 2026 anniversary show (museum website).
- Free days are paid for by Salt Lake County's Zoo, Arts and Parks programme, the Utah Division of Arts and Museums and Art Bridges Foundation (museum website); Art Bridges, founded by Alice Walton, granted $400,000 over three years in 2023 (museum website).
- Powder Art Foundation funded the first Land Art Week, 1 to 3 October 2026 (museum website).
- Bank of America cardholders get free entry on the first full weekend of each month under its Museums on Us programme (museum website).
- An Andrew W. Mellon Foundation grant, matched by the University of Utah, paid for a joint research initiative with the Marriott Library on landscape and Land art, which produced the shared Museum+Library Search catalogue (museum website).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean money: university, state and county support and private gifts, with one family, the Prices, as the principal named donor.
