KUNSTHALLE BASEL – Basel, Switzerland
Website: https://www.kunsthallebasel.ch (not checked: fetch not permitted in this run, 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Founded 1872; run by the Basler Kunstverein (founded 1839). Neoclassical building by Johann Jakob Stehlin-Burckhardt (1869-72), renovated 2004 by Miller & Maranta; the Swiss Architecture Museum shares the building. Library of about 30,000 items (Wikipedia).
- Director Mohamed Almusibli since 2024, succeeding Elena Filipovic (2014-2023).

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Basel: mixed public and private.
- Original construction was financed from two Rhine ferry services (Wikipedia).
- Existing record: Canton Basel-Stadt CHF 950k a year for 2024-27 (32%), third-party grants about CHF 660k (30%), rent 22%, members 8%. The cantonal document could not be re-retrieved this run.

Red flags: Political pressure over director's Gaza letters
- Wikipedia (citing ARTnews) reports pushback after Almusibli's appointment because he had signed a Gaza ceasefire letter. The existing record's detail (an SVP cantonal MP demanded a subsidy cut in November 2023; the canton did not act) could not be re-checked this run.

BOTTOM LINE
No funder issues found. Worth watching is political pressure on the cantonal subsidy linked to the director's statements.
