STUDIO VOLTAIRE: London, United Kingdom
ID: studio-voltaire
Website: https://studiovoltaire.org

OVERVIEW
- Not-for-profit arts and education organisation at 1A Nelsons Row, Clapham, London SW4 7JR, championing emerging and under-represented artists; a registered charity and part of Arts Council England's National Portfolio (Studio Voltaire website).
- Founded in 1994 by a group of artists and creatives in a disused tram shed; it moved to Nelsons Row in 1999 (Studio Voltaire website).
- Building: the annexe was built in 1894 as a Mission Hall and Sunday School for Clapham Methodist church, and the gallery occupies the Victorian former mission hall (Studio Voltaire website). Wikipedia calls it a former Methodist chapel.
- A two-year capital project from 2019 to 2021 remodelled the gallery, added a public cafe and garden and a permanent House of Voltaire space, and added 42 percent more artists' studios. Studio Voltaire's supporters page gives the project cost as £2.8 million, completed in October 2021.
- Programmes: exhibitions, performances and live events, artist development, the Loewe Foundation / Studio Voltaire Award residency established in 2021, and the participation programme Not Our Class since 2011 (Wikipedia).
- House of Voltaire sells limited edition artworks (Studio Voltaire website); Joe Scotland is director (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 6 pm, with free admission (Studio Voltaire website).

FUNDING
Studio Voltaire: mixed: public funding (Arts Council England, Mayor of London), trusts and foundations, corporate patrons and individual supporters
- Public funders: Arts Council England, including its Cultural Capital Kickstart Programme; the Mayor of London's Strategic Investment Fund; and Stride, backed by the London Boroughs of Lambeth, Lewisham, Southwark and Wandsworth (supporters and funders page).
- Trusts and foundations: the Garcia Family Foundation, the Wolfson Foundation, Citybridge Trust, Garfield Weston Foundation, the Foyle Foundation, Cockayne - The London Community Foundation, the Mila Charitable Organisation, Henry Moore Foundation, the Hartfield Foundation and Span Trust.
- Corporate patrons: BakerMcKenzie and Fletcher Gallery Services. Gold supporters: Asymmetry Art Foundation, LOEWE and Marandi Foundation. Silver supporters: Christie's, Hauser & Wirth and Yana Peel. Sustainability funders: Voltsmart and the Wolfson Foundation.
- The Studio Voltaire Council core funds the programmes; its co-chairs are Ruth Chapman, Thomas Dane and Jill Garcia.
- Programme supporters include Art Fund, the British Council, Creative Scotland, Freelands Foundation, Paul Hamlyn Foundation, Lambeth Council, Japan House London, Kvadrat and the Elephant Trust. Building supporters include Cork Street Galleries, Givenchy and Hauser & Wirth.
- Bloomberg Philanthropies: "supported this website via the Digital Accelerator Programme" (supporters and funders page, no year given).

BOTTOM LINE
Studio Voltaire is funded by a mix of public money (Arts Council England, the Mayor of London), named trusts and foundations, two corporate patrons and a council of individual supporters. Its supporters page also names Bloomberg Philanthropies as a supporter of its website.
