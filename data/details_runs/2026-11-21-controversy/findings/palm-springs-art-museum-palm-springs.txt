Red flags: 2026 outcome of the board's investigation into anonymous allegations
- On 15 May 2026 the board announced the completion of an investigation commissioned by a Special Committee of its Audit Committee, begun in December 2025 after anonymous allegations received in October 2025, and carried out by the law firm Barnes & Thornburg and the forensic accounting firm RSM US (museum statement, 15 May 2026).
- The board stated: "The anonymous allegations of fraud, theft, and other malicious conduct were not substantiated", naming among them allegations of sales tax fraud and payroll fraud (museum statement).
- The statement says the investigation "confirmed a long-standing, Board-acknowledged practice" of using art sale proceeds "for purposes beyond strictly the acquisition of art during periods of operating stress", and that the museum plans to repay this internal loan in full by 2030 (museum statement).
- It says "some of the Museum's historical governance, reporting, and recordkeeping practices fell short of the Board's standards" and that the investigation found "certain procedural deficiencies" in the 2025 board officer nominations. It found the director search, "while expedited, was not improperly manipulated" (museum statement).
- The remedial measures listed include mandatory annual governance training for all trustees and a review of the policies on deaccession proceeds and restricted donor funds (museum statement).
