FONDAZIONE SANDRETTO RE REBAUDENGO: Turin, Italy
ID: fondazione-sandretto-re-rebaudengo
Website: https://fsrr.org

OVERVIEW
- Private non-profit contemporary art foundation with a museum building at Via Modane 16, Turin, in front of the Fergat public garden; further venues are Palazzo Re Rebaudengo in Guarene, the Art Park on San Licerio Hill in Guarene and the Isola di San Giacomo in Venice (the foundation's website).
- Launched in Turin in April 1995 on the initiative of the collector Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo, its president and founder; Francesco Bonami was artistic director for about twenty years (the foundation's website; Artribune, Marco Enrico Giacomelli, 23 February 2015).
- Named for its founder, Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo (the foundation's website).
- Turin building: a museum designed by Claudio Silvestrin, chosen through a competition, opened in 2002; about 3,500 m2, with a main exhibition space of about 1,000 m2 that divides with movable walls. The City of Turin owns the land, which the foundation holds on a 99-year loan for use (Artribune, 2015).
- Art Park on San Licerio Hill, opened in 2019 (the foundation's website): works by Paul Kneale, Ludovica Carbotta, Carsten Höller, Wilhelm Mundt, Manuele Cerutti and Mark Handforth (Finestre sull'Arte, Redazione, 13 July 2021).
- Programme: commissioning works from young artists, exhibitions, free cultural mediation and education (the foundation's website).
- Turin: Thursday 20:00 to 23:00 (free), Friday to Sunday 12:00 to 19:00; full ticket EUR 7, reduced EUR 5 for students under 26 and over-65s, free for children under 12; Palazzo Re Rebaudengo and the Art Park are free; no architectural barriers (the foundation's contacts page).

FUNDING
Fondazione Sandretto Re Rebaudengo: private non-profit foundation launched by Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo, with public grants, banking foundations, members and a renewable-energy partner
- Public contributions received in 2025: Regione Piemonte EUR 36,000 (balance of its 2023 activity grant), Comune di Torino EUR 56,108.11 (for the project "Memoria Collettiva nello spazio pubblico") and the Ministry of Culture EUR 41,000 (annual state contribution) (the foundation's transparency page).
- Partners named on the partners page: Regione Piemonte, Fondazione CRT, Fondazione Compagnia di San Paolo and ASJA, which designs, builds and manages biogas, wind and photovoltaic plants (the foundation's partners page).
- In 2015 Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo said public funding was about 18% of a budget of EUR 1.7 to 1.8 million, that Regione Piemonte had backed the project from the start, and that the former banking foundations Fondazione CRT and Compagnia di San Paolo have an important role (Artribune, 2015).
- Asja Ambiente is the foundation's sustainability partner and planted more than 2,500 trees in the Art Park (Finestre sull'Arte, 2021).
- Membership tiers under the scheme "Insieme a Patrizia per l'Arte" range from EUR 900 to EUR 2,500 (the foundation's membership page).
- Board of directors: Angelo Di Summa, Carlo Pavesio, Andrea Ganelli, Giovanni Lageard, Fabio Alberto Regoli, Agostino Re Rebaudengo, Emilio Re Rebaudengo, Eugenio Re Rebaudengo and Guido Saracco; Massimo Lapucci of Fondazione CRT and Stefano Lucchini, named by Fortune Italia on 21 March 2023 as Intesa Sanpaolo's chief institutional affairs and external communication officer, are on the advisory board (the foundation's About page; Fortune Italia).
- Social reports for 2021 to 2025 are published (the foundation's transparency page).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation was launched by its president Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo. Its money comes from public grants of EUR 36,000 (Regione Piemonte), EUR 56,108 (City of Turin) and EUR 41,000 (Ministry of Culture) in 2025, the partners Fondazione CRT and Compagnia di San Paolo, membership fees, and ASJA, which builds biogas, wind and solar plants, as a partner.
