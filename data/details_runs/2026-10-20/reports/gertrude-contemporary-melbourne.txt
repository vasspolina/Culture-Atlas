GERTRUDE CONTEMPORARY: Melbourne, Australia
ID: gertrude-contemporary-melbourne
Website: https://gertrude.org.au

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run contemporary art space and studio complex at 21-31 High Street, Preston South, in a former furniture warehouse, with a satellite gallery, Gertrude Glasshouse, at 44 Glasshouse Road, Collingwood (stated by the space).
- Began in 1983 as artist studios at 200 Gertrude Street, Fitzroy, in a converted textiles factory; in 1985 it became what it calls "Australia's first combined gallery and studio complex" (stated by the space).
- Louise Neri was founding director (Wikipedia). It was renamed Gertrude Contemporary Art Spaces in 2000 and Gertrude Contemporary in 2010 (stated by the space).
- Incorporated as an association in 1985; a voluntary board of up to 10 is chaired by the artist Callum Morton, with Tracy Burgess as Executive Director and Board Secretary (annual report 2025).
- The Studio Program supports 16 artists over two years, with eight invited each year by competitive application (stated by the space).
- In 2025 it held 14 exhibitions, 46 public programs and 42 education programs, with 26,573 onsite visitors (annual report 2025).
- Visiting: Gertrude Contemporary is open Tuesday to Saturday 11am to 5pm; Gertrude Glasshouse Thursday to Saturday 12 to 5pm. Both close on public holidays.

FUNDING
Gertrude Contemporary: grant-funded: Australian, Victorian and local governments, with private patrons and a long-term sponsor
- Revenue in 2025 was $1,237,429, of which grants and funding were $980,977 and donations and fundraising $154,243; expenses were $1,248,617, a deficit of $11,188 (financial report in annual report 2025).
- Core funding comes from the Visual Arts, Craft and Design Framework, an initiative of the Australian, State and Territory Governments, and from the Victorian Government through Creative Victoria. The annual report also names multi-year core funding from Creative Australia through its Four-Year Funding for Organisations and Creative Victoria's Creative Enterprises Program (annual report 2025; supporters page).
- The Victorian Department of Education Strategic Partnerships Program funds the ARTNOW and Contemporary Art on the Road education programs, with four-year direct grants for 2026 to 2029.
- The City of Darebin supports Gertrude as a Key Cultural Organisation through Darebin Arts, and the City of Yarra supported the 2025 Glasshouse program.
- Project funding in 2025: the Gordon Darling Foundation for the anniversary publication, the Copyright Agency Cultural Fund and Creative Australia's Plus1 Matched Funding for the exhibition program, and the Albert & Barbara Tucker Foundation for the Same Page Art Book Fair 2026 (annual report 2025; supporters page).
- Proclaim has sponsored the flagship Octopus exhibition since 2002 and supported the whole 2025 Past is Prologue program (annual report 2025).
- Private patrons in 2025: Michael Schwarz and David Clouston (principal patrons of Gertrude Glasshouse for a decade) and Michael and Janet Buxton (longstanding support) (annual report 2025). The supporters page also lists the Mori Provan Foundation, Sarah and Ted Watts, 101 Collins and named individuals.
- The Keir Foundation supported the Contact High: New York program in July 2025 (annual report 2025).
- The supporters page names Flys Alone, United Measures and Manfax as Production Partners, and trade firms such as SPI Equip Formwork and Service Lifts as Apex Circle supporters.

BOTTOM LINE
Grants and funding were $980,977 of Gertrude Contemporary's $1.24 million revenue in 2025; the annual report names the Australian and Victorian governments, the Department of Education and two city councils among its funders. Private money comes from named patrons, foundations and the Proclaim sponsorship. Its financial report and board are published.
