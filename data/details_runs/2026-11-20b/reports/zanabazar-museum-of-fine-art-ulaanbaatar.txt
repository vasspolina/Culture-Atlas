ZANABAZAR MUSEUM OF FINE ART: Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia
ID: zanabazar-museum-of-fine-art-ulaanbaatar
Website: http://www.zanabazarmuseum.mn/ (checked 2026-11-20; down)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Mongolian fine art in Chingeltei District, Ulaanbaatar (Wikipedia); the National Centre for Cultural Heritage (NCCH) lists it among Mongolia's museums.
- Founded in 1966 (NCCH; Google Arts & Culture). Wikipedia says the chairman of the Mongolian Craftsmen's Union Committee proposed it in 1965 and it opened on 23 July 1966; it began with over 300 exhibits, and its modern art became a separate Arts Gallery in 1989 (Google Arts & Culture).
- Named in 1995 for Gombodorjiin Zanabazar (1635 to 1724), the first Jebtsundamba Khutuktu, whose works it holds, among them the Sita Tara statues, the Five Dhyani Buddhas and the Bodhi Stupa (Google Arts & Culture).
- Building: built by a Russian merchant in 1905 and later used as a bank (Wikipedia).
- Collection: more than 16,000 objects in 10 halls covering Mongolian art from ancient times to the early 20th century (NCCH); Google Arts & Culture gives nearly 16,600 objects in 12 galleries, including thangkas, coral masks and B. Sharav's paintings "A Day in Mongolia" and "Airag Feast". Wikipedia gives more than 20,000.
- Director in November 2025: P. Baigalmaa (Chinggis Khaan National Museum). It joined Google Arts & Culture in 2015 with a virtual tour (Wikipedia).
- Recent programme: a solo show of more than 70 silk paintings by the master artisan Ukhaanzaya Dorjnamjin, 15 to 21 October 2025 (Qazinform, citing MONTSAME).
- Neighbouring tower: the museum sued the capital city's planning office over its approval of a 28-storey office and banking building for Trade and Development Bank beside the museum, arguing that groundwater settlement could crack its protected building, that the tower would stand three metres from its protected zone and that no protective measures were planned. The first-instance court dismissed the claim, the appeal court upheld that, and on 2 December 2025 the Supreme Court upheld both rulings (iKon.mn, December 2025, "Г.Занабазарын нэрэмжит музейн дэргэд 28 давхар барилга барихыг эсэргүүцсэн нэхэмжлэлийг гурван шатны шүүх хэрэгсэхгүй болгов", translated: three levels of court dismiss the claim against a 28-storey building beside the museum).

FUNDING
Zanabazar Museum of Fine Art: listed by Mongolia's National Centre for Cultural Heritage, with a long-running UNESCO project
- The NCCH, on the government domain ncch.gov.mn, carries the museum's page with its collection, contacts and website (NCCH).
- Since 2004 it has run the UNESCO project "Development of Museums and Protection of Nomadic Cultural Heritage", covering display, preservation and staff training (Wikipedia); Google Arts & Culture also describes its cooperation with UNESCO on preservation and training.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum belongs to Mongolia's national heritage network, with UNESCO support and no private funder on record. It publishes no budget, so who pays its running costs is the open question.
