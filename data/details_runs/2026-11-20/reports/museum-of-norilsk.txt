MUSEUM OF NORILSK (MUZEI NORILSKA) AND POLART RESIDENCY: Norilsk, Krasnoyarsk Krai, Russia
ID: museum-of-norilsk
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Norilsk's municipal museums have been joined since 2016 in the museum and exhibition complex "Muzei Norilska" (Lenta.ru, from a Nornickel press release); Snob counts it among the most important of the city's 13 cultural bodies (Snob, 2026).
- Director: Natalia Fedianina, who also founded and curates the PolArt polar art residency (Snob, 2026); as director of the Norilsk Museum Exhibition Complex she oversees the planned Arctic Museum of Modern Art, AMMA (The Art Newspaper, 2021).
- PolArt began in 2016, has hosted more than 50 residents and takes up to seven a year; Fedianina says residents are chosen for strong ideas, not portfolio or fame (Snob, 2026).
- AMMA is to fill a former shopping centre of about 8,500 square metres, designed by Ilya Mukosey, with an extension for new halls (The Art Newspaper, 2021). In 2023 its opening was planned for 2026 (AKM).
- In spring 2026 the museum showed "Vladimir Potanin's Olympic collection: 80 years of hockey glory", more than 60 items (Snob, 2026).

FUNDING
Museum of Norilsk: city museum, with its new art museum and several programmes paid for by the mining company Nornickel
- Norilsk Nickel (Nornickel) funds AMMA in full; 3 billion roubles (about $40 million) is earmarked for it, and 1.1 billion roubles for a second, local City Museum (MAMA) planned for 2026, out of 150 billion roubles the company committed to developing Norilsk (The Art Newspaper, 2021).
- In 2023 Nornickel picked PromStroiInzhiniring for AMMA design and documentation work worth 177 million roubles (AKM).
- The Potanin Olympic collection show was organised by the Russian International Olympic University and Nornickel, with support from the Night Hockey League (Snob, 2026).
- Nornickel and the Rodchenko School of Photography and Multimedia backed an April 2026 course on artistic research of territories (Snob, 2026).

Red flags: Nornickel's 2020 diesel spill in Norilsk
- At the end of May 2020 about 21,000 tonnes of diesel leaked from a damaged tank at Thermal Power Plant No. 3 of the Norilsk-Taimyr Energy Company, a Nornickel subsidiary (Meduza).
- On 5 February 2021 an arbitration court ordered the subsidiary to pay 146.2 billion roubles (about $1.96 billion) on a claim by the regulator Rosprirodnadzor, which had put the damage at 148 billion roubles (Meduza).

Red flags: sanctions on Nornickel's chief Vladimir Potanin (2022)
- The UK sanctioned Potanin on 29 June 2022, saying he kept growing his wealth while backing the Kremlin, including by buying Rosbank after the invasion of Ukraine (UK government).
- The US sanctioned Potanin, president and largest shareholder of Nornickel, on 15 December 2022, with his holding company Interros and Rosbank; the US did not designate Nornickel itself (Fortune).

BOTTOM LINE
Municipal at its base, but its new money comes from a miner fined over a major fuel spill and led by a sanctioned owner. Worth watching: how far the city's art scene comes to depend on the company once AMMA opens.
