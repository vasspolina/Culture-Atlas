THE HEPWORTH WAKEFIELD: Wakefield, UK
ID: the-hepworth-wakefield
Website: https://hepworthwakefield.org

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at Gallery Walk, Wakefield, West Yorkshire, on the south side of the River Calder (Wikipedia). Opened on 21 May 2011.
- Succeeded the municipal art collection begun in 1923 as Wakefield Art Gallery (Wikipedia).
- Named for the sculptor Barbara Hepworth, who was born and educated in Wakefield (Wikipedia). It holds 44 plaster and aluminium working models donated by the Hepworth family, and the Tim Sayer Bequest of 2016 added over 400 works.
- Building: designed by David Chipperfield, who won a RIBA-managed competition, and built by Laing O'Rourke at a cost of 35 million pounds; ten trapezoidal blocks with a concrete facade (Wikipedia). Wikipedia says the design is "not universally popular with local people."
- Awards: UK Museum of the Year in 2017 (Wikipedia). It launched the biennial 30,000 pound Hepworth Prize for Sculpture in 2015.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, closed Mondays except bank and Wakefield school holidays. Exhibition entry is 13 pounds or 11 pounds; free for members, Wakefield District residents and under-18s; the garden is free.

FUNDING
The Hepworth Wakefield: mixed: local council and Arts Council England as partners, with trusts, foundations and corporate supporters
- Registered charity 1138117 (The Hepworth Wakefield), income 4,236,583 pounds in the year to 31 March 2025 (Charity Commission data via Find that Charity).
- The museum's supporters page names Wakefield Council and Arts Council England as Partners.
- Major Supporters named: Bloomberg Philanthropies, Art Fund, Henry Moore Foundation, the Julia Rausing Trust, the 29th May 1961 Charitable Trust, the Eridge Trust, the Liz and Terry Bramall Foundation, the Nicholas Berwin Charitable Trust, the Ofenheim Charitable Trust, the John S Cohen Foundation and the Terra Foundation for American Art.
- Corporate supporters and business members named: G F Smith, Grand Central Rail, King & McGraw and Little Greene.
- Barbara Hepworth Campaign 2025, major supporters named: Art Fund and the Wolfson Foundation, Garfield Weston Foundation, The Headley Trust, The National Lottery Heritage Fund, the Julia Rausing Trust, the Deborah Loeb Brice Foundation, the Forster Family Foundation and the Garcia Family Foundation. In 2025 the museum and Art Fund raised 3.8 million pounds to keep Hepworth's Sculpture with Colour (Oval Form) Pale Blue and Red in the UK (Wikipedia).
- Building funders in 2011: Wakefield Council, Arts Council England and the Heritage Lottery Fund, with Yorkshire Forward, the Homes and Communities Agency and the European Regional Development Fund (Wikipedia).
- Bloomberg Philanthropies: the museum announced a partnership on 27 May 2021 that put its guide on the Bloomberg Connects app and gave it a free audio guide for the first time.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One critical piece, from the 2011 opening, aimed at the public spending on the museum.
1. The Jackdaw: Brian Lewis, "Art's urbane guerillas hit Wakefield: the Hepworth Museum" (July-August 2011)
URL: https://www.thejackdaw.co.uk/?p=168
- Describes a protest, "Operation Good Morning Mr Courbet", staged by Lewis and the "Free University of Chantry Bridge" at the opening of the 35 million pound building, with 21 paintings and a 16-page leaflet titled "No, No, No - Hepworth".
- Objects to the 35 million pound spend on the gallery at the expense of local artists, writers and museums, to reliance on arts consultants and their promotional data, and to promoting Hepworth as a "mausoleum artist" and to the programme of retrospectives by "eminent international artists".
- Names Peter Box, leader of Wakefield council, and David Chipperfield; reports that council officials agreed to read the leaflet.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Hepworth Wakefield is a mix of council and Arts Council England partnership funding, lottery and heritage funds, charitable trusts, Art Fund and a small group of corporate supporters, with Bloomberg Philanthropies listed as a major supporter.
