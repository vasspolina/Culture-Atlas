LE 19, CRAC: Montbéliard, France
ID: le-19-crac-montbéliard
Website: https://le19crac.com/en/about

OVERVIEW
- Regional centre for contemporary art at 19 avenue des Alliés, in the town centre of Montbéliard in front of the railway station (le19crac.com; DCA).
- Founded in 1993 and opened in 1995; since 2018 it holds the national label CACIN (Centre d'Art Contemporain d'Intérêt National) and belongs to the DCA network and the regional networks Seize Mille and TRAC (le19crac.com).
- Building: a 1920s warehouse, formerly the first office of the Peugeot car company, with an industrial aesthetic (le19crac.com).
- On site: a 550 m² exhibition space showing three to four exhibitions a year, plus off-site projects with partners; it supports artists financially and logistically, invites external curators and involves non-professionals (le19crac.com).
- Director: Adeline Lépine-Delaborde (DCA member page).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday 2 pm to 6 pm and Sunday 3 pm to 6 pm; free entry (le19crac.com).

FUNDING
Le 19, Crac: public: the State (DRAC Bourgogne-Franche-Comté), Région Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, the City of Montbéliard and the Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération
- The centre names its four public funders: the French government, the Burgundy-Franche-Comté Region, the City of Montbéliard and the Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération (le19crac.com).
- The DCA member page names the State funder as the DRAC Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, the regional cultural affairs office (DCA).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Le 19 is public: the French State through the DRAC, the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté Region, the City of Montbéliard and the Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération, all named by the centre itself. Entry is free.
