ID: mostyn

WIDER CONTEXT
- In October 2007 Conwy County Borough Councillor John Pitt resigned as one of Oriel Mostyn's 14 trustees, after board chairman Huw Owen wrote to Conwy County Borough Council suggesting a more sympathetic council representative replace him (Daily Post).
- Pitt criticised the trustees for spending £3.7m of mainly public money on a revamp of a building "in good repair", saying they were spending money "like oil sheikhs"; £2.7m was coming from the Arts Council of Wales (Daily Post).
- Director Martin Barlow defended the move, saying the chairman's letter pointed out that Pitt had attended one meeting out of seven, and that £3.7m was good value because a new building would have cost more than £5m (Daily Post).
