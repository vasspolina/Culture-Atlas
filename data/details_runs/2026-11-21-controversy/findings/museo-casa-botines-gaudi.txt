WIDER CONTEXT
- By 6 July 2017 the Fundación EspañaDuero, owner of the Botines building, gave the Fundación MonteLeón, which had had its registered seat there since the 1990s, an ultimatum by burofax to leave the building within a month, because it wanted to give the whole building over to a museum (El Español, 6 July 2017).
- MonteLeón's manager, Fernando García, objected to the manner of the notice: "No ha habido ningún tipo de preaviso verbal, diálogo o negociación previa" (translated: "There was no verbal notice, dialogue or prior negotiation of any kind"). He said MonteLeón's board would decide its reply at its meeting on 10 July (El Español).
