ART GALLERY OF NOVA SCOTIA – Halifax, Canada
Website: https://agns.ca (not checked this run because the fetch was blocked; 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Founded in 1908 as the Nova Scotia Museum of Fine Arts to hold the province's 200 Crown works, and renamed in 1975. It is the provincial art museum (Wikipedia).
- The main building combines the former Dominion Building (1867, occupied from 1988) and two floors of the Provincial Building (1998), about 6,200 m² in all, with about 1,810 m² of exhibition space. A Yarmouth branch opened in 2006. It closed in January 2020 after smoke damage and reopened in July 2021.

FUNDING
Art Gallery of Nova Scotia: mainly public (provincial).
- The existing record says it is a provincial crown agency, with 2024-25 supporters including the Province, Arts Nova Scotia, Canada Council, BMO, Scotiabank, TD Bank Group, Ubisoft, the Sobey foundations and Benjamin Bridge. Not re-checked this run, because the annual report PDF was blocked.
- New building at Bishop's Landing: estimated at C$130–140M, to be funded with C$80M from the province, C$30M from the federal government and C$30M raised by the gallery. KPMB's design was chosen in November 2020, and the province put the project on hold in 2022 over rising costs (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Bank sponsor convicted
- TD Bank pleaded guilty in October 2024 to money-laundering conspiracy and Bank Secrecy Act failures, paying about $3B (NBC News, per the existing record; the article could not be fetched this run).

BOTTOM LINE
A provincially funded museum. The new building is on hold. The only flag is a sponsor, TD Bank, and its 2024 guilty plea. This record was only partly re-checked.
