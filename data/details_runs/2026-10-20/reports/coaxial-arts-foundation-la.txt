COAXIAL ARTS FOUNDATION: Los Angeles, USA
ID: coaxial-arts-foundation-la
Website: https://coaxialarts.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run non-profit art space in a storefront at 1815 South Main Street in downtown Los Angeles, dedicated to experimental video, sound and performance art (Matt Stromberg, Hyperallergic, 27 March 2019; Coaxial website).
- Founded by Eva Aguila; her partner Brock Fansler is its treasurer (Joshua Oduga, "A Los Angeles Residency Envisions a Radical Future for Experimental Art", Hyperallergic, 26 March 2024).
- Hyperallergic (2024) says the residency launched in 2016 with inaugural residents JJ Stratford and Suzy Poling, and that residents work without curator oversight and receive studio space and access to video and sound equipment. The Coaxial website says it has run residencies since March 2015.
- Hyperallergic (2024) describes a Downtown LA storefront with a large exhibition and performance area.
- Programmes include residencies, workshops, screenings, concerts and talks (Hyperallergic, 2019). Aguila's early work included a 2014 collaboration with KCHUNG radio for the Hammer Museum's "Made in L.A." biennial (Hyperallergic, 2024).
- Publishes The Coaxial Arts Book, a 176-page volume documenting over six years of residencies, events and programmes (Coaxial website).

FUNDING
Coaxial Arts Foundation: grant-funded and earned income: public arts agencies, private foundations and ticket and admission income
- The Coaxial website says the foundation is supported by grants and corporate underwriting from the City of Los Angeles Department of Cultural Affairs, the Los Angeles County Arts Commission, the California Arts Council, the National Endowment for the Arts, the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, the Mike Kelley Foundation for the Arts, the Wilhelm Family Foundation, the Pasadena Arts Alliance and mediaThe Foundation (stated by the space).
- Andy Warhol Foundation: $60,000 in multi-year program support, Fall 2021 grant cycle, announced 12 January 2022 (Warhol Foundation).
- California Arts Council: $27,000 in General Operating Support for 2022-23, to help cover operating costs after pandemic-era drops in box office and entry revenue. Coaxial told the council "Before the pandemic 60% of our annual budget came from these earned income avenues" (California Arts Council).
- Hyperallergic reported in March 2024 that Coaxial had lost three key grants over two years and depends more on fundraising, including a ninth-anniversary benefit on 14 April at the venue Zebulon.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Coaxial is public arts grants and private foundations plus earned income: the Andy Warhol Foundation awarded $60,000 in its Fall 2021 cycle, announced in January 2022, and the California Arts Council $27,000 for 2022-23, and the space says it also receives support from Los Angeles city and county arts bodies and the National Endowment for the Arts.
