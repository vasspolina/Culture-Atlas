WIDER CONTEXT
- On 13 March 2022 Turkish diplomatic sources called "false" a Times of Israel report that Turkey would return the Siloam inscription to Israel during President Isaac Herzog's visit, and told Anadolu Agency that returning it to Israel, which Turkey views as a third country, was out of the question (Daily Sabah, 13 March 2022).
- On 16 September 2025 Benjamin Netanyahu said he had asked Turkey for the tablet in 1998; on 19 September 2025 Recep Tayyip Erdoğan said Turkey would not give Israel "even a single pebble belonging to Jerusalem" (bianet, 19 September 2025).
- The inscription has been held at the Istanbul Archaeology Museums since the late 19th century; it is registered as Ottoman Empire property and legally belongs to the Republic of Turkey (Daily Sabah; bianet).
