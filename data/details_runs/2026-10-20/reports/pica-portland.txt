PICA PORTLAND: Portland, USA
ID: pica-portland
Website: https://www.pica.org

OVERVIEW
- Portland Institute for Contemporary Art, a contemporary art organisation at 15 NE Hancock Street, Portland, Oregon.
- Founded in 1995 by Kristy Edmunds while she directed the Portland Art Museum's Art on the Edge program; PICA first worked from vacant or rented spaces around Portland (Wikipedia).
- Its annual Time-Based Art Festival (TBA) began in September 2003 and uses venues across the city; TBA:26 runs 9 to 13 September 2026.
- In 2016 PICA was gifted a 20-year no-rent lease on a building of about 16,000 square feet on North Williams Avenue by donor Allie Furlotti and the Calligram Foundation, and moved its offices there in November 2017 (Wikipedia). The address now listed on its website is 15 NE Hancock Street.
- Reuben Roqueñi was appointed Executive Director in 2023 (Wikipedia). Roya Amirsoleymani, Erin Boberg Doughton and Kristan Kennedy were named artistic directors in November 2017 (Wikipedia).
- PICA's regranting program, the Precipice Fund, began in 2011 with Andy Warhol Foundation money and had given $225,000 to 57 projects by 2016 (Wikipedia).
- The gallery was open Thursday 2 to 8 pm, Friday 12 to 6 pm and Saturday 12 to 4 pm for the exhibition MORE by Trisha Baga (20 August to 24 October 2026).

FUNDING
PICA Portland: mainly private giving: foundations, individuals and companies, with state arts money
- Fiscal year ending June 2025: revenue $2,018,235, of which contributions $1,817,964 (90.1 percent); expenses $2,474,760; net assets $1,371,707 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer). In the year to June 2024, revenue was $2,412,312 and expenses $2,331,200 (ProPublica).
- Government: the TBA page says PICA receives support from the Oregon Arts Commission, a state agency funded by the State of Oregon and the National Endowment for the Arts, and that TBA:26 is supported by the Oregon Cultural Trust. The support page directs matching donations to the Oregon Cultural Trust.
- Foundations named in the donor list: Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Collins Foundation, Ford Family Foundation, Ford Foundation, Jackson Foundation, Japan Foundation, Kinsman Foundation, Marie Lamfrom Charitable Foundation, Miller Foundation, The Parsons Art Foundation, Proteus Fund, SN Charitable Foundation, Vernissage Fund, The Zephyr Charitable Foundation, New England Foundation for the Arts, Oregon Community Foundation, Western Arts Alliance and National Performance Network.
- Companies named in the donor list: Pacific Power Foundation, Nike, Netflix, Google, Wieden + Kennedy, Hewlett Packard Enterprise Foundation, MODA Healthcare, Pittman & Brooks PC and Travel Portland.
- PICA's offices were in the Wieden+Kennedy headquarters from about 2001 to 2012 (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
PICA is paid for mainly by contributions, which were 90 percent of its fiscal 2025 revenue, from named foundations, individuals and companies, with support from the Oregon Arts Commission and the Oregon Cultural Trust. ProPublica holds its Form 990s from 2002 to 2025, and its donor list is on its support page.
