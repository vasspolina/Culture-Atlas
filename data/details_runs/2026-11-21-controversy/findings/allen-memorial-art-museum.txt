Red flags: 2023 seizure and 2024 return of a Schiele drawing claimed as Nazi loot
- In September 2023 the Manhattan District Attorney's office seized in place Egon Schiele's "Girl with Black Hair", a watercolor and pencil drawing dated 1911 that the museum acquired in 1958, valued at about $1.5 million (Ideastream Public Media, Carrie Wise, 14 September 2023). The office acted on suspicion that the work was stolen from Fritz Grünbaum, a Jewish collector who died in the Holocaust, and the work was subject to claims by his heirs (The Art Newspaper, Benjamin Sutton, 14 September 2023).
- Two other Schiele works were seized the same week, from the Art Institute of Chicago and the Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh (Ideastream Public Media, 14 September 2023).
- Oberlin College said: "We are confident that Oberlin College legally acquired Egon Schiele's Girl with Black Hair in 1958, and that we lawfully possess it. We are cooperating with the Manhattan District Attorney's criminal investigation" (Ideastream Public Media; The Art Newspaper, 14 September 2023).
- The Manhattan District Attorney's office stated on 26 July 2024 that a work from the Allen museum at Oberlin College was returned to the relatives of Fritz Grünbaum in January 2024.
