CASINO LUXEMBOURG, FORUM D'ART CONTEMPORAIN: Luxembourg, Luxembourg
ID: casino-luxembourg
Website: https://www.casino-luxembourg.lu

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 41 rue Notre-Dame in Luxembourg City's Ville Haute, founded in 1996. It presents exhibitions mostly by younger international artists (Casino Luxembourg).
- Kevin Muhlen is director general and Ruta Franke deputy director (Paperjam).
- Building: opened in 1882 as the Casino Bourgeois, designed by Pierre and Paul Funck, where Franz Liszt gave his last piano recital in July 1886. After the State bought it in 1959 it became the Foyer Européen and a glass and steel pavilion by René Maillet was added (Wikipedia).
- For Luxembourg City's 1995 year as European Capital of Culture the Swiss artist Urs Raussmüller adapted it into an exhibition space; in March 1996 it became a forum for contemporary art (Wikipedia). It hosted Manifesta 2 in 1998 (Lëtzebuerger Journal).
- On site: two exhibition rooms, a bookshop, the Infolab library with about 7,000 publications on art since the 1960s, and Casino Display, an artistic research lab (Casino Luxembourg; Wikipedia).
- Spring 2001: "Lady Rosa", a pregnant replica of the Gëlle Fra, the Luxembourg City monument, caused a scandal (Lëtzebuerger Journal, 14 August 2026).
- Visiting: admission is free (Casino Luxembourg); open daily except Tuesday from 11 am (Wikipedia). Guided tours, talks, concerts, performances and workshops are offered.

FUNDING
Casino Luxembourg: funded by the Ministère de la Culture, in partnership with Les Amis des Musées d'Art et d'Histoire Luxembourg
- The Ministère de la Culture funds the centre (Casino Luxembourg).
- Les Amis des Musées d'Art et d'Histoire Luxembourg is listed as a partner (Casino Luxembourg).
- Since 2024 the Casino has been an établissement public by law (Paperjam, 25 October 2026).
- Governance: the board of directors is chaired by Delphine Munro, with Claudine Hemmer as vice-chair and members Anne Greiveldinger, Laurence Klopp, Pascal Kremer, Ruud Priem, Louis Reuter, Begga Sigurdardottir and Silvano Vidale. The staff is 22 people in Luxembourg in 2026 (Paperjam).

BOTTOM LINE
The Luxembourg Ministry of Culture funds the Casino, which has been an établissement public since 2024 and names Les Amis des Musées d'Art et d'Histoire Luxembourg as a partner.
