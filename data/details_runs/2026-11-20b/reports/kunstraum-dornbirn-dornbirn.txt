KUNSTRAUM DORNBIRN: Dornbirn, Austria
ID: kunstraum-dornbirn-dornbirn
Website: https://www.kunstraumdornbirn.at/en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art venue in the Stadtgarten park of Dornbirn, Vorarlberg, at Jahngasse 9, with its office at Marktstraße 33 (Kunstraum website).
- Founded in 1987 as a private non-profit association to present and communicate contemporary art; it has shown in the historic assembly hall since 2003 (Kunstraum website).
- The Dornbirn turbine maker Rüschwerke built the brick hall in 1893 to assemble its ever larger turbine wheels; the company closed in 1984, and when the site was remodelled from 1999 to 2000 into the Stadtgarten, which also holds the inatura museum, the hall was left unchanged (Kunstraum website).
- The hall measures 30 by 14 metres and 11.15 metres high, with about 350 square metres of exhibition space; it has single-paned windows and is not heated (Kunstraum website).
- It holds up to four exhibitions a year; Ursula Palla's "Pale Light" runs from 20 November 2026 to 28 February 2027, and it also runs education programmes, tours and a friends' association (Kunstraum website).
- Thomas Häusle is director; the board is Gerald A. Matt (president), Roland Jörg, Judith Berger-Neustädter, Peter Rhomberg, Sylvia Volz and Uli Zumtobel (Kunstraum website).
- Matt succeeded Ekkehard Bechtold, president for fifteen years, as unpaid president on 1 July 2019 (German Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, closed Mondays; 4 euros for adults and 6.50 euros for families, free for under 19s, students, apprentices, people with disabilities and friends of the Kunstraum (Kunstraum website).

FUNDING
Kunstraum Dornbirn: mainly public: the City of Dornbirn, Land Vorarlberg and the Republic of Austria, with Dornbirner Sparkasse
- The association states that it is supported by the City of Dornbirn, the Province of Vorarlberg, the Republic of Austria and Dornbirner Sparkasse (Kunstraum website).
- Land Vorarlberg grants for programme and running costs: 50,000 euros in 2017 (52,500 requested) and 55,000 euros a year from 2018 to 2021, plus 15,000 euros in 2018 and 10,000 euros in 2021 for the cross-border "Heimspiel" exhibition (Vorarlberg government answer to a Landtag question, Zl. 29.01.368, 2 January 2023).
- Friends: annual membership costs 40 euros, 60 euros for families, 400 euros for promoters and 1,200 euros for patrons; members get free entry (Kunstraum website).
- Partners in kind: inatura Dornbirn (ticketing), Studio Stefan Amann (communications and catalogues), Roland Adlassnigg's Atelier für außergewöhnliche Angelegenheiten (installations) and Günter Richard Wett (photography and film) (Kunstraum website).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In March 2012 Gerald Matt, then director of the Kunsthalle Wien since 1996, asked to end his contract after a three-month suspension over allegations of mixing private and official work and of citizenships promised for sponsorship (ORF Wien, 2012).
- In December 2012 the Economic and Corruption Prosecutor's Office (WKStA) dropped its investigation of Matt for attempted prohibited intervention and breach of trust, finding that the interventions were not confirmed; it said much of the breach-of-trust complaint had become time-barred (ORF Wien, 7 December 2012).
- The WKStA also dropped its investigation of Thomas Häusle, then president of the Kunsthalle Wien association (ORF Wien, 7 December 2012).
- Matt now presides over the Kunstraum Dornbirn board and Häusle directs it.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from city, province and state plus a regional savings bank, with no flagged funder.
