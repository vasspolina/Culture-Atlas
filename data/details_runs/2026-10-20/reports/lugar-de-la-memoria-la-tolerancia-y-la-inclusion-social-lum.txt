LUGAR DE LA MEMORIA, LA TOLERANCIA Y LA INCLUSIÓN SOCIAL (LUM): Lima, Peru
ID: lugar-de-la-memoria-la-tolerancia-y-la-inclusion-social-lum
Website: https://lum.cultura.pe (checked 2026-10-24; only its 2023 annual report PDF was opened)

OVERVIEW
- State museum in the Miraflores district of Lima, about the victims of the violence of 1980 to 2000 between Shining Path, the MRTA, the security forces and self-defence committees (LUM Memoria 2023).
- Inaugurated on 17 December 2015 as an institution of the Peruvian state. It is one of the 56 museums run by the Culture Ministry's General Directorate of Museums (LUM Memoria 2023).
- Germany offered US$2 million in 2009 to build a memorial museum. President Alan García first rejected the offer, then accepted it after pressure from NGOs and opponents including Mario Vargas Llosa, who headed the planning committee (Wikipedia).
- Building designed by architects Sandra Barclay and Jean Pierre Crousse; the pedestrian entrance is on Bajada San Martín (LUM Memoria 2023).
- On site: a permanent exhibition with a 1980 to 2000 timeline, the room "Un Pueblo, todos los pueblos" and 18 audiovisual testimonies; temporary shows; education programmes; a documentation and research centre (LUM Memoria 2023).
- No reporting found of opening days or admission, because the site's pages were not read.

FUNDING
Lugar de la Memoria: state-funded: Peru's Ministry of Culture, with small foreign foundation and embassy grants for projects
- The 2023 opening budget was S/ 1,835,678, all from "Recursos Ordinarios", the ordinary state budget; most of it paid the salaries of 17 staff on administrative service contracts (CAS). Spending was S/ 1,818,319.23. In November the ministry added S/ 421,500 under its "Con Punche Perú 2" plan for outside services and maintenance, of which S/ 233,531.90 was spent (LUM Memoria 2023).
- Fundación Gerda Henkel financed scanners and the rescue of the archives of the Partido Unificado Mariateguista (LUM Memoria 2023).
- Fundación Ford supports the research project on the Jáuregui family and the community of Soras, Ayacucho (LUM Memoria 2023).
- The embassies of Germany, Spain, France, Colombia and Mexico supported publications and the VIII International Meeting in December 2023; Canada's embassy supported a mural; Ukraine's embassy and the EU delegation supported a photo exhibition (LUM Memoria 2023). No amounts are given.
- Patronato member Ramón Barúa Alzamora gave private support for publications.
- No reporting found of corporate sponsors.

Red flags: State censorship or punishment
- Director removed: the Culture Ministry removed director Manuel Burga, in post since 2018, in early January 2025, after he had recently signed a contract to stay until at least July 2025. Vice-minister Jamer Chávez Antícona, who took over the institution, told him "no hay ninguna razón, solo es un cambio" (Infobae, 9 January 2025).
- Unverified: the exit was due to political pressure from the parties Renovación Popular and Fuerza Popular, from unnamed sources cited by Infobae.
- Exhibitions at risk: the show "Wiphalas por la memoria", on deaths in the protests of December 2022 and early 2023, was in doubt, as were shows on forced sterilisations under Fujimori and on the Accomarca massacre (Infobae).
- Film barred: Apreci, the Peruvian cinema press association, said the Culture Ministry stopped the closing screening of Mohanad Yaqubi's "Revolutionaries Never Die" at the LUM for the Lima Alterna festival. According to Apreci, a ministry representative cited diplomatic relations and said Palestinian cinema could not be shown in a state institution. Apreci's statement is dated 9 October 2026; the festival moved the film to an independent cultural centre on 18 October (EFE via Infobae, 10 October 2026).
- Jurisdiction: in August 2026 several public figures backed a proposal to move the LUM's administration to the Justice Ministry. Culture Minister Alberto Beingolea said on 2 August "No voy a cerrar el LUM", on 18 August that Peru's museums sit within the Culture Ministry, and on 24 August denied a dispute with Justice Minister Ernesto Álvarez (El Comercio headlines).
- Closure of 2023: Miraflores inspectors closed the museum on 28 March 2023, citing safety certification; it stayed shut for 30 days, until 30 April (LUM Memoria 2023). Critics including the Ombudsman, Amnesty International Peru, the EU and the German government called the closure political (Wikipedia).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. An Artnet News report on the 2023 closure exists, but its page returned HTTP 403 and was not read.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is clean: the Culture Ministry pays the budget and foreign foundations and embassies give small project grants. The weak points are state control of the content, shown by the 2025 removal of the director and the October 2026 barring of a Palestinian film. Watch the August 2026 proposal to shift the museum to the Justice Ministry.
