NORTH PYONGAN PROVINCIAL REVOLUTIONARY HISTORY MUSEUM: Sinuiju, North Korea
ID: north-pyongan-provincial-revolutionary-history-museum
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Two-storey exhibition on revolutionary activity in North Pyongan Province, in the centre of Sinuiju on the Chinese border, across from the Chinese city of Dandong (Koryo Tours).
- The museum sits directly behind an open square holding the bronze monuments of Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Il (Koryo Tours).
- The North Korea Information Portal of South Korea's Ministry of Unification lists it under a longer name, in the atlas's translation the North Pyongan Province Comrade Kim Il Sung Revolutionary Museum, on the station square in Kwanmun-dong, Sinuiju.
- Sinuiju is the capital of North Pyongan Province and the main overland port of entry for tourists and foreign trade (Koryo Tours).
- Koryo Tours says the displays cover the leaders' activities and those of other figures in the region, and present Sinuiju's postwar rebuilding as part of the national story.
- Koryo Tours says photos are not allowed inside.

FUNDING
North Pyongan Provincial Revolutionary History Museum (Sinuiju): state-run: a provincial state museum
- The state announces its budget only as yearly percentage changes. KCNA claimed on 24 March 2026 that 2026 revenue would reach 100.5 percent of 2025 and spending 105.8 percent, with funds for the cultural sector at 103.1 percent of last year's.
- The same KCNA report divides state revenue between the central budget, put at 72.2 percent for 2026, and regional budgets.

Red flags: US sanctions on the North Korean government, 2016
- Executive Order 13722 of 15 March 2016 blocks property of the Government of North Korea and the Workers' Party of Korea that is in the United States or in the control of US persons; section 9 defines the Government as the DPRK government and its agencies, instrumentalities and controlled entities (Federal Register, 18 March 2016).

Not related: the Sinuiju History Museum, also in Sinuiju. Koryo Tours says the two are not to be confused.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a provincial state museum of Kim Il Sung's revolutionary history, paid for by the North Korean state. The weak point is the US blocking order on that state.
