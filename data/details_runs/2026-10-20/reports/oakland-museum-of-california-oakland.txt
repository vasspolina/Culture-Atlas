OAKLAND MUSEUM OF CALIFORNIA: Oakland, USA
ID: oakland-museum-of-california-oakland
Website: https://museumca.org (checked 2026-10-24; loads normally)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of California art, history and natural sciences at 1000 Oak Street, Oakland, on a campus beside Lake Merritt (museum website, Wikipedia).
- Opened in 1969 by merging the Oakland Public Museum, the Oakland Art Gallery and the Snow Museum of Natural History; Oakland voters had approved a $6.6 million bond in 1961 to start the campus (Wikipedia).
- Building: a modernist complex by Kevin Roche John Dinkeloo and Associates, landscape by Dan Kiley; Mark Cavagnero Associates designed the 2009 to 2013 renovation (Wikipedia).
- On site: art, history and natural science galleries, temporary exhibition galleries, an auditorium, a restaurant and roof gardens; more than 1.8 million objects in all (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday 11:00 to 17:00, Fridays until 21:00. Adults $25, seniors $22, youth 13 to 17 and students $18, children 12 and under free; $1 for Museums for All (EBT) cardholders; free on First Sundays (museum website).
- Accessibility: three entrance ramps and an elevator serving Levels 1 to 3, but not the roof gardens (museum website).

FUNDING
Oakland Museum of California: mixed private: donations, corporate supporters and earned income, with a city payment schedule agreed in 2011
- Finances: for the fiscal year ending June 2025 it reported revenue of $17.4 million, expenses of $20.4 million and contributions of $13.7 million (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- City of Oakland: Nonprofit Quarterly (13 June 2011) reported that from 1 July 2011 the nonprofit Oakland Museum of California Foundation would run the museum entirely, the city would keep ownership of the building and collection, and the city would pay the foundation $5 million a year for nine years, with a final payment of $3 million due in 2022. The article does not say the payments ended; no reporting found of payments made. A 2002 city bond, Measure G ($23.6 million), supported the renovation (Wikipedia).
- Corporate supporters, listed by tier with no years (museum website): Presenter, Kaiser Permanente. Sustainer, Bank of America and Pacific Gas and Electric Company. Benefactor, Bloomberg Philanthropies, Chevron, Dodge & Cox, Donahue Fitzgerald LLP, Levi Strauss & Co., Oakland Roots and Soul SC and The PG&E Corporation Foundation. Director tier, nine more names including EBMUD, Port of Oakland and Orrick.
- Chevron announced a $1 million gift on 6 February 2006 for educational programming at the museum (Chevron press release).
- Bernard Osher Foundation: provides free admission for children (stated by the museum). Bank of America and the Women's Board (translated from the Spanish release) support the Friday Nights series (museum press release, 14 July 2026).
- No reporting found of any other corporate gift amount,.

Red flags: fossil fuels (Chevron)
- Chevron is a Benefactor-level corporate supporter on the museum's supporters page, which gives no year. The only amount found is the $1 million gift announced in 2006.

Red flags: PG&E criminal plea
- PG&E is listed as a Sustainer, and The PG&E Corporation Foundation as a Benefactor, on the museum's supporters page.
- Announcement: on 23 March 2020 PG&E said it would plead guilty to 84 counts of involuntary manslaughter and one count of unlawfully starting a fire in the 2018 Camp Fire, with $4 million in fines including investigation costs (KTVU).
- Plea: PG&E pleaded guilty on 16 June 2020 in Butte County Superior Court before Judge Michael Deems to 84 counts of involuntary manslaughter (Wikipedia, NBC News, 17 June 2020). NBC reported a maximum fine of $3.5 million plus $500,000 for investigation costs, that no one would be imprisoned for the company's crimes, and that formal sentencing was still to come. No reporting found of the sentence as pronounced.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Bloomberg Philanthropies: in January 2025 protesters at London's Institute of Contemporary Arts demanded that the ICA "cut ties with Bloomberg Philanthropies," which sponsors the New Contemporaries exhibition, saying it is "directly implicated in facilitating settlement infrastructure in the West Bank." The ICA called the protest "misguided" (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 16 January 2025).
- Connection: Bloomberg Philanthropies is a Benefactor-level supporter of the museum. No reporting found of a campaign aimed at the Oakland museum over it.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a privately run nonprofit with a long list of named corporate supporters, and a city payment schedule agreed in 2011. The weak points are two current corporate names: Chevron, and PG&E after its 2020 guilty plea over the Camp Fire. Watch Bloomberg Philanthropies, which sponsors the museum and has been the target of a protest at another arts venue.
