KUNSTHALLE MAINZ: Mainz, Germany
ID: kunsthalle-mainz-mainz
Website: https://www.kunsthalle-mainz.de/de/partners-and-supporters

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition house for international contemporary art at Am Zollhafen 3 to 5 in the Mainz harbour district, run by the Stiftung Kunsthalle Mainz.
- Opened on 1 March 2008 (Wikipedia). The foundation received its official recognition on 12 February 2007 and was launched by Stadtwerke Mainz AG, which is also its founder (stated by the Kunsthalle).
- Building: the former boiler and machine house in the Zollhafen, planned in 1887 by Eduard Kreyßig and converted by the Mainzer Stadtwerke in 2006 to a design by Günter Zamp Kelp's Berlin office (Wikipedia).
- Directors: Natalie de Ligt (2008 to 2011), Thomas D. Trummer (2012 to 2015), Stefanie Böttcher (since June 2015); Yasmin N. Afschar led it on an interim basis from January 2023 to March 2024 (Wikipedia).
- Governance: the foundation has a board (Vorstand) that runs it and a Kuratorium of members of public life that supervises it (stated by the Kunsthalle). Wikipedia states that the board includes one member of the Mainzer Stadtwerke AG board, one representative of the City of Mainz and one of the state of Rhineland-Palatinate.
- On site: temporary exhibitions, guided tours, a free-admission "Freistunden" slot on three dates per exhibition, and the HURRA! Jugendkunstschule for children and young people.
- Visiting: Wednesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, closed Monday and Tuesday. Admission EUR 8, reduced EUR 5, families EUR 18, annual pass EUR 30, children under 6 free, refugees free. All rooms are step-free, doors are wider than 90 cm, there is a wheelchair-accessible toilet, and an Easy Language tour is listed.

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Mainz: municipal-utility foundation with city and state support: Mainzer Stadtwerke, City of Mainz and the Rhineland-Palatinate culture ministry
- Founder and host: Stadtwerke Mainz AG founded the foundation and the foundation's address in the imprint is c/o Stadtwerke Mainz AG, Rheinallee 41.
- The partners page lists Mainzer Stadtwerke AG and Mainzer Fernwärme GmbH first, then the Landeshauptstadt Mainz, Volksbank Darmstadt-Mainz and the Rotary Club Mainz-Aurea Moguntia as logos.
- City of Mainz: EUR 30,000 to the Stiftung Kunsthalle Mainz in each of 2024 and 2025 (City of Mainz institutional funding list).
- Rheinhessen Sparkasse makes the "Freistunden" format possible. The Rhineland-Palatinate Ministry for Science, Continuing Education and Culture has supported the art education programmes for children and young people since 2009. The Stiftung Kunst, Kultur und Soziales der Sparda-Bank Südwest eG funded the inclusive programme "Mit allen Sinnen" in 2022.
- The Kulturstiftung des Bundes funded the 2019 project "Between Us" (Wikipedia). HURRA! is funded through the BKJ programme "Künste öffnen Welten" within the federal programme "Kultur macht stark".
- Ownership of the utilities: Mainzer Stadtwerke AG is a municipal company of the City of Mainz. Mainzer Fernwärme GmbH is two-thirds owned by Mainzer Stadtwerke and one-third by Kraftwerke Mainz-Wiesbaden AG; Mainzer Stadtwerke and ESWE Versorgungs AG each hold 50% of Kraftwerke Mainz-Wiesbaden. The district heating network takes base load from the Mainz waste-to-energy plant and mid load from the gas and steam power plant and the CHP engines of Kraftwerke Mainz-Wiesbaden (Wikipedia, Mainzer Stadtwerke). Wikipedia states combined heat and power in gas power plants as the main source of district heating.

BOTTOM LINE
Kunsthalle Mainz is a foundation set up and housed by the municipal utility Mainzer Stadtwerke, with the City of Mainz paying EUR 30,000 a year in 2024 and 2025. The Rhineland-Palatinate ministry has supported the art education programmes since 2009, and the Rheinhessen Sparkasse and a Sparda-Bank foundation support the Freistunden and an inclusive programme.
