CONTEMPORARY ART PLATFORM (CAP): Kuwait City, Kuwait
ID: contemporary-art-platform-cap-kuwait-city
Website: https://www.capkuwait.com

OVERVIEW
- Art centre in the Design District of Shuwaikh Industrial, Area 2, Block 1, Street 28, Kuwait; a listing gives the building as Life Center, Industrial Shuwaikh Block 2 (My Art Guides).
- Founded in 2011 as a "non-profit private organization" (CAP). Al-Jarida names its founder as Amer Al-Huneidi (translated from Arabic).
- Spaces: a main exhibition space for curated and collective shows, the Art Room and the Design Room, over 700 square metres in total (My Art Guides).
- Programme: exhibitions of local, international, emerging and established artists, workshops, seminars, weekly film screenings, artist talks, panel discussions and school exhibition tours (CAP).
- A public library holds one of Kuwait's largest collections of art books (CAP).
- Visiting: open Saturday to Thursday, 10 am to 9 pm (CAP).

FUNDING
Contemporary Art Platform: a non-profit private organization, per CAP
- Al-Jarida reported on 17 October 2024 that CAP organised the exhibition "A Tribute to Lebanon" (more than 120 works by 60 Lebanese artists, from CAP's own collection and not for sale) with the Lebanese Business Council in Kuwait and the Lebanese Women's Council in Kuwait.
- The exhibition collected in-kind donations for people affected by the conflict in Lebanon, and included a fundraising campaign for shelter and emergency relief run by UNHCR with the Tanmiya Charity Association (Al-Jarida, Azza Ibrahim, translated).
- Al-Jarida reports that founder Amer Al-Huneidi attended the opening, and quotes UNHCR's representative in Kuwait, Nesreen Al-Rubaian, thanking him for his support of the campaign and for including it in the exhibition (translated).

BOTTOM LINE
CAP describes itself as a non-profit private organization founded in 2011, and Al-Jarida names Amer Al-Huneidi as its founder. Its October 2024 exhibition "A Tribute to Lebanon" was organised with the Lebanese Business Council in Kuwait and the Lebanese Women's Council in Kuwait and included a UNHCR and Tanmiya Charity Association fundraising campaign.
