ID: fokal-port-au-prince

WIDER CONTEXT
- In September 2016 the organisers of Massimadi, a four-day LGBTQ film, art and performance festival in Port-au-Prince led by the group Kouraj, said it had been called off after numerous threats of violence and a prohibition by a government commissioner; FOKAL and other co-hosts had been threatened with arson and other attacks (Associated Press, via CBS News, 27 September 2016).
- Lorraine Mangones, FOKAL's executive director, said: "FOKAL has been receiving threats of outrageous violence" (Associated Press, 27 September 2016).
- The capital commissioner, Jean Danton Leger, confirmed he issued an order to block the festival, telling a local radio station it was in part to protect Haiti's "moral and social" values; he had received a complaint in the preceding days from Senator Jean Renel Senatus (Associated Press, 27 September 2016).
