CHEONGJU CRAFT BIENNALE: Cheongju, South Korea
ID: cheongju-craft-biennale-cheongju
Website: https://www.okcj.org

OVERVIEW
- Craft biennale in Cheongju, North Chungcheong province; the 2025 edition was the 14th, ran 60 days from 4 September to 2 November 2025, and had 402,539 visitors (Hankyung for the edition and dates, The City Maker for the visitor total).
- First held in 1999 (Korea JoongAng Daily).
- The 2025 edition, "Building the World: Re_Crafting Tomorrow", had 72 countries, 23 simultaneous exhibitions, more than 1,300 artists and over 2,500 works (PR Newswire, Segye Ilbo).
- Main venue: the Cheongju Culture Factory, a former tobacco manufacturing plant that operated from 1946 to the late 1990s and employed more than 3,000 workers, reopened through an urban regeneration project; nearby are the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Cheongju, and the Dongbu Warehouse (PR Newswire).
- Kang Jae-young is artistic director (Hankyung); Thailand was the guest country in 2025 (inews24).
- Organised by the Cheongju Craft Biennale Organizing Committee, chaired by Cheongju Mayor Lee Beom-seok; the executive director is Byun Kwang-seop (The City Maker).
- Visiting: open 10:00 to 19:00, closed Mondays except during the Chuseok holiday (inews24).

FUNDING
Cheongju Craft Biennale: public and corporate: the city of Cheongju and national Korea Art Festival support, with corporate partners including Hyundai Motor
- The committee is chaired by the mayor of Cheongju, and city culture officials attended the 2025 results meeting (The City Maker).
- The Korean government designated the biennale and the Culture Factory part of its "Local 100 Cultural Attraction" programme, and support came through the Korea Art Festival, including ticket subsidies, support for emerging artists and art travel packages (PR Newswire, issued by the organisers).
- Hyundai Motor sponsors the Hyundai Translocal Series, whose first exhibition, "Entangled and Woven", was first presented at the 2025 biennale and co-organised with the Whitworth, The University of Manchester, with eight artists and collectives based in Korea and India (Hankyung; Hyundai Motor press release of 18 February 2025; Hyundai Art Lab, which says Hyundai Motor "invites and supports art institutions worldwide").
- Partners named by the organisers: Hyundai Motor Company, Genesis Cheongju and Hyundai Department Store Chungcheong Branch (partnership exhibitions), Aero K (stamp tour) and Emart24 (nationwide promotion); the Chungcheongbukdo Office of Education supported the Children's Biennale (PR Newswire).

Red flags: Hyundai Motor subsidiary named in a US child labour complaint
- Hyundai Motor supports the Hyundai Translocal Series at the 2025 biennale (Hyundai Motor press release, 18 February 2025).
- On 31 May 2024 the US Department of Labor filed a federal complaint naming Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama LLC, SMART Alabama LLC and the staffing agency Best Practice Service LLC. It alleges the three jointly employed a 13-year-old girl on an assembly line in Luverne, Alabama, for 50 to 60 hours a week between 11 July 2021 and 1 February 2022 (UPI, Doug Cunningham, "Labor Department sues Hyundai, Alabama suppliers for alleged child labor violations"). The only Hyundai entity it names is Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama LLC.
- Wikipedia states that Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama was incorporated in 2002 as a subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Company.
- Wikipedia states that Reuters reported in July 2022 that SMART Alabama, a Hyundai subsidiary, had hired minors as young as 12. Hyundai said it "does not tolerate illegal employment practices at any Hyundai entity" (Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia states that in September 2022 the Department of Labor fined SL Alabama, a unit of the supplier SL Corp, $30,076 and a federal consent decree followed, and that neither the state nor the federal labor department accused Hyundai or Kia of wrongdoing in those penalties.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the biennale is public and corporate: the mayor of Cheongju chairs the organising committee, the Korean government supports it through the Korea Art Festival, and Hyundai Motor supports the Translocal Series and is a named partner. The US Department of Labor's May 2024 child labour complaint names Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama, a subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Company, as the Hyundai defendant.
