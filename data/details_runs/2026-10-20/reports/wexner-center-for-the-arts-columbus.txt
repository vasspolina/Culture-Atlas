WEXNER CENTER FOR THE ARTS: Columbus, USA
ID: wexner-center-for-the-arts-columbus
Website: https://wexarts.org/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 1871 North High Street on The Ohio State University campus; it does not collect art (Wikipedia).
- Opened on 17 November 1989, replacing the University Gallery of Fine Arts (Wikipedia). Leslie H. Wexner made the founding gift.
- Named for Wexner's father, Harry L. Wexner (stated by the Wex).
- Designed by Peter Eisenman with Richard Trott and landscape architect Laurie Olin, from a 1982 to 1983 competition, on the site of the Armory, torn down in 1959 after a fire (stated by the Wex).
- On site: galleries, film and video screenings, a store and a café (stated by the Wex).
- Galleries are open Tuesday to Sunday, 9:30 am or 10 am to between 4 pm and 7 pm depending on the day, and closed Monday. Gallery admission has been free since 2022 (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Wexner Center for the Arts: university and donor-funded: Ohio State, the Wexner Center Foundation and gifts, plus Ohio state money
- The Wexner Center Foundation, chaired by Leslie H. Wexner, reported revenue of $1,322,513 and expenses of $2,262,665 for the year to June 2025, with net assets of $14.0 million (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- WOSU (11 June 2026) reports the Center receives $3.3 million in grants and gifts.
- On 20 July 2023 the Wex announced over $977,000 from the Ohio Arts Economic Relief Grant Program, administered by the Ohio Department of Development, for general operations and staff development.

Red flags: Epstein-linked
- Wexner link: Hyperallergic (22 May 2026) states that Les Wexner "had granted" Jeffrey Epstein power of attorney over his fortune for decades. WLOS (9 April 2020) reports that Wexner employed Epstein as his personal money manager in the late 1980s and that Wexner said he severed ties 12 years earlier. Wexner has denied knowing of Epstein's crimes (Hyperallergic).
- Money to the Wex: an Ohio State review released on 9 April 2020, with EY, found $336,000 in donations and pledges from Epstein and his foundation, all to the Wexner Center: $260,000 received from the J. Epstein Foundation between 1990 and 1997, a $75,000 pledge accountants could not confirm as received, and a $1,000 membership gift. Ohio State gave the sum to the Ohio Attorney General's Human Trafficking Initiative (WLOS).
- Leadership: Les Wexner is chairman and trustee of the Wexner Center Foundation, per its 990 for the year to June 2025 (ProPublica).
Red flags: protest over the name
- Wex Workers United, the Center's union, said in a statement reported on 22 May 2026 that the name "does a profound disservice" and that "artists are choosing not to work with us" because of Wexner's association with Epstein (Hyperallergic). An Ohio State spokesperson declined to comment on the statement, said each renaming request "is thoughtfully considered", and said about 500 requests have been filed under the University Naming Review Procedure since Wexner was formally questioned.
- WOSU (11 June 2026): one artist and one grant withdrew support, and the grantee and the artist pointed to the Wexner name. Interim executive director Cat Predmore declined to name either or give an amount.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Recent art-press coverage is about the Wexner name, staff conditions and leadership; older criticism concerns the building's design.
1. Hyperallergic: Rhea Nayyar, "Workers Push to Rename Wexner Center for the Arts Over Epstein Ties" (22 May 2026)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/workers-push-to-rename-wexner-center-for-the-arts-over-epstein-ties/
- Union staff say the name "does a profound disservice" to artists and community members, and that some artists refuse to work with the Center.
2. Hyperallergic: Rhea Nayyar, "Wexner Center Director Departs Amid Staff Complaints" (29 October 2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1053106/wexner-center-director-departs-amid-staff-complaints/
- Anonymous staff describe low morale and poor leadership under executive director Gaetane Verna; university documents cited by the piece show 28% staff turnover in under two years.
- Reports a no-confidence letter signed by over a dozen staff, a deficit of roughly $1.1 million (both per the Columbus Dispatch) and Verna stepping away on 29 October 2025.
3. Christian Science Monitor: Laura Van Tuyl, "A Building Waiting To Be A Building" (11 December 1989)
URL: https://csmonitor.com/1989/1211/lwex.html
- Describes the building as looking disordered, with no obvious front door, and the project as a gamble for Ohio State and the architects.
- Criticises the building, not the funding. Not an art-press outlet.

BOTTOM LINE
The Center was founded on Leslie Wexner's gift and he chairs its foundation, so the money is tied to a man whose Epstein links are documented. Ohio State's 2020 review found $336,000 of Epstein's own giving went to the Center, $260,000 of it received from 1990 to 1997. Worth watching: further withdrawals and any Ohio State renaming decision.
