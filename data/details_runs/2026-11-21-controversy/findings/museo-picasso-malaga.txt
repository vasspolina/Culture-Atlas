Red flags: 2023 strikes over the staff collective agreement
- Staff began protests at the museum entrance on 20 May 2023 under the slogan "Luxury Museum, Cut-price workers"; 93% of employees took part in a vote and 85% of voters backed a strike on 2 June 2023 (Artdaily, 30 May 2023).
- The workers said they had lost 25% of their purchasing power after eleven years without a raise; the company offered an 8% wage increase with no retroactive effect for 2022 (Artdaily).
- The works committee called further strikes on 14 and 20 June 2023 and said the company was trying to impose a text that had not been negotiated (El Salto, 6 June 2023).
- The foundation's manager, Guillermo Peiró Posadas, said: "Ellos dicen que han tenido una pérdida de poder adquisitivo del 25% y es fácil calcular que no es verdad" ("They say they have lost 25% of their purchasing power and it is easy to calculate that this is not true", translated), and that the foundation offered "el 18% de recuperación" over the term of the agreement (El Independiente, 6 June 2023).
- Strikes were scheduled for 18 to 22 September 2023, closing the museum for five days (The Art Newspaper, 4 August 2023).
- After another failed meeting at the Andalusian mediation body SERCLA, the works committee called further strikes for 26, 27 and 30 September and 2 October 2023, and a protest at the main entrance during the 2 October opening of El eco de Picasso (Europa Press, via Última Hora, 25 September 2023).
