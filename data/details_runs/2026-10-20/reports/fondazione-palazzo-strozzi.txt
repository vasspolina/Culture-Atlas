FONDAZIONE PALAZZO STROZZI: Florence, Italy
ID: fondazione-palazzo-strozzi
Website: https://www.palazzostrozzi.org (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition foundation in Palazzo Strozzi, Piazza Strozzi, at the corner of Via Tornabuoni and Via Strozzi in central Florence; the annual report places it at the intersection of Via Tornabuoni and Via Strozzi (2025 annual report; stated by the space).
- Set up in 2006 by the Comune di Firenze, the Province, the Camera di Commercio and a consortium of private companies, the Associazione dei Partner di Palazzo Strozzi; first president Lorenzo Bini Smaghi, first director James Bradburne. Arturo Galansino has been director general since 2015 (2025 annual report).
- The palace was begun on 6 August 1489 for Filippo Strozzi, with Giuliano da Sangallo and Simone del Pollaiolo, il Cronaca; the Strozzi heirs sold it to the Istituto Nazionale delle Assicurazioni in 1937, and since 2024 it has belonged to the Comune di Firenze (2025 annual report).
- Six exhibitions in 2025 on 1,838 square metres, including Tracey Emin and Beato Angelico; 384,176 visitors and 47,707 participants in education and access programmes (2025 annual report).
- Visiting: the site showed opening 10 am to 8 pm on the day checked. No reporting found of current ticket prices or venue access details.

FUNDING
Fondazione Palazzo Strozzi: public-private: 47% private gifts and sponsors, 38% its own earnings, 15% public bodies
- 2025 total income €11.68 million (2025 annual report).
- Own earnings €4.40 million, 72% from tickets, including €2.24 million for Beato Angelico and €708,000 for Tracey Emin (2025 annual report).
- Public €1.74 million: Comune di Firenze, Regione Toscana and Città Metropolitana di Firenze €500,000 each; Camera di Commercio €200,000; Ministry of Culture library grant and 5x1000 €43,000 (2025 annual report).
- Private €5.54 million: Fondazione CR Firenze €1.1 million; Partners Committee €928,000; Fondazione Hillary Merkus Recordati €600,000; Intesa Sanpaolo €464,000; direct sponsorships €2.23 million from over 25 backers including AON, Gucci, Max Mara, Gruppo Beyfin, the Antinori family and the Jay Pritzker Fund; Friends and Patrons about €214,000 (2025 annual report).
- The Partners Committee's Circle, which names a board member, is Christian Levett, Salvatore Ferragamo, Rocco Forte Hotels, Deloitte, Findomestic (BNP Paribas group), Amanda Platek, Toscana Aeroporti, Gruppo Beyfin, Gucci, Maria Manetti Shrem and Palazzo Strozzi Foundation USA (2025 annual report).
- Board 2025: president Luigi De Siervo, honorary president Leonardo Ferragamo (2025 annual report).

Red flags: fossil fuels (Enegan)
- Enegan, a Florence energy retailer that sells gas as well as electricity, is one of the Partners in the Palazzo Strozzi Partners Committee for 2025 (Enegan; 2025 annual report).
- Since 2021 Enegan has supplied the foundation's electricity, certified renewable through guarantees of origin (2025 annual report).
- No reporting found of a protest, open letter or objection aimed at the foundation over Enegan.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism. The reviews found are favourable; the closest the art press comes is a note on the narrow range of private lenders for the Emin show.
1. Finestre sull'Arte: Emanuela Zanon, "Sesso e solitudine sono intrecciati e complementari. Com'è la mostra di Tracey Emin a Firenze" (2025)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/recensioni-mostre/recensione-mostra-tracey-emin-firenze-sesso-e-solitudine-intrecciati-e-complementari
- Calls the public-private set-up a "modello virtuoso"; reports the president's figure of about 75% of the budget from the foundation's own resources, which makes the curator "un manager con competenze aziendalistiche e amministrative".
- Notes the loans came from "un ristretto ventaglio di prestatori", mainly private collectors and galleries such as White Cube, and misses My Bed.
- Criticises the show's selection, not the institution.
2. Finestre sull'Arte: editorial staff, Palazzo Strozzi facade vandalised (2021)
URL: https://finestresullarte.info/en/news/florence-palazzo-strozzi-facade-vandalized-with-huge-writing
- "Jeff Koons marry me" was sprayed across two sides of the palace during the Koons show; Galansino called it a serious act.
- Not criticism of the institution.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is open and spread out: local government, a banking foundation, Intesa Sanpaolo, the Recordati foundation, ticket sales and dozens of luxury, finance and Florentine firms. The one funder flag is Enegan, a gas and power retailer that is a Partner and supplier. Worth watching: whether Enegan's role grows beyond the Partners list.
