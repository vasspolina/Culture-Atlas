THE COMMON GUILD: Glasgow, UK
ID: the-common-guild-glasgow
Website: https://www.thecommonguild.org.uk/support

OVERVIEW
- Visual arts organisation in Glasgow, now based at 5 Florence Street on the south bank of the River Clyde, in a former school opened in 1894 as Adelphi Terrace Public School and being developed into studios and creative businesses by Assumption Studios (stated by the space).
- Founded in 2006 (Wikipedia); Katrina Brown was Founder Director and stepped down after 19 years, and Chloë Reith has been appointed Director (stated by the space).
- From 2008 to 2018 it showed exhibitions at 21 Woodlands Terrace, a Victorian townhouse overlooking Kelvingrove Park (stated by the space).
- From 2008 to 2013 it worked with Glasgow's Gallery of Modern Art and the Art Fund to build a collection of international contemporary work for Glasgow, and in 2013 it curated Scotland + Venice for the 55th Venice Biennale (stated by the space).
- Solo exhibitions have included Martin Creed, Roni Horn, Tacita Dean and Wolfgang Tillmans, and it commissioned Duncan Campbell in 2014 and Janice Kerbel in 2015 (Wikipedia).
- The building holds offices, a library of over 1,000 contemporary art books, and exhibition and event spaces (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: exhibitions are open Thursday to Saturday, 12 to 5pm; booking is generally free and visitors who can pay are encouraged to donate (stated by the space).

FUNDING
The Common Guild: Creative Scotland, grants and donations (stated by the space)
- Creative Scotland: Gareth Harris reported in The Art Newspaper on 31 January 2025 that Common Guild "will receive £702,000 over the allocated three-year period" in Creative Scotland's multi-year funding round.
- Thanks list on the support page, undated: the Ampersand Foundation, Arthouse (accommodation partner), British Council Scotland, the High Commission of Canada, Culture Ireland, Elephant Trust, Flanders: State of the Art, Fluxus, Goethe-Institut, Hope Scott Trust, the Henry Moore Foundation, Outset and Pro Helvetia (stated by the space). The page also names Creative Scotland as a support body.
- Individuals thanked: Alistair and Susan Duff, Emma and Fred Goltz, and Sigrid and Stephen Kirk (stated by the space).
- Scottish charity register (OSCR, SC021428): income of GBP 379,248 and expenditure of GBP 383,499 in the year to 31 March 2025; income of GBP 389,422 in the year to 31 March 2024.
- Trustees: Alice Wyllie, Orsod Malik, Lucy Askew, Jeremy Glen, Nicholas Van Jonker, Chaz Scott, Jordan McCleary and Anne Hardy (OSCR).

BOTTOM LINE
The Common Guild is supported by Creative Scotland, which will give it GBP 702,000 over three years (The Art Newspaper, 31 January 2025), together with grants from trusts and foreign cultural agencies and gifts from individuals. Its income was GBP 379,248 in the year to 31 March 2025 (OSCR).
