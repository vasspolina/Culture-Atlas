SA SA ART PROJECTS: Phnom Penh, Cambodia
ID: sa-sa-art-projects-phnom-penh
Website: http://www.sasaart.info/

OVERVIEW
- Not-for-profit, artist-run space in Phnom Penh, on Street 350 since the White Building was demolished in 2017 (Cambodianess, 22 October 2022).
- Established in 2010 by the Cambodian art collective Stiev Selapak, in response to the absence of a not-for-profit, artist-run space embracing diverse and experimental art practices. The co-founders include Khvay Samnang, Lim Sokchanlina and Vuth Lyno (Biennale of Sydney).
- Began in an apartment in the White Building, a 1960s housing complex also called the Municipal Apartments, which housed municipal staff and then cultural workers and low-to-mid income families (Vuth Lyno, Afterall, 11 November 2020).
- Programmes: free arts classes, education programmes, workshops, residencies for young Cambodian artists, and exhibitions (Cambodianess).
- Runs the Pisaot artist residency and a reading room in a converted 1990s Khmer-style house; the library began in 2011 and holds about 200 books plus art magazines and a photo archive of the White Building's artist community (Serine ahefa Mekoun, Contemporary And, 9 November 2022).
- Keeps the White Building Art Archive, an online database of visual and audio material (Biennale of Sydney).
- Was a guest at documenta fifteen in Kassel in 2022, alongside which the collective planned an open workshop house in Phnom Penh for young Cambodian artists (Kathrin Eitel, südostasien, issue 1/2022).

FUNDING
Sa Sa Art Projects (Phnom Penh): donor-funded: a New Zealand-based foundation, benefit art auctions and project assistance
- Rei Foundation Limited, Auckland, New Zealand, partnered with Sa Sa from April 2018 to January 2024, covering an art education programme, a bridging programme for new artists, an exhibition programme, the Pisaot residency and cross-disciplinary collaborations (Rei Foundation project page). Rei Foundation renewed its support for 2019 to 2020 on 7 October 2019, paying for contemporary art and photography classes and English and professional skills workshops (Rei Foundation journal). Its about page names Reiko Fukutake as founder.
- Online artist auction: more than 90 Cambodian and international artists donated works in October 2022 to raise funds for the free classes, education programmes, workshops, residencies and exhibitions; manager Chum Chanveasna said the online auction was first held in 2020 because of COVID-19 and would now be held every two years (Cambodianess).
- Presentation at the 21st Biennale of Sydney (2018) was made possible with assistance from Reliable Source Industrial (Cambodia) Co., Ltd and the Australia-ASEAN Council of the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (Biennale of Sydney).

WIDER CONTEXT
- documenta fifteen (2022) drew criticism in Germany. Taring Padi's mural People's Justice was condemned by the German and Israeli governments as antisemitic and was covered and removed days after the 18 June 2022 opening; general director Sabine Schormann resigned on 17 July 2022; an investigation released in January 2023 said the show "served as an echo chamber" for antisemitism (Wikipedia).
- The criticism concerned the mural and the exhibition as a whole, not Sa Sa; Wikipedia lists Sa Sa Art Projects among the exhibition's participants and südostasien describes it as a guest at documenta fifteen.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Sa Sa is small and donor-based: a New Zealand foundation (Rei Foundation, 2018 to 2024), artist-donated auctions and, for a 2018 biennale presentation, assistance from a Cambodian company and the Australia-ASEAN Council of the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade.
