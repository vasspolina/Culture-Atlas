CASTLEFIELD GALLERY: Manchester, United Kingdom
ID: castlefield-gallery-manchester
Website: https://www.castlefieldgallery.co.uk

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery and artist development organisation at 2 Hewitt Street, Manchester, behind Deansgate railway station. It is a registered charity (number 515571) and a company (number 01838334) (accounts).
- Founded in 1984 by the Manchester Artists Studio Association (MASA), whose members were former Manchester Polytechnic students. Matthew Pendergast, the gallery's Curator and Deputy Director, writes that it was the first dedicated contemporary art space in Manchester and the second in the UK outside London; the first exhibition was John Hoyland's "Recent Paintings" in March and April 1984 (Oxford Road Corridor, November 2023).
- Programme: exhibitions, commissions, artist development and artist exchange. Castlefield Gallery Associates supports more than 400 members, and Castlefield Gallery New Art Spaces provides affordable making and project space in the North West. Ryan Gander is its Artist Patron.
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 12pm to 5.30pm; closed Monday, Tuesday and between exhibitions. Free to visit.
- Access: step-free entrance, a lift to all floors, a wheelchair-accessible toilet on the first floor, assistance dogs welcome and large-print handouts. The Deansgate/Castlefield Metrolink stop serves the gallery.

FUNDING
Castlefield Gallery: Arts Council England and Manchester City Council grants, trusts, and individual and corporate donors
- In the year to 31 March 2025 the charity reported cash income of £334,618 and donated services of £336,817 (accounts).
- Arts Council England: £71,288 in 2024/25 (National Portfolio Organisation funding, the grant expected to extend to the end of 2027/28). Manchester City Council: £19,622 (Cultural Partnership Grant, confirmed until the end of 2025/26). Together £90,910 in public grants (accounts, note 12).
- The accounts credit Manchester City Council, the Haworth Trust and the Brian Mercer Trust with funding 30 fully funded Associates' memberships. The gallery's own site lists Arts Council England, Manchester City Council, Fenton Arts Trust and the Brian Mercer Trust as funders.
- Cash donations were £54,410, from New Art Spaces corporate donations, regular giving and one-off gifts (accounts). Named individual patrons on the support page include Hilary Jack, Sandeep Ranote, Adrian Slatcher, John Williams, Simon Bushell, Cathy Mocke, Roland Hill (Silver Patron) and Chris Klingenberg (Commissioning Patron). Patron tiers run from £60 to £1,200 a year.
- One trustee received £690 for mentoring artists in 2024/25 (accounts, note 11). Total restricted funds were £205,495 at 31 March 2025.
- Trustees in 2024/25: Ceri Hand (Chair from 26 March 2025), Alisha Kadri, Adrian Slatcher, Mariama Attah, Margaret Bourke, Katie Fallen, Paul Flint, Kate Jesson, Chara Lewis and Sandeep Ranote (accounts).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Castlefield Gallery is grants from Arts Council England (£71,288) and Manchester City Council (£19,622) in 2024/25, charitable trusts including the Haworth Trust, the Brian Mercer Trust and Fenton Arts Trust, and individual and corporate donors. Cash income in the year to 31 March 2025 was £334,618, plus £336,817 in donated services.
