SINUIJU HISTORY MUSEUM: Sinuiju, North Korea
ID: sinuiju-history-museum
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- History museum in Sinwon-dong, an urban subdivision of Sinuiju on the Chinese border (Koryo Tours).
- Founded on 30 November 1947 (Koryo Tours).
- Shows historical remains from prehistoric times to the modern era, including antiquities excavated and collected in the province, and materials on the region's history (Koryo Tours).
- The North Korea Information Portal of South Korea's Ministry of Unification gives a floor area of 2,300 square metres on three floors, with four display rooms and a relic store, and says the displays run from primitive society to the March First Movement of 1919.
- Room one, on the first floor, holds maps of revolutionary relics and of the main sites in North Pyongan, Stone Age and Bronze Age pottery and material on Tangun and Kojoson; room two, on the second floor, covers Koguryo, Paekche and Silla, with armour, arrowheads and photographs of the Kwanggaeto stele (North Korea Information Portal).
- The third floor covers Palhae and Koryo in room three and the Joseon period to modern times in room four, which shows the armour and swords of the Pyongan militia leaders Jong Pong Su and Ri Rim and the tools of the 19th-century brassware makers of Napchong in Jongju (North Korea Information Portal).
- The last section shows material on Kim Hyong Jik, Kim Il Sung's father, and on Kim Il Sung's own struggle (North Korea Information Portal).
- The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, published by the Academy of Korean Studies in South Korea, names it with the Kangwon, Kaesong, Wonsan and Kanggye museums as one of North Korea's representative regional history museums.
- Sinuiju holds the Sino-Korean Friendship Bridge and the former end of the Broken Bridge, which US forces bombed in the Korean War (Koryo Tours).
- Koryo Tours says photos are not allowed inside.

FUNDING
Sinuiju History Museum (Sinuiju): state-run: a state history museum
- North Korea has a history museum in each province-level unit, and the Korean Central History Museum in Pyongyang represents and oversees them (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture).
- The state announces its budget only as yearly percentage changes. KCNA claimed on 24 March 2026 that 2026 revenue would reach 100.5 percent of 2025 and spending 105.8 percent, with funds for the cultural sector at 103.1 percent of last year's.

Red flags: US sanctions on the North Korean government, 2016
- Executive Order 13722 of 15 March 2016 blocks property of the Government of North Korea and the Workers' Party of Korea that is in the United States or in the control of US persons; section 9 defines the Government as the DPRK government and its agencies, instrumentalities and controlled entities (Federal Register, 18 March 2016).

Not related: the North Pyongan Provincial Revolutionary History Museum, also in Sinuiju. Koryo Tours says the two are not to be confused.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a provincial history museum paid for by the North Korean state. The weak point is the US blocking order on that state.
