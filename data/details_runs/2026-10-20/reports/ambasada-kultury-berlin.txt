AMBASADA KULTURY: Berlin, Germany
ID: ambasada-kultury-berlin
Website: https://ambasada.art (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Initiative of Belarusian culture workers and activists in exile, with no gallery of its own; it calls itself a network of "non-localized culture ambassadors" (stated by the initiative). CEC ArtsLink lists it as founded in Minsk and now based in Berlin; the initiative's own PerspAKTIV page tags it "Belarus/Lithuania".
- Founded in 2021 by Valentina Kiselyova and Anna Chistoserdova (spellings as on the Berlin Art Week and Kalektar pages), who earlier ran the Podzemka gallery in Minsk (2004 to 2009) and the contemporary art gallery Ў (2009 to 2020) (Berlin Art Week; CEC ArtsLink).
- Ў gallery was dissolved on 29 October 2020; Wikipedia says that year its co-owner Aliaksandr Vasilievič was described as a political prisoner, and that he and his partners divested their stakes in the gallery and its bar. Kiselyova left Belarus in June 2021 and has lived and worked in Berlin and Poznań since (Kalektar).
- Projects listed on its site: Open Muzej, Antiwarcoalition.art, EVAa, PerspAKTIV, a residency under CEC ArtsLink's Art Prospect programme, and Secondary Archive. Chistoserdova also manages the perspAKTIV residency for Belarusian culture workers in Germany and Poland (CEC ArtsLink).
- Hosted one-month residencies in Berlin for artists and curators working on forced migration, as a host in CEC ArtsLink's Art Prospect programmes (2022 to 2024) (CEC ArtsLink).
- On 13 September 2021 it staged a Berlin Art Week programme at Haus der Statistik, Otto-Braun-Str. 74, with videos and live performances by Zhanna Gladko, Aleksander Komarov, Marina Naprushkina and Sergey Shabohin (Berlin Art Week).
- Visiting: it has no public venue.

FUNDING
Ambasada Kultury: mainly public: German Federal Foreign Office support for the PerspAKTIV residencies
- PerspAKTIV: funded by the German Federal Foreign Office, with Civil Society Cooperation also listed as a supporter. The project ran in two periods, 2022 to 2023 and 2023 to 2025, with partners Belarusian community RAZAM (Germany), the IN SITU Foundation for Contemporary Art (Poland, 2022 to 2023) and the Arsenał City Gallery in Poznań (Poland, 2023 to 2025). (the initiative's PerspAKTIV page).
- CEC ArtsLink: the initiative is a host partner for the Art Prospect residencies (CEC ArtsLink participant page).
- Antiwarcoalition.art, founded in early 2022 with Ambasada Kultury as a founding partner, names as supporters the European Cultural Foundation, the Goethe-Institut and the Swedish Institute (ZKM).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Ambasada Kultury is German Federal Foreign Office support for a residency programme, which ran to 2025. A linked coalition platform, Antiwarcoalition.art, lists European foundations and cultural institutes as supporters. Worth watching: what follows the PerspAKTIV project.
