MUZEUM SZTUKI IN ŁÓDŹ (MS1 & MS2): Łódź, Poland
ID: muzeum-sztuki-lodz
Website: https://msl.org.pl (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern and contemporary art in Łódź with two main sites: ms1 in the former palace of the industrialist Maurycy Poznański, which holds Władysław Strzemiński's Neoplastic Room, and ms2 at ul. Ogrodowa 19, a converted 19th century spinning mill of the Izrael Poznański factory (English Wikipedia; museum website). It also runs the Herbst Palace Museum (museum website).
- Its origins are in the avant-garde group "a.r.", whose members included Strzemiński and Katarzyna Kobro; their International Collection of Modern Art opened to the public on 15 February 1931 and moved to the Poznański palace in 1948 (English Wikipedia).
- ms2 opened in autumn 2008 with about 3,000 square metres for the collection and 600 for temporary shows (English Wikipedia).
- Director: Daniel Muzyczuk, a curator there since 2011 and chief curator from 2015, acting director from July 2024 and appointed by the culture ministry in 2025 on a selection committee's unanimous recommendation (Szum, 24 July 2024; ArtReview, 11 August 2025). He plans to extend the building for storage and exhibitions (ArtReview).
- Current shows: the collection display "Sposoby widzenia" (Ways of Seeing) at ms2, 17 October 2025 to 31 December 2026, and Suzanne Treister at ms1, 9 October 2026 to 14 March 2027 (museum website; translation mine).
- Visiting: Tuesday and Wednesday 9:00 to 16:00, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 18:00, Friday 12:00 to 20:00, closed Monday (museum website).

FUNDING
Muzeum Sztuki: public: a cultural institution of the Łódź Voivodeship self-government, co-run and co-financed by the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage
- The museum's footer calls it a cultural institution of the Łódź Voivodeship self-government co-financed by the Minister of Culture and National Heritage. Notes from Poland (Piotr Kosiewski, 26 September 2022) says the ministry and the marshal of Łódź province administer it jointly.
- 2023 accounts: revenue of about PLN 26.0 million, of which PLN 16.91 million was the budget subsidy, PLN 7.56 million other subsidies and PLN 0.87 million sales of goods; costs were PLN 26.85 million and the year closed with a net loss of PLN 979,972 (profit and loss account, signed by director Andrzej Biernacki on 15 April 2024).
- The ms1 palace was restored with EU money: the project cost PLN 4,875,181, of which PLN 1,998,025 came from the European Regional Development Fund under the Łódź regional operational programme, with the rest from the region (museum website).
- English Wikipedia says the regeneration of ms2 was paid for by the EU's Integrated Regional Development Operational Programme, the ministry, the Voivodeship Office and the Marshal Office in Łódź.
- The museum's "partner ms" page invites companies, institutions and individuals to pay towards exhibitions, conferences and books.

Red flags: 2022 removal of director Jarosław Suchan
- Suchan, director since 2006, was not renewed by the Law and Justice government in November 2021 and stayed as acting director; at the end of April 2022 deputy culture minister Jarosław Sellin told him he was dismissed. "I didn't get any explanation," he said (The Art Newspaper, 30 May 2022).
- Kosiewski writes that he was dismissed overnight despite a ministry contract to the end of 2022, and that the marshal's opposition had blocked a further term; culture minister Piotr Gliński told the PAP news agency that the main reason for naming Andrzej Biernacki was his views (Notes from Poland).
- Biernacki, who had run the private Browarna Gallery in Łowicz since 1991, became acting director on 25 April 2022 and won the director's competition with 9 votes and 1 abstention, for a three-year term from 15 November 2022 (PAP via dzieje.pl). He said he would shift the collection's focus from international to Polish artists, especially from Łódź (The Art Newspaper).
- Richard Armstrong of the Guggenheim called the dismissal a "shock to the museum community worldwide"; Glenn Lowry of MoMA and Maria Balshaw of Tate condemned it, and an open letter from the Polish contemporary art community attacked Biernacki's appointment (The Art Newspaper).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism concerns the 2022 change of director, not the museum's programme.
1. The Art Newspaper: Anny Shaw with Gareth Harris, "'A shock to the community worldwide': directors of Tate, Guggenheim and MoMA condemn ousting of Polish museum head" (2022)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/05/30/a-shock-to-the-museum-community-worldwide-leading-museum-directors-condemn-ousting-of-polish-museum-head
- Presents the dismissal as part of the government's push for control over museums.
- Armstrong links it to the departures of the heads of Zachęta and the National Museum in Warsaw and calls it a move to reduce museums to "ideological puppets".
2. Notes from Poland: Piotr Kosiewski, "Łódź art museum epitomises failures of Polish government's cultural policy" (2022)
URL: https://notesfrompoland.com/2022/09/26/lodz-art-museum-epitomises-failures-of-polish-governments-cultural-policy/
- Argues that the ministry's personnel policy is non-meritocratic, divides institutions into "ours" and "yours", and undermined trust in the museum.
- A news site, not art press.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Muzeum Sztuki is clean public money. The weak point is the funders' political control over who leads it.
