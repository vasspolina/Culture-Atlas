AL MA'MAL FOUNDATION FOR CONTEMPORARY ART: Jerusalem, Palestine
ID: al-mamal-foundation-jerusalem
Website: https://almamal.org (checked 2026-10-25; the site loads; its newest news post is dated 25 February 2022). The address almamalfoundation.org is a different site that has nothing to do with the foundation; it redirects to an essay-writing review website.

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art foundation in a former tile factory in the Old City of Jerusalem, near the New Gate and the Collège des Frères school.
- Founded in 1998 by Jack Persekian, an artist and curator, after his Anadiel Gallery (opened in the early 1990s on Salah al-Din Street, moved to the Old City in 1996) and its circle decided to open a contemporary art foundation (Jerusalem Story, 12 September 2024). Persekian is named as director in Gulf News (2018). The Jerusalem Story piece lists Shireen Mufdi as deputy director.
- Named al-Ma'mal, Arabic for "factory," after the tile factory.
- Building: a Palestinian hand-painted tile factory founded in 1900 by the Kassissieh family; it closed in the mid-1970s after the Jerusalem Municipality withdrew licences for Old City factories and stood abandoned until the mid-1990s (Jerusalem Story). Gulf News reports the family leased it to the group for 20 years, in exchange for paying the taxes owed and renovating it. Renovation began in 1998, and Israel's Antiquities Authority fined the group and took it to court for lacking a construction permit, which the article says Palestinians almost never obtain (Gulf News).
- What is there: exhibition and multipurpose halls, a library, a rooftop garden with a stage, a basement with glass floors showing archaeological remains, and an artist residence. Programmes: residencies, exhibitions, film training, art classes in schools, concerts, and the Jerusalem Show, which has joined the Qalandiya International biennial.
- Visiting: the site gives no opening hours or admission and states no accessibility details; the pages read cover venue hire only.

FUNDING
Al Ma'mal Foundation: grant-funded by foreign cultural foundations, governments and development agencies
- The foundation's site (almamal.org) shows 27 donor logos under "Donors" with no dates or amounts. Their labels read Norwegian Ministry, FfAI, British Council, European Union, Sida, DOEN, Qattan, Ford Foundation, IFA, Italian agency for development, Open Society, Pontifical mission, Prince Claus, République française, Swiss Rep Office, UNDP, AECID Spain, 4Cs, Arts of Carved Stones, Australian Council of Arts, Belgium, Bohen Foundation, Campus France, Consul General de France, Darat Al Funun and Dutch NL, plus one unlabelled logo. The European Cultural Foundation is not among them.
- The 2009 Jerusalem Show, organised by the foundation, lists 15 supporters, including the Ford Foundation, Prince Claus Fund, British Council, Goethe-Institut Ramallah, AECID, the Palestinian Investment Fund, Darat al Funun and the Sharjah Art Foundation (e-flux announcement, October 2009). This is a list of event supporters, not shown to fund the foundation itself.
- Renovation: a UNESCO grant made the project possible (Gulf News, 2018). The same article says the group did not receive Gulf money that a 1998 newspaper report said it had obtained.
- No reporting found of a grant from the Israeli state, of accounts, or of a published board.
- Qalandiya International, the biennial the foundation takes part in, was founded in 2012 by seven Palestinian cultural institutions (Jerusalem Story).

Not related: almamalfoundation.org. It is a different website, unrelated to Al Ma'mal; its address redirects to an essay-writing review site.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the foundation is foreign cultural, government and development funding, with no fossil fuel, tobacco, arms or Gulf state funder shown to pay for it. The weak point is transparency: the donor logos are undated, with no amounts, board or accounts published. Worth watching: whether the foundation still programmes, as its newest news posts date from 2022.
