Red flags: 2025 removal of Vasily Slonov's portrait before Museum Night
- Before the 43rd Museum Night, "Предел прочности", on 1 November 2025, the portrait of the artist Vasily Slonov was taken down from the board of honour in the museum's basement, along with steel glasses he had recently given to the collection kept there (Novaya Gazeta, Aleksey Tarasov, 20 December 2025).
- Novaya Gazeta reported that in 2024 Slonov was sentenced to a year of corrective labour for publicly displaying extremist symbols, eight-pointed stars painted on roly-poly toys, and that he is contesting the sentence. Its author called the removal "обычная самоцензура" ("ordinary self-censorship", translated), the work of neither the security services nor the local authorities (Novaya Gazeta, 20 December 2025).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 28 October 2025 Elena Penzina, a United Russia deputy of the Krasnoyarsk Krai legislative assembly, wrote of the museum's current and past exhibitions that they showed "целенаправленной и осмысленной работе руководства музея по обесцениванию российской истории и культуры" ("purposeful and deliberate work by the museum's management to devalue Russian history and culture", translated) (Novaya Gazeta, 20 December 2025).
- Penzina agreed with the Siberian Federal University lecturer Olga Finogenova that "такая деятельность называется идеологической диверсией" ("such activity is called ideological sabotage", translated) and wrote that she was preparing a request to the Ministry of Culture (Novaya Gazeta, 20 December 2025).
