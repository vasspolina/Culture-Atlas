SALZBURGER KUNSTVEREIN: Salzburg, Austria
ID: salzburger-kunstverein
Website: https://www.salzburger-kunstverein.at

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art association in the Künstlerhaus at Hellbrunner Straße 3, Salzburg.
- Founded in 1844 by Prince-Archbishop Friedrich Schwarzenberg, representatives of the art-minded middle class and artists; its archive history page calls it one of the first such institutions in Austria. Within two months 600 members had registered (the association's website).
- The Künstlerhaus was built in 1885 with an exhibition hall and studios. The association calls it the only freestanding building from the 1880s in Salzburg. German Wikipedia gives the handover to the association as 1 August 1885.
- Exhibition space of 500 square metres, 21 studios, a library and a restaurant. The association organises talks, publications, lecture performances, symposia, screenings, workshops and education projects.
- About 900 members, around 700 of them artists, plus an international Advisory Circle that has informally supported the director since 2024 (the association's website).
- Directors since 1992 per Wikipedia: Hildegund Amanshauser (1992), Silvia Eiblmayr (1993 to 1995), Amanshauser again (1996 to 2004), Hemma Schmutz (2005 to 2013), Séamus Kealy (2014 to March 2023) and Mirela Baciak from July 2023. E-flux (26 May 2023) says Baciak was appointed for five years with a possible extension.
- Residency programmes for artists, curators and writers, including a writer residency with ArtReview in 2026.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 19:00, with free admission. Step-free access is through the rear entrance on Franz-Hinterholzer-Kai; the front entrance has stairs.

FUNDING
Salzburger Kunstverein (Salzburg): membership association with public support: the federal arts ministry plus the City and the Land of Salzburg residencies
- The International Artist-in-Residence programme is offered in cooperation with the Federal Ministry for Arts and Culture (Bundesministerium für Kunst und Kultur), and the Writer-in-Residence programme is supported by the ministry (stated by the association).
- The City of Salzburg and the Land of Salzburg each offer a residency programme within the Kunstverein, part of the 30-year-old AIR Artists-in-Residence exchange (stated by the association). German Wikipedia states that since 2015 the association has run the residency programme in cooperation with the Federal Chancellery of Austria, the Land Salzburg and the City of Salzburg.
- About 900 members (stated by the association).
- The association works with companies and private individuals on projects, events and exhibitions, and rents out its Großer Saal (200 square metres), studio and Ringgalerie for private and business events.
- German Wikipedia states that the founding in 1844 was financed by the sale of shares (Aktien bzw. Anteilsscheine) to members.

BOTTOM LINE
The Kunstverein is a member association. The federal arts ministry supports its residency programmes, the City and the Land of Salzburg each offer a residency inside it, and it has about 900 members and rents out its spaces for events.
