MACAAL – Marrakech, Morocco
Website: https://macaal.org (ok, checked 2026-10-20; shows the 2026 programme)

OVERVIEW
- The Museum of African Contemporary Art Al Maaden, at Al Maaden, Sidi Youssef Ben Ali, Marrakech. It describes itself as an independent non-profit and "a project of the Fondation Alliances".
- Founded by Othman Lazraq with his father Alami Lazraq (The Art Newspaper). It opened locally in November 2016 during COP22 and internationally on 24 February 2018. The building is by Didier Lefort.
- Closed from spring 2023 after the earthquake. It reopened on 2 February 2025 with the permanent display "7 Contours, 1 Collection", a media library and a site-specific commissions programme (The Art Newspaper; Euronews).
- Artistic director: Meriem Berrada. Programmes include school tours and workshops. Ticket prices and opening hours were not found in the sources retrieved.

FUNDING
MACAAL: funded by the Lazraq family's corporate foundation
- Fondation Alliances, set up in 2009 by Othman Lazraq, is the charitable foundation of the property company Groupe Alliances (Euronews; The Art Newspaper). The 2025 overhaul had "significant support from Fondation Alliances" (Euronews).
- The money ultimately comes from Groupe Alliances (Alliances Développement Immobilier), listed on the Casablanca exchange. Alami and Othman Lazraq run it, and The Art Newspaper calls it Morocco's largest hotel builder. Forbes has named Alami Lazraq one of Africa's wealthiest men (The Art Newspaper).
- The collection of about 2,000 works was built by Alami Lazraq over 40 years.
- No reporting found of other funders, public grants or published accounts.

Red flags: Funder's debt crisis
- Alliances was suspended from the Casablanca Stock Exchange in 2016 with debt of nearly 8.5 billion dirhams. Asset transfers cut debt to 2 billion dirhams by end-2018, and private debt was restructured into 10-year bonds. The company reported recovery from 2019 and paid its first dividend in 10 years for 2023 (Challenge.ma, a Moroccan business magazine, reporting company figures). A Telquel headline of 29 Dec 2015 (seen in search results, article not opened) says Alami Lazraq lost Alliances' construction subsidiaries.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a single-family corporate foundation project with no other named funder or published accounts. No misconduct at the museum was found. Worth watching: its full dependence on Groupe Alliances, whose 2015–2018 debt crisis shows how exposed the museum is to the company's fortunes.
