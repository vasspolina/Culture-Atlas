KUNSTHAUS BREGENZ: Bregenz, Austria
ID: kunsthaus-bregenz
Website: https://www.kunsthaus-bregenz.at (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at Karl-Tizian-Platz in central Bregenz, Vorarlberg, near the vorarlberg museum and the Landestheater, not far from the lakefront (Wikipedia).
- Commissioned by the State of Vorarlberg; construction ran from 1990 to 1997, and it opened in July 1997 with a James Turrell exhibition (Wikipedia). Edelbert Köb was founding director until 2000; Eckhard Schneider 2000 to 2008; Yilmaz Dziewior 2009 to 2015; Thomas D. Trummer since 1 May 2015 (the space's history page).
- Designed by the Swiss architect Peter Zumthor, who won the Mies van der Rohe Award for European Architecture in 1998 for it (the space's history page).
- Building: a glass and steel frame with a cast concrete core; 912 etched glass panels on the facade diffuse daylight into three top-lit exhibition floors with exposed concrete and terrazzo floors (Wikipedia). No reporting found of what the site was before.
- On site: the exhibition halls, an adjoining administration building with a café, an art education programme, and a collection focused on archive, art and architecture, including Zumthor's models (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00. The homepage lists no ticket prices (they are sold online) and links an accessibility statement.

FUNDING
Kunsthaus Bregenz: mainly public with sponsors: State of Vorarlberg and corporate sponsors led by Hypo Vorarlberg
- The State of Vorarlberg commissioned the building and runs the museum through its cultural venues company KuGes, whose supervisory board extended Trummer's contract to May 2030 (Der Standard).
- The Land's 2012 audit office report found KuGes (KUB, the vorarlberg museum and the Landestheater) had income of 8.7 million euros in 2011, 7.0 million euros of it state subsidy. KUB's subsidy was 2 million euros in 2011 and was projected at 2.6 million by 2014, because a main sponsor had dropped out (vol.at, 7 December 2012). No reporting found of current subsidy figures.
- At the tenth anniversary in 2007, the Kunsthaus earned most of its programme budget itself through admissions, publications and private sponsorship (vol.at, 6 July 2007).
- Hypo Vorarlberg is the principal sponsor. Hypo Vorarlberg and Vorarlberger Kraftwerke AG are longstanding sponsors who make a special contribution to major survey exhibitions; Zumtobel has supported the museum for many years (the space's fundraising page). No amounts or start years are given.
- Also listed on the space's fundraising and partners pages: illwerke vkw AG, Mader Flatz Schett, the KUB Freunde association, Hugo Boss (linked to the Hugo Boss Prize), and UNIQA as a 2026 partner.
- illwerke vkw AG is 95.5 percent owned by the Land Vorarlberg (NEOS motion to the Vorarlberg Landtag, 20 June 2022, a party document). The company's own April 2026 tariff sheet shows it also sells natural gas to households in its network area. No reporting found of how much of its income comes from gas.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. No piece criticising the museum, its sponsors or its funding was found.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is mostly the state's, with a long list of named but unquantified Vorarlberg sponsors. The weak points are the lack of published sponsor amounts or current subsidy figures, and illwerke vkw, a state-owned utility that sells both hydro power and natural gas. Watch for a published annual report.
