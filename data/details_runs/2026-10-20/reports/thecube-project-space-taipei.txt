THECUBE PROJECT SPACE: Taipei, Taiwan
ID: thecube-project-space-taipei
Website: https://thecubespace.com

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit art space in the Gongguan and Shida area of Taipei, on the 2nd floor of No. 13, Lane 1, Lane 136, Section 4, Roosevelt Road, behind the east side of Shui Yuan Market and a short walk from Gongguan MRT Station Exit 1 (the space's about page).
- Founded in April 2010; it is registered as a non-profit organisation under Taipei's performing arts group regulations. The Chinese name is 立方計劃空間 (the space's about page).
- Mission: to promote contemporary art and in-depth humanities thinking, connect international and local networks and engage with art history, through exhibitions, talks, exchange and archival resources (the space's about page).
- Its exhibition Altering Nativism: Sound Cultures in Post-War Taiwan, organised with the Museum of National Taipei University of Education and the Kaohsiung Museum of Fine Arts, was published as a Chinese and English book in June 2014 (the space's publication page).
- Programmes: exhibitions, talks, publications and satellite projects including Sound Meridians, Talking Drums Radio, Sonic Shaman, Sound Traces and the CIT International Curators' Forum.
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 2pm to 8pm.

FUNDING
TheCube Project Space: public and private grants plus crowdfunding: National Culture and Arts Foundation, Taipei City Department of Cultural Affairs, a foundation and an individual donor
- The about page names as supporters: the National Culture and Arts Foundation (NCAF), the Taipei City Department of Cultural Affairs, the Lu Fu Life Aesthetics Foundation (陸府生活美學基金會) and Mr. Chen Bo-wen (陳泊文).
- NCAF funded Altering Nativism through its 2012 Production Grants to Independent Curators in Visual Arts (the space's publication page).
- The space runs fundraising campaigns: the 2020 tenth-anniversary fundraiser, the 2024 "立方15群募" campaign and a 2026 crowdfunding campaign for the Sonic Shaman project (the space's website).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind TheCube is public and philanthropic: a national arts foundation, the Taipei city culture department, the Lu Fu Life Aesthetics Foundation, an individual donor and fundraising campaigns.
