ÁJTTE, SWEDISH MOUNTAIN AND SÁMI MUSEUM: Jokkmokk, Sweden
ID: ajtte-jokkmokk
Website: https://www.ajtte.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Sweden's main museum of Sámi culture and a specialist museum of the nature and culture of the mountains, at Kyrkogatan 3 in Jokkmokk (ajtte.com).
- A foundation formed in 1983 runs it; a smaller museum that had existed in Jokkmokk since 1965 was merged into the new building, and the museum opened in June 1989 with eight times the floor space (ajtte.com).
- Ájtte means storage hut in Sámi, and the museum describes itself as a store that keeps things for the future (ajtte.com).
- Its area of work covers the traditional Sámi settlement area, from the mountains in the west through the forest land, a thousand kilometres from north to south (ajtte.com).
- It runs the Jokkmokk alpine garden, a library and research projects, and its base exhibition is "Här i Sápmi" ("Here in Sápmi") (ajtte.com; title translated by the atlas).
- It displays a sejte, a Sámi sacrificial stone that Eric von Rosen removed from Sieberbuollda in the early 1900s and that was long held by the Ethnographic Museum; visitors leave coins at it (SVT, 2022).
- Apart from the museum chief it had 24 employees in 2020 (Region Norrbotten audit, 2020).
- In November 2026 the foundation was recruiting a new museum chief, a post that requires Sámi competence and knowledge of Indigenous issues (ajtte.com).
- Visiting: children aged 0 to 16 enter free with a guardian; guided tours cost SEK 150 per person and must be booked. All exhibitions are reachable by wheelchair, the lecture hall has a hearing loop, and texts come in five languages (ajtte.com).

FUNDING
Ájtte: mainly public: Swedish state, Norrbotten Region and Jokkmokk Municipality
- The founders of the foundation are the Swedish state, Norrbotten County Council (now Norrbotten Region), Jokkmokk Municipality and the two Sámi organisations Svenska Samernas Riksförbund and Same Ätnam (ajtte.com).
- The 2026 state budget gives the museum SEK 1.5 million under the appropriation "Contributions to certain museums", with the same sum proposed for 2027 (Budget bill 2026/27:1, expenditure area 17).
- Wikipedia states that the founders agreed in 1983 on a long-term state contribution that comes from a court decision on compensation for hydropower development of Lapland's rivers, that the government appoints the chair and three board members, and that state funds cover about half the budget.
- The Sámi Parliament's culture board granted Ájtte SEK 200,000 for 2026 for work on Sámi languages, stories and the history of Sápmi (SVT, 2026).
- The Friends of Ájtte association has paid for many purchases for the collections; members get free entry to the museum and the alpine garden (ajtte.com).
- An auditor appointed by Norrbotten Region reviews the foundation's accounts and management (Region Norrbotten audit, 2020).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Ájtte is Swedish public money from the state, the region and the municipality, with small Sámi Parliament grants and members' support. The size of each founder's share is worth watching.
