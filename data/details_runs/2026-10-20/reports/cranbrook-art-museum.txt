CRANBROOK ART MUSEUM: Bloomfield Hills, USA
ID: cranbrook-art-museum
Website: https://cranbrookartmuseum.org (checked 2026-10-24; its support and exhibitions-list pages returned 404, the exhibition pages loaded)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum of the Cranbrook Educational Community, a National Historic Landmark District in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan, at 39221 Woodward Avenue (Wikipedia; Cranbrook).
- The Cranbrook estate began as a 174-acre farm bought in 1904; Cranbrook Academy of Art was founded in 1932 and the museum building was completed in 1942 under the architect Eliel Saarinen. It closed for renovation in 2009 and reopened in November 2011 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday 11am to 5pm, Thursday until 8pm, closed Monday and Tuesday. Admission $12, seniors and Detroit residents $10, students $8, children 12 and under, ArtMembers and EBT or WIC cardholders free, and Thursdays free. Barrier-free access at the front turnaround and the New Studios Building; free parking.

FUNDING
Cranbrook Art Museum: private: foundations, a corporate sponsor and members, inside the Cranbrook Educational Community
- No separate accounts for the museum were found. Its parent, Cranbrook Educational Community, reported revenue of $99,927,365, contributions of $15,736,629, expenses of $102,459,564 and net assets of $421,024,553 for the year to June 2025 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Exhibition credits name the Gilbert Family Foundation (Drawing in Thread, Monumental Color, Akea Brionne: A Dreaming Hour, Labyrinth/Laboratory), the DeRoy Testamentary Foundation (three shows), the George Francoeur Art Museum Exhibition Fund (Drawing in Thread), the Clannad Foundation and David Klein Gallery (Labyrinth/Laboratory), and MillerKnoll with Appalachian Stitching Inc., the Harry Bertoia Foundation and an anonymous donor (Harry Bertoia: Void and Matter). No amounts or years are given (stated by the museum).
- On 6 April 2021 Jennifer and Dan Gilbert gave $30 million personally to Cranbrook Academy of Art, for fellowships, visiting artists, tuition relief and other costs. The release says the Art Museum will also receive funding for public engagement projects by diverse artists in the Detroit area and on campus; neither the release nor The Art Newspaper gives an amount for the museum. The release names the donors as Jennifer and Dan Gilbert, not the foundation. Jennifer Gilbert is a vice chair of the Cranbrook trustees (ProPublica).
- Dan Gilbert's fortune comes mainly from Quicken Loans, now Rocket Mortgage, a mortgage lender he co-founded in 1985 as Rock Financial (Wikipedia).
- Casinos: a joint venture with Caesars Entertainment operated casinos in Cleveland and Cincinnati, the first opening in May 2012. In 2013 Rock Ventures acquired a majority of Greektown Superholdings, owner of the Greektown Casino-Hotel in Detroit. Gilbert sold Greektown to Vici Properties in 2019 and all interests in the Ohio casinos in 2021 (Wikipedia). No reporting found of current gaming money behind the gifts or of an objection to them.
- The Gilbert Family Foundation is credited on four of the museum's exhibition pages; the pages read give no years or amounts and do not say where the foundation's money comes from.
- No reporting found of a donor list or board for the museum itself.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the museum found; coverage of its shows is admiring. One piece criticises the Academy, the museum's parent school, and does not name the museum.
1. Hyperallergic: Maya Pontone, "Students Protest After Michigan Art School Takes Down Palestinian Flags" (2023)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/856847/students-protest-after-cranbrook-academy-art-school-takes-down-palestinian-flags/
- On 4 November 2023 administrators of Cranbrook Academy of Art removed Palestinian flags from student studios and other campus spaces; students and alumni sent an open letter to school leadership, and some students boycotted the prospective-student open house on 11 November.
- The letter argues that the school's email calling the imagery "deeply triggering" suggests the flag's existence is triggering, and that Cranbrook "is making a choice to erase the Palestinian identity from public life". President Aimeclaire Roche said in a 10 November statement that the letter "in no way reflects the views and values of Cranbrook"; chief communications officer Vijay Iyer said common areas are kept free of messaging that could be read as political statements by Cranbrook.
- About the Academy only: the article does not name the Cranbrook Art Museum. The Academy is a separate school in the same Educational Community.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is foundation, corporate and member giving inside a large private school complex, with the Gilbert Family Foundation prominent; no amounts are published. The weak points are the lack of any donor list or accounts for the museum alone, the undated and unquantified Gilbert Family Foundation credits, and the 2023 flag removals at the Academy, which the press piece does not link to the museum. The Gilberts' fortune is mortgage lending; their casinos were sold in 2019 and 2021. Watch the Gilbert and DeRoy credits.
