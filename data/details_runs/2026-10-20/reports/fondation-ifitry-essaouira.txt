FONDATION IFITRY: Essaouira, Morocco
ID: fondation-ifitry-essaouira
Website: https://biennalecasablanca.org/

OVERVIEW
- Artists' residency on the coastal road between Essaouira and Safi, about 50 km from Essaouira and 70 km from Safi, directly opposite the Contemporary Art Centre (Casablanca Biennale website).
- Created in 2008 by Mostapha Romli and made a foundation in 2014 (The Art Newspaper France, Olivier Rachet, 3 August 2022); the Casablanca Biennale website describes Romli as a Moroccan artist, photographer and publisher who initiated the Biennale in 2012.
- Facilities: twelve ocean-facing pavilions of 20 to 120 m2 with one to three bedrooms, and workshops for engraving, screen printing, painting, ceramics, photography and video (Casablanca Biennale website).
- The pavilions are described as an extension of the Contemporary Art Centre of Essaouira (Casablanca Biennale website), whose collection holds about 2,000 works by Moroccan and other artists (The Art Newspaper France, 2022).
- Artists reach the residency by applying to a selection committee or by invitation for exhibition projects, according to Romli (The Art Newspaper France, 2022).
- By 2022 it had hosted more than 570 artists from 73 countries; by January 2014 the count was 374 artists from 45 countries (The Art Newspaper France, 2022; Le360, Bouthaina Azami, 25 January 2014).
- Since 2014 the Casablanca Biennale has operated under the aegis of the Maroc Premium Foundation, which also oversees a contemporary art center established in 2011, a collection of contemporary works and the BIC Project Space in Casablanca, opened in 2019 (Casablanca Biennale website).
- The 6th Casablanca Biennale, conceived by the curator Marisa Caichiolo, runs from 10 December 2026 to 10 January 2027 in Essaouira and cites the Ifitry residency (Casablanca Biennale website).

FUNDING
Fondation Ifitry: founded by the artist Mostapha Romli; artists donate works made during their stay to support it
- Some artists donated works made during their stay at the residency to support the foundation (Le360, Bouthaina Azami, "Naissance d'une fondation, une exposition exceptionnelle", 25 January 2014).
- The same Le360 article describes a collective show of works made at Ifitry at the Sofitel Casablanca Tour Blanche, part of an exhibition cycle started by Maroc Premium and Sofitel Luxury Hotels (Le360, 2014).

BOTTOM LINE
Fondation Ifitry is the artists' residency created by Mostapha Romli in 2008. Its documented support is works donated by artists in 2014 and a collective show at a Casablanca hotel within an exhibition cycle started by Maroc Premium and Sofitel Luxury Hotels. The Casablanca Biennale that Romli initiated operates under the Maroc Premium Foundation.
