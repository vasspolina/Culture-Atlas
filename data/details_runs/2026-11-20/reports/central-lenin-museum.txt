CENTRAL LENIN MUSEUM: Moscow, Russia
ID: central-lenin-museum
Website: https://lenin.shm.ru (checked 2026-11-20; a virtual museum of the collection run with the State Historical Museum)

OVERVIEW
- Former central museum of Vladimir Lenin in the Red Square building that once housed the Moscow City Duma (The Moscow Times).
- On 31 May 1924 the Moscow Committee of the Communist Party decided to set up a Lenin Institute with a museum department; the Central Lenin Museum was created in September 1935 and opened on 15 May 1936 (lenin.shm.ru).
- It was rebuilt on a large scale for Lenin's centenary in 1970 and received the Order of Lenin in 1974 (lenin.shm.ru).
- It was disbanded in 1993 by order of the Russian president (lenin.shm.ru).
- The collection holds over 100,000 items, long closed to the public (lenin.shm.ru).
- In 2014 the building held "The Myth of the Beloved Leader", about 1,000 items from the archive, which presented Lenin without ideology and looked at how the myth was made (The Moscow Times).
- The building now houses the State Historical Museum's Museum of the Patriotic War of 1812 at Ploshchad Revolyutsii 2/3, reopened on 4 September 2012; adult tickets cost 2,000 roubles (The Moscow Times; shm.ru).

FUNDING
Central Lenin Museum: closed; its collection is held by the State Historical Museum, a Russian state museum
- The Communist Party's activity was suspended in 1991, which, the museum's site says, decided the museum's fate in post-Soviet Russia (lenin.shm.ru).
- The State Historical Museum holds the copyright to the virtual Lenin museum and runs its 1812 museum in the building (lenin.shm.ru; shm.ru).

Red flags: Communist Party museum of the Lenin cult (1936 to 1991)
- The museum was created on a Communist Party decision and opened in 1936 as the main Lenin museum of the USSR (lenin.shm.ru).
- Its first exhibition was shaped under Stalin to show how close he was to Lenin; after Khrushchev's secret speech in the 1950s the museum was "ideologically cleansed" of references to Stalin (The Moscow Times).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Coverage is of the 2014 archive exhibition, which looked at the Soviet cult critically.
1. The Moscow Times: Kevin O'Flynn, "Red Square Exhibit Sheds New Light on Soviet Cult of Lenin" (2014)
URL: https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2014/10/26/red-square-exhibit-sheds-new-light-on-soviet-cult-of-lenin-40765-a40765
- The new Russian state closed the museum soon after its brief perestroika-era displays.
- Quotes staff member Olga Grankina, there since 1976: "It is normal to preserve things, to keep them and think about them."

WIDER CONTEXT
- Russia's culture minister Olga Lyubimova is under Western sanctions: the EU listed her on 16 December 2022, citing ministry money for the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics; the US Treasury listed her on 24 February 2023; the UK listed her on 17 July 2023 with an asset freeze (The Art Newspaper; Interfax).
- Her ministry extended the contract of Alexei Levykin, director of the State Historical Museum, which holds the Lenin collection, for five years in February 2023 (BFM). The sanctions name the minister, not the museum.

BOTTOM LINE
A party museum that no longer exists; what remains is a collection in Russian state hands. Worth watching: how the State Historical Museum shows the Lenin collection.
