RIDDU RIĐĐU: Manndalen, Norway
ID: riddu-riddu-festival-manndalen
Website: https://riddu.no (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- An annual international Indigenous music and culture festival in Olmmáivággi (Manndalen) in Gáivuotna (Kåfjord) Municipality, Nord-Troms (Riddu Riđđu).
- Young people in Kåfjord started it in 1991 to turn shame about their Sea Sámi background into pride through music and culture; in the early years Sámi road signs were shot at and families split over it (Riddu Riđđu, translated by the atlas).
- Since 1 January 2010 it has been run by the company Riddu Riđđu Festivála AS (Brønnøysund Register), which has five permanent staff, a year-round crew of 100 and about 170 volunteers at festival time; its address is the Centre for Northern Peoples in Manndalen (Riddu Riđđu, translated by the atlas).
- Beside concerts it runs a children's festival, Mánáidfestivála, and each year it honours a "Northern People of the Year" (Riddu Riđđu, Kritiikin Uutiset).
- The 2025 festival drew 6,149 visitors and included a debate between the two leading candidates in that year's Sámi Parliament election and the commissioned work "The Arctic is not for sale" (Riddu Riđđu). In March 2026 the festival named Erle Bårdsdatter Sæther festival director for four years from 1 September 2026, after Sajje Solbakk's fourth and last festival (Riddu Riđđu).
- The next festival runs from 7 to 11 July 2027 (Riddu Riđđu).

FUNDING
Riddu Riđđu: mainly public: the Sámi Parliament, Arts Council Norway, the Ministry of Culture, Troms County and Kåfjord Municipality
- The association Riddu Riđđu Searvi, whose members are mostly festival crew, owns the company outright; the company does not aim to make a profit, and its board includes representatives of Troms County, the Sámi Parliament and Kåfjord Municipality (Riddu Riđđu, translated by the atlas).
- The festival's home page shows as partners, without amounts: Kåfjord Municipality, Troms County, the Sámi Parliament, Arts Council Norway, Talent Norge, the Ministry of Culture, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Lásságámmi, Ramsalt and Coop Nord (Riddu Riđđu, checked 2026-11-20).
- The Sámi Parliament's direct festival grant was NOK 1,667,000 for 2025, raised by a permanent NOK 56,000 to NOK 1,723,000 in its revised budget of March 2025 (Sámi Parliament).
- The Ministry of Foreign Affairs granted NOK 1M a year for three years for an international delegate programme, announced in December 2020 (Riddu Riđđu).
- Nodal festival status in 2009 gave it a fixed line in the state budget, as one of 12 festivals in Norway (Riddu Riđđu, translated by the atlas).
- Accounts for 2025: operating income NOK 15.93M, of which NOK 3.36M sales, and a profit of NOK 527,261 (Regnskapsregisteret).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: public Norwegian and Sámi money, with no flagged funder.
