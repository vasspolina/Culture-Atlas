ID: groeningemuseum

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 2 September 2026 Kristl Strubbe resigned as director of Musea Brugge, barely a year after her appointment; the city's statement gave a lack of mutual trust and cohesion in the management team as her reason (VRT NWS, Stefaan Struyve, 3 September 2026).
- Unverified: the union ACV, through Johan Beernaert, said Strubbe consulted staff too little, cited at least 20 reports to the prevention adviser and called it "een vorm van pestgedrag" (translated: a form of bullying), from VRT NWS.
- Strubbe denied every accusation of bullying and said no complaint had been filed with the city or the confidential counsellor; culture alderman Nico Blontrock (CD&V) confirmed there were no complaints against her (VRT NWS, 3 September 2026).
- Connection to this museum: the Groeningemuseum is one of the city museums run by Musea Brugge; the VRT report does not name the Groeningemuseum.
