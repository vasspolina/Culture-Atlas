AMON CARTER MUSEUM OF AMERICAN ART – Fort Worth, USA
Website: https://www.cartermuseum.org (loads normally, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Founded in 1961 by the bequest of Amon G. Carter Sr. (1879–1955) to house his Remington and Russell paintings and sculpture. It stands in Fort Worth's Cultural District at 3501 Camp Bowie Blvd.
- Admission is free for general admission, special exhibitions and public events (FY2025 annual report).
- Andrew J. Walker stepped down as executive director in August 2025 after 14 years. Robert Stein, chief information officer of the National Gallery of Art, was named his successor on 9 Sept 2026 and starts on 9 November (Artnet).
- The board president is Karen J. Hixon.

FUNDING
Amon Carter Museum: mainly private, through an endowment, foundations and individuals, plus small public grants.
- FY2025 (year to 30 Sept 2025): contributed revenue was $12.38M (FY2024: $16.73M). Investment return was $5.81M and earned income $0.44M. Expenses were $16.83M. Endowments stood at $60.8M and net assets at $93.6M (annual report).
- 1,387 donors gave $4.83M: individuals $2.43M, foundations $1.53M, members $0.44M, government and other $0.24M, corporations $0.19M.
- $100,000+ donors: Amon G. Carter Foundation, Art Bridges, Arts Fort Worth, Charles C. Butt, Mary H. Cain Foundation, Fort Worth Tourism PID, J. Tomilson Hill III, Mr and Mrs Larry V. Kalas, Mary Potishman Lard Trust, Louella Baker Martin, Dr and Mrs John L. Nugent, Richard A. Russack, Mr and Mrs Rex A. Sinquefield, Texas Commission on the Arts, and David Gibson and Chikako Terada (gift in kind).
- Walton Family Foundation: $20M endowment over five years (Jan 2017) for exhibitions and education. It is the museum's largest gift.
- Public grants: City of Fort Worth, Arts Fort Worth, Fort Worth Tourism PID and Texas Commission on the Arts.
- Corporate leaders: BNSF Railway, CBRE, Fidelity Investments, Frost, Luther King Capital Management, Kelly Hart & Hallman, Eagle Energy Systems Ltd and others (support page). No reporting found on what Eagle Energy Systems does.

Red flags: Cowboy exhibition closed and relabelled
- Cowboy opened on 28 Sept 2024. By 11 October it had closed for about two weeks, then reopened with a "mature content" sign at the entrance. The museum's communications director said it needed time to decide how to talk to visitors. It was not clear which works had drawn complaints (Dallas Voice).
- Artist rafa esparza said: "I see this as a form of censorship" (Dallas Voice, an LGBTQ outlet).

BOTTOM LINE
A free museum funded mostly by its endowment and by local foundations and individuals. The founder's foundation is still a top donor. No reporting found of controversial funders. The one issue on record is the 2024 Cowboy closure, which an artist called censorship.
