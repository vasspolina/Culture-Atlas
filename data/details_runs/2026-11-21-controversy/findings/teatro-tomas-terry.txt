Red flags: 2024 cancellation of Ulises Toirac's show
- Unverified: that Cuban authorities cancelled his show set for Saturday at 5:00 pm in the Ateneo Hall, from the comedian Ulises Toirac on Facebook, as reported by CubaHeadlines (Olivia Torres, 20 June 2024). He asked why no one took responsibility for the cancellation or explained it.
- Unverified: that the people responsible work at the theatre, from Toirac, who answered a Cienfuegos resident: "The culprits (those closest to you) are in the Ateneo Hall of the Terry Theater. You can go and ask them." (CubaHeadlines)
- Unverified: that he was denied work for his views, from Toirac: "Beyond denying someone work because of their ideas or what they say, the way the power makes it clear that speaking freely is impossible is brutally cowardly." (CubaHeadlines)
