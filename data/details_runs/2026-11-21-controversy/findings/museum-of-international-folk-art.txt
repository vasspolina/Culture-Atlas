WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2001 the museum showed "Our Lady", a digital photo collage by the Los Angeles artist Alma Lopez of the Virgin of Guadalupe in a flower-covered two-piece outfit with a bare-breasted female angel, in the exhibition "Cyber Arte: Where Tradition Meets Technology" (CBS News and AP, 4 April 2001; The Advocate, 28 April 2001).
- Archbishop Michael Sheehan of Santa Fe called the image insulting, said it should be removed and said it treats the Virgin "as if she were a tart"; Catholic protesters held prayer vigils against the work (CBS News and AP, 4 April 2001; The Advocate, 28 April 2001).
- On 4 April 2001 the museum's regents began considering whether to remove the work and postponed the meeting because about 300 people attended and roughly 250 more could not get in (CBS News and AP, 4 April 2001).
- Lopez said the image shows the Virgin as "a strong woman, like us" and that "It has nothing to do with sex or sexuality" (CBS News and AP, 4 April 2001; The Advocate, 28 April 2001).
