ID: shakespeare-theatre-company

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2012 the Shakespeare Theatre Company went to court for an injunction to keep occupying the Lansburgh Theatre, after its landlord, Lansburgh Theatre, Inc., a non-profit created to serve as the theatre's landlord, demanded a 700 percent increase in base rent and moved to evict the company (BroadwayWorld, 15 June 2012).
- Managing Director Chris Jennings said: "Taking this dispute to court is a last resort" (BroadwayWorld, 15 June 2012).
