MUSEO DE ARTE CONTEMPORÁNEO DE PUERTO RICO: San Juan, Puerto Rico
ID: museo-de-arte-contemporáneo-de-puerto-rico-san-juan
Website: https://www.mac-pr.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum in Santurce, San Juan, on Avenida Juan Ponce de León at Avenida Roberto H. Todd (museum website; Wikipedia).
- Founded by artists and civil society sponsors and officially instituted as a non-profit on 8 October 1984. Before it had a home it offered services in public spaces such as Plaza Las Américas and in contributors' offices; in 1988 the University of the Sacred Heart gave it a provisional home free of charge (Wikipedia).
- On 20 October 2002 it signed a contract with Governor Sila Calderón granting it rights to the Rafael M. Labra building, built in 1916 as a public school and designed by Adrian C. Finlayson. The building is listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places (1987) and was restored by Otto Reyes Casanova from 1995 to 2002 (Wikipedia).
- Collects, promotes and studies art made since the mid-20th century in Puerto Rico, the Caribbean, Latin America and their diasporas (Wallace Foundation brief, May 2026).
- Programmes include MAC en el barrio, which has run more than 80 projects in 20 locations over ten years (Wallace Foundation brief).
- Visiting: open Friday to Sunday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. (museum website).

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Contemporáneo de Puerto Rico: mixed: a non-profit funded by US foundation grants and donors
- Legal form: non-profit, EIN 66-0420190 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Revenue for the year ending June 2025 was 4,947,523 dollars, of which 3,647,051 dollars were contributions; expenses were 3,820,432 dollars. Revenue was 4,010,439 dollars in 2024 and 2,893,243 dollars in 2023 (ProPublica, Form 990 data).
- Mellon Foundation: 1,000,000 dollars awarded on 23 June 2023 to support general operations over 36 months (Mellon Foundation).
- Teiger Foundation: 150,000 dollars for three years of programming by curators Marina Reyes Franco, Abdiel D. Segarra Ríos and Alexandra T. Méndez García, with exhibitions from September 2025 to 2028 (Teiger Foundation, 27 August 2025).
- Frankenthaler Climate Initiative: a technical assistance grant to evaluate and design a sustainable air-conditioning and ventilation system with solar backup for the Labra building (Frankenthaler Climate Initiative).
- Sponsorship levels: Amigxs 1,000 dollars, Benefactor 2,500, Mecena 10,000 and Legado 25,000, billed annually (museum sponsorship page).
- Leadership in the Form 990 for 2025: Marianne Ramirez Aponte, Executive Director; Rafael Flores Perez, President; board members include Salvador Alemany, Pedro Munoz Marin, Maria E. Torres, Ana L. Rivero Iturregui and Rachid Molinary (ProPublica).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is donations and grants, led by a 1 million dollar Mellon Foundation grant (2023) and a 150,000 dollar Teiger Foundation programming grant (2025), with contributions of 3,647,051 dollars in the year ending June 2025.
