WHW (WHAT, HOW & FOR WHOM) / GALERIJA NOVA: Zagreb, Croatia
ID: whw-galerija-nova-zagreb
Website: https://www.whw.hr (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- WHW (Što, kako i za koga) is a curatorial collective founded in Zagreb in 1999; its members are the curators Ivet Ćurlin, Ana Dević, Nataša Ilić and Sabina Sabolović and the designer and publicist Dejan Kršić (WHW).
- The Zagreb team also includes the curator Ana Kovačić and Gordana Borić, who manages finances and EU projects; the office is at Bogovićeva 1 (WHW).
- From 2003 to 2023 WHW ran Galerija Nova, a non-profit gallery in central Zagreb owned by the City of Zagreb (WHW).
- Galerija Nova was founded in 1975 by the artists Ljerka Šibenik and Mladen Galić as part of the Centre for Cultural Activities of the Socialist Youth of Zagreb; it moved from Teslina 1 to Mihanovićeva 28 in 1979 and to Teslina 7 in 1992 (WHW).
- In May 2023 WHW announced that the Teslina 7 premises had been returned to a private owner under a restitution decision and that the owner had asked the gallery to leave; later 2023 shows moved to HDLU, the Ribnjak Youth Centre and POGON (WHW).
- In 2018 WHW launched WHW Akademija, a free, independent study programme for young artists (WHW).
- Ćurlin, Ilić and Sabolović were artistic directors of Kunsthalle Wien from June 2019 to June 2024, and in August 2024 were named artistic directors of Skulptur Projekte Münster 2027 (WHW).

FUNDING
WHW / Galerija Nova: public and foundation grants: Croatian state and Zagreb city money, EU programmes and private arts foundations
- WHW's support page lists the same funders for 2025 and 2026: the Kultura Nova Foundation, the Ministry of Culture and Media, the City of Zagreb's Office for Culture, the Government Office for Associations, the EU's Creative Europe and Erasmus+ programmes, ERSTE Foundation / Kontakt Collection, the Foundation for Arts Initiatives and the Trust for Mutual Understanding. The 2022 to 2024 lists add the Croatian Audiovisual Centre, and 2022 the National Foundation for Civil Society Development (WHW).
- The City of Zagreb's 2025 culture budget gave the association 85,500 euros across eight programmes in three lists, among them 25,000 euros for the exhibition "Nadamo se (između redaka)", 18,000 euros for WHW Akademija and 10,000 euros for the Akademija exhibition (City of Zagreb, approved programmes 2025).
- The Ministry of Culture and Media gave 10,000 euros for the 2025 exhibition programme (ministry list of approved visual arts programmes, 2025).
- Kultura Nova gave 25,000 euros in 2024 for the programme "Sjeti se slobode" (Remember Freedom), the first organisational development category (up to 25,000 euros), and lists it under multi-year institutional support in 2025 (Kultura Nova).
- After the 2023 move the City Office for Culture continued to support WHW's programmes (WHW, May 2023).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In December 2022 the City of Vienna's panel found all twenty applicants for the Kunsthalle Wien directorship wanting, including WHW, and readvertised the post (Artforum, 19 December 2022, citing Der Standard).
- Kunsthalle Wien board member Boris Marte, board chair of the Erste Foundation, resigned in protest, saying the board had not been consulted (Artforum).
- On 10 January 2023 the museum confederation L'Internationale published a letter calling the city's explanation inadequate and asking councillor Veronica Kaup-Hasler to reconsider (L'Internationale).
- This concerns the Vienna institution three WHW members directed, not WHW's Zagreb funding; ERSTE Foundation, whose chair resigned from the Kunsthalle board, is also on WHW's own funder list.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public and foundation money; nothing in its funding raises a flag. A permanent home for Galerija Nova is the open question.
