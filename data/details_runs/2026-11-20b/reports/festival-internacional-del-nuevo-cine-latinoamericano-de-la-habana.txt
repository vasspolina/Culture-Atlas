FESTIVAL INTERNACIONAL DEL NUEVO CINE LATINOAMERICANO DE LA HABANA: Havana, Cuba
ID: festival-internacional-del-nuevo-cine-latinoamericano-de-la-habana
Website: https://habanafilmfestival.com (checked 2026-11-20; did not load)

OVERVIEW
- Annual December film festival in Havana; the first edition opened on 3 December 1979, when ICAIC invited more than 600 Latin American filmmakers, and its top prize is the Gran Premio Coral (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Alfredo Guevara founded it and was its president when he died on 19 April 2013 (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Tania Delgado heads it: Prensa Latina calls her the festival's president (December 2025), OnCuba its director (December 2024).
- The 46th edition ran from 4 to 14 December 2025, dedicated to Guevara's centenary with Mexico as guest of honour. Prensa Latina, a state agency, counted more than 2,000 submissions, 114 selected productions and 283 screenings.
- In 2025 it added a film market, Isla Abierta, run with ICAIC from 7 to 11 December, and showed seven films from Mexico's Estudios Churubusco Azteca (Prensa Latina English).

FUNDING
Havana film festival: state-funded through ICAIC and the Ministry of Culture
- ICAIC convened the first edition and organises the festival (Spanish Wikipedia); EcuRed, the state encyclopedia, places ICAIC in the Ministry of Culture's system (archive copy of 18 January 2025).
- 2025: a film-making fellowship for women over 50 was launched with the UN Population Fund (Prensa Latina).

Red flags: 2024 removal of Orlando Mora's short Matar a un hombre
- The short was programmed in the fiction shorts competition and was not shown. Mora said two screenings failed in blackouts and it was then dropped despite promises; Delgado told him the films would be rescheduled (OnCuba, 17 December 2024).
- Mora wrote that he sensed it was censored for essentially homophobic reasons. The film school EICTV and the Asamblea de Cineastas Cubanos objected (OnCuba).

Red flags: 2023 exclusion of La Habana de Fito and Llamadas desde Moscú
- The Asamblea de Cineastas Cubanos, led in signature by Fernando Pérez, said the two documentaries were dropped from the 44th edition without explanation and that the selection committee had been pressured to exclude them (EFE in El Universal, December 2023; OnCuba, 1 December 2023).
- It said the forces that control ICAIC interfere with the festival's independence. The dispute began over La Habana de Fito, shown on state television without its director's permission; an open letter to the Ministry of Culture had over 600 signatures (EFE in El Universal).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism concerns films dropped from the programme and state control over selection, not the films shown.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2016: ICAIC dropped Carlos Lechuga's Santa y Andrés after it was preselected for the 38th edition (OnCuba, 8 December 2016).
- 2014: the 36th edition dropped Laurent Cantet's Regreso a Ítaca although it was printed in the catalogue. Twelve filmmakers said the highest authorities of the Ministry of Culture and ICAIC forced the festival's directors to withdraw it, in a statement later joined by National Film Prize winners (OnCuba, 13 April 2015).

BOTTOM LINE
The festival's money is state money with no funder flag. Its weak point is state control over what it shows, which filmmakers keep contesting in public. Worth watching: the 2026 selection and who backs the new film market.
