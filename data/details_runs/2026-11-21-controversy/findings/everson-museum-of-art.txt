ID: everson-museum-of-art

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2020 the museum sold Jackson Pollock's "Red Composition" (1946) at a Christie's auction on 6 October, to buy "more work from artists of color, women and other marginalized groups" (WRVO, via WSKG, 24 September 2020; The Art Newspaper, 15 October 2020). The Art Newspaper gives the price as $12m; Hyperallergic gives $13 million.
- A Los Angeles Times art critic called the sale "inexcusable" and said "the Everson is betraying its legacy" (WRVO, via WSKG). Director and CEO Elizabeth Dunbar said "The Everson is not neutral" and "We want to stand on the right side of history and we want to make positive change for our community." (WRVO, via WSKG)
- A petitioner filed claims with the Commissioner of the New York State Department of Education challenging the sale, then abandoned them (Hyperallergic, Cassie Packard, 23 October 2020; registration wall; only the summary line was read).
- Jessica Arb Danial, president of the board of trustees, answered critics in a piece she wrote for The Art Newspaper (15 October 2020): "fine arts experts and critics may try to shame the Everson and other like-minded museums for the decision to deaccession", and said the proceeds "can only be used to acquire more art and care for our existing collection."
