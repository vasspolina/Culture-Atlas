BISHKEK SCHOOL OF CONTEMPORARY ART (BISCA): Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
ID: bishkek-school-of-contemporary-art-bisca
Website: https://bishci.com/en

OVERVIEW
- Self-organised art platform in Bishkek that calls itself "a socially engaged art platform" and "an informal, self-organizing association" (BiSCA website). It operates mainly online and says it has no premises or infrastructure for physical events.
- Established in autumn 2020. The website names seven members: Bermet Borubaeva, Oksana Kapishnikova, Diana Ukhina, Kanaiym Kydyralieva, Ravshan Ta Jing, Alima Tokmergenova and Syinat Zholdosheva; nine people are actively involved. The Prince Claus Fund calls Borubaeva, and NYU calls Tokmergenova, a co-founder of BiSCA; the Bulbul residency site lists Zholdosheva as chief curator and a co-founder of the residency.
- Work since 2020: exhibitions, research projects, festivals, residencies, lectures and artist talks, online and offline; twelve numbered issues of the zine Aralash; participation in an Asia Art Archive exhibition in Hong Kong.
- TRASH festival: Borubaeva co-founded the TRASH Environmental Art Festival in 2009 and co-curates it (Wikipedia). The fifth edition, "Interpreting the History of Pollution: Art Prospect and TRASH-5", ran from 20 September to 22 October 2023 in Bishkek, produced and curated by CEC ArtsLink, BiSCA and Tazar Kyrgyzstan; the Trash Museum opened on 7 October 2023 as part of it (CEC ArtsLink).
- Bulbul Art-Residence: a family-run residency in the village of Besh-Kungei, near Bishkek, opened in 2023, with three bedrooms, a shared bathroom, a kitchen and a studio (Bulbul website). It offers standard, no-project, women's and zine residencies.
- Visiting: events and talks are mostly online; the residency takes applications year-round.

FUNDING
BiSCA: volunteer-run collective: unpaid members, residency fees and partner projects
- The members work on the school without pay (BiSCA website). 24.kg reported on 25 September 2020 that Diana Una said the group was "involved in the creation of the school at no cost".
- Bulbul residency fees: 30 euros a day, or 20 euros a day from November to February; 800 euros for one month, or 600 from November to February; optional curated support from 100 euros (Bulbul website).
- Partner projects: BiSCA's website lists Mobile Library Kyrgyzstan, "organised in partnership with cultural collaborators in different countries throughout Asia", and its participation in an Asia Art Archive exhibition in Hong Kong. TRASH-5 was produced with CEC ArtsLink and Tazar Kyrgyzstan.
- Award to a co-founder: Wikipedia says Borubaeva received a Prince Claus Fund award in 2022.

BOTTOM LINE
BiSCA is an unpaid, self-organised collective; its documented income is Bulbul residency fees, and it runs projects with partners such as CEC ArtsLink, Tazar Kyrgyzstan and Asia Art Archive. TRASH-5 was produced with CEC ArtsLink and Tazar Kyrgyzstan, and the Mobile Library is run with partners across Asia.
