TEOR/éTICA: San José, Costa Rica
ID: teoretica-san-jose
Website: https://teoretica.org

OVERVIEW
- Independent, private, non-profit project for art and thought at Calle 7 Av. 11, Carmen, San José, with an emphasis on contemporary art of Central America and the Caribbean (stated by the space).
- Founded in 1999 on the initiative of the artist, curator and researcher Virginia Pérez-Ratton (1950 to 2010); the founding assembly was Rolando Castellón, Paulo Herkenhoff, Claude Tournon and Bruno Stagno (stated by the space).
- Pérez-Ratton was the first director of the Museum of Contemporary Art and Design (MADC) in San José from 1994 to 1998, received the Prince Claus Award in 2002 and Costa Rica's Magón National Prize for Culture in 2009, and died on 6 October 2010 (Wikipedia).
- Lado V is presented as its study and documentation centre (stated by the space).
- It offers grants for research and production, including the Beca Catalizadora (Catalyst Grant, title translated) for organisations and El Flotador for emerging Central American artists; the ARS Teoretica Foundation exists to administer and support the project (stated by the space).
- It runs La Diabla Impresos, a RISO printing service (stated by the space).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Friday 11:00 to 18:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 16:00 (stated by the space).

FUNDING
TEOR/éTica: private non-profit, with international arts-network collaborators
- The site's footer block headed "Colaboradores" shows three logos linking to ffaiarts.net, artscollaboratory.org and doen.nl: FFAI, Arts Collaboratory and DOEN (stated by the space).
- Arts Collaboratory was set up in 2007 by two Dutch foundations, DOEN and Hivos, as a funding programme and a platform for knowledge-sharing among artist initiatives in Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Middle East; the Mondriaan Fund joined soon after (Arts Collaboratory history page).
- The site describes the project as "independiente, privado y sin fines de lucro" (independent, private and non-profit; translated); the ARS Teoretica Foundation exists to administer and support it (stated by the space).

BOTTOM LINE
TEOR/éTica is a private non-profit founded by Virginia Pérez-Ratton in 1999 and administered by the ARS Teoretica Foundation. It lists FFAI, Arts Collaboratory and DOEN as collaborators; Arts Collaboratory was set up in 2007 by the Dutch foundations DOEN and Hivos.
