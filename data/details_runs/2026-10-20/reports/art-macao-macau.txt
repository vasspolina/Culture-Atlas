ART MACAO: Macau, China
ID: art-macao-macau
Website: https://www.artmacao.mo (checked 2026-10-20; redirects to the 2025 edition's pages; the record's website field holds an SCMP article, which returned 403)

OVERVIEW
- Citywide art event in Macau run by the Macao SAR government, held every two years since its first edition in 2019; its full name is "Art Macao: Macao International Art Biennale".
- The first edition marked the 20th anniversary of the 1999 handover; the government claimed more than 16 million visits (Macao Magazine, a government publication).
- The 2023 edition, the third, took the theme "The Statistics of Fortune", with 31 exhibitions and over 200 artists (Tatler Asia).
- The 2025 edition, chief curator Feng Boyi with Liu Gang and Wu Wei, ran July to October under the theme "Hey, what brings you here?": nearly 30 exhibitions at 28 venues, over 160 artists and about 5 million visits (Cultural Affairs Bureau).
- It has no building: the Main Exhibition fills the Macao Museum of Art, and Special Exhibitions sit inside casino resorts, among them Picasso prints at Grand Lisboa Palace, a Cai Guo-Qiang AI show at MGM Macau and a show at The Venetian Macao (artmacao.mo).
- Entry to the Main Exhibition was free (Cultural Affairs Bureau, August 2025).

FUNDING
Art Macao: public and casino-funded: Macao government with the six casino concessionaires
- The Cultural Affairs Bureau organises it under the Secretariat for Social Affairs and Culture; the Macao Government Tourism Office co-organises (Cultural Affairs Bureau).
- Casino co-organisers in 2025: Galaxy Entertainment Group, Melco Resorts & Entertainment, MGM, Sands China, SJM Resorts and Wynn Resorts Macau, which present the Special Exhibitions (Cultural Affairs Bureau, artmacao.mo).
- The 2023 edition cost MOP12 million, with the six resort groups covering a significant share (Tatler Asia).
- No reporting found of the 2025 budget or of each operator's contribution.

Red flags: casino operators as co-organisers and funders
- All six of Macau's casino licence holders co-organise the event and stage exhibitions in their resorts (Cultural Affairs Bureau, 2025).
- Under concession contracts signed on 16 December 2022 for 2023 to 2032, the six pledged MOP108.7 billion for non-gaming projects and overseas markets; culture and art is one of 10 named areas (Macau Business).
- Sands China's Wilfred Wong said gaming remains the company's main income and that the art is not judged on financial return (Tatler Asia, 2023).
- Tatler Asia reports that non-gaming commitments such as art were a key factor when the licences were awarded.

BOTTOM LINE
Art Macao is a government event paid for in large part by the casino industry, whose culture spending forms part of the licence terms it owes the state. The money is gambling money by design. Worth watching: published budgets and each operator's share, which no source found sets out.
