THE WAR MEMORIAL OF KOREA (전쟁기념관): Seoul, South Korea
ID: the-war-memorial-of-korea
Website: https://www.warmemo.or.kr/ (checked 2026-11-20; fails its certificate check)

OVERVIEW
- War museum in Yongsan-dong, Yongsan-gu, on the former site of the army headquarters (Wikipedia).
- Building finished in December 1993; opened on 10 June 1994 to exhibit and memorialise Korea's military history, with the stated aims of preventing war through the lessons of the Korean War and of peaceful reunification (Wikipedia).
- The main building has three floors above ground and two below; galleries flanking the facade carry black marble walls with the names of Korean soldiers and police killed in the Korean War, the Vietnam War and later clashes, and of UN soldiers killed in Korea; the Statue of Brothers stands in the plaza (Wikipedia).
- Collection: more than 6,000 items, with large weapons, tanks and aircraft outdoors and a war history room, a history learning room and a large-weapons hall built for the 30th anniversary indoors (Seoul Metropolitan Government media hub, 2026).
- Free; open every day except Monday; guided tours can be booked online (Seoul media hub).

FUNDING
War Memorial of Korea: public: run by the Korea War Memorial Organization, a body under the Ministry of National Defense
- Operator: the Korea War Memorial Organization (전쟁기념사업회), a corporation set up by the War Memorial Organization Act (전쟁기념사업회법) to collect war records and build and run the memorial (the Act, read on the taxcanvas.kr mirror; The Korea Times).
- The Act lets the state and local governments subsidise its costs within their budgets, lets individuals, companies and groups donate, lets the state lend it state property free of charge, and lets it charge admission and fees (the Act, articles 12 to 14).
- The Defense Minister approves its annual plan and budget, receives quarterly reports and audited accounts, and supervises it; the President appoints the chairman on the minister's recommendation, and the chairman appoints the directors other than the vice-chairmen, including one nominee each from the defence, veterans and culture ministries (the Act). The chairman also chairs the board (CBS Nocut News).
- Democratic Party lawmaker Bu Seung-chan (부승찬) said its debt rose from 5.858 billion won in 2020 to 7.394 billion won in 2023, and that the chairman's base salary rose 10 per cent to 128.64 million won in 2024 with no board vote, citing board minutes (The Fact, 4 October 2024). The organisation cited the pay gap with heads of similar public bodies and with its own staff.

Red flags: 2026 audit finding against chairman Baek Seung-joo
- The Board of Audit and Inspection found that in July 2023 chairman Baek Seung-joo (백승주) asked the controlling shareholder of a tenant in the memorial building to help set up a supporters' association, and the shareholder paid its entire 50 million won endowment in August 2023; the law bars public bodies from raising donations directly (Kyunghyang Shinmun, 8 January 2026).
- It also found 25 private trips in his official car between June 2023 and January 2025, including to golf courses, and eight occasions when he had the driver take him to and from overseas trips, a child's wedding or outside lectures. The organisation told the auditors he never explicitly asked for the endowment. The auditors told the Defense Minister to prepare "appropriate measures" (Kyunghyang Shinmun).
- A Defense Ministry official said on 19 January 2026 that the ministry had decided to discipline him and was weighing how (CBS Nocut News).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 9 June 2026 the organisation removed a poster for a children's programme that set South Korea's term for the war beside China's "War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea"; an official said the aim was a "critical analysis" of China's claim (The Korea Times; Munhwa Ilbo).
- A memorial hall in Dandong, China, that promotes Beijing's account of the war was put on a teacher-training itinerary. On 15 June 2026 Defense Ministry spokesperson Chung Binna (정빛나) called this "a grave error", Defense Minister Ahn Gyu-back ordered a thorough investigation, and the ministry opened an internal inspection (Korea JoongAng Daily; Munhwa Ilbo).

BOTTOM LINE
Public money under direct Defense Ministry control, with no private sponsor; the 2026 audit finding against its chairman is its one Red flag.
