MUSEO DE LOS PINTORES OAXAQUEÑOS: Oaxaca, Mexico
ID: museo-de-los-pintores-oaxaqueños-oaxaca
Website: https://www.oaxaca.gob.mx/comunicacion/cumple-mupo-19-anos-como-referente-del-arte-contemporaneo-en-oaxaca/

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Oaxacan art (MUPO) on Avenida de la Independencia in the historic centre of Oaxaca de Juárez, at the corner of García Vigil street (Oaxaca Lending Library kiosk; Spanish Wikipedia).
- Opened as a museum in 2003 (the Spanish Wikipedia infobox says 2003, its introduction says 2004).
- The colonial building opened on 26 January 1686 as the Colegio de las Doncellas de Nuestra Señora de la Presentación, paid for by the priest Juan Gómez de Tapia and the philanthropist Miguel Fernández Fiallo to educate girls who lacked access to schooling (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Later uses: the first seat of the Museo Nacional de Antropología e Historia in 1933, the State Regional Museum until 1986, the City Council until 1992 and the state Secretaría de Turismo from 1993 to 2003 (Spanish Wikipedia; Oaxaca Lending Library kiosk).
- The museum has no permanent collection and four temporary exhibition rooms named for Rufino Tamayo, Rodolfo Morales, Rodolfo Nieto and Francisco Gutiérrez; activities include dance classes, guided tours, lectures, book presentations and educational services (Spanish Wikipedia).
- The museum states its aim as promoting and developing the visual arts in Oaxaca and beyond, with a focus on conserving, analysing and sharing works made in Oaxaca (Oaxaca Lending Library kiosk).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00 (Spanish Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Museo de los Pintores Oaxaqueños: mainly public: Oaxaca state government
- It is run by the Patronato de las Unidades de Servicios Culturales y Turísticos del Estado de Oaxaca, and the state government started the renovation that adapted the building for art exhibitions (Spanish Wikipedia).
- In May 2025 the Secretaría de las Culturas y Artes (Seculta), headed by Flavio Sosa Villavicencio, and the Instituto del Patrimonio Cultural del Estado de Oaxaca (Inpac) began intervention works at state cultural spaces including MUPO, covering facades, doors and windows, signage, accessibility, electrical systems and civil protection (Excélsior, Patricia Briseño, 30 May 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution of Oaxaca housed in a 1686 colonial building, run by the state's cultural and tourist services patronage and maintained by the state government through Seculta and Inpac. The money documented is state money.
