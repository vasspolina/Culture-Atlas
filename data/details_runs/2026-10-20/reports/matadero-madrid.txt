MATADERO MADRID: Madrid, Spain
ID: matadero-madrid
Website: https://www.mataderomadrid.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary creation centre of the Madrid City Council's Area of Culture, Sports and Tourism, at Plaza de Legazpi 8, in the former municipal slaughterhouse and cattle market beside Madrid Río park (Matadero brochure; Matadero website; esmadrid.com).
- Created in 2006 and housed in pavilions designed by Luis Bellido in the early 20th century as a slaughterhouse and cattle market (Matadero brochure). Wikipedia says the slaughterhouse closed in 1996 and the centre opened in 2006.
- The pavilions were converted by Madrid architecture studios including Langarita y Navarro, Churtichaga-Quadra Salcedo, Arturo Franco and Fabrice van Teslaar, Ginés Garrido and Antonio García Abril (Matadero brochure). The centre has won a Mies van der Rohe emerging architecture award and COAM awards.
- Municipal programmes: Intermediae (art and community; it opened Matadero in 2007), Medialab (experimental research and production) and the Centro de residencias artísticas (Matadero brochure). Cineteca Madrid and Naves del Español en Matadero are public centres with independent artistic directors; Naves del Español are run by the Teatro Español and hold the Sala Fernando Arrabal with more than 600 seats and the Sala Max Aub (Matadero brochure).
- Private partners inside the site: Central de Diseño, run by the Fundación Diseño Madrid (DIMAD); Extensión AVAM, run by Artistas Visuales Asociados de Madrid; Casa del Lector, which depends on the Fundación Germán Sánchez Ruipérez; and MAD Madrid Artes Digitales, an immersive experience centre (Matadero brochure).
- Nave 16 has more than 4,000 square metres, divided into three modules for exhibitions, the artists' residency centre and the immersive centre (Matadero brochure).
- Centro Danza Matadero in Nave 11 is presented as Spain's first public venue devoted entirely to dance (esmadrid.com). Tickets are sold through Madrid Destino's online shop; events list accessibility features such as induction loops, easy-read materials and sign language (Matadero website).

FUNDING
Matadero Madrid: mainly public: the Madrid City Council, through Madrid Destino
- Matadero describes itself as the centre of the City Council's Area of Culture, Sports and Tourism (Matadero brochure); the website's footer names Madrid Destino Cultura Turismo y Negocio, S.A. (Matadero website).
- In 2017 the culture delegate Celia Mayer presented a funding model for the Naves backed by a Madrid Destino budget of 1.2 million euros for that season, so that companies no longer depend on ticket sales (El Español, Alberto Ojeda, 7 March 2017).
- The private partners named above run their own spaces within the site: the Fundación Germán Sánchez Ruipérez (Casa del Lector), the Fundación DIMAD (Central de Diseño) and Artistas Visuales Asociados de Madrid (Extensión AVAM) (Matadero brochure).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One dispute documented, in 2017, about the change of programme at the Naves del Español rather than the centre's funding.
1. El Español (El Cultural): Alberto Ojeda, "Las 'artes vivas' de Mateo Feijóo suscitan la hostilidad del gremio escénico" (2017)
URL: https://elespanol.com/el-cultural/escenarios/20170307/artes-mateo-feijoo-suscitan-hostilidad-gremio-escenico/198981052_0.amp.html
- Mateo Feijóo's programme moved the Naves away from text-based theatre, renamed the space the Centro Internacional de Artes Vivas, gave priority to dance and performance and dropped the Frinje festival.
- Part of the theatre sector saw the change as an encroachment on a key space for showing their work; the article's headline speaks of the "hostilidad del gremio escénico" (hostility of the stage guild; translated). The names of Fernando Arrabal and Max Aub were removed from the signage, and Mayor Manuela Carmena wrote to the Max Aub Foundation and to Arrabal assuring them the names would stay.

BOTTOM LINE
Matadero is a municipal centre of the Madrid City Council, operated through Madrid Destino, with 1.2 million euros of Madrid Destino money documented for the Naves in the 2017 season. Private foundations run Casa del Lector, Central de Diseño and Extensión AVAM inside it.
