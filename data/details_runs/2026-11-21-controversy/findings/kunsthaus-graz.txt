ID: kunsthaus-graz

WIDER CONTEXT
- In late September 2012 an activist group formed at the steirischer herbst Marathon Camp poured oil in the Kunsthaus during a protest tour of Graz, to draw attention to the Kunsthaus sponsor Raiffeisenbank and its "Investitionen in die Ölindustrie" ("investments in the oil industry", translated) (Falter 40/2012, "Steiermark kurz", 3 October 2012).
