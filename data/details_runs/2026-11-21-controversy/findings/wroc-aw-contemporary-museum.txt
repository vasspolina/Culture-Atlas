ID: wroc-aw-contemporary-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- An inspection by the City of Wrocław, the museum's organiser, found "nierzetelne prowadzenie ksiąg rachunkowych, brak ksiąg inwentarzowych muzealiów" ("unreliable bookkeeping, no inventory books for the museum's objects", translated) (wroclaw.pl, 3 December 2020).
- The city's page says the museum had not carried out a reliable inventory of its collections, that the 2019 inventory rested on incomplete and unreliable data, and that works deposited by the Lower Silesian Society for the Encouragement of Fine Arts in 2011 had been received without a list; the collections were recorded in the accounts at 24,322,231 zł on 31 December 2019 (wroclaw.pl, 3 December 2020).
- The organiser notified the Wrocław Stare Miasto district prosecutor's office of a possible offence, and the staff the report held responsible asked to end their employment (wroclaw.pl, 3 December 2020).
- The acting director committed to filling all gaps in the inventory by 31 December 2020 and to linking a full inventory to the accounts (wroclaw.pl, 3 December 2020).
