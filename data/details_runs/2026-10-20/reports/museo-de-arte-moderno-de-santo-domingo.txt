MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE SANTO DOMINGO: Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-santo-domingo
Website: No museum website found; the record lists none. Not checked on 2026-10-24.

OVERVIEW
- State modern art museum in the Plaza de la Cultura Juan Pablo Duarte complex, Santo Domingo (El Día, 2016).
- Opened on 15 December 1976 (El Día, 2016).
- Designed and built by Dominican architect José Miniño; a concrete building with three levels and a basement (El Día; El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Remodelled for about four years and reopened during the COVID-19 pandemic (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Holds more than 1,500 pieces, of which 80 to 100 are shown at a time (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Hosts the Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales, held at the museum since 1976; the 2025 edition showed 207 works chosen from 609 registered (Acento, José Rafael Sosa, 2025).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00 with free admission, as reported in October 2022 (El Inmobiliario). No reporting found of current hours or accessibility information.

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Santo Domingo: public: Dominican Ministry of Culture
- Artists' open letters hold the Ministry of Culture and the museum jointly responsible for the Bienal (El Nuevo Diario, 2020; Diario Libre, 2021).
- Museum director Federico Fondeur sits on the Ministry's organising commission for the XXX Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales, sworn in by Culture Minister Milagros Germán (El Día, 25 October 2022); museum curator Ana A. Fernández is also a member (El Día, 2022).
- No reporting found of a budget figure, a private sponsor or a donor list for the museum.

Red flags: Protest or boycott
- Postponement: the Ministry postponed the opening of the 29th Bienal in December 2019, saying remodelling of the museum was unfinished (Diario Libre, 16 December 2019). El Nuevo Diario reports the notice came a day before the new opening date, in a communiqué.
- Protest: the Comité de la Bienal Invisible called a protest at the Plaza de la Cultura on 17 December 2019 at 5:30 p.m., demanding that the biennials be held on schedule (Diario Libre).
- July 2020 letter: La Bienal Invisible, La Otra Bienal and La Cooperativa called the handling of the 29th Bienal negligent, blaming the Ministry and the museum; the signers, more than 30 artists, curators and managers, included Guadalupe Casasnovas and Raquel Paiewonsky. They said works sent for the 29th edition had been stored at the museum for nearly two years and that no progress reports or completion dates for the renovation were released (El Nuevo Diario, 16 July 2020). The article names Minister Eduardo Selman and María Elena Ditrén, who had directed the museum for 16 years.
- June 2021 letter: more than 60 artists and cultural workers denounced the "deficiente manejo" of the 29th Bienal, saying the selection list included withdrawn works and works shown elsewhere, and that the museum opened its rooms without an announcement while works were still being installed. They addressed Minister Carmen Heredia and museum director Federico Fondeur (Diario Libre, 23 June 2021). The article carries no reply from the Ministry or the museum.
- 2025: the unpublished catalogues of the 2021 and 2023 Bienales drew criticism; artist Ramón Peña said he would not take part again (Acento).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism is frequent but comes from newspapers and artists' collectives, not art press. It concerns the Bienal's management and the Ministry, more than the museum's collection.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution with no private sponsor named, so the money is not the issue. The weak points are the repeated delays and Bienal management that artists blamed on the Ministry and the museum in 2019, 2020 and 2021, and the lack of published budget or catalogue information. Worth watching: whether the permanent Bienal committee announced in 2025 is created and publishes accounts.
