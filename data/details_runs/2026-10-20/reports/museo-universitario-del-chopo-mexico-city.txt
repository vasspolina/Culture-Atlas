MUSEO UNIVERSITARIO DEL CHOPO: Mexico City, Mexico
ID: museo-universitario-del-chopo-mexico-city
Website: https://www.chopo.unam.mx/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art and culture museum of the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM) in the Santa María la Ribera neighbourhood of Mexico City, under UNAM's Coordinación de Difusión Cultural (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Opened as a university museum on 25 November 1975 (Spanish Wikipedia; Rafael Paz, Gaceta UNAM, 21 August 2025, on its 50th anniversary).
- The building, known as El Chopo, is a prefabricated Jugendstil iron-and-glass structure designed by Bruno Möhring, first used at a 1902 exhibition in Düsseldorf, and assembled in Santa María la Ribera between 1901 and 1905 by the engineer Luis Bacmeister for the entrepreneur José Landero y Coss (Spanish Wikipedia).
- The building housed the Museo Nacional de Historia Natural from 1913 and joined UNAM in 1929. After the natural history museum closed in 1964 it stood abandoned, and a 1970 plan to sell its iron was stopped by its monument status. UNAM restored it from 1973 (Spanish Wikipedia).
- It closed in October 2005 for a redesign by the architect Enrique Norten and reopened on 18 May 2010 (Spanish Wikipedia).
- On site: galleries, the Foro del Dinosaurio Juan José Gurrola, a documentation centre, the Café del Chopo and an auditorium, with exhibitions, music, dance, theatre, film and low-cost workshops (Spanish Wikipedia). The Tianguis Cultural del Chopo began inside the museum on 4 October 1980 and now meets on Saturdays on Juan Aldama; it was recognised as intangible cultural heritage of Mexico City in 2023 (Gaceta UNAM). The Festival Internacional de Diversidad Sexual grew from a Semana Cultural Lésbica Gay begun in 1987 (Gaceta UNAM).
- Sol Henaro Palomino has directed it since January 2024 (UNAM 2024 report), and calls it "un centro social travestido de museo" (a social centre disguised as a museum; translation; Gaceta UNAM, 21 August 2025).
- In May 2026 the Juan Gabriel exhibition "Un lugar de ambiente. Legado y disidencia", organised with the 39th Festival Internacional por la Diversidad Sexual, was due to open on 28 May (Roberto Frías, Gaceta UNAM, 25 May 2026). The museum cancelled the opening that day, postponing it "hasta nuevo aviso" (Eduardo Alavez, Chilango, 29 May 2026), then opened to the public hours later and held the official inauguration on 13 June; the show runs to 28 August (Frida Juárez Bautista, El Universal, 19 June 2026).
- Henaro told El Universal that the delay was an administrative matter to settle with the artist's representatives. Unverified: social media users said it was a copyright problem, as reported by El Universal, which says the reasons "siguen sin esclarecerse" (remain unclear; translation).

FUNDING
Museo Universitario del Chopo: mainly public: UNAM, itself funded by the federal government
- The museum belongs to UNAM, a public university. UNAM's 2026 budget is 59,878 million pesos, of which 53,749 million (89.8%) is federal subsidy and 6,129 million (10.2%) is the university's own income (Mirtha Hernández, Gaceta UNAM, 12 February 2026).
- UNAM's 2024 annual report describes the museum's programme under Henaro, including two new exhibition spaces, El Gabinete for emerging artists and an outdoor space for interventions.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is UNAM's budget, about 90% federal subsidy in 2026. The documented episode is the cancelled and then reinstated opening of the Juan Gabriel show in May 2026, for which the museum gave an administrative reason.
