LE 19, CRAC (CENTRE RÉGIONAL D'ART CONTEMPORAIN): Montbéliard, France
ID: le-19-crac-montbéliard
Website: https://le19crac.com/en/about (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Regional centre for contemporary art at 19 avenue des Alliés, opposite Montbéliard Ville railway station and 15 minutes from the Belfort-Montbéliard TGV station (le19crac.com; DCA).
- Founded in 1993 and opened in 1995; since 24 December 2018 it holds the national label Centre d'art contemporain d'intérêt national (le19crac.com; Montbéliard council deliberation of 18 December 2023).
- It is an association under the French law of 1901, chaired by Liliane Dangel (2024 to 2027 objectives agreement and its 2025 amendment).
- Building: a 1920s warehouse that was the first office of the Peugeot car company, with a 550 m² exhibition space (le19crac.com).
- Programme: three to four exhibitions a year, off-site projects and artist residencies, among them projects under the Culture Santé, Culture Justice and Artistes plasticiens au lycée programmes (le19crac.com).
- Director: Adeline Lépine-Delaborde, in post since 2022 after curating Veduta at the Lyon Biennale from 2016 to 2022 (Mondriaan Fund profile; DCA). The team lists eight staff and a civic service volunteer (le19crac.com).
- Current season: the group show "La femme aux bras poissons", 19 September 2026 to 3 January 2027, and the fifth Pôle Position show of young artists from Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, 10 October 2026 to 3 January 2027 (le19crac.com).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday 2 pm to 6 pm, Sunday 3 pm to 6 pm, closed Mondays and public holidays; free entry; an easy-to-read visitor guide is offered (le19crac.com).

FUNDING
Le 19, Crac: public: the State (DRAC Bourgogne-Franche-Comté), the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté Region, the City of Montbéliard and Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération
- The centre names its four public funders: the French State, the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté Region, the City of Montbéliard and the Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération (le19crac.com); DCA names the State funder as the DRAC Bourgogne-Franche-Comté.
- Since 1993 the City and the State have funded it through successive agreements; the agglomeration joined in 2009 and the Region signed for the first time in the 2024 to 2027 agreement, which values the four-year programme at 1,838,500 euros and plans about 440,000 euros of income and spending a year (Montbéliard council deliberation, 18 December 2023).
- State: the DRAC paid 194,500 euros in 2023: 150,000 euros for running costs, 15,000 euros for public programmes and 29,500 euros earmarked for projects (2020 to 2023 financial report annexed to the agreement).
- Region: 85,000 euros a year for running costs and 14,000 euros earmarked in 2021 to 2023 (same report).
- City of Montbéliard: its grant fell from 110,000 euros in 2020 to 94,500 euros in 2023 and was set at 94,500 euros for 2024; the centre's project text says its 2024 to 2027 budget stays at the 2023 level that followed this cut; the City also lends the building, a storage room and two artists' rooms free of charge, with a 2023 rental value of 44,786.41 euros for the avenue des Alliés building (same agreement).
- Pays de Montbéliard Agglomération: 35,000 euros a year for running costs plus 8,000 euros earmarked in 2023, and an extra grant pledged for 2024, the year the area was French Capital of Culture (same agreement).
- Covid support of 47,437 euros in 2020 and 56,896 euros in 2021, mainly social-charge exemptions, produced operating surpluses of 55,384 and 62,534 euros (same report).
- Members of the 19 Club pay from 15 euros a year (le19crac.com).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: the money is French public money from four levels of government under a signed four-year agreement. Worth watching is the City's shrinking share and whether the State and Region keep their grants level.
