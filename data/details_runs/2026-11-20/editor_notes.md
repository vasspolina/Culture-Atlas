# Editor notes, details run of 20 November 2026

Things the research and checking agents found that the details import does not change: record
fields outside `details` (funding, watch, flags, tier, grade, website) and calls on grading.

## Grade or flag questions

- mitsubishi-ichigokan-museum: the museum's corporate members page (checked 20 Nov 2026) lists
  Mitsubishi Heavy Industries as a Premium Support Member (arms: its own defence page lists fighter
  jets, missiles, destroyers, submarines, tanks) and ENEOS Holdings as a Support Member (petroleum,
  oil and gas production). The report carries both as Red flags; the record's flags and grade do not.
- casa-de-los-tres-mundos (fundaci-n-casa-de-los-tres-mundos-granada): its only Red flags heading is
  a 2004 to 2015 court case over who owns one eighth of the house. Documented, but in no flag
  category.
- three revolutions exhibition (North Korea): flags the state's 2021 weapons show held on the site,
  a use of the venue rather than a funder.
- tower-of-david-museum: a Red flag about Rockefeller Museum tours moved to WIDER CONTEXT (it concerns
  another museum). The "over 84 per cent self-funded" figure and the 1989 Duffield support were cut:
  the Jewish Chronicle page could not be opened by any agent.
- acropolis-museum: the Parthenon sculptures Red flag moved to WIDER CONTEXT (the dispute is between
  Greece and the British Museum).

- national-centre-for-the-performing-arts-ncpa: report adds a Red flag for a strategic partnership
  with ENN, a natural gas group, through the NCPA's own foundation (Xinhua). Fossil fuels on the
  record would follow if it holds.
- nanjing-museum: report adds a former director's 2026 bribery conviction (3 years, 200,000 yuan).
- sesc-são-paulo-são-paulo: the 2017 petition against Judith Butler's seminar at Sesc Pompeia moved
  from WIDER CONTEXT to Red flags (a petition aimed at the space).

- national-museum-of-china: report keeps a Red flag for 2024 agreements with Qatar Museums and the
  Saudi Museums Commission (named Gulf state partners); the museum's 2024 data report does not say
  what they cover.
- nanjing-massacre-memorial-hall: the Japanese government's 2007 objections were moved to WIDER
  CONTEXT (a state that neither funds nor controls the space; older than five years).
- antarctic-biennale: researcher flagged the main sponsor's company (Kaspersky) as US-sanctioned in
  2024; the checker was told to state exactly what the US did and to whom, and to move it to WIDER
  CONTEXT if the sanctions and five-year rules are not met (the voyage was in 2017).

## Record fields that are wrong

- nanjing-museum: the record's watch line speaks of a "nationwide museum audit"; the sources describe
  a province-wide campaign.
- crac-valparaiso: cracvalparaiso.cl does not resolve; cracvalparaiso.org serves a casino spam blog.
- alta-museum-world-heritage-rock-art-centre: year_founded is 1991; the museum was founded in 1978 as
  a municipal museum and moved into the new building in 1991.
- museum-of-the-macedonian-struggle: the record says visits are by compulsory guided tour; the museum
  now offers individual visits.
- kaviar-factory-henningsvaer: if the record says it files no accounts, it is wrong: KaviarFactory
  Drift AS (org. no. 912114074) files them.
- palazzo-delle-esposizioni: the record may say Palaexpo runs the Scuderie del Quirinale and the Casa
  del Jazz; Ales took over the Scuderie in 2016 and Fondazione Musica per Roma the Casa del Jazz in
  2018. Palaexpo now runs the Palazzo, MACRO, the Museo delle Periferie and the 2026 Mattatoio
  programme.
- akureyri-art-museum: the director is now Sigríður Örvarsdóttir (Wikipedia still names Hlynur
  Hallsson).
- quadriennale-di-roma: the Rome Chamber of Commerce as a funder could not be confirmed (2022 accounts
  show the ministry, the Prime Minister's Office, the region and the city).
- defence-services-museum and national-museum-of-the-islamic-revolution-and-holy-defense: the first
  carries a US sanctions flag (commander-in-chief, 2019 and 2021); the second notes the IRGC co-launched
  the 2005 design competition, with no sanctions flag added.
- festspillene-i-nord-norge-harstad: report adds Aker BP (oil) as a 2026 partner, from a logo on the
  home page; the checker was told to confirm it from text or cut it. Fossil fuels on the record would
  follow if it holds.

- bangabandhu-memorial-museum: the record says banks gave the trust Tk 1,360 million in 2017. The
  Daily Star says 37 banks gave over Tk 136 crore to three bodies together (this trust, the Prime
  Minister's Education Assistance Trust and the Shuchona Foundation).
- military-trophy-park: the record says the presidential website calls the park a display of
  "Armenian war crimes". The president.az speech does not; Report.az describes the war-crimes
  displays.
- scotts-discovery-hut-hut-point: the record's watch line ("visitors ... are likely to encounter
  it") is Wikipedia wording with a hedge.
- mitsubishi-ichigokan-museum: website mitsubishi-museum.jp does not resolve; the live site is mimt.jp.
- institute-of-contemporary-art-indian-ocean-icaio: icaio.org redirects to an unrelated domain.
