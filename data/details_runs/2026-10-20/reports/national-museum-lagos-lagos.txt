NATIONAL MUSEUM, LAGOS: Lagos, Nigeria
ID: national-museum-lagos-lagos

OVERVIEW
- National museum at Onikan on Lagos Island, administered by the National Commission for Museums and Monuments (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1957 by the English archaeologist Kenneth Murray to preserve Nigeria's historical artefacts (Wikipedia).
- Holds an estimated 47,000 objects in wood, ivory, metal and terracotta, among them Nok-culture heads, Benin ivories, Igbo-Ukwu bronzes, Egungun costumes and Akwete cloth. A virtual tour built on an adapted Google Street View was added in 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Africanews (with AFP, 4 June 2026) reports that one of the museum's three galleries was remodelled and opened in April 2026, with touchable artefacts and unrestricted photography, and that another gallery is closed for renovation. Daily Trust (Adebayo Gbenga, 14 April 2026) names the remodelled "Echoes of the Past" gallery.
- Daily Trust reported that the project began by digitising about 200 artefacts, and that later phases will cover expansion, conservation and visitor facilities.

FUNDING
National Museum, Lagos: administered by the National Commission for Museums and Monuments, with IHS Nigeria as 2026 renovation sponsor
- Wikipedia says the National Commission for Museums and Monuments administers the museum.
- IHS Nigeria partnered with the Commission to renovate the museum; the work, commissioned by Minister Hannatu Musawa, whose ministry covers arts, culture, tourism and the creative economy, covered restoration of the main gallery, display and lighting systems, air conditioning, a solar inverter system and security cameras (TechEconomy, Destiny Eseaga, 12 April 2026). Daily Trust calls the partner IHS Towers, and Africanews (AFP) says the renovation was privately funded.
- The renovation builds on IHS Nigeria's earlier sponsorship of the museum's digitisation, including Nigeria's first digital museum of antiquities (TechEconomy; Brand Communicator, Sherifat Tijani, 14 April 2026).
- Daily Trust reports that Musawa stressed public-private partnerships for sustaining cultural infrastructure and that the Commission's Director General, Olugbile Holloway, said the museum had not had an extensive renovation for two decades.
- AFP reports that Nigerian authorities are seeking partners to fund future upgrades in preparation for repatriated artefacts (Africanews).
- Vanguard (Japhet Alakam and Prisca Sam-Duru, "FG is committed to National Endowment for Arts", 23 June 2013) reported that Minister of Tourism, Culture and National Orientation Edem Duke said the ministry was about to sign a memorandum of understanding with the Ford Foundation to renew and expand the National Museum in Onikan.

Not related: the Lagos Museum, a Lagos State project planned within the Lagos House and Presidential Lodge in Onikan, which Brand Spur (20 February 2018) reports is funded by a $600,000 Ford Foundation donation for its architectural design and early work. It is a different institution from the National Museum in Onikan.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is administered by the National Commission for Museums and Monuments, and its 2026 renovation was carried out with IHS Nigeria, which also sponsored the museum's digitisation. In June 2013 the ministry said it was about to sign a memorandum of understanding with the Ford Foundation on renewing the museum.
