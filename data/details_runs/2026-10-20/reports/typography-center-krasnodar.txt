TYPOGRAPHY CENTER FOR CONTEMPORARY ART: Krasnodar, Russia
ID: typography-center-krasnodar
Website: https://typography-online.ru (checked 2026-10-25; live as an archive: the banner says the site has not been updated since 24 February 2022)

OVERVIEW
- Independent contemporary art centre in Krasnodar, known as Tipografiya (Typography). It is closed and its legal entity was liquidated in 2025.
- Founded in 2012 by the patron Nikolai Moroz and the group ZIP (Russian Art Archive); the archive gives 26 July 2012 as its start date.
- Opened in the space of the former printing house "Sovetskaya Kuban" (Cyclowiki, Russian Art Archive); the name Tipografiya means Typography.
- ZIP formed in 2009; its name abbreviates the Krasnodar Measuring Instruments Plant, where the artists' studio stood until 2015 (Russian Art Archive). The centre later moved to the plant's site at Zipovskaya Street 5, building 33 (Afisha, January 2022).
- Programmes: exhibitions, lectures, a library, Radio Fantasia, the REDGIFT gallery and the Krasnodar Institute of Contemporary Art (KISI), which offered a three-month School of Contemporary Art from November 2012 (the centre's site; Cyclowiki for KISI).
- Visiting: closed since 12 May 2022 according to the centre's own site; no admission or opening hours apply.

FUNDING
Typography Center: privately patronised, with small grants; no funder list published
- Nikolai Moroz: the Russian Art Archive names him as the patron who founded the centre in 2012 with ZIP; 7x7 calls him an artist and philanthropist. The pages read give no amounts.
- In 2018 the centre received a grant from the Garage museum, and Cosmoscow named it "Institution of the Year" that year (Afisha, 2022).
- Forbes.ru (17 January 2018, read on Rambler Weekend) reports Anastasia Kizilova's "Found Project" exhibition at the centre and says the Vladimir Smirnov and Konstantin Sorokin Foundation funds the initiative.
- Foreign funding: the Russian Ministry of Justice register entry cited "foreign funding of preparation for the events within the Year of Germany in Russia 2020/21" among its reasons for listing the centre (OVD-Info). The pages read name no foreign donor and no amount.
- No reporting found of a fossil fuel, gambling, tobacco, arms, Gulf state or other flagged funder.
- No reporting found of published accounts or an annual report; the centre's site posts reports to the Ministry of Justice dated 15 July and 15 October 2022.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 6 May 2022: the Russian Ministry of Justice added the Krasnodar Centre for Contemporary Art "Tipografiya" to its register of non-profit organisations acting as foreign agents (Novaya Gazeta, 6 May 2022; 7x7; OVD-Info). The entry cited social media posts assessing officials and state bodies, public statements by Elena Ishchenko, curator and director of the legal entity, and the foreign funding for Year of Germany events (OVD-Info).
- 14 May 2022: the centre announced it was closing its space because staff no longer considered it safe, and planned a sale of furniture and works. Its Instagram statement said it would not close the legal entity or stop working (7x7).
- 7x7 (16 May 2022) reports that since the start of the "special operation" the centre stopped exhibitions and moved its hall into a warehouse service to save on rent and utilities, while continuing classes for teenagers and adults with autism.
- 11 June 2025: the legal entity was liquidated according to the Unified State Register of Legal Entities, and the Ministry of Justice announced on 5 September 2025 that it removed the centre from the foreign-agent register because of the liquidation (Caucasian Knot, 6 September 2025; OVD-Info gives the removal year as 2025). The centre's own site says the legal entity stopped working in March 2023.
- No page opened reports Russian state funding of the centre; the listing is a state action against an independent organisation.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the centre was a private patron, a Garage grant and project foundations; no flagged-industry funder turned up. The weak points are that no accounts or funder amounts are public and that the register cites unnamed foreign funding for German-year events. The centre has been closed since 2022 and its legal entity was liquidated in 2025.
