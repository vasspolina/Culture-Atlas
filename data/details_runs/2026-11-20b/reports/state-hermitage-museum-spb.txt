THE STATE HERMITAGE MUSEUM: Saint Petersburg, Russia
ID: state-hermitage-museum-spb
Website: https://hermitagemuseum.org (checked 2026-11-20; its security certificate failed standard verification)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of art and culture in six historic buildings along Palace Embankment, among them the Winter Palace (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1764, when Catherine the Great bought paintings from the Berlin merchant Johann Ernst Gotzkowsky (Wikipedia).
- Mikhail Piotrovsky has directed it since 1992; Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin reappointed him for five years on 10 September 2025 (Expert).
- Visiting: free on the third Thursday of each month and daily for students; 2,812,913 visitors in 2022 (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
The State Hermitage Museum: mainly public: the Russian federal budget, with Gazprom, Magnit and Sber sponsoring galleries and shows
- A 1996 decree placed it under the patronage of the Russian president and ordered its upkeep funded from a separate federal budget line from 1997 (Decree No. 862 of 12 June 1996).
- Its 2017 state subsidy, more than 5 billion rubles, was the largest among the ten most visited museums of Moscow and Saint Petersburg (News.ru, 2018, reprinted by Rambler).
- For 2026 it has a federal capital investment subsidy of 929 million rubles, 6.2 times the previous year's (Delovoy Peterburg, 27 May 2026).
- Stated by Magnit in a company press release (Retail.ru, 2025): the retailer, a partner since late 2023, helped with a 2023 restoration and backed shows in 2024 and 2025.
- Sber was general partner of a show of contemporary Indian art opened on 4 June 2026 (Lenta.ru, citing Sber's press service).

Red flags: Gazprom sponsorship, 2022
- Gazprom was general sponsor of the "Peter the Great Gallery", opened in January 2022 with its management board chairman Alexey Miller present (Komsomolskaya Pravda); two more halls opened with its support on 21 June 2022 (Gazprom press release on AK&M).

Red flags: 2026 sponsorship by sanctioned state bank Sber
- The US Treasury imposed full blocking sanctions on Sberbank on 6 April 2022 and called it majority-owned by the Russian government (US Treasury press release).
- The UK has listed PJSC Sberbank since 1 March 2022, with an asset freeze, noting the Russian government's controlling share (UK Sanctions List, FCDO, checked 20 November 2026).

Red flags: EU sanctions on the director, 2026
- The EU listed Piotrovsky on 23 April 2026 as a "close associate" of Putin who "actively supported and justified" the war, citing his backing of the absorption of Ukrainian museum items and of digs in occupied Crimea (The Art Newspaper, 29 April 2026).
- Canada sanctioned him in August 2023 (The Art Newspaper, 2023).

Red flags: 2022 boycott by foreign partners
- Hermitage Amsterdam returned a loan show and cut ties, the Hermitage Foundation UK suspended ties, the Hermitage's Italian foundation suspended operations, the International Advisory Board suspended its work on 15 March 2022 and the Bizot group suspended Piotrovsky (The Art Newspaper, 4 April 2022).

Red flags: 2026 Accounts Chamber finding on the storage complex
- Reviewing 2025 federal culture spending, the Accounts Chamber found shortcomings in building the storage complex, a risk of ineffective use of 25.5 million rubles and 358.1 million rubles unspent on 1 January 2026 (Delovoy Peterburg).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism since 2022 concerns the director's support for the war.
1. The Art Newspaper: Sophia Kishkovsky, "Isolated by Russia boycotts, Hermitage director calls for 'cultural bridges between the nations'" (2022)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/04/04/russia-boycott-hermitage-museum
- Ukrainian cultural figures condemned Piotrovsky's silence on the invasion; in 2021 he told the paper "Putin's been my person since the 1990s".
2. USA Art News: "State Hermitage Museum curator quits over Russia's war in Ukraine" (2022)
URL: https://usaartnews.com/news/state-hermitage-museum-curator-quits-over-russias-war-in-ukraine
- Head of contemporary art Dmitry Ozerkov resigned on 2 October 2022 over the war.
- Quotes Piotrovsky in Rossiiskaya Gazeta: "We are all militarists and imperials."
3. Novaya Gazeta Europe: Anna Matveeva, "The emperor" (2025)
URL: https://novayagazeta.eu/articles/2025/10/20/the-emperor-en
- Traces Piotrovsky from reformer to war supporter who backed Putin in three elections.
- Cites a 2021 Proekt report that businesses tied to the Hermitage belonged to friends of the Piotrovsky family.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2013 the Accounts Chamber found inflated Hermitage restoration contracts, with damage above six billion rubles; ministry official Boris Mazo was jailed for eight years (Novaya Gazeta Europe).
- Hermitage archaeologist Alexander Butyagin, arrested in Poland in December 2025 at Ukraine's request over digs in occupied Crimea, was freed in a prisoner exchange on 28 April 2026 (The Art Newspaper).
- In 2026 Ukrainian prosecutors changed the charge against him to violations of the laws and customs of war. Unverified: Ukraine's Defence Intelligence says 14 artefacts were taken during his Myrmekion expedition (United24 Media).

BOTTOM LINE
Not clean: state money sits beside sponsorship from Gazprom and a sanctioned state bank, under a director sanctioned for backing the war.
