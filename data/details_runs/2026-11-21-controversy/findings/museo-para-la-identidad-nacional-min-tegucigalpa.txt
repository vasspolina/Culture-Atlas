CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Hyperallergic: Annette Lin, "How a Museum Wields Its Power in Crafting a National Identity" (2019)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/how-a-museum-wields-its-power-in-crafting-a-national-identity/
- Behind a registration wall; only the introduction was read. The points below are from Nonprofit Quarterly's account of the piece (Eileen Cunniffe, "Can a Single Museum Speak for the Nation?", 1 October 2019).
- The permanent collection's emphasis on Maya artefacts gives visitors the impression that the Maya were "the only culture to ever occupy pre-colonial Honduras", though they occupied only "the western fringes of the modern state"; Lin writes that this "underscores the disconnect between an artistic construct of a country and the lived experience of the majority of its citizens."
- The museum largely fails to acknowledge current unrest in Honduras, and Lin asks: "What does this place have to do with how Hondurans see themselves?"
- Lin contrasts a painting in the temporary galleries of a woman trying to leave the country with her two children with "the elevator music version of Honduran society the permanent exhibitions presented."
