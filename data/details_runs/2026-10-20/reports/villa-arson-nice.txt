VILLA ARSON: Nice, France
ID: villa-arson-nice
Website: https://villa-arson.fr

OVERVIEW
- National higher art school and contemporary art centre on a hill in the Saint Barthélemy neighbourhood of northern Nice, overlooking the city and the coast (Wikipedia).
- The site has the school, the art centre, residencies, a publishing shop, a library and Radio Arson (Villa Arson website).
- The estate was an 18th-century villa bought in 1812 by Pierre-Joseph Arson, later a hotel and then the Clinique de Cyrnos; in 1964 the city of Nice ceded it to the State for an art school that André Malraux wanted (French Wikipedia).
- The new school buildings were designed by Michel Marot and inaugurated in 1972 (French Wikipedia).
- In 2002 the Ministry of Culture designated it a grand établissement for contemporary art (Wikipedia), and in 2003 it became an établissement public administratif under the Ministry of Culture (French Wikipedia). Since 2020 it has been a component of the Université Côte d'Azur (French Wikipedia).
- Directors: Sylvain Lizon from 2019 to 2025; Ulrika Byttner, formerly head of the École supérieure d'art et de design Le Havre-Rouen, named by Minister of Culture Rachida Dati after the board's opinion (Ministry of Culture press release, 19 January 2026).
- Visiting: open to the public every afternoon from 14:00 to 18:00, until 19:00 in July and August; exhibitions from 12 November 2026 to 4 April 2027 include a show on Édouard Glissant and one on Serge Hélénon (Villa Arson website).

FUNDING
Villa Arson: public establishment under the French Ministry of Culture
- It is an établissement public administratif under the Ministry of Culture (French Wikipedia). Minister of Culture Rachida Dati named Ulrika Byttner director after the board's opinion (Ministry of Culture press release, 19 January 2026).
- The Francis Bacon MB Art Foundation entered a partnership with Villa Arson in 2017 and awards a €10,000 "Villa Arson Artist Support Scholarship" every two years to a Villa Arson graduate with a DNSEP master's degree in painting or drawing (MB Art Foundation).

BOTTOM LINE
Villa Arson is a national school and art centre run as a public establishment under the French Ministry of Culture. The one private funding line documented is a €10,000 scholarship every two years from the Francis Bacon MB Art Foundation.
