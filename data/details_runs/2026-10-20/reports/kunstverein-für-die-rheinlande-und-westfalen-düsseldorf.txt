KUNSTVEREIN FÜR DIE RHEINLANDE UND WESTFALEN: Düsseldorf, Germany
ID: kunstverein-für-die-rheinlande-und-westfalen-düsseldorf
Website: https://kunstverein-duesseldorf.de

OVERVIEW
- Members' art association on the second floor of the Kunsthalle Düsseldorf at Grabbeplatz 4, near the Kunstakademie and the Kunstsammlung NRW (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1829 and one of the oldest institutions of its kind in Germany. It first met in Düsseldorf Castle (stated by the association). Wikipedia dates the founding statute to 23 January 1829.
- The Kunstverein has shared the Kunsthalle building since 1967. The brutalist concrete building was designed by Konrad Beckmann; artists and residents campaigned successfully against its proposed demolition in the late 1990s (stated by the association).
- The exhibition hall measures 360 square metres on the upper floor. The programme combines solo shows with thematic group exhibitions, talks, lectures, tours, screenings and publications, and the association has offered studio and travel grants to Kunstakademie students for over 20 years (stated by the association).
- The association says it has over 2,000 members; Wikipedia reports more than 2,500 in early 2022.
- Gloria Hasnay is director. The Vorstand is Georg Kulenkampff (chairman), Lilli von Bodman, Prof. Donatella Fioretti, Prof. Dr. Markus Heinzelmann, Katrin Mayer, René Tilgier, Nicola Treyde and Renate Ulrich, with an advisory board of 24 people.
- Wikipedia states that the association holds a 25.1 percent stake in the non-profit company that runs the Kunsthalle.
- The City of Düsseldorf has postponed the renovation of the building, planned from summer 2026, by three further years, according to a City of Düsseldorf press release of 28 January 2026 quoted by the association. The association's planned move to a temporary venue at Kapuzinergasse 24 is on hold.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday and public holidays 11:00 to 18:00, Thursday to 21:00. Admission is 7 euros, reduced 4 euros; free for under 18s, members, people with disabilities and their companions. On the last Thursday of each month from 18:00 to 21:00 Stadtwerke Düsseldorf offers free visits to the Kunstverein and the Kunsthalle. The exhibition floor has no steps and a lift at the back serves it with staff help; the building is not fully accessible.

FUNDING
Kunstverein für die Rheinlande und Westfalen: members' association with city and company support: City of Düsseldorf, de Haen-Carstanjen & Söhne and Stadtwerke Düsseldorf
- The sponsors page names Landeshauptstadt Düsseldorf and de Haen-Carstanjen & Söhne as supporters, and Stadtwerke Düsseldorf as permanent partner.
- The sponsors page lists Stadtwerke Düsseldorf and Neustart Kultur under pandemic-related infrastructure investments in 2020 and 2021. Neustart Kultur was a funding programme of the Federal Government Commissioner for Culture and the Media (Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia states that Stadtwerke Düsseldorf had sales of 3,058 million euros in 2023, that EnBW holds a 54.95 percent majority after the 2005 sale and that the City of Düsseldorf holds just over 25 percent. Its 2022 electricity supply labelling gives renewables 61.8 percent, nuclear 3.7 percent and fossil fuels and other sources 32.6 percent (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The association is a members' association supported by the City of Düsseldorf, the company de Haen-Carstanjen & Söhne and the utility Stadtwerke Düsseldorf, with Stadtwerke Düsseldorf and Neustart Kultur listed for pandemic-related infrastructure investments in 2020 and 2021. Stadtwerke Düsseldorf, majority-owned by EnBW with the City of Düsseldorf holding just over 25 percent, is the permanent partner and offers a monthly free evening.
