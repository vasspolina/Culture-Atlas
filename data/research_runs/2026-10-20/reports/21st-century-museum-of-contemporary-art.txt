21ST CENTURY MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART – Kanazawa, Japan
Website: https://www.kanazawa21.jp (not checked: the fetch was blocked on 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal contemporary art museum that opened on 9 October 2004. The circular building, 112.5 m in diameter, is by SANAA (Kazuyo Sejima and Ryue Nishizawa). Permanent works include Leandro Erlich's Swimming Pool (Wikipedia).
- Entry to the museum is free, and exhibitions are charged separately. The exhibition zone is open 10:00–18:00 (until 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays) and closed on Mondays (museum.or.jp). No accessibility information was found.
- Visitors: about 1.57M in the first year, and 971,256 in 2020, when it was still among the world's ten most-visited art museums (Wikipedia). A city plan gives a FY2023 figure of 1,974,773 for its designated facilities, but the table layout makes the match uncertain.
- The museum will close for repairs from 6 May 2027 to March 2028, including damage to glass panels from the 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake. It will operate temporarily from the former Bank of Japan Kanazawa branch (Kumanichi).

FUNDING
21st Century Museum: mainly public (City of Kanazawa)
- It is run by the 公益財団法人金沢芸術創造財団 (Kanazawa Arts Creation Foundation) as designated manager (指定管理者). The foundation took that name in 2003 when it took over the museum, and the city provided 100% of its JPY 40M endowment (city reform plan 2024–2028, stated by the city).
- Fee income of about JPY 226–254M is listed in the plan; the year it applies to is unclear.
