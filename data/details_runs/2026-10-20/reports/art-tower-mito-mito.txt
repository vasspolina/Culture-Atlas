ART TOWER MITO: Mito, Japan
ID: art-tower-mito-mito
Website: https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- City-owned arts complex at 1-6-8 Gokencho, Mito, Ibaraki, about 10 minutes by bus from the north exit of Mito Station plus a two-minute walk (Art Tower Mito).
- Opened in March 1990 for the centenary of Mito's city status (Wikipedia); Mayor Kazunobu Sagawa proposed it and the rule that 1% of the city budget pays for its running costs (Ibaraki Prefectural Library volunteers' newsletter, 2020).
- Arata Isozaki designed it, with acoustics by Nagata Acoustics; its 100-metre titanium-clad tower follows a Boerdijk-Coxeter helix; the complex cost ¥9,711 million (Wikipedia).
- It holds a 680-seat concert hall, a 636-seat theatre and a contemporary art gallery; the Mito Chamber Orchestra is resident. Directors have been critic Yoshida Hidekazu (吉田秀和), conductor Seiji Ozawa and now Katayama Morihide (片山杜秀).
- The Mito City Arts Promotion Foundation, set up on 31 March 1988 and a public interest foundation since April 2012, runs it; its chair is Fukuda (福田三千男).
- Open 9:30 to 18:00, gallery 10:00 to 18:00, closed Mondays (Tuesday after a holiday) and at New Year; gallery entry ¥900; free ground parking for wheelchair users, wheelchair and pushchair loans.

FUNDING
Art Tower Mito: mainly public: Mito City subsidies and commissions, plus local company supporters
- Mito City endowed the foundation with its full ¥100 million fund (Art Tower Mito).
- In the year to March 2025 ordinary revenue was ¥1,070.2 million: subsidies ¥592.6 million and commissioned income ¥254.8 million (together 79%), admissions ¥104.9 million, grants ¥45.6 million, donations ¥32.8 million (foundation accounts).
- In 2015 the annual budget was about ¥1.03 billion: about ¥1 billion from the 1% city share plus about ¥25 million in donations (Ibaraki Prefectural Library newsletter).
- The site lists 22 supporters, undated, including Suntory Holdings, NEC, ANA, Joyo Bank, Mito Shinkin Bank, Mito Securities, Ibaraki Toyopet, the Genden Fureai Ibaraki Foundation and Yoshida Sekiyu (Art Tower Mito).
- No reporting found of what each supporter gives.

Red flags: petroleum retailer Yoshida Sekiyu on the supporters list
- Yoshida Sekiyu (Yoshida Oil Co.) is one of 22 listed supporters (Art Tower Mito, checked October 2026).
- It runs ENEOS fuel stations around Mito, Hitachi and Naka-Minato, recruiting staff for them in October 2026 (Mynavi Baito).
- No reporting found of the amount it gives or of any protest about it.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Art Tower Mito is overwhelmingly the city's own, set by a 1% budget rule since 1990. The one flagged name is a local fuel retailer among 22 small supporters, with no amount published. Worth watching: the supporters' contributions, which the accounts report only as a donation total.
