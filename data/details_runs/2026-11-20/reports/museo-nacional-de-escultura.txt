MUSEO NACIONAL DE ESCULTURA: Valladolid, Spain
ID: museo-nacional-de-escultura
Website: https://www.cultura.gob.es/mnescultura/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- State museum of Spanish sculpture on three sites in Valladolid: the Colegio de San Gregorio, the Palacio de Villena and the Casa del Sol with its annex, the church of San Benito el Viejo (museum website).
- It grew out of the provincial fine arts museum set up in 1842 in the Colegio de Santa Cruz with art taken from convents after Mendizábal's 1836 nationalisation of church property (museum website).
- The Second Republic made it a national museum in 1933 under its present name and moved it to the Colegio de San Gregorio (museum website).
- The Palacio de Villena was rehabilitated by the architect F. Rodríguez Partearroyo, finished in 1998; San Gregorio was restored by Nieto y Sobejano, finished in 2009, and the project won the National Prize for Conservation and Restoration of Cultural Property in 2007 (museum website).
- San Gregorio shows polychrome wood sculpture of the 15th to 18th centuries; since 2011 the Casa del Sol holds the copies from the former Museo Nacional de Reproducciones Artísticas, which doubled the holdings from 3,000 to 6,000 works (museum website).
- The Palacio de Villena houses temporary exhibitions, the library, the archive, the photography and restoration workshops and an 18th-century Neapolitan nativity scene (museum website).
- Current shows: the counts of Gondomar (20 October 2026 to 14 February 2027, free), "Operación Altarpiece" on the fight against illicit trafficking in cultural goods (from 2 July 2026) and Los Ribalta, on loan from the Museo de Bellas Artes de Valencia (5 November 2026 to 7 February 2027) (museum website).
- The museum had 180,226 visitors in 2025, 5.56% more than in 2024 and its fifth year of growth in a row (Ministry of Culture, 2 January 2026).
- Director: Alejandro Nuevo Gómez, a state museum curator appointed to the post by a Ministry of Culture resolution of 18 April 2022 (BOE, 26 April 2022) and named as director on the museum website.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 14:00 and 16:00 to 19:30, Sundays and holidays 10:00 to 14:00, closed Mondays. Admission 3 euros, reduced 1.50 euros, annual card 25 euros; free on Saturday afternoons, Sunday mornings and for pensioners, the unemployed, under-18s and over-65s (museum website).

FUNDING
Museo Nacional de Escultura: state-funded: Spain's Ministry of Culture
- Owner and operator: one of the State Museums run directly by the Ministry of Culture through the Directorate General of Cultural Heritage and Fine Arts (Ministry of Culture, 2 January 2026; museum website).
- Board: a Patronato of a president, seven ex officio members and up to nine members named by the Ministry, which promotes the Ministry's guidelines and society's support for the museum (museum website).
- Acquisitions: the Ministry bought Luisa Roldán's terracotta group "La Virgen aprendiendo a leer" for this museum for 275,000 euros by right of pre-emption in December 2025 (Infobae, citing Europa Press).
- Exhibitions: the Directorate General awarded production and installation of the Gondomar show to GrupoLa Labad & Arteaga SL for 52,269.69 euros (BOE, 28 August 2026).
- Friends: the Fundación de Amigos del Museo Nacional de Escultura, a private initiative of citizens and companies founded in 1980, buys and donates works, pays for restorations and seeks sponsors; it reports more than 739 members (museum website; friends' website).
- Sponsorship: the museum invites corporate patrons and points to the 40% corporate tax deduction under Law 49/2002 (museum website).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's money is clean: it is Spanish state money, with private support limited to its friends' foundation and corporate patrons. No red flag is on record.
