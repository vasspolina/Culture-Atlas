PENNSYLVANIA ACADEMY OF THE FINE ARTS: Philadelphia, USA
ID: pennsylvania-academy-of-the-fine-arts
Website: https://www.pafa.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of American art and art school at 118-128 North Broad Street in Philadelphia, known as PAFA.
- Founded in 1805, with a charter dated 26 December 1805, by the painter Charles Willson Peale, the sculptor William Rush and other artists and business leaders.
- Its main building, by Frank Furness and George W. Hewitt, opened in 1876.
- PAFA calls itself the first school and museum of fine arts in the United States (stated by the space).
- Visiting: Thursday, Saturday, Sunday and Monday 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Friday to 8:00 PM, closed Tuesday and Wednesday; adults USD 25, students USD 10, children 12 and under free.

FUNDING
Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts: privately funded: donors, programme fees and endowment income
- Fiscal year to June 2025: revenue $17,818,875 and expenses $16,136,591; contributions $9,703,429, programme service revenue $3,285,202, investment income $2,357,415; net assets $94,708,032 (Form 990 data, ProPublica).
- The Mellon Foundation gave $1,000,000 in 2024 to support the reinstallation of the permanent collection.
- In July 2024 PAFA had about $5.5 million toward a $10 million replacement of the 1876 building's HVAC system and proposed a $5 million request to Pennsylvania's redevelopment capital assistance programme (Philadelphia Inquirer, on PAFA's news page).

Red flags: petition against the end of the degree programmes
- On 9 January 2024 the board voted unanimously to end the BFA and MFA programmes after the 2024 to 2025 academic year, citing "rising costs, expanding requirements and dwindling enrollment" (WHYY). President Eric Pryor announced it.
- 37 first-year and sophomore students had to transfer; some staff lost their roles after June 2024.
- A Change.org petition started by Kelsey Gavin on 11 January 2024, "Justice for PAFA Faculty, Staff, and Students", drew 611 signatures. It called the decision unethical and asked for an audit and for the administration and board to answer for admitting students that autumn.
- Unverified: the college president rejected funding offers that would have saved the degree programmes, from the petition, an advocacy source.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Coverage of the closure is news reporting, not criticism of the museum's programme.

URL: https://hyperallergic.com/866079/pafa-historic-philadelphia-art-school-axes-degree-programs/
- Reports that PAFA would stop offering undergraduate and graduate degrees after the 2024 to 2025 school year.
- States that the school was running a $3 million deficit.
- Names PAFA; a news report, not a review.
- Paywalled; only excerpts were read.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind PAFA is private giving, fees and investment income, with no funder objected to. The weak point is governance: the board ended degree teaching at short notice in 2024 and students petitioned against it. Worth watching: the state capital request and whether the certificate programmes replace the lost tuition.
