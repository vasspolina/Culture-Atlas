ID: casino-luxembourg

WIDER CONTEXT
- Sanja Iveković's "Lady Rosa of Luxembourg" was installed near the Place de la Constitution in spring 2001 as part of the Casino Luxembourg exhibition "Luxembourg, les Luxembourgeois" (luxembourg.public.lu).
- Thirty patriotic and Resistance associations joined opponents of the work, collected 5,000 signatures and protested what they called a "misrepresentation" of their monument; some called for the Minister for Culture to resign (luxembourg.public.lu).
- The page states that the artist wanted to draw attention to the problems faced by women in war, crises and conflict (luxembourg.public.lu).
