ID: rubell-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2015 Senator Orrin Hatch, chairman of the US Senate Finance Committee, wrote to 11 US museums, including the Rubell Family Collection in Wynwood, asking for information on attendance, opening hours, trustees and grant-making; his letter expressed concerns that a few institutions "offer minimal benefit to the public". A spokeswoman for the Rubell Family Collection said the Rubells were unavailable for comment (The Art Newspaper, Javier Pes and Julia Halperin, 5 December 2015).
