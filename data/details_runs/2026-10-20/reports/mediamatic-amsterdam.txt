MEDIAMATIC: Amsterdam, Netherlands
ID: mediamatic-amsterdam
Website: https://www.mediamatic.net

OVERVIEW
- Art and science foundation at Mediamatic Biotoop, Dijksgracht 6, 1019 BS Amsterdam, in Dijkspark within walking distance of Central Station (Mediamatic).
- Founded in 1983 in Groningen and moved to Amsterdam in 1986; in 1983 its focus was media technology (Mediamatic history page). Mediamatic.net went online in 1994 (Mediamatic history page).
- Founders Willem Velthoven and Jans Possel stepped back from their active roles in 2024 after four decades; Marcel Sturing is the new general director, and Velthoven stays involved in 2025 as his coach (annual report 2024, translated).
- Mediamatic published a paper magazine from 1985 to 1999 (Mediamatic).
- Programme in 2024: 4 new exhibitions and installations, 120 workshop sessions, 14 events, 37 tours, 7 residencies and 58 publications (annual report 2024, translated). The venue is also rented out for events.
- The waterside restaurant Mediamatic ETEN closed on 1 July 2024; its kitchen is now used for TestTafel, a project with De Sering (annual report 2024, translated).
- Since 2025 it has been part of the Zoöp network and uses the Zoöp organisational model. It is an ANBI (Dutch charity status) with a Raad van Toezicht, a board of supervisors (Mediamatic).

FUNDING
Mediamatic: subsidy-funded: Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie and Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst (AFK), with earned income of 28% in 2024
- Mediamatic's about page lists the Mondriaan Fonds, the Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie and the Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst as its sponsors (Mediamatic).
- 2024 accounts: Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie €474,660 and AFK/Gemeente Amsterdam €215,592, within €699,207 of subsidies and contributions out of €972,864 total income (annual report 2024, translated).
- Stimuleringsfonds: in July 2024 it rejected Mediamatic's application for four-year institutional funding of €1,800,000 after years of structural support. Mediamatic then won a project grant of €250,000 for two years (€125,000 a year for 2025 and 2026) and transitional funds of €164,403 from the same fund (annual report 2024, translated).
- AFK: four years of support (Kunstenplan 2025 to 2028) at €215,000 per year, €215,208 in the accounts (annual report 2024, translated).
- Earned income fell from 46% to 28% of income in 2024; the 2025 budget is half the size of 2024's actual figures, and many staff left (annual report 2024, translated).
- 2024 supervisory board: Hajo Doorn (chair, paid €1,750) and Farid Tabarki (member, paid €1,400) (annual report 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
Mediamatic is a non-profit foundation funded mainly by Dutch public arts funds: the Stimuleringsfonds, the AFK and the Amsterdam municipality, and its about page also lists the Mondriaan Fonds. After the Stimuleringsfonds rejected its €1.8 million four-year bid in 2024, it runs on a €250,000 two-year grant, €215,000 a year from the AFK and earned income of 28% in 2024.
