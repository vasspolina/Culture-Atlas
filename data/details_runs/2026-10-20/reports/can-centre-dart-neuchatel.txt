CAN CENTRE D'ART NEUCHÂTEL: Neuchâtel, Switzerland
ID: can-centre-dart-neuchatel
Website: https://can.ch

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Rue des Moulins 37 in the pedestrian zone of Neuchâtel's historic centre, 10 to 15 minutes on foot from the train station (CAN).
- Founded in 1995 under the impulse of Jean-Pierre Huguet, Joël von Allmen, Marc-Olivier Wahler, Ivo Zanetti, Jérôme Brandt and Sven de Coulon (CAN).
- Huguet directed the centre until his death at the end of 2006; Wahler handled artistic direction until 2000. A new team and a new association took charge in 2008, and in 2018 the director post was removed in favour of collective management (CAN).
- Run by the non-profit association of the same name: a six-person office, a committee of artists and art historians chaired by Kyung Roh Bannwart, and about one hundred ordinary members (CAN).
- On site: exhibitions, a shop for publications and editions, and an archive browsable by artist, date or project. The centre says it has held more than 500 events with more than 1,500 artists.
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 18:00 during exhibition periods; admission free. The reception and main spaces are reached by lift or stairs; the lift and toilets fit a wheelchair user but do not meet official standards (CAN).

FUNDING
CAN Centre d'art Neuchâtel: City and Canton of Neuchâtel, Loterie Romande and private foundations, with one-off project support
- Annual public support, as listed by CAN: Ville de Neuchâtel and République et Canton de Neuchâtel.
- Annual institutional and foundation support, as listed by CAN: Loterie Romande, Fondation Philanthropique Famille Sandoz and Fondation Ernst & Olga Gubler-Hablützel.
- One-off public support listed by CAN: Fonds cantonal d'art contemporain (Geneva), Ville de Genève, Canton de Vaud, Swisslos Culture Canton de Berne, Kultur Stadt Bern, Kanton Basel-Stadt, Kanton Zürich and Etat du Valais.
- One-off institutional and foundation support listed by CAN: Ambassade de France en Suisse, Pro Helvetia, Ernst Göhner Stiftung, Fondation Erna et Curt Burgauer, Fondation Dr. Georg et Josi Guggenheim, Fondation Bonhôte pour l'art contemporain, Artlink, Fondation Casino de Neuchâtel and the Mondriaan Fund.
- Fondation Artkunst Neuchâtel, founded in 2012, supports the operating costs of CAN, excluding exhibitions and events. Nicolas Raufaste sits on that foundation's board and is also a member of CAN's office (Fundraiso; CAN).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind CAN is annual funding from the City and Canton of Neuchâtel, Loterie Romande, Fondation Philanthropique Famille Sandoz and Fondation Ernst & Olga Gubler-Hablützel, plus one-off support from other Swiss cantons and cities, Pro Helvetia, Fondation Casino de Neuchâtel and further foundations, all as listed on CAN's support page.
