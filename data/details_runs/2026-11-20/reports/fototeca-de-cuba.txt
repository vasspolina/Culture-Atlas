FOTOTECA DE CUBA: Havana, Cuba
ID: fototeca-de-cuba
Website: http://www.cnap.cult.cu/ (checked 2026-11-20; down)

OVERVIEW
- Photo archive, museum and gallery in an 18th-century two-storey building at Mercaderes 307 on Plaza Vieja in Old Havana (Cuba Business Report).
- Opened on 16 November 1986 to keep, study and promote the country's photographic heritage and to show international photography (EcuRed; CNAP).
- It holds the national photographic archive, transferred from the Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes (Cuba Business Report). English Wikipedia puts the collection at more than 700,000 photographs; EcuRed says the collection is being digitised and catalogued.
- Two galleries: María Eugenia Haya upstairs for established photographers, and Joaquín Blez on the ground floor for emerging ones. EcuRed says it holds about 16 exhibitions a year at its own address.
- Runs the Coloquio de Fotografía from 2006 and publishes the annual Revista de Fotografía Cubana FC from 2005 (EcuRed).
- Organises Noviembre Fotográfico, a month of photography across Havana. EcuRed dates it from 2008, and OnCuba (2012) says the Fototeca and CNAP have convened it every year since 2008. Prensa Latina called the 2025 edition the 19th; yearly editions from 2008 would make 2025 the 18th.
- The 2025 edition ran in November under Fototeca director Lissette Solórzano, with 128 Cuban and 74 foreign photographers confirmed; Brazil and the province of Matanzas were the guests (Prensa Latina).
- Visiting: Artcrónica's directory lists Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00 (the listing is undated).

FUNDING
Fototeca de Cuba: mainly public: Ministry of Culture through CNAP
- EcuRed says it answers to the Consejo Nacional de las Artes Plásticas (CNAP). CNAP, a Ministry of Culture body, lists the Fototeca among its institutions and Noviembre Fotográfico among its main events (archive copy of 2023-12-08).
- English Wikipedia says it was created in 1986 as a division of the Cuban State Cultural Heritage Fund.
- It gives two grants to photographers living in Cuba: a creation grant for a body of photographic work and a research grant for a study of photography (EcuRed). In November 2025 the María Eugenia Haya research grant went to Lucy Gmorell and a young talent prize to Lisandra Álvarez (Prensa Latina).
- The 2025 jury was the Fototeca's director and chief specialist with staff of two other CNAP centres, the Centro de Desarrollo de las Artes Visuales and the Centro de Arte Contemporáneo Wifredo Lam (Prensa Latina).
- Partner venues carried parts of the 2025 festival: Habana Espacios Creativos hosted the first central show, Factoría Habana a show on Pierre Verger, and the Ludwig Foundation a colloquium for the centenary of Raúl Corrales. The edition marked 80 years of the United Nations and of Cuba's diplomatic relations with Canada (Prensa Latina).

BOTTOM LINE
The Fototeca is a Cuban state body paid through CNAP and the Ministry of Culture, and its festival jury is drawn from CNAP's own centres. The sources give different festival counts: a 2008 start in EcuRed and OnCuba, and a 19th edition in 2025 in Prensa Latina. Worth watching: who pays for Noviembre Fotográfico's foreign guests and for the grants.
