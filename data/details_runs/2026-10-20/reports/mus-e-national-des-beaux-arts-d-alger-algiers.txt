MUSÉE NATIONAL DES BEAUX-ARTS D'ALGER: Algiers, Algeria
ID: mus-e-national-des-beaux-arts-d-alger-algiers
Website: http://www.musee-beauxarts.dz

OVERVIEW
- National museum of fine arts in the Hamma district of Algiers, opposite the Jardin d'Essai, with about 8,000 works (Wikipedia, French Wikipedia).
- It grew out of Algiers's municipal collections: a municipal museum opened in 1908 and closed in 1928, and a national museum of fine arts took over its collection. It was inaugurated on 5 May 1930 and opened to the public in April 1931, tied to the 1930 centenary of French presence in Algeria (modernism.ro, Lucian Muntean, 22 December 2024; Wikipedia).
- Building: designed by architect Paul Guion, with work beginning in 1928 (Wikipedia). Funds from the 1930 centenary paid for 498 works in two years (Wikipedia).
- Collection: paintings, prints, about 800 sculptures, about 2,000 numismatic pieces and decorative arts (French Wikipedia, Wikipedia). It includes the 1982 Mohamed Racim bequest of 61 works (French Wikipedia).
- On 14 May 1962 more than 300 works were moved to the Louvre (modernism.ro); they were repatriated to Algeria by 1970 (Wikipedia). In November 1961, OAS commandos bombed a Bourdelle statue in the courtyard (Wikipedia).
- Directors have included Jean Alazard (1930 to 1960), Jean de Maisonseul (1962 to 1975) and Malika Dorbani Bouabdellah (until 1994) (French Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Musée National des Beaux-Arts d'Alger: national museum under Algerian state control
- Artessere describes it as a national institution that the 1962 Evian accords left under the control of the Algerian State.
- Gifts: Sonatrach handed over 174 canvases by Juanita Guccione in 2012, and BNP Paribas gave two Étienne Dinet paintings in 2012 (French Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a national institution under Algerian state control. The documented private involvement is two gifts of artworks in 2012, from Sonatrach and BNP Paribas.
