CENTRO CULTURAL DE LA REPÚBLICA EL CABILDO: Asunción, Paraguay
ID: centro-cultural-de-la-republica-el-cabildo
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Cultural centre of the Paraguayan National Congress in the former Cabildo (town council) building on Avenida de la República, between Río Manduvirá and Río Tebicuary, Asunción (Última Hora, 2021).
- Opened on 14 May 2004 with Chilean president Ricardo Lagos and the writer Augusto Roa Bastos present; ABC Color says Miguel Carrizosa then presided over Congress and that Carlos Mateo Balmelli, from the presidency of Congress, had the idea and carried it out with director general Margarita Morselli (ABC Color, 2019). English Wikipedia gives 14 March 2004.
- Morselli led it for 20 years; Senate president Basilio Núñez appointed the journalist and political analyst Aníbal Saucedo Rodas in her place in July 2024 (La Nación; Última Hora, 2024).
- Building: refitted and given its current design under Carlos Antonio López, with the Spanish architect Pascual Urdapilleta; it housed the executive until 15 November 1894 and later Congress (Spanish Wikipedia).
- On site: the Sala del Barro, Sala de Arte Sacro, museum rooms for the Cabildo, music and film, and a permanent advisory council that includes members of the International Association of Art Critics (Spanish Wikipedia). Its collection runs from mid-20th-century to current Paraguayan art, including Olga Blinder, Carlos Colombino and Livio Abramo (Última Hora, 2021).
- It also runs the Casas Bicentenario for visual arts, literature, music, dance and theatre (La Nación, 2024).
- A fire in December 2020 destroyed the Congress Symphony Orchestra's rehearsal hall and offices in the newer block; the heritage building and museum rooms were spared (Última Hora, 2020). It reopened to the public in May 2023 (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Visiting: all activities and spaces of El Cabildo and its Casas Bicentenario are free (La Nación, 2025).

FUNDING
Centro Cultural de la República El Cabildo: public: the Paraguayan National Congress's budget
- It depends on the National Congress (Última Hora, 2021). Saucedo Rodas said in July 2024 that the budget it administers is 31,000 million guaraníes, shared with the Congress's Central Library and Archive; Núñez called the budget the main problem (La Nación, 12 July 2024).
- Decree 4309 of 28 July 2025 raised Congress's budget by G. 4,280 million, of which G. 1,880 million is for repairing and extending El Cabildo (La Nación, 30 July 2025; Última Hora, 30 July 2025). Última Hora says the funds come from an external loan; La Nación describes a budget expansion and does not mention a loan.
- The G. 1,880 million pays for a two-storey block beside the Casa Bicentenario de la Música Agustín Pío Barrios, with a rehearsal room for the Congress orchestra and an exhibition room and store for the collection, now kept partly in a Central Bank vault (La Nación, 1 August 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism, and none in art magazines; three Última Hora pieces criticise the centre's cultural policy, its new director's appointment and a Congress spending decree that includes it.
1. Última Hora: unsigned, "Artistas piden a Lugo la destitución de Margarita Morselli" (2017)
URL: https://www.ultimahora.com/artistas-piden-lugo-destitucion-margarita-morselli-n1104822
- A manifesto signed by 300 artists and cultural activists asked Congress president Fernando Lugo to remove Morselli; the artist Ricardo de la Vega spoke for the group.
- They wanted a popular cultural policy reaching the whole population, not a select group in Asunción, and said neighbourhoods such as the Chacarita had been sidelined.
- They called hiring the foreign group Il Divo for the 2011 bicentennial a very serious mistake.
2. Última Hora: unsigned, "Bachi designa como director del Cabildo a Aníbal Saucedo Rodas" (2024)
URL: https://www.ultimahora.com/bachi-designa-como-director-del-cabildo-a-anibal-saucedo-rodas
- Notes that Saucedo Rodas sat on the Colorado Party's governing board, served twice as an Itaipú councillor, under Nicanor Duarte Frutos and Horacio Cartes, and was put forward by former senator Juan Carlos Galaverna.
- Says his daughter was dismissed from the Yacyretá binational entity after working 56 days in six months, then reinstated, which it reads as the party's use of public jobs for political ends.
3. Última Hora: Redacción ÚH, "Congreso tiene luz verde para gastar en construir un obelisco" (2025)
URL: https://www.ultimahora.com/congreso-tiene-luz-verde-para-gastar-en-construir-un-obelisco
- Calls Decree 4309 "un despilfarro impropio de un país en crisis" (translation: a waste unfit for a country in crisis), and says Congress presidents acquired buildings to place political operators, "utilizando incluso como pantalla el arte y la cultura" (translation: even using art and culture as a screen).
- The criticism targets Congress, not El Cabildo's programme.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Última Hora says Congress holds seven other premises besides its main building, worth over G. 118,880 million by the Senate's own inventory, and recalls criticism of an officials' car park and a "quincho de oro" in its courtyard.
- El Cabildo's extension is paid from the decree that article criticises.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money with no private or flagged funder, but the centre sits inside a Congress whose spending and appointments the press attacks. Worth watching: the new block's construction and whether a loan pays for it.
