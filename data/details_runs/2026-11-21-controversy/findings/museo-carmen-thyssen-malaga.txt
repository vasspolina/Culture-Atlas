ID: museo-carmen-thyssen-malaga

WIDER CONTEXT
- In April 2011, about three weeks after the museum opened, the board member Tomás Llorens resigned. His resignation letter said he was leaving because Javier Ferrer, until a month earlier General Coordinator at Málaga City Hall, had been named manager without a selection process and with no museum management experience (Euro Weekly News, 14 April 2011).
- Baroness Thyssen replied that Llorens wanted to "manage the museum as he pleased" and that she would not allow it (Euro Weekly News, 14 April 2011).
- El Salto later quoted his reason as the museum having "dejado de tener el grado de credibilidad histórico-artística que debería esperarse de un museo de su naturaleza" ("stopped having the degree of art-historical credibility to be expected of a museum of its kind", translated) (El Salto, Jose Durán Rodríguez, 27 September 2018).
