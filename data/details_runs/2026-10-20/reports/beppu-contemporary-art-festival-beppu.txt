BEPPU CONTEMPORARY ART FESTIVAL: Beppu, Japan
ID: beppu-contemporary-art-festival-beppu
Website: https://www.beppuproject.com (checked 2026-10-20; the organiser's site, the festival has no site of its own)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art festival, "Mixed Bathing World" (Konyoku Onsen Sekai), held at sites across the hot-spring city of Beppu, Oita Prefecture.
- Organised by the festival's executive committee with NPO BEPPU PROJECT running the office; BEPPU PROJECT formed in April 2005 around the artist Junya Yamaide and became an NPO in 2006 (artscape).
- Three editions: 11 April to 14 June 2009 (about 91,000 visitors), 6 October to 2 December 2012 (117,348 visitors) and 18 July to 27 September 2015 (53,825 visitors) (P3).
- Takashi Serizawa of P3 was general director in 2009 and 2012 (P3); Yamaide was executive producer of the 2012 edition (ASEF culture360).
- The 2012 edition had eight projects in visual arts and dance, with Christian Marclay, Ann Veronica Janssens, Shilpa Gupta and Qiu Zhijie (ASEF culture360).
- The triennial series ended with the 2015 edition; from 2016 BEPPU PROJECT ran "in BEPPU", a solo-artist festival (artscape). Its successor, ALTERNATIVE-STATE, run by the executive committee, invites one artist group every half-year to make eight works over four years (Bijutsu Techo; BEPPU PROJECT).
- Visiting: no festival edition is running; ALTERNATIVE-STATE works are installed in the city, such as space II by the art team me, unveiled on 23 December 2025 in the Tetsuwan area.

FUNDING
Beppu Contemporary Art Festival: public grants and backing with local corporate sponsors: Agency for Cultural Affairs, Beppu City and 28 sponsors
- 2009: Beppu City was a named organiser alongside the executive committee (P3).
- 2015: the Japan Foundation Asia Center was co-organiser (P3).
- 2025: the executive committee's ALTERNATIVE-STATE project names the Agency for Cultural Affairs as grant-giver (助成) and 28 sponsors (協賛), including ANA, Oita Bank, JR Kyushu Building Management, Tokiha department store, Umi Jigoku, Oita Gas and Oita Mirai Shinkin Bank; Beppu City and the Oita education boards are backers (BEPPU PROJECT, no amounts).
- BEPPU PROJECT had 22 staff and turnover of about 300 million yen in 2018 (Wantedly, 2020).
- Corporate membership of BEPPU PROJECT costs 50,000 yen a unit a year (stated by the space).
- The festival reports for 2012 to 2014 are posted as PDFs; their text could not be extracted, so their income lines are not checked here.

Red flags: fossil fuels (Oita Gas)
- Oita Gas (Oita Gas Co., Ltd.) is a sponsor of the executive committee's 2025 ALTERNATIVE-STATE work (BEPPU PROJECT, November 2025).
- Oita Gas, founded in 1911 and based in Beppu, makes city gas and supplies it by pipeline to Oita, Beppu and Yufu; sales were 21.3 billion yen in the year to March 2026 (Mynavi company profile).
- The company finished converting its supply to natural gas in 2005 (Mynavi; ja.wikid.org).
- No reporting found of a protest, open letter or boycott aimed at the festival, BEPPU PROJECT or its sponsors.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found, such as Makoto Hashimoto's 2012 report in Tokyo Art Beat (the page carries AD markers) and Judith Staines's 2013 piece on ASEF culture360, is admiring.

BOTTOM LINE
The festival runs on state, city and local business money, with BEPPU PROJECT as the operator. The one weak point is Oita Gas, a natural-gas utility, among the current sponsors of the committee's work. Worth watching: whether the posted festival accounts show the size of the corporate share.
