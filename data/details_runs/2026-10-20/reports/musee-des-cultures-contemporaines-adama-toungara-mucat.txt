MUSÉE DES CULTURES CONTEMPORAINES ADAMA TOUNGARA (MuCAT): Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire
ID: musee-des-cultures-contemporaines-adama-toungara-mucat
Website: https://mucat.net

OVERVIEW
- Museum of contemporary art in the Abobo commune of Abidjan, on 3,500 m² opposite the Abobo town hall (Connectionivoirienne, 11 March 2020).
- Named for Adama Toungara, identified in 2020 as the Grand Médiateur de la République and one of the country's largest art collectors. Inaugurated on 11 March 2020 by First Lady Dominique Ouattara (Connectionivoirienne, 11 March 2020).
- Built through the Adama Toungara Foundation (Connectionivoirienne, 29 February 2020). Connectionivoirienne calls it the first building in Côte d'Ivoire dedicated to contemporary culture; Fraternité Matin (30 June 2021) calls it the first museum entirely dedicated to contemporary art.
- Designed by architect Issa Diabaté (Connectionivoirienne, 11 March 2020).
- Facilities: a 400 m² main exhibition hall, a 120 m² small hall, a performance space, a shop, a library and media library, storage and archive rooms, workshops and parking (Connectionivoirienne, 11 March 2020).
- Opened with the pan-African travelling show "Prête-moi ton rêve", initiated by the Fondation pour le Développement de la Culture Contemporaine Africaine: about 50 works by nearly 30 African artists, 11 March to 29 April 2020, after stops in Casablanca (June 2019) and Dakar (December 2019) (Fraternité Matin, 12 March 2020).
- Since 2022 it has hosted the annual Africa Foto Fair, organised by Aida Muluneh (Art Basel, Luc-Roland Kouassi, 27 February 2025).
- Director General in December 2022: Nathalie Varley Meplon; 60 per cent of visitors come from Abobo, she stated (Djasso, Ethmane Issa, 21 December 2022).

FUNDING
Musée des Cultures Contemporaines Adama Toungara: privately funded: the Adama Toungara Foundation
- Construction: Director Nathalie Varley Meplon stated the building was constructed "sur fonds propres", from own funds (Djasso, 21 December 2022).
- Foundation: the museum was realised by the Fondation Adama Toungara, and Toungara's daughter Bintou manages it (Connectionivoirienne, 29 February 2020).
- Not a town hall project: Commissioner general Yacouba Konaté stated that Abobo town hall did not invest in the project and that a partnership links the foundation and the commune. He said the museum is not meant to replenish the foundation's finances and would seek partnerships to operate (Connectionivoirienne, 29 February 2020).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is private: the Adama Toungara Foundation built it, and the director states it was built from own funds. Konaté stated in 2020 that it would seek partners to operate.
