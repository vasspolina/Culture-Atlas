POLA MUSEUM OF ART: Hakone, Japan
ID: pola-museum-of-art-hakone
Website: https://www.polamuseum.or.jp/en/

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 1285 Sengokuhara, Hakone, Kanagawa Prefecture, in Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park (e-flux directory; English Wikipedia).
- Opened in September 2002, run by the Pola Art Foundation (Japanese Wikipedia). The foundation was established in 1996 (Mecenat Association).
- Built to show about 9,500 works collected over decades by Tsuneji Suzuki, second head of the Pola founding family; the collection now numbers about 10,000 (Japanese Wikipedia).
- Designed by Nikken Sekkei and built by Takenaka Corporation (Japanese Wikipedia). Most of the building sits underground; only the glass atrium shows above the forest floor (Alan Gleason, Artscape).
- Collection: French Impressionist and École de Paris painting, Western and Japanese-style paintings, prints, East Asian ceramics, glass and cosmetic tools (English Wikipedia; e-flux directory). A 670-metre nature trail was added in 2013 (English Wikipedia).
- Visiting: the museum's own notice gives hours of 9:00 to 17:00 with last admission at 16:30.

FUNDING
Pola Museum of Art: foundation-run: the Pola Art Foundation, with a collection gathered by Tsuneji Suzuki of the Pola founding family
- The Pola Art Foundation operates the museum and also runs overseas internships for young artists, research programmes for curators and international exchange (Mecenat Association).
- Japanese Wikipedia says the museum opened in September 2002 to display the collection of Tsuneji Suzuki, second head of the Pola founding family; English Wikipedia says the works were acquired by the former head of the Pola cosmetics group.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is operated by the Pola Art Foundation, established in 1996 (Mecenat Association), and displays the collection of Tsuneji Suzuki, second head of the Pola founding family (Japanese Wikipedia). The foundation also runs internships for young artists, programmes for curators and international exchange.
