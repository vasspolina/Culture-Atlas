OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art institution at Lange Voorhout 102 in The Hague's Museum Quarter, near the Binnenhof and about a 10-minute walk from The Hague Central Station (denhaag.com).
- The building is the former United States embassy, designed by the Hungarian-American architect Marcel Breuer and completed in 1959; it is a national monument, and denhaag.com compares its windows and facade to the MET Breuer in New York.
- Its programme pairs exhibitions with lectures, performances and discussions (denhaag.com).
- DutchCulture describes three strands: a Main Space, the Institute for Art and Criticism for debates, publications and courses, and the Volkspaleis for festival events.
- No reporting found on the pages read of when West was founded, by whom, or who directs it.
- Listed shows: "Blood, Guts, and Anarchy - The Work of Kathy Acker", 25 October 2026 to 28 February 2027, and group shows to 26 December 2026; guided tours of the former embassy run 1 November to 31 December (denhaag.com).
- Visiting: denhaag.com lists Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 18:00 and Thursday 12:00 to 21:00. museum.nl lists Monday and Tuesday closed, Thursday to 22:00 and no Sunday hours, so the two listings differ. The Netherlands Museum Pass is valid (museum.nl). denhaag.com lists exhibition prices of 0,00 to 12,50 euros and the guided tour at 12,50 euros. Its Dutch page says a back entrance is opened by a staff member and the lift was out of order, so other exhibition spaces were only partly accessible; the English page says "No accessibility info available".

FUNDING
West Den Haag: public: city, national ministry and national arts infrastructure
- The City of The Hague and the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science give structural support (denhaag.com; the Dutch page says "structureel ondersteund").
- Since 2017 West has been one of two Hague visual art institutions in the national Basic Infrastructure (BIS), with the Mauritshuis (denhaag.com).
- ANNA Vastgoed en Cultuur is named as a partner for catering, workspaces, meeting rooms and guided tours (denhaag.com). It is not described as a funder.
