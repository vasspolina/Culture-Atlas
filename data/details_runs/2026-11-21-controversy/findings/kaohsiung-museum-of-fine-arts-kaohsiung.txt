WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2015 the Kaohsiung City Government informed director Hsieh Pei-ni (謝佩霓) that her term would end on 30 July; arts groups protested the handling as 「粗糙逼退、糟蹋人才」 ("a crude forcing out, a waste of talent", translated) (Storm Media, 18 June 2015).
- In May and June 2016 artists' groups, among them the 中華民國視覺藝術協會 (視盟), petitioned against the city's plan to turn the museum into an administrative corporation, and the artist Yao Jui-chung (姚瑞中) returned his appointment letter as a member of the museum's collection committee in protest (The Reporter, 30 June 2016).
- Culture Bureau director Shih Che (史哲) apologised at a public forum: 「讓外界如此不安，是我的責任。」 ("It is my responsibility that the public is so uneasy", translated). The city council passed the ordinance on 14 June 2016 (The Reporter).
