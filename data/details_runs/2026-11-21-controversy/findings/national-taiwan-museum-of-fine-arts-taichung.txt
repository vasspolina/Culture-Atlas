ID: national-taiwan-museum-of-fine-arts-taichung

WIDER CONTEXT
- In October 2020 Mirror Media reported that a NT$4.4 million furniture purchase for the museum's planned national children's art education centre included a Danish Fritz Hansen sofa set costing over NT$1.2 million for a VIP room, and that legislators had raised the purchase. The museum replied that it bought the imported sofas to respect the design's intellectual property and patents and avoid replica furniture (Mirror Media, 10 October 2020).
- Director Lin Chih-ming (林志明) said "沒有這件事" ("there is no such thing", translated), and on 6 October 2020 the museum said it would take legal action over the magazine's reporting to defend his reputation (Taiwan News, 12 October 2020).
- On 12 October 2020 the Ministry of Culture said minister Lee Yung-te had earlier sternly warned Lin over his family members' conduct and had asked its civil service ethics office (政風處) to investigate whether there were administrative or legal lapses. Lee approved Lin's request to return to National Taipei University of Education at the end of his secondment, with his term ending on 26 October 2020, and required him to cooperate with the inquiry (Taiwan News, 12 October 2020).
