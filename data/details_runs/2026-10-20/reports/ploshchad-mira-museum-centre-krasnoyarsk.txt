PLOSHCHAD MIRA MUSEUM CENTRE: Krasnoyarsk, Russia
ID: ploshchad-mira-museum-centre-krasnoyarsk
Website: https://mira1.ru

OVERVIEW
- Museum and contemporary art centre at Ploshchad Mira 1, on the Yenisei embankment in Krasnoyarsk (Putevka).
- Opened on 17 April 1987 as the 13th branch of the Central V.I. Lenin Museum, the only one in Siberia and the Russian Far East; it became the Krasnoyarsk Cultural-Historical Museum Centre in 1991 and was renamed Ploshchad Mira (Peace Square) in 2017 (Russian Wikipedia).
- The Krasnoyarsk Museum Biennale has run since 1995, and Museum Night has been held twice a year since 2002, the first in Russia (Snob; Russian Wikipedia).
- Designed by Areg Demirkhanov, an honoured architect of the RSFSR, with Alexander Bakusov, Vladimir Korotkov and Vladimir Rivin: a Soviet modernist building of two polyhedra of different sizes joined by a gallery (Russian Wikipedia; Putevka).
- About 5,000 square metres of exhibition space, of which the permanent display takes a third, and 700 metres of the Soviet-era display are kept (Snob). It also has a library created with the Mikhail Prokhorov Foundation and an Educational Centre founded in 1999 (Russian Wikipedia).
- In 2025 the Cosmoscow fair named it Museum of the Year, and the Cosmoscow foundation put money into its collection (Snob).
- Directors: Mikhail Shubsky 1991 to 2016, Maria Bukova 2016 to 2023, Olga Temnikova from 2023 (Russian Wikipedia); Sergey Kovalevsky is artistic director and commissioner of the biennale (Sreda, V-A-C).
- Visiting: Putevka lists open Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 19:00, Thursday 13:00 to 21:00, closed Monday; full ticket 250 roubles, concession 200 roubles; ramps and lifts for visitors with reduced mobility.

FUNDING
Ploshchad Mira Museum Centre: mainly public: the Krasnoyarsk Krai regional government, with earned income, grants and local business
- Founder: the Ministry of Culture of Krasnoyarsk Krai is the single founder of the institution, which is a regional state autonomous cultural institution (Rusprofile, from the state company register).
- Russian Wikipedia states the budget comes mainly from state funds: a targeted subsidy of 40 to 50 million roubles a year as of 2019 to keep the museum running, about 70 to 80 percent of the whole budget. The rest comes from the museum's own earnings, grants and help from local entrepreneurs.
- Cosmoscow: after naming the museum Museum of the Year in 2025, the Cosmoscow foundation invested what Kovalevsky called substantial money in the collection (Snob).
- Prokhorov Foundation: co-created the museum library, and the museum's BookGames programme won the foundation's 2020 competition (Russian Wikipedia).
- Past years: the museum worked closely with consulates and international foundations, mainly to bring internationally known artists to Siberia (Snob).
- Kovalevsky says the museum can provide space for artists but not material support (Sreda, V-A-C).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is regional state funding from the Krasnoyarsk Krai culture ministry, which Russian Wikipedia puts at 70 to 80 percent of the budget in 2019, topped up by earned income, grants and, since 2025, investment by the Cosmoscow foundation in the collection (Snob). The ministry is the single founder of the institution (Rusprofile).
