ID: ballroom-marfa

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2014 Presidio County commissioners agreed to send Ballroom Marfa a "notice of non-compliance" over its land lease at Vizcaino Park for the planned Drive-In. The letter would advise that the lease could be terminated unless commissioners heard more of the gallery's plans for working with community groups; in exchange for essentially free rent, the gallery had agreed to let local community groups use the space (Marfa Public Radio, Travis Bubenik, 11 June 2014).
- Later that day Ballroom issued a statement signed by Virginia Lebermann and Fairfax Dorn saying the board had decided that "the project has outgrown the original vision" and "As of now further development of the Drive-In is indefinitely deferred" (Marfa Public Radio).
