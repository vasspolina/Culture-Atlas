KADIST: Paris, France
ID: kadist-paris
Website: https://kadist.org/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art organisation with its hub and exhibition space at 19bis/21 rue des Trois Frères in Montmartre, Paris 18e, and staff and advisors on five continents (kadist.org).
- The idea came at a family dinner in Montmartre in 2001, when the curator Sandra Terdjman proposed an art project to her uncle Vincent Worms, then a venture capitalist in San Francisco; the Paris gallery opened in 2006 (Mission Local).
- Worms is president and Terdjman vice-president; both are co-founders. Joseph del Pesco is Americas director and Marie Martraire collection director (kadist.org).
- The collection holds over 2,600 works by about 1,400 artists, nominated by a worldwide network of advisors and chosen by a group vote (kadist.org).
- It runs exhibitions, online programmes, residencies and co-productions with museums; its October 2026 to February 2027 season runs shows and screenings in Paris, Shanghai, Dakar, Barcelona and Gennevilliers (e-flux).
- Résidence Tallard, its artists' residency in the Hôtel de Tallard on rue des Archives in the Marais, was converted by the architect Lina Ghotmeh (Lina Ghotmeh). Its autumn 2026 residents were the choreographer nora chipaumire, with Festival d'Automne, and the peacebuilder Martine Kessy Ekomo-Soignet, with the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs (e-flux).
- Its San Francisco space at 3295 20th Street, opened in 2011, closed on 6 September 2025; the organisation said no one would be laid off (Mission Local).
- Visiting Paris: Thursday and Friday 11 to 19, Saturday 15 to 19, or by appointment (kadist.org).

FUNDING
KADIST: private: a French family's money, through a French endowment fund and US foundations
- Its operations are funded privately by a French family; del Pesco said the San Francisco closure had nothing to do with funding (Mission Local).
- It operates through the French endowment fund AKAT KADIST and two US 501(c)(3) entities, Artnow International Inc and KADIST Collection Manager Inc (kadist.org).
- Artnow International, San Francisco, year to December 2024: revenue USD 1,421,960, of which contributions USD 1,417,716; expenses USD 1,458,912; Vincent Worms chairman, unpaid (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Form 990-PF).
- KADIST Collection Manager, Delaware, tax-exempt since August 2023: contributions of USD 44,720,192 in 2023, then total assets of USD 41,643,265 at December 2024. Martraire is president; Worms and Terdjman are directors (ProPublica, Form 990-PF).
- Mission Local found that on KADIST's 2023 tax form direct charitable activities reached USD 18,492,971, against USD 1,329,224 in 2022, which the organisation could not immediately explain.
- Worms comes from venture capital: he was part of the team that founded Partech in San Francisco in 1982 as Paribas Technologies, the venture arm of the French bank Paribas (Partech), and was managing director of Partech International for most of his professional career (AFIELD). In 2001 he founded a family foundation, TSADIK (AFIELD).

BOTTOM LINE
Private family money, with no flagged funder. The weak point is opacity about which family members or companies give the money.
