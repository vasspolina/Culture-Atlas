ID: temple-bar-gallery-studios

WIDER CONTEXT
- The former chief executive, Marian Lovett, took an unfair dismissal claim to the Employment Appeals Tribunal after she was told on 21 June 2010 that her employment was being terminated; her redundancy took effect in August 2010. Temple Bar Gallery and Studios Ltd argued it was entitled to make her redundant after a 35 per cent cut in Arts Council funding, from €372,000 to €240,000, and a restructuring that cut staff from five to three (The Irish Times, Mary Carolan, 29 August 2012).
- The tribunal upheld her claim and awarded her €30,000, finding that the duties of the new studio development officer post were "practically the same" as those of the chief executive. The company denied a "sham redundancy". Lovett called for an investigation into the use of public money at the gallery and for "an objective and independent assessment of management and membership structures" (The Irish Times, Tim O'Brien, 26 October 2012).
