BOISE ART MUSEUM: Boise, USA
ID: boise-art-museum-boise
Website: https://www.boiseartmuseum.org/

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 670 Julia Davis Drive in Boise, Idaho, in Julia Davis Park (Wikipedia, museum website).
- The Boise Art Association was founded on 8 October 1931. The museum opened in 1937 as the Boise Gallery of Art, a partnership between the Association, the City of Boise and the Works Progress Administration, and was renamed the Boise Art Museum in 1988 (Wikipedia).
- Building: the original Art Deco and Egyptian Revival building was designed by Tourtellotte & Hummel (1937). It was renovated in 1988 by Trout Architects and Mark Mack, and CSHQA added a sculpture court and garden in 1997 and 1998, bringing it to 34,800 square feet (Wikipedia).
- Collection of about 3,500 works, focused on contemporary realism and modern and contemporary ceramics, including the largest public collection of works by the Idaho artist James Castle (Wikipedia).
- A 501(c)(3) non-profit, accredited by the American Alliance of Museums (museum website). Melanie Fales is Executive Director/CEO (annual report 2025-26).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday 10am to 5pm and Sunday noon to 5pm, closed Monday. Admission is $9 general, $7 for seniors 62+ and military, $5 for students, free for members and children 5 and under; First Thursday is pay what you can, and seniors are free on the third Wednesday. Free EnChroma glasses are offered to visitors with red-green colour blindness.

FUNDING
Boise Art Museum: mixed private and public: foundations, individuals, business partners, and Idaho and federal arts grants
- Form 990 data (ProPublica), year to April 2026: revenue $1,789,289, contributions $834,695, expenses $1,701,311, net assets $6,099,340. The museum's financial overview for the same year reports total support and revenues of $2,456,390, total expenses of $1,899,670 and endowment investments of $3,846,101.
- Earlier years (Form 990, ProPublica): revenue $1,626,067 in 2025, $2,025,957 in 2024 and $1,776,534 in 2023.
- Its own statement says support comes from members, contributions and grants from individuals, corporations and foundations, plus the Idaho Commission on the Arts and the National Endowment for the Arts (annual report 2025-26).
- The Jeffrey Gibson: They Teach Love exhibition was supported by the Steiner Family Foundation. The Seniors Create! programme was supported by E.A. Michelson Philanthropy and the National Endowment for the Arts (annual report).
- The ArtReach education programme was supported by the Laura Moore Cunningham Foundation, the J.R. Simplot Company Foundation, the Gladys E. Langroise Advised Fund in the Idaho Community Foundation, the Idaho Commission on the Arts and the National Endowment for the Arts. The Sahm Family Foundation supported a museum purchase and an artist talk.
- Art Bridges Foundation's Access for All programme supports First Thursday, and the museum is part of the Art Bridges Boise Cohort (annual report).
- Idaho Power sponsors the Especially for Seniors programme, held on the third Wednesday of each month with free admission for visitors 62 and over (annual report 2025-26; museum website, checked 25 October 2026).

BOTTOM LINE
The Form 990 for the year to April 2026 shows expenses of $1.70 million and revenue of $1.79 million, of which $834,695 was contributions; the annual report shows total expenses of $1.90 million and total support and revenues of $2.46 million. Support comes from members, individuals, foundations and corporations, plus Idaho Commission on the Arts and National Endowment for the Arts grants.
