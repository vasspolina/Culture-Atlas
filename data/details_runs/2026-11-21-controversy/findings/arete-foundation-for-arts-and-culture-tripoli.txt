WIDER CONTEXT
- "Sun on Closed Windows", an Arabic collection of work by 25 Libyan writers edited by Laila Moghrabi and Khaled Mattawa and published in May 2017, is a joint project of the Arete Foundation for Arts and Culture and Darf Publishers of London; after a literary event in Zawiya on 26 August 2017, the Libyan authorities, in particular the Ministry of Culture, condemned its content as against "public morality" and ordered the confiscation of all copies (English PEN, 4 September 2017).
- The writers and editors received death threats, and an Islamic group that controls Zawiya arrested the event's organiser and released him the following day (English PEN, 4 September 2017).
- The Presidency Council's culture and information authority said permission was never given for the book's publication; the publishers said they were given permission in 2013 (Libya Herald, Michel Cousins, 5 September 2017).
- The connection to the space: English PEN names Mattawa as president of the Arete Foundation and the book as an Arete project; neither source names Arete as a target of the ban.
