OMAN ACROSS AGES MUSEUM: Manah, Oman
ID: oman-across-ages-museum
Website: https://omanacrossages.om (checked 2026-11-20; down: security certificate error)

OVERVIEW
- National history museum in the Wilayat of Manah, Al Dakhiliyah Governorate, near Hisn Al Shomoukh (Oman News Agency in the Oman Observer, 2023).
- Set up under royal orders of Sultan Qaboos bin Said, who laid the foundation stone on 14 July 2015; Sultan Haitham bin Tarik opened it on 13 March 2023 (The National; Oman Observer).
- The site covers 300,000 square metres, with about 66,591 square metres of building designed in the shape of mountains (The National). In November 2017 the Oman Observer reported COX Architecture of Australia as lead consultant, Aurecon as a key consultant, and Shapoorji Pallonji Middle East with Oman Shapoorji as builders.
- Museum Studio says the Royal Court commissioned it to write the masterplan, run the architectural competition and design the displays across 9,000 square metres of galleries.
- Galleries: a History Gallery from Oman's geological formation through the Magan civilisation to the Al Busaidi dynasty, and a Renaissance Gallery whose display covers the first five years of the "blessed renaissance", with speeches by both sultans (Oman Observer, 2023).
- Exhibits include a mammoth jaw about 35 million years old from Dhofar, a trilobite fossil from Mahut and a reconstructed Majan reed ship (The National).
- The Library of Hisn Al Shomoukh, more than 46,000 titles, moved into the museum; there are children's labs and a lecture hall (Oman Observer, 2023).
- Director General at opening: Eng. Al Yaqdhan Abdullah Al Harthi (Oman Observer, 2023).
- Visiting: open 9 am to 9 pm, with a free bag room (Oman Observer, 2023).

FUNDING
Oman Across Ages Museum: state-funded: a public body of the Sultanate of Oman under Royal Court Affairs
- Royal Decree 15/2023 of 12 March 2023 set up the museum with legal personality and financial and administrative independence, under the Sultan's royal patronage (decree.om, an unofficial English translation by a private website).
- The decree transferred to it all allocations, assets and artefacts relevant to the museum from Royal Court Affairs, along with the staff concerned, and applies the Diwan of Royal Court's financial and staff laws to it (decree.om).
- Construction ran under the supervision of Royal Court Affairs (Oman Observer, 2017).

Red flags: Gulf state body: Royal Court Affairs board, 2023
- Royal Decree 15/2023 gives the museum a board of trustees chaired by the Secretary General of Royal Court Affairs, with members appointed by royal order (decree.om, unofficial translation).
- Nasr Hamoud Al Kindi, Secretary General of Royal Court Affairs, chairs the board of trustees and chaired the main committee of the museum project; he received the Sultan at the opening (Oman News Agency in the Oman Observer, 2023).

BOTTOM LINE
Royal court money from start to finish, with no private funder in view, in a museum the royal court runs. Its budget and building cost are worth watching for.
