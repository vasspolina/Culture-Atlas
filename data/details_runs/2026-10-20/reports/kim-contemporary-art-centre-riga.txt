KIM? CONTEMPORARY ART CENTRE: Riga, Latvia
ID: kim-contemporary-art-centre-riga
Website: https://kim.lv

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Sporta iela 2 in Riga, with Hanzas iela 22 also listed as an address (stated by the centre).
- Founded in 2009; the centre's about page names Zane Čulkstēna as founder and chair of the board, with Evita Goze as the other board member. It is registered as the association "KIM?" (stated by the centre).
- The name is a question, "kas ir māksla?" ("what is art?") (stated by the centre; LSM English).
- Kim? has moved between locations over about 15 years (Art Focus Now). In June 2024 the Latvian government gave it a 30-year lease on a former yeast factory and later school, on condition that Kim? renovates it within five years (Art Focus Now, Ariadne Arendt, 12 June 2024). LSM English (1 February 2024) names the building as Hanzas iela 22 and records the Saeima's willingness to hand over suitable premises free of charge; the building was used for educational purposes from 1948 to 2015.
- Planned renovation by architect Vilnis Mičulis: exhibition spaces, a museum café and Latvia's first international art residency (Art Focus Now).
- It has organised over 200 exhibitions and runs an education programme and publications (stated by the centre). It also organises the Riga Contemporary art fair; the second edition ran 2 to 5 July 2026 at Hanzas Perons (e-flux announcement).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 18:00, closed Mondays; entry is free (stated by the centre).

FUNDING
Kim? Contemporary Art Centre: mixed: Latvian state and city money, project sponsors and charity auctions
- Ministry of Culture of the Republic of Latvia and State Culture Capital Foundation of Latvia: supported Kim?'s presentation at June Art Fair 2021, held during Basel Art Week (Kim?'s own project page, which gives its run as 20 to 26 September 2021).
- Government of Latvia: the 30-year lease of the Hanzas iela 22 building, with a renovation budget of roughly €3 million (Art Focus Now, 12 June 2024; LSM English).
- Riga City Council: collaborates with Kim? on the Riga Contemporary fair (e-flux announcement, 9 June 2026).
- Riga Contemporary 2026 sponsors: general sponsor Citadele, public programme partner Sorainen, children's zone supporter BTA, supporter Inchcape (e-flux announcement).
- Black Market charity auction: the sixth edition (3 December 2025) raised money for the Hanzas iela 22 renovation; 31 artists are featured and Sorainen and River Propertie are named as supporters. Kim? credits previous fundraising campaigns with paying for the architectural vision, the minimum construction project submitted to the building authority, building maintenance, site clean-up and initial renovation works (Kim? page).

BOTTOM LINE
Kim? is an association run by its founder and one other board member. The money documented behind it is Latvian public support (Ministry of Culture, State Culture Capital Foundation, Riga City Council, a state building lease) and business sponsors of its fair and charity auctions.
