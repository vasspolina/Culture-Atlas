MUSÉE YVES SAINT LAURENT MARRAKECH: Marrakech, Morocco
ID: mus-e-yves-saint-laurent-marrakech-marrakech
Website: https://museeyslmarrakech.com/en

OVERVIEW
- Museum devoted to the work of fashion designer Yves Saint Laurent, on Rue Yves Saint Laurent near the Jardin Majorelle in Marrakech (Wikipedia; Fondation Jardin Majorelle press release, 15 November 2022).
- Inaugurated in October 2017 and opened to the public on 19 October 2017 (Wikipedia).
- Pierre Bergé said it was "natural to build a museum dedicated to the work of Yves Saint Laurent in Morocco" (Wikipedia). It shows the collection of the Pierre Bergé - Yves Saint Laurent Foundation; a second museum for the collection stands at the former fashion house headquarters in Paris (Wikipedia).
- Designed by Studio KO and built by the Moroccan subsidiary of Bouygues; about 4,000 m² in terracotta, concrete and terrazzo, with earth bricks made from Moroccan earth (Wikipedia).
- Inside: a permanent hall for Saint Laurent's work (over 7,000 garments and about 30,000 accessories, according to Wikipedia), a hall for painter Jacques Majorelle, temporary exhibition halls, an auditorium, a shop and bookshop, a café-restaurant and a research library of over 5,000 volumes (Wikipedia).
- Run by the Fondation Jardin Majorelle, created by decree no. 2.11.647 of 4 November 2011 and chaired by Madison Cox in 2022 (foundation press release, 15 November 2022). The foundation looks after the Jardin Majorelle and its two museums.
- Visiting: open every day except Wednesday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., last entry 5:30 p.m. A single QR-code ticket from tickets.jardinmajorelle.com covers the Jardin Majorelle, the Pierre Bergé Museum of Berber Arts and this museum (museum website).

FUNDING
Musée Yves Saint Laurent Marrakech: privately funded: Pierre Bergé's sale of Moroccan art, run by the Fondation Jardin Majorelle
- Construction: The Art Newspaper (Claudia Barbieri Childs, "Yves Saint Laurent's partner sells art to support their charities", 30 September 2015) reported that Bergé hired Artcurial to sell 240 lots of Moroccan, Islamic and Orientalist art and craftwork from the couple's Marrakech villa and garden complex, at the Es Saadi Palace hotel on 31 October, with proceeds going to the Fondation Jardin Majorelle and toward the new museum. Wikipedia states the museum was financed by an auction of Moroccan works of art held by Bergé.
- Result: Le360 (1 November 2015, no author named) reported the sale reached about €1.4 million including fees, 3.5 times the estimate, and quoted Bergé telling AFP he wanted to "find money to build the museum" (quotation translated). The article is titled "La collection d'art marocain Bergé/Saint-Laurent vendue à 1,4 million d'euros". Le360 added that the sale would also help maintain the Majorelle garden, which drew nearly 800,000 visitors.
- Operator: the Fondation Jardin Majorelle, a charitable foundation that manages the villa and garden complex (The Art Newspaper).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is Pierre Bergé's 2015 sale of Moroccan art, which reached about €1.4 million according to Le360. The Fondation Jardin Majorelle, a charitable foundation, runs it with the Jardin Majorelle.
