LAHORE BIENNALE FOUNDATION: Lahore, Pakistan
ID: lahore-biennale-foundation-lahore
Website: https://www.lahorebiennale.org/

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit foundation that organises the Lahore Biennale, a contemporary art biennial held in venues across Lahore (Biennial Foundation).
- Established in 2014 by the artist Qudsia Rahim and the businessman Osman Khalid Waheed (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 16 March 2016). Rahim is co-founder and executive director; Naazish Ata-Ullah and Raza Ali Dada are co-chairs of the board (lahorebiennale.org).
- Editions: LB01, 18 to 31 March 2018; LB02, early 2020, curated by Hoor Al Qasimi; LB03 "Of Mountains and Seas", 5 October to 8 November 2024, curated by John Tain; LB04 is slated for early 2027, curated by Nav Haq (Biennial Foundation).
- Rashid Rana, named artistic director of the first biennale in 2016, stepped down in September 2017; the foundation's statement cited "differing views on the vision" (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 27 December 2017).
- LB03 used about a dozen locations including the Lahore Fort in the Walled City and Shalimar Gardens, with a Climate Congress from 2 to 8 November; admission was free (Biennial Association).

FUNDING
Lahore Biennale Foundation: mixed: government bodies and corporate sponsors, with grants from foundations
- Founding patrons listed on the foundation's board page include the Government of Punjab, Nayyar Ali Dada Associates, Ferozsons Laboratories Limited, the Babar Ali Foundation, Nairang Galleries and ArtNow (lahorebiennale.org).
- AkzoNobel and the British Council were named as backers in 2016, with "private and public bodies" (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 16 March 2016); in December 2017 the organisers said the biennale is backed by government bodies (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 27 December 2017).
- Habib Bank Limited (HBL) was lead sponsor of LB02 in January 2020 and partnered with the 2018 edition (Aga Khan Development Network press release, 27 January 2020).
- The Bank of Punjab signed a memorandum of understanding with the foundation at the LB03 curtain raiser at Alhamra Arts Council, Lahore, for a partnership covering the next three editions; Punjab Chief Minister Syed Mohsin Raza Naqvi attended (ProPakistani press release, 6 February 2024).
- Getty Foundation: the LB03 Climate Congress, 2 to 8 November 2024, is supported by a grant from the Getty Foundation (Biennial Foundation; Biennial Association). The grant of 220,000 dollars was awarded to Iftikhar Dadi of Cornell University, who co-convened the Congress with John Tain, under the Getty's Connecting Art Histories initiative (Cornell Department of History of Art and Visual Studies, 4 February 2025).

Red flags: Fossil fuels (Getty Foundation grant)
- The Getty Foundation supports the 2024 Climate Congress, the closing programme of the third Lahore Biennale, with a grant (Biennial Foundation; Cornell).
- The Getty Foundation is part of the J. Paul Getty Trust, which can spend up to 0.75% of its endowment on gifts and grants (Wikipedia).
- The Trust's endowment began with J. Paul Getty's bequest of nearly 660 million dollars in Getty Oil stock, received in full in 1982 (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Protest or boycott
- In September 2024 the artist and activist Zulfikar Ali Bhutto Jr announced on Instagram that he was boycotting the Lahore Biennale 2024 and accused the organisers, the Lahore Biennale Foundation, of greenwashing. He said artists were brought in from Karachi, Paris and Los Angeles and called the festival a "decadent party" rather than a platform for environmental advocacy. The article names no sponsors (Images Staff, Dawn Images, "Activist Zulfikar Ali Bhutto Jr is boycotting the Lahore Biennale over greenwashing", updated 23 September 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the foundation is a mix of Punjab government bodies, banks and companies as sponsors, and foundation grants including the Getty Foundation's grant for the 2024 Climate Congress, whose endowment began with Getty Oil stock. The documented flags are that Getty grant and the September 2024 greenwashing boycott announced by the activist Zulfikar Ali Bhutto Jr against the organisers.
