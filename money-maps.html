One map for each space in the Culture Atlas. The space is the globe in the middle. Each circle around it is a funder, a flag or a documented tie read from that space's record. Each line is a tie the record states. Olive circles are other spaces that name the same funder. One finger or a mouse drag turns the map in 3D. Pinch with two fingers, or use the scroll wheel or the W and S keys, to fly forward and back through it. Drag with two fingers, or hold Shift and drag, to slide. The arrow keys turn it.

The circles are read from the funding text and the flag, Epstein and WikiLeaks fields of each record, so a name can be split or sorted into the wrong group. Select the globe to see the full record and its sources. Where the record names no funder, the map is empty.
