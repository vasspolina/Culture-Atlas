LA VIRREINA CENTRE DE LA IMATGE: Barcelona, Spain
ID: la-virreina-barcelona
Website: https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/lavirreina/en

OVERVIEW
- Centre for the image (Centre de la Imatge) at La Rambla 99, in the Palau de la Virreina, with the Barcelona City Council logo on its website (La Virreina website).
- Its exhibition halls host a multidisciplinary project that aims to place cultural policies at the centre of its exhibition activity; the palace is also the headquarters of the Barcelona Institute of Culture (Amics de la Rambla).
- Its director Valentín Roma was appointed director of the MACBA unanimously by the general council of the museum consortium on 10 June 2026; he is to start there in the coming weeks (Ara, Antoni Ribas Tur, 10 June 2026, "Valentín Roma es el nuevo director del Macba").
- Named for the palace, which took its name from the viceroy's widow, Maria Francesca de Fivaller-Clasquerí i de Bru, who lived there after the death of her husband, Manuel d'Amat, Viceroy of Peru, in 1782 (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Building: a baroque and rococo palace built from 1772 for Manuel d'Amat (English Wikipedia); Catalan Wikipedia names Josep Ausich i Mir as the architect and English Wikipedia names Carles Grau. Barcelona City Council bought it in 1944, and it was declared a monument of national interest in 1941 (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Earlier uses: first exhibition in 1940 (Amics de la Rambla), the Museu d'Arts Decoratives from 1949 (Catalan Wikipedia), and the City Council's Department of Culture from 1986 (Amics de la Rambla). It also houses the Gabinet Numismàtic de Catalunya and the Museu Postal (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Visiting: free admission; open Tuesday to Sunday and public holidays, 11 am to 8 pm; closed on 1 and 6 January, 1 May, 24 June, and 25 and 26 December. Groups of more than 10 people book in advance (La Virreina website).

FUNDING
La Virreina Centre de la Imatge: public: Barcelona City Council
- Barcelona City Council has owned the palace since 1944 (Catalan Wikipedia), and the centre's website is on the City Council's own domain, ajuntament.barcelona.cat, with the City Council logo (La Virreina website).
- The palace is the headquarters of the Barcelona Institute of Culture, the City Council's culture institute (ICUB) (Amics de la Rambla; English Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The funder is Barcelona City Council, which owns the building; the palace is the headquarters of the City Council's culture institute, ICUB. Admission is free.
