MUSEO DEL PRADO: Madrid, Spain
ID: museo-del-prado
Website: https://www.museodelprado.es

OVERVIEW
- National art museum in Madrid, opened to the public in November 1819 as the Royal Museum of Paintings and Sculptures to show the Spanish Crown's collection (Wikipedia).
- The building was designed by Juan de Villanueva in 1785 under Charles III, originally for a Natural History Cabinet (Wikipedia).
- After Isabella II was deposed in 1868 the museum was nationalised and renamed Museo del Prado; its collection later absorbed the Museo de la Trinidad (1872) and the Museo de Arte Moderno (1971) (Wikipedia).
- Miguel Falomir has been director since 2017 (Wikipedia); he signs the museum's funding agreements published in the state gazette.
- In 2024 the museum had 3,457,057 visitors, with 45 percent entering free (EFE, 9 July 2025).
- The Salón de Reinos rehabilitation works were funded with about 20.5 million euros of the 2024 public subsidies (EFE).

FUNDING
Museo del Prado: mainly public, with large own income and corporate foundation benefactors
- 2024 accounts: total income 85.2 million euros, of which own income 37.2 million (ticket revenue about 27 million) and public subsidies just over 48 million, of which about 20.5 million went to the Salón de Reinos works; sponsorship brought in 6 million euros and the year ended with a surplus of 22.8 million (EFE, 9 July 2025).
- The state allocation is around 50 million euros a year and has been frozen below that level, with the state budget rolled over annually since 2023 (Marina Merino, que.es, 27 June 2026). The article says the museum's annual budget is 80 million euros and Falomir is asking permission to create an endowment fund from the annual surplus.
- Wikipedia says the museum's 2003 bylaws, approved by the Cortes Generales, require it to reduce state support gradually from 80 percent to 50 percent of its budget.
- Fundación BBVA: benefactor for four years under an agreement signed 2 January 2025, 625,000 euros a year and 2,500,000 euros in total, supporting the Grandes Exposiciones programme and audiovisual projects (state gazette BOE, 14 January 2025).
- Fundación AXA: benefactor for four years under an agreement signed 28 February 2024, 625,000 euros a year and 2,500,000 euros in total, sponsoring one temporary exhibition a year (BOE, 9 March 2024).
- Fundación Iberdrola España: funds three scholarships in the restoration workshop and Technical Documentation Service, at 1,500 euros per month each, in an agreement signed on 20 December 2024 and in force to 31 December 2027, with a maximum commitment of 116,526 euros (BOE, 4 January 2025). The agreement states it is not an advertising sponsorship contract.
- Fundación Endesa: 25,000 euros, in two instalments of 12,500 euros, for the fourth edition of the Marcos de Mira educational programme for people in socially vulnerable situations, signed 16 April 2026 (BOE, 27 April 2026).
- Fundación Bancaria "la Caixa": an addendum signed on 22 February 2023 renewed its benefactor status and extended the 11 December 2019 agreement for a touring exhibition, "El siglo del retrato" (provisional title), to no later than 21 December 2025 (BOE, 6 March 2023).

BOTTOM LINE
The Prado is a state museum whose 2024 income, by EFE's figures, was about 56 percent public subsidy and 44 percent own income, with 6 million euros from sponsorship. The foundations of BBVA, AXA and la Caixa are benefactors, and the foundations of Iberdrola España and Endesa fund scholarships and an educational project, each under a dated agreement in the state gazette.
