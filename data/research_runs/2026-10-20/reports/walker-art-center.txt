WALKER ART CENTER – Minneapolis, USA
Website: https://www.walkerart.org/ (ok, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Multidisciplinary contemporary art centre in Minneapolis, led by executive director Mary Ceruti (MPR News). It posts annual reports up to 2024-25 with audited statements (stated by the space).
- On 23 January 2026 it closed for the day and cancelled a performance in support of a Minnesota economic blackout protesting ICE operations in the Twin Cities (Artnet; reported by an independent outlet).

FUNDING
Walker Art Center: mainly private contributions and endowment, with corporate, foundation and state support.
- Form 990 for the year to June 2025: revenue $53.5M, of which contributions $24.8M; expenses $31.8M; net assets $444.8M (ProPublica).
- State: $672,710 from Minnesota's Arts & Cultural Heritage Fund for operating support, FY2024 (Minnesota Legacy site).
- Corporate supporters by tier, undated (stated by the space): $100,000+ Bloomberg Philanthropies and Principal Foundation; $50,000-99,000 Ameriprise Financial, U.S. Bank and U.S. Bank Foundation; $25,000-49,000 3M, Augeo, Best Buy, The Cargill Foundation, Pohlad Companies, RBC Wealth Management, Thomson Reuters, Thrivent, United Health Foundation and others. Bloomberg Philanthropies is also listed for in-kind gifts (its Bloomberg Connects app).
- Matching gifts include Archer Daniels Midland, Mastercard, Microsoft and UnitedHealth Group (stated by the space).

Red flags: Campaigned funder
- Bloomberg Philanthropies, a $100,000+ supporter, is the target of a 29 May 2026 open letter by more than 260 UK artists (Artists for Palestine UK) asking five UK theatres to end partnerships with it, citing its funding of the Bloomberg-Sagol Center at Tel Aviv University, which they say trains West Bank settlement leaders. Unverified: allegations made by an advocacy group; reported by The New Arab, a Qatari-backed outlet critical of Israel; no Bloomberg response in the article.

Red flags: Labour dispute
- Walker Worker Union says its president and secretary were fired in March 2025 for union activity; about 100 people rallied on 10 April 2025. The Walker says one role was eliminated in a restructuring and denies union-busting (MPR News).

BOTTOM LINE
The Walker is well documented, with audited accounts and tiered supporter lists. The existing flag stands: Bloomberg Philanthropies is a $100,000+ supporter and the subject of an artists' campaign over Israel-linked funding; a 2025 union dispute is also worth watching.
