MAIIAM: Chiang Mai, Thailand
ID: maiiam
Website: https://maiiam.com/en

OVERVIEW
- Private contemporary art museum at 122 Moo 7, Tonpao, San Kamphaeng District, about 20 minutes from central Chiang Mai (museum website).
- Founded by Jean Michel Beurdeley and Patsri Bunnag (called late by ArtAsiaPacific), with their son Eric Bunnag Booth; ASEF Culture360 gave its opening date as 3 July 2016 (museum website; ASEF Culture360, 17 June 2016).
- Named for Chao Chom Iam, a royal consort to King Rama V and Booth's great-great-aunt (museum website).
- Housed in a converted warehouse of about 3,000 square metres in the Sankampaeng crafts district, with four exhibition halls, a screening room and an indoor and outdoor space for openings and performances; the architects are allzone, described by the museum website as a Bangkok-based team (ASEF Culture360). The museum's directions identify it by its "building with the mirror wall" on Sankampang Road (museum website).
- The museum shares the family's private collection, built over three decades (ASEF Culture360). ArtAsiaPacific says Booth acquired works by Montien Boonma at the recommendation of the curator Gridthiya Gaweewong, and names Vasan Sitthiket, Kosit Juntaratip, Araya Rasdjarmrearnsook and Mit Jai Inn among the artists in his collection (ArtAsiaPacific, 1 May 2020).
- The first exhibition was a retrospective of Apichatpong Weerasethakul, "The Serenity of Madness" (Larry's List).
- Adult admission is 200 baht, seniors 150 baht and students 100 baht. Open 10:00 to 18:00; the visit page says every day except Tuesday and its footer says every Friday through Monday (museum website).

FUNDING
MAIIAM: private: a collector initiative sharing the founding family's collection
- ASEF Culture360 called it a private collector initiative in 2016 that would share the Bunnag-Beurdeley family's collection (ASEF Culture360, 17 June 2016).
- Beurdeley is a former owner of a Paris gallery specialising in Asian art and antiques who moved to Bangkok in 1998 with his wife (Larry's List).
- ArtAsiaPacific records Booth's work for the Jim Thompson fashion label and cultural foundation, and says that in 2020 he was overseeing a 3,000 square metre expansion of the Jim Thompson Art Center in Bangkok.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One mild criticism of the building, in a profile of Booth.
1. ArtAsiaPacific: no byline, "1342°C" (1 May 2020, May/June 2020 issue)
URL: https://www.artasiapacific.com/1342-c/
- The writer says MAIIAM, "with its gleaming facade, can be cold and intimidating for locals", and describes Booth's new space "Manifesto by MAIELIE" as "intended as a warm, welcoming place for workshops about art, culture, and politics".
- Criticises the building's manner, not the funding.

BOTTOM LINE
MAIIAM is privately held: the Beurdeley-Bunnag family founded the museum, which shares the family's own collection.
