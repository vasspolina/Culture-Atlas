WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2019 the museum lost its Mali salon gallery at Korzo 24 after 70 years as an exhibition space. The owner of the premises, Croatian Radiotelevision (HRT), notified the museum on 15 June 2019 that it had three months to leave, so that HRT could use the room as a studio for Rijeka 2020, European Capital of Culture. A meeting to ask for more time or shared use brought no agreement (Novi list, 4 September 2019).
- HRT said it had not "thrown out" the museum but legitimately ended the lease of a space it owns, and regretted that it could not allow a longer stay (Novi list, 4 September 2019).
- Eighteen museum employees started a petition on Avaaz asking for a new lease between the museum and HRT in 2021; it had 957 signatures when Novi list reported it. The petition called the removal of the gallery "nepromišljenim činom" (a thoughtless act, translated). Acting director Ivo Matulić did not sign it and said the museum had already asked HRT for an extension and for the space back after 2020 (Novi list, 4 September 2019).
- The gallery closed with an event titled "Doviđenja, Mali salone!" in September 2019. Speakers at the closing objected to the decision; Janka Vukmir called the return of the space to the broadcaster a symbolic gesture of non-support for culture (Novi list, 13 September 2019).
