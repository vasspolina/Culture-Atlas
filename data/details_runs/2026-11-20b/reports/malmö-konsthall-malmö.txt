MALMÖ KONSTHALL: Malmö, Sweden
ID: malmö-konsthall-malmö
Website: https://malmokonsthall.se (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal contemporary art hall at S:t Johannesgatan 7, by Triangeln station, about a 15-minute walk from Malmö Central; it is part of the City of Malmö's culture administration (Kulturförvaltningen) (Malmö Konsthall website).
- Opened on 22 March 1975 with a Munch exhibition and the group show "Ögon-Blickar/New Media 1". In 1931 members of Skånes Konstförening set up a foundation and a lottery to build a hall, a plan never carried out; the city decided in 1971 to build a municipal one (Malmö Konsthall, history page).
- Building: Klas Anshelm won the competition; the hall has a large north-facing skylight and over 500 roof lanterns whose light can be shut off one by one (Malmö Konsthall, architecture page). Wikipedia says it was built from 1971 to 1974 in concrete, glass, wood and aluminium and won the 1974 Kasper Salin Prize.
- In 1994 Jan Holmgren of White Arkitekter added the "Mellanrummet", linking the hall to the 1915 Hantverkshuset, with reception, café, lecture hall and bookshop (Malmö Konsthall, architecture page).
- No permanent collection: about 2,000 square metres of exhibition space, three large shows a year, close to 600 exhibitions since 1975 and over 250,000 visitors a year (Malmö Konsthall website).
- Directors: Eje Högestätt 1975 to 1986, Björn Springfeldt 1986 to 1990, Sune Nordgren 1990 to 1996, Bera Nordal 1997 to 2002, Lars Grambye 2003 to 2007, Jacob Fabricius 2007 to 2013, Diana Baldon 2014 to 2016 and Mats Stjernstedt from 2017 (Malmö Konsthall, "50 years" page). The city announced Stjernstedt's appointment on 12 September 2016 (Malmö stad).
- Season: Otobong Nkanga's "I dreamt of you in colours", her first large show in Sweden, runs from 10 October 2026 to 28 March 2027, co-produced with the Musée d'Art Moderne de Paris and the Musée cantonal des Beaux-Arts de Lausanne (Malmö Konsthall website).
- On site: the restaurant SMAK, a bookshop, the children's workshop Verkstan and the youth programme Malmö Konsthall U (Malmö Konsthall website).
- Visiting: free admission; open Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 17:00, Wednesday to 19:00, closed Monday, major holidays and the weeks before a new show (Malmö Konsthall website).

FUNDING
Malmö Konsthall: public: a unit of the City of Malmö's culture administration
- Owner and operator: the City of Malmö; the hall is part of the city's culture administration, which hired its head in 2016 through Culture Director Elisabeth Lundgren (Malmö Konsthall website; Malmö stad, 12 September 2016).
- KOAS, the hall's studio for artists with disabilities: the Allmänna arvsfonden granted about 6 million kronor over three years in autumn 2024; the studio is also backed by Region Skåne and MKB Fastighets AB, which owns its 570-square-metre premises in Sorgenfri (Malmö Konsthall, KOAS page).
- Region Skåne supported the 2023 preliminary study behind KOAS. The project runs from 2025 to 2027, by the end of which the hall aims to have a lasting organisation and funding model in place (Malmö Konsthall, KOAS page).
- The 1931 foundation: the proceeds of the Stiftelsen Malmö Konsthall av år 1931's lottery have paid for an annual grant to a Malmö artist since 1997 (Malmö Konsthall, history page).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: city money, with project grants for its disability studio. Worth watching is how KOAS is paid for after 2027.
