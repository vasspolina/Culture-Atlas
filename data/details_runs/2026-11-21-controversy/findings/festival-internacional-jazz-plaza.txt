Red flags: 2025 cancellation of a Pavel Giroud documentary screening
- The filmmaker Pavel Giroud said on Facebook that the festival's 40th edition cancelled, without any explanation, the screening of his documentary set for Saturday 1 February 2025 at the 23 and 12 cinema, which he described as "part of the Jazz Festival program" (CiberCuba, 3 February 2025).
- The Observatorio de Derechos Culturales denounced on Facebook "the climate of censorship that, once again, characterizes the Jazz Plaza Festival" and called the cancellation a "violation of the cultural rights of the audience attending Jazz Plaza 2025" (CiberCuba).
