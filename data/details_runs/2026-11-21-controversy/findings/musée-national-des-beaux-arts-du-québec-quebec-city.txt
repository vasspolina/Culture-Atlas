Red flags: 2024 near doubling of the Espace Riopelle budget
- The Espace Riopelle pavilion was priced at C$42.5m when the museum chose its architects, Les Architectes FABG, in September 2022; in April 2024 the museum put the cost at C$84m (The Art Newspaper, Benjamin Sutton, 5 April 2024).
- The Quebec government's contribution rose from C$20m to C$44m, the Jean-Paul Riopelle Foundation's from C$20m to up to C$25m, and the MNBAQ Foundation's quadrupled to C$10m (The Art Newspaper). The City of Quebec's share rose from $2.5 million to $5 million (Quebec Chronicle Telegraph, Ruby Pratka, 9 April 2024).
- The museum called the new figure "a more accurate estimate of the cost", after "a comprehensive assessment of project costs" between October 2022 and December 2023 (The Art Newspaper).
- Director general Jean-Luc Murray told Radio-Canada the increase came from inflation and imprecise early estimates, and said: "For a project of this scale, we think it's very reasonable" (Quebec Chronicle Telegraph).
