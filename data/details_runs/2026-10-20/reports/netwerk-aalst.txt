NW (FORMERLY NETWERK AALST): Aalst, Belgium
ID: netwerk-aalst
Website: https://nw-aalst.be/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art and film centre at Houtkaai 15, 9300 Aalst, on the right bank; it calls itself an open house for contemporary art and film (NW website).
- Founded in 1981 as the socio-cultural centre Netwerk in a vacant wholesale building in central Aalst; it moved after 2001 into the former Passementerie Van den Brulle textile factory (Dutch Wikipedia).
- The Flemish Community recognised Netwerk Galerij as a visual arts centre and Netwerk as a performing arts centre in 2001, and the merged centre as a visual arts kunsthal in 2005 (Dutch Wikipedia).
- Became Netwerk Aalst from 2017 and added a film house in 2019 (Dutch Wikipedia).
- After about a year and a half closed for renovation, it reopened in March 2024 as NW, with artistic director Laura Herman; the opening exhibition was "Ritual in Transfigured Time" on Maya Deren (VRT NWS, 21 March 2024).
- On site: exhibitions, an arthouse cinema, artist residencies, a bookshop, a café and the Lokaal Dendy local programming, which includes Lokaal Solidair, Young NW and Kitchen Stories (NW website).
- Visiting: exhibitions open Saturday and Sunday 13:00 to 18:00, Thursday and Friday by appointment. Exhibition admission is pay-what-you-can from 5 to 20 euros with a reference price of 8.50 euros; free under 26; 2 euros with a UIT-pas or kansenpas. Film tickets are 10 euros, 6 euros under 26. The entrance is step-free with a lift to the first floor (NW visit page).
- NW helped shape and endorses the principles of "Juist is Juist", which cover solidarity, transparency, sustainability and care, including fair pay and a safe workplace (NW website).

FUNDING
NW: public recognition plus philanthropic and ticket income
- The Flemish Community recognised Netwerk Galerij and Netwerk in 2001 and the merged centre as a kunsthal in 2005 (NW website, Dutch Wikipedia). Its legal form is a non-profit association (Dutch Wikipedia).
- Fonds Vrienden van NW: a fund of the King Baudouin Foundation that supports NW's projects. The King Baudouin Foundation says it was set up on the initiative of "bevriende filantropen" (friendly philanthropists), unnamed. Memberships run from 50 euros (Club Dendy) to 1,500 euros (Club NW Plus, aimed at private sponsors and companies), and donations to the foundation carry a 45% tax reduction (NW Friends page).
- The Friends fund also offers company partnerships linked to a specific exhibition (NW Friends page).
- Ticket and shop income: exhibition and film tickets, a bookshop and limited art editions made each year by invited artists (NW website, Friends page).

BOTTOM LINE
NW is a non-profit association recognised by the Flemish Community since 2001, with a donor fund held at the King Baudouin Foundation and income from tickets and its shop.
