MAC LIMA: Lima, Peru
ID: mac-lima-lima
Website: https://maclima.pe/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at Av. Grau in the Barranco district of Lima, with three galleries of 100, 200 and 1,000 square metres in an industrial-style building surrounded by a terrace and park (MAC Lima).
- Inaugurated in late January 2013 on land donated by the Municipality of Barranco, on the site of the former Barranco Zoo and its artificial lake; it is run as a private non-profit organisation (English Wikipedia).
- It succeeds the Instituto de Arte Contemporáneo (IAC), opened in 1955 in the former premises of the Galería de Lima, which moved to Casa Mujica in 1966 and used the Museum of Italian Art building from 1969 to 1972 (English Wikipedia).
- Architect: Frederick Cooper Llosa (English Wikipedia).
- The Spanish Wikipedia infobox lists Pedro Pablo Alayza as director and Eduardo Hochschild Beeck as president.
- The museum's programme includes the education programmes Escuela Vecina, #aulamac and Domingos en el MAC (Spanish Wikipedia), and Contemporáneo 1, a permanent collection exhibition opened in December 2024 (English Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 19:00, closed Monday (MAC Lima).

FUNDING
MAC Lima: private non-profit, on land from the Barranco municipality
- English Wikipedia states that the museum is run as a private non-profit organisation and was built on land donated by the Municipality of Barranco; Spanish Wikipedia describes the site as land ceded in concession by the district municipality.
- Spanish Wikipedia states that a 1990s auction brought in geometric art by artists including Jesús Rafael Soto and Carlos Cruz-Diez for the collection.

Red flags: 2018 petition against the exhibition "Canon"
- "Canon: Vírgenes de la Puerta" by Juan José Barboza-Gubo and Andrew Mroczek opened at MAC Lima on 7 February 2018 and ran through 1 April; a parallel show, "Canon: Los Chicos and Anda", was at the Cultural Center of Spain (The Advocate, Advocate.com Editors, "Trans Photography Under Attack In Peru", 27 February 2018).
- A CitizenGo petition was addressed to Luis Tejada Chacón, director of the Spanish Cooperation Agency for International Development, to Enrique Stiglich, director of the Museum of Contemporary Art in Lima, and to Ernesto de Zulueta Habsburgo-Lorraine, Spain's ambassador to Peru. It demanded the closing of the exhibitions and had more than 20,000 signatures by 27 February 2018; the groups the article names are Tradición y Acción por un Perú Mayor, Revuelta Contra el Arte Moderno and the Asociación Antonin Scalia (The Advocate).
- Spanish Wikipedia states that conservative groups and congressman Carlos Tubino denounced the exhibition as blasphemous, which led to a bill to criminalise religious defamation, and that the museum publicly defended the works.

BOTTOM LINE
MAC Lima is a private non-profit museum built on land from the Barranco municipality. The documented issue is the 2018 CitizenGo petition, addressed to the museum's director and to Spain's ambassador, demanding the closing of its "Canon" exhibition.
