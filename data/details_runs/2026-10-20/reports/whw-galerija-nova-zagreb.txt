WHW (WHAT, HOW & FOR WHOM) / GALERIJA NOVA: Zagreb, Croatia
ID: whw-galerija-nova-zagreb
Website: https://www.whw.hr

OVERVIEW
- Curatorial collective and non-profit programme in Zagreb. WHW (Što, kako i za koga) runs WHW Akademija, an international education programme for emerging artists launched in 2018, and managed the city-owned Galerija Nova from 2003 to 2023 (WHW).
- WHW was established in Zagreb in 1999; its members are Ivet Ćurlin, Ana Dević, Nataša Ilić and Sabina Sabolović, with the designer and publicist Dejan Kršić; its office is at Bogovićeva 1, Zagreb (WHW).
- Galerija Nova is a non-profit city gallery founded in 1975 by the artists Ljerka Šibenik and Mladen Galić, as part of the Center for Cultural Activities of the Socialist Youth of Zagreb (WHW).
- On 18 May 2023 WHW announced that the gallery's premises had been returned to a private owner under a restitution decision and that the new owner had asked the gallery to leave; the remaining 2023 programmes moved to HDLU, the Ribnjak Youth Centre and POGON (WHW).
- Wider work: the 11th Istanbul Biennial (2009), the Croatian pavilion at the 54th Venice Biennale (2011) and, for Ćurlin, Ilić and Sabolović, the artistic directorship of Kunsthalle Wien from June 2019 to June 2024 and of Skulptur Projekte Münster 2027 (WHW).

FUNDING
WHW / Galerija Nova: public and foundation grants: Croatian state and Zagreb city funds, EU programmes and arts foundations
- WHW's support page lists the same supporters for each year from 2023 to 2026: the Kultura Nova Foundation, the Ministry of Culture and Media of the Republic of Croatia, the City of Zagreb (City Office for Culture, Inter-City and International Cooperation and Civil Society), the Croatian Audiovisual Centre, the Office for Associations of the Government of the Republic of Croatia, the EU Creative Europe programme, ERSTE Foundation / Kontakt Collection, the Foundation for Arts Initiatives, the Trust for Mutual Understanding and the Erasmus+ programme (Agency for Mobility and EU Programmes). The 2022 list adds the National Foundation for Civil Society Development.
- Galerija Nova is a city-owned gallery (WHW), and the City Office for Culture continued to support WHW's programmes after the 2023 move (WHW announcement, 18 May 2023).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Kunsthalle Wien: in December 2022 the City of Vienna rejected all 20 applicants for the director post, including WHW, whose contract was due to end in June 2024. Veronica Kaup-Hasler, the city's executive councillor for culture, said the jury found none of the concepts convincing enough for a further term; WHW said it never received an explanation and noted pressure over ticket sales and visitor numbers, which COVID-19 restrictions had affected (Hyperallergic, Avedis Hadjian, "Art Collective Ousted From Viennese Kunsthalle Speaks Out", 23 January 2023).
- ERSTE Foundation chief executive Boris Marte resigned from the Kunsthalle's supervisory board in protest (Hyperallergic). The L'Internationale board published a public letter on 10 January 2023 calling the city's explanation inadequate (L'Internationale).
- Connection: this concerns the Vienna institution that three WHW members directed, not Galerija Nova or WHW's Zagreb funding. ERSTE Foundation, whose chief executive resigned from the Kunsthalle board, is also on WHW's support list.

BOTTOM LINE
WHW and Galerija Nova are funded by Croatian ministry and Zagreb city grants, EU programmes and European arts foundations such as ERSTE Foundation and the Trust for Mutual Understanding. The Kunsthalle Wien dispute concerns the City of Vienna's decision, not WHW's funding.
