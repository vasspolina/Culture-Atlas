MAMAC (MUSÉE D'ART MODERNE ET D'ART CONTEMPORAIN): Nice, France
ID: mamac
Website: https://www.mamac-nice.org/en/ (checked 2026-11-20; states the museum is closed for renovation)

OVERVIEW
- The City of Nice's museum of modern and contemporary art, at Place Yves Klein next to Place Garibaldi, and one of the city's 11 museums (museum website).
- Inaugurated on 21 June 1990 (museum website).
- Building: by the architects Yves Bayard and Henri Vidal, four square towers with a red ochre base and Carrara marble surfaces, linked by glass footbridges, built over the covered Paillon river, with ten exhibition rooms and public roof terraces (museum website).
- Collection: more than 1,400 works by 370 artists, centred on New Realism, American Pop Art, the Nice school, Yves Klein and Niki de Saint Phalle, who made a large donation in 2001; the Klein room relies on long-term loans from the Yves Klein Archives (museum website).
- Closed since 7 January 2024 for renovation as part of the Promenade du Paillon extension, to bring the building up to current ecological standards; the museum thanked 3,000 visitors on the closing day (museum website; Nice Premium, 7 January 2024; Monaco Life, 9 January 2024).
- Renovation: diagnostics, relocation and securing of the works began in 2024; the City then ended the contract of the first architect, whose project was too far from its expectations, and launched a new consultation in early 2025, keeping the 2029 reopening target (Nice Premium, 27 February 2025).
- During the closure the collection is shown elsewhere: "Anne and Patrick Poirier. Memories of the ruins" runs at the Nice Cimiez archaeology museum from 7 November 2026 to 5 September 2027 (museum website).
- Director: Céline Kopp since 1 January 2026, after Hélène Guenin left after nine years and Johanne Lindskog, director of the Nice fine arts museum, served as interim director from February 2025; Kopp ran the Magasin in Grenoble from 2022 and before that a contemporary art centre in Marseille for ten years (museum team page; Nice Premium, 25 September 2025).

FUNDING
MAMAC: public: the City of Nice, which owns it and pays for its renovation
- Owner and operator: the City of Nice (museum website).
- State: a 1985 agreement with the State brought State money for a five-year purchasing programme, and a 1987 agreement had the City and the State finance the building (museum website).
- Renovation: the City's tender for the architect's contract set the works budget at 24,778,000 euros before tax and estimated the architect's fee at 3,256,435.80 euros over 72 months; the notice states the project receives no EU funds (BOAMP notice, 19 March 2025).
- Architect: the City chose a group led by Freaks Architecture with In Situ Benaïm-Nivaggioni, Egis, Ducks Scéno and Peutz on 23 February 2026, for 2,749,076.85 euros before tax, and signed in March 2026 (BOAMP award notice, 23 March 2026).
- Friends: the Association des Amis du MAMAC raises money from members towards acquisitions and restoration of works (museum website).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Nice Premium reported on 30 November 2025 that about a dozen employees of the Magasin in Grenoble described sick leaves, dismissals and disciplinary procedures; eight of eleven employees struck for two hours on 19 November 2025, and unions asked for independent mediation and an organisational audit.
- The same article reports that the labour inspectorate refused the dismissal of a staff representative who had reported alleged aggression and sexual harassment, and that management kept up legal action. The article gives the employees' and unions' account only.
- The dispute concerns the Magasin, not MAMAC; it is relevant because Kopp now directs MAMAC.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: municipal money. Worth watching are the renovation's schedule and cost, and how the new director manages staff.
