TE URU WAITAKERE CONTEMPORARY GALLERY: Auckland, New Zealand
ID: te-uru-waitakere-contemporary-gallery-auckland
Website: https://teuru.org.nz/pages/support

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at 420 Titirangi Road, Titirangi, West Auckland, beside Lopdell House (Wikipedia; gooood).
- Opened in 1986 inside Lopdell House as Lopdell House Gallery; it closed in 2012 for a building project and reopened on 1 November 2014 in a custom-built gallery (Wikipedia).
- The name Te Uru refers to Te Hau a Uru, "wind from the west", chosen in consultation with the iwi Te Kawerau ā Maki (Wikipedia).
- The new building was designed by Julie Stout of Mitchell & Stout Architects (Wikipedia) and completed in 2014 with about 1,700 square metres of floor area, a green pre-coated aluminium exterior, a central core of unpainted plaster and a fabric lantern ceiling over the main gallery; the steep site has a cliff behind it and views to the Manukau Harbour (gooood).
- Andrew Clifford was the inaugural director from 2013 and left in 2023 to lead the Sarjeant Gallery (Wikipedia).
- Runs the annual Portage Ceramic Awards, described by Wikipedia as New Zealand's leading ceramics prize.
- The operator is Te Uru Waitākere Contemporary Gallery Incorporated, a registered charity (CC41215) registered on 30 June 2008 and previously named Lopdell House Society, renamed on 23 December 2014 (New Zealand Charities Register).

FUNDING
Te Uru Waitakere Contemporary Gallery: charity supported by the Waitākere Ranges Local Board of Auckland Council, with gifts from a Benefactors Collective, sponsors, members and donors
- Te Uru's About page says the gallery is "supported by the Waitakere Ranges Local Board of Auckland Council" and lists joining its benefactors collective, membership, sponsorship and donations to its operational costs as ways to support it (stated by the space). For the redevelopment, the Lopdell House Development Trust "secured funding from Auckland Council, ASB Community Trust" and other funders (stated by the space).
- Income for the year ended 30 June 2024 was NZ$1,170,774 and expenditure NZ$1,133,946; the year to 30 June 2023 showed income of NZ$939,495 and expenditure of NZ$938,328 (New Zealand Charities Register).
- Board: Pamela Peters is chairperson since 21 September 2025, with Tosswell Grumley as treasurer and Claudia Jowitt as secretary (New Zealand Charities Register).

BOTTOM LINE
Te Uru is a charity supported by the Waitākere Ranges Local Board of Auckland Council, with gifts from its benefactors collective, sponsors, donors and members. Its income was NZ$1.17 million in the year to 30 June 2024.
