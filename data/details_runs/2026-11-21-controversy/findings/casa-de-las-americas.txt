ID: casa-de-las-americas

WIDER CONTEXT
- A report by Yoe Suárez for Diario de Cuba and Connectas, carried by El Tiempo on 18 July 2021, says texts by and about the writer Abel Sierra, who lives in the United States, disappeared from La Ventana, the official information site of Casa de las Américas: two interviews, and reviews by two Cuban intellectuals of his essay Del otro lado del espejo, which Casa de las Américas itself awarded a prize in 2006.
- The writer Legna Rodríguez Iglesias, who won the Premio Casa de las Américas for theatre in 2016, searched La Ventana for her name and did not find it (El Tiempo).
- Sierra said La Ventana had also removed texts by Carlos A. Aguilera, Carlos Velazco and Elizabeth Mirabal; all of them live abroad (El Tiempo).
- Sierra received second-hand messages putting the removals down to a migration of the platform to other software, and answered: "Eso es una excusa" ("That is an excuse", translated) (El Tiempo).
- Unverified: that the removals were "un ejercicio sistemático orientado al borrado de los intelectuales cubanos exiliados" ("a systematic exercise aimed at erasing exiled Cuban intellectuals", translated), from Abel Sierra in El Tiempo.
