FONDAZIONE ANTONIO RATTI: Como, Italy
ID: fondazione-antonio-ratti-como
Website: https://fondazioneratti.org/

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit foundation for textiles and contemporary art, based at Villa Sucota, via per Cernobbio 19, Como, on Lake Como (the foundation's website).
- Created in 1985 by the silk industrialist Antonio Ratti to share his passion for art and textiles and to preserve his textile collection; his daughter Annie Ratti has headed it since 2002 (the foundation's website).
- Named for Antonio Ratti, born in Como in 1915, who founded the silk firm Tessitura Serica Antonio Ratti in 1945 and in 1995 funded the Antonio Ratti Textile Center at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York (the foundation's website).
- Villa Sucota is a historic building on Lake Como where the foundation moved in 2010, as part of the Kilometer of Knowledge project; italia.it calls it an 18th-century mansion (the foundation's website; italia.it).
- On site: a textile collection of more than 30,000 pieces, a public library of more than 7,800 volumes (the foundation's website), and a park with permanent works by Gerry Bibby, Jimmie Durham, Liliana Moro, Matt Mullican and Richard Nonas (Irenebrination, Anna Battista, 14 October 2021).
- The Artists' Research Laboratory (CSAV), founded by Annie Ratti in 1995, takes young international artists each year to work with an invited artist who leads the edition: Hilary Lloyd was the invited artist in 2023 (the foundation's art lab page).
- Open Monday to Friday 09:30 to 12:30 and 14:30 to 17:30, closed Saturday and Sunday; admission is free (the foundation's website).

FUNDING
Fondazione Antonio Ratti: private: created by textile industrialist Antonio Ratti, headed by his daughter, with sponsors for projects
- Board of directors: Annie Ratti (chair), Paolo De Santis (vice chair), and members Stefano Baia Curioni, Candido Manzoni, Ambrogio Cremona Ratti, Daria Caccia Dominioni, Viva Ratti Pistoi, Sebastiano Ratti Pistoi and Maria Acqua Marina Ratti Pasek (the foundation's website).
- The Ratti Group is chaired by Antonio Ratti's daughter Donatella; he founded the firm Tessitura Serica Antonio Ratti in 1945 (the foundation's website).
- The exhibition "Antonio's Dream: A Journey Through Art and Textiles" (10 October 2021 to 31 January 2022), organised by the foundation in collaboration with the Comune di Como, was supported by Fondazione Cariplo, Ratti S.p.A., Mondriaan Fonds, Accion Cultural Española, Confindustria Como, Banca Generali and Teatro Sociale di Como, with technical sponsors Flos, Fratelli Rossetti, Como Contract and Nespoli Adriano (e-flux announcement).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation was created in 1985 by the silk industrialist Antonio Ratti and is headed by his daughter Annie Ratti, with several Ratti family members on its board. The 2021 exhibition "Antonio's Dream" was supported by Ratti S.p.A., Fondazione Cariplo, the Mondriaan Fonds, Accion Cultural Española, Confindustria Como, Banca Generali and Teatro Sociale di Como (e-flux).
