ID: musee-d-arts-de-nantes

WIDER CONTEXT
- In September 2015 Blandine Chavanne, director of the museum (then the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Nantes) since 2006, resigned, citing municipal "interference"; after local authorities cancelled a planned Georgia O'Keeffe exhibition she announced she was "slamming the door" on her position (Apollo, 9 September 2015, linking to Ouest-France).
