ID: centro-cultural-la-moneda

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 9 May 2018 the Ministry of Cultures, Arts and Heritage announced that Beatriz Bustos Oyanedel would replace Alejandra Serrano Madrid as executive director after eleven years, and that artist Gonzalo Cienfuegos Browne would replace María Eliana Arntz as board president (Radio Universidad de Chile, 9 May 2018).
- Serrano was removed after a call from then Culture Minister Alejandra Pérez (La Tercera, 8 December 2018). She said Pérez took "una decisión de gobierno arbitraria" (translated: "an arbitrary government decision") "sin nunca sentarse a discutir con los miembros del directorio" (translated: "without ever sitting down to discuss it with the board members"), and that the board "permitió esta irregularidad" (translated: "allowed this irregularity") when the new director was named before the foundation had a president (La Tercera, 8 December 2018).
- Serrano said three of the five people in the exhibitions area had resigned and the education and communications coordinators had been dismissed after her exit: "Las tres áreas principales del centro han sido desmanteladas" (translated: "The centre's three main areas have been dismantled") (La Tercera, 8 December 2018).
