AKADEMIE SCHLOSS SOLITUDE: Stuttgart, Germany
ID: akademie-schloss-solitude-stuttgart
Website: https://www.akademie-solitude.de (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Residency academy for artists and researchers in the late-Baroque Schloss Solitude, on a hill overlooking Stuttgart; a project space is on Römerstrasse in the city centre.
- Opened in 1990 as a foundation under public law, "financially supported by the German province of Baden-Württemberg" (the Akademie's about page). It has hosted about 1,700 young artists from more than 120 countries (the Akademie's own count; German Wikipedia gives more than 1,400 fellows).
- Named for the castle it occupies. Directors: Jean-Baptiste Joly (founding director until 2018), Elke aus dem Moore (until 2022), and Anne Fleckstein since February 2024 (German Wikipedia).
- Building: the castle includes a lecture hall, two workshops, a library, a cafeteria and public areas; a renovated timber barn hosts concerts. Fellows live in furnished studios in the former officers' buildings (the site lists 42 live/work studios and three guest studios; German Wikipedia says 45).
- What is there: fellowships of six, nine or twelve months across seven fields, including visual arts, music and performance, digital arts, architecture and design, literature and academic research (German Wikipedia). German Wikipedia says that from 2020 the Akademie set up a programme with the KfW Stiftung in which a group of seven fellows works on a shared thematic focus over nine months; the Akademie pages read do not mention the KfW Stiftung.
- Visiting: the pages read give no opening hours, admission or accessibility details.

FUNDING
Akademie Schloss Solitude: mainly public: the state of Baden-Württemberg
- The Akademie says it is "financially supported by the German province of Baden-Württemberg" and is a foundation under public law (about page).
- Its Foundation Board includes a representative of the State Ministry and representatives of the Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts and the Ministry of Finance. The Board of Trustees lists two members from the advisory board of Kunststiftung Baden-Württemberg, four members of the Landtag (AfD, Bündnis 90/Die Grünen, SPD, CDU) and the head of Stuttgart's Kulturamt (the Akademie's Foundation Bodies page, undated).
- KfW Stiftung: partner in a thematic programme from 2020 (German Wikipedia only). No amount found.
- No reporting found of the size of the state grant, or of accounts or annual reports in the pages read.

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 30 October 2023 the Akademie published a statement on the situation in the Middle East that condemned the Hamas attack on Israel, especially its targeting of civilians, and expressed solidarity with those who lost family and friends in Israel, Gaza and the other Palestinian territories. The statement does not mention an open letter or a ministry (the Akademie's statement).
- Unverified: fellows circulated a statement with "antiisraelischen Positionen" in October 2023, and a review by Science Minister Petra Olschowski had no consequences for the fellows, from German Wikipedia, which cites the Stuttgarter Zeitung. The Stuttgarter Zeitung piece was not read and the statement's text was not found; the Akademie's own statement and Policy of Respect do not mention it.
- Policy of Respect: the Akademie has a published policy for staff, fellows, jury members, partners, guests and service providers. It names forms of discrimination including antisemitism and anti-Muslim racism, and sets sanctions from formal warnings to termination of fellowships and reports to the police. The page gives no introduction date and does not link the policy to the 2023 letter.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Akademie is the state of Baden-Württemberg, a public funder; no fossil fuel, tobacco, arms or Gulf state funder was found. The weak point is that state ministries sit on the foundation's board, and German Wikipedia, citing the Stuttgarter Zeitung, reports an October 2023 fellows' statement and a ministerial review (Unverified; the Akademie's pages do not mention either). Worth watching: the size of the state grant and the KfW Stiftung funding, which the pages read do not quantify.
