WIDER CONTEXT
- Michel Pidancet, grandson of Jacques Majorelle's second wife, filed a suit against the Fondation Pierre Bergé-Yves Saint Laurent for dedicating the museum's inaugural exhibition in October 2017 to Jacques Majorelle "without consulting with him and his mother" (The Art Newspaper, Sarah Belmont, 26 November 2018).
- In a separate case, the Paris Court of Appeal on 20 November 2018 rejected the claims of Michel Pidancet and Jacqueline Hamann against the foundation and the Jardin Majorelle company over the use of the names "Majorelle" and "Jardin Majorelle", and ordered them to pay a total of 60,000 euros to the foundation and the company (Le360, 22 November 2018); The Art Newspaper reported that the sum covered legal fees.
- In December 2020 the French Ministry of the Interior ordered an inspection by the Inspection générale de l'administration of the accounts of the Fondation Bergé-Yves Saint Laurent, presided over by Madison Cox, after Le Canard enchaîné reported that the foundation was faulted for making profits (Bladi.net, citing Le Canard enchaîné, 12 December 2020).
- Alain Minc, a former member of the foundation's board, wrote in a letter relayed by Le Canard enchaîné that "Près de 50% du chiffre d'affaires échappe à une analyse précise" ("Nearly 50% of turnover escapes precise analysis", translated); a spokesman for Cox said the government had been informed of all the documents required (Bladi.net, 12 December 2020).
- The connection: Jardin Majorelle SCA, which Cox also heads, runs the Musée Yves Saint Laurent and the Berber museum in Marrakech (Bladi.net, 12 December 2020).
