AZERBAIJAN NATIONAL MUSEUM OF ART: Baku, Azerbaijan
ID: azerbaijan-national-museum-of-art
Website: https://www.nationalartmuseum.az (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at Niyazi street 11/9 in central Baku.
- Created in 1936, when the Council of People's Commissars of the Azerbaijan SSR made the art department of the Azerbaijan State Museum an independent museum (Wikipedia); AzerNews gives 1937.
- It occupies two 19th-century buildings, one of them the De Bour mansion, which it entered in 1951; a glass and steel passage joined them in 2013.
- The museum counts more than 18,000 exhibits in six collections (museum history page); AzerNews reported over 3,000 on show in 60 rooms in 2023.
- Shirin Melikova is director; earlier directors include Chingiz Farzaliyev.
- Visiting: Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 10:00 to 18:00, Wednesday 12:00 to 21:00, weekends 11:00 to 19:00, closed Monday; AZN 10 for foreign citizens, AZN 7 for Azerbaijani citizens, AZN 3 for students.

FUNDING
Azerbaijan National Museum of Art: unconfirmed: a state museum by its 2011 national status
- The Cabinet of Ministers gave the State Art Museum "National" status by Decision No. 94 of 10 June 2011 (museum history page).
- In 2023 the museum signed a five-year cooperation memorandum with the State Art Museum of Uzbekistan and worked with the Georgian National Museum and a university in Izmir (AzerNews).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 1993 artworks were stolen from the museum and later recovered (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution by its 2011 status, but which body pays for it is unconfirmed, so the atlas leaves its grade open. Worth watching: the Ministry of Culture's budget lines and any sponsor named on a future exhibition.
