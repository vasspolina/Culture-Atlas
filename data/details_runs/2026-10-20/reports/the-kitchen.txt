THE KITCHEN: New York, USA
ID: the-kitchen
Website: https://thekitchen.org (checked 2026-10-24; the /about/ and /support/ pages opened and name few funders)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit centre for experimental performance, video, music and exhibitions, with its home at 512 West 19th Street in Chelsea closed for renovation since fall 2022 (stated by the Kitchen); programming runs from a temporary satellite at Westbeth Artists Housing, 163B Bank Street, and at partner venues.
- Founded in 1971 by Steina and Woody Vasulka; opened on 15 June 1971 in the kitchen of the Mercer Arts Center in Greenwich Village, moved to SoHo in 1973, and to Chelsea in 1986 (Wikipedia, the Kitchen).
- Named for the Mercer Arts Center kitchen it first rented (Wikipedia).
- The Chelsea building is a former ice house; the Kitchen bought it in 1987 (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: live performance, exhibitions and public programming. Legacy Russell is executive director and chief curator; she was named in 2021, after Tim Griffin (2011 to 2021) (Wikipedia, the Kitchen).
- Visiting: the satellite office is open Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm; programme hours vary. No admission or accessibility details found on the pages opened.

FUNDING
The Kitchen: mainly private philanthropy: foundations, individual donors and a capital campaign
- Mellon Foundation gave $2,000,000 on 8 June 2023 over 36 months for infrastructure and staffing, the largest single gift in the Kitchen's 50-year history (Mellon Foundation grants database, the Kitchen).
- Andy Warhol Foundation gave $80,000 in multi-year programme support, Fall 2023 cycle (Warhol Foundation).
- The Kitchen's About page shows a Bloomberg Philanthropies logo as a supporter. No amount or year is given.
- The renovation is funded by a separate capital campaign. Artforum's headline puts the campaign at $28 million (paywalled; not read). No donors are named on the pages opened.
- Finances (Form 990, Haleakala Inc, the Kitchen's legal name, ProPublica): FY2025 revenue $2.38M, of which $2.14M was contributions, against expenses of $3.41M and total assets of $16.5M. FY2023 revenue was $6.74M against $2.91M expenses.
- Robert Soros chairs the board of directors (the Kitchen's About page).
- After Hurricane Sandy in 2012 flood damage of about $450,000, grants from Time Warner, the Art Dealers Association of America and the Andy Warhol Foundation helped cover what insurance did not (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of a state or city budget line in the pages opened.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kitchen is foundation and individual giving, with Mellon the largest named funder. The names to know are Bloomberg Philanthropies, listed as a supporter with no amount, and board chair Robert Soros; no reporting found of a campaign or objection aimed at either at the Kitchen. Watch the capital campaign's donor list when the Chelsea building reopens.
