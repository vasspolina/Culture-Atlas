MUSEO REVOLTELLA: Trieste, Italy
ID: museo-revoltella
Website: https://museorevoltella.it/english/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Civic gallery of modern art at Via Diaz 27, Trieste, filling a block off Piazza Venezia with three buildings: the Revoltella Palace, the Brunner Palace and the Basevi building, which holds the offices (museum website).
- Baron Pasquale Revoltella (1795 to 1869), a financier and early shareholder of Assicurazioni Generali, left his palace and collection to the city; the museum opened in 1872 (museum website).
- Friedrich Hitzig designed the palace in 1853; the city bought the Brunner Palace in 1907, and its conversion to Carlo Scarpa's design ran from 1968 to 1991 (museum website).
- Collection: 19th century painters such as Francesco Hayez and Domenico Morelli, and 20th century Italians including Giorgio Morandi, Giorgio de Chirico, Lucio Fontana and Alberto Burri (museum website).
- A rebuilt entrance, ticket area and bookshop reopened in May 2025; the auditorium and the Sala Scarpa are next (InTrieste, 20 May 2025).
- Current show: Achille Funi's 1918 "Autoritratto in riva al mare (Chiavari)", newly acquired, with related works, to 11 January 2027 (museum website).
- Visiting: admission is charged, free on the first Sunday of the month (InTrieste, 3 January 2026); a year's card for the civic museums costs 15 euros until 6 January 2027 (museum website).

FUNDING
Museo Revoltella: public: the Comune di Trieste, on the founder's bequest, with regional acquisition grants
- Bequest: Revoltella's codicil of 13 October 1866 left the palace to the city as a perpetual foundation, with 100,000 Austrian florins whose interest was to buy art each year; the city was to bear only taxes, custody and upkeep (museum website).
- Operator: the Comune di Trieste (museum website).
- Governance: the Curatorio, a board named by the city council under the will, last listed in 2017 (museum website).
- Region: the Funi self-portrait was bought with a Regione Friuli Venezia Giulia grant for civic museum acquisitions under its 2025 stability law (museum website).
- European Union: NextGenerationEU money under the PNRR paid for the 2025 entrance works (InTrieste).
- Volunteers: since 1994 the Amici dei Musei "Marcello Mascherini" and Cittaviva have provided about eighty volunteers (museum website).

Red flags: 2022 abolition of the civic museums' director post
- From 1 July 2022 the city put all its museums under an official who also ran tourism and cultural and sports events (open letter to the mayor, 30 July 2022).
- The letter, led by Giuliana Carbi, Veit Heinichen, Ariella Reggio and Nicoletta Zanni with 213 further signatures, asked the mayor to restore the director post (Trieste Contemporanea).
- The city refused, aiming for "meno generali e più colonnelli" (fewer generals, more colonels, own translation) (il manifesto, 25 February 2023).

Red flags: 2023 to 2026 disputes over pay and conditions of museum attendants
- Attendants hired through the contractor Euro & Promos FM spa, founded by Sergio Bini, in 2023 a regional assessor, were paid 5 euros gross an hour, leading to strikes, labour cases and a Corte dei conti file; the company said it kept to the tender (il manifesto, 2023).
- On 19 December 2025 the union Fesica-Confsal filed a legal complaint against the cooperative Cristoforo, which staffs the civic museums with about a hundred workers, many on call, accusing it of fining, suspending and dismissing union members (il Dolomiti, December 2025).
- On 24 March 2026 the union protested at the town hall over unpaid holiday supplements and called the city "complice"; culture assessor Giorgio Rossi said he was not satisfied with the cooperative (il Dolomiti, March 2026).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Two pieces criticise how the city runs the Revoltella and its other museums; neither criticises the collection.
1. Finestre sull'Arte: Federico Giannini, "Sul caso dei musei di Trieste. Il vero problema? Le abitudini delle amministrazioni" (2022)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/opinioni/caso-musei-trieste-problema-abitudini-amministrazioni-locali
- The 2022 change was a reshuffle putting museums under the manager for tourism and sport, which he calls "molto discutibile" (highly questionable, own translation).
- Since Laura Carlini took over a single museums and libraries service in 2017, the museums had lacked directors for at least five years.
- Local authorities ignore ICOM and Ministerial Decree 113/2018, which require a qualified director. Does not name the Revoltella.
2. il manifesto: Marinella Salvi, "Il triste destino del Revoltella" (2023)
URL: https://ilmanifesto.it/il-triste-destino-del-revoltella
- The Revoltella is "oggi senza un direttore" (today without a director, own translation); the city says curators are enough.
- The Curatorio has no role or budget and met once, for the assessor to praise visitor numbers.
- The city buys ready-made shows from Arthemisia and treats museums as tourism.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money. The weak points are governance and labour: no director, a dormant board, and contractors in open dispute with museum staff.
