DARWIN FESTIVAL: Darwin, Australia
ID: darwin-festival-darwin
Website: https://www.darwinfestival.org.au/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Annual multi-arts festival of outdoor concerts, theatre, dance, comedy, film and visual art in Darwin each August (stated by the space).
- First held in July 1979 as the Bougainvillea Festival, a floral festival to brighten the city after Cyclone Tracy; it became the Festival of Darwin in 1996 and Darwin Festival in 2003 (stated by the space).
- Artistic directors: Malcolm Blaylock (2003 to 2008), Jo Duffy, Edwina Lunn, Andrew Ross, Felix Preval (2017 to 2022) and Kate Fell (2023 to 2026); Ben Graetz is named for 2027 to 2030 (stated by the space).
- Venue partners include Brown's Mart, the Darwin Railway Club, Darwin Ski Club and MAGNT (stated by the space).
- Visiting: the 2027 festival runs 4 to 22 August. No reporting found of ticket prices or access details on the pages opened.

FUNDING
Darwin Festival: mainly public with corporate sponsors: Northern Territory Government, City of Darwin and gas company INPEX
- Principal partner: Tourism and Events NT; local government partners are the City of Darwin and the City of Palmerston (stated by the space).
- The festival signed a four-year funding agreement with the Northern Territory Government through the Northern Territory Major Events Company in December 2021; no amount was given (Tourism and Events NT).
- Major partner: INPEX. Star partners include South32, BW Offshore, Beetaloo Energy and McDonald's; distinguished partners include Darwin Port, Mindil Beach Casino Resort, Power & Water and the NT News (stated by the space, 2026).
- Foundation partner: the Tim Fairfax Family Foundation (stated by the space).
- No reporting found of current revenue, grant or sponsorship amounts; annual reviews from 2016 to 2025 are posted on Issuu, but their contents could not be read.

Red flags: fossil fuels (INPEX)
- INPEX, a major partner in 2026, is Japan's largest oil and gas exploration and production company; the Japanese government's JOGMEC holds 18.96% (Wikipedia).
- INPEX runs the Ichthys LNG plant at Bladin Point in Darwin, fed by an 889 km pipeline; first shipment was in October 2018 (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of a campaign aimed at INPEX's sponsorship.

Red flags: Santos sponsorship dropped under pressure
- In June 2022 the "Darwin Festival-Dump Santos" campaign of Larrakia, Tiwi Islands and Central Australian traditional owners, backed by almost 200 artists and creative producers, asked the festival to drop Santos and end fossil fuel sponsorship (NT Independent).
- Santos held naming rights to the opening night concert; the board said Santos had partnered with the festival for 26 years (NT Independent, ABC).
- On 18 October 2022 Fossil Free Arts NT offered A$200,000 over two years if the festival ended the Santos deal; members included the Ethinvest, Graeme Wood, Lenko, McKinnon and Oranges & Sardines foundations (CorpWatch, ABC).
- Santos ended its sponsorship when the contract expired on 31 December 2022, saying the board "now has alternative sponsorship options"; 300 artists and producers and the band Yothu Yindi had joined the campaign (ABC, Canberra Times).

Red flags: gag clause on sponsors
- Performers' contracts barred them from speaking publicly about sponsors, artist Kris Keogh said; Fossil Free Arts NT linked the clause to singer Leah Flanagan's 2018 opening-night criticism of Santos. The festival declined to comment, citing confidentiality (Katherine Times, 2022).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. The criticism on record came from campaign groups and artists' open letters, reported in news outlets, and centred on Santos.

BOTTOM LINE
The festival runs mainly on Northern Territory and city money, with a long list of local sponsors. The weak point is fossil fuel money: Santos left in 2022 under campaign pressure, but INPEX, which runs Darwin's LNG plant, remains a major partner, with South32, BW Offshore and Beetaloo Energy among smaller partners. Worth watching: whether the gag clause on sponsors still stands and whether the campaign turns to INPEX.
