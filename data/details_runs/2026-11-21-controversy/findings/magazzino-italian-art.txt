WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2012 Nancy Olnick and Giorgio Spanu planned to build the museum on their estate on Avery Road. "Neighbors filled a public hearing to protest the scale of the project", which they argued did not fit the residential and rustic character of the road. At the hearing Olnick said, "We feel the same way as the rest of the neighbors" (The Highlands Current, Alison Rooney, 23 September 2016).
- After the hearing the founders put the project on hold and later built it on Route 9. Magazzino's director, Vittorio Calabrese, said: "The decision, after the 2012 situation, was to listen to the community, to relocate and not create any tensions" (The Highlands Current).
