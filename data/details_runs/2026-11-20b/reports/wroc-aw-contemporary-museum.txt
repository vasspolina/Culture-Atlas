WROCŁAW CONTEMPORARY MUSEUM: Wrocław, Poland
ID: wroc-aw-contemporary-museum
Website: https://muzeumwspolczesne.pl/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at pl. Strzegomski 2 in Wrocław, in a cylindrical six-storey air-raid shelter about 25 metres high and 31 metres across, built in 1942 to designs by the Wrocław architect Richard Konwiarz (stated by the museum).
- The shelter served as a field hospital during the 1945 siege of Festung Breslau and after 1989 housed wholesalers, supermarkets, a pub and a second-hand clothes shop (stated by the museum).
- Wrocław City Council created the museum by resolution VI/66/11 of 10 February 2011 as a municipal cultural institution; its aim is to build and show an international collection of contemporary art (notes to the 2024 accounts). It has been in the shelter since 2011 (stated by the museum).
- CH+ Architekci and VROA Architekci converted the shelter, exposing its concrete and adding a glass rooftop café (stated by the museum).
- Directors: Dorota Monkiewicz, co-author of the museum's concept, from 2009 to 2016 (City of Wrocław); Andrzej Jarosz, who beat Monkiewicz in the director's competition and died on 10 December 2019; Sylwia Świsłocka-Karwot from 16 January 2021 (City of Wrocław). Piotr Lisowski, a curator there since December 2016 and acting director from 16 January 2020, was chosen director by a city selection panel by 8 votes to 3 (Wrocław Culture Council minutes, February 2026) and is named as director on the museum's public information page.
- It holds the Jerzy Ludwiński Archive as a permanent display; current shows include "The Adventures of a Person Who Sees", 25 September 2026 to 29 March 2027 (stated by the museum). In 2024 it had 18 full-time posts (notes to the 2024 accounts).
- Visiting: Tuesday and Wednesday 10:00 to 17:00, Thursday to Saturday 12:00 to 20:00, Sunday 12:00 to 18:00, closed Monday. Tickets 20 zł, reduced 10 zł, family 25 zł; free on Thursdays. No visitor parking apart from spaces for wheelchair users (stated by the museum).

FUNDING
Wrocław Contemporary Museum: public: the City of Wrocław, with Culture Ministry grants for acquisitions
- The museum calls itself a municipal cultural institution of the City of Wrocław (partners page); the President of Wrocław appoints and dismisses its director (public information page).
- 2024 accounts: subsidies of PLN 4,996,626 out of revenue of PLN 5,130,584 from sales and subsidies, plus PLN 219,903 of other operating income; costs were PLN 4,552,813 and the year closed with a net profit of PLN 812,884. In 2023 subsidies were PLN 3,679,325 (profit and loss account 2024).
- Earned income in 2024 was small: PLN 105,585 from tickets, PLN 10,395 from publications, PLN 2,618 from workshops and tours and PLN 7,664 from rent (notes to the 2024 accounts).
- The building and the administrative building, with their land, are used under lease or similar agreements, not owned (notes to the 2024 accounts).
- The Ministry of Culture and National Heritage co-finances "Expanding the International Collection of Contemporary Art" from its Culture Promotion Fund (partners page; translation mine). Works of art on the books rose by PLN 706,667 in 2024 to PLN 14.6 million (notes to the 2024 accounts).
- Media patrons named on the partners page include TVP 3 Wrocław, TVP Kultura and Radio Wrocław Kultura.

BOTTOM LINE
A small city museum that lives almost entirely on public subsidy, with ministry money for its collection and little earned income; every funder it names is public.
