HARTWARE MEDIENKUNSTVEREIN (HMKV), NOW KUNSTHALLE DORTMUND: Dortmund, Germany
ID: hmkv-hartware-medienkunstverein-dortmund
Website: https://www.kunsthalledortmund.de

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition venue for contemporary and media art on level 3 of the Dortmunder U (Leonie-Reygers-Terrasse, 44137 Dortmund), with the special exhibition area on level 6 also used (Kunsthalle Dortmund website, German Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1996 in Dortmund as a registered association by Iris Dressler, Hans D. Christ and Jan-Peter E. R. Sonntag; Inke Arns has been artistic director since 2005 (German Wikipedia).
- The association's name was Hartware MedienKunstVerein (HMKV). On 28 July 2026 the City of Dortmund reported that it is now called Kunsthalle Dortmund and that the programme's course stays unchanged; the Hartware MedienKunstVerein e.V. operates the Kunsthalle Dortmund (City of Dortmund, Kunsthalle Dortmund website).
- Earlier venues included a 400 square metre space on Güntherstraße (2001 to 2002), the Museum am Ostwall and, from 2003 to 2010, the PHOENIX Halle, a 2,200 square metre building from 1895; it has been in the Dortmunder U, a former brewery tower, since May 2010 (German Wikipedia).
- Programme: exhibitions, a monthly Video of the Month and public tours of the current exhibition on Sundays and public holidays (Kunsthalle Dortmund website).
- Admission to the exhibitions on level 3 is free (City of Dortmund, 28 July 2026).
- The chair of the association is Stefan Hilterhaus (City of Dortmund, 28 July 2026).

FUNDING
HMKV / Kunsthalle Dortmund: publicly funded: State of North Rhine-Westphalia and City of Dortmund
- The association names the Ministry of Culture and Science of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia, the Kulturbetriebe Stadt Dortmund and the Dortmunder U as its funders (Kunsthalle Dortmund website).
- On 2 March 2018 the Regierungspräsident of Arnsberg handed the association a grant notice for 200,000 euros for its 2018 annual programme, including the exhibition "Computer Grrrls" and the digitisation of its archive of about 90 exhibitions (Bezirksregierung Arnsberg, press release of 6 March 2018).
- Earlier project support came from the Kunststiftung NRW (the 2011 JUMP prize), the Kulturstiftung des Bundes (co-support of "The Oil Show") and the European Commission (funding of "Arctic Perspective") (German Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind HMKV is public: the State of North Rhine-Westphalia, the City of Dortmund through its Kulturbetriebe and the Dortmunder U, with project funds from Kunststiftung NRW, the Kulturstiftung des Bundes and the European Commission. It is a registered association.
