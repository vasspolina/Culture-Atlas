MUSEO DE ARTE DE EL SALVADOR (MARTE): San Salvador, El Salvador
ID: museo-de-arte-de-el-salvador-marte-san-salvador
Website: https://museomarte.org/

OVERVIEW
- Private non-profit museum of Salvadoran art at Final Avenida de la Revolución, Colonia San Benito, San Salvador, beside the Monumento a la Revolución.
- Opened to the public on 22 May 2003 (museum site); the Asociación Museo de Arte de El Salvador runs it, and English Wikipedia says the association was established in 2000. The museum says it has received more than 160,000 visitors since opening.
- Designed by Salvadoran architect Salvador Choussy (born 1947) on a 6,520 m2 plot; English Wikipedia gives the construction cost as US$1.8 million.
- The building has 2,968 m2 of built area and five exhibition rooms totalling 1,208 m2: three hold the permanent Salvadoran collection and two host temporary shows. A multipurpose hall and a new exhibition room were added in 2007.
- Its design links to the 1950s Monumento a la Revolución by Óscar Reyes and Kurt Schulze, and the museum holds sketches and plans for Francisco Zúñiga's 1956 Monumento a la Constitución, donated by the Fundación Zúñiga Laborde.
- Programmes: exhibitions of national and international artists, virtual exhibitions, guided thematic tours, creative labs, teaching guides, teacher training and family activities. Wikipedia lists the Lunes Musicales concert series since 2015.

FUNDING
Museo de Arte de El Salvador (MARTE): private non-profit funded by contributions from individuals, companies and organisations, as the museum states
- The museum states that it works thanks to contributions from individuals, companies and organisations, and invites anyone who loves art to support its programmes through Amigos del Museo (stated by the museum).
- Online donations of US$10, US$30, US$50 and US$100 are taken through Wompi; the museum says US$50 pays for a tour for a group of 25 people without resources and US$100 pays transport for a student group.
- Companies and institutions can become sponsors by writing to the museum, which issues donation receipts on request through its accounting office (stated by the museum).
- The museum's board is led by President Michel Langlais; Eugenia Lindo is Executive Director and Jaime Izaguirre is Artistic Director (museum site).
- English Wikipedia names Eli Lecha de Lindo as the first president of the association; Spanish Wikipedia says Eugenia Lindo has been executive director since 2020.

BOTTOM LINE
MARTE is a private non-profit museum that says it runs on contributions from individuals, companies and organisations. The Asociación Museo de Arte de El Salvador runs it, and Michel Langlais is president of its board.
