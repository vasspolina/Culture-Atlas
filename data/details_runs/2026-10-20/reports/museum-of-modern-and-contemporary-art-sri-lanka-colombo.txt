MUSEUM OF MODERN AND CONTEMPORARY ART SRI LANKA: Colombo, Sri Lanka
ID: museum-of-modern-and-contemporary-art-sri-lanka-colombo
Website: https://mmca-srilanka.org

OVERVIEW
- Museum of 20th and 21st century art, design, craft, architecture, performance and the moving image from Sri Lanka. It has no fixed location and plans to use different spaces project by project (museum website).
- A Founding Committee of artists, museum experts, art historians, collectors and supporters formed in 2016; the museum was established as a not-for-profit in 2019 and launched in December 2019 with the exhibition "One Hundred Thousand Small Tales", curated by Sharmini Pereira (museum website; John Keells Foundation).
- Venue: from November 2021 the museum used a 3,800 square foot space on the second floor of Crescat Boulevard, Colombo 3, hosted through a partnership with the John Keells Holdings Property Group (ft.lk, 17 December 2021).
- Leadership: Ajit Gunewardene chairs the Museum Committee; an eight-member Board of Directors meets with the leadership team every other month; Sharmini Pereira is Chief Curator and Sandev Handy Senior Curator (museum website).
- Programmes: trilingual education for students and teachers; a collection programme of cataloguing, storage and a collection policy is under way (museum website).

FUNDING
Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art Sri Lanka: privately funded non-profit: a corporate foundation, a bank and individual patrons
- John Keells Foundation: primary sponsor of the museum from its December 2019 launch, as the foundation states (page dated 31 March 2020). The John Keells Foundation website calls the foundation the Group's CSR entity and says the Group allocates a minimum of 1% of annual profit to social responsibility initiatives.
- Nations Trust Bank: major benefactor for three years, 2022 to 2025 (The Island, "NTB partners with Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art", 17 July 2022).
- 2022 exhibition "Encounters" (11 February to 28 August 2022): supported by the European Union, Foundation for Arts Initiatives, John Keells Foundation and Asian Hotels and Properties PLC (The Island, 26 October 2022).
- Founding Patrons: 19 entries for 2026, including Ajit and Chandani Gunewardene, Abbas and Alnaas Esufally, and Kumar and Ranmali Mirchandani (museum website).
- Accounts: the museum's website posts its 2025/2026 annual audit report; Ernst and Young was appointed for the 2021 audit. Registered under company number GA00216102.

WIDER CONTEXT
- The Island (6 August 2025, "'City of Dreams' opened: Govt. urged to enact dedicated casino law") reports that the Finance Ministry issued the City of Dreams Sri Lanka casino licence in 2023. It quotes Harsha de Silva, chair of the Committee on Public Finance and an SJB MP, urging the government to enact a casino law and set up a gaming authority "without further delay", and faulting the earlier government for not creating a dedicated regulator. His criticism concerns the licensing and regulation, not the museum or the foundation.
- Connection: the article names John Keells PLC as Melco's local partner in the resort, managing the Cinnamon Life hotel; Wikipedia says John Keells Holdings partnered with Melco in April 2024 to run the gaming facility, which opened on 2 August 2025. John Keells Holdings is the Group behind the museum's primary sponsor, and its website lists eight sectors: Leisure, Transportation, Retail, Consumer Foods, Property, Financial Services, IT & Innovation and Plantation Services (keells.com). The museum and the foundation are not named in the piece.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a private non-profit backed by the John Keells Foundation, Nations Trust Bank and individual Founding Patrons, and it posts an audit report. The sponsor's parent group, John Keells Holdings, is a diversified group and Melco's partner in the City of Dreams Sri Lanka casino resort, which opened in August 2025.
