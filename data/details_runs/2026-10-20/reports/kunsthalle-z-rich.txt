KUNSTHALLE ZÜRICH: Zürich, Switzerland
ID: kunsthalle-z-rich
Website: https://www.kunsthallezurich.ch

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art institution in the Löwenbräu complex at Limmatstrasse 270, Zürich, where it has been since 1996 (stated by the Kunsthalle).
- Founded in 1985, run by the Verein Kunsthalle Zürich. Directors: Bernhard Mendes Bürgi (1990 to 2001), Beatrix Ruf (2001 to 2014), Daniel Baumann (2015 to 2024) and Fanny Hauser (since 2025).
- Governance: the Verein board is chaired by Michael Ringier, with Mirjam Staub-Bisang (treasurer), Gerd Schepers (vice-president), Barbara Gerber (representative of the City of Zürich), Chantal Blatzheim, Anette Bollag-Rothschild, Jürg Haller, Maja Hoffmann, Florian Rajki, Mark A. Reutter and Barbara Weber; the annual report names Audita GmbH as auditor.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00. Admission CHF 12, reduced CHF 8, combined ticket with Museum Haus Konstruktiv CHF 20; free for members, children up to 16, students of the ZHdK and F+F, Thursday evenings 17:00 to 20:00 and holders of ZürichCARD, Raiffeisen MemberPlus, the Swiss Museum Pass and KulturLegi.

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Zürich: public subsidy and private support: City of Zürich and Canton Zürich subsidies of CHF 1,233,200, patrons and foundations
- 2025 accounts (annual report 2025): total income CHF 2,422,103 (2024: CHF 2,487,363).
- Subsidy of the City of Zürich: CHF 1,033,200 in 2025 (2024: CHF 1,020,900). Subsidy of the Canton Zürich: CHF 200,000 in both years.
- Private donations (Zuwendungen Private): CHF 350,000 in 2025 (2024: CHF 340,000). Grant from the Stiftung Kunsthalle: CHF 225,500 in 2025 (2024: CHF 227,500). Project contributions: CHF 133,998 in 2025 (2024: CHF 239,608).
- Own income in 2025: gala and education income CHF 284,549, membership fees CHF 111,080, admissions CHF 46,087 and shop sales CHF 31,632. The year closed with a surplus of CHF 8,798.
- The annual report thanks for support of its exhibitions and programmes: Adam Mickiewicz Institute, Emalin, Ernst Göhner Stiftung, Ernst und Olga Gubler-Hablützel Stiftung, Evelyn Lingg, Foksal Gallery Foundation, Galerie Neu, LEAP Art Foundation, Luma Stiftung, Max Kohler Stiftung, Mirjam Staub-Bisang und Martin Bisang, Philaneo, Reena Spaulings Fine Art, Ringier AG, Sadie Coles HQ and the Swedish Embassy in Bern.
- Corporate patrons listed for 2025: Bank Julius Bär & Co. AG, Burger Collection, E.A.T. Engadin Art Talks Stiftung, Hauser & Wirth, Hesta AG, Laboratoire Osler GmbH and Migros-Genossenschafts-Bund, alongside a list of named individual patrons.

BOTTOM LINE
Kunsthalle Zürich is financed about half by public money: the City of Zürich and Canton Zürich together paid CHF 1,233,200 of CHF 2,422,103 income in 2025. The rest comes from patrons, foundations, the Stiftung Kunsthalle, galleries and its own income, all itemised in the annual report, which names Audita GmbH as auditor.
