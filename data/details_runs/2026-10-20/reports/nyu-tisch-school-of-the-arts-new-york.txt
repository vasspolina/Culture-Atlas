OVERVIEW
- Performing and media arts school of New York University at 721 Broadway, at Waverly Place in Greenwich Village, Lower Manhattan (Wikipedia).
- Founded on 17 August 1965 as the School of the Arts at New York University, to give "conservatory training in theater and film in the context of a research university" (Wikipedia).
- Named in 1982 for Laurence and Preston Robert Tisch, after Dean David Oppenheim solicited a donation from them that made possible the purchase and renovation of 721 Broadway (Wikipedia).
- Building: 721 Broadway; since 2013 three programmes have been housed at NYU's Brooklyn tech hub, MAGNET (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: institutes for Performing Arts, Film and Television (Maurice Kanbar Institute) and Emerging Media, the Clive Davis Institute of Recorded Music, a dual MFA/MBA with NYU Stern, high school and continuing education programmes (Wikipedia). Rubén Polendo is listed as dean (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: it is a teaching school; no reporting found of public opening hours or admission.

FUNDING
NYU Tisch School of the Arts: university school with named donors: part of NYU, with a family-named gift and foundation grants
- Parent: NYU. No reporting found of the school's own budget or accounts.
- Tisch family: the 1982 gift from Laurence and Preston Robert Tisch paid for 721 Broadway. A cited headline in Wikipedia gives the gift as "7.5 Million". The page does not give the amount in its text.
- Dean's Council: members include Steve Tisch and other Tisch relatives (Washington Square News). A 2016 school release, read as a search extract, names Andrew, Jonathan and Steve Tisch as longtime members.
- Google: a three-year grant to the Interactive Technology Program for a storytelling lab (Tisch institutional giving page, read as a search extract; no amount or year found).
- Steven & Alexandra Cohen Foundation: scholarship funds for the Tisch Summer High School programme since 2006, nearly 100 full scholarships, and a 2009 gift that launched a Talent Identification Process (Tisch giving page, read as a search extract).
- HFPA Charitable Trust: post-production awards for Graduate Film students and stipends for Moving Image Archiving and Preservation students (Tisch giving page, read as a search extract).
- Tisch Asia, the school's Singapore campus (opened 2007, closed after the 2013 decision): Singapore's Economic Development Board disbursed S$11.68 million in loans and S$5.3 million in grants, and NYU contributed over S$20 million in subsidies from 2007 to 2011 (Singapore Ministry of Trade and Industry replies of 14 January and 5 February 2013).

- NYU: an NYU spokesperson did not respond to the student paper's requests for comment. No reporting found of any NYU action on his council seat.

BOTTOM LINE
The documented funders are NYU, the Tisch family and named foundations and companies; no money from Epstein or a flagged industry is documented. The weak point is the Dean's Council seat of Steve Tisch, whose emails with Epstein were released by the Department of Justice and who has not been accused of a crime in them.
