NEW YORK ACADEMY OF ART: New York, USA
ID: new-york-academy-of-art-new-york
Website: https://nyaa.edu/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Graduate school for figurative art at 111 Franklin Street, Tribeca (school website).
- Teaching began in 1980 as the New York Drawing Association, with collector Stuart Pivar as key funder; later accounts list Andy Warhol as a founder and funder (Wikipedia).
- Since 1990 it has occupied a five-storey, 42,000 square foot building designed by Benjamin Warner in 1861; TRA Studio renovated it after a 2001 fire (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: a two-year MFA, a one-year certificate, a summer residency and continuing education (school website); about 100 students (Wikipedia).
- Paul R. Provost became president in April 2025, succeeding David Kratz (Wikipedia).
- Public events: the Tribeca Ball, first held in 1994, and the Take Home a Nude auction at Sotheby's (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
New York Academy of Art: privately funded: tuition, donations and fundraising events
- Form 990 for the year to June 2025: revenue $8,845,834, of which program services $6,337,108 and contributions $2,634,384; fundraising ran at a net loss of $608,856; expenses $10,011,292 (ProPublica).
- Trustees in that filing include chair Eileen Guggenheim and Brooke Shields (ProPublica).
- Warhol left the school a bequest after his death in 1987 that let it buy the Franklin Street building, and the Warhol Foundation had given it more than $1 million by 2004 (Wikipedia).
- Van Cleef & Arpels was title sponsor of the 15 April 2025 Tribeca Ball (Tribeca Citizen, sponsored post by the Academy).

Red flags: Jeffrey Epstein's board seat (1987 to 1994) and his gifts after his 2008 plea
- Epstein came to the Academy through Pivar and sat on the board from 1987 to 1994 (Academy statement, printed by Artforum, 2019).
- After his Florida prison sentence he bought tickets to one fundraising event in 2012 and two in 2014, and gave $30,000 in 2014 (same statement).
- In August 2020 the board said it would give $30,000, the amount Epstein gave in scholarship support, to a charity for victims of sexual assault (The Art Newspaper).
- In March 2026 the Academy sent $65,900 in Epstein-linked donations to Girls Educational and Mentoring Services (GEMS), saying this redirected all his money received after his 2008 conviction; board members matched the sum (USA Art News).
- The board's email to alumni admitted "serious failures in judgment and governance" and announced an ethics committee on donor policies (Türkiye Today, citing The New York Times).
- Guggenheim retired as chair on 1 April 2026, a month early, and stays on the board (USA Art News).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Unverified: alumna Maria Farmer alleges that Guggenheim, then dean of students, introduced her to Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell at her 1995 thesis show and pressed her to sell them a painting cheaply. Guggenheim denies introducing them (Artforum; USA Art News).
- In May 2020 a petition called for Guggenheim's resignation as chair (Wikipedia). A law-firm investigation released in June 2020 concluded that "critical aspects" of Farmer's allegations against Guggenheim were untrue, and the board backed its chair. After Ippolita Rostagno, Alina Lundry and Valerie Cooper resigned from the board on 30 July, the executive committee apologised to Farmer in August 2020 (The Art Newspaper).
- USA Art News, citing an FBI document in the Epstein files, reports that the FBI interviewed Guggenheim in 2007 in a federal investigation into the sexual exploitation of minors.
- These concern the chair and the same Epstein link.

BOTTOM LINE
The school's regular funding carries no flag. Its documented fault, admitted by its board, is Epstein as board member and as donor after his conviction; watch what the ethics committee changes.
