CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. The Spectator: Michela Wrong, "The truth about the Rwandan genocide" (2024)
URL: https://spectator.com/article/the-truth-about-the-rwandan-genocide/
- Wrong credits the memorial's power and its remembering of victims as individuals, and argues that "The problem is not what the memorial shows, but what it omits."
- She argues that it gives little time to the Rwandan Patriotic Front's invasion before the genocide and leaves out the 1995 Kibeho massacre and the killings of Hutu refugees in the DRC in 1996 and 1997.
- She argues that the memorial "has served to airbrush an inconvenient history" and cements a government narrative that undermines reconciliation.
- A comment from the Aegis Trust on the page says her critique "overlooks its role as birthplace of peace and values education".
