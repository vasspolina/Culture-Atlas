ID: thalia-theater

Red flags: 2024 boycott call over a season with almost no women
- The association Pro Quote Bühne called for a boycott of Joachim Lux's farewell season at the Thalia Theater; taz described the season as "einen fast frauenfreien Spielplan" ("an almost women-free programme", translated) and counted one work by a woman and one woman director across 13 premieres at the two venues (taz, 25 April 2024).
- Lux told the Hamburger Abendblatt that this was "gar kein Mangel an Wertschätzung" ("not a lack of appreciation at all", translated) and that the season was meant "langjährige Arbeitsbeziehungen noch einmal zu runden und abzuschließen" ("to round off and close long-standing working relationships", translated) (as reported by taz, 25 April 2024).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In December 2015 the director Alvis Hermanis left the Thalia Theater in protest against Germany's openness to immigrants and refugees, and the theatre had to drop a play whose premiere was planned for the following year; another Hermanis production, Late neighbors, stayed in the programme (LSM, 7 December 2015).
- Thalia manager Joachim Lux said the theatre supports humanitarian assistance to immigrants and called the episode an example of the deep political differences in Germany (LSM, 7 December 2015).

- The commentary calls the boycott justified and says the programme alone is reason enough for it.
- It argues that Lux's explanation is worse than the programme, since the working relationships he wants to close already left out women writers and directors.
- It says the arts pages tolerated this for 16 years and that Lux should refrain from further work as a theatre official worldwide.
