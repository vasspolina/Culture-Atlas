THE KITCHEN: New York, USA
ID: the-kitchen
Website: https://thekitchen.org (checked 2026-10-24; the /about/ and /support/ pages opened and name few funders)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit centre for experimental performance, video, music and exhibitions, with its home at 512 West 19th Street in Chelsea closed for renovation since fall 2022 (stated by the Kitchen); programming runs from a temporary satellite at Westbeth Artists Housing, 163B Bank Street, and at partner venues.
- Founded in 1971 by Steina and Woody Vasulka; opened in 1971 in the kitchen of the Mercer Arts Center in Greenwich Village, moved to SoHo in 1973, and to Chelsea in 1986 (Wikipedia, the Kitchen).
- Named for the Mercer Arts Center kitchen it first rented (Wikipedia).
- The Chelsea building is a former ice house; the Kitchen bought it in 1987 (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: live performance, exhibitions and public programming. Legacy Russell is executive director and chief curator; she was named in 2021, after Tim Griffin (2011 to 2021) (Wikipedia, the Kitchen).
- Visiting: office hours are Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm; programme hours vary. No admission or accessibility details found on the pages opened.

FUNDING
The Kitchen: mainly private philanthropy: foundations and a capital campaign
- Mellon Foundation gave $2,000,000 to Haleakala, Inc. on 8 June 2023 over 36 months for infrastructure and staffing (Mellon Foundation grants database). The Kitchen's press page calls it the largest single gift in its 50-year history (stated by the Kitchen).
- Andy Warhol Foundation gave $80,000 in multi-year programme support, Fall 2023 cycle (Warhol Foundation).
- The About page has a logo under "Thank you to our supporters" that links to bloomberg.org. The image carries no text credit, and no amount or year is given.
- The renovation is funded by a separate capital campaign. An Artforum article whose address reads "launches 28 million capital campaign" is paywalled and was not read. No donors are named on the pages opened.
- Finances (Form 990 of Haleakala Inc, the Kitchen's legal name, EIN 13-2829756, ProPublica): FY2025 revenue $2.38M, of which $2.14M was contributions, against expenses of $3.41M and total assets of $16.5M. FY2023 revenue was $6.74M against $2.91M expenses.
- Robert Soros is listed as chairperson (the Kitchen's About page).
- After Hurricane Sandy in 2012 flood damage of about $450,000, grants from Time Warner, the Art Dealers Association of America and the Andy Warhol Foundation helped cover what insurance did not (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of a state or city budget line in the pages opened.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kitchen is foundation giving, with Mellon the largest named funder. The weak point is thin disclosure: a logo linked to Bloomberg Philanthropies on the About page has no amount or year, and no capital campaign donors are named. No reporting found of a campaign or objection aimed at Bloomberg at the Kitchen. Watch the capital campaign's donor list when the Chelsea building reopens.
