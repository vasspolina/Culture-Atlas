INSTITUTE OF CONTEMPORARY ART INDIAN OCEAN (ICAIO): Port Louis, Mauritius
ID: institute-of-contemporary-art-indian-ocean-icaio

OVERVIEW
- Gallery at 51 Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Street, Port Louis, in a historic stone building (Lonely Planet France).
- Founded in May 2015 by Salim Currimjee, a painter, architect and collector (Lonely Planet France). Boston College describes it as "an independent, nonprofit foundation that is a platform for art education and public art programming in Mauritius" (Boston College news).
- Its stated aim is "faire de Maurice le carrefour artistique et culturel de l'océan Indien", in Lonely Planet's French text (translation: to make Mauritius the artistic and cultural crossroads of the Indian Ocean).
- Exhibitions change every quarter and show Mauritian, Indian and South African artists (Lonely Planet France). In 2020 it held the solo exhibition "Walk of Life" by the artist Portia Zvavahera (Kettle's Yard).
- Currimjee also co-curated "Indian Ocean Current: Six Artistic Narratives" at the McMullen Museum of Art, Boston College, in 2020 (Boston College news).
- Visiting: open Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:00 (Lonely Planet France).

FUNDING
Institute of Contemporary Art Indian Ocean: nonprofit foundation: set up by Salim Currimjee
- Founder: Currimjee established the institute in 2015 (Boston College news, Lonely Planet France).

BOTTOM LINE
The institute is a nonprofit foundation founded in 2015 by the painter, architect and collector Salim Currimjee.
