OVERVIEW
- Exhibition space for international and local contemporary art at Lothringer Strasse 13 in Haidhausen, near Weissenburger Platz, with about 700 square metres for changing group shows (muenchen.de; City of Munich).
- Founded in 1980 as the "Künstlerwerkstatt" in a former machine factory; more than 200 exhibitions by over 1,000 artists followed, among them Louise Lawler, Fischli/Weiss, Albert Oehlen and Gregor Schneider (German Wikipedia).
- The exhibition rooms are in a former engine-grinding workshop (Motorenschleiferei) in a rear building; the front building holds a research and information room also used for events (City of Munich).
- From 1980 to 1998 artists, guest curators and Kulturreferat staff organised the shows; in 1998 the city adopted a concept of changing independent curators, each for about two years (German Wikipedia).
- From 1997 to 2010 part of the building held the video and art archive "Spiegel", and from 1992 to 1998 the upper floors housed the city-funded Medienlabor München (German Wikipedia).
- The street-side project room lothringer13_laden was renamed Lothringer13_Florida in 2014 and moved to the rear building; a collective team runs it for interdisciplinary and media projects (German Wikipedia).
- Kalas Liebfried has been artistic director since April 2025 (Openart München); the directorship is limited to a term of up to five years (e-flux).
- Visiting: Wednesday to Friday 12:00 to 20:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, closed Monday and Tuesday, open only while an event runs; admission free, step-free access (muenchen.de).

FUNDING
Lothringer 13 Halle: public: a cultural institution of the Kulturreferat of the City of Munich
- The Halle is a cultural institution of the Kulturreferat (culture department) of the Landeshauptstadt München, a municipal art space run by the city (City of Munich; e-flux). The Kulturreferent is Marek Wiechers (City of Munich organigram, March 2026).
- Entry is free, so there is no ticket income (muenchen.de).

BOTTOM LINE
The municipal money behind the Halle is clean. Nothing in the funding draws a red flag.
