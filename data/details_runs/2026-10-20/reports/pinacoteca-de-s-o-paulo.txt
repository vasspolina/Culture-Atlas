PINACOTECA DE SÃO PAULO: São Paulo, Brazil
ID: pinacoteca-de-s-o-paulo
Website: https://pinacoteca.org.br/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum of the State of São Paulo in three buildings: Pina Luz (Praça da Luz 2), Pina Estação (Largo General Osório 66) and Pina Contemporânea (Avenida Tiradentes 273).
- Founded in 1905 by the São Paulo state government, with roots in the Liceu de Artes e Ofícios of 1873 (Wikipedia). It has been run since 2006 by the social organisation Associação Pinacoteca Arte e Cultura (APAC) (Wikipedia).
- Pina Luz: designed by Ramos de Azevedo with Domiciano Rossi, built from 1897 and partly opened in 1900; Paulo Mendes da Rocha led its 1993 to 1998 renovation, which won the Mies van der Rohe Prize for Latin America (Wikipedia).
- Pina Estação: built in 1914 as a warehouse of the Companhia Sorocabana, also by Ramos de Azevedo; the Pinacoteca began managing the former DOPS building in 2003. Pina Contemporânea opened on 4 March 2023, designed by Arquitetos Associados (Wikipedia).
- On site: exhibitions, courses and activities in each building.
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Monday, 10 am to 6 pm, last entry 5 pm; closed on 25 October 2026 for the elections. Full ticket R$40, half price R$20; free on Saturdays and on the second Sunday of each month; free for children up to 10, over-60s, public school groups and people with disabilities plus one companion.

FUNDING
Pinacoteca de São Paulo: mixed public and corporate: São Paulo state management contract, B3 as maintainer, sponsors and tax-incentive law
- The museum is run by APAC under a management contract with the state culture secretariat; APAC's site lists Contrato de Gestão 001/2018 and Contrato de Gestão 005/2023 (APAC). No amounts are shown on the pages opened.
- B3, the Brazilian stock exchange, is the museum's maintainer and pays for the free second Sundays (Pinacoteca ticket page).
- InfinitePay is master sponsor for all of 2026, announced 14 April 2026; the amount was not disclosed (Marcas Mais).
- Itaú customers get 50% off tickets paid with an Itaú card (Pinacoteca ticket page). The sponsors page under the heading "Patrocinadores da Pinacoteca em 2026" shows a single image panel (file name marked "jul26") with no alt text, so no company is named in its text.
- Lei Rouanet, the federal culture tax-incentive law, appears as a logo link in the seals section of the museum's sponsors page, without amounts.
- Patron programme: 130 named patrons and one anonymous patron, all individuals or pairs of individuals, listed on the site (updated 11 April 2025), including Alexandre Birman and Vera Lafer. No amounts are given.
- No reporting found of Equinor or Ultra as sponsors in the text of any page opened; the sponsor panel is an image whose logos could not be read.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little criticism of the museum. The one piece found is an opinion column that largely praises the programme and does not examine its funding.
1. ArtReview: Oliver Basciano, "São Paulo's Pinacoteca Mounts a Challenge to Bolsonarista Politics" (2021)
URL: https://artreview.com/pinacoteca-rehang-challenges-brazilian-museum-politics/
- Argues that the collection rehang led by director Jochen Volz answers the Bolsonaro government, with galleries on dictatorship, landscape, gender and Indigenous life and race running through several rooms, and that the rehang "is at its most powerful" where the museum faces up to its own historic complicity.
- Concedes that some readers will object to artworks serving as "plot devices" for a narrative set by curators.
- Names no funders or sponsors; not a critique of the museum's money.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's money comes from a state contract, the stock exchange B3, named corporate sponsors and tax-incentive law, and no source opened ties any of them to a documented controversy. The weak points are that the 2026 sponsor panel is an image with no text, that no amounts are published on the pages opened, and that Equinor and Ultra, named as fossil fuel sponsors elsewhere, could not be found in any text source, so no fossil fuel flag is given.
