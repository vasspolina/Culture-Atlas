FUNDACIÓN CASA WABI: Puerto Escondido, Mexico
ID: fundación-casa-wabi-puerto-escondido
Website: https://casawabi.org/en/

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit civil association that connects contemporary art with 14 local communities on the Oaxacan coast through programmes based in Puerto Escondido; it also runs Casa Wabi Sabino in Mexico City and Casa Nano in Tokyo (Casa Wabi).
- Founded by the Mexican artist Bosco Sodi, who has lived in New York for years (Forbes México, Carlos López Fe, "Él es el guerrillero del arte", 29 August 2016); the Puerto Escondido building opened in late 2014 (Dezeen, Amy Frearson, 14 January 2016).
- Named for the Japanese philosophy of wabi-sabi, which finds beauty in the simple, the imperfect and the unconventional (Casa Wabi).
- Designed by the Japanese architect Tadao Ando, with a concrete wall 312 metres long on a site that shares 550 metres of coastline (Dezeen).
- The campus has a multipurpose palapa, six bedrooms, eight studios, a screening room and a 450 square metre gallery (Casa Wabi).
- Five programmes: residencies, exhibitions, clay, cinema and a mobile library (Casa Wabi).
- Visits are guided tours by advance reservation: Monday to Friday at 10:00, 12:00 and 16:00 and Saturday and Sunday at 10:00, 12:00 and 14:00. The donation is 600 Mexican pesos, with half price for Mexican students and teachers and INAPAM cardholders, and free entry for children under 12 and Oaxaca residents; most of the tour is outdoors on unpaved paths (Casa Wabi).

FUNDING
Fundación Casa Wabi: artist-founded non-profit; public funding and a bank sponsorship reported in 2016
- Bosco Sodi is named as the client of the Ando building (Dezeen). Sodi told Forbes México that as a successful artist he has an obligation to give back, and that this is why he created Casa Wabi.
- The Art Newspaper (Jane Morris, "Artist retreat expands with new Mexico City space", 25 October 2016) reported that the foundation receives funding from Mexico's ministry of culture and the state of Oaxaca, and has sponsorship from Deutsche Bank.
- Monocle (Alexa Firmenich, "Shore start", October 2015) reported that the foundation "wholly funds each programme".
- The foundation states that it is a non-profit and that all donations are tax-deductible (Casa Wabi).

BOTTOM LINE
Casa Wabi is an artist-founded non-profit started by Bosco Sodi, with a building by Tadao Ando. The Art Newspaper reported in 2016 that it receives funding from Mexico's ministry of culture and the state of Oaxaca and has sponsorship from Deutsche Bank. Visitors pay a 600 peso donation for a guided tour.
