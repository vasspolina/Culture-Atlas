TEATRO REAL: Madrid, Spain
ID: teatro-real-madrid
Website: https://www.teatroreal.es

OVERVIEW
- Madrid's royal opera house, known as "El Real" (Wikipedia).
- Ferdinand VII began construction on 23 April 1818 and Queen Isabella II opened it on 19 November 1850 with Donizetti's "La favorite"; a Royal Order of 7 May 1850 referred to it as the Teatro de Oriente (Wikipedia).
- The original architects were Antonio López Aguado and Custodio Moreno (Wikipedia).
- A Royal Order halted activities in December 1925 after construction of the Madrid Metro damaged the building. It reopened as a concert hall on 13 October 1966, closed again after a final concert on 13 October 1988, was remodelled from 2 January 1991 and reopened for opera on 11 October 1997 (Wikipedia).
- The programme includes opera, the Voces del Real concert series, Flamenco Real, cinema broadcasts of opera, guided visits, courses and workshops (stated by the space).
- Since 1995 a public foundation, the Fundación del Teatro Real, has managed the theatre; its board of trustees includes representatives of the Ministry of Culture, the Community of Madrid and Madrid City Council (Wikipedia). The foundation describes itself as belonging to the institutional public sector (Teatro Real transparency page, translated from the Spanish).

FUNDING
Teatro Real: public and private mix: 36.75 per cent public administrations in the 2023 budget, the rest ticket sales, sponsorship and other income
- 2023 budget of the Fundación del Teatro Real, approved by its board on 12 December 2022: total EUR 75,187,672, of which public contributions from public administrations were EUR 27,629,709, or 36.75 per cent; the other 63.25 per cent was private income from ticket and subscription sales, private sponsorship and other income (Teatro Real, sources of financing, 2019 to 2023). The public share in 2023 was EUR 20,244,709 from the Ministry of Culture and Sport, EUR 4,100,000 from the Community of Madrid and EUR 3,285,000 from Madrid City Council (same document).
- Public contributions in earlier years: EUR 14,692,800 (26.04 per cent) in 2019, EUR 15,542,800 (41.59 per cent) in 2020, EUR 17,092,870 (34.19 per cent) in 2021 and EUR 20,269,572 (31.44 per cent) in 2022, the last on provisional figures (same document).
- Private patronage: the Board of Patrons (Junta de Protectores), chaired by Claudio Aguirre, meets twice a year, and senior managers from associate, sponsor or patron companies can attend. Companies receive a 40 per cent tax deduction on donations (stated by the space).
- Named sponsors: Telefónica sponsors the production of Mozart's "Las bodas de Fígaro", and the Fundación Rosalía Portela will fund the theatre's educational music project (stated by the space, homepage).

BOTTOM LINE
Teatro Real is financed by a public foundation whose 2023 budget was about 37 per cent public money from public administrations, with the rest from ticket sales, sponsorship and other private income. The theatre names Telefónica as a production sponsor and the Fundación Rosalía Portela as an education funder.
