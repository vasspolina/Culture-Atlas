JAKARTA BIENNALE: Jakarta, Indonesia
ID: jakarta-biennale
Website: https://jakartabiennale.id (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Recurring contemporary art exhibition based at Taman Ismail Marzuki (TIM), the Jakarta arts complex, with programmes at collective spaces across the city (Jakarta Biennale website).
- Its precursor was the Pameran Besar Seni Lukis Indonesia 1974 at TIM. Art school students answered it with the Desember Hitam statement against decorative painting; the students were expelled and lecturers who defended them were dismissed (Elly Kent, Art and Australia, 2024).
- It is auspiced by Dewan Kesenian Jakarta, the Jakarta Arts Council; Art and Australia says arms of the collective ruangrupa implemented it for the ten years before 2024.
- Since 2024 it has been run by Majelis Jakarta, an "open network of various art initiatives in Jakarta" that began meeting in 2023; its page lists 16 collectives, among them Gudskul Ekosistem, PannaFoto Institute and Serrum Art Handling (Jakarta Biennale website).
- The 2024 edition ran from 1 October to 15 November in the Emiria Soenassa and S. Sudjojono galleries at TIM, organised by 20 collectives from Majelis Jakarta without a traditional theme or curatorial team (NOW! Jakarta, 2024).
- The 2026 edition, "KEBAL", concerns residents "displaced" from Jakarta by rising property and living costs; its main show ran from 4 to 28 October 2026 at TIM's Gedung Trisno Soemardjo, with programmes at collective spaces from 7 October to 24 November (Jakarta Biennale website). The Creative Economy Ministry put it at nine locations.

FUNDING
Jakarta Biennale: collective-run under the Jakarta Arts Council, with national ministry support
- Organiser: Majelis Jakarta, which the Jakarta Arts Council initiated (NOW! Jakarta).
- 2026 support: on 31 August 2026 Deputy Creative Economy Minister Irene Umar received a Jakarta Arts Council delegation and said the ministry was ready to connect the event with partners, including for venues; the council's deputy chair Berto Tukan thanked the ministry for its support. The ministry proposed the October programme for its "Oktober Kreasi" series (Ministry of Creative Economy, 31 August 2026).
- 2024 support: the Baku Konek residency shown in the 2024 edition was co-organised by ruangrupa and the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology (e-flux Criticism).
- History: the 2000 edition was cancelled for insufficient funds, and the Jakarta Biennale Foundation was set up in 2014 (e-flux Criticism).
- Expected government grant funding rounds failed to open in mid-2024; Art and Australia (2024) calls publicly funded biennials vulnerable to the waxing and waning of political will.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Two reviews of the 2024 edition criticise its curation and cohesion; they concern the exhibition, not its funders.
1. e-flux Criticism: Innas Tsuroiya, "Jakarta Biennale 2024, '50 Tahun Jakarta Biennale 1974-2024'" (5 December 2024)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/criticism/644742/jakarta-biennale-2024-50-tahun-jakarta-biennale-1974-2024
- The decentralised method amounts to "un-curation"; the main show gives little information about its curatorial framework and lacks cohesion.
- Works sit without separation from other programmes, and many events were announced at short notice, often the day before.
- Judges the show "no greater than the sum of its parts" but argues the lumbung model could help new movements grow outside the metropolitan centres.
2. Art and Australia: Elly Kent, "Contemporary Jakarta: A tale of two art events" (2024)
URL: https://artandaustralia.com/59_2/contemporary-jakarta-a-tale-of-two-art-events.html
- Finds the 2024 Biennale lacked cohesion: the exhibition design gave visitors little help with the connections between works, and lighting, location and access let down strong pieces.
- Argues biennials depend on key figures rather than resilient institutions and succession planning.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: an artist-run event on public support. The weak point is disclosure: worth watching is whether a budget and a funder list are published.
