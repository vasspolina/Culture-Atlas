NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE ISLAMIC REVOLUTION AND HOLY DEFENSE: Tehran, Iran
ID: national-museum-of-the-islamic-revolution-and-holy-defense
Website: https://iranrhdm.ir (checked 2026-11-20; down, the server did not respond)

OVERVIEW
- Garden museum of the Iran-Iraq war of 1980 to 1988 in Abbas Abad, a hilly area of northern Tehran planned before the revolution for embassies and state institutions, which now also holds the National Library and the Book Garden (Rivetti, IJMES, 2020).
- In 2005 the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps and the Tehran municipality launched the first public call for architectural designs (Rivetti).
- Narges Madanipour, head of the city council's culture commission, gave its floor area as 205,000 square metres and its opening as the Iranian year 1391, 2012 to 2013 (EcoIran, 2024); AFP gives 2012, while Rivetti dates the opening of the main building to 2016.
- The complex has a main building, the Khorramshahr mosque and a panorama pavilion on the defence of Khorramshahr; the main halls run from the mid-1970s to 1989, with reproduced trenches, martyrs' belongings, a simulation of the 1980 attack on the Abadan refinery and a bombing simulation room (Rivetti).
- In 2019 the panorama pavilion's supervisor told Rivetti it was being expanded to add "anti-imperialist resistance from other countries, such as Syria and Yemen".
- More than 300,000 visitors a year, according to deputy director Seyyed Mohsen Hajbabian, mostly young men and school groups on trips organised by Islamic institutions (AFP, 2018).

FUNDING
National Museum of the Islamic Revolution and Holy Defense: public: the Tehran municipality, under a statute approved by the Supreme Leader
- Building: the Tehran municipality paid for the garden museum while Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf was mayor (Radio Farda, 2023).
- Statute: Article 13 makes the Tehran municipality responsible for the budget and staff needed to complete, maintain and run the museum, through its annual budget; gifts, endowments and government credits are further sources (statute amendment, Official Gazette no. 22214, 24 June 2021). The same amendment transferred the staff, equipment and annual credits of the Tehran province office for Holy Defense works to the museum.
- Approval: the Supreme Leader and Commander-in-Chief approved the amendment, communicated in a letter of 28 April 2021, and the Armed Forces General Staff sent it to the Gazette; the board of directors proposes the annual budget to a board of trustees (Official Gazette).
- Budget: Tehran city council chair Mohsen Hashemi said in February 2018 that the garden museum's budget had been 55 billion tomans in earlier years, 23 billion the year before and 13 billion in the draft for 1397; he said the mayor and the council chair sit on its board of trustees (Tejarat News, 2018).
- In August 2024 the council passed a plan obliging the municipality to create a Tehran Holy Defense museum section inside the garden museum; council chair Mehdi Chamran said its money could come through a budget amendment (EcoIran).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Academic and press coverage treats the museum as a state ideological project.
1. International Journal of Middle East Studies: Paola Rivetti, "The Museum of the Islamic Revolution and Holy Defence in Tehran" (2020)
URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-journal-of-middle-east-studies/article/museum-of-the-islamic-revolution-and-holy-defence-in-tehran/9B3B50FC9316139B37733016754D9699
- Calls the museum an "ideological artifact" that stabilises a "sanitized" version of a contested war and reduces plural histories to the state-approved one.
- The halls centre the revolution on Khomeini, mention no other political forces and erase the revolts in Kurdistan and the clashes between revolutionary factions.
- Argues that young visitors, mostly Hezbollah affiliates, rework the museum's "resistance" into an "intimate struggle" of self-improvement.
- Read in the author's proof copy in Dublin City University's repository, not the published text.
2. AFP (Hürriyet Daily News): "Tehran museum lionizes war in which Iran took on 'world'" (2018)
URL: https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/tehran-museum-lionizes-war-in-which-iran-took-on-world-135970
- Photo montages show Western, Arab monarchy and Soviet support for Saddam Hussein, but the museum mentions neither Israel's support for Iran nor US arms supplied under the Iran-Contra deal.
- Hajbabian: "The message of the museum is perseverance, resistance and the protection of our honor."

WIDER CONTEXT
- Radio Farda reported in 2023 that the Foundation for the Preservation of Holy Defense Works, a state body under the Armed Forces General Staff founded on Khamenei's order in the Iranian year 1369, took over 423 billion tomans of public money in the year 1402 alone through at least 17 museum projects in the national development budget, six times the credits for Iran's 25 UNESCO World Heritage sites; the projects are part of 2,257 billion tomans set aside for Holy Defense museums through 1405.
- The head of that foundation proposes the museum's alternate board members under the 2021 statute (Official Gazette), which places the museum inside this programme.

BOTTOM LINE
Municipal public money, which alone is no red flag on the atlas. The weak point is its purpose: a state museum of the official war story, tied to the military's national museum programme.
