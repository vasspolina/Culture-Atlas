KAPELICA GALLERY (KERSNIKOVA INSTITUTE): Ljubljana, Slovenia
ID: kapelica-gallery-kersnikova-institute-ljubljana
Website: https://kersnikova.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary and research-based art gallery in Ljubljana, listed by the Slovenian culture portal at Likozarjeva ulica 1, where the Kersnikova Institute moved in 2021.
- Established in 1995 by the Student Organisation of the University of Ljubljana (ŠOU), according to the Slovenian culture portal culture.gov.si. Jurij Krpan, an architect and curator, has led the gallery since its founding (culture.gov.si).
- Named for a former chapel (kapelica) at Kersnikova 4, the gallery's earlier premises (culture.gov.si).
- Managed by the Kersnikova Institute, a Ljubljana nonprofit founded in 2000 as the K6/4 Institute through a restructuring of the University of Ljubljana's Student Organisation, which remains a founder; it took the name Kersnikova in 2014 (culture.gov.si).
- Programme: spatial installations, performances, sound research and lectures on scientific and technological applications, biopolitics and urban phenomena (Kersnikova). Artists shown have included Stelarc, Orlan and Survival Research Laboratories (culture.gov.si).
- Kersnikova also runs BioTehna, a laboratory for artistic research into living systems founded in 2012, and Rampa, a hacker laboratory and incubator for young creators founded in 2013 (Kersnikova).

FUNDING
Kapelica Gallery (Kersnikova Institute): supported by the Slovenian Ministry of Culture, the City of Ljubljana and European Union programmes
- Kersnikova's website lists 21 supporters under its supporter entries: the Ministry of Culture of the Republic of Slovenia and the City of Ljubljana (Mestna občina Ljubljana); ŠOU; the European Commission, Creative Europe, Horizon 2020, the European Regional Development Fund, Interreg DIVA and the Swiss contribution; and the projects ART4MED, Arc-hive, STARTS, CreaTures, Biofriction, MAST, SYSTEM, DOIT, DITOS, AILab, konS and MJU.
- The Slovenian culture portal lists EU-funded Kersnikova projects from 2003 to 2025, including Creative Europe projects.

BOTTOM LINE
The Slovenian Ministry of Culture and the City of Ljubljana are named as supporters of the Kersnikova Institute, which manages the gallery, alongside European Union programmes and ŠOU, the student organisation that founded it.
