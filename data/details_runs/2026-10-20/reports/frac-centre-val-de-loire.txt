FRAC CENTRE-VAL DE LOIRE: Orléans, France
ID: frac-centre-val-de-loire
Website: https://www.frac-centre.fr

OVERVIEW
- Public collection of contemporary art and experimental architecture in Orléans, in the building Les Turbulences on the former Subsistances militaires (military supplies) site (Frac Centre-Val de Loire website).
- The Fonds régionaux d'art contemporain were created in 1982 through a partnership between the State and the regions; the Frac Centre-Val de Loire was created in 1983 (Frac website).
- In 2006 an international competition for the rehabilitation of the Subsistances militaires site was won by the architects Jakob + MacFarlane; Turbulences was inaugurated in 2013 alongside the 9th ArchiLab (Frac website).
- Collection: contemporary art and experimental architecture from 1950 to the present, with about 1,000 architectural models, about 1,000 artworks and more than 16,000 drawings by 94 artists and architects (Frac website).
- Programme: the ArchiLab exhibitions since 1999, the Orléans Architecture Biennial (2017), the Rabat Biennial of Contemporary Art (2019) and the Vierzon Biennale of Art and Architecture (third edition in 2022) (Frac website).
- Directors: Marie-Ange Brayer from 1997, Abdelkader Damani from 2015, and Christelle Kirchstetter, who was previously co-director of the Frac Grand Large in Dunkirk (Frac website; Les Fracs network page).
- Open Wednesday to Sunday 14:00 to 19:00, ticket office closes at 18:30, open until 20:00 on the first Thursday of each month (Frac website).

FUNDING
Frac Centre-Val de Loire: public: State, Région Centre-Val de Loire and City of Orléans
- Since 2015 the Frac has been run as a public body for cultural cooperation (établissement public de coopération culturelle) associating the State, the Région Centre-Val de Loire and the City of Orléans (Frac recruitment notice for an administrator, uploaded January 2025).
- Its forecast budget for 2025 is EUR 2,471,250, and it employs 26 permanent staff (Frac recruitment notice).
- Its missions follow the Ministry of Culture's "Frac" label, which the Fracs have held since 2017 (Frac recruitment notice; Frac website).
- The administrator post follows the multi-year objectives agreement and contributes to the search for public funding, including European, and private funding; the Région Centre-Val de Loire is carrying a project to extend the collection stores (Frac recruitment notice).

BOTTOM LINE
The Frac is a public cultural body associating the State, the Région Centre-Val de Loire and the City of Orléans, with a forecast 2025 budget of EUR 2,471,250.
