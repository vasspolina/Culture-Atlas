SÁMI CENTER FOR CONTEMPORARY ART (SÁMI DÁIDDAGUOVDDÁŠ): Karasjok, Norway
ID: s-mi-center-for-contemporary-art-s-mi-d-iddaguovdd-karasjok
Website: https://samidaiddaguovddas.no (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Sámi contemporary art centre with an exhibition hall and offices at Suomageaidnu 14, Karasjok; it calls itself the leading international center for contemporary Sámi artists (stated by the centre).
- Established in 1986 (stated by the centre); it invited guests to a 40th-anniversary celebration on 13 and 14 November (centre's website). The foundation Stiftelsen Sàmi Dàiddaguovddàs was set up on 23 August 2013 and registered on 9 January 2014 (Brønnøysund Register Centre).
- Its aim, in its own words, is supporting, communicating and visualizing Sámi contemporary art in Europe, in indigenous areas and at the largest art arenas in the world, with room for experimental practice and artistic freedom of speech.
- It runs exhibitions, artist talks and an artist-in-residence programme, reopened in June 2024, and has a gallery shop selling books, art, dáiddaduodji (handicrafts) and Sámi music (centre's website).
- Staff: about five full-time posts, three of them permanent (director vacancy notice, February 2026); the register lists 11 employees.
- Leadership: the board appointed Marija Griniuk director for four years from 25 July 2022, and in May 2025 appointed the lawyer Line Kalak managing director, the first Sámi leader since the centre became a foundation (centre's announcements). In February 2026 the board advertised the director post again, with a deadline of 6 March 2026 (FINN.no); the centre's About page now lists Monika Anti as acting director.
- Board: chair Geir Tore Holm, appointed by SDS, the Sami Artists' Association; deputy chair Maja Kristine Jåma, appointed by the Sámi Parliament; members Gerd Wevling Matre and Liselotte Wajstedt (SDS) and Svein Atle Somby (Karasjok Municipality), with deputies from the same three bodies (centre's website).
- Visiting: free entry; open Thursday and Friday 10:00 to 16:00, Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 16:00 (centre's website).

FUNDING
Sámi Dáiddaguovddáš (Karasjok): mainly public: an annual direct grant from the Sámi Parliament
- The Sámi Parliament's budget gives the foundation a direct grant of NOK 6,090,000 for 2025, up 3.6 percent from NOK 5,878,000 in 2024 (Sametinget budget 2025), and NOK 6,279,000 for 2026, unchanged in the June 2026 revision (Sametinget revised budget 2026).
- Accounts: operating income of NOK 7.73 million in 2023, NOK 9.18 million in 2024 and NOK 7.92 million in 2025; 2025 closed with an operating loss of NOK 229,000 and equity of NOK 1.61 million (Brønnøysund accounts register).
- The Sámi Parliament funded its participation in the ArtVilnius fair in Lithuania, 4 to 6 October 2024 (stated by the centre).
- The director's duties include grant work with the Sámi Parliament, state bodies and private foundations (FINN.no, 2026).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean, public money: an Indigenous parliament's grant with no corporate sponsor in view. Worth watching is the turnover in the director's post since 2022.
