ID: fondation-vincent-van-gogh-arles

WIDER CONTEXT
- An appeals court in Aix-en-Provence ordered the Van Gogh Foundation in Arles to return Francis Bacon's "Homage to Van Gogh", lent to its museum in 1991 and never returned, to the heirs of John Edwards, overturning an earlier ruling that the painting could stay; the court said "The painting was not given as a gift, nor was there any promise of a gift" (Expatica, archived copy). The Evening Standard reported the order on 22 April 2010 (Terry Kirby, listed by the Francis Bacon catalogue raisonné site).
- The foundation's director Mary Gruber said she was "in shock" and added "We will now bury the hatchet" with the heirs; its lawyer Bernard Jouanneau said a further appeal was possible (Expatica).
- The order was against Yolande Clergue's association, known as the Fondation Van Gogh, which the present foundation names as its origin; the association began in 1985 the collection of homages to Van Gogh that included works by Bacon (foundation's history page).
