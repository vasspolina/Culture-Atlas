Red flags: 2025 dismissal of the director
- The board of the foundation that runs LABoral removed Pablo de Soto as director-gerente at the start of February 2025, citing "pérdida de confianza" (loss of confidence) (El Salto, 11 February 2025; miGijón, 7 February 2025). He had been chosen in 2022 after a selection process with more than 70 candidates (El Salto).
- A report by the Inspección General de Servicios of the Principado de Asturias faulted his financial management and team coordination and stated that liquid assets fell 55.35% between 2022 and 2024 (miGijón). It also cited irregular payments and an altered invoice; De Soto denied irregularities and called one item a minor administrative error (El Salto).
- De Soto disputed the treasury figures with an analysis by the firm Fidelitas and said the inspectors' visits in 2024 took place during his paternity leave (miGijón).
- The Asociación de Directores de Centros de Arte Contemporáneo de España (ADACE) objected: "La destitución se produce de modo fulminante y de forma unidireccional" (El Salto). It noted that LABoral had had four directors in under a decade (El Salto).
- "más de 700 profesionales y creadores nacionales e internacionales han defendido el trabajo del exdirector" (El Salto); their letter called for LABoral to be free of arbitrary decisions by the regional culture department (El Salto).
- Three members of the centre's scientific commission, Laura Baigorri, María Ptqk and Pau Waelder, resigned, saying there were no sufficient grounds for the dismissal (El Salto).
- The authorities defended the measure, "citando cuestiones de gestión y funcionamiento interno" (El Salto). Two staff members told miGijón, offering only their word, that "la mayoría de la plantilla suscribe todas las afirmaciones del documento de la Inspección General" (miGijón).
- Pablo León, director general de Cultura y Patrimonio, took over in the interim (miGijón).
