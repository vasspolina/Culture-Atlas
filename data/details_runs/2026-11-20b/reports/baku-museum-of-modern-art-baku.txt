BAKU MUSEUM OF MODERN ART: Baku, Azerbaijan
ID: baku-museum-of-modern-art-baku
Website: http://www.moma.az (checked 2026-11-20; the https address fails with a certificate error)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern art at Yusif Safarov Street 5, Khatai district, Baku (museum website).
- Opened on 20 March 2009 at the initiative of First Lady Mehriban Aliyeva and with the support of the Heydar Aliyev Foundation (museum website).
- The artist Altay Sadigzade conceived the museum, its architecture, design and the selection of the collection; the two-storey building has no cornered halls, walls set at angles, open passages and metal beams, and is white throughout (museum website).
- Collection: more than 800 works by Azerbaijani painters and sculptors of the last 70 years, centred on the avant-garde of the 1960s and 1970s, with works by Picasso, Chagall and Dalí from private collections (museum website).
- It also has a children's art section, a video hall, an exhibition hall, a library, a bookshop and an art café and restaurant (museum website).
- Wikipedia says Aida Mahmudova, founder of YARAT and a niece of Mehriban Aliyeva, has been director since 2012; in October 2026 Trend named Rena Mammadova as director.
- Anar Huseynzade's solo show "O" opened there in October 2026 in the presence of Alena Aliyeva and Anar Alakbarov, assistant to the president and executive director of the Heydar Aliyev Foundation (Trend).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 20:00, closed Mondays; 5 manat, 2 manat for students, free for schoolchildren and preschoolers (museum website).

FUNDING
Baku Museum of Modern Art: funded by a ruling-family foundation: the Heydar Aliyev Foundation
- The Heydar Aliyev Foundation, which the First Lady heads, funded the museum (Wikipedia; museum website).
- OCCRP describes the foundation as a non-governmental organisation set up and run by the family of President Ilham Aliyev (Roxana Jipa, Victor Ilie and Daniel Bojin, 2015).
- Show backers: "Geyzer", held at the museum from 17 January 2026, was the flagship exhibition of the "Oil Boom Smiles at Everyone" festival, supported by the Heydar Aliyev Foundation, sponsored by bp and organised by the Magsud Ibrahimbeyov Creativity Center (Trend, 18 January 2026).

Red flags: 2026 bp sponsorship of the "Geyzer" exhibition
- bp sponsored the November 2025 to February 2026 festival whose flagship exhibition, "Geyzer", opened at the museum on 17 January 2026 (Trend).

WIDER CONTEXT
- OCCRP (4 December 2015) reported that the Heydar Aliyev Foundation's Bucharest headquarters, a house bought for 6.5 million euros, is provided rent-free by the Romanian company Curzon Properties, whose majority stake passed in March 2009 to Ciaba Ltd, a British Virgin Islands company that documents obtained by OCCRP show was owned by the president's daughter Arzu Aliyeva. The article does not mention the museum.
- The Art Newspaper (Anna Sansom, 30 October 2020) reported that YARAT closed Ahmet Ogut's exhibition three weeks early after he accused it of using his exhibition banner as a "propaganda tool" in the Azerbaijan-Armenia conflict. The article is about YARAT, not the museum.
- Hyperallergic (Nevdon Jamgochian, "Artwashing a Dictatorship", 28 February 2021) asks whether spending millions on contemporary art and architecture helped Azerbaijan artwash its image; the body is behind a registration wall, and only the headline, standfirst and caption were read this time.
- These concern the foundation that funds the museum and the art organisation founded by its long-time director.

BOTTOM LINE
Ruling-family money with no independent funder, plus current fossil fuel sponsorship of a show held there.
