JUMING MUSEUM: New Taipei, Taiwan
ID: juming-museum-new-taipei
Website: https://www.juming.org.tw/

OVERVIEW
- Sculpture park and museum at No. 2 Xishi Lake, Jinshan District, New Taipei City; the museum says the site covers 11 hectares, of which 8 are open to visitors.
- Founded by the sculptor Ju Ming (born 1938, died 2023), who began turning barren land in Jinshan into a park and museum in 1987; it opened on 19 September 1999 after twelve years of work (museum's founder page, Wikipedia).
- Named for its founder; the operator is the Ju Ming Culture and Education Foundation (財團法人朱銘文教基金會).
- Wikipedia states that Ju Ming personally designed the architecture and landscaping, and that the main museum building is pyramid-shaped.
- On site: a first exhibition room, the main building, outdoor sculpture areas, a Children's Art Center, a café, a tea house and a gift shop. The current exhibition, "恆形 雕塑作為方法", runs to 20 December 2026.
- Open 10:00 to 17:00, tickets sold until 16:00, closed Mondays but open on national holidays. Admission NT$350; NT$320 for students and visitors aged 65 and over; NT$160 for a disability certificate holder plus one companion; NT$280 for groups of 15 or more and for art students. Free for children aged 6 and under and for Jinshan District residents. Closed 21 to 25 December 2026 for installation work.

FUNDING
Juming Museum: founder-funded, now run by a foundation on admissions, donations and exhibition sponsors
- The museum's founder page states that Ju Ming funded the museum himself, using his lifetime savings.
- The foundation takes ticket income at the rates above and invites donations through its "Support & Join" menu.
- For the current exhibition "恆形 雕塑作為方法", the museum names the Ministry of Culture as guidance unit (指導單位) and two sponsors: 新北北海溫泉洲際酒店 (an InterContinental hotel) and 虹鋼鍛藝設計股份有限公司.
- Governance: the foundation's page lists a board of nine, chaired by 葉樹姍, with directors 王玉齡, 吳素美, 官政能, 林淑玲, 翁玉琴, 黃育徵, 鄭鉦涼 and 魏自立. The accounting advisor is 誠正聯合會計師事務所 and the legal advisor is 六合法律事務所.
- The site publishes annual reports for 2016 to 2025 as PDF files.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the founder's own lifetime savings, and it is now run by a foundation that takes admissions, donations and exhibition sponsorship. The current show lists the Ministry of Culture as guidance unit and, as sponsors, an InterContinental hotel and a metalwork firm.
