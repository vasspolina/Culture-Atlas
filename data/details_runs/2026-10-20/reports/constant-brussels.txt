CONSTANT (ASSOCIATION FOR ART AND MEDIA): Brussels, Belgium
ID: constant-brussels
Website: https://constantvzw.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit association for art, media and technology with an office at Rue du Fort 5A, Saint-Gilles, Brussels, and a studio at Chaussée de Jette 388, Koekelberg (Constant).
- Founded in 1997 as an association; its original statutes focused on supporting audiovisual media and electronic arts (Constant).
- Its focus later moved to free software, feminisms, gender, copyright alternatives and networked artistic work; the 2023 to 2027 programme is called SPLINT, "Speculative, Libre, Intersectional Technologies" (Constant).
- Constant Studio, rented since 2022, is a former workshop for electronic appliances with two floors of about 150 square metres; the ground floor holds residencies and workshops and the first floor is divided into 11 units used by artists, collectives and researchers (Constant).
- Window installations are visible from the street at the office; the group also runs worksessions, publishes books and hosts projects such as the feminist server network ATNOFS.
- Visiting: the office is open Monday to Friday, 10 am to 5 pm, and Constant asks visitors to call beforehand; all events are free. The office has a 20.5 cm entrance step and no lift, and Constant states its spaces are not yet accessible to everyone.

FUNDING
Constant: publicly funded: Flemish, Francophone and Brussels authorities, with EU and foundation project grants
- Constant's Support page lists as supporters: the Departement Cultuur, Jeugd, Sport en Media of the Vlaamse Overheid; the Vlaamse Gemeenschapscommissie; Maribel funding via FE.BI; Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles (Arts Numériques); the Brussels-Capital Region; the Services du Gouvernement Francophone Bruxellois; the Dutch-language services of the municipality of Saint-Gilles; and Wallonie-Bruxelles International (ATNOFS, Iterations and Cqrrelations).
- Project grants shown on the same page: the Creative Europe Programme of the European Union (Iterations), the European Cultural Foundation (ATNOFS, A Transversal Network of Feminist Servers), the Ville de Bruxelles (Open coil) and Innoviris (SpecXcraft).

BOTTOM LINE
Constant is a Brussels non-profit whose Support page lists Flemish, Francophone, Brussels and municipal public bodies, plus Creative Europe, the European Cultural Foundation and Innoviris as project funders.
