ZANABAZAR MUSEUM OF FINE ART: Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia
ID: zanabazar-museum-of-fine-art-ulaanbaatar
Website: http://www.zanabazarmuseum.mn/

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Mongolian fine art in Chingeltei District, Ulaanbaatar (Wikipedia). Google Arts & Culture gives the address as Sambuu St, CHD 4th khoroo, Ulaanbaatar 15170. The postal address in the footer of the National Centre for Cultural Heritage (NCCH) page is Khan-Uul district, 21st khoroo, Buyant-Ukhaa 2, the centre's own address.
- Galleries: 12 according to Wikipedia and Google Arts & Culture, 10 exhibition halls according to the NCCH page, covering Mongolian art from ancient times to the early 20th century (NCCH).
- Founded in 1966 (NCCH); Wikipedia says it was proposed in 1965 by the chairman of the Mongolian Craftsmen's Union Committee and opened to the public on 23 July 1966.
- Named in 1995 for Gombodorj Zanabazar, the sculptor whose works it holds, including Sita Tara statues, the Five Dhyani Buddhas and the Bodhi Stupa (Google Arts & Culture).
- Building: a Russian merchant built it in 1905 and it was later used as a bank (Wikipedia).
- Collection: nearly 16,600 objects according to Google Arts & Culture, more than 16,000 according to the NCCH page, and more than 20,000 according to Wikipedia, which names paintings by Marzan Sharav. The museum joined Google Arts & Culture in 2015 with a virtual tour (Wikipedia).
- Since 2004 it has run a UNESCO project, "Development of Museums and Protection of Nomadic Cultural Heritage" (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: the NCCH page lists hours of Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:30.
- Neighbouring tower: the museum sued the Capital City Urban Planning and General Master Plan Office over its approval of a 28-storey office and banking building planned by Trade and Development Bank beside the museum. The museum argued that groundwater could cause settlement that cracks its building, that the tower would stand three metres from its protected zone, and that no measures were planned to protect the heritage building. The first-instance court dismissed the claim, the appeal court left that ruling in place, and on 2 December 2025 the Supreme Court upheld both rulings (iKon.mn, G. Mörön, 4 December 2025, title translated: "Court dismisses three-tier lawsuit opposing 28-storey building near the G. Zanabazar Museum of Fine Arts").

FUNDING
Zanabazar Museum of Fine Art: listed on the website of the National Centre for Cultural Heritage; UNESCO project since 2004
- The NCCH site (ncch.gov.mn) carries a page for the museum with hours, contact details and a description of its collection.
- UNESCO project since 2004 on museum development and the protection of nomadic cultural heritage (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum was founded in 1966, is listed on the National Centre for Cultural Heritage site and has run a UNESCO project since 2004 (Wikipedia). Its lawsuit over the neighbouring Trade and Development Bank tower was dismissed at three court levels, the last on 2 December 2025 (iKon.mn).
