RUANG MES 56: Yogyakarta, Indonesia
ID: ruang-mes-56-yogyakarta
Website: https://www.mes56.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist collective in Yogyakarta that develops photography and contemporary art through a critical, contextual approach (Good News from Indonesia, 20 October 2026).
- Formed in 2002; Wimo Ambala Bayang co-founded it with friends from ISI, the Indonesian Art Institute in Yogyakarta, and remains an active member (oddweb.org biographies).
- Art Asia Pacific calls it Indonesia's first contemporary photography collective; its members are predominantly male, and many studied at the Indonesian Institute of the Arts, the first school in the country to offer a photography degree.
- The name MES 56 comes from the address of the group's first outpost (Art Asia Pacific, 13 April 2017).
- The collective runs a house that serves as a studio, an educational space, a playground and a place to live (oddweb.org; Good News from Indonesia).
- Programmes include workshops, discussions, communal and individual projects, and art residencies for young photography-based artists (Mayanga Anggrian, Jurnal Imajinasi, 2018).
- Exhibitions: "The History of Boys: The MES 56 and Beyond" at DECK, Singapore, with eight MES photographers and three non-members, on view until 23 April 2017 (Art Asia Pacific); "Foam X Ruang Mes 56" at Foam, Amsterdam, 15 September to 12 November 2017 (photography-now.com); "Di Atas Hitam dan Putih" with 17 members at EDSU House, Sleman, from 16 October 2026 to 3 January 2027 (Good News from Indonesia).

FUNDING
Ruang MES 56 (Yogyakarta): self-funded: the collective's own money and programmes
- Formed in 2002 with self-funding (oddweb.org biographies).
- The collective has run programmes to sustain itself independently for more than fifteen years (exhibition listing on photography-now.com, 2017).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the collective is its own: oddweb.org says it was formed in 2002 with self-funding, and photography-now.com says its programmes have sustained it independently for more than fifteen years.
