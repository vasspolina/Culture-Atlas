MUSÉE D'ART ET D'HISTOIRE (MAH): Geneva, Switzerland
ID: musee-d-art-et-d-histoire-geneva
Website: https://www.mahmah.ch (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The City of Geneva's encyclopedic museum at Rue Charles-Galland 2, with about 650,000 objects covering 15,000 years (stated by the space).
- Founded in 1910, with the Musée Rath, the Maison Tavel and the Bibliothèque d'art et d'archéologie attached to it.
- Its collection joins archaeology, fine arts, graphic arts, applied arts, watchmaking, jewellery and coins.
- Marc-Olivier Wahler has directed it since 2020.
- The city ran an international architecture competition to restore and enlarge the building, won by the project "UMWELT" from Cabinet Fanny Noël Diogo Lopes Architecture, Atelier Plum and AFRY Suisse (City of Geneva).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 18:00, Thursdays 12:00 to 21:00. Visitors choose what they pay for the entry ticket.

FUNDING
Musée d'art et d'histoire: mainly public: the City of Geneva, with foundations, banks and watchmakers thanked for project support
- The museum is a City of Geneva service: its pages are signed "Musée d'art et d'histoire de la Ville de Genève" and its staff use city addresses.
- The works on the building were estimated at CHF 200 million with a 25 per cent margin of uncertainty, against CHF 130 million for the earlier Jean Nouvel project (Radio Lac, 4 May 2022).
- Foundations thanked on the museum's site for support over the years include the Fondation Gandur pour l'art, Fondation Leenaards, Fondation Ernst Göhner, Fondation BNP Paribas Suisse, Fondation Genevoise de Bienfaisance Valeria Rossi di Montelera and Sandoz Fondation de Famille.
- Banks and companies on the same list include UBS, Credit Suisse, Lombard Odier, Mirabaud, Barclays Bank (Switzerland), EFG Group, Migros Pour-Cent Culturel, SIG, Audemars Piguet, Chopard, Piaget and Vacheron Constantin.
- Jean Claude Gandur founded the Addax and Oryx Group in 1987, focused on oil production in Africa, and led Addax Petroleum until Sinopec took it over in August 2009 for USD 7.3 billion (Wikipedia).
- In March 2010 he committed CHF 20 million to the City of Geneva for the renovation and enlargement of the museum. Jean Nouvel's enlargement project was rejected in a referendum in February 2016 and Gandur withdrew from it (Wikipedia).
- In May 2024 his foundation announced that it had chosen Caen for a museum of his collection (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The city pays for the museum and that money is not a flag. Jean Claude Gandur's oil money was to co-finance an extension, but Geneva voters turned the project down in 2016 and he withdrew. Worth watching: who, besides the city, pays for the new CHF 200 million project.
