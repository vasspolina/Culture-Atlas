ID: salvador-dali-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2020 a change.org petition titled "Shame on you, Dali Museum", started by a user named "Innocent Bystander", criticised the museum's layoffs after it received federal Paycheck Protection Program money; it had 381 signatures on 24 June (Creative Loafing Tampa Bay, 24 June 2020) and 465 by 1 July, against a goal of 500 (Spectrum Bay News 9, 2 July 2020).
- Unverified: the petition says the museum "recently fired many local staff members after taking a large sum of PPP money" and kept "highly-paid upper management", from the petition as reported by Creative Loafing Tampa Bay.
- The museum said it paid all staff in full from its own budget for four weeks, the loan covered full pay for eight more weeks, and it kept "over 80% of our staff" after the loan period, with pay cuts that were larger for senior staff (Spectrum Bay News 9; Creative Loafing Tampa Bay).
- Asked about severance and about criticism that the cuts came as the museum planned an expansion of nearly $40M, a museum PR representative said "this is a human resources issue" and declined to give specifics (Creative Loafing Tampa Bay).
