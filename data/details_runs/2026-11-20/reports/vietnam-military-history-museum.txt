VIETNAM MILITARY HISTORY MUSEUM: Hanoi, Vietnam
ID: vietnam-military-history-museum
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- National museum of the People's Army on Thăng Long Boulevard, Nam Từ Liêm district, Hanoi; the old museum was at 28A Điện Biên Phủ Street, inside the Thăng Long Imperial Citadel grounds (Wikipedia).
- Origins: on 17 July 1956 the army's General Political Department set up a board to build an Army Museum, its forerunner (VietnamPlus, 2026).
- The new building opened on 1 November 2024 after work from 2019 on a 386,600 square metre site (Nhân Dân). It has four floors above ground and a basement, 23,198 square metres of footprint and 64,640 square metres of floor space, fronted by a 45 metre Victory Tower of stacked five-pointed stars standing for 1945 (Thời báo Tài chính).
- Chief architect: Trịnh Việt A, design director at Nikken Sekkei of Japan, whom the defence ministry's leadership also asked to design the displays (Nhân Dân).
- More than 150,000 objects, four of them national treasures: MiG-21 jets 4324 and 5121, T-54B tank 843, which broke through the Independence Palace gates on 30 April 1975, and a map of the Ho Chi Minh Campaign. Six themed galleries use 3D mapping and more than 60 video clips (VOV).
- Nearly 4 million visitors by June 2026 (VietnamPlus).
- Visiting: entry 40,000 dong since 12 April 2025; open 8:00 to 11:30 and 13:00 to 16:30. Free for under-16s, over-80s, war invalids, Heroic Vietnamese Mothers and heroes of the armed forces; half price for older citizens over 60, students and people with severe disabilities (Thời báo Tài chính).

FUNDING
Vietnam Military History Museum: public: the Ministry of National Defence and the army's General Political Department
- Operator: the museum belongs to the General Political Department of the Vietnam People's Army and heads the army's museum system, as one of six national museums (Thời báo Tài chính).
- Building: the General Political Department was the project owner for phase 1, worth 2,500 billion dong (Nhân Dân). The Central Military Commission, the Ministry of National Defence and the department carried out the project from 2020 (VietnamPlus).
- Ticket sales: began after five months of free entry (Thời báo Tài chính).

WIDER CONTEXT
- VietnamPlus (2026) describes the museum as "an important part" of the army's system of Party and political work, and as a "red address" for education in revolutionary tradition.
- Connection: the museum is run by the army department responsible for that political work.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Vietnamese state's, through the army, and raises no funding flag. What a visitor should weigh is the museum's place in the army's political work, not its funders.
