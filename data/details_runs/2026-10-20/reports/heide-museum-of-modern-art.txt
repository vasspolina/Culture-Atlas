HEIDE MUSEUM OF MODERN ART: Melbourne, Australia
ID: heide-museum-of-modern-art
Website: https://www.heide.com.au (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Public art museum and sculpture park in Bulleen, Melbourne, on the Yarra River (Wikipedia), with five gallery spaces on 6.5 hectares of parkland (Heide about page). The site was a Wurundjeri gathering place and later the home of John and Sunday Reed, from 1935 (Heide about page).
- The Victorian State Government bought the property and 113 artworks from the Reeds in 1980; the museum opened to the public in November 1981 (Wikipedia).
- Buildings: Heide I, the 1870s farmhouse; Heide II, designed by David McGlashan of McGlashan Everist and built 1964 to 1967; Heide III, designed by Andrew Andersons of Peddle Thorp Architects in 1993 and extended in 2005 by O'Connor + Houle Architecture (Wikipedia).
- On site: a collection of more than 3,800 works (the page's collection policy section says more than 3,700), a sculpture park, and the Sidney Myer Education Centre, built in 2005 (Heide about page; Wikipedia).
- Visiting: closed Mondays; Tuesday to Friday 10:00 to 16:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 17:00. Adults $27, concession and Tuesday seniors $22, MobTix (for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander visitors) $17; free for under-16s, members, Manningham residents and the Gardens and Sculpture Park. Accessibility details were not found.

FUNDING
Heide: mixed: Victorian and federal arts agencies, the local council, private donations and sponsors
- Government partners on Heide's partners page, read from the logo links because the page prints no names: Creative Victoria, Creative Australia and Manningham City Council. No amounts are given on the pages read.
- Private giving: Heide says it relies on private donations for at least a third of its annual operating revenue (stated by the space).
- Major Project Partners: one logo links to tuckerfoundation.org.au and four other logos carry no name; the page text names none of them, so the Tucker Foundation is unconfirmed as a funder. Supporting partners, read from the logo links: ABL, Gunn Taylor, IAS DAS, Merry People, Taubmans, Yalyal, Leif Products, Erebus Studio and Studio Mass; media partners, also from the links: Clemenger BBDO, Broadsheet, The Saturday Paper and Cinema Nova.
- Board: Philip Crutchfield KC (chair), Sally Cox, Alane Fineman, Mark Fraser, Matt Heine, Cassy Liberman and James van Smeerdijk.
- No page opened names Gordon Darling, Downer or Shine On foundation gifts, a Melbourne Water grant or a North East Link Community Fund grant as support for Heide, so they are unconfirmed; North East Link appears on Heide's donate page only as a road project affecting access to the museum. The annual report that lists donors could not be read.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Heide is public and philanthropic: the Victorian and federal arts agencies, the local council, private donors and sponsors, with no flagged funder found on the pages read. The weak points are that donor names and amounts sit in an annual report this review could not read, and several partner logos carry no name. Worth watching is whether Heide publishes its donor list.
