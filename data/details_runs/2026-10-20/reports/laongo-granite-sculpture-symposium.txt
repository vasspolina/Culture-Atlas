LAONGO GRANITE SCULPTURE SYMPOSIUM: Laongo, Burkina Faso
ID: laongo-granite-sculpture-symposium
Website: none of its own; the organising ministry's domain (www.communication-culture.gov.bf) did not resolve when checked 2026-10-25 (down)

OVERVIEW
- Open-air granite sculpture site and international symposium at Laongo, about 35 km from Ouagadougou (Wikipedia); the 2026 announcement places it in Bassitenga province, Wikipedia in Oubritenga province.
- Founded by the Burkinabe sculptor Siriki Ky; the first edition opened on 13 January 1989 with 18 sculptors from 13 countries (Wikipedia). Ky is commissioner general of the 2026 edition (Sidwaya).
- Named for the village of Laongo, where invited sculptors carve granite on site.
- Site: a granite outcrop used as an open-air workshop and gallery; some works combine granite with metal (Wikipedia). Wikipedia notes that in 2010 the legal status of the site was unclear.
- What is there: carved works from the editions (Africanews, October 2024, says over 60 artists since 1988 and more than 2,000 works; Le360 gives no total). The 2026 edition adds 15 trainees in a stone-cutting school, a colloquium on 10 and 11 November, a fair of 40 stands and a catalogue (Burkina24, Sidwaya).
- Visiting: the 15th edition runs 2 to 23 November 2026 with free admission (Sidwaya); Africanews quotes Ky saying the site receives visitors all year round. No accessibility information found.

FUNDING
Laongo Granite Sculpture Symposium: public: Burkina Faso's culture ministry
- The Ministry of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism issued the 2024 call for applications and organises the event (noocultures.info; Sidwaya).
- Each professional artist in 2024 received a participation fee of 1,000,000 FCFA, a 300,000 FCFA bonus for assigning exploitation rights of the works, an international return flight, lodging, meals and local transport; trainees received lodging, meals and local transport (2024 call).
- The 2026 edition is under the "very high patronage" of President Captain Ibrahim Traore (Burkina24). The artistic patron is the Malian artist Abdoulaye Konate (Sidwaya).
- No reporting found of any corporate, foundation or foreign funder, of a budget, or of published accounts.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found is event reporting from Burkinabe and African news outlets, and none of it criticises the symposium.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Human Rights Watch, World Report 2026: the junta led by Ibrahim Traore cracked down on political opposition, the media and dissent. Unidentified men abducted the journalists Serge Oulon, Kalifara Séré and Adama Bayala, all critics of the junta, in June 2024; Séré and Bayala were freed in 2025 and Oulon is still missing. Authorities arrested three journalists in March 2025 and released them in July 2025.
- Connection: the symposium is run by a ministry of that government and carries the president's patronage; the 2024 edition also featured the Alliance of Sahel States (Le360).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Burkinabe state's; no private or flagged funder is named, but no budget or accounts are published either. The weak points are the symposium's direct dependence on the junta's ministry and presidential patronage, and the unclear legal status of the site. Worth watching: the November 2026 colloquium on the site's identity and heritage and any published budget.
