Red flags: 2022 cancellation of a Konsthall C show at Sergels torg
- "All set for solidarity", a text-based exhibition of works by Sepidar Hosseini curated by the group Settings together with Konsthall C, was due to open at Sergels torg on Monday 27 June 2022; the organisers were told the day before that it was cancelled (Flamman, Paulina Sokolow, opinion column, 15 July 2022).
- District director Jesper Ackinger said the messages on the posters were "för politiska" ("too political", translated); the column names the Norrmalm district committee of Stockholm as the body that stopped the show (Flamman, 15 July 2022).
- Ackinger said art should avoid being read as party-political, "särskilt på Sergels torg och särskilt inför en valrörelse" ("especially at Sergels torg and especially ahead of an election campaign", translated) (Flamman, 15 July 2022).
