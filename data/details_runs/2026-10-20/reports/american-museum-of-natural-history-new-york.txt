AMERICAN MUSEUM OF NATURAL HISTORY: New York, United States
ID: american-museum-of-natural-history-new-york
Website: https://www.amnh.org/ (checked 2026-10-20; served a CAPTCHA verification page to automated requests, so its pages could not be read)

OVERVIEW
- Natural history museum at 200 Central Park West in Theodore Roosevelt Park, on Manhattan's Upper West Side, opposite Central Park.
- Founded by state legislation signed on 6 April 1869 from an idea by naturalist Albert S. Bickmore; it opened in the Central Park Arsenal on 22 May 1871 and moved to its first building on 77th Street in 1877 (Wikipedia).
- 21 linked buildings with 45 permanent halls, including the Rose Center for Earth and Space (2000) and the Richard Gilder Center, designed by Studio Gang and opened on 4 May 2023 (Wikipedia).
- The equestrian Theodore Roosevelt statue was removed in January 2022; on 27 January 2024 the museum closed its Eastern Woodlands and Great Plains halls under new NAGPRA rules (Wikipedia, Hyperallergic).
- President: Sean M. Decatur. Admission and access details could not be read from the museum's own site.

FUNDING
American Museum of Natural History: mixed: private donors and foundations, corporate patrons and city and state capital funds
- In the year to June 2025 revenue was $246,764,235, of which contributions were $153,511,874 and programme revenue $27,938,269; expenses were $278,576,256 and net assets $1,123,065,690 (Form 990, ProPublica).
- Decatur was paid $1,216,070 and Lisa Gugenheim, listed as museum director until 30 June 2024, $1,738,936 in the year to June 2025 (Form 990).
- Citi, Chase and Bank of America are listed as corporate patrons of the museum, according to Hyperallergic's 2023 report on the protest; no museum donor list could be read.
- David H. Koch gave about $23 million over 23 years; the dinosaur wing still carries his name (Hyperallergic, 2016 and 2023).

Red flags: Koch board seat and dinosaur wing
- In 2015 an open letter from 39 scientists demanded Koch's removal from the boards of AMNH and the Smithsonian natural history museum (Hyperallergic).
- His term ended on 9 December 2015; the museum called it the "normal course of business" (Hyperallergic, 2016).
- The David H. Koch Dinosaur Wing keeps the name.

Red flags: Rebekah Mercer and climate denial funding
- Revolting Lesbians campaigned for her removal from 2018, including a rally and an open letter from more than 400 scientists (Hyperallergic, 2020).
- Unverified: the Mercer Family Foundation gave more than $53 million to groups including the Heartland Institute and the CO2 Coalition, from Revolting Lesbians, an activist group campaigning for her removal, citing Form 990 filings (via Hyperallergic).
- The museum said "Ms. Mercer's term expired in December" and gave no reason for not renewing her (Hyperallergic).

Red flags: Sackler names
- In May 2022 the museum still had the Sackler Institute for Comparative Genomics and the Sackler Educational Laboratory (Hyperallergic).
- Both have since removed the Sackler name (Wikipedia); no museum announcement or date found.

Red flags: 2023 protest over bank patrons
- On 2 September 2023 about 30 Extinction Rebellion activists protested in the Koch Dinosaur Wing with a banner: "Mass extinction funded by Citi, Chase, Bank of America, Koch Industries" (Hyperallergic).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Sustained criticism of board members and donors, and of the handling of Native remains, mostly in Hyperallergic and from activist groups.
1. Hyperallergic: Benjamin Sutton, "Introducing the New Koch Zero: Billionaire Leaves American Natural History Museum Board" (2016)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/269664/introducing-the-new-koch-zero-billionaire-leaves-american-natural-history-museum-board/
- Reports the exit after the scientists' letter and petitions; the museum called it the normal course of business.
2. Hyperallergic: Valentina Di Liscia, "Rebekah Mercer, Funder of Climate Change Denial, No Longer On American Museum of Natural History Board" (2020)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/544448/rebekah-mercer/
- Reports, from an anonymous employee, that her name was "quietly removed" from the trustee list.
3. Hyperallergic: Elaine Velie, "American Museum of Natural History Closes Native Exhibition Halls" (2024)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/868254/american-museum-of-natural-history-closes-native-exhibition-halls-nagpra-regulations/
- Shannon O'Loughlin of the Association on American Indian Affairs: it took "a complete revision of the NAGPRA regulations" to make clear to institutions that they lack legal or ownership rights to Native cultural heritage; the article notes the museum holds over 2,000 Native American human remains.
4. Science for the People NYC: Cliff Conner, "Memo to SftP NYC" (2023)
URL: https://scienceforthepeople.org/2023/05/06/memo-to-sftp-nyc/
- Calls the Gilder Center "a science-themed high-finance operation" built on parkland; an openly activist source.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum dropped Koch, Mercer and the Sackler name from its board and rooms, but the Koch name stays on the dinosaur wing and bank patrons drew a 2023 protest. Watch the corporate patron list, which could not be read from the museum's site.
