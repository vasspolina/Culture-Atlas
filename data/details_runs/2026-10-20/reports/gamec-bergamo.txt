GAMEC: Bergamo, Italy
ID: gamec-bergamo
Website: https://www.gamec.it

OVERVIEW
- Gallery of modern and contemporary art at Via San Tomaso 53, Bergamo, in a fifteenth-century former monastery of the Dimesse and Servite (Wikipedia; gallery website). It also uses Palazzo della Ragione for exhibitions (2025 accounts).
- Opened in 1991; Studio Gregotti Associati International converted the building (Wikipedia). The exhibition space is about 1,500 square metres (gallery website; Wikipedia).
- Run by the Associazione per la Galleria d'Arte Moderna e Contemporanea di Bergamo, whose founding members are the Comune di Bergamo and TenarisDalmine; the association was constituted on 11 June 1999 (gallery's 2025 accounts).
- Director: Lorenzo Giusti (gallery website).
- The collection of about 3,000 works was assembled mostly through private donations, with core holdings of the Spajani, Manzù and Stucchi collections (Wikipedia).
- The city is converting the former Palazzetto dello Sport into a new GAMeC home designed by C+S Architects, an €18 million project; works are due to finish in July 2027 and the opening in spring 2028 (Wikipedia).
- In 2025 the gallery used Palazzo della Ragione for the Maurizio Cattelan exhibition Seasons (gallery's 2025 accounts).

FUNDING
GAMeC: public-private: City of Bergamo and TenarisDalmine, with 27 named private contributors
- 2025 income was €2,073,965 against total charges of €2,059,939, a surplus of €14,027 (2025 accounts, signed Associazione GAMeC).
- Contributions and donations were €1,723,000: €1,637,000 in donations and €86,000 in project grants (2025 accounts).
- From the founding members: the Comune di Bergamo €735,000 (€519,000 in 2024) plus €15,000 for Palazzo della Ragione, and TenarisDalmine €370,000 recognised as income, with €550,000 received from TenarisDalmine in 2025 (2025 accounts).
- The Comune also provides in kind, under a convention: the offices and exhibition spaces on Via San Tomaso with their furnishings, and the water, gas, lighting, heating, telephone and cleaning (2025 accounts).
- Donations from non-members were €517,000 in 2025 (€354,500 in 2024).
- Sponsorships were €100,000: Bonaldi €25,000, Banca Intesa €25,000 and IGT Lottery €50,000 (€0 in 2024). Ticket income was €110,099.
- The accounts number 27 private contributors for 2025, including TenarisDalmine, Carvico, Brembo, Bonaldi Motori and Euro Tech, Planetel, Lovato Electric, Banca Intesa, Interchimica, IGT Lottery, Flow Meter, Publifarm, 3V Sigma, Pedrali, Nuova Demi, Italgen, Persico, FAE Technology, Curnis, Linificio e Canapificio Nazionale, Istituto Clinico Quarenghi, SABO, Besenzoni, Uniacque, FMCH and A2A.
- Fondazione Cariplo made possible the education project Guida per un Giorno (2025 accounts).
- The accounts state the board is the Consiglio Direttivo of five members.

Red flags: Gambling
- IGT Lottery sponsored GAMeC with €50,000 in 2025 (nothing in 2024) and is listed among its private contributors (2025 accounts).
- Wikipedia says IGT's lottery business was renamed Brightstar Lottery in 2025, and describes IGT as a multinational gambling company that produces slot machines and other gambling technology.
- Brightstar Lottery's release of 18 November 2025 says its subsidiary LottoItalia S.r.l. signed an agreement to operate the Italy Lotto for nine years and will manage the licence until November 2034.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In May 2011 Tenaris agreed to pay the US Department of Justice US$8.9 million after it disclosed bribes paid to officials of an Uzbek state-controlled oil firm (Wikipedia). Tenaris makes steel pipes for the petroleum industry (Wikipedia).
- TenarisDalmine, a founding member of the association that runs GAMeC and the first private contributor named in its 2025 accounts, is owned by Tenaris SA (Global Energy Monitor).

BOTTOM LINE
GAMeC is financed by the City of Bergamo (€735,000 in 2025) and by TenarisDalmine, a founding member that paid €550,000 in 2025, plus 27 named private contributors. Its accounts for 2025 document a €50,000 sponsorship from IGT Lottery, the lottery business renamed Brightstar Lottery in 2025, which operates the Italy Lotto (Wikipedia; Brightstar release).
