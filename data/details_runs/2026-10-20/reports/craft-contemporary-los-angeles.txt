CRAFT CONTEMPORARY: Los Angeles, USA
ID: craft-contemporary-los-angeles
Website: https://www.craftcontemporary.org/

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 5814 Wilshire Boulevard, Los Angeles, focused on contemporary art made from craft media and processes (Craft Contemporary).
- Painter Edith Robinson Wyle founded it as The Egg and the Eye, a restaurant and gallery that opened on 1 November 1965; by 1973 it was the non-profit Craft & Folk Art Museum, and in 2018 the board renamed it Craft Contemporary (Craft Contemporary).
- Building: a three-story neo-Georgian building designed in 1930 by Gilbert Stanley Underwood, renovated by Hodgetts & Fung and completed in 1995; the facade carries a geometric design by Los Angeles artist Brent Spears (Craft Contemporary).
- Rody N. Lopez is Executive Director; Corinna Cotsen and Wally Marks III are Board Co-Chairs (Craft Contemporary).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 5 pm, and the first Thursday of each month 12 pm to 8 pm; closed Monday and Tuesday. Admission $12; $10 for adults 65 and over, students and educators; free for children 12 and under, EBT holders and members; pay what you wish on Sundays (Craft Contemporary visit page).

FUNDING
Craft Contemporary: donor-funded: foundations, corporate partners, city and county arts funds, individuals
- Revenue was $1,068,541 and expenses $1,931,396 in the fiscal year to 30 June 2025; contributions were $972,504, of which government grants were $36,662 (Form 990 FY2025; ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Annual donor recognition list for 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026: 13 foundations, including the Getty Foundation, the Henry Luce Foundation and The Ralph M. Parsons Foundation; 8 corporate partners, including Sony Pictures Entertainment and First Citizens Bank; 7 community partners, including LACMA and KCRW; and government support from the City of Los Angeles Department of Cultural Affairs, the LA County Department of Arts and Culture and the office of Supervisor Holly J. Mitchell.
- Perenchio Foundation: a $560,000 unrestricted grant, announced by the museum on 8 July 2026, that includes the establishment of operating reserves (Craft Contemporary press release).
- The donor list shows individual donors in tiers up to Benefactors of Craft at $25,000 to $49,999.

Red flags: Fossil fuels
- The Getty Foundation is listed under Foundations on the annual donor recognition list for 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 (Craft Contemporary).
- Wikipedia states that J. Paul Getty founded the Getty Oil Company in 1942 and left nearly $660 million in Getty Oil stock to the J. Paul Getty Trust, which received the full bequest in 1982, and that the Trust can spend up to 0.75% of its endowment on gifts and grants. The Getty Foundation began in 1984 as the Trust's Getty Grant Program (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
Craft Contemporary is funded mainly by contributions, which were 91% of FY2025 revenue, from foundations including the Getty, Luce, Parsons and Perenchio foundations, corporate partners, Los Angeles city and county arts agencies and individual donors. Wikipedia states that the J. Paul Getty Trust, whose Getty Foundation is on the 2025-26 donor list, was endowed with Getty Oil stock.
