421 ARTS CAMPUS (FORMERLY WAREHOUSE421): Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates
ID: 421-arts-campus-formerly-warehouse421-abu-dhabi
Website: https://www.421.online (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Arts campus in the Mina Zayed port district of Abu Dhabi, at Zayed Port next to the Abu Dhabi Vegetable Market and Mina Center. It describes itself as an independent platform for emerging artists and creative practitioners in the UAE and the region.
- Established in 2015 as Warehouse421 in a renovated warehouse; since renamed 421. Backed by the Salama bint Hamdan Al Nahyan Foundation (The Gazelle, 2017; Gulf News, 2018). Faisal Al Hassan is general manager (The National, 2021).
- Earlier name: Warehouse421. The building sits between warehouses 422 and 423 and has no number plate of its own (The Gazelle).
- Building: converted from a set of warehouses at Mina Zayed port, in a commercial area dating to 1968, designed by Bjarke Ingels Group with the older structure kept, perforated steel cladding and a new public plaza (Gulf News).
- On site: galleries, studios, courtyards, co-working spaces, a library, a cafe and an outdoor plaza used for screenings and performances. The space says it has held over forty exhibitions and more than 1,000 public programmes.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 8pm, closed Monday. All exhibitions are free; most talks and workshops are free, with pre-registration required. Student discounts on request. Free parking, with parking for People of Determination and a drop-off area; the page gives no further accessibility details.

FUNDING
421 Arts Campus: privately funded by a royal family foundation
- The Salama bint Hamdan Al Nahyan Foundation supports the venue (The Gazelle, 5 November 2017), is called its parent organisation (The National, 16 May 2021) and, in Gulf News (2018), is named as having established the venue. Gulf News describes the foundation as a "philanthropic organisation that develops and supports strategic initiatives" in arts, culture, heritage, education and health. Wikipedia says Sheikha Salama bint Hamdan Al Nahyan founded the foundation, serves as its president, and is married to Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, the UAE's president and ruler of Abu Dhabi.
- No amounts, accounts or budget were found. The campus's about page does not name the foundation; its partners page names the foundation only as commissioner of the National Pavilion UAE.
- Grants it gives out: an Artistic Research Grant of up to Dh75,000 for two or three artists a year in the Middle East, North Africa and South Asia region, and a Curatorial Development Exhibition Programme of up to Dh20,000 per applicant (The National, 16 May 2021).
- Learning programmes are developed with the Abu Dhabi Department for Education and Knowledge, SEDRA Foundation for Inclusion, New York University Abu Dhabi and Zayed University (stated by the space).
- Its partners page lists older projects without a date for each: Art Dubai 2018 with Dubai Culture and sponsors Julius Baer and Piaget, the National Pavilion UAE at the Venice Biennale (commissioned by the same foundation, supported by the UAE Ministry of Culture and Knowledge Development), and others. These are partner projects, not shown to fund the campus.
- No reporting found of a government body funding or developing the campus itself.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the campus is a private foundation of the UAE president's wife; no fossil fuel, tobacco, arms or gambling funder was found, and no source opened shows a government body paying for it. The weak points are that the foundation's amounts are not published and the campus's about page does not name the foundation. Worth watching: the education partnerships with the Abu Dhabi Department for Education and Knowledge and two universities, which the campus lists as learning-programme partners, not funders.
