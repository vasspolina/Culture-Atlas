MUSEU DE ARTE BRASILEIRA DA FAAP (MAB): São Paulo, Brazil
ID: museu-de-arte-brasileira-da-faap-são-paulo
Website: https://www.faap.br/mab/

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Brazilian art on the São Paulo campus of the Fundação Armando Alvares Penteado (FAAP), at Rua Alagoas 903, Higienópolis (MAB website).
- The will of Armando Alvares Penteado provided for a museum; FAAP was established in 1947 and the museum opened to the public on 10 August 1961 with the exhibition "Barroco no Brasil", about 300 pieces. Its first directors were Lucia Comenale and Roberto Pinto de Souza (MAB website). Portuguese Wikipedia dates the opening to 1960 and names his widow as Annie Alwis.
- FAAP is a private higher-education institution with campuses in São Paulo, Ribeirão Preto and São José dos Campos (Portuguese Wikipedia).
- The building's hall holds plaster sculptures by Aleijadinho and architectural elements from Minas Gerais and Bahia made for the 1961 opening, and stained glass by Casa Conrado (MAB website). The Jardim Esculturas spreads large sculptures across the campus.
- The collection has about 3,500 works of 19th-, 20th- and 21st-century Brazilian art, including modernism, abstraction from the 1950s on and contemporary art, plus a foreign art collection linked to FAAP's programmes; its core was donated by Annie Penteado (MAB website).
- Programmes: guided visits for schools and groups, the talk series "Conversas no MAB" since 2016, and teacher training with the São Paulo municipal education department since 2017 and the CEETEPS state technical education centre since 2018 (MAB website).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 9:00 to 20:00, last entry 19:00, closed Monday; guide dogs are allowed (MAB website). For the Miró show, tickets were R$ 50 full and R$ 25 half price from Tuesday to Friday, R$ 60 and R$ 30 on weekends and holidays (Saulo Tafarelo, CNN Brasil, 22 July 2026).
- Current exhibition: "Miró: Mestre das Formas", more than 100 works, organised by the Instituto Totex in partnership with FAAP and MAB, curated by Jordi J. Clavero, with loans from the Fundació Joan Miró in Barcelona, the Fundació Miró Mallorca, the Museu de Arte Contemporânea de Mallorca and private European collections (CNN Brasil, 22 July 2026). It opened on 7 August 2026 and was extended to 13 December 2026 (Jornal do Brasil, Caderno B, 11 October 2026).
- "FAAP na coleção do MAM: a formação do artista" ran from 26 March to 28 June 2026 in the MAB's Salão Cultural, about 160 works from the Museu de Arte Moderna de São Paulo by 85 artists who studied or taught at FAAP; FAAP and the MAM present it (MAM press kit).

FUNDING
Museu de Arte Brasileira da FAAP: private: kept by the FAAP foundation
- FAAP was initially funded by Armando Alvares Penteado, and his widow and brother continued it after his death (Portuguese Wikipedia). The museum's collection core was donated by Annie Penteado (MAB website).
- The MAM show "FAAP na coleção do MAM" is a realização of the Ministério da Cultura through the federal Lei Rouanet, of São Paulo's municipal culture and creative economy secretariat through the PROMAC programme, and of the Museu de Arte Moderna de São Paulo (MAM press kit, 2026).
- The Miró exhibition is organised by the Instituto Totex, whose CEO is Roberto Souza Leão (CNN Brasil, 22 July 2026; Redação Forbes, Forbes Brasil, 3 August 2026).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is kept by the FAAP foundation, instituted in 1947 from the will of Armando Alvares Penteado, and its collection core was donated by Annie Penteado. The documented outside backing is project-based: the Instituto Totex organises the 2026 Miró show, and the MAM show runs under Lei Rouanet and São Paulo's PROMAC.
