KATARA CULTURAL VILLAGE: Doha, Qatar
ID: katara-cultural-village
Website: https://www.katara.net (checked 2026-11-20; the pages return only the title "Katara" without scripts)

OVERVIEW
- Cultural and commercial complex on the eastern coast of Doha between West Bay and the Pearl, built in 2010 on reclaimed land and soft-opened in October 2010 during the Doha Tribeca Film Festival (English Wikipedia).
- It is run by the Cultural Village Foundation (Katara), created by Amiri Decision No. 38 of 2010 to advance cultural life (Qatar News Agency, the state news agency, 2022). Dr Khalid bin Ibrahim Al Sulaiti is its general manager (Qatar Tribune; Qatar News Agency).
- Venues include a 5,000-seat amphitheatre of 3,275 square metres, opened on 11 December 2011 with a concert by Vangelis, and a 550-seat Opera House that is home to the Qatar Philharmonic Orchestra (Qatar News Agency, 2022).
- Tenants include the Doha Film Institute, the Qatar Fine Arts Association, Katara Art Studios, the Qatar Music Academy and the Arab Postal Stamp Museum (Qatar News Agency, 2022). English Wikipedia adds a 1.5 kilometre beach, two mosques, a maritime heritage museum opened in November 2015 and the 21 High St open-air mall.
- Programmes: the Katara Prize for Arabic Novel, launched in 2014, drew a record 2,610 entries for its 12th edition in 2026 (Qatar News Agency); in October 2026 the foundation launched the Katara International Academy for training (Qatar News Agency).
- The Chedi Katara Hotel and Resort, with 59 rooms and suites and 32 villas and chalets, opened in November 2022 (Qatar Tribune).

FUNDING
Katara Cultural Village: state-funded: a Qatari government foundation, with private partners on commercial projects
- The Art Newspaper (10 March 2016) called Katara "a government-funded complex" expected to become profitable in time, and reported, citing two people close to the matter, that its spending had been cut as Qatar's culture spending fell.
- The foundation's Katara Prize for Arabic Novel has a 2026 prize pool of 375,000 dollars, with top awards of 30,000 dollars per winner (Qatar News Agency, March 2026).
- The Chedi Katara was built under a Build-Operate-Transfer partnership between Katara and A Triple Company, chaired by Sheikh Abdul Rahman bin Abdullah bin Ghanem Al Thani. Al Sulaiti called it the first public-private partnership in hospitality and hotel projects (Qatar Tribune, 13 November 2022).

Red flags: Gulf state body: Qatari government foundation set up in 2010
- The Cultural Village Foundation was set up by Amiri Decision No. 38 of 2010, a decree of the Emir (Qatar News Agency, state agency). The Art Newspaper called Katara government-funded, and in the hotel deal Katara was the public-sector partner (Qatar Tribune).

Not related: Katara Hospitality, a Qatari state-owned hotel company owned by the Qatar Investment Authority (English Wikipedia). It shares the Katara name but is a separate company.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Katara is Gulf state money.
