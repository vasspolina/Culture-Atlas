FRAC DES PAYS DE LA LOIRE: Carquefou, France
ID: frac-des-pays-de-la-loire-carquefou
Website: https://fracdespaysdelaloire.com

OVERVIEW
- Regional contemporary art fund (Fonds régional d'art contemporain) based at La Fleuriaye in Carquefou, northeast of Nantes, with a collection of more than 2,000 works (Frac website, French Wikipedia).
- Created in 1982 as part of the national Frac network, a partnership between the French State and the regions (Frac website); its first home was the Abbaye royale de Fontevraud, then the Garenne-Lemot villa in Clisson from 1988 and a converted warehouse in Nantes in 1994 (Frac website).
- The Carquefou building opened in 2000 as the first Frac building designed for the purpose, by the architect Jean-Claude Pondevie (Frac website).
- French Wikipedia gives the Carquefou exhibition hall as 418 square metres; that article carries a notice about promotional tone and missing sources.
- A second exhibition space at the Hangar à bananes in Nantes opened in 2021 and closed in April 2025 (Frac website); French Wikipedia gives major budget cuts as the reason for the closure.
- The Frac buys work by living artists each year and runs about twenty exhibitions a year across the region's five departments, with school and hospital partnerships (Frac website).
- Claire Staebler has directed the Frac since 2022, after Laurence Gateau (French Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Frac des Pays de la Loire: funded by the State and the Pays de la Loire Region, with Sodebo as main patron
- The State, through the regional cultural affairs directorate (DRAC), and the Pays de la Loire regional council support the Frac (Frac patronage page).
- Sodebo, through its company foundation, is the Frac's "grand mécène" (main patron) and has supported it since 2012 (stated by the space).
- Collection RAJA, Art Contemporain was project patron of Xavier Veilhan's exhibition "De plain-pied", shown at the Nantes site from 17 May to 1 September 2024 (stated by the space).

BOTTOM LINE
The Frac is supported by the State through the regional cultural affairs directorate and by the Pays de la Loire Region. Its patronage page names Sodebo's company foundation as main patron since 2012 and Collection RAJA as project patron of one 2024 exhibition.
