AOMORI MUSEUM OF ART: Aomori, Japan
ID: aomori-museum-of-art-aomori
Website: https://www.aomori-museum.jp (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Prefectural art museum at 185 Chikano, Yasuta, Aomori City, next to the Sannai-Maruyama site, a Jomon-period settlement that is part of a World Heritage listing (museum English site; Wikipedia).
- Established by Aomori Prefecture under the Aomori Prefectural Museum of Art Ordinance (Ordinance No. 69 of 2005); opened on 13 July 2006 (museum site; prefectural ordinance).
- Building by the architect Jun Aoki, shaped after the excavation trenches of the nearby site: white-painted brick over a ground cut into trenches, with white cube galleries beside galleries with exposed earthen floors and walls (museum site).
- Aleko Hall, a four-storey atrium about 21 m by 19 m and 19 m high, displays Marc Chagall's backdrop for the ballet "Aleko" (museum site).
- Collection and outdoor works: Shiko Munakata, Yoshitomo Nara, Tohl Narita; Nara's "Aomori Dog" sculpture outside; Nara's bronze "Miss Forest" in the Hakkakudo, whose elevator is out of service, so visitors in wheelchairs or with strollers cannot view it (museum site). Also a shop and a cafe. The museum says it has welcomed over 4.5 million visitors (stated by the museum).
- It belongs to the Aomori Gokan project, a collaboration of Aomori Prefecture's five public art museums (museum site).
- Visiting: 9:30 to 17:00, last entry 16:30; closed days are on the museum calendar. Collection exhibitions: adults 700 yen, university students 400 yen, groups of 20 or more 560 yen and 320 yen; high school students and under 18 free with a ticket collected at the desk. Wheelchairs and strollers can be borrowed at the 1F desk, there is a multipurpose restroom and 18 accessible parking spaces, and staff help visitors on the stairs to the Aomori Dog.

FUNDING
Aomori Museum of Art: prefectural museum, established by Aomori Prefecture, with media and corporate supporters
- Aomori Prefecture establishes the museum and sets its fees by ordinance; the governor sets exhibition fees and can waive them (prefectural ordinance).
- Supportship Club, founded in 2016: its special corporate members are Aomori Asahi Broadcasting, Aomori Cable Television, Aomori Tanaka Gakuen, Aomori Television, Aomori Broadcasting Corporation, Aomori Michinoku Bank, Aomori Mitsubishi Electric Equipment Sales, Abe Shigumi, ABITS, Kamikita Agricultural Products Processing, Jogakura Kanko, Tsugaru Kaikyo Ferry, Tohoku Chemical Industries, Maruyo Suisan and Yoshida Sangyo Cooperative. Special corporate members pay 100,000 yen per unit, with any number of units allowed, and general corporate members pay 30,000 yen a year (museum site).
- Rie Shiga exhibition, 31 October 2026 to 14 March 2027: organised by a committee of the museum, Aomori Broadcasting Corporation, Aomori Television, Aomori Asahi Broadcasting and the Aomori Prefecture Tourism and International Exchange Organization; grants from the Ai Style Arts and Culture Foundation and the Nomura Foundation; endorsed by the prefectural and city boards of education and local newspapers and broadcasters (museum exhibition page).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a prefectural institution established by Aomori Prefecture under a 2005 ordinance, with fees set by the governor. Local broadcasters, a bank and other firms support it through a Supportship Club founded in 2016. Worth watching: the Rie Shiga exhibition from 31 October 2026, backed by three broadcasters and two foundations.
