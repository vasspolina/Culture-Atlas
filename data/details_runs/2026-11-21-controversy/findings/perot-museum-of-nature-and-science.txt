ID: perot-museum-of-nature-and-science

WIDER CONTEXT
- June 2014: the Dallas Morning News science writer Anna Kuchment noticed that a panel on the causes of climate change, "including manmade influences like carbon emissions", had been omitted from a Perot Museum exhibit since its installation in December 2012 (Nonprofit Quarterly, Jason Schneiderman, 17 June 2014, citing the Dallas Morning News).
- Nonprofit Quarterly stated that the earth sciences hall where the panel was to hang was named for the Rees-Jones Foundation, started by the Chief Oil & Gas founder Trevor Rees-Jones and his wife Jan, and that ExxonMobil had given more than a million dollars (Nonprofit Quarterly).
- Steve Hinkley, vice president of programs, said he learned of the omission only when a reporter began asking about it, and had a temporary panel installed. He said "neither the museum's donors nor its board of directors has any say in museum content" and that none had ever opposed the display of information about climate change (Nonprofit Quarterly).
