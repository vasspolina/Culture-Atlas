YOKOHAMA MUSEUM OF ART: Yokohama, Japan
ID: yokohama-museum-of-art-yokohama
Website: https://yokohama.art.museum/

OVERVIEW
- Municipal art museum at 3-4-1 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, in the Minato Mirai 21 district of Yokohama, next to the Yokohama Landmark Tower (museum website, Wikipedia).
- Opened on 3 November 1989 under a Yokohama city ordinance, first as a facility of the Yokohama Exposition (museum annual report FY2024).
- Building by Kenzo Tange: a facade 180 metres wide, with granite among its exterior cladding, and the central Grand Gallery (museum website, annual report).
- A large renovation closed the museum from the end of fiscal 2020; the works were completed in December 2023, and it reopened on 15 March 2024 with the 8th Yokohama Triennale. After about eight months of further closure it reopened more rooms in November 2024, and the full museum opened on 8 February 2025 with the exhibition "Okaeri, Yokohama" (annual report FY2024).
- On site: nine galleries, an art library, atelier rooms, a 240-seat lecture hall, a shop, a cafe and a free-entry "Free Area" around the Grand Gallery; the collection held 15,684 works as of March 2025 (annual report FY2024).
- Visiting: open 10:00 to 18:00; the Free Area gallery is free to enter (museum website).

FUNDING
Yokohama Museum of Art: mainly public: the City of Yokohama pays the operator, the Yokohama Arts Foundation, a management fee
- Operator: the Yokohama Arts Foundation, designated manager from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2033 under the city's designated manager system (annual report FY2024, museum website).
- FY2024 income was 1,096,373,766 yen: the designated manager fee 880,780,564 yen, own projects 91,289,883 yen, user fees 22,619,380 yen, operating support from the City of Yokohama 40,085,000 yen, and miscellaneous income 61,598,939 yen. Expenditure was 1,076,792,603 yen, including 350,047,341 yen on personnel and 177,295,096 yen on the large renovation (annual report FY2024).
- HEART to ART corporate programme: in FY2024 five companies took part: NTT TechnoCross, KSP, Japan Real Estate Investment Corporation, Joytec and Yokohama Shinkin Bank. Their activities included a drawing workshop (Japan Real Estate Investment), outreach visits (Yokohama Shinkin Bank) and volunteers (NTT TechnoCross) (annual report FY2024). The museum's corporate page now lists six partners: NTT TechnoCross, KSP, Japan Real Estate Investment Corporation, Joytec, Daiichi Sangyo and Yokohama Shinkin Bank (museum website).
- Other support schemes: the Yokohama Museum of Art Cooperation Association (individual and corporate members) and the Yokohama Cultural Fund, set up by the City of Yokohama in 1981 (museum website).
- The foundation's board is headed by chairman Seiichi Kondo, with the museum's director Mika Kuraya as an executive director; its councillors include the head of the Yokohama Chamber of Commerce and Industry (annual report FY2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the City of Yokohama's: 880.8 million of its 1.096 billion yen income in FY2024 was the management fee paid to the operating foundation, with a further 40.1 million yen of city operating support. The HEART to ART programme brought in five companies in FY2024, with workshops, outreach and volunteering.
