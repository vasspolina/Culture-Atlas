KUNSTHALLE TBILISI: Tbilisi, Georgia
ID: kunsthalle-tbilisi-tbilisi
Website: https://kunsthalletbilisi.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art initiative in Tbilisi without a permanent building, led by the curators Irena Popiashvili and Lika Chkuaseli (The Art Newspaper, 16 May 2018). Its own site describes it as a roving exhibition space founded by the two curators, showing Georgian and international art in locations across Tbilisi (archived February 2023).
- Launched on 18 May 2018 with two solo shows: Nika Kutateladze in a Soviet studio apartment on Pavlov Street and Angelica Mesiti in a winery on Petriashvili Street (The Art Newspaper, Lorena Muñoz-Alonso, 16 May 2018).
- The Art Newspaper calls it "the nomadic project Kunsthalle Tbilisi" and describes it as chief among the initiatives supporting education and local artists in the city (23 May 2018).
- In 2024 it used a rented apartment as a venue: "Conspiracy Bar" by the collective Around the Studio ran from 27 September to 7 November 2024 in a rented apartment, with video and film screenings, music and experimental theatre (Kunsthalle Tbilisi site, archived July 2025).
- Visiting: for that exhibition, Thursday to Sunday 14:00 to 19:00 and by appointment.

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Tbilisi: private non-profit, per The Art Newspaper
- The Art Newspaper (23 May 2018) writes that in Tbilisi "commercial galleries, art fairs and privately funded non-profits" step in, and calls Kunsthalle Tbilisi chief among these initiatives.

BOTTOM LINE
Kunsthalle Tbilisi is a non-profit that works in changing venues, including a rented apartment in 2024, led by its two founding curators. The Art Newspaper's article of 23 May 2018 places it among the commercial galleries, art fairs and privately funded non-profits of Tbilisi.
