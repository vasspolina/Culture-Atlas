KUNSTHAUS BREGENZ – Bregenz, Austria
Website: https://www.kunsthaus-bregenz.at (sponsor and partner pages load; /en/about-kub/ could not be fetched, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum (KUB) on Lake Constance, commissioned by the State of Vorarlberg and designed by Peter Zumthor; built 1990–1997, opened July 1997 with James Turrell. Daylight building with a skin of 912 etched glass panels; three top-lit floors, a ground floor of about 500 m², two basement levels; separate admin building with café.
- Operated by the Vorarlberger Kulturhäuser-Betriebsgesellschaft (KUGES), the state's culture-house company. Director Thomas D. Trummer since 2015; KUGES supervisory board extended his contract to May 2030 (Der Standard).
- Holds a collection ("Archive Art Architecture", contemporary Austrian art). Access partners include the Blinden- und Sehbehindertenverband Vorarlberg, Aktion Demenz, Mensch Zuerst and Hunger auf Kunst & Kultur. No hours or prices found in sources checked.

FUNDING
Kunsthaus Bregenz: mainly public (State of Vorarlberg), with a state-owned bank as principal sponsor.
- Land Vorarlberg: subsidises KUB through KUGES. Audit office (Landesrechnungshof, Dec 2012) put KUB's subsidy at €2.0M (2011) rising to €2.6M by 2014 "because a main sponsor dropped out"; KUGES had €8.7M revenue in 2011, €7.0M of it state subsidy (vol.at).
- Hypo Vorarlberg: "Principal sponsor", funds major survey exhibitions (KUB site, undated). The bank is majority-owned by the State of Vorarlberg.
- vkw (illwerke vkw): "longstanding sponsor" (KUB site). Main owner is the State of Vorarlberg; it runs hydropower and also supplies natural gas and operates the gas grid (company presentation 2025).
- Zumtobel (lighting), KUB Freunde, Mader Flatz Schett: supporters (KUB site). UNIQA: "Partnership 2026". Hugo Boss: Hugo Boss Prize partner.
- No published KUB budget for recent years found.

Red flags: Principal sponsor named in the Panama Papers (2016)
- In April 2016 Hypo Vorarlberg CEO Michael Grahammer resigned after the bank was named in the Panama Papers; Austria's financial market authority reviewed its accounts. A 2012 probe into a Virgin Islands firm linked to Russian oligarch Gennady Timchenko (US-sanctioned) was dropped in 2013 for lack of evidence. Grahammer denied the bank broke any laws (AFP via ARY News; RTÉ).

Red flags: Sponsor sells natural gas
- illwerke vkw is mostly hydropower but supplies natural gas and runs Vorarlberg's gas grid (1,865 GWh/year delivered) (illwerke vkw, stated by the company).

BOTTOM LINE
KUB is a state institution funded mainly by the State of Vorarlberg, with sponsors that are largely state-owned regional companies. Nothing found against the museum itself; worth watching are the principal sponsor's 2016 Panama Papers exposure and vkw's gas business.
