VISUAL – Carlow, Ireland
Website: https://www.visualcarlow.ie/ (ok, checked 2026-10-20; the /support page in the record returned an error)

OVERVIEW
- VISUAL Centre for Contemporary Art and the George Bernard Shaw Theatre, Carlow, established 2009. Designed by British architect Terry Pawson after an RIAI international competition, on a site donated by Carlow College; includes a 330-seat theatre (stated by the space).
- Operated by Carlow Arts Centre Ltd (CACL), a company limited by shares whose sole shareholder is Carlow County Council. The 2023–2027 strategic plan describes a voluntary board, a CEO/Artistic Director, and 7 full-time-equivalent staff plus 9 casual staff (stated by the space).
- Board listed on its site, chaired by Coilín O'Reilly (appointed 2026); includes two county councillors (Fergal Browne, Ken Murnane) and a Carlow County Council culture officer (Michael Brennan).
- Gallery open Tue–Sat 11am–5:30pm, Sun 2pm–5pm, closed Mon; exhibitions free. The site has font-size and contrast controls and links to an accessibility page (stated by the space).

FUNDING
VISUAL: mainly public (local authority and state).
- Building: €18 million joint project of the Carlow local authorities, including a €3.17 million Department of Arts, Sport & Tourism grant under the ACCESS programme (stated by the space and Carlow County Council).
- Further small state capital grants: €20,000 (ACCS 2017, drawn down Dec 2018) and €10,000 (Small Capital Grant 2015, drawn down Nov 2016), per a 2023 Dáil written answer.
- Running funders shown as logos: the Arts Council, Carlow County Council, Carlow LEO; footer logos also show Arts and Humanities Entrepreneurship Hubs and Carlow County Development Partnership (stated by the space).
- The strategic plan gives no amounts or income breakdown; it names securing core funding as an objective. 2024 financial statements are linked from the governance page but were not read in this run.
- No reporting found of corporate sponsors or private donors.
- No reporting found of any link between VISUAL or its board and Jeffrey Epstein.

Not related: School of Visual Arts (New York) — a US art school whose MFA chair David A. Ross resigned in 2026 after emails with Jeffrey Epstein were released (ABC News). It has no connection to VISUAL in Carlow.
