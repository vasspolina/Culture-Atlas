CAPACETE: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
ID: capacete-rio-de-janeiro
Website: http://capacete.org

OVERVIEW
- International artistic residency in the Glória neighbourhood of Rio de Janeiro, with the residency house at Rua Benjamin Constant 129 and the Criolense Kitchen Club at Rua Santo Amaro 33 (CAPACETE history page).
- Conceived by the artist Helmut Batista in 1997 (CAPACETE history page), who is listed as its overall director. Rubens Takamine is artistic director.
- Its own history page calls it the oldest residence promoting public programmes and cultural exchanges in Brazil.
- The site reports more than 600 artists or cultural agents in residence, more than 800 public lectures and more than 24 publications between 1997 and 2025 (stated by CAPACETE).
- Past participation includes three São Paulo Biennials, two Panoramas of Brazilian Art, the Tate Turbine Hall and Documenta 14 in Athens (stated by CAPACETE).
- Residencies last up to three months.

FUNDING
CAPACETE: FUNARTE, the Ministry of Culture and the Foreign Ministry of Brazil supported its 2013 Frankfurt exhibition
- 2013: CAPACETE's exhibition at Portikus in Frankfurt, part of Brazil's programme as guest of honor at the 2013 Frankfurt Book Fair, listed FUNARTE, the Brazilian Ministry of Culture and the Brazilian Foreign Ministry as supporters (e-flux announcement, 21 September 2013).
- The same announcement states CAPACETE "was recently forced to discontinue part of its activities due to insufficient local financial support."
- The application page says CAPACETE cannot fund any resident through the application process and will try to help selected applicants find funding.
- The previous artistic board, 2010 to 2021, included Andrea Fraser, Suely Rolnik, Denise Ferreira da Silva, Esther Schipper, Marcia Fortes, Krist Gruijthuijsen and Amilcar Packer. The current artistic board (2022 to 2026) is Tanja Baudain, Jorge Vasconcelos, Jarbas Lopes, Katharina, Mariana Pimentel, Marli Matsumoto, João Modé and Anna Linemann (CAPACETE).

BOTTOM LINE
CAPACETE is a residency led by its founder Helmut Batista. FUNARTE, the Ministry of Culture and the Foreign Ministry of Brazil are the funders named on its 2013 Frankfurt exhibition announcement.
