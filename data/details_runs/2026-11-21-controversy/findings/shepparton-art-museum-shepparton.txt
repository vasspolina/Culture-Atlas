WIDER CONTEXT
- Initial estimates put the new building at A$30m against a final figure of about A$50m, paid for by Greater Shepparton council and the Australian government with about A$15m each, Victoria with A$12.5m and private contributions of A$7.5m (The Art Newspaper, Tim Stone, 7 January 2022).
- Councillor Fern Summer has opposed the museum since a "fanciful business case" was approved in 2014; in a 2020 Facebook post she wrote that the museum "was dangled in front of Councillors like a carrot", and she was re-elected on an anti-SAM platform, saying councillors voted for budget increases and that "the top end of town really played us" (The Art Newspaper).
- Rebecca Coates, artistic director and chief executive, who announced her departure a week after the 20 November 2021 opening, said the friction is partly because the museum challenges the town's agricultural image: "That's not always going to be comfortable for people" (The Art Newspaper).
