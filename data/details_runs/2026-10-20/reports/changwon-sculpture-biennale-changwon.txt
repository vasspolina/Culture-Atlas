CHANGWON SCULPTURE BIENNALE: Changwon, South Korea
ID: changwon-sculpture-biennale-changwon
Website: https://www.changwonbiennale.or.kr

OVERVIEW
- Sculpture biennale held across Changwon, Masan and Jinhae in Gyeongsangnam-do; the 2024 edition used four venues: Seongsan Art Hall, the Seongsan Shell Mound, the Dongnam Stadium of the Changwon Complex Culture Center and the Changwon Municipal Masan Moon Shin Museum of Art (Newsgn, 12 November 2024).
- The Biennial Foundation says the biennale was established on the opportunity presented by the unification of the formerly separate cities of Changwon, Masan and Jinhae.
- The first edition paid tribute to the contemporary sculptor Moon Shin (Biennial Foundation).
- Hosted and organised by Changwon City and the Changwon Culture Foundation (Artbava for 2024, Hankook Sotong Today for 2026); the biennale's address is 181 Jungang-daero, Uichang-gu, Changwon (Biennial Foundation).
- 7th edition, "Silent Apple" (Artbava's English title), 27 September to 10 November 2024, artistic director Hyeon Seewon: 86 artists and collaborators from 16 countries, 177 works including 47 new commissions, and 107,710 visitors, about 20% more than in 2022 (Newsgn, 12 November 2024).
- 8th edition, "Resonance Field", 30 September to 15 November 2026, with co-artistic directors Cho Hye-jung and Zhang Jun, the first Korea and China co-direction in the biennale's history; 74 teams and 81 artists (Hankook Sotong Today, 30 September 2026).
- Visiting: the Masan Moon Shin Museum of Art, one 2024 venue, is open Tuesday to Sunday 09:00 to 18:00 and closed on Mondays (Artbava).

FUNDING
Changwon Sculpture Biennale: public and corporate: Changwon City and its Culture Foundation host it, with national and provincial supporters and local corporate sponsors
- Changwon City and the Changwon Culture Foundation are the hosts and organisers in 2024 and 2026 (Artbava, Hankook Sotong Today).
- The 2024 edition lists as supporters the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, Gyeongsangnam-do province, the Korea Industrial Complex Corporation Gyeongnam regional office, BNK Kyongnam Bank and the Gyeongnam Mecenat Association (Artbava).
- BNK Kyongnam Bank announced on 21 October 2022 that it had given 25 million won to the Changwon Culture Foundation for the promotion and running of the 2022 edition (Twig24, "BNK Kyongnam Bank sponsors Changwon Sculpture Biennale with 25 million won", translated title).
- The 2024 edition also had 53 young people from local universities as interns through the Ministry of Employment and Labor's work-experience programme (Newsgn).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the biennale is public and corporate: it is hosted and organised by Changwon City and the Changwon Culture Foundation, with the national culture ministry, the provincial government and the Korea Industrial Complex Corporation listed as 2024 supporters, plus BNK Kyongnam Bank and the Gyeongnam Mecenat Association. The one corporate amount on record is BNK Kyongnam Bank's 25 million won in 2022.
