FONDATION IFITRY: Essaouira, Morocco
ID: fondation-ifitry-essaouira
Website: https://biennalecasablanca.org/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Artists' residency on the coastal road between Essaouira and Safi, 50 km from Essaouira and 70 km from Safi, directly opposite the Contemporary Art Centre of Essaouira (Casablanca Biennale website).
- Created in 2008 by Mostapha Romli and made a foundation in 2014 (The Art Newspaper France, Olivier Rachet, 3 August 2022). The Biennale website describes Romli as a Moroccan artist, photographer and publisher who initiated the Casablanca International Biennale in 2012.
- Facilities: twelve ocean-facing pavilions of 20 to 120 m2 with one to three bedrooms, and workshops for engraving, screen printing, painting, ceramics, photography and video; it takes visual, performing, sound and literary artists (Casablanca Biennale website).
- The pavilions are described as an extension of the Contemporary Art Centre, and their walls hang works by Moroccan and international artists (Casablanca Biennale website); the centre's collection holds about 2,000 works (The Art Newspaper France, 2022).
- Artists apply to a selection committee or are invited for exhibition projects, according to Romli (The Art Newspaper France, 2022).
- By 2022 it had hosted more than 570 artists from 73 countries; by January 2014 the count was 374 artists from 45 countries (The Art Newspaper France; Le360, Bouthaina Azami, 25 January 2014).
- The 6th Casablanca Biennale, "Fabricating the Impossible", curated by Marisa Caichiolo with more than 80 artists, moves to Essaouira from 10 December 2026 to 10 January 2027, partly at the Ifitry residency, to strengthen ties with it (Casablanca Biennale website). It had first been announced for 11 December 2025 to 10 January 2026 in Casablanca (La Vie Éco, 2025).

FUNDING
Fondation Ifitry: private: founded by the artist and publisher Mostapha Romli, tied to the Maroc Premium Foundation's art centre, with donated works
- Since 2014 the Casablanca Biennale has operated under the Maroc Premium Foundation, which also oversees the Contemporary Art Centre (established 2011), a collection of contemporary works and the BIC Project Space in Casablanca, opened in 2019 (Casablanca Biennale website). The Biennale calls itself an independent curatorial platform in a context where public art infrastructure remains underdeveloped (stated by the Biennale).
- The foundation is attached to ("adossée au") the Contemporary Art Centre of Essaouira (The Art Newspaper France, 2022).
- Some artists donated works made during their stay to support the foundation (Le360, "Naissance d'une fondation, une exposition exceptionnelle", 25 January 2014).
- In January 2014 a collective show of works made at Ifitry was held at the Sofitel Casablanca Tour Blanche, part of an exhibition cycle started by Maroc Premium and Sofitel Luxury Hotels (Le360).

BOTTOM LINE
Ifitry is a private residency built around one artist-publisher, with no state or corporate funder named beyond a hotel exhibition partner and no flagged money on record. Its finances are not published.
