MENIL COLLECTION: Houston, USA
ID: menil-collection-houston
Website: https://www.menil.org/ (checked 2026-10-24; live, carries a "© 2026 The Menil Collection" notice)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 1533 Sul Ross Street, Houston, holding the collection of John and Dominique de Menil: Surrealist works by Max Ernst and René Magritte, pieces by Picasso, Warhol and Rothko, antiquities, Byzantine icons and large African and Indigenous American holdings. Only about 5% is on view at a time (Wikipedia).
- The de Menils settled in Houston in 1941 after leaving Nazi-occupied France. The collection now holds more than 25,000 objects and the main building, designed by Renzo Piano, opened in 1987 (Wikipedia).
- Named for the founders. The Menil Foundation, established in 1954, governs the museum; Rebecca Rabinow replaced Josef Helfenstein as director in 2016 (Wikipedia).
- Campus: the Cy Twombly Gallery, the Dan Flavin Installation, the Byzantine Fresco Chapel and the Menil Drawing Institute, opened in 2018. The Rothko Chapel is a separate, independent organisation (Wikipedia).
- The Byzantine Fresco Chapel's frescoes were taken from a church in Lysi, Cyprus, and recovered from the illegal art trade in the 1980s; in September 2011 the museum announced they would return to Cyprus in February 2012 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday 11 am to 7 pm, closed Monday and Tuesday. Admission is always free. All buildings have accessible entrances and some wheelchairs are available (museum website).

FUNDING
Menil Collection: privately funded: the de Menil family's oilfield-services fortune, investment income and donors, with free admission
- Form 990 (ProPublica), year to June 2025: revenue $37,261,644, of which contributions $12,370,839, investment income $9,718,623 and sales of assets $12,173,832; expenses $31,282,405; net assets $525,467,914. In FY2024 revenue was $36.4 million against expenses of $27.2 million.
- The family fortune came from Schlumberger: Dominique de Menil was an heiress to the "Schlumberger Limited oil equipment fortune", and her husband John became president of two regional branches of the company (Wikipedia). Her father Conrad Schlumberger co-founded the company with his brother Marcel in 1926 (Wikipedia).
- Corporate sponsors on the museum's page, by tier: $100,000+ Bloomberg Philanthropies and Loro Piana; $35,000+ Frost; $30,000+ Tri Brand; $25,000+ HEB, Hotel Daphne, Paul Hastings, SLB, UBS; $15,000+ Fayez Sarofim & Co., Marek, PNC, White & Case; $10,000+ Hightower Advisors, Hotel Saint Augustine, Houston Trust Company, Kirkland & Ellis, U.S. Capital Wealth; $5,000+ others, including Bank of America. The page does not match sponsors to projects or years.
- No reporting found of the composition of the endowment or of a board list.

Red flags: fossil fuels
- SLB, an oilfield services company that rebranded from Schlumberger in 2022, is listed at the $25,000+ tier on the museum's corporate support page, which shows "© 2026" and a "Fiscal Year 2027 Corporate Committee" (menil.org/corporate-support, read 24 October 2026; Wikipedia).
- The founders' wealth came from the same company (see Funding); no source read states how much of the present endowment derives from it.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little criticism of the Menil's funding in art media. The one sustained row, in 2011 to 2013, concerned a work in its collection.
1. Arts and Culture Texas: Devon Britt-Darby, "Response: The Menil's fake deaccessioning scandal" (2013; a local arts blog)
URL: https://artsandculturetx.com/?p=4540
- Argues the Menil mishandled The Art Guys Marry a Plant: it acquired the work with little public attention in 2011, sited it near the Rothko Chapel and declined a public discussion. Calls the Art Guys' deaccessioning scandal fake.
- Names the Art Guys, Rainey Knudson, director Josef Helfenstein and curator Toby Kamps. Criticises the museum's handling and the artists' claim; concerns neither funders nor money.

WIDER CONTEXT
- More than 260 British artists asked five UK theatres (the National Theatre, Royal Shakespeare Company, Young Vic, Royal Court and Almeida) in May 2026 to end ties with Bloomberg Philanthropies, calling it "complicit in grave war crimes" against Palestinians through its work with Israeli settlement leaders (The New Arab, 29 May 2026).
- Connection: Bloomberg Philanthropies sits in the Menil's top sponsor tier. The campaign names the theatres, not the Menil, and no reporting found of a campaign aimed at the Menil.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Menil is the de Menil family's oilfield-services fortune, held in net assets of about $525 million, plus gifts and a corporate sponsor list that includes SLB, the successor to the family company. The weak point is that oil link and the lack of itemised sponsor information. Worth watching: Bloomberg Philanthropies in the top tier while a campaign targets it elsewhere, and any change in the sponsor list.
