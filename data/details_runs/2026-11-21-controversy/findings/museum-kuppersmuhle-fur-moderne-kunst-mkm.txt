ID: museum-kuppersmuhle-fur-moderne-kunst-mkm

WIDER CONTEXT
- The first extension of the museum, designed by Herzog & de Meuron, failed in 2014 "wegen Pfusch bei den Schweißarbeiten" ("because of botched welding work", translated) (Volksstimme, Dorothea Hülsmeier, dpa, 5 April 2017).
- The costs had risen to an estimated 40 million euros, and the white steel framework rusted beside the museum for years before it was scrapped; the city and the collectors Sylvia and Ulrich Ströher "zogen die Notbremse" ("pulled the emergency brake", translated) (Volksstimme, dpa, 5 April 2017).
