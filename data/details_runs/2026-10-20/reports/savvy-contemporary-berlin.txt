SAVVY CONTEMPORARY: THE LABORATORY OF FORM-IDEAS: Berlin, Germany
ID: savvy-contemporary-berlin
Website: https://savvy-contemporary.com

OVERVIEW
- Independent artistic organisation and discursive platform at Reinickendorfer Straße 17 in Berlin-Wedding, where it has been located since 2016. It calls itself "the laboratory of form-ideas" and has around 25 members (stated by the space).
- Founded in 2009 in Berlin-Neukölln. Bonaventure Soh Bejeng Ndikung was artistic director until 2022, Renan Laru-an from 2023 to 2025; Lema Sikod and Lynhan Balatbat-Helbock are now executive directors (SAVVY website).
- Wikipedia calls Ndikung the founding director of SAVVY Contemporary and says he was to take over the Haus der Kulturen der Welt (HKW) in Berlin on 1 January 2023. The board page of SAVVY friends e.V., the space's friends' association, names him as founder and chairperson of SAVVY Contemporary.
- The space situates itself "at the threshold of the West and the non-West", working in exhibition-making, research, sonic and visual cultures and embodied knowledges.
- On site: a participatory archive on German colonial history, a documentation centre of performance arts, a library, a residency programme, a series of SAVVY books, the record label SAVVY records, the radio platform SAVVYZΛΛR and educational projects with schools.
- Current project: "Architectural Echotones. A Poetics of the Surrounds" with the Brazilian collective Vilanismo, 11 September to 22 November 2026, part of the travelling programme of the 36th São Paulo Bienal.
- Visiting: during exhibitions Thursday to Sunday 14:00 to 19:00; free entry, donations welcome; accessible by wheelchair (project page).

FUNDING
SAVVY Contemporary (Berlin): public project funding and donations: Berlin Senate culture department, Kulturstiftung des Bundes and SAVVY friends
- The space states that it works independently of commercial interests and is partly funded by the Berlin Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion; it also receives other public funding, private sponsorship, donations and contributions from SAVVY friends e.V.
- The space states: "Our financial situation as a non-profit is precarious."
- "Architectural Echotones" (2026) is supported by the Fundação Bienal de São Paulo, the Berlin Senate Administration for Culture and Social Cohesion, the Brazilian Embassy in Berlin, SAVVY friends e.V., Zielinsky Gallery and private donors.
- "Unraveling the (Under)Development Complex" (funded by the TURN2 Fund of the Kulturstiftung des Bundes from 2021 to 2024; Berlin exhibition 26 November 2022 to 27 January 2023) was led by SAVVY with partners Bandjoun Station in Cameroon, African Digital Heritage in Nairobi and the Goethe-Institutes in Johannesburg and Abidjan (Kulturstiftung des Bundes).
- SAVVY friends e.V. is a membership association: 4.16 euros a month (50 euros a year). Its chairman is the artist Thomas Eller Wang and its treasurer is Wolfgang von Dallwitz. Its page lists supported projects from 2017 to 2019, 2021 to 2024 and 2026 (SAVVY website).

BOTTOM LINE
SAVVY runs on public money plus member and private donations: the Berlin Senate culture department partly funds it, the Kulturstiftung des Bundes funded its "Unraveling" project, and SAVVY friends e.V. and private donors support its projects. It states that its own finances are precarious.
