WIDER CONTEXT
- After BMC chief Ajoy Mehta ordered a review of the civic body's agreement with the museum trust, the BMC drew up measures to take more operational control: hiring all staff through a panel of BMC officers, an internal audit on the accountant general's guidelines, a gallery register and inventory, and an expert authentication committee. DNA reported that the agreement bars the BMC from ending it "unilaterally" and that managing trustee Tasneem Mehta would head the museum until 2023 (DNA, Chaitanya Marpakwar, 17 January 2016).
- In September 2016 the BMC decided to send the trust a rejoinder claiming violations of the contract. Tasneem Mehta said the notice had not reached her and that "I have scrupulously ensured that all proceedings of the museum are clean" (The Asian Age, DC Correspondent, 9 September 2016).
