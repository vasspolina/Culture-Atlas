MILWAUKEE SYMPHONY ORCHESTRA: Milwaukee, USA
ID: milwaukee-symphony-orchestra-milwaukee
Website: https://www.mso.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Professional symphony orchestra based at the Bradley Symphony Center, 212 West Wisconsin Avenue, downtown Milwaukee (mso.org).
- Founded in 1959 as the renamed Milwaukee Pops Orchestra, a part-time ensemble founded about ten years earlier; Harry John Brown was its first music director (Wikipedia).
- Its permanent home opened to the public in autumn 2021 in the restored Warner Grand Theatre; the orchestra bought the theatre in December 2017 and plays in Allen-Bradley Hall (mso.org, Wikipedia).
- Ken-David Masur was music director from 2019 to 2026; the orchestra announced in October 2024 that his tenure would end with the 2025-26 season (Wikipedia).
- Also runs the Milwaukee Symphony Chorus, founded in 1976, plus education and sensory-inclusive programmes (Wikipedia, mso.org).
- Box office open Monday to Friday 10:00 to 16:00 and Saturday 12:00 to 17:00, and 90 minutes before performances; wheelchair seating on both levels of Allen-Bradley Hall, a telecoil hearing loop and infrared headsets (mso.org).

FUNDING
Milwaukee Symphony Orchestra: privately funded: individual donors, foundations and corporate gifts, with ticket sales and an endowment
- In the year to 31 August 2025 operating revenue was USD 18.42 million: unrestricted contributions USD 12.58 million, performance revenue USD 4.22 million, other revenue USD 1.55 million and government support USD 70,000. Operating expenses were USD 25.29 million (2024-25 annual report).
- Corporate and foundation gifts in 2024-25: United Performing Arts Fund USD 1,000,000 and above; Argosy Foundation, The Lynde and Harry Bradley Foundation and Laskin Family Foundation USD 250,000 and above; Greater Milwaukee Foundation funds, Herzfeld Foundation, Rockwell Automation and We Energies Foundation USD 100,000 and above (annual report).
- Public bodies listed: National Endowment for the Arts and Wisconsin Arts Board at USD 25,000 and above; Milwaukee Arts Board and Milwaukee County Arts Fund at USD 5,000 and above (annual report).
- Endowment commitments of USD 1,000,000 and above came from the Jane Bradley Pettit Foundation, Richard Bradley, the Cudahy Foundation, the Herzfeld Foundation, the Laskin Family Foundation and named individuals (annual report).
- David and Julia Uihlein are honorary co-chairs of the board (MSO board page).
