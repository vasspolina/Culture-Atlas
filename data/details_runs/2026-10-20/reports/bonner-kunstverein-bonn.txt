BONNER KUNSTVEREIN: Bonn, Germany
ID: bonner-kunstverein-bonn
Website: https://www.bonner-kunstverein.de

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit, member-based art association at Hochstadenring 22, Bonn, with over 1,000 members and three parts: the exhibition space, a studio building and the Artothek, a collection of over 2,000 works (Bonner Kunstverein website).
- Founded on 24 May 1963 by Bonn artists and art-interested citizens at the Galerie Schütze in Bad Godesberg; founders included the painters Paul Magar, Gerhard Neumann and Ernemann Sander, and the first chairman was the journalist Thankmar von Münchhausen (German Wikipedia).
- Building: since 1986 it has occupied about 800 square metres of the former Blumenhalle at August-Macke-Platz in Bonn's old town, converted into an exhibition hall by Haus-Rucker-Co; a renovation completed in December 2006 won a 2010 BDA architecture award (German Wikipedia).
- Since 2004 it has run a studio building with eight subsidised workspaces and an annual Open Studios event (website).
- Director: Viktor Neumann, since spring 2025. Earlier directors were Margarethe Jochimsen (1978 to 1986), Annelie Pohlen (1986 to 2004), Christina Végh (2005 to 2015), Michelle Cotton (2015 to 2019) and Fatima Hellberg (2019 to 2025) (website).
- Awards: an AICA "Special Exhibition" award in 2021 and the ADKV Prize for Germany's best Kunstverein in 2022 (website).
- Visiting: exhibition space open Tuesday to Sunday, 12 to 18; Artothek open Friday 14 to 19 and Saturday 11 to 16.

FUNDING
Bonner Kunstverein: member-based non-profit: membership contributions, with state arts funding and foundations behind individual projects
- Members support it "through an annual tax-deductible contribution" (website).
- The Dora Budor exhibition "My Energy Comes From Freedom" (27 June to 6 September 2026) credits Kunststiftung NRW, the Ministry of Culture and Science of North Rhine-Westphalia, the Kemmler Foundation, Art Explora (as part of the Art Explora and Cité internationale des arts residency programme) and Galerie Molitor (e-flux announcement).
- Kunststiftung NRW is a foundation created by the government of North Rhine-Westphalia and established on 12 September 1989 (Wikipedia).
- The city of Bonn helped the association to its first own venue, the Kleiner Königshof, in 1978. The December 2006 renovation of the Blumenhalle was started with money from the benefit auction "Der Kunst ihre Räume" under director Christina Végh and supplemented by city funds; the Atelierhaus studios are city-supported (German Wikipedia).
- In 1985 Peter-Mertes Weinkellerei agreed to enable two scholarships a year for young artists in the Rhineland, and Sparkasse Köln-Bonn made long-term loans of artworks to the Artothek in 1992 and 1994 (German Wikipedia).
- The board is chaired by Dr. Anke Schierholz, with Gisela Clement as vice chair and Thomas Weppelmann as treasurer (website).

BOTTOM LINE
The Kunstverein is funded by member contributions and city of Bonn support. Its 2026 Dora Budor exhibition was credited to Kunststiftung NRW, the North Rhine-Westphalia Ministry of Culture and Science, the Kemmler Foundation, Art Explora and Galerie Molitor. The ten-person board is listed on the Kunstverein's website.
