MUU HELSINKI CONTEMPORARY ART CENTRE: Helsinki, Finland
ID: muu-helsinki
Website: https://muu.fi

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition and event space of the Artists' Association MUU in the Cable Factory (Kaapelitehdas), Tallberginkatu 1 C, Helsinki: a 160 square metre space with a six metre ceiling, which the association describes as an international project space for new areas of contemporary art (MUU website).
- The association, MUU ry, was founded in 1987. It is a national interdisciplinary art organisation that represents and promotes new media art in Finland, a member of the Artists' Association of Finland, and had 740 members at the end of 2025 (MUU website; MUU activity report 2025).
- The association moved into the renovated and extended Cable Factory space at the start of 2021 (activity report 2025). Helinä Hukkataival's retrospective "Never A Lazy Moment" (4 December 2021 to 6 February 2022) was the official inauguration of MUU Helsinki Contemporary Art Centre (Noba).
- Exhibition service fees charged to artists ended in 2021, and MUU pays a fee to all artists who show work in its exhibitions and events; in 2025 it paid artists 49,666 euros in fees, against 21,720 euros in 2024. In the 2000s the fees charged to artists covered about 20% of exhibition costs (activity report 2025).
- Programme: exhibitions, the BOOKED artists' book event, MUU Gala (a video art showcase held every two years since 2014) and the E75 art bus project, a trip to Europe planned for spring 2026 as part of Oulu2026 European Capital of Culture (activity report 2025).
- Governance: director Timo Soppela, chairperson Minka Heino and a seven-member board in 2026 (MUU website).
- Visiting: MUU Gala (18 to 21 October 2023) was free with pre-registration (Cable Factory event page). The activity report 2025 describes the space as accessible and barrier-free.

FUNDING
MUU Helsinki Contemporary Art Centre: a member-run artists' association supported by Taike and the City of Helsinki
- The MUU website states that the centre is maintained by the Artists' Association MUU, which is supported by the Arts Promotion Centre Finland (Taike) and the City of Helsinki.
- Members: membership applications are open twice a year; the 2022 membership fee was 95 euros (MUU website).
- Per the activity report 2025, MUU applies every year for operating grants from Taike and the City of Helsinki, and for project grants from various sources. Taike awarded a three-year grant for 2022 to 2024. The Finnish Heritage Agency (Museovirasto) has paid an exhibition fee grant since 2023, and MUU was among the first organisations to receive it.
- Other income: membership fees and the association's own activities; the 2025 financial statement showed a surplus of 25,031.30 euros (activity report 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The centre is run by a non-profit artists' association of 740 members and supported by the Arts Promotion Centre Finland (Taike) and the City of Helsinki. Member fees, project grants, an exhibition fee grant from the Finnish Heritage Agency and the association's own activities make up the rest.
