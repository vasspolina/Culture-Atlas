GROPIUS BAU – Berlin, Germany
Website: https://www.berlinerfestspiele.de/gropius-bau (not checked 2026-10-20: fetch blocked by a permission prompt that timed out)

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition house in a neo-Renaissance building by Martin Gropius and Heino Schmieden, built 1877-81 for Berlin's Museum of Applied Arts; damaged in 1945, rebuilt from 1978 and reopened 1981; renovated 1998-99; listed since 1966 (Wikipedia).
- Run by the Berliner Festspiele, a division of KBB – Kulturveranstaltungen des Bundes in Berlin GmbH. The building is rented from the Federal Real Estate Agency (BImA), which took it over in 2019; about 11,239 sq m; 52 staff on average in 2024; full renovation not expected to start before 2029/30 (KBB environmental statement 2025).
- Director: Jenny Schlenzka since 2024, announced March 2023; predecessor Stephanie Rosenthal, 2018-22 (ArtReview).
- Opening hours, prices and accessibility: not retrieved this run.

FUNDING
Gropius Bau: mainly public (federal).
- The Federal Republic of Germany is sole shareholder of KBB, which is funded by the Federal Government Commissioner for Culture and the Media (BKM) (KBB environmental statement 2025, stated by the operator; Berlin Senate culture page).
- The federal government fully finances KBB, including the Berliner Festspiele with the Gropius Bau, HKW and the Berlinale (Berlin Senate culture page, official).
- KBB reported €18.8M in event revenue across all its venues in 2024 (KBB environmental statement). No separate Gropius Bau budget found.
- Third-party funding is raised through KBB central services (director job posting, C& / e-flux 2022). No named sponsors found this run.

BOTTOM LINE
The Gropius Bau is a federally owned and federally funded exhibition house, run through KBB; no private funders of concern turned up. Nothing specific to watch beyond the planned renovation. The €12M budget figure in the existing record could not be confirmed.
