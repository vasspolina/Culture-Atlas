Red flags: 2023 dismissal of the mudac director after her probation period
- The Fondation Plateforme 10 ended its collaboration with Beatrice Leanza, director of the mudac since 1 January 2023, at the end of her probation period, citing strategic differences that, its communiqué said, did not allow her appointment to be confirmed (Radio Lac with ATS-Keystone, 4 July 2023).
- A recruitment jury had elected Leanza unanimously; Marco Costantini, her deputy, took over as interim director, and Patrick Gyger, director of Plateforme 10, said "Il assurera la mise en œuvre de la suite de la programmation dans la sérénité" ("He will carry out the rest of the programme calmly", translated) (The Art Newspaper France, Emmanuel Grandjean, 5 July 2023).
