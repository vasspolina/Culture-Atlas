THE STATE TRETYAKOV GALLERY: Moscow, Russia
ID: state-tretyakov-gallery-moscow
Website: https://tretyakovgallery.ru (the main site could not be opened from our connection on 2026-10-24; its partners site https://partners.tretyakov.ru loaded that day)

OVERVIEW
- State museum of Russian art in Moscow, built around the collection of the merchant Pavel Tretyakov (Wikipedia).
- Pavel Tretyakov gave about 2,000 works to Moscow in 1892; the gallery opened on 15 August 1893 and was named the State Tretyakov Gallery in 1918 (Wikipedia).
- Holds more than 130,000 exhibits; its contemporary art branch, the New Tretyakov, stands on Krymsky Val (Wikipedia).
- Directors: Zelfira Tregulova from 2015 to February 2023; Elena Pronicheva until January 2026; Olga Galaktionova named on 14 January 2026 by Culture Minister Olga Lyubimova (ArtReview, Novaya Gazeta Europe).

FUNDING
The State Tretyakov Gallery: state museum, supported by state-controlled banks, oil and gas companies and private donors
- The partners site says more than 80 companies and private individuals support the gallery; it gives no amounts or years.
- Corporate donors, with what they paid for: Transneft (design surveys for the Krymsky Val building, donations); VTB (title sponsor of the Vrubel, Diaghilev and Roerich shows); Sberbank; Gazprombank (donation, design work, exhibitions); PSB Bank (donation, exhibitions); NOVATEK (three exhibitions); LUKOIL (Shchusev exhibition); Surgutneftegas (a studio, the Grabar exhibition); Severstal; the Vladimir Potanin Charitable Foundation; Rosatom; Ingosstrakh. The Renova Foundation is listed with no support described (partners.tretyakov.ru).
- Private individuals on the current partners page: Leonid Mikhelson (exhibition projects in Samara) and Alisher Usmanov (a donation to the statutory activities), with no amounts or years.
- Board of trustees, undated: chaired by Andrei Belousov, "Minister of Defence of the Russian Federation"; the other 15 include the heads of Gazprombank, Renova Group, VTB, Transneft, PSB and Severstal (partners.tretyakov.ru).
- No reporting found of the gallery's federal budget.

Red flags: sanctioned banks and a sanctioned donor
- VTB: the US Treasury imposed full blocking sanctions on 24 February 2022. PSB Bank: designated 22 February 2022. Sberbank: fully blocked 6 April 2022. Gazprombank: designated 21 November 2024 (US Treasury).
- Usmanov, a private donor: designated by the US Treasury on 3 March 2022 for operating in Russia's technology sector.

Red flags: oil and gas sponsors
- Transneft, LUKOIL, Surgutneftegas and NOVATEK are on the current partners page (partners.tretyakov.ru). The US Treasury designated Surgutneftegas on 10 January 2025 and LUKOIL on 22 October 2025.

Red flags: arms and defence
- PSB Bank, a gallery donor, is described by the US Treasury (22 February 2022) as a state-owned bank that finances Russia's defense sector.

Red flags: state censorship and removal of the director
- About 4 November 2022: the Ministry of Culture cancelled the ninth Moscow Biennale three days before its 7 November opening at the New Tretyakov, citing responsibility for the show's "artistic and ethical context" (USA Art News).
- February 2023: the Ministry did not renew Tregulova's contract, reported on 13 February, after a visitor complained of "signs of a destructive ideology" and a late-January ministry letter asked the gallery to adjust displays to "spiritual and moral values". The Ministry called it a contract ending; she learned of it from the press (ArtReview, Finestre sull'Arte).
- Her successor Pronicheva is the daughter of Vladimir Pronichev, a former FSB deputy director (Finestre sull'Arte; Forbes Russia, via ArtReview).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Trustees on US lists who are not shown to pay the gallery themselves: Andrey Kostin of VTB (6 April 2018, Ukraine-related program), Viktor Vekselberg of Renova Group (6 April 2018), Petr Fradkov of PSB (22 February 2022), Olga Golodets of Sberbank and Andrey Akimov of Gazprombank (8 May 2022) (US Treasury, OFAC).
- Connection: they sit on the board of a state museum; Belousov chairs it as Minister of Defence, and the page gives him no further role.

BOTTOM LINE
The Tretyakov is a state museum whose director the Ministry of Culture replaced in 2023 and whose board the Defence Minister chairs. Its named donors include VTB, PSB, Sberbank, Gazprombank, LUKOIL, Surgutneftegas and Usmanov, all on US sanctions lists, and state censorship is documented in 2022 and 2023. Worth watching: the new director and the unpublished amounts.
