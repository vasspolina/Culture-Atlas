BULEGOA Z/B: Bilbao, Spain
ID: bulegoa-zb-bilbao
Website: https://bulegoa.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- An "office for art and knowledge" at Solokoetxe 8 bajo, in the Solokoetxe district of Bilbao (stated by the space).
- Full name "Bulegoa zenbaki barik" (Basque: "office without a number"; own translation). Founded in 2010 by Beatriz Cavia, Miren Jaio and Leire Vergara with Isabel de Naverán, who stayed until 2018; it marks its anniversary each November, the 16th in 2026 (stated by the space).
- Team: the three founders, with Silvia Coppola on production and Auria Etxeandia on communication; advisers Elena Casado, Yaiza Hernández, Itziar Okariz, Ana Vujanović and the late Guadalupe Echevarría (1948 to 2023) (stated by the space).
- Works on critical pedagogy, situated knowledge, collaborative methods, feminisms, decolonial perspectives and cultural translation, through seminars, screenings, performances, talks and artistic projects (stated by the space).
- Programme lines: Cine_ilegal (session 125 in November 2026), "El ensayo de la exposición (1977-2017)", "Material Voices", a series on feminist genealogies of exhibition-making begun in 2021, and an open-call residency for 2027 offering up to 500 euros for travel and housing and up to 200 euros for production (stated by the space).
- Partner of the Dutch Art Institute from 2017 to 2025, running eight COOP study groups in its "Curating Positions" series (Dutch Art Institute).
- A 2026 article in Arte, Individuo y Sociedad by Irantzu Sanzo-San Martín and Concepción Elorza-Ibáñez de Gauna studies it as a case of the independent Bilbao initiatives that responded to the gentrification set off by the "Guggenheim effect" (abstract read).

FUNDING
Bulegoa z/b: public: Basque, provincial, city, Spanish and EU culture funding, plus a New York foundation for one programme
- Public bodies: the site's footer credits "the support of" the Basque Government (Eusko Jaurlaritza), the Diputación Foral de Bizkaia, Bilbao City Council, the European Union's Creative Europe Programme and Spain's Ministry of Culture and Sports, without years or amounts (stated by the space).
- Foundation for Arts Initiatives, New York: supported a new project from 2020 to 2023, and the space credits it for "Material Voices" (stated by the space).
- "Formas de navegación: Cabotaje" was a public programme run with fluent in Bilbao and Santander within Bertatik Bertara / Tan Cerca 2020 (stated by the space).

BOTTOM LINE
A small office run on public culture grants and one US foundation's project money, with no corporate sponsor and no Red flag.
