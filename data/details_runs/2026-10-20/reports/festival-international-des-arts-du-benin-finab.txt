FESTIVAL INTERNATIONAL DES ARTS DU BÉNIN (FINAB): Cotonou, Benin
ID: festival-international-des-arts-du-benin-finab

OVERVIEW
- Multidisciplinary arts festival held in Cotonou, with the 2025 edition also in Porto-Novo and Ouidah (Financial Afrik, 16 February 2025).
- Initiated by Ulrich Adjovi, who says the idea came from Benin lacking a major festival like its neighbours; the project took nine months to launch (La Nation, Josué F. Mehouenou, 10 February 2026).
- Organised by Empire Événementiel, a subsidiary of Groupe Empire, which Adjovi founded in 2012. The group describes itself as pan-African, in entertainment, events, media, nightclubs and hospitality, operating in Benin, Togo, Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Congo and Nigeria (Journal du Cameroun, press release of 29 March 2024).
- Second edition: 23 to 28 April 2024 at the Palais des Congrès in Cotonou, with over 100 African artists; the first edition drew over 100,000 visitors and over 200 exhibitors, according to the organiser's release (Journal du Cameroun).
- Third edition: 21 February to 2 March 2025, theme "Tisser les liens : le narratif africain à travers l'art" (Financial Afrik). La Nouvelle Tribune cites 200,000 participants, 426 stands and 251 artists as results of the 2025 edition (Armel Tognon, 21 February 2026).
- Fourth edition: 20 February to 1 March 2026, on the theme of cultural and creative industries, with Nigeria, Togo, Lebanon and Morocco days, a hunt for 30 official masks hidden in Cotonou worth up to 1 million FCFA each, and fashion at the centre (La Nation).
- It was launched at Family Beach on 20 February 2026 by Interim Minister of Tourism, Culture and Arts Shadiya Alimatou Assouman (La Nouvelle Tribune). Matin Libre reported that the final day, 1 March 2026, drew more people than the venue could hold (4 March 2026).

FUNDING
Festival International des Arts du Bénin: privately organised with government and sponsor support: Groupe Empire, the Beninese state's arts fund (FDAC), MTN and Canal+ Impact
- Empire Événementiel organises it "with the support of the Beninese government and national and international partners" (La Nation, translated). William Codjo, director of the Agence de développement des arts et de la culture, spoke for the partner institutions, which include the Fonds de développement des arts et de la culture (FDAC).
- The 2024 release describes the festival as fitting the national cultural strategy backed by President Patrice Talon (Journal du Cameroun).
- MTN is a partner of the 2026 edition: Adjovi said that when organisers lack the money, partners like MTN help, and he credited MTN's partnership for the fourth edition's success (Matin Libre, "Finab 2026: Clap de fin sur de belles notes avec les couleurs de MTN", 4 March 2026).
- Canal+ Impact was the strategic partner of the 2025 edition, with Canal+ Bénin saying the partnership reflects its commitment to cultural and creative industries and education (Benin Web TV, Angèle M. Adanle, 23 February 2025).
- Adjovi said organising the festival needs "des centaines de millions" (Matin Libre).
- Adjovi started in entertainment by buying a Cotonou discotheque on credit; Groupe Empire now runs nightclubs, restaurants, game rooms and accommodation in Cotonou's Haie Vive district, holds a Nicolas franchise and a Subway franchise agreement, and represents Universal Music in Benin, Togo and Burkina Faso (Forbes Afrique, 1 April 2023).

BOTTOM LINE
FINAB is a commercial group's event: Groupe Empire organises it, with the Beninese state's arts fund and corporate partners MTN and Canal+ Impact documented as supporters.
