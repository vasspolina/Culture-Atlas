QAGOMA: Brisbane, Australia
ID: qagoma-brisbane
Website: https://www.qagoma.qld.gov.au/

OVERVIEW
- Art museum in South Bank, Brisbane, made up of the Queensland Art Gallery (QAG) and the Gallery of Modern Art (GOMA), about 150 metres apart in the Queensland Cultural Centre (Wikipedia).
- Founded on 29 March 1895 as the Queensland National Art Gallery; it moved into its permanent building, designed by Robin Gibson, in 1982 (Wikipedia).
- GOMA, the second building, opened in 2006 and won the 2007 RAIA National Award for Public Architecture (Wikipedia).
- GOMA houses the Australian Cinematheque, and QAGOMA hosts the Asia Pacific Triennial of Contemporary Art; the collection holds more than 20,000 works, with strengths in Asian, Oceanian, Australian and Indigenous Australian art (Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia gives 1,146,277 visitors for 2019-20, a fall it attributes to the COVID-19 pandemic.
- In February 2021 the Queensland Police Union and the Police Minister objected to a helmet artwork showing a burning police car; director Chris Saines kept it on display, saying the gallery defends artists' right to make challenging statements (4BC, 2 February 2021).

FUNDING
QAGOMA: public statutory body with corporate partners
- Legal status: the Queensland Art Gallery Act 1987 makes the Board of Trustees a body corporate and states that it is a statutory body under the Financial Accountability Act 2009. The Act lets the board set up a foundation committee to encourage gifts, donations and bequests, and lets it charge for goods, services and facilities (Queensland legislation).
- Board chair: philanthropist Paul Taylor, already a board member, was announced as chair on 22 July 2025, replacing Professor Emeritus Ian O'Connor, who had held the role since 2017 (Queensland Government statement).
- Shayher Group: a $1 million sponsorship of QAGOMA from 2021 to 2026, announced on 18 May 2021 (Indulge Magazine).
- Make, Believe, Magic: The Worlds of The Jim Henson Company (2026): supporters are Strategic Partner Tourism and Events Queensland, Shayher Group, Carnival Cruise Line, Gadens, Screen Queensland and Brisbane Festival (InDaily, Phil Brown, 3 September 2026).
- Carnival Cruise Line says on its own site that it "is proud to partner with QAGOMA" for the Henson exhibition.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Carnival Cruise Line is a wholly owned subsidiary of Carnival Corporation & plc (Wikipedia).
- Carnival Corporation was convicted in 2016 of illegally dumping oily waste and covering it up over eight years, and paid a $40 million fine (Miami Herald, Taylor Dolven and Caitlin Ostroff, via ADN, 4 June 2019).
- On 3 June 2019 Carnival Corporation pleaded guilty in the US District Court in Miami to six probation violations and agreed to a $20 million fine (Miami Herald via ADN, 4 June 2019).
- The cruise line's parent company is the party in those cases. QAGOMA's link is the cruise line's partnership in the 2026 Henson exhibition.

BOTTOM LINE
QAGOMA is a Queensland statutory body. The documented corporate support is Shayher Group's $1 million sponsorship for 2021 to 2026 and the 2026 Henson exhibition supporters, who include Carnival Cruise Line; its parent, Carnival Corporation, has US convictions dated 2016 and 2019 for dumping oily waste and violating probation.
