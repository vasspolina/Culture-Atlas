CHAPTER – Cardiff, UK
Website: https://www.chapter.cymru (ok, checked 2026-10-20). The address in the record, https://www.chapter.org, now redirects (302) to askchapter.org, the site of an unrelated US Medicare insurance agency (checked 2026-10-20).

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre on Market Road, Canton, Cardiff, founded 1971 by artists Christine Kinsey and Bryan Jones with journalist Mike Flood, in the former Canton High School (built 1905) (Wikipedia).
- Programme: film, theatre, performance, a free gallery, live music, comedy; café, bars and over 60 cultural workspaces; three theatres and two cinemas (Wikipedia, Visit Wales).
- Open daily: Mon–Wed 9am–9pm, Thu–Sat 9am–10pm, Sun 9am–9pm; admission to the building is free. Audio-described and captioned film screenings; Visit Wales lists hearing, mobility and visual access facilities (stated by the space / Visit Wales).
- Registered charity Chapter (Cardiff) Limited, no. 500813, registered 17 May 1971; ten trustees named on the register.

FUNDING
Chapter: mainly public grant plus earned income (film, bars, workspace rents) and trusts.
- Arts Council of Wales: offered £400,000 for 2024/25 in the 2023 Investment Review (Arts Council of Wales decision document). The record's figure of £681k core grant could not be confirmed from sources retrieved here.
- Income for year to 31 March 2025: £3,567,569; expenditure £3,879,071; 83 employees (charity register via Open Charities). No breakdown of income found.
- National Lottery and BFI (British Film Institute) appear as logos on the website; the site says support also comes from "visitors, supporters, trusts and foundations" (stated by the space).
- Film screenings provide about 20% of income; a 2015 donation from local benefactor David Seligman led to theatre and studio spaces being renamed (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found confirming the Cardiff Council, Welsh Government, Garfield Weston, Foyle or Gulbenkian UK Branch (Experimentica 2026) grants listed in the record; they were not checked against primary sources in this run.
- No reporting found of any fossil fuel company sponsorship or funding of Chapter.

BOTTOM LINE
Chapter is a long-running charity funded mainly through the Arts Council of Wales, the National Lottery and BFI, and its own earned income; no source found links it to fossil fuel money, so the existing "Fossil fuels" flag is unsupported. The main thing to watch is that its old domain, chapter.org, now redirects to an unrelated US insurance business.
