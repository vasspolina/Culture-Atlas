ID: acca

Red flags: 2023 board member's resignation after complaints about her posts
- Arts lawyer and curator Alana Kushnir resigned from ACCA's board following complaints about social media posts she made after 7 October 2023 that were critical of artists who had made statements supportive of Palestine; Crikey also reported at least two competing open letters supporting opposing sides of the conflict (Crikey, 7 December 2023).
- ACCA's board told Crikey that her resignation followed "a number of internal discussions and meetings related to social media activity and the board of directors' code of conduct" (Crikey, 7 December 2023).
- Kushnir replied that ACCA was "using my decision to resign from the Board of Directors as an opportunity to twist the truth", said one un-minuted meeting was called that did not address the board's code of conduct, and said no evidence was presented to substantiate the allegations the CEO and certain board members had made against her (Crikey, 7 December 2023).
