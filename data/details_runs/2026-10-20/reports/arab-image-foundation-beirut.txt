ARAB IMAGE FOUNDATION: Beirut, Lebanon
ID: arab-image-foundation-beirut
Website: https://arabimagefoundation.org (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Photography archive and research centre in Kantari, Beirut, holding roughly 500,000 photographic objects dating back to 1860 from South West Asia, North Africa and their diasporas (stated by the foundation). Wikipedia gives two figures, 500,000 and over 600,000.
- Registered in Beirut as an independent association in 1997 (stated by the foundation). Founded by the photographers Fouad Elkoury and Samer Mohdad and the artist Akram Zaatari; Lucien Samaha was also an original member (Wikipedia).
- Premises: moved to Kantari in 2023 from earlier premises in Gemmayze, used from 2011. The Kantari space has preservation, digitisation and research labs, a shared library, an auditorium and a public work and gathering space shared with partner organisations (stated by the foundation).
- Programmes: collecting, conservation, digitisation, research, exhibitions and a residency programme; it has co-led photograph conservation workshops in the region (stated by the foundation; Wikipedia). It holds an online database with 1,895 items digitised on the main page.
- The foundation is governed by a General Assembly that elects the board every three years. The current board is Vartan Avakian (chair), Rayya Badran (vice-chair), Donald Choubassi (treasurer), Fabiola Hanna (secretary), Nora Kennedy and yasmine habib (members) (stated by the foundation).
- Visiting: the site footer lists Tuesday to Friday, 10 am to 5 pm (stated by the foundation).

FUNDING
Arab Image Foundation: foundation-funded: six funders named on its about page
- The about page thanks its funders for "continued support" and shows six logos: Stichting DOEN, Gerda Henkel Stiftung, Culture Resource (Mawred), FfAI, Hillman Family Foundations and the UCLA Library.
- The support page asks for $25,000 to expand the alternative energy system that protects the collections and $20,500 for auditorium seating, sound equipment and lighting.
- Mellon Foundation: both grants went to the University of Delaware, not to the foundation, which is named as a collaborating partner: $560,000 over 42 months from 9 December 2010 for workshops in photograph conservation in the Middle East, with the Metropolitan Museum of Art as collaborator; and $440,000 over 36 months from 5 June 2014 for the next phase of the Middle East Photograph Preservation Initiative, in collaboration with the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Getty Conservation Institute and the Arab Image Foundation (Mellon Foundation grant records).
- The 2018 exhibition "Against Photography: An annotated history of the Arab Image Foundation" (11 May to 19 August 2018) was organised by the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea and MACBA Barcelona, with K21 Art Collection, Düsseldorf and the Sharjah Art Foundation as partner organisations (e-flux).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The coverage cited is a descriptive review of Akram Zaatari's 2018 exhibition about the foundation's history.
1. Ciel variable: Claudia Polledri, "The Arab Image Foundation through Akram Zaatari's Eyes: Or, Variations on the Theme of Photography" (issue 109, Spring-Summer 2018)
URL: https://cielvariable.ca/en/?p=122142
- Reviews Zaatari's exhibition "Against Photography", which traces the history of the foundation, founded in Beirut in 1997 to preserve regional photographic heritage after the civil war.
- Argues that the show combines Zaatari's institutional role at the foundation with his artistic practice, so it is neither a plain institutional history nor a retrospective of his work.
- Notes that the show makes no moral judgments.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation's money comes from six named funders, among them foundations and a university library. The Mellon money went to a University of Delaware project in which the foundation is a collaborating partner, not to the foundation itself. Worth watching: the foundation's appeal for energy and auditorium funds after its 2023 move.
