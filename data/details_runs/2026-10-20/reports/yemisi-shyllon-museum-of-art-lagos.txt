YEMISI SHYLLON MUSEUM OF ART: Lagos, Nigeria
ID: yemisi-shyllon-museum-of-art-lagos
Website: https://ysma.pau.edu.ng/

OVERVIEW
- University art museum on the main campus of Pan-Atlantic University in Lekki, Lagos State (Wikipedia).
- Founded through an agreement between the university and the collector Prince Yemisi Shyllon, signed in June 2015 after he proposed in September 2014 to house works from his collection there; it opened to the public in October 2019 (Wikipedia). The Art Newspaper reported the opening for 19 October 2019.
- Named for Shyllon. The agreement covered his donation of more than 1,000 artworks, taken from the 7,000 works of his Omooba Yemisi Adedoyin Shyllon Art Foundation, created in 2007 (Wikipedia, The Art Newspaper).
- Building: a two-storey concrete building designed by the Spanish architect Jess Castellote, with about 1,300 square metres of exhibition space for around 400 works; Castellote became the museum's first director in December 2018 (The Art Newspaper, Wikipedia). Construction finished in June 2019 (Wikipedia).
- On site: exhibition galleries with displays expected to change every two years; the museum aims to welcome students from other Lagos universities and schools (The Art Newspaper).
- Visiting: admission free for all visitors from 15 June 2026, the first time since the museum was established (Leadership).

FUNDING
Yemisi Shyllon Museum of Art: privately endowed university museum: the collector Yemisi Shyllon, with 2026 free admission paid by Aisha and Gbenga Oyebode
- Shyllon pledged "financial support for the construction and long-term sustainability" of the museum through regular payments (Wikipedia). The Financial Times, as reported by The Art Newspaper, put his grant at 600 million naira (about $1.7 million), meant to subsidise costs for the first ten years, with the agreement also aiming to attract sponsorship.
- THISDAY (Vanessa Obioha, "Prince Yemisi Shyllon: A Collector's Craving for Culture") quotes Shyllon saying he funded the establishment of the museum, and says he sponsors television programmes on science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics.
- Free admission from 15 June 2026: funded by the art collectors and philanthropists Aisha and Gbenga Oyebode for an initial three years. Gbenga Oyebode also sits on the museum's Advisory Board (Leadership, Chika Izuora).
- Gbenga Oyebode co-founded the law firm Aluko & Oyebode in 1993 and is chairman of Okomu Oil Palm Company Plc, in which he holds 4.37 percent (Billionaires.Africa, 18 October 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a privately endowed university museum paid for by the collector Yemisi Shyllon, with free admission from June 2026 funded by Aisha and Gbenga Oyebode. Shyllon's grant was put at about $1.7 million by the Financial Times as reported by The Art Newspaper.
