KESTNER GESELLSCHAFT: Hannover, Germany
ID: kestner-gesellschaft-hannover
Website: https://kestnergesellschaft.de

OVERVIEW
- Art association (Kunstverein) showing international contemporary art in central Hannover. It calls itself one of the largest art associations in Germany and says it has substantial support from some 2,500 members, the Förderkreis, plus corporate partners, sponsors and volunteers. Wikipedia gives the address as Goseriede 11.
- Founded in 1916 as the Kestner-Gesellschaft e.V. by Hannover citizens, including the entrepreneurs Hermann Bahlsen, August Madsack and Fritz Beindorff, to bring the international avant-garde to the city (Kestner Gesellschaft history page).
- Named for the diplomat, art historian and archaeologist August Kestner (1777 to 1853).
- Closed by force in 1936 under Nazi pressure after the dismissal of its Jewish director Justus Bier became unavoidable; it reopened in 1948 on Warmbüchenstraße (Kestner Gesellschaft history page, Wikipedia).
- Since 1997 it has used the former Goseriede swimming pool, a Jugendstil building put up in 1902 to 1905 under city architect Carl Wolff; the renovation by Koch Panse won the BDA Prize in 1997 (Wikipedia, Kestner Gesellschaft history page). Wikipedia names Eva Birkenstock as director from August 2025.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 am to 6 pm, Thursdays until 8 pm, closed Mondays. Admission is EUR 7, reduced EUR 5, free for members and free every Friday. The building is described as "almost barrier-free", with a ramp, an elevator and an accessible toilet.

FUNDING
Kestner Gesellschaft: mixed: state of Lower Saxony, members and 32 named corporate partners
- The Kestner Gesellschaft states that it is funded by the State of Lower Saxony (its home page shows the Niedersächsisches Ministerium für Wissenschaft und Kultur) and that NDR accompanies its exhibitions and events as a cultural partner.
- Membership: the Förderkreis and some 2,500 members support the association (Kestner Gesellschaft).
- Its corporate partners page (copyright 2026) names 32 corporate partners and sponsors: ADIURO. WIDER CONTEXT
- Talanx and HANNOVER Rück SE are both named on the Kestner Gesellschaft's corporate partners page. Clean Energy Wire (Benjamin Wehrmann, "German insurers cover U.S. LNG industry, despite sustainability pledges, report", 23 February 2024) summarised investigative reporting by Correctiv: Allianz, Hannover Re, Talanx and Munich Re had entered into contracts for insuring LNG projects in the U.S., contracts that were often signed by subsidiary companies.
- Connection: the article names the insurers and does not name the Kestner Gesellschaft.

BOTTOM LINE
The Kestner Gesellschaft is funded by the State of Lower Saxony, members and a named list of 32 corporate partners. Two of them, the insurers Talanx and HANNOVER Rück, were named in Correctiv's 2024 reporting on US LNG insurance contracts, as summarised by Clean Energy Wire; the Gerhold family's GETEC group, an energy services company, is another partner.
