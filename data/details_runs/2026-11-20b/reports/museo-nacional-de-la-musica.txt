MUSEO NACIONAL DE LA MÚSICA: Havana, Cuba
ID: museo-nacional-de-la-musica
Website: https://dcubamusica.cult.cu/ (checked 2026-11-20; the site of its parent body, the Instituto Cubano de la Música, which has no page for the museum; the http www address returned a 502 error)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Cuban musical history and instruments in the Casa Pons, Calle Capdevila 1 between Habana and Aguiar, at the entrance to Old Havana (EcuRed; Spanish Wikipedia; Prensa Latina, 2024).
- EcuRed dates its inauguration to 9 September 1971; the first donation that year, two guitars and decorations of the troubadour María Teresa Vera, went to the Sala de las Mamparas of the Museo Colonial (EcuRed).
- Diario de Cuba gives its first name as the Museo y Archivo de la Música Cubana, renamed Museo Nacional de la Música from 1976.
- The building: the Menorcan merchant Francisco Miguel Pons y Seguí bought three ruined houses in 1904, and architect Francisco Ramírez Ovando finished his palace in May 1907 (Diario de Cuba, 2025; Spanish Wikipedia).
- Pons's family sold it to the state in 1936 for the foreign ministry; Diario de Cuba says the state never paid its debt and the house was seized ("intervenida") in 1959, then used by the maritime police and border guards.
- Architect Daniel Taboada restored it from 1971 to 1981 for the museum (Diario de Cuba, 2025).
- Timba.com found most of the museum closed for remodelling in 2015.
- Ten rooms, five permanent: mechanical instruments, Afro-Cuban instruments, Cuban ensembles, organology, and a room of pieces that belonged to the musician Hubert de Blanck (EcuRed; Prensa Latina, 2024).
- UNESCO's LACULT cultural directory names Pedro Figueredo's original manuscript of the national anthem as the flagship of the collection, counts more than 500,000 documents and objects, and says UNESCO classed the holdings as heritage to be preserved (archive copy of 22 June 2025); Diario de Cuba gives about 500,000 objects.
- The Odilio Urfé library and archive, founded in 1949, merged with the museum in 1997 (IAML directory); Sul Ponticello and Timba.com give 1998. The library holds over 140,000 items and joined IAML in 1995 and IASA in 1999 (IAML directory).
- A 100-seat hall, the Sala Gisela Hernández, hosts conferences, launches and recordings (EcuRed).
- Timba.com named Jesús Gómez Cairo as director in 2015; Prensa Latina named Osmani Ibarra as technical deputy director in 2024.

FUNDING
Museo Nacional de la Música: mainly public: the Cuban Music Institute of the Ministry of Culture, with donated collections
- EcuRed lists the museum among the institutions of the Instituto Cubano de la Música, created on 11 March 1989 as the Ministry of Culture body for Cuban music.
- Building: the Oficina del Historiador restored the house over the last decade and reopened it in January 2025 (Diario de Cuba, 2025).
- Collections came through donations and voluntary deposits from musicians and collectors, starting with the Urfé family's manuscripts; the library also buys documents from institutions and individuals (IAML directory).
- In April 2024 the Cuban Embassy in Italy announced that the Italian guitarist Davide Lo Surdo would donate a guitar from his Cuban tour, which was sponsored by the travel firms TravelNet, Color Travels and Comunque Viaggi (Prensa Latina).
- Earned income: EcuRed lists paid services, including recording small ensembles and renting the Sala Gisela Hernández for performances and recordings.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs on Cuban state money through the Music Institute and the Havana heritage office, with collections built from donations. There is no private sponsor of note. Worth watching: published figures for the 2025 restoration and the library's purchases.
