STATE OF CONCEPT ATHENS: Athens, Greece
ID: state-of-concept-athens
Website: https://stateofconcept.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art institution in Koukaki, Athens, at Tousa Botsari 19 (State of Concept website). Wikipedia calls it the first institution of its kind to operate in Greece.
- Founded in 2013 by the art critic and curator iLiana Fokianaki, who is its founding director (State of Concept website).
- The programme examines power structures, geopolitics, national identity and cultural histories from a feminist and intersectional approach, using the framework of "ministries", "departments" and "bureaus" to critique institutions (State of Concept website).
- Programmes include The European Condition (2015 to 2017), State Affairs (from 2017), Department of Justice (2018 to 2020), The Bureau of Care (from 2020), Bona-Fide (from 2021), Coalition of the Care-full (from 2022) and Truth will not take Care of itself (from 2023, with Forensic Architecture/Forensis) (State of Concept website).
- Free one-to-one consultations for artists and students, which the website says have reached over 1,000 people since 2013.
- Exhibitions have included work by Tania Bruguera, Cao Fei, Kader Attia and Forensic Architecture (Wikipedia).
- The website's home page states: "State of Concept's physical space in Athens is permanently closed." The team page lists Fokianaki as founding director and Konstantina Melachrinou as production manager and press officer.

FUNDING
State of Concept Athens: non-profit funded by grants and cultural partners: European Cultural Foundation, Outset Contemporary Art Fund (Greece), Goethe Institut Athens
- European Cultural Foundation: a Culture of Solidarity Fund grant in 2020 for The Bureau of Care (Wikipedia); the foundation also supports the European Pavilion project (State of Concept website).
- Outset Contemporary Art Fund (Greece): named as a supporter of State of Concept on the page for the exhibition "a.O. - b.c. An Audiovisual Diary" (7 December 2019 to 1 February 2020; State of Concept website).
- Goethe Institut Athens supported the same exhibition, held in collaboration with the Fine Arts School of Athens; Dourakis Winery supported the opening (State of Concept website).
- The Coalition of the Care-full programme is part of the European Pavilion project launched by the European Cultural Foundation, developed with the Camargo Foundation, the Kultura Nova Foundation and Fondazione CRT. FfAi supported the Department of Justice chapter (State of Concept website).
- Wikipedia describes it as a not-for-profit, independent institution.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The art press item is an ArtReview review of one 2020 exhibition; its reservations concern the exhibition, not the organisation.
1. ArtReview: Max L. Feldman, "a.O. - b.c.: An Audiovisual Diary at Athens School of Fine Arts and State of Concept" (7 February 2020)
URL: https://artreview.com/ar-january-february-2020-review-state-of-concept/
- Reads the group show as a study of the Greek national imaginary after the Olympics and the 2010 debt crisis, and calls it "less a diary than a neurotic symptom".
- Reservations: the title is opaque, the roughly 60 works by about 80 contributors are unwieldy, and the historical scope is limited.
- Criticises the show, not the institution.

BOTTOM LINE
State of Concept is a small non-profit founded and directed by Fokianaki, funded by grants from the European Cultural Foundation and Outset Contemporary Art Fund (Greece) and by support from the Goethe Institut Athens. The money documented is grant and partner support for individual programmes and exhibitions.
