MONTRESSO ART FOUNDATION (JARDIN ROUGE): Marrakech, Morocco
ID: montresso-art-foundation-jardin-rouge-marrakech
Website: https://www.montresso.com

OVERVIEW
- Artist residency and art space among olive groves in a small village about twenty kilometres from Marrakech, run by the Montresso Art Foundation, which says it has been settled in Morocco since 2009 (Rivet).
- A private initiative of the French art collector and patron Jean-Louis Haguenauer, who bought the land in 2002; Jardin Rouge opened in 2009 (Arterritory, Una Meistere, 16 February 2020).
- On site: eight artists' studios; about thirty artists of various nationalities are hosted each year, and eighty had been hosted in total by the time of the February 2020 interview (Arterritory).
- The residency is open to painters, photographers and sculptors from anywhere in the world; stays last from one month to 90 days (ONArt, 20 November 2022).
- Selected artists get accommodation, meals, an individual workshop, equipment and transport; in agreement with the artist, a selection of works made during the residency is offered for purchase (TransArtists).
- Visits are by appointment (Arterritory, TransArtists).

FUNDING
Montresso Art Foundation (Jardin Rouge): private: a private initiative of the collector Jean-Louis Haguenauer
- The foundation funds the production of the artistic project approved by the selection committee and covers transport, individual accommodation, meals, equipment and an individual workshop (TransArtists); ONArt says the foundation finances the artist's production.
- Jardin Rouge is described as a private initiative of Haguenauer (Arterritory).

BOTTOM LINE
Montresso is a private initiative of the collector Jean-Louis Haguenauer, who bought the land in 2002 and opened Jardin Rouge in 2009, according to Arterritory. The foundation pays for residents' production, transport, accommodation and meals.
