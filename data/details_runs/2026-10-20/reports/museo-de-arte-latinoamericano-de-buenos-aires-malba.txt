MUSEO DE ARTE LATINOAMERICANO DE BUENOS AIRES (MALBA): Buenos Aires, Argentina
ID: museo-de-arte-latinoamericano-de-buenos-aires-malba
Website: https://malba.org.ar/

OVERVIEW
- Latin American art museum on Avenida Figueroa Alcorta in Palermo, Buenos Aires, run by the non-profit Fundación Malba (Wikipedia).
- Founded by businessman Eduardo Costantini, who donated 223 works from his personal collection in 2001, the base of the museum; it opened in 2001 (Malba site).
- Designed by the Córdoba studio AFT Arquitectos (Gastón Atelman, Martín Fourcade, Alfredo Tapia) after an international competition; the large volumes are clad in limestone (Malba site).
- Holds more than 700 works by about 200 Latin American artists; Malba Puertos, a second site in Escobar about 50 km from the main building, opened in 2024 (Malba site). In December 2025 it acquired the Daros Latinamerica Collection of 1,233 works (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Thursday to Monday 12:00 to 20:00, Wednesday 11:00 to 20:00, closed Tuesday (Malba site).
- In August 2026 Costantini's ex-wife Teresa Costantini went to court over works she says she handed over for the founding collection out of trust, not as a donation. Malba says the works belong to the Fundación, which received them in 1997, when the couple had been divorced since 1995; she says the claim is not against Malba as a cultural project (Caras, Diego Esteves, 21 August 2026).

FUNDING
Malba: private foundation museum, paid mainly by corporate presenting partners and sponsors, with founder gifts, donors, tickets and memberships
- Revenue in 2024 was 19,011,239,845 pesos, up from 16,972,340,428 in 2023, with a surplus of 6,523,151,007 pesos (Fundación Malba audited statements, audit by Deloitte & Co. dated 10 July 2025).
- Presentaciones y sponsors: 11,035,694,439 pesos, 58 percent of 2024 revenue, against 4,440,307,181 pesos in 2023.
- Founder donations: 2,191,394,826 pesos, 11.5 percent; other donations: 1,676,948,861 pesos, 8.8 percent.
- Exhibition tickets: 1,840,791,368 pesos, 9.7 percent; shop and merchandise: 1,073,709,093 pesos, 5.6 percent; memberships: 906,274,974 pesos, 4.8 percent.
- Perfil (Adriana Lorusso, 12 November 2020) reported that Costantini would keep paying for most of Malba's activities through a dedicated fund for the next 15 years and would keep the veto power the original statute gives him as founder.
- The museum states its own earnings cover roughly half its annual budget, and that the Fundación Malba covers the annual deficit (Malba transparency page).
- Its sponsors page groups its partners under corporate partners, sponsors, collaborators, media partner and technology support.
- Governance: the Consejo is made up of the Fundación's president, two vice presidents and four members (Malba transparency page). Perfil (Adriana Lorusso, 12 November 2020) reported that Teresa Bulgheroni would become president of the Consejo de Administración, with Costantini as honorary president; Teresa Aguirre Lanari de Bulgheroni signed the 2024 statements as president, and CIMAM says she has been vice president of the Fundación since July 2025.

BOTTOM LINE
Malba is a private foundation museum founded and still part-funded by Eduardo Costantini, with 58 percent of its 2024 revenue coming from presenting partners and sponsors and an audited surplus of 6.5 billion pesos. The accounts for 2005 to 2024 are public and audited by Deloitte, and the founder's gifts made up 11.5 percent of 2024 revenue.
