RAW MATERIAL COMPANY: Dakar, Senegal
ID: raw-material-company-dakar
Website: http://www.rawmaterialcompany.org/ (checked 2026-10-24; down: connection refused on both the home and about pages)

OVERVIEW
- Independent art centre in Dakar that describes itself as a centre for art, knowledge and society, with an exhibition gallery of about 100 square metres, studio apartments for residents, a resource centre with a library and archive, and a rooftop restaurant, La Compagnie (Africanah, an earlier address).
- Founded in 2008 by the curator Koyo Kouoh, first as a mobile site for artistic practice; it opened a fixed address at 4074 bis Sicap Amitié 2 on 21 April 2011 with an exhibition by the photographer Georges Osodi (au-senegal.com). The 2024 programme gives the address as Rue sans soleil, Zone B Villa 2A, Dakar.
- Kouoh was founding artistic director from 2008 to 2019, then moved to Zeitz MOCAA in Cape Town; she died on 10 May 2025 aged 57 (Wikipedia). A Koyo Kouoh Foundation, based in Basel and founded in 2026, says supporting it will help sustain the continued life of the Dakar institution (Designboom).
- Programmes: the Kër Issa RAW Residency, the RAW Académie, a residential study programme led in 2025 by Felwine Sarr and capped at ten fellows and tuition-free (e-flux), the Condition Report symposium series, Friday public programmes and publications.
- Taking a break: it paused for "ten moons" from 1 September 2015 to reconsider its programme (Contemporary And).
- Visiting: the 2024 programme lists Monday to Friday, 12 pm to 6 pm; the 2014 programme listed free admission, Tuesday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm.

FUNDING
Raw Material Company: foundation- and donor-funded: Mellon Foundation, Arts Collaboratory, Osiwa and others
- Its 2024 programme acknowledges Arts Collaboratory, the Open Society Initiative for West Africa (Osiwa), the Mellon Foundation, the Foundation for Art Initiative, Little Sun and Porticus, and the Sharjah Art Foundation (e-flux, 2024 programme). No amounts are given.
- The Mellon Foundation awarded Myriad USA $345,000 over 24 months on 21 December 2022 to support RAW's curatorial work, artistic education and archiving (Mellon Foundation).
- The 2025 RAW Académie lists the Mellon Foundation, Little Sun, the Community Arts Network and the Creative Industries Fund NL as supporters (e-flux).
- In 2014 RAW said it was supported by self-generated funds, with additional support from Arts Collaboratory, Office for Contemporary Art Norway, Foundation for Arts Initiatives, Johann Jacobs Museum, Goethe-Institut and the Royal Netherlands Embassy in Dakar (e-flux, 2014 programme).
- No reporting found of annual accounts or a board.

Red flags: Gulf state body, Sharjah Art Foundation
- The Sharjah Art Foundation is acknowledged for its "valuable support" in RAW's 2024 programme; the role and amount are not stated (e-flux).
- The foundation was founded in 2009 by Hoor Al Qasimi, daughter of the ruler of Sharjah; Wikipedia lists its parent organisation as the Department of Culture and Information of the Government of Sharjah.
- No reporting found of the size of the support. Kouoh took part in the foundation's March Meeting in 2019 (Sharjah Art Foundation site).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism of the institution found in art media. The press coverage concerns the 2014 attack on RAW.

WIDER CONTEXT
- The Art Newspaper (Anny Shaw, 5 June 2014) reported that "Precarious Imaging: Visibility and Media Surrounding African Queerness," co-organised by Kouoh, opened at RAW on 11 May 2014, that the centre was vandalised a day later, and that the show was cancelled on 31 May after pressure from extremist Islamic organisations.
- The Art Newspaper attributes to the news service Times24.info the claim that the Senegalese government ordered Dak'Art exhibitions on homosexuality suspended; it did not confirm this independently.
- Connection to the space: this was RAW's own exhibition, and CIMAM and e-flux published an appeal for Kouoh and RAW in June 2014.

BOTTOM LINE
RAW is funded by foundations and one Gulf state-linked foundation, and no source opened ties any of them to a flagged industry. The weak point is the Sharjah Art Foundation, a Gulf state-linked body whose support is acknowledged without an amount. Worth watching: RAW's future after Kouoh's death and whether a funder list with amounts is posted once its website is back.
