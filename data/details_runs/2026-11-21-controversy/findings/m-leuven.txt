ID: m-leuven

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2019 police seized six 16th-century wooden sculptures on show in the exhibition Borman and Sons at M Leuven, following a complaint from the Church of Saint Gaugericus in Boussu-lez-Mons (CODART, 27 November 2019, citing an M Leuven press release of 25 November 2019).
- The sculptures were on loan from Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen in Rotterdam and are fragments of the Mary Altarpiece, which is still in the Boussu church. The seizure was "soft", so the works stayed on view at M until the exhibition closed on 26 January 2020 (CODART).
- KIK-IRPA states that the police acted at the request of the Church Fabric Committee of Boussu and the organisation Gy Seray Boussu, and that elements of the altarpiece were stolen in 1914, 1971 and 1981.
- M Leuven's chair Denise Vandevoort said: "We are surprised by the complaint." Peter Carpreau, head of the Department of Old Masters, said: "We complied with all the international regulations for the loan" (CODART).
- In January 2020 Boijmans was to remove the six statuettes, valued at 128,000 euros, from its collection the following week and return them to the Boussu church; "Il aura fallu 10 ans de négociations pour voir le retour des statuettes" ("It took 10 years of negotiations to see the statuettes return", translated) (DH, Belga report, 22 January 2020). KIK-IRPA states the altarpiece is back in the Boussu church.
