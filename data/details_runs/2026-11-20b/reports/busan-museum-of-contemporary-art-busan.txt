BUSAN MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART (부산현대미술관): Busan, South Korea
ID: busan-museum-of-contemporary-art-busan
Website: https://www.busan.go.kr/moca_en (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Public contemporary art museum set up by Busan Metropolitan City at 1191 Nakdongnam-ro, Saha-gu, on Eulsukdo island in the lower Nakdong estuary, which the museum describes as known for its birds (Busan city; museum website).
- Its origins are a study for a dedicated Busan Biennale space and a basic plan for a second municipal museum; the building was finished in 2017 and the museum opened on 15 June 2018 (museum website).
- Building: three storeys and 15,312 square metres on a 29,900 square metre site, with 5,910 square metres of galleries, storage, a seminar room, a children's library and a reference room (museum website; Busan city).
- The facade carries a "Vertical Garden" by Patrick Blanc with 175 Korean native plant species, and the children's art library is by the architect Lee Ki-chul (Newsis, 27 May 2018).
- It focuses on contemporary art and new media art, and names "nature and ecosystem" among its priorities (stated by the museum).
- Directors: Kim Seongyoun from 2017 and Kang Seungwan from 2022 (museum website). The city advertised the post as an open civil-service position on 6 August 2026 and on 18 September 2026 announced no successful candidate (Busan Personnel Committee).
- It hosted the 2018 Busan Biennale (Wikipedia) and the main venue of Busan Biennale 2026, "Dissident Chorus", from 29 August to 1 November 2026; the museum closed from 20 July to 28 August to install it (Busan city; museum notice).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00; closed Mondays and 1 January. Admission is free except special exhibitions; the 2026 Biennale charged adults 16,000 won at the museum (museum website; Busan city).

FUNDING
Busan Museum of Contemporary Art: public: Busan Metropolitan City
- The museum is an office of Busan Metropolitan City, which set it up, announced its opening (Newsis, 2018) and appoints its director as a fourth-grade local curatorial official (Busan city; Busan Personnel Committee).
- A rooftop restaurant for about 60 people was planned with nearly 2 billion won of city money in 2024 (KNN, 16 September 2024). The museum listed its rooftop restaurant as open over Chuseok in September 2026 (museum notice).
- Busan Biennale 2026 at the museum was presented by the city with the Busan Biennale Organizing Committee (Busan city).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: A 2024 local news report covers objections to the rooftop restaurant, and a 2026 review finds the Biennale's display at the museum weaker than its other venues.
1. KNN (Busan-Gyeongnam broadcaster): Hwangbo Ram (황보람), "부산현대미술관 옥상에 음식점 추진 논란" (2024; own translation: Controversy over a plan for a restaurant on the Busan Museum of Contemporary Art rooftop)
URL: https://news.knn.co.kr/news/article/162684
- Eulsukdo, a migratory bird site, is a natural monument and a protected heritage zone; the restaurant would open at night on the building that holds the art storage, and the plan showed no emergency exit other than the existing stairs.
- Park Sang-hyeon (박상현) of the Busan Environmental Movement said night lighting would confuse migrating birds; Song Sang-jo (송상조), chair of the city council's administration and culture committee, said operating costs and hours need scrutiny; an unnamed art-sector source said major museums keep restaurants away from storage.
- The museum said it would install special glass to limit light pollution.
2. e-flux Criticism: Adeline Chia, "Busan Biennale 2026, 'Dissident Chorus'" (2026)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/criticism/6782475/busan-biennale-2026-dissident-chorus
- Calls it an uneven biennial, stronger in its off-site venues than in the main venue at the museum, where the curation "feels more staid and generic", with "a few needlessly large installations".
- Criticises the Biennale's display, not the museum.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the Busan city budget, with no private funder and no Red flag.
