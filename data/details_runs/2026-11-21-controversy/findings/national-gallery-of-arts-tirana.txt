ID: national-gallery-of-arts-tirana

WIDER CONTEXT
- The 24th edition of the Onufri International Exhibition of Visual Arts, an annual show the gallery organises under the Ministry of Culture, was not held in 2018. Artists appealed to Prime Minister Edi Rama, Culture Minister Mirela Kumbaro and gallery director Erzen Shkololli not to close it; Ardian Isufi, dean of the Faculty of Visual Arts, called its closure that year a national misfortune ("fatkeqësi kombëtare", translated) (Shqiptarja, Valeria Dedaj, 3 December 2018).
- At a press conference in the gallery on 21 March 2019 the painter Ermir Grezda protested with a placard reading "Galeria e Arteve, Shkollolli 2019, jo- artistëve të Tiranës!" ("The Gallery of Arts, Shkollolli 2019, no to Tirana's artists!", translated), saying that "Kjo galeri nuk funksionon fare për ne që jemi artistë të këtij vendi" ("This gallery does not work at all for us who are artists of this country", translated) (Shqiptarja, Valeria Dedaj, 21 March 2019).
