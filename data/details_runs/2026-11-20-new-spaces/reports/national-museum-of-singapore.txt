NATIONAL MUSEUM OF SINGAPORE: Singapore, Singapore
ID: national-museum-of-singapore
Website: https://www.nationalmuseum.nhb.gov.sg (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Singapore's national history museum at 93 Stamford Road.
- Established in 1849 by the Singapore Institution Committee as the Raffles Library and Museum, a section of a library at the Singapore Institution (Wikipedia).
- It moved to Stamford Road in 1887, into a building designed by Henry McCallum and built in a scaled-down version by J. F. McNair.
- Named the National Museum of Singapore in 1965, the Singapore History Museum from 1993 and the National Museum again in 2006.
- A redevelopment of three and a half years, with W Architects, ended with a reopening on 7 December 2006.
- Upgrading began in September 2023 with staggered gallery closures; the Singapore History Gallery closed in November 2025. The museum stays open with exhibitions and programmes.
- Visiting: daily 10am to 7pm; permanent galleries are free for Singapore citizens and permanent residents; tourists and foreign residents pay SGD 18, concessions SGD 13, children aged 6 and below free.

FUNDING
National Museum of Singapore: public: the National Heritage Board and its parent ministry
- The museum's site names it an institution of the National Heritage Board, supported by the Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth.
- The National Heritage Board is a statutory board under that ministry and operates the national museums (National Heritage Board).
- The museum is raising a capital campaign for the revamp from individuals, foundations and corporations (museum's upgrading notice).
- A Straits Times headline cited by Wikipedia put the 2006 makeover at $132 million.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is run by a statutory board under Singapore's culture ministry, and no funder is objected to. Worth watching: who gives to the capital campaign for the revamp.
