Red flags: 2026 budget committee cut of the museum's 2027 funding to 1,000 pesos
- In the 2027 budget debate, the Comisión Especial Mixta de Presupuestos approved amendments that cut the line for the Fundación Museo de la Memoria y los Derechos Humanos from 2,642 million pesos to 1,000 pesos. Five other lines for memory sites and programmes were cut to 1,000 pesos each in the same vote (El Desconcierto, Cristian Neira, 11 November 2026).
- The amendments were presented by the Republican deputies José Carlos Meza, Agustín Romero and Fernando Ugarte during review of the culture ministry's budget (El Desconcierto).
- Human rights organisations rejected the cuts. An open letter to the culture minister, Francisco Undurraga, questioned the cut and answered the argument El Desconcierto attributes to government deputies, "estos sitios no son de cultura, sino ideológicos y no nos representan para nada" (these sites are not cultural but ideological and do not represent us at all, translated), with "La barbarie no es un tema ideológico; es la negación de la civilización y la cultura" (barbarism is not an ideological matter; it is the negation of civilisation and culture, translated) (El Desconcierto).
- In a column on Radio Universidad de Chile, Patricio López wrote that no technical explanation had been offered, that similar proposals had been made in every November budget debate since 2022, and that there was still time for the executive to correct the cut (Radio Universidad de Chile, 16 November 2026).
