USINE KUGLER (FÉDÉRATION DES ARTISTES DE KUGLER): Geneva, Switzerland
ID: usine-kugler-geneva
Website: https://usinekugler.ch

OVERVIEW
- Former tap factory in the Jonction area of Geneva, now artists' studios and exhibition and performance spaces; the Fonderie Kugler is at Avenue de la Jonction 19, 1205 Geneva (guide-contemporain.ch).
- The Fédération des artistes de Kugler (FAK) is the federation of the artist associations based at the site: Kugler Est, Atelier Drozophile, Cheminée Nord, Espace Kugler, Kuglof, La Database59, L-Ouest, L-Sud and Verrière Sud (usinekugler.ch).
- The Fonderie Kugler sits alongside about 220 active professional artists' studios, keeps its industrial architecture and has a 400 m2 modular space for artistic research and emerging arts; it is managed collectively through an office and run by the FAK (guide-contemporain.ch).
- The City of Geneva lists the Fonderie among its creation venues, with residencies, new-music concerts, benefit evenings for Geneva non-profit associations, contemporary art exhibitions, festivals and children's workshops (geneve.ch).
- The Canton of Geneva's 2021 heritage days visit no. 17 passed through "l'Usine et l'Usine Kugler", among six former industrial sites converted into artists' studios and creation venues (ge.ch).
- A fire broke out at the site in November 2002; access was then limited to strict hours and the site was placed under a security company's supervision. Laurent Moutinot said the basement was definitively condemned and that contracts signed on 15 March 2005 let most former occupants return, with more limited space (Geneva Grand Council debate, 28 April 2005).
- Visiting: programme listings on the website include exhibitions at Espace Kugler Gallery and Espace Cheminée Nord and concerts. Tram 6 or 14 to Jonction, or tram 9 to Délices.

FUNDING
Usine Kugler: self-managed artist federation; public bodies handle the site and list the Fonderie
- A usinekugler.ch event listing describes the Fonderie Kugler as "un lieu alternatif et autogéré" (title translated: an alternative, self-managed venue). The federation argues for moderate rents in exchange for usage charters (usinekugler.ch).
- After the 2002 fire, security at the site cost 30,000 francs a month, according to Esther Alder in the Geneva Grand Council debate of 28 April 2005; Laurent Moutinot said the situation was provisional, pending development of the whole Pointe de la Jonction.
- Petition P 1417, on support for the associations and artists installed in the former Kugler factory, was referred by the Grand Council to the Conseil d'Etat by 37 votes to 17, with 9 abstentions (Geneva Grand Council, 28 April 2005).

BOTTOM LINE
The activity at Usine Kugler is run by an artist federation and its member associations, which describe themselves as self-managed. The documented public involvement is the handling of the site after the 2002 fire as debated in the Grand Council in 2005, the contracts signed in March 2005, and the City of Geneva listing the Fonderie among its creation venues.
