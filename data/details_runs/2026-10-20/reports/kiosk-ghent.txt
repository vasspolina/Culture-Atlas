KIOSK: Ghent, Belgium
ID: kiosk-ghent
Website: https://kiosk.art

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art exhibition space at Louis Pasteurlaan 2 on the Bijloke site in Ghent, on the campus of the KASK School of Arts (e-flux directory; Ghent city visitor site).
- An initiative of the non-profit Kunstensite vzw and University College Ghent's School of Arts; its exhibition programme began in 2006 in a small glass pavilion (artlead.net).
- Since 2010 it has been in the former anatomical theatre of the Clinical and Outpatient Institute, a neo-Gothic building from 1905 designed by Louis Cloquet (artlead.net; Ghent city visitor site).
- The programme centres the artist, shows emerging and established artists through long development processes, and favours new productions. Since January 2021 it has also used its website for art projects (stated by KIOSK).
- Team named by KIOSK: Simon Delobel (director), Sara Plantefève-Castryck (mediation, press and communication), Thomas Peeters and Sam Van Ingelgem (production managers). Kunstensite vzw is its governing board: Claudine Hellweg (chair), Lies Leliaert, Filip Rathé, Shila Anaraki, Anne-Françoise Lesuisse, Claire Stragier and Sevie Tsampalla.
- Open daily 12:00 to 18:00; entry is free (Nosbaum Reding exhibition page for the April to June 2026 show; Ghent city visitor site).

FUNDING
KIOSK: public support: a university-college art school, the Flemish government and the City of Ghent (stated by KIOSK)
- KIOSK's Mission page lists three logos under "KIOSK is supported by:", linked to the websites of the School of Arts Ghent (schoolofartsgent.be), the Flemish government (vlaanderen.be) and the City of Ghent (stad.gent).
- The School of Arts is also the co-founder of the initiative (artlead.net).

BOTTOM LINE
KIOSK is an initiative of the non-profit Kunstensite vzw and the School of Arts Ghent, and its Mission page lists the school, the Flemish government and the City of Ghent as its supporters. The money documented is public.
