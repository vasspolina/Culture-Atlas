THE STATE TRETYAKOV GALLERY: Moscow, Russia
ID: state-tretyakov-gallery-moscow
Website: https://tretyakovgallery.ru (the main site could not be opened from our connection on 2026-10-24; its partners site https://partners.tretyakov.ru loaded that day)

OVERVIEW
- State museum of Russian art in Moscow, built around the collection of the merchant Pavel Tretyakov (Wikipedia).
- Tretyakov began collecting in 1856, gave about 2,000 works to the city in 1892, and the gallery opened in 1893 (Wikipedia).
- Named the State Tretyakov Gallery in 1918; the fairy-tale façade, designed by Viktor Vasnetsov, was built in 1902 to 1904 (Wikipedia).
- Holds more than 130,000 exhibits, including Malevich's "Black Square"; its contemporary art branch, the New Tretyakov, stands on Krymsky Val, with satellite galleries in Kaliningrad and Vladivostok (Wikipedia).
- Directors: Zelfira Tregulova from 2015 to February 2023; Elena Pronicheva from 2023; Olga Galaktionova from January 2026 (ArtReview, Novaya Gazeta Europe).
- Visiting: opening days and prices not read.

FUNDING
The State Tretyakov Gallery: state museum, supported by state-controlled banks, oil and gas companies and oligarch donors
- The partners site says more than 80 companies and individuals support the gallery; it gives no amounts.
- Corporate donors named, with what they paid for: Transneft (design surveys for rebuilding the Krymsky Val building, donations to the museum's statutory work); VTB (air-conditioning repairs at the New Tretyakov, general sponsor of the Vrubel, Diaghilev and Roerich shows); Sberbank; Gazprombank (donation, design work for Krymsky Val, exhibitions); PSB Bank; NOVATEK; LUKOIL (Shchusev exhibition); Surgutneftegas (the Studio in Lavrushinsky Lane, the Grabar exhibition); Severstal; Metalloinvest; the Vladimir Potanin Charitable Foundation; Renova Charitable Foundation; Rosatom; FESCO; Ingosstrakh (partners.tretyakov.ru).
- Board of trustees, chaired by Andrey Belousov, Minister of Defence of Russia; members listed include Gazprombank chairman Andrey Akimov, Renova chairman Viktor Vekselberg, VTB president Andrey Kostin, Transneft chairman Nikolai Tokarev, PSB chairman Pyotr Fradkov, Severstal chairman Alexei Mordashov, Sberbank's Olga Golodets and the head of VGTRK Oleg Dobrodeev (partners.tretyakov.ru). The list is undated.
- The Ministry of Culture sets the gallery's leadership (see below). No reporting found of the gallery's federal budget.

Red flags: oil and gas sponsors
- Transneft, LUKOIL, Surgutneftegas, NOVATEK and Gazprombank are named on the current partners page, with the exhibitions and works they paid for (partners.tretyakov.ru).

Red flags: sanctioned banks and trustees
- VTB is a named sponsor and its president sits on the board. Russian state bodies held over 90 percent of VTB's capital as of February 2022; the US, EU, UK and Canada sanctioned it (Wikipedia).
- Trustee Viktor Vekselberg: OFAC sanctioned him and Renova in April 2018; the UK and EU did so in March 2022 (Wikipedia).

Red flags: state censorship and removal of the director
- 3 to 4 November 2022: the Ministry of Culture cancelled the ninth Moscow Biennale three days before its 7 November opening at the New Tretyakov, saying the exhibits did not match the status of the venue. The Biennale's own works included pieces from occupied Donetsk and Luhansk (USA Art News).
- February 2023: the Ministry did not renew Tregulova's contract after a visitor complaint of "signs of a destructive ideology" and a January ministry letter asking the gallery to align displays with "spiritual and moral values". Tregulova learned of it from the press (ArtReview, Finestre sull'Arte).
- Her successor Pronicheva is the daughter of Vladimir Pronichev, a former deputy head of the FSB (ArtReview, Finestre sull'Arte).
- May 2023: the gallery gave up Rublev's "Trinity" to the Church despite its restorers' warnings (The Art Newspaper).
- Unverified: an anonymous employee told the Moscow Times that the complaint resembled Soviet-style letters written by officials, reported by Finestre sull'Arte.

BOTTOM LINE
The Tretyakov is a state museum whose director is chosen by the Ministry of Culture and whose board is chaired by the Defence Minister. Its named donors are mostly state-controlled banks and oil and gas companies, and the record shows direct state censorship in 2022 and 2023. Worth watching: the January 2026 change of director and the unpublished amounts behind each sponsor.
