THE STATE HERMITAGE MUSEUM: Saint Petersburg, Russia
ID: state-hermitage-museum-spb
Website: https://hermitagemuseum.org

OVERVIEW
- Art and culture museum in six buildings in Saint Petersburg, including the Winter Palace, the Small, Old and New Hermitage, and the Hermitage Theatre; the General Staff Building and Menshikov Palace have been added (Wikipedia).
- Catherine the Great acquired a collection from the Berlin merchant Johann Ernst Gotzkowsky in 1764; the museum marks its founding each year on 7 December (Wikipedia).
- It began as the private apartments of the Winter Palace, a residence of the Russian emperors, and is federal state property (Wikipedia).
- Mikhail Piotrovsky has directed it since 1992; his father Boris Piotrovsky led it from 1964 to 1990 (Wikipedia). On 10 September 2025 Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin reappointed him for five years (TASS).
- A 1996 presidential decree placed the Hermitage under the patronage of the President of the Russian Federation (Decree No. 862 of 12 June 1996).
- Visiting: closed Mondays; free on the third Thursday of each month for all visitors and daily for students and children; foreign tourists pay more than Russian and Belarusian citizens. It drew 2,812,913 visitors in 2022 (Wikipedia).
- Canada added Piotrovsky to its sanctions list in August 2023, and the Council of the European Union listed him on 23 April 2026, saying he "actively supported and justified Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine" (The Art Newspaper, 31 August 2023 and 29 April 2026).
- In June 2022 Piotrovsky told Rossiiskaya Gazeta "We are all militarists and imperials" and "it is my country and I must be with it"; on 2 October 2022 Dmitry Ozerkov, head of contemporary art, announced his resignation, saying "I don't intend to have anything in common with today's Russia" (USA Art News, 3 October 2022).
- Unverified: Ukraine's Defence Intelligence (HUR) says the State Hermitage's Myrmekion expedition in occupied Crimea took 14 artifacts, and Ukrainian prosecutors notified the expedition's archaeologist Alexander Butyagin of suspicion in 2024 and changed the charge in 2026 to violations of the laws and customs of war (United24 Media, 7 October 2026).

FUNDING
The State Hermitage Museum: mainly public: the Russian federal budget, with Gazprom as general sponsor of its Peter the Great Gallery
- The 1996 decree ordered the Government to fund the Hermitage's current maintenance from federal budget appropriations and to list its maintenance as a separate line in the federal budget from 1997 (Decree No. 862).
- Gazprom was general sponsor of the permanent exhibition "Peter the Great Gallery", which opened in the Winter Palace on 14 January 2022; the chairman of Gazprom's management board, Alexey Miller, attended the opening, and Gazprom helped restore the ivory chandelier (Komsomolskaya Pravda, 17 January 2022). Two more halls opened on 21 June 2022 with Gazprom's support, and Piotrovsky thanked the company (AK&M, reprinting a Gazprom press release, 21 June 2022).

Red flags: Fossil fuels
- Gazprom was general sponsor of the Peter the Great Gallery opened in January 2022 and supported two further halls in June 2022 (Komsomolskaya Pravda, 17 January 2022; AK&M, 21 June 2022).

Red flags: Boycott by partners and advisory bodies
- After the 2022 invasion Hermitage Amsterdam returned a loan show and cut ties with the St Petersburg museum, the Hermitage Foundation UK cut ties, the International Advisory Board announced an "indefinite suspension" on 15 March 2022, and the Bizot group suspended Piotrovsky's membership (The Art Newspaper, 4 April 2022).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The three pieces below, from 2022 to 2025, concern the director's stance on the war.
1. The Art Newspaper: Sophia Kishkovsky, "Isolated by Russia boycotts, Hermitage director calls for 'cultural bridges between the nations'" (2022)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/04/04/russia-boycott-hermitage-museum
- Piotrovsky had not condemned the invasion; he said "Putin's been my person since the 1990s" in a 2021 interview with the paper, and Putin awarded him Russia's Order of Friendship in 2016.
2. USA Art News: Romario, "State Hermitage Museum curator quits over Russia's war in Ukraine" (2022)
URL: https://usaartnews.com/news/state-hermitage-museum-curator-quits-over-russias-war-in-ukraine
- Reports Ozerkov's resignation and Piotrovsky's Rossiiskaya Gazeta interview backing the war.
3. Novaya Gazeta Europe: Anna Matveeva, "The emperor" (2025)
URL: https://novayagazeta.eu/en/articles/2025/10/20/the-emperor-en
- Traces Piotrovsky from reformer to supporter of the war, says he publicly backed Putin in the 2012, 2018 and 2024 elections, and reports staff departures and a 2021 Proekt report that businesses tied to the Hermitage belonged to friends of his family.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Hermitage is mainly the Russian state, which owns the museum and placed it under presidential patronage in 1996, with Gazprom as general sponsor of its Peter the Great Gallery in 2022. Canada (2023) and the EU (2026) sanctioned the director for his public support of the war, and European partners cut ties in 2022.
