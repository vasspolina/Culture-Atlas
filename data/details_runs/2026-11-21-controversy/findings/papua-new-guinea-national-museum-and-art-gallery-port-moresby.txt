WIDER CONTEXT
- On 6 February 2018 PNGi Central reported, from leaked documents, that the museum had had no permanent director for over a year. Alois Kuaso was appointed acting director in May 2017, and his predecessor had also served on an acting basis (PNGi Central).
- PNGi Central reported that a National Gazette notice appointed Kuaso on an acting basis for nine months, while the Regulatory Statutory Authorities (Appointment to Certain Offices) Act 2004 limits acting status to three months (PNGi Central).
- Unverified: a letter from the then minister, obtained by PNGi Central, alleges that the museum's Board of Trustees stood in the way of a permanent appointment, from PNGi Central.
- The Auditor General's August 2017 report, quoted by PNGi Central, states that when the auditor raised the selective tender for the K1,356,500 contract with management, "they did not respond to this query" (PNGi Central).
- A 2009 Public Accounts Committee report, quoted by PNGi Central, states that the museum "did not produce Financial Statements for six years and thereby avoided Audit for the same period" (PNGi Central).
