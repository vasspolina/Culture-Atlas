DAZIBAO: Montreal, Canada
ID: dazibao-montreal
Website: https://dazibao.art

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre and non-profit organisation at 5455 de Gaspé Avenue, suite 109 (ground floor), Montréal, in the Mile End (stated by the space; e-flux directory).
- Founded in 1980 as one of the first Quebec and Canadian centres devoted exclusively to photography; it has embraced the full range of image-based practices since the early 1990s, moved to Pôle de Gaspé in 2014 and developed a digital component in 2020 (stated by the space).
- Mandate: "an exhibition center dedicated to the dissemination and outreach of contemporary image-based practices", presenting local and international work and supporting artists over long periods (stated by the space).
- Programme: exhibitions, residencies, sessions, publications (Les Portables, Monographies, Les Essais, Les Études), a bookshop, a video library and outreach projects.
- Visiting: gallery open Tuesday to Saturday, 12 pm to 5 pm; offices Tuesday to Friday, 10 am to 5 pm; entry is free. A wheelchair-accessible ramp is on the building's northwest side and the gallery is on the ground floor.

FUNDING
Dazibao: public support: Quebec, Canadian and Montreal arts councils and governments
- Dazibao states that it receives financial support from the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec, the Canada Council for the Arts, the Conseil des arts de Montréal, the ministère de la Culture et des Communications and the Ville de Montréal (Dazibao call for proposals page).

BOTTOM LINE
Dazibao is a not-for-profit centre that receives financial support from the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec, the Canada Council for the Arts, the Conseil des arts de Montréal, the Quebec culture ministry and the City of Montreal. Entry is free.
