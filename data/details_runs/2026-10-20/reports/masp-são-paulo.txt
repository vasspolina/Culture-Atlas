MASP: São Paulo, Brazil
ID: masp-são-paulo
Website: https://masp.org.br/ (checked 2026-10-24; redirects to https://masp.com.br/, live)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art museum at Avenida Paulista 1578, São Paulo. It holds more than 8,000 works (Wikipedia).
- Founded by the media owner Assis Chateaubriand of Diários Associados with the Italian critic Pietro Maria Bardi; opened 2 October 1947 on the upper floors of the Diários Associados headquarters (Wikipedia).
- Present building designed by Lina Bo Bardi, a concrete-and-glass block suspended on four pillars over a 74-metre free span; Queen Elizabeth II inaugurated it on 7 November (Wikipedia).
- A 14-storey annex, the Pietro Maria Bardi Building, opened on 28 March 2025; it adds 7,821 square metres and raises exhibition space by 66% (Poder360; The Art Newspaper).
- Collection strongest in European painting, with Brazilian, Latin American and North American art and smaller African, Asian and ancient holdings (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday 10 am to 6 pm, Thursday until 8 pm, closed Monday. Adults R$75, students and over-60s R$37; free for all visitors on Tuesdays and Friday evenings 6 to 10 pm, and for people with disabilities and one companion (museum website).

FUNDING
MASP: corporate-sponsored and private donors, with BNDES as Financial Supporter
- Partner tiers on the museum's page: Strategic Partner Itaú; Master Sponsors Nubank, AkzoNobel, B3, BASF, Bradesco, Citi, Google, Levy e Salomão, Havaianas, Instituto Votorantim, McKinsey & Company, Klabin, QI Tech, Vitru Educação, Uber, Vivo; Financial Supporter BNDES; Sponsors Tintas Coral, Goodyear, Bloomberg, Levisky, Ultra, Unilever, Unipar.
- Vitru Educação joined as master sponsor in June 2025: R$2 million direct, up to R$7 million a year with incentive-law funds, for the public-programmes and mediation programme (Times Brasil).
- The annex cost about R$250 million (Poder360) or $43 million over six years (The Art Newspaper), funded entirely by private donors, named by The Art Newspaper as Cleusa Garfinkel, Denise Aguiar Alvarez of Fundação Bradesco, André and Lilian Esteves, and Ronaldo Cezar Coelho. Individual amounts are not stated. Poder360 counts donations from more than 20 families.
- No reporting found of MASP's total revenue in the pages read.

Red flags: fossil fuels
- Ultra is listed under Sponsors on the museum's partner page (masp.org.br/en/support/parcerias, read on 24 October 2026). The page shows no year, only an annual-schedule link that reads 2025. It does not describe Ultra or say how much it pays.
- Ultra Group (Ultrapar Participações S.A.) controls the fuel distributor Ipiranga, which has more than 6,500 service stations (Wikipedia). The partner page does not say which company "Ultra" is.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism found concerns curatorial decisions and the annex's design, not funders.
1. ArtReview: unsigned, "Censorship row engulfs Museum of Art São Paulo" (2022)
URL: https://artreview.com/censorship-row-engulfs-museum-of-art-sao-paulo/
- MASP removed photographs of the Landless Workers Movement (MST) by João Zinclar, André Vilaron and Edgar Kanaykõ from its 2022 survey Histórias Brasileiras; Hélio Menezes of the Instituto Moreira Salles called it censorship, and co-curators Sandra Benites and Clarissa Diniz said they felt "wronged and disrespected".
- Notes that the same week a book launch by Guilherme Boulos, leader of the Homeless Workers Movement, was cancelled.
- The museum's leadership says the photos arrived after the deadline and the museum argued the Boulos event was political; the co-curators and artists say they were never told of a deadline.
2. The Art Newspaper: Ela Bittencourt, "Museu de Arte de São Paulo traces its own history and evolution across exhibitions in its new tower" (2025)
URL: http://www.theartnewspaper.com/2025/04/02/museo-arte-sao-paulo-annex-opening-exhibitions
- A review of the annex's opening shows. Calls the annex "a striking visual counterpoint" to the old building; notes tunnel work would continue until late 2025.
- Criticises neither funders nor management.

WIDER CONTEXT
- More than 260 British artists asked five UK theatres (the National Theatre, Royal Shakespeare Company, Young Vic, Royal Court and Almeida) in May 2026 to end ties with Bloomberg Philanthropies, calling it "complicit in grave war crimes" against Palestinians through its work with Israeli settlement leaders (The New Arab, 29 May 2026).
- Poder360 reports social-media users nicknamed the annex's black design "a CPU da avenida Paulista".
- Connection: "Bloomberg" is a named MASP sponsor on the partner page; the campaign names the theatres, and no reporting found of a campaign aimed at MASP.

BOTTOM LINE
MASP's money is mostly private and corporate, with Itaú as strategic partner and BNDES as Financial Supporter. The weak point is Ultra, a sponsor that shares its name with a group owning a fuel distributor; Bloomberg is also a sponsor while a campaign targets Bloomberg Philanthropies at UK theatres. Worth watching: whether MASP publishes sponsor amounts and how it answers the 2022 censorship row.
