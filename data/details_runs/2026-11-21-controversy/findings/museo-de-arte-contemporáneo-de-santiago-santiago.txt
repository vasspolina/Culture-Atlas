WIDER CONTEXT
- January 2021: Chile's Consejo de Monumentos Nacionales rejected a US$50 million project by the architect Gonzalo Mardones to bury the MAC's Parque Forestal building and the avenue José María Caro underground, promoted by a company headed by Roberto Durán. The CMN did not confirm the decision officially; at least two councillors confirmed it to El Mostrador, whose sources said it was agreed "por unanimidad de los consejeros presentes señalar que el proyecto no es de interés público en opinión del CMN" (El Mostrador, 14 January 2021, republished by CPI on 15 January 2021).
- The reasons reported were that the University of Chile did not support the project and that its logo and name had been used to create a false sense of support (El Mostrador).
- Parque Forestal residents and the mayor of Santiago, Felipe Alessandri, also opposed it. University pro-rector Alejandro Jofré said: "Es un proyecto externo a la universidad que en ningún caso ha sido encargado, ni diseñado por nosotros", adding that it "no considera la visión de los vecinos y tampoco de nuestra comunidad" (El Mostrador).
