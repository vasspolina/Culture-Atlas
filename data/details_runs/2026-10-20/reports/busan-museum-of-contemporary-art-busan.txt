BUSAN MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Busan, South Korea
ID: busan-museum-of-contemporary-art-busan
Website: https://www.busan.go.kr/moca_en

OVERVIEW
- Public contemporary art museum at 1191 Nakdongnam-ro, Saha-gu, on Eulsukdo island, which KNN describes as a bird migration site.
- Construction began in November 2013 and finished in February 2017; the museum opened officially on 15 June 2018 and was registered as a Type 1 museum in May 2018 (museum site). Its origins go back to a 2005 study for a dedicated Busan Biennale space and a 2009 basic plan for a second municipal museum.
- Kim Seongyoun became first director on 11 May 2017 and Kang Seungwan second director on 27 June 2022 (museum site).
- The exterior carries a "Vertical Garden" by Patrick Blanc with 175 plant species native to Korea, and the children's art library was designed by the architect Lee Ki-chul (Newsis, 27 May 2018).
- The museum focuses on contemporary art including new media art, with attention to nature and ecosystems (stated by the museum). It was a venue of the 2018 Busan Biennale (Wikipedia) and hosts the first part of the 2026 edition (e-flux).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00; closed Mondays and 1 January. Admission is free except for special exhibitions (museum site); the Busan Biennale 2026 exhibition at the museum charges 16,000 won for adults (Busan Metropolitan City).

FUNDING
Busan Museum of Contemporary Art: public: Busan city government
- The museum describes itself as a public art museum and sits on the Busan city government's website; the Busan city government announced its 2018 opening (Newsis).
- A proposed rooftop restaurant would use nearly 2 billion won in city funds (KNN, 16 September 2024).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One local news report, from 2024, covers objections to a proposed rooftop restaurant; it is not art-press criticism of the programme.
1. KNN (Busan-Gyeongnam broadcaster): Hwangbo Ram, "부산현대미술관 옥상에 음식점 추진 논란" (16 September 2024; my translation: Controversy over a plan for a restaurant on the Busan Museum of Contemporary Art rooftop)
URL: https://news.knn.co.kr/news/article/162684
- Reports that the museum wants a rooftop restaurant seating about 60, open at night, on the building that houses the art storage; an art-sector source said major museums typically keep restaurants away from storage areas, and the report notes fire concerns.
- Park Sang-hyeon of the Busan Environmental Movement Association warned that birds are highly sensitive to light and that night operations could disrupt their migration routes on Eulsukdo, which the report calls a protected heritage zone; Song Sang-jo, chair of the city council's administration and culture committee, said operating costs and hours need scrutiny.
- The museum said it would install special glass to limit light pollution and make supplementary design changes for safety.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a public art museum of the Busan city government. The one documented dispute is the 2024 rooftop restaurant plan, which an environmental group and a city councillor questioned over bird habitat and operating costs, and an art-sector source over fire safety near the art store.
