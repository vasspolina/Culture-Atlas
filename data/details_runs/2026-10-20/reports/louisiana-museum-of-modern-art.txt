LOUISIANA MUSEUM OF MODERN ART: Humlebæk, Denmark
ID: louisiana-museum-of-modern-art
Website: https://louisiana.dk (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum in Humlebæk, about 30 km north of Copenhagen on the Øresund coast; it draws over 700,000 visitors a year (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1958 by businessman Knud W. Jensen (Wikipedia).
- Named after a 19th-century landowner whose three wives were all called Louise (Wikipedia).
- Original building by architects Jørgen Bo and Vilhelm Wohlert, expanded several times; a sculpture park overlooks the sea (Wikipedia).
- On site: 6 to 10 temporary exhibitions a year, a permanent Yayoi Kusama installation, a concert hall opened in 1976, a children's wing, a café and a shop. The Louisiana Literature festival has run since 2010 and the Louisiana Channel web TV since 2012 (Wikipedia, stated by the museum).
- Poul Erik Tøjner has been director since 2000 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Friday 11:00 to 22:00, weekends 11:00 to 18:00, closed Monday; under-18s enter free. The site lists a practical info and accessibility page but gives no details on its front page.

FUNDING
Louisiana Museum of Modern Art: mixed: own revenue, state grants and private foundations, with Augustinus Fonden a repeat funder
- Wikipedia states the museum is independent, receives government grants, and draws most of its funding from its own revenue and private foundations.
- Augustinus Fonden's grants list names "Louisiana Museum / Louisiana-Fonden" for DKK 2,000,000 to realise the exhibition "Lucian Freud: Drawing into Painting" in 2026. The list is titled Uddelinger 202(x) and the last year digit could not be read in the file.
- Augustinus Fonden also paid for the museum's expansions in 1979 to 1984 and 1989 to 1990 (Wikipedia).
- The museum's 2024 annual report is posted only as a scanned image PDF; its text could not be read, so no figures from it are repeated here.
- No reporting found of the full list of 2024 donors and sponsors in a readable source.

Red flags: Tobacco, Augustinus Fonden
- Augustinus Fonden was founded in 1942 by tobacco manufacturer Ludvig Augustinus (Wikipedia, lex.dk). Its investment company, Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, is described as the foundation's investment vehicle (Wikipedia).
- That company reported holdings above 25% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group as of 1 May 2025 (Scandinavian Tobacco Group's shareholder page).
- Augustinus Fonden's 2026 Freud grant is current funding, and it also paid for the museum's two earlier expansions.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's income is a mix of its own revenue, state grants and private foundations. The weak point is Augustinus Fonden, a repeat funder of expansions and a 2026 exhibition, which is tied to the tobacco group through its investment company. Watch the amounts: the annual report is scanned and the donor list was not readable.
