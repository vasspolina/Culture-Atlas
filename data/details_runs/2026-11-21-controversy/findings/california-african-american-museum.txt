ID: california-african-american-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- January 2021: one former and one current employee sued the museum, its executive director George O. Davis, the State of California and the California Science Center in Los Angeles Superior Court, alleging gender harassment and discrimination, retaliation and false promise (City News Service via KFI AM 640, 22 January 2021).
- Unverified: the suit claims Davis made "highly improper and obviously unwelcome comments" about the two women's appearance, sent one of them an email firing her while she was on vacation after she complained, and told the other she "would pay the price" after she used the museum's whistleblower hotline, from City News Service.
- Todd Hawkins, president of the museum's board, said: "The California African American Museum takes allegations of harassment and discrimination seriously, but declines to comment on pending litigation" (City News Service).
- 14 June 2021: Judge Michael P. Linfield, who had issued a tentative ruling dismissing the state and the California Science Center from the suit, gave the plaintiffs 15 days to amend their complaint (City News Service via KFI AM 640, 14 June 2021).
