# Editor's notes: flag and grade questions from the controversy pass

The pass adds text to the Details reports. It does not touch flags, tiers or grades. The checkers
listed the findings that could change one under the atlas's flag rules; they are kept here for the
editor to rule on, batch by batch.

## c002 and c003

Protest or boycott (staff disputes since November 2021):

- castello-sforzesco: the 2022 Filcams-Cgil state of agitation and strike mandate by outsourced reception staff. The dispute is mainly with the City of Milan.

- museo-picasso-malaga: strikes from June to October 2023 over the collective agreement.

Protest or boycott (aimed at the space or its funder):

- museo-archeologico-nazionale-di-napoli: Mi Riconosci's 2025 public criticism of the unpaid photo competition.
- opernhaus-zurich: the 2025 open letter by Helvetia for Ukraine and the protest at the premiere.
- sadler-s-wells: the 2024 UKLFI complaint; Marin's 2024 Gaza statement, which named the sponsor Barclays; Keegan-Dolan's 2025 withdrawal over Barclays, aimed at a funder.

State censorship or punishment, by the space itself:

No flag, by the checker's reading: anne-frank-house (a copyright lawsuit), istanbul-archaeology-museums (requests from a state with no role in the museum), national-asian-culture-center-acc-gwangju (the act was the Biennale Foundation's).

- teatro-regio-di-parma: no flag yet. The former general director's trial has no verdict.
