WEXNER CENTER FOR THE ARTS: Columbus, USA
ID: wexner-center-for-the-arts-columbus
Website: https://wexarts.org/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 1871 North High Street on The Ohio State University campus; it does not collect art (Wikipedia).
- Opened on 17 November 1989, replacing the University Gallery of Fine Arts (Wikipedia). Leslie H. Wexner made the founding gift (stated by the Wex).
- Named for Wexner's father, Harry L. Wexner.
- Designed by Peter Eisenman with Richard Trott and landscape architect Laurie Olin, from a 1982 to 1983 Ohio State competition; it stands on the site of the Armory, torn down in 1959 after a fire (stated by the Wex). The building cost $43 million (Wikipedia).
- On site: galleries, a film and video theatre, performance space, a bookstore and a café; programmes cover exhibitions, film, performing arts, education and artist residencies.
- Galleries are open Tuesday to Sunday (Sunday 10 am to 4 pm, Tuesday and Wednesday 9:30 am to 5 pm, Thursday and Friday 9:30 am to 7 pm, Saturday 10 am to 5 pm) and closed Monday. Gallery admission became free in 2022 (Wikipedia). CART captioning and ASL interpretation on request, two weeks ahead.

FUNDING
Wexner Center for the Arts: university and donor-funded: Ohio State, the Wexner Center Foundation and gifts, plus Ohio state money
- The Wexner Center Foundation, chaired by Leslie H. Wexner, reported revenue of $1,322,513 and expenses of $2,262,665 for the year to June 2025, with net assets of $14.0 million (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).
- Interim executive director Cat Predmore said the Center receives $3.3 million a year in grants and gifts (WOSU, June 2026).
- In July 2023 the State of Ohio gave over $977,000 through the Ohio Arts Economic Relief Grant Program for operations and staff development (stated by the Wex).
- Hyperallergic cites the Columbus Dispatch: fiscal year 2024 ended about $2.5 million short of expected income, with a $1.1 million deficit.
- The Wex posts annual reports with donor lists; the 2024-25 report was not readable here, so its donor tiers are not stated.

Red flags: Epstein-linked
- Wexner link: Hyperallergic (May 2026) reports that Les Wexner gave Jeffrey Epstein power of attorney over his finances for decades. Wexner says he cut ties after Epstein's first charge in 2007 and denies knowing of Epstein's crimes.
- Money to the Wex: an Ohio State review by EY found $336,000 in donations and pledges from Epstein and his foundation, all of it to the Wexner Center: $260,000 received 1990 to 1997, a $75,000 pledge not confirmed as received, and a $1,000 membership. Ohio State gave the sum to the Ohio Attorney General's Human Trafficking Initiative (AP, April 2020).
- A 2007 gift of $2.5 million from the COUQ Foundation, which Epstein directed, was found to have come from the Wexner Children's Trust and the Leslie H. Wexner Charitable Fund (AP).
- Leadership: Wexner chairs the Wexner Center Foundation board (ProPublica).
Red flags: protest over the name
- Wex Workers United, the Center's union, called in May 2026 for the Wexner name to be removed (Hyperallergic). Ohio State declined to comment; about 500 renaming requests have gone through its review since 2022 with no building renamed.
- In June 2026 one artist and one grant withdrew, citing the name. Predmore declined to name either or give the amount (WOSU).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Recent art-press coverage is about the Wexner name, staff conditions and leadership. Older criticism concerns the building's design.
1. Hyperallergic: Rhea Nayyar, "Workers Push to Rename Wexner Center for the Arts Over Epstein Ties" (2026)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/workers-push-to-rename-wexner-center-for-the-arts-over-epstein-ties/
- Union staff say the name "does a profound disservice" to artists and community members, and that some artists refuse to work with the Center.
- Sets out Wexner's Epstein ties and Ohio State's refusal to comment.
2. Hyperallergic: Rhea Nayyar, "Wexner Center Director Departs Amid Staff Complaints" (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1053106/wexner-center-director-departs-amid-staff-complaints/
- Anonymous staff describe low morale and poor leadership under executive director Gaetane Verna; a university document cites 28% staff turnover in under two years.
- Reports a no-confidence letter, a $1.1 million deficit, and Verna stepping away on 29 October 2025. No statement from the Center appears.
3. Christian Science Monitor: Laura Van Tuyl, "A Building Waiting To Be A Building" (1989)
URL: https://csmonitor.com/1989/1211/lwex.html
- Describes the building as a jumble with no obvious entrance; calls the $43 million project a risk for Ohio State and Eisenman.
- Criticises the building, not the funding. Not an art-press outlet.
4. Artforum: workers at the Wexner Center call for the name to be dropped (2026)
URL: https://www.artforum.com/news/workers-at-wexner-center-for-the-arts-call-for-name-drop-1234750724/
- Reports that unionized staff demand removal of Les Wexner's name, and Ohio State's reply that it has a procedure for name requests.
- Paywalled; only a search excerpt was read.

BOTTOM LINE
The Center was founded on Leslie Wexner's gift and he still chairs its foundation, so the money is tied to a man whose Epstein links are documented. $336,000 of Epstein's own giving went to the Center from 1990 to 1997. Worth watching: further grant and artist withdrawals, any Ohio State renaming decision, and the appointment of a permanent executive director (Predmore is interim).
