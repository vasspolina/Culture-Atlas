WIDER CONTEXT
- In October 2020 the frescoes, mosaics and images of Christ at Kariye were covered with white curtains after work on the building, ahead of its opening for Friday prayer on 30 October (Bianet, 28 October 2020).
- Mahir Polat, secretary general of the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality, wrote on Twitter on 27 October 2020 that covering the Kariye frescoes and mosaics would destroy the building's character and artistic value (Bianet, 28 October 2020).
- Culture ministry officials told Habertürk that the photographs showed "otomatik perdeleme sistemi" ("an automatic curtain system", translated), not plaster, and that the frescoes were curtained during prayer times and uncovered afterwards (Habertürk, 28 October 2020).
