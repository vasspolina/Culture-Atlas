Red flags: 2024 dismissal of the deputy director
- On 24 November 2024 Minister of Culture and Youth Jorge Rodríguez dismissed the museum's deputy director, Elvira Alfaro Arauz; museum staff asked the minister to reconsider, and the decision stood (La Nación, Roger Bolaños Vargas and Sofía Sánchez Ramírez, 11 December 2025).

Red flags: 2025 dismissal of the director
- Rodríguez dismissed Esteban Calvo Campos as director of the museum on Friday 5 December 2025. The ministry's press office said: "El motivo es porque las métricas de desempeño no estaban siendo cumplidas", and did not name the metrics when asked (La Nación, Sofía Sánchez Ramírez and Fernando Chaves Espinach, 9 December 2025).
- Unverified: Calvo told La Nación that the minister's office had pressed him to make an urgent direct-award hiring of a choir costing about ¢5 million, paid from museum funds, for the ministry's Christmas events, one at the museum and two at other venues; a second source gave La Nación the same account. The hiring did not happen (La Nación, 11 December 2025).
- The ministry did not answer La Nación's questions about the hiring request before publication (La Nación, 11 December 2025).

Red flags: 2026 refusal to release records on a loan of works to the presidential house
- La Nación asked on 16 and 30 September 2026 for the board minutes, the agreement and the documents on the loan of works from the collection to Casa Presidencial, and the museum declined to provide them (La Nación, Fernando Chaves Espinach, 16 October 2026).
- Through the Ministry of Culture and Youth's press office, the museum said "su formalización nunca se concretó" and that the procedure is suspended; La Nación noted that board minutes and documents on institutional agreements are public information (La Nación, 16 October 2026).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 30 September 2020 Planning Minister Pilar Garrido told a legislative commission that the Museo de Arte y Diseño Contemporáneo "pasaría a fusionarse con otro, el Museo de Arte Costarricense (MAC)" (Semanario Universidad, Vinicio Chacón Soto, 7 October 2020).
- The visual artist Sara Mata said her collective of contemporary artists wrote to the ministers of culture and planning about the merger and had received no reply (Semanario Universidad, Natalia Díaz Zeledón, 24 October 2020).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Meer: Juan Carlos Flores Zuñiga, "Coleccionismo disfuncional y la narrativa estatal" (2022)
URL: https://www.meer.com/es/69626-coleccionismo-disfuncional-y-la-narrativa-estatal
- Blames the quality of the collection on "la ausencia de criterios rigurosos por parte de los curadores y las autoridades museísticas".
- Argues the museum cannot justify accepting the Baduel donation, and that acquired works end up mostly in storage for lack of space.
- Says the acquisition regulation and founding law set no conceptual or aesthetic criteria and delegate decisions to curators and the board: "Faltan actores críticos e independientes para generar confianza y transparencia."
