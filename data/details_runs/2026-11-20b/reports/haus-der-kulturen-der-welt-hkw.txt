HAUS DER KULTUREN DER WELT (HKW): Berlin, Germany
ID: haus-der-kulturen-der-welt-hkw
Website: https://www.hkw.de (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Venue for international contemporary arts and debate at John-Foster-Dulles-Allee 10, in the former Kongresshalle on the Spree in the Grosser Tiergarten and the government quarter; it has used the building since it was founded in 1989 (German Wikipedia; HKW imprint).
- The Haus der Kulturen der Welt GmbH was registered on 3 November 1988; the HKW is now a division of Kulturveranstaltungen des Bundes in Berlin GmbH (KBB), which also runs the Berlinale and the Berliner Festspiele (German Wikipedia; HKW imprint).
- Hugh Stubbins designed the Kongresshalle as the American contribution to the Interbau exhibition of 1957; Berliners call it the "Schwangere Auster" (pregnant oyster) (German Wikipedia).
- Before the Second World War the site held Magnus Hirschfeld's Institut für Sexualwissenschaft, remembered in the Magnus-Hirschfeld-Bar and the Lili-Elbe-Garten (German Wikipedia).
- The south roof collapsed on 21 May 1980; after rebuilding from 1984 the hall reopened on 9 May 1987, and a partial restoration ended in 2019 (German Wikipedia).
- Bernd M. Scherer led the house from 1 January 2006; Bonaventure Soh Bejeng Ndikung has been Intendant since 1 January 2023 and sits on the KBB management (German Wikipedia; HKW imprint).
- Three programme areas: literature, society and science; visual arts and film; music and performing arts. Since 2013 it has run multi-year projects such as the Anthropozän-Projekt (German Wikipedia).
- Autumn 2026: the 36th Bienal de São Paulo, Berlin Edition, from 12 September to 6 December 2026, and the "heimaten" festival (HKW).

FUNDING
HKW: public: the German federal government
- The HKW "wird institutionell gefördert durch" the Federal Government Commissioner for Culture and the Media (BKM) (HKW imprint).
- German Wikipedia states that it receives regular funding from both the Federal Foreign Office and the BKM, and lists it as an intermediary organisation of the Foreign Office for cultural policy abroad.
- The chair of the KBB supervisory board is the federal culture minister, Wolfram Weimer (HKW imprint; German Wikipedia).
- English Wikipedia names the HKW among the federal "lighthouses of culture" funded by the federal government.
- Adam Broomberg writes that the HKW "is funded almost entirely by the federal government" (Hyperallergic, 5 November 2025).

Red flags: 2025 Chefket concert cancelled after the federal culture minister's call
- Weimer sharply criticised a planned appearance by the rapper Chefket on 7 October 2025 at HKW, part of Jan Böhmermann's exhibition "Die Möglichkeit der Unvernunft" (translated: "The Possibility of Unreason"); he objected to a T-shirt Chefket wears in photos showing a map of Palestine without Israel, a motif the federal government considers antisemitic (Jüdische Allgemeine, dpa, 29 September 2025).
- Weimer called on the HKW director to intervene; the director pointed to Böhmermann's artistic freedom and clear stance on antisemitism (Jüdische Allgemeine).
- On 29 September 2025 Böhmermann, his group Royale and HKW as venue cancelled the evening: "Diesen Einspruch nehmen wir ernst" (translated: "We take this objection seriously") (Jüdische Allgemeine).
- In a talk with Weimer at HKW, Böhmermann said the event should never have been planned for 7 October (German Wikipedia, citing t-online, 9 October 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One opinion piece in the art press argues that the HKW's federal funding brings state control and attacks its handling of Gaza under Ndikung.
1. Hyperallergic: Adam Broomberg, "The Irony of a Fascism Exhibition in Germany" (5 November 2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/1055123/the-irony-of-a-fascism-exhibition-in-germany-berlin-hkw/
- Argues the HKW, "once a custodian of critical culture", has become "a model of capitulation to state control", and that its federal funding "has a price: preemptive obedience".
- Says Ndikung's appointment came with the condition that he publicly renounce the BDS movement, which he did in 2022 (Broomberg's claim).
- Criticises the show "Global Fascisms": "Not one work in the exhibition acknowledges the world burning just beyond the door"; calls Roee Rosen's "Gaza War Tattoos" an aestheticisation of atrocity.
- Unverified: Broomberg writes that in 2024 Ndikung and the other KBB directors reported a Berlinale employee to the police over an internal email.
- Does not mention Weimer or Böhmermann. Read in an archived copy.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2022 appointment: the federal antisemitism commissioner Felix Klein and the Israeli ambassador Ron Prosor accused the incoming director Ndikung of closeness to the BDS movement in the Funke Mediengruppe papers; Ndikung said "Ich habe die BDS-Bewegung nicht unterstützt und unterstütze sie auch heute nicht" (Jüdische Allgemeine, dpa, 28 October 2022).
- Culture minister Claudia Roth said she had no objection to the procedure or its result; the selection fell in the term of her predecessor Monika Grütters (Jüdische Allgemeine).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is federal, and the federal culture minister chairs the supervisory board. The documented weak point is political pressure on the programme from that same funder, seen in the Chefket cancellation of 2025.
