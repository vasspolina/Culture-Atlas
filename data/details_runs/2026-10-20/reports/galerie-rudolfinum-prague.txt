GALERIE RUDOLFINUM: Prague, Czechia
ID: galerie-rudolfinum-prague
Website: https://www.galerierudolfinum.cz

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at Alšovo nábřeží 12, Prague 1, in a neo-Renaissance building on the bank of the Vltava that it shares with the Czech Philharmonic (stated by the gallery).
- Founded in 1994 (gallery website); Wikipedia gives the opening as 1 January 1994 and says it has no permanent collection and about 1,500 square metres of exhibition space.
- The Rudolfinum building was designed by Josef Zítek with Josef Schulz, opened on 8 February 1885 and is named for Crown Prince Rudolf of Austria (Wikipedia).
- Director in 2025: Julia Bailey (gallery annual report 2025).
- Programme: exhibitions, performances, lectures, publications and the Artpark education project; 2025 shows included Antony Gormley / Pavla Melková, Poetics of Encryption, Iván Argote's Radical Tenderness and Kateřina Vincourová's Skin Care (annual report 2025).
- The 2025 report counts 109,280 visitors, the second highest figure in the gallery's history.
- Visiting: closed Monday; Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, Thursday until 20:00. The annual report says admission to exhibitions stayed free throughout 2025 with a Pay What You Wish system added; the admission page now lists Pay What You Wish for adults 26 and over with a minimum of 200 CZK, free entry for ages 0 to 15 and on the first Sunday of every month, and 120 CZK for ages 65 and over and students aged 15 to 26 (gallery website).

FUNDING
Galerie Rudolfinum: public institution supported by the Czech Ministry of Culture, with private patrons, a technology partner and earned income
- The gallery calls itself a public institution supported by the Ministry of Culture. It is part of the Czech Philharmonic and operates as an independent unit with its own budget (annual report 2025).
- For 2025 the Ministry of Culture set a financial contribution of CZK 21,435,805 for its operation, including an extraordinary CZK 1,980,000 for the Kateřina Vincourová exhibition (annual report 2025).
- Samsung became the gallery's Technology Partner in 2025 with the exhibition Poetics of Encryption, providing exhibition support throughout 2025 and upgrading the gallery's front-of-house digital sites (annual report 2025); the partners page lists Samsung as Technology Partner.
- Abakus, the foundation of the Avast founders Pavel Baudiš and Eduard Kučera (abakus.cz), is the gallery's long-term sponsor (annual report 2025); the partners page lists Abakus as Gallery Partner.
- Revenue from exhibitions and projects in 2025 came from admission and membership fees, sale of goods, advertising and donations; the report gives the shares by exhibition. The director's foreword thanks the Members and Patrons of the Galerie Rudolfinum Club, sponsors, partners and the Ministry of Culture (annual report 2025).
- An advisory committee first met in 2025; it includes a curator, Helena Musilová, representing the Ministry of Culture (annual report 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
Galerie Rudolfinum calls itself a public institution supported by the Ministry of Culture, which allocated a contribution of CZK 21,435,805 for its 2025 operation. Private support comes from club members, patrons and donors, the Abakus foundation as long-term sponsor and Samsung as Technology Partner.
