SESC SÃO PAULO: São Paulo, Brazil
ID: sesc-são-paulo-são-paulo
Website: https://www.sescsp.org.br (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The São Paulo state regional administration of the Serviço Social do Comércio (Sesc), a Brazilian private non-profit institution kept by businesses in trade, services and tourism (sescsp.org.br; English Wikipedia).
- Decree-Law 9,853 of 13 September 1946, signed by President Eurico G. Dutra, gave the Confederação Nacional do Comércio the task of creating Sesc (Câmara dos Deputados text); it belongs to the "Sistema S" group with Senac, Senai and Sesi (Portuguese Wikipedia).
- In 2024 the São Paulo network had 47 units and recorded more than 28 million visits (2024 management report).
- Abram Abe Szajman chairs the regional council and Luiz Deoclecio Massaro Galina is regional director (2024 management report); Galina's predecessor Danilo Santos de Miranda led Sesc São Paulo for 40 years and died on 29 October 2023 (Câmara Municipal de São Paulo).
- Sesc Pompeia, Rua Clélia 93, is a 1938 oil-drum factory converted by Lina Bo Bardi between 1977 and 1986; it opened on 22 January 1982 with two concrete sports towers, a theatre, library and pool (Wikipedia).
- Sesc 24 de Maio showed "HIP-HOP 80'sp", more than 3,000 pieces, until 29 March 2026 (e-flux).
- Visiting: hours and prices vary by unit; the free Credencial Plena for workers in commerce, services and tourism firms is required for the pools (sescsp.org.br).

FUNDING
Sesc São Paulo: mainly a compulsory levy on companies in commerce, services and tourism
- Total revenue in 2024 was R$3.78 billion: R$2.996 billion in contributions, R$512 million in property income (receita patrimonial), R$240 million from services and R$35 million in other income (2024 management report).
- Gross compulsory revenue was R$2.993 billion; the Receita Federal, the federal tax service, kept R$59.9 million for collecting it and R$88.0 million went to Fecomércio, the state commerce federation, leaving R$2.845 billion net (2024 management report).
- The 1946 decree-law obliges commerce employers to pay Sesc a monthly contribution of 2% of their employees' pay (Câmara dos Deputados text).
- Under Decree 6,632/2008 one third of net compulsory revenue, R$948 million in 2024, must go to education, half of it as free provision (2024 management report).
- Capital spending in 2024 was R$777.6 million, R$407.9 million of it on works (2024 management report).

Red flags: 2017 court ban on a play at Sesc Jundiaí
- On 15 September 2017 a court order stopped "O Evangelho Segundo Jesus, Rainha do Céu", a monologue with the trans actress Renata Carvalho, at Sesc Jundiaí, under a daily fine of R$1,000. Judge Luiz Antonio de Campos Júnior of the 1st Civil Court of Jundiaí wrote that staging Jesus as a travesti offended countless people. Sesc appealed (Vermelho, 16 September 2017, a partisan left-wing outlet). Jornal Opção confirmed the injunction and the cancellation the same day.
- The play was also banned in Salvador in 2017 and in Rio de Janeiro and Pernambuco in 2018 (English Wikipedia).

Red flags: 2017 petition against Judith Butler's seminar at Sesc Pompeia
- Sesc Pompeia hosted Butler at the seminar "Os fins da democracia" on 7 to 9 November 2017. An anonymous online petition asking Sesc to cancel her visit reached 88,821 signatures, and the Sesc Pompeia Facebook page received 386 negative reviews in about 24 hours from 26 October (Vermelho, 1 November 2017).
- About 3,000 people posted positive reviews in a counter-campaign; Sesc representatives declined to comment (Vermelho).
- By 7 November the CitizenGO petition had more than 366,000 signatures, and about 50 protesters with a witch doll bearing Butler's face faced about 100 counter-protesters outside Sesc Pompeia during the colloquium (Exame, from AFP, 7 November 2017).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The one item on record is news coverage that defends Sesc's programme rather than criticising the institution.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In late 2018 Paulo Guedes called for cuts of up to 50% in Sistema S funding; the Confederação Nacional da Indústria rejected the idea (Congresso em Foco, 1 April 2020).
- Provisional Measure 932/2020, signed by Jair Bolsonaro and Guedes on 30 March 2020, halved contribution rates for the Sistema S entities, Sesc among them, until 30 June 2020 (Congresso em Foco).
- Bill PL 6505/19 by deputy Eli Borges would make contributions optional, cap them at 1% of payroll and cap directors' pay (Jornal Contábil, 3 May 2021, from Agência Câmara).
- The 2024 management report names legislative proposals and lawsuits that question the compulsory revenue.
- The levy is Sesc São Paulo's main income, so these proposals concern its budget directly.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is clean of private sponsors and flagged industries: a levy set by federal law and Sesc's own income. The weak point is political exposure, both of the levy and of a programme that drew a court ban and a mass petition. Watch the levy's legal status.
