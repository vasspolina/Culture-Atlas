MAXXI: Rome, Italy
ID: maxxi
Website: https://www.maxxi.art (checked 2026-10-24; live)

OVERVIEW
- National museum of contemporary art and architecture in Rome's Flaminio neighbourhood, at Via Guido Reni 4A; it has two parts, MAXXI Art and MAXXI Architecture (Wikipedia, museum website).
- Managed by a foundation created by the Ministry of Culture. Zaha Hadid won the design competition in 1998; the building stands on the site of a former military barracks. It opened in 2010 and won that year's Stirling Prize (Wikipedia). MAXXI L'Aquila, in Palazzo Ardinghelli, opened in October 2020 (Wikipedia).
- Maria Emanuela Bruni was named president on 28 March 2025 (accounts 2025).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday 11 am to 7 pm, closed Monday. Full ticket €15 at the desk, €14 online; under-18s free (museum website).

FUNDING
MAXXI: mainly public: the Ministry of Culture, with Enel, Regione Lazio and corporate sponsors
- Production value in 2025 was €21,823,228; net result €3,267 (accounts 2025).
- Operating grants in 2025 totalled €15,141,122: Ministry of Culture €14,003,860; Enel €600,000; Regione Lazio €350,000; ALES €187,262 (accounts 2025).
- Enel and Regione Lazio are members of the foundation; each designated a board member in 2026 (accounts 2025).
- Sponsorship was €1,096,735 in 2025 (€1,166,639 in 2024), including KME at €300,000 for the Gallery 1 architecture shows (2024 to 2028) and Bulgari at €60,000 for the MAXXI Bulgari Prize (agreement worth €750,000 for 2023 to 2028) (accounts 2025).
- Ticket income at the Rome site was €1,250,401 in 2025 and €1,807,255 in 2024 (accounts 2025).
- Eni: the accounts list "Progetto Eni" at €48,750 in 2025, for cataloguing Eni's archives through the museum's architecture archive centre; the centre has worked with Eni's archive since 2012. From November 2025 Maurizio Nannucci's "More than meets the eye" has been shown on the Gazometro G3 in Rome in a MAXXI and Eni collaboration (accounts 2025).
- No reporting found of a Leonardo entry in the 2025 accounts. They list a contribution from MED-OR Italian Foundation, with no separate amount, for the exhibition Mediterranea (17 May to 31 August 2025), made with Telespazio and e-GEOS (accounts 2025).

Red flags: fossil fuels
- Eni, an oil and gas company, paid MAXXI €48,750 in 2025 for archive cataloguing under an agreement running since 2012, and collaborated on the November 2025 Gazometro G3 event (accounts 2025). Six artists named Eni in a boycott letter on 27 October 2025 (Hyperallergic).

Red flags: boycott over Eni, Leonardo and Gaza
- 27 October 2025: artists Tania Bruguera, Phil Collins, Siniša Mitrović, Alessandra Saviotti, Gemma Medina and Dora Garcia published a letter withdrawing from "1+1: The Relational Years", two days before it opened. They demand that MAXXI denounce Israel's genocide in Gaza and end ties with companies complicit in crimes against humanity (Hyperallergic, Nero Editions). Curator Nicolas Bourriaud said only Collins, Garcia and Bruguera were in his show.
- The letter names a "history of partnerships" with Leonardo S.p.A., Med-Or, e-GEOS and Telespazio, without specifying them (Hyperallergic).
- Mediterranea opened on 17 May 2025, produced by the Fondazione MAXXI and the Med-Or Italian Foundation, which Artribune calls an offshoot of Leonardo; Artribune recalls protests over it, undated, and a May 2024 occupation of the Rome building by pro-Palestinian activists (22 September 2025).
- September 2025: artist Sara Leghissa staged "MAXXI Boycott" outside MAXXI L'Aquila during the Performative05 festival, 11 to 14 September, to which MAXXI had invited her (Artribune).
- MAXXI did not answer Hyperallergic's inquiries; an editor's note says the article was later updated with a MAXXI comment, not reproduced in the text read.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism comes from activists and artists and is reported, not argued by critics.
1. Hyperallergic: Isa Farfan, "Artists Call to Boycott Maxxi Museum Over Alleged Israel Ties" (2025)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/artists-call-to-boycott-maxxi-museum-over-alleged-israel-ties
- Reports the boycott letter and demands, the Eni collaboration and a 2023 Reuters report on Israeli gas exploration licences awarded to Eni and others. Names Bourriaud; says it is unclear whether the artists' works were removed.
2. Artribune: Valentina Muzi, "\"Le istituzioni culturali sono complici del genocidio\". La performance contro il MAXXI a L'Aquila" (2025)
URL: https://www.artribune.com/?p=1182113
- Reports Leghissa's poster text accusing MAXXI and cultural institutions of complicity in genocide; recalls the Mediterranea protests and the 2024 occupation. Does not mention Eni.
3. Nero Editions: unsigned, boycott call (27 October 2025)
URL: https://www.neroeditions.com/artists-pull-out/
- A boycott call signed by Italian groups, with the six artists' statement; not independent reporting. Argues that "neutrality is complicity" and names Leonardo, Eni, Med-Or, e-GEOS and Telespazio.

BOTTOM LINE
MAXXI's money is mostly Italian public money, with a small oil-and-gas link: Eni at €48,750 and a joint event. The weak points are that link and the 2025 boycott; the accounts name MED-OR Italian Foundation but no Leonardo entry. Worth watching: Eni and Med-Or collaborations.
