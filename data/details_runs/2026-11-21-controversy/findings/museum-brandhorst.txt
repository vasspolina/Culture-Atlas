WIDER CONTEXT
- In its 2015 annual report (24 March 2015) the Bavarian Supreme Audit Office (Oberster Rechnungshof) found that natural lighting of the galleries, demanded by the Staatsgemäldesammlungen, cost 5.3 million euros extra and that the daylight control proved prone to faults and costly to run; it asked that such complex systems be avoided in future (Bayerischer Oberster Rechnungshof, Jahresbericht 2015, TNr. 28).
- About 800 defects were found in the shell construction at acceptance; when the museum opened only 3 of 41 thermally activated building circuits worked, and although most were fixed later, some circuits were shut down for good, the hoped-for energy savings did not come and the building uses more energy than planned (Bayerischer Oberster Rechnungshof, 2015).
- The audit office said more careful planning and stricter site supervision would have avoided many defects in the museum, which cost 46 million euros (Bayerischer Oberster Rechnungshof, 2015).
