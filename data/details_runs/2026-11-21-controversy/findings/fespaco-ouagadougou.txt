ID: fespaco-ouagadougou

WIDER CONTEXT
- In February 2015 the FESPACO organisers announced that Abderrahmane Sissako's film "Timbuktu", about the occupation of the northern Malian city by jihadist armed groups in 2012 and 2013, would be withdrawn from the official competition of the 24th edition; the festival's statement cited the security context in West Africa and read "le comité d'organisation a décidé du retrait de Timbuktu de la compétition" (translated: the organising committee decided to withdraw Timbuktu from the competition) (Afrik.com, Assanatou Baldé, 27 February 2015).
- On 27 February 2015 Burkina Faso's culture minister Jean-Claude Dioma announced that the "gouvernement du Burkina Faso a décidé de la diffusion de Timbuktu" (translated: the government of Burkina Faso has decided that Timbuktu will be shown), with reinforced security measures, and the film stayed in competition (Afrik.com, 27 February 2015).
