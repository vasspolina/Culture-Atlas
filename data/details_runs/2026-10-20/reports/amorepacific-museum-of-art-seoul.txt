AMOREPACIFIC MUSEUM OF ART: Seoul, South Korea
ID: amorepacific-museum-of-art-seoul
Website: https://apma.amorepacific.com/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Corporate art museum inside the Amorepacific global headquarters at Hangang-daero 100, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (museum site; W Magazine, Ann Binlot, 27 August 2025).
- The headquarters opened in Yongsan in 2018 (Wikipedia); W Magazine places the museum in David Chipperfield's "luminous cube". Directed by Seungchang Jeon (press release, December 2025).
- The museum shows ancient Korean art and contemporary art; it also supports researchers and young writers (Artessere).
- The building holds exhibition space in the basement and an atrium over the first three floors with the lobby, shop, the "APMA Cabinet" exhibition space and apLAP, a library (Artessere).
- 2026 programme: "APMA, Chapter Five", 1 April to 2 August, about 50 works by over 40 artists from the museum's contemporary collection; "Jonas Wood", 1 September 2026 to 28 February 2027; and "Sol LeWitt: Open Structure" in the second half of 2026, planned by the museum in collaboration with the Sol LeWitt Foundation and the Tokyo Metropolitan Museum of Contemporary Art (exhibition page). The first two dates are from the press release of 23 December 2025.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 6 pm; closed Mondays, 1 January and the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. W Magazine (27 August 2025) described admission as free.

FUNDING
Amorepacific Museum of Art: corporate-owned: hosted in the headquarters of Amorepacific Corp
- The museum's website is run by Amorepacific Corp. and the museum sits in the company's headquarters (museum site; W Magazine).
- Amorepacific was founded in 1945 by Sungwhan Suh as Pacific Chemical; he handed it to his second son Kyungbae Suh in 1997. The parent is Amorepacific Holdings (renamed from Amorepacific Group in 2025). 2025 revenue was 4.25 trillion won and net income 247.3 billion won (Wikipedia).

WIDER CONTEXT
- The Korea Fair Trade Commission fined Amorepacific 500 million won in August 2014 for forcing door-to-door sales agencies to send high-performing employees to new or struggling agencies. The commission said it was the highest fine it could impose because it could not obtain documentation for door-to-door sales (Korea JoongAng Daily, 18 August 2014).
- On 6 April 2020 the commission fined Amorepacific Group and its affiliate Cosvision 48 million won each, 96 million won in total, over fixed deposits of 75 billion won given to Cosvision without a fee, which helped it obtain five low-interest loans from the Korea Development Bank worth 60 billion won (Korea Times, 6 April 2020; the article body also prints 9.6 billion won, inconsistent with its headline).
- Both cases concern the museum's owner, Amorepacific.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a company museum: Amorepacific, a cosmetics group, hosts it in its headquarters. The company's weak points are a 2014 fine of 500 million won and a 2020 fine of 48 million won from the Korea Fair Trade Commission.
