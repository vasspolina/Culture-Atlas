BWA WROCŁAW: Wrocław, Poland
ID: bwa-wrocław-wrocław
Website: https://www.bwa.wroc.pl

OVERVIEW
- Public contemporary art institution (BWA Wrocław, Galerie Sztuki Współczesnej) with galleries across Wrocław: BWA Wrocław Główny, Dizajn, Studio and SIC!, plus the art magazine "Biuro" (City of Wrocław, 2020).
- Its gallery Awangarda was built in the early 1970s on the site of the Hatzfeldt Palace at ul. Wita Stwosza, an 18th-century palace whose final design is credited to Carl Gotthard Langhans and which was badly damaged in 1945 (City of Wrocław, 8 September 2020).
- In October 2018 the gallery left that building, which experts found in poor condition with some areas at risk of collapse, for about 1,000 to 1,200 square metres in the Wrocław Główny railway station (City of Wrocław, October 2018).
- Marek Puchała directed BWA from 2002 to 2020 (City of Wrocław, 8 September 2020); Maciej Bujko took over on 17 September 2020 after a competition decided by a nine-member committee chaired by Jerzy Pietraszek, director of the city's Culture Department (City of Wrocław, 8 September 2020).
- A new headquarters is planned at ul. Kolejowa 63, in a former hall from the Breslau era used to assemble and repair the Kraftpost postal buses, with about 4,500 square metres, three exhibition halls, a video art room and an auditorium; opening is planned for the turn of 2028 and 2029 (stated by BWA).
- 32,677 visitors came in 2024 (BWA annual report).

FUNDING
BWA Wrocław: public: basic subsidy including the City of Wrocław's
- 2024 budget: 7,496,064.69 zloty ("w zł: 7 496 064,69"). The annual report's table of sources at 31 December 2024: 82 per cent "dotacja podstawowa instytucji (w tym miejska)" (basic subsidy, including the municipal one); 8 per cent targeted grants; 5 per cent national grant programmes; 1 per cent European programmes; 3 per cent "przychody z działalności (z biletów, wynajmów, inne)" (revenue from tickets, rentals, other); 1 per cent other; and 0 per cent "środki spoza sektora finansów publicznych (niezależni sponsorzy, donatorzy)" (funds from outside the public finance sector: independent sponsors, donors). Translations are mine.
- The 2024 report names the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage, through its Culture Promotion Fund, as funder of the renovation of the Galeria Dizajn; the National Centre for Culture with the State Fund for the Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons (PFRON) on the "Projektowanie uniwersalne" project; and the Goethe-Institut, the Foundation for Polish-German Cooperation and the Zentrum für Kunst und Urbanistik on the "Laboratorium badań nad szczęściem" project.
- The new headquarters is a partnership between the City of Wrocław and PFR Nieruchomości, which are converting and refurbishing the hall together (BWA).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind BWA Wrocław is public: 82 per cent of its 2024 budget of 7,496,064.69 zloty was the basic subsidy including the municipal one, and the report shows 0 per cent from independent sponsors and donors. Named project funders are the Ministry of Culture, PFRON and German cultural bodies. The new headquarters is a partnership between the City of Wrocław and PFR Nieruchomości.
