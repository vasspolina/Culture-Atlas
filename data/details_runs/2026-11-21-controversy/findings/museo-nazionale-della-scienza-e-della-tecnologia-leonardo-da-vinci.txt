Red flags: 2026 audit office finding on dropping the national staff contract
- The Corte dei conti, in its determination no. 59/2026 on the foundation's 2024 accounts (decided 24 March 2026, sent to Parliament on 16 April 2026), reported that the foundation had told it that it no longer applied the Federculture national collective contract to its employees, only an internal regulation; the foundation had 174 staff at 31 December 2024 (Corte dei conti report on 2024).
- The court wrote that the Ministry of Economy and Finance's representative on the board of auditors contested this decision, and that the decision "non appare effettivamente in linea con la previsione di legge" ("does not in fact appear in line with the law", translated); it asked the foundation to restore the contractual terms for its staff, as it had in its previous report (Corte dei conti report on 2024).

Red flags: 2026 audit office finding on public procurement rules
- The court held that the foundation, as a body governed by public law, is subject to the public contracts code, a status the foundation contests, and recommended that it apply the code in full, as it had for the contracts it reported to the court in 2024 and 2025, among them the renovation works on its building in Milan (Corte dei conti report on 2024).
