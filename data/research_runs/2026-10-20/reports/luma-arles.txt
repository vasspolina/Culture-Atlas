LUMA ARLES – Arles, France
Website: https://www.luma.org/arles (indexed in search results; direct fetch of the root not permitted in this run, 2026-10-20; sub-pages retrieved)

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre on the Parc des Ateliers, a former SNCF railway workshop site (about 7 hectares per LUMA; 11 hectares per Le Temps) in Arles.
- Created by Maja Hoffmann's LUMA Foundation (Zurich, founded 2004); LUMA Arles endowment fund set up in 2013. Frank Gehry's 56-metre LUMA Tower opened June 2021; buildings rehabilitated by Selldorf Architects; park by Bas Smets.
- Hoffmann signed an agreement with government officials in 2008 to develop the project (Artnet). Gehry's 2011 two-tower plan was rejected by France's Commission of Historical Monuments; a single tower was approved in 2013 (Artnet).
- Access: all programming is free for Arles residents; park open to all (stated by the space, FAQ).

FUNDING
LUMA Arles: privately funded by Maja Hoffmann's LUMA Foundation.
- The LUMA Foundation (Swiss law) created and finances the LUMA Arles endowment fund (stated by the space). Partners ACCM and CCIPA are named, with no amounts given.
- Cost: about €150M reported by Artnet (2021) and Le Temps; Hoffmann declined to confirm the figure. Le Temps cites Le Ravi estimating this equals about ten years of the city's investment budget.
- Ultimate source: Hoffmann's fortune as a member of the Roche founding family (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of the land price or terms under which the SNCF site passed to LUMA.
- No reporting found of published accounts, budget or donor list.

Red flags: Private influence over a small city
- Besides LUMA, Hoffmann has bought and renovated the Hôtel du Cloître, is expanding the Hôtel Arlatan, owns 10% of the Nord Pinus and holds stakes in restaurants; the city financed fibre-optic cable for Arlatan guests (Le Temps, Swiss daily).
- The local magazine L'Arlésienne questioned urban-planning constraints and the democratic legitimacy of private actors shaping the city (reported by Le Temps). Mayor Hervé Schiavetti praised her role in job creation.

Not related: LUMA Westbau (Zurich) — a separate LUMA Foundation venue, same funder.

BOTTOM LINE
LUMA Arles is funded by one private patron and publishes no accounts, so its finances cannot be checked. Worth watching: the undisclosed cost and land terms, and the extent of Hoffmann business interests in Arles.
