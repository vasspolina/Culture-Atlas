ID: museion-bolzano

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2006 police impounded a toilet, the work of two artists on display at the Bolzano Museum of Modern Art, that flushed to the sound of the Italian national anthem; prosecutors argued the anthem is a national emblem that should never be open to ridicule, while the museum's defence lawyers argued it is not a national symbol (BBC News, 6 November 2006).
- In 2008 Franz Pahl, president of the South Tyrol regional council, wanted Martin Kippenberger's crucified frog removed from the museum and went on hunger strike over it; Pope Benedict XVI wrote to him that the crucified frog hurt the religious feelings of many people, and Culture Minister Sandro Bondi said the work offended reason and common sense (Die Welt, Eckhard Fuhr, 29 August 2008).
