ID: museum-wurth

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 28 May 2008 the Amtsgericht Heilbronn issued a penalty order (Strafbefehl) of 700 daily rates against Reinhold Würth for tax evasion; the amount of the fine was not given, and the Stuttgart prosecutors said Würth now had a criminal record (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- Der Spiegel reported that costs incurred for the whole group were booked mainly at one company instead of at the group companies that caused them; the prosecutors said the repaid tax damage was taken into account. Penalty orders of 700 and 600 daily rates went to two other people from the group, and investigations against three representatives of Würth Group companies were dropped against payments totalling 1.5 million euros (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- Würth said he had agreed "nach sehr reiflicher Überlegung" ("after very careful consideration", translated) to take responsibility for disputed and complex tax questions of the Würth Group (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- Connection: Reinhold Würth built the Würth Group (Der Spiegel), the company that owns and pays for Museum Würth.
