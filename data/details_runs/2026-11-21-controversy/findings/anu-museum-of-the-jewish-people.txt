Red flags: 2025 call by Yad L'Achim to stay away over displays on mixed marriage
- Yad L'Achim objected to displays that present mixed marriage as normative alongside historical Jewish figures, saying "Presenting assimilation as legitimate is crossing a red line" (Haredim 10, 21 December 2025).
- Yad L'Achim said "the public is called to stay away from the place" until the museum corrects the displays, and asked parents and educators not to bring people to the museum (Haredim 10, 21 December 2025).
- Yad L'Achim said museum officials first called the display innocent and later said that changing the content on such a scale is "a complex process that takes time" (Haredim 10, 21 December 2025).
