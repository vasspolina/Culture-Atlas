NATIONAL GALLERY SINGAPORE: Singapore, Singapore
ID: national-gallery-singapore-singapore
Website: https://www.nationalgallery.sg/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- National museum of Singapore and Southeast Asian modern art at 1 St Andrew's Road in the Civic District, facing the Padang.
- Government-announced in 2006 and opened to the public on 24 November 2015; its collection traces to artworks donated by Dato Loke Wan Tho from 1960 (Wikipedia).
- Occupies two national monuments, the former Supreme Court and former City Hall, joined by bridges and a new basement; designed by Studio Milou Singapore with CPG Consultants, at a development cost of about S$532 million (Wikipedia).
- A registered charity and Institution of a Public Character with a Keppel Centre for Art Education for children (annual report).
- Open daily 10am to 7pm; the gallery lists no prices on its homepage. Singapore citizens and permanent residents receive free general admission (Wikipedia). It recorded 2,284,000 visitors in 2025/26.

FUNDING
National Gallery Singapore: mainly public: Singapore government grant, topped up by founding corporate partners, patrons and donors
- In the year to 31 March 2026 the gallery received S$57.9 million in grants from government (S$55.3 million the year before), S$5.9 million in grants from others, S$8.4 million in cash donations and S$2.3 million in donations in kind, against total expenditure of S$93.5 million (financial report, audited by PricewaterhouseCoopers).
- Stated by the gallery: "government funding forms the majority of our operating base".
- Founding partners: DBS, UOB, Keppel, Far East Organization, Ngee Ann Kongsi and Singtel (Chairman's message); Tote Board is a development partner. Keppel gave S$12 million to the education centre (Wikipedia).
- Corporate Patrons in 2025/26: Applied Materials, Bloomberg, Citadel, Frasers Property Corporate Services, Mingxin Foundation, Van Cleef (Richemont) and Warburg Pincus. Marina Bay Sands appears in the donors and patrons list. Major donors include Dalio Philanthropies and Stephen Riady.
- No reporting found of individual gift amounts for Marina Bay Sands, Bloomberg or Citadel.

Red flags: Marina Bay Sands (gambling)
- Marina Bay Sands is a casino resort owned by Las Vegas Sands, with about 15,000 square metres of gaming space (Wikipedia).
- It is listed among the gallery's current donors and patrons (annual report 2025/26).

Red flags: Keppel (founding partner)
- In December 2017 Keppel Offshore & Marine and a US subsidiary agreed to pay US$422,216,980 across US, Brazilian and Singaporean authorities over bribes paid through an agent in Brazil from about 2001 to 2014; the US subsidiary agreed to plead guilty (Maritime Professional, citing Keppel's announcement).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism of the gallery's funders found in art media. The two pieces found discuss state control of the Singapore arts scene; neither criticises the gallery itself.
1. e-flux Criticism: Kathleen Ditzig, "Singapore Art Week" (2018)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/criticism/241117/singapore-art-week
- Argues Singapore's art scene is linked closely to state sponsorship and national development goals.
- Describes the National Gallery as holding one of the best collections of Southeast Asian art; praises it rather than criticising.
2. Asia Times: Kirsten Han, article on state funding of artists in Singapore (2017)
URL: https://asiatimes.com/2017/08/state-versus-arts-singapore/
- Argues state funding comes with conditions, citing the 2015 withdrawal of a National Arts Council grant from Sonny Liew's graphic novel.
- Mentions the gallery only in photo captions; its text does not discuss it. Not an art-press outlet.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2023 protesters covered the Whitney Museum's entrance in fake blood and demanded the removal of Citadel's Ken Griffin from its board (Hyperallergic, Hakim Bishara).
- Citadel is a Corporate Patron of the National Gallery Singapore. No reporting found of any protest against Citadel or Bloomberg at this gallery.

BOTTOM LINE
Most of the gallery's money is Singapore government grant, which the gallery states plainly, and the audited accounts are published. The weak points are the casino operator Marina Bay Sands among its patrons, the Keppel bribery resolution of 2017 by a founding partner, and Citadel, a campaign target elsewhere. Watch whether gift amounts are ever disclosed.
