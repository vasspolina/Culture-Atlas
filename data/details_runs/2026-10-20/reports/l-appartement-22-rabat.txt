L'APPARTEMENT 22: Rabat, Morocco
ID: l-appartement-22-rabat
Website: http://www.appartement22.com (checked 2026-10-25; online and active, in French, English and Arabic)

OVERVIEW
- Independent, experimental contemporary art space at 279 avenue Mohamed V in Rabat, with exhibitions, workshops, symposia, residencies, a publishing line and a radio station (the space's website; Contemporary And).
- Founded in 2002 (October 2002, according to Contemporary And) by the independent curator Abdellah Karroum. Gatherings began in summer 2002 in Karroum's own apartment, which overlooked the capital's main street; he turned his home into a space for research and action because Moroccan institutions did not prioritise this kind of work (Karroum, C&, 2013).
- No reporting found on the origin of the name.
- Building: an apartment, not a purpose-built space; no architect or materials are given on the pages opened.
- On site: a shared personal library, artist videos, portfolios, project archives and books by Editions Hors'champs (C&, 2013). A residency programme began in 2004. In October 2007 it launched RADIOapartment 22 (R22), described as the first independent radio station for art and culture in Morocco (Contemporary And).
- Its retrospective "L'appartement 22 (2002-2022)," curated by Karroum, ran from 11 October 2022 to 7 January 2023 at Lot 219 in Fès, Morocco (Kohllective).
- Visiting: no reporting found of opening days, admission or accessibility. The site lists recent events including a film cycle for Ramadan 1444 and a 20th anniversary programme.

FUNDING
L'Appartement 22: funders not published: no funder is named on its own site, and the only named supporters are for the founder's research
- The space's website names no funders or partners; its "Links | Thanks" section was not readable.
- Prince Claus Fund and Clark Art Institute: the Biennial Foundation's biography of Karroum says his research is funded in part by the Prince Claus Fund (Amsterdam) and the Clark Art Institute (Williamstown, Massachusetts). The biography is dated 11 April 2012 and describes support for Karroum's research, not a grant to the space. No grant amount or year is stated. Unverified: that either body funds the space itself; no page opened says so.
- Publishing: the space's library offers Editions Hors'champs books and limited editions (C&, 2013). No reporting found of sales figures.
- Founder's other role: Karroum was appointed director of Mathaf: Arab Museum of Modern Art in Doha, Qatar, in June 2013 (C&, 2013). No reporting found of any Qatari money paying for L'appartement 22.
- No reporting found of annual reports, accounts, a board or other grant-makers for the space itself. No page opened mentions "non-commercial international research grants".

BOTTOM LINE
No flagged funder was found behind L'Appartement 22, but little is published about its money at all: the only named supporters are the Prince Claus Fund and the Clark Art Institute, and that is for the founder's research as of 2012. The weak points are the absence of any funder list, accounts or board on the space's own site, and the founder's 2013 role at Mathaf in Doha, which is not shown to involve the space. Worth watching: the "Thanks" section of its site and any grant records.
