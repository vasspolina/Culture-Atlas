ZOMA MUSEUM: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
ID: zoma-museum-addis-ababa
Website: https://www.zomamuseum.org (checked 2026-10-24; serves online gambling spam, not a museum site)

OVERVIEW
- Art and architecture complex in the Mekanisa area of Addis Ababa, on a two-acre site in a corner of the city (The Reporter for the neighbourhood; Wisconsin Public Radio for the two acres).
- Co-founded by curator and cultural anthropologist Meskerem Assegued and artist Elias Sime, who began working together in 2002 through the Zoma Contemporary Art Center (The Reporter); the museum opened on 24 March 2019 after more than 20 years of planning (Wisconsin Public Radio).
- Named for Zoma Shiferraw, a young Ethiopian artist who died of cancer in 1979 (James Cohan Gallery press release).
- Built in the Ethiopian vernacular manner from mud, straw, stone, wood and cement; Sime carved patterns into the walls drawing on Ethiopian culture, and the textured walls are designed so visually impaired visitors can also appreciate the architecture (The Reporter, James Cohan).
- On site: a gallery, library, children's centre, edible garden, elementary school, art and vernacular architecture school, amphitheatre and museum shop (The Reporter). A terraced hillside garden, a kindergarten, goats, sheep and cows were described in 2019 (Wisconsin Public Radio).
- Visiting: no official hours, admission or accessibility information could be read, because the museum's website serves spam.

FUNDING
Zoma Museum (Mekanisa): self-funded: the founders' own money and sales of Sime's art
- In 2013 the founders bought the Mekanisa land with their own money and proceeds from sales of Sime's artwork (The Reporter, 2021).
- Zoma Village Entoto, a second complex on Mount Entoto above Addis Ababa: the founders provide all the financing and expect a return on the investment one day (The Art Newspaper, 2024).
- No reporting found of a corporate or state funder behind the Mekanisa museum itself.
- No reporting found of accounts, a foundation or a board for the museum.

Red flags: ties to Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's government
- Unity Park: Ethiopia's prime minister commissioned Sime and Assegued to build a smaller version of Zoma at Unity Park, which opened in October 2019 (The Art Newspaper, 2024).
- Unity Park's cost and funders: commentator Alemayehu G. Mariam wrote that the park cost more than US$160 million and was paid for by the United Arab Emirates government and contributions from wealthy Ethiopians, with no tax dollars (Al Mariam's Commentaries, 2020). In July 2023 the prime minister said the UAE government financed Unity Park and oversaw the process, including the choice of contractors (Sifter, 2023).
- Transparency: answering questions about the funding in 2023, Abiy told critics to "audit Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed himself" (Sifter, 2023).
- Entoto land grant: Abiy granted the land in the Entoto Mountains on which Zoma Village Entoto stands (The Art Newspaper, 2024). A free land grant from a head of government is state patronage.
- No reporting found that the founders have criticised the government in public.

Not related: the Zoma Foundation of Bentonville, Arkansas, the private foundation of Ben Walton and Lucy Ana Walton, criticised in 2020 for paying $20,000 toward a Colorado state government coordinator position. It has no stated link to the museum (InfluenceWatch).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little criticism of Zoma itself was found. The coverage read is admiring or neutral, and no critic makes a full case against the museum. Two further pieces, an Artforum column and an ARTnews article on Sime, sit behind paywalls and could not be read.
1. Hyperallergic: Seph Rodney, "Art That's Too Lovely to Make Its Politics Convincing" (2017)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/377661/art-thats-too-lovely-to-make-its-politics-convincing/
- Reviews Sime's assemblages of reclaimed electronic waste: recycling is an act of conscience, but the work is "swamped by its own aesthetics".
- Argues its beauty invites an unearned hopefulness and turns the making into a story collectors will like.
- Criticises the art, not the institution; does not mention Zoma.
2. The Art Newspaper: Alexander Morrison, profile of the Zoma founders' projects (2024)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2024/05/20/how-an-artist-and-curator-duo-are-transforming-addis-ababa-with-their-eco-driven-projects
- Not criticism: it records the Unity Park commission, the Entoto land grant from Abiy and the founders' self-financing of Zoma Village Entoto.
- Notes that the Kenya-based outlet The Elephant has questioned local authorities' spending priorities, such as parks, playgrounds, museums and electric cars, given the country's economic and humanitarian struggles. It does not name Zoma in that point.

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2023 Prosperity Party MP Seid Mohammed questioned the sources of funding for Abiy's costly projects in a parliamentary budget session, saying lawmakers alone should approve budgets (The Reporter, Sisay Sahlu, "Finance Minister pressed on funding for PM's 'fancy' projects").
- The Reporter wrote that critics accuse Abiy of favouring "lavish projects like resorts, parks, and palaces" over the needs of ordinary Ethiopians.
- On the Addis Ababa Corridor Development project, historian Wondewosen Kebede said "Piassa is the core of urbanization. There are untouchable zones where heritage sites are located," and residents complained of "arbitrary and inadequate compensation and replacement housing" (The Reporter, 30 March 2024).
- Ethiopian architect Nahom Teklu said rapid projects carried out without public consultation raise the question of whose vision is shaping Addis Ababa (Christian Science Monitor, 6 January 2025).
- Zoma's founders built a smaller version of Zoma at Unity Park and received Abiy's Entoto land grant, which places them inside the spending critique; none of these pieces names Zoma.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Mekanisa museum itself is the founders' own money and Sime's art sales, and no outside funder was found. The weak points are the founders' closeness to the Abiy government (the Unity Park commission, the Entoto land grant), the link through Unity Park to UAE money, which Abiy answered by telling critics to audit the UAE's leader, and the absence of any public distance from the government. Watch Zoma Village Entoto and any state role in its operation.
