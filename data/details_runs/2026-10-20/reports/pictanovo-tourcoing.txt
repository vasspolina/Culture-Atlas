PICTANOVO: Tourcoing, France
ID: pictanovo-tourcoing
Website: https://www.pictanovo.com/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Regional film, series, animation and video game agency, run as an association, based at 21 rue Edgar Quinet in Tourcoing; it presents itself as "Images en Hauts-de-France".
- Created in 1985 as the Centre régional de ressources audiovisuelles (CRRAV) on the initiative of the Nord-Pas-de-Calais region; merged with the Pôle Images Nord-Pas-de-Calais in 2013 and took the name Pictanovo; its remit covered the whole Hauts-de-France region from 1 January 2016 (French Wikipedia).
- Aid goes through eight funds: feature films and series, short films, video games, animation, documentaries, new media, young authors, and editorial programmes covering two to five works; reading committees select the projects (French Wikipedia).
- Services on its site: equipment rental, training, a talent directory, project submission and a location office for film shoots.
- Its president is Grégory Tempremant and its managing director is Godefroy Vujicic (Pictanovo site).
- Visiting: No reporting found of public opening hours or admission; the site is aimed at film and media professionals.

FUNDING
Pictanovo: mainly public: Hauts-de-France region, with the state and the CNC (national film centre)
- The Hauts-de-France region is the main funder and presides over the association; the Métropole européenne de Lille, the Porte du Hainaut and Valenciennes communities and Amiens Métropole are members by right (French Wikipedia).
- The grants sit within a partnership between the state, the region and the CNC (French Wikipedia). Pictanovo's site shows a strip of four funder logos without names or amounts.
- Pictanovo states that it invests 8.6 million euros a year in film and audiovisual creation and backs about 230 works a year (stated by Pictanovo).
- The governance page counts 24 board members, 12 of them by right, and links a PDF of the board's composition that was not read. No reporting found of an annual report or accounts on the pages opened, or of private donors.

Red flags: Epstein-linked, Caroline Lang
- Caroline Lang presided over "le fonds régional Pictanovo" (L'Essentiel de l'éco; the article gives no further title). Mediacités reported on 5 February 2026 that she gave up her functions at Pictanovo and at the Séries Mania festival on Wednesday 4 February 2026. L'Essentiel de l'éco adds that she announced on 2 February that she was leaving her January 2026 post as head of the independent producers' union SPI.
- Prytanee: in 2016 she and Jeffrey Epstein founded Prytanee, an offshore art-investment company, in equal shares (Public Sénat; L'Essentiel de l'éco, citing US Justice Department documents). Public Sénat places it in the British Virgin Islands and L'Essentiel de l'éco in the US Virgin Islands. She says she never declared her stake to the French tax authorities and never received funds from the company (Public Sénat).
- On 6 February 2026 the French national financial prosecutor (PNF) announced a preliminary investigation into Caroline Lang and her father Jack Lang for "blanchiment de fraude fiscale aggravée", laundering of aggravated tax fraud, over facts revealed by Mediapart (Public Sénat).
- Public Sénat writes that Epstein's last will, "datant de 2018", leaves her five million dollars; L'Essentiel de l'éco says the will was signed two days before his death. She says she learned of the bequest from the press and received nothing (L'Essentiel de l'éco).
- Denials: Jack Lang "continue de clamer son innocence" (Public Sénat). Her lawyer, Laurent Merlet, says she received no funds and that what the Langs said is "la pure vérité" (Public Sénat, my translation). No charge has been brought and L'Essentiel de l'éco states that no evidence links her to Epstein's crimes.
- No reporting found of Epstein money reaching Pictanovo itself.

BOTTOM LINE
Pictanovo's own money is public, from the Hauts-de-France region and its partners, and no reporting links it to a flagged funder. The weak point is its former fund president, Caroline Lang, who is under a preliminary investigation by the national financial prosecutor over a company she co-founded with Jeffrey Epstein; she left her Pictanovo functions on 4 February 2026 and no charge has been brought. Worth watching: the outcome of the PNF inquiry and who now presides over the fund, and whether Pictanovo publishes accounts.
