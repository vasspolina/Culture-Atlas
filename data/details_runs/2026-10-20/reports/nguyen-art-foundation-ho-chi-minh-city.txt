NGUYEN ART FOUNDATION: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
ID: nguyen-art-foundation-ho-chi-minh-city
Website: https://nguyenartfoundation.com

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art organisation in Ho Chi Minh City with two permanent sites inside EMASI schools: EMASI Nam Long (147 Street No. 8, Nam Long Residential Area, District 7) and EMASI Van Phuc (2 Street No. 5, Van Phuc Residential City, Thu Duc City) (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 2018 by collector Quynh Nguyen, who describes it as "a branching support structure" for contemporary art in Vietnam with four strands: collection, exhibitions, education and public programmes, and development projects (NAF website).
- It grew out of Nguyen's personal collection (Vietcetera, Valeria Mertsalova). Wikipedia puts the collection at more than 1,000 works, mainly by artists from Vietnam and Southeast Asia.
- Quynh Nguyen has led Viet Huong JSC, a flavour and fragrance company, since 1995; she co-founded Renaissance International School Saigon in 2007, opened the International Modern Art Gallery in Houston in 2014 and co-founded the EMASI Schools (NAF team page).
- Team: Quynh Nguyen, founder; Bill Nguyen, director; Nhat Q. Vo, manager; Vicky Do, curator (NAF team page). The collection is managed by the MoT+++ team (Art and Market interview, 2020).
- Exhibitions and displays are held at EMASI Schools and Renaissance International School Saigon, where students meet artists and help curate shows as part of the art curriculum (NAF website, Vietcetera, 2020).
- NAF co-sponsored the A. Farm residency from 2018 to 2021; in an interview with Patrizia Sandretto Re Rebaudengo of 5 December 2025, Nguyen said NAF plans to focus more on collection building and research, including bilingual publications, from 2026 (Il Sole 24 Ore).
- NAF is a patron of CIMAM and a member of the Southeast Asian Collector's Circle (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Nguyen Art Foundation: self-funded: the Nguyen family's private money
- Wikipedia calls NAF a "privately funded institution"; the Art and Market interview with Quynh Nguyen (17 February 2020) states it is privately funded by the Nguyen family.
- The founder, Quynh Nguyen, has led Viet Huong JSC, a Vietnamese flavour and fragrance company, since 1995 (NAF team page).
- A. Farm sales were split between the artist and A. Farm, and A. Farm's share went toward residency operating costs (Art and Market interview).
- Nguyen says works that no longer fit the collection can be sold to fund new acquisitions (Art and Market interview).
- Vietcetera (2020) quotes Nguyen saying it will be at least a decade before Vietnam gets its first contemporary art museum, which she expects to be privately founded and based in Saigon.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation is privately funded by the Nguyen family, according to the founder's 2020 interview and Wikipedia. The founder has led the flavour and fragrance company Viet Huong JSC since 1995 and co-founded the EMASI schools, which house its two permanent sites.
