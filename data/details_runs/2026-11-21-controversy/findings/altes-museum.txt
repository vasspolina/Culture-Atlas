Red flags: 2024 return of 25 looted antiquities to Italy
- On 13 June 2024 Hermann Parzinger, President of the Stiftung Preußischer Kulturbesitz, signed an agreement at the Italian Embassy in Berlin to return 25 objects from the Antikensammlung to Italy: 21 Apulian vases of the 4th century BCE and four other pieces (Staatliche Museen zu Berlin, 13 June 2024).
- The Stiftung said that "with a high degree of certainty" the items were acquired through archaeological looting, and called the return voluntary (Staatliche Museen zu Berlin, 13 June 2024).
- Berlin bought the 21 vases in 1984 from the Basel dealer Christoph F. Leon; polaroid photographs of four of them were found in the Geneva office of the smuggler Giacomo Medici, who was convicted in 2004 (The Art Newspaper, Catherine Hickley, 13 June 2024).
- Parzinger said four of the vases "are proven to have been from illegal excavations"; the other 17 are so similar that they are presumed to come from the same grave (The Art Newspaper, 13 June 2024).
- Italy is lending Berlin two painted panels from Lucanian graves and a bronze suit of armour for several years (Staatliche Museen zu Berlin; The Art Newspaper).
