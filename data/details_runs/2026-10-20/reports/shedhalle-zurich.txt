SHEDHALLE (ROTE FABRIK): Zürich, Switzerland
ID: shedhalle-zurich
Website: https://shedhalle.ch (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Institution of contemporary and critical art at Seestrasse 395, 8038 Zürich, inside the Rote Fabrik, a compound in the city that has been in use as a culture and recreation centre since the early 1980s (Shedhalle).
- It evolved in 1986 from an interest group of local artists who felt under-represented in the established art system (Shedhalle).
- The Rote Fabrik is a former industrial complex by Lake Zürich. The Standard Telephon und Radio AG left the Wollishofen site in 1972 and the city bought it; the city opened it as a provisional cultural centre in 1980 and 1981 and voters approved a culture and leisure centre there in 1987 (SRF, 12 March 2020).
- Since the mid-1990s it has gained wide renown for exhibitions on political and social issues (Fillip, 2006).
- Run by Verein Shedhalle, an association with members who pay a yearly contribution. Ketty Ghnassia is listed as management and Laura Amann Marín and Jen Kratochvil as artistic directors. Teresa Chen and Gabrielle Schaad are co-presidents of the board, and Barbara Gerber sits on it as the representative of Kultur Stadt Zürich (Shedhalle).
- Current programme: Hellenalgia, Vol. 1: Exhausted, 22 October to 22 November 2026; open Thursday and Friday 12 to 7 pm and Saturday and Sunday 12 to 5 pm. Admission is CHF 5, 15 or 25; free for holders of residence permit N, F or S. The venue is wheelchair accessible and texts are available in simple language (Shedhalle).

FUNDING
Shedhalle: City of Zurich and Canton of Zurich funds, with private foundations for projects
- Kultur Stadt Zürich has a seat on the association's board (Shedhalle team page).
- Supporters of the project Loving Shedhalle: Cultural Fund of the Canton of Zurich (Special Project), City of Zurich (Contribution Fund of the Department of Finance), Memoriav, Ernst Göhner Foundation, Stanley Thomas Johnson Foundation, Else v. Sick Foundation, Cassinelli-Vogel Foundation, Dr. Adolf Streuli Foundation, videocompany.ch and ZHdK, Zurich University of Arts (Shedhalle).
- SRF reports that the Rote Fabrik operation is subsidised with 2.4 million francs a year and that its funding and status remain subject to political debate (SRF, 12 March 2020, translated). This is the whole centre, not the Shedhalle alone.
- The association lists statutes and annual reports for 2019 to 2024 on its site.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One 2006 essay in Fillip describes an artists' complaint about the Shedhalle; it concerns the space's direction, not its funders.
1. Fillip: Barnaby Drabble, "We Want Everything: Assembled Thoughts on Artist-led Culture in Zürich" (Fillip 4, Fall 2006)
URL: https://fillip.ca/content/we-want-everything
- Some Freie Szene artists felt the Shedhalle had become so professionalised that it was closed to them, and that the space had been taken from the artists who founded it.
- The curators at the time, Katharina Schlieben and Sönke Gau, countered that artists had shaped the space's history and decision-making.
- Drabble criticises both sides for becoming entrenched, the artists turning the discussion into ritualised protest and the curators pressing for a "constructive" dialogue.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Shedhalle is public and philanthropic: the City of Zurich and the Canton of Zurich cultural fund back the Loving Shedhalle project, the Rote Fabrik operation carries a 2.4 million franc annual subsidy per SRF, and private foundations back individual projects. The association Verein Shedhalle runs the space with a board that includes a representative of Kultur Stadt Zürich.
