LIVINGSTONE OFFICE FOR CONTEMPORARY ARTS (LOCA): Livingstone, Zambia
ID: livingstone-office-for-contemporary-arts-loca-livingstone
Website: https://www.locazm.org/

OVERVIEW
- Artist-initiated non-profit library and research centre at Plot 34, 34 Likute Way, Livingstone, which calls itself "an experimental think-tank" (LoCA, e-flux).
- Founded in 2014 by Anawana Haloba and Victor Mutelekesha, with a network of artists and curators from across the African diaspora including Gabi Ngcobo and Milumbe Haimbe (ArtReview, Chaze Matakala, "Who Builds Zambia's Art World?", 19 January 2026).
- ArtReview describes Livingstone as Zambia's tourism capital and quotes Haloba saying it was a natural place to build an art centre.
- Programmes: workshops, incubators and mini-labs since 2020, and the Sonic Solidarities incubator in August 2025 (LoCA, ArtReview).
- The Mosi-oa-Tunya Perennial, its first large international exhibition on the theme "Smuggling as Resistance", opened on 12 September 2026 and runs to March 2027 across Livingstone, Zambia, and Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe (LoCA).
- The Perennial has an international advisory committee of ten, including Gabi Ngcobo, Elvira Dyangani Ose and N'Gone Fall (e-flux).
- Open Monday to Wednesday 10:00 to 15:00, Thursday 10:00 to 17:00, Friday and Saturday 10:00 to 13:00, closed Sunday (LoCA).

FUNDING
LoCA: artist-founded non-profit, working through institutional partners
- The "Our Sponsors" page, which gives no years or amounts, names 15 sponsors: ARAC, National Arts Council Zambia, OnCurating, the African Film Institute in New York, Chimurenga, National Gallery of Zimbabwe, Ora Loapi, WayiWayi, Livingstone National Museum, National Gallery Livingstone, Picha, Zeitz MOCAA, LuCAC, Njabala Foundation and Tenthaus (LoCA).
- An e-flux announcement of 19 June 2025 lists 13 "institutional comrades/partners" of the Perennial, among them Zeitz MOCAA, the National Gallery of Zimbabwe, the Njabala Foundation and the National Arts Council (Zambia).
- ArtReview reports a "void of government funding for the arts" in Zambia and says LoCA works with local cultural institutions including the National Gallery in Livingstone (Chaze Matakala, 2026).

BOTTOM LINE
LoCA is an artist-initiated non-profit founded in 2014. Its named sponsors and Perennial partners are cultural institutions, museums and arts organisations, among them Zeitz MOCAA and the National Arts Council Zambia.
