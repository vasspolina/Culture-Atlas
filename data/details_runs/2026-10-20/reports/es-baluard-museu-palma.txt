ES BALUARD MUSEU: Palma, Spain
ID: es-baluard-museu-palma
Website: https://esbaluard.org

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern and contemporary art at Plaça Porta Santa Catalina 10, Palma, on the perimeter of the bastion of Sant Pere, part of the Renaissance walled enclosure (the museum's history page).
- Inaugurated on 30 January 2004 by the King and Queen of Spain; Palma City Council ceded the site in 1997. A foundation runs it, made up of the Balearic government, the Consell de Mallorca, Palma City Council and the Fundació d'Art Serra (the museum's organisation page).
- Pere A. Serra Bauzà is listed as the board's Founding President; the Fundació d'Art Serra and Serra Bauzà donated or deposited works in the collection (the museum's organisation page).
- The bastion dates from the last quarter of the 16th century and military use ended in 1952. In 1963 the owners tried to destroy the wall with lime bombs; the land was left abandoned until 1997 (the museum's history page).
- The building was designed in 2003 by Lluís García-Ruiz, Jaume García-Ruiz, Vicente Tomás and Ángel Sánchez Cantalejo. It covers 5,027 square metres, 2,500 of them for exhibitions, over three storeys. A restored cistern, the Aljub (353 square metres), is part of the museum (the museum's history page).
- The museum's holdings include more than 800 works; David Barro has been director since April 2024 (the museum's organisation page; Bonart, 16 April 2024).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 20:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 15:00, closed Monday. General ticket €8, reduced €5.50, €2 for visitors who come by bike, pay-what-you-decide on Fridays from €0.10; members enter free. Ramps connect floors 0 to 2, two lifts serve the museum and wheelchairs can be borrowed (the museum's visit page).

FUNDING
Es Baluard Museu: mainly public: Balearic government, Palma City Council and the Consell de Mallorca, with a smaller grant from Spain's Ministry of Culture
- 2025 budget of €2,565,000, up 11.7 percent on 2024: Govern balear €990,000, Palma City Council €775,000, Consell de Mallorca €750,000, Ministry of Culture €50,000 (Última Hora, Adrián Malagamba, "Los ambiciosos próximos cinco años de Es Baluard Museu", 20 November 2024).
- The Fundació d'Art Serra sits on the board, represented by Carme Serra, Miquel Serra and Miquel Rullán (the museum's board notice of 4 December 2023), and supplied the founding collection.
- On the board notice of 4 December 2023 the president of the Govern, Margalida Prohens, presided. The museum's organisation page lists the mayor of Palma, Jaime Martínez, as president and the president of the Consell de Mallorca, Llorenç Galmés, as vice president; a notice of 15 December 2025 says Martínez takes the presidency for three years.
- The collaborators page lists sponsors including Hotel Saratoga and Summum Hotel Group, and media partners Ara Balears, Diario de Mallorca and IB3, as linked logos.

BOTTOM LINE
Es Baluard is a public museum run by a foundation of three Balearic public bodies and the Fundació d'Art Serra. All four contributions itemised in its 2025 budget of €2,565,000 are public money (Govern, Palma City Council, Consell de Mallorca and the Ministry of Culture). Corporate sponsors are hotel groups and local media.
