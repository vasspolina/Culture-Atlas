WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 2018 the gallery demanded that state bodies return hundreds of works lent to them for temporary use; the loan periods had expired under 26 contracts, loans were meant to last at most three years, and director Arman Tsaturyan said the paintings were being held illegally (JAMnews, 12 July 2018).
- The Ministry of Emergency Situations held the most, 94 canvases; the most valuable works were at the Prime Minister's residence, the government building and the parliament (JAMnews).
- Tsaturyan said he had appealed to earlier culture ministers without result (JAMnews).
- The Ministry of Culture under Lilit Makunts returned the paintings it held and wrote to the heads of the other bodies: «կտավները պետք է անհապաղ վերադարձվեն, այլապես հարցի լուծմամբ կզբաղվեն իրավապահ մարմինները» ("the canvases must be returned without delay, otherwise law enforcement bodies will deal with the matter", translated). At the time of the report only the Ministry of Emergency Situations and the Prosecutor General's Office had responded (JAMnews).
