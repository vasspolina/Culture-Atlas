APEXART: New York, USA
ID: apexart-new-york
Website: https://apexart.org (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit exhibition space at 291 Church Street in Tribeca, Lower Manhattan, that picks most of its shows by open call, in which jurors do not see the submitters' names (Wikipedia; apexart website).
- Founded in 1994 by Steven Rand, who also directs it (Wikipedia). The tax filing is under the name Apex Art Curatorial Program Inc, a 501(c)(3) exempt since April 1998, with Rand listed as president and executive director (ProPublica).
- Brooklyn Rail (Darragh McNicholas, September 2014) reports Rand living above the gallery and credits apexart with more than 170 exhibitions featuring over 1,500 artists, four books of essays and a residency programme.
- Programmes: the NYC Open Call and International Open Call, an Invited Curator series, an Unsolicited Proposal Program, a Franchise Program that stages shows outside New York, a one-month fellowship abroad, and publications (Wikipedia; apexart website; e-flux).
- The 2025 to 2026 season included exhibitions in New York, Seoul, Addis Ababa, Lagos and Casablanca (e-flux). In 2026 it listed shows in Tribeca and in La Paz, Bolivia (apexart website).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday, 11 am to 6 pm (e-flux, 2025 to 2026 season).

FUNDING
apexart: mainly private giving plus public arts grants: foundations, national, state and city arts agencies, and foreign cultural bodies
- Finances (ProPublica, Apex Art Curatorial Program Inc): 2025 revenue $668,067, expenses $694,684, contributions $663,949, net assets $102,706; 2024 revenue $712,957, expenses $764,291; 2023 revenue $693,524, expenses $657,819; 2022 revenue $720,160. Contributions are nearly all of revenue every year.
- Foundations listed on apexart's support page as supporters past and present: 291 Foundation, Affirmation Arts Fund, The Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, The Buhl Foundation, Degenstein Foundation, Epstein-Teicher Philanthropies, Fifth Floor Foundation, Foundation for Contemporary Arts, Genie and Donald Rice Charitable Trust, The Greenwich Collection, Jewish Communal Fund, The Kettering Family Foundation, Lambent Foundation Fund of Tides Foundation, The Milton and Sally Avery Arts Foundation, The Mindel Family Charitable Annuity Trust, The Puffin Foundation, Trust for Mutual Understanding, Wender Weis Foundation for Children and William Talbott Hillman Foundation.
- Government: the National Endowment for the Arts, the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs with the City Council, and the New York State Council on the Arts (apexart support page).
- Foreign bodies listed: the British Council, Pro Helvetia, the Danish Arts Council, the Mondriaan Foundation, the Nordic Culture Fund, the Swedish Arts Grants Committee, FRAME Finnish Fund for Art Exchange, the Austrian Cultural Forum, the French Embassy cultural services, the Turkish Cultural Foundation, CONACULTA, the Etant donnés Foundation, the Hungarian Cultural Center, the Mexican Cultural Institute of New York, Seoul Art Space Geumcheon and the consulates of Hungary and the Netherlands.
- Corporate and institutional supporters listed: Jack Shainman Gallery, J.W. Allen Insurance Company, The Seneca Real Estate Group, Stephen Lasar Architects, West Wind Studios, Gotham Vapor Lounge LLC, Fashion Institute of Technology, Rhode Island School of Design, A Gathering of the Tribes, AKV Berlin, Charlotte Art League, Fence Magazine and Residency Unlimited. The page also lists 142 individual entries.
- apexart says it is "not a granting organization": it funds winning exhibitions itself, with $10,000 per exhibition including up to a $2,000 honorarium (apexart open call page).
- ProPublica lists Steven Rand as president and executive director and Rosemary O'Neill as treasurer.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The one profile found, in the Brooklyn Rail, is favourable.
1. Brooklyn Rail: Darragh McNicholas, "Twenty Years of apexart: A Profile of Founder Steven Rand" (2014)
URL: https://brooklynrail.org/2014/09/art/twenty-years-of-apexart-a-profile-of-founder-steven-rand/
- A profile of the founder and the open call process; the only critical content is Rand's own argument that the best creative people are not necessarily the best networkers, since networking "gets you the best networker".

BOTTOM LINE
apexart runs on about $700,000 a year in contributions from foundations, public arts agencies, foreign cultural bodies and individuals, and none of the named foundations or agencies is in a flagged industry. Its net assets fell from $180,657 in 2023 to $102,706 in 2025 (ProPublica). Worth watching: how the 2025 deficit of $26,617 develops.
