MUSEUM TINGUELY – Basel, Switzerland
Website: https://www.tinguely.ch (not checked: fetch not permitted in this run, 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Jean Tinguely's work on the Rhine, designed by Mario Botta, opened 1996. Niki de Saint Phalle donated more than 50 works from Tinguely's estate soon after opening (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Museum Tinguely: corporate-funded (Roche).
- Existing record, from the museum's own Roche page: Roche built the museum for its centenary and has paid all operating costs since 1996, and funds acquisitions. That page could not be re-retrieved this run; Wikipedia's article does not mention Roche funding.
- Roche is controlled by the founding Hoffmann and Oeri families: a family pool holds 45% of voting shares, Maja Oeri a further 5% (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Funder's price-fixing conviction
- Roche pleaded guilty in 1999 to a worldwide vitamin price-fixing conspiracy and paid $500M in US criminal fines (Wikipedia). The EEC also fined it in 1973 for vitamin price-fixing and market sharing; the UK's ABPI suspended it for six months in 2008 (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum depends on a single corporate funder, Roche. Roche's past antitrust penalties are dated; no reporting found of issues with the museum itself.
