ID: espacio-cultural-patino

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 21 October 2021 staff of the Fundación Simón I. Patiño's centres in La Paz and Cochabamba were called to meetings with the director of the Espacio Patiño in La Paz and the foundation's auditor, told that the documentation centres and libraries in both cities were closing, and asked to sign resignation letters, which they did (Página Siete, Jorge Soruco, reprinted by eju.tv, 28 October 2021).
- After the foundation said in statements that the libraries would keep operating, 13 former employees went public; one said "Nos han engañado. Somos profesionales a los que dijeron mentiras para que dejáramos los cargos" (They deceived us. We are professionals who were told lies so that we would leave our posts, translated) (Página Siete, reprinted by eju.tv).
- Unverified: in an open letter on social media the Cochabamba staff said they had suffered workplace harassment "desde hace varios meses" (for several months, translated), and that in September 2021 the foundation's director in Geneva, Frédéric Debrey, had promised job stability while announcing a salary cut for "problemas económicos" (economic problems, translated) (Página Siete, reprinted by eju.tv).
- The former staff said they were organising, with the Colegio de Bibliotecarios, to seek reinstatement and public redress. José Baudoin, the foundation's director nacional de cultura, told Página Siete that the La Paz spaces would resume activities the following week and that work was under way to improve the centres' services (Página Siete, reprinted by eju.tv).
