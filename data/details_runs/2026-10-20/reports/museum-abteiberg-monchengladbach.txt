MUSEUM ABTEIBERG: Mönchengladbach, Germany
ID: museum-abteiberg-monchengladbach
Website: https://museum-abteiberg.de

OVERVIEW
- Municipal museum of contemporary art in the historic centre of Mönchengladbach, built into the face of a hillside next to the cathedral and a baroque abbey (hollein.com).
- Called the Städtisches Museum Abteiberg; the city of Mönchengladbach was the client, planning ran 1972 to 1973 and the building was completed in 1982 (hollein.com).
- Designed by the Austrian architect Hans Hollein. The building is a walk-on structure whose surface is open to the public and used for open-air sculpture, and the interior is a sequence of white spaces arranged as a three-dimensional matrix rather than a linear route (hollein.com).
- Before 1982 the collection was housed in the city hall (1901 to 1904), the Karl-Brandts-Haus (1926 to 1944, destroyed in the Second World War) and the Oskar-Kühlen-Haus (Wikipedia).
- Johannes Cladders directed the museum from 1967 to 1985 (Wikipedia). He started his programme in September 1967 with a major Joseph Beuys exhibition and coined the term "anti-museum" in 1968, a concept that took form in the building opened in 1982 (Kulturstiftung des Bundes).
- Directors since: Dierk Stemmler (1985 to 1994), Veit Loers (1995 to 2003) and Susanne Titz (since 2004) (Wikipedia).
- The collection includes Expressionism, Constructivism, Dada, Informel, Zero, Nouveau Réalisme, Pop Art and Minimal Art (Wikipedia). The museum's site lists a Sammlung/Archiv Andersch among its collection sections.

FUNDING
Museum Abteiberg: mainly public: the city of Mönchengladbach
- Operator: a municipal museum run by the city of Mönchengladbach, which was also the client for the building (Wikipedia, hollein.com). The city's logo appears in the footer of the museum's site.
- Supporters' society: the Museumsverein Mönchengladbach works on the museum's behalf (Wikipedia).
- New public square: Börse Express reported on 5 August 2026 that construction had begun in early August on a square of about 900 square metres at the museum, with a budget of about 490,000 euros, 80 percent of it from external funding.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is public: it is a city museum of Mönchengladbach in a building the city commissioned and finished in 1982, with a supporters' society alongside.
