ID: les-rencontres-d-arles

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2021 a tribune in Libération signed by writers, publishers, historians and photographers, among them Gaël Faye, Scholastique Mukasonga, Atiq Rahimi and Stéphane Audouin-Rouzeau, asked Hubert Védrine, president of the festival's board, to step back from the presidency after his remarks on the genocide of the Tutsis in Rwanda in the review Éléments (Libération tribune, June 2021; Livres Hebdo, 28 June 2021).
- The signatories wrote that his presence on the board "ne permet pas de poursuivre un dialogue serein avec les photographes africains" (translated: "does not allow a calm dialogue with African photographers to continue") (Libération tribune, June 2021).
- Védrine answered in Libération with a right of reply, calling the attacks "incompréhensibles" (translated: "incomprehensible"), and wrote of the festival, "à la présidence non exécutive desquelles j'ai été réélu il y a un mois" (translated: "to whose non-executive presidency I was re-elected a month ago"), "je suggère qu'on les laisse à l'écart de cette controverse historique" (translated: "I suggest it be kept out of this historical controversy") (Livres Hebdo, 28 June 2021).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. 1000 Words: Peter Watkins, "Arles 2025: staging radicality within safe limits?" (2025)
URL: https://1000wordsmag.com/arles-2025-staging-radicality-within-safe-limits/
- The festival declared its support for the Ukrainian people in 2022, but its 2025 edition included no mention or representation of Ukrainian artists or institutions; the author calls the absence of any tangible mention of present political realities "deeply disappointing and troubling".
- Photography's institutions stayed silent on Gaza, "prioritising corporate sponsorship above moral clarity", and Nan Goldin's address on Gaza was left to carry the subject alone.
- The strongest moments came from outside the official programme, among them the NO-PHOTO poster campaign by a collective of artists and activists, which printed descriptions of photographs from Gaza beside black rectangles.

2. American Suburb X: Sunil Shah, "No Photo 2025" (2025)
URL: https://americansuburbx.com/?p=87090
- No Photo 2025, by an anonymous group of international artists and activists, was a poster intervention in Arles during the festival, outside its programme, described as "an activation against complicity and silence".
- The author faults the board members and senior figures responsible for the festival, "who turn a blind eye in order to maintain their position", over Gaza.
