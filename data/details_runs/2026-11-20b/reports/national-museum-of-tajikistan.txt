NATIONAL MUSEUM OF TAJIKISTAN (Осорхонаи миллии Тоҷикистон): Dushanbe, Tajikistan
ID: national-museum-of-tajikistan
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- National history and art museum at Ismoili Somoni Street 11, next to State Flag Square in central Dushanbe (Eurasia.Travel; Tajik public procurement register). Tajik name Osorkhonai Millii Tojikiston (Осорхонаи миллии Тоҷикистон), Russian Natsionalnyy muzey Tadzhikistana (Национальный музей Таджикистана) (English Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1934 with 530 items from the Tajik SSR Academy of Sciences and expeditions; it was named for the miniaturist Kamoliddin Behzad, was renamed the National Museum of Tajikistan in 2011 and moved to its present building in March 2013 (Eurasia.Travel).
- The earlier Behzod museum was set up by decree of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee in 1934 on the basis of an economic achievements exhibition, gained national status by government decision in 1999 and worked from the 1960s in a former technical college building; its third floor covered Tajik history and the work of President Emomali Rahmon (Orexca, an older description; translation ours).
- Building: 22 exhibition halls over 15,000 square metres (Eurasia.Travel); 24,000 square metres in all (English Wikipedia).
- Departments: natural history, ancient and medieval history, modern and contemporary history, and fine and applied arts (English Wikipedia).
- Collection: over 50,000 objects, including Samanid coins, finds from the Sogdian castle on Mount Mug, the wooden Iskandar mihrab of the 11th to 12th century and the clay Buddha from the Ajina-Tepa temple restored by a Hermitage team (Eurasia.Travel). Hellenistic statuettes from Takht-i Sangin are on display (English Wikipedia).
- Director: the public procurement register names Ibodullozoda Akhliddin Ibodullo (Ибодуллозода Ахлиддин Ибодулло; romanisation ours) as head (Tajik public procurement register).

FUNDING
National Museum of Tajikistan: public: a Tajik state institution set up by presidential decree
- Legal status: Presidential Decree No. 675 of 27 July 2011 set up the state institution "National Museum of Tajikistan" (British Museum International Training Programme partner page, citing the museum).
- Register: the public procurement register lists it as the state institution (Государственное учреждение) "Национальный музей Таджикистана", a state enterprise, with a state registration certificate dated 28 October 2013 (Tajik public procurement register).
- Earlier ministry: Orexca, describing the earlier Behzod museum, says it had been under the Ministry of Culture since its founding.
- International partners: the earlier museum ran restoration, storage and documentation projects with the Open Society Institute, UNESCO and Restaurateurs Without Borders (Orexca, older description). It is a partner institution of the British Museum's International Training Programme (British Museum ITP).
- Unverified: a Koryo Group travel blog says the new building was built in 2011 by order of President Emomali Rahmon.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: Tajik state money. Worth watching is how closely its displays follow the president's own record, as the earlier museum's did.
