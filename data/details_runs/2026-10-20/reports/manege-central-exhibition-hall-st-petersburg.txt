MANEGE CENTRAL EXHIBITION HALL: St Petersburg, Russia
ID: manege-central-exhibition-hall-st-petersburg
Website: https://manege.spb.ru/about/

OVERVIEW
- Museum and exhibition complex at Isaakievskaya Square 1, St Petersburg, which calls itself the largest exhibition space in the city centre (Manege website).
- The building is a riding hall designed by the Italian architect Giacomo Quarenghi, finished in 1804; the Life Guards Horse Regiment it served was established by decree of Empress Anna Ioannovna in 1730 (Manege website).
- After 1917 it became a warehouse and in 1930 to 1932 a garage for state security agencies; it was converted into an exhibition hall in 1973 to 1977 and opened in November 1977 (Manege website).
- Restoration, technical re-equipment and a change of programme ran from 2013 to 2016, and the hall reopened on 25 June 2016; the former Maly Manege branch became the Museum of St Petersburg Art of the 20th and 21st Centuries in 2016 (Manege website).
- Main activity: art exhibitions, plus lectures, master classes, seminars, film screenings, concerts and educational events for adults and children. On site: an exhibition hall, a bookshop and a cafe (Manege website).
- Pavel Prigara is listed as director in a Sobaka.ru profile of 7 February 2017. Anna Yalova, previously deputy director for development, was acting director from September 2022 and became director in February 2023; vice-governor Boris Piotrovsky announced it (Delovoy Peterburg, 16 February 2023).
- On 25 October 2026 the exhibition hall was open 11:00 to 21:00, the bookshop 11:00 to 20:45 and the cafe 11:00 to 20:30; the site offers a version for visitors with low vision.

FUNDING
Manege: public institution working to a state assignment; its site lists corporate partners and sponsors
- The site's official documents include a report on fulfilling the state assignment (file named for 2025) and a financial and economic activity plan for 2025 to 2027 (Manege website, official documents page).
- The logo of the Committee for Culture of St Petersburg appears in the site header and footer, and the Committee, the Russian Ministry of Culture, the Russian Culture Foundation and the national projects of Russia are named in the page section "cultural institutions and representations" (Manege website).
- The partnership page lists as partners and sponsors: PhosAgro, Bank Saint Petersburg, VTB Bank, PSB Bank, Rosseti Lenenergo, the digital TV operator Tricolor, the logistics group Admiral, Okhta Mall, the Peredvizhnik company, the management company Theorema, the Benois business centre, the Grand Hotel Europe and the House of Books. Media partners are TASS, The Art Newspaper Russia, Kommersant, Izvestia, Peterburgsky Dnevnik, the television channel Saint Petersburg and Metro Petersburg. The page says in general terms that partners help fund exhibitions, public programmes and educational events (Manege website).
- Board of trustees: Semyon Mikhailovsky, rector of the Repin St Petersburg Academy of Arts, as chair; Olga Galaktionova, director of the Tretyakov Gallery since January 2026; Mikhail Piotrovsky, director of the Hermitage; and Valery Fokin, president of the Alexandrinsky Theatre (Manege website).

BOTTOM LINE
Manege is an exhibition hall that publishes a state assignment report and a financial plan for 2025 to 2027 on its own site, and shows the St Petersburg Committee for Culture's logo. Its partnership page lists corporate partners and sponsors, among them PhosAgro, VTB, PSB and Rosseti Lenenergo.
