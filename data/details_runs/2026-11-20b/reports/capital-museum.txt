CAPITAL MUSEUM: Beijing, China
ID: capital-museum
Website: https://www.capitalmuseum.org.cn/ (checked 2026-11-20; the English site at en.capitalmuseum.org.cn loads only a page title)

OVERVIEW
- The Capital Museum (首都博物馆) describes itself as a large, comprehensive city museum of local history belonging to Beijing municipality, and a national first-grade museum (museum website, our translation of 北京市属大型地志类综合性城市博物馆).
- It was founded in 1981 and first opened on 1 October 1981 in the Beijing Confucius Temple (museum website; Chinese Wikipedia).
- Its present building at 16 Fuxingmenwai Dajie, Xicheng District (复兴门外大街16号), has 64,000 square metres of floor space and opened on 18 May 2006 on the former site of the First Ministry of Machine Building's automobile bureau (museum website; Chinese Wikipedia).
- ArchDaily credits the building to the French firm AREP, and China Architecture Design Group lists it among its works; Chinese Wikipedia names the architect Cui Kai (崔恺). The oval bronze-clad hall carries patterns from Western Zhou bronzes found in Beijing (Chinese Wikipedia).
- The collection covers Beijing history, bronzes, ceramics, calligraphy and painting, jade and Buddhist sculpture, with more than 200,000 objects (Chinese Wikipedia; World Museum Guide).
- Since the start of the 14th Five-Year Plan (2021 to 2025) it has also run the Beijing Courtyard House Museum, the White Pagoda Temple, the Beijing Cultural Exchange Museum at Zhihua Temple and the Lao She Memorial as branches, and the Beijing Grand Canal Museum, its eastern branch, opened at the end of 2023 (museum website).
- The director is Guo Jingning (郭京宁), who is also the museum's Communist Party secretary (museum website).
- Visiting: open 9am to 5pm, last entry 4pm, closed on Tuesdays, with online booking; the permanent displays are free (museum website; Chinese Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Capital Museum: public: a Beijing city institution under the Beijing Municipal Cultural Heritage Bureau
- The museum, with three of its branches, is one of 17 budget units of the Beijing Municipal Cultural Heritage Bureau (北京市文物局), a body directly under the city government (bureau's 2026 budget).
- The bureau's 2026 budget across its 17 units is 1,946.06 million yuan, of which 1,009.74 million yuan is city general budget money and 237.98 million yuan is income from the units' own services; the bureau put the 5% fall from 2025 down to finished building projects including the Grand Canal Museum (bureau's 2026 budget).
- The 2006 building was paid for with a special allocation from the Beijing city government after the plan was approved in 1999; it was a key cultural project of the city's tenth five-year plan (Chinese Wikipedia).
- The museum buys services by public tender: its own site lists procurement and award notices through November 2026 (museum website).

BOTTOM LINE
A city-run museum paid for from the Beijing budget, with no private, corporate or foreign funder named. Nothing in its money raises a flag.
