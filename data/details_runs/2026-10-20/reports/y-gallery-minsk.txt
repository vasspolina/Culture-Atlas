Ў GALLERY OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Minsk, Belarus
ID: y-gallery-minsk
Website: https://ygallery.by

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at Kastryčnickaja 19A in Minsk, with an exhibition space, the Ў wine bar, a design shop and the Literary House Logvinov (Wikipedia). It was one of the main contemporary art institutions in Belarus for more than twelve years (CIMAM).
- Opened in October 2009 (Wikipedia); CIMAM describes it as a scaled-up version of the smaller gallery Podzemka. Valentina Kiseleva was director and curator and Aliaksandr Vasilievič the co-owner and creator (Wikipedia).
- Named for the Belarusian letter Ў, which sets the Belarusian alphabet apart from other East Slavic alphabets (Wikipedia).
- Programme: in 2010 it launched START, a programme of solo exhibitions and public discussions for young Belarusian artists. It held more than 120 exhibitions in its own building, mostly by Belarusian artists, and 14 abroad, in such places as Vilnius, Poznań, Lublin, Moscow and Stockholm, and won "Best Foreign Gallery" at ArtVilnius in 2011 and 2016 (Wikipedia).
- 2020: programmes were cancelled from the end of March because of the pandemic, and the space served for over three months as a warehouse and distribution hub for medical supplies for ByCovid-19. Its last exhibition, Touchable Distance, opened in late October 2020 with 28 artists from 11 countries (MoMA post, "On Forms of Political Organizing Illuminating the Future", 10 February 2021).
- Closure: in summer 2020 Vasilievič, whom CIMAM calls a businessman and philanthropist, was detained while standing surety for the presidential candidate Viktar Babaryka (CIMAM, Anna Karpenko, "Nothing is There", 30 November 2021). The MoMA post says a politically motivated criminal case was brought against one of the co-owners at the end of August 2020 and the gallery office and homes were searched. Wikipedia says the gallery was dissolved on 29 October 2020; CIMAM and the MoMA post say it closed in November 2020, and CIMAM says its art directors left Belarus for security reasons. Wikipedia reports that the owners sold their stakes in the gallery and bar but kept the Ў brands.

FUNDING
Ў Gallery: co-owned by Aliaksandr Vasilievič, a businessman and philanthropist
- Wikipedia names Aliaksandr Vasilievič as co-owner and creator, and CIMAM describes him as a businessman and philanthropist. Visitors could buy paintings and sculptures by modern Belarusian artists, and the gallery had a wine bar and a design shop (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery was co-owned by Aliaksandr Vasilievič, a businessman and philanthropist, and works by Belarusian artists were on sale there (Wikipedia, CIMAM). It closed in autumn 2020: a criminal case was brought against a co-owner at the end of August (MoMA post) and its art directors left Belarus (CIMAM).
