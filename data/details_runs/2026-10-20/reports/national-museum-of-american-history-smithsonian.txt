OVERVIEW
- Smithsonian museum of US history, science and technology on Constitution Avenue by the National Mall, Washington.
- Opened in 1964 as the Museum of History and Technology; renamed the National Museum of American History in 1980 (Wikipedia).
- From 2000 its official name carried the donor's name, "Behring Center" (Commonplace).
- The building was one of the last designed by McKim, Mead & White and stands on the site of two temporary war buildings from 1942. An 85 million dollar renovation by Skidmore, Owings and Merrill, led by Gary Haney, ran from 2006 to 2008 and added an atrium and a chamber for the Star-Spangled Banner (Wikipedia).
- A 37 million dollar west wing renovation began in 2012, with floors reopening in stages from 2015 to 2017.
- Smithsonian museums are free to enter (Wikipedia). No reporting found of current opening hours on the pages read.

FUNDING
National Museum of American History: mainly public: US Congress through the Smithsonian, with private donors
- The Smithsonian's budget is about 1.25 billion dollars a year, about two-thirds from federal appropriations; the rest comes from an endowment of about 2.86 billion dollars, private and corporate gifts, memberships and earned revenue (Wikipedia).
- The Smithsonian is governed by a 17-member Board of Regents that includes the Chief Justice and the Vice President (Wikipedia).
- Kenneth Behring gave 80 million dollars in 2000 and received the "Behring Center" name and an advisory role on two exhibits. Catherine Reynolds pledged 38 million dollars in 2001 for an exhibit on American "achievers", then withdrew all but 1.5 million after negative press (Commonplace, 2002).
- General Motors was offered naming rights to a transportation hall for 10 million dollars (Commonplace).
- No reporting found of a current donor list for this museum on the pages read.

Red flags: White House pressure on exhibits, 2025
- 27 March 2025: President Trump signed the executive order "Restoring Truth and Sanity to American History", which targets "improper ideology" at Smithsonian museums (Wikipedia, Al Jazeera).
- Reported on 31 July 2025: the museum removed a 2021 temporary label saying Trump was impeached twice from its presidency exhibit, so the exhibit said only three presidents had seriously faced removal (Al Jazeera, citing the Washington Post). The Smithsonian said the label did not meet its standards and that "We were not asked by any Administration or other government officials to remove content" (Al Jazeera, PBS).
- Unverified: the removal followed White House pressure, from the Washington Post, relying on an unnamed source, via Al Jazeera.
- 12 August 2025: the White House told Smithsonian Secretary Lonnie Bunch it would review eight museums, this one included. Within 120 days museums should begin replacing "divisive or ideologically driven language" on placards and wall text (NBC News).

Red flags: donors and exhibit content, 2000 to 2002
- Critics, including museum scholars and historical associations, accused Secretary Lawrence Small of letting donors shape the museum; historian Barbara Clark Smith warned of a "privatized public sphere" (Commonplace).
