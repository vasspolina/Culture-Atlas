CENTRO DE ARTE FUNDACIÓN ORTIZ-GURDIÁN: León, Nicaragua
ID: centro-de-arte-fundaci-n-ortiz-gurdi-n-le-n
Website: https://cultura.fundacionortizgurdian.org

OVERVIEW
- Private non-profit art centre in León, in colonial-era houses opposite the San Francisco church; the foundation also runs a second centre in Managua, in the ground floor of Edificio Málaga, Plaza España.
- The Fundación Ortiz-Gurdián was founded in 1996 by the collectors Patricia Gurdián de Ortiz and Ramiro Ortiz Mayorga; the León centre opened in 2000 in the Norberto Ramírez house (DANTEmag, 2015).
- The León centre occupies six houses built between the 18th and 19th centuries: Norberto Ramírez, Derbyshire, Martha Juárez, Francisco Juárez, Deshon and Delgadillo (foundation website). DANTEmag dates the Derbyshire house to September 2002 and the restored Delgadillo house to December 2006, and says the Ortiz Gurdián family bought and restored four of the houses from 1999.
- Collections include Latin American painting in the Derbyshire house and a contemporary art museum in the Deshon house (DANTEmag).
- The foundation began with the Nicaraguan Painting Biennial (first edition 1997, widened to visual arts in 2001) and helped launch the Central American Visual Arts Biennial; nine editions of the local and regional biennials have been held (foundation website).
- The Managua centre, the Ortiz-Gurdián Foundation and Banpro Art Centre, opened on 11 December 2013 with the sponsorship of Banco de la Producción (Banpro) (foundation website; DANTEmag).
- Since 2003 the foundation has run a programme that diagnoses and treats early-stage breast cancer (foundation website).

FUNDING
Centro de Arte Fundación Ortiz-Gurdián: private family foundation: founders' money and Banpro sponsorship
- Founders: the Ortiz Gurdián family bought and restored the León houses (DANTEmag). Ramiro Ortiz Mayorga is the president of Banpro, a bank of the Promerica financial group (Infobae, 16 June 2021).
- Banpro: sponsored the opening of the Managua centre in 2013 (foundation website) and, through the foundation, sponsors the Mariana Sansón National Prize for Poetry Written by Women, awarded annually since 2005 (DANTEmag).
- Biennials abroad: Promerica Bank became the Honduran sponsor of the regional biennial in 2004 and is named as the El Salvador sponsor (DANTEmag).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Nicaraguan police arrested Luis Alberto Rivas Anduray, general manager of Banpro, on the night of Tuesday 15 June 2021 (Infobae, 16 June 2021). The police accused him of inciting foreign interference in internal affairs and calling for military interventions, and cited the law on defending the people's rights to independence, sovereignty and self-determination for peace, approved by the National Assembly in December 2020.
- Connection: Banpro sponsored the opening of the foundation's Managua centre, and Ramiro Ortiz Mayorga, a co-founder of the foundation, is Banpro's president. The police action was against the sponsor's general manager, not the foundation.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the centre is the Ortiz Gurdián family, who founded the foundation and restored the houses, and sponsorship from Banpro, a bank of the Promerica group whose president, Ramiro Ortiz Mayorga, is a co-founder of the foundation. In June 2021 Nicaraguan police arrested Banpro's general manager.
