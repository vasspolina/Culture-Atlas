BIENAL DE ARTE PAIZ: Guatemala City, Guatemala
ID: bienal-de-arte-paiz-guatemala-city
Website: https://bienaldeartepaiz.org/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Biennial of contemporary art organised by Fundación Paiz para la Educación y la Cultura, whose address is 11 Avenida 33-32, zona 5, Guatemala City. It has been held without interruption since 1978 (stated by the biennial).
- Started in 1978 as a cultural project of the family business Almacenes Paiz S.A., as an open competition in painting, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, porcelain and photography; winning works joined the Colección de Arte Paiz of more than 300 works. The 16th edition in 2008 introduced a curatorial format (stated by the biennial).
- The foundation's website names Graciela Andrade de Paiz as founder and calls it an "organización familiar"; María Regina Paiz is board president, Sonia Hurtarte executive director and Francine Birbragher-Rozencwaig artistic director.
- 24th edition, "The World Tree" (El Árbol del Mundo): 6 November 2025 to 15 February 2026, curated by Eugenio Viola, artistic director of MAMBO in Bogotá, with 46 artists and collectives across 11 venues in Antigua Guatemala and Guatemala City, in partnership with the Ruta Maya Foundation (e-flux). The Art Newspaper called it the largest and longest in the biennial's history, with 46 artists from 30 countries.
- 25th edition (2027), the 50th anniversary: curated by Cuauhtémoc Medina, with an open call from 1 to 30 October 2026 (stated by the biennial).
- Beyond museums and galleries the biennial has used streets, markets, plazas and community centres, including Antigua Guatemala, San Juan Comalapa, Atitlán and Izabal (stated by the biennial).
- Fundación Paiz created its first permanent exhibition and programming venue, which soft-launched with a Carlos Martiel performance on 6 November 2025; the review said it would open at the end of the biennial (The Art Newspaper).

FUNDING
Bienal de Arte Paiz: privately funded: the Paiz family's foundation
- Fundación Paiz is a private non-profit that began as a corporate social responsibility organisation more than four decades ago and is currently family run (Biennial Foundation).
- The foundation also runs a scholarship programme named for Graciela Andrade de Paiz, the Premio Monteforte Toledo literary prize and the Marimba festival, which started in 1980 as an event in Paiz stores (foundation site).
- The foundation works with international cooperation donors for its scholarship and training programmes (Biennial Foundation).
- The foundation site takes donations through a "Dona ahora" button.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One review, mostly positive, with reservations about unevenness in some venues and projects and about one artist's work.
1. The Art Newspaper: Constanza Ontiveros Valdés, "Guatemala's Bienal de Arte Paiz nurtures connections across geography and history" (22 December 2025)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2025/12/22/guatemala-24th-bienal-de-arte-paiz-the-world-tree
- Calls the edition "uneven in some venues and projects", often because contemporary work is installed in historic spaces.
- Says Orlan's "Self-Hybridization" series risks "verging on cultural appropriation" and could have offered more context. This criticises the art, not the institution.
- Describes works on pollution, colonialism and state oppression, including Regina José Galindo's performance as the incarcerated reporter José Rubén Zamora on the plaza in front of Guatemala's supreme court.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the biennial is the Paiz family, through the family foundation that grew out of its retail business Almacenes Paiz. International cooperation donors support the foundation's scholarship and training programmes.
