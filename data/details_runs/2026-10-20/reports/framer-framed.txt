FRAMER FRAMED: Amsterdam, Netherlands
ID: framer-framed
Website: https://framerframed.nl (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art venue for exhibitions on art, visual culture and politics in the former Oostergasfabriek at Oranje-Vrijstaatkade 71 in Amsterdam Oost, with a second site, Framer Framed Noord, at Zuideinde 369 in Amsterdam (framerframed.nl; Ammodo).
- Founded in 2009 as an independent initiative questioning the role of museums in a globalizing society; it began as a travelling project and opened a fixed exhibition space in 2014 at the Tolhuistuin (stated by the space and by Ammodo).
- The name is taken from the work of Trinh T. Minh-ha (stated by the space).
- It moved in 2019 into the purification hall of the Oostergasfabriek, about 1,000 square metres on the ground floor, where CBK Oost had been based; the space's news page, quoting Het Parool (June 2019), says it left the Tolhuistuin because the rent was set to rise by about 220%.
- Programme: about ten exhibitions a year on contemporary art, visual culture and politics (Ammodo); Werkplaats Molenwijk (a community space, artist residency and research platform) opened in 2018 and closed permanently on 31 December 2025, and Framer Framed Noord is listed at its Zuideinde 369 site (framerframed.nl).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 18:00; entry is free on a "Pay what you can" basis. Wheelchair accessible; assistance dogs welcome.

FUNDING
Framer Framed: mainly public: the Dutch education and culture ministry, the city's arts fund and the Oost district, plus a lottery fund
- The Ministry of Education, Culture and Science, Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst, Stadsdeel Oost and VriendenLoterij Fonds are named as funders on the space's about page (stated by the space); no amounts or years are given.
- The Council for Culture gave a positive recommendation for Framer Framed in the national basic cultural infrastructure (BIS) for 2025 to 2028; a related headline on the space's site refers to its advice for 2021 to 2024. No amount is stated on the pages read (framerframed.nl).
- Ammodo lists Framer Framed as a "Project supported by Ammodo"; the page gives no amount or years.
- Other income, per the space's foundation page: grants and donations, a Friends network, gifts and bequests, consultancy, rentals and sales. It has been a registered Dutch charity (ANBI) since 1 January 2016.
- Directors Cas Bool and Josien Pieterse have led the executive board since 2019. The supervisory board is Una Henry (chair), Paula Albuquerque (vice-chair), Hans Schamlé, Vinca Kruk, Barbara Wegelin and Amira Gad.
- No reporting found of any link between Framer Framed and "Rabo ArtLab".
- No reporting found of annual reports or accounts on the space's site; only the 2025 ANBI form is linked.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Framer Framed is public and charitable: the Dutch state, the city's arts fund, a city district and a lottery fund, with no corporate or flagged funder found. The weak point is transparency: amounts per funder are not published on the site, and no annual reports are posted. Worth watching is the next round of national and city funding.
