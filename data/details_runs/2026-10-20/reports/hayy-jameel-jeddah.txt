HAYY JAMEEL: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
ID: hayy-jameel-jeddah
Website: https://hayyjameel.org

OVERVIEW
- Arts and creative complex of 17,000 square metres in the Al Muhammadiyah district of Jeddah, developed and managed by Art Jameel (ArtAsiaPacific, hayyjameel.org).
- Opened to the public on 6 December 2021 after an opening first slated for late 2020; the launch marked 75 years of Jameel family philanthropy (Arab News, ArtAsiaPacific, Abdul Latif Jameel press release).
- "Hayy" is the Arabic word for neighbourhood (hayyjameel.org).
- Designed by the studio waiwai, whose principal architects are Wael Al Awar and Kenichi Teramoto; the cinema was designed by Jeddah-based architects Bricklab (Abdul Latif Jameel press release, hayyjameel.org).
- On site: a museum, a children's zone called Hayy Explorers and Hayy Cinema, a 200-seat venue described as Saudi Arabia's first independent audiovisual centre (hayyjameel.org, Arab News).
- Art Jameel was set up to continue the Jameel family's "tradition of supporting its communities"; its chairman and founder is Fady Jameel, whose grandfather Abdul Latif Jameel started the family business in 1945 (hayyjameel.org founder page).
- Open Wednesday to Monday, 2pm to 10pm, closed Tuesday (hayyjameel.org). The opening press release said entrance is free to all.

FUNDING
Hayy Jameel: private family philanthropy: Art Jameel, founded by Fady Jameel
- Art Jameel develops and manages the complex. Fady Jameel is also Deputy President and Vice Chairman, International Operations, at Abdul Latif Jameel, the family business (hayyjameel.org founder page).
- Abdul Latif Jameel's press release says the opening marks 75 years of Jameel family global philanthropy (stated by Abdul Latif Jameel).
- Partners at the opening: the Delfina Foundation of London co-curated the exhibition "Staple" with Art Jameel, and the opening season included "Illuminate: a Noor Riyadh Capsule", light works by 11 Saudi artists (Abdul Latif Jameel press release).

BOTTOM LINE
Hayy Jameel is the project of one family: Art Jameel, founded by Fady Jameel of the Abdul Latif Jameel group, builds and runs it as part of the family's philanthropy. The Delfina Foundation and Noor Riyadh appear as programme partners at the opening.
