DESIGN MUSEUM HOLON: Holon, Israel
ID: design-museum-holon
Website: https://www.dmh.org.il/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Design museum at Pinchas Ayalon 8 in Holon.
- Opened in March 2010 in a building designed by Ron Arad Architects.
- Holon mayor Moti Sasson and the city's managing director Hana Hertsman directed the commission for the building (museum About page).
- On site: galleries and the Aharon Feiner Eden Materials Library, which the entrance ticket includes.
- Liraz Cohen Mordechai is acting chief curator (Ynet).
- Visiting: Monday and Wednesday 10:00 to 16:00, Tuesday 10:00 to 20:00, Thursday and Saturday 10:00 to 18:00, Friday 10:00 to 14:00, closed Sunday; adults ILS 45, Holon residents ILS 32, children under 5 free.

FUNDING
Design Museum Holon: municipal: the Holon municipality
- "The Holon municipality has invested 17 million dollars in the establishment of the Design Museum Holon" (museum About page).
- Ynet reports that the museum's budgets were unchanged at the time of its report and that the municipality still recognises the museum's importance.
- The museum has a Friends of the Museum scheme and a founders list.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Curator Mia Dvash resigned amid tensions and disagreements between Holon's cultural institutions and the municipality (Ynet, Dar Moussafir).
- Those tensions led to the closure or downsizing of several city institutions, among them the Center for Puppetry and the Center for Digital Art, whose head Udi Edelman resigned.
- For the exhibition "The Circle: Designing New Realities", some international designers declined to take part. Cohen Mordechai told Ynet that some creators worried how the international design community would view an exhibition in Israel.
- The museum is funded by the same municipality that cut its sister institutions.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the city's and no funder is objected to. The weak points are the museum's dependence on one municipality that has been closing other cultural institutions, and the reluctance of some foreign designers to show in Israel. Worth watching: the city's next culture budget and who is appointed chief curator.
