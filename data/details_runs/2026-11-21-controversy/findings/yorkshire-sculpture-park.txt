Red flags: 2023 decision not to show Erwin Wurm's Fat Car works
- Erwin Wurm's exhibition at Yorkshire Sculpture Park, running until 28 April 2024, left out his Fat Car series, real cars coated in plastic with a layer of polyester (The Art Newspaper, 3 July 2023).
- Wurm said of the park, "They do not want to show them", and said he believed the word "fat" was the reason (The Art Newspaper, relaying his remarks to the Telegraph).
- The director, Clare Lilley, said "there was real disquiet from members of staff about how they could interpret those works in a positive way to our audience" and "I think that it is right that we're sensitive to the hurt that people would inevitably feel" (The Art Newspaper).
