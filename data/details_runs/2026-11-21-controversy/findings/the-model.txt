ID: the-model

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2009 Michael Keohane, former president of the Yeats Summer School, publicly branded the renaming of the Model Arts and Niland Gallery as "The Model, home of The Niland Collection" a public disgrace. The director, Seamus Kealy, replied that "the real disgrace here is that our neighbour on Castle Street doesn't have his facts right" and called the criticism "ill informed and glib" (Sligo Today, 29 July 2009; read from the web.archive.org copy, as the site is gone).
