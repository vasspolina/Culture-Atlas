MUSEUM OF MODERN BELARUSIAN STATEHOOD: Minsk, Belarus
ID: museum-of-modern-belarusian-statehood
Website: http://msbd.museum.by (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- State museum of Belarusian history since 1990 at 38 Karl Marx Street, entrance 4, in central Minsk (museum website).
- The same address is given by the President's own portal as the seat of the Presidential Property Management Directorate (president.gov.by, archived 2022).
- Opened in 2012 for the 20th anniversary of Belarus's declaration of sovereignty, announced by Svetlana Gavrilova of the Ministry of Culture (Nasha Niva, reposting BelTA, 17 May 2012). The museum dates its exhibition's opening to August 2012.
- Alexander Lukashenko visited on 22 June 2012 and said that in two decades "we have built in fact a new European country"; his portal described the main theme as the country's achievements since independence and "the formation of the presidential state" (president.gov.by).
- The President's portal lists the first presidential decrees, state awards and festival prizes among the exhibits (president.gov.by).
- Six halls: an introductory hall of models of buildings built or rebuilt since independence; state structure, with presidential and state symbols, banknotes, state awards and Heroes of Belarus; the economy, from the Academy of Sciences to petrochemicals and farming; culture and sport, with Olympic medals; religion and foreign policy, with gifts to the President from foreign delegations (museum website).
- Holds about 4,000 items, 800 of them on display, and a conference hall used for temporary shows, "patriotic" events and talks with foreign delegations (museum website).
- The exhibition concept is credited to S. I. Bespansky, O. I. Ladisov, D. G. Shlyakhtin, M. U. Gleb and I. B. Laptyonok (museum website).
- Visiting: Monday to Saturday 10.00 to 19.00, last entry 18.30, closed Sunday (museum website). Sputnik8 lists a ticket of 5 roubles and says visits need booking with passport details five days ahead.

FUNDING
Museum of Modern Belarusian Statehood: state-funded: a branch of the Ministry of Culture's National Historical Museum
- Owner and operator: the state institution National Historical Museum of the Republic of Belarus, of which it is a branch (museum website).
- Collection: assembled from 2010, with part of it placed on deposit by state government bodies (Nasha Niva, reposting BelTA, 2012).

BOTTOM LINE
Only Belarusian state money, with no outside funder. The weak point is its purpose: a display of the presidency as the story of the state.
