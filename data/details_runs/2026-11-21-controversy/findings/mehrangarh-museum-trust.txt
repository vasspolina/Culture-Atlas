ID: mehrangarh-museum-trust

WIDER CONTEXT
- A stampede at the Chamunda Devi temple, which stands within the premises of Mehrangarh Fort, killed 214 people on 30 September 2008, the first day of Navratri, and injured nearly 500 others (The PinkCity Post, 21 January 2017).
- The government set up the Justice Chopra commission to investigate; in January 2017 The PinkCity Post reported that the home department had forwarded the commission's report to the chief minister's office for further action.
- According to The PinkCity Post, the report found that the police, the Mehrangarh Museum Trust, the Ummed Religious Trust and the district administration "were caught unprepared in controlling the crowd", and recommended that a case be registered under section 304-A of the Indian Penal Code against negligent parties.
- The report named VIP treatment given to the then member of parliament Jaswant Singh Bishnoi as one reason the crowd swelled; Bishnoi was quoted as saying he was not given any VIP treatment and was not responsible for the incident (The PinkCity Post).
