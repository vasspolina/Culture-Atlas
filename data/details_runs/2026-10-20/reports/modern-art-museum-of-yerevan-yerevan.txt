MODERN ART MUSEUM OF YEREVAN: Yerevan, Armenia
ID: modern-art-museum-of-yerevan-yerevan
Website: https://mamy.am/

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern art at 7 Mashtots Avenue in central Yerevan (Yerevan municipality museum list).
- Founded in 1972 by the painter Henrik Igityan, who directed it for 37 years, with support from Yerevan's then mayor, G. Hasratyan, and from leading Armenian artists of the 1960s (museum website).
- The museum states that it was the first modern art museum in the former Soviet Union (museum website, Yerevan municipality museum list). Its first exhibition consisted entirely of works by Armenian artists of the 1960s, living in Armenia and the diaspora, who gave their works to the museum (museum website).
- The museum's own history quotes the art historian Alexander Kamensky (1979): the initiators had "neither a building for exhibitions nor funds to buy artists' works".
- Collection: the core is the 1960s generation, including Minas Avetisyan, Ashot Hovhannisyan and Vruyr Galstyan, with works added by 1980s artists such as Sargis Hamalbashyan and Artur and Ararat Sargsyan, and later works by foreign artists (museum website).
- Director: Nune Avetisyan (Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday, 11:00 to 18:00; tickets 1,500 AMD for adults, 1,000 AMD for students, 500 AMD for pupils (Yerevan municipality).

FUNDING
Modern Art Museum of Yerevan: municipal museum with artists' gifts and private donors
- The museum is listed among the museums on the Yerevan municipality's culture pages (Yerevan municipality).
- Of the Soviet years the museum's history says "the government provided no financial support to this cultural institution"; the collection grew from artists' donations (museum website).
- In June 2022 Yerevan Municipality and Eduardo Ernekyan, named as a National Hero of Armenia, supported the 50th anniversary exhibition of more than 300 works from the museum's archive, held in the former Ministry of Foreign Affairs building on Republic Square (Yerevan municipality, 16 June 2022). On the anniversary the museum received Yerevan's golden medal.
- Donors to the collection: benefactor Grigor Muradyan brought works by Emil Kazaz in 2004; the Kapan Copper-Molybdenum Combine donated sculptures by Karen Petrosyan in 2010 (museum website).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum was founded in 1972 on artists' donations and the backing of the Yerevan mayor, and the municipality lists it among its museums and supported its 2022 anniversary exhibition. The other documented money is gifts of artworks from artists, a benefactor and the Kapan Copper-Molybdenum Combine.
