BENGAL FOUNDATION: Dhaka, Bangladesh
ID: bengal-foundation
Website: https://bengalfoundation.org/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit cultural trust headquartered at Bengal Shilpalay in Dhanmondi, Dhaka, that stages exhibitions, concerts and publishing.
- Founded in 1986 by industrialist Abul Khair Litu, its chairman, to "promote and showcase Bangladeshi art on a larger platform" (Wikipedia).
- Bengal Shilpalay began in 2000 with a solo show by Mohammad Kibria and reopened in April 2019 as a renovated, museum-like space with halls named for Quamrul Hassan and Subir Choudhury; Khair said it would no longer run commercial operations (The Daily Star).
- Since 1999 it has run international art camps with nearly 300 artists; it also runs Bengal Publications, the magazine Kali O Kalam, artist documentaries and the Bengal Classical Music Festival, which it calls the largest classical music festival by performers on one stage (stated by the space).
- No reporting found that could be checked on gallery hours, admission or accessibility.

FUNDING
Bengal Foundation: family company money: a private trust funded by Abul Khair Litu's Bengal Group
- Registered as a private trust and "funded entirely by the Bengal Group" (Wikipedia).
- Litu chairs Bengal Group Ltd., co-founded AB Bank, Bangladesh's first private bank, in 1981, and chairs the Bengal Institute for Architecture, Landscapes and Settlements (Bengal Institute).
- No reporting found of the foundation's budget, accounts, outside sponsors or state grants.

Red flags: corruption case against the founder
- The Anti-Corruption Commission approved a charge sheet in July 2024 against Litu, chairman of Bengal Group Limited, for acquiring illegal assets of over Tk 903 million beyond his known income and hiding over Tk 1.24 million in his asset declaration (The Financial Express).
- The ACC case was filed on 23 April 2017; he was questioned on 1 November 2017 over bank embezzlement and tax evasion (The Financial Express).
- The ACC also found Tk 25.26 million in illegal assets beyond the known income of his wife, Mahua Khair (The Financial Express).
- In June 2007 police arrested Litu and a court sent him to Dhaka Central Jail for 30 days (The Daily Star).
- No reporting found of a verdict in the ACC case.

Not related: Bengal Group of Industries, the Dhaka conglomerate behind Bengal Plastic and RTV, founded in 1969 by Morshed Alam, who chairs it. Its Wikipedia page does not mention Litu or the foundation; Litu's company is Bengal Group Ltd.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation runs on one family company's money with no published accounts. The weak point is its founder and funder, charged in 2024 with holding Tk 903 million in assets beyond his known income. Worth watching: the outcome of the ACC case and whether the foundation ever publishes accounts.
