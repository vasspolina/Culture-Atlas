ID: cricoteka-krakow

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2012 the main contractor halted work on Cricoteka's new building on Nadwiślańska Street because it feared for the strength of the structure's supports (Radio Kraków, 13 June 2012).
- The contractor, Chemobudowa, left the site; its director Józef Pilch said "Nie będziemy budować wadliwie skonstruowanej siedziby Cricoteki" ("We will not build a defectively designed Cricoteka headquarters", translated) (Radio Kraków, 15 June 2012).
- The investor, the Marshal's Office, commissioned an expert opinion, and the authors of the design said it has no structural errors (Radio Kraków, 15 June 2012).
- The building, worth over 42 million złoty, had first been due the previous year, when a flood upset the plans, and was by then expected the following year (Radio Kraków, 13 and 15 June 2012).
