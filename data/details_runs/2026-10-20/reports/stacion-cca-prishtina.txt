STACION, CENTER FOR CONTEMPORARY ART PRISHTINA: Pristina, Kosovo
ID: stacion-cca-prishtina
Website: https://stacion.org

OVERVIEW
- Independent contemporary art centre in Prishtina with two spaces, the Stone House and the Boxing Club, and a listed address on Zija Prishtina Street (Stacion; e-flux).
- Established in 2006 by two artists and an architect; Albert Heta and Vala Osmani are named as central to its vision (Stacion).
- Stone House: in the courtyard of the Museum of Ethnography, renovated through a civil-public partnership with the Museum of Kosovo under its director Arber Hadri; Stacion's main working space and exhibition venue since 2006 (Stacion).
- Boxing Club: opened in 2014 as the second space, inaugurated on 12 September 2014 with "Action Boxing Club" by Elana Katz, curated by Albert Heta (Stacion).
- The Museum of American Art in Prishtina, established in 2014, has the Stone House as its permanent home (Stacion).
- Programmes listed on its site include the Summer School as School, the Artists of Tomorrow Award and Cultivating Connections; e-flux says it has had a policy research department since 2014. Stacion has been secretariat of RRIPAK, the Network of Independent Art and Culture Organizations of Kosovo, since 2015 (Stacion).
- Stacion campaigns for a Museum of Contemporary Art in Prishtina, proposed as a public-civil-private partnership in the former Gërmia Shopping Centre, a building saved from demolition by a public campaign (Stacion).

FUNDING
Stacion: project funding: Kosovo ministries, the Prishtina municipality, foundations and local businesses
- The Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports of the Republic of Kosovo supports the 2024 open call for curatorial research projects, "Strengthening Cultural Engagement and Artistic Growth in Kosovo (FAKRRA)", with RRIPAK, Hej Taxi, DZG and x-print (Stacion).
- Museum of American Art in Prishtina (hosted by Stacion): supported by ERSTE Foundation, Trust for Mutual Understanding, the Ministry of Diaspora of the Republic of Kosovo, the Austrian Federal Chancellery, the Ministry of Education of the Republic of Kosovo, Kosova Foundation for Open Society, X-print and DZG (Stacion).
- "Back to the Future" (2009 to 2011): the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports of Kosova, the Directorate for Culture, Youth and Sports of the Municipality of Prishtina, Kosova Foundation for Open Society, Europlakat, Banka Ekonomike, Arda Rei, Dardania Insurance Company, Kosova Art Gallery, KTV, Rrokum and DZG (Stacion).
- Partners for conferences: the National Gallery of Kosovo, which hosted "Altered Identities: Contemporary Art and Nationalism" and "Culture Policies as Crisis Management" (Stacion).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Stacion is project funding: the Ministry of Culture, Youth and Sports supports its 2024 open call, the Prishtina municipality's culture directorate and local firms supported Back to the Future in 2009 to 2011, and ERSTE Foundation and Kosova Foundation for Open Society support the Museum of American Art in Prishtina that it hosts.
