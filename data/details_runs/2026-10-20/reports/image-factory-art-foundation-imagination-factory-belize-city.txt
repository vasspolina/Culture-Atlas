IMAGE FACTORY ART FOUNDATION (IMAGINATION FACTORY): Belize City, Belize
ID: image-factory-art-foundation-imagination-factory-belize-city
Website: https://imagefactorybelize.com

OVERVIEW
- Art foundation and gallery at 91 North Front Street, Belize City, hosting exhibitions by local, regional and international artists, workshops, classes and residencies (Wikipedia, article on Yasser Musa).
- Founded in 1995 by Yasser Musa, a Belizean visual artist, teacher, poet and publisher (Wikipedia; Breaking Belize News, Aaron Humes, "25 years of the Image Factory", 5 June 2020). The 501 Academy page says it started in 1995 and is now called the Imagination Factory, a name used in 2025 for its 30th anniversary.
- Musa is the son of former Belizean prime minister Said Musa (Wikipedia).
- By 2015 the foundation had published over 200 books and worked with over 500 artists, according to Musa (News 5 Belize, Isani Cayetano, "Image Factory Turns Twenty", 26 June 2015).
- In 2019 it hosted the Ka'añaar graphic arts exhibition pairing the Cuban artist Alex Falco Chang with the Belizean artist Carlos "Lito" Quiroz, attended by the Cuban Ambassador Lissette Perez and Embassy Counsel Orestes Hernandez, with thanks to the National Institute of History and Culture and the UBAD Education Foundation (Amandala, 29 June 2019).
- The Library of African and Indigenous Studies hosted the exhibition alongside the Image Factory (Amandala).

FUNDING
Image Factory Art Foundation: founder-led: free space from a former prime minister
- The 501 Academy site, which credits Belize's Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Technology in its footer, states: "He has generously provided free, for thirty years, space for our Image Factory (1995), now the Imagination Factory (2025)" ("Said Musa Project 1" page).
- Art in the Classroom, a project to teach educators to make art from basic materials, was described in 2006 as a joint initiative of Hivos, the Image Factory Art Foundation and the Ministry of Education (News 5 Belize, "We are Belize highlights 25 years of art", 13 September 2006).
- Yasser Musa coordinated the 2019 Ka'añaar exhibition (Amandala).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation was developed by Yasser Musa, and the 501 Academy page says his father, Said Musa, a former prime minister, has provided space for it free for thirty years. In 2006 the Art in the Classroom project was a joint initiative of Hivos, the Image Factory Art Foundation and the Ministry of Education.
