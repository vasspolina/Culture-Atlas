SITE SANTA FE: Santa Fe, USA
ID: site-santa-fe
Website: https://www.sitesantafe.org/en/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art institution at 1606 Paseo de Peralta in Santa Fe's Railyard district (SITE Santa Fe; Metropolis, 2017).
- Established on 1 February 1995 to host a contemporary art biennial; the local gallery owner Laura Carpenter conceived the idea in 1992 and raised more than $1 million in donations (Wikipedia). The first biennial opened on 14 July 1995, curated by Bruce W. Ferguson with Vince Varga (Wikipedia).
- Its flagship exhibition is the SITE SANTA FE International Biennial, listed on its site as the International (SITE Santa Fe).
- The building was a former beer warehouse (Metropolis, 2017). New York architect Richard Gluckman converted the rail side warehouse into 19,000 feet of exhibition space (Wikipedia); SHoP Architects later added an extension that brought the building out to Paseo de Peralta (Metropolis, 2017).
- Programmes: the International, year-round exhibitions of local and international artists, and Creativity & Learning, which includes Young Curators, events, group visits and tours (SITE Santa Fe).
- Visiting: open Monday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 10am to 5pm and Friday 10am to 7pm; closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Admission is free (SITE Santa Fe).

FUNDING
SITE Santa Fe: mainly contributions: donors, a Business Council of local firms and benefit sponsors
- FY2024 revenue was $6,505,608, of which $4,220,967 was contributions; expenses were $4,490,693 (ProPublica Form 990 data). Contributions were $1,846,649 of $2,390,541 revenue in the December 2023 filing and $2,769,026 of $3,204,609 in the year to June 2023.
- Business Council: 31 members give in tiers from $1,000 to $15,000 (Sponsor $1,000, Partner $2,500, Sustainer $5,000, Lead $10,000, Visionary $15,000) to support the Every Day Free Admission Program. Members include Century Bank, Thornburg Investment Management, The Santa Fe New Mexican, Gruet Winery, La Fonda on the Plaza, Hotel Santa Fe Hacienda & Spa, and the galleries Anthony Meier, Charlotte Jackson Fine Art, Zane Bennett Contemporary Art, Aaron Payne Fine Art and K Contemporary (SITE Santa Fe).
- 2026 Annual Benefit sponsorship tiers: Vanguard $25,000, Visionary $15,000, Activator $5,000, Innovator $2,500, with commitments due by 31 March 2026 (SITE Santa Fe).
- The founding campaign raised more than $1 million in donations, about a third of it spent on converting the warehouse (Wikipedia).
- Its own site says it is "generously supported by forward-thinking art and culture-inspired community members."

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind SITE Santa Fe is mainly contributions: they made up $4.2 million of its $6.5 million revenue in FY2024. The named supporters are 31 Business Council members, local galleries, hotels, wineries, Century Bank and Thornburg Investment Management, and benefit sponsors, with the council supporting the Every Day Free Admission Program.
