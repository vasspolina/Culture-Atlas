DARB 1718 CONTEMPORARY ART AND CULTURE CENTER: Cairo, Egypt
ID: darb-1718-contemporary-art-and-culture-center-cairo

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art and culture centre in Fustat, Old Cairo, founded by the artist Moataz Nasr (Nasreldin or Nasr El-Din in some outlets). It offers theatre, art and filmmaking classes and co-created the event "Something Else" with Simon Njami (Moleskine Foundation).
- Nasr conceived the space in 2001 and opened it in 2007 in Fustat; the government closed it in its first year, and it reopened in its current space in 2008 (Moleskine Foundation).
- Named in remembrance of the 1977 "bread riots" (Artmajeur).
- Demolition: its main building was demolished on 6 January 2024 (Artmajeur, Jean Dubreil, 17 January 2024). Bab Masr (Amany Ebrahim, 24 June 2025) says it came after a notification from the district head, to make way for a road, and that the foundation says it was not legally notified and filed case No. 65501 before the Council of State. Nasr said on a TV show that works by 150 international artists were destroyed (Artmajeur).
- The foundation is seeking a new location; companies have offered sites, but it wants a place close to its community (Bab Masr, June 2025).

FUNDING
Darb 1718: non-profit, founder-led: a Moleskine Foundation fund, Arts Collaboratory membership and artist-donated auction sales
- At the 2007 opening, government, businesses and friends supported the space (Moleskine Foundation).
- Moleskine Foundation: Darb 1718 was one of the recipients of the Creativity Pioneers Fund in 2022 (Moleskine Foundation).
- Arts Collaboratory lists Darb 1718 as one of its 25 member organisations. Wikipedia says Arts Collaboratory was established in 2007 by Hivos and DOEN Foundation and is implemented with the Mondriaan Foundation.
- 2025: a 10-day art auction at the Tarathuna Foundation aimed to raise money for a new headquarters. Artists donated works and set the share of each sale they wished to receive, with the rest going to the foundation. The foundation prefers in-kind support such as computers, chairs, musical instruments and help building a new theatre (Bab Masr).

BOTTOM LINE
Darb 1718 is a founder-led non-profit. Its documented support is a Moleskine Foundation Creativity Pioneers Fund grant in 2022, membership in the Arts Collaboratory network and artist-donated auction works in 2025. Its Fustat headquarters was demolished in January 2024.
