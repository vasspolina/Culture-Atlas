ID: esplanade-theatres-on-the-bay

WIDER CONTEXT
- In June 2018 the Esplanade took down a 1989 sketch by Vincent Leow, showing a naked person astride a giant chicken, from an exhibition of his sketchbooks on its third-level Community Wall, after the Facebook group Singaporeans Defending Marriage and Family accused it of "promoting bestiality" and parents lodged written complaints (Coconuts Singapore, 6 June 2018).
- Chief executive Yvonne Tham first asked patrons to "draw their own interpretations of a drawing that is not a realistic rendering", then said "This is solely Esplanade's error of judgement" and that the Esplanade "agreed to not continue displaying the drawing" after discussion with the artist (Coconuts Singapore).
