TASHKEEL: Dubai, UAE
ID: tashkeel-dubai
Website: https://tashkeel.org

OVERVIEW
- Studio, gallery and residency organisation for visual artists and designers in the UAE, with its main site in Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, and a makerspace at Unit 89, Alserkal Avenue, Al Quoz (stated by the space).
- Founded in 2008 by Sheikha Lateefa bint Maktoum bin Rashid Al Maktoum (stated by the space).
- Wikipedia describes the main site as a 2,800 square metre building constructed in 1987, with a gallery, studios for fine art, digital work, 3D, photography, printmaking and textile printing, a darkroom, a library, a lounge and a garden. Wikipedia also lists a traditional house in Al Fahidi with five artist studios and two gallery spaces.
- Programmes: Tanween, a one-year programme in which UAE-based designers develop a UAE-inspired product; Critical Practice, a one-year programme for visual artists ending in a solo presentation; residencies; and the Make Works UAE platform linking creatives to fabricators (stated by the space).
- In 2022 it launched a commercial consultancy, Tasmeem Tashkeel, and it runs Tashkeel Nad Al Sheba, a members-only club for creative professionals (stated by the space).
- Visiting: Tashkeel Nad Al Sheba is open daily from 8am to 10pm; the Alserkal makerspace is open Saturday to Thursday from 10am to 7pm and closed on Fridays and public holidays (stated by the space).

FUNDING
Tashkeel: founded by Sheikha Lateefa bint Maktoum bin Rashid Al Maktoum, with income from its own consulting and retail services and project partners
- Founder: Sheikha Lateefa bint Maktoum bin Rashid Al Maktoum established it in 2008 (Art Dubai listing, tashkeel.org).
- Own income: "All profits from its consulting and retail services are reinvested" in an annual programme of professional development, community engagement, exhibitions and publications (Art Dubai listing, stated by the space).
- Partners named by Wikipedia: Dubai Culture & Arts Authority, Delfina Foundation and Art Dubai for the A.I.R residency, 2011 to 2017; Van Cleef & Arpels for the Middle East Emergent Designer Prize; the Khatt Foundation for Arabic type design courses; Youth xHub, a UAE federal government initiative organised by the Emirates Youth Council; Dundee Contemporary Arts (2019 residency), C.A.P (Kobe Studio Y3) (2016 residency), Weltformat and Pro-Helvetia (2017 exhibition).
- ENOC, Expo 2020 Dubai: Tashkeel and ENOC partnered to present ENOC's "Reimagine Energy" theme through public art. Artists eL Seed and Wissam Shawkat made works on the exterior of ENOC's Expo pavilion, shown from 21 October 2021 to 1 April 2022 (Tashkeel project page; HiDubai Focus, 28 September 2021). ENOC's Group CEO describes it as "a wholly-owned entity of the Government of Dubai" (HiDubai Focus), and Wikipedia describes it as a state-owned energy group in the oil, gas and coal industry.

BOTTOM LINE
Tashkeel was founded in 2008 by Sheikha Lateefa bint Maktoum bin Rashid Al Maktoum and takes income from its own consulting and retail services. Its project partners include Dubai Culture & Arts Authority (2011 to 2017 residency), Van Cleef & Arpels and, for the 2021 to 2022 Expo 2020 public art commission, ENOC, which its CEO describes as wholly owned by the Government of Dubai.
