WIDER CONTEXT
- The government of the autonomous Karakalpakstan region dismissed Marinika Babanazarova, director of the museum since 1984, on 24 August 2015 without specifying a reason (Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty, 25 August 2015).
- Museum staff protested the decision, saying in a statement that they had no doubts about her honesty (Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty, 25 August 2015).
