MUSEO NACIONAL DE HISTORIA NATURAL DE CUBA: Havana, Cuba
ID: museo-nacional-de-historia-natural-de-cuba
Website: http://www.mnhnc.inf.cu/ (checked 2026-11-20; down; archive copies of 2021 and 2022 read)

OVERVIEW
- National natural history museum at Calle Obispo 61, corner of Oficios, on the Plaza de Armas in Old Havana (museum site). English Wikipedia says the building is a former United States embassy.
- The museum's own history dates it to a 1959 project, the Museo Cubano de Ciencias Naturales. The project became the Museo de Ciencias "Felipe Poey" in 1962 to 1963, and in the stage from 1964 to 1980 its exhibitions opened to the public for the first time.
- A restructuring from 1985 to 1991 turned it into the Museo Nacional de Historia Natural. The Capitolio was chosen as its home among the sites considered, and the history page marks a "new seat" stage from 1992 (museum site).
- Spanish Wikipedia lists fossil, mammal, invertebrate and geology exhibits, among them a mounted skeleton of the giant Cuban sloth Megalocnus rodens and a moving replica of the planet.
- The museum's research follows the rules of the national science and technology system under CITMA (museum site).
- Its digital magazine Savia marked its seventh anniversary on 7 February 2018, so it began on 7 February 2011. Before 2011 it was a bulletin of the museum's teaching centre (museum site).
- Visiting: a notice on the museum's site, archived 2022-01-20, announced a restart of public service from Friday to Sunday, 10:00 to 16:00. Reservations were preferred, and walk-in visits were accepted when there was capacity.

FUNDING
Museo Nacional de Historia Natural: mainly public: Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment (CITMA)
- The museum's history page says it came under the Ministry of Culture in the 1985 to 1991 stage and, from the stage that starts in 1992, under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Environment, where it remains.
- English Wikipedia, citing that history page, says the museum had financial problems from 1980 to 1984, before the restructuring.
- On 9 July 2016 in Havana the museum signed a memorandum of understanding with the American Museum of Natural History (AMNH), New York, on research, exhibitions and education; the Cuban Environmental Agency also signed (AMNH press release).
- Under it AMNH presented "¡Cuba!", developed with the Havana museum's staff, from 21 November 2016. AMNH named the Lila Wallace-Reader's Digest Endowment Fund as major funder and the Dalio Ocean Initiative as supporter. That money paid for AMNH's exhibition in New York (AMNH press release).
- In autumn 2015 AMNH's Explore21 programme sent a team of its own and Cuban scientists to Humboldt National Park (AMNH press release).
- "¡Cuba!" later toured: the Exploreum Science Center in Mobile, Alabama, showed it from 1 February to 27 July 2024 (OnCuba).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is paid by the Cuban state through CITMA. Its one documented foreign partner is the American Museum of Natural History, whose exhibition funders paid for work in New York. Worth watching: any published budget or donor data.
