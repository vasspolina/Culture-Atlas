BASIC SPACE: Dublin, Ireland
ID: basic-space-dublin
Website: https://basicspace.ie

OVERVIEW
- Independent, voluntary-led, artist-run organisation in Dublin that runs residencies, exhibitions, talks and educational events with artists and art institutions across Ireland (Basic Space site, Visual Artists Ireland).
- Founded in 2010 by Kari Cahill, Hannah Fitz, Greg Howie and Hugo Byrne (Photo Ireland wiki); Visual Artists Ireland (Siobhán Mooney, 8 September 2023) says four former students founded it during the recession, out of frustration at the lack of exhibition space for emerging artists in Dublin.
- Its first venue was a vacant warehouse behind Vicar Street, with studios and exhibition space. It later became a resident of the Irish Museum of Modern Art's Residency Programme and moved to Eblana House in 2013 through the Vacant Space Scheme; Eblana House closed in April 2016 (Photo Ireland wiki).
- From 2016 it moved around Dublin without a fixed base. Visual Artists Ireland says empty space was "swallowed up by development", so it became a nomadic organisation working with established institutions. In 13 years it had initiated more than 50 projects through 19 co-directors; the co-directors then were Siobhán Mooney, Laura Harvey-Graham and Ella Litton (Visual Artists Ireland).
- Programme: Basic Talks, a talks series held at venues including TU Dublin and NCAD; the BS/AR residency series (2019 and 2020 editions); exhibitions listed from 2017 to 2021, including "On Belonging", "Miraculous Thirst" and "Asylum Archive" (Basic Space site). The Photo Ireland wiki records studios for 47 artists to date.

FUNDING
Basic Space: project-funded: funded project by project, with a talks series and fundraisers
- Visual Artists Ireland (2023) says Basic Space is "currently funded on a project-by-project basis". Its monthly Basic Talks, run with the Hugh Lane Gallery, provide "steady funding".
- Eblana House was secured through the Vacant Space Scheme (Photo Ireland wiki).
- Visual Artists Ireland describes a past table-quiz fundraiser and further fundraising events planned for the autumn of 2023.

BOTTOM LINE
Basic Space is a small artist-run group funded project by project, with its monthly Basic Talks series with the Hugh Lane Gallery, which Visual Artists Ireland says provides steady funding. No flag applies.
