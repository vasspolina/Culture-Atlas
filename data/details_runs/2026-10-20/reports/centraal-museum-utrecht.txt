CENTRAAL MUSEUM: Utrecht, Netherlands
ID: centraal-museum-utrecht
Website: https://www.centraalmuseum.nl

OVERVIEW
- City museum of art, design, fashion and Utrecht history at Agnietenstraat 1, in the medieval Agnietenklooster near the Nicolaaskerkhof; the building is a Rijksmonument (Dutch Wikipedia).
- A civic museum opened in Utrecht's city hall in 1830 and was open to the public from 1838; in 1921 it moved to the Agnietenklooster and merged several Utrecht collections, which gave it its name (Dutch Wikipedia). A 1987 extension (the former stable complex as exhibition space) and a 1999 glass entrance building were followed by a renovation from January 2014 that turned the entrance building into a museum café (Dutch Wikipedia).
- Collection of over 50,000 objects, including about 8,000 fashion items and the largest collection of Rietveld designs (Dutch Wikipedia).
- Also runs the nijntje (Miffy) museum, in the former house of Dick Bruna, and manages the Rietveld Schröderhuis, a UNESCO World Heritage site (Dutch Wikipedia). The three museums belong to the Centraal Museum Foundation (English Wikipedia).
- Bart Rutten is artistic director and Anastasia van Gennip business director (Dutch Wikipedia). In 2023 the three museums had 342,893 visitors, 93,666 of them at the Centraal Museum (English Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 17:00; Friends of the museum enter the museum, the Miffy museum and the Rietveld Schröder House free.

FUNDING
Centraal Museum: public partners, funds and corporate sponsors: Municipality of Utrecht, Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and VriendenLoterij; main sponsor Mercis bv
- Trusted partners: the museum's sponsor page lists the Municipality of Utrecht, the VriendenLoterij (Friends Lottery) and the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science. On 27 May 2020 the municipality named the museum among five Utrecht cultural organisations of national importance with a chance of state emergency support (gemeente Utrecht).
- Main sponsor: Mercis bv, shown on the sponsor page.
- Funds and foundations shown as logos on the sponsor page: Van Baaren Foundation, Mondriaan Fund, VSB Fund, Culture Fund (Cultuurfonds), K.F. Hein Fund, Fentener van Vlissingen Fund, Rembrandt Association, Hartwig Art Foundation, Zabawas, Ammodo, Elise Mathilde Fund, Boellaard Fund, Dioraphte, Zadelhoff Culture Fund and Niemeijer Fund Foundation.
- Centraal Partners: JDE Professional, Rabobank, Sligro Food Group, Norday and Tapart. Centraal Members include Jan Bakers Architects, MO Human Resources, Creative Valley, Heyligers Architects, Hoog Catharijne, Jaarbeurs, KAAN architects, Ploeg Lawyers, Rietmeesters and Bee Improve.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Centraal Museum is the Municipality of Utrecht, the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and the VriendenLoterij as trusted partners, main sponsor Mercis bv, 15 funds and foundations including the Mondriaan Fund and the Cultuurfonds, and corporate partners including JDE Professional, Rabobank and Sligro Food Group.
