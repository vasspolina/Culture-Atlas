KUNSTHALLE TBILISI: Tbilisi, Georgia
ID: kunsthalle-tbilisi-tbilisi
Website: https://kunsthalletbilisi.org (checked 2026-11-20; down; archive copies of 2023 and 2025 read)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art initiative in Tbilisi without a permanent building, led by the curators Irena Popiashvili and Lika Chkuaseli (The Art Newspaper, 16 May 2018). Its own site describes it as a roving exhibition space founded by the two curators, showing Georgian and international art in locations across Tbilisi (archived February 2023).
- Popiashvili left her New York gallery in 2012 to become the first female dean of Georgia's Academy of the Arts, then moved to the Free University, where she developed the kunsthalle (Artforum).
- Launched on 18 May 2018 with two solo shows: Nika Kutateladze in a Soviet studio apartment on Pavlov Street and Angelica Mesiti in a winery on Petriashvili Street (The Art Newspaper, Lorena Muñoz-Alonso, 16 May 2018). The launch ran alongside the first Tbilisi Art Fair, 16 to 20 May 2018 (Artforum).
- The Art Newspaper calls it "the nomadic project Kunsthalle Tbilisi" and describes it as chief among the initiatives supporting education and local artists in the city (23 May 2018).
- In 2023 it showed "Patterns of (In)Security" by Sabine Hornig and Tamuna Chabashvili at Gallery Artbeat (the artist's site).
- "Conspiracy Bar" by the collective Around the Studio ran from 27 September to 7 November 2024 in a rented apartment, with video and film screenings, music and experimental theatre (Kunsthalle Tbilisi site, archived July 2025).
- In 2026 it presented "Prayer of the Outsiders", curated by Popiashvili, at the Tbilisi Peace Cathedral in parallel with the Tbilisi Art Fair of 21 to 24 May: prayer rugs from the mosque of Lambalo beside works by five young artists (reWoven).
- Visiting: for the 2024 exhibition, Thursday to Sunday 14:00 to 19:00 and by appointment.

FUNDING
Kunsthalle Tbilisi: mainly private: unnamed private donors, with a small municipal share (stated by the founder in 2018)
- Popiashvili told Artforum in 2018: "most of our funding is coming from private donors, with only a small fraction of municipal funds."
- She gave that as the reason for having no building: the money should go into new works and support for artists, "not brick-and-mortar maintenance", with the question of a building left for later (Artforum).
- The Art Newspaper (23 May 2018) writes that in Tbilisi "commercial galleries, art fairs and privately funded non-profits" step in, and counts Kunsthalle Tbilisi among them.
- Partners on single projects: the 2018 opening talks were billed with the Goethe-Institut and the Tbilisi Art Fair (Office for Art); Gallery Artbeat hosted the 2023 show in its space (the artist's site); the rug restorers reWoven co-presented the 2026 show (reWoven).

BOTTOM LINE
Kunsthalle Tbilisi runs on private donations with a small municipal share, by its founder's 2018 account, and works in borrowed venues: an apartment, a gallery, a cathedral. The weak point is openness: its donors are unnamed and its website was down in November 2026. Worth watching: who the donors are and how large the municipal share is.
