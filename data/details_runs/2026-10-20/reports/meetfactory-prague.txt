MEETFACTORY: Prague, Czech Republic
ID: meetfactory-prague
Website: https://www.meetfactory.cz

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit international centre for contemporary art at Ke Sklárně 3213/15, Praha 5, in the Smíchov district, in an industrial building between a motorway and a railway line (MeetFactory).
- Founded in 2001 by the artist David Černý. It started in Holešovice, left after the 2002 floods and reopened in 2005 in Smíchov (MeetFactory); Wikipedia says its programmes began after a major reconstruction in 2007.
- Czech Wikipedia says the Smíchov building was a glassworks, built in the 1920s as one of the last buildings of the Josef Inwald firm; Škoda later made diesel engines there and the Czechoslovak State Railways took it over.
- Three galleries (the MeetFactory Gallery, the Kostka Gallery and the Wall Gallery on the side facade), a music hall, a theatre and a residency programme with fifteen studios that host over thirty artists, curators, musicians, directors and writers a year (MeetFactory).
- Board: David Černý, musician David Koller and film and theatre director Alice Nellis (MeetFactory).
- Open from 13:00 to 20:00, plus hours set by the evening programme (MeetFactory).

FUNDING
MeetFactory: public grants and partners: a City of Prague grant of 12 million CZK in 2026, with the Ministry of Culture and the State Culture Fund among the listed partners
- City of Prague: "supported in 2026 by a grant from the City of Prague amounting to 12.000.000 CZK" (MeetFactory).
- MeetFactory's partner page lists as partners of the centre: the City of Prague, the Ministry of Culture, Budvar, the State Culture Fund and Praha 5.
- Exhibition and residency programme partners listed: the Ministry of Culture, the State Culture Fund, Norway Grants, Creative Europe, the National Recovery Plan, EU Next Generation, the French Institute, Czech Centres, the Swiss Embassy, the Cervantes Institute, the Visegrad Fund and Praha 5.
- Music programme partner: EEA Grants. Theatre programme partners: the Ministry of Culture, the State Culture Fund, Praha 5 and the Goethe-Institut.
- Product partners: Quantcom, Benefit Plus and Ecomail. Media partners include Radio 1, Radio Wave, Czech Radio Vltava, GoOut and ArtMap.

BOTTOM LINE
MeetFactory is a non-profit founded by David Černý, with a 2026 City of Prague grant of 12 million CZK. The Ministry of Culture, the State Culture Fund, Creative Europe, Norway Grants and foreign cultural institutes are named as programme partners, and Budvar is listed among its partners.
