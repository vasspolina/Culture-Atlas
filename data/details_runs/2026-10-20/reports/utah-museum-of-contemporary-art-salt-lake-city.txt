UTAH MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Salt Lake City, USA
ID: utah-museum-of-contemporary-art-salt-lake-city
Website: https://utahmoca.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at 20 S. West Temple in downtown Salt Lake City (UMOCA website).
- Operating since 1931 under three names: the Art Barn (1931 to 1958), the Salt Lake Art Center (1959 to 2010) and the Utah Museum of Contemporary Art (2011 to the present) (UMOCA website).
- The FY2024-25 annual report says it was founded by Alta Rawlins Jensen in 1931 as a gathering space for artists during the Great Depression.
- Programmes: exhibitions, an Art Truck (48 visits in FY2024-25), family art Saturdays and workshops, artist residencies, and an annual gala (FY2024-25 annual report).
- FY2024-25 figures from the annual report: 25 exhibitions with 217 participating artists, 63,753 visitors and outreach participants, and 56 K-12 school tours.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Thursday 11:00 to 18:00, Friday 11:00 to 21:00, Saturday 11:00 to 18:00; closed Sunday and Monday. Admission is a suggested $10 donation and visitors can pay less. The museum is ADA accessible, with a ramp, an elevator to the lower galleries, loan wheelchairs and gender-inclusive restrooms.

FUNDING
Utah Museum of Contemporary Art: public grants, foundations, corporate and individual donors
- Revenue was $1,504,862 and expenses $1,215,013 in the fiscal year ending June 2025; contributions were about 79 percent of revenue (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Form 990).
- Supporters at $50,000 and above in FY2024-25: Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Institute of Museum and Library Services, Salt Lake County Arts & Culture, Salt Lake County Zoo, Arts & Parks (ZAP) Program, Utah Division of Arts & Museums, and the Utah State Board of Education (Professional Outreach Program in the Schools) (FY2024-25 annual report).
- $25,000 to $49,999: Arlo Rich Walje and Dr. Laura Nelson, Giving Mangos, Lawrence T. & Janet T. Dee Foundation, Mountain Crane Services LLC, Salt Lake City Arts Council and The Kahlert Foundation.
- $15,000 to $24,999: Art Bridges Foundation, Big D Corporation, Bonnie and Lee Kirkpatrick, George S. and Dolores Doré Eccles Foundation, Lauren and Tyson Call/TLC Impact Fund, Lauren Bald and Cameo Winn/Atypical Design, Richard K. & Shirley S. Hemingway Foundation, Sallie Shatz, Sunroc Corporation and Zions Bank.
- Lower tiers in the same report include the Marriner S. Eccles Foundation, Smith Entertainment Group, Cache Valley Electric, B.W. Bastian Foundation and many individuals.
- Board: the FY2024-25 report lists Rich Walje as President. Walje is the former CEO of Rocky Mountain Power and in 2022 was CEO of RAW-Energy, Inc. (UMOCA announcement, 13 December 2022). The museum's about page now lists Virginia Pearce as President and Walje as President Emeritus.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is a mix of Salt Lake County, Utah and federal grants, foundations, corporate and individual donors, with contributions at 79 percent of revenue in the year ending June 2025 (ProPublica). The top tier in the FY2024-25 report, $50,000 and above, lists the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, the Institute of Museum and Library Services, Salt Lake County and Utah public bodies.
