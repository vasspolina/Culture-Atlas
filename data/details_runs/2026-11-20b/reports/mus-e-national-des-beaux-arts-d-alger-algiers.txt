MUSÉE NATIONAL DES BEAUX-ARTS D'ALGER: Algiers, Algeria
ID: mus-e-national-des-beaux-arts-d-alger-algiers
Website: http://www.musee-beauxarts.dz (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- National museum of fine arts on the wooded hill of the Hamma district of Algiers, facing the Bay of Algiers and the Jardin d'Essai; Algerian state media now call it the Musée public national des Beaux-Arts (museum website, Radio Algérienne).
- It took over the collection of the Algiers municipal museum, which opened in a former barracks on 30 May 1908 and closed in 1928; the new museum was inaugurated on 5 May 1930 and opened to the public in April 1931, as one of the projects of the centenary of French Algeria (Wikipedia, French Wikipedia).
- Building: designed by Paul Guion and begun in 1928, with furniture by Louis Fernez; the museum gives more than 4,000 square metres of built space, as much again in gardens, and three exhibition levels (Wikipedia, museum website).
- Collection: about 8,000 works, including about 800 sculptures and a numismatic collection, from Delacroix, Monet and Matisse to Baya, Yellès and Racim, with the 1982 Mohamed Racim bequest of 61 works (French Wikipedia).
- The museum also holds the "Art et révolution" fund given by artists from around the world at independence, and since 2007 two rooms of orientalist art named after Mohamed Temmam and Mohamed Louaïl (museum website).
- On 26 November 1961 OAS commandos bombed a Bourdelle statue in the courtyard; about 300 works were moved to the Louvre in 1962, and 157 paintings and 136 drawings came back under a Franco-Algerian agreement of 11 July 1968 (Wikipedia, French Wikipedia).
- Directors have included Jean Alazard (1930 to 1960), Jean de Maisonseul (1962 to 1975) and Malika Dorbani Bouabdellah (until 1994) (French Wikipedia); Dalila Orfali was director in January 2025 (Radio Algérienne).
- Recent shows: Italian painting from the collection in January 2025, an M'hamed Issiakhem commemoration in December 2025, and the calligraphy, illumination and miniature show "L'élévation par le trait" until the end of June 2026 (Radio Algérienne, Horizons).
- In summer 2025 it opened in the evenings under the culture ministry's "Nuit des Musées" (Afrik.com).

FUNDING
Musée National des Beaux-Arts d'Alger: public: the Algerian state, through the Ministry of Culture and Arts
- Operator: a national public museum; Culture and Arts Minister Zouhir Ballalou opened its Italian painting exhibition with the Italian culture minister Alessandro Giuli in January 2025 (Radio Algérienne, 23 January 2025).
- Under the Evian accords of March 1962, institutions financed by the colonial administration in Algeria stayed under the control of the Algerian state (Wikipedia).
- Founding money: the building works begun in 1928 were paid from public credits, and credits for the 1930 centenary exhibition bought 498 works in two years (Wikipedia, French Wikipedia).
- Gifts of works: in 2012 Sonatrach, the national oil company, handed over 174 canvases by Juanita Guccione that it had bought from her son in 2004, and BNP Paribas, through its Algerian subsidiary, gave two Étienne Dinet paintings (French Wikipedia).
- In 2013 the Algiers notary Salim Becha gave the culture ministry about 8,000 archaeological pieces and artworks, part of which went to the museum (French Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the Algerian state, with no current corporate or flagged funder.
