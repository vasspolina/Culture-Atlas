PANORAMA 1453 HISTORY MUSEUM: Istanbul, Turkey
ID: panorama-1453-history-museum
Website: https://panoramikmuze.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Panoramic history museum in Topkapı Culture Park, Zeytinburnu, on the site of the former Thrace bus terminal, near the Edirnekapı, Topkapı and Silivrikapı walls and next to the gate where Sultan Mehmed II entered the city in 1453 (museum).
- The Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality began building it in 2005 and completed it in 2008; it opened in 2009 (museum). Prime Minister Recep Tayyip Erdoğan praised it at the opening (Hürriyet Daily News, 2009).
- Its centrepiece is a painting covering a half-sphere 38 metres across: 2,350 square metres of painted surface and a 650 square metre three-dimensional foreground with replica cannons, about 10,000 figures in all (museum).
- Painter and animation director Haşim Vatandaş conceived and coordinated the painting, made with seven other artists over three years (museum).
- An entrance exhibition on the history of the city and Sultan Mehmed II was prepared by Erhan Afyoncu and Coşkun Yılmaz, with architect M. Hilmi Şenalp as consultant and design by Özkul Eren (museum).
- Visiting: open every day except Monday, 08:30 to 16:30; entry is paid by card or İstanbulkart only; the painting platform is not suitable for strollers (museum).

FUNDING
Panorama 1453 History Museum: public: the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality, through its culture company Kültür AŞ
- Operator: Kültür AŞ, the municipality's culture company, which holds the museum's copyright and contact address and also runs Miniatürk and the Şerefiye Cistern (museum).
- Construction: built by the municipality as a 2.1 million lira investment (Hürriyet Daily News, 2009).
- Tickets: at opening, entry cost 5 lira, 3 lira for students and 10 euros for tourists (Hürriyet Daily News, 2009).
- Partner: the "Dream of Sultan Mehmed" projection mapping show was made by Kültür AŞ with TUCE Investment (museum).

Red flags: 2025 corruption investigation reaching Kültür AŞ
- On 20 May 2025, in the third wave of the corruption investigation into the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality's advertising tenders, 22 people were detained, among them the municipality's press and public relations head, Taner Çetin (NTV, 20 May 2025).
- Unverified: Çetin organised tenders for companies close to him and took bribes from the winners, and, citing information from MASAK, the financial crimes investigation board, payments ran through firms that won work from Medya AŞ and Kültür AŞ, with money going on to other staff of the two companies. These are the prosecutors' allegations as NTV reported them.
- On 11 November 2025 the Istanbul Chief Public Prosecutor's Office sent its indictment in the municipality investigation to the 40th High Criminal Court, naming 402 suspects, 105 of them in detention (DHA, 11 November 2025).
- Unverified: income from irregular Kültür AŞ tenders went to the "system" the prosecutors say the accused built. This is the indictment's allegation as Memurlar.net reported it on 11 November 2025.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism is academic: the museum as an instrument of the AKP era's Ottoman and Islamic history.
1. Contemporary Southeastern Europe (University of Graz journal): Lorenzo Posocco, "Nationalism, Politics, and Museums in Turkey under the Justice and Development Party (AKP): The Case of the Panorama Museum 1453" (2018)
URL: https://www.contemporarysee.org/nationalism-politics-and-museums-in-turkey-under-the-justice-and-development-party-akp-the-case-of-the-panorama-museum-1453
- Argues that museums built under the AKP focus on Ottoman and Islamic heritage and less on the Greek, Roman, Byzantine and Kemalist traditions.
- Sets out to trace the material links between the government, the artists and historians, and the new museums that exhibit this history.
- Only the abstract was read, not the full article.

BOTTOM LINE
Municipal money with no flagged sponsor: clean. The weak point is the operator's place in an undecided criminal case.
