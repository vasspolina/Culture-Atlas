Red flags: 2025 Mexican competition fine on FEMSA's OXXO
- Mexico's Instituto Federal de Telecomunicaciones (IFT) concluded that Telcel, OXXO and IMMEX coordinated to block AT&T from selling SIM cards in OXXO stores from 12 January 2021 to 24 January 2024, a refusal to deal in breach of Article 10 of the Federal Economic Competition Law, and fined Telcel 1,782.6 million pesos and OXXO and its subsidiary 19.5 million pesos each (Mobile Time Latinoamérica, Aline Sarmiento, 14 July 2025).
- FEMSA said: "no coincidimos con los fundamentos de dicha resolución, ya que no refleja la realidad de nuestro modelo de negocio" (we do not agree with the grounds of that resolution, as it does not reflect the reality of our business model, translated), and said it would challenge the decision in court (Mobile Time Latinoamérica).
- The connection to the collection: Mobile Time Latinoamérica describes OXXO as owned by FEMSA and IMMEX as a FEMSA group company; CIMAM states that "Colección FEMSA is part of Fomento Económico Mexicano, S.A." and has FEMSA's backing.

Red flags: 2023 Chilean competition settlement over OXXO's purchase of OK Market
- Cadena Comercial Andina, the FEMSA company behind OXXO in Chile, agreed to pay 500 annual tax units (about 380 million Chilean pesos) to the state in a partial settlement with the Fiscalía Nacional Económica, approved by the Tribunal de Defensa de la Libre Competencia, after acknowledging that it "no envió dentro del plazo las cartas de renuncia de algunos contratos" (did not send within the deadline the resignation letters for some contracts, translated), a mitigation measure set for its purchase of the OK Market chain (The Clinic, 1 August 2023).
- A second charge by the Fiscalía, over the delivery of false information, was still pending at that date; the company called the Fiscalía's action "excesiva" (excessive, translated) (The Clinic).
- The connection to the collection: The Clinic names Cadena Comercial Andina as the company of Mexico's FEMSA, of which the collection is part (CIMAM).
