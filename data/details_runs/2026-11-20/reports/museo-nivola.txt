MUSEO NIVOLA: Orani, Italy
ID: museo-nivola
Website: https://www.museonivola.it (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of the life and work of the sculptor Costantino Nivola (Orani 1911 to East Hampton 1988), at Via Gonare 2, Orani, in the province of Nuoro, Sardinia (museum website).
- Opened in 1995 in the town's former wash-house, restored by the architects Peter Chermayeff and Umberto Floris (Italian Wikipedia).
- The site grew in stages along the hill by the Su Càntaru spring: a pavilion by Chermayeff for the sandcasts in 2004, a park in 2009, and in 2012 a third, partly underground building after an international competition won by Gianfranco Crisci (Italian Wikipedia; museum website).
- The collection holds more than 200 works, first selected by Nivola's widow, Ruth Guggenheim; sculptures, paintings, drawings and sandcasts are shown in rotation (Italian Wikipedia; museum website). The museum had 11,019 visitors in 2022 (Italian Wikipedia).
- The Fondazione Costantino Nivola runs the museum and its park. Artribune reported on 19 August 2025 that Roberto Concas had succeeded Giuliana Altea, president from 2015 to 2025; the foundation's page, last updated on 9 February 2026, names Giuseppe Matteo Pirisi as president.
- The director is Chiara Gatti, appointed in February 2026 after Luca Cheri, director until 2025 (Artribune, 10 February 2026; museum website).
- Current season: the museum is closed from 9 November 2026 for a new display and reopens on 28 November; the exhibitions "Munari e Regina. Le affinità concrete" and "Ruth Guggenheim Nivola, Sculture leggere" run from 29 November 2026 to 21 March 2027. Works from the collection are going to the Triennale in Milan for "Costantino Nivola. Scolpire il vento", curated by Cecilia Alemani (museum website).
- Visiting: open 10:30 to 19:30, closed on Wednesdays. Tickets cost 7 euros, or 5 euros reduced; entry is free for under-18s, visitors with disabilities and a companion, and residents of Orani. A lift reaches the permanent collection (museum website).

FUNDING
Museo Nivola: mainly public: the Sardinian Region and the Comune di Orani, through the Fondazione Costantino Nivola, with the Fondazione di Sardegna as main partner
- Founders: the foundation was set up in 1990 by the Regione Sardegna, the Comune di Orani and the artist's family under Sardinian Regional Law 35 of 31 July 1990; the deed was signed in 1991 by Ruth Guggenheim Nivola, the mayor of Orani, Tonino Rocca, and Giovanni Dettori for the Region (foundation page).
- The 1990 law gave the Comune di Orani 1.5 billion lire to buy and adapt the buildings, acquire Nivola's works and papers and buy land for a public park, and gave the foundation 150 million lire a year for its management and 100 million lire a year for a Costantino Nivola sculpture prize (text of the law, Edizioni Europee).
- The law ties the money to stable public management of the museum and park and to a yearly report to Orani and the Region (text of the law).
- Main partner: the Fondazione di Sardegna (Artribune, 19 August 2025).
- Own income: tickets, a shop whose sales support the museum's activities, and donations by PayPal or bank transfer (museum website).
- Accounts: the foundation posts its final accounts for each year from 2015 to 2025 under "Amministrazione trasparente" on its website.
- 2015 crisis: Altea took over when the foundation was in serious crisis and close to closing (L'Unione Sarda, 23 June 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is paid for mainly with Sardinian regional and municipal money through a foundation the Region, the town and the Nivola family set up by law in 1990, with the Fondazione di Sardegna as its main partner. Worth watching: the changes of president and director since 2025 and the new display opening on 28 November 2026.
