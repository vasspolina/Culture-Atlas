CENTRALE FIES: Dro, Trentino, Italy
ID: centrale-fies-dro
Website: https://www.centralefies.it

OVERVIEW
- Research centre for contemporary performance practices at Loc. Fies 1, Dro, in the Trentino province (Luoghi del contemporaneo, Italian Ministry of Culture).
- Run by the cooperative Il Gaviale società cooperativa, via Gramsci 13, Dro, named as operator in the footer of the centre's website. The project was launched in 1999 by Barbara Boninsegna and Dino Sommadossi together with Il Gaviale, building on the Drodesera festival, which began in 1980 (Centrale Fies).
- Housed in an early twentieth-century hydroelectric plant, partly still active, owned by Hydro Dolomiti Energia (Lampoon Magazine, Eugenia Pacelli, 18 September 2024). The centre says it was among the first in Italy to repurpose industrial archaeology for artistic and cultural use.
- Programme: artistic research, residencies, productions and co-productions (the centre cites more than 150), live performances, exhibitions, talks and training. Archive and library; event room rental.
- Fies Core, a cultural hub in the plant, links culture with tourism, agriculture, design and education.
- Long-term European partnerships include the APAP network (since 2011) and FIT Festivals In Transition (since 2015), stated by the centre.

FUNDING
Centrale Fies: public and private by the centre's own account: Autonomous Province of Trento, Autonomous Region of Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol, Ministry of Culture, Municipality of Dro, EU Culture Programme, Hydro Dolomiti Energia, Cassa Rurale Alto Garda and Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Trento e Rovereto
- Stated by the centre: it combines public and private contributions in a "modello di sostenibilità ibrido" (translation: hybrid sustainability model). Its About page names Hydro Dolomiti Energia, the Autonomous Province of Trento, the Autonomous Region of Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol, the Municipality of Dro, the Ministry of Culture, Cassa Rurale Alto Garda, Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Trento e Rovereto, APAP and the European Union Culture Programme.
- Hydro Dolomiti Energia is both the owner of the plant (Lampoon Magazine) and a named supporter. The Dolomiti Energia group page that its web address leads to calls hydroelectric production its "marchio di fabbrica" (translation: trademark) and states 1.5 GW of installed hydroelectric capacity.
- The Trentino culture portal says the Drodesera Fies festival has animated the structure through a partnership with Enel; the page is undated.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Centrale Fies is a mix of provincial, regional, national and European public funding, Cassa Rurale Alto Garda and a bank foundation, and Hydro Dolomiti Energia, a hydroelectric producer that owns the plant the centre works in. The operator is the cooperative Il Gaviale.
