LUNDS KONSTHALL: Lund, Sweden
ID: lunds-konsthall-lund
Website: https://www.lundskonsthall.se (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal art hall at Mårtenstorget 3 in Lund, about a 10-minute walk from Lund Central Station; the site signs itself "Lunds konsthall - en del av Lunds kommun" (Lunds konsthall, a part of Lund municipality; translation) (Lunds konsthall website).
- Opened on 21 September 1957 with the exhibition "Svensk nutidskonst"; it mainly shows Swedish and Nordic contemporary art, with one or more international shows a year (Swedish Wikipedia).
- Building: designed by the Lund architect Klas Anshelm, with alternating high and low rooms, separate spaces for sculpture and large pictures, and open balconies (Swedish Wikipedia). The English history page gives about 650 square metres on two floors around an open atrium and some 50,000 visitors a year (Lunds konsthall website).
- Programme: it also commissions and manages Lund's public art, "Lunds konst", and holds the municipal art collection (Lunds konsthall website).
- Staff: Åsa Nacking heads Lunds konst, the exhibitions, public art and the municipal collection, and Andreas Nilsson is artistic director (Lunds konsthall website, staff page). Swedish Wikipedia names Nacking as head of the hall.
- Season: "Carla Zaccagnini. mahi-mahi-effekten", which the hall calls the artist's largest solo show in Europe so far, runs from 19 September to 29 November 2026, followed by the Vintersalong of Skåne-based artists from 12 December (Lunds konsthall website).
- Visiting: free admission; open Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 17:00 and Thursday 12:00 to 20:00, closed Monday; there is a lift, an accessible toilet and a wheelchair to borrow (Lunds konsthall website).

FUNDING
Lunds Konsthall: municipal: Lund municipality
- Owner and operator: Lund municipality; the hall is part of the municipal administration and its staff use municipal addresses (Lunds konsthall website). Swedish Wikipedia names Lund municipality as its founder.
- Building: Gamla Sparbanken, a savings bank, paid for the building through a donation to the city of Lund; the bank had decided in 1931 to set up a foundation for a concert hall and art salon in Lund (Swedish Wikipedia).
- Budget pressure in 2015: Kunstkritikk reported that a conservative minority in Lund, with Sweden Democrat support, sought to save 3 million kronor from the culture and leisure board's budget by targeting the hall, and that the hall was ordered to repay its 2014 deficit in 2015 (Kunstkritikk, 17 December 2015).
- Friends: Lunds konsthalls vänner, an independent association founded in 1992, supports the hall; membership is 250 kronor a year and the association funds a grant for a young artist (Lunds konsthall website).
- Public art: new commissions are paid from one percent of the cost of building or renovating public facilities (Lunds konsthall website).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Three pieces, from 2015 and 2016, all aimed at the municipal board's plan to "reconstruct" the hall; they defend the hall and its director.
1. Kunstkritikk: Frida Sandström, "The End of Lund's Konsthall as We Know It" (2015)
URL: https://kunstkritikk.com/lunds-konsthall-to-be-discontinued/
- Reports that director Åsa Nacking called the decision a shutdown "without discussion"; board vice chairman Peter Bergwall (Green Party) denied it was a closure.
- Quotes Anders Kreuger, a former deputy director, and the artist Matts Leiderstam, who called the decision another attempt to get rid of Nacking.
2. e-flux: "An open letter in support of Lunds konsthall" (2015)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/28536/an-open-letter-in-support-of-lunds-konsthall/
- Museum directors and university professors, first published in Sydsvenska Dagbladet, called the decision "hurried" and said the City of Lund "would commit a serious error".
- Linked it to the government's October 2015 report on museums, which proposed keeping museums "at an arm's length" from political power.
3. Kunstkritikk: Anders Kreuger, "Lund's Konsthall: Time and Again" (2016)
URL: https://kunstkritikk.com/lunds-konsthall-time-and-again/
- Argues the conflict is political, not about Nacking's competence, and calls her a "seasoned institutional practitioner".
- Reports that the politicians denied ever wanting to close the hall and decided to hire an outside consultant.

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 10 December 2015 Lund's culture and leisure board decided to close the hall with the aim of reopening it later in reconstructed form (Swedish Wikipedia). The board said the hall would be adapted to "the present and future Lund" (Kunstkritikk).
- Board chairman Joakim Friberg (Social Democrat) said the reconstruction was unrelated to the savings and followed a council decision of 26 November 2015 (Kunstkritikk).
- The protest was aimed at the hall's own owner, Lund municipality; the hall stayed open and Nacking still leads its art programme.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: municipal money in a building a savings bank gave the city. The weak point is political: the hall's budget and mission depend on the local board, as the 2015 dispute showed.
