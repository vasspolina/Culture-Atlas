21ST CENTURY MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Kanazawa, Japan
ID: 21st-century-museum-of-contemporary-art
Website: https://www.kanazawa21.jp/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- City museum of contemporary art in central Kanazawa; ground was broken in April 2002 and it opened on 9 October 2004 (museum history page).
- Building: a round steel and reinforced concrete structure 113 metres across, two storeys above ground and two below, designed by Kazuyo Sejima and Ryue Nishizawa of SANAA (museum architectural data).
- Collection: mainly works made since 1980, earlier works since 1900 that explain them, and works tied to Kanazawa; more than 2,900 works by the designer Awazu Kiyoshi came by bequest in 2006 (museum website).
- Permanent works include Leandro Erlich's "The Swimming Pool" (Bijutsu Techo, February 2025); there are also an art library, Theater 21, a museum shop and a cafe-restaurant (museum website; Annual Report; museum.or.jp).
- Director: Washida Meruro since 1 April 2025, the museum's fifth, after Hasegawa Yuko, director from April 2021 (Bijutsu Techo, 25 March 2025).
- Visitors: more than 1.97 million in the 2023 financial year (Tokyo Art Beat, 26 February 2025).
- Exhibitions in 2025: "Layers of Accumulated Time" (29 April to 28 September 2025) drew 216,837 visitors; an Otobong Nkanga project and a show for the Noto Peninsula after the 2024 earthquake also ran (Annual Report 2025 to 2026).
- Closure: the museum is to close from May 2027 to about March 2028 for building repairs, new air conditioning and electrical systems and repair of damage from the 2024 Noto earthquake, when ceiling glass fell in the galleries (Tokyo Art Beat, 26 February 2025); "From Today to Tomorrow", its last special exhibition before the closure, runs from 31 October 2026 to 18 April 2027 (Internet Museum).
- Visiting: entering the building is free; exhibition tickets vary, for example 1,200 yen for adults in 2025. The exhibition zone opens 10:00 to 18:00, to 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays, and closes on Mondays and over the New Year (museum website; Annual Report).

FUNDING
21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art: mainly public: the city of Kanazawa, with corporate members and show sponsors
- Owner and operator: a Kanazawa city museum, whose repairs the city decides (Bijutsu Techo, February 2025), run by the public interest foundation Kanazawa Art Promotion and Development Foundation (Annual Report).
- Sustain membership: annual dues per unit are 500,000 yen (Platinum), 300,000 yen (Gold) and 50,000 yen (Standard) for companies, and 200,000 or 50,000 yen for individuals; they count as tax-deductible donations to the foundation (museum website).
- Members: Platinum, My Book Service; Gold, Kanazawa Energy, Impress Art Business Division, Fukumitsuya and Dimple; Standard, about 100 companies, bodies and individuals, among them Hokkoku Bank, the Kanazawa Chamber of Commerce, Nikkiso, Yokogawa Electric's Kanazawa office, Takenaka Corporation's Hokuriku office, Iwatani Central Hokuriku and the SANAA office (museum website).
- Show backers in 2025: grants from the Japan Arts Fund; sponsorship from OMO5 Kanazawa Katamachi by Hoshino Resorts; support from the Hokkoku Shimbun; co-organisation by the Japan Arts Council and the Agency for Cultural Affairs; support from Hermès Japan and ESTEC Holdings for the Nkanga project; a grant from the Hong Kong Arts Development Council (Annual Report 2025 to 2026).

Red flags: gas utility Kanazawa Energy among Gold sustaining members (2026 list)
- Kanazawa Energy is a Gold member at 300,000 yen a year per unit on the members page checked in November 2026 (museum website).
- It makes, supplies and sells city gas, runs five hydropower plants and sells electricity; it was founded on 13 May 2021 and took over the city's gas and hydropower businesses on 1 April 2022 (Kanazawa Energy company profile, 2025; company website).
- Its shareholders are Hokuriku Electric Power 48.0 percent, Toho Gas 43.0 percent, the city of Kanazawa 3.0 percent, Hokkoku Bank and the Hokkoku Shimbun 2.0 percent each, and Matsumura Bussan and Komatsu Gas 1.0 percent each (Kanazawa Energy company profile, 2025).
- Iwatani Central Hokuriku, a Standard member at 50,000 yen a unit, is an LP gas retailer based in Nonoichi, Ishikawa (Be Connect company directory).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism; the one critical piece dates from the opening year and concerns the building and the museum's idea of itself.
1. Icon: Kieran Long, "21st Century Museum" (issue 020, February 2005)
URL: https://www.iconeye.com/back-issues/21st-century-museum-icon-020-february-2005
- "The biggest criticism" is that Sejima and Nishizawa "ask us to believe in the building's architectural conceits, rather than making us feel their effects"; with its monochrome colour scheme and long corridors it is like a "highly-appointed hospital".
- Calls the museum's description of itself, appealing to "an undefined and vague common consciousness", "banal".
- Doubts that a collection shaped by consensus will be of "enduring quality" and calls the museum "a curiosity shop"; the same piece calls the architecture "quietly radical".

BOTTOM LINE
Mainly clean city money, with one current fossil-fuel tie in the top tier of sustaining members.
