WIDER CONTEXT
- On 12 December 2014 court officials in Tashkent said three former senior employees of the Tashkent State Arts Museum had been sentenced to prison for fraud: the former senior curator Mirfaizi Usmanov to nine years and three months, and the former head of the restoration lab and a restorer to eight years each (Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty, 12 December 2014).
- They were found guilty of replacing paintings by Soviet artists with copies between 1999 and 2014 and selling the originals to unknown individuals; authorities said they did the same with about 25 paintings from the museum's European art collection. RFE/RL wrote that the verdicts could not be independently verified (Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty, 12 December 2014).
