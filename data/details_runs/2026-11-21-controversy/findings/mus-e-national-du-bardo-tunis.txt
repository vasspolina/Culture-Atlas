Red flags: 2023 objections to the prolonged closure
- In April 2023 the museum had been closed for more than 21 months; planned reopenings around 20 March and on 18 April 2023 had not taken place, no official source had spoken, and the only statements said "tout est prêt depuis un moment, dans l'attente d'un feu vert de la présidence de la République" ("everything has been ready for a while, waiting for a green light from the presidency of the Republic", translated) (Webdo, Hatem Bourial, 17 April 2023).
- In August 2023 Kapitalis wrote that the museum stayed closed although the parliament sharing its site had reopened months earlier, and quoted Hassen Zargouni, head of Sigma Conseil: "La fermeture prolongée du Musée du Bardo n'est ni justifiable ni souhaitable pour nous autres Tunisiens" ("The prolonged closure of the Bardo Museum is neither justifiable nor desirable for us Tunisians", translated). Zargouni rejected security, money and works as explanations and asked whether it was a matter of the authorities' will (Kapitalis, Imed Bahri, 19 August 2023).
- In September 2023 Reuters reported that there had been no firm information about the reopening since the new parliament started work that year (Reuters via Arab News, 5 September 2023).
- At the reopening ceremony on 14 September 2023 the culture minister Hayet Ketat Guermazi said that "contrairement à ce qui a été dit, le musée avait continué de fonctionner" ("contrary to what was said, the museum had continued to operate", translated) and that staff had worked throughout to finish a careful restoration (Webmanagercenter, 14 September 2023).
