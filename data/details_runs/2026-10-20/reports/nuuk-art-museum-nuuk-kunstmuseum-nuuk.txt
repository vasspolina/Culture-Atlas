NUUK ART MUSEUM (NUUK KUNSTMUSEUM): Nuuk, Greenland
ID: nuuk-art-museum-nuuk-kunstmuseum-nuuk
Website: https://www.nuukkunstmuseum.com (checked 2026-10-24; loads normally)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal art museum at Kissarneqqortuunnguaq 5 in Nuuk, described on its own site as Greenland's largest art museum (stated by the museum).
- Founded by the businessman Svend Junge, who opened it as an art museum in 2005; after Junge's death the municipality took over the building, fixtures and collection in 2007 (museum website). Wikipedia gives its inauguration as 22 May 2005 and states it was donated to the citizens of Kommuneqarfik Sermersooq by Svend and Helene Junge.
- Not named for a person.
- Building: a church built in 1958 for the Seventh-day Adventists, designed by Jens Johan Frederiksen. In the late 1990s the oil company NunaOil bought the church and its clinic and made them offices. A temporary exhibition room was added in 2007 (museum website).
- Collection: works by European artists who depicted Greenland, such as Emanuel A. Petersen and Harald Moltke, and by Greenlandic artists such as Pia Arke (Wikipedia). In 2024 it bought the whole "Melting Barricades" work by Inuk Silis Høegh and Asmund Havsteen-Mikkelsen (museum website).
- Visiting (winter hours, 28 September 2026 to 31 May 2027): closed Monday to Wednesday; Thursday 11:00 to 21:00 with free entry; Friday to Sunday 11:00 to 17:00; admission 40 kr otherwise. The site gives no accessibility information.

FUNDING
Nuuk Art Museum: municipal, with a 2024 donation from Augustinus Fonden for one collection purchase
- Kommuneqarfik Sermersooq runs the museum; the museum's contact address is on the municipality's sermersooq.gl domain. No reporting found of its annual budget or operating grant.
- Augustinus Fonden: the museum says a "generous donation from the Augustinusfonden" let it buy the entire "Melting Barricades" collection in 2024, and its homepage speaks of "a large funding" from the foundation (stated by the museum). The exhibition of the work ran 10 October to 30 December 2024. No reporting found of the amount.
- No reporting found of other named donors, sponsors or a board.

Red flags: tobacco money (Augustinus Fonden)
- Augustinus Fonden owns Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, which it uses as the holding company for its investments (Wikipedia).
- Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker held more than 25% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group as of 1 May 2025; C.W. Obel held more than 10%, and the company's investor page lists no other holder above 5% (Scandinavian Tobacco Group).
- The donation was made in 2024, so the funder is current.
