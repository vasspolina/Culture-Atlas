ARAB-HEBREW THEATRE OF JAFFA (AL-SARAYA): Jaffa, Israel
ID: arab-hebrew-theatre-of-jaffa-al-saraya
Website: http://www.jaffatheatre.org.il/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Bilingual theatre in the Old Saraya House at 10 Mifratz Shlomo Promenade in Old Jaffa, a multi-arched building (Wikipedia, Time Out).
- Two companies share it: the Hebrew company Teatron Mekomi (Local Theatre), founded in 1990 by Yigal Ezrati and Gabi Eldor, and the Arab company Al-Saraya, founded in 1998 (Wikipedia).
- The Tel Aviv municipality gave the building to the two companies (Wikipedia).
- Artistic directors named on Wikipedia: Mohammad Bakri, Yigal Ezrati and Gabi Eldor, with Adib Jahshan directing the Arab company; Mahmoud Abu Arisha was the theatre manager in 2024 and 2025 (Arab News).
- Small and large stages; plays in Arabic and Hebrew, four festivals a year including the Yafa Festival of Contemporary Arab Culture; the October to December 2026 programme includes "Endgame", "Titus Andronicus" and "Rami and Bassam: Apeirogon".
- Shows mostly start at 20:30 or 21:00; tickets are sold through outside links, and the site links to an accessibility page without detail on the pages read.

FUNDING
Arab-Hebrew Theatre of Jaffa: partly public: Israel's Ministry of Culture and the Tel Aviv-Yafo municipality
- The Ministry of Culture and the Tel Aviv municipality fund it in part (Wikipedia).
- In 2017 Ezrati put public funding at nearly NIS 900,000, a significant share of the budget (The Jewish Independent).
- The site links to a support page and a "public board" page but names no funders on the pages read.
- No reporting found of private or corporate funders, or of current amounts.

Red flags: state moves to cut funding over programming
- In September 2017 Culture Minister Miri Regev asked the Finance Ministry to cut or stop the theatre's funding under the Nakba Law; the ministry's legal adviser flagged two events, "Notebooks from Prison" and an evening for the poet Dareen Tatour (The Jewish Independent).
- In 2022, before Netflix began streaming the film "Farha" on 1 December, Finance Minister Avigdor Liberman told his office to stop the theatre's funding "in whatever way is necessary" because it planned to screen the film; Culture Minister Hili Tropper called for funding to be reevaluated (Ynet).
- No reporting found of whether either cut took effect.

Red flags: police bans on film screenings
- In August 2024 police barred a screening of Mohammad Bakri's "Jenin, Jenin 2" as incitement and questioned manager Mahmoud Abu Arisha (Arab News).
- In October 2024 police ordered the screening of the documentary "Lyd" cancelled (Arab News).
- In February 2025 the theatre petitioned the High Court of Justice, calling the bans an abuse of power (Arab News). No reporting found of a ruling.

- The space's own director: the theatre is identified with ending the occupation, "not with a party".

BOTTOM LINE
The money is mainly city and state support, which is not a flag in itself. The weak point is that the state funders have repeatedly tried to cut that money, and police have banned screenings, over the theatre's Palestinian programming. Watch the High Court petition and the size of current ministry grants.
