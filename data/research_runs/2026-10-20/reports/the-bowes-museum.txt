THE BOWES MUSEUM – Barnard Castle, UK

OVERVIEW
- Founded by John Bowes and Joséphine Coffin-Chevallier, Countess of Montalbo; built from 1869 in French Second Empire style (Jules Pellechet with John Edward Watson); opened 1892.
- Collection of about 15,000 European fine and decorative art objects, including the Silver Swan automaton and works by El Greco, Goya and Canaletto.
- Registered charity no. 1079639.
