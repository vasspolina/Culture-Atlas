KIN ARTSTUDIO: Kinshasa, DR Congo
ID: kin-artstudio-kinshasa

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run studio and residency in Kinshasa; the Pro Helvetia interview (12 November 2024) writes the name both as Kin ArtStudio and as KinArt Studios, and names Vitshois Mwilambwe Bondo as its founder.
- Founded in 2011 by the visual artist Vitshois Mwilambwe Bondo (Art Africa magazine, 18 January 2017; Atelier Mondial). Bondo himself says Kinstudio began in 2010 as a project to support younger emerging artists, started as a master class in 2012, and found a physical space in 2011 (MOMAA interview, 1 March 2021).
- Bondo describes it as a non-profit with a library and free internet access for research, and says he invites German artists to lead workshops (MOMAA).
- Art Africa (2017) describes working studios, an office, a resident studio, exhibitions, workshops and a mobile gallery project. Atelier Mondial lists five studios of about 50 m² in a 1,500 m² area, a 500 m² room used as wood, painting, digital and photo studios, and a multifunctional exhibition space; it says the organisation recently moved to the former CPA Zaire concession, "a huge building that once housed a textile factory".
- TEXAF describes Kin ArtStudio as a collective of artists housed in the TEXAF concession in Kinshasa and shows its "Young Congo" exhibition of 11 artists curated by Bondo (TEXAF news item, in Dutch).
- Bondo also directs the Congo Biennale: he had the idea in 2013, previewed "Young Congo" in 2017, and the biennale began in 2019 (MOMAA).
- Swiss artist Daniela Brugger held a residency at Kin ArtStudio from August to the end of October 2024, published by Pro Helvetia (Pro Helvetia interview).

FUNDING
Kin ArtStudio: self-funded and sponsor-supported: the founder's art sales, plus sponsors and residency partners
- Bondo says he started self-funding from the money he made from his artwork and sought sponsors, especially for the biennale; he says the government does not support Kinstudio because it is not a priority, and that businesses were willing to help him only if a white man is present (MOMAA).
- Bondo says he works in partnership with Foundation Montresso (MOMAA).
- Pro Helvetia: its Johannesburg office published the Brugger residency interview, which says Kin ArtStudio hosted the residency and quotes that in Kinshasa "there is no such thing as government funding" (Pro Helvetia). Atelier Mondial lists Pro Helvetia Johannesburg among the partner organisations of its Kinshasa location.
- TEXAF: its news item describes Kin ArtStudio as a collective of artists housed in the TEXAF concession in Kinshasa.

BOTTOM LINE
Kin ArtStudio is an artist-run non-profit that Bondo says he first funded from his own art sales, with sponsors sought for the Congo Biennale. He names Foundation Montresso as a partner, Pro Helvetia Johannesburg is listed as a partner organisation of its Kinshasa location, and TEXAF houses the collective. Bondo says the government does not support it; the documented money is private.
