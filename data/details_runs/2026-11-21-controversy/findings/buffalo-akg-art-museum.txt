ID: buffalo-akg-art-museum

Red flags: 2023 donor's posts against a staff union
- In December 2023 the donor Jeffrey Gundlach posted on X that "Unionizers are descending upon the Buffalo AKG Art Museum" and that if the museum closed because of union cost pressures "there will be no jobs at all"; the posts were later deleted (WIVB, 18 December 2023).
- Gundlach wrote: "I am not on the Board, I am not a Trustee" (WIVB).
- Director Janne Sirén said Gundlach is not part of the leadership team, that "his views do not reflect ours", and that the museum would "bargain with the union in good faith" (WIVB).

Red flags: 2025 layoff of 13 union members
- The museum planned to lay off 13 unionized visitor experience workers and hire 11 non-union security workers, effective 3 April 2025; Buffalo AKG Workers United, which had ratified its first contract that December, called it "union busting" (Buffalo Toronto Public Media, 10 March 2025).
- Andrea Harden, director of talent and culture, called it an "operational shift" and said "this is in no way any type of retaliation" (Buffalo Toronto Public Media).
- Workers and community members rallied outside the museum on 7 March 2025; organiser Casey Moore said it was "appalling to see AKG take a page from Elon Musk's playbook" (Hyperallergic, 7 March 2025).
- Unverified: the National Labor Relations Board determined that the layoffs were unlawful retaliation and ordered reinstatement with full back pay, from AKG Workers United News, the union's own news page, 31 May 2026.
