CEAAC (CENTRE EUROPEEN D'ACTIONS ARTISTIQUES CONTEMPORAINES): Strasbourg, France
ID: ceaac-strasbourg
Website: https://ceaac.org/fr/le-ceaac/a-propos/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 7 rue de l'Abreuvoir, Strasbourg, run as an association under the local law of 1908. The Ministry of Culture announced on 1 July 2024 that it awarded the centre the label "centre d'art contemporain d'intérêt national" and names Alice Motard as its director.
- Has worked from its current building since 1995; the centre says it played a driving role in placing artworks in Alsace's public space in its early years.
- The building is the former Neunreiter store: Eugène Neunreiter, founder in 1865 of the glassware and porcelain firm of that name, bought the property in 1878 and set up a sales shop, warehouses and housing. Ferdinand Kalweit was the architect and Adolf Zilly the painter; main building campaigns date from 1898 and 1902, with an extension in 1923 (Mérimée notice).
- The brick main building stands on cast-iron posts and has three levels. The whole site (shop, courtyard, street building and gate) has been a listed historic monument since 26 August 2015, and Éric Gauthier was the architect of the 1995 renovation (Mérimée notice).
- Programme: an average of three exhibitions a year, talks, performances and concerts, off-site projects, and outreach for school, after-school and vulnerable audiences. Residency partners are basis e.V. (Frankfurt), Hangar (Barcelona), Budapest Gallery, IUNO (Rome) and MeetFactory and the Institut français (Prague); a production residency for invited artists has run since 2023.
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 2 pm to 6 pm, closed on public holidays; admission is free; guided tours on the first Saturday and Sunday of each month at 3 pm (e-flux directory).

FUNDING
CEAAC: four named public funders and one private patron: the State (DRAC Grand Est), Région Grand Est, Collectivité européenne d'Alsace and the City of Strasbourg; SAS Neunreiter
- Public funders named on the centre's own page: DRAC Grand Est (the State's regional culture service), Région Grand Est, Collectivité européenne d'Alsace and the City of Strasbourg. The ministry's 2024 announcement thanks the Région Grand Est, the City of Strasbourg and the Collectivité européenne d'Alsace as partners.
- Private patron: SAS Neunreiter, the only sponsor the centre lists. The Mérimée notice lists the building's owner as private property of the Société des établissements de porcelaine et de verrerie Neunreiter, the firm named after Eugène Neunreiter, who founded it in 1865 and built the complex the centre occupies.
- Networks it belongs to: Arts en résidence, BLA_!_, Plan d'Est (Grand Est visual arts) and Tôt ou t'Art (e-flux directory).

BOTTOM LINE
The centre names four public funders: the State, the Grand Est region, the Alsace collectivity and the City of Strasbourg. Its one private patron is SAS Neunreiter, which shares its name with the Neunreiter firm that the Mérimée notice lists as owner of the building the centre occupies.
