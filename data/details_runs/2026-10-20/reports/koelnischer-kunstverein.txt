KÖLNISCHER KUNSTVEREIN: Cologne, Germany
ID: koelnischer-kunstverein
Website: https://koelnischerkunstverein.de

OVERVIEW
- Member-based association for contemporary art at Hahnenstraße 6 in Cologne; one of its rooms is the Riphahnsaal (stated by the Kunstverein).
- Founded on 2 April 1839; founder members included Eberhard von Groote (first president), Johann Maria Farina and Joseph Mathias de Noël. A share cost 5 talers and conferred membership, and early annual exhibitions in the Gürzenich festival hall were selling exhibitions with a members' tombola (stated by the Kunstverein).
- Directors since 1913 named by the Kunstverein: Paul Cassirer (1913), Toni Feldenkirchen and Josef Haubrich (1942 to 1972), Wulf Herzogenrath (1973 to 1989), Marianne Stockebrand, Udo Kittelmann, Kathrin Rhomberg, Moritz Wesseler and Nikola Dietrich (2018 to 2023).
- Its history page lists the exhibition "Happening and Fluxus", organised by Harald Szeemann (stated by the Kunstverein).
- After 1933 it did not toe the Nazi line but did not go into opposition either; in 1935 it was placed, like other German Kunstvereine, under the Reichskammer der bildenden Künste, and its own major shows included "New German Art" (1935) and "The German West" (1939, 1942) (stated by the Kunstverein).
- Board: Dr. Thomas Waldschmidt (chair), Heike van den Valentyn (deputy chair), Ulrike Remde (treasurer), Christian DuMont Schütte (secretary) and six further members; Dr. Wolfgang Strobel is honorary chair.
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 18:00; public guided tours are free (stated by the Kunstverein).

FUNDING
Kölnischer Kunstverein: member-supported association with public project grants: City of Cologne, State of North Rhine-Westphalia and Kunststiftung NRW
- Members: regular and supporting memberships, with Jahresgaben and editions offered exclusively to members; donations are accepted by bank transfer to a Sparkasse KölnBonn account (stated by the Kunstverein).
- Amelie von Wulffen, Jonas Lipps (26 September to 14 December 2025): the exhibition and catalogue were supported by the Ministry of Culture and Science of North Rhine-Westphalia, and the page names the City of Cologne as a supporter too (Kunstverein exhibition page).
- Sean Snyder, "No Apocalypse, Not Now" (2013): supported by Stiftung Kunst und Soziales der Sparda-Bank West (annual partner 2013), Kunststiftung NRW, RheinEnergieStiftung Kultur and the City of Cologne (Kunstverein exhibition page).

BOTTOM LINE
The Kölnischer Kunstverein is an association of members, and its documented outside money is public project funding from the City of Cologne and the State of North Rhine-Westphalia, plus foundation grants such as Kunststiftung NRW (Sean Snyder exhibition, 2013) for individual exhibitions.
