YAN'AN REVOLUTIONARY MEMORIAL HALL: Yan'an, China
ID: yan-an-revolutionary-memorial-hall
Website: http://www.yagmjng.com (checked 2026-11-20; returned a server error)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of the 13 years, from late 1935, when the Communist Party's Central Committee was based in Yan'an, at Wangjiaping on the east bank of the Yan River in Baota district (China Daily, state media).
- Founded on 1 July 1950 as one of the first revolutionary memorials of the People's Republic; its expanded building opened on 28 August 2009 (China Daily).
- The building covers 29,853 square metres, 10,677 of them for exhibitions, on a 287 mu site; a 16 metre bronze statue of Mao Zedong stands on its 38,000 square metre square (Cyberspace Administration of China, China Daily, both state sources).
- The Yan'an revolutionary sites, the hall included, were in the Central Propaganda Department's first hundred national patriotic education demonstration bases in July 1997 (list published on The Paper's government channel).
- The National Cultural Heritage Administration rated it a national first-class museum in May 2008; China Daily lists it as a national base for patriotic, revolutionary tradition and "Yan'an spirit" education and a national anti-corruption education base.
- The main exhibition, "The Great Journey", on the Central Committee's 13 years in Yan'an, shows 2,021 objects and more than 900 photographs; the hall holds over 36,000 objects and draws nearly 4 million visits a year (Cyberspace Administration of China, 2025).
- Xi Jinping took the new Politburo Standing Committee to the exhibition on 27 October 2022, days after the 20th Party Congress, and called Yan'an "a holy site of the Chinese revolution" (CGTN, state media).

FUNDING
Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall: public: a Chinese state memorial hall
- Entry has been free since the 2009 building opened (China Daily).
- The 2009 building was named among the "100 classic and quality projects" for the People's Republic's 60th anniversary (Qilu Net via Tencent News).

BOTTOM LINE
The hall is a Chinese state institution and carries no funder flag. The weak point is purpose: the Party leadership uses it as a shrine to its Yan'an years.
