ID: montgomery-museum-of-fine-arts

Red flags: 2023 to 2025 dispute between the museum's boards and the mayor over control of the museum
- In February 2023 city officials placed the museum's director on administrative leave without consulting the boards, and Mayor Steven Reed appointed Jennifer D. Dobbs, the city's Director of Cultural Affairs, as interim director without consulting the board, leaving the museum without a permanent leader for multiple years (Yellowhammer News, Grayson Everett, 5 February 2025).
- In early 2025 billboards created by the group For Freedoms and bearing the museum's logo went up; the museum said its leadership and governing boards had not approved them, and Reed said in a statement that the billboard should be removed (Yellowhammer News, 5 February 2025).
- On 4 February 2025 the joint board said the conflict with the mayor's office had existed for two years, that "the Mayor's Office has effectively exerted control over the operation of Museum without meaningful board consultation", and that both boards had opposed this; it said the billboard episode could have been prevented if Reed had understood his legal responsibilities (Yellowhammer News, 5 February 2025).
- The city replied that "there is no 'Joint Board' overseeing the MMFA", that the Association "does not have legal oversight of museum operations", that the previous director was removed after an investigation that substantiated serious policy infractions, that the Association board had been signing off on contracts without proper oversight, that museum employees decided on their own to display the billboards, and that "the City will not surrender its legal authority to a nonprofit board" (WVAS, Dan Peck, 10 February 2025).
- In June 2025 the City Council was due to vote on an ordinance and governance agreement restructuring the museum's board. Reed said a lawsuit filed earlier that year naming the city as a defendant prompted the review, and cited Section 94 of the Alabama Constitution; the city's position, backed by an opinion from Attorney General Steve Marshall, that legal governance rests with the city-appointed board drew criticism from some members of the Joint Board (Yellowhammer News, Sherri Blevins, 3 June 2025).
- Reed said: "The museum is not going anywhere" and "It's not about control for us. It's about stewardship" (Yellowhammer News, 3 June 2025).
