BELARUSIAN STATE MUSEUM OF THE HISTORY OF THE GREAT PATRIOTIC WAR: Minsk, Belarus
ID: belarusian-state-museum-of-the-history-of-the-great-patriotic-war-minsk
Website: https://warmuseum.by/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- National war museum at Prospekt Pobediteley 8, Minsk, beside the "Minsk, Hero City" stela in the Pobeda (Victory) museum and park complex (stated by the museum).
- The Communist Party of Belarus decided on 30 September 1943 to create the museum, starting from an exhibition opened in Moscow in November 1942; its first two exhibitions opened in the Trade Union House on Freedom Square on 22 October 1944 (museum history page; EHRI), and the Council of People's Commissars approved its name on 27 October 1944 (museum history page). English Wikipedia gives 25 October 1944 as the opening date.
- It moved to Karl Marx Street 12 in the mid-1960s and then to a new building on Central (now October) Square, which opened in stages in February 1966 and November 1967 (museum history page; EHRI).
- The current building was designed from October 2008 by the architect Viktor Kramarenko; Alexander Lukashenko laid a time capsule on the site on 24 April 2010, and the exhibition opened on 2 July 2014 with Lukashenko and the Russian president Vladimir Putin (museum history page; Attractions Management).
- The building covers more than 15,000 square metres in a half ring on a rise by the obelisk, with a facade designed as a Victory salute and the exhibition on four floors (museum history page). The museum's older site gives 10 halls of 4,200 square metres with more than 8,000 items on show, and holdings of 30 collections with more than 155,000 items.
- Branches: the Old Border military history complex at Stankovo and the memorial complex to internationalist servicemen in Minsk (stated by the museum).
- The director is Rimma Rum (museum administration page).
- The museum calls itself the most popular in Belarus, with about 570,000 visitors a year (stated by the museum).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 19:00, last entry 18:30, closed Monday. Adults 20 roubles (BYN), school pupils 10, students 16, with reduced prices every third Tuesday; free for children under 7, war veterans, disabled visitors, former camp and ghetto prisoners and conscripts. Lifts, ramps, no thresholds in the halls, tactile signs and sign-language tours on request (museum visitor pages).

FUNDING
Belarusian State Museum of the History of the Great Patriotic War: state museum of Belarus
- The museum's own title is «Учреждение «Белорусский государственный музей истории Великой Отечественной войны»», in English the institution Belarusian State Museum of the History of the Great Patriotic War (translation); its site links to the Ministry of Culture and the President's portal.
- Earned income: tickets, a 10-rouble audio guide, a café, a souvenir shop, quests and hourly hire of a 161-seat conference hall; the Victory Hall is offered for civic and patriotic ceremonies of national importance, such as oath-taking and the award of shoulder straps (museum visitor and rental pages).
- Its rental page cites Presidential Decree No. 138 of 16 May 2023 on the free use of state-owned property (museum rental page).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One magazine report on the permanent exhibition, published in a political weekly rather than the art press.
1. The Nation: David Broder, "In Belarus, World War II Is Fresh in the Memory" (2025)
URL: https://www.thenation.com/article/world/world-war-nazi-germany-historical-memory/
- Describes the halls as heavy on military hardware, with Stalin-era footage and painted mannequins recreating wartime photographs, alongside personal objects of partisans.
- Argues that the "anti-Belarusian genocide" theme, protected by a penal article against denial, subsumes Jews and Roma into Belarusian victimhood: the museum has two cabinets on Nazi antisemitism but does not say how large a share of Belarusian dead and partisans were Jewish, and leaves out the Polish partisans.

WIDER CONTEXT
- At the 2014 opening Lukashenko said Belarusians "loathe" those who "adjust the history to match specific political goals and steal the Great Victory", and complained that the West answered the Soviet contribution with "dictate and sanctions" (President of Belarus portal).
- The museum's history page calls the new building a museum project without precedent in the republic's history; the speech above was given at its opening.

BOTTOM LINE
No flagged money: a state museum that also earns from tickets and room hire. The weak point is political, not financial: the state uses the museum to present its account of the war.
