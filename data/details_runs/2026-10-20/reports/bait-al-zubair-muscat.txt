BAIT AL ZUBAIR: Muscat, Oman
ID: bait-al-zubair-muscat
Website: https://www.baitalzubair.com/ (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Private museum of Omani heritage and a cultural foundation on Al Saidiya Street in Old Muscat.
- Opened as a museum in 1998 by Mohammad Al Zubair, Economic Affairs Adviser to Sultan Qaboos in 2008, in a house his father Shaikh Al Zubair Bin Ali built in 1914 (Gulf News); one family business history gives 1988 instead.
- Named for the Al Zubair family, whose private collection of khanjars, weapons, costumes, jewellery and household objects it shows.
- A complex of traditional houses: Bait Al Bagh, Bait Al Dalaleel, Bait Al Oud (opened 26 March 2008), Bait Al Nahdah, a garden with a model Omani village, and Zafran Café; it won the first Sultan Qaboos Award for Architectural Excellence in 1999.
- Gallery Sarah, opened in 2013 in memory of the museum's art adviser Sarah White, shows contemporary art; the foundation also runs a learning centre, courses and events such as a Sufi music festival.
- Open Saturday to Thursday 9:30am to 6pm; OMR 1 for Omanis and GCC nationals, OMR 3 for foreign visitors, free under 12 (Experience Oman). No accessibility details found.

FUNDING
Bait Al Zubair: family-funded: the Al Zubair family, owners of The Zubair Corporation
- The museum grew from and shows the family's private collection, run by Bait Al Zubair Foundation LLC (Gulf News); Experience Oman says it was established in 1998 by the Zubair family.
- The Zubair Corporation, founded in 1967 as Muscat Trading Company by Mohammad Al Zubair after he left Petroleum Development Oman, was renamed in 1973; it ranked 32nd on Forbes Middle East's 2022 list of Arab family businesses, chaired by Rashad Mohammad Al Zubair.
- Its interests span services, tourism, real estate, telecom, energy and education (Forbes Middle East); a 2009 US embassy cable counted about 2,400 staff and 60 firms.
- A 2009 US embassy cable says Mohammad Al Zubair served as Minister of Commerce from 1974 to 1983, then as the Sultan's adviser for economic planning, and that the family's "long-standing personal relationship with Sultan Qaboos has brought them tremendous wealth" (WikiLeaks).
- No reporting found of state grants, a land grant, outside sponsors or published accounts for the museum.

Red flags: oil and gas money
- The Zubair Corporation created Zubair Oil and Gas (Zogas) in the early 1990s; it worked in Abu Dhabi from 1991 and then supplied pipeline and support services to Petroleum Development Oman (Family Business Histories).
- ARA Petroleum, set up in 2014 and described as part of The Zubair Corporation's business, explores for and develops hydrocarbons in Oman and, through ARA Petroleum Tanzania, in Tanzania (ARA Petroleum).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is the Al Zubair family's own foundation and publishes no accounts; the family's money comes from The Zubair Corporation, which includes current oil and gas exploration through ARA Petroleum. The family's closeness to the late Sultan is on record, but no state patronage of the museum is. Worth watching: whether the foundation ever publishes accounts.
