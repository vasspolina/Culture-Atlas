Red flags: 2023 return of a Nazi-looted Schiele drawing
- On 20 September 2023 the Manhattan District Attorney, Alvin L. Bragg, Jr., announced the return of seven Egon Schiele drawings to the family of Fritz Grünbaum, an Austrian-Jewish cabaret performer whose art collection the Nazi regime stole (Manhattan District Attorney's Office, 20 September 2023).
- The Office's Antiquities Trafficking Unit seized the drawings in 2023 from several holders, the Morgan Library among them; the Morgan's work was Schiele's "Self Portrait", valued at $1 million (Manhattan District Attorney's Office, 20 September 2023).
- The Office said the drawings were seized and voluntarily surrendered by the holding institutions and estates after they were presented with evidence that the Nazis stole them (Manhattan District Attorney's Office, 20 September 2023).
