FESTIVAL INTERNACIONAL JAZZ PLAZA: Havana, Cuba
ID: festival-internacional-jazz-plaza
Website: http://festivaljazzplaza.com (checked 2026-11-20; returned a 502 error)

OVERVIEW
- Annual jazz festival in Havana, first held in 1980 and organised by the Instituto Cubano de la Música (EcuRed, state encyclopedia, archive copy of 23 February 2020).
- It grew out of jam sessions at the Casa de la Cultura of the Plaza municipality organised by the musician Bobby Carcassés, whom an EFE text carried by 14ymedio in 2024 calls its founding father.
- Víctor Rodríguez is president of the festival, Roberto Fonseca its artistic director and Indira Fajardo president of the Instituto Cubano de la Música (Prensa Latina, a state agency; festival press note for the 41st edition).
- The 40th edition ended on 2 February 2025 with about 300 shows and guests from the United States, Canada, Brazil, Nigeria and other countries (14ymedio, an independent outlet).
- The 41st edition ran from 25 January to 1 February 2026 in Havana, Santiago de Cuba, Santa Clara and, for the first time, Holguín; Prensa Latina counted more than 1,500 Cuban and foreign artists and reported its closing gala in the Sala Avellaneda of the Teatro Nacional.
- Havana venues listed for 2026 include the Teatro Nacional, the Teatro Martí, the theatre of the Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes, the Casa de la Cultura de Plaza, Fábrica de Arte Cubano and the Basílica Menor de San Francisco de Asís (festival dossier, 2026).
- The 42nd edition is set for 24 to 31 January 2027, dedicated to dance, with 20 stages in Havana, activities in Santiago de Cuba, Holguín, Camagüey, Villa Clara and Matanzas and a proposed sub-venue at the Centro Cultural México-Cuba Benny Moré in Mexico City (teleSUR, state media, October 2026).

FUNDING
Festival Internacional Jazz Plaza: mainly public: Ministry of Culture through the Instituto Cubano de la Música
- The Instituto Cubano de la Música is one of the Ministry of Culture's institutes (EcuRed). EFE, in 14ymedio in January 2024, says the festival has been coordinated by the Centro Nacional de Música Popular, the Instituto and the Ministry of Culture.
- China: the festival's own press note for the 41st edition says it has belonged to the International Alliance of Silk Road Arts Festivals since 2025 "gracias al apoyo de la Embajada de la República Popular China en Cuba" ("thanks to the support of the Embassy of the People's Republic of China in Cuba", our translation). The same note says the festival contacted new collaborators in Mexico, Colombia and Spain.
- The 2026 sponsorship dossier offers brands association with an audience it describes as international tourists, collectors and music industry professionals, many from the United States and Canada (festival dossier).
- Organisers said that by November 2025, 220 international artists and young Cubans living abroad had confirmed for 2026 (Prensa Latina), and that more than 120 US artists had confirmed for 2027 (teleSUR).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One report from an independent outlet critical of the government; it concerns exiled musicians and a cancelled film screening.
1. 14ymedio: José Lassa, "Termina el Festival Jazz Plaza y la noche habanera vuelve a quedar en silencio" (2025)
URL: https://www.14ymedio.com/cartelera-cultural/termina-festival-jazz-plaza-noche_1_1110819.html
- Calls the festival, once more, a festival of the absent: Chucho Valdés, Arturo Sandoval and Paquito D'Rivera, all exiled in the United States, were missing from the Irakere tribute, and Cuban jazz is more alive outside the island than inside.
- Reports that the organisers suspended a screening of Pavel Giroud's documentary "Manteca, mondongo y bacalao con pan"; Giroud, who lives abroad, remarked ironically on the sudden change to the programme.

BOTTOM LINE
The festival's money is Cuban state money, and none of it falls under a funder flag. Its weak points are editorial, not financial. Worth watching: which companies take up the sponsorship offer and what the Chinese embassy's backing involves.
