ID: galerie-rudolfinum-prague

WIDER CONTEXT
- Summer 2009: Vladimír Darjanin, the new director of the Czech Philharmonic, which then ran the gallery, planned to dismiss gallery director Petr Nedoma and faced criticism after it. Nedoma objected that Darjanin had offered free gallery entry with tickets to his own Dvořákova Praha festival without consulting him, since the gallery is state-funded; Darjanin denied any conflict of interest (ČT24, 21 July 2009).
- After strong opposition from the professional community, Nedoma was confirmed in post until the end of 2009. On 20 October 2009 Minister of Culture Václav Riedlbauch decided to move the gallery out of the Philharmonic's administration and under the Uměleckoprůmyslové museum from 1 January 2010 (Novinky/Právo, 21 October 2009).
- Nedoma said: "Pokud by nedošlo k tomuto řešení, dal bych výpověď" ("Had this solution not come about, I would have resigned", translated) (Novinky/Právo).
