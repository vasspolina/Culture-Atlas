CRAC VALPARAÍSO (CENTRO DE RESIDENCIAS DE ARTE CONTEMPORÁNEO): Valparaíso, Chile
ID: crac-valparaiso
Website: https://cracvalparaiso.cl (checked 2026-11-20; down, the domain does not resolve); the older address http://www.cracvalparaiso.org serves spam: an unrelated blog on Chilean art carrying casino and essay-writing advertisements

OVERVIEW
- Collaborative non-profit platform for socio-artistic productions across the city of Valparaíso, with offices at Avenida Prat 798, oficina 574 (stated by the space in its TransArtists listing).
- An independent project with a flexible, autonomous management model, running since 2007 (stated by the space; TransArtists and Dutch Art Institute listings).
- It runs residencies for artists and researchers as city practices: residents hold talks, workshops, performances and seminars with the local community, which feed the centre's archive (stated by the space).
- Residencies last one to three months; artists propose a project directly or answer an open call, at any time, in Spanish or English (stated by the space).
- Focus areas are research residencies, water and coastal work, urban projects and socially engaged practice, across installation, photography, media art, drawing and painting, curating and architecture (stated by the space).
- It also runs a public community archive, a printed publication and a digital publisher, and sets out "to rethink from a Latin American city what public art means in relation to other cities" (stated by the space).
- Residents get a room in the historic district or a similar space; the offices have work tables, wireless internet and a documentary archive (stated by the space).

FUNDING
CRAC Valparaíso: self-sustained non-profit, as stated by the space
- Its listings on TransArtists and the Dutch Art Institute say it "has been self-sustained since 2007" (stated by the space).
- The Mondriaan Fund paid for at least one residency at CRAC: the Dutch Art Institute announced Eva Olthof (DAI, 2011) as "the next Mondriaan-funded resident" (Dutch Art Institute).

BOTTOM LINE
Nothing in CRAC's money is flagged, but the only account of it is the platform's own. The Mondriaan Fund is its one outside funder on record. With its website down and its old domain taken over by gambling spam, the space's current activity is open to question.
