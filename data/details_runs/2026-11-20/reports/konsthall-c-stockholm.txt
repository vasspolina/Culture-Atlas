KONSTHALL C: Stockholm, Sweden
ID: konsthall-c-stockholm
Website: https://konsthallc.se (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit exhibition space in a former communal laundry at Cigarrvägen 14 in Hökarängen, a suburb of southern Stockholm (e-flux listing; e-flux call for applications, 20 May 2023).
- Began in 2004 as a public artwork by the artist Per Hasselberg, made with local community organisations (Creative Time Summit page).
- It describes itself as "an art gallery, public artwork, and an experiment in urban renewal" that has grown from an artist-run project space into "an established contemporary art platform" (e-flux call, 2023).
- Programme: several exhibition spaces, an artist-in-residence platform, a citizens' archive and a network of local artists (e-flux call).
- Kunstkritikk (Hans Carlsson, 5 February 2015) writes that the venue has long focused on Hökarängen, with attention to social planning and the history of the Swedish welfare state.
- Directors named in the press: Karolin Tampere with the artist duo akcg in 2013 to 2014, then Jens Strandberg and Jenny Richards from 2015 (Kunstkritikk, 2015).
- In 2023 Ulrika Flink was artistic director and Erik Annerborn managing director, when a three-year artistic directorship for 2024 to 2026 was advertised (e-flux call).
- Legal form: the association Kulturföreningen Konsthall C (Region Stockholm decisions).
- For 2025 it estimated 26,000 visitors and participants, 21,000 of them in person, 43% aged 25 or under (Region Stockholm decision, 18 December 2024).
- Open Thursday to Sunday, 12:00 to 17:00 (e-flux listing).

FUNDING
Konsthall C: public support: Region Stockholm, the City of Stockholm and the Swedish Arts Council
- The undated page description of its website names the City of Stockholm, the Swedish Arts Council (Kulturrådet), Kulturbryggan, Region Stockholm and Stockholmshem as supporters and ABF Stockholm as partner for its public programme (stated by the space). Region Stockholm's 2024 file also records support from the City of Stockholm, the Swedish Arts Council and sponsors.
- Region Stockholm operating support: SEK 250,000 in 2024, then SEK 300,000 a year for 2025 and 2026, against SEK 350,000 requested; the 2024 file gives a total turnover of SEK 3,048,811 (Region Stockholm culture committee decision of 18 December 2024, KN 2024/925).
- Region Stockholm added an extra payment of SEK 73,896 for 2025 (delegation decision 37/2025 of 16 September 2025, KN 2025/1082). On 17 December 2025 its culture administration proposed confirming SEK 300,000 for 2026 (KN 2025/538).
- City of Stockholm, Farsta district: for 2024 the district administration proposed, for the district committee to decide, an association grant of SEK 30,000 against SEK 50,000 requested, for family viewings, workshops for older people and local partnerships, and SEK 28,000 against SEK 50,000 requested for an autumn-holiday day camp (Farsta district report, 30 January 2024).
- Stockholmshem: Kunstkritikk (2015) reported that Konsthall C's seminars and exhibitions on gentrification took Stockholmshem's project to give artists cheap studio space in Hökarängen as a starting point.

Not related: Bonniers Konsthall, a separate art institution at Torsgatan 19 in Stockholm whose website ties its vision to Jeanette Bonnier. Konsthall C is a different organisation, the association Kulturföreningen Konsthall C in Hökarängen.

BOTTOM LINE
Konsthall C runs on clean local and national public grants; no private funder of note is on record. Worth watching: its tie to Stockholmshem, which supports it and ran the artists' studio project its own programme examined for gentrification.
