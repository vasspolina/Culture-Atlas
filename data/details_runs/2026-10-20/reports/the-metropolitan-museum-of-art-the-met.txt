THE METROPOLITAN MUSEUM OF ART (THE MET): New York, USA
ID: the-metropolitan-museum-of-art-the-met
Website: https://www.metmuseum.org (checked 2026-10-24; repeated fetches returned "too many requests", so its pages could not be read)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 1000 Fifth Avenue on the eastern edge of Central Park, with a second site, The Cloisters (Wikipedia).
- Incorporated on 13 April 1870; the first part of the Fifth Avenue building, designed by Calvert Vaux and Jacob Wrey Mould, went up in 1880 (Wikipedia).
- About 1.5 million works in 17 curatorial departments; 5,727,258 visits in fiscal 2025 (Wikipedia).
- The City of New York owns the building and contributes utilities, heat and part of the guardianship costs.
- Visiting: New York State residents pay what they wish; others have paid a fee since March 2018, $25 at the time (Wikipedia). Opening hours and accessibility could not be confirmed.

FUNDING
The Met: mixed: endowment, private gifts, admissions and city support
- Form 990, year to June 2025 (ProPublica): revenue $907.4M, expenses $502.0M, contributions $356.4M, net assets $5.77B.
- Endowment $3.3B as of 2021 (Wikipedia). Oscar L. Tang and Agnes Hsu-Tang gave $125M in November 2021, the largest gift in the museum's history (Wikipedia).
- David H. Koch gave $65M for the plaza redesign (Hyperallergic, 2014). A US embassy cable dated 19 June 2005 (WikiLeaks) calls Shaykh Nasser of Kuwait an honorary board member; it describes no gift.
- No reporting found of Bloomberg Philanthropies, Tiffany & Co. or Bank of America amounts.

Red flags: Sackler family, opioids
- The Sackler family gave $3.5M toward the Sackler Wing in 1974 (Hyperallergic).
- 10 March 2018: about 100 people from the PAIN Sackler collective, including Nan Goldin, protested at the Temple of Dendur in the Sackler Wing (Hyperallergic, 12 March 2018).
- 15 May 2019: after a board of trustees vote, president Daniel Weiss said the Met would stop accepting gifts from Sacklers tied to Purdue Pharma (The Art Newspaper).
- In December 2021 the Met announced it would remove the Sackler name from locations in the museum (Wikipedia, citing a Bloomberg report of 9 December 2021).

Red flags: Koch Plaza
- September 2014, at the plaza's unveiling: at the end of an Occupy Museums action, three members of The Illuminator were arrested at the Met after projecting "KOCH=CLIMATE CHAOS" on the facade. The protest aimed to symbolically rename the plaza (Hyperallergic, 12 September 2014).

Red flags: 2024 protests citing board members' arms investments
- Wikipedia says several protests at the Met in 2024, during the Israel-Gaza war, cited board members' investments in RTX Corporation, an arms maker. It dates one protest to 24 March 2024 and names no group.
- On 11 March 2024 Met Workers For Palestine, 158 staff, fellows and volunteers, sent director Max Hollein an open letter asking for a public statement; the Hyperallergic report does not mention RTX.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism centres on the museum's donors, in protests and press.
1. Hyperallergic: Benjamin Sutton, "Protesters at Metropolitan Museum Chant 'Shame on Sackler'" (2018)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/431941/protest-metropolitan-museum-sackler-wing-opioid-crisis-nan-goldin/
- Reports the PAIN protest against donors who profited from the opioid crisis; the petition had over 32,000 signatures.
- Jillian Sackler said Arthur Sackler died before OxyContin was developed. The Met declined to comment.
2. Hyperallergic: Julia Friedman, "Three Arrested, Charged in Koch Plaza Protest at Metropolitan Museum" (2014)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/148627/three-arrested-charged-in-koch-plaza-protest-at-metropolitan-museum/
- Protester Kyle Depew called the Met's acceptance of Koch money a "gross combination of big money and low culture".
3. The Art Newspaper: Nancy Kenney, "The Met says it will stop accepting gifts from Sacklers associated with Purdue Pharma" (2019)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2019/05/15/the-met-says-it-will-stop-accepting-gifts-from-sacklers-associated-with-purdue-pharma
- Not criticism: president Daniel Weiss called suspending the gifts "the prudent course of action".

WIDER CONTEXT
- Epstein: ARTnews (paywalled; only a search-result excerpt was read) reports a copy of a $5,000 cheque dated 29 April 2014 from Epstein's Enhanced Education foundation to the Met for the Costume Institute Benefit. No opened page confirms it.
- NBC News (11 July 2019) says Epstein lists the Met as a recipient of contributions from the Jeffrey Epstein Foundation between 2010 and 2012, with no amount. A Met spokesperson said the museum had received no major donations from Epstein or his foundation, only that he bought tickets to two benefits in the early 1990s.

BOTTOM LINE
Most of the Met's money is the endowment, gifts and admissions, with the city owning the building. The weak points are the Sackler and Koch money that protesters targeted and the 2024 protests citing board members' investments. The Epstein items (a 2014 cheque per an ARTnews excerpt, a 2010 to 2012 listing per NBC) are unconfirmed by an opened page. Watch the donor lists and new naming gifts.
