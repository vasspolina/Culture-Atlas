FONDATION H: Antananarivo, Madagascar
ID: fondation-h-antananarivo
Website: https://www.fondation-h.com

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art foundation in the centre of Antananarivo, at the foot of the Queen's Palace and the Prime Minister's palace (The Art Newspaper France, Anaël Pigeat, 23 June 2023).
- Founded in 2017 by the entrepreneur and patron Hassanein Hiridjee, who is its president; Madagascar has recognised it as a public-utility foundation since 2018 (the foundation's website).
- Hiridjee began the collection in 2010; its about 200 works all belong to the foundation (The Art Newspaper France, 2023).
- Building: a restored and enlarged historic building in brick and wood with some metal additions, designed by the German architect Otmar Dodel; works lasted two and a half years (The Art Newspaper France, 2023). The foundation's website gives the building as 2,200 m2 (The Art Newspaper France: 2,000 m2), powered by rooftop solar panels and ventilated naturally without air conditioning.
- The new space opened in April 2023 (Start Journal, 8 December 2023).
- On site: galleries, a monumental hall, a library, mediation spaces, a courtyard and a garden (The Art Newspaper France, 2023), and a café (the foundation's website).
- Programmes, all free: the Prix Paritana for young Malagasy artists since 2017, artist residencies in Antananarivo and Paris, the children's Hay workshops since 2021, the Ainga training for artists since 2022, and Saturday morning mediation (the foundation's website).
- The Paris exhibition space in the Marais, opened on 3 September 2020 with the Cité internationale des arts, closes in September 2026, according to the foundation as quoted by Billionaires.Africa (21 July 2026).
- Entry is free; open Tuesday to Saturday 09:00 to 17:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 16:00 (the foundation's website). It drew more than 110,000 visitors in its first ten months (The Art Newspaper, Georgina Adam, 18 June 2024).

FUNDING
Fondation H: private: founded and presided over by Axian chief executive Hassanein Hiridjee, with free entry
- Hiridjee is chief executive of the Axian Group, a pan-African group in telecommunications, energy, real estate and financial services (Billionaires.Africa, 2026; Start Journal, 2023). The Art Newspaper calls him an heir to Axian.
- The foundation is a recognised public-utility foundation with free programmes (the foundation's website).
- Hiridjee says the Axian Foundation, the group's separate philanthropy, has built 200 schools in cooperation with the government over the past 15 years (The Art Newspaper, 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
Fondation H is a foundation set up and presided over by Hassanein Hiridjee, chief executive of the Axian Group, and it holds his donated collection. Its programmes and entry are free. The money behind it is tied to its founder, whose group works in telecommunications, energy, real estate and finance.
