KASKADENKONDENSATOR (KASKO): Basel, Switzerland
ID: kaskadenkondensator-basel
Website: https://kasko.ch

OVERVIEW
- Self-managed project space for contemporary art, performance and art mediation in the Werkraum Warteck pp, in the Wettsteinquartier near the Rhine in Basel (Kasko info page).
- Founded in 1994 as an association; the founding members included the artists Renée Levi, Renate Buser and Marianne Schuppe; the venue is in the Werkraum Warteck pp, a former brewery (German Wikipedia).
- Space: it operates within the Werkraum Warteck pp. The Kasko info page says the space was being renovated in 2021 and the website relaunched.
- Programme: performance, experimental and process-based art, exhibitions, exchange projects and art mediation, with a network of independent art spaces in Switzerland and abroad (German Wikipedia). From 2008 to 2020 Chris Regn organised the programme, and since 2021 a team led by Lysann König, with a core team of about 12 artists, performers and mediators, has run it.
- Accessibility: the space is wheelchair accessible and describes itself as feminist, with flat hierarchies and an aim to be barrier-free (Kasko info page).

FUNDING
Kaskadenkondensator (Kasko): costs covered by state contributions from the Canton of Basel-Stadt, foundations and occasional rental income; supporters GGG Basel and Christoph Merian Stiftung
- The Kasko info page states that it covers its costs through state contributions from the Canton of Basel-Stadt, contributions from other foundations and occasional rental income.
- Supporters named for 2025: Kanton Basel-Stadt, GGG Basel and Christoph Merian Stiftung. Memoriav supports the digital archiving of the Kasko archive.
- In a release of 16 January 2007 the Basel-Stadt government approved CHF 25,000 a year for 2007 to 2010 to the Verein Kaskadenkondensator for infrastructure and operations in the Werkraum Warteck pp (Basel-Stadt government).
- Membership costs CHF 60 a year, CHF 30 for students, with patronage from CHF 150; members get free admission to events, and donations are accepted.
- Governance: the board consists of Martina Böttiger, Chris Regn, Sara Grütter, Steven Schoch and Yolanda Esther Natsch; the co-leaders are Lysann König, Pam Meyer and Yolanda Esther Natsch.

BOTTOM LINE
Kasko is an artist-run association supported in 2025 by the Canton of Basel-Stadt, GGG Basel and Christoph Merian Stiftung, with Memoriav supporting its archive and members paying CHF 60 a year; in 2007 the Basel-Stadt government approved CHF 25,000 a year for 2007 to 2010.
