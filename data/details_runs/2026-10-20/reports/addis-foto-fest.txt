ADDIS FOTO FEST: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
ID: addis-foto-fest
Website: https://addisfotofest.com (checked 2026-10-25; the page loads, lists catalogues for 2010 to 2018 only and says a new edition "launches soon" with no dates)

OVERVIEW
- Biennial international photography festival in Addis Ababa, with exhibitions, portfolio reviews, conferences, projections and film screenings over about a week.
- Founded in 2010 by Aida Muluneh, who directs it; produced by Desta for Africa Creative Consulting (The Reporter, 2018; the festival's site). Held every other December since 2010 (Wikipedia).
- The fifth edition was set to start on 6 December 2018, run five days and bring a record 152 participants from 61 countries (The Reporter, 24 November 2018).
- Venues in 2018: the main collection at Kana Studio, and workshops, talks and master classes at the Ale School of Fine Art and Design; the festival opened at the Sheraton hotel (The Reporter, 8 December 2018).
- Programme: the 2018 exhibition "Photography in Ethiopia" showed 34 Ethiopian photographers, 16 of whom took a workshop with Muluneh.
- Later editions: a sixth edition for December 2020 is not confirmed; the Nuku Studio call page did not load. No reporting found of any edition after 2018. No visiting hours, admission or accessibility information found.

FUNDING
Addis Foto Fest: privately run as a business; the last sponsor list found (2018) names an embassy and corporate sponsors
- Muluneh said in a 2016 Art Africa interview that the festival is run by a private company: "We are not an NGO but a business." She said most cultural funding in Africa has come from overseas cultural institutions, which has helped artists but raised concerns about self-sustainability and thematic independence, and that she argues for direct investment rather than grants and sees corporate sponsorship as a business relationship that gives organisers more freedom (Art Africa, 5 September 2016).
- 2018 organisers named in The Reporter (8 December 2018): Desta for Africa Creative Consulting and the Embassy of Switzerland in Ethiopia.
- 2018 supporters named in The Reporter (24 November 2018): the Embassy of Switzerland, Sheraton Addis, Canon, the National Geographic Society, Marriott, Castel Winery, the Roger Ballen Foundation and Tourism Ethiopia; the article adds "among many others" without naming them.
- At the 2018 press conference Muluneh said organisers had tried to secure local sponsorship but had not been as successful as they hoped (The Reporter).
- No reporting found of sponsors, accounts or a board after 2018.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the festival is private: a producing company, embassy and corporate sponsors, with no flagged industry or Gulf state funder found in the 2018 list. The weak points are that the last published sponsor list is from 2018 and that no edition after 2018 is confirmed. Worth watching: whether the "new edition" promised on the site is held and who sponsors it.
