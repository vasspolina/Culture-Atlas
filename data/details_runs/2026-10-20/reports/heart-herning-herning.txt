HEART HERNING: Herning, Denmark
ID: heart-herning-herning
Website: https://heartmus.dk (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of contemporary art at Bitten og Aage Damgaards Plads 2, Herning, in central Jutland.
- Began as Herning Art Museum in 1977 in the Angli shirt factory; state-recognised in 1980; it opened in a new building on 9 September 2009 and took the name HEART (HEART; Slots- og Kulturstyrelsen).
- Built on the collection of Aage Damgaard (1917 to 1991), a Herning shirt manufacturer who invited artists including Piero Manzoni to work at his Angli factories and kept most of what they made; he said art was "good business" (stated by the space).
- The American architect Steven Holl designed the 2009 building; Wikipedia says its plan is meant to evoke shirt sleeves.
- The building holds galleries, a 150-seat auditorium, rehearsal rooms and a media library (Wikipedia), and the site also has a sculpture park and a cafe (stated by the space).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 4 pm, closed Monday; DKK 85 for adults, DKK 70 for pensioners and students, free under 18.

FUNDING
HEART Herning: mainly public with foundation and earned income: Herning municipality, the Danish state, private foundations
- HEART is a self-owned institution and Herning Kommune is its main public funder. In 2018 income was DKK 16.1 million: DKK 8.2 million from the municipality, DKK 1.4 million from the state, DKK 4.6 million non-public grants and DKK 1.9 million earned income (Slots- og Kulturstyrelsen, 2019).
- The agency said the municipality's annual grant had risen markedly to secure the museum's finances and that the new building was costly to run and maintain.
- Board: seven members; two are appointed by Midtjysk Skole- og Kulturfond, two by Herning Kommune, and these four appoint the other three (Slots- og Kulturstyrelsen, 2019). The current board is Lars Damgaard (chair), Johs. Poulsen (deputy chair), Niels Viggo Malle, Mette Bock, Dann Karlsen and Michael Bjørn Nellemann (stated by the space).
