TOWADA ART CENTER: Towada, Japan
ID: towada-art-center-towada
Website: https://towadaartcenter.com/ (checked 2026-10-24; opens; admission prices are not on the pages read)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal contemporary art museum at 10-9 Nishi-Nibancho, Towada, Aomori Prefecture, in a city of about 66,000 people (Japanese Wikipedia); the museum is the hub of the city's Arts Towada project.
- Opened on 26 April 2008. The idea was first studied in 2001, the basic concept finished in 2004, and the name decided in 2007 (the museum). Arts Towada reached its grand opening in 2010.
- Towada City is the owner; since April 2012 a designated manager, Nanjo and Associates Group, has run it (the museum, Japanese Wikipedia).
- Architect Ryue Nishizawa designed it as separate galleries, one per artwork, linked by glass corridors (the museum).
- 38 permanent works by 33 artists or groups, mostly large installations made for the museum, including Yoko Ono, Yayoi Kusama and Yoshitomo Nara (Japanese Wikipedia). Cube Cafe & Shop on site.
- Visiting: open 9:00 to 17:00, last entry 30 minutes before closing, closed Mondays (or the Tuesday after a Monday holiday). Three accessible parking spaces behind the centre. A renovation closure is planned tentatively from 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2028 (the museum). Admission prices were not found.

FUNDING
Towada Art Center: mainly public: city of Towada and national power-siting subsidies, with local company members
- Construction: Japanese Wikipedia states that ¥2.284 billion, over 90% of the project cost, came from the "three power laws" subsidies (電源三法交付金) drawn from Aomori's Rokkasho nuclear fuel reprocessing and radioactive waste facilities, and that the city acknowledged it could not have built the museum without them. It cites the Kahoku Shimpo series "Kakunen saiko: izon, kofukin de kokyo shisetsu / Genshiryoku manee 1"; the newspaper article itself was not opened.
- Running costs: the city's FY2017 power-siting grant list includes the museum's operation and maintenance, with a project cost of ¥7,539,224 and a grant of ¥5,000,000 (Towada City).
- Corporate members (TAPS), as of 11 August 2026 (names romanised from the Japanese page): S course: Izumi Denki Koji-ten, Kamikita Kensetsu, Konno Kensetsu, Towada Gas, Nakatsuna-gumi and one anonymous company. The A course has nine members, including Towada Taxi and the Towada Oirase Tourism Organization. Annual fees are not published on the page; membership supports the museum's learning programmes.
- No reporting found of a private foundation or national government grant for the museum's operation.

Red flags: Towada Gas, fossil fuels
- Towada Gas, an S course corporate member, delivers gas and kerosene (company site).
- The company site does not mention the museum, and no amount of its membership is published.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The building was paid for mostly with national power-siting subsidies tied to Aomori's nuclear facilities, per Japanese Wikipedia citing Kahoku Shimpo, and running costs partly draw on the same grant system. The corporate member list includes Towada Gas, a fossil fuel retailer. Watch the city's siting-grant list and the member fees, which are not published.
