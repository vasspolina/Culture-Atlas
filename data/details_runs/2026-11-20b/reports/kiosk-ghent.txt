KIOSK: Ghent, Belgium
ID: kiosk-ghent
Website: https://kiosk.art (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art exhibition space at Louis Pasteurlaan 2 on Campus Bijloke in Ghent (KIOSK).
- An initiative of the non-profit Kunstensite vzw and University College Ghent's School of Arts; the programme began in 2006 in a small glass pavilion (artlead). Kunstensite vzw has been on the Belgian company register since 30 May 2007 (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises).
- Since 2010 it has occupied the former anatomical theatre of the Clinical and Polyclinic Institute, built in 1905 by the neo-Gothic architect Louis Cloquet; glass and metal structures sit behind the brick facades (artlead).
- The programme centres on the artist, shows emerging and established names through long development processes and favours new productions; since 2021 its website has also carried online art projects (KIOSK, "Mission"; School of Arts Ghent).
- 2026 season: Joost Pauwaert, "A Good Hammering" (opened with a performance on 24 January), Hisae Ikenaga, "Anatomies of Use" (4 April to 7 June), her first solo show in Belgium, Veva Leye and Jelle Martens, "ordeal more print" (12 September to 22 November), and the group show "Stress Test" on fatherhood and artistic practice (31 October to 20 December), made when an artist asked to postpone his exhibition to care for his child (e-flux; School of Arts Ghent).
- Team named by KIOSK: Simon Delobel (director), Sara Plantefève-Castryck (mediation, press and communication), Thomas Peeters and Sam Van Ingelgem (production managers) and Vijai Patchineelam ("etc.-artist").
- Visiting: open Monday to Sunday, 12:00 to 18:00, with free entry (Ghent city visitor site; Nosbaum Reding); KIOSK states that its building is accessible to all visitors regardless of mobility needs (KIOSK, "Contact").

FUNDING
KIOSK: public support: a university-college art school, the Flemish government and the City of Ghent (stated by KIOSK)
- KIOSK names its funders itself: under its own heading "KIOSK is supported by:" it shows three linked logos, for the School of Arts Ghent, the Flemish government (vlaanderen.be) and the City of Ghent (stad.gent) (KIOSK, "Mission").
- The School of Arts KASK & Conservatorium, part of HOGENT, lists KIOSK among its artistic activities, and KIOSK's contact addresses are on the hogent.be domain (School of Arts Ghent; KIOSK, "Contact").
- Friends: an annual friendship costs 50 euros and comes with a KIOSK artist edition (KIOSK, "Friend").
- Governing board of Kunstensite vzw (KIOSK, "Mission"): Claudine Hellweg (chair), Lies Leliaert, Filip Rathé, Shila Anaraki, Anne-Françoise Lesuisse, Claire Stragier and Sevie Tsampalla.

Not related: KIOSK, the Flemish government's online portal through which arts organisations file applications for operating subsidies. It shares the name only (Flemish arts subsidy handbook 2023 to 2027).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money: a school-based art space backed by Flemish and Ghent public bodies.
