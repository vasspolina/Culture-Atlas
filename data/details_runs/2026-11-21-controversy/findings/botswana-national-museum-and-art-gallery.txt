CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Mmegi: Sandy Grant, "The National Museum: Tragedy and humiliation" (2018)
URL: https://www.mmegi.bw/ampArticle/203342
- Grant writes, in the museum's 50th anniversary year, that it had been effectively closed for at least four years, and asks how the country could have "allowed its flag ship National Museum to become so embarrassingly dysfunctional".
- He traces the problems to the 1967 Act: for most of its 50 years the museum had no Board, and the Act does not spell out the museum's objectives.
- He writes that with the minister, the permanent secretary and the director all "disinterested", the museum "could only slide downward".
- He notes that despite a P25 million recurrent budget in 2018 the museum had no historian or specialists, no website, and a photographic archive closed to the public.
