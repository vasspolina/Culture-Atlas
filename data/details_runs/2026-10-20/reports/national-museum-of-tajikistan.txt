NATIONAL MUSEUM OF TAJIKISTAN: Dushanbe, Tajikistan
ID: national-museum-of-tajikistan

OVERVIEW
- National history and art museum on Ismoil Somoni Street, next to State Flag Square in central Dushanbe (Eurasia.Travel).
- Founded in 1934 with 530 items from the Tajik SSR Academy of Sciences and expeditions; it was formerly named for the Persian miniaturist Kamoliddin Behzad, was renamed the National Museum of Tajikistan in 2011 and moved to its present building in March 2013 (Eurasia.Travel).
- The earlier museum, the Behzod museum, was founded by a decree of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee in 1934 on the basis of an exhibition of economic achievements, held national status from 1999 by government decision, and worked in a former technical college building at 31 Aini Street (Orexca, an older description).
- The present building has 22 exhibition halls over 15,000 square metres, and Wikipedia gives a total area of 24,000 square metres (Eurasia.Travel; Wikipedia).
- Four departments: natural history, ancient and medieval history, modern and contemporary history, and fine and applied arts (Wikipedia). Research departments cover written heritage, archaeology and numismatics (Eurasia.Travel).
- Over 50,000 objects from the Bronze Age to the early 20th century, including Samanid coins, Sogdian pottery, material from the Sogdian castle on Mount Mug, the 11th to 12th century Iskandar mihrab and a large clay Buddha from the Ajina-Tepa temple restored by a team from the Hermitage Museum (Eurasia.Travel). Hellenistic sculptures from Takht-i Sangin are in the displays (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
National Museum of Tajikistan: state museum under the Ministry of Culture
- Orexca, in an older description of the earlier Behzod museum, says that since its founding the museum has been under the Ministry of Culture of Tajikistan.
- Unverified: a Koryo Group travel blog says the museum was built in 2011 by order of President Emomali Rahmon; Eurasia.Travel says the museum moved into its present building in March 2013.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a state institution under Tajikistan's Ministry of Culture, housed since March 2013 in its present building.
