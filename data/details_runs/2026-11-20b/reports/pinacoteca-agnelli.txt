PINACOTECA AGNELLI: Turin, Italy
ID: pinacoteca-agnelli
Website: https://www.pinacoteca-agnelli.it/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum on the roof of the Lingotto, the former Fiat car factory in Turin, entered from Via Nizza 262 (museum website).
- Run by the foundation "Pinacoteca del Lingotto Giovanni e Marella Agnelli", based at the Lingotto, set up to show works given or lent by Giovanni Agnelli and Marella Agnelli Caracciolo (foundation statute).
- Building: the Scrigno, a five-storey steel and glass structure by Renzo Piano suspended over the roof, built in 2002 for the collection (Artribune, March 2022; museum website).
- Collection: 25 works from the 18th to the mid 20th century, including six views of Venice by Canaletto, two views of Dresden by Bernardo Bellotto, seven paintings by Henri Matisse, a 1913 canvas by Giacomo Balla, two Picasso portraits and two Canova plaster dancers (museum website).
- Also on the roof: La Pista 500, Fiat's old test track, now a roof garden with outdoor works by international artists; Casa FIAT, a free display on the history of Fiat; and the FIATCafé500 (museum website).
- Leadership: Ginevra Elkann has been president since 2007 and Sarah Cosulich director since 2021 (Social Report 2024); Cosulich's first programme opened on 27 May 2022 with Picasso and Dora Maar, made with the Fondation Beyeler, and a Sylvie Fleury show (Artribune, March 2022).
- Visitors: 136,864 in 2024 (Social Report 2024).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, Friday to 20:00, closed Monday; full ticket 12 euros, reduced 8 euros for ages 13 to 25, over-65s and groups; free for under-12s, disabled visitors and a companion, and holders of the Abbonamento Musei (museum website).

FUNDING
Pinacoteca Agnelli: private: an Agnelli family foundation, sponsored by Stellantis and the Exor-owned Lingotto Investment Management
- Founders: Giovanni Agnelli, Marella Caracciolo Agnelli, Margaret Agnelli de Pahlen, John, Lapo and Ginevra Elkann, Paolo Fresco, Gianluigi Gabetti, Francesca Gentile Camerana, Franzo Grande Stevens and Alessandro Nasi (Social Report 2024). The founders approve the accounts and appoint the board (foundation statute).
- Board: Ginevra Elkann (president), Lapo Elkann (vice-president), John Elkann, Olivier François and Barbara Negro; secretary Gianluca Ferrero (Social Report 2024).
- 2024 accounts: 4.277 million euros in after 0.109 million of tax, from 3.702 million of "ricavi per la gestione" (management revenue) and 0.684 million from cultural activities: sponsorship, tickets, merchandise, guided tours and room hire. After 0.754 million of structural costs and 0.068 million set aside, 3.455 million went to programmes. The foundation reports a patrimonio (endowment) of 4.2 million euros (Social Report 2024).
- Stellantis, Main Partner: pays towards the museum's activities and paid for the development of La Pista 500 and of Casa 500, since turned into Casa FIAT; the letter to stakeholders thanks FIAT as Main Partner (Social Report 2024).
- FCA Partecipazioni owns the building; the Consorzio Lingotto manages the complex (Social Report 2024).
- Lingotto Investment Management: Official Sponsor since September 2024 (Social Report 2024). It is a London investment firm wholly owned by Exor N.V. (Exor website).
- Others thanked: the Regione Piemonte, the City of Turin, the Soprintendenza and the Amici della Pinacoteca Agnelli friends' association (Social Report 2024).

Red flags: 2022 US criminal plea by Stellantis's FCA US over diesel emissions
- FCA US, now a unit of Stellantis, pleaded guilty in June 2022 to criminal conspiracy in a US Justice Department diesel-emissions fraud case (Rappler, from Reuters, 2 August 2022).
- The conviction rests on false and misleading statements to US regulators and customers about the emissions controls of more than 100,000 Jeep Grand Cherokee and Ram 1500 diesel vehicles of model years 2014 to 2016 (US Justice Department and EPA joint release, 31 July 2022).
- A federal court in Detroit ordered a fine of 96,145,784 dollars, a forfeiture money judgment of 203,572,892 dollars and three years of organisational probation (same release).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 15 December 2025 the Turin preliminary judge Antonio Borretta ordered prosecutors to charge John Elkann, with Gianluca Ferrero, with an inaccurate tax return for two tax years, and dropped the case against Lapo and Ginevra Elkann (Euronews, 16 December 2025).
- The case turns on whether Marella Caracciolo, co-donor of the collection, was resident in Italy for tax in her last years; in July 2025, 183 million euros were paid to settle the debt with the Italian treasury (Euronews).
- At the preliminary hearing in September 2026 the judge refused Margherita Agnelli as civil party against her son John Elkann but admitted her against Ferrero and the notary Remo Morone on a charge of falsification; Elkann's lawyer said his client had always acted within the law (Calcio e Finanza, 11 September 2026).
- Elkann and Ferrero are the board member and secretary named under FUNDING; no court has ruled on the charges.

BOTTOM LINE
Private family money with no flagged funder. The main sponsor's US criminal conviction is the name to know; the board's tax case is one to watch until a court decides it.
