BERGEN KUNSTHALL: Bergen, Norway
ID: bergen-kunsthall-norway
Website: https://www.kunsthall.no (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre in Bergen with exhibition galleries, the Landmark Café and the Landmark live events programme of concerts and club nights.
- Founded in 1838 as Bergens Kunstforening (Bergen Art Association) (Bergen Kunsthall website, Wikipedia).
- Housed in an early functionalist building designed by architect Ole Landmark in 1935 (Wikipedia, Bergen Kunsthall website).
- Since 1953 it has curated and produced the annual Festspillutstillingen, a solo exhibition. Its 2017 show "BRIDGIT" by Charlotte Prodger was followed by her Turner Prize win in 2018 (Wikipedia).
- Director: Kjersti Solbakken. Education: the Kunsthall receives over 4,000 pupils a year for exhibition workshops, and all its learning programmes are free (Bergen Kunsthall learning page).
- Visiting: exhibitions Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 17:00, Thursday until 20:00; closed 23 to 26 December, 31 December, 1 January, 1 May and 17 May. Guided tours Sundays at 13:00 and 14:00 during exhibition periods. Ticket prices and accessibility details were not on the pages read.

FUNDING
Bergen Kunsthall: mainly public with foundations and private family funds: Norwegian state, city and county grants plus named foundations
- The Kunsthall says it is supported by the Ministry of Culture and Equality, the City of Bergen and Vestland County.
- Supporters listed on its About page: Arts Council Norway, Regionalt kulturfond Vestland, Fritt Ord Foundation, Nordic Council of Ministers, the Mondriaan Fund, Culture Ireland, Korean Foundation for International Cultural Exchange (KOFICE), KORO's Local Communities Programme, Kulturrom, the Cultural Schoolbag (DKS), Norwegian Visual Artists' Fund, the Relief Fund for Visual Artists, the Norwegian Association of Art Societies, Familien Brynildsens Legat, Sparebanken Norge, The Bergesen Foundation, H. Westfal-Larsen og Hustru Anna Westfal-Larsens Almennyttige Fond and EGD Holding. No amounts or years are given.
- H. Westfal-Larsen og Hustru Anna Westfal-Larsens Almennyttige Fond was founded by Hans Westfal-Larsen (1872 to 1936) and his wife; he founded the Westfal-Larsen group of shipping companies in Bergen (Wikipedia). No reporting found of how the fund is financed today.
- EGD Holding AS is owned 100% by Espen Galtung Døsvig and holds EGD Shipholding, EGD Property, EGD Capital and Argentum AS (Proff, company register data). Its 2025 profit before tax was NOK 257,037 thousand.
- No reporting found of the origin of The Bergesen Foundation's money.
- No reporting found of the Kunsthall's annual accounts or of amounts from any funder.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kunsthall is mainly Norwegian state, county and city grants plus Nordic and Norwegian foundations, with no flagged funder found. The weak points are that the three private funders (EGD Holding, the Westfal-Larsen fund and the Bergesen Foundation) give no published amounts, and that the Kunsthall publishes no donor figures. Worth watching are the 2023 to 2025 accounts in the Norwegian register for the size of private gifts against public grants.
