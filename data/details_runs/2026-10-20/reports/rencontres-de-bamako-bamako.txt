RENCONTRES DE BAMAKO: Bamako, Mali
ID: rencontres-de-bamako-bamako
Website: https://biennialfoundation.org/biennials/bamako-encounters/

OVERVIEW
- Biennale of contemporary African photography and video held in Bamako, Mali, since 1994 and also called Bamako Encounters and the African Biennale of Photography (Biennial Foundation).
- Organised by Mali's Ministry of Culture and co-produced with the Institut français; exhibitions are spread across Bamako in museums, galleries, foundations, art centres, public venues and French cultural centres (Biennial Foundation).
- The Musée national du Mali was the main venue of the 14th edition, held from 16 November 2024 to 16 January 2025 under the theme "Kouma", the spoken word, with Morocco as guest of honour (Le360 Afrique, Diemba Moussa Konaté, 18 November 2024).
- Past artistic directors include Bisi Silva (2015), Bonaventure Soh Bejeng Ndikung (2019) and Lassana Igo Diarra (2024) (Wikipedia, Biennial Foundation). The 2013 edition was cancelled because of security concerns (Wikipedia).
- The 15th edition runs from 26 November 2026 to 26 January 2027 under the theme REFABULATION(S), with Armelle Dakouo as curator (Biennial Foundation, 4 June 2026). It shows 49 artists, 25 of them women, in installation, video, sound and sculpture as well as photography, with a tribute to Malick Sidibé (DakArt News on africa.com, 6 September 2026).

FUNDING
Rencontres de Bamako: organised by Mali's Ministry of Culture and co-produced by the Institut français, with Royal Air Maroc as a long-standing partner
- Mali's Ministry of Handicrafts, Culture, Hotel Industry and Tourism is the organiser; the 2026 call names its general delegate as El Hadj Amadou Diop (Biennial Foundation, 4 June 2026).
- The Institut français co-produces the event (Biennial Foundation). Wikipedia describes the biennale as jointly run by the government of Mali and the Institut français.
- Le360 describes Royal Air Maroc as the event's long-standing partner, which has backed the biennial for years (Le360, 18 November 2024).
- For the 2007 edition, Christian Kravagna reported that Mali's Ministry of Culture and CULTURESFRANCE, backed by the French ministries of foreign affairs and culture, funded the event, and that Fondation Orange Mali hosted the Quartiers d'Orange venue (springerin, 1/2008).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One critique, from 2008, questions French influence over the biennale's funding and organisation while praising parts of the programme.
1. springerin: Christian Kravagna, "Bamako 2007: On the seventh Rencontres africaines de la photographie" (2008)
URL: https://springerin.at/en/2008/1/bamako-2007/
- Argues that the event gives the impression that "links between France and its former West African colonies are still strong": a long list of French logos on the catalogue and poster, and market-panel speakers who were mostly French gallery owners or photo experts.
- Finds the theme "Dans la ville et au-delà" loose, and the curation by Paris-based Simon Njami technically and aesthetically strong but mostly documentary or photojournalistic.
- Highlights the Cinéma Numérique Ambulant project as a translation of the studio-photography tradition into contemporary practice. Says Dak'art has often been criticised for organisational shortcomings while the Bamako programme ran almost without a hitch, which he links to Western ideas of orderly scheduling and the "positive aspects" of colonialism.

BOTTOM LINE
The Rencontres de Bamako are organised by Mali's Ministry of Culture and co-produced by the Institut français, with Royal Air Maroc as a long-standing partner. For the 2007 edition, Christian Kravagna reported funding from Mali's Ministry of Culture and CULTURESFRANCE, and a venue hosted by Fondation Orange Mali.
