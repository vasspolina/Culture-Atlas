Red flags: 2026 dispute over the design of the new museum building
- On 7 July 2026 Vice Minister of Culture Romina Muñoz defended the winning design, "Ecos del Sol" by Campo Baeza + MAODA, on Radio Sucesos: "Esto va más allá de los gustos estéticos y particulares" (This goes beyond personal aesthetic tastes, translated), and "Se tienen que respetar los criterios (del jurado)" (The jury's criteria must be respected, translated) (Primicias, 8 July 2026).
- On 9 July 2026 a presentation of the winning design at the Colegio de Arquitectos de Pichincha lasted more than two hours and ended amid shouts of protest (Primicias, 14 July 2026).
- Muñoz, executive director of the MuNa from 2021 to 2023 (Expreso, 9 July 2026), told Extra after leaving the vice ministry: "Me pidió la ministra Gilda Alcívar mi renuncia" (Minister Gilda Alcívar asked me for my resignation, translated); the ministry had not replied at publication (Extra, 9 July 2026).
- On 14 July 2026, after the government rejected its design, Campo Baeza + MAODA issued a statement saying "Este procedimiento quebranta la transparencia e integridad de un proceso de concurso público cuyas bases han sido publicadas y aceptadas de manera explícita por todos los equipos participantes" (This procedure breaks the transparency and integrity of a public competition whose rules were published and explicitly accepted by every participating team, translated) (Primicias, 14 July 2026).
