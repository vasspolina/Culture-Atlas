STATE MUSEUM OF THE HISTORY OF THE TEMURIDS (AMIR TIMUR MUSEUM): Tashkent, Uzbekistan
ID: state-museum-of-the-history-of-the-temurids-amir-timur-museum
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- State museum in central Tashkent dedicated to Amir Timur and his dynasty; the nearest metro station is named after Timur, and visitors pass Amir Timur Square with his equestrian statue on the way (Museum Studies Abroad).
- Opened on 18 October 1996, the 660th anniversary of Timur's birth (UzDaily). Built under President Islam Karimov, whose drive to form a national identity around a glorified history it followed; its teal dome recalls the Gur-e Amir in Samarkand, Timur's tomb, and its image went on the 1,000 som banknote in 2001 (Museum Studies Abroad, 2024).
- Three storeys: entrance and ticket window in the basement, a central hall with historical murals on the first floor and most objects on the second, among them weapons, gowns, coins and musical instruments of the Timurid period; the collection numbers more than 5,000 objects (Museum Studies Abroad, 2024).
- Closed for reconstruction from 25 February 2026 (UzDaily). President Shavkat Mirziyoyev toured the rebuilt museum on 9 April 2026: a first-floor section titled "The Great Sahibkiran, Founder of the Second Renaissance", second-floor interactive displays using artificial intelligence, 3D exhibits and objects bought from foreign collections (Office of the President).
- On 2 October 2026 it opened a travelling exhibition of about 50 portrait replicas in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, for its 30th anniversary (Dunyo).

FUNDING
State Museum of the History of the Temurids: public: the Uzbek state, through the Academy of Sciences and the Tashkent city budget
- Operator: the Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Uzbekistan, whose press service announced the 2026 closure (UzDaily; Academy of Sciences).
- 2026 reconstruction: a presidential resolution of 5 February 2026 for Timur's 690th anniversary ordered the Tashkent city administration to rebuild, equip and digitise the museum and create a new exhibition by the end of 2026, using additional sources of the city's local budget (resolution text in the state newspaper Yangi O'zbekiston's Tajik edition; my translation).
- The same resolution says its measures are paid from budget money allocated to the ministries and bodies concerned, from extra-budgetary money and from other sources the law does not forbid (same; my translation).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Museum Studies Abroad (2024) quotes a Karimov saying displayed on the second floor: whoever wants to understand who the Uzbeks are "should recall the image of Amir Temur".
- The 2026 resolution declared April "Amir Temur Month" every year, with meetings in schools, research centres and mahallas (Office of the President); Mirziyoyev said people should be brought to museums mahalla by mahalla to learn their history (Office of the President).
- Connection: the museum's 2026 rebuilding was ordered by that same resolution.

BOTTOM LINE
Wholly state funded and clean of private money. The issue is purpose, not money: the museum was built under one president and rebuilt under another to serve the state's commemoration of Timur.
