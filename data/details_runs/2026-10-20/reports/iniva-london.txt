INIVA (INSTITUTE OF INTERNATIONAL VISUAL ARTS): London, United Kingdom
ID: iniva-london
Website: https://iniva.org

OVERVIEW
- Visual arts organisation that runs exhibitions, commissions, residencies and the Stuart Hall Library, at Chelsea College of Arts, 16 John Islip Street, Pimlico (Chelsea College of Arts).
- Founded in 1994 to address "an imbalance in the way culturally diverse artists and curators were being represented" in the UK; Stuart Hall was its first chair (Wikipedia, Chelsea College of Arts).
- Was based at Rivington Place in Shoreditch, a five-floor building designed by David Adjaye that Iniva shared with Autograph ABP; it opened on 5 October 2007 and cost £5 million (Wikipedia).
- Moved from Shoreditch to Chelsea College of Arts in Pimlico (Wikipedia, Chelsea College of Arts).
- The Stuart Hall Library holds more than 10,000 items, including over 4,000 exhibition catalogues, on contemporary art by artists of African, Asian, Latin American and Oceanic heritage. It was established in 1994 and renamed in 2007 in honour of Hall, Iniva's founding chair (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: the library is open Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 5pm. Membership is free and it is open to the public. It is reference only, so materials are read on site (Chelsea College of Arts, Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Iniva: Arts Council England National Portfolio Organisation, plus project grants from named funders
- Arts Council England: Iniva is a National Portfolio Organisation (Iniva funders page) and is funded by Arts Council England (Wikipedia).
- In the 2015 to 2018 round, Iniva "lost 61.1% of its funding, receiving a total grant of £685,032 over the next three years", while its sister organisation Autograph ABP received £2.1 million (Apollo, "Arts Council Funding: Winners and Losers", 2 July 2014).
- National Lottery Heritage Fund: project funder of "Living Legacies: Collaboration, Community and Radicality". The Clothworkers' Foundation gave a one-off capital grant to the same project (Iniva funders page).
- BFI Screen Heritage Fund (National Lottery): supports the digitisation of Iniva's moving image collection (Iniva funders page).
- Paul Mellon Centre: Digitisation Grant for a project on Black British and diasporic exhibition practices (Iniva funders page).
- Hauser & Wirth Institute: project supporter of "Representation and Accessibility in the Artist Files" (Iniva funders page).
- Goldsmiths' Foundation: inaugural grant for core organisational costs (Iniva funders page).
- Westminster City Council: project funder of "Present Land" (Iniva funders page).
- The Ampersand Foundation: supports exhibitions at the Stuart Hall Library in 2026 and 2027 (Iniva funders page).

BOTTOM LINE
Iniva is an Arts Council England National Portfolio Organisation, with project grants from the National Lottery Heritage Fund, the BFI Screen Heritage Fund, charitable trusts and Westminster City Council. In the 2015 to 2018 round its Arts Council grant was cut by 61.1% to £685,032 over three years.
