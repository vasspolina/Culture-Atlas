Red flags: 2023 refusal to circulate a fellows' open letter on Gaza
- At the end of October 2023 the Akademie's leadership received an anonymous email signed "fellows" with a link to an open letter whose signatories included current and former fellows, asking the leadership to spread it through the Akademie's channels; the letter was published on a private, anonymous Instagram account, and the ministry says its reach and the reactions to it were small (Baden-Württemberg Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts, reply in Landtag Drucksache 17/5894, issued 11 January 2024).
- The leadership met the current fellows who had signed and told them it would not circulate the letter because it was "einseitig von antiisraelischen Positionen geprägt" (one-sidedly shaped by anti-Israeli positions, translated) and repeated arguments also used in antisemitic discourse (Landtag Drucksache 17/5894).
- The ministry states that the Akademie published its statement of 30 October 2023 at the ministry's request, and that the leadership wrote the text and agreed it with the ministry (Landtag Drucksache 17/5894).
- On 28 November 2023 Stephen Brauer and other FDP/DVP members of the state parliament tabled a motion asking the government to report on the episode, including what the letter's content suggested about "möglicherweise antisemitische Grundhaltungen" (possibly antisemitic attitudes, translated) among the signatories (Landtag Drucksache 17/5894).
- The ministry's reply, signed by Minister Petra Olschowski, says the letter criticises Israeli policy in Gaza and uses vocabulary typical of Israel-related antisemitism; the signing fellows told the leadership they did not want their criticism understood as antisemitic and expressed grief for the Israeli victims of 7 October (Landtag Drucksache 17/5894).
- The ministry adds that any discrimination, including antisemitism, can lead to the immediate end of a fellowship, and that after the statement the Akademie held an internal workshop with outside experts on democracy education and antisemitism research (Landtag Drucksache 17/5894).
