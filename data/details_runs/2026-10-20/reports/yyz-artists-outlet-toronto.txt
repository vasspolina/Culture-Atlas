YYZ ARTISTS' OUTLET: Toronto, Canada
ID: yyz-artists-outlet-toronto
Website: https://www.yyzartistsoutlet.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run exhibition space and publisher at #140-401 Richmond St. W, Toronto; its mission is to "offer the space for our community to show, share, and grow" (stated by YYZ).
- Founded in 1979 by graduates of the Ontario College of Art and Design, who staged their first show at 567 Queen Street West (stated by YYZ). The Community Stories project says several Toronto artists founded it as an artist-run centre.
- YYZ says it started on Queen Street, moved to Spadina and has been at 401 Richmond since 1997.
- The name reflects a commitment to artists supporting artists, and YYZ jokes that it is not an airport (stated by YYZ).
- Programmes: the publishing arm YYZBooks (founded 1988), the film and video programme YYZTV for Rogers Cable (1992), the print publication YYZine (2001) and a window exhibition space added in 2002 (stated by YYZ).
- Exhibitions in the 1980s included Joanne Tod's Replications (Dark-Haired Girls) (1981) and Paul Wong's Confused: Sexual Views (1984); the first YYZBOOKS title was Philip Monk's Struggles with the Image (1988) (Community Stories).
- Managed by a volunteer board of directors and curated by consensus (stated by YYZ).
- Visiting: free entry, Wednesday to Saturday 12 PM to 5 PM when exhibitions are on view; the office is open by appointment.

FUNDING
YYZ Artists' Outlet: public grants and donations: Ontario Arts Council operating grants, the Ontario Trillium Foundation and private donors
- Ontario Arts Council: $41,966 in the Multi-Year Operating programme, year 3, in the March 2016 results for Visual Arts Artist-Run Centres and Organizations. The March 2015 results list the same amount in year 2 (Ontario Arts Council).
- YYZ's site says additional support is available from the Ontario Arts Council Exhibition Assistance grant.
- The 2002 window space was funded by a donation from Margie Zeidler and funds from the Ontario Trillium Foundation (stated by YYZ).
- Registered charity (CanadaHelps), taking donations through CanadaHelps.

BOTTOM LINE
YYZ is a volunteer-governed artist-run charity whose documented funding is Ontario Arts Council grants of $41,966 in 2015 and 2016, a donation from Margie Zeidler and Ontario Trillium Foundation funds for its window space, and public donations.
