ILMIN MUSEUM OF ART: Seoul, South Korea
ID: ilmin-museum-of-art-seoul
Website: https://ilmin.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at 152 Sejong-daero, Jongno District, in front of Gwanghwamun Square, one minute on foot from Gwanghwamun Station (Ilmin website).
- Opened in 1996. Wikipedia reports it opened in December 1996 as Ilmin Art Hall with three exhibition rooms, closed for about a year of renovation and reopened in February 2002.
- Run by the Ilmin Cultural Foundation, a non-profit founded in 1994 to honour Kim Sang-man (1910 to 1994), who was president of the newspaper Dong-A Ilbo from 1971 (Ilmin Cultural Foundation page, in Korean).
- Building: the former Dong-A Ilbo newspaper building, a five-storey stone structure (Wikipedia). The fifth and sixth floors hold the Presseum, a newspaper museum on 100 years of Korean newspapers (Ilmin website).
- Programme: exhibitions, lectures, workshops and performances, a documentary archive and the Ilmin Visual Culture book series (Ilmin website). The foundation awards the biennial Ilmin Cultural Prize of 50 million won (Ilmin Cultural Foundation page).
- Collection: the foundation holds about 2,000 works, made up of the Ilmin Collection of Goryeo and Joseon ceramics, paintings and calligraphy collected by Kim Sang-man, art from Dong-A Ilbo publications, and contemporary works (Ilmin Cultural Foundation page). Wikipedia states that the newspaper entrusted over 1,200 paintings and illustrations to the museum.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 11am to 7pm, closed Mondays, 1 January, Lunar New Year's Day and Chuseok. Admission is 9,000 won for adults and 7,000 won for students up to age 24; disabled visitors and everyone on the last Wednesday of each month pay half; membership holders, preschoolers and Discover Seoul Pass holders enter free (Ilmin website).

FUNDING
Ilmin Museum of Art: run by a non-profit foundation: the Ilmin Cultural Foundation
- The museum is operated by the foundation, which was set up in 1994 to honour Kim Sang-man's wishes (Ilmin Cultural Foundation page).
- The foundation keeps the Ilmin Collection that Kim assembled, together with Dong-A Ilbo art and contemporary works.
- The foundation funds the Ilmin Cultural Prize of 50 million won every two years (Ilmin Cultural Foundation page).

Red flags: protest over the chief curator
- 21 to 24 October 2016: after Soma Kim and other women accused chief curator Youngjune Hahm of sexual harassment, Hahm apologised, acknowledged that he had abused his position as a curator and professor, and said he would resign (ArtAsiaPacific, HG Masters, 28 October 2016).
- On 24 October 2016 a group of a dozen art students protested in front of the Ilmin Museum of Art's building in Gwanghwamun, according to the Korea Herald as cited by ArtAsiaPacific; the museum's chief curator was the subject of the accusations.
- ArtAsiaPacific reported that the museum had not released an official statement about Hahm's resignation.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One art press report, from 2016, covers the accusations against the chief curator and the student protest. It concerns the curator's conduct, not the programme or the foundation's funding.
1. ArtAsiaPacific: HG Masters, "Ilmin Museum Chief Curator to Step Down After Sexual Harassment Complaint" (2016)
URL: https://artasiapacific.com/news/ilmin-museum-chief-curator-to-step-down-after-sexual-harassment-complaint
- Reports that Soma Kim, a fashion photographer and artist, wrote on 21 October 2016 about Hahm touching her inappropriately, and that other women later made similar allegations against Hahm, who had been chief curator since 2015.
- Hahm acknowledged wrongdoing and abuse of his position as curator and professor, and disputed one allegation.
- Cites the Korea Herald for the protest by a dozen art students outside the museum building on 24 October.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is a non-profit foundation named for the former Dong-A Ilbo president Kim Sang-man, which runs the museum in the newspaper's former headquarters and keeps the collection Kim assembled along with the newspaper's art. The one documented controversy is the 2016 resignation of the chief curator after harassment accusations.
