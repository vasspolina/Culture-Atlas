ID: townhouse-gallery-cairo

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 9 April 2016, three days after the partial collapse of the main building at 10 Nabrawy Street, government authorities ordered the rest of the building demolished and all residents to leave; the evacuation was halted on 10 April after a representative of the National Organization for Urban Harmony intervened (The Art Newspaper, Aimee Dawson, 13 April 2016).
- Daily News Egypt reported on 12 April 2016 that authorities were to demolish the building "despite calls by workers and residents to stop evacuation".
- Eyewitnesses cited by The Art Newspaper said heavily armed security forces began demolishing the building on 11 April. The Art Newspaper later reported that "Egyptian armed police enforced the partial demolition of its main building in April" (Anny Shaw, 9 November 2016).
- Mada Masr reported that demolition was halted again on 13 April, and an official report to the Cairo governor's office stated that the building should be restored (as reported by The Art Newspaper, 13 April 2016).
- Director William Wells described the demolition as illegal and said the building "stands in legal limbo, with several court cases involved" (The Art Newspaper, 9 November 2016).
