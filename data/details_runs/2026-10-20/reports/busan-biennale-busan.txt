BUSAN BIENNALE: Busan, South Korea
ID: busan-biennale-busan
Website: http://www.busanbiennale.org/BBOCkr

OVERVIEW
- International contemporary art biennale held in Busan in even-numbered years; it began in 1998 when the Busan Youth Biennale, the Sea Art Festival and the Busan Outdoor Sculpture Symposium merged, and was earlier called the Pusan International Contemporary Art Festival (Wikipedia). The City of Busan's own page dates its origin to the Busan Youth Biennale of 1981 and says it took its current name after the organizing committee was established in 2000.
- Presented by Busan Metropolitan City in cooperation with the Busan Biennale Organizing Committee (Busan Metropolitan City).
- The 2026 edition, "Dissident Chorus", runs from 29 August to 1 November 2026 with 46 artists and collectives from 22 countries, directed by Evelyn Simons and Amal Khalaf, who were chosen through an open call that drew over 70 teams (Busan Metropolitan City).
- Venues in 2026: Busan Museum of Contemporary Art on Eulsukdo, plus Space OneZ, a marine warehouse on Yeongdo, and the former Busan Nam High School (e-flux, Busan Metropolitan City).
- Earlier venues have included the Busan Museum of Modern Art, the Busan Museum of Art, beaches and F1963, a former KISWIRE factory in Suyeong that hosted the 2016 main exhibition (Wikipedia).
- Open daily 10:00 to 18:00 except Mondays; the Busan Museum of Contemporary Art charges 16,000 won for adults, Space OneZ and the former Busan Nam High School are free, and a free shuttle links the three venues (Busan Metropolitan City).

FUNDING
Busan Biennale: public body: presented by Busan Metropolitan City with its Organizing Committee
- Busan Metropolitan City presents the 2026 biennale in cooperation with the Busan Biennale Organizing Committee (Busan Metropolitan City); Wikipedia states the biennale is hosted by the city and the Organizing Committee.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One review of the 2026 edition calls it uneven; it criticises the curation, not the funding or governance.
1. e-flux Criticism: Adeline Chia, review of Busan Biennale 2026, "Dissident Chorus" (29 September 2026)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/criticism/6782475/busan-biennale-2026-dissident-chorus
- Calls it "an uneven biennial, more conceptually focused and powerful in its off-sites" than at the main venue.
- Writes that the curation at the Busan Museum of Contemporary Art "feels more staid and generic," citing familiar tropes and "a few needlessly large installations for visual drama."
- Says the biennial's open celebration of underdogs and dissent makes it "an accidental foil to the political quarrels plaguing the concurrent Gwangju Biennale."
- Criticises the curation, not the institution or its funders.

BOTTOM LINE
The biennale is presented by Busan Metropolitan City in cooperation with its Organizing Committee. The published review of the 2026 edition, by Adeline Chia in e-flux Criticism, concerns the curation.
