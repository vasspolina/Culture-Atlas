ID: centraal-museum-utrecht

WIDER CONTEXT
- In March 2014 the Restitutions Committee advised that "View of a Dutch Harbour with Figures" by Adam Willaerts (NK 2729), a Dutch National Art Collection painting on loan to the Centraal Museum, be restituted to the heir of its original owner, Sam Bernhard Levie, who sold it involuntarily in 1940; the Minister accepted the advice (Restitutions Committee, 14 March 2014).
- In 2019 the Centraal Museum was to return the painting "Pronkstilleven" by Jan Davidsz to the family of the Jewish collector Jacob Lierens, who "was forced to sell under duress", after a recommendation by the Dutch government's restitution committee (Jewish News, from JTA, 1 June 2019).
