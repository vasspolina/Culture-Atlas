ADACHI MUSEUM OF ART: Yasugi, Japan
ID: adachi-museum-of-art
Website: https://www.adachi-museum.or.jp/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum with Japanese gardens at 320 Furukawa-cho, Yasugi, Shimane Prefecture (museum website).
- Founded in 1970 by Adachi Zenko (1899 to 1990), then aged 71, in the village where he was born, to thank his hometown and support culture in Shimane (stated by the museum).
- The collection holds about 2,000 works, with about 120 by Yokoyama Taikan at its core (Japanese Wikipedia). In 1979, after about two years of negotiation, Adachi bought several Taikan works from the Kitazawa Collection, among them "Autumn Leaves" (museum website).
- Also on show: contemporary Japanese paintings, children's picture books, wood carvings and ceramics by Kitaoji Rosanjin, who has had a hall of his own since April 2020 (museum website; Wikipedia).
- The gardens cover about 165,000 square metres and include a karesansui dry garden, a moss garden and a white gravel and pine garden (nippon.com, 28 December 2022).
- Sukiya Living Magazine: The Journal of Japanese Gardening, a magazine based in the United States, has ranked the gardens first among Japanese gardens every year since its ranking began in 2003, the 23rd year in a row in 2026 (Japanese Wikipedia; nippon.com; museum website). The museum has three stars in the Michelin Green Guide Japan (museum website).
- The director is Adachi Takanori (nippon.com).
- Current season: the autumn exhibition on seven painters runs to 30 November 2026, followed by the winter exhibition "Vertical And Horizontal" from 1 December 2026 to 28 February 2027 (museum website).
- Visiting: open 9:00 to 17:30 from April to September and 9:00 to 17:00 from October to March. Admission is 2,500 yen for adults, 2,000 yen for university students, 1,000 yen for high school students and 500 yen for younger pupils; a free shuttle bus runs to the museum (museum website).

FUNDING
Adachi Museum of Art: private: a public interest foundation built on its founder's business fortune, with paid admission
- Operator: the Adachi Museum of Art public interest incorporated foundation, certified by Shimane Prefecture (Japanese Wikipedia).
- The founder's money: Adachi hauled charcoal by handcart at 14, became a textile wholesaler in Osaka after the Second World War and dealt in real estate, and began buying Japanese paintings (stated by the museum).
- Earned income: the museum charges admission and says it receives over 600,000 visitors a year; about 640,000 came in 2017 (museum website; Japanese Wikipedia).
- What the foundation pays for besides the museum: the Adachi Museum Award at the Inten exhibition, created in 1995, with a second award at the spring Inten from 2005 (Japanese Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a private foundation that rests on the fortune of its founder, a textile wholesaler and property dealer, and on ticket sales to the more than 600,000 visitors a year it reports. Its director, Adachi Takanori, carries the founder's surname. Worth watching: how much of the budget ticket sales cover, which the foundation's accounts would show.
