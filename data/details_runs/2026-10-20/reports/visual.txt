VISUAL: Carlow, Ireland
ID: visual
Website: https://visualcarlow.ie (checked 2026-10-24; loads)

OVERVIEW
- VISUAL Centre for Contemporary Art and the George Bernard Shaw Theatre, on Old Dublin Road in central Carlow.
- Established in 2009 as a joint project of the Carlow local authorities (VISUAL website). The operator is Carlow Arts Centre Limited, which is recruiting a CEO and artistic director (VISUAL news page).
- Architect Terry Pawson won an RIAI international competition; the initial investment was €18 million, and Carlow College donated the site (VISUAL website).
- On site: exhibition spaces the site calls unique in Ireland for their scale, with up to 15 exhibitions a year, and a 330-seat theatre.
- Visiting: closed Monday; Tuesday to Saturday 11am to 5:30pm; Sunday 2pm to 5pm. Free admission to gallery exhibitions; some events are ticketed. The accessibility page was not read.

FUNDING
VISUAL: mainly public: the Arts Council and Carlow County Council
- The build was a joint project of the Carlow local authorities with a €3.17 million grant from the Department of Arts, Sport and Tourism under the ACCESS Programme (VISUAL website).
- Current funders shown on the site: the Arts Council, Carlow County Council and Carlow LEO, with logos for the Arts and Humanities Entrepreneurship Hubs and the Carlow County Development Partnership (stated by the space; undated).
- Earlier figure: in December 2016 the then CEO Ann Mulrooney said VISUAL would receive €95,000 from the Arts Council, up 21% on the year before, while facing a 7% cut in local authority funding (KCLR).
- The strategic plan 2023 to 2027 sets the aim of a broader funding base and lists the Carlow Framework Agreement 2020 to 2027 between Carlow County Council, the Arts Council and the County Management Association (VISUAL strategic plan).
- No reporting found of private donors, corporate sponsors or a patrons' scheme. The board page could not be opened.
- No reporting found linking VISUAL to Jeffrey Epstein, through money, leadership or any other route. A search on 24 October 2026 returned nothing connecting them, and the About page does not mention him.
