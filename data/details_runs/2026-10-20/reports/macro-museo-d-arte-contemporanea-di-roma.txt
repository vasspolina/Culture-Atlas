MACRO (MUSEO D'ARTE CONTEMPORANEA DI ROMA): Rome, Italy
ID: macro-museo-d-arte-contemporanea-di-roma
Website: https://www.museomacro.it

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at via Nizza 138, Rome, run by the City of Rome's special company Palaexpo (museum website). Wikipedia describes it as occupying a former Peroni brewery on Via Nizza.
- The project began in the late 1990s; six rooms opened in September 1999 and the museum officially opened on 11 October 2002 (Wikipedia).
- Since 2003 it has also used the MACRO Future annex, two refurbished buildings of 1,000 square metres each in the former Testaccio slaughterhouse; Wikipedia credits Gioacchino Ersoch for the Testaccio building and says the French architect Odile Decq was given an extension to display the permanent collection from July 2004.
- Azienda Speciale Palaexpo, "an instrumental entity of the City of Rome", manages it, along with Palazzo Esposizioni Roma, the Museo delle Periferie scheme and the Mattatoio di Roma (museum website).
- Cristiana Perrella was named director in March 2025, the first woman to lead the museum, chosen from 44 applicants after a selection run by Palaexpo; she succeeded Luca Lo Pinto (Finestre sull'Arte, 26 March 2025).
- The museum reopened on 11 December 2025 with four exhibitions, a permanent cinema, a restaurant, a bar and educational spaces (Finestre sull'Arte, 10 December 2025).
- Open Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 19:00 and Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 19:00; closed Monday; last entry 30 minutes before closing. Admission was free on 12, 13 and 14 December 2025.

FUNDING
MACRO: mainly public: the City of Rome (Roma Capitale), through its special company Palaexpo
- The reopening season is promoted by the Assessorato alla Cultura of Roma Capitale and Azienda Speciale Palaexpo, which also produces and organises it (Finestre sull'Arte, 10 December 2025).
- Lo Pinto says the site is municipal property run by Palaexpo, while the building and the collection are still owned by the Soprintendenza (Artribune, 4 October 2024).
- Palaexpo's board is chaired by Marco Delogu, with Ivana Della Portella as vice president and Mino Dinoi, Francesco Madeo and Francesca Barbi Marinetti as members; Fabio Merosi is director general (Palaexpo).
- The museum website names the Art Bonus, the Italian tax-credit scheme for donations to public culture, and links a "support MACRO" page.
- Lo Pinto said acquisition funds had fallen to almost nothing, so museums rely on private donations or art fairs to buy works (Artribune, 4 October 2024).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The one critical account comes from the museum's own outgoing director.
1. Artribune: Livia Montagnoli, "Luca Lo Pinto e il museo MACRO di Roma. Intervista di fine mandato al direttore uscente" (2024)
URL: https://www.artribune.com/professioni-e-professionisti/who-is-who/2024/10/intervista-luca-lo-pinto-direttore-uscente-macro-roma/
- Lo Pinto says the lack of managerial and administrative continuity has been the MACRO's "tallone d'Achille" (Achilles' heel; translated) and that the issue of precarity will always have to be faced.
- He says Palaexpo gave him freedom, and that the Soprintendenza did not accept the artists' proposal to keep the "Retrofuturo" works on long-term loan.

BOTTOM LINE
MACRO is a city museum: Roma Capitale and its special company Palaexpo promote, produce and manage it. Its former director, Luca Lo Pinto, described the museum's lack of managerial continuity in an interview of 4 October 2024.
