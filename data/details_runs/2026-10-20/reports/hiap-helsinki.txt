HIAP (HELSINKI INTERNATIONAL ARTIST PROGRAMME): Helsinki, Finland
ID: hiap-helsinki
Website: https://www.hiap.fi

OVERVIEW
- International artist residency and exhibition programme with its office, Gallery Augusta, a project space and residency studios on Suomenlinna island, and further studios on the fourth floor of the Cable Factory (Tallberginkatu 1) in Helsinki (hiap.fi/about).
- Founded in 1998 by a coalition of Helsinki arts organisations; it runs as a non-profit association (hiap.fi/about).
- Members of the association include Uniarts Helsinki's Academy of Fine Arts, Kunsthalle Helsinki, the Cable Factory, the Finnish Museum of Photography, Pixelache Festival, Zodiak Center for New Dance, Artists' Association MUU, AV-arkki, Gallery Huuto and Pro Artibus, plus one individual (hiap.fi/about).
- Residencies last from 1 to 11 months for artists, curators and other cultural practitioners (hiap.fi/about).
- The director is Emma Beverley; the board for 2026 to 2027 is chaired by Vappu Renko of the Cupore Center for Cultural Policy Research (hiap.fi/about).
- HIAP Open Studios on Suomenlinna is on 31 October 2026, 15:00 to 20:00 (hiap.fi).

FUNDING
HIAP: public funders: Taike (Arts Promotion Centre Finland), the Ministry of Education and Culture and the City of Helsinki
- HIAP names Taike Arts Promotion Centre Finland, the Ministry of Education and Culture and the City of Helsinki as its main funders (hiap.fi/about).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind HIAP is Finnish public funding: Taike, the Ministry of Education and Culture and the City of Helsinki, which HIAP names as its main funders (stated by HIAP).
