DUNDEE CONTEMPORARY ARTS – Dundee, UK
Website: https://www.dca.org.uk/ (loaded 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre at 152 Nethergate with two galleries, two cinema screens, a print studio and learning spaces. Opened 19 March 1999, designed by Richard Murphy Architects, final cost £9M. It was set up as a partnership between Dundee City Council, the University of Dundee and the DCA company (Wikipedia).
- Charity SC026631. Company SC175926, incorporated 23 May 1997 (own site; Companies House).
- Centre open daily (Sun–Thu 10:00–23:00, Fri–Sat to midnight); galleries open Tue–Sun. Accessibility pages are linked but were not checked.
- Chair: Steve Grimmond; director: Beth Bate. 14 trustees are listed (own site; 2024-25 annual report).

FUNDING
Dundee Contemporary Arts: mainly public (Creative Scotland and Dundee City Council), plus trusts.
- Core funders: Creative Scotland and Dundee City Council (own site). Creative Scotland's Multi-Year Funding rose from about £2M in the previous round to nearly £4M over three years, 2025–28 (The Skinny).
- Dundee City Council cut its grant by 9.9% for 2025-26 (Third Force News, Feb 2025).
- 2024-25 funders named in the annual report include Paul Hamlyn Foundation, Art Fund, Henry Moore Foundation, Freelands Foundation, British Council, Mondriaan Foundation, Canada Council, Quebec Embassy, UK Shared Prosperity Fund, University of St Andrews, Corra Foundation, Leng, Northwood, Tay, Turtleton, R J Larg, Gordon Fraser and Alexander Moncur trusts, and 71 Brewing as an exhibition sponsor.
- In 2023 the director told MSPs of a £300,000 funding gap against about £450,000 in reserves (Third Force News, Sept 2023).
- No reporting found of Baillie Gifford support. DCA is not on Baillie Gifford's sponsorship directory.

BOTTOM LINE
Funding is publicly led, with mainstream trusts and foundations on top. No contested funder or campaign was found. Worth watching: the financial strain (the 2023 funding gap and the 2025 council cut), not who the funders are.
