GRAN TEATRO DE LA HABANA ALICIA ALONSO: Havana, Cuba
ID: gran-teatro-de-la-habana-alicia-alonso
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- Theatre complex on the block bounded by San Rafael, San José, Consulado and the Paseo del Prado in Havana (EcuRed, state encyclopedia).
- The Teatro Tacón, designed by Antonio Mayo and officially opened on 15 April 1838, stood on the site before (EcuRed).
- The Centro Gallego bought the site in 1906 for its social palace, a neo-baroque building by the Belgian architect Paul Beleu that opened in 1915, with marble groups by Giuseppe Moretti on the main façade (EcuRed).
- From 1985, on Alicia Alonso's initiative, the whole building took the name Gran Teatro de La Habana, keeping "García Lorca" for the main hall (EcuRed).
- It houses the Ballet Nacional de Cuba, the Teatro Lírico Nacional and the Ballet Español de Cuba, and has been the main stage of the Havana International Ballet Festival since 1960 (EcuRed).
- The Council of State renamed it for Alonso in September 2015 (EcuRed; EFE in Diario las Américas).
- After a capital repair from 2013 to 2015 it reopened on 1 January 2016 with President Raúl Castro and Alonso present. EFE reported a renovated García Lorca hall seating 1,300, an enlarged lobby, a new café and a small performance space.
- It suffered two fires after the reopening, the second in March 2018 from an electrical fault (Diario de Cuba, an independent outlet).
- About two years before the 2025 closure, deputy director Enmanuel George announced a temporary closure for termite damage to the García Lorca hall's woodwork and loose sections of the slab above its false ceiling (Diario de Cuba); CiberCuba dates that closure to February 2023.
- In October 2025 a note from the theatre said the García Lorca hall and other spaces were closed for "un proceso de inversión constructiva" (a building investment process, own translation) (Diario de Cuba; CiberCuba).

FUNDING
Gran Teatro de La Habana Alicia Alonso: state-funded: Ministry of Culture through the Consejo Nacional de las Artes Escénicas
- The Consejo Nacional de las Artes Escénicas (CNAE), created on 1 April 1989 with its own legal personality, is one of the Ministry of Culture's councils (EcuRed). Its president, Lillitsy Hernández Oliva, explained the 2025 repair on Canal Habana's Hola Habana (Periódico Cubano; CiberCuba).
- 2013 to 2015 repair: façades, lobbies, boxes, roof and stage floor were restored, with new seating, curtains, air conditioning, acoustics, stage machinery, rehearsal rooms, a recording studio and more than 20 dressing rooms (EcuRed). Culture Minister Julián González described the restoration at the reopening (EFE). Periódico Cubano calls it a multimillion investment; CiberCuba says it cost millions of pesos.
- 2025 repair: Hernández named air-conditioning leaks, a deteriorated third-floor roof and damage from sound vibrations (Periódico Cubano; CiberCuba).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Three reports from independent outlets describe repeated decay and repairs within a decade of a costly restoration; one quotes readers asking where the money went. None is art criticism.
1. Diario de Cuba: DDC, "Cierra el Gran Teatro de La Habana para su tercera reparación en 12 años" (2025)
URL: https://diariodecuba.com/cultura/1760355134_63336.html
- Counts the 2025 closure as the third repair in about 12 years.
2. Periódico Cubano: "Cubanos tras reparación del Gran Teatro de La Habana: '¿Otra vez? Se robaron los recursos'" (2025)
URL: https://www.periodicocubano.com/cubanos-estallan-por-reparacion-del-gran-teatro-de-la-habana-otra-vez-se-robaron-los-recursos/?amp=1
- Says the work has drawn criticism of management and the use of public money, and calls for more transparent management.
- Unverified: social media users quoted by the paper say money and materials from the earlier repair were stolen.
3. CiberCuba: "The Gran Teatro de La Habana is being repaired once again after a failed million-dollar investment" (2025)
URL: https://en.cibercuba.com/node/312823
- Describes rubbish, urine stains and people sleeping at the entrance, and quotes users denouncing mismanagement.
4. Havana Times: "Electrocution Risk from Leaks at Havana's National Theater" (2023)
URL: https://havanatimes.org/features/electrocution-risk-from-leaks-at-havanas-national-theater/
- Quotes a musician who said in March 2023 that the Gran Teatro and the Karl Marx Theater were closed because of termites. Mostly about the Teatro Nacional.

BOTTOM LINE
This is a state theatre paid by the Ministry of Culture through the CNAE, with no outside funder named. The weak point is the use of public money, with a costly restoration followed by new repairs within a decade. Worth watching: the cost and reopening date of the current works.
