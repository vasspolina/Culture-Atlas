MAK MUSEUM OF APPLIED ARTS: Vienna, Austria
ID: mak-museum-of-applied-arts-vienna
Website: https://www.mak.at/en

OVERVIEW
- Applied arts, design, architecture and contemporary art museum at Stubenring 5, in Vienna's Innere Stadt (Wikipedia; museum website).
- Founded in 1863 under Emperor Franz Joseph I with Rudolf von Eitelberger as first director; it opened in 1864 in temporary rooms and moved in 1871 into its purpose-built Neo-Renaissance building by Heinrich von Ferstel (Wikipedia).
- Lilli Hollein has directed it since 2021, its first female director (Wikipedia).
- Branches include the Geymüllerschlössel in Vienna, the MAK Tower (closed), the Josef Hoffmann Museum in Brtnice, Czech Republic, and the MAK Center in Los Angeles; the online collection holds more than 300,000 objects (Wikipedia).
- On 27 August 2026 two men carried out a daytime smash-and-grab raid and took a 673-diamond platinum Van Cleef & Arpels necklace from 1939; Wikipedia states it has not been recovered. The Landeskriminalamt issued international wanted notices with photos for the two suspects (W24).
- The levels and toilets are reachable for visitors with disabilities by a wheelchair-accessible lift, and companions of visitors with disabilities enter free (museum website). Porsche Holding Salzburg has supported free-entry days on 29 December 2024 (museum website) and 28 December 2025 (vienna.at, 22 December 2025).

FUNDING
MAK: mainly public: base funding (Basisabgeltung) of 11.7 million euros, plus ticket, rental and sponsorship income
- Total revenue in 2024 was 16,051 thousand euros against expenses of 15,663 thousand: base funding (Basisabgeltung) 11,707, turnover from admissions, education, rentals, leases and sponsoring 3,490, subsidies 412, other income 228 and donations 214 (MAK annual report 2024).
- 2024 sponsors thanked in the annual report: Wienerberger AG for the exhibition "PROTEST/ARCHITEKTUR. Barrikaden, Camps, Sekundenkleber"; Porsche Holding Salzburg for the start of a multi-year partnership and free-entry days (also a TikTok video for the free-entry day, "sponsored by Porsche Holding Salzburg"); CC Real GmbH for "PECHE POP" and advertising space at Wien Mitte The Mall; Wiener Städtische for free entry on MAK Day; Flughafen Wien AG for advertising screens; Swarovski for a floral installation; Ernst Ploil for the "PECHE POP" catalogue; Dorotheum for "ICONIC AUBÖCK"; Vöslauer and Ottakringer as event partners. Cornelius Grupp and Alfred Heinzel supported the "DIREKTION FÜR ALLE!" space, opened in 2024 (annual report).
- Support groups: the MAK Art Society (MARS), the MAK Director's Cercle and International Friends of the MAK Vienna, a US 501(c)(3) organisation (annual report).
- The Kuratorium (board) to the end of 2024 was chaired by Astrid Gilhofer; members include Elisabeth Gürtler, Claudia Oetker, Barbara Glück of the Mauthausen Memorial, Martin Böhm of Dorotheum, and representatives of two federal ministries, the public service union and the staff council (annual report). The International MAK Advisory Board, chaired by Gertie Gürtler, lists Ronald S. Lauder, Susanne Porsche and Thaddaeus Ropac among its members (annual report).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One local press item, on security after the 2026 theft; it criticises the museum's management, not its funding.
1. W24: W24 Redaktion, "Nach Millionenraub im MAK: Kritik an Sicherheitsvorkehrungen" (31 August 2026)
URL: https://www.w24.at/News/2026/8/Nach-Millionenraub-im-MAK-Kritik-an-Sicherheitsvorkehrungen
- A witness told Die Presse online that the exhibition had "keinerlei Sicherheitsvorkehrungen, keine Taschenkontrollen, nichts" (no security measures, no bag checks, nothing; title and quotation translated), and asked why no alarm went off. She called the museum's account "beschönigend" (embellished).
- Director General Lilli Hollein had pointed to the highest security standards when speaking to the APA. The museum said it had ten security staff and video cameras before the robbery and that its staff reacted calmly and followed the planned procedures. Metal detectors are now in use at the entrance, and rucksacks and bags must be left at the cloakroom; the detectors are to stay at least until the exhibition ends on 27 September.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Radio Free Asia (Alim Seytoff and Roseanne Gerin, 27 November 2024) reported that Volkswagen sold its operations in Xinjiang, a joint-venture plant in Urumqi with SAIC. Activists and experts had accused VW of allowing Uyghur forced labour there; VW said in December 2023 that its audit showed no signs of human rights violations, and Adrian Zenz judged the audit "unsuited to meaningfully assess the presence or absence of forced labor".
- Connection: Porsche Holding Salzburg, the MAK's partner since 2024, is owned by Volkswagen AG (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The MAK is mainly publicly funded: base funding covered 11.7 of 16.05 million euros of 2024 revenue, with the rest from admissions, rentals, sponsorship, subsidies, donations and named sponsors including Porsche Holding Salzburg, Wienerberger and Wiener Städtische. The documented points are the Volkswagen AG ownership of its partner Porsche Holding Salzburg and the criticism of security after the August 2026 theft.
