TENT ROTTERDAM: Rotterdam, Netherlands
ID: tent-rotterdam
Website: https://www.tentrotterdam.nl (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art space and production house for Rotterdam artists at Coolhaven 32, in Delfshaven, with a fixed venue and nomadic projects around the city; it is a programme of CBK Rotterdam (stated by the space).
- CBK Rotterdam's exhibition platform, first on the Nieuwe Binnenweg and later at Villa Alckmaer, became TENT in 1999 at Witte de Withstraat 50; TENT moved to the Coolhaven in 2024 (CBK Rotterdam).
- Building: Dr. Aart Verwey founded a laboratory for testing ores, food and farm produce in 1901, and it moved into a purpose-built building at Coolhaven 32 in 1953, where it grew to about 110 staff under three generations of the Boddaert family; a fire broke out in the fume-cupboard area in 2012 (stated by the space, from research by historian Ferrie Weeda).
- CBK Rotterdam, founded in 1982, runs three other programmes beside TENT: BKOR (the city's collection of public sculpture), Sculpture International Rotterdam and Art Office, with more than 2,000 registered artists (CBK Rotterdam). Ove Lucas is its director (CBK Rotterdam).
- Programme: the Dolf Henkes Prize 2026, devoted to social art practice, went to Jaasir Linger (1 April to 17 May 2026); "Lace Stories", a group show on the wedding curated by Kapish, runs from 22 November 2026 to 21 February 2027 (stated by the space).
- Visiting: Thursday to Sunday 12:00 to 18:00. Admission EUR 1; free for neighbours in postcodes 3015 CG to 3024 AR, children up to 18, and Rotterdampas, Museumkaart and ICOM card holders. Wheelchair users are asked to call ahead (stated by the space).

FUNDING
TENT Rotterdam: mainly public: City of Rotterdam subsidy through CBK Rotterdam
- CBK Rotterdam was a municipal service until 1 January 2006, when it became an independent foundation, Stichting Centrum Beeldende Kunst, with a supervisory board (CBK Rotterdam). It has held ANBI charity status since 2014 (CBK Rotterdam).
- 2025 accounts: income of EUR 4.459 million, of which EUR 4.021 million was the City of Rotterdam's structural subsidy and EUR 114,000 incidental subsidies; private funds brought EUR 133,000 and public income EUR 18,000 (CBK Rotterdam, summary accounts 2025).
- In 2024 the city's structural subsidy was EUR 3.940 million of EUR 4.102 million total income (same accounts).
- TENT's material costs were EUR 366,000 in 2025 and EUR 270,000 in 2024; staff costs are booked for CBK as a whole (summary accounts 2025). CBK's permanent staff are paid on the municipality's pay rules (CBK Rotterdam).
- The 2025 ANBI filing names B. Mousavi as chair, I. Pronk as vice-chair and N. de Windt as a member of the foundation's board (CBK Rotterdam ANBI form 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind TENT is clean public money, and no funder belongs to a flagged industry. The record carries no red flag.
