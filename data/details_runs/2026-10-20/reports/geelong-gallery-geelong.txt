GEELONG GALLERY: Geelong, Australia
ID: geelong-gallery-geelong
Website: https://www.geelonggallery.org.au

OVERVIEW
- Regional public art gallery on Little Malop Street in Johnstone Park, Geelong, about a three-minute walk from Geelong Railway Station (stated by the gallery).
- Geelong Progress League members petitioned for a gallery in 1895; it opened on 31 May 1900 in the Geelong City Hall as Geelong Art Gallery (Wikipedia).
- The current building opened in 1915 as a memorial to George M. Hitchcock and was extended in 1928, 1937, 1938, 1956 and 1971 and renovated in 2001 and 2017 (Wikipedia).
- The collection holds over 6,000 Australian and international works, including Eugene von Guerard's "View of Geelong" (1856) and Frederick McCubbin's "A bush burial" (1890); since 2016 it also holds the Colin Holden Print Collection (Wikipedia).
- Jason Smith directed the gallery from 2016 until early 2025; Humphrey Clegg was appointed director and CEO in 2025 (Wikipedia; annual report 2024-25).
- In 2024-25 it presented 17 exhibitions, 585 public programs and 96 learn programs, welcomed 69,844 visitors and recorded 64,322 digital exhibition visits (annual report 2024-25).
- Visiting: free entry, open daily 10am to 5pm. The gallery states its facilities are accessible to all and offers a pre-visit information document.

FUNDING
Geelong Gallery: grant-funded: grant income of A$1.67 million of A$2.85 million revenue, with the City of Greater Geelong and the Victorian Government named as partners, plus philanthropic foundations and individual donors
- Revenue in 2024-25 was A$2,850,977 before donated works of art, including grant income of A$1,525,777 and Creative Victoria multiyear funding of A$140,000; expenditure was A$2,972,381 (audited accounts in the annual report 2024-25).
- The President thanks the City of Greater Geelong and the Victorian Government as the gallery's partners, and a City of Greater Geelong representative sits on the board.
- Other 2024-25 income: ticketed exhibitions A$145,721, fundraising A$56,508, sponsorship and prize income A$40,552 and shop revenue A$205,274 (annual report).
- Major donors named for key initiatives in 2024-25: The Ian Potter Foundation and The Sir Wilfred and C.H. (Roger) Brookes Charitable Foundation, both giving multi-year funding to the exhibition program (annual report).
- 2024-25 donors include the Alan and Mary-Louise Archibald Foundation, Colin Holden Charitable Trust, the Anthony Costa Foundation, Dimmick Foundation, Ruth Fagg Foundation Trust, The Hope Family Foundation, Stuart Leslie Foundation, JB Ryan Perpetual Trust, Trawalla Foundation, The Will and Dorothy Bailey Charitable Fund, and individuals (annual report).
- The Peggy and Leslie Cranbourne Foundation supported six exhibitions in the Learn space; the Geelong Community Foundation and the Anthony Costa Foundation funded the Youth Projects Coordinator role (annual report).
- The annual report counts 139 financial donors and 805 members.
- The partners page (for 2020 to 2021) lists the City of Greater Geelong, Creative Victoria and local firms including Crowe, Harwood Andrews, Pidgeon Ward and Provincial Media.

BOTTOM LINE
Geelong Gallery's 2024-25 revenue of A$2.85 million included A$1.67 million of grant income and Creative Victoria multiyear funding, and its annual report names the City of Greater Geelong and the Victorian Government as its partners. The rest comes from named philanthropic foundations, individual donors, members, ticketed shows and local sponsors, and its audited accounts and donor list are published.
