Red flags: 2024 to 2025 dispute over removing the Knickerbocker Greys cadet group from the armory
- Governor Kathy Hochul signed a state law in December 2024, spearheaded by State Senator Liz Krueger and State Assemblyman Alex Bores, that prevents the Park Avenue Armory Conservancy from evicting the Knickerbocker Greys, a youth cadet group founded in 1881, and lets it keep its 800-square-foot space in the armory's basement (West Side Spirit, Jack Ahern, 29 September 2025).
- The Seventh Regiment Armory Conservancy, which holds a 99-year state lease from 2006, filed a federal lawsuit on 5 September 2025 in Manhattan federal court, saying the cadets' use of the room would interfere with its $73 million renovation plan for the basement and calling the law "a clear abuse of legislative power" (New York Post via AOL, Gabrielle Fahmy, 13 September 2025).
- The Conservancy argues the law violates the Contracts Clause, the Takings Clause and its due process rights, and calls it a "targeted political favor" obtained through lobbying and influence; David Menegon, commandant of the Greys, said the group teaches "leadership, empathy, and confidence" (West Side Spirit, 29 September 2025).
- On 25 November 2025 Judge George B. Daniels of the US District Court for the Southern District of New York granted a preliminary injunction barring Empire State Development from enforcing the law, finding the Conservancy "more likely than not to succeed on the merits of their Contracts Clause claim" (Loeb & Loeb, counsel to the Conservancy).
