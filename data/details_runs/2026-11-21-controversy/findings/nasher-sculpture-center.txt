ID: nasher-sculpture-center

WIDER CONTEXT
- By February 2013 the Nasher Sculpture Center and Museum Tower had been unable to agree in 18 months on a solution to the sunlight from the tower, which the Nasher contends disrupts its controlled environment and threatens works in the garden and galleries (CultureMap Dallas, Jennifer Chininis, 13 February 2013).
- In November 2012 Museum Tower rejected the Nasher's proposed louver solution as unattractive, unpleasant and unacceptable to homebuyers. The Nasher installed mesh panels on its windows and glass roof for exhibitions in 2012 and 2013; its spokesperson Jill Magnuson said they "come nowhere close to eliminating the blinding light effects in our galleries" (CultureMap Dallas).
- The Association of Art Museum Directors issued a statement "expressing concern" over the "impasse", saying the Nasher "has negotiated in good faith with the developers of Museum Tower" and that it hoped "all parties will work together for an acceptable solution" (CultureMap Dallas).
