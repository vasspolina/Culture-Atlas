THE GLUCKSMAN: Cork, Ireland
ID: the-glucksman-cork
Website: https://www.glucksman.org/about/information/history

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum in the lower grounds of University College Cork (UCC). It is run by ArtGallery (UCC) DAC, a designated activity company whose registered office is UCC's Secretary's Office (the company's 2025 annual report).
- Opened on 14 October 2004 by President Mary McAleese (the museum's history page). Formerly called the Lewis Glucksman Gallery; Fiona Kearney was its inaugural director (Wikipedia).
- Named for Lewis Glucksman, one of its founding donors, whom the museum calls an American financier and philanthropist. Wikipedia describes him as a Wall Street financier and chairman of Lehman Brothers, Kuhn, Loeb Inc.
- Building: designed by O'Donnell + Tuomey. Wikipedia reports the Royal Institute of the Architects of Ireland named it Best Public Building in Ireland in 2005 and that it was shortlisted for the 2005 Stirling Prize.
- Flooded in the Cork floods of 2009 and closed from November 2009 to January 2010 for repairs (Wikipedia).
- It had 91,207 visits in 2017 and was the first museum in Munster fully accredited under the Museum Standards Programme for Ireland (the museum's history page). In 2022 it received the European Museum Academy's Art Museum Award for its social role (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 11am to 5pm, closed Monday. Entry is free (suggested donation 5 euro).

FUNDING
The Glucksman: mainly public: University College Cork subvention and the Arts Council, with small earned income
- Year ended 30 September 2025: income 871,074 euro (audited financial statements, detailed schedule unaudited). UCC subvention 427,720; Arts Council 191,815 (strategic funding 111,815, other grants 80,000); other grants 12,527; fundraising and gifts 40,881; education 48,693; shop sales 38,024; craft fair 29,788; rental income 30,000; curatorial services 31,172; gallery hire 20,454. Operating expenses were 859,711 euro and the profit for the year was 8,992 euro.
- The 2024 equivalents were subvention 405,094, Arts Council 107,000, other grants 32,664 and fundraising and gifts 61,242 euro.
- UCC pays the subvention to cover the company's annual operating expenses (financial statements, note 7). The Arts Council funds core artistic work and touring and dissemination of work.
- The museum's funding page names UCC and the Arts Council of Ireland, and says other local, national and international funding bodies also give programme funding.
- Founding donor: the museum is named for Lewis Glucksman, one of its founding donors (the museum's history page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Glucksman is public: a university subvention that covers operating costs, and Arts Council grants, with shop, education, hire and small gifts income. The museum is named for Lewis Glucksman, a founding donor whom Wikipedia describes as a Wall Street financier.
