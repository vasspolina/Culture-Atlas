KONSTHALL C: Stockholm, Sweden
ID: konsthall-c-stockholm
Website: https://konsthallc.se

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit exhibition space in a former communal laundry at Cigarrvägen 14 in Hökarängen, a suburb of southern Stockholm (e-flux listing; Creative Time Summit page).
- Began in 2004 as a public artwork by the artist Per Hasselberg, made with local community organisations (Creative Time Summit page). It describes itself as "an experiment in urban renewal" and has grown from an artist-run project space into what it calls "an established contemporary art platform" (e-flux call for applications, 20 May 2023).
- Programme: several exhibition spaces, an artist-in-residence platform, a citizens' archive and a network of local artists (e-flux call). Kunstkritikk (Hans Carlsson, 5 February 2015) writes that the venue has long focused on Hökarängen, with attention to social planning and the history of the Swedish welfare state.
- Directors named in the press: Jens Strandberg and Jenny Richards from 2015, succeeding Karolin Tampere (Kunstkritikk, 2015); Ulrika Flink as artistic director and Erik Annerborn as managing director in 2023, when a three-year artistic directorship for 2024 to 2026 was advertised (e-flux call).
- Legal form: the association Kulturföreningen Konsthall C (Region Stockholm decision, 16 September 2025).
- Open Thursday to Sunday, 12:00 to 17:00 (e-flux listing).

FUNDING
Konsthall C: public support: Region Stockholm operating grant
- Region Stockholm's Kulturförvaltningen granted Kulturföreningen Konsthall C operating support (verksamhetsstöd) of SEK 300,000 for 2025, plus an extra payment of SEK 73,896 decided on 16 September 2025 (delegation decision 37/2025, KN 2025/1082).

Not related: Bonniers Konsthall, a separate art institution at Torsgatan 19 in Stockholm, whose website ties its vision to Jeanette Bonnier. Konsthall C is a different organisation, the association Kulturföreningen Konsthall C in Hökarängen.

BOTTOM LINE
Konsthall C is a non-profit association in a former laundry in Hökarängen. The funding documented on a page is Region Stockholm operating support of SEK 300,000 for 2025 plus SEK 73,896 extra.
