Red flags: 2022 dispute over moving works to a planned ministry museum in Antigua
- In July 2022 the museum's director Rudy Cotton said the ministry's new administration planned a new museum in Antigua to gather the most important pieces of all the national museums, and that the museum had given about 26 pieces to it against his wishes (Prensa Libre, Marysabel Aldana, 29 July 2022).
- Cotton said he feared the museum would be closed for at least 10 to 15 years: "Las obras van para el museo de ellos y con eso buscan cerrar el Museo Nacional de Arte Moderno Carlos Mérida" (the works are going to their museum and with that they seek to close the Museo Nacional de Arte Moderno Carlos Mérida, translated) (Prensa Libre, 29 July 2022).

Red flags: 2023 director's complaint over building works and moving the collection
- On 7 September 2023 the museum's administration said in a statement that the Ministry of Culture and Sports and its Vice Ministry of Cultural and Natural Heritage had taken arbitrary actions against cultural heritage, including moving works of art to places without suitable conditions (Emisoras Unidas, 7 September 2023).
- Cotton said the ministry under Minister Felipe Amado Aguilar Marroquín was moving the collection to the Museo de Historia Natural without proper care: "Las mil 200 obras están siendo trasladas al Museo de Historia Natural" (the 1,200 works are being moved to the Museo de Historia Natural, translated) (Prensa Libre, Marysabel Aldana, 7 September 2023).
- Unverified: Cotton said the minister had harassed him and taken legal action against him, with three administrative records, two criminal complaints and a judicial complaint seeking his immediate dismissal, which Prensa Libre reported were still before the courts (Prensa Libre, 7 September 2023); Emisoras Unidas reported Cotton saying the complaints had been dismissed.
- The ministry called the accusations false and said they were Cotton's personal claims, not the museum's as an institution (Prensa Libre, 7 September 2023), and that the works followed the procedures the law sets for heritage buildings: "son falsas las acusaciones por parte del director del museo" (the accusations by the museum's director are false, translated) (Emisoras Unidas, 7 September 2023).
