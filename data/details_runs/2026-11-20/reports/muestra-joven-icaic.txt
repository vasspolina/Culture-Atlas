MUESTRA JOVEN ICAIC: Havana, Cuba
ID: muestra-joven-icaic
Website: http://www.muestrajoven.cult.cu (checked 2026-11-20; down; archive copies of 2021 read)

OVERVIEW
- Film exhibition for young Cuban directors, held in April by the Instituto Cubano del Arte e Industria Cinematográficos (ICAIC); its board of directors includes filmmakers (Muestra Joven site; OnCuba).
- The competition covers fiction, documentary and animation by directors aged 35 or under, with a poster contest and "Haciendo Cine", a lab for low-budget projects that ends in a pitch to bodies that support production (Muestra Joven site).
- OnCuba reported one edition of close to 50 films at the Chaplin and 23 y 12 cinemas and the Centro Cultural Fresa y Chocolate.
- Havana Times, citing IPS Cuba, dates its start to 2001. The 18th edition ran in April 2019. ICAIC postponed the 19th, set for 7 to 12 April 2020, without a new date.
- Havana Times published on 22 July 2025 a translation of a 14ymedio piece that says the Muestra has disappeared without explanation.

FUNDING
Muestra Joven ICAIC: mainly public: Ministry of Culture, through ICAIC
- ICAIC, created in 1959, is a Ministry of Culture body (Wikipedia) and sponsors the event (Muestra Joven site).
- In 2014 the Royal Norwegian Embassy proposed a joint fund with the Muestra for young directors' short films. ICAIC would not sign without the Ministry of Culture's authorisation, and the embassy launched the Fondo Noruego para el Cine Cubano on its own (OnCuba).
- That fund pays 500 to 5,000 CUC per project and in its first three years helped finish 44 films (OnCuba).
- Adolfo Mena, overall winner of an earlier edition, said he paid for his shorts with help from the Norwegian Embassy, the Ludwig Foundation, the Escuela Internacional de Cine y Televisión and the theatre El Público (OnCuba).

Red flags: ICAIC's removal of films from the programme, 2018 and 2020
- 2018, 17th edition: ICAIC moved the work-in-progress film "Quiero hacer una película" by Yimit Ramírez to a 24-seat room, and he withdrew it. ICAIC's leadership said it would defend the institution's right to its own decisions (OnCuba). Havana Times states the film was pulled from the opening section on the claimed ground that it disrespected José Martí.
- 26 February 2020: the Muestra's board said ICAIC's presidency censored the documentary "Sueños al pairo" (José Luis Aparicio Ferrera and Fernando Fraguela Fosado, on the musician Mike Porcel) over political and ideological differences (OnCuba, Havana Times). Aparicio told OnCuba that ICAIC objected to its treatment of the 1980 Mariel exodus.
- 28 February 2020: ICAIC replied that, as owner of the archive images, it refused their use in a montage whose context it does not share, and that the film cannot be shown in Cuba or abroad (OnCuba).
- Stated by the Muestra's board: ICAIC replaced its director Carla Valdés León with the institute's vice president, denied it control of its Facebook page and postponed the Muestra without consulting it (OnCuba).
- Havana Times lists earlier removals in 2004, 2009 and 2011; after the last, the Muestra's director Fernando Pérez resigned over differences with ICAIC.

Red flags: Filmmakers' withdrawal from the 19th edition (February 2020)
- At least six filmmakers withdrew works in solidarity with the directors of "Sueños al pairo": Daniela Muñoz Barroso, Regis Guedes, Carla Valdés León, Carlos Lechuga (with Marcos Castillo), Josué García Gómez and Marcos Alejandro Yglesias Ravelo (OnCuba). The target was ICAIC's presidency.
- The board condemned the censorship and asked for an assembly of filmmakers and institutions (OnCuba, Havana Times).

WIDER CONTEXT
- 14ymedio reported in January 2026 that the Assembly of Cuban Filmmakers rejected Resolution 61 of the Ministry of Culture and the Film Commission, which sets filming fees. The Assembly says it concentrates control in ICAIC and could work as indirect censorship.
- ICAIC ran the Muestra.

BOTTOM LINE
The event's money is state money through ICAIC; Norway's fund reaches the filmmakers, not the Muestra. The weak points are documented: ICAIC's 2018 relocation of a film, its 2020 removal of a documentary, the filmmakers' walkout and, per 14ymedio in 2025, the event's disappearance. Worth watching: whether ICAIC revives the Muestra and who governs it.
