KAPELICA GALLERY (KERSNIKOVA INSTITUTE): Ljubljana, Slovenia
ID: kapelica-gallery-kersnikova-institute-ljubljana
Website: https://kersnikova.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Gallery for contemporary and research-based art at Likozarjeva ulica 1, Ljubljana (culture.si portal).
- Established in 1995 by the Student Organisation of the University of Ljubljana (ŠOU) and led since then by the architect and curator Jurij Krpan (culture.si).
- Named after a former chapel (kapelica) at Kersnikova 4, its earlier premises (culture.si).
- Managed by the Kersnikova Institute, a non-profit founded in 2000 as the K6/4 Institute through a restructuring of ŠOU, which remains a founder with the Union of Student Clubs of Slovenia; it took the name Kersnikova in 2014 (culture.si).
- The institute had to leave Kersnikova Street in 2020 after ŠOU and the City of Ljubljana sold the site, and has been at Likozarjeva since 2021 (culture.si).
- Artists shown include Survival Research Laboratories, Stelarc, Orlan, Franco B. and Ron Athey (culture.si).
- The institute also runs BioTehna, a lab for artistic research into living systems, and Rampa, a hacker lab (culture.si).
- Kersnikova lists nine crew members, among them Krpan; its legal representative is Klemen Kristan, and it has six employees and four regular contractors (Kersnikova; Odprti računi).

FUNDING
Kapelica Gallery (Kersnikova Institute): mainly public: EU programmes, the Slovenian Ministry of Culture and the City of Ljubljana
- In 2025 the institute's income was 751,157 euros: 423,209 from European funds, 92,220 from the state, 42,200 from the city, 177,965 in other donations and subsidies and 15,372 from sales; spending was 823,684 euros (Odprti računi, figures posted by the institute).
- The Ministry of Culture awarded the institute 110,000 euros a year for its 2026 to 2029 programme, in the transdisciplinary programmes category (ministry results, JPR-PROG-2026-2029).
- The ministry also gave 5,720 euros in 2025 to show Dorotea Dolinšek's "Biosimbiotični eksoskelet" at DA Fest in Sofia (ministry results, JCP-MED-VIZ-JES-2025).
- Kersnikova lists 21 supporters: the Ministry of Culture, the City of Ljubljana (Mestna občina Ljubljana) and ŠOU; the European Commission, Creative Europe, Horizon 2020, the European Regional Development Fund, Interreg DIVA and the Swiss contribution; and projects including ART4MED, STARTS, CreaTures, Biofriction and AILab.
- culture.si lists EU projects with Kersnikova, among them European Media Art Platform Extended and the European ARTificial Intelligence Lab, and records it as a Creative Europe recipient.
- Its sponsorship page invites company sponsors and offers them talks and meetings with artists (stated by the space).
- The institute holds status as an organisation in the public interest in culture (Odprti računi).

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery runs on public money, mostly EU project grants with Slovenian state and city support, and none of its named funders falls under a flag.
