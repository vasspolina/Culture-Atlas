LE CONFORT MODERNE: Poitiers, France
ID: le-confort-moderne-poitiers
Website: https://www.confort-moderne.fr

OVERVIEW
- Concert, exhibition and arts venue at 185 rue du Faubourg du Pont-Neuf in Poitiers, near the university campus (Wikipedia).
- Opened on 20 May 1985, founded by Françoise Bordage, Yorrick Benoist, Francis Falceto and Philippe Auvin; the association L'Oreille est hardie, founded in 1977, has run it since 1985 (Wikipedia).
- Its name comes from "Confort 2000", the electrical appliance warehouse that the venue took over (Wikipedia).
- The city of Poitiers owns the buildings; a major renovation in 2017 was led by architect Nicole Condorcet (Wikipedia).
- On site: two concert halls of 800 and 200 seats, exhibition spaces (Entrepôt and Galerie Jardin), a restaurant, the Fanzinothèque, Jazz à Poitiers and the record shop Transat (Wikipedia, the venue's website).
- Open Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 18:00 and Saturday 14:00 to 18:00, and on event evenings; exhibitions are free; reduced rates can be offered depending on the concert and promoter, with proof, to job seekers, Carte Culture holders and under-16s accompanied by an adult; the venue is fully accessible to people with reduced mobility and free ear protection is available at the box office on concert evenings (the venue's website).

FUNDING
Le Confort Moderne: part public, part self-financed: city of Poitiers, département, région and State
- French Wikipedia states that the venue is financed in part by local authorities (the city of Poitiers, the département and the région) and by the State, and that it is self-financed for another part of its budget.
- The city of Poitiers was the client for the 2017 rehabilitation and extension, budgeted at 4.8 million euros excluding VAT (Climat Conseil project page).
- The city cut its subsidy in 1992 (Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Le Confort Moderne comes from the city of Poitiers, which owns the buildings, the département, the région and the State, with another part self-financed (French Wikipedia). The venue has been run since 1985 by the association L'Oreille est hardie, founded in 1977.
