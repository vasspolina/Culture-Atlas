ID: staatliche-kunsthalle-karlsruhe

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 2 February 2021 the Baden-Württemberg arts ministry announced that the Limbach Commission had recommended returning Erich Heckel's painting "Geschwister" from the Kunsthalle to the heirs of the Jewish collector Max Fischer; the Kunsthalle and the ministry had at first not considered it a restitution case and put it to the commission with the heirs' consent in September 2019 (Ministry of Science, Research and Arts Baden-Württemberg, press release 017/2021).
- In August 2020 the state agreed a payment at current market value with the heirs of Alfred and Gertrude Sommerguth for Franz Krüger's "Pferdestall", which the family owned until 1939 and which came into the Kunsthalle's possession in 1972; the painting stays in Karlsruhe and the sum was not disclosed (dpa via Jüdische Allgemeine, 4 August 2020).
