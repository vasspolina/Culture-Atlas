LE CONSORTIUM: Dijon, France
ID: le-consortium
Website: https://www.leconsortium.fr (checked 2026-10-25; redirects to https://www.consortiummuseum.com, where the centre now calls itself the Consortium Museum)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 37 rue de Longvic, Dijon, about a 20-minute walk from Dijon railway station and 10 minutes on foot from the musée des Beaux-arts (Consortium Museum website).
- Founded in 1977 by Xavier Douroux and Franck Gautherot, young academics who showed contemporary art on the first floor of an alternative bookstore. Éric Troncy joined in 1995 and Seungduk Kim in 2000; later directors included Stéphanie Moisdon and Anne Pontégnie (the museum's history page).
- Earlier names and moves: it grew out of the association Le Coin du Miroir; it received the Art Center label in 1982 and moved to a former appliance store; in 1983 Le Coin du Miroir, À La Limite and Déjà Vu merged; in 1991 it moved to a 4,000 m2 factory on the outskirts (Wikipedia).
- Building: since 2011 it occupies a 4,000 m2 building designed by Shigeru Ban (the museum's history page); Wikipedia credits Shigeru Ban and Jean de Gastines.
- What is there: exhibitions (October 2026: "The Wing, La Mer" and "L'Almanach 26", both to 21 March 2027), a collection of more than 500 works, mostly gifts from exhibiting artists, shown on the top floor, and the New Patrons (Nouveaux commanditaires) commissioning programme run with the Fondation de France. Les Presses du Réel, a publisher, and the Académie Conti in Vosne-Romanée are affiliated projects.
- Visiting: Wednesday to Sunday, 2 p.m. to 6 p.m., open until 8 p.m. on Fridays; admission €5, free on Fridays from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. Free entry for under-18s, disabled visitors and their helper, French students and others. The page gives no step-free access details.

FUNDING
Le Consortium: mixed: French state and local government, plus private supporters
- Sponsored by the Ministry of Culture and the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté regional directorate of cultural affairs (DRAC), the City of Dijon, the Bourgogne-Franche-Comté Region and the Côte-d'Or Departmental Council (stated by the museum). No amounts found.
- Private: the endowment fund Le Consortium Unlimited and Les Amis du Consortium, plus, named "in particular": DVF, Groupe Roger Martin, the Daniel Xu & Flora Huang Foundation, the M ART Foundation, Vanessa Guo & Jean-Mathieu Martini of Galerie Marguo, Page Gallery, Yan Pei-Ming and Ellen Wu (stated by the museum; no amounts or years).
- Le Coin du Miroir runs the museum; US donors can give through Friends of Fondation de France (Friends of Le Coin du Miroir page).
- No reporting found of annual accounts, of the size of any private gift, or of what each named supporter pays for.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money is French public funding plus a named group of private patrons and galleries; nothing found ties a funder to a flagged industry or a documented controversy. The weak points are that no amounts or accounts are posted and that several private supporters are commercial galleries or collectors with a market interest in the programme. Worth watching: any published accounts and the size of the private share.
