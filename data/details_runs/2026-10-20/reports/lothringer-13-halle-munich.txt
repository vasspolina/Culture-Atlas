LOTHRINGER 13 HALLE: Munich, Germany
ID: lothringer-13-halle-munich
Website: https://www.lothringer13.com

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition space for international and local contemporary art at Lothringer Straße 13 in Haidhausen, Munich, near Weißenburger Platz, with about 700 square metres in rotating group shows (muenchen.de).
- Founded in 1980 as a Künstlerwerkstatt in a former machine factory (German Wikipedia).
- The exhibition rooms are in a former engine-grinding workshop (Motorenschleiferei) in a rear building; the front building houses a space for research and information (City of Munich).
- Between 1980 and 1998 the city's culture department, guest curators and staff organised the exhibitions; since 1998 rotating independent curators have led the programme (German Wikipedia).
- A second unit, Lothringer13_Florida, a collective space for interdisciplinary and media projects, was created in 2014 (German Wikipedia).
- German Wikipedia states that Lisa Britzger became artistic director in 2020 and Kalas Liebfried took over as head in April 2025.
- Open Wednesday to Friday 12:00 to 20:00 and Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, closed Monday and Tuesday, and open only during a running event; admission is free and the entrance is barrier-free (muenchen.de).

FUNDING
Lothringer 13 Halle: public: run by the Kulturreferat of the city of Munich
- The Halle is run by the Kulturreferat of the Landeshauptstadt München, and entry to the exhibitions is free (City of Munich).
- German Wikipedia states that the Halle is funded by city grants, project money and donations.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Lothringer 13 Halle is municipal: the Kulturreferat of the city of Munich runs it, and German Wikipedia lists city grants, project money and donations as its funding.
