SHARJAH ART FOUNDATION: Sharjah, UAE
ID: sharjah-art-foundation-sharjah
Website: https://www.sharjahart.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art foundation whose exhibitions, performances and screenings run across the emirate of Sharjah, often in historic buildings repurposed as cultural and community centres (SAF press release, July 2026).
- Established in 2009 by Emiri Decree to take its programmes beyond the Sharjah Biennial, which began in 1993; Hoor Al Qasimi founded it, is President and Director, and has directed the Biennial since 2003 (SAF press release, July 2026).
- Programmes: the Biennial, the March Meeting, grants, residencies, commissions and publications; its collection holds over 5,000 objects (SAF website). Sharjah Biennial 16 (2025) showed more than 650 works at more than 17 venues (SAF press release).
- In July 2026 the ruler also made her President of the Sharjah Museums Authority (SAF press release).
- Open Saturday to Thursday 9:00 to 21:00 and Friday 16:00 to 21:00; most exhibitions are free, with free tours in five languages and wheelchair-accessible washrooms at every venue (SAF website).

FUNDING
Sharjah Art Foundation: state-backed: Sharjah government money and the ruler's patronage, with corporate sponsors
- The Foundation calls itself "a legally independent public body established by Emiri Decree and supported by government funding, grants from national and international nonprofits and cultural organisations, corporate sponsors and individual patrons"; it names no amounts (stated by the space).
- Ghalya Saadawi wrote in June 2011 that the Foundation was funded by the ruler, Sheikh Sultan, and through Sharjah's Ministry of Culture (e-flux Journal, issue 26).
- Sharjah Biennial 16 was "made possible" by the Sharjah Tourism and Commerce Development Authority, Crescent Enterprises and Alef Group, whose CEO Issa Ataya said it was "proud to sponsor" the edition (SAF press release, 2025).
- Crescent Enterprises, whose CEO Badr Jafar called it a "proud patron and strategic partner" of the 2025 Biennial (SAF press release), is the Crescent Group's arm for its non-petroleum businesses; the group's other main subsidiary, Crescent Petroleum, is in its words the Middle East's "oldest private oil and gas company" (Crescent Group website).
- The ruler is known to buy work from Sharjah's exhibitions (Melissa Gronlund, The Art Newspaper, 2025).

Red flags: Sharjah government funding under the ruler's daughter, set up by Emiri Decree in 2009 (Gulf state body)
- Hoor Al Qasimi, the ruler's daughter, runs the government-funded Foundation (The Art Newspaper, 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Substantial criticism of the 2011 dismissal and censorship; later reviews mostly admire the Biennial.
1. e-flux Journal: Ghalya Saadawi, "Post-Sharjah Biennial 10: Institutional Grease and Institutional Critique" (2011)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/journal/26/67970/post-sharjah-biennial-10-institutional-grease-and-institutional-critique
- Says the Foundation and its New York PR firm ran a campaign defending the ruler's decision to oust Persekian; argues that institutions everywhere depend on state or capital money.
2. ArtAsiaPacific: unsigned, "Director's Ouster Jeopardizes Sharjah Art Foundation's Future" (2011)
URL: https://www.artasiapacific.com/news/director-s-ouster-jeopardizes-sharjah-art-foundation-s-future/
- Reports Hoor Al Qasimi's statement that the work paired sexually explicit language with religious references "in an overt and provocative manner", and that an audio work was altered to avoid clashing with the call to prayer.
3. Jadaliyya: unsigned, "Boat Rocking in the Art Islands: Politics, Plots and Dismissals in Sharjah's Tenth Biennial" (2011)
URL: https://www.jadaliyya.com/Details/23938
- Reports 1,627 signatures on the petition, which pledged a boycott of the Foundation; questions Western museums' ties to the UAE's rulers.
4. Sharjah Call for Action, on e-flux: "An unwarranted dismissal in Sharjah" (2011)
URL: https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/35699/an-unwarranted-dismissal-in-sharjah/
- Open letter: the ruler dismissed Persekian on 6 April 2011; other works were taken down, altered or put "under review".
5. The Art Newspaper: Nadine Khalil, review of Sharjah Biennial 15 (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/02/20/sharjah-biennial-15-delivers-important-postcolonial-narrativebut-loses-its-experimental-edge
- Finds the curating "a bit too homogeneous" and the experimental flavour of past editions gone. Criticises the exhibition, not the institution.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2011: after an outcry over Mustapha Benfodil's installation "Maportaliche" at the 10th Biennial, the work was removed and director Jack Persekian was dismissed; the Foundation said the petition was "deliberately misinforming the public" (The National, April 2011).
- The Art Newspaper (2025) wrote that self-censorship persists in Sharjah, but that under Hoor Al Qasimi the Foundation widened what can be said about migrant labourers.
- On 11 May 2015 the Gulf Labor artist Walid Raad was turned back at Dubai airport on his way to the Foundation's March Meeting (The Art Newspaper, 2015).
- The connection: the ruler who dismissed its director in 2011 is its patron; the 2015 ban stopped an artist reaching its own event.

BOTTOM LINE
A Gulf state body: the emirate's money under its ruling family, with a Biennial sponsor from a family group founded on oil. Watch the sponsors of Sharjah Biennial 17.
