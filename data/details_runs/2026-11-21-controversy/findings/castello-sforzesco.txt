ID: castello-sforzesco

Red flags: 2022 dispute over pay of outsourced museum reception staff
- In March 2022 about 200 reception workers employed by outside companies at Milan's civic museums and libraries, among them the exhibition rooms of the Castello Sforzesco, declared a state of agitation; Il Giorno reported full-time pay of about 750 euros a month (Il Giorno, 24 March 2022).
- Roberta Griffini of Filcams-Cgil Milan said the City of Milan had not opened talks on guarantees and protections in the next tender, despite repeated requests (Il Giorno, 24 March 2022).
- The workers were employed under the national contract for private security and fiduciary services, applied at the 2018 change of contractor; Filcams asked for the culture-sector contract and for every worker to be kept on at the next change (Il Giorno, 24 March 2022).
- At an assembly on 25 May 2022 the workers gave the union a mandate for an eight-hour strike package and called a two-hour assembly for 31 May, which would close the civic museums for two hours (Il Giorno, 26 May 2022; Finestre sull'Arte, 28 May 2022).
