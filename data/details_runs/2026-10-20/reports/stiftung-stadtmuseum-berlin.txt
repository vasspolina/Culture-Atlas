STIFTUNG STADTMUSEUM BERLIN: Berlin, Germany
ID: stiftung-stadtmuseum-berlin
Website: https://www.stadtmuseum.de

OVERVIEW
- Public-law foundation, officially the Landesmuseum für Kultur und Geschichte Berlins, running Berlin's city history museums. A state law created it on 23 June 1995, bringing together museums that had been run by support associations (German Wikipedia).
- Sites: Märkisches Museum, Museum Ephraim-Palais, Museum Nikolaikirche, Museum Knoblauchhaus, Marinehaus, Museumsdorf Düppel and a Berlin exhibition in the Humboldt Forum (German Wikipedia). Its website lists the Ephraim-Palais, Knoblauchhaus, Nikolaikirche, Museumsdorf Düppel and the Berlin exhibition in the Humboldt Forum as exhibition venues.
- The Märkisches Museum was founded in 1874; the building in Köllnischer Park was designed by Ludwig Hoffmann, built from 1899 to 1907 and opened on 10 June 1908 (Wikipedia).
- The Märkisches Museum has been closed since the start of 2023 for a full renovation led by the Berlin architects SSP Rüthnick; the Marinehaus opposite is being converted into a second site with an interior concept by the architects ADEPT (foundation press release, 25 October 2023). The foundation's October 2023 press release gave 2028 for reopening; BauNetz Wissen, describing the foundation's building journal, gives 2028/29 for the museum and creative quarter at Köllnischer Park.
- The foundation holds about 4.5 million objects, most of them in the central depot in the Poelzig-Halle in Hakenfelde since 2010 (German Wikipedia).
- The Jüdisches Museum Berlin was a founding part of the foundation and became a federal foundation in 2001 (German Wikipedia).
- In summer 2026 the foundation began presenting itself under the brand name "Berlin Museum"; the foundation name is unchanged (German Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Stiftung Stadtmuseum Berlin: public: set up by the State of Berlin, with federal and lottery foundation money for the renovation of the Märkisches Museum and Marinehaus
- Public-law foundation set up by Berlin state law in 1995 (German Wikipedia). Berlin's culture senator Joe Chialo took part in the October 2023 handover of the Märkisches Museum to the building client BIM Berliner Immobilienmanagement GmbH (foundation press release).
- Renovation of the Märkisches Museum and Marinehaus: the Berlin Senate's reply of 15 December 2015 (Abgeordnetenhaus document 17/17500) put the total financing need at 65 million euros and listed 32.5 million euros from the federal government, as a grant of 50 percent of costs, on the condition that Berlin pays an equal amount; 10.3 million euros from the Stiftung Deutsche Klassenlotterie Berlin (Lotto foundation), whose basic funding commitment it said was in place; and 22.2 million euros from the Berlin Senate Chancellery, cultural affairs, budget.
- Of that, 38 million euros were planned for the Märkisches Museum and 27 million euros for the Marinehaus (same Senate reply).
- The foundation's October 2023 press release says the federal government's Commissioner for Culture and Media and the Stiftung Deutsche Klassenlotterie Berlin fund the project.
- Verein der Freunde und Förderer des Stadtmuseums Berlin e. V., the museum's friends association, runs the crowdfunding campaign "Rettet das SEZ-Modell!" to restore and display the architectural model of the Sport- und Erholungszentrum (foundation website).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation was set up by Berlin state law in 1995 and its renovation money is public: the federal government's culture commissioner and the Berlin Lotto foundation fund the Märkisches Museum and Marinehaus project (foundation press release, 2023). The Senate's 2015 reply planned its 65 million euros as 32.5 million federal, 22.2 million Berlin and 10.3 million Lotto foundation. The Märkisches Museum is closed, with reopening planned for 2028 or 2029.
