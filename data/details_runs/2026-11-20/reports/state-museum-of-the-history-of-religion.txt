STATE MUSEUM OF THE HISTORY OF RELIGION: Saint Petersburg, Russia
ID: state-museum-of-the-history-of-religion
Website: https://www.gmir.ru (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Federal museum of the history of religions at Pochtamtskaya Street 14, near the city's Legislative Assembly and St Isaac's Cathedral (gmir.ru).
- The museum states that it was set up within the USSR Academy of Sciences by decisions of 7 September 1930 and 26 April 1931, with the historian of religion and ethnographer V. G. Bogoraz-Tan as founder and first director (gmir.ru).
- Its first collections came from the Kunstkamera, the Hermitage, the Academy of Sciences library and the Russian Museum, objects used in an anti-religious exhibition the Academy staged in the Winter Palace in April 1930 (gmir.ru).
- It opened in Kazan Cathedral on 15 November 1932 and was named the State Museum of the History of Religion and Atheism in 1954 (gmir.ru).
- The museum's own history states that after its transfer to the USSR Ministry of Culture in 1961 its work turned towards open atheist propaganda, under direct pressure from the Leningrad regional Communist Party committee (gmir.ru).
- It took back its original name in 1990; a decision of May 1991 gave it the Pochtamtskaya Street building, and it moved there after reconstruction in 2000 (gmir.ru).
- Curatorial departments cover the religions of the East, Orthodoxy, archaic societies and the ancient world, and Western Christianity, with a manuscripts and rare books department and a research library; it runs "virtual branches" in museums in Buryatia, Chechnya, Komi, Dagestan and the Volga region (gmir.ru).
- Ekaterina Teryukova, deputy director for research since 2007, became director on 2 October 2024, and her predecessor Lyubov Musienko became the museum's president (Fontanka, 2024).
- Visiting: open Thursday to Monday 10:00 to 18:00 and Tuesday 13:00 to 21:00, closed Wednesday. Adults pay 450 roubles, pensioners and visitors aged 7 to 18 pay 250; disabled visitors of groups 1 and 2, veterans and participants in the war in Ukraine with their families enter free, and the first Monday of the month is a free day for schoolchildren, students and pensioners (gmir.ru).

FUNDING
State Museum of the History of Religion: public: Russian federal budget
- A federal state budgetary institution of culture whose founder is the Russian Federation; the Ministry of Culture exercises the founder's powers (gmir.ru, official documents; financial plan 2022).
- Its 2022 financial plan put total income at 178,779,164 roubles, of which 165,879,378 was the federal subsidy for its state assignment and 11,910,000 came from its own paid activities (financial plan 2022 to 2024, signed 10 October 2022).
- The same plan listed 811,055 roubles in 2022 from grants and donations by individuals and organisations, without naming the givers, and kept the federal subsidy at 165,879,378 roubles for 2023 and 2024 (financial plan 2022 to 2024).

WIDER CONTEXT
- The UK designated Russia's Minister of Culture, Olga Lyubimova, on 17 July 2023 for supporting policies that undermine Ukraine's sovereignty (UK Sanctions List).
- Lyubimova leads the ministry that acts as the museum's founder.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs on public money from the Russian state, with no flagged funder. The unnamed private donors are worth watching.
