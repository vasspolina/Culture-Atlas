ANITKABIR (ATATÜRK MAUSOLEUM AND MUSEUM): Ankara, Turkey
ID: an-tkabir-ataturk-mausoleum-and-museum
Website: https://www.anitkabir.tsk.tr (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Mausoleum of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, founder of the Turkish Republic, on a hill in Ankara; it also holds the tomb of İsmet İnönü (Anıtkabir Command).
- Designed by Emin Onat and Orhan Arda, chosen by competition (Wikipedia). The Ministry of Public Works tendered the first stage, and Prime Minister Şükrü Saracoğlu attended the groundbreaking on 9 October 1944 (Anıtkabir Command). Atatürk's remains were moved here on 10 November 1953 (Wikipedia).
- Critics of the building included Şevki Vanlı, who called it "the structure in Turkey with the greatest Nazi influence" (Wikipedia).
- The Atatürk and War of Independence Museum fills 3,000 square metres beneath the Hall of Honour; its first section, Atatürk's belongings, opened on 21 June 1960. The complex also holds Atatürk's library and study (Anıtkabir Command) and his restored cars (Time Out İstanbul, 2018).
- The Peace Park covers 630,000 of the site's 750,000 square metres, planted with saplings from abroad and from regions of Turkey (Anıtkabir Command).
- Visits: 3,966,286 in 2022, 6,030,680 in 2023, 6,550,480 in 2024 and 8,242,170 in 2025; the busiest day of 2025 was 10 November, the anniversary of Atatürk's death, with 1,219,148 (NTV, 2 January 2026, from the Anıtkabir Command's figures).
- Visiting: open every day from 09:00, closing at 16:30 from 1 February to 14 May, 17:00 from 15 May to 31 October and 16:00 from 1 November to 31 January. Groups that book with the Command get a free guide; an audio guide in six languages is paid. The site states it can be visited without barriers (Anıtkabir Command).

FUNDING
Anıtkabir: public: run and paid for by the Turkish armed forces
- Law No. 2524, published in the Official Gazette on 15 September 1981, makes the General Staff responsible for all services at Anıtkabir and its museum, library and other facilities, and pays all its costs from the Ministry of National Defence budget (Law No. 2524, mevzuat.gov.tr).
- The same law repealed Law No. 6780, under which the Ministry of Education had run the site, and moved the Ministry of Culture's 1981 allocation for it to the defence budget (Law No. 2524).
- Ceremony requests go to the Ministry of National Defence, the General Staff, the 4th Corps and Ankara Garrison Command, and the Anıtkabir Command (Anıtkabir Command).
- The Anıtkabir Association, founded in 1968 in a building at the site, pays for needs of the Command that the state budget does not cover (Wikipedia).
- Türkiye İş Bankası supports the Command and the Association in conserving Atatürk's cars: it had climate-controlled display cases made for the 1934 and 1935 Lincolns, restored through the Antique Automobile Federation, and restoration began of the 1936 Cadillac the bank gave Atatürk (Time Out İstanbul, 2018).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Criticism is academic and concerns the state cult the site serves, not its money.
1. University of Edinburgh (PhD thesis): Canan Neşe Karahasan, "Exhibiting 'Turkishness' at a time of flux in Turkey: an ethnography of the state" (2015)
URL: https://era.ed.ac.uk/handle/1842/15920
- Compares Anıtkabir, under the command of the armed forces, "the guardian of secularism", with Topkapı Palace under the culture ministry, as state institutions displaying competing pasts.
- Argues that Anıtkabir "re-sacralises and humanises Atatürk's cult".
- Only the abstract was read, not the full thesis.

WIDER CONTEXT
- The 1982 regulation bans ceremonies, marches and demonstrations at Anıtkabir for any purpose other than respect for Atatürk (Wikipedia).
- On 22 April 2025 the Ankara Governor's Office refused the CHP's request to march from the first parliament building to Anıtkabir the next day, citing traffic and the route not being a designated rally area (NTV).
- On 23 October 2025 CHP deputy chair Yankı Bağcıoğlu said political chants at official ceremonies had gone on for nine years and objected to police searching uniformed officers at the 30 August 2025 ceremony (Cumhuriyet).
- Connection: the state bodies that run the mausoleum decide who can gather there, and how.

BOTTOM LINE
State money only: no funding concern. What matters is political control of a national shrine, not its funders.
