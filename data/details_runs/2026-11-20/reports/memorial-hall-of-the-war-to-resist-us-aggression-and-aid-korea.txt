MEMORIAL HALL OF THE WAR TO RESIST US AGGRESSION AND AID KOREA: Dandong, China
ID: memorial-hall-of-the-war-to-resist-us-aggression-and-aid-korea
Website: https://www.kmycjng.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- State memorial to China's part in the Korean War on Yinghua Mountain above the Yalu River in Dandong, facing the North Korean city of Sinuiju across the river (museum website).
- It began in 1953 as a preparatory office for a Liaodong provincial museum, took the name Andong Memorial of the War to Resist US Aggression and Aid Korea in September 1958, closed for a time after the Cultural Revolution began in 1966 and reopened on a Dandong Party committee decision in 1972 (Chinese Wikipedia, citing the museum's history page).
- The Party Central Committee, the State Council and the Central Military Commission approved a new building in October 1990; it opened in July 1993 on the 40th anniversary of the armistice (Chinese Wikipedia; English Wikipedia).
- The Propaganda Department of the Party Central Committee listed it in July 1997 in its first hundred national patriotic education demonstration bases (list published on The Paper's government channel).
- After central approval on 3 June 2014 it closed on 29 December 2014 for expansion and reopened on 19 September 2020, on the 70th anniversary of China's entry into the war (museum's 2025 accounts; Xinhua, state media).
- The site covers 182,000 square metres: a 23,800 square metre exhibition hall, a 53 metre memorial tower inscribed in Deng Xiaoping's calligraphy, a panorama hall with a 132.15 metre painting of the Chongchon River battle, and a national defence education park with Soviet T-34 tanks, a MiG-15 and other weapons, with more than 20,000 relics in the collection (museum website).
- Deputy curator Gong Shaoshan said the expansion raised the exhibition area from 5,400 to 23,845 square metres and the items shown from over 700 to more than 1,600 (China Daily, state media).
- The Central Military Commission classes it as a national major war memorial, and the museum reports more than 20 million visits since it opened (museum's 2025 accounts).
- Shi Chunqing is Party secretary and director (museum website).
- Kim Jong Un visited on 24 October 2026 with Wang Huning, chairman of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, and laid a basket at the memorial tower (Xinhua, state media).
- In June 2026 South Korea's Defence Ministry opened an inspection after the operator of the War Memorial of Korea put the Dandong hall on a teacher training trip, calling the itinerary "a grave error" (Korea JoongAng Daily).
- Visiting: free, booked ahead through the museum's WeChat account, mini program or website; open Tuesday to Sunday 9:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00, closed Mondays except national holidays (museum notice, Liaoning Daily, October 2026).

FUNDING
Memorial Hall of the War to Resist US Aggression and Aid Korea: state-funded: the Dandong city budget, on central approval
- A fully funded public institution of county rank under the Dandong culture and tourism bureau, with 110 staff posts since April 2022; it has also been the city's Korean War research centre since November 2021 (museum's 2025 accounts).
- 2025 income was 38.36 million yuan: 36.86 million (96.07 percent) from general public budget appropriations and 0.85 million in fees, mainly for guided tours (museum's 2025 accounts).
- 2025 spending was 37.34 million yuan: 21.65 million on running costs, of which 13.99 million on wages, and 15.69 million on projects, mainly payments for the expansion, relic digitisation and research on the "spirit" of the war (museum's 2025 accounts).
- Dandong city started the expansion in June 2013 on the instruction of central leaders. Payments for it were still being made in 2025 outside the year's budget, so project spending reached 1,155 percent of the amount budgeted (museum's 2025 accounts).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the hall is Chinese public money and carries no funder flag. Its weak point is its purpose: it is run by the state as a site of patriotic and national defence education. Worth watching: the full cost of the 2014 to 2020 expansion.
