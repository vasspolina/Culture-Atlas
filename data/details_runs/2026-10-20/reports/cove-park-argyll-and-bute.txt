COVE PARK: Argyll and Bute, United Kingdom
ID: cove-park-argyll-and-bute
Website: https://covepark.org

OVERVIEW
- Artists' residency centre at Peaton Hill, Cove, on the Rosneath Peninsula, on a 50-acre site overlooking Loch Long (Northings, 2006).
- Founded in 1999 by Eileen and Peter Jacobs (Northings, 2006); registered as a Scottish charity on 26 October 1999 (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator data via Find that Charity).
- Named buildings: the Jacobs Building takes the founders' name. Cameron Webster Architects designed the new artists' centre, a single-storey building with two accommodation units, two studios, workshop and performance spaces, built under a £1.4 million contract awarded to Clark Contracts in November 2015 (Project Scotland).
- Earlier accommodation: two refurbished pods originally commissioned by the BBC for its "Castaway 2000" programme, and converted freight containers called "Cubes"; the Scottish Arts Council agreed funding for six new Cubes in 2006 (Northings).
- Programme: residencies for artists, plus the engagement programme Collective Home: Community, Creativity & Action in Argyll, which director Alexia Holt discussed in The Lochside Press (16 March 2026).
- In January 2026 Cove Park was developing a soundproofed studio for musicians and sound artists with an adjacent residency unit, designed by Cameron Webster Architects (The Lochside Press, 24 January 2026).

FUNDING
Cove Park: Creative Scotland, charitable trusts and individuals
- Income was £610,540 and spending £524,173 in the year to 31 March 2025 (OSCR data via Find that Charity).
- Creative Scotland: £286,294 from its Culture Collective initiative for the Collective Home engagement programme, reported on 16 March 2026; Cove Park is one of 23 organisations across Scotland to receive Culture Collective funding, and Creative Scotland's Karen Dick said a Scottish Government budget commitment lets Culture Collective build on its pilot programme (The Lochside Press).
- 2015 artists' centre: funded by Creative Scotland and a number of trusts and individuals, including major awards from the Robertson and Monument Trusts (Project Scotland, 13 November 2015).
- 2026 sound studio: major funding came from the Bridge Awards (The Lochside Press, 24 January 2026).
- 2006: the Scottish Arts Council agreed funding for six new Cubes (Northings).

BOTTOM LINE
Cove Park is a registered charity with income of £610,540 in the year to 31 March 2025. Creative Scotland awarded it £286,294 of Culture Collective money in March 2026, and the Robertson and Monument Trusts made major awards toward its 2015 artists' centre. The founders Eileen and Peter Jacobs started it in 1999 and the Jacobs Building carries their name.
