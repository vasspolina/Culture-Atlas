ID: chinati-foundation

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2021 the Chinati Foundation board resolved not to renew Rob Weiner's contract. An open letter in the Big Bend Sentinel of 1 April 2021, signed by 89 people including Larry Bell, Roni Horn, Robert Irwin, Zoe Leonard, Eileen Myles, Wallace Shawn and Christopher Wool, began: "We, the undersigned, write to express our disapproval of the termination of Rob Weiner's contract" (Big Bend Sentinel).
- The same letter called the decision part of a "larger pattern" in Chinati's leadership and said the leadership had strayed from Donald Judd's values. In a separate letter, Richard Maxwell, a former artist in residence, wrote that the word "boycott" came to mind and that if Chinati insisted on a future without Weiner he would not go there (Big Bend Sentinel).
- The Chinati Foundation Board of Trustees replied in the same issue that it "made the difficult but unanimous decision to advise Rob that we intend to end our consulting arrangement," and said it had not accepted Weiner's resignation four years earlier (Big Bend Sentinel).
- Reporting director Jenny Moore's departure on 2 August 2022, Marfa Public Radio wrote that the decision not to renew a longtime employee's contract "sparked a backlash among some in the art world"; Moore said "change at any organization is inevitable" (Marfa Public Radio).
