AMMAN DESIGN WEEK: Amman, Jordan
ID: amman-design-week
Website: https://ammandesignweek.com (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Biennial design festival held across Amman. The 2026 edition ran from 2 to 17 October across Amman and, for the first time, Aqaba, with 59 participating spaces, 204 events and over 220 designers (Yanko Design, 30 September 2026).
- Founded in 2016. The Jordan Times (30 August 2016) calls it an initiative of Queen Rania and says she conceived it; Yanko Design says she founded it. Earlier editions followed in 2017 and 2019; 2026 is the fourth, returning after seven years (Yanko Design).
- Not tied to one building. The 2026 main exhibition is at the Ras El Ain Hangar, with other venues at Darat Abu Adnan in Jabal Al Lweibdeh, Iraq Al Amir including Qasr Al Abd, and Ayla Oasis in Aqaba (e-flux, 2026 edition).
- Programmes: exhibitions, a student exhibition and mentorship programme, school partnerships through the Madrasati initiative, and an Ithra Design Week x ADW residency pairing designers from Jordan and Saudi Arabia (Yanko Design).
- 2026 curators: Faysal Tabbarah for exhibitions, with the theme "Accumulations", and Khaled Al-Bashir for programmes (Yanko Design; e-flux for Tabbarah). The 2025 codirectors were Bisher Tabba and Sarah Hejazin (Queen Rania's office, 25 August 2025).
- Visiting: the 2026 main exhibition was free and open to the public (e-flux).

FUNDING
Amman Design Week: royal-patronage festival with a municipal partner and private sponsors
- Queen Rania Al Abdullah is the patron; the 2017 edition was held under her patronage (Queen Rania's office, 4 October 2017).
- Greater Amman Municipality: organised the event with ADW in 2016 (Jordan Times) and again in 2026 (e-flux).
- 2016 sponsors: Al Manaseer Group, Zain, Aramex and Al Markaziya, the Lexus exclusive dealer in Jordan (Jordan Times, 30 August 2016).
- 2019: "The European Union in Jordan supported the event" (EU Medbridge, 14 October 2019).
- 2026: the Sukhtian Foundation supported the third Student Exhibition (e-flux). Queen Rania's office said in August 2025 that the edition would be supported by private and public sector partners.
- Aqaba component developed with WUJUD Art Foundation (Yanko Design; e-flux).
- The Ithra Design Week x ADW residency pairs designers from Jordan and Saudi Arabia (Yanko Design).

BOTTOM LINE
Funding is a mix of royal patronage, a municipal partner, Jordanian corporate sponsors and, in 2019, EU support. For 2026 the Sukhtian Foundation and WUJUD Art Foundation are the named partners. Worth watching: the full 2026 sponsor list and the Ithra residency.
