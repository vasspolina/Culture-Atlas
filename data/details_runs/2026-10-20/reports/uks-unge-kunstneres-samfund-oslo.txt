UKS (UNGE KUNSTNERES SAMFUND / YOUNG ARTISTS' SOCIETY): Oslo, Norway
ID: uks-unge-kunstneres-samfund-oslo
Website: https://www.uks.no

OVERVIEW
- Membership organisation for professional artists and an exhibition space for contemporary art at Keysers gate 1 in Oslo, at Tullinløkka (UKS website, Store norske leksikon).
- Founded by artists in 1921; it has run its own exhibition venue in Oslo since 1937 (UKS website).
- Joining UKS includes membership in the Norwegian Association of Visual Artists (NBK). In 2024 UKS had about 700 members and was the largest basic organisation within NBK (Store norske leksikon).
- Exhibitions are chosen by working artists on the board and the director, with the programme drawn from an annual open call (UKS website).
- Besides exhibitions it holds artist talks and public events and administers artist grants and residency places (Store norske leksikon), and it does political work through consultations and hearings; its main projects are climate and sustainability, Palestine, the studio situation and the report on artists' living conditions (UKS website).
- Governed by a chair and six board members elected for two-year terms by the annual general assembly; the current chair is Ragnhild Aamås (UKS website).
- Its archive of nearly 100 years is held by the Riksarkivet in Oslo (Store norske leksikon).
- Visiting: during exhibition periods open Wednesday 12 to 17, Thursday 12 to 19, Friday to Sunday 12 to 17; there is no entrance fee (UKS website).

FUNDING
UKS: mainly public: the Norwegian Ministry of Culture
- UKS describes itself as a non-profit organisation with the Ministry of Culture as its principal funder (UKS website).
- Store norske leksikon states it is mainly financed by the Ministry of Culture and Equality.

BOTTOM LINE
UKS is an artist-run membership organisation financed mainly by the Norwegian Ministry of Culture, with a published board elected by its members.
