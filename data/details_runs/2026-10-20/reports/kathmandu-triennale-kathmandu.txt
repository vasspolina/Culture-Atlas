KATHMANDU TRIENNALE: Kathmandu, Nepal
ID: kathmandu-triennale-kathmandu
Website: https://kathmandutriennale.org/

OVERVIEW
- International contemporary art exhibition held across venues in Kathmandu; its site footer calls it the "premier arts festival of Nepal" (stated by the Triennale).
- Began in 2009 as the Kathmandu International Art Festival (KIAF), with editions in 2009 and 2012 on the status of women and climate change; it became the Kathmandu Triennale in 2017 (Triennale's own story page, Kathmandu Post).
- Organised by the Siddhartha Arts Foundation (SAF), which initiated the festival and its predecessor. For the fifth edition SAF co-organised with the Nepal Academy of Fine Arts (NAFA), which signed a memorandum of understanding with SAF in December 2023 and is named the premier national partner (Kathmandu Post, Post Report, 29 December 2023).
- Fourth edition, "Kathmandu Triennale 2077": 11 February to 31 March 2022, organised by the Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation of Nepal and SAF, with Hong Kong's Para Site as partner institution. Artistic director Cosmin Costinas, with co-curators Sheelasha Rajbhandari and Hit Man Gurung. Venues were Patan Museum, Bahadur Shah Baithak, Nepal Art Council, Taragaon Museum and Siddhartha Art Gallery (e-flux, Triennale).
- Fifth edition, "Coexistence": February 2026, with artistic directors Natalie King and Sujan Chitrakar. The programme includes a Students' Triennale, satellite exhibitions and workshops beyond the Kathmandu Valley, and more than 100 artworks (Triennale story page, Kathmandu Post, 12 August 2024).

FUNDING
Kathmandu Triennale: organised by the Siddhartha Arts Foundation with the Nepal Academy of Fine Arts; the 2022 edition was organised with Nepal's culture ministry; Australian Embassy patronage
- The Triennale's supporter page names the Siddhartha Arts Foundation and the Nepal Academy of Fine Arts as organisers for 2026. The 2022 edition was organised jointly with Nepal's Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation.
- The Australian Embassy "supported and patronised previous editions" and hosted a reception to meet potential patrons for the 2026 edition (Kathmandu Post, 12 August 2024).
- Organisers said in December 2023 that they planned to raise money through a "basket fund" and would work with central, provincial and local government bodies to widen the Triennale's reach (Kathmandu Post, 29 December 2023).
- A review of the 2022 edition by Yifawn Lee in Orientations (24 August 2022) says more than seventy local, national and international non-profit organisations, government entities, corporations, media sponsors and individuals helped make it happen.

BOTTOM LINE
The Triennale is organised by the Siddhartha Arts Foundation with the Nepal Academy of Fine Arts, and the 2022 edition was organised with Nepal's culture ministry. The Triennale's own page and the Kathmandu Post (12 August 2024) name the Australian Embassy as a supporter of earlier editions.
