CULTURGEST: Lisbon, Portugal
ID: culturgest-lisbon
Website: https://www.culturgest.pt

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre and foundation (Culturgest, Fundação Caixa Geral de Depósitos) at Rua Arco do Cego 50, Lisbon, in the head-office building of Caixa Geral de Depósitos (CGD). Campo Pequeno metro station is about 450 m away; a second venue operates in Porto.
- Set up by Caixa Geral de Depósitos, a state-owned financial group, as a private foundation; it opened to the public in October 1993 (stated by the foundation).
- Programme: performing arts, music, visual arts, cinema and contemporary thought, plus lectures, debates, workshops, guided tours and programmes for schools and families. It also holds and catalogues the CGD art collection online.
- On site: two auditoriums (the Emílio Rui Vilar Auditorium and a Small Auditorium), galleries, a box office, a bookshop and a café.
- Visiting: galleries, box office and bookshop are open Tuesday to Sunday, 11am to 6pm, and closed in August. Auditoriums, box office and galleries are step-free by ramp or lift. Exhibition admission is free for disabled people and one companion; shows offer 50% off for disabled, Deaf and blind people plus one companion.

FUNDING
Culturgest: corporate-foundation funded: Caixa Geral de Depósitos, a state-owned bank
- CGD gave the foundation an initial endowment of EUR 3.5 million and supports it with an annual allocation of funds and by providing the spaces and facilities it uses (stated by the foundation).
- The foundation's site posts a table of financial support received for 2009 to 2025 and audit reports for 2004 to 2025.
- Public-utility status was confirmed in 2013, 2018 and 2023 (stated by the foundation).
- Board of Administration for 2024 to 2026: Paulo Moita de Macedo (president), Marc Maurits Peter Deputter, Francisco Viana, Maria João Gonçalves and Manuela Duro Teixeira; Ernst & Young is the statutory auditor (stated by the foundation).

BOTTOM LINE
Culturgest is funded by Caixa Geral de Depósitos, a state-owned financial group, through an initial EUR 3.5 million endowment and a yearly allocation, and it uses the bank's headquarters building. The foundation publishes its accounts and board.
