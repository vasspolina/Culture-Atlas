MUMOK: Vienna, Austria
ID: mumok
Website: https://www.mumok.at (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Federal museum of modern and contemporary art at Museumsplatz 1, in Vienna's MuseumsQuartier (German Wikipedia; mumok website).
- Opened on 20 September 1962 as the Museum des 20. Jahrhunderts in the 20er Haus; Werner Hofmann was the founding director. It was renamed in 1991 (German Wikipedia).
- Its full name, Museum moderner Kunst Stiftung Ludwig Wien, refers to Peter and Irene Ludwig, whose loans and gift of about 230 works in 1981 led to the Österreichische Ludwig-Stiftung (German Wikipedia).
- Building: the current building by Ortner & Ortner opened on 15 September 2001, clad in volcanic rock, with 4,800 m2 of exhibition space (German Wikipedia).
- Collection of about 10,000 works by roughly 1,600 artists, with strengths in classical modernism, Pop Art, Fluxus and Viennese Actionism (German Wikipedia). The museum closed from 8 January to 6 June 2024 for renovation, which German Wikipedia puts at about six million euros; visits fell 35.41 per cent to 142,322 that year (Vienna.at, citing APA).
- Directors: Karola Kraus from 2010 and Fatima Hellberg from 1 October 2025 (German Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm. Ticket categories are regular, students under 27, seniors 65 and over, and under 19; the Bundesmuseen Card and annual passes are accepted. The accessibility page was not read.

FUNDING
mumok: mainly public: the Republic of Austria, with corporate sponsors and Ludwig foundations
- Federal museum, funded by the Republic of Austria (German Wikipedia; mumok website).
- The mumok website lists sponsors Dorotheum, Uniqa, Gerngross and legero united the shoemakers; patrons the Peter und Irene Ludwig Stiftung, the Art Mentor Foundation Lucerne and the Österreichische Ludwig-Stiftung; cooperation partners Vöslauer, Kattus Wiener Schaumwein Manufaktur and Hofkellerei des Fürsten von Liechtenstein; media partners Ö1 Club, Falter and Der Standard.
- The sponsors page gives no amounts or years, and does not list Audi or Erste Bank.
- 2023 financial statements (mumok website, PDF, text extracted locally): federal base funding (Basisabgeltung) EUR 11,196,000 (2022: TEUR 10,088); sponsoring revenue EUR 181,631.80 (2022: TEUR 185); donations and other grants EUR 383,634.92 (2022: TEUR 636).
- Same statements: a sponsor contract with Legero Schuhfabrik worth EUR 20,202.50 net in 2023, owned by Stefan Stolitzka, a trustee since 1 January 2012; SLE Schuh supported the fundraising dinner with EUR 6,000.
- No reporting found of a fossil fuel, tobacco, gambling or arms funder among those named.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the institution. The only critical piece found is a newspaper column attacking the appointment of the current director.
1. News (Austria): Heinz Sichrovsky, "Das Mumok versinkt in Phrasen und Antisemitismus" (column, page dated 31 May 2024)
URL: https://www.news.at/a/spitzentoene-mumok-phrasen-antisemitismus-13375447
- Unverified: Sichrovsky says Fatima Hellberg signed an open letter titled "Befreiung der Palästinenser" whose first version did not mention Israeli victims of Hamas, and calls her position antisemitic. He gives no response from Hellberg or the museum. The page is an opinion column in a weekly news magazine, not art press.
- Says she gave buzzword-filled remarks at her presentation and has led only two Kunstvereine, in Stuttgart and Bonn, never a museum.
- Demands her contract be dissolved. Translation of the headline (ours): "The Mumok sinks into platitudes and antisemitism."
2. The Art Newspaper: Catherine Hickley, "Austria names Fatima Hellberg to run mumok, Vienna's museum of modern art" (2024)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2024/04/12/austria-names-fatima-hellberg-to-run-mumok-viennas-museum-of-modern-art
- Not criticism: reports her appointment from 24 candidates by a selection committee and her plan to extend the museum's "rigorous and bold tradition."
- Does not mention the museum's finances or any controversy.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the Austrian state, with named Austrian corporate sponsors and Ludwig foundations, and no flagged funder appears among them. The weak points are that the sponsors page gives no amounts (the 2023 statements show federal base funding of EUR 11,196,000 against sponsoring revenue of EUR 181,631.80), that a sponsor, Legero, is owned by a trustee, and that the current director's appointment drew an opinion column accusing her, without response on the page, of signing a letter on Palestine. Worth watching: the first programme under Hellberg and any response to that column.
