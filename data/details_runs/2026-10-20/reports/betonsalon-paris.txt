BÉTONSALON, CENTRE D'ART ET DE RECHERCHE: Paris, France
ID: betonsalon-paris
Website: https://www.betonsalon.net (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 9 esplanade Pierre Vidal-Naquet, Paris 13th arrondissement, on the Grands Moulins campus of Université Paris Cité, near Bibliothèque François-Mitterrand (metro 14, RER C).
- A non-profit association founded in 2003; it has been based at the university in the 13th arrondissement since 2007. Co-founder Mélanie Bouteloup directed it from 2003 until 2020, and Émilie Renard was named director in 2021 (Bétonsalon site).
- Its 2018 fifteenth-anniversary press release calls it the only "centre d'art conventionné" located in a university in France and describes its programme as exhibitions, seminars, workshops and long-term research residencies and co-productions with partner institutions (translation).
- From 2016 to 2020 it also ran a second site, the Villa Vassilieff (Bétonsalon site).
- The Ministry of Culture labels it a Centre d'art contemporain d'intérêt national, and the site describes it as an "établissement culturel de la Ville de Paris".
- Programme in autumn 2026: the exhibition "Magnanrama" on Nathalie Magnan (25 September to 12 December 2026), workshops, and a cross-residency with Temple Bar Gallery + Studios.
- Visiting: free entry; open Wednesday to Friday 11:00 to 19:00 and Saturday 14:00 to 19:00. The site offers contrast and text-size tools.

FUNDING
Bétonsalon: mainly public: City of Paris, the Ministry of Culture and Île-de-France Region, with Université Paris Cité
- The site states it has the support of the Ville de Paris, the regional Directorate of Cultural Affairs of Île-de-France (Ministry of Culture) and the Région Île-de-France, in collaboration with Université Paris Cité (stated by the centre).
- Its 2018 fifteenth-anniversary press release credits support from Université Paris Diderot - Paris 7, the regional cultural affairs directorate, the Île-de-France Region and Leroy Merlin - Quai d'Ivry, and says its Académie vivante programme receives support from the Fondation Daniel et Nina Carasso. It names Pernod Ricard as the first patron ("premier mécène") of the Villa Vassilieff, the second site that closed in 2020, alongside the City of Paris and the Île-de-France Region.
- The 2018 press release lists Bétonsalon as a member of the networks Tram and d.c.a (association française de développement des centres d'art).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Bétonsalon is public: the City of Paris, the state through the regional cultural affairs directorate, and the Île-de-France Region, with Université Paris Cité as host. The private lines named in its 2018 anniversary press release are Leroy Merlin - Quai d'Ivry and the Carasso foundation, and Pernod Ricard at the Villa Vassilieff.
