CASA DE LA ARQUITECTURA: Madrid, Spain
ID: casa-de-la-arquitectura-madrid
Website: https://lacasadelaarquitectura.es (checked 2026-11-20; the address https://casadelaarquitectura.gob.es returned a 502 error that day)

OVERVIEW
- Spain's national architecture museum in La Arquería de Nuevos Ministerios, Paseo de la Castellana 67, Madrid (museum website; Wikipedia).
- Opened on 18 December 2023 at an event led by Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, who said it formally met one of the mandates of the Law on the Quality of Architecture (La Moncloa). It succeeds the Museo Nacional de Arquitectura y Urbanismo, created on 29 December 2006 (Wikipedia; BOE).
- Law 9/2022 on the Quality of Architecture made it a state-owned, state-run museum attached to the Ministry of Housing and Urban Agenda, and Royal Decree 1125/2023 of 19 December created and regulates it (BOE; The Objective).
- Governance: a director appointed by the Housing Minister on a senior-management contract, after a public preselection with the board and an expert committee; and a consultative board of trustees chaired by the Housing Minister (BOE).
- Its website also covers the Spanish Biennial of Architecture and Urbanism, the Ibero-American Biennial, the Spanish Pavilion at the Venice Architecture Biennale and the National Architecture Award (museum website).
- Its opening shows included "Ecosistemas.zip", a survey of about 300 young architecture studios (Arquitectura Viva).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 11:00 to 20:00, Sundays and holidays 11:00 to 15:00; closed 24 and 25 December, 1 and 6 January and 1 May. Exhibitions, guided tours and workshops are free (museum website).

FUNDING
Casa de la Arquitectura: public: Spain's Ministry of Housing and Urban Agenda
- Owner and operator: the Ministry of Housing and Urban Agenda. The director runs the museum within its budget, and its collection is the architectural drawings, plans, documents and objects assigned to the ministry (BOE).
- Extension tender: in December 2025 the ministry put out to tender the design of the museum's extension and the renewal of the Nuevos Ministerios outdoor spaces for 1,722,974.15 euros including VAT (Forbes España).
- Extension result: of 37 entries, "La casa-jardín" by Josep Ferrando Architecture and Carles Enrich Studio won the 30,000 euro first prize; the jury was chaired by Iñaqui Carnicero. The winners will design and direct the works under a contract worth more than 1.65 million euros, and the ministry will also pay for construction, estimated at close to 14 million euros (Arquitectura Viva, June 2026).
- Contracted services: visitor information is run by Sedena SL and cultural mediation by Magmacultura SL, which took over that service in November 2025 (SUT union).

Red flags: 2026 strike over cuts to contracted mediators' hours
- In February 2026 Magmacultura cut two cultural mediators' working time from 90 to 60 per cent of full time, from 21 February (SUT union).
- SUT called an indefinite strike from 5 March, joined by Sedena's information staff; the Housing Ministry did not attend the mediation meeting on 4 March (SUT union).
- The museum stayed closed for eight days of the strike; after three meetings Magmacultura withdrew the change and agreed to regularise pay from 21 February (SUT union). The account comes only from the union that led the strike.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the Housing Ministry, with no private funder named. The weak point is the ministry's use of outsourced staff, which closed the museum in 2026.
