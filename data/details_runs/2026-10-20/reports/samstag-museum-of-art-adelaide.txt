SAMSTAG MUSEUM OF ART: Adelaide, Australia
ID: samstag-museum-of-art-adelaide
Website: https://samstag.adelaide.edu.au/about/

OVERVIEW
- University art museum in the Hawke Building at 55 North Terrace, Adelaide, on the corner of Fenn Place and North Terrace (the museum's website). It describes itself as South Australia's largest contemporary art gallery (stated by the museum).
- Opened on 11 October 2007 with the exhibition "Wonderful World". Its roots are the College Gallery of about 1977 at the Underdale campus; it became the University of South Australia Art Museum in 1991 and moved to the City West campus in 1998.
- In 2007 it was renamed the Anne & Gordon Samstag Museum of Art.
- In 2026 the museum became part of Adelaide University, formed by the merger of the University of South Australia and the University of Adelaide.
- Besides the exhibition programme it manages the university's art collection and administers the Samstag International Visual Arts Scholarships.
- Leadership: Director Erica Green. The board has seven members: Amanda Vanstone AO (chair), Nell, Julie Ewington, Stephanie Grose, Anna Hackett, Professor Irene Watson and Erica Green ex officio.
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 17:00, free entry. All areas are accessible by wheelchair and pram; quiet sessions run on Wednesday mornings and Auslan interpreting can be arranged on request.

FUNDING
Samstag Museum of Art (Adelaide): part of Adelaide University, with a private bequest funding its scholarships
- The museum is part of Adelaide University, which was formed from the University of South Australia and the University of Adelaide (the museum's website).
- The Samstag Scholarships were established in 1991 by the bequest of Gordon Samstag, an American artist who taught at the South Australian School of Art from 1961 to 1970. Adelaide University runs them through the museum on behalf of Gordon Samstag's United States trustee, the Bank of America. Each scholar receives institutional fees for a year, a $75,000 tax-free allowance and travel expenses; 152 scholarships have been awarded.
- Archie Moore's "Dwelling" (2024) was made in partnership with the Adelaide Film Festival (Adelaide Film Festival, 2024).
- The museum is a tax-deductible gift recipient and accepts donations.

BOTTOM LINE
The Samstag is a university museum, now part of Adelaide University. The Gordon Samstag bequest pays for the scholarships it administers, with the Bank of America as the American trustee.
