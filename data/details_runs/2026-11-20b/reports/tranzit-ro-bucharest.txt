OVERVIEW
- Romanian non-governmental organisation for contemporary art, "initiated and funded by ERSTE Foundation", announced on 15 December 2011 and fully active from early 2012 (e-flux).
- A closed call chose five people to run it: Matei Bejenaru, Livia Pancu, Lia Perjovschi, Attila Tordai-S and Raluca Voinea (e-flux). Temporary Art Review (2017) named Voinea as director of tranzit.ro Bucharest.
- It is the fifth member of the tranzit.org network, founded in 2002, with associations in Austria, the Czech Republic, Hungary and Slovakia; each operates autonomously (e-flux; ERSTE Foundation).
- It works as a network of permanent spaces in Bucharest, Iași and Cluj (My Art Guides); Temporary Art Review (2017) also lists Sibiu and an administrative headquarters.
- The Bucharest space is at Str. Gazelei 44, sector 4, in a residential neighbourhood in the south of the city; the Cluj space is at Sámuel Brassai Street 5 (My Art Guides).
- Current programme: "Cultivators of Life", a two-year research and production programme on gardens run by tranzit.ro/București in 2025 and 2026 with MODEM in Debrecen, Hungary, ends with an exhibition curated by Adelina Luft and Krisztián Gábor Török at MODEM from 6 September 2026 (MODEM).

FUNDING
tranzit.ro: foundation-funded: ERSTE Foundation, with Romanian state project grants
- ERSTE Foundation initiated and funded tranzit.ro (e-flux, 2011). In 2017 the organisation said most of its funding came from "the constant support of the ERSTE Foundation", and that it also applied to Romanian state funds, the Goethe-Institut and EU programmes (Temporary Art Review).
- ERSTE Foundation is described as the main partner of tranzit.org on the 2026 MODEM exhibition page.
- "Cultivators of Life" is co-funded by Romania's National Cultural Fund Administration (AFCN) (MODEM).
- ERSTE Foundation is the biggest Austrian savings bank foundation and the core shareholder of Erste Group Bank; it spends part of its dividends on projects in Central and South Eastern Europe (English Wikipedia).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One profile in which the organisation's team answers questions; it describes the funding conditions of the Romanian art scene rather than criticising tranzit.ro.
1. Temporary Art Review: Adriana Ledecouvreur, "tranzit.ro" (2017)
URL: https://temporaryartreview.com/?p=14516
- The introduction says financing art in post-communist economies "demonstrates a particular (old) mixture of arts, culture, financing and politics."
- The team says the local scene is "constantly menaced by underfunding" and that artists are precarious.
- Not criticism of the institution: the answers come from the tranzit.ro team.

BOTTOM LINE
tranzit.ro lives mainly on Austrian bank-foundation money, topped up by Romanian state grants; neither comes from an industry the atlas flags.
