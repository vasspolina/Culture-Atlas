MUZEUM SZTUKI IN ŁÓDŹ (MS1 & MS2): Łódź, Poland
ID: muzeum-sztuki-lodz
Website: https://msl.org.pl

OVERVIEW
- Museum of modern and contemporary art in Łódź with two main sites: ms1 in the former palace of the industrialist Maurycy Poznański, which holds Władysław Strzemiński's Neoplastic Room, and ms2 at ul. Ogrodowa 19, a converted 19th century spinning mill of the Izrael Poznański factory (Wikipedia; museum website).
- Origins in the avant-garde group "a.r." (Władysław Strzemiński and Katarzyna Kobro were members), whose International Collection of Modern Art opened to the public on 15 February 1931; it moved to the Poznański palace in 1948 (Wikipedia).
- ms2 opened in autumn 2008 with about 3,000 square metres for the permanent collection and 600 for temporary exhibitions (Wikipedia).
- Director: Daniel Muzyczuk, appointed by the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage after a selection committee recommended him unanimously; he had been acting director since July 2024 (ArtReview, 11 August 2025).
- Visiting: ms1 and ms2 exhibitions are open Tuesday and Wednesday 9:00 to 16:00, Thursday 12:00 to 18:00, Friday 12:00 to 20:00, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 18:00, closed Monday. The museum joined the "Muzeum za 1 zł" scheme for people aged 8 to 26 (museum website).

FUNDING
Muzeum Sztuki: public: a cultural institution of the Łódź Voivodeship self-government, co-financed by the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage
- The footer of the museum's website names the Łódź Voivodeship self-government (Samorząd Województwa Łódzkiego) as its organiser and the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage as co-financier. Notes from Poland (Piotr Kosiewski, 26 September 2022) says the museum is administered jointly by the ministry and the marshal of Łódź province.
- Wikipedia reports that the regeneration of ms2 was funded by the EU's Integrated Regional Development Operational Programme, the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage, the Voivodeship Office and the Marshal Office in Łódź.

Red flags: Director Jarosław Suchan removed in 2022
- Jarosław Suchan, director since 2006, was told at the end of April 2022, at a meeting with deputy culture minister Jarosław Sellin, that he was dismissed. He said: "I didn't get any explanation." The Art Newspaper (Anny Shaw, 30 May 2022) says Poland's ruling Law and Justice party did not renew his contract in November.
- Notes from Poland (Piotr Kosiewski, deputy director of the Batory Foundation's ideaForum, 26 September 2022) writes that the culture ministry would not allow Suchan a further term, that "the marshal's opposition" of Łódź province stood in the way, and that he was dismissed overnight without a specific reason. It says the ministry and the marshal administer the museum jointly.
- The Art Newspaper says the Polish government replaced him with the artist Andrzej Biernacki, who has run the private Browarna Gallery in Łowicz since 1991 and has no experience running a public institution, and who plans to shift the collection's focus from international artists to Polish artists, especially from Łódź. Culture minister Piotr Gliński told PAP that the main reason for the nomination was Biernacki's views (Notes from Poland).
- Richard Armstrong of the Guggenheim called the removal a "shock to the museum community worldwide" in a letter to Gliński; Glenn Lowry of MoMA and Maria Balshaw of Tate also condemned it, and a Polish contemporary art community open letter criticised Biernacki's appointment (The Art Newspaper).
- Kosiewski argues that the ministry's handling of the museum, including Biernacki's appointment without competition, shows ideologically driven personnel choices that undermine competence and public trust (Notes from Poland).
- Daniel Muzyczuk, acting director since July 2024, took over from Biernacki (ArtReview).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism concerns the 2022 dismissal of the director, not the museum's programme.
1. The Art Newspaper: Anny Shaw, with Gareth Harris, "'A shock to the community worldwide': directors of Tate, Guggenheim and MoMA condemn ousting of Polish museum head" (30 May 2022)
URL: http://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/05/30/a-shock-to-the-museum-community-worldwide-leading-museum-directors-condemn-ousting-of-polish-museum-head
- Reports that Suchan's contract was not renewed by the Law and Justice party and that he was dismissed without explanation, and that the government replaced him with an artist without museum experience.
- Armstrong links the case to the departures of the directors of the Zachęta National Gallery and the National Museum in Warsaw and calls it a move to reduce these museums to "ideological puppets".

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a public institution of the Łódź Voivodeship, co-financed by the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage, with EU and ministry money behind the ms2 building. The documented weak point is the 2022 removal of director Jarosław Suchan, which Notes from Poland attributes to the ministry, and the government's installation of Andrzej Biernacki, followed by Daniel Muzyczuk's takeover as acting director in 2024 and his appointment by the ministry in 2025.
