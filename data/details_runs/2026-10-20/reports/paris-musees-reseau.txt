PARIS MUSEES (RESEAU DES MUSEES DE LA VILLE DE PARIS): Paris, France
ID: paris-musees-reseau
Website: https://www.parismusees.paris.fr

OVERVIEW
- Public institution that manages 14 of the City of Paris's municipal museums: Musée d'Art Moderne de la Ville de Paris, Maison de Balzac, Musée Bourdelle, Musée Carnavalet, the Catacombs, Musée Cernuschi, Musée Cognacq-Jay, the Archaeological Crypt of Notre-Dame, Musée Galliera, Musée de la Libération (Jean Moulin), Petit Palais, Musée de la Vie Romantique, Maison de Victor Hugo and Musée Zadkine (Wikipedia).
- Created by the Council of Paris on 20 June 2012 and a local public administrative establishment since 1 January 2013; its headquarters is at 27 rue des Petites-Écuries, Paris 10e, and it employs about 1,000 people (Wikipedia, English and French).
- Before 2013 the museums were run directly by the City, while the company Paris Musées produced exhibitions and catalogues under a public service delegation. Paris Musées began as an association founded in 1985 and became a single-shareholder company in 2008 (French Wikipedia).
- Delphine Levy, who led the reform project, was named chief executive and Anne Hidalgo became chair of the board (English Wikipedia). Anne-Sophie de Gasquet was Director General in April 2023 (The Art Newspaper edition France, Philippe Régnier, 14 April 2023).
- The 14 museums drew about 3 million visitors in 2018 and held 1,002,636 works at the end of 2018 (French Wikipedia).
- Museum staff, including guides and storytellers, went on strike from 4 November 2025 over pay, according to Le Monde as cited by French Wikipedia.

FUNDING
Paris Musees: mainly public: the City of Paris, plus ticket sales and patronage
- City of Paris subsidy: about 55 million euros, stable in 2023 compared with 2022 (Director General Anne-Sophie de Gasquet, interviewed by Philippe Régnier, "Anne-Sophie de Gasquet : À Paris Musées, un vrai engagement dans le domaine du développement durable", The Art Newspaper edition France, 14 April 2023).
- Ticket revenue: about 20 million euros. Own-source revenue, which de Gasquet lists as patronage, venue rentals, international projects and audio guides alongside ticketing, rose from 13 percent of the budget in 2013 to 39 percent at the time of the interview (same interview).
- Pandemic support: exceptional City grants of 10 million euros in 2020 and 4 million euros in 2021 to cover deficits (same interview).
- The City of Paris, which owns the museums, sets the priorities: enhancing the collections, exhibitions, publications and wider audiences (French Wikipedia).
- A February 2011 reform article by Charlotte Uher for the Institut Francais pour la Recherche sur les Administrations et les Politiques publiques (IFRAP, a think tank) describes the pre-2013 set-up as financed partly by the City and partly by publishing revenue.

BOTTOM LINE
Paris Musees is a City of Paris public establishment: the City subsidy was about 55 million euros in 2023, and own-source revenue, including tickets, patronage and rentals, was 39 percent of the budget in April 2023.
