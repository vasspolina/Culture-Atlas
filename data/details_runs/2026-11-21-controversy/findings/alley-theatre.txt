ID: alley-theatre

WIDER CONTEXT
- January 2018: artistic director Gregory Boyd announced on 9 January his retirement, effective 11 January (Playbill, Olivia Clement, 12 January 2018).
- Unverified: current and former employees told the Houston Chronicle that Boyd bullied them, behaved abusively and inappropriately touched female actors, from Playbill citing the Houston Chronicle; at least a dozen current and former employees described verbal and mental abuse, especially of younger actors, from ABC13 (13 January 2018). Boyd repeatedly refused to comment (ABC13).
- The board's first statement did not address the harassment or abuse claims; it renewed its commitment to "providing a dignified and respectful workplace" and appointed a special committee to assess the workplace (ABC13).
- 26 January 2018: managing director Dean Gladden apologised "for recent events and the actions that led to these news stories", said "We should have handled it differently", and stated that when board leadership learned of "the depth of staff concerns" about Boyd's behaviour it asked him to proceed with his retirement, under a negotiated contract that included a severance payment. CultureMap reported the severance as $383,000. The Alley hired Walker Consulting Group to assess the workplace (CultureMap Houston, Tarra Gaines, 26 January 2018).
