CENTRE POMPIDOU: Paris, France
ID: centre-pompidou
Website: https://www.centrepompidou.fr (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- National centre for modern and contemporary art and culture in the Beaubourg area of Paris's 4th arrondissement.
- Named for President Georges Pompidou, who decided on it in 1969; President Valéry Giscard d'Estaing opened it on 31 January 1977 (stated by the space).
- Designed by Renzo Piano and Richard Rogers, with Gianfranco Franchini also involved; the design was chosen among 681 competition entries (Wikipedia).
- It houses the Musée National d'Art Moderne (more than 140,000 works), the Bpi public library and IRCAM, with a local outpost in Metz and partnerships in Málaga, Brussels, Shanghai and, since 4 June 2026, Seoul (stated by the space).
- The Paris building closed in 2025 for about five years of works, with reopening planned for 2030; a storage site at Massy, about 15 km south of Paris, was due to open in 2026 (Apollo).

FUNDING
Centre Pompidou: mainly public: French state, with Saudi and foreign partnership money for the renovation
- Its 2025 operating budget was 183.2 million euros (The Korea Herald).
- The state pays 262 million euros for the technical renovation (Apollo).
- The Royal Commission for AlUla gave 50 million euros towards "Centre Pompidou 2030", announced on 3 December 2024 during Emmanuel Macron's state visit to Saudi Arabia. Receipts then stood at 408 million euros: 262 million from the state and 146 million from international projects and partnerships, with about 60 million still to raise by 2028 (The Art Newspaper France).
- Centre Pompidou Hanwha in Seoul runs under a four-year partnership with the Hanwha Foundation of Culture (Kyunghyang Shinmun). No reporting found of the fee.
- No reporting found of the state's current share of the operating budget.

Red flags: Saudi state money for the renovation
- The Royal Commission for AlUla gave 50 million euros (The Art Newspaper France).
- The museum's trade union said it spoke out against the Saudi partnership for ethical reasons (Apollo, 2024).

Red flags: protest over the Hanwha partnership
- Hanwha partnered in 2021 with the Israeli arms companies Elbit Systems and Elta Systems to make and export arms supplies to the Israel Defense Forces (ArtAsiaPacific).
- An open letter dated 27 May 2026, with an op-ed in Libération, signed by more than 100 art professionals including Ali Cherri, Dominique Gonzalez-Foerster and Ariella Aïsha Azoulay, called the partnership "art-washing" and demanded it end (ArtReview).
- In May 2026, on the eve of the opening, 33 culture, climate and peace groups rallied outside Centre Pompidou Hanwha (Kyunghyang Shinmun). The Hanwha Foundation of Culture said it would continue to engage with a wide range of perspectives from the art community (The Korea Herald).

Red flags: finances under audit
- The Cour des comptes found an unsustainable economic model for 2013 to 2022; its president Pierre Moscovici said the Centre could not fund its projects on its own (ArtReview, 2024).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Steady criticism since 2024 of the closure, sponsorship, the Saudi gift, the finances and Hanwha.
1. Apollo: Catherine Bennett, "How will Paris cope without the Pompidou Centre for five years?" (2024)
URL: https://apollo-magazine.com/pompidou-centre-closure-five-years-refurbishment/
- Gallerist Daniel Templon called the full closure absurd; a petition for partial closure had more than 14,700 signatures, including Alain Seban, Catherine Millet and Daniel Buren.
- A union representative said Nike sponsorship went against the public mission; observers accused Saudi Arabia of art-washing.
2. ArtReview: staff, "Centre Pompidou faces financial difficulties, France's Court of Auditors reports" (2024)
URL: https://artreview.com/centre-pompidou-faces-financial-difficulties-frances-court-of-auditors-reports/
- Renovation delays added about 200 million euros; Massy was to cost about 254 million euros plus 142 million in extra costs.
3. The Art Newspaper: Gareth Harris, "Saudi Arabia to give €50m towards Centre Pompidou refurbishment" (2024)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2024/12/09/saudi-arabia-to-give-50m-towards-centre-pompidou-refurbishment
- An unnamed French critic called the deal part of a drive to promote Saudi Arabia's cultural credentials; the piece notes an average of 129 executions a year there from 2015 to 2022.
4. ArtAsiaPacific: Yuqian Fan, "French Artists Call for Boycott of Centre Pompidou Hanwha" (2026)
URL: https://artasiapacific.com/news/french-artists-call-for-boycott-of-centre-pompidou-hanwha/
- Reports a Chanel show at the Seoul museum on 26 May, followed by a Libération op-ed alongside the letter, and cites Hanwha's 2021 Elbit and Elta deals.

BOTTOM LINE
The Paris centre runs first on state money, but its renovation leans on 50 million euros from a Saudi state body and on foreign partnership revenue, including a partnership with an arms group. The weak points are the Saudi gift, the Hanwha partnership under boycott, and finances the national auditor called unsustainable. Worth watching: the unpublished Hanwha fee and the 60 million euros still to raise.
