ILULISSAT ART MUSEUM (ILULISSAT KUNSTMUSEUM): Ilulissat, Greenland
ID: ilulissat-art-museum
Website: https://ilulissatmuseums.gl/the-ilulissat-art-museum/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- One of Greenland's two small art museums, with Nuuk Kunstmuseum, in Ilulissat (lex.dk).
- It opened on 15 August 1995 as Greenland's first art museum. It began under Ilulissat Museum, which handed over a collection centred on the painter Emanuel A. Petersen; the two museums separated in 2004, when the art museum got its own head (lex.dk).
- The house was built in 1923 as the colony manager's residence, designed by Helge Bøysen-Møller (ilulissatmuseums.gl, Visit Greenland, Scan Magazine).
- The collection grew from a former private collection given to the municipality and is added to every year. One floor shows Greenlandic artists, a room for the local painter Peter Rosing, and colonial-era paintings by Emanuel A. Petersen and Harald Moltke "in a critical context"; the other shows up to six international exhibitions a year (ilulissatmuseums.gl).
- It runs a creative workshop for local young people, a library on Greenlandic art and a shop selling signed works by Greenlandic artists (Visit Greenland).
- Andreas Hoffmann is director (Scan Magazine); the museum's own site calls it Greenland's most visited art museum (ilulissatmuseums.gl).
- Visiting: one DKK 100 ticket covers this museum and the Cultural History Museum. Summer (15 June to 20 September): Tuesday to Friday 10 to 17, weekends 12 to 16. Autumn and winter (27 September to 30 April): Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 10 to 16, Saturday 12 to 16 (ilulissatmuseums.gl).

FUNDING
Ilulissat Art Museum: public: Avannaata Municipality
- A municipal museum (lex.dk); its museum office takes bookings at an Avannaata Municipality address (ilulissatmuseums.gl).
- The current exhibition, Ina Rosing's "Do You Remember the Sea", is supported by Nunafonden (ilulissatmuseums.gl).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money, with no flagged funder. Its one named private backer pays for a single show, not the running costs.
