ARSENAL, VOLGA-VYATKA BRANCH OF THE PUSHKIN MUSEUM: Nizhny Novgorod, Russia
ID: arsenal-volga-vyatka-branch-of-the-pushkin-museum-nizhny-novgorod
Website: https://arsenal-museum.art/o-nas/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum inside the Nizhny Novgorod Kremlin, building 6 (Arsenal website).
- The institution has run exhibition and interdisciplinary projects since 1997 and has been a branch of the Pushkin State Museum of Fine Arts since 2020 (Arsenal website). Earlier it was the Nizhny Novgorod branch of the State Centre for Contemporary Art (GTsSI), which was handed the Arsenal building in 2003; the branch has occupied it since 2011 (Kommersant, Citizen K, 2011; Pushkin Museum branch page).
- Building: built in 1837 to 1843 to a design by Anton Leer and attached to a 16th-century Kremlin wall; it housed the Defence Ministry's map archive and stood unrepaired for more than 150 years. Evgeny Ass worked on adapting it, and the first phase of about 1,700 square metres had opened by 2011 (Kommersant, Anna Tolstova, 30 May 2011). The Arsenal site calls it an architectural monument of late classicism.
- Anna Gor headed the branch in 2021 (Kommersant interview, 30 June 2021) and is now listed as adviser to the head of the branch; Alexander Komarov is head of the branch, with deputies Elena Belova and Andrei Bogolepov (Arsenal website).
- Programmes: exhibitions, the Vasari festival of texts on art, a graphics festival that began in 2022, children's programmes and accessibility projects (Pushkin Museum branch page).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 20:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 20:00, closed Monday; the ticket office closes at 19:15. Entry is free on Wednesdays with registration. Metro: Gorkovskaya station, then walk along Bolshaya Pokrovskaya to Minin and Pozharsky Square (Arsenal website; Pushkin Museum branch page).

FUNDING
Arsenal: mainly public: federal funds through the Pushkin State Museum of Fine Arts, with corporate sponsors and family patrons
- Federal: since 2020 the branch belongs to the Pushkin State Museum of Fine Arts (Arsenal website).
- Support page: the Council of Patrons was founded by Sergey Ivanushkin, Yuri Gaysinsky and Irina Sedykh and their families. Membership costs 2,000 to 8,000 roubles for Friends of Arsenal and 50,000 to 250,000 roubles for corporate members; sponsorship starts at 250,000 roubles (translated from the Russian).
- Sponsors and partners named on that page: SPAR (general partner of the "Svobodnaya sreda" social campaign), Yandex Go (Night of Museums and Vasari), Mercure Nizhny Novgorod Centre (hotel partner), Unilin (children's programme "Leyka"), GK Karkas Monolit ("Own City" exhibition project), the United Metallurgical Company (OMK, general partner of the "Named by Vasari. Mannerism" exhibition), AestetikMedGrupp NN (Doctors' Club project) and Bank PSB, listed as a partner of the Pushkin Museum.
- Kommersant (interview with Anna Gor by Tatiana Salakhetdinova, 30 June 2021): Gor thanked the United Metallurgical Company and its foundation OMK-Uchastie for supporting the Shukhov exhibition, said attendance had fallen by about a third because Kremlin restoration work limited entry, and said this had "strongly affected budgets" (my translation).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The only criticism is a 2011 account of local attacks on the branch's predecessor.
1. Kommersant (Citizen K): Anna Tolstova, "Отвоеванный Арсенал" ("The Reclaimed Arsenal") (30 May 2011)
URL: https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/1650856
- Reports that local cultural conservatives and the pro-government youth movement "Idushchie vmeste" blamed the GTsSI branch for every sin "up to pornography", and demanded that the building become a patriotic education centre named after Minin and Pozharsky (translated from the Russian).
- Not art press.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Bank PSB is listed on the Arsenal support page as a partner of the Pushkin Museum. PSB is the abbreviation of Promsvyazbank (Wikipedia), and the Pushkin Museum's board of trustees page lists Promsvyazbank with Pyotr Fradkov as chairman. The board is chaired by Marat Khusnullin, Deputy Chairman of the Government of the Russian Federation, and lists representatives of Sberbank, Gazprombank, Transneft, Sibur and Rosseti (Pushkin Museum board page).
- Wikipedia states that the UK announced sanctions against Promsvyazbank on 22 February 2022, that the US also imposed sanctions on it, and that the Russian government nationalised the bank in 2018.
- Connection to the Arsenal: PSB and the trustees belong to the parent museum; the Arsenal support page lists Bank PSB as a partner of the Pushkin Museum.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Arsenal is federal, through the Pushkin Museum, with local corporate and family patrons. The weak point is the parent museum's partner and trustee list, which includes the sanctioned Promsvyazbank, other large banks and companies, and a board chaired by a deputy head of government. Worth watching is whether any such partner funds the Arsenal's programme directly.
