THE NEWBRIDGE PROJECT: Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom
ID: the-newbridge-project-newcastle
Website: https://thenewbridgeproject.com

OVERVIEW
- Artist-led studio, gallery and bookshop at the Shieldfield Centre, 4-8 Clarence Walk (via Stoddart Street), Newcastle upon Tyne. It describes itself as "an artist-led space that supports artists, curators and communities."
- Established in 2010. In 2015 it occupied a 29,000 square foot office block in the city centre, offered affordable studios to early-career artists and had around 80 studios, a large exhibition space and an independent bookshop (Corridor8, Niomi Fairweather, 4 October 2015).
- Registered as a charity (no. 1173849) and a company (no. 10582100), registered 18 July 2017 (Find that Charity).
- Building: the Shieldfield Centre is a disused five-storey 1960s office block that NewBridge moved into in 2022. Topo Architects led a 63,000 pound redesign, which created studios for more than 120 artists, ceramics, print and wood workshops, a bookshop, a gallery and an events space. It won the Adaptive Reuse Award at the Lord Mayor's Design Awards and the Architects' Journal Small Projects Sustainability Prize in 2024 (Newcastle University, 21 June 2024).
- Programmes: The Collective Studio, Practice Makes Practice, Create/Disrupt, Kaleidoscope Network and Co-Act for artist development; ceramics, wood, darkroom and print workshops; a Monday coffee morning, a monthly memory cafe and a monthly Saturday social. It describes itself as a Gallery of Sanctuary.
- Visiting: gallery and bookshop open Wednesday 1pm to 5pm and Thursday to Saturday 12pm to 5pm.

FUNDING
The NewBridge Project: mixed public and charitable: Arts Council England, Newcastle City Council, trusts and the university
- The website names as funders: Arts Council England, Newcastle City Council, the Garfield Weston Foundation, the Jerwood Foundation, Community Foundation North East, Newcastle University and the North East Mayoral Strategic Authority.
- Income was 593,833 pounds in the year to 31 March 2025 (Charity Commission data via Find that Charity).
- Newcastle University's Institute for Creative Arts Practice funded part of the Shieldfield Centre redesign (Newcastle University, 21 June 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind NewBridge is public and charitable: Arts Council England, the city council, regional authority, university and trusts.
