STEDELIJK MUSEUM AMSTERDAM – Amsterdam, Netherlands
Website: https://www.stedelijk.nl (blocks automated checks via robots.txt, 2026-10-20; reachable in search results)

OVERVIEW
- Amsterdam's municipal museum of modern and contemporary art and design. Director Rein Wolfs; managing director Margot Gerené (2024 report summary).
- 574,244 visitors in 2024, 51% first-time.

FUNDING
Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam: mainly public (City of Amsterdam), with lottery, corporate and foundation support.
- Total income 2024 €37,540,888; City of Amsterdam subsidy €23,228,444, about 61.9% (stated by the space, 2024 report summary with auditor's opinion).
- Main partner: VriendenLoterij (Dutch charity lottery).
- Loyal partners: IMC, ABN AMRO, Mastercard, Polestar, Teijin.
- Other partners: DGTL, Amsterdam Dance Event, Institute for Night Culture, Amsterdam Art Week, WeArePublic, Museumnacht, Stadspas.
- Funds: Mondriaan Fund, Ammodo, Fonds 21, Cultuurfonds, Vereniging Rembrandt, Blockbusterfonds, Art Mentor Foundation Lucerne, Pro Helvetia, Bank of America Foundation, ELJA Foundation, Zadelhoff Cultuur Fonds, Robben Foundation, Stichting Zabawas, Stichting Niemeijer Fonds, Administrația Fondului Cultural Național; large gift from the Don Quixote Foundation via the Stedelijk Museum Fund.
- ABN AMRO extended its partnership by two years from 11 March 2026: one exhibition by a female artist a year (main partner of the 2026 Yayoi Kusama show), the ABN AMRO Art Award exhibition, the "Blikopeners" youth programme (stated by ABN AMRO, 13 March 2026).

Red flags: Partner bank's money-laundering settlement
- ABN AMRO paid €480M (€300M fine plus €180M disgorgement) in April 2021 to settle a Dutch prosecution for structural anti-money-laundering failures and culpable money laundering covering 2014 to 2020 (Netherlands Public Prosecution Service).

Red flags: Loan refusal for Gaza protest
- In June 2024 the museum declined to lend Ahmet Öğüt's "Bakunin's Barricade" to the Not Surprised Collective to shield student protesters at Gaza war demonstrations; in November 2024 Öğüt asked for the work to be taken off display. The museum said lending originals for a demonstration clashes with its duty to preserve the collection (The Art Newspaper, 5 Nov 2024). This is a governance dispute, not a funding issue.

BOTTOM LINE
The Stedelijk is mostly city-funded and publishes audited summaries naming its partners. Worth watching: ABN AMRO's renewed partnership given its 2021 money-laundering settlement.
