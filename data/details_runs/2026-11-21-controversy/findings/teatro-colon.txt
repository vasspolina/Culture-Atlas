ID: teatro-colon

Red flags: 2024 removal of the director
- On 23 August 2024 the City of Buenos Aires replaced Jorge Telerman as director of the Teatro Colón; Telerman wrote that the City's Culture Minister had told him of "su decisión de reemplazarme como director del Teatro Colón" ("her decision to replace me as director of the Teatro Colón", translated) (La Nación, 23 August 2024).
- La Nación wrote that Telerman had backed Martín Lousteau's campaign and was left out of the picture after Lousteau's primary rival, Jorge Macri, became head of the city government.
- Unverified: sources quoted by the Buenos Aires Times described "an unsustainable relationship" between Telerman and the City Culture Minister, Gabriela Ricardes, who "did not want Telerman at the head of the theatre" (Buenos Aires Times, Claudio Corsalini, 28 August 2024).
- The City Culture Ministry's communiqué named Julio Bocca and Gerardo Grieco as the theatre's new direction (Buenos Aires Times, 28 August 2024).

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 1 April 2020 Eternautas (Goliardos SRL), the contractor that ran the theatre's guided tours, dismissed all 25 guides and coordinators, citing fees from October 2019 to March 2020 that the City Government had not paid, and the pandemic (Tiempo Argentino, Gastón Rodríguez, 2 April 2020).
- The guides rejected the grounds and called on Goliardos SRL and the City Government to reconsider under decree DNU 329/20, which barred dismissals; other theatre workers planned to ask the City to take them on (Tiempo Argentino, 2 April 2020).
