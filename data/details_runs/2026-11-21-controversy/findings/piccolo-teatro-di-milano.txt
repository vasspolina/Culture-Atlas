Red flags: 2023 board appointment of Geronimo La Russa
- In November 2023 the Minister of Culture Gennaro Sangiuliano named Geronimo La Russa, eldest son of the Senate president Ignazio La Russa and president of the Automobile Club di Milano, to the Piccolo Teatro's board in place of Andrea Cardamone (Avvenire, 7 November 2023).
- Opposition MPs of the Five Star Movement, Avs and the Pd objected to the choice; Pierfrancesco Majorino, Pd group leader in Lombardy, called it "difficile da giustificare" ("hard to justify", translated) (Avvenire, 7 November 2023).
- The mayor Giuseppe Sala said the decision was "totalmente legittima" ("entirely legitimate", translated) but that he had "delle remore" ("some reservations", translated), as the city appoints people with a certified record in culture (Open, 9 November 2023).
- La Russa replied in a statement that his professionalism had earned him his roles, and the undersecretary for culture Vittorio Sgarbi defended the appointment (Avvenire, 7 November 2023).

WIDER CONTEXT
- In September 2020 the two board members named by Regione Lombardia stayed away from the board meetings called to designate a new director; the city's culture councillor Filippo Del Corno said the Region "ha scelto invece di boicottare i lavori" ("has chosen instead to boycott the work", translated), and the City and the Ministry moved to co-opt two extra board members to break the deadlock and allow a majority vote; Il Giorno named Claudio Longhi as the probable choice (Il Giorno, 30 September 2020).
