KHARKIV MUNICIPAL GALLERY: Kharkiv, Ukraine
ID: kharkiv-municipal-gallery
Website: https://mgallery.kharkiv.ua

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at Chernyshevska Street 15, Kharkiv, described by Wikipedia as one of the first Ukrainian galleries of contemporary art; it opened in 1996 and joined the Association of Art Galleries of Ukraine in 1997 (Wikipedia).
- Spaces: a main exhibition hall of about 100 square metres and an underground space of about 140 square metres, the ARTbasement (ARTpidval), which has worked with youth and experimental art since 2008 (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: the NonStopMedia youth projects festival since 2003, a curatorial intensive first held in 2015, Night of Museums in Kharkiv, Night of Performance since 2016, the retrospective series "Our Heritage" and residency exchanges since 2011 (Ukrainian Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia lists Tetiana Tumasian as director and curator.
- Wartime: Objectiv.tv reported on 11 March 2023 that the gallery had not operated since the full-scale war began, with six staff, two of them in Kharkiv. According to the report, the city council and Mayor Ihor Terekhov had agreed remotely with the director that the city's communal news programme "Харків: новини" would use the premises at Chernyshevska 15 temporarily, and staff were told they would move to a children's art school at Yesenina Street 8A. After public backlash, Terekhov phoned the director on 11 March and, according to the gallery, said the premises would remain gallery space, that the city guarantees its return, and that the collection, archive and library would stay in the sealed storage on site. The gallery said it was waiting for documented guarantees (Objectiv.tv, Olena Nahorna, "Переселення «муніципалки» заради комунального ТБ: що гарантує Терехов (відео)", 11 March 2023; title translated: relocation of the "municipal gallery" for communal TV: what Terekhov guarantees).

FUNDING
Kharkiv Municipal Gallery: municipal: an institution of Kharkiv city
- Wikipedia describes it as a municipal gallery, and the 2023 Objectiv.tv report shows the city council and the mayor deciding how its premises are used.

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery is a city of Kharkiv institution, and the city council decides how its premises are used.
