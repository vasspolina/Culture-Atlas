TRIPLE CANOPY: New York, NY, USA
ID: triple-canopy-new-york
Website: https://canopycanopycanopy.com/

OVERVIEW
- New York nonprofit (501(c)(3)) magazine that publishes digital art and literature, public conversations, exhibitions and books. It is certified by W.A.G.E. (Working Artists and the Greater Economy) and is a member of the Coalition of Small Arts (CoSA) New York (Triple Canopy website).
- Founded in 2007 as an "informal, nonhierarchical, geographically dispersed editorial collective"; the first issue appeared on 17 March 2008 (Triple Canopy website).
- Offices: 264 Canal Street, 3W, New York, NY 10013 since summer 2016; before that 155 Freeman Street in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, for about five years, and a shared storefront at 177 Livingston Street, Brooklyn, for nine months in 2010 (Triple Canopy website). Wikipedia says the office and venue are in Chinatown, shared with Electronic Arts Intermix.
- Staff include director Molly Kleiman and editor Alexander Provan (Triple Canopy website). The board is led by president Josh Kline and includes Lynne Tillman, Mónica de la Torre and treasurer Steven Goldglit.
- Programmes: Publication Intensive, a free two-week programme on publishing history and practice, and public events at its venue (Wikipedia). By December 2018 it had published 25 issues and 12 books with more than 900 contributors (Wikipedia).
- Its archive was acquired by NYU's Fales Library and Special Collection, announced in July 2015 (Wikipedia).

- Andy Warhol Foundation: a $100,000 multi-year programme support grant in the Fall 2022 cycle (Warhol Foundation grants archive).
- Past supporters listed on the same page include the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation, Fundación Jumex, the Royal Norwegian Consulate General in New York, the J. M. Kaplan Fund's Furthermore programme and the Shelley & Donald Rubin Foundation.
- The website directs tax-deductible online contributions to a membership page, and lists a Director's Circle of individual donors.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Triple Canopy is US arts philanthropy and public arts grants: the Warhol Foundation ($100,000 in the Fall 2022 cycle), the National Endowment for the Arts, the New York State Council on the Arts and the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs, all listed on its website as current institutional support, alongside individual members and a Director's Circle.
