AROS – Aarhus, Denmark
Website: https://www.aros.dk (ok, checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- ARoS Aarhus Kunstmuseum descends from the Aarhus Art Association of 1847 (founded 24 March 1847; first exhibition 1859).
- Current building at Aros Allé 2 by Schmidt Hammer Lassen (competition 1997, construction from 2000, opened 8 April 2004).
- Olafur Eliasson's rooftop "Your rainbow panorama" opened May 2011, donated by Realdania.
- Expansion "The Next Level" (James Turrell Skyspace "The Dome", new gallery, Art Square) broke ground 15 June 2022; a Salling Galleri opened June 2025 (2025 annual report).
- 649,448 visitors in 2025. Director: Rebecca Matthews. Board chair: Laura Hay Uggla.

FUNDING
ARoS: mixed public and private; public subsidy about 31% of income in 2025.
- 2025 total income DKK 163.6M (stated by the space, 2025 annual report). Earned revenue DKK 84.0M.
- Public subsidies DKK 50.3M: Aarhus Municipality operating grant DKK 17.9M; state via Aarhus Municipality DKK 31.4M; state direct DKK 1.0M.
- Sponsors and foundations DKK 29.3M: main sponsors/business clubs DKK 10.6M, project sponsors DKK 14.8M, foundation money for acquisitions DKK 2.7M.
- Aarsleff (construction group) is Main Corporate Partner, continuing in 2026 (stated by the space).
- Augustinus Fonden supported the Barbara Kruger, Isaac Julien, Anna Boghiguian and Jenkin van Zyl exhibitions in 2025 (stated by the space). No source retrieved confirmed the existing record's "DKK 2M for 2026 projects".
- Ny Carlsbergfondet supported Igshaan Adams works, acquisitions/donations and the van Zyl show (stated by the space).
- Salling Fondene: an ARoS press release (Sept 2023, seen in search results) is titled as saying the Salling foundations had contributed DKK 100M in total to the expansion; the page text could not be retrieved. The 2025 annual report names Salling Galleri and says board member Marianne Bedsted is director of Salling.
- Realdania donated the rooftop work (stated by the space).

Red flags: Tobacco-linked foundation money
- Augustinus Fonden fully owns Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, its investment company (Wikipedia; CAF fact sheet 2024).
- Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker held more than 25% of Scandinavian Tobacco Group as of 7 March 2022 (STG shareholder page). CAF says its returns fund Augustinus Fonden's grants, mainly to art and culture.
- So part of the money Augustinus Fonden gave for ARoS's 2025 exhibitions ultimately comes from tobacco-company returns.

BOTTOM LINE
ARoS publishes audited accounts and names its funders; its income is mainly earned revenue and municipal/state subsidy. The flag rests on Augustinus Fonden, whose investment company is a major Scandinavian Tobacco Group shareholder; this is worth tracking if Augustinus grants grow.
