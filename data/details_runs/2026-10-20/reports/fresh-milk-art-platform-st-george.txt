FRESH MILK ART PLATFORM: St. George, Barbados
ID: fresh-milk-art-platform-st-george
Website: https://freshmilkbarbados.com

OVERVIEW
- Artist-led platform for Caribbean visual artists, with its studio and the Colleen Lewis Reading Room on Walkers Dairy, a working dairy farm in St. George, Barbados (Fresh Milk).
- Established in 2011 as a social practice experiment, partly in response to the attrition of BFA students at Barbados Community College, the island's only BFA programme (Fresh Milk); Business Barbados names Annalee Davis as founding director. It began operations in July 2011 and was incorporated as a not-for-profit company on 6 July 2012 (Fresh Milk 2024 financial statements).
- Unverified: Wikipedia states that Davis's studio is on a working dairy farm that operated historically as a 17th-century sugarcane plantation; the sentence is unsourced there.
- Runs a micro-residency (usually one month, 2013 to 2019, then partnerships with local off-site hosts), the biennial Caribbean Linked residency with Ateliers '89 Foundation (Aruba) and ARC Magazine, the annual Tilting Axis conference with ARC Magazine, the biennial Transoceanic Visual Exchange film and video exhibition, and the Caribbean Art Map (Fresh Milk).
- Visits to the studio and the reading room are by appointment (Fresh Milk).

FUNDING
Fresh Milk Art Platform: grant-funded: Mellon Foundation general operating support, with smaller restricted grants
- Mellon Foundation: an unrestricted grant of US$350,000 (BBD 700,000) over three years from 2024, covering 2024 to 2026 (Business Barbados, 28 February 2024; Fresh Milk 2024 financial statements). Davis said she had worked "on a shoestring budget" for twelve years before it.
- 2024 accounts: revenue was BBD 396,128, of which grant funding was BBD 383,333 (BBD 233,333 unrestricted, the first year of the Mellon grant, and BBD 150,000 restricted), donations BBD 2,556, contributions in kind BBD 9,671 and other income BBD 568. BBD 466,667 of the Mellon grant is deferred to 2025 and 2026 (Fresh Milk 2024 financial statements).
- 2023 accounts: revenue was BBD 124,309, of which grants were BBD 36,863 and contributions in kind BBD 78,050 (Fresh Milk 2024 financial statements).
- Fresh Milk/CLF Artistic Fellowship (2025 to 2026): a partnership between the Clara Lionel Foundation, the Mellon Foundation and Fresh Milk, launched on 9 August 2025. Clara Lionel Foundation and Mellon also support the TENDER regranting programme, as do the National Cultural Foundation and the Panta Rhea Foundation (Fresh Milk).
- The financial statements are compiled by an accountant, Ashok Merai, who states he did not conduct an audit. The directors are Annalee D. Davis, Simone Mangal and Natalie A. McGuire.

BOTTOM LINE
Fresh Milk runs on foundation grants, chiefly the Mellon Foundation's US$350,000 unrestricted grant for 2024 to 2026, whose first year made up BBD 233,333 of its BBD 396,128 revenue in 2024. The Clara Lionel Foundation, the National Cultural Foundation and the Panta Rhea Foundation support its fellowship and regranting work. The 2024 financial statements are compiled by an accountant and not audited.
