ROCKBUND ART MUSEUM: Shanghai, China
ID: rockbund-art-museum
Website: https://www.rockbundartmuseum.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at 20 Huqiu Road, Huangpu District, Shanghai (museum website), on the northern tip of the Bund area (Institute of Network Cultures); the nearest Metro station is East Nanjing Road, on Lines 2 and 10 (Wikipedia).
- Opened in 2010 as part of the Rockbund Urban Renaissance project to renovate heritage buildings at the north end of the Bund (DutchCulture); Geert Lovink dates the opening to May 2010, timed with the Shanghai World Expo (Institute of Network Cultures).
- The museum occupies the former Royal Asiatic Society building, completed in 1933 and designed by Palmer and Turner; the same building housed one of China's first modern museums, the earlier Shanghai Museum (Wikipedia).
- In 2005 the Shanghai Bund de Rockefeller Group Master Development Co. Ltd. obtained the right to develop the area and chose to restore the building as a public contemporary art museum; British architect David Chipperfield restored and redesigned it from 2007, extending it eastward and adding a ground-floor plaza, a rooftop terrace and a skylight (Wikipedia).
- The museum has no permanent collection and shows exhibitions such as Zeng Fanzhi (2010), Song Dong (2017) and Philippe Parreno (2017) (Wikipedia). Its opening show was curated by Cai Guo-Qiang, and Hsiangling Lai was director at the opening (Institute of Network Cultures).
- Open Wednesday to Sunday 11:00 to 18:00 with last admission at 17:30; Tuesday is a member day, open 12:00 to 18:00 (museum website).
- e-flux describes it as a non-profit arts organization in Shanghai.

FUNDING
Rockbund Art Museum: founders Lynn and Thomas Ou, with lead sponsors ROCKBUND and Ping An
- The museum's support page names Lynn and Thomas Ou as founders and ROCKBUND and Ping An as lead sponsors, with no years or amounts.
- At the 2010 opening, Lovink wrote that the Rockbund Investment Corporation owns the museum and that the board of the Rockbund was its sole sponsor; director Hsiangling Lai told him the municipality had no plans to build a contemporary arts museum and wanted private developers to establish such institutions (Institute of Network Cultures, 18 July 2010).
- DutchCulture states that the museum receives "substantial financial support to the production of original new artworks".

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One blog review from the 2010 opening, on the museum's dependence on a property developer and its first show.
1. Institute of Network Cultures: Geert Lovink, "Cia Guo-Qiang's Romantic Resolution of the Peasant Question" (18 July 2010; title as published)
URL: https://networkcultures.org/geert/?p=495
- Places the museum in the Rockbund real estate redevelopment, owned by the Rockbund Investment Corporation, whose brochure promises "the most elite luxury area in Shanghai"; says the board of the Rockbund is the museum's sole sponsor and asks the director what would happen to the museum in a recession or a collapse of the real estate market.
- On the opening show "Peasant Da Vincis", curated by Cai Guo-Qiang, notes "romantic overtones" and calls the works "anthropological artefacts", but says the problem is not brutal appropriation: the rural makers are credited and he sees no hint of exploitation.
- Mostly about the exhibition, not the institution, apart from the sponsorship point.

BOTTOM LINE
Rockbund Art Museum is a non-profit museum inside the Rockbund property development on the Bund. Its support page names founders Lynn and Thomas Ou and lead sponsors ROCKBUND and Ping An, and in 2010 the Rockbund Investment Corporation owned it and the Rockbund board was its sole sponsor.
