GALERIA FOKSAL (FOKSAL GALLERY): Warsaw, Poland
ID: galeria-foksal-warsaw
Website: http://galeriafoksal.pl

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art gallery on Foksal Street, Warsaw, housed in an annexe to the Zamoyski Palace (stated by the gallery).
- Founded in 1966 by the critics Wiesław Borowski, Anka Ptaszkowska and Mariusz Tchorek and artists including Tadeusz Kantor and Henryk Stażewski; Borowski was nominally its director for nearly 35 years (stated by the gallery).
- Named for Foksal Street, where it stands (stated by the gallery). Wikipedia dates the opening to 1 April 1966 with Zbigniew Gostomski's "Optical Objects".
- Interior designed by the artists Zbigniew Gostomski, Edward Krasiński and Zygmunt Targowski, including the cabinets, bookshelves and chairs, which remain unchanged (stated by the gallery).
- Before it joined its present institution it operated under the Visual Arts Studio until 1984 and then the Warsaw Agency for Art Exhibitions (stated by the gallery).
- Current curators named on the contact page: Zuzanna Mielczarek (programme curator), Katarzyna Krysiak and Martyna Stołpiec.
- Visiting: free admission; open Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday noon to 6 PM, Thursday noon to 7 PM, Saturday noon to 4 PM. Online sign language interpreting is offered.

FUNDING
Galeria Foksal: public: the regional government of Mazovia
- The gallery is part of the Mazovian Institute of Culture (formerly the Mazovian Centre for Culture and Arts), "financed by the Marshal Office of the Province of Mazovia" (stated by the gallery). The Marshal Office is the Mazovia regional government.
- Wikipedia likewise describes the gallery as government funded through the Mazovia Region Centre for Culture and Art.

Not related: the Foksal Gallery Foundation at Górskiego Street, Warsaw. It was established at the gallery in 1997, and some of its members later left to set up a separate institution; the gallery states it is "a separate body, without any direct links with Foksal Gallery". Wikipedia describes the foundation as a commercial entity that left the gallery in 2001.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Galeria Foksal is public: it is part of a regional cultural institute financed by the Mazovia provincial government. The commercial Foksal Gallery Foundation is a separate organisation.
