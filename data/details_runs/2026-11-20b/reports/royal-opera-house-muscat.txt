ROYAL OPERA HOUSE MUSCAT: Muscat, Oman
ID: royal-opera-house-muscat
Website: https://rohmuscat.org.om (checked 2026-11-20; did not load)

OVERVIEW
- Opera house in the Al Qurum area of Muscat, the first in the Gulf and the second in the Arab region after Cairo (The National, 2011).
- Sultan Qaboos bin Said ordered it in 2001; it opened in October 2011 with Puccini's "Turandot", conducted by Placido Domingo (Arab News; The National).
- Royal Decree 105/2010 of 3 October 2010 established the Royal Opera House with legal personality and financial and administrative independence, under the Sultan's honorary patronage (decree.om, English translation).
- Designed by WATG, with a 1,100-seat auditorium whose proscenium can expand or shrink, and seat-back screens for subtitles (Arab News).
- Programme: visiting opera, ballet and orchestras; it opened a season with the world premiere of "Sindbad: The Omani Sailor", an Arabic-language grand opera co-produced with MÜPA Budapest (Times of Oman).
- Director General and Artistic Director: Umberto Fanni (Oman Observer, 2024; travellingforbusiness, 2024).
- Visiting: ticket prices at opening were 13 to 60 rials (The National, 2011).

FUNDING
Royal Opera House Muscat: state-funded: a public body under the Sultan's patronage, led by senior royals and royal court officials
- The National wrote at the opening that ticket sales could not come near recouping costs, and that audiences and musicians would, in the early days at least, depend on "the largesse of the Sultan".
- Arab News wrote that budget constraints were set aside in favour of luxury.
- In 2024 the Minister of the Diwan of Royal Court, Sayyid Khalid Hilal Al Busaidi, presented Fanni with the Order of Felicitation conferred by the Sultan (Oman Observer, 17 January 2024).
- Mandarin Oriental, Muscat has run a hotel tie-in with the house since the 2024 to 2025 season, selling packages with premium tickets and a branded balcony at the Royal Circle (travellingforbusiness, 2024); the hotel is a client, not a funder.

Red flags: Gulf state body: the Omani state and royal court, 2010 to now
- Its own site says the house is guided by Sultan Haitham bin Tarik, and that a Higher Committee led by Sayyid Fahad bin Mahmoud Al Said, Deputy Prime Minister for the Council of Ministers, shapes its vision and considers policies recommended by the Board of Directors (archived copy, 2025).
- Nasr Hamoud Al Kindi, Secretary General of Royal Court Affairs, is deputy chairman of the Board of Directors (Oman Observer, February and March 2023).
- Sayyid Dr Kamil Fahad Al Said, Secretary General in the General Secretariat for the Council of Ministers, is a board member (Teatro La Fenice, 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Omani state's and the Sultan's, with no private sponsor of weight in view. Its budget and accounts are worth watching for.
