HUBEI MUSEUM OF ART: Wuhan, China
ID: hubei-museum-of-art-wuhan
Website: http://wlt.hubei.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/gysyjs/ggwhfw/ggmsg/202010/t20201030_2987804.shtml

OVERVIEW
- Provincial art museum at No. 1 Sanguandian, Donghu Road, Wuchang District, Wuhan, on the East Lake shore next to the Hubei Provincial Museum (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism; China Daily).
- It is managed by the Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism, which describes it as a government-invested public-interest cultural institution (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism, in Chinese).
- It opened on 6 November 2007 and became free to enter on 30 October 2008. It was renamed from 湖北省艺术馆 to 湖北美术馆 in July 2010 and was named a first-batch National Key Art Museum by the Ministry of Culture in 2011 (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism, in Chinese).
- Building: four floors above ground and one below, 12 exhibition halls including a video hall, on a 15,318 square metre site with about 25,000 square metres of floor space (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism). The reference site Maigoo gives the total investment as 140 million yuan.
- Collection: more than 5,400 works, with six collecting specialities: lacquer art, industrial prints, sculpture, watercolour, contemporary art and 20th-century Hubei art (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism).
- Programmes: a permanent display of 20th-century Hubei art documents, triennials of lacquer art, industrial prints and sculpture, a talks series and children's workshops (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism).
- Visiting: free admission. Maigoo lists Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00, closed Monday except public holidays.

FUNDING
Hubei Museum of Art: mainly public: provincial government
- The provincial Department of Culture and Tourism manages the museum, which it describes as government-invested.
- The Department's budget portal carries a "2026 unit budget disclosure catalogue" for the museum, posted on 3 March 2026 (Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism).
- Admission is free (Maigoo).
- The building cost 140 million yuan (Maigoo).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is public: it is a government-invested institution managed by the Hubei Department of Culture and Tourism, which lists a 2026 unit budget disclosure catalogue for it on the provincial budget portal.
