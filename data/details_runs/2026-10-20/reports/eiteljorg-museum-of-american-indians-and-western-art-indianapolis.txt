EITELJORG MUSEUM OF AMERICAN INDIANS AND WESTERN ART: Indianapolis, USA
ID: eiteljorg-museum-of-american-indians-and-western-art-indianapolis
Website: https://eiteljorg.org (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Native American art and art of the American West at 500 West Washington Street in White River State Park, downtown Indianapolis, next to the Indiana State Museum.
- Founded by the Indianapolis businessman Harrison Eiteljorg (1903 to 1997), who chaired its board until his death; it opened in June 1989 (Encyclopedia of Indianapolis, Wikipedia).
- Named for its founder, whose collection of Native American objects and Western paintings and sculpture formed its core (Wikipedia).
- In June 2005 an expansion doubled its public space with new galleries, an education centre and gardens (Wikipedia); its Native American galleries were rebuilt under the $55 million Project 2021 campaign (annual report 2023).
- Native American and Western galleries, the biennial Eiteljorg Contemporary Art Fellowship for Native artists, the annual Indian Market and Festival, Día de Muertos and Jingle Rails, a café and store. Kathryn Haigh has been president and chief executive since 2023.
- Open Monday to Saturday 10am to 5pm, Sunday noon to 5pm. Enrolled tribal members and Indiana college students enter free; $5 Access Pass tickets and $5 off for veterans and military. Its passenger lift was out of service when checked, limiting access to upstairs galleries.

FUNDING
Eiteljorg Museum: private: foundations, an endowment and corporate sponsors, led by Lilly Endowment
- In 2023 revenue was $11,534,509, including contributions of $8,526,446 and investment income of $1,345,318; expenses were $10,508,814 and net assets $66,248,081 (Form 990, ProPublica).
- Gifts were 55% of 2023 revenue, earned income 23% and endowment support 22% (annual report 2023).
- Lilly Endowment Inc. is listed among the largest 2023 donors ($85,000 to $3,000,000) and presented the 2023 Contemporary Art Fellowship; Eli Lilly and Company is also a donor (annual report 2023).
- The Christel DeHaan Family Foundation gave a $2.75 million legacy gift to the endowment in November 2023 (annual report 2023).
- The City of Indianapolis gives annual operating support through the Indy Arts Council (annual report 2023).
- Corporate partners include Citizens Energy Group, PNC Bank, Fifth Third Bank, Centier Bank, Capital Group, OneAmerica, Raymond James, Hiatus Tequila and Sun King Brewery (corporate giving page, undated).

Red flags: coal money behind the founder, and a current gas utility sponsor
- Harrison Eiteljorg ran the Morgan family's coal business: general manager of Morgan Mines from 1942, and from 1947 president of Morgan Coal, which had "strip mines and reserves in Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, and Pennsylvania"; he also leased coal deposits in Colorado (Encyclopedia of Indianapolis).
- Citizens Energy Group, the trade name of the City of Indianapolis's public utilities department, which runs a public charitable trust providing natural gas service in the city (Indiana State Board of Accounts audit), is on the current corporate partner list, gave $5,000 to $19,999 in 2023 and sponsored Día de Muertos 2023 (corporate giving page, annual report 2023).
- No reporting found of a protest, boycott or sponsor dropped under pressure at the Eiteljorg.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found describes its Native galleries and fellowship favourably.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs mainly on Indianapolis foundations, led by Lilly Endowment, a sizeable endowment and local corporate sponsors. The weak points are a founding collection built on coal mining money and a gas utility among current sponsors, though both are small beside the foundation gifts. Worth watching: the corporate partner list and how Native artists' groups view the museum's sponsors.
