QUADRIENNALE DI ROMA: Rome, Italy
ID: quadriennale-di-roma
Website: https://quadriennalediroma.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Governatorato of Rome instituted the national art Quadriennali in 1927, to be held at the Palazzo delle Esposizioni, with 250,000 lire a year plus one million lire per edition (Palazzo delle Esposizioni website).
- The first edition, directed by general secretary Cipriano Efisio Oppo, opened on 3 January 1931 and drew over 200,000 visitors; Mussolini called it "historic" (Palazzo delle Esposizioni website).
- A royal decree made it an autonomous body in 1937, with a statute signed by minister Giuseppe Bottai; the 1939 edition ran under ministerial supervision, artists' origins were documented after the 1938 racial laws, and the 1943 edition was the last under Oppo (Palazzo delle Esposizioni website).
- Today it is a foundation under private law, created by transforming the former public body Esposizione Nazionale Quadriennale d'Arte di Roma (Lazio Region).
- Its seat is Villa Carpegna; in 2022 its use still had to be settled by an agreement with Roma Capitale, and the foundation set aside 15,000 euros against that risk (2022 accounts).
- The 18th Quadriennale, "Fantastica", ran at Palazzo Esposizioni Roma from October 2025 to January 2026 in five curated sections (Intesa Sanpaolo; Artribune).
- Leadership: Culture Minister Alessandro Giuli appointed Andrea Lombardinilo, a sociologist at the University of Chieti-Pescara and president of the Fondazione Michetti, as president on 13 March 2025, after the death of Luca Beatrice (Ministry of Culture; Artribune). Gennaro Sangiuliano had appointed Beatrice in February 2024, succeeding Umberto Croppi (Domani).

FUNDING
Quadriennale di Roma: public: mainly the Ministry of Culture, with the Lazio Region and Roma Capitale
- Members: the Ministry of Culture, the City of Rome and the Lazio Region take part in the foundation (Lazio Region).
- 2022 income: ministry grants of 1,816,396 euros, including special projects and the archive and library, within which the base contribution was 1,162,495 euros; 600,000 euros from the Prime Minister's Office for its 95th anniversary programme; 140,000 euros from the Lazio Region; 77,469 euros from Roma Capitale (2022 accounts).
- Archive: the Archivio Biblioteca della Quadriennale won 53,901 euros in extra ministry grants in 2022 for its running and purchases (2022 accounts).
- 2025: a ministry decree of 24 July 2025 gave the foundation 191,944 euros from a separate operating fund for ministry-backed bodies, a smaller share than before because the previous allocation had included extra money for the 18th Quadriennale (Ministry of Culture decree no. 260).
- Sponsor: Intesa Sanpaolo was main partner of the 18th Quadriennale (Intesa Sanpaolo).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The 2025 edition drew mixed to negative reviews aimed at its curatorial structure rather than its funding.
1. Artribune: Alessandra Mammì, "Dispersiva e poco coesa. Qualche nota sulla Quadriennale di Roma 2025" (2025)
URL: https://www.artribune.com/?p=1191761
- Calls the show "sei piccole mostre" (six small shows) by curators who do not talk to each other; the BRH+ layout overlaps the sections rather than joining them.
- The re-staging of the 1935 Quadriennale, curated by Walter Guadagnini, drops the fascist propaganda that defined the original and becomes an "innocua e debole galleria di quadri belli del 1935".
- Praises several artists and notes that the concept announced by Beatrice never fully took shape.
2. Finestre sull'Arte: Federico Giannini, "Fantastica ma educata: l'arte italiana allo specchio (dei curatori). Com'è la 18ª Quadriennale" (2025)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/recensioni-mostre/recensione-18a-quadriennale-di-roma-fantastica-ma-educata
- Finds the show chaotic, like an episodic film without a thread, with curators rather than artists as the protagonists of its signage.
- Judges much of the painting "sostanzialmente epigonale" and the art "carini, potabili e rassicuranti".
- Cites a budget of 2.6 million euros and finds the result "un poco modesto" for it; criticises both the institution's framing and the art.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the state, region and city, with one bank sponsor for the 2025 edition. Its leadership and most of its budget depend on the culture ministry, and that dependence is the thing to watch.
