MODERNA MUSEET: Stockholm, Sweden
ID: moderna-museet
Website: https://www.modernamuseet.se

OVERVIEW
- Swedish state museum of modern and contemporary art and design on Skeppsholmen in Stockholm, main entrance at Exercisplan 4 (Wikipedia, museum website).
- Opened in 1958 in the Exercishuset on Skeppsholmen; Pontus Hultén was the first director, from 1958 to 1973, and in 2005 bequeathed more than 700 works with his archive and library (Wikipedia).
- Rafael Moneo's building on Skeppsholmen replaced temporary premises in the Spårvägshallarna, where the museum worked from 1994 to 1998 (Wikipedia).
- A second site, Moderna Museet Malmö, opened in 2009 in a building that had housed the Rooseum contemporary art centre (Wikipedia).
- Director since 2019: Gitte Ørskou (Wikipedia).
- Collection of about 6,000 paintings, 25,000 prints, 400 art videos and 100,000 photographs, with Picasso, Dalí, Duchamp and Matisse (Wikipedia); a restaurant, a shop and an art library are on site (museum website).
- Closed Mondays; open 10:00 to 20:00 on Tuesdays and Fridays and 10:00 to 18:00 on other days. Admission is 170 SEK, reduced 140 SEK; free for visitors aged 18 and under and for Klubb Moderna members; free Fridays from 18:00 to 20:00 (museum website).

FUNDING
Moderna Museet: mainly public: the Swedish state pays about 70 percent, with a small share from corporate sponsors
- 2025 income was 271.4 million SEK: 191.1 million from state appropriation, 63.0 million from fees and other payments, 16.8 million from grants and 0.6 million from financial income (annual report 2025). The appropriation is 70 percent of income.
- Sponsorship and barter brought in 2.7 million SEK in 2025: sponsorship income of 1.0 million and barter of 1.6 million; exhibition grants were 2.8 million, art purchase funds received 12.3 million and donated works were valued at 17.4 million (annual report 2025).
- Fees from the 100 Friends programme were 1.5 million SEK in 2025 (annual report 2025).
- Bank of America's Art Conservation Project finances the conservation of Jean Tinguely's iron sculptures in Le Paradis Fantastique, begun in 2023 and continued in 2025 (annual report 2025).
- The museum's support page shows logos of the sponsors Bank of America, Jung and Patriksson and lists Hotel Skeppsholmen and Panasonic Connect as partners (museum website).
- The museum was a single-head state agency until 31 December 2025 and has had a board since 1 January 2026 (annual report 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little direct criticism of the museum; the one art-press piece on it concerns the quality of its programme and a protest held on its premises.
1. Kunstkritikk: Frans Josef Petersson, "The Swedish Art Spring" (22 January 2024)
URL: https://kunstkritikk.com/the-swedish-art-spring
- Calls the museum's spring programming weak, "supposedly due to an ongoing renovation of the building", and asks where the Moderna of old has gone.
- Reports a sit-in at the museum by Stockholm Cultural Workers for Palestine, whose petition asks Swedish art institutions to press the government over Gaza and to say why they showed more concern for Ukrainian cultural workers than for Palestinians.

BOTTOM LINE
Moderna Museet is mainly paid for by the Swedish state, which supplied 191.1 million of its 271.4 million SEK income in 2025. Corporate sponsorship and barter totalled 2.7 million SEK, and the named sponsors include Bank of America, whose conservation project pays for work on Tinguely's Le Paradis Fantastique.
