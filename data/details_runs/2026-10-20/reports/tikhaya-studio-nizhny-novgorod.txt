TIKHAYA STUDIO: Nizhny Novgorod, Russia
ID: tikhaya-studio-nizhny-novgorod
Website: https://obdn.ru/articles/kak-eto-rabotaet-studiya-tihaya

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run studio with an exhibition hall and open storage at Ulyanova Street 4 in Nizhny Novgorod (The Blueprint), about three minutes' walk from the Kremlin (RBC Style, 9 August 2024).
- Founded in spring 2015 by the artist Artem Filatov, who is its director (RBC Style, 1 May 2025; obdn.ru, 8 September 2022). It opened on the base of art workshops (obdn.ru).
- Named for the street's former name, Tikhonovskaya, which came from a church demolished in Soviet times (obdn.ru). RBC Style says Filatov also ties the name to a quiet, hospitable way of dealing with visitors.
- Filatov and other artists had worked before in a workshop on Studenaya Street, which they left after a flood (The Blueprint, 18 April 2025).
- Building: in spring 2022 a second, neighbouring building opened, with an exhibition hall, open storage for residents' work and an archive of Nizhny Novgorod contemporary art from the early 2000s (RBC Style, 9 August 2024). RBC Style (8 May 2025) says the building had burned several times before it was renovated.
- Staff and artists: the studio has 9 permanent artists plus invited authors (obdn.ru). Filatov calls it a "studio-commune" (The Blueprint), which names Filatov, chief curator Alisa Savitskaya and development director Natasha Korenchenko as key figures.
- Visiting: visitors must tell the studio in advance (RBC Style, 1 May 2025). The studio's Telegram channel publishes a monthly digest of events (obdn.ru). A tenth-anniversary exhibition, "Our doors are always closed" (translation), ran from 20 April to 31 August 2025 (RBC Style).

FUNDING
Tikhaya Studio: privately funded: artists' shared rent, patrons' money, and regional and museum grants
- Rent: artists pay for their workplaces and share the space; The Blueprint gives examples of 1,500 roubles a month for a workplace and, in 2017, 4,500 roubles a month.
- Patrons: RBC Style (8 May 2025) says the 2022 expansion was financed by the entrepreneurs Ruslan Filatov and Rustam Korenchenko, the studio's main patrons; Rustam is the husband of development director Natalya Korenchenko. The same article says Artem Filatov's father proposed renovating the building for artist rentals.
- Garage Museum: the studio received a grant from the Garage Museum of Contemporary Art in 2022 for archive work, and develops an archive and research programme on the local scene with Garage (obdn.ru, 8 September 2022). Garage was founded in 2008 by Roman Abramovich and Darya Zhukova (Meduza, 26 April 2024).
- Regional grant: the studio takes part in a regional grant programme for creative industries; a grant funded an internship programme on working with archives (RBC Style, 8 May 2025).
- Sales: the studio works with collectors and returns to art fairs, starting with the Kontur fair in Nizhny Novgorod (RBC Style, 8 May 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
Tikhaya is a privately funded artist-run space paid for by the artists' rent, named entrepreneur patrons who financed the 2022 move into the second building, a regional creative-industries grant and a 2022 Garage Museum grant for archive work.
