MEDRAR FOR CONTEMPORARY ART: Cairo, Egypt
ID: medrar-for-contemporary-art-cairo
Website: https://medrar.org

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art space for contemporary and media arts, at 10 Gamal El Din Abou El Mahasen Street, 8th floor, Garden City, Cairo (Medrar).
- Running since 2005; Medrar began as a collective of visual artists working with a new medium, and its first project was the Cairo Video Festival, through which Medrar was later established (Experimental Cinema directory). Medrar's about page describes it as a non-profit art space running since 2005, thanks Dia Hamed as co-founder and lists Mohamed Allam as director.
- Projects: the Cairo Video Festival, Medrar Studio, MedRX, Roznama and Medrar TV, plus a collaborative space for media artists, workshops, events and documentation of audio-visual work on Egypt's contemporary art scene (Medrar).
- The Cairo Video Festival is a platform for video art with about ten days of screenings, artist talks and discussions (Experimental Cinema directory). The credits of the 9th festival (2019) list Mena El Shazly as artistic director (Cairo Video Festival acknowledgements).

FUNDING
Medrar for Contemporary Art: grant-funded: arts foundations, an embassy and the Arab Fund for Arts and Culture
- Medrar's about page thanks as supporters the Young Arab Theatre Fund, the Foundation for Arts Initiatives, Culture Resource's Abbara Program, the Roberto Cimetta Fund, the Netherlands Embassy in Cairo and the Arab Fund for Arts and Culture (AFAC), the last through a grant from the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation.
- AFAC lists a 2019 grant to Medrar from its North Africa Cultural Program, aimed at long-term financial stability and wider artistic outreach (AFAC).

BOTTOM LINE
Medrar is a small independent art space whose documented money comes from arts funds and foundations, one embassy and a Swiss development agency grant passed through AFAC. AFAC lists a 2019 grant from its North Africa Cultural Program.
