Red flags: 2022 calls by police to remove a painting from an exhibition
- In May 2022 people including law enforcement called for the removal from the museum of Magnus Juliano's painting "Mother Land Theme Park Black Panther Gift Shop", which shows Winnie the Pooh dead with his hands cuffed and Piglet, as a police officer, aiming a gun at him; it hung in "Black and Brown Faces: Paying Homage To", an exhibition organised by Paloozanoire (The National Desk, 31 May 2022, citing WLWT and Newsweek).
- Cincinnati Fraternal Order of Police President Dan Hils said: "So it's saying cops are murderers, which I know is a total fabrication and a total lie." Juliano said: "Humanity is a joke right now if we're more upset over a painting than police brutality" (The National Desk, 31 May 2022, citing WLWT).
- Museum officials did not take the painting down early; they posted a sign outside the front doors saying "its staff was not involved in the art selection" for the exhibition, and the painting came down on 19 June 2022, the scheduled end date (Inside the Magic, Rebekah Barton, 27 June 2022, citing WLWT).
