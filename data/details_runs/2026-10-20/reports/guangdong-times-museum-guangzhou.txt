GUANGDONG TIMES MUSEUM: Guangzhou, China
ID: guangdong-times-museum-guangzhou
Website: https://www.timesmuseum.org/

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art museum at Times Rose Garden Phase III, Huangbian North Road, Baiyun District, Guangzhou; the exhibition hall is on the 19th floor of a residential tower, open 10:00 to 18:00 (Baiyun District government page, 25 May 2022; ArtAsiaPacific).
- Set up in 2003 as a branch of the Guangdong Museum of Art, co-founded by Times Property (now Times China) and that museum; it became an independent institution and opened to the public on 31 December 2010 (Baiyun District government page).
- The exhibition space, 1,200 square metres in ArtAsiaPacific's figure, was designed by Rem Koolhaas and Alain Fouraux (The Art Newspaper, 26 March 2024).
- It programmed four to six exhibitions a year (Baiyun District government page).
- With Times China's support it initiated Times Art Center Berlin in November 2018 (Baiyun District government page).
- The museum announced on 17 August 2022 that it would close on 9 October 2022 after the show "River Pulses, Border Flows"; seven employees were to be dismissed, cutting the team to three (ArtAsiaPacific, Jenna Chan, 23 August 2022).
- It reopened in March 2024 in its 2010 space. Curatorial staff fell from 18 to three, it works with several guest curators, and the Social Practice Lab and Media Lab did not return for now (The Art Newspaper, Lisa Movius, 26 March 2024).

FUNDING
Guangdong Times Museum: corporate-backed until 2022: developer Times China, now running on a quarter of its budget and seeking several donors
- "Times China has been its core funder since its inauguration" (Baiyun District government page, 25 May 2022).
- Times China had halted housing projects during Covid lockdowns and faced loan repayments and mortgage strikes; the museum attributed its 2022 closure to the economic downturn. ArtAsiaPacific reports that severance pay was under negotiation because there was insufficient cash flow for a one-off payment.
- Reopening: a fundraising auction in January 2024 of works from 65 mostly regional artists helped fund the reopening; the museum now runs on a quarter of its previous budget (The Art Newspaper, 26 March 2024).
- Associate director and chief curator Nikita Yingqian Cai said the museum will pursue a "model of sustainability" and rely on a range of donors rather than one backer (The Art Newspaper, 26 March 2024).
- The Art Newspaper's 27 September 2023 report (Lisa Movius, "What's behind China's spate of museum closures and downsizings?") describes the museum as "backed by a property company" and says it ceased its exhibition programme in August 2022.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum was the developer Times China, its core funder from its opening in 2010 until the 2022 closure. Since the March 2024 reopening it runs on a quarter of its previous budget; a January 2024 artists' auction helped fund the reopening, and the museum says it will rely on several donors.
