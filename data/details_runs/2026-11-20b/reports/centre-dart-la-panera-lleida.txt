CENTRE D'ART LA PANERA: Lleida, Spain
ID: centre-dart-la-panera-lleida
Website: https://www.lapanera.cat (checked 2026-11-20; the server's certificate chain could not be verified, so its pages were read in Internet Archive copies of February 2025 and October 2026)

OVERVIEW
- Public contemporary art centre in a medieval building in Lleida's old town, named after Carrer de la Panera (Wikipedia). It calls itself a platform for producing and spreading contemporary visual art, with research, education and public programmes and projects in schools, health centres and community services (centre website).
- Opened in 2003, taking over the Biennal d'Art Leandre Cristòfol that the city began in 1997; the centre's collection grew out of the biennial (centre website).
- Building: the Almodí, built in the 12th and 13th centuries as the city's exchange for grain, oil and grapes, of which a colonnade of 21 stone columns 5.70 metres high survives. The cathedral canons bought it in 1606 and added a floor, hence "La Panera dels Canonges"; after the 1835 Desamortització it passed to the Paeria (city council), which lent it to the army as a cavalry barracks from 1860 and later a national police barracks, until it returned to the Paeria in the late 1980s (centre website).
- Programme: a documentation centre focused on typography, graphic arts and publishing, with a collection of special editions and artists' books; a typographer residency; artist residencies in primary and secondary schools; the MiniPanera family space; and public programmes on art and technology and on functional diversity, and a strand called Afectivitat fluvial (centre website).
- Christian Alonso won the competition to direct it in 2022 (Bonart; Wikipedia). The 13th Biennal, "Extractivismes", curated by María Íñigo Clavo and Alonso, followed a prologue show by Ignacio Acosta from October 2024 to February 2025 (Afterall).
- A new exhibition cycle, with Fernando Renes, Albert Potrony and Martina Manyà, runs from 24 October 2026 to 7 February 2027 (centre website).

FUNDING
Centre d'Art La Panera: public: Lleida City Council (the Paeria), with the Generalitat de Catalunya
- The centre states that it is a public facility created through cooperation between the Ajuntament de Lleida and the Department of Culture of the Generalitat de Catalunya (centre website).
- It belongs to the Generalitat's Network of Territorial Visual Arts Centres of Catalonia, with ACVic, Bòlit (Girona), Centre d'Art Tarragona, Tecla Sala (L'Hospitalet), Fabra i Coats (Barcelona), Mataró Art Contemporani and Lo Pati (Amposta); the network's centres are municipally owned and promoted by the Generalitat (centre website).
- The Paeria owns the building (centre website) and hires the directors (Bonart).
- The network's research project Quasi Veu was launched by the Generalitat's Department of Culture during the COVID-19 crisis (centre website).

Red flags: 2025 protest over directors' freelance contracts and the missing public call
- Arts bodies including the Catalan Association of Art Criticism (ACCA), the Catalan Artists' Assembly Platform (PAAC) and the Catalan Museum Professionals' Association (AMC) publicly denounced the Paeria for not calling a public competition for the centre's direction after announcing the end of the previous term on 28 August; an internal staff member ran it on an interim basis (Bonart).
- Since 2003 several directors held the post on freelance commercial contracts, which the bodies said breaks Generalitat order CLT/225/2021 for public visual arts facilities and the code of good practice for appointing directors (Bonart).
- After an ACCA meeting on 10 December, the Paeria cited administrative difficulties and promised news in February; in March it announced only a new interim management (Bonart).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism concerns how the city hires the centre's directors; reviews of the programme are admiring.
1. Bonart: "La Panera: opacity and violation of good practices" (2025)
URL: https://www.bonart.cat/en/n/45305/la-panera-opacity-and-violation-of-good-practices
- Says the centre is "in a delicate moment" because of years of irregular contracts for its directors.
- Calls for an immediate public competition with a four-year contract, saying other city councils have already corrected such practices.
2. Afterall: Daniela Ghinsberg, "In the Family of Things" (2025)
URL: https://www.afterall.org/articles/in-the-family-of-things-the-13th-leandre-cristofol-art-biennial-at-centre-dart-la-panera/
- Not criticism: praises the 13th Biennal for works that ask the viewer to slow down and look with care, and says the show left the writer with hope.

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from the Lleida city council and the Generalitat, with no private sponsor. The weak point is governance: insecure contracts for its directors and a delayed open competition.
