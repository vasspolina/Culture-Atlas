WADSWORTH ATHENEUM MUSEUM OF ART: Hartford, USA
ID: wadsworth-atheneum-museum-of-art-hartford
Website: https://www.thewadsworth.org

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 600 Main Street, Hartford, Connecticut (museum website, archived November 2024).
- Incorporated on 1 June 1842 and opened on 31 July 1844; Wikipedia calls it the oldest continually operating public art museum in the United States.
- The first collection consisted of 78 paintings, two marble busts, one portrait miniature and one bronze sculpture; the building was constructed on the site of the family home of Daniel Wadsworth (Wikipedia).
- The original Gothic Revival "castle" of 1842 to 1844 was designed by Alexander Jackson Davis and Ithiel Town; four wings were added over the years, and the building was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1970 (Wikipedia).
- The collection holds about 50,000 objects spanning more than 5,000 years, including European Baroque art, French and American Impressionism and Hudson River School landscapes; a $33 million renovation of five buildings finished in 2015 (Wikipedia).
- The Amistad Center for Art & Culture presents exhibitions with the museum, and the museum runs the Art Bridges Cohort Program partnerships and the MATRIX series (annual report FY2024).
- Visiting: open Thursday to Sunday, noon to 17:00, with the BMuse Cafe open during museum hours; the Auerbach Art Library is open Friday 10:00 to 17:00 by appointment (museum website, archived November 2024).

FUNDING
Wadsworth Atheneum Museum of Art: private and corporate philanthropy: foundations, companies, estates and individuals, with Connecticut state support
- Fiscal year ending June 2025: revenue $10,554,764 and expenses $19,280,762; contributions were $5,258,779 (49.8 percent) and investment income $2,933,680 (27.8 percent); net assets $132,875,977 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Form 990).
- FY2024 philanthropy list, $50,000 and above: Art Bridges, the Connecticut Department of Economic and Community Development, the Hartford Foundation for Public Giving, Travelers, the Saunders Foundation, the Estate of James and Tina Collias, the Mary G. Dowling Trust, the Samuel R. Peterson Trust and individual donors (annual report FY2024).
- $25,000 to $49,999: The Hartford, the European Fine Art Foundation, the Kohn-Joseloff Foundation, the Scripps Family Fund for Education and the Arts and individuals.
- $10,000 to $24,999: Henkel Corporation, Lincoln Financial Foundation, Stanley Black & Decker, Consigli Construction Co. Inc., Connecticut Humanities and foundations including the Alexis Gregory Foundation and the George A. and Grace L. Long Foundation.
- Corporate members include Bank of America, Liberty Bank, Lincoln Financial, Global Atlantic Financial Group, Talcott Financial Group and Travelers.
- Exhibitions are funded by the Wadsworth Exhibition Fund, supported by the Estates of James Lyon, Karen Kelleher and Susannah Schickman. "Styling Identities: Hair's Tangled Histories" (21 March to 11 August 2024) was sponsored by the Hartford Foundation for Public Giving, The Edward C. & Ann T. Roberts Foundation, the Henkel Corporation and Travelers.
- Frank Sumner's 1927 bequest of $1 million established an acquisitions endowment (Wikipedia).
- Sustaining support is provided by the Greater Hartford Arts Council's United Arts Campaign with support from the Department of Economic and Community Development, Connecticut Office of the Arts (annual report FY2024, Exhibitions & Sponsors).

Red flags: fossil fuels
- Viking Fuel Oil Company, Inc. is on the FY2024 philanthropy list in the $1,499 to $500 tier (annual report FY2024). A Viking Fuel Oil Company at 10 Crossroads Plaza, West Hartford, is listed as delivering home heating oil and on- and off-road diesel and as in the fuel oil business since 1933 (OilMonster directory).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is private and corporate philanthropy from foundations, companies, estates and individuals, an acquisitions endowment, and support from the Connecticut Department of Economic and Community Development; contributions were 49.8 percent of revenue in the year ending June 2025. The one flagged line is a heating-oil dealer, Viking Fuel Oil Company, in the $1,499 to $500 tier of the FY2024 donors.
