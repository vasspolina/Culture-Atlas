Red flags: 2023 dismissal of the director
- The Junta de Andalucía removed Juan Antonio Álvarez Reyes as director of the CAAC after 14 years, and on 31 October 2023 its Governing Council approved Jimena Blázquez Abascal as the new director (Última Hora, from Europa Press, 4 November 2023).
- The culture minister, Arturo Bernal, said "después de 14 años entendíamos que el proyecto de Álvarez Reyes estaba ya agotado y necesitábamos de un nuevo impulso" ("after 14 years we understood that Álvarez Reyes's project was exhausted and we needed a new impetus", translated), and that "el procedimiento seguido ha sido impecable" ("the procedure followed was impeccable", translated). He said the post is a senior executive appointment made directly by the Governing Council, not by public competition, and the replacement was made the same way (Última Hora, 4 November 2023).
- In protest at the director's "cese fulminante" (summary dismissal, translated), the members of the CAAC's Comisión Técnica decided to resign, stating their "desacuerdo y preocupación por el procedimiento" (disagreement and concern about the procedure, translated) (Última Hora, 4 November 2023).
- CIMAM's Museum Watch said it was "very alarmed" by the dismissal, said Álvarez Reyes had been hired through a public competition more than a decade earlier and was told of his dismissal without justification, and stated that "The action of the Junta de Andalucía contravenes the Code of Best Practices" (Artdaily, 5 March 2024).

- Dropping the open competition used for earlier appointments is "un retroceso inadmisible" ("an unacceptable step back", translated).
