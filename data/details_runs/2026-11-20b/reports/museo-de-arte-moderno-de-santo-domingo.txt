MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE SANTO DOMINGO: Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-santo-domingo
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- State modern art museum in the Plaza de la Cultura Juan Pablo Duarte complex, Santo Domingo (El Día, 2016).
- Opened on 15 December 1976 as the Galería de Arte Moderno and renamed Museo de Arte Moderno in 1992 (El Inmobiliario, 2022; El Día).
- Designed by José Miniño, with three levels and a basement (El Día; El Inmobiliario).
- It reopened during the COVID-19 pandemic after four years closed for remodelling, and holds about 1,500 pieces, of which 80 to 100 are shown at a time (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Director: after Carmen Heredia became culture minister in August 2020 she named the architect Federico Fondeur (Diario Libre, 2021); Acento still called him director in July 2026. María Elena Ditrén had directed it for 16 years by 2019 (El Nuevo Diario, 2020).
- It hosts the Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales: the 31st edition in 2025 received 609 entries and showed 207 works, open until 17 December 2025 (Acento, José Rafael Sosa).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00, free of charge, as reported in October 2022 (El Inmobiliario).

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Santo Domingo: public: the Dominican Ministry of Culture
- The museum sits under the Dirección General de Museos, and the Ministry of Culture has run it since the ministry was created in 2000 (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Building: in December 2019 the ministry said its remodelling of the museum was unfinished (Diario Libre, 2019).
- Bienal prizes, awarded at the ministry's Bienal: in 2025 a Gran Premio of RD$1 million and nine prizes of RD$300,000 each; Minister Roberto Ángel Salcedo promised a decree creating a permanent Bienal committee and larger prizes for 2027 (Presidencia de la República, September 2025).
- The ministry acts as the superior authority over the Bienal's organising commission (Resolution 18-2025); Fondeur sat on the commission for the 30th Bienal, sworn in by Minister Milagros Germán (El Día, 25 October 2022).

Red flags: 2025 protests over the ministry's annulment of a Bienal prize
- On 22 October 2025 Minister Salcedo's Resolution 18-2025, on an appeal by the Colegio Dominicano de Artistas Plásticos, annulled the prize given to David Pérez (Karmadavis) for a potted royal palm, ruling that it used perishable material, and upheld the other prizes (Ministry of Culture).
- The jury wrote to the minister that reading "perecedero" by its dictionary meaning voided its whole award and that it was not told of the appeal (Acento, 28 October 2025).
- The artist Noa Batlle gave up her honourable mention in protest (Acento, 30 October 2025), and the prize-winners asked the minister to restore all the jury's awards (Acento, 7 November 2025).
- An open letter on Change.org with at least 155 verified signatures, including Gerardo Mosquera, Pablo León de la Barra and Coco Fusco, asked for the resolution to be revoked (Acento, 18 November 2025).

Red flags: 2025 boycott over unpublished Bienal catalogues
- The catalogues of the 2021 and 2023 Bienales were never published, and the photographer Ramón Peña said he would stop taking part in protest (Acento, 9 September 2025).

Red flags: 2019 to 2021 protests over the delayed 29th Bienal
- On 16 December 2019, one day before the opening, the ministry postponed the 29th Bienal, saying the museum's remodelling was unfinished; the Comité de la Bienal Invisible called a protest at the Plaza de la Cultura for 17 December (Diario Libre).
- July 2020: La Bienal Invisible, La Otra Bienal and La Cooperativa called the handling negligent, blaming Minister Eduardo Selman and Ditrén, and said submitted works had sat in the museum for nearly two years (El Nuevo Diario).
- June 2021: more than 60 artists and curators denounced the "deficiente manejo" (poor handling) of the 29th Bienal, saying the selection list included works withdrawn before selection (Diario Libre).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Frequent criticism, from newspapers and artists' collectives rather than art press, of the ministry's running of the Bienal.
1. El Nuevo Diario: "Artistas califican de negligencia la no celebración de la Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales" (2020)
URL: https://elnuevodiario.com.do/artistas-califican-de-negligencia-la-no-celebracion-de-la-bienal-nacional-de-artes-visuales/
- Artists say the Bienal should not depend on one building and should have its own permanent management committee.
- Names the Ministry of Culture and the museum.
2. Diario Libre: "Más de 60 artistas denuncian 'deficiente manejo' de 29 Bienal de Artes Visuales" (2021)
URL: https://www.diariolibre.com/revista/cultura/mas-de-60-artistas-denuncian-deficiente-manejo-de-29-bienal-de-artes-visuales-ED27063986
- The letter says state cultural institutions "carecen de voluntad política" (lack the political will) to answer to the artistic community; it names Minister Heredia and Fondeur.
3. El Nuevo Diario: José Rafael Sosa, "Bienales de Artes Visuales de Santo Domingo, o el cuento de nunca acabar" (2025)
URL: https://elnuevodiario.com.do/bienales-de-artes-visuales-de-santo-domingo-o-el-cuento-de-nunca-acabar/
- Lists complaints about the Bienales since 1998 and sets the palm prize against the rules, which exclude installations of perishable materials or that could harm the museum's structure.
- The museum is the venue, not the main target.

BOTTOM LINE
A state museum paid for by the Ministry of Culture, with no private sponsor. Its disputes concern how the ministry runs the Bienal held there: delays, missing catalogues and an overturned jury award.
