Red flags: 2022 cancellation of a Teodor Currentzis concert
- In October 2022 the Kölner Philharmonie cancelled a concert by the conductor Teodor Currentzis planned for 27 January 2023 (OperaWire, Francisco Salazar, 4 October 2022).
- Director Louwrens Langevoort said the decision did not mean the Philharmonie did not appreciate Currentzis's artistic achievements, but that more than half a year after the outbreak of war "an attitude towards the political situation should be recognizable", and that the activities and financing of his ensembles MusicAeterna and Utopia suggested he "is very close to the Russian regime" (OperaWire, 4 October 2022).
