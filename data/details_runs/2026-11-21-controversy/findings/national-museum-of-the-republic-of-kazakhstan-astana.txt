Red flags: 2023 conviction of the former director for embezzlement
- On 16 May 2022 the Anti-Corruption Service said it was conducting a pre-trial investigation into officials of the National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan over the suspected embezzlement of 146 million tenge of budget money under a 1.2 billion tenge state procurement contract for the international exhibition "Шествие Золотого человека по музеям мира" (The Golden Man's Procession Through the World's Museums), with searches under way with a court's sanction (Tengrinews, 16 May 2022).
- On 20 June 2023 the Interdistrict Criminal Court of Astana, Judge Baglan Idirisov, found Arystanbek Mukhamediuly, the museum's former director and a former minister of culture and sport, guilty under Article 28 part 3 ("organisation of a crime") and Article 189 part 4 ("misappropriation or embezzlement, that is theft of entrusted property on an especially large scale"), and sentenced him to eight years in prison without confiscation of property, with a lifetime ban on holding state service posts (Zakon.kz, Anastasia Prilepskaya, 20 June 2023).
- The court convicted him of embezzling 221,411,730 tenge in staging the "Шествие Золотого человека" exhibition, with the help of his former deputy Almaz Nurazkhan and two other defendants (Zakon.kz).
- Nurazkhan was sentenced to seven years with a lifetime ban on state service posts, and the two other defendants to seven years each (Zakon.kz).
