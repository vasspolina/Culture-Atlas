SOFIA CITY ART GALLERY: Sofia, Bulgaria
ID: sofia-city-art-gallery
Website: https://sghg.bg/en/ (checked 2026-10-25; live)

OVERVIEW
- Municipal gallery of Bulgarian art at 1 General Gurko Street, Sofia, with its entrance from Knyaz Al. Batenberg Street (sghg.bg).
- Began in 1928, when Mayor Vladimir Vazov decreed a commission to set up a municipal museum of Sofia with a museum, a library and an archive; a picture gallery was part of its work from the start (sghg.bg history).
- Housing: various buildings from 1929; a purpose-built building opened in 1941 at 3 Banski Square and was destroyed in bombing; the gallery received its own space at 1 General Gurko Street in 1973, which opened in 1977. The library and archive separated in 1952 and the museum and the gallery became separate institutions (sghg.bg history).
- Not named for a person. No reporting found of the architect of the General Gurko Street building.
- What is there: four departments (painting, sculpture, graphics, contemporary art and photography) and a collection of more than 23,000 works by Bulgarian artists from the late 19th century to the present; branches include the Vaska Emanouilova Gallery (opened 2006) and the Dechko Uzunov Gallery-Museum (2016); some sculptures stand in the City Garden (sghg.bg history).
- Programme example: the "Other Eye" project, launched in 2010, in which people who are not art historians curate shows from the permanent collection; "Portrait of a Friend" (2019) was its fifth, curated by the filmmaker Iglika Trifonova (Sofia Globe).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 19:00, Sunday 11:00 to 18:00, closed Monday and on Christmas, New Year and Easter; free on the second and fourth Thursday of each month. The gallery says the address is accessible to people with impaired motor functions. The ticket price is on a pricing page that was not read (sghg.bg).

FUNDING
Sofia City Art Gallery: mainly public: Sofia Municipality
- Sofia Municipality resumed annual funding for buying artworks in 2007, after an 18-year break (sghg.bg mission page). The page gives no amounts.
- The Partners page shows six logos: the Ministry of Culture, Sofia Municipality, America for Bulgaria Foundation, Aurubis Bulgaria, Institut Français and Goethe-Institut. The logos carry no text; the names come from the links behind them, and the page does not say what each pays for or how much.
- Media partners shown: BNT, BNR, BTA and Kultura (sghg.bg).
- No reporting found of what Aurubis Bulgaria or America for Bulgaria Foundation gave the gallery beyond the logos.
- No reporting found of a budget, annual report, accounts or board. The pages read name no director.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Search of Bulgarian and English coverage returned exhibition listings and municipal items only.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the gallery is public: a municipal institution funded by Sofia, with a ministry, a foundation, a company and two cultural institutes shown as partners. The weak points are that no budget, accounts, board or director is published and the partner logos are not labelled with a role. Worth watching: what Aurubis and America for Bulgaria Foundation actually give.
