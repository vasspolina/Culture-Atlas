CCA LAGOS (CENTRE FOR CONTEMPORARY ART): Lagos, Nigeria
ID: cca-lagos
Website: https://ccalagos.org

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit visual art organisation at 9 McEwen Street, Yaba, Lagos.
- Founded in December 2007 by curator Bisi Silva (1962 to 2019), who directed it until her death; the centre's website lists Ade Hassan, MBE as Executive Director.
- Occupied a five-storey building in Yaba that it rented from its start (Contemporary And, 2022); it closed for renovation and reopened on 12 February 2025 with the exhibition "Archives and Memories", curated by Favour Ritaro and run until 31 May 2025. The reopening was Phase 1 of a three-phase plan.
- Renovation designs for the building were by Greycave and Studio Contra Ltd; the plans included a gallery, bookshop, library, experimental studio, a lift and wheelchair access (Contemporary And).
- Programmes: the Àsìkò art school (begun 2010), Art-iculate Lectures, CCA Talks, workshops, an archive residency, the Art-iculate publishing initiative and a library. A May 2026 piece in Kurating counts over 7,000 books and a digital archive of about 3,000 records, and says a Lagos Archives Digitization Project launched on 24 April 2026.

FUNDING
CCA Lagos: self-generated income and a benefit auction: artists' donated works sold at Sotheby's in 2022
- Operating budget: for nearly two decades the centre ran "on a shoestring budget sourced from its exhibitions and publication programs" (Contemporary And, Sabo Kpade, 28 June 2022).
- Sotheby's CCA Lagos Benefit auction, 15 to 22 March 2022, within Sotheby's online Modern & Contemporary African Art sale: 25 donated lots to give the centre a permanent home and renovate it. Donors named by Sotheby's include El Anatsui (listed as a CCA trustee), Yinka Shonibare, Kehinde Wiley, Abdoulaye Konaté and Sokari Douglas Camp; Contemporary And adds Joy Labinjo and Michaela Yearwood-Dan.
- Contemporary And reports that over one million dollars was raised, that no seller's commission was charged so the full hammer price went to CCA, and that some proceeds were used to secure purchase of the five-storey building.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind CCA Lagos is its exhibition and publication income and a 2022 benefit auction of works donated by artists, among them El Anatsui, Yinka Shonibare and Kehinde Wiley. The auction proceeds went to securing and renovating the building.
