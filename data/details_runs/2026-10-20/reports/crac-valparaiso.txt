CRAC VALPARAÍSO (CENTRO DE RESIDENCIAS DE ARTE CONTEMPORÁNEO): Valparaíso, Chile
ID: crac-valparaiso
Website: https://cracvalparaiso.cl

OVERVIEW
- Collaborative non-profit platform for socio-artistic productions across the city of Valparaíso, with offices at Avenida Prat 798, oficina 574 (TransArtists).
- Self-sustained since 2007, as a project with a flexible, autonomous management model (TransArtists; Dutch Art Institute).
- Residencies for artists and researchers are treated as city practices; residents often run temporary community actions such as talks, workshops, performances and seminars. Residencies last one to three months, and applications are accepted on an ongoing basis (TransArtists).
- It also runs a public community archive, a printed publication and a digital publisher; listed focus areas are research residencies, water and coastal work, urban projects and socially engaged practice (TransArtists).
- Accommodation is a room in the historic district or a similar space; the offices have work tables, wireless internet and a documentary archive on contemporary art, the public sphere, and city and territory (TransArtists).

FUNDING
CRAC Valparaíso: self-sustained non-profit, as stated by CRAC
- TransArtists and the Dutch Art Institute both state that CRAC has been "self-sustained since 2007".

BOTTOM LINE
CRAC Valparaíso is described by TransArtists and the Dutch Art Institute as a self-sustained, independent non-profit platform for residencies in Valparaíso, running since 2007. That is the platform's own description.
