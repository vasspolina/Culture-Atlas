KUNST HALLE SANKT GALLEN: St. Gallen, Switzerland
ID: kunst-halle-sankt-gallen-st-gallen
Website: https://www.kunsthallesanktgallen.ch/de/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art venue at Davidstrasse 40 in St. Gallen; it shows new art, supports artists in making new work and works with art venues in Switzerland and abroad.
- Founded in 1985 (Wikipedia). From 1988 it used the building at Wassergasse 24, which had to be vacated at the end of 1992 because of demolition, and it then moved to Davidstrasse 40 (Wikipedia).
- Leadership: Josef Felix Müller (first artistic director), Dorothea Strauss (curator, 1996 to 2001), Gianni Jetzer (2001 to 2006) and Giovanni Carmine (since 2007) (Wikipedia).
- Legal form: Verein Kunst Halle Sankt Gallen, governed by a board chaired in 2024 by Maria Nänny; Larissa Kasper was proposed as her successor as president (annual report 2024).
- Member of the Association of Swiss Museums (VMS) and the association of Swiss institutions for contemporary art (VSIZK) (Wikipedia).
- On site: exhibitions, events, art education for school classes, adults and families, and publications.
- Visiting: open only while an exhibition is on, Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 18:00, Saturday and Sunday 11:00 to 17:00, closed Monday. Admission CHF 7; CHF 2 for students and KulturLegi holders; free for members, Raiffeisen cardholders, Swiss Museum Pass holders, under-18s and school classes from the canton of St. Gallen. The main entrance has stairs; the step-free route is a ramp of about 8% gradient through the courtyard at the corner of Geltenwilerstrasse and Davidstrasse, and visitors phone for the door to be opened. The accessible toilet is on the first floor.

FUNDING
Kunst Halle Sankt Gallen: public subsidy and private support: City and Canton of St. Gallen subsidies of CHF 404,036 of CHF 684,261 income, plus foundations, members and a patrons' club
- City of St. Gallen: CHF 208,100 in 2024; the town has paid an annually renewable subsidy since 1990 (annual report 2024; Wikipedia).
- Canton of St. Gallen: CHF 195,936 in 2024 (annual report 2024).
- Other grants: CHF 164,692 in 2024 (annual report 2024). Named supporters: Swisslos Kulturförderung, Kulturförderung Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Pro Helvetia, Abteilung Kultur Basel-Stadt, Città di Lugano, HEAD Genève, Susanne und Martin Knechtli-Kradolfer-Stiftung, Ernst und Olga Gubler-Hablützel Stiftung, Landis & Gyr Stiftung, Erna und Curt Burgauer Stiftung, Dr. Georg und Josi Guggenheim-Stiftung, Ria & Arthur Dietschweiler Stiftung, Office for Contemporary Art Norway, Straubenzeller Fonds, the Lisson Gallery and the Thaddaeus Ropac Gallery. Swisslos is the cantonal public lottery distributor.
- Raiffeisen funds the education programme (annual report 2024).
- Members: CHF 25,873 from members paying up to CHF 500 and CHF 29,000 from members paying CHF 500 or more; the Club der 20 paid CHF 50,000 in 2024 (annual report 2024). Friends pay CHF 500 a year, firm members up to CHF 1,500, patrons CHF 100 to 400.
- The Club der 20 list in the 2024 annual report names individuals with their firms, among them Jeannine Bruderer and Daniel Brunner of Helvetia Versicherungen, Luca Vadas and Martin Zuber of UBS Switzerland AG, Roland Eugster of Soplar sa and Bruno Looser of OTG St.Gallen AG. The Friends list names Barbara Staubli of Bank Julius Bär & Co. AG, Zürich, and C. Sylvia Weber and Barbara Rohner of Würth Management AG, Rorschach.
- Tickets and events brought in CHF 10,274 in 2024.
- The 2024 accounts closed at CHF 1,353.84, reviewed by the auditors Viola Schumacher and Pascal Wiesli (annual report 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kunst Halle is public subsidy from the City and Canton of St. Gallen (CHF 404,036 of CHF 684,261 operating income in 2024), foundations and cantonal and national cultural funds, and a members' base that includes the Club der 20 (CHF 50,000). Raiffeisen funds the education programme, and the 2024 annual report lists individuals from UBS, Helvetia and Bank Julius Bär among the Club der 20 and Friends.
