ACCA (AUSTRALIAN CENTRE FOR CONTEMPORARY ART): Melbourne, Australia
ID: acca
Website: https://acca.melbourne (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at 111 Sturt Street, Southbank, in the Melbourne Arts Precinct (stated by ACCA).
- Established in 1983 and moved in 2002 into its purpose-built building (stated by ACCA).
- Building: designed by Wood Marsh and completed in 2002, with four large gallery spaces and a rusty steel facade; the complex also includes facilities for the Chunky Move dance company and Malthouse Theatre. It won a RAIA Institutional Architecture Award in 2003 (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: new commissions, exhibitions, publications, digital initiatives, education, outreach and public programmes (stated by ACCA).
- Leadership: Max Delany was artistic director and CEO from early 2016 and announced on 8 July 2024 that he would step down in November 2024 (ACCA); Myles Russell-Cook was appointed CEO and artistic director in November 2024 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Friday 10am to 5pm and Saturday and Sunday 11am to 5pm, Monday by appointment only; gallery entry is free. The about page links to an access page whose content was not read.

FUNDING
ACCA: mainly public with philanthropic and corporate partners: Victorian, Australian and Melbourne governments, plus the Ian Potter Foundation
- Government partners listed on ACCA's current partners page: Creative Victoria, Creative Australia (with the Visual Arts and Crafts Strategy), the City of Melbourne, DET (as printed on the page) and the Victoria State Government. No dates or amounts are given.
- Trust and foundation partner listed: the Ian Potter Foundation. No amount or year found.
- Corporate partners listed: Dulux, KWM, Wood Marsh, Supernormal, Hannah St Hotel, Fish Nankivell, Jackson Clements Burrows, Andrew Curtis Photography, ah ah ah art! and Peter Corrigan Music.
- Media partners listed: 3RRR, Art Guide Australia, Broadsheet, The Guardian and Vault. Event partners: Capi, Firecracker and Stomping Ground.
- ACCA says it receives "generous support of various government, philanthropic and corporate organisations" and names no further supporters on the page.
- Board (ACCA's people page): Dr Terry Wu (chair), Charlotte Day (deputy chair), Dr Theresia Spencer, John Tuck, Lisa Bowman, Maree Clarke, Elisa Clements, Paul Cross, Lisa Fox, Patricia Piccinini and Sarah Lynn Rees.
