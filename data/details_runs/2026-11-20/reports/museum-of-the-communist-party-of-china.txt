MUSEUM OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY OF CHINA: Beijing, China
ID: museum-of-the-communist-party-of-china
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- The Party's permanent museum of its own history, at 7 Beichen East Road in the Olympic Park, Chaoyang district, Beijing, beside the China Science and Technology Museum (Visit Beijing, People's Daily).
- The Party Central Committee under Xi Jinping decided to build it soon after the 19th Party Congress in late 2017; ground was broken on 26 August 2018, it was completed on 5 May 2021 and opened on 18 June 2021, with Xi among its first visitors (China Daily, People's Daily, gov.cn, all state media).
- The Beijing Institute of Architectural Design, led by chief architect Shao Weiping, won the design over seven bidders; the plan has the shape of the character gong ("worker"), with 28 columns on each side for the 28 years before 1949 and marble from near Jiajin Mountain on the Long March route (China Daily, People's Daily).
- More than 200 organisations and nearly 50,000 people took part in building it (People's Daily).
- Xi said in 2020 that it should be a "sacred hall", a spiritual home for Party members and an educational centre to guide the public to follow the Party (China Daily).
- The opening exhibition showed more than 2,600 images and over 3,500 objects, based on the Party's two "historical resolutions" (People's Daily; own translation).
- It reported more than 3.5 million visits by 30 June 2024, including over 10,000 group visits; Wu Xiangdong was its head (Xinhua via gov.cn). In October 2026 it opened two exhibitions for the 90th anniversary of the Long March (Xinhua).
- Visiting: free with no booking; open 9:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00, closed Mondays except holidays (Visit Beijing, May 2026).

FUNDING
Museum of the Communist Party of China: state-funded: Party Central Committee, central government and Beijing city
- The Central Propaganda Department set up a leading group to coordinate the project, the plans, the surrounding area, the exhibition and the collecting of relics (People's Daily, the Party newspaper).
- The National Development and Reform Commission, the Ministry of Finance, the Government Offices Administration of the Party Central Committee and Beijing city supported and took part in the building throughout (People's Daily).
- The China Federation of Literary and Art Circles and the China Artists Association organised teams from the Central Academy of Fine Arts, Tsinghua and other art schools to make the five sculptures on the square (People's Daily, China Daily).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the Chinese party-state's and carries no funder flag. The weak point is purpose: the Party built it under Xi's personal direction to present its own history.
