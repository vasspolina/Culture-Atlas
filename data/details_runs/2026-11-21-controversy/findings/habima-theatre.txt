WIDER CONTEXT
- In November 2019 about 50 actors employed under a collective agreement were not paid their October wages; after an emergency meeting the actors announced they would strike within 14 days if not paid, and the theatre owed actors about 2 million shekels (Maariv, Yuval Bagno, 17 November 2019).
- The actors' union SHaM filed an urgent motion at the Tel Aviv Regional Labour Court against the theatre, its CEO Odelia Friedman and its CFO Benny Tzarfati over union dues deducted from actors' pay and not passed on; the theatre's management said the Finance Ministry had not transferred the funding allocated to it by law (Maariv, 17 November 2019).
- In September 2019 the company Power Dialing applied to have the theatre liquidated (Maariv, 17 November 2019).
- On 25 November 2019 Tel Aviv District Court President Eitan Orenstein declined to liquidate Habima and ordered a general creditors' arrangement; he wrote "שוכנעתי שהבימה היא חברה מדממת, חדלת פירעון הן מהבחינה המאזנית והן מהבחינה התזרימית" ("I am convinced that Habima is a bleeding company, insolvent both on its balance sheet and in its cash flow", translated) (Globes, Avishai Grinzaig, 25 November 2019).
- The court put the debts at over 75 million shekels by the state's figure and at least 45 million by the company's, with Bank Leumi owed about 49 million and employees about 4 million (Globes, 25 November 2019).
