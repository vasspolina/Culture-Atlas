STATE HERMITAGE MUSEUM: St Petersburg, Russia
ID: state-hermitage-museum-st-petersburg
Website: https://support.hermitagemuseum.org/ru/sponsors (checked 2026-10-24; loads, but its certificate failed verification in one fetch tool; the 2026 sponsor list below was read with certificate checking off)

OVERVIEW
- Russia's state art and culture museum, in six historic buildings on Palace Embankment including the Winter Palace (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1764, when Catherine the Great bought a collection of paintings from the Berlin merchant Johann Ernst Gotzkowsky; over three million items (Wikipedia).
- Mikhail Piotrovsky has been director since July 1992; the museum is federal state property (Wikipedia).
- Its Amsterdam branch (Amstelhof building opened 19 June 2009) is now the H'ART Museum.
- Visiting: closed on Mondays; foreign visitors pay more than citizens of Russia and Belarus (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
State Hermitage Museum: state-owned, with Russian state-controlled banks, oil companies and oligarch trustees as sponsors
- The museum is federal state property; no reporting found of its budget split between the state and its own income.
- Its 2026 sponsor list names, by circle: Vladimir Potanin and Vladimir Putin among the Jupiter circle; Rosneft, VTB, SBER, Magnit, Rosatom Mashinostroenie and Samsung Electronics Rus in the Athena circle; T-Bank, Gazprom Neft, Rosseti, Rostelecom and the Vladimir Potanin Charitable Foundation in the Nike circle; the Gazprombank "Social Responsibility" charitable foundation and Alfa-Bank among others in the Atlas circle (Hermitage Friends Club). No amounts or years of payment are given.
- Board of trustees: Potanin has chaired it since April 2003; members listed include Anton Siluanov, German Gref, Alexei Kudrin, Leonid Fridlyand, Mikhail Shvydkoi and Piotrovsky, with JTI Russia (LLC) as corporate member (Hermitage Friends Club, Wikipedia).

Red flags: sanctioned banks, oil companies and a donor on the sponsor list
- VTB (Athena circle): full blocking sanctions from the US Treasury on 24 February 2022.
- Sber (Athena circle): fully blocked by the US Treasury on 6 April 2022, the day Alfa-Bank (Atlas circle) was too.
- Gazprombank: the US Treasury designated it on 21 November 2024 under Executive Order 14024. Its "Social Responsibility" charitable foundation, named as an AO Gazprombank entity, is on the list.
- Gazprom Neft (Nike circle): designated by the US Treasury on 10 January 2025. Rosneft (Athena circle): 22 October 2025.
- Potanin gives personally and through his foundation; his donations to the Hermitage endowment fund total $5 million (Wikipedia). Canada sanctioned him on 6 April 2022 and the US Treasury on 15 December 2022 (Wikipedia, Kelley Drye).

Red flags: fossil fuel sponsors
- Rosneft and Gazprom Neft are on the 2026 sponsor list.

Red flags: boycott and resignation over the war
- Hermitage Amsterdam cut ties on 3 March 2022, saying the attack on Ukraine made keeping the distance "no longer tenable" (AP, via PBS).
- Dmitry Ozerkov, head of the museum's modern arts section, announced on 2 October 2022 that he had quit and left Russia (RFE/RL, 3 October 2022).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism found concentrates on the director's politics and the museum's foreign partnerships after the 2022 invasion.
1. Hyperallergic: Rachel Spence, "How the Hermitage Museum Artwashes Russian Aggression" (2022)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/715271/how-the-hermitage-museum-artwashes-russian-aggression/
- Foreign partnerships, especially with the Louis Vuitton Foundation, lend legitimacy to a government that represses artists.
- Piotrovsky is openly loyal to Putin and led United Russia's St Petersburg ticket in 2021.
2. RFE/RL: North.Realities, "Top Official At Hermitage Museum In St. Petersburg Leaves Russia In Protest At The War In Ukraine" (2022)
URL: https://www.rferl.org/a/hermitage-director-ozerkov-leaves-russia-ukraine-invasion/32063557.html
- Ozerkov: "I left because I don't intend to have anything in common with today's Russia."
- Reports that Piotrovsky praised the war in a June interview. News outlet, not art press.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Piotrovsky was sanctioned by Canada on 31 August 2023 and listed by the EU on 23 April 2026 as a "close associate" of Putin who "actively supported and justified" the war, citing his backing for unauthorised digs in occupied Crimea; the museum gave no comment (The Art Newspaper, 29 April 2026).
- Trustee Herman Gref, head of Sberbank, was designated by the US Treasury on 24 March 2022. No reporting found of a personal donation.
- Early December 2025: Poland detained Hermitage archaeologist Alexander Butyagin on a Ukrainian request over excavations at Myrmekion, Crimea (Novaya Gazeta Europe, 11 December 2025); Poland released him in a prisoner exchange on 28 April 2026 (The Art Newspaper).
- Connection: Piotrovsky and Butyagin work for the museum and Gref sits on its board; none is shown to fund it.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Hermitage is Russian state money plus sponsors that include US-sanctioned VTB, Sber, Gazprom Neft and Rosneft, the Gazprombank foundation, and Potanin as donor and board chair. The director is sanctioned by Canada and the EU. Worth watching: the yearly sponsor list.
