WIDER CONTEXT
- In December 2019, 40 named participants of the first Feminnale of Contemporary Art, from 22 countries, published an open letter to the President of Kyrgyzstan saying the minister of culture "arbitrarily banned several works from the show"; they demanded "that Azamat Zhamankulov be removed from office given his professional inaptitude for this position", asked that Mira Dzhangaracheva be reinstated as the museum's director, and called for a ban on censorship in law (open letter posted on Art Leaks, 9 December 2019).
- In April 2021 the Pervomaisky District Court of Bishkek, Judge Saltanat Esenkulova, dismissed the abuse-of-office case against the former director Yuristanbek Shygaev on the grounds of the statute of limitations, with the prosecution supporting the defence's request. According to the investigation, in December 2013 he had two paintings, Nikolai Sverchkov's "Horse and Dog" and Nikolai Ge's "Portrait of Bakunin", removed for scanning without a written order from the Ministry of Culture, and they were damaged; he paid 672,000 soms in compensation (24.kg, Aida Dzhumashova, 22 April 2021).
