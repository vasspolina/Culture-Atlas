B7L9 ART CENTRE (KAMEL LAZAAR FOUNDATION): Tunis, Tunisia
ID: b7l9-art-centre-tunis
Website: https://www.kamellazaarfoundation.org/project/b7l9-art-centre

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art centre in Bhar Lazreg, a neighbourhood on the outskirts of Tunis, described by the foundation as Tunisia's first non-profit contemporary art centre.
- Opened on 16 March 2019 (L'Economiste Maghrébin); the foundation calls it its first permanent venue. The Art Newspaper (Tim Cornwell, 22 March 2019) reported the opening and the inaugural exhibition "Climbing through the Tide", curated by Başak Şenova, with 50 artists from 21 countries.
- Run by the Kamel Lazaar Foundation, a Swiss-law foundation created in 2005 and headquartered in Geneva with offices in Tunis and London. Kamel Lazaar is its founder and Lina Lazaar its president (foundation site). The Art Newspaper names Lina Lazaar as vice president in 2019.
- Named for the Arabic phonetic rendering of Bhar Lazreg (foundation site).
- Building: the facade was co-designed with residents and reinterprets First World War "dazzle camouflage" ships (foundation site). The Art Newspaper quotes the education minister Hatem Ben Salem saying that after 2011 land was taken and that about nine in ten buildings in the area were illegal.
- On site: a 500 sq m exhibition hall, a screening room, a dance and music studio, flexible event spaces and a garden (foundation site); the programme covers exhibitions, residencies, a library, concerts, workshops, talks and children's arts education. The Art Newspaper put the white-cube gallery at 1,500 sq m.
- Visiting: during the 2019 opening exhibition the space was free and open Monday to Friday, 11:00 to 19:00 (L'Economiste Maghrébin).

FUNDING
B7L9 Art Centre: privately run: the Kamel Lazaar Foundation, with 18 listed partners
- Operator: the Kamel Lazaar Foundation, created in 2005 by Kamel Lazaar, a Tunisian-Swiss investment banker who founded Swicorp in 1987 (Wikipedia). Wikipedia describes Swicorp as an investment banking, private equity and asset management firm headquartered in Riyadh. This is the founder's own banking history, not a funder of the centre.
- Partner listing: the foundation's undated partners page lists 18 partners: the German Federal Foreign Office, the European Union, Goethe-Institut, Pro Helvetia, Institut Français de Tunisie, Tunisair, the Tunisian Ministry of Education, the Office National du Tourisme Tunisien, the 1-54 Contemporary African Art Fair, the University of Applied Arts Vienna, Mille, Tunisie.co, Cérès Editions, Manouba Youth Center, the Tunisian National Orchestra, Die Deutsche Kammerphilharmonie Bremen, Zukunftslabor and Uteek Digital. The page is a partner listing, not a funding list.
- Staff named on the foundation site for the centre: Meriem Zeghidi (project manager), Souheila Ghorbel (programme manager), Sirine Ismail (artistic mediator).

BOTTOM LINE
The centre is run by a private Swiss-law foundation set up by Kamel Lazaar, the founder of the Riyadh-based investment bank Swicorp. The foundation's partners page lists 18 public and cultural bodies and companies from Germany, the EU, Switzerland, France, Austria and Tunisia. No flag applies.
