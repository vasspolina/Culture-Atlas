WIDER CONTEXT
- In January 2014 the PAV faced closure: the renewal of its Convenzione with the bodies that govern Turin's museum system was delayed, while the city government announced it would halve culture funding (Artribune, Helga Marsala, 13 January 2014).
- The association acPav, which runs the PAV's projects, launched an online petition addressed to Mayor Piero Fassino asking citizens, artists, critics, art journalists and museum directors to sign (Artribune, 13 January 2014).
- PAV staff gave figures of 70,000 visitors in 2013 against 20,000 in 2012, costs cut from one million to 500,000 euros, and staff cut from 14 to 7 (Artribune).
