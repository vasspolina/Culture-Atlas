KIRAN NADAR MUSEUM OF ART: New Delhi, India
ID: kiran-nadar-museum-of-art-new-delhi
Website: https://www.knma.org/ (checked 2026-10-24; loads)

OVERVIEW
- Private non-profit museum of modern and contemporary art at DLF South Court Mall, Saket, New Delhi, on the ground floor next to Select Citywalk Mall; a second branch is in Noida (KNMA site, Global Indian).
- Founded by the collector Kiran Nadar, who has collected since 1988; it was established on 1 January 2010 and began as a gallery in HCL Technologies' premises in Noida. A year later it moved to Saket (Wikipedia, Global Indian).
- Named for its founder. Nadar is Chairperson of the museum and a trustee of the Shiv Nadar Foundation; she married Shiv Nadar, founder of HCL Technologies (Global Indian).
- Branches total about 34,000 square feet (Global Indian). A planned building of 1 million square feet, designed by Adjaye Associates after an invitation-only competition, is announced on the home page (stated by the museum).
- More than 7,000 artworks made accessible and over 100,000 visitors a year (Global Indian, 2022). The collection ranges from Raja Ravi Varma and M. F. Husain to Anish Kapoor (Wikipedia).
- Visiting at Saket: Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 20:00, closed Monday; free for all, with registration at reception; wheelchair access and in-person help (KNMA visit page).
- Leadership listed: Manuel Rabaté, Chief Executive Officer and Director; Roobina Karode, Artistic Director and Chief Curator. The Art Newspaper's headline says Rabaté left the Louvre Abu Dhabi to head it.

FUNDING
Kiran Nadar Museum of Art: private: the founder and the Shiv Nadar Foundation, amounts unpublished
- Wikipedia and Global Indian say the Shiv Nadar Foundation supports the museum. The museum's own team page does not say how it is funded, and no amounts were found.
- HCL Technologies is the company Shiv Nadar founded; the museum began in its Noida premises (Wikipedia, Global Indian).
- The team page lists only one trustee, Kiran Nadar, and one adviser, Deepanjana D. Klein, Special Advisor to the Chairperson.
- No reporting found of donors, corporate sponsors or government grants. No reporting found of the FCRA statements named in the Atlas record, which could not be located on the pages opened.

Red flags: dismissal of a curator and an artist's withdrawal, 2023
- In early July 2023 the museum terminated Sandip K Luis, manager of curatorial research and publications, after a Facebook post of 15 May criticising "Jana Shakti: A Collective Power", a state exhibition at the National Gallery of Modern Art marking 100 episodes of Prime Minister Modi's radio show, and Nadar's role in it (The Art Newspaper, Sabrang India).
- Nadar advised that exhibition (The Art Newspaper, Sabrang India). The museum is private; this is a link to a state show, not state funding.
- Hundreds of people in the art and academic community signed a campaign demanding Luis's reinstatement (The Art Newspaper).
- The Bangladeshi photographer Shahidul Alam withdrew his retrospective "Singed but not Burnt", due to open on 17 July 2023, citing Nadar's "clear endorsement" of events tied to the government's propaganda (The Art Newspaper, Sabrang India).
- The museum did not comment on Alam's withdrawal or Luis's termination (Sabrang India). No reporting found of Luis's reinstatement.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: One substantial piece in the art press, from The Art Newspaper in 2023, on the firing and the standing of private museums; other coverage found is news reporting.
1. The Art Newspaper: Sabih Ahmed, "Controversy at Kiran Nadar Museum of Art in Delhi raises important questions over private museums in public life" (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/08/02/controversy-kiran-nadar-museum-of-art-in-delhi-india-private-museums
- Argues that a private museum with public roles, especially one receiving public subsidy or CSR-linked funding, should answer to staff, the public and the art community, and that patronage can become patronising.
- Names Nadar, Luis and Alam; reports no official statement from the museum, only a public defence of Nadar and the museum by the gallerist Peter Nagy.
2. Sabrang India: report on Alam's withdrawal (2023)
URL: https://sabrangindia.in/bangladeshi-dissenting-voice-shahidul-alam-withdraws-from-knma-event-noida
- Not an art-press essay: reports Alam's letter, which raised Nadar's endorsement of government propaganda events and the censure of those who critique them.
- Quotes a statement from "concerned individuals" on the Kafila portal calling the dismissal a "high-handed way of functioning."

BOTTOM LINE
No flagged funder was found behind the museum, but its funding is not published: the Shiv Nadar Foundation is named only in secondary sources and no amounts appear. The weak point is the 2023 dismissal of a curator who criticised the founder's role in a state exhibition, and the loss of Alam's show. Worth watching: donor disclosure, and how the planned 1 million square foot building is financed.
