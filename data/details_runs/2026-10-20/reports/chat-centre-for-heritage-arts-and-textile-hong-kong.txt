CHAT (CENTRE FOR HERITAGE, ARTS AND TEXTILE): Hong Kong, Hong Kong
ID: chat-centre-for-heritage-arts-and-textile-hong-kong
Website: https://www.themills.com.hk/en/chat/introduction/

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit textile art centre on the 2nd floor of The Mills, 45 Pak Tin Par Street, Tsuen Wan, in the former Nan Fung Cotton Mills; Wikipedia places it in Unit 6 (CHAT website, Wikipedia).
- Nan Fung Cotton Mills was founded in 1954 by Chen Din Hwa, who also founded Nan Fung Group; Units 4 to 6 stopped spinning in 2008 and became warehouses (Wikipedia, citing Ming Pao).
- Nan Fung announced the revitalisation of the mills in 2014; CHAT opened on 16 March 2019 with the inaugural exhibition "Unfolding: Fabric of Our Life" (Wikipedia).
- Run by Mill6 Foundation, a non-profit founded in 2015 (Nan Fung Group). Takahashi Mizuki is Executive Director and Chief Curator (CHAT website).
- The gallery space, named the D. H. Chen Foundation Gallery, was designed by Assemble and HATO (Wikipedia).
- CHAT runs three seasonal exhibitions a year with workshops and tours (Wikipedia). Its community project Seed to Textile began in 2019 with indigo seeds from India (The Mills website), and since 2023 CHAT has run the ANTEPRIMA x CHAT Contemporary Textile Art Prize (CHAT press releases).
- Visiting: free admission; open 11:00 to 19:00, closed Tuesdays; free docent-led tours; wheelchair users enter at ground floor on Pak Tin Par Street and take the lift to the 2nd floor (CHAT website).

FUNDING
CHAT: private: a property group and a family foundation. Nan Fung Group is main donor and the D. H. Chen Foundation is founding donor
- The D. H. Chen Foundation is the founding donor and Nan Fung Group the main donor (Nan Fung Group, Wikipedia).
- Nan Fung invested HK$700 million to convert Units 4, 5 and 6 into The Mills, which also holds a retail floor, the Nan Fung Studios incubator and a lab run by the Hong Kong Research Institute of Textiles and Apparel (Wikipedia, citing HK01 and Ming Pao).
- Board of Mill6 Foundation: Vanessa Cheung, Group Managing Director of Nan Fung Development Ltd and founder of The Mills; Vincent Cheung, Group Chief Executive Officer of Nan Fung Development Ltd; Billy Hui, Deputy Head of Property Division, and Nelson Tang, Group Chief Financial Officer, both of Nan Fung Development Ltd; Karen Cheung, trustee and Chief Strategist of The D. H. Chen Foundation; Wallace Lam, former Chief Executive of Shanghai Commercial Bank; Joyce Wang of Joyce Wang Studio; and Charles Donohoe of Apollo Management Asia Pacific (CHAT website).
- Vanessa Cheung told China Daily Hong Kong ("A mission to build relationships and inspire generations", Dai Kaiyi, 11 September 2020) that "My desire to create The Mills was inspired by my grandfather", and said she found three of the original factories used for storage.
- ANTEPRIMA partners with CHAT on the Contemporary Textile Art Prize; the first edition's winners, Yee I-Lann, Roziah Binti Jalalid and Julitah Kulinting, were named on 29 November 2024 (CHAT press release).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Wikipedia (The Mills, Hong Kong), citing HK01 and Ming Pao, reports that on 6 May 2020 a photo posted by then-Chief Executive Carrie Lam led to an HK01 reporter finding a workshop making anti-epidemic masks on the 4th floor of the mill complex. Staff and security guards expelled the reporter and the room was sealed with plywood.
- Wikipedia, citing Ming Pao of 17 May 2020, says the 2018 revitalisation deed did not allow "industrial purposes" in Units 4 and 5, which Nan Fung Cotton Mills (No. 2) Co. leased to Nan Fung Workshop Incubation Center Ltd. It reports the lawyer Tse Tin Leung saying the site is limited to small samples, and questioning whether large-scale production breaches the deed.
- Connection: the workshop was in the mill complex that Nan Fung Group developed, and Nan Fung Group is CHAT's main donor. CHAT is in Unit 6.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind CHAT is private: Nan Fung Group, the property group that developed The Mills, is the main donor and the D. H. Chen Foundation is the founding donor. Four of the eight Mill6 Foundation directors are Nan Fung Development executives.
