MORGAN LIBRARY & MUSEUM: New York, USA
ID: morgan-library-museum-new-york
Website: https://www.themorgan.org

OVERVIEW
- Museum and research library at 225 Madison Avenue, at East 36th Street, in Murray Hill, Manhattan (Wikipedia).
- Banker J. P. Morgan bought a brownstone on the site in 1880 and later the surrounding lots to house his collection; Charles McKim of McKim, Mead & White designed the main building, completed in 1906 (Wikipedia).
- Morgan hired Belle da Costa Greene as his personal librarian around 1905; she led the collection until retiring in 1948 (Wikipedia).
- After Morgan's death in 1913 his son Jack Morgan kept the collection; in 1924 it became a public institution with a $1.5 million endowment, and it opened to the general public in December 1942 (Wikipedia).
- Expansion by Renzo Piano and Beyer Blinder Belle, with most new space underground, reopened in April 2006 under the current name (Wikipedia).
- Holdings of more than 350,000 objects, including over 1,100 illuminated manuscripts, three Gutenberg Bibles and about 12,000 drawings and watercolours (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Morgan Library & Museum: private: the Morgan family endowment, foundations and donors
- Jack Morgan, J. P. Morgan's son, announced a $1.5 million endowment for the library when it became a public institution in 1924; the Fellows group was formed in 1949 to raise funds for the collections (Wikipedia).
- Eugene and Clare Thaw gave $10 million for a conservation centre in 1999 (Wikipedia).
- A centennial campaign begun in 2023 aimed to raise $50 million, and Katharine Rayner's $10 million gift endowed the director's position (Wikipedia).
- Form 990 for the year ending March 2025: total revenue $55,087,598, of which contributions $29,030,601; expenses $41,926,115; net assets $426,442,469. Director Colin B. Bailey received $772,730 in compensation plus $123,309 in other compensation (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).

BOTTOM LINE
The Morgan is financed by the 1924 endowment of $1.5 million announced by Jack Morgan, later gifts such as the Thaw and Rayner gifts, and $29.0 million in contributions in the year to March 2025 (Form 990). Its net assets were $426.4 million at the end of the fiscal year ending March 2025 (Form 990).
