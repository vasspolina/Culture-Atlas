FUNDACIÓ ANTONI TÀPIES: Barcelona, Spain
ID: fundació-antoni-tàpies-barcelona
Website: https://museutapies.org

OVERVIEW
- Museum and foundation at Carrer d'Aragó 255 in Barcelona, in the former Editorial Montaner y Simón building by Lluís Domènech i Montaner (Museu Tàpies).
- Created in 1984 by the painter Antoni Tàpies to advance knowledge of modern and contemporary art, and opened in June 1990 (Museu Tàpies).
- Named for its founder; it holds one of the most complete collections of his work, mostly made up of donations by Antoni and Teresa Tàpies (Museu Tàpies).
- The programme combines temporary exhibitions, symposia, lectures and film seasons with publications, and its library houses the largest archive on Tàpies' work (Museu Tàpies).
- Renamed Museu Tàpies on 1 July 2024 after the board of trustees approved the change; Ferran Rodés and director Imma Prieto announced it (Bonart, 2 July 2024). Wikipedia also states the 2024 rename.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 19:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 15:00; general admission is 15 euros and 9 euros for students and people over 65, with free entry for visitors under 16, unemployed people with accreditation and Friends of the museum. The museum closes temporarily for renovation from 13 October 2026 (Museu Tàpies).

FUNDING
Fundació Antoni Tàpies: private foundation with public subsidies of 1.41 million euros in 2021 and 2.76 million euros in 2025 from the Generalitat de Catalunya, the Barcelona city council, the Spanish Ministry of Culture and the Diputació de Barcelona
- Subsidies posted by the museum totalled 1,414,055 euros (2021), 1,649,169 euros (2022), 1,900,533 euros (2023), 1,971,646 euros (2024) and 2,757,406 euros (2025) (Museu Tàpies, Grants 2021-2025).
- Generalitat de Catalunya: a named grant of 478,733 euros in 2021 and 2022, 728,733 euros in 2023, 778,733 euros in 2024 and 728,733 euros in 2025, plus 150,000 euros for commemorations in 2024 and again in 2025.
- Ajuntament de Barcelona: a named grant of 29,228 euros (2021), 277,046 euros (2022), 511,410 euros (2023), 261,410 euros (2024) and 489,317 euros (2025), plus Plan Avanza payments of 122,182 euros a year from 2021 to 2024.
- Spanish Ministry of Culture: a named grant of 68,740 euros (2021), 100,000 euros (2022 and 2023) and 250,000 euros (2024 and 2025); a line labelled "Capitalitat" of 650,000 euros (2021), 350,000 euros (2023), 228,921 euros (2024) and 821,079 euros (2025); a centenary contribution of 600,000 euros (2022); and a further 100,000 euros in 2025.
- Diputació de Barcelona: 150,000 euros in 2024 and in 2025. Creative Europe paid 12,826 euros (2021), 26,629 euros (2022) and 3,906 euros (2023).
- Governance: the board is chaired by Ferran Rodés i Vilà, with Teresa Barba Fàbregas as vice-president. It includes three members of the artist's family, two trustees from the Generalitat de Catalunya, two from the Ajuntament de Barcelona and six elective trustees including Manuel J. Borja-Villel (Museu Tàpies).
- The museum posts accounts with audit reports for 2021 to 2024 (Museu Tàpies).

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation is a private body whose published subsidies rose from 1.41 million euros in 2021 to 2.76 million euros in 2025, paid by the Generalitat de Catalunya, the Barcelona city council, the Spanish Ministry of Culture and the Diputació de Barcelona. The Generalitat and the city council also hold seats on its board alongside the artist's family and elective trustees.
