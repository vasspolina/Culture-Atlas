CASA BLOC MUSEUM-APARTMENT: Barcelona, Spain
ID: casa-bloc-museum-apartment
Website: https://www.dissenyhub.barcelona/en/activity/guided-tour-museum-apartment-casa-bloc-dwelling-111 (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Dwelling 1/11, a 60 square metre duplex on the first floor of block 2 of the Casa Bloc social-housing complex in Sant Andreu, at Carrer de l'Almirall Pròixida on the corner of Passeig de Torras i Bages (Catalan Wikipedia; Disseny Hub).
- The republican Generalitat's Institut Contra l'Atur Forçós commissioned Casa Bloc in 1932 from the GATCPAC architects Josep Lluís Sert, Josep Torres Clavé and Joan Baptista Subirana; President Francesc Macià laid the first stone in March 1933 (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Five seven-storey blocks in an inverted "S". Work stopped in the civil war; the Diputació de Barcelona took over the complex and the Franco regime finished it, housing military families, war orphans and widows in its more than 200 flats. It returned to the Generalitat with democracy and was declared a Bé Cultural d'Interès Nacional in 1992 (Catalan Wikipedia).
- In 1997 INCASÒL, the Diputació and Barcelona City Council agreed a rehabilitation by the architects Víctor and Marc Seguí, which ended in July 2008 with the demolition of the 1948 "Bloc fantasma". In 2022 a team of architects, archaeologists and historians was announced to draft a master plan for conserving the complex (Catalan Wikipedia, citing La Vanguardia).
- The flat was restored by Marta Montmany, Rossend Casanova and Víctor and Marc Seguí, with a period kitchen, bathroom, laundry with shower, hydraulic floor tiles and folding dining-room doors taken from other flats, and opened to guided visits in February 2012 (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Casanova, one of its restorers, calls it "a restored space, original in the strict sense of the term", not a recreation; the sideboard, wardrobes, bed and bedside tables were made to instructions published in the GATCPAC journal A.C. (Docomomo Journal 47, 2012).
- Guided tours on Saturdays at 11:00, in Catalan, and in Spanish on the last Saturday of the month; 5 euros a person, 75 euros for a group of up to 15 booked 15 days ahead. Closed in August, the first two weekends of September, 1 January, 1 May, 24 June and 25 and 28 December. Only the lower floor of the duplex is wheelchair accessible (Disseny Hub).
- A member of Iconic Houses, the network of house museums (Iconic Houses).

FUNDING
Casa Bloc Museum-Apartment: public: Barcelona City Council through the Museu del Disseny, and the Generalitat's land institute INCASÒL
- Commission: the Institut de Cultura de Barcelona, through the Museu del Disseny (then DHUB), and INCASÒL commissioned the flat's restoration (Catalan Wikipedia).
- Visits: Disseny Hub Barcelona, on a Barcelona City Council website, runs the tours and sells the tickets (Disseny Hub).
- Building: INCASÒL, the Generalitat's land institute, commissioned the complex's rehabilitation under the 1997 agreement with the Diputació de Barcelona and the City Council (Catalan Wikipedia).
- The initiative came from DHUB's own study of interior design (Docomomo Journal).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean public money from Barcelona's city council and the Generalitat, with no private sponsor. The weak point is how little of it the public can reach.
