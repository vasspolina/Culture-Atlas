NASHER SCULPTURE CENTER – Dallas, USA
Website: https://www.nashersculpturecenter.org (loaded 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum and sculpture garden in the Dallas Arts District, founded by collector Raymond D. Nasher; opened 2003 (Wikipedia).
- Building by Renzo Piano, garden by Peter Walker (PWP Landscape Architecture); the Beck Group built it. The site, across from the Dallas Museum of Art, was bought by Nasher in 1997 (Wikipedia).
- Houses the Raymond and Patsy Nasher Collection; director Jeremy Strick since 2009; runs the Nasher Prize for sculpture since 2015 (Wikipedia).
- Admission is free on the first Saturday of each month (own site). Hours, prices and accessibility details were not checked.

FUNDING
Nasher Sculpture Center: privately founded, now funded by foundations, individuals and some public arts grants.
- Construction: the Nasher Foundation paid the full $70 million for design and construction (Wikipedia).
- The space's own Foundation & Government Support page names 20 partners: Abe Zale Foundation, Buddy Taub Foundation, Burnham Foundation, Carl B. and Florence E. King Foundation, City of Dallas Office of Cultural Affairs, Communities Foundation of Texas, Dorothea L. Leonhardt Fund, Eugene McDermott Foundation, FABA (Fundación Almine y Bernard Ruiz-Picasso para el Arte), Goss-Michael Foundation, Henry Luce Foundation, Hoblitzelle Foundation, Llagas Foundation, Lyda Hill Foundation, Meadows Foundation, National Endowment for the Arts, Pro Helvetia, TACA, Texas Commission on the Arts, Texas Instruments Foundation. It also features The Dallas Foundation. (Stated by the space; no amounts given.)
- Governance: GuideStar lists seven trustees (self-reported, 17 Nov 2025): David Haemisegger (President), Andrea Nasher, Nancy Nasher Haemisegger, Sarah Haemisegger, Marc Pechersky, Stephen Stamas, Tyree Collier. Four of the seven carry the Nasher or Haemisegger family names.
- The FY2025 revenue figure in the existing record ($14.0M) could not be rechecked: the ProPublica pages were not retrieved.

Not related: Nasher Museum of Art at Duke University (Durham, North Carolina) — a separate university museum that carries the same family name. It is a different institution.

BOTTOM LINE
No reporting found of a contested funder. Money comes from the founding family's foundation, Texas and national foundations, and city, state and federal arts grants that the space names itself. Worth knowing: the board is mostly family, and there is a long dispute over glare from the neighbouring Museum Tower, which is owned by the Dallas Police and Fire Pension fund (CBS Texas, 2013). That dispute is about the building, not about funding.
