STAATLICHE MUSEEN ZU BERLIN: Berlin, Germany
ID: staatliche-museen-zu-berlin
Website: https://www.smb.museum/en/home/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Fifteen collections, three research institutes and the Gipsformerei, covering art, archaeology and ethnology from almost all cultures, on sites including Museum Island, the Kulturforum, Charlottenburg, Dahlem and the Humboldt Forum (smb.museum).
- Founded in 1823 by King Friedrich Wilhelm III of Prussia as the Königliche Museen (Wikipedia). It belongs to the Prussian Cultural Heritage Foundation (SPK), set up in 1957 to preserve the cultural property of the former state of Prussia (smb.museum).
- Leadership: a reformed SPK law came into force on 1 December 2025; a collegial Executive Board under SPK president Marion Ackermann now runs the foundation and includes Klaus Biesenbach, director of the Neue Nationalgalerie (SPK).
- Visitors: the SPK museums recorded 3,632,077 visits in 2025; the Neue Nationalgalerie had 608,000 and the Hamburger Bahnhof about 445,000 (SPK).
- The Pergamon Museum has been fully closed since October 2023; full reopening is planned for 2036 or 2037 (Berliner Zeitung; The Irish Times).
- Visiting: free for under-18s, people on benefits such as Bürgergeld and friends' association members; students and severely disabled visitors pay half (smb.museum).

FUNDING
Staatliche Museen zu Berlin: mainly public: the federal government, Berlin and the other German states through the SPK, plus friends' associations, collectors and sponsors
- The SPK's 2025 operating budget is about EUR 275.3 million: federal allocations about EUR 196.4 million, Berlin about EUR 38.6 million, the other states about EUR 20 million. A further EUR 143.1 million goes to construction; since 2003 the federal government has borne the whole cost of SPK construction (SPK budget page).
- Sparkassen-Finanzgruppe has been main sponsor since 2011; it renewed in 2014 for three years and in July 2017 for two, when the insurer Feuersozietät Berlin Brandenburg joined (smb.museum press releases).
- Single shows: the 2014 Schlüter exhibition was sponsored by the Kulturstiftung der Länder, the Kuratorium Museumsinsel and Sparkassen-Finanzgruppe, with the Ernst von Siemens Kunststiftung and the Rudolf-August Oetker-Stiftung among further supporters (smb.museum).
- Friends' associations: the museums list about twenty, from the Numismatische Gesellschaft (1843) and the Kaiser Friedrich-Museums-Verein (1897) to the Freunde der Nationalgalerie; many acquisitions depend on them (smb.museum).
- Collectors: Friedrich Christian Flick gave 270 works to the Nationalgalerie in 2008 and 2015; Egidio Marzona gave 372 works in 2014; Heinz Berggruen let the museums buy 165 works in 2000 "for a fraction of their estimated value" (smb.museum).

Red flags: Pergamon Museum renovation costs, 2024
- The Federal Office for Building and Regional Planning told Der Spiegel the renovation would cost at least EUR 121.4 million more, about EUR 1.5 billion in all, and that further rises could not be ruled out (Berliner Zeitung, 16 February 2024).
- The Irish Times (Derek Scally, 15 August 2024) reported original costings had risen 500 per cent, and that Der Spiegel blamed the SPK as well as the building office for late changes of plan.

Red flags: berlin modern construction costs, 2024 to 2025
- The Federal Audit Office criticised the Nationalgalerie's new building at the Kulturforum as too expensive and harmful to the climate (taz, 9 February 2024).
- Costs were due to rise to about EUR 526 million, against an early EUR 200 million, with completion delayed to 2028 (Berliner Zeitung, 24 July 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: News reports criticise the SPK's structure and its handling of restitution, not the museums' funders.
1. The Art Newspaper: Catherine Hickley, "Panel recommends dissolution of 'dysfunctional' Prussian Cultural Heritage Foundation" (2020)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2020/07/08/panel-recommends-dissolution-of-dysfunctional-prussian-cultural-heritage-foundation
- Citing Die Zeit, a panel commissioned in 2018 by culture minister Monika Grütters called the SPK "dysfunctional" and "structurally overwhelmed".
2. The Art Newspaper: Chiara Zampetti Egidi, "Overdue review shakes up Germany's largest cultural institution" (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/01/23/overdue-review-shakes-up-german-culture-foundation
- The December 2022 reform gave the institutions own budgets but postponed financing.
- Quotes Tagesspiegel calling the response to the reform paper "devastating".
3. The Art Newspaper: Catherine Hickley, "Yet again, a US court dismisses Nazi-era Guelph Treasure art lawsuit" (2023)
URL: https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/07/18/once-again-a-us-court-dismisses-guelph-treasure-art-lawsuit
- The heirs of Frankfurt dealers say the 1935 sale to the Prussian state, backed by Hermann Goering, was coerced; their lawyer called it theft.
- A US appeals court upheld the SPK's immunity on 13 July 2023; Germany's Advisory Commission rejected the claim in 2014.
4. AFP via Hürriyet Daily News: "Row erupts in Germany over restitution of bronzes" (13 June 2023)
URL: https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/row-erupts-in-germany-over-restitution-of-bronzes-183882
- After Muhammadu Buhari said returned bronzes would go to the Oba of Benin, German officials questioned further returns; SPK president Hermann Parzinger said future returns were not in doubt.
- Peju Layiwola called the Western reaction overblown and "an excuse".

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2004: the Flick Collection opened at the Hamburger Bahnhof amid protests; the collector's grandfather employed up to 50,000 forced labourers and camp inmates, and victims' groups called it "blood money" (The Irish Times, 23 September 2004).
- In 2021 the collection was being withdrawn from Berlin because the Rieckhallen faced demolition; almost 300 of its works had gone to the Nationalgalerie in two gifts (dpa via t-online, 29 May 2021).

BOTTOM LINE
Federal and state money carries the museums, and no flagged funder pays them. The weak points are building projects far over budget and disputed restitution claims.
