GRAZER KUNSTVEREIN: Graz, Austria
ID: grazer-kunstverein
Website: https://grazerkunstverein.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit, member-based art organisation on the ground floor of the historic Palais Trauttmansdorff, Burggasse 4, in central Graz (stated by the space).
- Founded in 1986; early artistic directors were Peter Pakesch and Helmut Strobl (1986 to 1987), then Elisabeth Printschitz (1987 to 1993), Eva Maria Stadler (1994 to 2005), Søren Grammel (2005 to 2011), Krist Gruijthuijsen (2012 to 2016), Kate Strain (2016 to 2021) and Tom Engels (2021 to 2025).
- Yana Foqué is now executive-artistic director (stated by the space).
- Programme: exhibitions, performances, screenings and discussions with Austrian and international artists; in autumn 2026 it shows Martin Maloney (18 September to 13 December) and Frank Wasser (1 September to 31 December).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Saturday, 12:00 to 18:00. No reporting found of admission prices or accessibility on the pages read.

FUNDING
Grazer Kunstverein: mainly public with corporate patrons: city, province and federal arts ministry, plus members and Styrian companies
- Structural support from the city of Graz, the Federal Ministry for Arts, Culture, Civil Service and Sport and the province of Styria, as well as legero united, con-tempus.eu and its members (e-flux, announcement of Tom Engels's appointment, September 2021).
- Patrons named on the space's about page (checked 2026-10-24, no year given): legero united (initiator of con-tempus.eu), Weitzer Hotels, Industriellenvereinigung Steiermark, AVL List GmbH, Flanders State of the Art and Weingut Peter Skoff. No amounts are given.
- AVL List GmbH: a Graz company that develops, simulates and tests drive systems for the automotive and other industries; Helmut List is listed as its owner (Wikipedia). trend (Markus Groll, "Warum AVL List verstärkt mit dem Rüstungsgeschäft liebäugelt", updated 3 October 2025) reports that AVL is seeking new fields of activity increasingly in the defence industry, has regularly attended defence trade fairs in recent years and takes a "dual use" approach, with management saying civil topics can be implemented very well in the military sector too (translation). The article names no defence customers, products or revenue, and no page read shows that AVL currently makes defence products.
- Board: Georg Mayer-Heinisch (chairman), Alexander Isola (cashier), Stefan Stolitzka (secretary), Georg List, Katrin Bucher Trantow and Alois Kölbl.
- No reporting found of annual reports, accounts or amounts for any funder.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kunstverein is mostly public, from the city, province and federal ministry, with local company patrons on top. The weak points are that no amounts or accounts are published and that the patron AVL List told trend in 2025 it is looking at defence business; no page read shows it currently makes defence products. Worth watching are AVL's defence activity and whether the Kunstverein publishes accounts.
