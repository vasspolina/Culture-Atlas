WYSING ARTS CENTRE: Bourn, UK
ID: wysing-arts-centre-bourn
Website: https://www.wysingartscentre.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary arts residency centre and campus at Fox Road, Bourn, South Cambridgeshire, near Cambridge; a registered charity (Wikipedia, Panorama event listing).
- Created in 1989 by Jenny and Terry Brooks and the artists Annie and Age Bunnetat, who wanted to provide "space, time, funding and a creative environment" for visual arts practice (Wikipedia). Rosie Cooper is the director (Cambridge105).
- Site: an eleven-acre campus on a former dairy farm, built around a restored 17th-century farmhouse, with ten buildings including 24 low-cost artists' studios, a gallery, education facilities, a recording studio and a ceramics studio. A £1.7 million capital project was completed in 2008 (Wikipedia).
- On site: an international residency programme, exhibitions, retreats and a programme for young artists. It ran the Wysing Polyphonic music festival and joined the Plus Tate network in 2010 (Wikipedia).
- A launch event on 25 April 2026 presented three new commissions, including a permanent sculpture by Tai Shani in the refurbished reception, and site developments of a rainwater garden, renewable energy systems and a fully accessible live and work studio for visiting disabled artists (Panorama).

FUNDING
Wysing Arts Centre: public bodies and charitable foundations: Arts Council England, local authorities, Garfield Weston and Freelands
- Arts Council England: National Portfolio Organisation (Wikipedia). In 2016 it received £150,000 under Arts Council England's Catalyst: Evolve programme, which matches private fundraising (Cambridge Network, 27 July 2016).
- Capital project (2025 to 2026): supported by Arts Council England, Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Combined Authority, South Cambridgeshire District Council, Cambridgeshire County Council, Garfield Weston Foundation and Thalia WB (Panorama).
- Freelands Foundation: in November 2021 it awarded Wysing £500,000 for The Syllabus, a ten-year artist development programme (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 10 November 2021). Freelands was established by Elisabeth Murdoch in 2015 (The Art Newspaper, Louisa Buck, 21 April 2020).
- Cambridge105 (9 May 2024) reported that the centre was set to benefit from Arts Council England funding to improve accessibility and reduce its environmental impact.
- Wikipedia names the Paul Hamlyn Foundation and the Henry Moore Foundation as additional trust supporters.

BOTTOM LINE
Wysing is a charity and an Arts Council England National Portfolio Organisation. Its 2025 to 2026 site project was supported by Arts Council England, three local public bodies, the Garfield Weston Foundation and Thalia WB, and Freelands Foundation, founded by Elisabeth Murdoch, gave it £500,000 in 2021.
