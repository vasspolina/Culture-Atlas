INSTITUTO CUBANO DEL ARTE E INDUSTRIA CINEMATOGRÁFICOS (ICAIC): Havana, Cuba
ID: instituto-cubano-del-arte-e-industria-cinematograficos-icaic
Website: http://www.cubacine.cult.cu (checked 2026-11-20; returned a 502 error)

OVERVIEW
- State film body at Calle 23 No. 1155, between 10 and 12, Vedado, Havana (EcuRed, state encyclopedia, archive copy of 14 February 2025).
- Created by Law No. 169 of the Council of Ministers, made public on 24 March 1959 over the signatures of Prime Minister Fidel Castro and Education Minister Armando Hart (EcuRed).
- EcuRed calls it the national film authority and main producer of features, documentaries and animation.
- It created the Cinemateca in 1960, the Grupo de Experimentación Sonora in 1969, the Havana film festival in 1979 and the Muestra Joven in 2000 (EcuRed).
- Ramón Samada left the presidency at his own request in 2023 and Susana Molina became acting president (OnCuba, July 2023). Alexis Triana was president in March 2025 (Prensa Latina).

FUNDING
ICAIC: state-funded through the Ministry of Culture
- EcuRed's Ministry of Culture page lists ICAIC among the institutes of the Ministry's system.
- From the 1990s, to face scarce resources, ICAIC took on co-productions and sold technical services to foreign productions (EcuRed).
- Fondo de Fomento del Cine Cubano: created under Decree-Law 373 of 2019 to finance independent films, separate from ICAIC's own budget. The state funds it, and it can also take income from Cuban or foreign people and companies (OnCuba, May 2020). Prensa Latina, a state agency, says it has backed about a hundred projects; its seventh round (October 2025) drew 53 short films.
- Restoration: Memorias del subdesarrollo was restored by the Cineteca di Bologna's L'Immagine Ritrovata laboratory, financed by The Film Foundation's World Cinema Project, and shown at Cannes in 2016 (Festival de Cannes).
- Triana credited the Fundación del Patrimonio Fílmico Colombiano with helping restore Cuban classics (Prensa Latina, March 2025).

Red flags: 2023 broadcast of La Habana de Fito without its director's permission
- State television showed an unfinished version of Juan Pin Vilar's documentary without his permission; an open letter to the Ministry of Culture drew more than 600 signatures and the Asamblea de Cineastas Cubanos formed in June 2023 (EFE in El Universal).
- July 2023: the Asamblea said "el cine cubano no es el Icaic" ("Cuban cinema is not ICAIC", our translation) after the culture minister said Cuban cinema is the cinema made by ICAIC (OnCuba).

Red flags: 2023 exclusion of two documentaries from the Havana festival
- December 2023: the Asamblea said the forces that control ICAIC had pressed the festival to exclude La Habana de Fito and Llamadas desde Moscú (EFE in El Universal).

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2020: ICAIC removed José Luis Aparicio and Fernando Fraguela's documentary on Mike Porcel, Sueños al pairo, from the Muestra Joven Aparicio said ICAIC objected to its treatment of the Mariel exodus. Seven directors withdrew their films and ICAIC postponed the 19th Muestra (OnCuba, 27 February 2020).
- 2018: ICAIC moved Yimit Ramírez's unfinished Quiero hacer una película to a 24-seat room; Ramírez withdrew it, and ICAIC's leadership defended the institution's right to make its own decisions (OnCuba, 2020).
- 2016: ICAIC dropped Carlos Lechuga's Santa y Andrés after it was preselected for the 38th Havana festival (OnCuba, 8 December 2016).
- 2014: twelve filmmakers said the highest authorities of the Ministry of Culture and ICAIC forced the festival to withdraw Laurent Cantet's Regreso a Ítaca; all the National Film Prize winners later joined their statement (OnCuba, 13 April 2015).

BOTTOM LINE
ICAIC's money is state money and raises no funder flag. Its weak point is its control over what Cuban audiences can see, which filmmakers contest openly. Worth watching: whether the Asamblea's demands are met.
