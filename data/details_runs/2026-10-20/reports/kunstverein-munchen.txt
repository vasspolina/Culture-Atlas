KUNSTVEREIN MÜNCHEN: Munich, Germany
ID: kunstverein-munchen
Website: https://www.kunstverein-muenchen.de

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art association at Galeriestraße 4 (Am Hofgarten) in Munich, in the historic arcades of the Hofgarten.
- Founded in 1823. Wikipedia dates the founding to 26 November 1823 by four artists, with royal approval on 31 December 1823, and gives its aim as "Förderung und Pflege der Bildenden Kunst" (promoting and nurturing the visual arts).
- The association says it was established in opposition to the Royal Academy of Fine Arts and has more than 2,200 members. Wikipedia reports about 2,000 members.
- Its first own gallery building opened in 1890 and was destroyed in the Second World War and not rebuilt (Wikipedia).
- It works on exhibitions, lectures, artist talks, symposia, film screenings, art trips and publications, and has co-produced with institutions including Haus der Kunst München, Kunsthalle Zürich, the ICA London and the Whitney Museum of American Art (stated by the association).
- Tom Engels is director (team page). The City of Munich council paper of 18 September 2025 says Maurin Dietrich, director since 2019, was expected to leave at the end of 2025. The board is Dr. Rüdiger Maaß (chairman), Julie Schemann (vice chairwoman), Aileen Faustmann (treasurer), Dr. Jan Mackert (secretary), Sarah Haugeneder, Marina Kayser-Eichberg, Nicolaus Schafhausen, Leo Stadler and Mirjam Zadoff.
- Current shows are an exhibition by Kitty Kraus and "George Tourkovasilis", presented with "Daybed" by Julie Peeters, both 26 September to 29 November 2026.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday 12:00 to 18:00; the window space Schaufenster am Hofgarten is open at all times. Admission is 5 euros, 3 euros for pensioners, students, people with disabilities and under 18s, 1 euro for the unemployed and art students; free for members, ADKV and ICOM members and children under 12. The association states that it is not barrier-free.

FUNDING
Kunstverein München: members' association with city support: City of Munich Kulturreferat, members and project sponsors
- The association describes itself as "a city-supported association under private sponsorship" (stated by the association).
- The City of Munich's Kulturreferat grants funds for the association's exhibition operation with events, lectures and publications, according to appendix 2 of a paper for the city council's culture committee of 18 September 2025 on grants for the 2026 budget year. The paper describes the association as one of the most traditional and, with more than 2,000 members, largest institutions of its kind in Germany.
- Flanders State of the Art supports the exhibition "George Tourkovasilis", presented with "Daybed" by Julie Peeters, 26 September to 29 November 2026 (exhibition page).
- Knackscharf Rent supports the Kitty Kraus exhibition, 26 September to 29 November 2026, which is produced in collaboration with Simian, Copenhagen (exhibition page).

Not related: the Kunstraum München e.V. It is a separate association, founded in 1972, with its own entry after the Kunstverein in the same council paper.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the association is a grant from the City of Munich's Kulturreferat and its more than 2,000 members, with project support for individual exhibitions from Flanders State of the Art and Knackscharf Rent. The association states its city support and its private sponsorship itself.
