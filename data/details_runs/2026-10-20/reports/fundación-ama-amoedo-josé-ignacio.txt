FUNDACIÓN AMA AMOEDO: José Ignacio, Uruguay
ID: fundación-ama-amoedo-josé-ignacio
Website: https://www.fundacionamaamoedo.org/

OVERVIEW
- Foundation supporting contemporary Latin American art, based at Casa Neptuna, a residency house by the ocean and surrounded by native forest in José Ignacio, Uruguay (Fundación Ama Amoedo).
- Founder and president Amalia Amoedo is a philanthropist, patron and art collector who has supported artists and cultural institutions for more than twenty years; the foundation runs through a non-profit established in Uruguay in 2021 for programmes, residencies and initiatives with regional and international reach, and one established in Argentina in 2025 to channel support to artists, cultural spaces, publications and projects there (Fundación Ama Amoedo).
- Named for its founder; Verónica Flom is director (Fundación Ama Amoedo).
- Casa Neptuna was designed by the Argentine artist Edgardo Giménez, with a brightly coloured exterior; it hosts two guests at a time, with a shared studio, bedrooms with private bathrooms, a kitchen and a multipurpose room (Fundación Ama Amoedo).
- Programmes: the FAARA residency, grants, FAARA Conecta, exhibition support, curatorial travel, regional connections and FAARA Sur (Fundación Ama Amoedo).
- Holds the Colección Ama Amoedo of about 800 works of Argentine and Latin American contemporary art from the 1920s to the present, which it lends to exhibitions and donates to institutions (Fundación Ama Amoedo).

FUNDING
Fundación Ama Amoedo: privately run by its founder; her family's Loma Negra cement fortune is background history
- La Nación (Alicia de Arteaga, 4 February 2023) reports that Amoedo installed "her foundation" at Casa Neptuna and identifies her as a granddaughter of Amalia Lacroze de Fortabat and a former president of arteBA.
- Loma Negra, the cement company, was sold in April 2005 for US$1,025 million to the Camargo Correa heirs; Fortabat then gave most of her properties in her lifetime to her daughter and grandchildren, among them "Amalita Amoedo" (La Nación, Loreley Gaffoglio, "La intrigante herencia de Amalita", 11 March 2012).
- La Nación (María Güiraldes, "Amalia Amoedo: por primera vez, abre las puertas de su fabuloso dúplex", 10 April 2019) reported that Amoedo oversees the Fundación Amalia Lacroze de Fortabat, the Fundación Policía Federal Argentina and the family's farmland, and that media estimated the fortune her grandmother left to her heirs at US$1.2 billion.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation is private and run by its president and founder, Amalia Amoedo, a granddaughter of Amalia Lacroze de Fortabat of the Loma Negra cement company. La Nación reports that she installed it at Casa Neptuna in José Ignacio and that Loma Negra was sold in 2005, after which Fortabat passed most of her property to her family.
