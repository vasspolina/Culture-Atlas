ID: gallerie-estensi

Red flags: 2022 dispute over separating the Ferrara gallery
- In November 2022 the culture undersecretary Vittorio Sgarbi announced that he wanted to detach the Pinacoteca di Ferrara from the Gallerie Estensi (il Resto del Carlino, Stefano Luppi, 9 November 2022).
- Federica Venturelli, city secretary of the Partito Democratico in Modena, and Vincenzo Pezzitola criticised the proposal; Sgarbi replied "Nessuna gara tra Ferrara e Modena, è la storia a legittimare l'autonomia dei musei ferraresi" ("No contest between Ferrara and Modena, history legitimises the autonomy of Ferrara's museums", translated), and the paper headlined his charge of "arroganza e ignoranza" ("arrogance and ignorance", translated) from the left (il Resto del Carlino, 11 November 2022).
- Giovanni Losavio, president of Italia Nostra, backed the split: "Ha ragione Sgarbi, i due istituti vanno separati" ("Sgarbi is right, the two institutes must be separated", translated) (il Resto del Carlino, Stefano Luppi, 12 November 2022).

Red flags: 2022 closure of the Palazzo Ducale di Sassuolo for a private wedding
- Unverified: Italia Nostra Modena said the state-owned Palazzo Ducale in Sassuolo, run by the Gallerie Estensi, was closed to the public all day on 1 October 2022 for a private paid wedding party, called this unacceptable and cited the cultural heritage code, which allows individual use of state cultural assets only "per finalità compatibili con la loro destinazione culturale" ("for purposes compatible with their cultural use", translated), from il Resto del Carlino (Stefano Luppi, 18 October 2022).
