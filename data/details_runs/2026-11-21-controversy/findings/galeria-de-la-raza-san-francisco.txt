ID: galeria-de-la-raza-san-francisco

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2018 the gallery tried to downsize into a third of its space at 24th and Bryant for $3,127 a month, the rent it had paid for both spaces; talks with the landlord's representative broke down even as Supervisor Hillary Ronen stepped in to mediate, and Ronen called the negotiations "brutally unfair". The landlord served a three-day pay-or-quit notice hours after a press conference on 29 October 2018 (Mission Local, Julian Mark, 29 October 2018).
- On 3 November 2018 gallery staff and allies, with Ronen, rallied outside the apartment of the family that owns the building. Executive director Ani Rivera said the rally was to show "the community they would be displacing"; the building's property manager called the rally "outrageous" and said the family was raising the rent to cover the cost of maintaining sidewalks, sewer lines and trees (KQED, Sonja Hutson, 3 November 2018).
