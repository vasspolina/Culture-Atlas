SELASAR SUNARYO ART SPACE: Bandung, Indonesia
ID: selasar-sunaryo-art-space-bandung
Website: https://selasarsunaryo.org/ (checked 2026-10-25; selasarsunaryo.org loads an unfilled template that shows placeholder keys instead of text, with no address, hours or ticket information, only two phone numbers and an email; the older address selasarsunaryo.com shows "Account Suspended")

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art space on Jalan Bukit Pakar Timur, in the Dago area of Bandung (CNN Indonesia, detikTravel, culture360).
- Founded by the artist Sunaryo, a former lecturer at the art and design faculty of the Bandung Institute of Technology, and opened on 5 September 1998 with his show "Titik Nadir", in which his works were covered in black cloth, which Manual calls "a sombre expression of the grief and uncertainty" of the socio-political turmoil of the time (detikTravel for the date, Manual).
- CNN Indonesia says Sunaryo wanted to make a public place where artists and the public could interact.
- Site and building: Sunaryo bought about 5,000 square metres of land and built the space himself (CNN Indonesia). No reporting found of the earlier use of the land.
- What is there: four exhibition rooms, each with a guide, and a cafe (detikTravel); hundreds of exhibitions, workshops and residencies since opening (Manual); a programme guided by a council of academics, critics and art practitioners (culture360).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 17:00; ticket Rp 35,000 (detikTravel). CNN Indonesia reported free entry in 2017 and described two exhibition rooms. The Manual feature said an inclusive programme for disabled visitors is a future priority; no reporting found of present accessibility arrangements.

FUNDING
Selasar Sunaryo Art Space: self-funded: the founder's land and building, backed by his foundation
- Sunaryo bought about 5,000 square metres of land and built the space (CNN Indonesia, 2017, our translation).
- culture360 says it was founded in 1998 "with direction and support from the Selasar Sunaryo Foundation" and calls it a non-profit; the profile was posted on 6 September 2010 and names only that foundation.
- No reporting found of any corporate sponsor, government grant or named donor. The earlier partner names (Goethe-Institut, Japan Foundation, British Council) were not found on any page opened.
- No reporting found of accounts, annual reports or a board. The 25th-anniversary exhibition in 2023 included work by Sunaryo, his son Arin Dwihartanto Sunaryo and his daughter-in-law Syagini Ratna Wulan (Manual), so the founder's family is part of the programme.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little criticism. The pieces found are descriptive or admiring and treat individual shows, not the institution or its money.
1. Art & Market: Tanya Michele Amador, "Selasar Sunaryo Art Space in Bandung Turns 20" (2018)
URL: https://www.artandmarket.net/exhibition/2018/10/21/selasar-sunaryo-art-space-in-bandung-turns-20
- Calls the space an artist-run initiative with a distinctive place in Bandung for nurturing and engaging young artists after twenty years, and says the performance and installation work was the strength of the anniversary show.
- Finds Sunaryo's stitched, patched abstract canvases deliberate and skilled, "certainly not an amateur" work.
- Mostly celebratory; it does not discuss funding or governance.
2. The Jakarta Post: Anindito Ariwandono, "Reconnecting with earth at Leuwigoèng" (2022)
URL: https://www.thejakartapost.com/culture/2022/12/13/reconnecting-with-earth-at-leuwigong
- Reviews the group show "Pulang Pada Tanah" ("Home to Soil") at Selasar Sunaryo; a photo caption notes some works are "rather raw in terms of their treatment".
- Criticises the treatment of some works, not the institution. Paywalled; only an excerpt was read.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the space is the founder's own: Sunaryo bought the land and built it, with a foundation behind it, and no sponsor or state funder is reported. The weak points are that no accounts, donors or board are published and that the founder's family shows work in the programme. Worth watching: whether a funder list or accounts are ever published.
