G.A.S. FOUNDATION (GUEST ARTISTS SPACE): Lagos, Nigeria
ID: g-a-s-foundation-guest-artists-space
Website: https://www.guestartistsspace.com (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit cultural centre with two sites: an urban hub in Oniru, Lagos, and a 54-acre farm, Ecology Green Farm, in Ijebu, Ogun State (BusinessDay, 2022).
- Founded in 2019 by the artist Yinka Shonibare, who modelled it on Guest Projects, his London residency programme where artists work in his studio for a month (The Art Newspaper, 2019).
- Lagos building: a brutalist-inspired structure around a central courtyard with live-work flats and a flexible project and exhibition space, designed by the architect Elsie Owusu with Nihinlola Shonibare (BusinessDay).
- Ijebu building: a residential space on the farm, designed by Papa Omotayo and built with local materials, including 40,000 bricks made from soil excavated for the foundations (BusinessDay).
- Programmes: residencies and fellowships, public events, the G.A.S. Library and Picton Archive (the library of the scholar John Picton and his wife Sue), and the African Arts Libraries Lab network (The Art Newspaper, 2025; the Impact Report 2024-25).
- The Lagos building was designed to be barrier-free because Shonibare uses a wheelchair (The Art Newspaper, 2019).
- No reporting found of opening days or admission.

FUNDING
G.A.S. Foundation: philanthropy-funded: Shonibare's UK foundation, individual patrons, art foundations and corporate partners including Still Earth Holdings, whose subsidiaries include an oil drilling contractor
- UK parent: the Yinka Shonibare Foundation (charity number 1183321) provides oversight and support; at the 2019 plan The Art Newspaper reported G.A.S. was mainly funded by it.
- Terra Foundation for American Art: $149,000 for CULTIVATION, a two-year residency on the farm (G.A.S. Impact Report 2024-25). Terra and Afreximbank (the African Export-Import Bank) also sponsored the 2025 Re:assemblages symposium (The Art Newspaper).
- Still Earth Holdings: the G.A.S. news page of 7 August 2024 names it the foundation's Founding Corporate Patron in 2022 and says its "transformative generosity" helped launch the Fellowship residency programme and support G.A.S. Fellows. No amount is stated. The page names Oyindamola Lami Adeyemi as Executive Chairperson of Still Earth Holdings.
- Still Earth Holdings' business: LEADERS magazine (January 2025) describes it as a group of companies founded in 2011 and says its subsidiary Tirex Petroleum & Energy, a drilling company, operates in the upstream sector of the oil and gas industry. ThisDay (21 January 2023) reports that Tirex completed a drilling and well intervention campaign for TotalEnergies at the Ikike field in OML99, five wells under a contract awarded in 2021. Tirex is a drilling contractor for TotalEnergies; no page read shows oil production by Still Earth Holdings or Tirex themselves.
- Deutsche Bank supports a three-year professional development and residency programme in Lagos and Ikise (Impact Report 2024-25). Art X/Access Bank, Goodman Gallery, kó Gallery and Tiwani Contemporary are listed as corporate partners.
- Institutions, trusts and foundations listed on the Thank You page include British Council, Goethe Institute, Pro Helvetia, Terra Foundation, A4 Arts and Yale School of Art.
- Patrons listed: Outset, Tafeta Gallery and Tilga Art Prize (Platinum); Olufemi A. Akinsanya and Niyi Adenubi and family (Gold); the Osahon Okunbo Foundation and Shonibare himself (Global Circle). No amounts are given.
- Board: Yinka Shonibare (chair), Bimpe Nkontchou, Tokini Peterside-Schwebig, Adeniyi Adenubi, Temitayo Ogunbiyi and Ugoma Ebilah.
- No reporting found of public accounts for the Nigerian foundation; the UK parent files with the Charity Commission (not opened).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. The pieces read (The Art Newspaper in 2019 and 2025, BusinessDay in 2022) announce or describe the foundation and contain no criticism.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind G.A.S. comes from Shonibare's own foundation, art-world patrons, cultural institutes and banks, and a Founding Corporate Patron, Still Earth Holdings, whose subsidiary Tirex is a drilling contractor for TotalEnergies. The weak points are that no donation amounts are published for most backers and no accounts for the Nigerian entity were found. Worth watching is whether Still Earth Holdings appears on future supporter lists; it is not on the current Thank You page.
