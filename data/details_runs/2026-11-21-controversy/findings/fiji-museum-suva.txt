WIDER CONTEXT
- In March 2019 Fiji Museum director Sipiriano Nemani said the Thurston Gardens land earmarked for the India High Commission was a former village site and a meke ground in the early 1930s, and that a cave there stored the National Museum's objects in the 1940s: "It's our right to protect the site" (Fiji Sun, 2 March 2019).
- Lands Minister Ashneel Sudhakar had said earlier: "The lot was not a burial site." (Fiji Sun, 2 March 2019).
