Red flags: 2023 dispute over racist terms at an exhibition opening
- At the opening of the MdbK exhibition "Re-Connect" in mid-May 2023, the artists Bibiana Malay and Grit Díaz de Arce read from their programme "The Dark Side of the GDR" and spoke the N-word and the Z-word (epd, Markus Geiler, 7 August 2023).
- Some of the audience left, Black activists criticised the programme sharply, and cooperation partners withdrew from working with the museum (epd, 7 August 2023).
- The museum called off an event with the duo planned for early June, saying it would first hold a dialogue with all involved and then reschedule it (epd, 7 August 2023).
- At the rescheduled event director Stefan Weppelmann apologised to all involved and said "Wir haben daraus gelernt" ("We have learned from it", translated); the museum added a content warning and engaged the activist Katharina Warda as moderator (epd, 7 August 2023).
