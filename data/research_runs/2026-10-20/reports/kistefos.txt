KISTEFOS – Jevnaker, Norway
Website: https://www.kistefosmuseum.com (loaded 2026-10-20; shows 2027 season)

OVERVIEW
- Museum and sculpture park on the site of the former Kistefos Træsliberi wood pulp mill (operated 1890–1955) on the Randselva river. Christen Sveaas, grandson of mill founder Anders Sveaas, bought back 85% of the company in 1993 and founded the museum. The park opened in 1996 (Wikipedia).
- The Twist, by Bjarke Ingels Group, opened 18 September 2019. It spans the river and holds three galleries. The Nybruket gallery (1896) hosts smaller shows (Wikipedia).
- The 2027 season runs 2 May to 17 October; the sculpture park is open year-round. Adults pay NOK 210, seniors 180, ages 20–25 pay 110, and under-20s are free (museum site). The site gives no accessibility information.
- About 90,000 visitors in 2024; the 2024 season ran 4 May to 13 October (2024 annual report).

FUNDING
Kistefos: mainly funded by its founder's company, with smaller public and savings-bank grants
- 2024 operating income was about NOK 74.7M, including NOK 39.1M in donations for art and investments, NOK 18.6M in sales and NOK 17.0M in other grants and gifts (2024 annual report, stated by the space).
- Kistefos AS, Christen Sveaas's investment company, gave about NOK 39.1M in 2024 for sculptures, investments and a loan for The Twist. Kistefos AS calls itself "the main sponsor" and says the building was funded with gifts from Sveaas, Kistefos AS and foundations (kistefos.no). Kistefos AS holds shipping, offshore, IT, finance and property interests, and Sveaas is its sole owner (Wikipedia).
- Stiftelsen Kistefos-Museets Driftsfond gave NOK 10M (2024).
- Riksantikvaren gave about NOK 1.8M (2024) for 2.3 positions and maintenance. It has given at least NOK 1M a year since 2006 (Wikipedia).
- Savings-bank foundations gave the following in 2024: Jevnaker/Lunner about 1.3M, DNB 1M, Gran about 335k, and Sparebank 1 Ringerike Hadeland 160k.
- Local and county government gave the following in 2024: Akershus fylkeskommune about 415k, Jevnaker kommune 400k and Ringerike kommune 250k. The website's supporter list names Viken and Innlandet fylkeskommune instead.
- Smaller contributors: AS Kistefos Træsliberi (about 971k), Stiftelsen UNI, Anders Sveaas Almennyttige Fond (chaired by Sveaas), Sat Sapienti and Radisson RED Gardermoen (site).
- Board: Camilla Magnus became chair in 2024, succeeding Erik Wahlstrøm, who had chaired it for 28 years (2024 annual report).

Context, not a finding against the space: Sveaas has donated to the Progress, Liberal and Conservative parties. Ahead of the 2025 election he gave NOK 2M to the Christian Democrats (Wikipedia). Kistefos sold an AHTS offshore vessel to Viking Supply Ships in March 2026 (Baird Maritime). That article names no oil and gas market.

BOTTOM LINE
Kistefos publishes detailed accounts naming its funders. It depends heavily on Christen Sveaas and Kistefos AS, which gave about half of 2024 income. No reporting found of misconduct linked to the museum. Worth watching: how far the museum relies on one private owner's company, whose holdings include offshore shipping.
