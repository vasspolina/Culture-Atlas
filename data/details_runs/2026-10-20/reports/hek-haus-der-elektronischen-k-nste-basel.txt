HEK (HAUS DER ELEKTRONISCHEN KÜNSTE): Basel, Switzerland
ID: hek-haus-der-elektronischen-k-nste-basel
Website: https://hek.ch (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Museum for digital and media art at Freilager-Platz 9, Münchenstein / Basel; tram 11 from Bahnhof SBB stops at "Freilager" (museums.ch).
- Founded in 2011 on the Dreispitz site, a former warehouse area redeveloped by the Christoph Merian Foundation; Sabine Himmelsbach has directed it since 2012 (Wikipedia).
- In 2014 it moved to Freilager-Platz and reopened on 21 November after more than a year of construction (Wikipedia).
- Building: 550 square metres of exhibition space, a foyer and café, a 200-square-metre event hall and a server room (Wikipedia). The pages read name no architect.
- Programme: temporary exhibitions on digital and media art, Thursday-evening Open Decks since 2024, the Mesh Festival with the Basel School of Art Design since 2024, and collecting and preserving digital art (Wikipedia; museums.ch).
- Visiting: Wednesday and Friday 12:00 to 18:00, Thursday 12:00 to 20:00, Saturday and Sunday 12:00 to 17:00. Adults CHF 12, reduced CHF 8, annual pass CHF 25, school classes free. Fully wheelchair accessible (museums.ch).

FUNDING
HEK: public funding from the two Basel cantons (and the Swiss federal culture office until its 2018 cut), plus an award foundation and a corporate award partner
- Public: in 2018 the Federal Office of Culture cut HEK's annual funding, and the cantons of Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft covered the losses so that HEK could continue (Wikipedia).
- Pax Art Awards: since 2018 HEK and Art Foundation Pax present the awards to Swiss media artists (Wikipedia).
- HEK also hosts the VH Award, which Wikipedia describes as hosted by Hyundai Motor Company.
- Members: the Friends of HEK membership has used NFT and blockchain technology since 2022 (Wikipedia).
- Christoph Merian Foundation: named only as the developer of the Dreispitz site where HEK was founded (Wikipedia); no funding from it is stated on the pages opened.
- Göhner and Binding are not named on any page opened, so they are unconfirmed as funders. No reporting found of annual cantonal or federal sums or of accounts. The cantonal funding documents found were unreadable PDFs.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind HEK is mainly public, from the two Basel cantons and, until the 2018 cut, the federal culture office, with a foundation and a corporate award partner on top; no flagged funder was found on the pages read. The weak point is that no amounts, accounts or supporter list could be read. Worth watching are the cantonal funding sums and whether HEK publishes a supporter list.
