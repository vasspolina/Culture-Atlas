PIER ARTS CENTRE: Stromness, UK
ID: pier-arts-centre-stromness
Website: https://www.pierartscentre.com

OVERVIEW
- Art gallery and museum on Victoria Street in Stromness, Orkney, in listed quayside buildings extended with a new building on the pier.
- Established in 1979 to house a collection of British modern art given by Margaret Gardiner, who donated it to be "held in trust for Orkney" (the centre's history page).
- The Pier Arts Centre Trust bought the original buildings in 1977 to house the Gardiner collection; the site includes an 18th-century house fronting the street and pier stores behind it (the centre's buildings page).
- Reiach & Hall Architects extended the listed buildings and pier; the centre reopened in July 2007 after a two-year project, with display space more than doubled, and won the RIAS Andrew Doolan Award for Best Building in Scotland 2007 (the centre's buildings page).
- The collection includes works by Barbara Hepworth, Ben Nicholson and Alfred Wallis, plus contemporary artists such as Sean Scully, Eva Rothschild and Olafur Eliasson, and Orkney artists such as Sylvia Wishart and Stanley Cursiter.
- The centre also runs the Linkshouse Orkney Arts Residency, a public library and archive, and education and outreach activities.
- It is an Accredited Museum (No. 452), holds a Recognised Collection of National Significance, and is a partner of Tate through the Plus Tate network.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:30 am to 5 pm, all year; admission is free. A lift serves all floors, accessible toilets are on each floor, and the entrance door does not open automatically, so visitors knock on the glass for help.

FUNDING
Pier Arts Centre: charity supported by Creative Scotland and Orkney Islands Council
- The centre's Partnerships and Awards page says the centre is supported by Creative Scotland and Orkney Islands Council.
- Income was £618,369 and expenditure £608,698 in the year to 31 March 2025; income was £1,394,112 in the year to 31 March 2024 and £620,615 in 2023 (Scottish Charity Regulator register, charity SC014815).
- Partners listed on the same page are Plus Tate, the Royal Scottish Academy, National Galleries of Scotland, ArtHunter, Own Art and Art Fund.
- Charity trustees named on the register: Hayley Green, Clare Gee, Michael Morrison, Lindsay Blair, Kristopher Bevan, James Walsh, Elsa Cox, Amanda Bettison and Ross Sinclair. The centre's About page names Kristopher Bevan as chair and Clare Gee as vice chair.

BOTTOM LINE
The Pier Arts Centre is a charity with a collection given by Margaret Gardiner in 1979, supported by Creative Scotland and Orkney Islands Council, with income of £618,369 in the year to March 2025. The Scottish Charity Regulator register documents the income and trustees.
