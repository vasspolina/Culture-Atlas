NEW NATIONAL THEATRE TOKYO (NNTT): Tokyo, Japan
ID: new-national-theatre-tokyo-nntt
Website: https://www.nntt.jac.go.jp/english/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Japan's national theatre for opera, ballet and dance, and drama, at 1-1-1 Hon-machi, Shibuya-ku, a minute's walk from Hatsudai station on the Keio New Line (theatre brochure 2026).
- The bill to build it passed in April 1966; the site was chosen in 1980, architect Takahiko Yanagisawa won the competition in 1986, building began in August 1992 and the theatre opened on 10 October 1997 with "Takeru" (theatre brochure).
- The Japan Arts Council set it up and the New National Theatre Foundation runs it on commission (Japanese Wikipedia). The foundation's president is Zeniya Masami (theatre website).
- Halls: the Opera Palace (1,814 seats, Japanese Wikipedia), the Playhouse and The Pit, an arena stage of up to 468 seats (theatre brochure).
- Companies and schools: the NNT Chorus and the National Ballet of Japan, with the NNT Opera Studio (1998), the NNT Ballet School (2001) and the NNT Drama Studio (2005) (theatre brochure).
- Output: 246 performances of 26 productions and 198,958 paying visitors in the 2024 financial year; 7,117 performances since 1997 (Annual Report 2024).
- Coming up: the 30th anniversary in 2027; in July 2025 the National Ballet of Japan danced "Giselle" at the Royal Opera House in London (Annual Report 2024).
- Visiting: box office +81 3 5352 9999, 10:00 to 18:00; group rates for ten or more (theatre website; brochure).

FUNDING
New National Theatre Tokyo: mainly public: state funds placed in trust, with ticket income and corporate sponsors
- The theatre's "basic financial means is provided by Government Subsidy" (theatre website).
- 2024 financial year (to 31 March 2025): ordinary income of 7,707,527,423 yen, of which 4,267,392,700 yen were "funds placed in trust", 2,422,707,498 yen performance revenue, 345,180,212 yen donations, 272,724,062 yen hall rental, 164,175,523 yen consigned projects and 150,010,000 yen from the Special Support Corporate Group (Annual Report 2024, accounts).
- Consigned projects include Agency for Cultural Affairs commissions, such as viewing support for audiences with visual or hearing impairments, and Japan Cultural Expo 2.0 productions with the Japan Arts Council (Annual Report 2024).
- Private support in 2024: 375 million yen in donations and 180 million yen in sponsorship, 555 million yen in all; 132 corporate and 695 individual supporting members at the year's end (Annual Report 2024).
- Special Support Corporate Group, 2024/2025 season: Onward Holdings, Kinoshita Group, Sumitomo Chemical, TBS Television, Toyota Motor and Pia (Annual Report 2024).
- Training: All Nippon Airways sponsors the ANA Scholarship for young singers and dancers abroad and the studio recitals (Annual Report 2024).
- Single productions: Van Cleef & Arpels ("La Bayadère", "Ballet Coffret"); Rohm Co., the Rohm Music Foundation and Sompo Japan (opera for high school students); Keio Corporation, Takara Tomy, Shogakukan, Coca-Cola Bottlers Japan and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Machinery Systems (children's ballet); Bloomberg L.P. (the Bloomberg Connects guide app); Nomura Real Estate Development and Nomura Real Estate Life & Sports (a children's programme) (Annual Report 2024).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: mostly state money, with ticket income and a spread of mainstream Japanese corporate sponsors. Worth watching are the single-show sponsors, which change each season.
