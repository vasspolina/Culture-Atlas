KUNSTVEREIN BIELEFELD: Bielefeld, Germany
ID: kunstverein-bielefeld-bielefeld
Website: https://www.kunstverein-bielefeld.de/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art association at Welle 61 in Bielefeld, with exhibitions in the Waldhof, a 16th-century noble courtyard of the Wolde family in the Weser Renaissance style (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1929 and known as the Bielefelder Kunstverein until 2019; its original goal was to support the Kunsthaus in the Hindenburgstraße and its exhibitions (Wikipedia). The association says it was founded to promote art and its public mediation.
- The activity is carried by the civic engagement of almost 1,000 members (stated by the association).
- Programme: thematic exhibitions made with international institutions and regional initiatives, plus solo presentations, aimed at young international artists and questions of contemporary society. The association also runs an Artothek, annual editions and education programmes.
- Victoria Tarak is director. The Vorstand is Andreas Wannenmacher (chair), Audrey Hörmann (deputy chair), Fabian Kauczok (treasurer), Prof. Roman Bezjak and Uli Horaczek, with an advisory board of twelve people.
- The current show is Christian Naujoks, "Kannst du hören, wie du hörst?", 10 October to 20 November 2026.
- In 2025 the association won the Annual Program prize of 40,000 euros in the first WESTSTERN Art Award for an exhibition series built around the five senses. The award was initiated by the entrepreneur Jan Fischer together with the LEAP Art Foundation (e-flux, 3 July 2025).
- Visiting: open Thursday and Friday 12:00 to 18:00, Saturday and Sunday 13:00 to 19:00; Monday to Wednesday by appointment; closed on public holidays.

FUNDING
Kunstverein Bielefeld: member-run: almost 1,000 members
- The association says its activity is carried by the civic engagement of almost 1,000 members (stated by the association). Wikipedia puts the membership at more than 1,000.
- In 2025 it won the Annual Program prize of 40,000 euros in the first WESTSTERN Art Award, initiated by the entrepreneur Jan Fischer together with the LEAP Art Foundation (e-flux, 3 July 2025).
- Wikipedia describes it as a non-profit registered association.

BOTTOM LINE
The association is a non-profit registered association carried by its almost 1,000 members. In 2025 it won a 40,000 euro prize in the first WESTSTERN Art Award, initiated by Jan Fischer and the LEAP Art Foundation.
