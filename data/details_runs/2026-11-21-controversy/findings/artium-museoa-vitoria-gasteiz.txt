WIDER CONTEXT
- On 4 September 2020 front-of-house staff at Artium held the first of three strike days against cuts and dismissals; the CNT union warned the strike would become indefinite from 15 September if negotiations made no progress (Gasteiz Hoy, 4 September 2020).
- The staff are employed by the contractor Magmacultura, whose budget was to be cut by 20% in the next tender, which the workers said threatened dismissals and a heavier workload (Gasteiz Hoy).
- The workers asked that the cuts not be applied, that all posts be kept and that service quality be guaranteed. Ane Romero of the ELA union said a very large majority of the staff had joined the strike (Gasteiz Hoy).
- The museum opened with 75% of its exhibition space that day. The Fundación Artium said it was willing to "solucionar este conflicto, con total respeto a la normativa vigente" (Gasteiz Hoy).
