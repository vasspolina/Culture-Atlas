RED BRICK ART MUSEUM: Beijing, China
ID: red-brick-art-museum
Website: http://www.redbrickartmuseum.org (checked 2026-11-20; the about page returned an empty page)

OVERVIEW
- Private contemporary art museum in the No. 1 International Art District (一号地国际艺术区), Cuigezhuang, in north-east Chaoyang District, Beijing (Visit Beijing; Our China Story).
- Founded by the collector and entrepreneur Yan Shijie (闫士杰, also written 阎士杰) and his wife Cao Mei; planning began in 2007 and the museum opened in May 2014 (Our China Story, Chinese edition). Its company profile gives the opening date as 23 May 2014 (Zhaopin).
- Dezeen describes it as a folk-based, non-profit art museum showing Chinese and world art (Dezeen, 2017).
- Architect: Dong Yugan, a professor at Peking University's Architecture Research Center, designed both building and garden (Our China Story; Dezeen).
- The site covers about 20,000 square metres, with nearly 10,000 square metres of exhibition space; it uses grey bricks from local village houses and red bricks associated with factory buildings (Visit Beijing; Our China Story, English edition).
- On site: nine exhibition spaces, two public leisure spaces, a children's room, a multipurpose hall and artist residency studios (Our China Story); a lecture hall, restaurant, cafe and members' club sit in the garden (Zhaopin).
- Yan Shijie curates its shows: in October 2026 the museum opened Bingyi's solo exhibition "象罔无形" (Unseen Paintings), covering her work since 2008 (Beijing Daily, 18 October 2026).
- Visiting: galleries open 10:00 to 17:30 from 1 October to 30 April and 10:00 to 18:00 from 1 May to 30 September; closed Mondays except on holidays (Our China Story).

FUNDING
Red Brick Art Museum: self-funded: the founding couple's private money, through their own company
- The founders invested nearly 400 million yuan over eight years before the 2014 opening (Our China Story, Chinese edition: "投资近4亿人民币，历时8年", our translation).
- The museum is run by Beijing Red Brick Contemporary Art Museum Co., Ltd. (北京红砖当代美术馆有限公司), registered as a limited liability company invested or controlled by natural persons, with 20 to 99 staff (Zhaopin company profile).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Very little. The one critical piece is a commentary on a news site by a former museum professional, about the museum's conduct at a 2017 protest, not its art or funding.
1. The Paper (澎湃新闻): Gu Yizhi (顾一指), "开幕式成'打戏'，美术馆建设的'道'依然崎岖" ("The opening becomes a brawl; the road of museum building is still rough", our translation) (2017)
URL: https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1700081
- Argues that comforting the dead employee's family should have come first, and that museums carry a humanitarian duty.
- Says that without a neutral mediator compensation talks were bound to fail, and that hard finances are no excuse for neglecting care for workers.
- Says the poor crisis response damaged the museum's name and reflects a lack of professionalism across China's museums. The author is described as a former museum professional and critic.

WIDER CONTEXT
- 2017 protest at an opening: Liu Yukuan (刘彧宽), the museum's public relations manager, died suddenly on 30 December 2016. On 27 May 2017 his parents came to an exhibition opening holding his portrait and handing out leaflets; more than ten museum staff dragged them away and forced them into two cars (The Paper, Gu Yizhi, June 2017).
- The family said the museum owed Liu his last month's wages and that he died of overwork. Unverified: the wage and overwork claims, made by the family; the museum called the wage claim false.
- The museum's statement admitted that "lack of experience" (经验不足) in handling the day had hurt Liu's parents' feelings and apologised (The Paper, our translation).
- The parents had bruises and abrasions on their hands and bodies; the dispute had not been settled when The Paper wrote.
- The protest was aimed at the museum itself, by a former employee's family.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum runs on the founding couple's own money through their private company, with no outside funder named. The weak points are the undisclosed source of the founder's wealth and the 2017 treatment of a dead employee's parents.
