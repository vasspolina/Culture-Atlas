ID: national-museum-of-denmark

WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 2018 auditors reported that the museum had thrown away around 1,000 historical artefacts damaged by mould, rats or insects, and criticised it for not solving storage problems pointed out as long ago as 2007 (The Copenhagen Post, Stephen Gadd, 5 July 2018).
- The museum put the cost at about 30 work years of cleaning and rescue work plus several million kroner in transport; acting deputy director Palle Maurice Jepsen said the items were "very well represented in collections already" (The Copenhagen Post, 5 July 2018).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Videnskab.dk: Søren M. Sindbæk, "Arkæolog: Jim Lyngvilds vikingeudstilling er flot, men præget af historieløs udklædningsleg" (2018)
URL: https://videnskab.dk/kultur-samfund/anmeldelse-jim-lyngvilds-samarbejde-med-nationalmuseet-er-paa-vildspor
- Sindbæk, a professor of archaeology at Aarhus University, ties the Viking exhibition made with Jim Lyngvild to director Rane Willerslev's push for a more commercial museum: "Det kommercielle har fået fuld damp" ("The commercial side has gone full steam ahead", translated).
- He writes that the museum's own leading Viking Age researchers were not involved in the exhibition.
- He argues that credibility is the basic material of a museum of cultural history and that chasing popularity this way can never be right for Denmark's main museum of cultural history.
