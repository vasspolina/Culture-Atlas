KUNSTMUSEUM BASEL: Basel, Switzerland
ID: kunstmuseum-basel
Website: https://kunstmuseumbasel.ch

OVERVIEW
- Public art museum in Basel with three sites: the main building (Hauptbau), the extension (Neubau) and the Gegenwart building for contemporary art on the Rhine in the St. Alban neighbourhood (museum website, Wikipedia).
- Its collection goes back to the Amerbach Cabinet, which the city and the University of Basel acquired in 1661; Wikipedia calls it the first municipally owned museum in the world open to the public.
- The main building was designed by Paul Bonatz and Rudolf Christ and built from 1931 to 1936. It moved there in 1936 (Wikipedia).
- The extension by Christ & Gantenbein was completed in April 2016 (Wikipedia).
- Holdings include the largest collection of works by the Holbein family, Upper Rhine art from 1400 to 1600, and modern and contemporary art. Wikipedia lists Elena Filipovic as director.
- Restitution: in April 2020 the museum agreed to pay the heirs of the Berlin collector Curt Glaser for about 200 works he sold while fleeing Nazi Germany, and kept works on paper worth more than $2 million; the city had held in 2008 that Glaser sold the works at fair value (JTA via Times of Israel). In January 2024 it rejected a claim for Henri Rousseau's painting bought in 1940 from Charlotte von Wesdehlen and proposed negotiating a "fair and equitable" solution (Keystone-SDA via swissinfo).
- Visiting: closed Monday; open Tuesday and Thursday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, Wednesday 10:00 to 20:00. Collection admission CHF 25 for adults over 20, CHF 12 for students and ages 13 to 19; free for children up to 12, with free collection entry on the first Sunday of each month. Exhibition rooms are wheelchair accessible and the museum runs barrier-free tours.

FUNDING
Kunstmuseum Basel: Canton Basel-Stadt, Basel foundations, a bank partner and corporate sponsors
- Canton of Basel-Stadt is listed among the museum's major donors, and Wikipedia says the Canton and the Laurenz Foundation funded the extension. The Basel government wrote in 2018 that it was guaranteeing CHF 925,000 more in operating funds for that year and would ask parliament for a permanent increase of CHF 2 million (Basel-Stadt press release, 7 September 2018).
- UBS is the museum's partner, listed under the heading "Partner" on the donors page; UBS Switzerland AG also appears in the page's list of companies.
- Sponsors named on the museum's page: Federal Office of Culture, KPMG AG, Novartis International AG, Trafina Privatbank AG, the L. + Th. La Roche-Stiftung, and a fund for artistic activities in the Museum für Gegenwartskunst of the Emanuel Hoffmann Foundation and the Christoph Merian Foundation. Kvadrat is an in-kind sponsor.
- Major donors named on the page include the Emanuel Hoffmann Foundation, the Laurenz Foundation, the Christoph Merian Foundation, the Foundation for the Kunstmuseum Basel, Nationale Suisse and Maja Oeri.
- Companies, permanent lenders and foundations listed on the page include Basler Kantonalbank, Bank Julius Bär, PricewaterhouseCoopers, MCH Group and the Ernst Göhner Stiftung.

WIDER CONTEXT
- FinCEN announced on 3 August 2026 a $125 million penalty against UBS Financial Services Inc. for willful violations of the Bank Secrecy Act. The release concerns anti-money laundering failures, including more than 50,000 foreign currency wires worth more than $10 billion that were inadequately monitored, not sanctions violations. The penalty is against the US subsidiary, not UBS Switzerland AG, the entity the museum lists.
- The Federal Reserve announced on 10 May 2004 that UBS AG of Zurich consented to a $100 million civil money penalty "in connection with U.S. dollar banknote transactions with counterparties in jurisdictions subject to sanctions under U.S. law", naming Cuba, Libya, Iran and Yugoslavia. The order is against UBS AG; the museum lists UBS and UBS Switzerland AG.
- The US Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York announced on 1 July 2020 (release reposted by the VA Office of Inspector General) a $678 million settlement with Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation of a False Claims Act and Anti-Kickback Statute suit, which alleged sham speaker programmes that paid doctors to prescribe its cardiovascular and diabetes drugs. The museum's sponsor is Novartis International AG, the Swiss parent of the settling subsidiary.
- UBS is the museum's partner and Novartis International AG a sponsor (donors page).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is funded by Canton Basel-Stadt, which pays operating funds, together with Basel foundations, UBS as partner and sponsors from banking, consulting and pharmaceuticals. The 2026 FinCEN penalty is against UBS Financial Services Inc., the 2004 Federal Reserve penalty against UBS AG and the 2020 settlement against Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation; each is a case against a bank or drugmaker entity and is listed as wider context.
