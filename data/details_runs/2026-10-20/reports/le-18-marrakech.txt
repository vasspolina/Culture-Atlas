LE 18, DERB EL FERRANE: Marrakech, Morocco
ID: le-18-marrakech
Website: https://le18marrakech.com

OVERVIEW
- Independent art space and artist residency in the medina of Marrakech, at 18 Derb El Ferrane, with exhibitions, residencies, talks, workshops and the yearly publication "Chergui" (le18marrakech.com).
- Opened in 2013 by Laila Hida, founder and director, with the curator Hicham Bouzid (le18marrakech.com; Contemporary And, Louise Thurin, 2 May 2023).
- Building: a riad at 18 Derb el Ferrane whose rooms surround a courtyard open to the sky (e-flux, Jamila Moroder, 30 September 2022; le18marrakech.com).
- Programme: the cycles Awal, Dabaphoto and Qanat; recent shows include "Organic Knowledge" (2024) and "Tears of Fog" (2025) (le18marrakech.com).
- Took part in documenta fifteen in 2022, where it set up a "relief center" at WH22 instead of showing finished works (e-flux, Jamila Moroder).

FUNDING
LE 18: independent, partner-funded: a structural patron, foundations, cultural institutes and embassies
- NOMAD has been its structural patron (mécénat structurel) from 2015 to the present (le18marrakech.com).
- Partners listed for 2013 to 2023 include Fondation Jardin Majorelle, AFAC, the British Council, the Goethe-Institut Marokko, the US Embassy in Morocco, Kamel Lazaar Foundation, Fonds Roberto Cimetta, Wallonie-Bruxelles International, Pro Helvetia, Fondation Dar Bellarj, Flanders State of the Art, Stimulering Funds, the Service de Coopération et d'Action Culturelle (SCAC) and Dar al-Ma'mûn (le18marrakech.com).
- Hida said in 2023 that the space operates "within a context of financial instability," and that the team had to "find other means of subsistence and rethink our production models" (Contemporary And).

WIDER CONTEXT
- e-flux (Jamila Moroder, "Field Notes: Jamila Moroder on LE 18, Documenta 15", 30 September 2022) writes that a poster by Villalobos reflects "a clear distrust of Documenta as a state- and corporate-sponsored German institution," and quotes the ruangrupa members Reza Afisina and Farid Rakun calling the exhibition "almost a state affair."
- The piece is a review of LE 18's contribution to documenta fifteen: LE 18 chose not to provide finished artworks for the opening after exhausting negotiations with documenta's bureaucracy, and showed leftovers and preparatory documentation in its relief center.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind LE 18 is a structural patron, NOMAD, plus project support from foundations, cultural institutes and embassies across 2013 to 2023, and the space describes its finances as unstable.
