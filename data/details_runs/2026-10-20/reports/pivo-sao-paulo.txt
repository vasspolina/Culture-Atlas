PIVÔ: São Paulo, Brazil
ID: pivo-sao-paulo
Website: https://www.pivo.org.br (checked 2026-10-25; loaded)

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit contemporary art space in the Copan building, Avenida Ipiranga 200, loja 54, in central São Paulo; about 3,500 square metres at one end of the building (ArtReview, 2013; pivo.org.br).
- Founded in 2011 and open to the public since 2012; Fernanda Brenner is the founder and artistic director (pivo.org.br). ArtReview (Claire Rigby, 2013) quotes her as co-founder.
- Building: the Copan is an S-shaped Oscar Niemeyer building with about 5,000 residents. ArtReview (2013) says Pivô's interior had been empty for about 20 years before renovation, and that the space opened alongside the 2013 São Paulo Bienal with the show "Da Próxima Vez Eu Fazia Tudo Diferente."
- Programme: in São Paulo, exhibitions, Pivô Research, Pivô Hosts, a library and Pivô Satellite; a Salvador branch, Pivô Boulevard, opened in 2023 in a house built in 1934 (pivo.org.br). ArtReview (2013) says it has hosted shows by the commercial galleries Mendes Wood and Emma Thomas.
- Visiting: the site lists Wednesday to Saturday 1pm to 7pm and Sunday 12pm to 6pm, and notes the venue is temporarily closed. No reporting found of admission prices or accessibility on the pages read.

FUNDING
Pivô: mixed private: individual donors, galleries and an annual auction, with a tax-incentive scheme open to sponsors
- Pivô's home page calls the annual auction its "principal fonte de financiamento da instituição" (main source of financing of the institution; translation; stated by Pivô).
- Pivô's About page (English) lists 19 named individual or couple "maintainers" plus anonymous ones, 11 patrons of Pivô Satellite, Instituto Impactarte as patron of Pivô Research, and a Gallery Circuit of nine galleries: Almeida e Dale, Carbono Galeria, Fortes D'Aloia & Gabriel, Galatea, Galeria Luisa Strina, Galeria Millan, Galeria Nara Roesler, Gomide & Co and Mendes Wood DM. No amounts are given.
- Supporters are grouped as realization, sponsorship, co-sponsorship, support, partners, media support and cultural support, but the logos on the page carry no text, so no corporate sponsor or public programme could be named with certainty.
- The About page lists annual reports and financial statements for 2020 to 2024; the files could not be read as text, so no figures are given here.
- Pivô's Support page describes a federal tax-incentive route (PRONAC, the "Lei de Incentivo à Cultura"): companies donate as sponsors, and individual maintainers direct part of their income tax. The page does not name the Lei Rouanet, and no sponsor is named in text, so any Lei Rouanet link is unconfirmed.
- Friends of Pivô membership tiers are listed from R$150 to R$5,000 (pivo.org.br support page).
- No reporting found of a government body funding Pivô directly.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism of Pivô found in art media. Coverage found is a launch profile.
1. ArtReview: Claire Rigby, feature on São Paulo (published online 2014, from the September 2013 issue)
URL: https://artreview.com/september-feature-sao-paulo/
- A largely upbeat profile of Pivô and other new spaces in the city centre.
- Co-founder Fernanda Brenner objects to the word "revitalisation," saying the centre "has never been abandoned, never been empty."
- Does not criticise Pivô or its money.

BOTTOM LINE
Pivô is funded by private donors, galleries and an auction, with annual reports and accounts listed on its site, and no fossil fuel, arms, tobacco or gambling funder was found. The weak points are that the accounts and the sponsor logos could not be read here, so the public and corporate shares are unconfirmed, and that commercial galleries are both funders and exhibitors. Worth checking the audited accounts for the tax-incentive share.
