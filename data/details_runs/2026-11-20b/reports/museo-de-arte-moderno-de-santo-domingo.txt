MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE SANTO DOMINGO: Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-santo-domingo
Website: none

OVERVIEW
- State modern art museum designed by José Miniño in the Plaza de la Cultura Juan Pablo Duarte, Santo Domingo, on three levels and a basement (El Día, 2016; El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Opened on 15 December 1976 as the Galería de Arte Moderno; renamed Museo de Arte Moderno in 1992 (El Inmobiliario).
- It reopened during the COVID-19 pandemic after four years of remodelling, and shows 80 to 100 of its about 1,500 pieces at a time (El Inmobiliario).
- Director: the architect Federico Fondeur, named by Minister Carmen Heredia after August 2020 (Diario Libre, 2021) and still director in July 2026 (Acento); El Nuevo Diario wrote in July 2020 that María Elena Ditrén had directed it for 16 years.
- It hosts the Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales; the 31st, in 2025, showed 207 of 609 entries (Acento).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00, free (El Inmobiliario, October 2022).

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Santo Domingo: public: the Dominican Ministry of Culture
- The museum sits under the Dirección General de Museos, and the Ministry of Culture has run it since the ministry was created in 2000 (El Inmobiliario, 2022).
- Bienal prizes at the ministry's Bienal: in 2025 a Gran Premio of RD$1 million and nine of RD$300,000; Minister Roberto Ángel Salcedo promised a decree for a permanent Bienal committee and larger prizes in 2027 (Presidencia, September 2025).
- The ministry is the superior authority over the Bienal's organising commission (Resolution 18-2025), on which Fondeur sat for the 30th Bienal (El Día, 25 October 2022).

Red flags: 2025 protests over the ministry's annulment of a Bienal prize
- On 22 October 2025 Resolution 18-2025, signed by Minister Salcedo on an appeal by the Colegio Dominicano de Artistas Plásticos, annulled the prize given to David Pérez (Karmadavis) for a royal palm work as perishable material and upheld the other prizes (Ministry of Culture).
- The jury wrote to the minister that the dictionary reading of "perecedero" voided its whole award and that it was not told of the appeal (Acento, 28 October 2025).
- Noa Batlle gave up her honourable mention in protest (Acento, 30 October 2025); the prize-winners asked the minister to restore the jury's awards (Acento, 7 November 2025).
- An open letter on Change.org with at least 155 verified signatures, among them Gerardo Mosquera, Pablo León de la Barra and Coco Fusco, asked for the resolution's revocation (Acento, 18 November 2025).

Red flags: 2025 boycott over unpublished Bienal catalogues
- The catalogues of the 2021 and 2023 Bienales were never published, and the photographer Ramón Peña said he would stop taking part in protest (Acento, 9 September 2025).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Newspapers and artists' collectives, not art press, criticise the ministry's running of the Bienal.
1. El Nuevo Diario: "Artistas califican de negligencia la no celebración de la Bienal Nacional de Artes Visuales" (2020)
URL: https://elnuevodiario.com.do/artistas-califican-de-negligencia-la-no-celebracion-de-la-bienal-nacional-de-artes-visuales/
- Calls the handling of the 29th Bienal negligent and argues that the Bienal should not depend on one building and should have its own permanent management committee.
2. Diario Libre: "Más de 60 artistas denuncian 'deficiente manejo' de 29 Bienal de Artes Visuales" (2021)

- Prints the letter, which says state cultural institutions "carecen de voluntad política" (lack the political will, my translation) to answer to the artistic community.
3. El Nuevo Diario: José Rafael Sosa, "Bienales de Artes Visuales de Santo Domingo, o el cuento de nunca acabar" (2025)
URL: https://elnuevodiario.com.do/bienales-de-artes-visuales-de-santo-domingo-o-el-cuento-de-nunca-acabar/
- Lists complaints about the Bienales since 1998 and sets the palm prize against the rules, which exclude perishable materials; the museum is the venue, not the target.

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 16 December 2019 the ministry postponed the 29th Bienal a day before it opened, saying the museum's remodelling was unfinished; the Comité de la Bienal Invisible called a protest for 17 December (Diario Libre).
- In July 2020 La Bienal Invisible, La Otra Bienal and La Cooperativa blamed Minister Eduardo Selman and Ditrén, and said submitted works had sat in the museum for nearly two years (El Nuevo Diario).
- In June 2021 more than 60 artists and curators denounced the "deficiente manejo" (poor handling, my translation) of the 29th Bienal, naming Minister Carmen Heredia and Fondeur (Diario Libre).
- The museum was the venue, and its directors were named.

BOTTOM LINE
A state museum paid for by the Ministry of Culture, with no private sponsor. Its disputes concern the ministry's running of the Bienal held there.
