SPEED ART MUSEUM: Louisville, USA
ID: speed-art-museum-louisville
Website: https://speedmuseum.org/ (checked 2026-10-24; live, listing events for October and November 2026)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 2035 South Third Street, Louisville, Kentucky, with a collection of Western art from antiquity to the present (SpeedMuseum.org; Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1927 by Hattie Bishop Speed in memory of her husband, James Breckenridge Speed, a Louisville businessman, art collector and philanthropist (Wikipedia).
- Named for James Breckenridge Speed (Wikipedia).
- Building: the 1927 Neoclassical building by Arthur Loomis; additions in 1954, 1973 and 1983; a $60 million expansion by Kulapat Yantrasast, with a 62,500 sq ft North Building, reopened in March 2016 (Wikipedia). The Elizabeth P. and Frederick K. Cressman Art Park, designed by Reed Hilderbrand at a cost of $22 million, was completed in October 2025 (Wikipedia).
- On site: galleries, a cinema and the art park (SpeedMuseum.org); Wikipedia gives the cinema 150 seats.
- Kathleen V. Jameson became director in September 2026, succeeding Raphaela Platow; the museum's centennial is in 2027 (Louisville Public Media).
- Visiting: closed Monday and Tuesday; Wednesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday noon to 5 p.m. Film tickets $12 ($8 for members); no general admission price was listed on the page opened (SpeedMuseum.org).

FUNDING
Speed Art Museum: private non-profit: gifts, investment income and corporate supporters
- Fiscal year ending September 2025 (Form 990 data, ProPublica): revenue $24.2 million, including $11.8 million in contributions and $8.8 million from asset sales; expenses $16.3 million; net assets $143.0 million. Fiscal 2024: revenue $17.1 million, expenses $16.9 million.
- Corporate supporters named on the museum's support page, undated and without levels: Brown-Forman, Churchill Downs Incorporated, Blue Grass Motorsport, Cisco, Ashbourne Farms, LG&E and KU, Dinsmore & Shohl, Kindred Healthcare, Beam Suntory, The Glenview Trust Co., Calhoun Construction, Heaven Hill, Republic Bank, Yum! Brands, Stoll Keenon Ogden and Commonwealth Bank & Trust.
- Spirits companies on that list: Brown-Forman, Beam Suntory and Heaven Hill.
- Alice Speed Stoll's bequest of over $50 million came around the time of the 1996 renovation (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of amounts for individual corporate supporters.

Red flags: Gambling
- Current listing: Churchill Downs Incorporated is the second name on the "Corporate Support" list of the museum's support page, opened on 24 October 2026; the page gives no year, giving level or amount (SpeedMuseum.org). Wikipedia says the company has racetracks, casinos and an online wagering company, and opened the Derby City Gaming historical racing parlor in 2018.
- Dated sponsorship: the company says that under a season-long partnership agreement it was presenting sponsor of the museum's exhibition "Tales from the Turf: The Kentucky Horse", which ran from 15 November 2019 to 1 March 2020 (Churchill Downs release, 22 June 2020). No dated sponsorship after 2020 was found.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- A Change.org petition begun on 7 April 2024 by Louisville resident Megan Himebrook asked the museum to cancel its Yayoi Kusama exhibition "Infinity Mirrored Room: Let's Survive Forever", citing what the petition describes as demeaning language about Black people in Kusama's earlier writing; it had 126 signatures when checked on 24 October 2026, and the petitioner says the museum sent a blanket statement and then stopped replying (Change.org, a petition by an individual, not a news report).
- Connection to the space: the petition targets an exhibition the Speed planned for July 2024.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum's money is mostly gifts, investment income and local corporate support, and its filings are public. The weak point is Churchill Downs Incorporated, a gambling company that is a listed supporter on the live support page and was the 2019 to 2020 presenting sponsor of a Speed exhibition, alongside three spirits firms. Watch for amounts per supporter and for the year of the support list.
