MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART CHICAGO (MCA): Chicago, USA
ID: museum-of-contemporary-art-chicago-mca
Website: https://mcachicago.org

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum at 220 East Chicago Avenue in the Streeterville neighbourhood of Chicago's Near North Side, on the site of a former National Guard armory (Wikipedia).
- Founded in 1967 and first housed at 237 East Ontario Street, a former Playboy Enterprises office; its first director was Jan van der Marck (Wikipedia).
- The current building, designed by the Berlin architect Josef Paul Kleihues, opened in 1996 with about 45,000 square feet of galleries and a 296-seat theatre. A 2017 renovation by Johnston Marklee, costing about 16 million dollars, redesigned 12,000 square feet (Wikipedia).
- A sculpture garden of about 34,000 square feet includes a work by Sol LeWitt (Wikipedia).
- Madeleine Grynsztejn has directed the museum since 2008 and announced in March 2026 that she would step down at the end of 2026 (Wikipedia). Her title is Pritzker Director (2025 Annual Report).
- Open Sunday 10 am to 5 pm and Wednesday to Saturday 10 am to 5 pm, Tuesday 10 am to 9 pm, closed Monday (museum website).

FUNDING
Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago: private nonprofit: individual and corporate donors, foundations and endowment, with small public grants
- Scale: in the fiscal year ended 30 June 2025 total revenue was 28.8 million dollars against expenses of 34.9 million dollars. Public support was 5.77 million dollars, special events 7.06 million dollars net, ancillary services 4.06 million dollars, tax appropriations 1.70 million dollars and government grants 0.31 million dollars. Investment spending allocation added 6.64 million dollars. Net assets were 174.1 million dollars (2025 Annual Report).
- Corporate donors in the 2025 donor list: Northern Trust at 100,000 dollars and above, BMO Bank at 25,000 to 49,999 dollars and Bank of America at 15,000 to 24,999 dollars. At 100,000 dollars and above the list also names Chanel, Google, NBC Universal and Hauser & Wirth; Christie's and Gagosian Gallery are at 25,000 to 49,999 dollars, and Discover Financial Services and Illinois Tool Works at 15,000 to 24,999 dollars (2025 Annual Report, gifts 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025).
- Public bodies at 100,000 dollars and above: Chicago Park District, the Illinois Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity and the Institute of Museum and Library Services. The Illinois Arts Council gave 50,000 to 99,999 dollars (2025 Annual Report).
- Foundations at 100,000 dollars and above include the Ford Foundation, the Mellon Foundation, Crown Family Philanthropies, Polk Bros. Foundation and Zell Family Foundation. Kenneth C. Griffin is among the individuals at the same level (2025 Annual Report).
- Corporate programme: the museum sells corporate partnerships at levels from Guarantor (10,000 to 24,999 dollars) to Philanthropist (25,000 dollars and up) in return for tickets, previews and events (museum website, Corporate and Foundation Giving).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Kenneth C. Griffin is listed as a donor at 100,000 dollars and above for gifts from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025 in the MCA's 2025 Annual Report.
- Griffin was targeted by protesters at the Whitney Museum in New York. On 22 November 2023 the Whitney's entrance was doused with fake blood during a march for Gaza, and the group Within Our Lifetime said protesters shouted "Ken Griffin is a terrorist"; Griffin sat on the Whitney's board until July 2023 (Hyperallergic, Hakim Bishara, "Whitney Museum Entrance Doused With Fake Blood", 23 November 2023).
- The protest was aimed at the Whitney. Griffin's connection to the MCA is his gift listed in the 2025 Annual Report.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is private and charitable: individual and corporate donors, foundations, an endowment and investment income, with small public grants. Named corporate funders in the 2025 donor list are Northern Trust, BMO Bank and Bank of America, and Kenneth C. Griffin is a donor at 100,000 dollars and above.
