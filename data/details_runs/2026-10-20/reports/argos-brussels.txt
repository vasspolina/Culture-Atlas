ARGOS: Brussels, Belgium
ID: argos-brussels
Website: https://www.argosarts.org/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Centre for audiovisual arts at Werfstraat 13 (rue du Chantier), 1000 Brussels, in the Les Quais district (argos website).
- Founded in 1989 in Brussels to stimulate and promote Belgian audiovisual arts; it began with screenings in cultural centres across Belgium and soon distributed videos to festivals, art institutions and television networks abroad (argos, "Our history").
- Founding directors were Koen Van Daele (1989 to 1993) and Frie Depraetere (1989 to 2013); Niels Van Tomme directed it from December 2018 to February 2024 (argos, "Our history").
- Building: since 1999 it has occupied a former storage facility, after several moves around the city (argos, "Our history").
- On site: two exhibition spaces, a projection room, a media library, a climate-controlled archive, studios, offices, a cafe and a bookshop; the collection holds more than 5,000 artist films and videos (argos, "Our history").
- Visiting: open Thursday to Sunday, 11:00 to 18:00. Visitors choose their contribution: 10 euros recommended, 5 euros for visitors with less to spend, 15 euros to give extra support. Entry is free for under-18s, ICOM members, Museumpass holders, visitors with disabilities and their assistant, and press; Paspartoe holders and Article 27 visitors pay a reduced price, with Article 27 at 1.25 euros (argos, "Visit us").

FUNDING
Argos: mainly public: the Flemish Government
- The site states "argos is supported by the Flemish Government"; since 1994 argos has been primarily subsidised by the Flemish Community (argos, "Our history").
- Board (argos, "Our team"): presidents Mohanad Yaqubi and Jens Van Lathem; members Anouk De Clercq, Kristof Van Hoorde, Tom Bonte, Sorana Munsya and Dirk De Wit. Joachim Naudts is general and artistic coordinator.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind argos is public: the Flemish Community has been its main subsidy provider since 1994, and the Flemish Government is its only named funder. Worth watching is its dependence on that single public funder.
