Red flags: 2023 member's withdrawal in protest at the Akademie's statement on artistic freedom
- The artist Candice Breitz, a member of the Akademie, objected to President Jeanine Meerapfel's press release of 15 December 2023, "In defence of artistic freedom" (Akademie der Künste, Senate statement, 21 December 2023).
- Breitz called the statement "too little, too late" on Instagram and said she was withdrawing her active membership from the academy until its leadership changes (The Art Newspaper, Catherine Hickley, 19 December 2023).
- The Akademie's Senate called Breitz's objection inaccurate and its tone defamatory, and confirmed its full confidence in Meerapfel (Akademie der Künste, Senate statement, 21 December 2023).
