ID: kunsthalle-wurth

WIDER CONTEXT
- On 28 May 2008 the Amtsgericht Heilbronn issued a penalty order (Strafbefehl) of 700 daily rates against Reinhold Würth for tax evasion; the amount was not published, and at the highest possible rate of 5,000 euros it would come to 3.5 million euros (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- The charge was that costs incurred for the whole group were booked mainly with one company instead of the group companies that caused them; prosecutors said the fine took account of the tax loss having been made good (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- Würth said in a statement that he had agreed "nach sehr reiflicher Überlegung" ("after very careful consideration", translated) to take responsibility for questions he called evidently disputed and complex (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008).
- Reinhold Würth built the Würth Group out of his family firm (Der Spiegel, 28 May 2008); the group owns and pays for the Kunsthalle.
