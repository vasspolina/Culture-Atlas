ID: musee-des-augustins

WIDER CONTEXT
- Francesco Guardi's "Le Grand Canal à Venise", bought by Hildebrand Gurlitt for the Linz museum on 1 February 1944 from a French private collection for 60,000 RM, was returned to France in 1946, assigned to the Louvre in 1950 and deposited at the Musée des Augustins in Toulouse in 1951 (French Ministry of Culture, Rose-Valland MNR database, notice MNR00286).
- The notice classes the painting as "Oeuvre assurément spoliée" ("work definitely looted", translated). It was restituted on 17 May 2005 to the heirs of Anna Gluge-Jaffé and removed from the provisional inventory (French Ministry of Culture, MNR database).
