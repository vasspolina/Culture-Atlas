KÜNSTLERHAUS STUTTGART: Stuttgart, Germany
ID: kuenstlerhaus-stuttgart
Website: https://kuenstlerhaus.de

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at Reuchlinstraße 4b in Stuttgart (address on the Künstlerhaus site), in the west of the city, in a former suitcase factory with four floors and about 600 m² of exhibition space (German Wikipedia).
- Founded by Stuttgart artists in 1978 (stated by the Künstlerhaus; German Wikipedia). Art historian Hans Dieter Huber has written scholarly texts on the institution and its building (stated by the Künstlerhaus).
- Organised as a registered association, Künstlerhaus Reuchlinstraße e.V. (Stuttgart council paper GRDrs 170/2024). The board is Ania Corcilius (first chairperson), Jochen Detscher (deputy) and Christoph Schwerdtfeger (treasurer); Juliane Gebhardt is managing director; Tamarind Rossetti and Stephen Wright have been artistic directors since 1 January 2024 (stated by the Künstlerhaus).
- Earlier artistic directors include Ulrich Bernhardt (1978 to 1986), Veit Görner, Ute Meta Bauer, Nicolaus Schafhausen, Fareed Armaly, Elke aus dem Moore, Axel John Wieder, Adnan Yildiz, Fatima Hellberg (2015 to 2019) and Eric Golo Stone from 2020 (German Wikipedia).
- Programme: six to eight exhibitions a year on two levels, workshops for audio, computer, print, film, photo and ceramics, a children's workshop for ages 4 to 14, and an annual studio fellowship with about 25 m² studios (German Wikipedia).
- Open Friday to Sunday, 14:00 to 18:00, and by appointment; entry is free. Events carry their own fees, for example €5 to €10 per class for the Haus Orkestra series; artist membership is €25 a year (stated by the Künstlerhaus).

FUNDING
Künstlerhaus Stuttgart: public: City of Stuttgart institutional grant, plus project support from foundations and partners
- City of Stuttgart: a council proposal of 12 July 2024 (GRDrs 170/2024, Culture Office budget) sets the institutional grant for "Künstlerhaus Reuchlinstraße e.V." at €650,000 for 2024 and €650,000 for 2025, as part of a raise the council decided to fund on 15 December 2023 for the 2024/2025 budget.
- Project support from the City of Stuttgart in 2026: the Art in Public Space Funding (KiöR) programme supports the Regenerative Walking series, and the city supports the Pop-up Glass Workshop (stated by the Künstlerhaus).
- Rainer Beck Foundation: funds Einan Kaku's project "East Wind Westward I". Istituto italiano di cultura Stoccarda and Kunstuniversität Linz support the exhibition "Laughing While the House Is Burning" (stated by the Künstlerhaus).

BOTTOM LINE
The Künstlerhaus is an artists' association whose documented core funding is the City of Stuttgart's institutional grant of €650,000 a year for 2024 and 2025. Foundations and partner institutions pay for individual projects.
