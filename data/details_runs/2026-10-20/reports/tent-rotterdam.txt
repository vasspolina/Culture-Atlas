TENT ROTTERDAM: Rotterdam, Netherlands
ID: tent-rotterdam
Website: https://www.tentrotterdam.nl

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art space at Coolhaven 32, Rotterdam, presenting Rotterdam artists and projects through a fixed location and nomadic projects; it describes itself as a programme of CBK Rotterdam (stated by the space).
- Grew out of a Rotterdam exhibition platform that became TENT from 1999, later at Witte de Withstraat 50 (CBK Rotterdam). The CBK site describes TENT as one of four CBK programmes, with SIR (Sculpture International Rotterdam), BKOR (public art collection and advice) and Art Office, which offers Rotterdam visual artists support through financial arrangements, meetings and an online community.
- CBK Rotterdam was founded in 1982. It was a municipal branch of service until 1 January 2006, when it became an independent foundation with a Supervisory Board (CBK Rotterdam).
- The Coolhaven building is a former port laboratory of Dr. Verwey, where food and animal feed were analysed for about seventy years; it was reconstructed for contemporary art exhibitions (museum.nl).
- Programme: TENT (IN) and TENT (OUT) exhibitions and projects, events, education, the podcast "Up Close", guest programming and an archive. The current exhibition is "Peel the Power", 18 July to 25 October 2026 (stated by the space).
- Visiting: open Thursday to Sunday 12:00 to 18:00 (stated by the space). The Netherlands Museum Pass is valid, and the venue is wheelchair accessible (museum.nl).

FUNDING
TENT: a programme of CBK Rotterdam, a foundation that was a municipal service until 2006
- TENT is run by CBK Rotterdam, based at Eendrachtsstraat 10, Rotterdam, which also runs BKOR, which manages the collection of sculptures in Rotterdam's public space and advises the municipality, citizens and artists on public works of art (CBK Rotterdam).
- CBK Rotterdam was a municipal branch of service until 1 January 2006 (CBK Rotterdam).

BOTTOM LINE
TENT is a programme of CBK Rotterdam, which was a municipal branch of service until it became an independent foundation on 1 January 2006, and which also runs BKOR, the manager of Rotterdam's public sculpture collection.
