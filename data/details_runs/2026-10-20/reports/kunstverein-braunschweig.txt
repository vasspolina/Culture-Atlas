KUNSTVEREIN BRAUNSCHWEIG: Braunschweig, Germany
ID: kunstverein-braunschweig
Website: https://kunstvereinbraunschweig.de

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art association at Lessingplatz 12 in Braunschweig, housed in the Villa Salve Hospes.
- Founded in 1832, one of the oldest institutions of its kind in Germany. The architect Peter Joseph Krahe, a founder of its predecessor the Verein der Kunstfreunde, was its first chair from 1834 and active until his death in 1840. It moved into the Villa Salve Hospes in 1946 (stated by the association).
- The villa is an early classicist town palace built from 1805 to 1808 by Krahe as a private residence, with Palladio's Villa Rotonda as one of its models. It offers nearly 400 square metres of exhibition space over two floors, and the Remise annex has hosted exhibitions since 1996 (stated by the association).
- Wikipedia states that the Verein der Kunstfreunde dissolved in 1923, that a Kunstverein Braunschweig e. V. formed under National Socialist direction in 1941 and moved into the villa in 1942, and that it was re-founded in 1946.
- It shows six to nine exhibitions a year, with a focus on supporting young positions, including funding for new works. Current and upcoming shows are Meisterschüler*innen der HBK Braunschweig 2026 (17 October to 8 November 2026) and ars viva 2026 with Ryan Cullen, Nazanin Noori and Prateek Vijan (5 December 2026 to 21 February 2027).
- Cathrin Mayer is director. The Vorstand is Jens Nowak (chair), Andreas Janßen (deputy chair), Timo Antons (treasurer), Annette Schütze, Dr. Johannes Waitz, Dr. Maresa Wischenbart-Backhaus and Prof. David Zink Yi.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Friday 12:00 to 18:00 (Thursday to 20:00), Saturday, Sunday and public holidays 11:00 to 18:00. Admission is 5 euros, reduced 3 euros; free on the first Sunday of each month and for members, under 6s and students of the HBK Braunschweig. The villa is not wheelchair accessible (12 steps, a landing and 5 more steps); the Remise annex is step free with a ramp.

FUNDING
Kunstverein Braunschweig: City of Braunschweig, Lower Saxony, federal culture bodies, foundations, companies and members
- The association's sponsors page opens its list with Stadt Braunschweig (Fachbereich Kultur und Wissenschaft), the Niedersächsisches Ministerium für Wissenschaft und Kultur and the federal Beauftragte für Kultur und Medien (BKM).
- Foundations on the same list include Kulturstiftung des Bundes, Stiftung Niedersachsen, Robert Bosch Stiftung, Alfried Krupp von Bohlen und Halbach Stiftung, Braunschweigische Sparkassenstiftung, Die Braunschweigische Stiftung, Stiftung Kunstfonds, Hypo-Kulturstiftung, DZ BANK Kunststiftung and Volkswagen art foundation. Neustart Kultur is also listed.
- Foreign bodies on the list include the Mondriaan Fund, Pro Helvetia, the Canada Council for the Arts, the British Council, the Japan Foundation, the Danish Art Foundation and several embassies.
- Companies on the list include NORD/LB, Bethmann Bank, Braunschweiger Privatbank, BS|ENERGY, ALBA Niedersachsen-Anhalt, Veolia, Lufthansa Group, Volkswagen Bank, Volkswagen Financial Services and Credit Suisse.
- A second list of Fördermitglieder (supporting members) names individuals and companies, among them ALBA Niedersachsen-Anhalt, Braunschweiger Privatbank, Gingco Communication, Hoffmann Maschinen und Apparatebau and NORD/LB Girozentrale. Jens Nowak and Andreas Janßen, the chair and deputy chair, appear on it.
- Wikipedia puts membership at about 500 in 2010 and about 400 in 2021.

BOTTOM LINE
The funding is a mix of public bodies (the City of Braunschweig, Lower Saxony and the federal culture commissioner), foundations, companies and supporting members, all named on the association's sponsors page.
