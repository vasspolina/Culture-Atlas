ID: villa-tugendhat

WIDER CONTEXT
- In February 2007, while the Tugendhat heirs' claim to the villa was pending, Brno City Hall criticised the family for auctioning Wilhelm Lehmbruck's "Torso of a Walking Woman", which had stood in the villa and had been returned to them the year before, at Sotheby's for one million pounds rather than selling it to the Moravian Gallery. City spokesman Pavel Zara said "the sale of the statue signals that the fate of the villa may not meet our expectations". The family said a statue could not be compared to the building and that part of the proceeds would have paid for repairs to the villa had it been returned to them (Radio Prague International, 7 February 2007).
- After the city voted in March 2007 to keep the villa, the family's lawyer Augustin Kohoutek said the assembly "changed its opinion 180 degrees" and that the family would go to court if the city offered no acceptable solution (Radio Prague International, 21 March 2007).
