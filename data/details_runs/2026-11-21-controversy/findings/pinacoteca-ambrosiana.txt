ID: pinacoteca-ambrosiana

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2014 the Ambrosiana lent its Sacred Family with St Anne and St John, always attributed to Bernardino Luini, to the exhibition "Bernardino Luini and His Sons" at Palazzo Reale in Milan; the curators Giovanni Agosti and Jacopo Stoppa judged it a work of Luini's workshop without first telling the museum, and the Ambrosiana withdrew the painting from the show (Finestre sull'Arte, 12 June 2014).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
1. Finestre sull'Arte: Federico Giannini, "The Bernardino Luini affair in Milan: all of us are the losers" (2014)
URL: https://www.finestresullarte.info/en/news-focus/the-bernardino-luini-affair-in-milan-all-of-us-are-the-losers
- The painting loses nothing on the formal level, so the museum's description of it as a "downgraded" work would have to be revised.
- The museum issued "unsympathetic press releases" and, "with a probably overreaction, took the painting back without wanting to attempt the slightest dialogue" with the curators.
- The lack of dialogue harmed research, the public and the credibility of the Italian art system, and "the loser is all of us".
