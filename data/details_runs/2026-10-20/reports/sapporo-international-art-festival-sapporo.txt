SAPPORO INTERNATIONAL ART FESTIVAL: Sapporo, Japan
ID: sapporo-international-art-festival-sapporo
Website: https://siaf.jp

OVERVIEW
- Triennial art festival in Sapporo that exhibits artworks and new forms of expression from around the world in museums, parks, public facilities, underground spaces, former theatres and snow storage facilities. Its Ainu-language name is Usa Mosir un Askay utar Sapporo otta Uekarpa (the festival's website).
- Editions: summer in 2014 (19 July to 28 September, 72 days) and 2017 (6 August to 1 October), then winter in 2024 (20 January to 25 February, 37 days); the 2020 edition was cancelled because of COVID-19. The Secretariat acknowledges that some people felt the 2024 run was too short and calls it "the best possible outcome given SIAF's advantage in media art", with budget size and the winter season also shaping the decision (Festival Timing and Budget page).
- SIAF2027, "Planet Snow" (upas mintar / upas nociw, Ainu words for "snow garden" and "snow star"), runs from 16 January to 21 February 2027 across 11 venues. Some venues have different dates: Sapporo Science Center from 5 January, the Hokkaido Museum of Modern Art from 9 January to 11 April (tentative), the Odori 500-m Underground Walkway Gallery from 16 January to 24 March, and the Sapporo Snow Festival Odori site from 4 to 21 February.
- Director team for 2027: Ogawa Hideaki (Artistic Director), Hosokawa Asami (Festival Director), Urushi Takahiro (School Director) and Maruta Tomoaki (Studio Director).
- Since 2025 the festival's activities have been organised in three frameworks: the Festival, SIAF School and SIAF Studio (the festival's website).
- Organiser: the Sapporo International Art Festival Executive Committee together with the City of Sapporo.

FUNDING
Sapporo International Art Festival: city-organised: City of Sapporo, with support from the national Agency for Cultural Affairs, foundations and corporate partners
- Organiser: Sapporo International Art Festival Executive Committee and the City of Sapporo.
- Supported by (SIAF2027): the Agency for Cultural Affairs of the Government of Japan (fiscal year 2026), the Embassy of Switzerland in Japan, the Japan Foundation for Regional Art-Activities, the Kao Foundation for Arts and Sciences and CLAIR (Council of Local Authorities for International Relations). New Chitose Airport International Animation Festival is listed under cooperation.
- Initiative Partners (corporations that, per the festival, use their expertise to co-create exhibitions and programmes): KOKUYO Co., Ltd., Nikkei Inc., Startbahn, Inc. and Wacom Co., Ltd. Official Partners: Dialogue on Art and Technology (DAT) and the Japan Graphic Designers Association Inc. Hokkaido Branch (JAGDA Hokkaido).
- The Secretariat says measures against snowfall and cold weather in winter increase the size of the budget (Festival Timing and Budget page).

BOTTOM LINE
The City of Sapporo and an executive committee organise the festival. SIAF2027 lists support from Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs, the Embassy of Switzerland in Japan, the Japan Foundation for Regional Art-Activities, the Kao Foundation for Arts and Sciences and CLAIR, and four corporate Initiative Partners (KOKUYO, Nikkei, Startbahn, Wacom).
