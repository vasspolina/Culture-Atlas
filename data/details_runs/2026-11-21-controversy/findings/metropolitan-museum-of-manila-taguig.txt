WIDER CONTEXT
- On 19 December 2019 the Sandiganbayan's Special Division, in a 42-page partial summary judgment, ordered the forfeiture to the government of over 160 paintings and artworks valued at $24.325 million, which it declared Ferdinand and Imelda Marcos had unlawfully acquired (The Philippine Star, Elizabeth Marcelo, 20 December 2019).
- The items ordered forfeited included the artworks listed in a "Report on the Metropolitan Museum of Manila's Art Collection" (The Philippine Star, 20 December 2019).
- The Presidential Commission on Good Government's complaint, filed in 1991, listed 27 paintings and sculptures "discovered at the sequestered Metropolitan Museum of Manila" with an assessed value of $548,445 (The Philippine Star, 20 December 2019).
