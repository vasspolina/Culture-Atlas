SOLOMON ISLANDS NATIONAL MUSEUM: Honiara, Solomon Islands
ID: solomon-islands-national-museum-honiara

OVERVIEW
- National museum in Honiara with several buildings, each with a gallery on themes such as inter-island politics and independence, plus offices and an auditorium with an outdoor stage (Wikipedia).
- Origins: the nucleus of an Ethnology and Natural History Museum collection was formed in 1952 and a building erected in 1953 (Solomon Islands Historical Encyclopaedia). The museum opened on 6 June 1969 and was included in the new Information, Broadcasting, Museum and Library Services department in 1972 (Solomon Islands Historical Encyclopaedia).
- Building: the first stage of the modern museum was built in 1968 in Coronation Gardens, opposite the Mendana Hotel, with a grant from the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation in Lisbon (Solomon Islands Historical Encyclopaedia). The Japanese Government built the museum library in 1999 (Ministry of Culture and Tourism press release, 2022).
- A refurbished library building opened on 6 May 2022; it houses more than 1,000 Melanesian collections and a pre-history gallery (Tourism Media Solomon Islands, 10 May 2022).
- The museum holds over 2,000 objects, including natural science specimens, archaeological artefacts, Second World War objects and marine and geological specimens, and has collected contemporary sculpture since 1972 (Wikipedia).
- It is a department of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism; Tony Heorake is the director (Wikipedia; British High Commission, 2024).
- The museum was looted during the 1999 to 2003 ethnic violence, and many cultural artefacts were lost (Wikipedia).
- A virtual museum with an openly accessible online catalogue of its collections launched in October 2024 at the National Museum Auditorium (British High Commission, 15 October 2024).

FUNDING
Solomon Islands National Museum: government department with foreign aid for buildings and digitisation
- Government: a department of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism (Wikipedia).
- Virtual museum: developed through the Digital Pasifik project, funded by the Australian Government; the National Library of New Zealand and the National Library of Australia gave technical support, the British High Commission and Australian High Commission were partners, and the National Museum will own and sustain the site (British High Commission, 15 October 2024; Sunday Isles, 16 October 2024).
- Japan: the Japanese Government built the museum library in 1999 (Ministry of Culture and Tourism press release, 2022).
- Library refurbishment, 2022: completed by the contractor Vavalu Construction, with "valuable support" to the contractor from BRED Bank (Tourism Media Solomon Islands, 2022).
- 1968 building: Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation grant for the first stage of the modern museum (Solomon Islands Historical Encyclopaedia).
- 1974: a museum curator funded by the United Kingdom Assistance Programme (Solomon Islands Historical Encyclopaedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is a department of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. Japan built its library in 1999, the Australian Government funded the Digital Pasifik project behind its 2024 virtual museum with the British High Commission as a partner, and the United Kingdom Assistance Programme funded a curator in 1974.
