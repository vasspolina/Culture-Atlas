RIDDODUOTTARMUSEAT: Karasjok, Norway
ID: riddoduottarmuseat-karasjok
Website: https://rdm.no

OVERVIEW
- Sámi museum foundation (RDM) that runs four cultural-history museums and manages the Sámi Parliament's art collection: De Samiske Samlinger in Karasjok, Kautokeino bygdetun, Porsanger museum and Kokelv sjøsamiske museum, plus the Sámi art store (Samisk kunstmagasin) (RDM annual report 2023, lokalhistoriewiki).
- Established in 2006 (lokalhistoriewiki). RDM is a private foundation; its founders are the municipalities of Kautokeino and Porsanger, the foundation Stiftelsen De Samiske Samlinger (Karasjok municipality) and Stiftelsen Kokelv sjøsamiske museum (RDM annual report 2023).
- The Sámi Parliament (Sametinget) appoints two board members including the chair, and Karasjok municipality appoints one; Sámi is the organisation's main language, and the main administration is at De Samiske Samlinger in Karasjok (RDM annual report 2023).
- It presents Sámi inland culture, reindeer herding, sea Sámi culture and the meeting of Sámi, Kven and Norwegian culture through objects, photographs and intangible heritage (RDM annual report 2023).
- De Samiske Samlinger was established in 1972 as the first Sámi cultural institution in Norway and holds more than 5,000 objects (Fortidsminneforeningen). The main building is a brutalist reinforced-concrete building from 1972, drawn by two Tromsø-based architects in close cooperation with the Sámi artist Iver Jåks (Fortidsminneforeningen).
- The museums had 14,467 visitors in 2023, up 1.5% on 2022; RDM had 17 full-time equivalent positions and the director was Anne May Olli (RDM annual report 2023).
- A planned new museum building in Karasjok would replace the 1972 building and house both the cultural-history and the art collections. Statsbygg is the developer on behalf of the Ministry of Culture and Equality and would own and manage it (Riksantikvaren, 25 August 2025; Fortidsminneforeningen).

FUNDING
RiddoDuottarMuseat: mainly public: the Sámi Parliament, Finnmark county and four municipalities
- 2023 public grants listed in the annual report: Sametinget NOK 13,122,000 (NOK 13,687,000 in 2022), Finnmark fylkeskommune NOK 800,000, Porsanger municipality NOK 564,584, Kautokeino municipality NOK 282,100, Hammerfest municipality NOK 282,225 and Karasjok municipality NOK 220,000. These total about NOK 15.27 million (RDM annual report 2023).
- The Sametinget figure excludes the Máze project and the Sámi Parliament's art purchase scheme (RDM annual report 2023).
- The 2023 result was a surplus of NOK 142,185 (RDM annual report 2023).
- The Sámi Parliament has administrative responsibility for RDM and is responsible for operating grants (lokalhistoriewiki).
- The state budget set aside NOK 5 million for further planning of the new Karasjok building (Fortidsminneforeningen).

Red flags: Protest over the planned demolition of the Karasjok museum building
- In June 2022 the Sámi Parliament, the museum's main funder, voted for demolition of the museum building for De Samiske Samlinger and a new building; the protests below are aimed at that decision and at the Statsbygg zoning plan that follows it (Riksantikvaren, Fortidsminneforeningen).
- Fortidsminneforeningen (the Society for the Preservation of Norwegian Ancient Monuments), through its Finnmark branch, protested the demolition, and its antiquarian committee backed the branch fully; in a letter to Statsbygg dated 19 April 2024 it asked Statsbygg to study keeping the building with an extension. It argues that the art by Iver Jåks is integrated in the architecture and cannot be moved without loss, and says Statsbygg's own 2019 feasibility study found renovation with an extension realistic.
- Riksantikvaren (the Directorate for Cultural Heritage) lodged a formal objection on 25 August 2025 to the detailed zoning plan for the new museum building, saying the cultural environment is not adequately addressed, the plan is too imprecise and the impact assessment has significant gaps, with the protected hunting-pit site and eight protected Sámi buildings on the museum grounds not sufficiently secured. It does not oppose demolition as such, but says reuse and rehabilitation should be the first choice.
- RDM's board has itself criticised Riksantikvaren: Ságat reported on 11 May 2026 under the headline "Riksantikvaren undergraver samisk selvbestemmelse" (title translated: Riksantikvaren undermines Sámi self-determination) that RDM's board holds that Riksantikvaren touches fundamental questions of Sámi self-determination (Ságat, Stein Torger Svala; paywalled, headline and lead only).

BOTTOM LINE
RiddoDuottarMuseat is a Sámi museum foundation financed by public money: NOK 13.1 million of about NOK 15.3 million in 2023 grants came from the Sámi Parliament, the rest from Finnmark county and four municipalities. The documented dispute concerns the planned demolition of the 1972 Karasjok museum building, opposed by a heritage society and objected to in zoning by Riksantikvaren.
