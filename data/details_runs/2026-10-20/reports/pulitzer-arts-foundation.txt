PULITZER ARTS FOUNDATION: St. Louis, USA
ID: pulitzer-arts-foundation
Website: https://pulitzerarts.org (checked 2026-10-25; loaded)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 3716 Washington Boulevard, St. Louis, in the Grand Center Arts District, presenting historical and contemporary art from around the world (pulitzerarts.org).
- Founded in 2001 by Emily Rauh Pulitzer, with her husband Joseph Pulitzer Jr., who died in 1993 before construction began in 1996 (Wikipedia; Washington University commencement archive).
- Richard Serra's courtyard sculpture "Joe" is named for the late Joseph Pulitzer Jr. (pulitzerarts.org).
- Building: designed by Tadao Ando, his first public building in the United States, completed in October 2001, known for on-site concrete and its use of light and water; a 2014 to 2015 expansion raised public space from about 6,800 to 10,400 square feet and the museum reopened on 1 May 2015 (Wikipedia). Ando was first hired in the early 1990s to renovate an abandoned car factory and showroom in the district (Wikipedia). Paul Goldberger of The New Yorker called the building "the greatest work of architecture to go up in St. Louis" (Washington University commencement archive).
- On site: a non-collecting museum with temporary exhibitions and three permanent works, Ellsworth Kelly's "Blue Black," Serra's "Joe" and Scott Burton's "Rock Settee"; free talks, concerts, readings and dance, and outdoor gardens (pulitzerarts.org; Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Thursday to Sunday 10am to 5pm, until 9pm on First Fridays; admission and parking are free (pulitzerarts.org). No reporting found of accessibility details on the pages read.

FUNDING
Pulitzer Arts Foundation: privately run: the founding family's foundation, free to visit; sources of revenue not published
- ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer, from its Form 990-PF filings, lists 2024 revenue of $10,237,241 ($10.2 million), expenses of $6,184,760 ($6.18 million, the figure that rounds to $6.2 million) and net assets of $113,550,180; 2023 revenue was $5,934,760 and expenses $6,049,885 (ProPublica, EIN 43-1752949). The sources of the revenue are not broken down on that page.
- Officers include Emily R. Pulitzer (chair), Cara McCarty (vice chair), William Bush (president) and Elkhanah Pulitzer (vice president), with 2024 pay of $0 for the chair, vice chair and vice president, $10,000 for the president, and $375,000 for the executive director, Cara Starke (ProPublica 2024 filing; pulitzerarts.org/about/board).
- Family background: Joseph Pulitzer Jr. was editor and publisher of the St. Louis Post-Dispatch (Washington University commencement archive). No source read states that newspaper money funds the foundation.
- The donate page names no funders, sponsors or endowment. No reporting found of corporate sponsors, a donor list or an annual report.
- A second ProPublica page, for EIN 43-1940989, returned HTTP 404 when opened, so it is not used.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the foundation is a private foundation led by the founding family, with free admission, and no flagged-industry funder was found. The weak points are that no donor list, annual report or breakdown of revenue is published, and that family members hold the chair and a vice-president post. Worth watching the Form 990-PF filings for the sources of the large yearly swings in revenue.
