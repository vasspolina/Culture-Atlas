CRÁTER INVERTIDO: Mexico City, Mexico
ID: crater-invertido-mexico-city
Website: https://craterinvertido.org

OVERVIEW
- Artist cooperative that self-manages a multidisciplinary house-workshop in Mexico City, with a risograph press and an experimental radio (stated by the space and by Arts Collaboratory).
- Since 2011 the cooperative has been made up of graduates of the National School of Visual Arts (UNAM) and the "La Esmeralda" School of Painting, Sculpture and Printmaking (INBA) (MUAC, UNAM). Time Out Mexico (2016) says the group came together at La Esmeralda under the name Grupo D.
- Publishes risograph-printed work through its own imprint, Cráneo Invertido (MUAC, UNAM).
- Time Out Mexico (review of February 2016) gave the address as Joaquín García Icazbalceta 32, colonia San Rafael, near Metro San Cosme.
- Programmes listed on its site: Casita, Radio Tropiezo, Imaginarios and Taller XD. It also takes part in decentralised processes on the care and defence of territories, biodiversity, shared learning and commonality (Arts Collaboratory).
- Took part in the 56th Venice Biennale (2015), the Jakarta Biennale (2015), Sonsbeek '16, the Gwangju Biennale (2016) and the Conundrum of Imagination in Vienna (2017) (Arts Collaboratory).
- Visiting: Time Out Mexico (2016) listed free entry and opening by appointment, depending on the event.

FUNDING
Cráter Invertido: member-run cooperative and member of the Arts Collaboratory network, which Hivos and DOEN Foundation started
- Arts Collaboratory lists Cooperativa Cráter Invertido among its 25 member organisations. DOEN Foundation is also a member and says it contributes to the network "through its funding, its network and its knowledge" (Arts Collaboratory).
- Wikipedia says Arts Collaboratory was established in 2007 by the Dutch organisations Hivos and DOEN Foundation and is implemented in collaboration with the Mondriaan Foundation. It gives long-term support to independent visual arts organisations in Africa, Asia and Latin America, plus project funds of up to 15,000 euros per project.

BOTTOM LINE
Cráter Invertido is a member-run artist cooperative and a member of the Arts Collaboratory network, which the Dutch organisations Hivos and DOEN Foundation started in 2007 to support independent visual arts organisations in Africa, Asia and Latin America.
