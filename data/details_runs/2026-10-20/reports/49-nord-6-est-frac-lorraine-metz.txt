49 NORD 6 EST FRAC LORRAINE: Metz, France
ID: 49-nord-6-est-frac-lorraine-metz
Website: https://www.fraclorraine.org (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Regional public collection of contemporary art for the Lorraine part of the Grand Est region, with a free exhibition space in the historic centre of Metz at 1 bis rue des Trinitaires. Its formal name is Fonds régional d'art contemporain de Lorraine.
- Founded in 1984 as a "nomad contemporary art fund" (Wikipedia), part of the national Frac programme started in 1982 on the principle of shared financing between the state and the regional councils.
- Named "49 Nord 6 Est" for the geodesic position of its building (Wikipedia).
- Building: since 2004 the Frac has occupied the Hôtel Saint-Livier, an hôtel particulier that Wikipedia dates to the 12th century and Metz tourism to the 13th century; the tourism page calls it Metz's oldest private mansion. The pages read name no architect.
- What is there: exhibitions, a documentation centre, publishing and education programmes, and a collection of about 1,900 works (Guide Tourisme France; other listings give more than 1,800 for Metz tourism, 1,530 for lesfrac.com and more than 800 for Wikipedia). The Frac also lends and shows work across Lorraine and works with Frac Alsace and Frac Champagne-Ardenne.
- Visiting: free entry. Open Wednesday to Friday 2 to 6 p.m., Saturday and Sunday 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; closed Monday and Tuesday, 25 December and 1 January. Free guided tours; programmes for ages 2 months to 11 years; offers adapted for blind or low-vision, deaf or hard-of-hearing visitors, visitors with reduced mobility, and visitors with psychological, developmental or cognitive disabilities.

FUNDING
49 Nord 6 Est Frac Lorraine: mainly public: Région Grand Est and the French state
- The Frac is supported by the Région Grand Est and by the Ministry of Culture through the DRAC Grand Est (Guide Tourisme France).
- No reporting found of amounts, budgets or accounts for the Frac Lorraine.
- Private patronage: the Frac's patronage page invites donations to support actions of the Frac Lorraine (own translation of "des actions du Frac Lorraine") for artists in Lorraine, France and abroad. It names no patron and no amounts, and no patron list was found on its partnerships page.
- Partners named on the Frac's partnerships page are venues for its "Cultures en dialogue" shows (a prison, a library, a hospital, a museum and others), not funders.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Frac is public, from the Région Grand Est and the French state, which is not a red flag. The weak point is transparency: no budget, accounts or patron names were found. Worth watching: whether the Frac publishes who its private patrons are.
