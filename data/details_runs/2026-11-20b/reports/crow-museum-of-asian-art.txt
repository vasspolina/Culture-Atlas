CROW MUSEUM OF ASIAN ART: Dallas, USA
ID: crow-museum-of-asian-art
Website: https://crowmuseum.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of Asian art run by The University of Texas at Dallas, with its original building at 2010 Flora Street in the Dallas Arts District and a second site in the university's Edith and Peter O'Donnell Jr. Athenaeum (museum website).
- Trammell and Margaret Crow bought their first piece of Asian art in the mid-1960s and opened the Trammell and Margaret Crow Collection of Asian Art as a free museum in December 1998 (museum website).
- Collection: art from China, Japan, India, Korea and Southeast Asia, bought mostly from dealers, auction houses and whole collections such as Morrie A. Moss's; the Asian art expert Clarence Shangraw chose 569 pieces for the permanent collection (museum website). The 2019 gift counted more than 1,000 works (UT Dallas, 24 January 2019).
- The Athenaeum is an arts and performance complex designed by Morphosis Architects; UT Dallas opened its first phase in 2024 (UT Dallas, July 2022; museum website).
- The Dallas Arts District site is open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 17:00, closed Mondays and major holidays (museum website).

FUNDING
Crow Museum of Asian Art: family gift to a state university: the Crow family, the O'Donnell Foundation and UT Dallas
- In January 2019 the Crow family gave UT Dallas the whole collection "together with $23 million of support funding" for the design and construction of a second museum on campus; the release names the founders' son Trammell S. Crow (UT Dallas, 24 January 2019).
- UT Dallas took over running the downtown museum, with Amy Lewis Hofland, director since 2002, leading both sites (UT Dallas, 2019).
- Athenaeum: by July 2022 the university had raised $73.9 million, including $25.45 million from the Crow family, $10.95 million from Margaret McDermott and $5 million from Bea Wallace (UT Dallas, July 2022).
- The O'Donnell Foundation gave $32 million: $22 million for building the Crow Museum and performance hall and $10 million for a programming endowment for the Athenaeum (UT Dallas, July 2022).
- Friends of the late Rick Brettell gave $500,000 for the Brettell Reading Room (UT Dallas, July 2022).

BOTTOM LINE
A family collection now owned by a public university, with building money from the Crow family and Texas foundations; none of the named funders carries a flag. Watch for new named donors to the Athenaeum.
