ID: hubei-provincial-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- In 2017 the museum showed "This is Africa", photographs by Yu Huiping, vice-president of the Hubei Photographers Association, which placed portraits of Africans beside animals, including a roaring lion beside a man and a monkey beside another man; the exhibition opened on 28 September 2017 (Euronews, 12 October 2017).
- Criticism spread online after a video posted on Instagram by Nigerian Edward E. Duke circulated (Euronews, 12 October 2017).
- The museum took the exhibition down in the week of 13 October 2017 after complaints from Africans (OkayAfrica, 13 October 2017).
