ENJOY CONTEMPORARY ART SPACE: Wellington, New Zealand
ID: enjoy-contemporary-art-space-wellington
Website: https://enjoy.org.nz

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run contemporary art space at 211 Left Bank, off Cuba Street in central Wellington (Enjoy).
- Established in June 2000 (Enjoy). Wikipedia names the founders as the artists Ciaran Begley, Ros Cameron and Rachel Smithies, working in a first-floor space at 174 Cuba Street until 2006.
- It was called Enjoy Public Art Gallery from 2001 to 2019 (Wikipedia). It moved to Level 1, 147 Cuba Street in 2006 and to its present ground-floor space in 2019 (Wikipedia).
- A Board of Trustees governs it, made up of Wellington-based artists and arts professionals (Wikipedia). Its Givealittle page says it has worked with more than 430 artists, published more than 160 writers and presented more than 200 exhibitions over 17 years.
- Programmes: exhibitions, an annual residency, an open call for proposals and public programmes (Enjoy).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Friday 11am to 6pm and Saturday 11am to 4pm. The galleries and reading room are free and open to all. There is a wheelchair accessible, gender-neutral bathroom, and the closest car park with mobility parking is 30 metres away in Victoria Street.

FUNDING
Enjoy Contemporary Art Space: primarily funded by Creative New Zealand, with Wellington City Council
- The supporters page says "Enjoy is primarily funded by Creative New Zealand" and "Enjoy receives funding from Creative New Zealand".
- The supporters page has a Grant Partners heading with a logo linking to wellington.govt.nz, and Wikipedia lists Creative New Zealand and Wellington City Council as its supporters.
- Enjoy says it needs donations, grants and sponsorships to develop its programme.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Enjoy is public: Creative New Zealand is the primary funder and Wellington City Council a grant partner, with donations, grants and sponsorships on top. Its galleries and reading room are free.
