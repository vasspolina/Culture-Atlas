LE FRESNOY, STUDIO NATIONAL DES ARTS CONTEMPORAINS: Tourcoing, France
ID: le-fresnoy-studio-national-des-arts-contemporains
Website: https://www.lefresnoy.net

OVERVIEW
- Art school and centre for producing and showing audiovisual, digital and artistic work at 22 rue du Fresnoy, Tourcoing, in the Métropole européenne de Lille (the space's website, Wikipedia).
- Founded in October 1997 by Alain Fleischer (Wikipedia).
- The building was a popular entertainment complex that operated from 1905 to 1984, with a cinema of 1,000 seats, a swimming pool later converted into a riding ring, a roller rink and a dance hall (Wikipedia).
- Architect Bernard Tschumi led the rehabilitation, placing the old buildings under a roof of about 100 by 80 metres on a site of about 11,000 square metres (Wikipedia).
- On site: the school, studios, exhibition and performance spaces, two cinemas, sound, image, film and video labs, a restaurant, the Bookstorming bookshop and a media library (Wikipedia).
- The school runs a two-year course for up to 24 students per session, admitted by competitive entrance exam, and the annual autumn exhibition Panorama presents new works by students and invited artists (Wikipedia).
- The institution is open Monday to Friday, 9:30 to 12:30 and 14:00 to 18:00; Panorama 28 (11 September 2026 to 3 January 2027) is open Wednesday to Sunday, 14:00 to 19:00 (the space's website).

FUNDING
Le Fresnoy: public: Ministry of Culture and Hauts-de-France Region, with the participation of the city of Tourcoing
- The space's partners page states that Le Fresnoy is financed by the Ministry of Culture and the Région Hauts-de-France, with the participation of the Ville de Tourcoing (the space's website).
- The European Regional Development Fund (FEDER) co-financed the technical equipment (the space's website).
- The Métropole européenne de Lille and the Département du Nord are listed on the partners page as exhibition and event partners (the space's website).
- Also listed as national and international exhibition and event partners: Neuflize OBC, Fondation d'entreprise Hermès, Fondation Daniel & Nina Carasso, Pinault Collection and TBA21 Thyssen-Bornemisza Art Contemporary; Télérama, Arte and Artpress are listed as media partners (the space's website).

BOTTOM LINE
Le Fresnoy is financed by the French Ministry of Culture and the Hauts-de-France Region, with the participation of the city of Tourcoing and European regional funds (FEDER) co-financing its technical equipment. The private foundations and companies on its partners page are listed as exhibition and event partners.
