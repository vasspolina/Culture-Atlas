GAZA BIENNALE: Gaza, Palestine
ID: gaza-biennale-gaza
Website: https://gazabiennale.org

OVERVIEW
- Decentralised biennial by Palestinian artists in Gaza, shown through "pavilions" hosted by institutions abroad and online (gazabiennale.org; Depo Istanbul).
- Announced on 20 November 2024 "from the land of Gaza" with over 40 artists, according to the biennial's own press statement; The Art Newspaper (12 December 2024) counts more than 50 Palestinian artists.
- Artist Tasneem Shatat, 26, from Khan Younis, leads the effort (Hyperallergic, Isa Farfan, 26 November 2024).
- Announced with sponsorship from the Al Risan Art Museum (The Art Newspaper). Hyperallergic describes the museum, also called the Forbidden Museum, as a Palestinian cultural institution "without walls" in the occupied West Bank, co-founded by Fidaa Ataya and the visual artist Andreas Ibrahim and inaugurated by the Palestinian Ministry of Culture at the summit of Al Risan mountain.
- Pavilions listed on the site include Padua, Berlin, T'karonto, Sarajevo, Istanbul, New York, Valencia, Edinburgh and Sweden, with dates from 2025 to a Sweden pavilion in May and June 2026.
- The Istanbul pavilion, "A Cloud in My Hand", ran at Depo from 19 September to 8 November 2025 during the 18th International Istanbul Biennale, curated by House of Taswir; more than twenty artists, including Alfredo Jaar, Walid Raad and Shirin Neshat, donated works (Depo Istanbul).
- On 14 January 2025 the biennial was shown digitally outside the Institute of Contemporary Art in London, in protest at the ICA's New Contemporaries exhibition and its funding from Bloomberg Philanthropies. The protest was backed by ARTCRY and the Palestinian Youth Movement (Huck, Cyna Mirzai, 14 January 2025).

FUNDING
Gaza Biennale: crowdfunded and donor-supported: Al Risan Art Museum, partner institutions and individual donations
- The aim is to raise $90,000 for the artists through institutional partnerships and donations (The Art Newspaper, Angella d'Avignon, 12 December 2024). The money goes directly to artists, mostly for basic needs, with some for art materials (Hyperallergic).
- The biennial site asks visitors to email the organisers to support the project or buy artworks, and links a Chuffed crowdfunding page for the New York pavilion (gazabiennale.org/support).
- The Istanbul pavilion's sponsor image lists House of Taswir, ad.dar, Unlimited, tuku, alpalab, EmedyaDesign, h.A.R.A.M and Al Risan Art Museum among its sponsors, and states it was made possible by family, friends, colleagues and artists (Depo Istanbul).

BOTTOM LINE
The Gaza Biennale aims to raise $90,000 for its artists through institutional partnerships and donations (The Art Newspaper), with the Al Risan Art Museum as sponsor and a Chuffed crowdfunding page for the New York pavilion. Its Istanbul pavilion lists House of Taswir, ad.dar, Unlimited, tuku, alpalab, EmedyaDesign, h.A.R.A.M and Al Risan Art Museum as sponsors.
