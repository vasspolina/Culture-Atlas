MUSEUM OF ART & PHOTOGRAPHY (MAP): Bengaluru, India
ID: museum-of-art-photography-map
Website: https://map-india.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Private art museum at 22 Kasturba Road in central Bengaluru, near Cubbon Park (MAP website).
- Founded in 2016 by the businessman and collector Abhishek Poddar (MAP website); it launched as a digital museum in 2020 and opened to the public on 18 February 2023 (Wikipedia).
- A unit and project of the Art & Photography Foundation, a not-for-profit (MAP website).
- The five-floor, 44,000 sq ft building was designed by Mathew & Ghosh Architects and has five galleries, an auditorium, a library, a restoration lab and a sculpture garden (Wikipedia). The land was bought through a donation from the Poddar family (USA Art News).
- USA Art News (July 2022) gave about 60,000 works, mostly donated by Poddar; Wikipedia now says over 20,000.
- Open Tuesday to Friday 10:00 to 18:30, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 to 19:30, closed Monday; entry is free on Tuesdays, other days by online "Access Pass".

FUNDING
Museum of Art & Photography: private philanthropy: the Poddar family, founding patrons and corporate donors
- MAP states that it receives no government funding and is governed by the foundation's trustees, an advisory board and an international advisory panel.
- The Poddar family donated the land and, with other philanthropists and companies, partly funded the building (USA Art News, July 2022).
- Poddar donated more than 7,000 artworks in 2017, and Christie's auctioned part of the family collection in December 2016, with proceeds used to establish the museum (Wikipedia, which carries a promotional-content banner).
- Founding patrons named on MAP's support page, undated: Avanee Foundation, Axis Bank, Bluespring, Citi, HBSF, HT Parekh Foundation, Infosys Foundation, Manipal Foundation, Mphasis F1 Foundation, RIST, Sasken Technologies, Tata Trusts, Torrent Group UNM Foundation and Wipro Foundation.
- USA Art News also names building donors Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, Sunil Munjal, Citi and Tata.
- Poddar is a trustee of the foundation; Wikipedia links him to the family businesses Matheson Bosanquet and Sua Explosives & Accessories, which makes and supplies mining explosives.
- No reporting found of donation amounts; the governance page names no trustees.

Red flags: Protest over the Venkatappa Art Gallery deal (2015 to 2016)
- In July 2015 (The News Minute gives June 2015) Karnataka's tourism department and its department of archaeology, museums and heritage signed a memorandum of understanding handing management of the government-owned Venkatappa Art Gallery to MAP, then a division of the Tasveer Foundation. Local artists learned of it in early 2016 (Hyperallergic).
- Artists formed the VAG Forum and held weekend actions in 2016, including a sit-in at Town Hall, a "Hug-VAG" day, a human chain and a day of art-making at the gallery; an online petition against the deal had over 1,000 signatures (Hyperallergic, 20 April 2016).
- Poddar replied that the initiative "is not in the realm of privatization nor is it a takeover" (Hyperallergic).
- On 12 May 2016 the artist Suresh Kumar said the government had not changed its stance (The News Minute). No reporting found of the final outcome of the memorandum.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: The criticism found is a single episode, the 2016 fight over the Venkatappa Art Gallery. No criticism found of the donors or of the museum since it opened in 2023.
1. Hyperallergic: Deepa Bhasthi, "Artists Protest Collector's Takeover of Indian Museum" (20 April 2016)
URL: https://hyperallergic.com/artists-protest-collectors-takeover-of-indian-museum/
- Reports artists' objection that the memorandum gives the foundation near-total control over programming, which could serve Poddar's collection and business interests.
- States that the Department of Kannada and Culture, which oversees the gallery, was not consulted, and that the Tasveer Foundation had no public website or known trustees.
- Quotes the artist Pushpamala N calling the deal a "sinister new precedent". The VAG Forum wanted the memorandum scrapped and welcomed MAP in Bengaluru but not at the gallery.
2. The News Minute: Sarayu Srinivasan, on the VAG dispute (12 May 2016)
URL: https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/no-answer-sight-bengalurus-artists-keep-questions-vag-43129
- Reports the government had not met any of the Forum's demands.
- MAP associate director Nathaniel Gaskell said rents would stay affordable and deserving artists could use the space free on some days. Unverified: Suresh Kumar of the VAG Forum said Poddar remarked that Tasveer should have a say in what is shown since it invested money; Kumar said nothing is in writing.
- News outlet, not art press.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is private and mostly named: the Poddar family's land and collection plus a list of founding patrons, though amounts are not published. The weak points are the 2016 deal to take over a public art gallery, which artists opposed, and the lack of named trustees on the museum's own page.
