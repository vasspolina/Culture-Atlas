S.M.A.K. (STEDELIJK MUSEUM VOOR ACTUELE KUNST) – Ghent, Belgium
Website: https://smak.be (news pages load normally, checked 2026-10-20; the about and corporate pages could not be fetched this run)

OVERVIEW
- The municipal museum of contemporary art in Ghent, founded by Jan Hoet (existing record). Philippe Van Cauteren is its artistic director. He has been named curator of the 2027 Art Encounters Biennial (e-flux).
- On 27 May 2026 the City of Ghent and the Flemish Government approved an agreement to turn S.M.A.K. into the Flemish museum for contemporary and current art from 1 January 2029. Until then there is a 2026–2028 transition period (Stad Gent; Belga).
- Under a wider Flemish museum reform, S.M.A.K. takes over the M HKA collection, and M HKA in Antwerp becomes an arts centre. The same reform scrapped a €130M subsidy for a new M HKA building (Belga).

FUNDING
S.M.A.K.: mainly public (City of Ghent, moving to Flanders), with corporate partners.
- City of Ghent: runs the museum now, through AGB Kunsten en Erfgoed. Flanders will take over the structural operating subsidy, which frees more than €3M a year for the city. Flanders is also paying a €950,000 transition budget (Stad Gent; Belga).
- ENGIE: with the ENGIE Foundation and CLW Ghent (now DUO²-Ghent), it has supported art workshops since 2012 for young people who have had difficult school experiences. The partnership placed third at the Belgian sponsorship awards in December 2021 (S.M.A.K.).
- National Lottery, Belfius, Jan De Nul, Interparking, Christie's, Hiscox and Duvel: named as partners in the existing record. Not re-checked this run, because the partner page was blocked and the search limit was reached.

Red flags: Energy-company sponsor
- ENGIE has sponsored youth workshops since 2012 (S.M.A.K., 19 Dec 2021). No reporting found of protests about this sponsorship.

BOTTOM LINE
A publicly funded museum moving from city to Flemish government funding by 2029. The only flagged sponsor is ENGIE's long-running support for youth workshops. Worth watching: the transfer and the takeover of the M HKA collection.
