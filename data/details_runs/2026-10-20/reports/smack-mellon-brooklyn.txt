SMACK MELLON: Brooklyn, USA
ID: smack-mellon-brooklyn
Website: https://smackmellon.org

OVERVIEW
- Nonprofit arts organisation at 92 Plymouth Street in DUMBO, Brooklyn, across from Brooklyn Bridge Park, that aims to "nurture and support emerging, under-recognized mid-career, and women artists" through exhibitions, studio space and technical help (Smack Mellon).
- Founded in 1995 by the artist Andrea Reynosa and the musician Kevin Vertrees in their loft at 135 Plymouth Street; Reynosa was the founding executive director (Wikipedia; Smack Mellon).
- From 1996 to 2004 it showed work in several DUMBO spaces donated by the Walentas family and Two Trees Management (Smack Mellon).
- Building: it moved to 92 Plymouth Street in October 2005. The 1880s structure, built by Robert Gair and designed by William Higginson, was a boiler house that supplied steam and power to nearby buildings; Sage & Coombe Architects converted it into gallery and studio space (Smack Mellon). The exhibition gallery is 6,000 square feet (Wikipedia).
- Programmes: exhibitions, an Artist Studio programme, public programmes and events, the Art Ready education programme and venue rental (Smack Mellon).
- Visiting: open Wednesday to Sunday, 12 to 6 pm; admission is free (Smack Mellon).

FUNDING
Smack Mellon: mixed public and private: city and state arts agencies, foundations and individual donors
- Form 990 data: FY2024 revenue was $735,010, of which $682,571 was contributions; expenses were $791,030. FY2023 contributions were $715,933 of $734,267 revenue (ProPublica).
- Government: New York City Department of Cultural Affairs, in partnership with the City Council and Councilmember Lincoln Restler; New York State Council on the Arts, with support from Governor Kathy Hochul and the New York State Legislature (Smack Mellon funders page).
- Foundations: Ruth Foundation for the Arts, the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Jerome Foundation, Jacques and Natasha Gelman Foundation, Lily Auchincloss Foundation, Wolf Kahn Foundation, Cornelia T Bailey Foundation, Robert Lehman Foundation, Milton and Sally Avery Arts Foundation, Select Equity Group Foundation and Roy and Niuta Titus Foundation (Smack Mellon funders page).
- Individuals: Bob Buckholz and Lizanne Fontaine, Carla Shen, Peter Coombe and Betty Chen, Sherri Marton and Robert Anderson, Stephen Sollins, Diana Wege, Heather Darcy Bhandari and an anonymous donor (Smack Mellon funders page).
- Space: the Walentas family and Two Trees Management provide space for programmes. The Downtown Brooklyn Partnership and Dumbo Improvement District are partners in the Downtown Brooklyn + Dumbo Art Fund grant, part of New York State's Downtown Revitalization Initiative (Smack Mellon funders page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Smack Mellon is mixed: contributions were $682,571 of $735,010 revenue in FY2024. Its funders page names New York City and New York State arts agencies, eleven foundations including the Warhol Foundation, individual donors, and the Walentas family and Two Trees Management, which provide space.
