WIDER CONTEXT
- In an open letter dated July 2024, nine experts of the Fondation Le Corbusier, among them Olivier Cinqualbre, Bruno Reichlin and Arthur Rüegg, objected to the very light grey (RAL 7037) given to the metal elements of the restored facade of the Immeuble Molitor at 24 rue Nungesser et Coli, the building whose top two floors hold the apartment-studio. They wrote that the tint was "proposed and then literally imposed by the project manager against the advice of the Fondation Le Corbusier and its experts" (Fondation Le Corbusier).
- The letter states "The Fondation Le Corbusier has not been listened to" in the restoration of a building on the World Heritage List of which it is "part owner and manager", and blames the urgency of removing the scaffolding before the Olympic Games (Fondation Le Corbusier).
- Introducing the letter, Fondation president Antoine Picon wrote that the work, "in particular the choice of colors, significantly alters the known state of the facade", and said work would resume in the autumn on the Boulogne-side facade at 23 rue de la Tourelle (Fondation Le Corbusier).
