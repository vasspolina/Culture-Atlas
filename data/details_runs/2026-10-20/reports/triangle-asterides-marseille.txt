TRIANGLE-ASTÉRIDES, CENTRE D'ART CONTEMPORAIN: Marseille, France
ID: triangle-asterides-marseille
Website: https://triangle-asterides.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre of national interest and international artist residency at the Friche la Belle de Mai, 41 rue Jobin, 13003 Marseille; the exhibitions are on the 4th floor and the office and artists' studios on the 2nd (Triangle-Astérides website).
- Founded by artists between 1992 and 1994 (e-flux directory); TransArtists gives 1994. It was created through the Triangle Network, remains a member, and merged the associations Triangle France and Astérides in 2018. It is one of the founding organisations of the Friche la Belle de Mai cultural cooperative (e-flux directory).
- It hosts exhibitions and research residencies for French and international artists plus local associated artists (e-flux directory). A residency listing on TransArtists offers an individual room in a 90 m² apartment and a 60 m² studio, a fee of 1,000 euros paid by the host, and studio visits and a public studio opening.
- Team: Victorine Grataloup (director), Florence Gosset (administrative and financial director) and Camille Ramanana Rahary (head of exhibitions and residency programmes). The board is chaired by Marianne Berger-Laleix of the Palais de Tokyo and includes Alice Malinge, Maxime Guitton, Saïd Berkane and the artist Claude Eigan (Triangle-Astérides website).
- Ex officio representatives of the public funders also sit on the board: the regional director of cultural affairs, the presidents of the regional council and the departmental council, and the deputy mayor of Marseille for culture (Triangle-Astérides website).
- Visiting: exhibitions are open Wednesday to Sunday, 14:00 to 19:00; tickets cost 8 euros, or 5 euros reduced, and are free for visitors under 18, students, job seekers and journalists. Step-free access is by lift, with the code from the Friche reception, and sign language tours are offered for each exhibition (Triangle-Astérides website).

FUNDING
Triangle-Astérides: public: Ministry of Culture (DRAC), City of Marseille, Région Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and Bouches-du-Rhône Department
- Public funders listed on its partners page: Ville de Marseille, Ministère de la Culture (DRAC Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur), Région Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and Département des Bouches-du-Rhône.
- France Relance and the EU Next Generation programme co-funded the purchase of IT, recording and broadcast equipment (partners page).
- Programming partners for 2025 include La Friche la Belle de Mai, the Canadian Embassy in Paris and Canadian Cultural Centre, Villa Médicis, Fondation Camargo and the museum [mac] of the City of Marseille.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Triangle-Astérides is French public funding: the Ministry of Culture through DRAC Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, the City of Marseille, Région Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and the Bouches-du-Rhône Department, whose representatives sit on its board.
