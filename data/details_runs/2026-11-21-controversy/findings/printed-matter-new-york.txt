Red flags: 2024 petition asking Printed Matter to join the cultural boycott of Israel
- At the 2024 New York Art Book Fair, many participants circulated a petition urging the organiser, Printed Matter, to support the Palestinian Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of Israel (PACBI) (It's Nice That, Elizabeth Goodspeed, "Elizabeth Goodspeed on the best bits of the New York Art Book Fair", 7 May 2024).
- Unverified: the petition says it was created by the artists' book community to amplify "the Printed Matter union's demands that were rejected internally in February", and asks the board and director to reconsider the decision against joining PACBI, from the petition's own page.
