WIDER CONTEXT
- A 13th panel of the "Gypsy Girl" mosaic, identified by researchers from Grenoble Alpes University and the Zeugma excavation director Kutalmış Görkay, had been sold and changed hands in the United States; ministry experts monitoring international auctions found it listed for sale again, Turkey renewed its restitution request, and the panel was returned after an investigation by U.S. Homeland Security Investigations (Daily Sabah, 15 July 2026).
- The ministry announced the return on 25 May 2026; the panel went first to the Anatolian Civilizations Museum in Ankara for conservation and documentation (Daily Sabah, 26 May 2026). It was handed over at a ceremony at the Zeugma Mosaic Museum on 14 July 2026 (Daily Sabah, 15 July 2026).
