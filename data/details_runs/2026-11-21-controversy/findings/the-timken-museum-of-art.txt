ID: the-timken-museum-of-art

WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 2014 the board of trustees replaced executive director John Wilson, who "officially, and abruptly, resigned", with the art conservator David Bull, based in New York City, as visiting director, giving 20% of his time to the museum; trustee Anita Crider took over day-to-day operations (KPBS, Angela Carone, 17 July 2014, via inewsource).
- Board president Tim Zinn said Wilson was a great curator but the board wanted a different fundraising and administrative approach (KPBS, via inewsource). Voice of San Diego (Kelly Bennett, 29 December 2014) reported that the board decided not to renew Wilson's contract and that Zinn declined to talk about the decision; in the same piece he said: "We're looking at things from a business perspective."
- Hugh Davies, director of the Museum of Contemporary Art San Diego, objected: "I can only think it comes from the leadership of the board," and "David Bull will not be able to raise money" (KPBS, via inewsource).
- Wilson said: "The board is ultimately the governing body and they made a conscious decision of the direction they want to move forward. I totally respect that." (Voice of San Diego)
