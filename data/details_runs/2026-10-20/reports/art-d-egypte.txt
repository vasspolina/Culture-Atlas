ART D'ÉGYPTE: Giza, Egypt
ID: art-d-egypte
Website: https://artdegypte.org (checked 2026-10-20; the www address fails with a certificate error, the bare domain loads)

OVERVIEW
- Cairo firm that calls itself privately owned and produces art events, best known for Forever Is Now, an annual contemporary art exhibition on the Giza Pyramids plateau.
- Founded by French-Egyptian curator and former art advisor Nadine Abdel Ghaffar in 2016 (designboom; a 2020 BMW press release gives 2017).
- She launched Forever Is Now in 2021 and Culturvator in 2023; the firm now trades as "Art D'Égypte by Culturvator" (designboom, Flair).
- It shows work at the Giza plateau, the Egyptian Museum, Bait Al Suheimy in Historic Cairo and Downtown Cairo venues (Flair); no reporting found of a building of its own.
- Other programmes: Cairo International Art District (sixth edition, 11 October to 8 November 2026), "Formed by Dialogue" at the Egyptian Museum and "Crafts Re-Imagined" (Flair).
- Forever Is Now 06 runs 4 to 28 November 2026 with eight artists, five of them women (Flair); the site shows three team members and no board.

FUNDING
Art D'Égypte: corporate-sponsored under state auspices: LMD, Suez Canal Bank, BMW, with Egypt's ministries and UNESCO
- State auspices: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities and Experience Egypt for 2026, with the Ministry of Culture's auspices in 2024; the exhibition has UNESCO's patronage (Flair, Daily News Egypt).
- 2026 sponsors: LMD (main sponsor and real estate partner, second year), Suez Canal Bank (gold sponsor and banking partner), BMW (automotive partner), Vodafone, EgyptAir, DHL, Signify, Nestlé, Orascom Pyramids Entertainment and the Sawiris Foundation for Social Development (Flair).
- 2024 partners: Alex Bank, Afreximbank, TotalEnergies, BICC Cables, DHL, EgyptAir, the Bibliotheca Alexandrina and the Sawiris Foundation (Daily News Egypt).
- BMW Group has partnered with Art D'Égypte's annual exhibition since its second edition in 2018 (BMW Group press release, November 2020).
- No reporting found of sponsorship amounts, accounts or the firm's ownership beyond its founder.

Red flags: TotalEnergies as 2024 partner
- TotalEnergies was a named partner of the fourth edition, 24 October to 16 November 2024 (Daily News Egypt).
- No reporting found of TotalEnergies in the 2026 partner list (Flair).

Red flags: boycott over Bernar Venet, October 2026
- Galleries suspended their participation and artists withdrew over the inclusion of French artist Bernar Venet, citing his 2022 "Artist of the Year" award in Israel and his Tel Aviv gallery, Har-El Printers & Publishers (Bab Masr).
- A petition gathered nearly 700 signatories, including Monica Hanna and Sarah El Masry of Mezj for Culture and Arts (Bab Masr).
- Cairo Gallery, Zahwa Gallery and Clegg Gallery suspended participation; artists Alia Issa and Mohamed Abdel Rahman withdrew (Bab Masr).
- Art D'Égypte first announced a review, then withdrew Venet's invitation, citing "its unwavering alignment with Egypt's position on the Palestinian cause" (Bab Masr).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Art D'Égypte is a founder's private firm carried by corporate sponsors and state auspices, with no published accounts or amounts. The weak points are a fossil fuel partner in 2024 and the October 2026 boycott that forced Venet out. Worth watching: whether TotalEnergies returns and whether the firm ever publishes what its sponsors pay.
