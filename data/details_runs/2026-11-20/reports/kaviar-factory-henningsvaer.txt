KAVIARFACTORY: Henningsvær, Norway
ID: kaviar-factory-henningsvaer
Website: https://kaviarfactory.com (checked 2026-11-20; serves spam: casino, betting and pharmacy text in several languages beside the venue's name)

OVERVIEW
- A private kunsthall for contemporary art in a former caviar factory, a concrete building from the 1950s on the edge of the sea in the fishing village of Henningsvær in Lofoten (Arterritory).
- The Oslo collectors Venke and Rolf Hoff acquired the building after the cannery closed in 2006 (Arterritory); it opened on 9 June 2013 (Visit Lofoten).
- The façade sign is a permanent work by the German artist Michael Sailstorfer, with the letters ART missing from KAVIARFACTORY, and the door handles are by Bjarne Melgaard (Arterritory, Visit Lofoten).
- The conversion won the Nordnorsk arkitekturpris in 2020 (Visit Lofoten).
- It shows works from The Hoff Collection and site-specific projects, among them an Ai Weiwei solo show in 2021 to 2022 that the artist extended from 3 to 15 months (Chart), and Yoko Ono's Onochord, sent from the couple's Henningsvær lighthouse for six weeks from 20 January 2018 (Arterritory, Chart).
- Rolf Hoff headed the board of the Lofoten International Art Festival for four years (Arterritory).
- Visiting: a shop with books and wool goods, and parking for guests (Visit Lofoten).

FUNDING
KaviarFactory: private: the Hoff family's own money, plus ticket and shop sales
- It is operated by KaviarFactory Drift AS, registered in Henningsvær on 21 May 2013 to run a kunsthall for contemporary art with a shop and café; Venke Hoff chairs the board and Rolf Hoff is the deputy board member (Brønnøysund Register).
- Accounts for 2025: operating income NOK 2.87M, of which NOK 2.6M sales, against costs of NOK 2.73M, a profit of NOK 108,762; in 2023 it made a loss of NOK 510,985 (Regnskapsregisteret).
- Venke Hoff says she bought the run-down factory herself, without concrete plans, because she felt "it should belong to the art world" (Arterritory).
- The Hoffs say being private lets them buy quickly: "For us, it is a discussion over dinner, and not a decision to be made by a committee" (Chart).
- Rolf Hoff's career led him to make signage, and producing works for artists has become a bigger part of his business (Arterritory).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: private collector money and the space's own sales, with no flagged source.
