THE FREEDOM THEATRE: Jenin, Palestine
ID: the-freedom-theatre
Website: https://thefreedomtheatre.org

OVERVIEW
- Theatre and youth arts centre in the Jenin refugee camp in the West Bank. It is registered as a nonprofit under the Palestinian Authority and as a foundation in Sweden, has a Palestinian team and an international advisory board (NPR).
- Roots: the Stone Theatre, a community theatre started in the camp by Israeli peace activist Arna Mer Khamis in 1987 (NPR). Her son Juliano Mer Khamis, Zakaria Zubeidi and Jonatan Stanczak opened the Freedom Theatre in 2006 (Wikipedia).
- An Israeli military operation destroyed the Stone Theatre in 2002 (NPR). Wikipedia states the original theatre sat on the top floor of a family house, destroyed by an Israeli bulldozer during the 2002 Battle of Jenin.
- Juliano Mer Khamis was shot dead by masked gunmen near the theatre on 4 April 2011; the murder remains unsolved (Wikipedia, NPR).
- Programmes: theatre, child and youth work, a theatre school, multimedia, the Freedom Bus and Voices Magazine (the theatre's website).
- On the night of 12 to 13 December 2023 Israeli soldiers raided the theatre, ransacked its offices and spray-painted a Star of David on a wall and a screening-room screen. Director Ahmed Tobasi was held about 14 hours and released, and Ismael Hussam Ibrahim, 25, was arrested and freed the next day (Al Jazeera, Mauricio Morales, 20 December 2023). An Israeli military spokesperson said the raid was part of "counterterrorism activity" in the camp and that those deemed uninvolved were released within days (NPR, Lauren Frayer, 28 January 2024).
- General manager Mustafa Sheta was taken from his home on 13 December 2023, held in administrative detention ordered by an Israeli military court without charge, and released on 30 March 2025 (PEN International).

FUNDING
The Freedom Theatre: foreign donor-funded: NGO and government donors named in its 2013 annual report, plus individual monthly donors
- The 2013 annual report names the Ford Foundation, Sida, AFAC, Medico International and the European Commission as major donors (the theatre's 2013 annual report summary).
- British Council: a £74,976 grant in March 2022, and grants worth tens of thousands of pounds in 2015 and January 2021 (The Jewish Chronicle, David Rose, 19 May 2022). The British Council told the paper the grants were awarded through a competitive open call and comply with government policy.
- The Jewish Chronicle (partisan) states that the British Council is funded by the Foreign Office and that the theatre backs the BDS movement, and says founder Zakaria Zubeidi led the al-Aqsa Martyrs Brigades. Conservative MP Robert Halfon called the funding "pretty shocking" and urged the government to act on its pledge to stop publicly funded bodies supporting BDS. The paper says it approached the theatre for comment.
- Individual donors: the theatre says that if 1,000 people gave 20 USD or EUR a month it could cover its entire running costs. Donations go through MECA (US), BSST (UK) and a bank account at Cairo-Amman Bank in Jenin.
- Donor clauses: in a letter signed by Sheta, posted on 9 August 2023, the theatre states that "a small percentage of our NGO donors also were forced to add a clause within their donation agreements" which it says undermines Palestinian self-determination, linked to new EU restrictive measures (stated by the theatre, posted by Nordic Black Theatre, 9 August 2023).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Freedom Theatre is foreign grants (the Ford Foundation, Sida, AFAC, Medico International and the European Commission named for 2013, British Council grants to 2022) and individual donors. The documented hostile actions against it are Israeli military raids and arrests, including the December 2023 raid, and the unsolved 2011 killing of Juliano Mer Khamis.
