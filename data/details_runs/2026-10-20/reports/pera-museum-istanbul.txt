PERA MUSEUM: Istanbul, Turkey
ID: pera-museum-istanbul
Website: https://www.peramuzesi.org.tr

OVERVIEW
- Private art museum in the Beyoğlu district of Istanbul, near İstiklal Avenue and Taksim Square (Wikipedia).
- Founded by the Suna and İnan Kıraç Foundation and established on 8 June 2005 (Wikipedia).
- Housed in the former Bristol Hotel, built in 1893 to designs by Achille Manoussos; architect Sinan Genim renovated it from 2003 to 2005 and kept the facade (Wikipedia).
- Permanent collections are Orientalist painting, including Osman Hamdi Bey, Anatolian weights and measures, and Kütahya tiles and ceramics (Wikipedia). The museum's website highlights four collection exhibitions.
- The museum also shows temporary exhibitions, Pera Film screenings, concerts and publications.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 19:00, with late opening to 22:00 on Fridays, and Sunday 12:00 to 18:00; closed Monday. Full ticket 450 TL, discounted 250 TL, groups of ten or more 300 TL, and students enter free on Wednesdays.
- The museum offers a Turkish Sign Language tour of its collection exhibitions (25 October 2026, 14:00) and has a page for disabled visitors.

FUNDING
Pera Museum (Istanbul): private: founded by a family foundation, the Suna and İnan Kıraç Foundation
- The Suna and İnan Kıraç Foundation was established on 27 October 2003 by Suna Kıraç, İnan Kıraç and İpek Kıraç, and works in education, healthcare, culture and art; it carries out its arts work through the Pera Museum and the Istanbul Research Institute (Pera Museum's page on the foundation).
- The museum's website credits the foundation and the Istanbul Research Institute in the page footer.
- The foundation's corporate page names İnan Kıraç as chair and İpek Kıraç as a founding member of its board.
- Background on the founders: Suna Kıraç was the daughter of Vehbi Koç and worked at Koç Holding from 1961 to 2010, leaving as Vice Chair of its board; İnan Kıraç was chief executive of the Koç Group from 1994 to 1998 and then founded Kıraça Holding; İpek Kıraç has been a member of the Koç Holding board since 2016 (the foundation's corporate page, Wikipedia).
- The Getty Foundation funded the foundation's digitisation of Millet Manuscript Library manuscripts from 2005 to 2007 (Pera Museum's page on the foundation).

BOTTOM LINE
The Pera Museum is a private museum founded by the Suna and İnan Kıraç Foundation, set up in 2003 by Suna, İnan and İpek Kıraç, which has long ties to the Koç Group. Its own website and the foundation's corporate page document the founders and chair.
