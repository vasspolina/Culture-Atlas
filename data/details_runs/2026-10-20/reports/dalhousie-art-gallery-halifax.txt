DALHOUSIE ART GALLERY: Halifax, Canada
ID: dalhousie-art-gallery-halifax
Website: https://artgallery.dal.ca/

OVERVIEW
- University art gallery at 6101 University Avenue, Halifax, in the Dalhousie Arts Centre of Dalhousie University (stated by the gallery; Canadian Encyclopedia).
- Began in October 1953 as one room in the Arts and Administration Building, run by faculty volunteers, growing out of an informal university art group; it moved to the Dalhousie Arts Centre in November 1971 (Dal News, Kevin Bradley, 3 December 2014). The Canadian Encyclopedia calls it the oldest public art gallery in Halifax and says it has had full-time professional staff since 1972.
- Building: the Dalhousie Arts Centre is in a pseudo-brutalist style of raw concrete walls, and replaced a row of Edwardian houses (Dal News, 2014).
- Collection: historical and contemporary works from Canada and abroad with an emphasis on Atlantic Canadian art, including Inuit sculpture and Canadian works on paper; the Richard and Jocelyn Raymond Graphic Art Collection (1910 to 1950) and the Carnegie Collection of Historic Prints (Canadian Encyclopedia).
- Programme: free exhibitions, lectures, films and artist presentations, publications, and a Butterfly Garden; the 71st Annual Student, Staff, Faculty and Alumni Exhibition runs 27 November to 20 December 2026.
- Visiting: free admission. Open Wednesday and Friday 11am to 5pm, Thursday 11am to 8pm, weekends noon to 5pm.

FUNDING
Dalhousie Art Gallery: university gallery with public grants: Dalhousie University, Canada Council for the Arts, Canadian Heritage
- The gallery is part of Dalhousie University.
- Canada Council for the Arts: $165,000 in fiscal year 2024-25, fifth among the highest-funded Nova Scotia organisations (Canada Council).
- Canadian Heritage, Museum Assistance Program (Collections Management): a $15,000 grant to Dalhousie University, Dalhousie Art Gallery, from 9 June to 1 December 2025, for digitising and migrating the permanent collection database (Government of Canada grants search).

BOTTOM LINE
Dalhousie Art Gallery is a university gallery supported by Dalhousie University, with documented public grants of $165,000 from the Canada Council (2024-25) and $15,000 from Canadian Heritage (2025). Admission is free.
