ALICE YARD: Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago
ID: alice-yard-port-of-spain
Website: https://aliceyard.blogspot.com (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Artist-run contemporary art space and network in Port of Spain; since 2020 it has been based at Granderson Lab, 24 Erthig Road, Belmont (stated by the space).
- Began in September 2006 during "Galvanise", a six-week art and performance programme (Caribbean Beat); the space's own site says people were first welcomed on 15 September 2006. A non-profit incorporated under the law of Trinidad and Tobago (stated by the space).
- The yard was the backyard of Alice, the great-grandmother of the architect Sean Leonard (Caribbean Beat).
- Original site: a paved yard behind an old house at 80 Roberts Street, Woodbrook. Leonard is described there as the custodian of the property (Caribbean Beat). The "Alice Yard Space" exhibition room, a box-like room with a glass front, followed in September 2007 (Caribbean Beat).
- Run and curated by Sean Leonard, the artist Christopher Cozier, the writer and editor Nicholas Laughlin, the designer Kriston Chen and the artist Shannon Alonzo, with a changing group of collaborators (stated by the space).
- On site and online: residencies since 2008, exhibitions, talks, screenings, the UN|FOLD small-press fair (launched 2024 with SPEC*), a shop with limited editions and design objects (stated by the space). It took part in documenta fifteen in Kassel in 2022, where it hosted a residency programme for the 100 days of the show (Artviewer, 3 September 2022).
- Visiting: events are announced on the blog. A Caribbean Beat profile of the Woodbrook yard said events were open to the public with no cover charge.

FUNDING
Alice Yard: mixed small funding: a US foundation grant plus project support from Nordic and local bodies and two named local firms
- Mellon Foundation: $150,000 over 36 months, awarded 22 February 2024, as unrestricted funding for the organisation's general operations (Mellon Foundation grant record).
- The space's site says Alice Yard and its programmes are supported by co-rd Ltd. and Basso Leonard Architects Collaborative Ltd. (stated by the space).
- The Nordic Culture Fund and OCA supported Waldane Walker's residency, in a post of March 2026 (stated by the space).
- Trinbagonians for Palestine, fLim club and IGDS IGNITE supported the screening of "A State of Passion" in September 2025 (stated by the space).
- Drinks are sold at an ad-hoc bar at events (Caribbean Beat).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Wikipedia's documenta fifteen page describes an antisemitism controversy: in January 2022 the blog Alliance Against Anti-Semitism Kassel made claims of ties to the BDS movement against documenta; a forum planned for May was cancelled on 4 May; the show opened on 18 June 2022; a mural was covered and taken down after the opening; and general director Sabine Schormann resigned on 17 July 2022.
- Connection to Alice Yard: the Wikipedia page names Alice Yard once, in its list of participants, with no further context, and does not name it in the controversy. Artviewer (3 September 2022) says Alice Yard hosted a residency programme for the 100 days of the show.

BOTTOM LINE
Alice Yard is a small non-profit funded by a Mellon Foundation operating grant awarded in February 2024, project support from the Nordic Culture Fund, OCA and local groups, and two named local firms, co-rd Ltd. and Basso Leonard Architects Collaborative Ltd. None of the named funders falls in a flagged industry. Worth watching: the Mellon grant's 36-month term and who supports the space after it.
