FONDATION PIERRE GIANADDA: Martigny, Switzerland
ID: fondation-pierre-gianadda
Website: https://www.gianadda.ch/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Museum and cultural centre at Rue du Forum 59 in Martigny, Valais, with temporary exhibitions, a Gallo-Roman museum, a car museum, a sculpture park and a concert season (foundation website; Swiss commercial register).
- Founded by the engineer Léonard Gianadda in memory of his brother Pierre, who died in a plane crash on 31 July 1976; the deed was signed on 24 February 1977 and the museum opened on 19 November 1978, Pierre's 40th birthday (foundation website, history page).
- Site: in June 1976 a Gallo-Roman temple was found where Léonard planned a 16-storey tower block; the ruins are shown in the central space (foundation website). The building is in Gard stone and concrete, in a 7,000-square-metre park (Universalis).
- What is there: about 50 vintage cars from 1897 to 1939 in the basement and, since the 1990s, 47 outdoor sculptures owned by the foundation (Universalis). The Vieil Arsenal was converted in 1995 and 1996 (foundation website).
- Over 45 years Léonard Gianadda organised 180 exhibitions seen by nearly 11 million visitors, and 46 music seasons (swissinfo, Keystone-ATS). He died on 3 December 2023 aged 88 (swissinfo).
- Leadership: on 19 November 2025 the commercial register recorded François Gianadda as president of the board, with Anouck Darioli and Jean-Henry Papilloud as vice-presidents (Swiss commercial register). Papilloud told Keystone-SDA that Léonard Gianadda had chosen the two vice-presidents, already involved in management in his lifetime (The Swiss Times, 1 February 2024). The board also includes Valais state councillor Mathias Reynard and Martigny's city president Anne-Laure Couchepin Vouilloz (foundation website).
- Oversight: the canton of Valais's Department of Security, Institutions and Sport, the supervisory authority, amended the statutes on 25 September 2023, ordered on 2 May 2024 that the two co-vice-presidents sign jointly until the board met, and lifted that provisional entry on 9 July 2025 (Swiss commercial register).
- Season: "Rodin selon Rilke", organised by the musée Rodin, Paris, ran from 26 June to 22 November 2026 (foundation website).
- Visiting: open daily 10:00 to 18:00; adults 20 francs, students under 25 and children from 10 lower rates; an audio and 3D tour for visitors with visual impairments (foundation website).

FUNDING
Fondation Pierre Gianadda: founder-built foundation, run on its own income and sponsors, with public officials on its board
- Founder's money: Léonard Gianadda gave the land and paid for almost all of the building (foundation website, history page). Le Temps reported that he gave more than 110 million francs in patronage over his life, the foundation among it (Le Temps, 4 December 2023; paywalled; only excerpts were read).
- Status: the foundation calls itself an institution of public law whose board sets the annual budget "sous réserve d'approbation par les autorités subventionnantes" (subject to approval by the subsidising authorities; translation) (foundation website).
- Canton: the foundation provides space to the canton's museums service for Roman finds from Martigny, the Gallo-Roman museum (foundation website).
- Ticket and shop income: paid admission, subscriptions, a shop and a restaurant (foundation website).
- Friends: the Amis de la Fondation give from 250 francs a year for a transferable two-person pass; their support is credited in the catalogues (foundation website).
- Sponsors: the partners page lists UBS, Banque Raiffeisen, the Fondation Philanthropique Famille Sandoz, the Fondation Coromandel, TCS, Groupe Mutuel, Le Nouvelliste, Henniez (Nestlé Waters), Christie's, Pommery, Laboratoires Théa, Caves Orsat et Domaines Rouvinez, Matériaux Plus, Buser Matériaux and Veuthey & Cie (foundation website, partners page).
- School projects: Les Petits Visiteurs and Les Apprentis Visiteurs are backed by Banque Raiffeisen Martigny Région and Etincelles de Culture (foundation website).
- Sister bodies: the Fondation Annette et Léonard Gianadda (2009, social aims) and the Fondation Léonard Gianadda Mécénat (signed 23 August 2019), set up to continue his patronage (foundation website).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean: founder's money, earned income, and corporate and foundation sponsors. Worth watching is how the foundation fares under its new board after Léonard Gianadda's death.
