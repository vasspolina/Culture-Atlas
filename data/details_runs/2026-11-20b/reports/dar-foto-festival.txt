DAR FOTO FESTIVAL: Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
ID: dar-foto-festival
Website: https://www.darfotofestival.com (checked 2026-11-20; down, returns 502 Bad Gateway)

OVERVIEW
- Annual photography festival in Dar es Salaam with exhibitions, portfolio reviews, street and documentary photo walks and talks with professionals (PH Museum).
- Founded by the Tanzanian photographer Andrew Stephen Munuwa, its Creative Director; the German photographer and curator Valentin Rudloff has been co-founder since 2023 (Humboldt Forum; The Citizen).
- The festival's own About page said Munuwa founded it in 2021 in the living room of his home in South Dar es Salaam (festival website, archive copy of April 2025); the Humboldt Forum gives 2020 and The Citizen says it launched in 2023.
- Munuwa is an independent photographer and curator from Dar es Salaam whose work centres on daily urban life in African cities (Humboldt Forum).
- 2024 edition, which The Citizen calls the second: 2 February to 8 March 2024 at the Old Boma building in Posta, theme "People, Economy, Change", 12 Tanzanian artists, more than 800 visitors, followed by a show at the Emerson Art Gallery in Stone Town, Zanzibar, from 21 June to 21 July 2024 (The Citizen, Ramadhani Ismail, 15 June 2024).
- 2025 edition: "Coexistence", 31 January to 1 March 2025 in Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar, with 15 artists from Congo, Sudan, Kenya and Tanzania (Art Africa Magazine; Musée Magazine).
- In 2024 the festival staged a pop-up presentation on coexistence at the Humboldt Forum in Berlin under its "Histories of Tanzania" programme, with work by Natalia Eric Musungu, Sam Vox, Eric Lugakingira, Vanessa Mwingira and a Dar es Salaam art collective (Humboldt Forum; Art Africa Magazine).

FUNDING
Dar Foto Festival: founder-led and self-produced through Tanzania Foto Collective Company Limited
- The festival is produced by Tanzania Foto Collective Company Limited, which describes itself as a creative enterprise "crafting bespoke content" in photography (festival website, archive copy of April 2025).
- Munuwa said in 2025 that the festival has no space of its own and rents a venue for every event; he named a permanent gallery, scholarships, residencies and funding programmes for photographers as long-term aims (Art Africa Magazine interview).
- Kedmon Mapana, Executive Secretary of BASATA, Tanzania's National Arts Council, said at the 2024 opening that "BASATA is keen to support the Dar Foto Festival in the coming years" (The Citizen).
- The Humboldt Forum in Berlin hosted the 2024 pop-up presentation as part of its own programme (Humboldt Forum).
- Munuwa said some of the artists' work shown in Berlin sold and the money went back into their practice (Art Africa Magazine interview).

BOTTOM LINE
Clean but thin: a small founder-run festival whose only institutional backing on record is a spoken pledge from Tanzania's arts council and one hosted show in Berlin.
