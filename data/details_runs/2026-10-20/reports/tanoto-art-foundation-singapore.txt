TANOTO ART FOUNDATION: Singapore, Singapore
ID: tanoto-art-foundation-singapore
Website: https://www.tanotoartfoundation.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Private, not-for-profit art foundation in Singapore for contemporary art, "one of Singapore's first private art foundations" (stated by the foundation); its address is UOB Plaza 1, 80 Raffles Place (e-flux directory).
- Launched in January 2025 with a symposium, "Soul Song of a New Organisation", on 14 January 2025 at the School of the Arts, Singapore, during Singapore Art Week (e-flux).
- Belinda Tanoto and Anderson Tanoto are the trustees; Xiaoyu Weng is artistic director (e-flux). Belinda Tanoto, daughter of the Indonesian tycoon Sukanto Tanoto, founded it as a not-for-profit (ART SG, Payal Uttam, 6 November 2025).
- ART SG reports it intends to open a permanent space for research, residencies and exhibitions.
- Programme: a Conversation Series held in Singapore, Sao Paulo, Hong Kong and Gwangju; support for commissions at the Singapore Biennale 2025 (ART SG, stated by the foundation).
- First exhibition: "Rituals of Perception", 21 January to 1 March 2026 at New Bahru School Hall, curated by Weng, with more than twenty artists; several works come from the Tanoto Family Collection; weekly public programmes are free (e-flux).

FUNDING
Tanoto Art Foundation: family-funded by the Tanoto family; the foundation publishes no funders or accounts
- The trustees are children of Sukanto Tanoto, who owns the Royal Golden Eagle group (RGE): paper, palm oil, viscose, construction, energy and property (Wikipedia).
- Works in its first exhibition come from the Tanoto Family Collection (e-flux).
- No reporting found of the foundation's funding sources, amounts, donors, annual report or accounts.

Red flags: Fossil fuels (family business)
- RGE's energy arm Pacific Energy, formerly Pacific Oil & Gas, explores for gas in Canada and also holds an LNG terminal and a gas power plant in China (Wikipedia).
- Nothing read states that this business pays the foundation. The link is the family: the foundation's trustees are the owner's children, and no other funder is named.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media. Coverage found is launch and exhibition announcements and one profile on ART SG's own site.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Greenpeace's 2013 report called Sukanto Tanoto's business empire "the single largest driver of deforestation in the world identified by Greenpeace"; APRIL denies the claims and pledged in June 2015 to end deforestation in its supply chain (Wikipedia).
- 30 October 2024: ICIJ and The Gecko Project reported that seven of nine former employees said the Tanoto family controlled Borneo Hijau Lestari, a pulpwood group linked to major deforestation. Unverified: the ownership claim, from former employees and Greenpeace; RGE calls it "unfounded, unsupported and untrue" (ICIJ).
- 8 February 2021: OCCRP reported that Tanoto bought the Ludwig complex in Munich in July 2019 for about 348 million euros through Luxembourg and offshore companies; Indonesia's financial intelligence head said his agency was investigating tax issues involving Tanoto's companies (OCCRP).
- None of these pieces names the foundation; the connection is that the foundation's trustees are the family's children.

BOTTOM LINE
The foundation does not say where its money comes from, and the only link read is the family that owns RGE, including a gas business and pulp and palm oil companies under deforestation allegations. No document reviewed ties a specific payment to the foundation. Worth watching: any donor list or accounts, and the planned permanent space.
