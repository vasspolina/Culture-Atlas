TOWER OF DAVID MUSEUM: Jerusalem, Israel
ID: tower-of-david-museum
Website: https://www.tod.org.il (checked 2026-11-20; refused the automated check with a 403 error)

OVERVIEW
- Museum of the history of Jerusalem in the citadel by Jaffa Gate in the Old City (Associated Press; Wikipedia).
- Opened in 1989, founded by the Jerusalem Foundation and initiated by the mayor of the time, Teddy Kollek (Wikipedia; Jewish Chronicle, 1 June 2023).
- The citadel was rebuilt by the Mamluk sultan al-Nasir Muhammad in 1310 and expanded by Suleiman the Magnificent between 1537 and 1541; the minaret that gives the site its name was built between 1635 and 1655 (Wikipedia).
- The museum reopened in the week of 1 June 2023 after a three-year renewal that restored the 400-year-old minaret and added excavations and new exhibits (Jewish Chronicle; Associated Press, 29 May 2023).
- A new sunken entrance, the Angelina Drahi Entrance Pavilion by Kimmel Eshkolot Architects, added 1,000 square metres; about 17 metres were excavated for it because city rules bar building above the height of the Old City wall (Jerusalem Post, 9 March 2024).
- On show: the Illes model of Jerusalem made for the 1873 Vienna World Fair, found in a Geneva University Library attic in 1984; newly found Hasmonean-period towers and a Crusader tunnel (Jewish Chronicle).
- The renovation drew on the 1930s excavation diary and photographs of the archaeologist Cedric Norman Johns (Jewish Chronicle). The director and chief curator is Eilat Lieber (Times of Israel).
- Since July 2025 the museum has run weekly group tours of the Rockefeller Museum, at 100 shekels a ticket; over 1,000 people joined in the first three months (Times of Israel, 13 December 2025).

FUNDING
Tower of David Museum: mixed: its own income, philanthropy led by the Clore Israel Foundation, and city and state money for the renewal
- Running costs: the museum is over 84 per cent self-funded (Jewish Chronicle, 1 June 2023).
- Renewal: a 50 million dollar project (Associated Press; Jerusalem Post), announced at 40 million dollars when work began in October 2020 (Blooloop). It was led by Dame Vivien Duffield through the Clore Israel Foundation, with the support of the City of Jerusalem, the Ministry of Jerusalem and Heritage and the Israel Ministry of Tourism (Jerusalem Post; Blooloop).
- Duffield and the Clore Israel Foundation also supported the museum's creation in 1989 (Jewish Chronicle).
- Entrance pavilion: paid for by the Lina and Patrick Drahi Foundation (Jerusalem Post, 9 March 2024).
- Rockefeller tours: the Israel Antiquities Authority opens the building and the Jerusalem municipality pays for the shuttle bus (Times of Israel).

WIDER CONTEXT
- Status of the site: Israel captured East Jerusalem, including the Old City, from Jordan in the 1967 war and annexed it, a step that is not internationally recognised; Palestinians seek East Jerusalem as the capital of a future state (Associated Press). The museum stands inside the Old City.
- Rockefeller Museum: a December 2023 document, confirmed by a Heritage Ministry spokesperson, lists selling or leasing the Rockefeller Museum to private entities for a hotel as an option. David Gurevich of Ariel University, a member of Israel's Archaeology Council, said there is an attempt to turn it into a luxury hotel (Times of Israel, 13 December 2025).
- Lieber said her museum's interest in the Rockefeller is historical and cultural, and the museum says it takes no part in the discussions about that building's future (Times of Israel). The dispute concerns the Rockefeller, where the Tower of David Museum only runs tours.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum earns most of its running costs, and its renewal was led by the Clore Israel Foundation, with the Drahi foundation, the city and two state ministries alongside. The weak point is the account of Jerusalem it gives from a site in the annexed Old City, which the Associated Press describes as leaning to the Israeli side.
