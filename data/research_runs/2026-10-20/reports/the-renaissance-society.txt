THE RENAISSANCE SOCIETY – Chicago, USA
Website: https://renaissancesociety.org (ok, checked 2026-10-20; the /support page could not be fetched in this run)

OVERVIEW
- Non-collecting contemporary art museum ("kunsthalle") founded in 1915 by University of Chicago faculty. It is on the 4th floor of Cobb Hall, 5811 S. Ellis Ave; it became an independent non-collecting museum on campus in 1974 (stated by the space; Wikipedia).
- Three exhibitions a year plus commissions, talks, screenings, concerts and publications. One gallery, nine staff (Chicago Sun-Times, Sept 2026).
- Executive director and chief curator since 2020: Myriam Ben Salah (Sun-Times). Board president: Nancy Lerner (GuideStar, board as of Oct 2025).
- All exhibitions and events are free. No accessibility information found on the about page.

FUNDING
The Renaissance Society: mainly private foundation and individual philanthropy, with a small university contribution.
- A financially independent 501(c)(3) (EIN 36-6109822); university grants and in-kind support are about 5% of its annual budget (stated by the space). It gets space and utilities free from the University (Sun-Times). Annual budget about $2 million (Sun-Times).
- Mellon Foundation: $750,000 over three years, unrestricted, announced 1 November 2024 (stated by the space).
- Program support: Teiger Foundation, Andy Warhol Foundation; general operations: Illinois Arts Council, University of Chicago Provost's Discretionary Fund (stated by the space).
- Next Century Fund ($5M campaign): $500,000 each from the Edlis Neeson, Pritzker Traubert and Zell Family foundations, announced 20 May 2016 (stated by the space; The Art Newspaper). Further major support from Jorge Cauz and Ugo Alfano Casati, Marilyn and Larry Fields, the Chauncey & Marion Deering McCormick Family Foundation (Conor O'Neil), Neil Ross and Lynn Hauser, and Richard Wright and Valerie Carberry.
- Mansueto Foundation: $1 million in 2019 for publications over ten years (Wikipedia; stated by the space).
- Annual benefit, recently staged as an artist commission (a 2026 event overseen by Maurizio Cattelan) (Sun-Times).
- No reporting found of corporate sponsorship or of controversy involving its funders.
- The record cites a second ProPublica EIN (36-2174240); it could not be checked in this run and does not match the Society's EIN.

BOTTOM LINE
The Ren is a small, free, university-affiliated kunsthalle funded by major arts foundations and Chicago philanthropic families, and it publishes its supporters. No red flags were found. The existing record is broadly confirmed; the second EIN in its sources should be checked.
