ANADOLU KÜLTÜR (DEPO AND DIYARBAKIR ARTS CENTER): Istanbul, Turkey
ID: anadolu-kultur-depo-and-diyarbak-r-arts-center
Website: https://www.anadolukultur.org (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit arts and culture organisation based in Istanbul that runs two centres: Depo in central Istanbul and the Diyarbakır Arts Center.
- Founded in 2002 by individuals from the art, business and civil society sectors (stated by the organisation). Wikipedia calls Osman Kavala the founder and chair of its board, and the Sigrid Rausing Trust lists him among the founders. The Diyarbakır Arts Center dates from 2002 as its first initiative; Anadolu Kültür's site says Depo was founded in 2009 as one of its initiatives (ArtReview dates Depo as a refuge for critical voices from 2008).
- Depo is housed in a former tobacco warehouse (ArtReview), at Koltukçular Çıkmazı Sokak No:1, Beyoğlu (Depo website). Its name in Turkish is Tütün Deposu, "Tobacco Warehouse".
- Stated aim: build bridges between ethnic, religious and regional groups and promote cultural rights.
- On site: Depo hosts exhibitions, screenings, panels and workshops and publishes the online journal Red Thread.
- Visiting Depo: open 11:00 to 19:00 every day except Sundays and Mondays. No reporting found of admission prices or accessibility on its site.

FUNDING
Anadolu Kültür: grant-funded: four named foreign and independent foundations and rights groups
- The organisation thanks Civil Rights Defenders, the Foundation for Arts Initiatives, the Sigrid Rausing Trust and Stiftung Mercator for institutional support (anadolukultur.org, undated).
- Sigrid Rausing Trust: supports the organisation from 2022 to 2026 under its Open Societies programme; its page gives £250,000 for previous grants and no amount for the current grant.
- In December 2018 the organisation said its operations were transparent, published its 2013 budget, and said Kavala sat on the board with no specific authority over use of its funds (Turkish Minute, reporting the foundation's statement).
- No reporting found of accounts, annual reports or amounts for the other three funders.
- No reporting found of Turkish state funding or of any corporate funder.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

WIDER CONTEXT
- Prosecution of the founder: Kavala was detained on 18 October 2017 and arrested on 1 November 2017. On 25 April 2022 a court sentenced him to life in prison over charges tied to the Gezi Park protests (Wikipedia). The European Cultural Foundation, German-Turkish Youth Bridge, Goethe-Institut, Heinrich Böll Foundation and Stiftung Mercator called the accusations against him unfounded and the sentence a defiance of a European Court of Human Rights ruling (joint statement). No reporting found of Turkish state funding of Anadolu Kültür, so this is context, not a funder issue.
- Unverified: Kavala used Anadolu Kültür and the Open Society Foundation to finance and organise the Gezi protests, from the Turkish investigation, repeated by President Erdoğan, who called Kavala the domestic instigator and George Soros the foreign organiser. Anadolu Kültür denied it, calling the idea "inconceivable" (Turkish Minute, 28 December 2018). On 16 November 2018, 13 people linked to Anadolu Kültür were detained; one academic was arrested and the others were released.
- ArtReview, Kaya Genç, 16 January 2024: describes Depo as a refuge for critical voices since 2008 and says Asena Günal, its general coordinator, was detained in 2018. Does not name Anadolu Kültür.
- Who chairs it: the Sigrid Rausing Trust page says Kavala "remains Chair"; Anadolu Kültür's own page lists Asena Günal as chair of its board.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Anadolu Kültür is foreign foundation and rights-group grants, named but without amounts or accounts for three of the four. The weak point is transparency, which the group itself answered in 2018 with a published budget. No art-media criticism of the organisation was found; the story around it is the state prosecution of its founder.
