PERMM MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART: Perm, Russia
ID: permm-museum-of-contemporary-art-perm
Website: https://permm.ru

OVERVIEW
- State museum of contemporary art in Perm, at Krisanova Street 4, in a former Mercedes-Benz dealership (Russian Wikipedia).
- Founded in 2009 out of the "Russkoe bednoe" exhibition, backed by Senator Sergei Gordeev, the Perm Krai administration and Moscow gallerist Marat Gelman; Gelman directed it until his dismissal in June 2013, and said the reason was censorship (Russian Wikipedia).
- It first occupied the Rechnoy Vokzal (river terminal) building, then moved to Gagarina Boulevard 24 in 2014 while the terminal was rebuilt, and moved to Krisanova Street on 1 September 2023, reopening in late October 2023 (Russian Wikipedia).
- Nailya Allakhverdieva directed it from 2019 to December 2024, and Elena Novikova has directed it since January 2026 (Russian Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 to 21:00 (Sobaka.ru).

FUNDING
PERMM Museum of Contemporary Art: mainly public: the Perm Krai budget, with private backing from Sergei Gordeev at its founding
- Perm Krai's Ministry of Culture oversees the museum; the ministry told Kommersant in December 2024 that Allakhverdieva resigned at her own request (Meduza, 5 December 2024).
- Novaya Gazeta's Julietta Sarkisyan writes that Gordeev, a developer and senator for Perm Krai from 2007 to 2010, supported the project financially, bought part of Gelman's collection and handed it to the museum, and funded the reconstruction of the River Terminal building that became its first home.
- In late January 2023 the Perm Krai Ministry of Culture said the museum would move to Krisanova Street; the building was advertised for lease at 6.2 million rubles a month (Ura.ru via Rambler, 21 February 2023).

WIDER CONTEXT
- State action against the museum, November 2024: searches took place at the museum and at director Allakhverdieva's home in the "justifying terrorism" case against founder Marat Gelman; the museum closed for a day and reopened on 22 November (RBC Perm, 22 November 2024; Meduza, 5 December 2024; The Art Newspaper Russia, Sofia Bagdasarova, 22 November 2024). The Art Newspaper Russia reports that Gelman was declared a foreign agent in 2021 and added in 2024 to Rosfinmonitoring's list of persons linked to terrorism and extremism.
- State action against the museum, March 2024: Allakhverdieva was searched in a criminal case involving Petr Verzilov (Meduza; The Art Newspaper Russia).
- Prosecutors, from November 2024: the Perm Krai prosecutor's office brought a suit in the Leninsky District Court challenging several museum deals it says involved people close to the museum's leadership; PERMM said the contracts were with curator and artist Arseny Sergeev, who curated the 2023 exhibition "And Yes and No" (Radio Svoboda, 8 January 2025). A further prosecutor's claim registered on 28 December 2024 asked the court to dismiss Allakhverdieva for "loss of trust" (Radio Svoboda).
- Resignation: Kommersant reported that her last day of work would be 6 December 2024; the Perm Krai Ministry of Culture told it she resigned at her own request (Meduza, 5 December 2024).
- Criminal case, March 2025: ProPerm reported that investigators opened a case under Article 148, Part 1 (offending believers' feelings) over works including Sergei Gorshkov's "Angel with a Trumpet" and Tatyana Anoshina's "Blue Cities", shown at PERMM in the early 2010s; the investigation holds that Allakhverdieva allowed their free display (Radio Svoboda, 27 March 2025 and 31 March 2025). The Dzerzhinsky District Court of Perm ordered her arrest in absentia; the Perm Regional Court cancelled it, and she remains on the interstate wanted list (Meduza, 1 July 2025).
- Novaya Gazeta's Julietta Sarkisyan, "The Perm 'Cultural Revolution'. The Finale" (2024), writes that the Perm cultural project of 2008 to 2013 was financed by cutting the number of libraries, children's camps and museums in Perm Krai, and describes rallies in June 2011 and April 2012 against the policy, with slogans including "Perm against PERMM" (title translated).
- Connection to the museum: the actors in these pages are criminal investigators, the Perm Krai prosecutor's office and courts, not the Perm Krai Ministry of Culture that oversees PERMM; PERMM was the centre of the cultural project, and Gelman was its first director.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind PERMM is the Perm Krai budget, with Sergei Gordeev's private funding at its founding. The documented problem is state action against the museum: searches in March and November 2024, prosecutor suits, and a criminal case against its former director over artworks the museum showed.
