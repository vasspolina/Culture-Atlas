ROYAL OPERA HOUSE MUSCAT: Muscat, Oman
ID: royal-opera-house-muscat
Website: https://rohmuscat.org.om (checked 2026-11-20; down: security certificate error)

OVERVIEW
- Opera house in the Al Qurum area of Muscat, the first in the Gulf and the second in the Arab region after Cairo (The National, 2011).
- Sultan Qaboos bin Said ordered it in 2001; it opened in October 2011 with Puccini's "Turandot", conducted by Placido Domingo (Arab News; The National).
- Designed by WATG, with a 1,100-seat auditorium whose proscenium can expand or shrink, and multimedia screens on the seat backs (Arab News).
- Programme: visiting opera, ballet and orchestras. Times of Oman reported in September 2025, from the house's announcement, that it would open its new season with the world premiere of "Sindbad: The Omani Sailor", an Arabic-language grand opera produced with MÜPA Budapest.
- Director General and Artistic Director: Umberto Fanni (travellingforbusiness, 2024). In January 2024 Sultan Haitham conferred the Order of Felicitation on him, handed over by Sayyid Khalid Hilal Al Busaidi, Minister of the Diwan of Royal Court (Oman Observer).
- For the 2024 to 2025 season Mandarin Oriental, Muscat sold room packages with two premium seat tickets and access to its private balcony at the Royal Circle (travellingforbusiness, a trade site, 3 October 2024).
- Visiting: ticket prices at opening were 13 to 60 rials (The National, 2011).

FUNDING
Royal Opera House Muscat: state-funded: a public body under the Sultan's patronage, led by senior royals and royal court officials
- The National wrote at the opening that ticket sales could not come near recouping costs, and that audiences and musicians would, in the early days at least, depend on "the largesse of the Sultan".
- Arab News wrote that budget constraints were set aside in favour of luxury.

Red flags: Gulf state body: royal decree and state leadership, 2010
- Royal Decree 105/2010 of 3 October 2010 established the Royal Opera House with legal personality and financial and administrative independence, under the Sultan's honorary patronage (decree.om, an unofficial English translation by a private website).
- The house's own site, in an archived copy of 13 August 2025 (the site is now down), says its leadership is guided by Sultan Haitham bin Tarik, and that a Higher Committee led by Sayyid Fahad bin Mahmoud Al Said, Deputy Prime Minister for the Council of Ministers, shapes its vision and considers policies recommended by the Board of Directors.
- Nasr Hamoud Al Kindi, Secretary General of Royal Court Affairs, is deputy chairman of the Board of Directors (Oman Observer, February 2023).
- Sayyid Dr Kamil Fahad Al Said, Secretary General in the General Secretariat for the Council of Ministers, is a board member (Teatro La Fenice; Times of Oman, 2025).

BOTTOM LINE
The money is the Omani state's and the Sultan's, with no private sponsor of weight in view. Its budget and accounts are worth watching for.
