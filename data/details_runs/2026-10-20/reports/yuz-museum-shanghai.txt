YUZ MUSEUM: Shanghai, China
ID: yuz-museum-shanghai
Website: http://www.yuzmshanghai.org/ (checked 2026-10-24; the connection failed with an expired security certificate, so the page could not be read)

OVERVIEW
- Private non-profit contemporary art museum in Shanghai, run by the Yuz Foundation. Since 2023 it has been in the Panlong Tiandi area, at No. 8, Lane 123, Panding Road, Qingpu District (Sotheby's).
- Founded by the Chinese-Indonesian collector Budi Tek, who opened a first Yuz Museum in Jakarta in 2006 (ArtReview) and the larger Shanghai museum on 17 May 2014 (Wikipedia). Tek died on 18 March 2022; his daughter Justine Alexandria Tek became museum director in 2022 (ArtReview).
- The name comes from Tek's Chinese name, Yu Deyao (Wikipedia).
- Original building: a renovated hangar of the former Longhua Airport in the West Bund, Xuhui District, converted by the Japanese architect Sou Fujimoto, with more than 9,000 square metres of space (Wikipedia). The current Panlong Tiandi site is a newly built centre of 2,000 square metres (Sotheby's).
- The collection began with Chinese contemporary oil paintings and holds roughly 1,500 works (Wikipedia, ArtReview). Wu Hung chairs the academic committee (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday 11am to 7pm, closed Monday (Sotheby's). Admission for the 2023 Qatar show was CNY 80 (Qatar Museums). No accessibility information was found.

FUNDING
Yuz Museum: privately funded by its founder's wealth, with corporate sponsorship of exhibitions and a three-way programme partnership with Qatar Museums and LACMA
- Source of the founder's wealth: Tek was president and director of the Indonesian poultry and restaurant group PT Sierad Produce Tbk, which listed in 1996 (Wikipedia).
- Site support from the state: NPR (Frank Langfitt, 2015) reported that a top official in Shanghai's Xuhui district offered Tek the use of an old aircraft factory at no cost after he complained on Weibo about land costs. The district government is developing the West Bund as a cultural district through a state-owned company.
- Hyundai Motor: presenting sponsor of exhibitions and public programmes with Yuz, announced 20 November 2020, including the Hyundai Art+Tech Program and the exhibition "The Abode of Illusions: The Garden of Zhang Daqian" (Hyundai newsroom). Amounts were not published.
- LACMA: in 2018 Tek announced a jointly owned foundation with the Los Angeles County Museum of Art to hold most of the Yuz collection (Wikipedia, Budi Tek). The foundation's funding is not reported in the sources opened.
- Qatar Museums: signed a cooperation agreement with Yuz and LACMA in November 2019. The Art Newspaper (4 December 2019) reported that its role covers exhibitions and "will focus on exhibitions and not extend to the foundation." The collection foundation is therefore a Yuz and LACMA project, not a Qatar Museums one.
- No reporting found of any payment by Qatar Museums to Yuz, or of the amount Hyundai pays.
- Revenue, accounts and board of the Yuz Foundation: no reporting found.

Red flags: partnership with Qatar Museums (Gulf state body)
- Qatar Museums, chaired by Sheikha Al Mayassa bint Hamad bin Khalifa Al-Thani, is a partner with Yuz and LACMA in a partnership formed in 2019 to develop and share exhibitions and programmes (Qatar Museums press release, 5 November 2023).
- Sheikha Al Mayassa described it as a six-year strategic partnership.
- The partnership was announced in Doha on 31 October 2019; its first show, "In Production: Art and the Studio System", opened in Shanghai on 7 November 2019, presented by Hyundai (Artlyst). Qatar Museums had worked with LACMA on exhibition exchange since 2012 (Artlyst).
- Qatar Museums organised "Watering the Desert | Contemporary Art from Qatar" at Yuz, from 8 November 2023 to 3 March 2024: 34 artists, designers and filmmakers, the first large show of Qatari and Qatar-based contemporary art in China.
- The Art Newspaper's 2019 report adds that Tek said Chinese authorities review all Yuz programmes, which he put down to local cultural sensitivities.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA

BOTTOM LINE
Yuz's own money comes from a private fortune built in Indonesian poultry, with a rent-free site offered by a district official and a sponsor in Hyundai. The Qatari link is a named, documented programme partnership with Qatar Museums, but the 2019 report says it does not extend to the collection foundation. Watch the foundation's governance and whether Qatar Museums or any other state body funds it, since no accounts or board are published.
