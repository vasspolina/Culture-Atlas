KIOSKO GALERÍA: Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia
ID: kiosko-galeria-santa-cruz
Website: https://www.kioskogaleria.com

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery and international residency at Calle Arenales 315, Santa Cruz de la Sierra (address as given on its website; a 2014 interview gives Arenales 319).
- Founded in 2006; Raquel Schwartz is its founding director (Delfina Foundation; El Deber interview, 28 December 2014). Its international contemporary art residency programme began in May 2007 (stated by the gallery).
- Schwartz also founded the RSV Foundation, and the website describes Kiosko as a project of Fundación RSV; she is also founding director of Nube Gallery (Delfina Foundation).
- In the 2014 interview Schwartz described the gallery's own building on Calle Arenales as the biggest challenge of the year, which took almost four years to finish and secure (Patricia Cadena, "Hay una crisis en el arte contemporáneo en Bolivia", eju.tv, 28 December 2014).
- The building has three storeys with two courtyards, two single bedrooms with private bathrooms, a kitchen, a living room and a shared workspace, and hosts exhibitions, workshops and talks (TransArtists).
- Residents are asked to give one public talk and one exhibition or final presentation (TransArtists).
- Open Monday to Friday, 15:00 to 20:00; entry is free (stated by the gallery).

FUNDING
Kiosko Galería: privately run by its founder's foundation, with residents' fees
- Fundación RSV, founded by Schwartz, is named on the gallery's website with the words "un proyecto de" ("a project of").
- The total cost of a residency is US$1,500, paid by the artist; it covers the programme, a shared study space, a single room, common spaces and basic services, a public talk, an open day, local guidance and production and curatorial support (TransArtists).
- Funded residencies take place irregularly; when available, a scholarship covers living and production costs for two months and exhibition costs, flights within Bolivia are covered and Kiosko pays 50 percent of international flights (TransArtists).

BOTTOM LINE
Kiosko Galería is a private space founded and directed by Raquel Schwartz and run through her Fundación RSV. The money documented is resident fees and scholarships offered by the gallery itself.
