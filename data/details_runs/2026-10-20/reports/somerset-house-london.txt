SOMERSET HOUSE: London, UK
ID: somerset-house-london
Website: https://www.somersethouse.org.uk

OVERVIEW
- Arts centre in a neoclassical complex on the Strand in central London, overlooking the River Thames (Wikipedia).
- Run by Somerset House Trust, a registered charity (No. 1063640) with trustees; the Trust became a registered charity in 1997, has overseen the whole complex since 2013, and appointed Jonathan Reekie as director in 2014 (Somerset House; Wikipedia).
- Edward Seymour obtained the land in 1539 and began a residence there around 1549; Sir William Chambers designed the present building, begun in 1776 and deemed complete by 1801, which originally housed the Royal Academy, the Royal Society, the Navy Board and tax and stamp offices (Wikipedia).
- Redeveloped as an arts centre after the 1984 Somerset House Act, with the Courtauld Institute of Art the first to move in, in 1989; Somerset House Studios opened in 2016 with affordable workspace for more than 100 artists (Wikipedia).
- On site: the Courtauld Gallery in the North Wing, exhibitions, events, online commissions, Somerset House Studios, coworking at Exchange, private offices and Makerversity; the courtyard hosts a winter ice rink and summer concerts (Somerset House; Wikipedia).
- Visiting: the Strand entrance and New Wing are open 8am to 11pm and the Courtauld Gallery 10am to 6pm (Somerset House). The courtyard and riverside terrace are open to the public (Wikipedia).
- A fire began on the roof in August 2024 and affected the West Wing, which held offices (Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Somerset House: self-generated with trust and corporate support: rents from creative businesses, private hire, ticket sales and sponsorship
- Wikipedia states the Trust receives no public subsidy and relies on income from rent and private hire for the upkeep of the estate, and on ticket sales, merchandising and sponsorship for its arts programme.
- Trusts, foundations and institutional partners named on its current supporters page: AKO Foundation, Art Fund, Buttinghill Foundation, Cockayne Grants for the Arts (a donor advised fund held at The Prism Charitable Trust), Embassy of Finland, Embassy of Sweden, Frame Contemporary Art Finland, Garfield Weston Foundation, Glass Castle Foundation, GRoW @ Annenberg, Jerwood Foundation, Julia Rausing Trust, The Linbury Trust, The Manolo Blahnik Foundation, National Lottery Heritage Fund, Nordic Culture Fund, Office for Contemporary Art Norway, Paul Hamlyn Foundation, The Rothschild Foundation, Royal Norwegian Embassy, Westminster City Council and The Woven Foundation.
- Corporate and brand partners: 886 by The Royal Mint, American Express, Aperol Spritz, Bank of America, Bloomberg, Dentsu, Gusbourne, HSBC UK, M&C Saatchi, ME London, Morgan Stanley, T. Rowe Price and Virgin Atlantic Holidays.
- Commissioning partners: ArtCentre Pasadena, Roberts Institute of Art, Vive Arts and the University of the Arts London Creative Computing Institute.
- Thanks for the transformation of Somerset House: Edmond J. Safra Philanthropic Foundation, Headley Trust, Heritage Lottery Fund, The Rothschild Foundation, The Garfield Weston Foundation, The Linbury Trust and The Monument Trust.
- Individual supporters include Keily Blair, Tim Bunting, Naomi Milgrom, Baroness Rebuck, Simon and Victoria Robey, and The Jonathan and Ronnie Newhouse Fund.
- Trustees: Baroness Rebuck is chair and Sophie Turner Laing deputy chair (Somerset House trustees page).
- Annual reports with financial statements for 2014-15 to 2023-24 are published on the website.

WIDER CONTEXT
- HSBC UK: on 19 October 2022 the UK Advertising Standards Authority upheld 45 complaints, including one from Adfree Cities, against two HSBC UK bus-stop posters from October 2021, one on net zero financing and one on tree planting. It found them misleading because they left out HSBC's contribution to greenhouse gas emissions, including financed emissions, and ordered that they not appear again in that form (Climate Change Litigation Databases).
- Connection: HSBC UK is named as a corporate and brand partner on Somerset House's current supporters page. The ruling concerned HSBC UK's own adverts and did not name Somerset House.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Somerset House is mainly earned and private: rents from creative tenants, hire, events and sponsorship, plus support from trusts and companies, with no public subsidy according to Wikipedia. Its corporate partners include HSBC UK, whose 2022 advertising ruling did not concern Somerset House.
