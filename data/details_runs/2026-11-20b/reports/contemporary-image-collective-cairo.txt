CONTEMPORARY IMAGE COLLECTIVE: Cairo, Egypt
ID: contemporary-image-collective-cairo
Website: http://www.ciccairo.com (checked 2026-11-20; down: the connection failed; the archived copy of August 2022 shows an "under construction" page)

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit arts initiative in Cairo working on visual culture, artistic practice and critical discourse, known as CiC (مركز الصورة المعاصرة) (e-flux; Arab Fund for Arts and Culture; CiC archived site).
- Founded in 2004 by a group of visual artists and professional photographers (e-flux; TransArtists).
- Sites: a villa in Mounira, then from 2010 a floor of a downtown building near Talaat Harb Square (Cairo 360, 23 June 2010); DutchCulture reported in 2025 that it lost its downtown location and found a new space in Garden City.
- Directors: the director Edit Molnár and the curator Aleya Hamza left in spring 2009, and by June 2010 Mia Jankowicz, formerly residencies curator at Gasworks, was its new artistic director (Cairo 360); Contemporary And reported in 2013 that Jankowicz had left.
- Facilities: a public library, a digital lab for scanning and printing, a darkroom for film processing, exhibitions, screenings, talks and education programmes (CiC archived site; Arab Fund for Arts and Culture).
- Its library led Rufoof, a shared online catalogue of Cairo art libraries; the first 4,000 items from CiC and Townhouse were catalogued (Contemporary And).
- It has no fixed residency programme but considers proposals (TransArtists).

FUNDING
Contemporary Image Collective: grant-funded non-profit: Ford Foundation grants from 2009 to 2017, British Council money for its library, an Arab Fund for Arts and Culture grant in 2024
- The Ford Foundation made four grants totalling 775,000 dollars: 120,000 in August 2009 for an emerging art space for film and photography (to 2011); 215,000 in November 2011 (to 2013); 240,000 in November 2013 (to 2015); and 200,000 in December 2015 for programming, audience building and organisational development (to 2017). The last three went to a grantee named Stiftelsen Contemporary Image Collective (Ford Foundation grants database).
- The Arab Fund for Arts and Culture lists a 2024 CiC project under its National Cultural Opportunities Fund for Egypt.
- British Council Egypt paid for Rufoof, covering staff, book buying and workshops; the Arab Digital Expression Foundation built the software pro bono (Contemporary And).
- TransArtists states that CiC seeks funds for all the programming it commits to and charges participants in some programmes a fee.
- DutchCulture reported in 2025 that artists in Egypt depend on grants from abroad.

BOTTOM LINE
CiC lives on project grants from foreign and regional funders, none of them flagged. Worth watching: who pays for the Garden City space.
