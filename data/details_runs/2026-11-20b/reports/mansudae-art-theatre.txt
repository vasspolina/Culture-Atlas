MANSUDAE ART THEATRE: Pyongyang, North Korea
ID: mansudae-art-theatre
Website: none (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Theatre (만수대예술극장) in Mansudae, Central District, Pyongyang, near the Grand People's Study House (Korean Wikipedia, English Wikipedia).
- Opened in 1976 (Korean Wikipedia, English Wikipedia). South Korea's Ministry of Unification gives its completion as January 1977, on Somun Street in Taedongmun-dong, with 60,000 square metres of floor space, a 2,200 square metre revolving stage and seats for 4,000.
- Home of the Mansudae Art Troupe, which became its resident company when the theatre was built in 1976 (Korean Wikipedia).
- Used for revolutionary operas, plays and assemblies hosted by the Workers' Party of Korea (Korean Wikipedia), and a venue for the Samjiyon Band (English Wikipedia, citing Yonhap).
- State occasions held there include Kim Jong Un's attendance at a Lunar New Year performance on 1 February 2022 with members of the Party Politburo Presidium, a Vietnamese national art troupe's performances in April 2019, and the 20th April Spring friendship art festival in 2002 (Ministry of Unification, from North Korean media).

FUNDING
Mansudae Art Theatre: state theatre: the Government of North Korea, with the Workers' Party of Korea as host and as supervisor of its resident troupe
- Party control: Korean Wikipedia's page on the Mansudae Art Troupe says it is the only Pyongyang troupe that works under the supervision of the Workers' Party of Korea (my translation), and Party assemblies are held in the theatre.
- Management: for the theatre's 20th anniversary in November 1996 the Party Central Committee sent a congratulatory letter praising the staff of its management bureau (Ministry of Unification, from North Korean media).
- Staff: the ministry's 2022 directory of North Korean institutions lists Kim Kwang Ho (김광호) as director and Jong Kwan Sun (정관순) as head of the management bureau (my romanisations).
- North Korea announces its state budget as yearly percentage changes, not amounts. KCNA claimed on 24 March 2026 that 2026 spending would reach 105.8 percent of 2025, with funds for the cultural sector at 103.1 percent of last year's and 15.8 percent of spending going to national defence.

Red flags: US sanctions on the North Korean government and the Workers' Party, 2016
- US Executive Order 13722 of 15 March 2016 blocks property in US jurisdiction of the Government of North Korea and the Workers' Party of Korea; section 9 defines the Government as the DPRK government and its agencies, instrumentalities and controlled entities (Federal Register, 18 March 2016).
- The theatre hosts Party assemblies and a troupe that works under the Party (Korean Wikipedia).

Not related: the Mansudae Art Studio (Mansudae Overseas Project Group of Companies), which the US Treasury blocked under the same order on 2 December 2016 (Federal Register, 7 December 2016). It is a separate state art studio sharing the Mansudae name. The Mansudae People's Theatre is also a different building (English Wikipedia).

BOTTOM LINE
The state and the Workers' Party stand behind the theatre, and the US blocking order covers both. The Party's use of the building for its assemblies is the point to watch.
