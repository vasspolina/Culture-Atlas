KUNSTRAUM DORNBIRN: Dornbirn, Austria
ID: kunstraum-dornbirn-dornbirn
Website: https://www.kunstraumdornbirn.at/en

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art venue in the Stadtgarten park of Dornbirn, Vorarlberg, at Jahngasse 9; its office is at Marktstraße 33.
- Founded in 1987 as a private non-profit organisation to present and communicate contemporary art; it has used the historic assembly hall since 2003 (stated by the space).
- The hall was built in 1893 by the Dornbirn turbine maker Rüschwerke to assemble its large turbine wheels; the company closed in 1984. Around 1999 and 2000 the industrial site was remodelled into the municipal Stadtgarten, and the assembly hall was left unchanged (stated by the space).
- The brick hall measures 30 by 14 metres, 11.15 metres high, with about 350 square metres of exhibition space. It is not heatable (stated by the space).
- It holds up to four exhibitions a year by established international artists and emerging ones. The current shows are Michail Pirgelis, "HYLE" (3 July to 8 November 2026), and Ursula Palla, "Pale Light" (20 November 2026 to 28 February 2027). It also runs education programmes, guided tours and a friends membership.
- A six-member managing board runs it: Dr. Gerald A. Matt (President), Roland Jörg, Judith Berger-Neustädter, Peter Rhomberg, Dr. Sylvia Volz and Uli Zumtobel.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, closed Monday except public holidays. Admission is 4 euros for adults and 6.50 euros for families; free for under 19s, students and apprentices, people with disabilities and friends of the Kunstraum.
