SINGAPORE ART MUSEUM: Singapore, Singapore
ID: singapore-art-museum-singapore
Website: https://www.singaporeartmuseum.sg (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum of Singapore, presenting art from a Southeast Asian perspective. Its main venue is Tanjong Pagar Distripark, 39 Keppel Road, and it also shows work in partner spaces across the island (Roots, Singapore Art Museum).
- It opened to the public on 20 October 1995 and was officially opened by Prime Minister Goh Chok Tong on 20 January 1996 (Wikipedia).
- The original home is the former St Joseph's Institution on Bras Basah Road, built in 1855 and now a National Monument (Roots); restoring it took more than two years and cost S$30 million (Wikipedia).
- SAM at 8Q, a contemporary wing in the former Catholic High School on Queen Street, opened in 2008 (Wikipedia). Both heritage buildings are closed for redevelopment (Roots).
- In January 2022 SAM opened a contemporary space at Tanjong Pagar Distripark with two galleries, an F&B space and residency studios (Wikipedia). It also organises the Singapore Biennale (Roots).
- Dr Eugene Tan has been Director of SAM since 2019 and, since 1 April 2024, Chief Executive Officer of National Gallery Singapore and SAM and Head of the Visual Arts Cluster, which comprises the Gallery, SAM and STPI (National Gallery Singapore media release, 13 March 2024).

FUNDING
Singapore Art Museum: Visual Arts Cluster governance with ministry and National Arts Council seats on the board, plus named private, corporate and foreign cultural donors
- Board: Edmund Cheng, Deputy Chairman of Wing Tai Holdings, chairs. Members include Eugene Tan, Edmund Koh (UBS), Tay Yu Jin (Mayer Brown), E-Len Fu (Kids21), Irene Tedja (Amitell Capital), Pierre Lorinet (Trafigura), Sumitra Pasupathy (Playeum) and Teh Hua Fung (Group ONE). Audrey Tan of the Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth and Elaine Ng, CEO of the National Arts Council, sit as ex-officio members (SAM About page).
- Annual reports for FY2015 to FY2025 are posted, with a financial year from 1 April to 31 March (SAM About page).
- The FY2025 report's Donors and Sponsors list names: Charles and Keith Family Fund, Chu Foundation, Culture Ireland, F. Widjaja Family, Far East Organisation, French Embassy in Singapore, Grab, Hong Leong Foundation, Institut Francais, JCCI Foundation Singapore, KEF, Lee Foundation, Mapletree Investments, Mondriaan Fonds, New Carlsberg Foundation, One Bangkok, Pro Helvetia, Singapore Tourism Board, Tanoto Art Foundation and the Grace, Shua and Jacob Ballas II Charitable Trust, along with individuals and a SAM Patrons list. Its list of benefit supporters includes DBS Bank Ltd, Diageo Rare & Exceptional and Wing Tai Holdings Ltd.
- SAM's 30th Anniversary Benefit on 23 January 2026 raised approximately S$1.95 million from donations, a charitable auction and a matching grant under the Tote Board Enhanced Fundraising Programme, with more than 240 supporters taking part (FY2025 report).
- The sixth edition of the open call This Little Red Dot, in celebration of Singapore's 60th birthday, was presented with major support from Mapletree and venue support from VivoCity (FY2025 report).
- The SAM ART SG Fund finances acquisitions for the permanent collection: founding benefactors Carmen Yixuan Li and Pure Yichun Chen, joined by Pierre Lorinet; it grew from SGD 150,000 to SGD 250,000 for works shown at ART SG in January 2026 (ART SG, 26 November 2025). Lorinet also appears in the donor list as Pierre and Bolor Lorinet.

BOTTOM LINE
The Singapore Art Museum sits in the Visual Arts Cluster led by Dr Eugene Tan, with a representative of the Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth and the chief executive of the National Arts Council holding ex-officio board seats. The FY2025 report names donors including Mapletree Investments, the Singapore Tourism Board, the Lee Foundation, the Tanoto Art Foundation, Pro Helvetia and Culture Ireland, and records a Tote Board matching grant for the 2026 benefit, which raised about S$1.95 million.
