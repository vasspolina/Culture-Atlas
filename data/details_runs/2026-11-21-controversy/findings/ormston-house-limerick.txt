ID: ormston-house-limerick

WIDER CONTEXT
- In July 2018 the board said Ormston House was "operating on a precarious and uncertain basis" because the building was being sold on the open market. Ormston House had raised €150,000 from philanthropic sources and the council had put forward €125,000, short of the market price (ilovelimerick, 26 July 2018).
- An online petition had 1,853 signatures by 29 July 2018. Senator Maria Byrne wrote to the culture minister Josepha Madigan asking that "this building is kept as a space for artists", and the metropolitan mayor Daniel Butler urged that it be saved for cultural and artistic groups (ilovelimerick, 26 July 2018).
- The board chair, Mark Mulqueen, wrote that the organisation was having to consider closure by December 2018 (TheJournal.ie, opinion, 15 August 2018).
