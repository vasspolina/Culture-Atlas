Red flags: 2025 protest over a stuffed cat in the exhibition "Facile ironia"
- The exhibition "Facile ironia. L'ironia nell'arte italiana tra XX e XXI secolo" at MAMbo showed a stuffed cat on a photocopier, which visitors can operate to copy its image, and several stuffed pigeons (GreenMe, 19 August 2025).
- LAV (Lega Anti Vivisezione) asked Mayor Matteo Lepore to order the removal of all stuffed animals from the show and called on the public to boycott the exhibition and write to MAMbo asking for the immediate removal of the installations; LAV said the work was not art but an "orribile spettacolo" ("horrible spectacle", translated), and its president Gianluca Felicetti said turning the body of a sentient being into an "oggetto concettuale" ("conceptual object", translated) is a lack of respect, not irony (GreenMe, 19 August 2025).
- Fratelli d'Italia filed a question about the work in the regional council (Il Resto del Carlino, 20 August 2025).
- MAMbo replied that the work is certified: the cat died in a road accident and was only afterwards stuffed and given to the artist (Il Resto del Carlino, 20 August 2025).
