CASA TRES PATIOS (C3P): Medellín, Colombia
ID: casa-tres-patios-medellin
Website: https://casatrespatios.org

OVERVIEW
- House at Carrera 50A #63-31 in the Prado neighbourhood of Medellín. The site describes the Fundación Casa Tres Patios as a non-profit organisation dedicated to promoting social justice.
- Global Voices (2014) says the house was founded by Tony Evanko and has offered artists and local communities a space since 2006. The foundation's own history page gives 2007 as its start.
- A 2012 shift moved the programme toward critical thinking and education for social justice, and after 2018 it introduced the Laboratorios Comunes de Creación, a pedagogical model (C3P history page).
- Programmes listed on the site: education (Escuela para el vivir común, Sueños, Escojo la vida, Las Chicharras), research, Inflexión, CuBO.X, multidisciplinary residencies, exhibitions, gender labs, migration and citizen culture.
- Prison projects: "Trazos de Libertad" (2021 to 2023) in the Bellavista and Pedregal prisons, and from 2024 "Sueños" in prisons and "Escojo la vida" for people who have completed their sentences (C3P history page).
- In 2014 Global Voices described a hackerspace, Un/Loquer, operating inside the house, and residencies for artists, architects and curators.

FUNDING
Casa Tres Patios: designated donations from the Foundation For Arts Initiative, Fundación Sofía Pérez de Soto, Fundación SURA and Arts Collaboratory, research service fees and an annual art auction
- C3P's financial page lists designated donations from the Foundation For Arts Initiative, Fundación Sofía Pérez de Soto, Fundación SURA and Arts Collaboratory.
- Fundación Sura and Fundación Sofía Pérez de Soto are named as partners of "Trazos de Libertad" (C3P history page).
- C3P's financial page states the foundation provides research services to public and private bodies in Colombia and abroad, which generate funds for its cultural and educational activities. Its history page names the Municipio de Envigado (2022, citizen culture) and Comfama (2023, school libraries) as research projects.
- Stichting Ashkana, the Dutch entity of Arts Collaboratory, has supported C3P on administrative matters (C3P history page). Wikipedia states Arts Collaboratory was established in 2007 by the Dutch organisations Hivos and DOEN Foundation and lists C3P as a partner with a residency programme in Medellín.
- In 2014 Global Voices reported that the house supports itself through an auction at the end of the year, in which artists donate works, and also receives support from some institutions.
- From 2012 to 2018 C3P took part in the Red de Artes Visuales (C3P history page).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind C3P is designated donations from the Foundation For Arts Initiative, Fundación Sofía Pérez de Soto, Fundación SURA and Arts Collaboratory (set up by the Dutch DOEN Foundation and Hivos), fees for research services and, as of 2014, an annual art auction.
