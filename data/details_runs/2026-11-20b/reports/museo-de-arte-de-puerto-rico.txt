MUSEO DE ARTE DE PUERTO RICO: San Juan, Puerto Rico
ID: museo-de-arte-de-puerto-rico
Website: https://mapr.org/en/ (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum at 299 Avenida de Diego in Santurce, San Juan, with 18 exhibition halls and a sculpture garden (English Wikipedia; museum site).
- Opened on 1 July 2000. The idea dates to 1995, when the Puerto Rico Tourism Company, with funding from the Government Development Bank, chose to build the museum around the old hospital instead of demolishing it (English Wikipedia).
- The building is the former San Juan Municipal Hospital, designed by William H. Shimmelphening and built in 1920; it was a hospital until 1966 and offices of the Department of Transportation and Public Works until 1975 (English Wikipedia).
- The collection includes José Campeche's "Virgen de la Soledad de la Victoria" (1782 to 1789) (English Wikipedia).
- María Cristina Gaztambide is executive director (museum site). The board is chaired by Juan A. Larrea French, with Gloria Benítez as secretary (ProPublica summary of the fiscal 2025 Form 990).
- 2025 was its 25th year (El Nuevo Día). "Armando Reverón: El Caribe y la ausencia renovada", the museum's first show of the Venezuelan painter, ran from March to October 2026 (El Nuevo Día); "Panteón de amor", on HIV and AIDS, opened on 18 November 2026 (museum site).
- Visiting: Wednesday, Friday and Saturday 10:00 to 17:00, Thursday 10:00 to 20:00 with free entry from 17:00, Sunday 11:00 to 17:00; closed Monday, Tuesday and holidays. Admission 20 dollars, 12 dollars for residents with ID (museum site).

FUNDING
Museo de Arte de Puerto Rico: private non-profit: donations, foundation and federal grants, and earned income
- The museum files as Museo De Arte De Puerto Rico Inc (EIN 66-0537284). Fiscal 2025 revenue was 7,308,690 dollars, of which 3,671,393 dollars (50.2 per cent) were contributions and 957,842 dollars program services; expenses were 7,911,697 dollars. In fiscal 2024 revenue was 12,360,346 dollars, 76.2 per cent of it contributions. Total liabilities stood at 22,375,274 dollars against assets of 40,974,710 dollars in 2025 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, summary figures only).
- Gaztambide says every gallery is named for its sponsor under a sponsorship contract, as has been museum policy since its founding (Sandra Rodríguez Cotto, a journalist, in her newsletter).
- Gloria Benítez and José Carrión III are "aportadores económicos" (financial contributors) of the museum (Rodríguez Cotto). Carrión is a former president of the island's Fiscal Oversight Board (Amsterdam News).
- Federal money: the Institute of Museum and Library Services awarded 343,521 dollars in fiscal 2024 for paid internships with the University of Sagrado Corazón and the University of Puerto Rico (IMLS).
- Foundations: the Mellon Foundation gave 250,000 dollars in 2018 for conservation and curatorial capacity for recovery and response efforts (Mellon Foundation); the Darryl Chappell Foundation sponsored the artist residency at the Center for Educational Innovation (Amsterdam News).
- Earned income: admissions, memberships, space rental for weddings and corporate events, and a shop (museum site).

Red flags: 2023 protest over the Cecilia Orta Allende gallery
- In May 2022 the museum mounted a wooden plaque with the name and likeness of the artist and activist Cecilia Orta Allende in a first-floor gallery; by late August 2023 it was gone, and the gallery bore the name of Valeria Antonia Carrión Benítez, only daughter of the donors Gloria Benítez and José Carrión III (Amsterdam News).
- The museum said Orta Allende's name stays on a larger space, the Galería-Taller Cecilia Orta Allende, grown from 731 to 1,157.5 square feet (Rodríguez Cotto). Gaztambide said the plaque moved to the Center for Educational Innovation, to guarantee the space's permanence (Amsterdam News).
- On 19 November 2023 Afro Puerto Rican activists led by Edwin Velázquez Collazo picketed the museum, calling the decision "un acto discriminatorio, racista y elitista" (a discriminatory, racist and elitist act; translation mine) (Rodríguez Cotto; Amsterdam News).
- José Antonio Orta, for the Orta Allende estate, failed in attempts to meet the board and the director (Rodríguez Cotto). The museum said it stayed in contact with the estate's executor and that the family did not call the protest (same source).

Not related: Museo De Arte Contemporaneo De Puerto Rico Inc (EIN 66-0420190). It files separately from this museum in the ProPublica listing.

BOTTOM LINE
No funder of the museum falls under a flag. The weak point is the sale of gallery names to donors, which led to the 2023 protest.
