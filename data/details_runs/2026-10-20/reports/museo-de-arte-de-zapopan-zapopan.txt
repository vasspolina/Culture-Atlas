MUSEO DE ARTE DE ZAPOPAN: Zapopan, Mexico
ID: museo-de-arte-de-zapopan-zapopan
Website: https://maz.zapopan.gob.mx/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum (MAZ) at Andador 20 de Noviembre 166 (myartguides directory), in the historic centre of Zapopan, next to the Basílica de Nuestra Señora de Zapopan (Mexico Desconocido).
- Inaugurated in 2002 (Mexico Desconocido); the building was designed by architects María Emilia Orendáin and Enrique Toussaint.
- It belongs to the Zapopan City Council (myartguides directory).
- Programmes include exhibitions, outdoor events, workshops, conferences, film screenings and a reading area (Mexico Desconocido).
- Viviana Kuri directs the museum; Chic Magazine (Michelle Freyría, "Viviana Kuri pone a Jalisco como punto de referencia artístico", 13 March 2024) reported she had directed it for eleven years.
- A second venue, EstaciónMAZ, opened on 28 September 2024 at avenida Hidalgo 352 in the Zapopan Centro complex, with Francis Alÿs's "Juegos de niñxs"; Kuri directs both (Players of Life, 30 September 2024; Chic Magazine).
- Visiting: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, open until 22:00 on Thursdays (myartguides directory). EstaciónMAZ is free every day (Players of Life).

FUNDING
Museo de Arte de Zapopan: mainly public: municipal government of Zapopan
- The museum belongs to the Zapopan City Council (myartguides directory).
- EstaciónMAZ, the second venue, cost 65.99 million pesos of municipal investment, and Mayor Juan José Frangie led its inauguration (Players of Life, 30 September 2024).
- Kuri told Chic Magazine in March 2024 that resources are always a challenge ("El tema de los recursos siempre es un reto") and that she was setting up EstaciónMAZ together with the museum team and the Ayuntamiento.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is the Zapopan municipal government, which owns it and paid 65.99 million pesos for its new second venue, EstaciónMAZ.
