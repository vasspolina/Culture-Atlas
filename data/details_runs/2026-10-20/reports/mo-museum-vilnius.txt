MO MUSEUM: Vilnius, Lithuania
ID: mo-museum-vilnius
Website: https://mo.lt

OVERVIEW
- Private museum of modern and contemporary art at Pylimo Street 17, Vilnius (Wikipedia); The Art Newspaper places the building about five minutes from the historic centre.
- Began as a private initiative of Viktoras and Danguolė Butkus, who started collecting art in 2008. The Modern Art Centre, founded in 2009, collected art and published art books in preparation for a museum, which opened on 18 October 2018 (Wikipedia).
- The building was designed by Studio Libeskind with the Lithuanian firm Do Architects, covers about 3,100 square metres and stands on the former site of the Lietuva Cinema (Wikipedia).
- The collection of the couple holds about 4,000 works (The Art Newspaper, 2018), mainly Lithuanian art from 1960 to the present; Wikipedia gives more than 6,000 works. At opening it planned four to six changing exhibitions a year with no permanent displays (The Art Newspaper).
- On site: two exhibition halls, a sculpture garden, a restaurant and a shop (The Art Newspaper). Wikipedia says the sculpture garden and terrace are always open.
- At opening, admission was €7 (The Art Newspaper). The museum reported more than 140,000 visitors in 2023, led by the exhibition "Vilnius Poker" (MO annual report 2023 summary).

FUNDING
MO Museum: self-funded: the founders' private money from the sale of the biotech company Fermentas, with up to half of its budget earned
- Viktoras Butkus funded the museum from the sale of his biotechnology company Fermentas to Thermo Fisher Scientific, which he called "the financial platform to bring our concept to life". The museum was described as a €15 million project and the couple had spent nearly €4.5 million on Lithuanian works dating back to 1960 (The Art Newspaper, Simon Hewitt, 19 October 2018).
- Wikipedia says the Butkus couple funded the construction, which began in 2017.
- MO's support page says the museum "earns up to 50% of its budget", and that sponsors, donors and community members support the rest.
- Membership and gift levels: Ambassador club 3,000 EUR a year for one person or 5,000 EUR for two; Friend club 500 EUR for one or 1,000 EUR for two; donations up to 1,500 EUR need no contract (MO support page).

BOTTOM LINE
MO is a private museum paid for by Viktoras Butkus from the sale of his biotech company Fermentas to Thermo Fisher Scientific. Its support page says the museum earns up to 50% of its budget and that sponsors, donors and community members support the rest.
