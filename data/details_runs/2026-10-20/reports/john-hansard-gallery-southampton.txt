JOHN HANSARD GALLERY: Southampton, United Kingdom
ID: john-hansard-gallery-southampton
Website: https://jhg.art

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art gallery at 142-144 Above Bar Street, Southampton, part of the University of Southampton (John Hansard Gallery website).
- Created in 1979 to unite two University galleries, the Photographic Gallery and the Fine Art Gallery. Named for John Henry Hansard (1895 to 1979), a University benefactor (gallery about page).
- Directors: Leo Stable (1979 to 1980), Barry Barker (1980 to 1987), Professor Stephen Foster (1987 to 2017) and Woodrow Kernohan (2017 to the present) (gallery about page).
- Its original home was the John Hansard Building, built in 1959 and converted to gallery use in 1979 and 1980 (Wikipedia). In 2018 it moved to Studio 144, a city-centre arts complex of around 75,000 square feet across two buildings that also houses City Eye and Nuffield Southampton Theatres. The gallery was due to open there on 12 May 2018 with an ARTIST ROOMS exhibition of Gerhard Richter's work, and the University said the move "triples the space available for public programming" (University of Southampton news, 19 December 2017).
- Wikipedia names CZWG as architect of the new building, with interior design by Glenn Howells.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday, 11am to 5pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Entry is free (gallery website).

FUNDING
John Hansard Gallery: part of the University of Southampton, with Arts Council England National Portfolio funding
- University of Southampton: the gallery is part of the University (gallery about page).
- Arts Council England: £1.8 million over three years (2023 to 2026) through its National Portfolio Organisations programme, for contemporary art exhibitions, engagement and creative learning, and outreach and community projects (University of Southampton news, "University arts venues to receive £2 million to inspire city through art and music", 9 November 2022). Arts Council England has funded the gallery since its inception, according to the gallery's about page.
- Studio 144: led by Southampton City Council and supported by the National Lottery through Arts Council England, with Grosvenor Britain and Ireland Developments Limited as partner (University of Southampton news, 19 December 2017). The gallery's about page gives the Arts Council's National Lottery funding toward Studio 144 as £7.2 million.
- Trusts, foundations and sponsors give additional money each year for exhibitions and educational activities (gallery about page).

BOTTOM LINE
The gallery is part of the University of Southampton, and Arts Council England has given it £1.8 million for 2023 to 2026. Southampton City Council led the Studio 144 building project it moved into in 2018.
