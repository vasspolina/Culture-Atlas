GALERIA FOKSAL (FOKSAL GALLERY): Warsaw, Poland
ID: galeria-foksal-warsaw
Website: http://galeriafoksal.pl (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit contemporary art gallery at ul. Foksal 1/4 in Warsaw, in an annexe to the Zamoyski Palace, named for the street it stands at the end of (stated by the gallery).
- Founded in 1966 by the critics Wiesław Borowski, Anka Ptaszkowska and Mariusz Tchorek with artists including Tadeusz Kantor, Henryk Stażewski, Zbigniew Gostomski, Edward Krasiński and Roman Owidzki; Borowski was nominally its sole director for nearly 35 years (stated by the gallery). Gostomski's "Optical Objects" opened it on 1 April 1966 (English Wikipedia).
- Gostomski, Krasiński and Zygmunt Targowski designed the interior, cabinets, bookshelves and chairs, which remain unchanged, and the lettering of its invitations and posters (stated by the gallery).
- It holds more than 40 years of texts and photographs of its events, by photographers including Tadeusz Rolke and Eustachy Kossakowski; the archive is open to the public (stated by the gallery).
- It worked under the Visual Arts Studio until 1984 and then the Warsaw Agency for Art Exhibitions (stated by the gallery). Jaromir Jedliński directed it from July 2006 to August 2008 (English Wikipedia).
- Staff named on the contact page: Zuzanna Mielczarek, programme curator, and the curators Katarzyna Krysiak and Martyna Stołpiec.
- 2026 is its 60th year: Honza Zamojski's "Early Greys. Selected Drawing Works 16-26", curated by Krysiak for the jubilee, runs from 20 November 2026 to 23 January 2027 (stated by the gallery).
- Visiting: free admission; Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday noon to 6 pm, Thursday noon to 7 pm, Saturday noon to 4 pm (stated by the gallery). The site links to an online sign language interpreter.

FUNDING
Galeria Foksal: public: the Mazovia regional government, through the Mazovian Institute of Culture
- The gallery is part of the Mazovian Institute of Culture, formerly the Mazovian Centre for Culture and Arts, "financed by the Marshal Office of the Province of Mazovia" (stated by the gallery). English Wikipedia likewise calls it government funded under the regional culture centre.
- The Mazovian Institute of Culture describes itself as an organisational unit of the Mazovia Voivodeship self-government (Mazovian Institute of Culture).
- Single shows draw other public money: the Zamojski show was made under a scholarship from the Minister of Culture and National Heritage, with ZAiKS as partner and MINT Magazine as media patron (stated by the gallery).

Not related: the Foksal Gallery Foundation at Górskiego Street, Warsaw. It was set up at the gallery in 1997; some of its members later left to found a separate institution, which the gallery calls "a separate body, without any direct links with Foksal Gallery". English Wikipedia says the foundation moved out in 2001 and works as a commercial entity.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind Galeria Foksal is clean: it is public money, with no funder that falls under a flag.
