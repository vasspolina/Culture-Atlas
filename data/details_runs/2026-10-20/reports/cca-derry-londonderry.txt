CCA DERRY~LONDONDERRY: Derry, United Kingdom
ID: cca-derry-londonderry
Website: https://www.ccadld.org/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre at 10-12 Artillery Street, Derry (Art Fund). Its Library/Workshop operates as a Project Space visible from Artillery Street (CCA annual report to March 2024).
- Traces its roots to 1992 as the Context Gallery, which developed in The Playhouse. It opened at Artillery Street in autumn 2012 in a building designed by Michael Carr Architects (Photoireland wiki).
- Registered with the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland as the Centre for Contemporary Arts Derry-Londonderry, number 101414, since 10 February 2015; it had four employees in the year to 31 March 2025 (OpenCharities).
- Programme: exhibitions, digital residencies, a Supports Programme for emerging artists with critique groups and mentoring, schools and families activities, podcasts and the Window Texts risograph series; a reciprocal residency with Kaunas Artists' House and the Lithuanian Culture Institute (annual report to March 2024). Catherine Hemelryk is named as Director in the 2023 and 2024 annual reports.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday, noon to 6pm; free to all (Art Fund).

FUNDING
CCA Derry~Londonderry: Arts Council of Northern Ireland as primary funder, with Derry City and Strabane District Council and project grants from trusts and charitable funds
- Arts Council of Northern Ireland is the primary funder (Photoireland wiki). The annual report to March 2024 records continued Arts Council funding with a nominal uplift, and the report to March 2023 records funding through its Annual Funding Programme.
- Derry City and Strabane District Council is listed as a supporter (Photoireland wiki) and helped produce new social narrative videos (annual report to March 2024).
- Caldwell & Robinson Solicitors and the Art Fund are listed as supporters (Photoireland wiki). The Art Fund funded the 2022 to 2024 digital and remote programmes (annual report to March 2023).
- Jerwood Developing Artists Fund funded the expanded Supports Programme from January 2023, and the Jerwood Foundation funded New Curating after the Jerwood Curatorial Accelerator (annual report to March 2024).
- a-n provided a bursary to the artist Laura Wilson and the NOMAS Foundation funded "South of Imagination" (annual report to March 2023).
- Income was £171,757 and expenditure £185,995 in the year to 31 March 2025 (OpenCharities).
- Trustees: Denzel Brown, Michael O'Callaghan, Shaun McLaughlin, Ailbhe Greaney, Joyce Patricia Cronin, Mhairi Sutherland, Noelle McAlinden and Susan Kelly (OpenCharities).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind CCA Derry~Londonderry is the Arts Council of Northern Ireland as primary funder, with Derry City and Strabane District Council and project funding from the Art Fund, Jerwood and the NOMAS Foundation. Income was £171,757 in the year to 31 March 2025.
