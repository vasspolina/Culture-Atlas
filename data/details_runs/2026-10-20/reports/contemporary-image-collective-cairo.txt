CONTEMPORARY IMAGE COLLECTIVE: Cairo, Egypt
ID: contemporary-image-collective-cairo
Website: https://www.arabculturefund.org/Projects/7437

OVERVIEW
- Independent non-profit arts initiative in Cairo, focused on visual culture, artistic practice and critical discourse (Arab Fund for Arts and Culture; e-flux).
- Founded in 2004 in Cairo by a group of visual artists and professional photographers (e-flux; TransArtists).
- Earlier sites: a villa in Mounira, then from 2010 an entire floor of a colonial-era apartment building in Downtown Cairo, near Talaat Harb Square (Cairo 360, Aleksandra Sekinger, 23 June 2010). TransArtists lists the address as 22 Abdel Khalek Tharwat, 4th Floor, Downtown.
- DutchCulture reported in August 2025 that CiC lost its downtown location and secured a new space in the Garden City district.
- Facilities and programme: exhibitions, educational programmes, a library, a workshop space and printing facilities (Arab Fund for Arts and Culture; DutchCulture). TransArtists lists a lighting studio, a darkroom, a digital lab and two multi-purpose rooms.
- It has no fixed residency programme and invites artists to occasional residencies within its curated programme (TransArtists).

FUNDING
Contemporary Image Collective: independent non-profit with grant-funded projects
- The Arab Fund for Arts and Culture lists Contemporary Image Collective under the National Cultural Opportunities Fund (NCOF), 2024, Egypt, on its supported projects pages.
- TransArtists states that CiC seeks funds for all programming it commits to, and that participants in some programmes pay a fee that varies with the programme.

BOTTOM LINE
Contemporary Image Collective is an independent non-profit arts initiative founded in 2004. The Arab Fund for Arts and Culture lists a 2024 project under the National Cultural Opportunities Fund, and TransArtists states that CiC seeks funds for its programming.
