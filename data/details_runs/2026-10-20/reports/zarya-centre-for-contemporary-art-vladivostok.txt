ZARYA CENTRE FOR CONTEMPORARY ART: Vladivostok, Russia
ID: zarya-centre-for-contemporary-art-vladivostok
Website: https://zaryavladivostok.ru

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art centre in the Zarya creative cluster in Vladivostok, at prospect 100-letiya Vladivostoka 155, building 2, entrance 10, 2nd floor (stated by the centre).
- Opened in 2013 on the initiative of the entrepreneur and patron Alexander Mechetin, founder and co-owner of Novabev Group, formerly Beluga Group (stated by the centre; ASEF Culture360, 27 November 2015).
- Building: a former sewing factory that gave the cluster its name (ASEF Culture360; stated by the centre).
- Programmes: exhibitions of Russian and international artists, including Yuri Sobolev's islands (2016) and a Russian kinetic art show (2017); lectures, film screenings, children's programmes, a library and a shop (stated by the centre).
- The centre says its residency has run since mid-2014 and is the only one of its kind in the Russian Far East, and that by mid-2026 it had hosted artists from 22 countries and carried out more than 120 projects. ASEF Culture360 gives December 2014 as the residency's opening.
- Alisa Bagdonaite was chief curator from 2014 to 2022 (stated by the centre).
- Programme partners named by the centre include the State Tretyakov Gallery, the Moscow Museum of Modern Art, the Shchusev State Museum of Architecture, the Mayakovsky State Museum, the State Museum of Cinema and the ROSIZO State Museum-Exhibition Center.
- In November 2020 AiF-Primorye reported that Mechetin had decided to move the team to a new project, the Golubitskoe Art Foundation at the Golubitskoe estate winery in Krasnodar Krai, and that the Vladivostok centre would close after fulfilling its partnership obligations. The centre's news page now lists exhibitions in Vladivostok from 11 May 2026 to 11 April 2027, including "Na nozhkakh", organised with the Smena Centre for Contemporary Culture in Kazan from 26 September 2026, and residency events in summer 2026.
- Visiting: open daily 12:00 to 20:00; admission is free (stated by the centre).

FUNDING
Zarya Centre for Contemporary Art: private patronage: initiated by Alexander Mechetin, co-owner of Novabev Group
- The centre says it was launched on the initiative of Mechetin, founder and co-owner of Novabev Group, formerly Beluga Group; ASEF Culture360 calls him founder of Russia's largest alcoholic beverage company.
- AiF-Primorye (14 November 2020) calls Mechetin the founder of the centre, a patron and co-owner of Beluga Group.
- The centre's news page lists the 2026 show "Na nozhkakh", organised with the Smena Centre for Contemporary Culture in Kazan, and an event by the publishing house MIF on 4 to 6 September.

BOTTOM LINE
The centre is private: it was started in 2013 by Alexander Mechetin, co-owner of the spirits group Novabev, formerly Beluga Group. The State Tretyakov Gallery and other museums are named by the centre as partners in its exhibitions.
