YAMAGUCHI CENTER FOR ARTS AND MEDIA (YCAM): Yamaguchi, Japan
ID: yamaguchi-center-for-arts-and-media-ycam-yamaguchi
Website: https://www.ycam.jp

OVERVIEW
- Media art centre in Yamaguchi City, Yamaguchi Prefecture, on the south side of Yamaguchi City Central Park, about 20 minutes' walk from Yamaguchi Station (Wikipedia).
- Opened on 1 November 2003, after a planning process that began in the late 1980s and a construction pause in 2002 (YCAM history). Run under the Yamaguchi City Foundation for Cultural Promotion (YCAM annual report FY2021).
- Directors: Toru Yamamoto became General Director in 2005, Akeo Adachi Director in 2007, Hajime Masuda in 2018, Tadayuki Miwa in 2020, Motoo Isobe in 2022, and Masuda returned in 2024 (YCAM history).
- Building by the architect Arata Isozaki, with a wavelike roof inspired by the surrounding mountain ridgelines; it also houses the Yamaguchi City Central Library (Wikipedia).
- On site: Studio A (multipurpose hall), Studio B (exhibition hall), Studio C (100-seat cinema) and a BioLab. YCAM produces and shows media art, theatre and dance, film screenings, concerts, lectures and workshops, and its YCAM InterLab team makes original works and researches media technology (Wikipedia).
- Visiting, for the 2026 exhibition on improvisation (17 October 2026 to 1 March 2027): open 10:00 to 19:00, closed Tuesdays and from 29 December to 3 January; general admission 1,000 yen, 500 yen for ages 29 and under and for Yamaguchi City residents, free for ages 18 and under and for members (YCAM).

FUNDING
Yamaguchi Center for Arts and Media: public: Yamaguchi City pays most of the programme budget, with Agency for Cultural Affairs grants
- FY2021 programme budget 305,140,375 yen: subsidies and commissions from Yamaguchi City 270,556,583 yen (88.7%), grants such as those from the Agency for Cultural Affairs 9,937,864 yen (3.3%), ticket income 10,489,700 yen (3.4%), other income 14,156,228 yen (4.6%). Facility upkeep of 174,707,603 yen is paid from Yamaguchi City's management fee and other income (YCAM annual report FY2021).
- Organisers of the 2026 exhibition are Yamaguchi City and the Yamaguchi City Culture and Promotion Foundation; funding comes from the Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan, under its fiscal 2026 Cultural Arts Creation Base Formation Project (YCAM).
- Corporate collaborations are research and commission work run by YCAM InterLab: the JackIn workshop developed with Sony Computer Science Laboratories researcher Shunichi Kasahara, and "Colokaru Garden for LIL KIDS", which Mitsukoshi Isetan commissioned for its children's education project and installed at Isetan Shinjuku (YCAM).

BOTTOM LINE
YCAM is a city-organised centre run under a city foundation: Yamaguchi City supplied 88.7% of its FY2021 programme budget, and its 2026 exhibition is funded by Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs. Its corporate links are commissioned research and development projects with Sony Computer Science Laboratories and Mitsukoshi Isetan.
