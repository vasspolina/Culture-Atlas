LA FRICHE LA BELLE DE MAI: Marseille, France
ID: la-friche-la-belle-de-mai
Website: https://www.lafriche.org

OVERVIEW
- Cultural complex in the Belle de Mai neighbourhood of Marseille, in a former tobacco factory of the SEITA; the site says it has been converted into a cultural venue since 1992 (lafriche.org, French page).
- Run since 2007 by a Société Coopérative d'Intérêt Collectif (SCIC) with variable capital (lafriche.org, French page).
- Named for its neighbourhood, Belle de Mai (lafriche.org).
- Building and site: the converted factory buildings, including a rooftop terrace of 8,000 m² and about 2,400 m² of exhibition space (lafriche.org).
- On site: about 70 organisations and 400 artists and creatives, 5 concert venues, shared gardens, a bookshop, a crèche, a sports area, a restaurant and a training centre; about 600 public art events a year and over 450,000 visitors a year (all stated by the site).
- Visiting: open every day; Monday 11:00 to 18:00, Tuesday to Saturday 11:00 to 19:00, Sunday 13:00 to 19:00. Exhibitions are accessible to disabled visitors, but not all venues on site are.

FUNDING
La Friche la Belle de Mai: public support: the State, Région Sud, the Bouches-du-Rhône département and the City of Marseille
- The site names the State's regional prefect (Préfet de la Région PACA), Région Sud, the Département des Bouches-du-Rhône and the Ville de Marseille as its supporters; its board brings together site users, such as artists and operators, and the public institutions that have accompanied the project "for 20 years" (lafriche.org, French page, translation).
- Building works 2024 to 2027 on the "Cathédrales" building, estimated at 2.64 million euros: Ville de Marseille 600,000 euros (the city council vote was scheduled for 28 June 2024), the State and Région Sud each matching that amount under the 2022 to 2027 State-Region plan, the Département expected to give 400,000 euros, and the SCIC 400,000 euros (Made in Marseille, Margot Geay, 28 June 2024).
- The SCIC employs nearly 400 people (Made in Marseille).

BOTTOM LINE
The funders are the State, Région Sud, the Bouches-du-Rhône département and the City of Marseille, named by the site itself and in the 2024 works plan, with the cooperative adding 400,000 euros of its own to the 2.64 million euro works.
