JORDAN NATIONAL GALLERY OF FINE ARTS: Amman, Jordan
ID: jordan-national-gallery-of-fine-arts
Website: https://nationalgallery.org/ (checked 2026-10-24)

OVERVIEW
- Art gallery in Jabal al-Weibdeh, a historic district of Amman, in two buildings in a park; the gallery's own page describes a compound of four buildings around a sculpture park (Wikipedia, gallery site).
- Founded by the Royal Society of Fine Arts, a private non-profit, and inaugurated on 19 February 1980 under the patronage of the late King Hussein and Queen Noor; the Society was founded in Amman in 1979 (Wikipedia, gallery site).
- Princess Wijdan Ali was founding director and is president of the Society's board of trustees; Khalid Khreis is director (Wikipedia, gallery site).
- Mohamed al-Asad oversaw a renovation and expansion that won the Aga Khan Award for Architecture in 2007; the Wijdan Art Space opened in the park in 2023 (Wikipedia).
- The permanent collection has over 2,000 works by more than 800 artists from 59 countries, mainly Asia and Africa (Wikipedia); the gallery's own page says over 3,000 works, so the sources conflict.
- Visiting: 9:00 to 17:00, closed Fridays and public holidays; no admission information on the page.

FUNDING
Jordan National Gallery of Fine Arts: patronage-funded: Jordanian companies and banks, plus a UAE ministry and a Sharjah foundation
- The partners page, undated in its text with logos from 2024 and 2025, lists patrons by tier. Diamond: UAE Ministry of Culture and Youth, Hikma Pharmaceuticals, Jordan Bromine Company. Platinum: Barjeel Art Foundation, Arab Potash. Gold: Abdul Hameed Shoman Foundation.
- Silver: Jordan Phosphate Mines, Jordan Petroleum Refinery, Jordan Kuwait Bank. Bronze: Cairo Amman Bank, Jordan Ahli Bank, Bank of Jordan, Bank al Etihad and Kemapco.
- Barjeel Art Foundation is a non-profit in Sharjah, UAE, set up in 2010 by Sultan Sooud Al Qassemi; it lent works to a 2017 show at this gallery (Wikipedia).
- The Jordan Petroleum Refinery Company is owned by the Government of Jordan and runs the country's only oil refinery, in Zarqa (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of amounts, of what the UAE ministry pays for, or of any state grant from Jordan. The page names no accounts or annual reports.

Red flags: Gulf state body
- The UAE Ministry of Culture and Youth is a Diamond-tier patron (gallery partners page). No reporting found of a signed agreement or an amount.

Red flags: Fossil fuels
- Jordan Petroleum Refinery, a government-owned oil refiner, is a Silver-tier patron (gallery partners page, Wikipedia). Its 2025 net income was JD 74.79 million (Wikipedia).

Red flags: Arab Bank, parent of the Shoman Foundation
- Arab Bank created the Abdul Hameed Shoman Foundation in 1978 (Wikipedia, Arab Bank).
- On 22 September 2014 a US federal jury in Brooklyn found Arab Bank liable for knowingly supporting terrorism in 24 attacks; the bank settled with 597 victims or relatives in 2015, and the Second Circuit overturned the verdict on 9 February 2018 (Wikipedia, Arab Bank).
- In 2005 the OCC and FinCEN fined its New York branch $24 million for an inadequate anti-money laundering programme; the consent order ended on 1 October 2018 (Wikipedia, Arab Bank).

BOTTOM LINE
The funding is a patronage list of Jordanian companies and banks, with a UAE ministry at the top tier. The weak points are that Gulf ministry patron, a government-owned oil refiner, and the Arab Bank history behind the Shoman Foundation. The gallery publishes no amounts or accounts, so the size of any patron's gift is unknown.
