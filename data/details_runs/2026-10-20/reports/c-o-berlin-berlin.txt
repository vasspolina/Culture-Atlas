C/O BERLIN: Berlin, Germany
ID: c-o-berlin-berlin
Website: https://co-berlin.org/en (checked 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition venue for photography and visual media in the Amerika Haus, Hardenbergstraße 22 to 24, near Zoologischer Garten station; about 2,500 square metres (Wikipedia; stated by the space).
- A private initiative founded in 2000 by photographer Stephan Erfurt, designer Marc Naroska and architect Ingo Pott; now run by a charitable foundation (stated by the space).
- Began in the old Royal Post Office (Postfuhramt), moved to a former foundry at Linienstraße 144 in 2001, returned to the Postfuhramt in June 2006, left it in March 2013 and reopened at Amerika Haus on 30 October 2014 (Wikipedia).
- The Amerika Haus was built in 1956 to 1957 to plans by Bruno Grimmek; its renovation won the 2015 BDA Prize Berlin (stated by the space).
- Up to twelve exhibitions a year, the C/O Berlin Talent Award, the After Nature Prize with the Crespo Foundation since 2024, and education programmes; Katja Eichinger chairs the advisory board (Wikipedia; stated by the space).
- Visiting: open daily 11:00 to 20:00. Tickets €15, reduced €8, €4 for people on social benefits; free up to and including age 18 and for companions of visitors whose severe-disability pass carries the B mark. The lift was out of service when checked (stated by the space).

FUNDING
C/O Berlin: mixed: admissions and sponsorship with a Berlin state grant
- Income comes from admissions, book sales, sponsorship, donations and the friends' association C/O Berlin Friends e.V. (stated by the space).
- The Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion funds the foundation; the state of Berlin has supported it since 2020 (stated by the space; Wikipedia).
- LOTTO-Stiftung Berlin supported the reopening of the building (stated by the space).
- Corporate Friends at €5,000 a year each: DKB AG (bank), GASAG AG (energy) and Pfizer Deutschland GmbH (stated by the space).
- Partner logos, unnamed in text, show Airbnb, ATS and the Crespo Foundation (stated by the space, logos only).
- No reporting found of the size of the Senate grant or of annual accounts.

Red flags: fossil fuels (GASAG)
- GASAG is a corporate Friend at €5,000 a year (stated by the space, undated page).
- GASAG supplies about 800,000 household and business customers in Berlin with natural gas, biomethane and green electricity; revenue was about €1.72 billion in 2025 (German Wikipedia).
- Its owners are E.ON (36.85%), Engie (31.575%) and Vattenfall (31.575%); Engie and Vattenfall have controlled it jointly since 1 January 2021 (German Wikipedia; Energie & Management, December 2020).
- No reporting found of a protest, open letter or boycott aimed at C/O Berlin or its sponsors.

BOTTOM LINE
C/O Berlin runs on ticket and book income, sponsorship, a Berlin state grant and a small circle of corporate Friends. The one flagged funder is GASAG, a Berlin gas supplier owned by E.ON, Engie and Vattenfall, at €5,000 a year. Worth watching: the size of the Senate grant, and whether the Friends list changes.
