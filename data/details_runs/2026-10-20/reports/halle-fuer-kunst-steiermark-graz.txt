HALLE FÜR KUNST STEIERMARK: Graz, Austria
ID: halle-fuer-kunst-steiermark-graz
Website: https://halle-fuer-kunst.at

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition house for contemporary art at Burgring 2 in Graz; open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00, with free admission (e-flux directory, gallery website).
- Formerly the Künstlerhaus Graz. Art associations began collecting money for a Künstlerhaus in 1903; the funds were confiscated after the 1938 Anschluss, and the building, designed by architect Robert Haueisen, opened on 20 June 1952 (German Wikipedia).
- The Land Steiermark owns the building; since January 2013 the Kunstverein Medienturm has run the house and set its programme (German Wikipedia).
- From 2003 to 2012 the house was part of the Universalmuseum Joanneum, and it reopened as Halle für Kunst Steiermark in 2021 (German Wikipedia).
- Sandro Droschl, head of the Kunstverein Medienturm, has led it since 2013 (e-flux directory); German Wikipedia says he won the selection process in 2012.
- The building was renovated in 2010 to 2012 and again from 2020; the e-flux directory calls the early-1950s modernist pavilion one of the first white cubes in Austria.
- Programme: solo and thematic group exhibitions, performances and residencies; the Trigon biennial ran there from 1963 to 1995 (e-flux directory, German Wikipedia). The 2026 to 2027 programme includes Katherine Bernhardt (24 September 2026 to 10 January 2027) and Josef Strau with Leroy Johnson (5 March to 9 May 2027) (gallery website).
- In 2018 the Vereinigung Bildender Künstler Steiermark, the Sezession Graz and the Berufsvereinigung Bildender Künstler protested that their exhibition time had been cut from about half a year to 2.5 weeks for all associations together, and submitted 66 white canvases to a call for entries Droschl had issued (German Wikipedia).

FUNDING
HALLE FÜR KUNST Steiermark: public: Land Steiermark, the Austrian federal government and the City of Graz
- Kronen Zeitung (krone.at), in an undated article by Christoph Hartner on the house's relaunch as Halle für Kunst Steiermark, reported that the Land raised the house's budget by €142,000 to €500,000, the federal government contributed €140,000 (40 percent more), and the City of Graz funding stayed at €102,000.
- German Wikipedia says the building was constructed under the Land Steiermark as building client, with funds from the Land and the federal government; the Land remains its owner.

WIDER CONTEXT
- "Heimatpflege-Notstand: Wie geht es nach dem steirischen Kultur-Eklat weiter?" (profil, Stefan Grissemann and Wolfgang Paterno, 22 March 2025; title translated: "Emergency in heritage care: what happens after the Styrian culture scandal?"): IG Kultur Steiermark's managing director Lidija Krienzer-Radojević said "Wir befinden uns in einer äußerst prekären Lage" (translated: "We are in an extremely precarious situation") and expected the current culture budget not to hold the 2024 funding level. Edith Draxl, former chair of the Kuratorium, gave funding sums of about €2.5 million in 2024 and €1.4 million in 2025, with cuts of 12 to 70 percent or rejections. Culture councillor Karlheinz Kornhäusl said the culture budget rose from just over €60 million in 2019 to about €81 million, with most going to large institutions.
- Connection: the article does not name the Halle für Kunst. The Land Steiermark, whose culture budget it criticises, owns the building and pays €500,000 of the house's budget according to the Kronen Zeitung.

BOTTOM LINE
The Halle für Kunst Steiermark is a building owned by the Land Steiermark and run by an art association, paid for by the Land Steiermark, the Austrian federal government and the City of Graz. The Land's culture budget was criticised in profil in March 2025.
