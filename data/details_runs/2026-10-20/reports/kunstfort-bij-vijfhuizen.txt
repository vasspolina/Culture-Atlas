KUNSTFORT BIJ VIJFHUIZEN: Vijfhuizen, Netherlands
ID: kunstfort-bij-vijfhuizen
Website: https://www.kunstfort.nl

OVERVIEW
- Art centre in the Fort bij Vijfhuizen, at Fortwachter 1 on the edge of Haarlemmermeer, close to Schiphol airport (stated by the Kunstfort).
- The fort was built in 1889 to 1890 as one of the forts of the Stelling van Amsterdam, a defence line laid out between 1880 and 1914 (Wikipedia; Kunstfort). The line has been on the UNESCO World Heritage list since 1996.
- The fort lost its military function in 1963 and has had an artistic one since 2005 (stated by the Kunstfort).
- Director: Rabiaâ Benlahbib, who took over in 2022 (Haarlems Dagblad, 6 August 2022, as listed on the Kunstfort press page).
- On site: presentation spaces, studio homes for artists, a restaurant, a gin distillery, a workshop, offices and guest quarters; a Zoöp since November 2023, with Bonnie Chopard as its speaker for the living (stated by the Kunstfort).
- Programme: group exhibitions inside the fort, an outdoor exhibition, education for primary schools, concerts, and partnerships with Cinekid, the Cruquius Museum and the Grazende Zwaan festival.
- Visiting: the fort building is open Thursday to Sunday 13:00 to 17:00; admission EUR 5.50, students EUR 3.50, Museumjaarkaart holders free. The fort island and the outdoor exhibition are free every day. The fort and the Genieloods are open to wheelchairs, mobility scooters, rollators and prams, the peninsula less so; there is a wheelchair-accessible toilet in the Genieloods.

FUNDING
Kunstfort bij Vijfhuizen: public grants: Mondriaan Fund and, as stated by the Kunstfort, the municipality of Haarlemmermeer
- Mondriaan Fund: Stichting Kunstfort Activiteiten, Vijfhuizen holds a Kunstpodium Basis grant for 2025 to 2028; the grant list shows EUR 225,000, and the scheme description states Basis grants are EUR 225,000 per year (Mondriaan Fund).
- The Kunstfort states that it is supported by the municipality of Haarlemmermeer and the Mondriaan Fund, with thanks to Staatsbosbeheer and Recreatieschap Noord-Holland.
- Structure: two foundations, Stichting Kunstfort Activiteiten (programme, KvK 67368905, with an ANBI publication form for 2024 on the site) and Kunstfort Beheer (fort management), each with its own board listed on the site. The chair of the programme board is listed as vacant; the management board is chaired by Peter Oussoren.
- The partners page lists as fellow fort users Fort 5, Gin Stokerij and CIC Company, besides individual studio holders. Wikipedia states that the foundation leases space to twelve artist studios and a restaurant.
- Programme partners: the art academies St. Joost and HKU, CDCD (Constructive Dialogues), Stichting Cruquius Concerten and the Zoönomisch Instituut.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Kunstfort is a Mondriaan Fund Kunstpodium Basis grant of EUR 225,000 per year for 2025 to 2028 and, as the Kunstfort states, support from the municipality of Haarlemmermeer, with studio and restaurant leases (Wikipedia). Both foundation boards are listed on the site.
