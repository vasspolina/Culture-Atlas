MEDRAR FOR CONTEMPORARY ART: Cairo, Egypt
ID: medrar-for-contemporary-art-cairo
Website: https://medrar.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit art space for contemporary and media art at 10 Gamal El Din Abou El Mahasen Street, 8th floor, Garden City, Cairo (Medrar).
- Running since 2005; it grew out of a collective of visual artists working with video, whose first project, the Cairo Video Festival, is the project through which Medrar was later established (Cairo Video Festival; Experimental Cinema directory).
- Medrar's about page thanks Dia Hamed as co-founder and lists Mohamed Allam as director, Mena El Shazly as the festival's artistic director, and a staff of nine including a financial officer.
- Programmes: the Cairo Video Festival, Medrar Studio, MedRX, Roznama and Medrar TV, with workshops, a collaborative space for media artists, and documentation of Egypt's contemporary art scene (Medrar).
- The Cairo Video Festival shows low-budget video art and experimental film chosen from an open call, with screenings, artist talks and discussions (Cairo Video Festival); the 9th edition was set for September 2019, the 10th edition's page is dated 2021, and the 11th was called for December 2024 (Experimental Cinema directory; festival site).

FUNDING
Medrar for Contemporary Art: grant-funded: foreign governments and cultural agencies, arts foundations and the Arab Fund for Arts and Culture
- Founding support, as Medrar's about page lists it: the Young Arab Theatre Fund, the Foundation for Arts Initiatives, Culture Resource's Abbara Program, the Roberto Cimetta Fund, the Netherlands Embassy in Cairo, and the Arab Fund for Arts and Culture (AFAC) through a grant from the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation.
- AFAC lists a 2019 grant from its North Africa Cultural Program for Medrar to find "a long-term sustainable solution for its financial stability" and widen its outreach.
- 10th Cairo Video Festival (2021): supported by the U.S. Embassy in Cairo, the Embassy of the Netherlands in Cairo, the Italian Cultural Institute in Cairo, the British Council in Egypt, Pro Helvetia Cairo, the Japan Foundation, Cairo, and the French Institute in Cairo; the festival credits say Medrar itself runs with AFAC support through the Swiss development agency grant (festival site).
- 11th Cairo Video Festival (2024): funded by the European Union in collaboration with EUNIC Egypt, and supported by Pro Helvetia Cairo, the French Embassy and French Institute in Egypt, the Italian Cultural Institute and the Canada Council for the Arts (festival site).

BOTTOM LINE
Medrar's money is project grants, none from a flagged funder. The weak point is its dependence on renewing them.
