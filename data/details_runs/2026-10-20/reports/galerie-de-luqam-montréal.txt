GALERIE DE L'UQAM: Montréal, Canada
ID: galerie-de-luqam-montréal
Website: https://galerie.uqam.ca/

OVERVIEW
- University contemporary art gallery in the Pavillon Judith-Jasmin, room J-R120, 1400 rue Berri, Montréal (stated by the gallery).
- Part of the Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM). French Wikipedia dates the gallery to 1975 and its move to the Pavillon Judith-Jasmin to 1979.
- French Wikipedia lists Louise Déry as director.
- It shows Quebec, Canadian and international contemporary art, with performances, film screenings, guided tours and round tables (Musées du Québec guide).
- It also looks after the UQAM art collection, which began in 1969 with a donation of nearly 3,000 prints from the former École des beaux-arts de Montréal (French Wikipedia); Esse (2020) puts the collection at over 4,000 works.
- Visiting: open Tuesday to Saturday, 12 to 6 p.m.

FUNDING
Galerie de l'UQAM: mainly public: a university gallery with the Canada Council for the Arts and the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec (CALQ) as named partners
- The gallery's website names three partners: the Conseil des arts du Canada, CALQ and UQAM (stated by the gallery).
- French Wikipedia says the gallery is funded by the Canada Council for the Arts.
- UQAM is a public university in the Université du Québec network (French Wikipedia).
- The gallery's "Faire un don" link leads to the online donation page of the UQAM Foundation (stated by the gallery).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the gallery is public: its parent is a public university, and the Canada Council for the Arts and the Quebec arts council CALQ are its named partners.
