SERLACHIUS MUSEUMS: Mänttä, Finland
ID: serlachius-museums-mänttä
Website: https://www.serlachius.fi (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Art museum in Mänttä, Finland, on two sites: Serlachius Manor (Kartano, Joenniementie 47) and Serlachius Pääkonttori (R. Erik Serlachiuksen katu 2) in central Mänttä, the former G. A. Serlachius headquarters (Serlachius).
- The paper mill owners began collecting art more than a hundred years ago; the collection holds about 10,000 works (Serlachius). It centres on Finland's Golden Age art, with old European art, older Finnish art, early modernist works and a growing body of contemporary art (foundation page).
- The Gösta Serlachius Fine Arts Foundation, established in 1933 to preserve and build Gösta Serlachius's art collection and "beautifying Mänttä with art", maintains the museum complex and the Serlachius Residency (foundation page).
- Joenniemi Manor was built in 1935 (Finnish Architecture) and thoroughly restored in 1983 and 1984 and taken into use as an art museum; the foundation bought the former G. A. Serlachius headquarters in 2000 (foundation page).
- Gösta's Pavilion, a glass and timber extension, was designed by the Barcelona studio MX_SI with the local partner Huttunen-Lipasti-Pakkanen Architects after an open competition in 2011, and has 5,700 square metres of gross area (Finnish Architecture); the foundation page dates the new museum building to summer 2014.
- Beside the galleries there are Restaurant Gösta, museum shops at both sites, an art sauna with public days every Tuesday, and an artist residency. The museum is open 11:00 to 18:00 and the site calls it largely accessible. An Anish Kapoor exhibition runs from 27 June 2026 to 4 April 2027 at Serlachius Manor (Serlachius).

FUNDING
Serlachius Museums: foundation-run: the Gösta Serlachius Fine Arts Foundation, established in 1933 to preserve and build Gösta Serlachius's art collection
- The foundation maintains the Serlachius museum complex and residency and bought the former G. A. Serlachius headquarters in 2000 (foundation page).
- Board: the Serlachius family association Sissi och Gösta Serlachius Ättlingar r.f. appoints four members: Henrik de la Chapelle (chair), Susanna Serlachius-Pressler (vice chair), John Lindahl and Gösta Serlachius. The Finnish Academy of Fine Arts sends Marjukka Korhonen, Åbo Akademi sends Marie-Sofie Lundström and the Artists' Association of Finland sends Maija Helasvuo (foundation page).
- In 1986, when the G. A. Serlachius company merged with Metsäliitto Industries, the foundation's rules were revised and the company's board representatives were replaced by representatives of the family association (foundation page).

BOTTOM LINE
The museums are maintained by the Gösta Serlachius Fine Arts Foundation, established in 1933 to preserve and build the art collection of Gösta Serlachius; the museum site says the paper mill owners began that collecting more than a hundred years ago. The foundation page names its board: four seats from the Serlachius family association and one each from the Finnish Academy of Fine Arts, Åbo Akademi and the Artists' Association of Finland.
