TIRANA ART LAB: Tirana, Albania
ID: tirana-art-lab
Website: https://www.tiranaartlab.org/en (checked 2026-10-25; loads)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit centre for contemporary art in Tirana with no permanent venue; it works with local partners and keeps an office and project space at Pallati i Ri, Rruga Isa Boletini, open by appointment (Tirana Art Lab website).
- Started in 2010. The website lists Adela Demetja as former founding director, 2010 to 2025, and Resina Meçani as executive director (Tirana Art Lab website).
- History: 2010 to 2014 events in Tirana, Shkodra and Pogradec; 2014 to 2018 an independent space in central Tirana with an exhibition space, a residency space and a public library of contemporary art books; since 2018 a focus on research and archiving, including Tirana Floating Archive, Tirana Floating Tours and Secondary Archive (Tirana Art Lab website).
- Building and site: no permanent location since 2018. For Performative Exhibition #01: Center of Integration (24 to 29 July 2018) the former premises were set up as an apartment (project page).
- What it runs: residencies, exhibitions, workshops, performances, lectures, publications, and from 2023 "Curating with Care", an alternative curatorial education programme with the Istituto Italiano di Cultura in Tirana. The Beyond Matter EU project (2019 to 2023) involved partners including ZKM Karlsruhe, Centre Pompidou, Ludwig Museum, Tallinn Art Hall, Aalto University and EPFL.
- Visiting: no public opening hours; the office is open by appointment. A Secondary Archive open call for women artists runs 15 September to 15 November 2026.

FUNDING
Tirana Art Lab: project-grant funded: EU, European foundations and cultural institutes, Albanian ministry and city
- Supporters named on its website: Creative Europe Programme, Allianz Kulturstiftung, Robert Bosch Foundation, Pro Helvetia, European Cultural Foundation, Goethe-Zentrum Tirana, Swiss Cultural Fund Albania, the Italian Ministry of Cultural Heritage and Tourism, the Albanian Ministry of Culture and the Municipality of Tirana, plus several embassies. The page gives no amounts or years.
- Beyond Matter: co-funded by the Creative Europe programme of the European Union, with Tallinn Art Hall and Hertz-Lab of ZKM Karlsruhe as partners; its residency call (2021 to 2022) offered three two-month residencies in Tirana covering travel, accommodation, a stipend and production costs (Biennial Foundation).
- Performative Exhibition #01 (2018): supported by the Italian Culture Institute in Tirana and a STEP travel grant managed by the European Cultural Foundation with support from Compagnia di San Paolo. No amounts are given (project page).
- Curating with Care: run with the Istituto Italiano di Cultura in Tirana (Tirana Art Lab website).
- No reporting found of annual accounts, an annual report, a standing funder page or a named board.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism found in art media.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the lab is project grants from the EU, German, Swiss and Italian foundations and cultural bodies, and Albanian ministry and city support, none from a flagged industry. The weak points are that no accounts, board or amounts are published, and that supporters are credited only per project. Worth watching: the leadership listing after the founding director's 2025 end date and how the Secondary Archive and Curating with Care are paid for.
