TRIENNALE MILANO: Milan, Italy
ID: triennale-milano
Website: https://triennale.org (checked 2026-10-24; returned 403 "forbidden" to every fetch, so its own pages could not be read)

OVERVIEW
- Design, architecture and art institution in the Palazzo dell'Arte in Parco Sempione, Milan, hosting the Milan Triennale international exhibition, the Museo del Design Italiano (opened 2007), a theatre and exhibitions (Wikipedia).
- The first exhibition was held in 1923 at the Villa Reale in Monza; the Triennale moved to Milan in 1933, became an autonomous legal body in 1931, and resumed the International Exhibition in 2016 after a pause of about twenty years (Italian Wikipedia).
- Founded by Guido Marangoni and the Bernocchi foundation (Italian Wikipedia).
- Palazzo dell'Arte was designed by Giovanni Muzio and built 1931 to 1933, financed by Antonio Bernocchi and his brothers Andrea and Michele (Wikipedia).
- Exhibitions, a design museum, the Teatro dell'Arte and a project library (Italian Wikipedia).
- Visiting: a search listing of the official site gives Tuesday to Sunday, exhibitions 10:30 to 20:00, last entry 19:00; travel guides give museum admission of about EUR 15, which could not be checked on the official site.

FUNDING
Triennale Milano: governed by public founding bodies, with corporate partners; revenue split not confirmed
- Governance: the nine-member board is drawn from the founding bodies: the Ministry of Culture (Stefano Zecchi, Dario Rinero), the Comune di Milano (Donatella Sciuto, Alberto Toffoletto), Regione Lombardia (Davide Rampello) and the Chamber of Commerce (Regina De Albertis, Antonio Calabrò), with Maria Adele Porro appointed jointly. Vincenzo Trione became president on 4 June 2026, succeeding Stefano Boeri (Artribune, 4 June 2026).
- The Italian Parliament's file on the foundation's accounts (Doc. XV) was listed, but the PDF text could not be extracted, so no revenue or grant figures could be confirmed.
- 23rd International Exhibition (15 July to 11 December 2022): Eni and Lavazza Group were Main Partners; Franklin Templeton, ViveArts and Scalo Milano Outlet & More were other partners; Salone del Mobile.Milano and ATM were institutional partners (Design Diffusion).
- Lavazza has been an institutional partner since 2019, and Deloitte a partner for 2025 to 2027, per a search listing of the Triennale partnership page, which could not be opened.
- Progetto Genesi, "Art and Human Rights", ran at the Triennale from 22 February to 2 April 2023, organised by Associazione Genesi, with Eni as main sponsor, per a listing of the Triennale event page.
- No reporting found, on pages I could open, of Eni's partnership amounts.

Red flags: Eni, oil and gas partner
- Eni was Main Partner of the 23rd International Exhibition in 2022 (Design Diffusion).
- Eni was main sponsor of Progetto Genesi at the Triennale in 2023 (event listing).
- Eni is an Italy-based energy group; the Italian state holds a 33.084% stake (Wikipedia).
- No reporting found of a protest or open letter aimed at Eni's partnership at the Triennale.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: No criticism of the institution found in art media. The criticism found concerns its former president Stefano Boeri, in Italian news.
1. Il Giorno: article on Boeri's suspension request (25 February 2025)
URL: https://www.ilgiorno.it/milano/cronaca/boeri-triennale-sospensione-bua4a1pg
- A judge barred Boeri for one year from taking part in architecture competitions, in an inquiry into the 2022 BEIC library competition, where prosecutors allege he and Cino Zucchi recognised the winners in advance and falsely declared no conflict of interest.
- On 24 February 110 professionals asked the Fondazione Triennale and the founding bodies to suspend him, saying the measure conflicts with the foundation's ethical code. Boeri's defence denies the accusations.
- Not art press; names the Triennale as the body he presides over.
2. ANSA via Espansione TV: report on the letter (25 February 2025)
URL: https://www.espansionetv.it/2025/02/25/architetti-e-urbanisti-boeri-venga-sospeso-dalla-triennale
- Same facts: the judge rejected the prosecutors' request for house arrest. Does not criticise the Triennale's funding.

BOTTOM LINE
The Triennale is run by public founding bodies, though its accounts could not be read, and it has taken oil and gas money from Eni as a main partner (2022) and main sponsor of a show (2023). The weak points are Eni and the 2025 inquiry into then-president Boeri; Trione took over in June 2026. Watch the new partner list and the foundation's accounts.
