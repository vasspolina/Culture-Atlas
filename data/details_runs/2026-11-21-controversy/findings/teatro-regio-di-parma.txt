ID: teatro-regio-di-parma

WIDER CONTEXT
- Parma prosecutors investigated Mayor Federico Pizzarotti, the city's culture councillor and the members of the theatre's board for abuse of office over appointments at the Teatro Regio; on 16 September 2016 the preliminary investigations judge Paola Artusi archived the case (La Notizia, 16 September 2016).
- La Nazione reported on 1 March 2023 that Anna Maria Meo, who ended her post as general director of the Teatro Regio in October 2022, "dovrà rispondere davanti al tribunale di peculato" ("will have to answer in court for embezzlement", translated) "nell'ambito dell'inchiesta della procura di Parma sui finanziamenti statali al teatro Regio in cui è coinvolto anche l'ex sindaco Federico Pizzarotti" ("in the Parma prosecutors' inquiry into state funding of the Teatro Regio, which also involves the former mayor Federico Pizzarotti", translated) (La Nazione, Ilaria Ulivelli, 1 March 2023).
