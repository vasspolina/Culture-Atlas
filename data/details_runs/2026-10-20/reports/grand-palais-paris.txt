GRAND PALAIS (RMN-GRAND PALAIS): Paris, France
ID: grand-palais-paris
Website: https://www.grandpalais.fr

OVERVIEW
- Exhibition hall and cultural venue in Paris, run by GrandPalaisRmn, which also runs the Ateliers d'art, museum shops, a photo agency and a publishing house (Grand Palais website).
- Built for the 1900 Universal Exposition on the site of the demolished Palais de l'Industrie; four architects, Henri Deglane, Albert Louvet, Albert Thomas and Charles Girault, shared the commission, and the palace opened on 1 May 1900 (Wikipedia).
- Designed in Beaux-Arts style, with stone facades, glass vaults and a metal structure; the nave is about 200 m long and 50 m wide, and the glazed roof covers about 14,000 square metres (Wikipedia).
- Closed in March 2021 for a four-year renovation; it hosted fencing and taekwondo at the Paris 2024 Olympics and reopened fully in June 2025 (Wikipedia, France.fr).
- The renovation added a new public entrance, lifts and a Children's Palace (Wikipedia). From June 2025 the nave hosts co-produced exhibitions with the Centre Pompidou, which is closed for works from 2025 to 2030 (France.fr).
- Programme listed on the venue's site includes exhibitions, concerts, festivals, trade fairs, workshops and film screenings; shows listed for 2026 include "Cezanne et nous" (23 September 2026 to 17 January 2027) and Paris Photo (12 to 15 November 2026).
- The site's accessibility statement reads "partly compliant".

FUNDING
Grand Palais: mainly public: the French state paid most of the renovation, with Chanel as private patron
- Renovation budget of €466 million: the French government €288 million, a €150 million loan from the Rmn, and €25 million from Chanel, which in return gets the ornamental entrance renamed after the house (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 13 February 2018).
- In September 2020 Rmn-Grand Palais scaled the renovation back, citing "financial drift" and "slippage in the schedule" (The Art Newspaper, Gareth Harris, 28 September 2020). Wikipedia gives €466 million as the cost of the overhaul.
- Chanel committed a new patronage of €30 million for the 2024 to 2028 artistic and cultural programme of GrandPalaisRmn, and has held its fashion shows at the Grand Palais since 2005 (Numero, Juan Luca Wick, 11 September 2024).
- The 2001 to 2007 restoration had a budget of €101.36 million, funded by the French government through the Ministry of Culture (Wikipedia).

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the institution. The trade press covered the cost overrun of the renovation.

- Rmn-Grand Palais took over the project, citing "financial drift" and "slippage in the schedule", and cut back the original plans, which The Art Newspaper Daily in France put at more than €600 million.
- Names no outside critic; concerns about finishing before the 2024 Olympics are raised without attribution.

BOTTOM LINE
The Grand Palais renovation budget of €466 million came from the French government (€288 million), a €150 million loan from the Rmn and €25 million from Chanel (The Art Newspaper, 2018); Chanel committed a further €30 million for the 2024 to 2028 programme (Numero, 2024). The documented weak point is the 2020 renovation overrun that forced the plans to be cut back.
