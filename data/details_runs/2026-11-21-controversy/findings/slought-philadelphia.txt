ID: slought-philadelphia

WIDER CONTEXT
- Slought Foundation and Penn Social Policy & Practice cancelled the opening of "An Anthology of Existences", an online archive about three local transgender women of color, planned for 28 April 2016 at Slought, the night before, after criticism on the event's Facebook page questioned the direct involvement of transgender women of color (Philadelphia Gay News, Jen Colletta, "Penn event cancelled amid community concerns", 28 April 2016).
- Nellie Fitzpatrick, director of the city's Office of LGBT Affairs, said she had not given the organisers permission to use her name in promotional materials. The organisers' cancellation message said: "we recognize that considerable efforts must be taken to do better and apologize for any pain this may have caused" (Philadelphia Gay News).
