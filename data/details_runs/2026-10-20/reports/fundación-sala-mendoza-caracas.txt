FUNDACIÓN SALA MENDOZA: Caracas, Venezuela
ID: fundación-sala-mendoza-caracas
Website: https://fundacionsalamendoza.com/

OVERVIEW
- Independent, non-profit exhibition space run as a civil association that practises self-management (Fundación Sala Mendoza).
- It states a continuous commitment to art creation and knowledge exchange since 1956 (Fundación Sala Mendoza).
- Started by Luisa Rodríguez de Mendoza and Eugenio Mendoza (Fundación Sala Mendoza).
- Located in the Urbanización Terrazas del Ávila inside the Universidad Metropolitana campus, on the ground floor of the Edificio Eugenio Mendoza Goiticoa (Fundación Sala Mendoza). Wikipedia describes Eugenio Mendoza Goiticoa (1906 to 1979) as a Venezuelan industrialist who founded Universidad Metropolitana in 1970.
- Four pillars: exhibitions, education (workshops, talks and diplomas), a documentation centre and a bookstore for local talent; it also runs the Premio Mendoza, which selects 20 artists each year (Fundación Sala Mendoza).
- Luisa Mariana Pulido is president, Antonio Fonseca is general manager and Humberto Valdivieso is director (Fundación Sala Mendoza).
- Open Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 15:00 (Fundación Sala Mendoza).

FUNDING
Fundación Sala Mendoza: started by Luisa Rodríguez de Mendoza and Eugenio Mendoza; runs a bookstore and an Amigos de la Sala membership programme
- The founders are Luisa Rodríguez de Mendoza and Eugenio Mendoza (stated by the space).
- The Premio Mendoza is an initiative of the Fundación Sala Mendoza (stated by the space).
- The space runs a bookstore and a membership programme, Amigos de la Sala (stated by the space).

BOTTOM LINE
The Sala Mendoza was started by Luisa Rodríguez de Mendoza and Eugenio Mendoza and works as an independent civil association with its own bookstore, membership programme and prize. It is housed in a building named for Eugenio Mendoza Goiticoa on the Universidad Metropolitana campus.
