ID: odesa-national-fine-arts-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- The Odesa Regional Council dismissed the museum's director, Oleksandr Roitburd, by decision No. 1050-VII of 4 September 2019, citing breaches of several points of his contract (Supreme Court of Ukraine press service, 10 February 2020).
- The Prymorskyi District Court of Odesa ruled on 10 February 2020 that the council's decision was unlawful and cancelled it (Supreme Court of Ukraine press service, 10 February 2020).
- The Odesa Court of Appeal reversed that ruling on 1 June 2020. The stated grounds were holding concerts, refusing council deputies an inspection of the storerooms and carrying out repairs unlawfully; Roitburd objected that the court ruled without the parties present and said the repairs, paid for without state money, saved works from a building in a state of emergency (24 Kanal, 3 June 2020).
- The head of the Odesa Regional State Administration, Maksym Kutsyi, backed Roitburd and said his contract would not be terminated (24 Kanal, 3 June 2020).
- In a ruling of 19 May 2021 the Supreme Court upheld the Prymorskyi court's decision that the dismissal was unlawful and overturned the appeal ruling; the decision is final (Texty.org.ua, 2 June 2021).
