BENGAL FOUNDATION: Dhaka, Bangladesh
ID: bengal-foundation
Website: https://bengalfoundation.org (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Non-profit cultural trust headquartered at Bengal Shilpalay in Dhanmondi, Dhaka, with programmes in visual art, music, film, crafts, publishing, theatre and education (Wikipedia, foundation website).
- Founded in 1986 by the industrialist Abul Khair, known as Litu, to "promote and showcase Bangladeshi art on a larger platform"; he still chairs it (Wikipedia, Bengal Institute).
- Bengal Shilpalay opened in 2000 with a solo show by Mohammad Kibria and reopened in April 2019 as a renovated, museum-like space with two exhibition halls; Khair said it would no longer run commercial operations and planned to show around 8,000 works from its own collection (The Daily Star, 8 April 2019).
- Since 1999 it has run international art camps with nearly 300 artists (Wikipedia).
- Units include the S M Sultan Bengal Art College, the Bengal Parampara Sangeetalay music school, Bengal Publications and the Bengal Classical Music Festival, which it calls the world's largest in performers, capacity and duration (stated by the space).
- Luva Nahid Choudhury, a founder trustee, is director-general; the board includes the architects Marina Tabassum and Kazi Khaleed Ashraf and the scholar Fakrul Alam (foundation website).

FUNDING
Bengal Foundation: family company money: a private trust funded by Abul Khair Litu's Bengal Group
- Registered as a private trust and "funded entirely by the Bengal Group" (Wikipedia, Bengal Institute).
- The foundation states that it is "independently funded by the efforts and means of the Board of Trustees" (foundation website).
- Source of the money: Khair chairs Bengal Group Ltd. and with partners set up AB Bank, Bangladesh's first private bank, in 1981 (foundation website, Bengal Institute).
- Founder trustees named by the foundation include Shafquat Hussain Khundkar, Shah Sayed Kamal, Nawshin Khair and Luva Nahid Choudhury (foundation website).

Red flags: 2024 corruption charge sheet against the founder
- In July 2024 the Anti-Corruption Commission approved a charge sheet against Litu, chairman of Bengal Group Limited, for acquiring illegal assets of over Tk 903 million beyond his known income and concealing more than Tk 1.24 million in his asset declaration (The Financial Express, 12 July 2024).
- The ACC case was filed on 23 April 2017, and on 1 November 2017 the ACC questioned him on charges including embezzlement of money from banks and tax dodging (The Financial Express).
- The ACC also found illegal assets of Tk 25.26 million beyond the known income of his wife, Mahua Khair (The Financial Express).

Red flags: 2007 detention of the founder
- In June 2007 a Dhaka court sent Litu, then Bengal Group chairman, to Dhaka Central Jail on 30 days' detention after a three-day remand that followed his arrest in Gulshan (The Daily Star, 9 June 2007).

Not related: Bengal Group of Industries. The Dhaka conglomerate behind Bengal Plastic was founded in 1969 by Morshed Alam, who chairs it; its Wikipedia page does not mention Litu or the foundation, whose funder is Bengal Group Ltd.

BOTTOM LINE
One family company pays for the foundation, so its money is only as clean as its founder's, and he faces an anti-corruption charge sheet.
