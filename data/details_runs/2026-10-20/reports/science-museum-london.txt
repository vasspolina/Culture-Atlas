SCIENCE MUSEUM: London, UK
ID: science-museum-london
Website: https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/ (checked 2026-10-24; working)

OVERVIEW
- National science museum on Exhibition Road, South Kensington, London SW7 2DD, part of the Science Museum Group of five museums.
- Founded in 1857 from the Royal Society of Arts collection and Great Exhibition surplus; independent since 1909 (Wikipedia).
- Sir Richard Allison designed the present building, opened in stages from 1919 to 1928; the Wellcome Wing followed in 2000 (Wikipedia).
- Galleries include the Energy Hall, Exploring Space, Making the Modern World, Wonderlab, Flight and an IMAX cinema; 2,817,852 visitors in 2025 (Wikipedia).
- Visiting: open daily 10.00 to 18.00, closed 24 to 26 December; free entry with a ticket booked online; Wonderlab, Power Up and IMAX are paid extras (museum website).

FUNDING
Science Museum: mainly public with corporate sponsors: DCMS grant, donations and sponsors
- Science Museum Group, year ending March 2023: total income 128 million pounds; 63% was grant in aid from the Department for Culture, Media and Sport; donations, grants and sponsors gave 24.4 million pounds (19%), including 13.6 million in donations and legacies; trading income was 20.1 million (Civil Society, 16 August 2023).
- Adani Green Energy: sponsors the Energy Revolution gallery; no amount is published (Museums Association).
- BP: sponsored the museum for nearly 25 years, including the former Energy Gallery, the 2015 Cosmonauts exhibition and the training academy; ended 31 August 2026 (The Art Newspaper). No amount is published.
- Equinor: sponsorship ended in 2024 (The Art Newspaper).
- No reporting found of a full sponsor list for 2023-24.

Red flags: BP sponsorship, fossil fuels
- BP's sponsorship of the Science Museum Group lasted nearly 25 years. The Group confirmed on 28 August 2026 that the partnership, by then sponsorship of the museum's training academy, would end on 31 August "at the end of its agreed term". It called terms "commercially sensitive" and said reports of a ten-year deal to 2028 were inaccurate (The Art Newspaper, 31 August 2026).
- Teachers pledged to boycott school trips to the museum until it ended its partnerships with BP and Adani, a campaign backed by the National Education Union (The Art Newspaper, 31 August 2026). In June 2022, 400 teachers signed a letter refusing to bring students to fossil fuel-sponsored exhibitions (USA Art News, 1 March 2023).
- Culture Unstained, a campaign group, adds a Prospect union staff survey showing unease (28 August 2026).

Red flags: Adani Green Energy, fossil fuels
- Adani Green sponsors Energy Revolution, announced in 2021 and opened in March 2024 (Museums Association, 28 March 2024). Adani Green is part of the Adani Group, which The Art Newspaper describes as the world's largest private developer of coal mines.
- 2021: trustees Hannah Fry and Jo Foster resigned from the Group's board over the Adani sponsorship, and former director Chris Rapley left its advisory board (The Art Newspaper).
- 23 March 2024: more than 150 protesters hung a 12-metre banner in the Energy Hall. Campaigners say a due diligence report on Adani's coal mining was buried (Museums Association).
- Unverified: that Adani Group companies manipulated their share prices, an allegation by Hindenburg Research, reported by USA Art News (1 March 2023); Adani-linked companies lost more than 100 billion dollars in share value.
- Chief executive Ian Blatchford said the trustees disagree with calls to disengage from entire sectors (Museums Association).

Red flags: campaigns
- Unverified: that BP's 2015 Cosmonauts sponsorship promoted its Rosneft stake and that Blatchford received Russia's Pushkin Medal from Putin for hosting it, from Culture Unstained, an openly anti-oil-sponsorship group (28 August 2026; no date for the medal).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is mainly state-funded; sponsors, donors and grants gave about a fifth of income in 2022-23. The weak points are Adani Green's continuing sponsorship and BP's nearly 25-year record, which ended on 31 August 2026. Watch whether the Adani deal ends and whether sponsor terms are published.
