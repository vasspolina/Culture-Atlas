ID: deutsche-oper-berlin

Red flags: 2025-26 strike and union complaint over outside workers
- Staff of the Deutsche Oper Berlin took part in a strike round in the Stiftung Oper in Berlin between December 2025 and February 2026: the Stiftung counted 41 strikers at the house on 18 December 2025, 37 on 23 January 2026 and 39 on 11 February 2026 (Berlin Senate, answer to written question 19/25181, 23 February 2026).
- Unverified: the union ver.di accused the Stiftung Oper in Berlin of trying to undercut the strike with outside workers, formally self-employed people and student assistants, from a ver.di press release of 6 February 2026 cited in the Senate answer.
- The Senate said it had received no indication of planned or actual strike-breaking; the Stiftung Oper said the right to strike is respected in all its houses, the Deutsche Oper Berlin included, and that it employs no student assistants (Berlin Senate answer, 23 February 2026).
- The same answer states that the Deutsche Oper Berlin works with one temporary staffing firm, ZAG Zeitarbeits-Gesellschaft GmbH Berlin, to cover staff absences and moving work during building works (Berlin Senate answer, 23 February 2026).
