MONTRESSO ART FOUNDATION (JARDIN ROUGE): Marrakech, Morocco
ID: montresso-art-foundation-jardin-rouge-marrakech
Website: https://www.montresso.com (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Artist residency and art space among olive groves about half an hour's drive from Marrakech, the last stretch through a village, run by the Montresso Art Foundation (Arterritory, Una Meistere, 16 February 2020); the foundation says it has been settled in Morocco since 2009 (Rivet).
- A private initiative of the French collector and patron Jean-Louis Haguenauer, who bought the land in 2002; Jardin Rouge opened in 2009 (Arterritory).
- The foundation has existed as a legal entity since 1981 and first held a collection begun in the 1980s; its patronage grew in the 1990s with creative workshops for young Russian artists, then group shows such as one at the Laennec hospital in Paris in September 2012 (director Estelle Guilié to Artribune, December 2021).
- On site: eight artists' studios and, since 2016, a 1,300 m2 exhibition space designed by Haguenauer that holds at least three shows a year; about thirty artists a year, many with a street-art background, and eighty in all by early 2020 (Arterritory); The Art Newspaper France (2022) gives about forty residencies a year, with open calls since 2017.
- The residency is open to visual artists of any nationality who show a significant career, for one to three months; a committee of the founder, the artistic director and members of the foundation chooses them (TransArtists).
- Public projects: the foundation took part in the Mohammed VI Museum's 2018 show "Jonone, Illuminer le Futur" and supported Hendrik Beikirch's large public murals in Marrakech and Rabat for a museum project (Artribune).
- Exhibitions include "XXL", with the graffiti artists Mohamed Saïd Chair, Poes and Skunkdog, in April 2019 (Brooklyn Street Art; Aujourd'hui le Maroc).
- Visiting: by appointment only (Arterritory; TransArtists; ONArt).

FUNDING
Montresso Art Foundation (Jardin Rouge): private: the collector Jean-Louis Haguenauer's money, plus sales to a friends' circle
- The foundation runs on an endowment that comes directly from Haguenauer; in recent years works made in residence have also been offered to collectors in the Amis de la Fondation Montresso group, and that money adds to his contribution (Artribune, 2021).
- Haguenauer was born in southern France and spends most of his time in Marrakech and Moscow, with which he has had close business ties since the 1980s (Arterritory, 2020); The Art Newspaper France calls him a businessman (Olivier Rachet, 3 August 2022).
- The foundation pays for the production of each approved project, transport, individual accommodation, meals, equipment and a studio (TransArtists); ONArt says it provides the artist with the material and financial means.
- The foundation's collection consists of work by its residents, and it planned a museum for that collection and Haguenauer's own (Arterritory, 2020).

BOTTOM LINE
The money on record raises no flag. Worth watching: which businesses the founder's fortune comes from.
