DR. BHAU DAJI LAD MUMBAI CITY MUSEUM: Mumbai, India
ID: dr-bhau-daji-lad-mumbai-city-museum
Website: https://www.bdlmuseum.org (checked 2026-10-24; returned an "Access Denied" error page, so no content could be read)

OVERVIEW
- City museum in Byculla East, near the Byculla Zoo in Jijamata Udyan (Rani Baug), Mumbai (Wikipedia).
- The idea dates to about 1850 and the 1851 Great Exhibition in London; the present building was built from 1862 to 1871, with David Sassoon, Sir Jamsetjee Jejeebhoy and Jagannath Shankarseth among its funders, and opened on 2 May 1872 as the Victoria and Albert Museum, Bombay (Wikipedia).
- Renamed on 1 November 1975 for Dr. Bhau Daji Lad, a physician, philanthropist, historian and Mumbai's first Indian Sheriff (Wikipedia).
- Wikipedia does not name the original architect; Municipal Commissioner Bhushan Gagrani described the building as a small-scale replica of the State Hermitage Museum in Saint Petersburg (ETV Bharat).
- Collection: archaeological finds, historical maps and photographs, a 17th-century Hatim Tai manuscript, the Kala Ghoda statue and an Elephanta basalt elephant (Wikipedia). It won the 2005 UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award of Excellence in Cultural Conservation.
- Visiting: open every day except Wednesday (ETV Bharat). It reopened in January 2025 (Wikipedia gives 8 January; Knocksense says 9 January) after more than four years closed, first for COVID-19 and then for renovation (ETV Bharat). No reporting found of opening hours, admission or accessibility on pages opened.

FUNDING
Dr. Bhau Daji Lad Mumbai City Museum: public-private partnership: Mumbai municipal corporation, the Jamnalal Bajaj Foundation and INTACH
- From 2003 the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, the Jamnalal Bajaj Foundation and INTACH refurbished the building, and the museum is run as a public-private partnership; it reopened on 4 January 2008 (Wikipedia). INTACH led the restoration, the Bajaj Foundation and the Corporation supported it (Piramal Realty blog).
- Amounts in an Outlook piece (the page is dated 5 February 2022; the year of the events is not stated): the Bajaj Foundation committed Rs 2 crore to match the civic outlay, the Corporation "recently created a corpus with Rs 10 crore" and the Bajaj Foundation added Rs 5 crore. A gallery was named after the donor. Its donor trustees were Rahul Bajaj, Niraj Bajaj and Minal Bajaj.
- 2023 to 2025 repairs: the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation paid for them. Knocksense (9 January 2025) gives Rs 2.8 crore; ETV Bharat (9 January 2025) quotes Municipal Commissioner Bhushan Gagrani saying the Corporation spent Rs 3 crore. The Wikipedia page opened gives no cost. Work began in March 2023 and ran 18 months (Knocksense).
- Governance: run as a trust; Tasneem Zakaria Mehta is honorary director and the board includes the mayor and municipal commissioner (Wikipedia). The board page of the museum site could not be opened.
- No reporting found of annual accounts or a donor list.

CRITICISM IN ART MEDIA
Overall: Little criticism of the museum's programme. The criticism found is about who controls it, from Indian news outlets rather than art magazines.
1. Outlook: Outlook News Desk, "Passage To Bombay" (page dated 5 February 2022)
URL: https://www.outlookindia.com/society/passage-to-bombay-news-236538
- Activist Shailesh Gandhi argues real control rests with nine permanent trustees because civic representatives keep changing, that exhibits were not valued before handover, and voices concern about public wealth passing into private hands.
- Mehta's appointment as managing trustee and honorary director added to unease. Mehta says the fears are unfounded.
2. Free Press Journal: FPJ Bureau, "Separate panel for Lad museum soon" (31 May 2019)
URL: https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/separate-panel-for-lad-museum-soon
- The Free Press Journal (31 May 2019) reported that the Corporation would appoint a separate panel to oversee the museum, with final authority resting with the civic body; who would head it was undecided.
- Unverified: the Corporation had long wanted to unseat Mehta over decisions it says she took without consulting it, from the Free Press Journal, which also quotes BJP's Manoj Kotak saying it had not been decided to remove her.
3. Scroll.in: Girish Shahane, "Why the Shiv Sena and its cousin want to undermine the good work at Mumbai's BDL museum" (2015)
URL: https://scroll.in/article/720585/scroll_in
- Defends the museum: a playground dispute over its expansion plot led the MNS and the Shiv Sena-run Corporation to cut the expansion budget and revoke a trust agreement that guided the museum's development.
- Criticises the municipal politicians, not the museum.

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the museum is clean: the Mumbai municipal corporation plus the Bajaj family foundation and INTACH, with no flagged funder found. The weak points are the long-running dispute over who controls the trust, the missing accounts and donor list, and conflicting cost figures for the 2023 to 2025 repairs. Worth watching is the board's make-up now and any renewed municipal move to take control.
