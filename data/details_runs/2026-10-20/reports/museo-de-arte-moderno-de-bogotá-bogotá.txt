MUSEO DE ARTE MODERNO DE BOGOTÁ (MAMBO): Bogotá, Colombia
ID: museo-de-arte-moderno-de-bogotá-bogotá
Website: https://www.mambogota.com (checked 2026-10-24; loads).

OVERVIEW
- Modern and contemporary art museum at Calle 24 #6-00 in central Bogotá, with about 4,000 works (Visit Bogotá).
- Opened in February 1963 under the art historian and critic Marta Traba, who became its director. Declared a District Cultural Heritage Site in 2001.
- Gloria Zea led it from 1969 to March 2016, followed by the artist Claudia Hakim (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Rogelio Salmona designed the current building in 1985, about 5,000 m² on four floors (Spanish Wikipedia).
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00, Sundays and holidays 12:00 to 17:00. No reporting found of current admission prices or accessibility.

FUNDING
Museo de Arte Moderno de Bogotá: mixed: Bogotá and national public money, donations, gala sponsors and earned income
- The museum's February 2026 statement lists "la Nación, el Distrito de Bogotá, becas, donaciones" and its own income: tickets, shop, rentals and events including the Gala MAMBO (El Espectador). Las2orillas (8 March 2026) calls it "una entidad privada" running on private and public resources.
- Gala sponsors, read from logo labels on the gala site (some names are incomplete). 2025: Fundación BAT under "Donación Privada"; Constructora Capital and Davivienda at corporate tables. 2024: Chopard and Bauer as main sponsors. 2023: Chopard, Banco de Bogotá, Postobón, Porsche and Scotia Wealth Management.
- Corporate membership "Mecenas MAMBO" starts at COP 15,000,000; the donations page names no donors (museum site). No reporting found of published accounts or a named board.

Red flags: Tobacco
- Fundación BAT: the 2025 gala sponsors page shows a logo labelled "Fundacion BAT" under a "Donación Privada" heading. The page gives no description, country or amount.
- BAT link: a sponsored article in La República (4 July 2025, marked "contenido patrocinado") says "La Fundación BAT en Colombia" runs social programmes and calls these entities "clave en la estrategia de sostenibilidad de BAT Group". The article does not say who owns the foundation, and no page opened says this is the foundation on the gala list.

Red flags: Protest over the dismissal of the chief curator
- Dismissal: on 6 February 2026 the board announced it would end the contract of chief curator Eugenio Viola, in post since 2019, citing a "revisión integral y permanente" of the museum (El Espectador). The museum later said talks on operational structure and curatorial resources reached no agreement.
- Viola says he formally alerted the board by the end of September 2025 to deteriorating working conditions (The Art Newspaper), and that the board ended his contract instead of ordering an internal review (ArtReview).
- Open letter: more than 140 artists, curators and cultural figures, including David Manzur and Óscar Muñoz, asked the board for a "pública, clara y argumentada" explanation (El Espectador, 23 February 2026).
- Staff: at least 19 employees left in the last year, a reported figure (Las2orillas, 8 March 2026). The museum said it has no formal evidence of mistreatment (El Espectador).
- Director: Martha Ortiz, director since 2024, resigned by a letter dated 8 April, which gives no reason (El Espectador, 9 April 2026). The Art Newspaper reports the director, whom it does not name, left amid accusations of harassment and a toxic work environment.
- Unverified: Revista Papel alleges that Ortiz ordered by WhatsApp on 25 July 2024 that Felipe Hernández Parias, dropped in the first screening for a design coordinator post, be given it. His mother directs IDARTES and said he won the contest. Las2orillas relays the allegation; the Papel article was not opened.

BOTTOM LINE
The museum mixes public money with gala sponsors and private donors. The one flagged name is Fundación BAT, listed as a 2025 gala donor: a sponsored article links the Colombian Fundación BAT to BAT Group, but no page opened says who owns the foundation on the gala list. The weak points are the chief curator's dismissal, staff departures and the director's resignation. Worth watching: whether accounts or a donor list are published.
