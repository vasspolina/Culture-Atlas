ID: gropius-bau

WIDER CONTEXT
- In October 2011 the museum removed a short video by Artur Żmijewski, 1 minute 32 seconds long, from the exhibition "Tür an Tür: Polen – Deutschland" (Der Tagesspiegel, 31 October 2011, headline "Der Gropius-Bau entfernt ein umstrittenes KZ-Video"; paywalled, only excerpts were read; Jüdische Allgemeine, 1 November 2011).
- Director Gereon Sievernich told dpa that the museum and its partner, the Royal Castle in Warsaw, had decided not to show the work "Aus Respekt vor den Opfern der Konzentrationslager und deren Nachfahren" (translated: "out of respect for the victims of the concentration camps and their descendants") (Jüdische Allgemeine, 1 November 2011).
- The taz reported that the film was pulled after an intervention by Hermann Simon, director of the Centrum Judaicum; Simon disputed the taz account; he said he had sent the museum his opinion in writing and called such a film "abstoßend" (translated: "repellent"), but said "Ich bin nicht der Mann, der zur Zensur aufruft" (translated: "I am not the man who calls for censorship") (Jüdische Allgemeine, 1 November 2011).
