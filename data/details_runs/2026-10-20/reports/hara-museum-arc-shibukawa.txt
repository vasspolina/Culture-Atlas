HARA MUSEUM ARC: Shibukawa, Japan
ID: hara-museum-arc-shibukawa
Website: https://www.haramuseum.or.jp/en/arc/about/

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary and traditional East Asian art museum at 2855-1 Kanai, Shibukawa, Gunma, on grounds adjacent to the Ikaho Green Bokujo recreational ranch (museum website, ART iT).
- Operated by the Foundation Arc-en-Ciel, which Toshio Hara established in 1977 and chairs. The foundation opened the Hara Museum of Contemporary Art in Shinagawa, Tokyo, in 1979 and opened the ARC as an annex in 1988 (museum website).
- The Tokyo museum, in a Western-style building first built as a residence in 1938, closed in January 2021. In a notice of November 2018 the foundation gave the age of the building, limits on accessibility and severe restrictions on reconstructing old buildings as its reasons (museum website, foundation notice on ART iT).
- The ARC was relaunched in April 2021 as the single venue, named after the French word for "rainbow" and the name of the foundation (museum website).
- The architect Arata Isozaki designed the ARC: three contemporary art galleries with pyramid-shaped skylit roofs, and the Kankai Pavilion in traditional shoin style, added in 2008 as a venue for the Hara Rokuro Collection of traditional East Asian art (museum website, foundation notice).
- Permanent outdoor installations on the grounds include works by Andy Warhol, Olafur Eliasson and Jean-Michel Othoniel (museum website).
- The museum says much of its energy comes from solar power generation (museum website).
- Visiting (ART iT listing for the 2024 exhibition): open 9:30 to 16:30, last entry 16:00, closed Thursdays except 2 May and during August. General admission 1,800 yen (1,500 online), students 1,000 or 800 yen, free for members; children of elementary and junior high age who live in Gunma enter free on Saturdays in term time.

FUNDING
Hara Museum ARC: foundation-run: the foundation set up by Toshio Hara, with membership fees and corporate supporting members
- The Foundation Arc-en-Ciel, which operates the museum, was established in 1977 by Toshio Hara (museum website).
- Membership: Friends 5,000 yen (single) or 8,000 yen (pair), individual supporting members 30,000, 50,000 or 200,000 yen, and corporate supporting memberships (museum membership page).
- Corporate supporting members as of November 2021: ARTEC, INC.; Ikaho Country Club Co., Ltd.; Mori Building Co., Ltd.; MRA Foundation; MUFG Bank, Ltd.; Nihon Tochisanrin Co., Ltd.; Takanawa High School; Takasago Thermal Engineering Co., Ltd. (museum membership page).

BOTTOM LINE
The museum is run by a foundation established in 1977 by Toshio Hara and offers paid membership. Eight corporate supporting members are listed as of November 2021, including MUFG Bank and Mori Building.
