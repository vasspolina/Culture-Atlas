MACBA (MUSEU D'ART CONTEMPORANI DE BARCELONA) – Barcelona, Spain
Website: https://www.macba.cat (not checked: fetch not permitted in this run, 2026-10-20)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art museum in Plaça dels Àngels, El Raval, in a white building by Richard Meier & Partners (about 120 x 35 m; construction about $35M). Opened 28 November 1995 (Wikipedia).
- Fundació MACBA created 1987; MACBA Consortium founded 1988.
- Directors include Bartomeu Marí (2008-2015, resigned March 2015 amid a censorship row over an Ines Doujak work), Ferran Barenblit (2015-2021), Elvira Dyangani Ose (2021-2026); Wikipedia lists Valentín Roma as director from 2026.

FUNDING
MACBA: mainly public, via a consortium.
- Consortium of the Generalitat de Catalunya, Ajuntament de Barcelona, Spanish Ministry of Culture and Fundació MACBA (Wikipedia, independent summary).
- In 2014 the city lent the museum a historic chapel, two halls and the surrounding square (Wikipedia).
