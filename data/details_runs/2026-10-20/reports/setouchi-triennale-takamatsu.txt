SETOUCHI TRIENNALE: Takamatsu, Japan
ID: setouchi-triennale-takamatsu
Website: https://setouchi-artfest.jp/en/support/grant-sponsor-donation/ (checked 2026-10-25)

OVERVIEW
- Contemporary art festival held every three years on islands and coastal areas of the Seto Inland Sea; visitors usually arrive through Takamatsu Port or Uno Port and take ferries to the islands (Japanese Wikipedia).
- The first edition was in 2010 and the sixth in 2025; the 2025 edition drew about 1.084 million visitors in total, after about 940,000 in 2010 (Japanese Wikipedia).
- English Wikipedia describes it as launched as a public-private partnership between the local prefectural and municipal governments and Benesse, an education publisher, and calls the Benesse Art Site the collective title for the Benesse-initiated art projects on Naoshima, Teshima and Inujima.
- The festival runs in spring, summer and autumn sessions across 17 islands and coastal areas; nine sites, including Naoshima, Teshima and Shodoshima, open in all three seasons. Its theme is the "Restoration of the Sea" (Setouchi Triennale).
- Fram Kitagawa, founder and director of the Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennial (English Wikipedia), is general director (Japanese Wikipedia, Setouchi Triennale). Japanese Wikipedia lists Soichiro Fukutake as general producer; the festival's page lists him as Honorary Adviser (Honorary Chairman, Fukutake Foundation).
- The Setouchi Triennale Executive Committee organises the festival. Its president is Toyohito Ikeda, Governor of Kagawa Prefecture; Hideaki Fukutake is chairman of the Fukutake Foundation (Setouchi Triennale).
- The volunteer nonprofit Koebi-tai has helped with daily operations since 2009 (English Wikipedia).

FUNDING
Setouchi Triennale: mixed: prefectural and local government, the Fukutake Foundation and family, Benesse, and corporate sponsors
- English Wikipedia lists the support as a combination of funds from the Fukutake Foundation, the Fukutake family's own investments, Benesse and government backing.
- The festival's 2025 sponsor page groups supporters as Partners (17), Corporate Sponsors, Corporate Donors (13) and Individual Donors (3), and is dated February 2025. Its Corporate Sponsors include Japan Tobacco, Cosmo Oil, Okayama Gas, Shikoku Electric Power, Chari-Loto, Kawasaki Heavy Industries and Mitsui & Co. (Setouchi Triennale).
- Kawasaki Heavy Industries is a diversified manufacturer. Its own results page for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 gives revenue of 170.7 billion yen for Powersports & Engine, 137.6 for Aerospace Systems, 86.9 for Energy Solution & Marine Engineering, 69.5 for Precision Machinery & Robot and 60.1 for Rolling Stock (Kawasaki Heavy Industries). The third-party site Kaisha Danmen gives fiscal 2025 revenue of 682.81 billion yen for Power Sports & Engines (29.5%) against 613.69 billion for Aerospace Systems (26.6%). Wikipedia states that it is a contractor for Japan's Ministry of Defence and has built the P-1 maritime patrol aircraft since 2007 and the C-2 transport since 2010.

Red flags: tobacco, Japan Tobacco
- Japan Tobacco (日本たばこ産業株式会社) is a Corporate Sponsor as of February 2025 (Setouchi Triennale).
- Wikipedia describes Japan Tobacco as a Japanese diversified tobacco company and one of the three largest international tobacco product manufacturers in the world; its brands include Camel and Winston (outside the USA) and Mevius. Wikipedia's infobox gives the Ministry of Finance 37.57% of the company.

Red flags: gambling, Chari-Loto
- Chari-Loto (株式会社チャリ・ロト) is a Corporate Sponsor as of February 2025 (Setouchi Triennale).
- Chari-Loto's own company page describes 株式会社チャリ・ロト as a wholly owned subsidiary of MIXI that runs the internet betting site Chari-Loto for keirin (bicycle racing) and auto race (motorcycle racing) tickets, sold since April 2008, and provides operating services for keirin venues including Takamatsu (chariloto.jp/company). PayPay Bank's page describes Chari-Loto as an internet betting service for keirin and auto race with an age limit of 20 (translated).

Red flags: fossil fuels, Cosmo Oil and Okayama Gas
- Cosmo Oil and Okayama Gas are Corporate Sponsors as of February 2025 (Setouchi Triennale).
- Wikipedia describes Cosmo Oil as a Japanese petrochemical company and Japan's third-biggest refiner by sales after Eneos and Idemitsu Kosan, with refineries at Ichihara, Yokkaichi and Sakai.
- Japanese Wikipedia describes Okayama Gas as a general gas utility and the largest city gas company in Okayama Prefecture, supplying Okayama City, the northeast part of Kurashiki, Akaiwa, Tamano, Soja and part of Hayashima (translated).

BOTTOM LINE
The money behind the Setouchi Triennale is a mix of prefectural and municipal governments, the Fukutake Foundation and family, Benesse, and a list of corporate sponsors. The Corporate Sponsors listed as of February 2025 include a tobacco maker (Japan Tobacco), an online betting operator (Chari-Loto), an oil refiner (Cosmo Oil) and a city gas utility (Okayama Gas).
