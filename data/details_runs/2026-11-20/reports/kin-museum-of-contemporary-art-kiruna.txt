KIN MUSEUM OF CONTEMPORARY ART (KONSTMUSEET I NORR): Kiruna, Sweden
ID: kin-museum-of-contemporary-art-kiruna
Website: https://kinmuseum.se (checked 2026-11-20)

OVERVIEW
- Norrbotten County's art museum for contemporary art, on floors 2 to 5 of the Kristallen town hall at Stadshustorget 1 in Kiruna's new centre, with a free art workshop on the ground floor (kinmuseum.se; e-flux directory).
- It opened in 2018; Kin is short for Konstmuseet i norr, and it takes the Barents Region as its starting point, with exhibitions and projects across Norrbotten's fourteen municipalities (e-flux directory).
- The town hall, designed by Henning Larsen with LKAB as client, opened on 22 November 2018 as the first major building of the relocated town, which the mining company is moving because the iron ore mine is causing the ground to subside (ARQA).
- Its first years brought a roof that kept leaking, psychosocial work environment problems and complaints from local artists that it was not visible enough (SVT, 2023).
- Maria Lind, a former director of Tensta konsthall, curator at Moderna Museet and cultural counsellor at the Swedish embassy in Moscow, became director in autumn 2023 and gave the museum its new name (SVT, 2023 and 2025).
- Visitors rose from about 3,000 a year to 13,000 in 2024 (SVT, 2025).
- Current programmes include "Om det fula och det vackra" with Johan Turi, Outi Pieski and Johanna Minde, guided tours of work by the Sámi artist Britta Marakatt-Labba, and the Konstfesten 2027 art festival (kinmuseum.se).
- Visiting: free parking near Kristallen and a disabled parking bay at the entrance; lifts reach all floors, there is a wheelchair-accessible toilet, and wheelchairs can be borrowed from the town hall's customer service (kinmuseum.se).

FUNDING
Kin: public: Kiruna Municipality and Norrbotten Region
- Kiruna Municipality pays SEK 4.4 million a year and Norrbotten Region SEK 6.6 million a year, and politicians from both sit on its board (SVT, 2025).
- In 2024 the museum spent SEK 2 million more than the two owners gave, drawing down its own equity, even counting the withdrawn municipal grant as restored (SVT, February 2025).
- It is a partner in the EU project ICCE (2024 to 2026) on accessible cultural centres, led by Lapland University of Applied Sciences and funded by the Interreg Aurora programme, the Regional Council of Lapland and Region Västerbotten (kinmuseum.se).

Red flags: 2024 cut in Kiruna's grant against the region's objection
- Late in 2023 Kiruna Municipality halved its contribution for 2024, cutting SEK 2.2 million, although Norrbotten Region had expressly opposed the cut (SVT, January 2025).
- The region's auditors found the cut only in January 2025; regional director Ulrika Sundquist wrote to the municipality demanding the money be restored (SVT).

Red flags: 2025 auditors' criticism of travel costs
- The auditors issued a formal remark against the museum's board because staff and invited artists made too many and too long trips, to Europe and Australia, with unclear purpose and incorrect approval (SVT, June 2025).
- Travel and hospitality cost SEK 1.1 million, a tenth of the museum's income; Lind said she had not imagined the rules would be read so literally (SVT).

BOTTOM LINE
Kin runs on Swedish local and regional public money, with no flagged funder. Its weak points are the owners' quarrel over its budget and the audit finding; the mining company's role behind its building is worth watching.
