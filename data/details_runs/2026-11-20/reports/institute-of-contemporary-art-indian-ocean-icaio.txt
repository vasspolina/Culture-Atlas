INSTITUTE OF CONTEMPORARY ART INDIAN OCEAN (ICAIO): Port Louis, Mauritius
ID: institute-of-contemporary-art-indian-ocean-icaio
Website: none working (checked 2026-11-20; the institute's former domain, icaio.org, redirects to an unrelated site)

OVERVIEW
- Gallery at 51 Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Street, Port Louis, in a historic stone building (Lonely Planet France).
- Boston College describes it as "an independent, nonprofit foundation that is a platform for art education and public art programming in Mauritius" (Boston College news).
- Its stated aim is "faire de Maurice le carrefour artistique et culturel de l'océan Indien", in Lonely Planet's French text (translation: to make Mauritius the artistic and cultural crossroads of the Indian Ocean).
- Exhibitions change about every three months, four a year, and show Mauritian, Indian and South African artists (Lonely Planet France; Holidify, a travel listing).
- Past shows: "Stories Retold" by Nalini Malani; "I love you Sugar Kane" in 2016, with a text by the curator Zasha Colah, co-founder of the Clark House Initiative in Bombay; "Visions of Port Louis: Constantin et ses amis", curated by Bernard Lehembre; and in 2020 "Walk of Life", a solo exhibition by Portia Zvavahera (Holidify; Le Mauricien, 3 April 2016; Kettle's Yard).
- The founder, Salim Currimjee, is a Mauritian artist and architect, born in Port Louis in 1963, who studied biology and art history at Williams College and architecture at Rice University (Latitudes; The Third Dot).
- Currimjee was a committee member at Tate Modern from 2010 to 2017 and has sat on the acquisition committee of the Fonds Régional d'Art Contemporain de la Réunion since 2018 (The Third Dot). With Prasannan Parthasarathi he curated "Indian Ocean Current: Six Artistic Narratives" at the McMullen Museum of Art, Boston College, in 2020 (Boston College news).
- Visiting: open Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:00 (Lonely Planet France).

FUNDING
Institute of Contemporary Art Indian Ocean: nonprofit foundation: set up by Salim Currimjee
- Founder: Currimjee established the institute in May 2015 (Lonely Planet France; Boston College news).

BOTTOM LINE
The institute is its founder's foundation, and the public record of it comes from partner museums and travel listings. Watch for a new website, published accounts or a named board.
