ID: rockbund-art-museum

WIDER CONTEXT
- A homoerotic papercut by the artist Xiyadie was removed from "An Opera for Animals", a joint presentation with Hong Kong's Para Site that opened at the museum on 21 June 2019. The Art Newspaper reported the removal in a piece on censorship of queer art in Shanghai that summer; it does not say who removed the work. The same piece says a 2016 Rockbund exhibition "failed to mention the artist Felix Gonzalez-Torres's sexuality entirely" (The Art Newspaper, Lisa Movius, 1 July 2019).
