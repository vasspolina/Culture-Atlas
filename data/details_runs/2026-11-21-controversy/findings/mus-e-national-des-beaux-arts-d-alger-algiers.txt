WIDER CONTEXT
- The 1962 transfer of the museum's works to the Louvre was decided by Christian Fouchet, the French Republic's high commissioner in Algeria (Académie des sciences d'outre-mer, Stéphane Richemond, review of Élisabeth Cazenave and Jean-Christian Serna, "Le patrimoine artistique français de l'Algérie", Abd-el-Tif, 2017).
- From independence Algeria demanded the return of the works under the Évian Accords; "La polémique enfla et des pourparlers tendus ne prirent fin qu'avec « la restitution » des œuvres qui eut lieu en 1970" ("The dispute grew and tense talks ended only with the 'restitution' of the works, which took place in 1970", translated) (Académie des sciences d'outre-mer review).
- The review states that the works had varied legal status, some bought by the Algerian museums from their own budgets and others deposited by the French state, by museums or by private collectors, which made the negotiations complicated (Académie des sciences d'outre-mer review).
