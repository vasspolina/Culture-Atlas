ID: national-art-museum-of-china

WIDER CONTEXT
- The 1989 exhibition "China/Avant-Garde" at the museum (called the National Art Gallery in the source) opened on 5 February 1989 with official permission; its agreement barred performance art and sexually explicit content. After Xiao Lu shot her installation "Dialogue" at the opening, the exhibition was shut for three days, reopened, and was closed two days later after a bomb threat, so it was open for "eight days and two hours of the planned fifteen days" (Bruce Altshuler, excerpt from "Biennials and Beyond", Phaidon Artspace).
- After the show the gallery fined the sponsors "for the many transgressions" and barred them from sponsoring exhibitions at the museum for two years (Phaidon Artspace).
